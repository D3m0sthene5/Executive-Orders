# Measurement contract — candidate alternatives, not a retroactive codebook change

Primary estimand A (**present normative operation**): Among authentic, distinct presidential executive-order instruments of defined issuance years, what fraction still supplies an attributable directive, permission, constitutive status or incorporated original provision with continuing applicability at a fixed cutoff? A spent repeal has ended normative operation if its command to repeal was executed, even though the repealed text remains repealed. Distinguish **constitutive statuses** that remain legally operative from **completed transactions**. This rule must be applied to both land and nonland categories with an explicit reason.

Alternative estimand B (**continuing EO-traceable legal consequences**): Does the EO still cause a presently cognizable legal condition, including completed modifications such as repeals or earlier dispositions? To avoid trivially classifying all old actions as alive, establish a material and traceable contemporary legal condition, a counterfactual test against the relevant successor law, and a principled cutoff for independent reenactment or replacement. This definition changes some NOs and cannot be represented as merely a more permissive quality threshold.

Alternative estimand C (**policy-lineage survival**): Does similar substantive policy still exist under any successor governmental authority? Distinct from EO validity/attribution; report separately. A statute repeating a policy need not mean the EO continues.

Mandatory episode fields: `instrument_document_id`, `operative_clause_id`, `scope`, `source_effective_date`, `conditional_entry`, `ever_operative` (yes/no/undetermined), `operative_interval_start`, `operative_interval_end`, `end_basis`, `legal_event_id`, `cessation_first_cause` (proved/interval/bound/unknown), `later_revival`, `source_attribution`, `collateral_rights`, `judicial_scope`, `evidence_grade`, `causal_reason`. For first-cessation research, report natural expiration according to *original* terms separately from automatic expiration due to a *later-added* condition and statutory/judicial/administrative termination. A given whole-order first cessation date must aggregate clause intervals; for mixed orders, report provision episodes to avoid erasing early partial cessations.

The 60-case blind sample needs cross-stratum weights; the first eight are targeted, source-based but still not certified whole-text legal audits. Unweighted agreement is not an error-rate estimate. Require two independent legal reviewers and hide baseline answers until each decision is frozen and hashed. After-key followups are separate records.

**Adoption gate:** legal examples and historical estates/treaty authority must survive blind analysis, externally qualified attorney review and published sensitivity counts. Until then codebook 2.0.0 remains unchanged and the current 2,515 statuses remain provisional.
