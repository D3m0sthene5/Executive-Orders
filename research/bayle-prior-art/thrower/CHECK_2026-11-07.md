# Independent check of the Thrower folder, 2026-11-07: findings and resolutions

An independent Opus check reproduced the outputs byte for byte and raised the eleven points below. "Fixed" means the script
was changed, `scripts/run_all.sh` was rerun (project commit pinned to `e146fa4b268b5f42c18226969c48329fa1d3d441`) and the
README, `SAMPLE15_hand_checked.csv` and every count were updated. "Disputed" means I kept my figure and give the evidence.
The checker's scratch scripts were read, not trusted: its `t85.py`, `fp.py` and `rev.py` logic was rerun against the pinned cache to
compare numbers.
