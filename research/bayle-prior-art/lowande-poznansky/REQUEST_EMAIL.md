Dear Professor Lowande,

I am a first-year law student at NYU School of Law. I am running a research project that tries to determine, for
every U.S. executive order, whether it still has legal effect and when and how it stopped having effect. We are working
from the primary texts, and we keep formal revocation separate from whether an order was still operating.

Your article with Professor Poznansky and the Supplementary Information are the closest prior work we have found, and I
have learned a lot from them. The replication deposit (doi:10.7910/DVN/TZ2QKY) says the underlying order dataset and
the SI materials are available on request and asks that errors be reported to you, so I am writing to ask for two things.

1. The order-level file behind `orders.Rda`, with the executive order number for each row, and the `codebook.pdf`
   that the readme lists (it is not in the public deposit). A CSV is ideal. We would cite it as you direct.
2. The orders behind the corrections described in SI Section A: the 20 revocations missing from Thrower's data, the 65
   orders credited to the wrong revoking president, and, if easy, the one order coded as revoked that was not, the 8
   supersessions and the 37 "nullified" cases. EO numbers and the revoking instrument are all we need.

Two small things we noticed, in case they are useful to you. The article text gives January 18, 2021 as the end of the
sample (p. 351), while the SI says the first half of 2021 and the deposit has 64 orders for 2021. And in the CSV
version of Thrower's list the column `revokedbyotherthaneo` is named differently from the PDF codebook, which lists
`revokedbyeo12553`; if you know which is right, that would help us read his data correctly.

In return, we are glad to share what we find. We are checking order dates, numbers and revocation records against the
official sources and will send you any corrections to the order-level data, with the evidence, in whatever form is
easiest for you. We will credit your data in anything we publish. If you would rather not share the file, a pointer to
anything already posted would still help.

Thank you for your time, and for making the data available.

Sincerely,
