## Ordering details
Sources: https://www.archives.gov/research/order/microfilm-pubs.html and https://www.archives.gov/research/order (both fetched).
- NARA sells **digitized rolls** of microfilm. Minimum information: publication number (M1118) and roll number(s).
  Roll numbers must be known to order, so the roll list is the bottleneck.
- Online: NARA Catalog / Order Online at https://eservices.archives.gov/orderonline (problems: orderonline@nara.gov).
- Mail: print the "Microfilm Order Form" (PDF) or send a letter with publication and roll numbers, name, address, daytime phone.
  Payment to National Archives Trust Fund, Cashier, Form 72 Order, 8601 Adelphi Road, College Park, MD 20740-6001.
- Phone/fax (credit card only): 866-272-6272; fax 202-357-5906. Purchase orders allowed for libraries and agencies.
- **Price: $125 per roll including shipping (U.S.), $135 international.** Microfiche $12/sheet U.S. The fee schedule page
  (https://www.archives.gov/research/order/fees.html, dated April 2018, reviewed 2018-09-18) also says $125 per digitized roll.
  Pages say prices may change without notice, so treat as "published, possibly stale". Allow up to 90 days.
- No M1118-specific order page was found.
