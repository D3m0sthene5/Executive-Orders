# X1 type definitions for blind labelling

Pick the ONE type that best describes what the order itself does (its main operative act). If none fits, use `unknown`. Class in brackets.

- `nra_code_approval` [swept]: approves/amends an NRA code of fair competition or NRA agreement, or administers NIRA/NRA bodies
- `canal_zone` [swept]: concerns the Canal Zone / Panama Canal government, regulations or personnel
- `retirement_exemption` [spent]: exempts a named person from compulsory retirement (or retains them past retirement age)
- `cs_waiver_individual` [spent]: lets named individual(s) be appointed, reinstated, transferred, retained or examined without regard to a civil-service rule or requirement
- `tax_return_inspection` [spent]: opens tax returns to inspection by a committee or official
- `alien_property_sale` [spent]: authorizes the Alien Property Custodian to sell/dispose of/reject bids on specific property
- `labor_hours_suspension` [spent]: suspends the eight-hour law for specific work or contracts
- `fund_allotment` [spent]: allocates, allots or transfers a sum of appropriated funds
- `closing_mourning` [spent]: closes offices / excuses employees on a day, or orders mourning or half-staff display
- `public_water_reserve` [land]: creates/changes/revokes a public water reserve (springs, water holes)
- `power_site_reserve` [land]: creates/changes/revokes a power-site or reservoir-site reserve
- `stock_driveway` [land]: stock driveway withdrawal
- `bird_refuge` [land]: bird reservation, game or wildlife refuge (establish, enlarge, diminish, withdraw for)
- `lighthouse_reservation` [land]: land reserved for lighthouse purposes
- `national_forest_boundary` [land]: national forest boundary change, or land/admin site in a national forest
- `mineral_withdrawal` [land]: petroleum, coal, oil shale, phosphate, potash or helium land withdrawal/restoration
- `military_naval_reservation` [land]: military or naval reservation (establish, enlarge, diminish, transfer, land for military/naval use)
- `indian_reservation_trust` [land]: Indian reservation lands or extension of the trust period on Indian allotments
- `land_withdrawal_general` [land]: any other withdrawal, reservation or restoration of public land (resurvey, classification, townsite, school, cemetery, use of a bureau, etc.)
- `property_transfer` [spent]: transfers a vessel, records, equipment or other non-land property between agencies
- `own_terms_expiry` [spent]: extends/continues a body, time limit or effective date until a stated date
- `patent_signing_designation` [standing]: designates a clerk to sign the President's name to land patents
- `defensive_sea_area` [standing]: defensive sea area or airspace reservation
- `state_office_permission` [standing]: permits federal employees to hold state/local office or take part in local elections
- `cs_rules_amendment` [standing]: amends civil service rules or schedules, or other civil-service matter for a class of positions
- `philippines` [swept]: concerns the Philippine Islands (government, personnel, lands)
- `wwi_emergency` [swept]: First World War emergency measure (1917-1921: war agencies, enemy, export control, war housing, etc.)
- `tariff_customs` [standing]: customs districts, ports of entry, tariff, consular fees
- `consular_foreign_service` [standing]: consular or diplomatic service, Foreign Service regulations or personnel
- `territories` [standing]: other order about Alaska, Hawaii, Puerto Rico, Virgin Islands, Guam, Samoa
- `pay_allowances` [standing]: pay, salary, allowances, per diem, compensation rates
- `organization_agency` [standing]: creates, abolishes, reorganizes or transfers an agency, board, commission or land district/office
- `regulations_issuance` [standing]: prescribes or amends general regulations not covered above
- `personnel_designation` [standing]: appoints or designates a person to a position or acting role
- `unknown`: none of the above
