## Family method counterexamples

1. EO 12553 expressly describes its targets as “no longer necessary”. Its 1986 revocation is an outer bound/candidate event, not proof that all clauses first ceased on that date. The acting rule remains on an APP mirror with `original_custody_verified: false`; keep that false until the owner's official-fixity condition is met.
2. EO 10010, 13 FR 6104–6105 (PDF pp.6–7; SHA-256 `848655600147d7404f229d58bf1507cc16f59274e83cbd083354952a6bf12b2b`), contains creation, member-disinterest, reporting and post-report status-quo obligations. Its bundle's two draft clauses require completeness review. EO 10015 amends its carrier/union lists and expressly applies as of October 18, 1948 despite November 5 signing (13 FR 6601; SHA-256 `144846d6cda2b8c9f6722e6a42d7acdde89ef946502246330f45784df894f609`).

4. EO 12358 section 4(b) originally ended the Drunk Driving Commission one year after April 14, 1982 (47 FR 16311–16312; SHA-256 `13e95375ccb33e30cc4a87fa5b5a66482f2e60ac1ab4ff5138faee851bd95894`).
