# P4 own-terms survey: five source-grounded patterns

The five principal decided orders are outside the target lists of the original
five P1 rules. Each was started from its case bundle, then its complete saved
official derivative was read. Five proposal files are in `pattern-rules/`.
They are research proposals, with no first-end claim or certificate. No new
claim batch was generated, and no original-custody flag was set.

| Target | Controlling source read | Own-terms pattern | What prevents first-end acceptance |
|---|---|---|---|
| EO13037, Commission to Study Capital Budgeting | EO13108 operative amendment, 63 FR 69175, PDF page 1 | Replaces the original report-plus-30-days expiration with **September 30, 1999**. | Actual earlier report/cessation, later changes, current-law note, compensation residuals and custody remain open. Original membership and reporting clauses also changed earlier under EO13066. |
| EO13038, Digital Television Broadcasters advisory committee | Original section4, 62 FR 12066, PDF page 2 | Actual report submission plus 30 days, unless extended. | Actual submission and extension history are necessary; successive report deadlines do not establish termination. |
| EO13090, Celebration of Women in American History commission | Original section3(d), 63 FR 36152, PDF page 2 | Actual final report submission plus 60 days. | EO13136 changes the report deadline and recites extending life; actual report, possible prior expiration/revival and expense residuals remain open. |
| EO13147, Complementary and Alternative Medicine Policy commission | Original section3(e), 65 FR 13234, PDF page 2 | Two years from March 7, 2000, unless extended beforehand; candidate March 7, 2002. | Must exclude extensions and earlier cessation. EO13167 changes membership only; original membership clause has a separate earlier amendment. |
| EO13369, Federal Tax Reform advisory panel | EO13386 section2, 70 FR 58289, PDF page 1 | Replaces report-plus-30-days expiration with **November 15, 2005**. | Earlier and later changes, current-law sources, support/travel residuals and custody remain open. Section6 protections/disclaimer require separate scope treatment. |

The fixed dates above come from actual sunset amendments. Neither was
calculated from a report deadline. Two additional calendar-looking leads
were rejected as complete-order shortcuts: EO13188 inserts January 11, 2003
into only the parent EO13111 committee provision, while the parent has
other functions and a current-law lead; EO12852's conditional two-year body
term does not resolve its independent revocation of EO12737. EO12668 also
has independent continuing Counsel-to-the-President implementation reports.

Twenty original/acting-order text witnesses are preserved in `saved-texts/`.
Every pattern quote is an exact substring and carries the source URL,
downloaded-PDF hash, derivative path/hash and page. Original page images and
published GPO fixity remain unreviewed. The current EO13446 saved witness
has PDF hash `d0e39c4ac971c4989b7838e73e7408540a41d68a6a834b613a8cf83f70267fd4`,
which differs from an older hash in the target bundle. Its cleanup revocation
is effective September 30, 2007, despite September 28 signing. The witnesses
must not be silently conflated.

`LIMITED_LOCAL_SWEEP.json` records hashes and number-reference hits across
86 locally saved text witnesses, with 20 target/source hit pairs. This sparse
checkout is not the complete saved corpus. The scan includes duplicates and
neighboring-page text, and the references remain unadjudicated. It does not
close the counterevidence step or establish absence of an extension.

The current production `family-rules` engine accepts only revocation,
rescission and supersession mechanisms. It cannot apply an actual-report
event or a conditional own-terms sunset. Consequently these custom-schema
pattern proposals remain outside `research/readiness/rules/`; no mechanism
was mislabeled and no engine change was made during this legal production
assignment. The coordinator can use them for a later narrowly scoped road
repair and then source-specific legal review.

Independent review is recorded in the parent package's `P4_REVIEW.md` and
the follow-up rule review. Actual model billing tokens are unavailable.
