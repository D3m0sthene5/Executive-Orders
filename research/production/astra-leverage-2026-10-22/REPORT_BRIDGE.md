# Bounded report-fact bridge

Source baseline: main `46a29e15d6ed4c252416b28e0ccc0590fcf6abc2`.

The Pullman packets retain report transmittal text that the current dossier cannot cite as a factual event. This change uses NORTON's existing registry and separate factual-answer and clause-application reviews. It exposes validated `FACT:` source spans only for explicit `later_events` selection. It creates no source store and changes no legal status, census, original grade, human certification, or NORTON twelve-order limit.

The claim-kit consumer verifies unique exact registry records, question schema and identity, answer schema, evidence, source bytes/text/hashes/quotes, independent answer review and independent clause-application review. The assembler and independent checker repeat those checks and refuse using a factual citation as an original, first end, current predicate, or savings source. The claimed operative text and its exact source span must also match the reviewed application: keeping a scope ID while changing clause boundaries does not preserve applicability. The readiness tracker also revalidates current facts: an unchanged claim fails when its source or review changes even if a checker receipt still matches the claim hash. The cited date, event meaning, entity and clause scope cannot be substituted, and removing the factual marker while retaining a `FACT:` evidence ID is rejected. Removing both bridge identifiers leaves an ordinary manually authored citation subject to the existing lifecycle review; this bridge does not forbid every other legal use of the same report.

Two producer candidates are prepared under `research/readiness/factual/astra-pullman-20261022/`: EO 10032 (PEB 68, transmittal letter dated April 11, 1949) and EO 10056 (PEB 75, transmittal letter dated July 29, 1949). These are explicitly `other_event` / `transmittal_letter_date_only`; neither is classified as delivery or receipt. Exact text spans, source hashes and acquisition receipts are preserved. The letters state that the reports are submitted herewith. The proposed answers explain that this text alone does not establish receipt, delivery, standstill expiration or legal cessation.

Both candidate applications pass the existing source-span and identity checks. Both candidate evidence records fail the existing `source_bytes_not_held` check: their raw PDFs are referenced only by the source-ladder download-cache paths and are not present in this checkout or tracked tree. No factual review or applicability review has been authored. Consequently the bridge emits **zero citable real facts**, **zero lifecycle claims**, and **zero additional ready dossiers**. Exact results are in `REPORT_BRIDGE_CANDIDATES.json`. Source custody remains a prerequisite, not a reporting waiver.
