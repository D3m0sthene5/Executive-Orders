# Panama 1922 control incident, 2026-11-08

The 50-order m001 exploratory batch planted three blind controls on EO:1410: wrong PDF leaf 119, neighboring EO:1409 on leaf 120, and an opening operative sentence changed from printed **Section 1** to **Section 2**. The first gpt-6-luna reviewer caught the first two but accepted the third. The image on leaf 120 (printed p. 118) unambiguously reads “Section 1. It shall be unlawful for any person to construct or place any sign...” Printed Section 2 is the sixty-day effective-date clause. This is a missed substantive one-digit change.

The Panama lane stopped immediately. Its eleven reported passes and two reported failures were quarantined as leads; no page-check credit or record was filed. Two fresh blind control-only retests, one gpt-6.1-sol and one gpt-6-luna with explicit sentence alignment, caught all three controls. A restarted 13-order full-batch gpt-6-luna reader again missed the same Section 1/2 change. This shows that the control-only retest did not establish reliability for the longer assignment. The second full-batch reader, gpt-6.1-sol, was stopped as soon as that miss was reported; it had caught all three controls but had not submitted full order verdicts. Neither restarted arm supplied an admissible 13-order set.

Likely failure mode: the cheap model saw that the page contained a Section 2 and matched that label without aligning the quoted operative sentence to the exact printed sentence. The initial full-batch assignment also diluted attention over thirteen orders. Corrective protocol: run the three controls first in a focused context, require the exact printed opening of each matching sentence, and only after all controls pass assign small blocks of real orders. Every real order still needs two independent blind reads. A future missed control stops the lane again. No order from the aborted Panama lane receives pass credit.

The images and held worklist are in the local work directory. Model token and dollar telemetry were not exposed; costs remain unknown rather than estimated as measured spend. No human-review or location-verification label was granted.
