## Findings that changed the plan

- The coordinator's list missed EO 7915. EO 7915, not EO 9830, is the first full restatement after 1903, and it
  took effect 1939-02-01. It kept Schedules A and B. EO 8043, effective the same day, then restated those schedules.
- EO 250 (1904) revoked a special rule of 1902. This shows special rules were treated as outliving EO 209, so the
  special-rule members stay open.
- Several pre-1896 orders that WPA calls "Civil Service Rules amended to classify" are in fact section 6
  directions to department heads (EO 54, 69 and 70). A text test routes them to the open branch B08. The first
  version of that regex missed EO 69's OCR "classi- fications" and placed it on a `no` branch; the regex was fixed.
- Under the brief's owner rule (an order that only amends another order is spent on issue), the dates of the
  amending members do not depend on the later restatements. The restatements now matter only for the general
  revisions themselves and for the latest end of the inserted text, which is given in the basis. This choice is
  flagged for the coordinator.
- Held texts are sometimes wrong. EO 3797's held text is a Shelley reinstatement order, and EO 72's and EO 79's
  slices are empty. The text test keeps such members out of the `no` branches unless the period-wide revocation
  by EO 89 decides them anyway (B12b).

## Tried and rejected

- Using EO 9830 or EO 10577 as the ending instrument for 1903-1936 amendments. Rejected: EO 7915 and EO 8043
  restated the text earlier (1939), and EO 9830's revocation list names no member.
- Dating amendments to the replacement of the rules (interval to 1896, 1903 or 1939). Rejected as the primary rule
  in favour of the owner's spent-on-issue rule. It is kept as the alternative in the doctrine's "Points for the
  coordinator".
- A `yes` or continuity lead for exceptions that still exist in 5 CFR 213.3102 (chaplains; presidential appointees
  without Senate confirmation). Rejected: the present text rests on OPM's 1982 republication under statute and EO
  12364, and the members' amendments were spent on issue.
- Deciding class orders as spent single acts. Rejected: the coverage they create continues, and the 1939 rules saved
  status granted by such orders.
