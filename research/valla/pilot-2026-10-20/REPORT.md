## Quality findings

The deterministic scan covered **331 active claims**, preserving exact prior-version
references and using existing filename/line precedence. It emitted **11 exact span
leads across eight claims**. Numbers and entity names are retained during
normalization. No model was called by the compiler, no similarity cluster decided
law, and no synthetic item entered production.

| Sample | Cases | Matched cases | Bounded examination |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Owner's motivating cases | 5 | 5 | Five particular first-end assertions require revalidation |
| Additional matched controls | 3 | 3 | One descriptive match; two additional mixed/finite-duty review leads remain unresolved |
| Separate frozen sample | 8 | 0 | No new confirmed instance of the seed-method defect in the limited independent preliminary review |

`SAMPLE.json` freezes exact claim hashes and pattern version before the eight-case
sample was read. Its reproducible identity-hash selection excludes seeds and matched
controls. `SAMPLE_REVIEW.jsonl` records every result, including partial claims and
existing material gaps. This review examined rationales and the quoted operative
footprints; it did not redo exhaustive whole-lifecycle source research for all eight.
Consequently, missed defects remain possible and general recall, precision and legal
accuracy are **not estimated**. Recovering five constructed seed examples is not a
validation of general accuracy.

Three of the eleven matched spans say only that no express sunset or
termination-on-performance clause exists (EO 11868, 13101 and 13148). The reviewer
rejected interpreting those spans alone as an assertion that performance is
irrelevant. Two occur in claims with a separate categorical first-end inference;
EO 11868 explicitly acknowledges unknown report delivery and possible winding up.
This is a concrete false-grouping limitation, not three proven erroneous legal
conclusions. Negative assertions can also match; the tests preserve them as leads.
The separate sample supplied no newly confirmed missed seed-method case under this
bounded review, not proof of zero missed cases in the corpus.

## Correction work and measured costs

Three identical real tasks (EO 13792, 13101 and 13148) were replayed through the
pinned existing batch compiler and Valla-assisted preparation, alternating arm
order. **All six attempts remain blocked and retained.** A five-case versioned
correction packet uses the ordinary claim-kit format, routes existing claims to
owner review, seals the Valla queue/attestations and leaves every approval false.
The packet is generated by `pilot.py` and retained in the GitHub run artifact.
`LOCAL_INTEGRATED.json` records its local hash and path; the GitHub receipt pins the
actual CI version. No claim, reservation, human review, status or census was changed.

The second local replay measured:

| Machine phase, three tasks total | Existing workflow | Valla-assisted |
|---|---:|---:|
| Producer packet preparation | 0.911 s | 1.871 s |
| Apply blocked correction records | 0.030 s | 0.917 s |
| Validate/integrate blocked records | 0.028 s | 0.938 s |

Both arms prepared real correction work with the same outstanding research. These
are machine timings, affected by order and cache state; they exclude unavailable
active producer, reviewer and coordination costs. No independent whole-case
replacement review was completed in either arm. Model token totals, billing and
complete total costs are unavailable. `LOCAL_COMPARISON.json` retains null fields
for them rather than implying zero. The initial failed replay and its six arm
records remain in `FAILED_LOCAL_V1_COMPARISON.json` and `ATTEMPTS.jsonl`; they are
additional overhead, not discarded from a claimed saving. Implementation, pilot,
coordination and initial-failure costs have not been amortized. **No total saving or
break-even is established.**

Junto ran its registered `F-valla` finder and created owner-seeded candidate
`valla_rework_salvage`, with status **unverified**, no verified-savings line and score
zero. No `add-test` was supplied with invented session totals. Its eventual test
must complete matched corrections and count preparation, review, correction,
coordination, integration and failed work in both arms, with quality outcomes.
The inherited Junto writer label is tool metadata, not a component reviewer identity.
Quality improvements and efficiency remain separate outcomes.

## Consumers and controlled pairs

The local integrated run executed the actual claim-checker on the five frozen
existing claims: five structural passes, zero all-checks-passed, two mechanical
quotation failures and three mechanically unverified claims in the sparse custody
context. Full per-claim failure logs were retained; Valla neither repaired another
producer's claim nor inferred that an existing mechanical pass would settle the
methodology. The actual tracker ran against unchanged canonical inputs and reported
230 ready. Valla's context is advisory preparation and does not change that count.
The bounded GitHub workflow repeats both consumers using its fuller held inputs;
its receipt, rather than this local subset, records the final CI results.

Local suites pass: 18 Valla tests, 176 claim-kit tests (three skips), 32 existing
Mountweazel tests, two paired-fixture tests, 19 Junto tests, 28 checker tests and 22
tracker tests. Isolation check: zero problems. The two fixed Junto allocation tests
now use their named three-finder fixture, so adding a finder does not silently change
the test's example budget. Scoring/allocation rules are unchanged.

Mountweazel now generates six controlled pairs, twelve fictional items. They test
layout invariance, deadline versus delivery, removal of performance evidence,
recurrence, omitted earlier events and savings exceptions. The cases stipulate
fictional rules, so the answer key resolves evidence behavior without deciding
contested EO law. Generated items and answer keys remain outside the repository,
the production gate rejects them, edited keys fail and missing/duplicate verdicts
are refused. The scoring harness was exercised with explicit expected and incorrect
labels; that is not a measured model-reviewer catch rate. **Reviewer blinding was
not technically enforced.** Independent reviewer sensitivity and a fully measured
correction-cost experiment remain outstanding, with no automatic legal promotion.
