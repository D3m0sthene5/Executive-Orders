# First cessation: bounded methodological examination

Sol examined the five frozen claims independently of their named Claude producers. This review confirms a defect in the support offered for particular clause-level first-end assertions. It establishes neither a replacement date nor the end of an entire order. The current packet protocol requires examination of actual performance and earlier cessation as well as deadlines, continuing commands and savings. The schemas enforce completeness and date relationships; their mechanical acceptance does not establish a sound first-cessation inference.

The shared inference under examination treats the absence of an express sunset, combined with a later by-number revocation, as sufficient to assign that revocation as every clause's first end. The correct observation that a deadline alone is not proof of completion does not supply the missing performance inquiry. A by-number search of other executive orders may miss a delivered report, issued guidance, completed establishment, statutory change or administrative implementation.

Two source-backed alternatives must remain available for each specified duty. Actual delivery or performance may exhaust a finite command, subject to retained commands and later effects. A provision may also impose continuing implementation, maintenance, revision or recurring duties, so a single completed act may leave normative work outstanding. Resolve the original text's scope and the controlling method before choosing between these readings. Where the codebook does not settle the distinction, record the alternatives and retain the gap. Neither a due date nor the existence of a report establishes its delivery date.

For EO 13792 section 2(e), inspect dated delivery evidence and the relationship between the final report and the review. For EO 13101 section 403(a), distinguish preparing guidance from its continuing use; section 302(a)(1) explicitly calls for revision as necessary, and section 302(a)(2) is biennial. For EO 13148 section 403(a), distinguish program establishment from continuing operation, including independent statutory obligations retained in section 902(c). For EO 13783 section 2(e), obtain agency-specific finalization and extension evidence, including section 2(c)'s no-responsibility route; the self-executing revocations and continuing implementation commands have separate effects. For EO 13807 section 4(b), establish the triggering CAP goal, any OMB extension and the guidance's issue date; section 5(b)'s project obligations and independent aquaculture incorporation require their own review.

This examination reads the held original clause texts and the frozen claim's rationale, first-end assertions and savings analysis. It does not attest complete original identity, complete clause inventory, factual delivery, exhaustive earlier-event research, the truth of every savings statement, or an independently reviewed replacement claim. Prior overall upheld verdicts supply none of those field approvals. Whole-case independent review remains required for every correction. The controlling frozen codebook is not separately located in this checkout; the draft operational-state file and the pinned protocols cannot amend it.
