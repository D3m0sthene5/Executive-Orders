# Serial reprints of executive orders, 1862-1936: digitized hosts and bulk-search prospects

## Conclusions

Two serials have fetched evidence of printing full executive-order texts and are digitized in full view with OCR: the Panama Canal Record (weekly, 1907-1941, Internet Archive and govinfo) and the Official U.S. Bulletin (daily, 1917-1919, HathiTrust and Internet Archive). The Canal Record is the better first target for the Canal Zone subset (about 30 orders) and for the civil-service, leave and general orders that it printed in full; I saw three full texts in one issue (21 August 1918). The Official Bulletin should hold most of the World War I and emergency group (about 290 orders, 1917-1919), but I confirmed only that its index points to a heading "President, United States - Proclamations and Executive Orders", not an order's full text. Treasury Decisions (customs, about 115 orders) is plausible but unverified: the one volume I could read (vol. 35, July-December 1918, first 100,000 characters) contained no executive order. The Statutes at Large did not print executive orders in the one volume checked (vol. 35, which holds acts, treaties and proclamations); this rules out the 1907-1936 volumes only in part, because I could not open later volumes. The remaining candidates (Interior Land Decisions, Lighthouse Service Bulletin, military bulletins, Serial Set, territorial gazettes) are either not digitized in a bulk-friendly way, not shown to print orders, or lists of officials only. The largest target groups (national forests about 490, land withdrawals about 450, military reservations about 145) have no serial identified in this pass that reprints them in full; the likeliest remaining route for them is Federal agency compilations that I did not find, and the Monthly Catalog of United States Government Publications, which lists individual order titles and dates (not texts) and therefore supplies a date and subject check for orders that were printed as separate slips.

Ranking by likely number of target orders with full text recoverable: (1) Official U.S. Bulletin, possibly up to 250 (unverified, bounded by the 290 WWI group); (2) Panama Canal Record, roughly 30 Canal Zone orders plus an unknown number of others, perhaps 60-100 in total (estimate); (3) Treasury Decisions, 0-100 (unverified); (4) all others, under 20 each or zero.

## Evidence by candidate

**Statutes at Large.** The govinfo front matter for volume 35 describes the volume as "Recent Treaties, Conventions, and Executive Proclamations" and lists proclamations from March 1907 to March 1909, mostly national-forest and monument proclamations. It does not mention executive orders. The govinfo collection page (https://www.govinfo.gov/app/collection/statute) returned only a title to my fetcher, and volume 47's front matter URL returned "page not found" (the file name I guessed was wrong). Volumes 36-49 are therefore unverified.

**Official U.S. Bulletin.** HathiTrust (catalog record fetched via https://catalog.hathitrust.org/api/volumes/oclc/1768275.html) lists 16 items, all full view, from Cornell (coo.31924065155792, coo.31924065155800, coo.31924093153801, coo.31924093153819, coo.31924093153827, coo.31924077395238, coo.31924093153835, coo.31924065395083, coo.31924065395091, coo.31924065395133), Michigan (mdp.39015020137835, mdp.39015020137819, mdp.39015020137744) and Illinois (uiuo.ark:/13960/t5m943933, uiug.30112060726533, uiuo.ark:/13960/t4cp12n0x). The Internet Archive item officialbulletin01unse (title "Official bulletin / Committee on Public Information", 1917-1918, vol. 1) has a 19 MB OCR text file. Whether Chronicling America holds the Bulletin is unverified: the LOC about page did not say, and I could not fetch its robots.txt.

**Panama Canal Record.** The Internet Archive item panamacanalr11191718isth (vol. 11) carries the University of Florida Smathers Libraries credit and the collection tag ufpancan, with OCR in djvu.txt, abbyy and hocr formats. The item titled "Panama Canal record" gives dates 1908-1941. The InfoDocket announcement (https://www.infodocket.com/2016/04/25/gpo-and-smathers-libraries-u-of-florida-make-historic-panama-canal-commission-publications-available-online/) says the Canal Record (weekly, 1907-1941) is available on govinfo. I did not confirm a UFDC (ufdc.ufl.edu) record or id pattern. The Canal Zone Official Gazette was not found.

**Treasury Decisions.** The Internet Archive Google scans (for example treasurydecisio02apprgoog, described as 1904 but its text is vol. 35, 1918) have djvu text. My check of one volume found no executive order in its first 100,000 characters, so the claim that Treasury Decisions reprinted customs executive orders is unverified. The full list of ids from an archive.org search is in the table.

**Interior, Forest Service, withdrawals.** The DOI Library page lists Land Decisions volumes 1-52 (July 1881-December 1929) as print; online series named are the administrative decisions (1881-1994 at doi.gov/solicitor/decisions) and Public Land Orders (BLM and govinfo GPO-DOI-BLM-PLO), which postdate the target period. No Forest Service compilation of national-forest proclamations and orders was found.

**Lighthouse, military, bird refuges.** Only the Lighthouse Service Bulletin was located (USLHS archive, five PDFs, 1912-1939). No digitized runs of War or Navy general orders, Army Regulations bulletins or Biological Survey notices were found in this pass.

**Serial Set.** The govinfo release page (https://www.govinfo.gov/features/congressional-serial-set-release) says the first release covered selected volumes from the 69th Congress (1925-1927), the 82nd Congress and several 19th-century Congresses, with PDFs, ALTO XML and full-text search; GPO's later announcement (https://www.gpo.gov/who-we-are/news-media/news-and-press-releases/gpo-and-the-library-of-congress-digitize-10000-volumes-of-the-united-states-serial-set) reports 10,000 volumes uploaded with nearly 6,000 remaining and about 2,000 more planned in fiscal 2026. I found no Serial Set document titled "Executive orders relating to ...".

**Directories.** Not independently fetched for contents; the govinfo Congressional Directory collection page returned no coverage details.

## Host robots and terms

- govinfo.gov/robots.txt (fetched): applies to all crawlers; blocks admin, login, search and /app/search/ paths; lists many sitemap indexes including bulk data sitemaps. Content under /content/pkg/ and /browse paths is not blocked. The API docs page at api.govinfo.gov/docs was refused to my fetcher by robots rules; the key requirement via api.data.gov is from the task brief, not verified by me.
- ufdc.ufl.edu/robots.txt (fetched): disallows /info/, a few named items, and .dzi and .jp2 files; sitemap at https://ufdc.ufl.edu/sitemaps/sitemap-index.xml. PDFs and text are not disallowed.
- archive.org/robots.txt (fetched): disallows only /control/ and /report/; sitemap at https://archive.org/sitemap/sitemap.xml. Downloads redirect to dnXXXXXX.ca.archive.org hosts.
- chroniclingamerica.loc.gov/robots.txt: fetch failed (client error); Chronicling America API and bulk OCR terms unverified. The LOC API documentation page also failed to fetch.
- HathiTrust (https://www.hathitrust.org/data): public-domain OCR datasets are available on request and a Data API serves page images and OCR; the page does not say anything about scraping, and the OAI feed and Hathifiles are metadata only.

## Recommendations

1. Pull the Canal Record OCR volumes from Internet Archive (ids above, 1907-1941) and regex for "EXECUTIVE ORDER" headings and the formula "By virtue of the authority vested in me"; match to numbered orders by date.
2. Pull the Official Bulletin OCR from Internet Archive or HathiTrust (1917-1919) and test for full texts under the President heading; the 1918 first-half index suggests orders are indexed by date and page.
3. Sample three Treasury Decisions volumes (1912, 1920, 1930) by hand before investing in bulk work.
4. Look elsewhere for national forests and withdrawals: agency compilations (Forest Service, General Land Office annual reports) were not found here and remain open.
5. Do not use Statutes at Large 1907-1936 as an order source unless a later volume is shown to print orders.

## Gaps

I made roughly 60 web requests. I could not verify Chronicling America, the govinfo API, later Statutes volumes, Treasury Decisions content, or any compilation of orders by subject. The Canal Record and Official Bulletin quotations come from a summarizing fetch tool applied to OCR text, not from my own reading of the page images.
