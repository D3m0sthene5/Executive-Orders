# References

The project's reference list for published scholarship and datasets it uses or measures itself against. Primary legal
sources (orders, statutes, the Federal Register) are cited where they are used, by URL and SHA-256, not here. Created
2026-11-06 by the Encyclopédie campaign (no earlier list was found). Add an entry whenever a README, report, job, finding
or draft relies on a work; cite a dataset separately from its article, in the form its repository recommends, with the
version.

## Prior work on the lifespan and revocation of executive orders

- Lowande, Kenneth, and Michael Poznansky. "Executive Action That Lasts." *Journal of Public Policy* 44, no. 2 (2024):
  344–365. https://doi.org/10.1017/S0143814X23000442. Open access.
- Lowande, Kenneth, 2023, "Replication Data for: Executive Action that Lasts", https://doi.org/10.7910/DVN/TZ2QKY,
  Harvard Dataverse, V1. Licence CC0 1.0. Released 2023-12-12. Held copy: `dataverse_files.zip`, 12,943 bytes, SHA-256
  `7c51d513fe68ec0b8ca0843a2dd28ec053e03b0d7093e0174cd3fba99c7e8d41` (not in git). The deposit holds 6,536 orders,
  1937–2021, with no order numbers; the order-level dataset is available from the author on request.
- Thrower, Sharece. "To Revoke or Not Revoke? The Political Determinants of Executive Order Longevity." *American
  Journal of Political Science* 61, no. 3 (2017): 642–656. https://doi.org/10.1111/ajps.12294.
- Thrower, Sharece, 2016, "Replication Data for: To Revoke or Not Revoke? The Political Determinants of Executive Order
  Longevity", https://doi.org/10.7910/DVN/LZGQOH, Harvard Dataverse, V1, UNF:6:HoWUG8bvlsiqOgxKJ59NUg== [fileUNF]
  (the Dataverse API reports version 1.1, released 2018-12-11). Terms: CC0. Held copy: `dataverse_files (1).zip`,
  8,080,973 bytes, SHA-256 `c641a12e2dbf8e827999cbb531dabbe7fcc402f20488c93da4847a95eda3f9a2` (not in git). Holds
  6,157 orders, 1937–2013, with order numbers.
