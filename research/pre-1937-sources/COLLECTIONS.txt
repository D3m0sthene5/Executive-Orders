Estimate method and sample
--------------------------
The target is the exact 6,679 all-no rows in the census at commit
2181b6cd47751d8d1eb690255cbc384ed53664ad. Title-only author indexes were read;
their body hashes, ID intersection, errors, and the census hash are preserved
in COVERAGE.json. Only hyphens immediately before letter suffixes were normalized
(130-B to canonical EO:130B); numeric suffixes such as 8-1 stay distinct.
Existing links may be redirects or imperfect transcriptions, so 1,639 remains
an availability estimate. Three author-index URLs failed; those are not absence
findings. The sample uses sixteen positions spread across the available number
range and four lettered IDs. SAMPLE.csv records all 20 exact identities.

Normal project format is retained: UTF-8, LF plain text under texts/;
SOURCES.jsonl supplies URLs, acquisition times, hashes, raw-page paths and text
paths; FRAGMENTS.jsonl supplies per-order representation records. Raw HTML is
under originals/. The unchanged generic extractor also retains some page
headings, licensing text, and editorial notes. These are readable transcriptions,
not clean primary-source editions. EO 1990's long 201-rule text reaches its
signature; short and lettered orders also retain their text and signatures.
EO 4517's page explicitly warns that its source document is unknown. EO 5470
refers to a diagram; no linked image or attachment was acquired. These limits
are recorded in SAMPLE.csv. No order's legal status was decided.

Tools reused without changes
----------------------------
jobs/_lib/fetch_pool.py: FetchPool.fetch, robots checks, bounded byte cache.
jobs/source-ladder/fetch_ladder.py: extract_text and write_text_lf.
The source-ladder HTTP client's broad captcha marker rejected ordinary wiki
pages containing an edit-captcha configuration string. The existing FetchPool
client retrieved the public HTML normally; no access control was bypassed and
no downloader/extractor was changed or added to the repository.

Scope correction: pre-1937 is not the same as pre-Federal Register. The sample
EO 7454 is dated September 1936 and cites 1 FR 1697. The target also contains
EO:7700A and three nonstandard unnumbered IDs; these were not silently dropped
from the target count, and none was substituted for a sampled text. The new
files do not alter the gap census, readiness outputs, source queue, or statuses.
