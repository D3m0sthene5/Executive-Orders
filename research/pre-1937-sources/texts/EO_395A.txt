619469Executive Order 395-A — Reserving Lands at Diamond Head, Kupikipikio Point, and Punchbowl Hill, Hawaii, for Military Purposes1906President of the United States

In accordance with the recommendation of the Secretary of War, all public lands at and near Diamond Head and at Kupikipikio Point and at Punchbowl Hill, in the Territory of Hawaii, included within metes and bounds, described as follows, are hereby temporarily withdrawn from sale, entry, or other disposition, for military purposes, until it can be determined by actual surveys what portions thereof will be required for permanent military reservations, viz:
