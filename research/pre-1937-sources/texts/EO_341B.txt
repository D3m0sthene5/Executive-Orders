Signed by President Theodore Roosevelt Saturday, July 15, 1905

617565Executive Order 341-B — Extending Time and Compensation for James L. Bristow, Special Panama Railroad Commissioner, to Complete Report1905President of the United States

It is ordered that Mr. Joseph L. Bristow, appointed Special Panama Railroad Commissioner by Executive Order dated January 13, be allowed until August 15, 1905, to complete the details in connection with his report, and will be paid his actual expenses and $15 per day up to that time. In addition he will be allowed an extra compensation of $2,500. The Isthmian Canal Commission is directed to provide the funds needed in the execution of this order.

Theodore Roosevelt.

The White House,

July 15, 1905.
