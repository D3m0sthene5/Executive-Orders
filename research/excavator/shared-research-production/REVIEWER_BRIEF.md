# Reusable reviewer brief

Use a fresh source-review context and record actual model family. Read the current repository/owner rules and the exact frozen shared brief with its scope. Independently test every supplied case; the brief approves no individual inventory, first ending or savings conclusion.

The coordinator supplies one external blinded claim file, a bounded blocked-case report, exact PACKET.json paths and source access. Read only those inputs and their cited or relevant primary sources. Do not open producer sheets, applied claim files, private keys, seed receipts, unrelated temporary directories or earlier case verdicts. Keys and raw plants remain outside the repository. This is instructed blinding under a shared filesystem, not technical access isolation.

For each claim record canonical SHA256 (sorted-key compact JSON, UTF-8, ensure_ascii=False), instrument_id, reviewer, actual model_family, independent:true, separate_context:true, review_kind:independent_ai_source_review, scope:whole_case, verdict and source-based reasons. Pin source hashes/spans and check working-text identity, custody, complete inventory, effective conditions, earlier endings, independent commands, savings and bounded search limits. A source-check pass does not decide legal effect.

Seal blind verdict bytes before any reveal. Separately adjudicate whether each rejected plant identified its intended material defect, without credit for unrelated objections. After reveal, examine and attest the exact genuine claim hash as upheld_after_reveal or reject it. Do not infer approval of a changed version. Record actual elapsed intervals and unavailable token/cost data. Review blocked and owner-routed members explicitly; do not omit unsuccessful attempts.
