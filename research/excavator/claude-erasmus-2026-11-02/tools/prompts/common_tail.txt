
THIS IS A RESUBMISSION RUN ("Erasmus", after the scholar who corrected his Greek New Testament through five editions). Every order in your packet already went through a full claim attempt that independent review found defective. Your brief, <PACKET>/ERASMUS_BRIEF.json, lists for each order: `kind` ("rejected_in_review" = the claim was never filed; "filed_claim_correction" = a filed claim with a source discrepancy, prepared as an owner-reviewable replacement proposal), the prior claim's location, and the exact defects (reviewer wording, or the Geddes audit flag). Use them as follows:
- Read the prior claim as a draft, never as evidence. The defects are pointers you must check against held sources yourself; fix every one that the sources confirm. If a defect shows the claim cannot be supported from held evidence (for example an earlier end that no held source resolves), block the order with the precise gap rather than forcing a claim.
- Then recheck everything else in the claim. Reviewers found that many defects were wording carried over from a sibling order's template (a standstill, an "Approval recommended" line, a non-derogation or status-quo sentence, a recital) that this order's text does not contain. Every sentence that describes what the order says must be true of THIS order's held text; every quotation must match the held bytes exactly, punctuation included; every instrument the reasoning names as a later event must be in later_events with a source; lettered EO numbers keep their suffix; Federal Register page and issue dates must match the cited source.
- For "filed_claim_correction" orders: correct the flagged discrepancy and anything else you find; keep the end date unless a held source shows it is wrong. Never edit the filed claim files under research/readiness/claims/.
