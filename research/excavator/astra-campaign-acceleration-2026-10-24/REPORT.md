## Proposed replacement and its falsification

The screened statement was: “Today, a producer reconstructs the operative approval and lifecycle implications separately for each enumerated NRA member. After this change, shared approval analysis occurs once per exact operative pattern; every target retains incorporated-instrument, condition, amendment, earlier-event, savings and counterevidence checks. The normal claim-kit packet receives the common analysis plus differences, eliminating repeated reconstruction.”

Two premises fail. Competent current use already shares the safe preparation/reasoning components. More importantly, the broad type is not a shared legal obligation. The necessary outcome was fixed as an **individually reviewed substantive lifecycle obligation accepted by the existing consumer**, not a string, classification or extracted section. No such obligation was replaced or completed in this search.

The sample contains one plain code approval (already cloned), five agreement approvals, two conditional/supplementary code approvals, one agreement modification, one exemption denial, one complaint disposition/notification, and one committee/division creation. These unequal-probability cell counts are **not population prevalence**.

Examples: EO6684B remains a “clean” clone although its operative object is an agreement rather than a code. EO6729 adds committee/recommendation/report duties. EO7010E adds modification, cancellation, publication and stay conditions. EO7323 creates institutions and duties under a different1935 Act. Three OCR representations contain apparent date/number errors requiring page checking; they were not silently corrected or authenticated. `FEASIBILITY_RESULT.json` preserves exact spans and individual residuals. The coordinator independently read all twelve representations and replayed source/span hashes. No statutory ending conclusion, production claim, sampled-review substitute or human approval follows.

This feasibility rejection precedes a treatment implementation, independent production review, planted-control trial and holdout. Those stages were not run or claimed. The small check answers whether the broad proposed replacement is justified; it is not a baseline-versus-treatment efficiency result.
