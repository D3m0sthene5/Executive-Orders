By virtue of the authority vested in me 
as President of the United States, and 
pursuant to the policy expressed, in sec­
tion 1 of the Water Pollution Control 
Act approved June 30, 1948 (Public Law 
845, 80th Congress), of recognizing, pre­
serving, and protecting the primary re­
sponsibilities and rights of the States in 
controlling water pollution, I hereby di­
rect the heads of the departments, agen­
cies, and independent establishments of 
the executive branch of the Government 
to take such action as may be practicable, 
in cooperation with State and local au­
thorities concerned with control of water 
'pollution, to insure the disposal of sew­
age, garbage, refuse, and other wastes 
accuinulated in the course or as ^re­
sult of Federal activities, and industrial 
or manufactured foodstuffs and 'other 
products destroyed by order or under the 
supervision of Federal regulatory au­
thorities, in such manner as will con­
form with programs formulated under 
State law and applicable to Stat agen­
cies and the public generally for the pres­
ervation and improvement of the qual­
ity of surface and underground waters.
