## EO 10740 individual analysis

The complete original, 22 FR 9379, signed November 21, 1957 and published November 23, comprises two numbered paragraphs. Paragraph 1 makes the Secretary of Interior chair of the Council and removes the Vice President, leaving the Secretaries of Defense, Agriculture, Labor, and Health, Education, and Welfare as the other members. Paragraph 2 further amends EO 10673 section 1, expressly identifying prior amendment EO 10704. There is no other operative paragraph, reporting command, sunset, fixed duration, performance contingency, separate transfer, savings, or private entitlement. “Shall hereafter” and “is further amended” operate immediately on signing; publication is not an express deferred commencement.

EO 10785 paragraphs 1-2 add Commerce and the Housing and Home Finance Administrator on October 17, 1958, leaving the Interior chair. The owner strike-and-insert rule treats that membership rewrite as continued amendment, not an end of 10740. The conforming paragraph 2 is likewise not treated as instantly exhausted merely because the amendment occurred. Exact before/after original spans will accompany the lifecycle version.

EO 10931 section 2 separately names and revokes EO 10740 of November 21, 1957. The original has no earlier own-term end, and the intervening 10785 rewrite is not a cessation under the owner ruling. Section 2 says “are hereby revoked,” contains no delayed effectiveness or savings, and its signed date is March 29, 1961. Both target paragraphs first end then. The parent's section 1 is rewritten in section 1 of the same instrument; that continuation does not defeat the separate explicit target revocation. Proposed status at October 14, 2026: NO, based on the source text, not an editorial label.

## EO 10785 individual analysis

The complete original, 23 FR 8085, signed October 17, 1958 and published October 21, also contains exactly two numbered paragraphs. Paragraph 1 retains the Interior chair and the prior Secretaries, adds Commerce and the Housing and Home Finance Administrator, and sets out the resulting membership. Paragraph 2 further amends parent section 1, identifying 10704 and 10740 as earlier amendments. The original does not import all parent commands as new target obligations. It has no additional command, separate implementation obligation, report deadline, sunset, contingent completion, savings, or independent private right. Its organizational “hereafter” language is immediate on signing.
