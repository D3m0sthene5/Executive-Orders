Change in the Membership of the Presi­

By virtue of the authority vested in me 
as President of the United States, it is 
ordered as follows:
1. The President’s Council on Youth 
Fitness shall hereafter be composed of 
the Vice President of th~£ United States, 
who shall be the Chairman of the Coun­

the Secretary of Health, Education, and 
Welfare.
2. Section 1 of Executive Order No. 
10673 of July 16, 1956, which established 
the President’s Council on Youth Fitnèss 
and designated the members thereof, is
