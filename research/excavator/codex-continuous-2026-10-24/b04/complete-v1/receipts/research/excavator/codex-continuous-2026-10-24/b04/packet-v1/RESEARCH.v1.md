## Complete inventory and chronology

EO 10673 (July 16, 1956), section 1 created the Council with the Vice President as chair and the Attorney General and Secretaries of Interior, Agriculture, Labor, and Health, Education, and Welfare. Sections 2-7 separately prescribe promotion/coordination, agency assistance, an advisory committee, evaluation, annual meetings/reports, and preservation of independent statutory agency functions. These are parent commands. EO 10704 does not reenact all those commands as independent obligations of its own.

EO 10704 (March 25, 1957), 22 FR 2005, has exactly two operative paragraphs. Paragraph 1 makes the Council consist of the Vice President as chair and the Secretaries of Defense, Interior, Agriculture, Labor, and Health, Education, and Welfare; Defense replaces the Attorney General in the parent's composition. Paragraph 2 amends EO 10673 section 1 accordingly. The title, authority recital and signature add no independent command. There is no report deadline, actual-performance contingency, fixed sunset, transferred property, new private right, or savings clause in this target. “Shall hereafter” is an immediate organizational command, so the operative start is signing on March 25; publication on March 27 is separately recorded provenance, not a deferred commencement.

EO 10740 (November 21, 1957), paragraphs 1-2, removes the Vice President and makes the Secretary of Interior chair; the other five Secretaries remain. The parent section 1 is further amended. Under the owner ruling, this is continuation amended. It is not the first legal ending of either 10704 paragraph. The original and amended exact text are retained for both scopes in the candidate's later events, with source references. The substantive chair/composition change is disclosed.

EO 10772 (June 30, 1958) changes only parent section 4: the Council chair, rather than the President, designates advisory committee members and its chair. It neither names nor revokes 10704 and does not rewrite parent section 1. It is disclosed as a related but out-of-scope parent amendment, not assigned an invented target ending. The October 21 unnamed-instrument ruling also excludes unnamed events as target ends.

EO 10785 (October 17, 1958), paragraphs 1-2, keeps the Interior chair and adds the Secretary of Commerce and the Housing and Home Finance Administrator. This is a second continuation amended, with both exact before/after texts recorded. Neither amendment has a deferred effective date. Their internal organizational “hereafter”/“further amended” commands support the signed dates as immediate amendments; their publication dates are not confused with signing.

EO 10931 (March 29, 1961), 26 FR 2677, section 1 rewrites the parent's membership with the Secretary of Health, Education, and Welfare as chair, restores the Attorney General, and retains the other listed offices. Section 2 separately expressly revokes EO 10704 by number and date, together with 10740 and 10785. This is a named whole-order revocation with “hereby” language, no deferred date, no performance condition, and no savings. Both original target paragraphs first end March 29, 1961. The conforming amendment is not treated as instantly spent in 1957; the earlier membership rewrites are not falsely converted into cessation. The whole-order end equals the latest clause end, March 29, 1961. Section 1 explains the parent continuation, while section 2 supplies the actual target ending.

EO 11074 (January 8, 1963), 28 FR 259-260, expressly makes its new Physical Fitness Council a continuation of the Youth Fitness Council (section 4). It preserves independent agency functions in section 3(a), and section 6 revokes 10673, 10772 and 10931. It does not restore 10704 or its old membership. This institutional continuity is affirmative counterevidence against any suggestion the Council disappeared in 1961. It does not postpone 10704's directly named revocation until 1963 or revive it afterward. The target-specific residual conclusion is bounded to its two commands, not a claim that the institution or every factual effect ceased.
