Title 3—The President
EXECUTIVE ORDER 11629
Delegation of Authority to the Secretary of State to Perform the Func­
tion Vested in the President by Article IV of the Convention Between 
the United States of America and Mexico for the Protection of  
Migratory Birds and Game Mammals (50 Stat. 1311; TS 912)
By virtue of the authority vested in me by the Constitution and laws of 
the United States, including section 301 of Title 3 of the United States 
Code, and as President of the United States, it is ordered as follows:
Section 1. The Secretary of State is hereby designated and empowered 
to perform, without the approval, ratification, or other action of the 
President, the function vested in the President by Article IV of the 
Convention between the United States of America and Mexico for the 
Protection of Migratory Birds and Game Mammals to determine, by 
common agreement with the President of Mexico, other species of birds 
which may be added to the list of migratory birds specified in and 
subject to the protection of the Convention.
Sec. 2. In carrying out his authority under this Order the Secretary 
of State shall consult with the Secretary of the Interior.
