# Second Geddes repair: independent engineering baseline

This review is bounded engineering and exact flag-to-historical-defect correlation. It neither approves any whole lifecycle nor supplies scientific model substitution, human certification, legal control adjudication, or new canonical claims.

Prospective model/time record: `CLOCK_START.json`. Configured model gpt-6-astra was confirmed by coordinator; token/cache/cash/active-labor telemetry remains unknown. Wall intervals include tool latency and waiting.

## Old 55 filed-comparator flags

`OLD_EVALUATION.json` is extracted read-only from the HEAD diagnostic archive. `OLD_55_CLASSIFICATION.json` independently enumerates 55 flagged claims, 156 flag instances: 116 stock-phrase flags on 32 claims, 20 event flags on 8 claims, 11 attributed-quotation flags on 11 claims, and 9 original-quotation flags on 7 claims. Sets overlap. All 55 original source text files were locally hashed against claim pins.

Principal bugs and cautions:

- All nine original-quotation flags identify native OVERRIDE records. The old linter compares producer-reconstructed native inventory spans to the obsolete dossier clause envelope rather than reconstructing authorized overrides. These require real source/parent/span checks, not a blanket OVERRIDE exemption.
- Old stock absence is judged within the min/max clause envelope, which cannot establish absence from the complete order. EO9702's exact source has the statutory “no change” recital after the board/report paragraphs. Word wrapping must be normalized for concept detection, while exact quotations require their own stricter normalization. Recognized full-order boundaries must exclude neighboring orders and agency material.
- Events must belong to the tracked command. EO10681's reason explicitly says EO10739/11120 amend other sections, and its parent EO10152 is an object. EO10739 also discusses an earlier parent amendment, EO10618; EO12189 describes its predecessor EO12186 as historical context. A nearby action verb or phrase “later events” is insufficient.
- Full-order recommendation discussions need attribution analysis. EO6293's claimed comma-separated phrase differs from actual punctuation (`Hugh S. Johnson.` / `Administrator.`). EO6487B's text has no approval-recommended line despite the rationale mentioning one. These are evidence cautions, not permission to relax exact quotation semantics merely to hit the filed-comparator target. Every remaining filed flag must still count in that metric.
- The old corpus cache trusts file size/mtime and ignores pin-manifest identity changes. Same-size bytes with restored mtime remain incorrectly trusted; newly added pins are omitted from a warm cache. Matching discovered-file counts do not establish equality of corpus identity sets.

## Independent mutations

`adversarial_probes.py` defines 15 independent synthetic checks before the final implementation freeze. Correct source semantics, source-role separation, inventory reconstruction, recitals, neighboring contamination, FR issue versus signing/filing, missing actor event, literal-name search, content hashes, manifest invalidation and exact-identity coverage are covered. `INITIAL_ADVERSARIAL_V2.json` records 8/15 baseline passes. V1 is retained because its two last cache/coverage probes were initially confounded; V2 separates their triggers. This is engineering mutation testing, not held-out scientific performance.

## Exact historical recall

Review labels remain strictly outside the production linter. Each rejected version gets credit only when a flag identifies at least one exact historical defect; generic warnings or unrelated flags are not catches. A review case with several defects needs the caught defect identified, not all silently deemed repaired. All 40 exact rows and hashes must remain the denominator.

Useful generic mechanical opportunities: suffix/date/title source binding (6275, 6312, 6578); own-source FR page/issue attribution (9538, 10138, 10139); absence of explicitly attributed stock clauses (10285, 10303, 10314, 10319, 11013, 12095); exact own-source quotations (11314, 11329, 11431); source-backed later-event omission explicitly admitted in reasoning (10634, 11026, 12398, 12429). These are development examples, never production ID allowlists.

Held source discovery by literal refuge/code name can expose potential candidates (6876, 8129, 8163), but number-only matches and undated occurrence counts cannot establish the exact naming-change defect. The packet-held union is narrower than all held text. Parent revocation/savings, imported conditions, deadline performance, semantic title substitutions and ambiguous grammatical legal scope remain substantive matters; a mechanical flag on those cases does not establish legal correction.
