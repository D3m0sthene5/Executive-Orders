FAMILY BRIEF (EO 12553 emergency and investigation boards). Most orders in your packet create a board (usually under section 10 of the Railway Labor Act) to investigate a dispute and report within a fixed time, and the provisional NO status rests on Executive Order 12553 of 1986-02-25, which revoked a table of orders "and any amendments thereto". Owner rulings apply: research/readiness/owner_rulings/2026-10-21-report-deadline.md (option C: the order ends on express revocation, normally EO 12553 on 1986-02-25; the report date and the end of any statutory standstill are recorded as later_events with the disclosed note "functionally spent on <date>") and 2026-10-21-eo-10449-never-constituted.md (a board never constituted is treated the same way, with that fact disclosed). For each order you must still: confirm from the held EO 12553 text that THIS order's number (with any letter suffix) is in the table; check for an earlier express revocation, supersession or own-terms end; record the report date only from a held report or held source (research/excavator/pullman-2026-10-20/PACKETS.jsonl has staged report dates, quotes and hashes for many of these orders; re-check any value you use against the bytes it cites); record extensions and amendments as later events. Not every order in the packet is a railway board; judge the others on their own text. The filed claim for EO 10080 in research/readiness/claims/claude-2026-10-31-apollo-g04.jsonl shows the accepted field format for an EO 12553 revocation; copy its structure, never its wording about the order.
