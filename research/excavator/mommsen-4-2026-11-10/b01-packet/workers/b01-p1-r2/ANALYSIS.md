EO 2249 orders DC departments and establishments closed and covered federal employees excused only on Wednesday 29 September 1915. The exceptions concern special public reasons and conflicting law, not an independent continuing command. Its one-day operative period ends as 30 September begins. The signing date, 27 September, is not the first cessation date. The clause evidence itself names the date. No savings clause appears; any past employee treatment is an accrued consequence.

EOs 2806, 2831, 2855 and 2881 each suspend the eight-hour prohibition only for a named construction, remodeling, reconstruction or repair project, respectively. The quoted 1917 statute's overtime proviso is a statutory condition, not a second presidential command or proof of work. Each says it takes effect from and after its signing date, but none states a completion date. The word Temporary and a desire for early completion are no evidence that the particular project was finished. No held completion record, specific rescission or other dated cessation source permits an exact or interval first endpoint. The four remain blocked on those source facts.

EO 2964A is a confidential order authorizing the Civil Service Commission, in its discretion, to permit examination-free employment for work it finds to be secret intelligence service during the present war. Permission does not prove any appointment occurred. The archival scan reads 19 September 1918 and bears a 20 September 1918 State Department stamp; the OCR date is wrong. The dossier points to EO 3461 but supplies no located change provision. Its annual-report text path is absent locally and the page is not held for this review. War termination and EO 3461's legal effect require exact source review; neither may be inferred from the order's age or a pointer.

EO 3885B is a Department of State notice by order of the President. The scan directs closure of all Executive Departments from 1 p.m. Tuesday 7 August 1923 and on Wednesday through Friday, 8-10 August. The last day is included and the command ceases as 11 August begins. Its printed signature is Charles E. Hughes. The typed date is 4 August. This single closure has no separate prospective command or savings clause.
