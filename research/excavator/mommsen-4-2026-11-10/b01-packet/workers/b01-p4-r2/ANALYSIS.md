## EO:2805

The February 12, 1918 order suspends eight-hour limits only for Bureau of Fisheries contracts to construct and equip buildings at the Marine Biological Station, Key West, during the present national emergency. The 1917 statutory quotation is authority, including its wage proviso, not a second presidential command. Neither the end of the relevant emergency nor completion of these contracts is established by held evidence. The work's objective and age cannot date first cessation. Blocked pending project/contract completion and emergency-ending primary evidence, plus any order-specific earlier extinguishment.

## EO:2830

The April 1, 1918 suspension applies to labor completing the Arlington Building identified by location. War-measures purpose is not an expiration date. No held construction completion or order-specific ending instrument establishes when the exception stopped operating. Blocked pending a completion or earlier-ending source.

## EO:2851

The April 27, 1918 suspension concerns construction of the proposed Boston Quarantine Station structures. The word proposed prevents assuming construction occurred; the desire for early completion does not show it happened. Blocked pending completion, abandonment or earlier-ending primary evidence.

## EO:2876

The May 31, 1918 page grants a wartime permission to appoint women elevator conductors without apportionment, then restricts transfers, promotions and assignment of persons so appointed. The permission alone does not prove an appointment. The restrictions may have a distinct residual life for any actual appointees. The referenced EO 3461 text path is absent; its target provision was not located in the frozen view. Blocked pending that full held source, the legally relevant war-period end, and evidence about the application/survival of the restrictions.

## EO:2902

The July 3, 1918 suspension concerns labor erecting and completing the Coast and Geodetic Survey building. Its wartime rationale does not date actual building completion. Blocked pending a completion or earlier-ending primary source.

## EO:3415

The March 3, 1921 page orders half-mast flags for Champ Clark's Washington funeral service on Saturday March 5 and Bowling Green service on Monday March 7, respectively. March 7 is the last commanded day; the prospective duty expires at the start of March 8. March 3 is signing, and March 5 does not end the second location's duty. This is self-expiration of a one-time, dated command, whether or not officials performed it. No independent future command or express savings clause appears. Accrued consequences, if any, are separate from prospective force. The clause evidence ID supports the scheduled ending. Producer submission uses the held working replica with its original-replacement obligation, not human certification.
