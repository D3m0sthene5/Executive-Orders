These conditions narrow that same day's leave; they neither create a recurring holiday nor extend the command. The whole clause's first operative cessation is **March 5, 1921**, the first day after the named leave, supported by `ORIGINAL:EO:3403` and characterized as `expiration_by_own_terms`. The date of signing is not the ending date. Departmental exceptions might have narrowed application on March 4 but do not advance the end of the entire scheduled leave. Payroll effects or accrued rights from the holiday can survive without keeping the excusal command operative. The sole paragraph and proviso form the complete inventory. The held OCR has errors but retains the full operative passage and was compared to the page; the exact working-text grade and replacement obligation remain.
