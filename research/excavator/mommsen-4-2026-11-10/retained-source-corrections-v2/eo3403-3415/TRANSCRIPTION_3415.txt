Executive Order.

As a mark of respect to the memory of Honorable Champ Clark, former
Speaker and member of the House of Representatives of the United States, it
is hereby ordered that the flags on public buildings in the District of Columbia
and the State of Missouri be placed at half-mast on the days of the funeral
service in Washington, Saturday, March 5th, and at Bowling Green, Missouri,
Monday, March 7th, respectively.

WOODROW WILSON

THE WHITE HOUSE,
    3 March, 1921.
