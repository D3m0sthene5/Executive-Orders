EXECUTIVE ORDER 9513
E xtending  the  P eriods  of S ervice or 
T raining  and S ervice of P ersons  I n ­
ducted  into  or A ssigned  to the  N avy , 
M arine  C orps, or C oast G uard
By virtue of the authority conferred 
upon me by section 8 of the act of Con­
gress approved December 20, 1941, . 55 
Stat. 846, and as Commander in Chief of 
the Army and Navy of the United States, 
and deeming such action necessary in 
the interests of national defense, it is 
ordered as follows:
The periods of service or training and 
service of all persons inducted under the 
provisions of the Selective Training and 
Service Act of 1940, as amended, who 
have been, or may hereafter be, inducted 
into or assigned to the Navy, Marine 
Corps, or Coast Guard shall be, and they 
are hereby, extended for the period of 
the existence of any war in which the 
United States is engaged and the six 
months immediately following the termi­
nation of any such war, as declared by 
proclamation of the President or by con­
current resolution of the Congress, un­
less such persons are sooner discharged.
