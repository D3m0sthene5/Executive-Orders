(4) Director o f the Office o f Personnel Management.
(5) Administrator o f Veterans Affairs.
(6) Administrator o f General Services.
(7) Chairman o f the Federal Communications Commission.
(8) Chairman o f the Equal Employment Opportunity Commission, Co- 
Chairman.
(9) Such other members as the President may designate.” .
