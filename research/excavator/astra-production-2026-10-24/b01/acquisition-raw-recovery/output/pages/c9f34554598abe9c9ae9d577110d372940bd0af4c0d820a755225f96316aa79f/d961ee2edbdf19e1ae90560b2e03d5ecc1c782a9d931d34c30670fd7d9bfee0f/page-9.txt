S ec. 108. (a) As used in section 301(a) of title 37 of the United 
States Code, the term “duty involving parachute jumping as an 
essential part of military duty” shall be construed to mean duty 
performed by members who, under such regulations as the Secretary 
concerned may prescribe, have received a rating as a parachutist or 
parachute rigger, or are undergoing training for such a rating, and 
who are required by competent orders to engage in parachute jump­
ing from an aircraft in aerial flight.
(b) Members required by competent orders to engage in parachute 
jumping shall be required to perform one or more parachute jumps 
from an airplane in flight during any three consecutive calendar 
months in order to be entitled to receive incentive pay for such period.
(c) Whenever, under authority conferred by the Secretary con­
cerned, the commanding officer of any member who has been required 
by competent orders to participate in parachute jumping certifies that 
on account of the absence of jump equipment or aircraft or on account 
of military operations of the particular command such member was 
unable to make the jumps required by this subsection, such member 
may comply with the minimum requirements by performing four 
jumps during a period of 12 consecutive calendar months, and such 
requirements may be met at any time during such period.
(d) Members of reserve components of the uniformed services who 
have complied with the requirements prescribed in this section shall 
be entitled to receive incentive pay for both active-duty and inactive- 
duty training performed during such period.
