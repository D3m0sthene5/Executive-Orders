as miscellaneous receipts any surplus of the fund (all assets, liabil­

appropriated to establish and maintain such fund. There shall be 
transferred to the fund the stocks of supplies, equipment, other 
assets, liabilities, and unpaid obligations relating to the services 
which the Secretary determines, with the approval of the Director of 
the Office of Management and Budget, will be performed.
