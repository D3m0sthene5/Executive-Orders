FILL-UP RIGHTS 
SEC. 11. Section 401(d) of the Federal Aviation Act of 1958 (49 
U.S.C. 1371(d)) is further amended by adding at the end thereof the 
following new paragraph: 
"(6) Any air carrier holding a valid certificate to engage in foreign 
air transportation is authorized, on any scheduled flight in foreign 
air transportation, to transport persons, property, and mail between 
points in the United States between which it is authorized to operate 
during such flight. The authority described in the preceding sentence 
shall be limited to one round-trip flight per day between any such pair 
of points, unless the Board authorizes more than one round-trip flight 
per day between any such pair of points.".
