(c) For the performance of any hazardous duty as prescribed in 
parts (3) to (9), inclusive, of subsection (a) of this section by officers 
and enlisted persons qualifying for the incentive pay authorized pur-
suant to said subsection, officers shall be entitled to be paid at the rate 
of $100 per month, and enlisted persons shall be entitled to be paid at 
the rate of $50 per month.
(d) The President may, in time of war, suspend the payment of 
incentive pay for the performance of any or all hazardous duty.
(e) No aviation cadet shall be entitled to receive any incentive pay 
authorized pursuant to this section.
(f) No member of the uniformed services shall be entitled to receive 
more than one payment of any incentive pay authorized pursuant to 
this section for the same period of time during which he may qualify 
for more than one payment of such incentive pay.
