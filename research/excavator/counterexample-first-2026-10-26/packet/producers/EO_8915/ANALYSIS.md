EO 9526 amends the enumerated orders by adding a paragraph beginning:

> shall cease at the expiration of the six

> declared by Proclamation No. 2487 of

It then vests jurisdiction in Interior and any other federal department/agency according to respective interests then of record. Its separate operative sentence says:

> The lands,
> however, shall remain withdrawn from
> appropriation as herein provided until

The concluding paragraph supersedes conflicting provisions only to the extent of conflict and provides:

> however, That any provision for the
> earlier return of jurisdiction over the
> public lands m any of said orders shall
> remain operative.

Thus the jurisdiction sunset cannot be promoted to whole-order cessation. Earlier jurisdiction return remains possible under the original notice condition. The retained withdrawal is a distinct continuing norm until otherwise ordered. The interaction of the original broader “without effect” language with the added withdrawal-retention sentence should be read with the express conflict rule and the narrower saving of earlier jurisdiction return; no unproved notice is used to extinguish either scope. Under the owner amendment ruling the underlying order continues amended; EO9526 is a later event, not itself a whole-order first end.
