## What the newly acquired sources establish

The audit independently recomputed hashes of all three primary documents listed in the repaired Panizzi probe’s `PRIMARY_FILES.json` and pinned both retained files and extracted text. DOI final-report PDF page 3 and OMB M-18-25 PDF page 1 were rendered and visually inspected.

- **DOI final report:** the document identifies itself as the final report required by EO 13792 §2(e). That identity does not establish an exact delivery date. Its recital of the August 24 deadline is not substituted for performance.
- **DOI testimony, January 30, 2018:** the agency states that the Secretary submitted both reports. This supports submission by the testimony date. The audit does not substitute the date of the later monument proclamations for either report’s transmittal.
- **OMB M-18-25, September 26, 2018:** the signed, dated memorandum says it is issuing the performance-accountability guidance and describes ongoing quarterly review and scorecards. It supplies performance evidence for a particular issuance task, not a whole-order termination conclusion or proof that no earlier issuance existed.

Each source’s exact repository path, URL, file hash, text hash, relevant excerpt and inspected page are in the associated audit JSON. Acquired document text establishes custody and a reviewable source. It does not by itself establish first cessation.
