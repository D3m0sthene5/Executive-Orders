## What the production line contains

| Function | Components | Handoff |
|---|---|---|

| Direct and evaluate work | Kean, Polya, Llull, NEYMAN, Hamilton, Junto | Decisions/results, beneficiary counts, forecasts, observed bottlenecks and measured cost comparisons |

### Runtime ingestion and scheduling are different

claim-kit generically reads job EDGES.csv and source records; the checker generically reads source records. Their after.txt files do not name Shepard/fr-pages or Cotton respectively. The published October 29 broad run lists claim-kit before Shepard and fr-pages. A multi-job acquisition run therefore must not be assumed to rebuild every consumer from its newest sources.

Do not add a naive after.txt edge from claim-kit to Shepard: X1 already runs after claim-kit and Shepard after X1, so that would introduce a dependency cycle. Use existing explicit phases: discovery/acquisition; verify retained output; then rebuild/prepare from that frozen generation. Assess small dependency changes against the whole graph.

The structural reduction from roughly 250 KB of startup notes to an at-most-8-KB brief removes recurring context, though complete net token savings are unmeasured.
