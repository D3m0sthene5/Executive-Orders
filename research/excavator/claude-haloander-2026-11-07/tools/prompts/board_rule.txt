COORDINATOR'S LEGAL RULE FOR THESE ORDERS (applying owner rulings D-20261028 evidence-decides, D-20261021-until-dates and D-20261106-justinian-r3; you must still test it against each order's own text):
Each order creates a Presidential Emergency Board under section 10 of the Railway Labor Act and commands it to investigate a dispute and report to the President, usually within a deadline. Under R3 the cleanup revocation (EO 12553 or EO 12379) is only an upper bound and is kept as the formal disposition; the report deadline is never an end.
(a) Board clause (create, investigate, report): performed, and so ended by completion of its task, when the board made its report. A held report whose letter of transmittal is dated D and says the board submits its report herewith evidences submission on D: first_end D, mechanism completion_of_task, citing the transmittal-letter span; disclose that receipt by the President may have been later. If the order is amended to extend the reporting time, that amendment is a later event; if a held source shows a supplemental report or reconvening, the end is the last report.
(b) No-change clause: where the order's own text says that from its date and for thirty days after the board has made its report no change except by agreement shall be made, treat that sentence as an operative clause of the order (even though it says "as provided by section 10"), ending by its own terms at the end of the thirtieth day after the report date D (inclusive reading; state the exclusive reading as the alternative). Compute the date exactly and show the arithmetic.
(c) Any other clause (compensation, a duty of another officer, a combined order) is decided on its own evidence.
Whole-order end: the latest clause end. Block the order with the precise gap if the held report's board number, creating order, parties or date do not match this order, if the transmittal date is unreadable in the held text, or if the report is held only as a custody record.
