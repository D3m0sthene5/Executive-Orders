undecided (status-overlay route, owner ruling 2026-11-05 on empty status histories). These orders have NO adopted provisional status. Each was prepared with a full frozen dossier, status unset, under PACKETPATH/undecided/EO_N/ (EVIDENCE.json, DOSSIER.json and a blank STATUS_DECISION.json). Many carry an explicit EMPTY status history (flags EMPTY_HISTORY_STARTED_UNDER_OWNER_RULING_2026_11_05 and NO_STATUS_OBSERVATION_LOCATED): this means the status-history search found no dated observation or later event; it is NOT evidence that the order is in force or ended. Read research/readiness/owner_rulings/2026-11-05-empty-status-history.md and, in jobs/claim-kit/BATCH.md, the section "Undecided identities".

You research the status from scratch, from the order's own terms and held sources, and decide it yourself (yes or no) with a source. One investigation serves both outputs:
(a) the lifecycle sheet: fill decisions.csv / clauses.csv exactly as for a decided order (apply will report the member as `awaiting_status_review`; that is the expected success state for an undecided member, not an error);
(b) the status decision: fill PACKETPATH/undecided/EO_N/STATUS_DECISION.json in place (keep every existing key; never edit EVIDENCE.json or DOSSIER.json). The kit's validator (jobs/claim-kit/status_overlay.py, function _decision_errors; read it) requires: approved, identity_confirmed, basis_review_confirmed, earlier_endings_checked, partial_scope_checked, savings_checked all true; recommended_provisional_status "yes" or "no"; a substantive rationale (at least 30 characters, no TODO); material_gaps exactly []; observations_reviewed equal to the history's observation_ids; conflict_resolutions with a substantive written resolution for EVERY history flag (including the empty-history flags: say what was searched and why the status still rests on the order's terms and the held sources); basis_evidence_ids naming exact evidence ids from the frozen base dossier's `evidence` map (for "no" at least one ACT:, TERM: or ACTOR_TEXT: id that actually ends the order; for "yes" at least one CURRENT: id, and current_predicate_checked_through "2026-10-14"); original_pages_confirmed true when the original requires a page check and you did it. The validator also REQUIRES that the dossier's original source is acceptable_as_original; if it is not (only a transcription or replica is held), or if the dossier holds no ending (ACT:/TERM:/ACTOR_TEXT:) or CURRENT: record that supports your status, the status proposal cannot be made: set the member's decisions.csv outcome to `blocked` with that exact reason, leave its STATUS_DECISION.json blank, do not build a full clause inventory for it, and still write its assessment row with your best-supported reading and source. For such a member the assessment row is the main product: read its held working text in full and run `cruden.py number EO:N --write <scratch>/EO_N.json` once (and read any held later instrument that names it), so the row rests on the text and the held later instruments, not on the register summary alone; say what kind of command it is (one-time, standing rule, office or body, land or property, personnel) and why its force on 2026-10-14 is yes, no or unknown. The coordinator's precheck below tells you which members have an acceptable original.
A claim whose status the ledger lacks is filed through this route as an isolated reviewed proposal; it never adopts a status. Do not block an order merely because its status is undecided.
