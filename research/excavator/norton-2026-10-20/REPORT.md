# NORTON bounded pilot, 2026-10-20

The narrow implementation runs, but this pilot **does not establish useful factual reuse**. Seven orders yield four exact registered questions and eight candidate clause applications. Two questions repeat across six applications. No factual answer or applicability record has independent substantive review; four attempted evidence records remain rejected or held. No legal claim was published, no additional integrated-ready case is attributed to NORTON, and no measured saving is claimed. Expansion is stopped by the implementation's 12-order limit and failed feasibility gate.

Existing claim owners and replacement proposals remain untouched. `questions` records define exact entity, object, scope, interval, proposition and event kind. `applications` pin source text and identify a clause. Candidate evidence, producer factual answers and independent factual reviews have separate files. Empty answers/reviews files mean no completed review, rather than acceptance by omission.

The initial census, before curated bindings, read 45,663 own-term records, retained 6,681 obligation leads, and found 4,464 distinct unbound keys. It also retained 31 claim-gap and 17 reviewer-finding obligations and screened out 7,982 event tags. Zero safely bound cross-application questions were automatically established. That conservative extraction result does not establish that the corpus lacks shared questions. `INITIAL_CENSUS.json` is the frozen observation. Repeated source representations of one clause do not increase the clause-investigation count.

## Review, experiment and costs

`PREREGISTRATION.json` defines a small matched family-level comparison through independent factual review, per-clause applicability, corrections, assembly, checker and integration. It forbids sharing discovered answers or sources between arms and charges a shared source to its family. The Llull feasibility event was registered as `L-20261020-56` in commit `4d456ec1f18da027b9691e13331e8681cd03ae4f`, before the pilot results directory was committed. The preregistered source preflight failed. The complete comparison was not run; it has no arm session bills or independent review timing. `results/ARM_STATUS.json` retains that result. The informed producer cannot supply an uncontaminated control arm.

The exact frozen finder input is `results/JUNTO_INPUT_CENSUS.json`, retained by content hash across later source refreshes. The observed question intersections register an **unverified** Junto candidate through `junto.py run --finder norton`, using stable saver key `norton_shared_factual_obligations` and owner-seeded origin. `add-test` is deliberately not called: actual full-arm bills and independently reviewed units are unavailable. Script timing is in the generated METRICS file; it is extraction/validation time, not total research cost. Model tokens, cached tokens, active labor and dollars remain unavailable and are not replaced with payload or synthetic-token estimates.

Four append-only source requests were submitted through the actual FETCH_QUEUE reader, one per family, preserving its original byte prefix. All four new requests validate. Seventeen pre-existing invalid queue lines remain unchanged and are itemized in `QUEUE_APPEND_AUDIT.json`; their existence does not invalidate the four new lines. Acquisition runs and failures must be inspected before treating any requested source as held.

## Validation and integration

The 15 NORTON, 177 claim-kit, 19 Junto and 56 road/freshness tests pass locally. NORTON tests cover all seven identity dimensions, similar names, different treaties, deadline/publication versus performance, wrong editions, stale currency, partial transfers without an EO number, source and quote hashes, missing raw bytes, self-review, changed-after-review records, unsupported applicability, duplicate applications and cross-order clause evidence. Claim-kit's added test proves factual fragments do not become originals, actor events or current predicates, and changed inputs refuse ingestion. Machine source candidate review remains distinct from independent legal/factual review.

The runner uses existing job discovery and consumer ordering. NORTON writes only OUTPUT_DIR. Its fragment kind is `shared_factual_obligations`; claim-kit exposes it as factual context and includes it in the dossier fingerprint, while the existing assembler and legal gates retain their authority. Case-bundles keep the generic attributed fragments. Downstream consumer execution and the final runner receipt are recorded in RUN_VERIFICATION.json when observed. Codebook, accepted statuses/admissions, other producers' claims, human review records and certification are unchanged.

Further work is conditional: acquire and authenticate the exact primary material, obtain independent source-based factual and applicability reviews, and rerun a fresh matched full-cost comparison. This report does not represent those steps as completed.

The first full local consumer rebuild exposed a circular freshness dependency: claim-kit removes its old own-term output before loading fragments. That rejected run is preserved in `results/LOCAL_CONSUMER_REJECTED_OWN_TERMS.json`. The correction retains prior-generation census hashes separately from the live registry, primary text, source manifest and compiler checks. A regression test verifies both successful rebuilding and rejection when the underlying factual registry changes. Case-bundles now explicitly runs after NORTON, and empty factual fields are omitted so unrelated dossier fingerprints remain unchanged.
