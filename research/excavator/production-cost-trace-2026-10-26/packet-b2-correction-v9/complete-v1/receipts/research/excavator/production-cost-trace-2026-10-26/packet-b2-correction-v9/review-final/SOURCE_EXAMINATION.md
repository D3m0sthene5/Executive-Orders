## Complete originals and ending instrument

The actual retained primary PDF pages were rendered and visually inspected, rather than relying on source declarations or machine text. EO8705 is the first order at 6 FR1313, PDF page1, signed March5,1941. Its three complete footprints are RuleI§1 election interference/competitive-service political management restrictions (with voting and opinion reservations); RuleXII§5 preference in reductions, including both good-record and equal-or-better sub-good rating comparisons; and RuleXV notice/explanation, discipline/dismissal and the appointing-officer ten-day failure followed by salary withholding through disbursing/auditing officers. The closing effective-as-of November7,1940 paragraph applies across the order and is a retroactive start. The ten days are a recurring case condition, not an order end.

EO8707 is the first order at 6 FR1399, PDF page1, signed March10,1941, with no deferred start or own sunset. RuleIX§2(c) requires separation without prejudice, six satisfactory months in a position whose probation exceeds six months, qualification or ability to qualify through noncompetitive examination, and completion of probation when reinstated into another long-probation position. RuleX§6(a) restates absolute appointment, excepting the stated six-month satisfactory-service long-probation class, with completion in the receiving long-probation position. The adjacent land order is outside this inventory. Neither the six months nor completion of a particular person's probation expires the order as a whole.

EO9830 was visually examined in full at PDF pages1,3–11 (12 FR1259,1261–1269); page2 is intervening contents. The opening expressly makes the order effective May1,1947, not its February24 signing date. PartII replaces the Civil Service Rules as a whole; PartIII names both EO8705 and EO8707. The page visibly dates the EO8705 entry March5, although the native OCR reads March6. This does not defeat the matching number, original, subject and source identity; OCR remains unchanged. The selected May1 named-target endpoint follows the owner ruling `research/readiness/owner_rulings/2026-10-21-unnamed-instruments.md`. It is not an assertion that the host provisions were substantively unchanged until then. Every original footprint is mapped; no additional independent command was found outside these rule amendments.

## Earlier mechanisms, versions and exceptions

EO8514 (5 FR2857, PDFpage1) separately authorizes Commission-permitted probationary transfers into appropriate national-defense positions without a six-month minimum. It predates and is not simply absorbed into EO8707. Complete EO9691 (11 FR1381–1383, PDFpages1–3), signed February4,1946, expressly revokes EO8514 in §9 and saves previously acquired reemployment rights. It does not name either reviewed target. Its §3 authorizes transitional regulations until revision and requires resumption within thirty days. Neither its recommendation deadline nor its other personnel-program dates is a whole-target sunset.

The September28 CSC instrument published October10,1942 at 7 FR8007, PDFpage1, supersedes RulesIII,V,VI,VII,VIII,IX,X and inconsistent joint regulations for covered positions. It excepts postal field positions subject to §18.9(j). Thus it is an important partial-scope predecessor mechanism for EO8707, but its list excludes EO8705's RulesI,XII,XV. The separate October13 CSC§18.8 instrument, published October20 at 7 FR8411, PDFpage1, replaces RuleIX for covered positions with prior-Commission-approved war-service reinstatement, actual service rather than retirement eligibility alone, and a one-year trial. Signing, filing and publication dates are distinct; neither instrument supplies a separately stated exact universal effective date or names EO8707. The cards properly disclose these earlier candidates under the owner convention.

All five pages of the Veterans' Preference Act of1944 (58 Stat387–391, June27) were visually examined. Section12 gives an independent statutory retention scheme and the material rating comparisons relevant to EO8705 RuleXII§5. Section13 gives an independent preference-eligible reinstatement route relevant to EO8707. Section14 protects covered permanent/indefinite preference eligibles who completed probation/trial, with cause, notice, response and appeal provisions; it qualifies rather than silently erases the old disciplinary context. Section18 saves prior preference-eligible rights under existing laws, Executive orders, rules and regulations; it is a saving tied to this act, not a prohibition on all subsequent executive revision. Sections19–21 retain administrative authority, scope exceptions and severability. None names either target.

The February7,1946 published Part27 introduction and relevant pages4,7–11 were inspected. The introduction replaces war-service Part18, allows earlier agency adoption and supplies a thirty-days-from-EO9691 formulation. EO9830§3.2 later calls March7,1946 the effective date of temporary regulations. The records disclose the apparent March6 arithmetic/March7 text difference without inventing one universal earlier cessation date. Initial §27.9(a)(1) uses completed probation/absolute appointment; initial §27.10 also permits the six-month probationary transfer class with completion of original probation, alongside status, qualification, release and related conditions. Sections27.16(f,g) expressly protect probation, status, tenure and RuleIX-equivalent benefits. Section27.1 substantially repeats politics, §27.12 supplies removal/retention links, and §27.15 substantially repeats the old salary chain.

The April2 CSC instrument at 11 FR4143/4145, PDFpages1 and3, was inspected through its complete continuation, signature, filing line and explicit March7,1946 effective clause. This is a Commission regulation adjacent to EO9712, not an operative part of EO9712. Amended §27.9(a)(1) expressly excepts (a)(6). Paragraph(a)(6) allows reinstatement of a former classified employee separated during probation only in the same agency, position type, grade and locality, within one year except for preference eligibles, to complete probation. **It imposes no six-month service minimum.** The separate (d) war-service-indefinite conversion route does require six months total satisfactory service, after the specified prior separation of thirty days or more, with agency discretion, reporting and post-audit. Importing (d)'s service minimum into (a)(6) changes eligibility and is a material source error.

The July CSC amendments at 11 FR7929, PDFpage3, were read across both columns. They add §27.9(b)(3)'s higher-grade certificate requirement to the April version, rewrite promotion conditions with exceptions, correct the retention cross-reference, and amend transitional §27.16(b,c). The scan says §27.16 where OCR says §27.10. The new six-month promotion restrictions do not turn into a universal reinstatement minimum. Filing July19 and publication July23 are established, but a universal effective date is not silently supplied. The cards retain distinct February, April and July versions.
