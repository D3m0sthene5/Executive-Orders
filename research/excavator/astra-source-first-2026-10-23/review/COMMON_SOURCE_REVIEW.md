## Scope, exceptions, savings, and date limits

The preamble says the purpose is to terminate establishing authorities for committees that are inactive or no longer necessary. That describes the cleanup purpose; it does not give a dated finding of earlier legal cessation for any particular committee or clause.

Each numbered revocation is unqualified by a section-level carve-out. The instrument contains no express savings clause, conditional trigger, retroactive clause, sunset, transfer provision, or separately stated effective date. Its express date is August 17, 1982. The absence of those provisions in this instrument is a finding about these two pages, not proof that no external statute, earlier instrument, accrued right, or independent command could matter.

“As amended” identifies the target order in its amended form. It does not itself name every amending order, revoke every such order as a separately inventoried instrument, or establish that every command in an amending order depends on the parent. Section 18 names two bodies in describing one target; it supplies no license to omit either body's own terms from that target's inventory.

A direct revocation is relevant ending evidence, but this common source does not alone establish the first or final legally defensible ending of every operative clause. Earlier fixed dates, conditional endings, actual events, amendments, savings, and independent provisions must be checked against the complete target and chain.
