## EO 13836: actual implementation without fictional report delivery

## EO 13839: a regulatory residue extends beyond the EO

The October 16, 2020 final rule, 85 FR 65940, was effective November 16, 2020. It expressly invokes independent statutory authority and says it assists only certain EO 13839 directives. The March 2021 OPM memorandum, PDF page 5, directs agencies to stop section 6 submissions, keep data under applicable records law, withdraw negotiating proposals and separately address CBA provisions. A video is evidence of educational activity, not proof that the section 7(d) government-wide initiative with the CHCO Council was completed. The 2022 amendment is partial; no claim is made that every regulation, personnel action or accrued claim ceased on December 12, 2022.
