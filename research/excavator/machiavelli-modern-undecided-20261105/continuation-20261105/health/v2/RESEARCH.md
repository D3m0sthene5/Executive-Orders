Two positive primary findings prevent substituting familiar branding activity for those exact tasks. It is **a statutory audit to Congress**, not the EO implementation report to the President, and USAID identity is not proof that the President selected the EO single logo. These are independent residual requirements, not revival evidence. Later treatment of individual branding rules needs its own instrument-specific record.
