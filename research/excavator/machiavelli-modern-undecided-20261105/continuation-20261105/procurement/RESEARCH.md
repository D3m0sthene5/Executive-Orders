## EO 13980

The complete original official PDF (86 FR 6817–6820) and complete EO 14029 PDF (86 FR 27025–27026) were read, rendered and visually inspected. EO 14029 §1 revokes EO 13980 on May 14,2021. Section 2 separately directs prompt consideration of rescinding implementing orders, rules and policies consistently with law and the APA. It does not make revocation contingent on their later rescission or automatically vacate regulations and prosecutions.

EO 13980 §4(a),(b) addresses future NPRMs/final rules, criminal-penalty statements, DOJ consultation and mens rea text. Section 4(c) adds strict-liability justification/publication duties and OIRA implementation guidance/monitoring. These continuing or recurring conditions cannot be exhausted by a single agency publication. Section 5(b),(c) supplies continuing referral/investigation policy. Their EO authority ended May 14,2021; no earlier complete displacement was located.

Section 5(a) says agencies should publish guidance within 45 days, including the four referral factors. March 4,2021 is the resulting requested deadline; it is not evidence of actual publication or a self-executing expiration. No particular 2021 agency 45 dayguidance, OIRA implementing document, covered rule/strict-liability submission, or referral/prosecution under this order was identified in the bounded public research. Occurrence remains unknown. The proposal leaves first-ending dates blank for those distributed one-time guidance scopes and retains May 14 as a supported no-later-than event. It does not label them “never activated.”

The search included exact 13980 terms on official Federal Register/GovInfo, OIRA/Reginfo, DOJ, Regulations.gov and selected agency sites, followed by a primary-source search-engine recheck.
