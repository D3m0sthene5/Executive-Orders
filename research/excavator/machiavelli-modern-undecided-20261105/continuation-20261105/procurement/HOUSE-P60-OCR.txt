Buy American

On April 17, 2017, the President signed Executive Order 13788, Buy American and Hire
American. Beyond implementing policies supporting American jobs and families, the Executive
Order required the Secretary of Commerce to, within 220 days of the signing of the order, submit
4 report to the President that includes specific recommendations to strengthen implementation of
“Buy American Laws.”

Q. What i the status of this report and the other requirements of EO 13788?

ANSWER:
‘The President issued an Executive Order on Buy American and Hire American on April
18,2017, The Executive Order directed the Department of Commerce to co-lead, with tl
Office of Management and Budget (OMB), an assessment of Executive Branch agencies’
implementation of and compliance with existing Buy American laws relating to federal
procurement and federal grants. The Department and OMB collected information from
“other agencies to assess their compliance with Buy American laws and use of waivers and
‘exceptions to the Buy American Act in federal procurement and federal financial
assistance.

In addition, the Department researched and analyzed the impact of U.S. free trade
agreements and the World Trade Organization Agreement on Government Procurement
‘on the operation of Buy American laws. The Department used these analyses to provide
recommendations on strengthening the implementation of Buy American laws and other
‘methods to increase procurement of domestically made goods. As required by the
Executive Order, the Secretary submitted the report with findings and recommendations to
the President on March 20, 2018.

Q. What efforts are being taken to ensure that we are using all available tools to give
domestic manufacturing operations, and their workers, the first shot at supplying goods,
products, and materials, for federal and federaly-assisted government procurements?

ANSWER:
‘The Department of Commerce's report submitted to the President pursuant to EO 13788
provides analyses and recommendations aimed at providing domestic manufacturers and
‘their workers first-shot opportunities at supplying goods, products, and materials, for
federally-assisted government procurements.
