## Earlier-ending tests and occurrence

The original two-year clause is deleted by EO 12965 before the original June 1995 period expires. It is **before-text**, not a second live term. EO 12965's nine detector rows are three labels across three witnesses of that same sentence, not nine commands. The official held PDF controls quotation review.

A June 29/30, 1997 lapse cannot be silently ignored merely because EO 13053 calls itself an extension. New primary context matters: the charter page identifies a renewal filed April 25, 1997, and its January 1999 amendment expressly recites signing and extension to February 28, 1999. The official history says a revised charter **was signed** on April 25. This is evidence of occurrence, rather than inference from a deadline or later intention. A report release or work-completion statement is not used as the source of an exact report-submission date or as an automatic sunset.

June 30, 1999 is the supported date for expiration of Council existence and prospective Council work, subject to bounded search for extensions. EO 13138 corroborates actual termination by September 30 but does not itself specify the earlier exact day.
