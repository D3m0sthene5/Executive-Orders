The final rule contemplated by EO 13881 **exists**. FAR Case 2019-016, FR Doc. 2021-00710, 86 FR 6180–6194, was published January 19, 2021; it was filed January 15 at 8:45 am. Its specific effective date is January 21; applicability begins with solicitations issued February 22 and resultant contracts. The FAC summary's default January 19 effective date does not override the specific rule. EO 13881 §3 uses issuance, so neither regulatory effectiveness nor procurement applicability automatically supplies the revocation date. The trigger had occurred by publication; the exact issuance/earliest-cessation date is not assumed. The rule implements >55% domestic content for ordinary manufactured products, <5% foreign iron/steel for iron/steel products, and civilian 20%/30% evaluation factors; DoD's 50% factor is unchanged. Operative amendments appear at 6186–6194, notably 6187. It proves actual rulemaking performance, not every agency's conformity or the separate §4 report.

EO 14005 §14(b) is expressly scoped to inconsistency. Its §8 directs consideration of stronger domestic-content/price rules. The subsequent FAR Case 2021-008 final rule, FR Doc. 2022-04173, 87 FR 12780–12798, effective October 25, 2022, actually implements higher ordinary-product thresholds: 60%, 65% for 2024–2028 deliveries, 75% from 2029, plus a 55% fallback through 2029 and enhanced critical-item framework. Its amendments retain EO 13881 in 25.101/25.201, preserve the iron/steel test and base 20%/30% factors, and allow certain contract-specific content thresholds. These are compatible retained implementations, not evidence of complete EO 13881 repeal. Changes in contract requirements do not retrospectively erase earlier task performance. The 2025 repeal of EO 14005 cannot itself revive previously extinguished EO 10582 provisions.
