## EO 13255: actual submission and the one-day GSA discrepancy

The official original changes only the §3(b) reporting date. EO 13227 §4(e) independently terminates the commission 30 days after submitting its final report unless the President extends it. The held final report's signed letter, July 1, 2002, is addressed to the President and affirmatively submits the report. GSA's historical administrative report separately says the commission reported its findings and recommendations to the President on July 1. These establish submission as a dated act, independently of the deadline. Receipt is not proved, but the actual legal predicate is submission; a receipt acknowledgment is not an added requirement.

Thirty calendar days after July 1 is July 31. GSA lists that as Expected Term Date. It also lists August 1 as Actual Term Date and gives member end dates of August 1. The report identifies EO 13227 as termination authority, and its remarks say the commission ends as prescribed by the order 30 days after report issuance. No separate presidential extension was located. The preferred legal proposal is July 31 under the operative submission language. An administrative first-inactive-day convention could explain August 1, but that explanation is an inference: the source does not define the field that way. The one-day contrary assertion is retained prominently for exact independent legal review. ## EO 13081 and EO 13102: affirmative submission, unresolved charter authority

The held NTIA final report has the co-chairs' signed December 18, 1998 letter to the Vice President affirmatively submitting the final report under EO 13038 §2. Actual White House receipt is unproved. The archived NTIA site separately dates public release December 18. Submission and public release are distinct facts.

The parent §4 says the committee terminates 30 days after submitting its report unless extended by the President. If no earlier valid termination or later presidential extension controls, December 18 plus 30 days yields January 17, 1999. That is a conditional legal calculation, not an established exact first ending.

Material counterevidence remains in the same report. Its “As amended” charter gives an October 31, 1998 duration unless properly renewed. The history describes work into December and a December 31 report deadline, but does not reproduce a later charter renewal. The archived agency homepage was updated January 4, 1999 yet mixes the December release fact with older October reporting/termination language and an older membership description; its About page remains dated May 1998. These mixed editions identify stale web text but do not prove which legal authority displaced the charter's October duration.

EO 13102 itself changes only the report deadline. It cannot simply be treated as an express charter renewal or as amendment of parent §4. Nor does later work prove legal renewal. The remaining question is a real legal-authority question: did the charter independently end the committee earlier, was it properly renewed, or did the presidential instrument control the charter inconsistency? Complete source and applicable authority review is needed. EO 13138's later recital establishes completion/termination by its September 30 revocation but does not supply the earlier date. Both proposals therefore retain first-end HOLD while recording the newly supported submission fact and the conditional January calculation.
