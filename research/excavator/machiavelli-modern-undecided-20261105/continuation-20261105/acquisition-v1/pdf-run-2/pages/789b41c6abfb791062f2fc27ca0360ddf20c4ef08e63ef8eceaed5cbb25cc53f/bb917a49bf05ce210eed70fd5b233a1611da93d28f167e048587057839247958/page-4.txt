B. 	 Enforcement of Buy American Laws and Waiver Usage 
As required by section 3(b)(i) and (ii) of the Order, agencies shall review the enforcement of the 
Buy American Laws, and the use of exemptions and waivers (as defined in section l(d) of the 
E.O.). To assist the DOC and other agencies in evaluating the impact of agency spending for 
foreign items on domestic jobs and manufacturing, agencies should review the level of spending 
conducted under each exception, the most prevalent products that were subject to BAA 
exceptions and waivers, and, government-wide, the largest contracts subject to BAA exceptions 
and waivers, for each of the last three fiscal years.
