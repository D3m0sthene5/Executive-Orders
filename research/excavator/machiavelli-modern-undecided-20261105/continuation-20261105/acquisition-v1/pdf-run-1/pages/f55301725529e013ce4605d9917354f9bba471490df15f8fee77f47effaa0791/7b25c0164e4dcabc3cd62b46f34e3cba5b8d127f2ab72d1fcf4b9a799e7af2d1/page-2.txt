Compliance with Executive Orders 
OPM plans to provide additional guidance further discussing the full reinstatement of the EOs 
and addressing questions that may arise pursuant to this reinstatement. In the meantime, agencies 
should immediately review the EOs to ensure that they are fully compliant with all requirements 
or are taking steps to become compliant with requirements at the soonest feasible opportunity. 
The three EOs can be found at: 
• Executive Order 13836 – Developing Efficient, Effective, and Cost-Reducing
