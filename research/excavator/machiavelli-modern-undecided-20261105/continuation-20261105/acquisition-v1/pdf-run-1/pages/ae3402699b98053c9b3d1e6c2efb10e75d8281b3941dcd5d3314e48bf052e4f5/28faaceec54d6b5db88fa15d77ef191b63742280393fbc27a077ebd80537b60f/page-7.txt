Progress Toward a Living Wage for Federal Employees 
Section 5 of the EO provides that the “Director of OPM shall provide a report to the President 
with recommendations to promote a $15/hour minimum wage for Federal employees.” OPM is 
conducting an analysis of its authorities and will work with the Chief Human Capital Officers to 
gather any information necessary to complete this report. 
Questions
