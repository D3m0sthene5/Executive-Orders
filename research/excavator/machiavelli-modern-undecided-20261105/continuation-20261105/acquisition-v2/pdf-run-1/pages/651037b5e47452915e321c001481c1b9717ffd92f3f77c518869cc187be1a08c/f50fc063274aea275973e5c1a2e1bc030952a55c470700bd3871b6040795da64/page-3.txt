Codification Guide
The following num erical guide is a  list of the parts of each title of the Code of Federal Regulations affected b y 
documents published in today's issue. A  cumulative list of parts affected, covering the current month to date, 
appears at the end of each issue beginning with the second issue of the month.
Monthly, quarterly, and annual cum ulative guides, published separately from the daily issues, include the 
section numbers as w ell as the part num bers affected.
