# Stage 20 — causal-span and shared-source ambiguity excavation

The full Opus recovery proved the original archive member bytes for **496** images. Many of those images are stored in shared Opus producer/reviewer shards. A directory match does **not** prove the image contains a particular EO, let alone the exact provision or legal cause.

This companion excavator operates on the **844** available Opus research narratives and the **496** hashed media paths. It emits:

- `CAUSAL_LEAD_PACKETS_844.jsonl`: per-case date mentions with **verbatim surrounding context**, machine-guessed research-check versus effective-event cues, cited later-order/statute references, sample shard image paths, and precisely identified legal gaps.
- `CAUSAL_LEAD_PRIORITIES_844.csv`: order-level human review shortlist scored by *recoverable research material*, never doctrinal correctness.
- `MEDIA_BATCH_ASSOCIATION_AUDIT.csv`: each verified image, possible accepted-case batch associations, how many other orders share the directory, and a compulsory `no_case_specific_evidence` label.
- `CAUSAL_CUE_SUMMARY.json`: population and ambiguity metrics grounded in these inputs, including the number of *uniquely* batch-associated media and cases with date mention contexts.

All labels from automated date contexts are **unverified research leads**. The extractor neither assigns a cause, establishes a date, authenticates presidential authorship, nor counts human certification. The Stage 19 claim and independent human-review gates are unchanged.

`python research/stage20/code/build_causal_cues.py` rebuilds these outputs deterministically (stdlib only); `--check` compares every generated byte. `python -m unittest discover -s research/stage20/code -p 'test_causal_cues.py'` tests the no-certification and many-to-many source safety rule. GitHub Actions performs the same work when this implementation or its evidence inputs change and commits the outputs on success.

This avoids accidentally presenting a PDF used by a 100-case shard as a separately verified source for 100 legal conclusions. To move a case forward, a reviewer still needs the page and text, the source's actual legal relationship to the EO, competing earlier-effect explanations, savings, and signed human adjudication.
