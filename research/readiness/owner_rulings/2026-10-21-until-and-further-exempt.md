# Owner ruling: "until" dates and "further exempt" readings (2026-10-21)

Pending ask 3 in the brief (EO 8554, 8392, 8828, 8058, 7300, 6933), and the question the
Junto session was holding (is the stated "until" day itself included?).

**Ruling, as a principle: always include more information when possible.**

Applied: record the end as the stated date, inclusive (the order runs through the end of
that day), and state in the claim that the exclusive reading (ends when that day begins)
is the alternative, so a human reviewer sees both. For "further exempt" extensions, record
each extension as a later event with its own date and quote. No codebook, status or count
changes. Certified: 0.
