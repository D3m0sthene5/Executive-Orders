# Owner ruling: strike-and-insert amendments (2026-10-21)

Decided by the owner, Danny, on 2026-10-21, answering question 2 of

**Ruling: option B, with the before-and-after text recorded.**

When a later order strikes words from a clause and inserts replacement text, or restates a
section "to read as follows", the clause continues, amended, until the parent order ends. The
amendment is a `later_event`, and that event records the clause text before and after. A
rewrite that replaces a whole section carries a note for the human reviewer that the substance
changed.
