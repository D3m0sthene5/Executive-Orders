# Owner ruling: report deadlines on emergency-board orders (2026-10-21)

Decided by the owner, Danny, on 2026-10-21, answering question 1 of
`research/production/owner-rulings-2026-10-20/BRIEF.md`.

**Ruling: option C, with both dates recorded.**

An emergency-board or board-of-inquiry order whose only command is to report "within thirty
days" does not end when the board reports, nor when the statutory standstill runs out. It
ends on express revocation (for most of these, EO 12553 of 1986-02-25). The report date and
the end of any standstill are spent facts: record each as a `later_event` and give the claim
a disclosed note, "functionally spent on <date>", so a human reviewer sees both.

Owner's reasoning, in his words: "I don't see why you wouldn't take more information. Just
denote both. Why would there be a subsequent cleanup order if they stopped being in effect
when they reported?"

Consequences:
- `jobs/claim-kit/AGENT_PROTOCOL.md` step 3, "A report deadline is never an end", is now
  owner-confirmed for this class.
- Pullman's staged packets can run: 88 core EO 12553 targets plus 9 extras. Claims under the
  C reading need no re-check of committed claims.
- Open point not yet ruled: EO 10449, a board never constituted. The same logic (it stayed on
  the books until revoked) suggests it is covered; confirm with the owner before claiming it.
- The 25 Lamm III report-relative sunsets and EO 9691 follow the same reading where their
  ending clause is a report deadline; a clause with its own fixed date is unaffected.
