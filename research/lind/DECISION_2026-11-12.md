Question (owner): which concrete intervention, chosen from current evidence, lowers the total cost of completing the
project without weakening its scientific and review standards?

Endpoint used throughout: **an order brought to "ready for human review"**: a provisional status, owner adoption and
every readiness step done (6/6). This is not human certification. A deferred or "terminal" order is not a completed
conclusion. Status progress short of ready (an accepted status, a producer-only status) is reported separately.

Revised the same day after the owner's correction pass (section 6). The earlier text said the 62 kept-out orders were
"43 failed after dispatch, 19 dispatch-blocked". That was wrong: 43 and 19 are the Papinian and Haloander shares. The
real split is 50 failed after dispatch and 12 dispatch-blocked.

Every dollar figure is a list-price API dollar, i.e. allowance, not cash. Cash spent: $0. Each figure is labelled:
- **observed**: read from a transcript or ledger;
- **allocated**: an observed total divided by a stated rule;
- **assumed**: a stated assumption;
- **projected**: arithmetic on the above for orders not yet attempted.

### 6.3 The single next experiment: stage 0 on the rebuilt kit, stratified

*Superseded in its details by section 7.3, after the rebuild.*

**Decision it resolves:**

- whether the rebuilt kit's yield justifies a stage 1.

These two decide most of the remaining undecided cost: yield is the denominator of every cost per ready, and the
soft gates decide which 2,073 orders are ever attempted.

**Preconditions (zero tokens, runner time only):**

**Draw** (`research/lind/stage0/draw.py`, seed 20261112):
- 15 orders that pass every gate and 15 that pass the hard gates but fail a soft gate, never attempted before;
- shuffled together and run on the hard-only route (`LARREY_LEVEL=hard`);

**Held constant (the Papinian configuration):**

- an Opus 5.5 status reviewer and two fresh blind Opus 5.5 arms;
- one planted control;
- the both-uphold rule, after-reveal review and completion;

**Changed:**
- the kit is the rebuilt one;
- the soft-failing orders are admitted;
- all submissions go to **one** review set, not one per batch. The two observed sets suggest a fixed per-set cost,
  but that is from two points.

**Costs, each recorded:**
- **Production** (observed rate $0.201 per attempt): about $6.
- **Review** (assumed fit $8.3 per set plus $0.72 per item): about $13-17 for 6-12 items.
- **Coordination** (lean $0.109 per attempt): about $3.3.
- **Correction:** recorded if any.
- **Integration:** PR and merge.
- **Owner effort:**
  - adoption decisions for each proposed status;
  - the Augur review sign-off, or a reviewer from another model family;
  - minutes recorded.
- **Setup:** already done in this PR.
- **Missing:** runner minutes; certification, which is out of scope.

**Endpoints:**
- **Primary:** orders ready (6/6) per arm.
- **Secondary, reported separately:**
  - accepted statuses;
  - producer-only statuses;
  - review dollars per item;
  - coordinator dollars per attempt.
- Certification is not measured.

**Limits:**
- Hard cap: **$35** of list-price allowance across all agents. The Savigny supervisor stops on measured spend.
- Augur stage-0 limits apply (1 lane, 30 orders, 6M units).
- Stop production if coordinator dollars exceed $0.25 per attempt after the first 10 orders.
- Stop and adjudicate if a planted control is missed.

**Every outcome** (h = ready among the 15 soft-failing orders, a = ready among the 15 that pass every gate):

| Result | Action |
|---|---|
| h of 2 or more | Soft gates exclude worthwhile work. Do not enforce them. Stage 1 draws from all hard-pass orders, with the cost per ready reported per arm. |
| h of 0 or 1 and a of 2 or more | Keep the soft gates as a queue order, not an exclusion. Every stage-1 batch keeps 1 in 5 orders from the soft-failing group, so false exclusion keeps being measured at production cost, not extra cost. |
| a + h of 1 or less | Yield on the rebuilt kit is not established. Stop. Diagnose the 30 at zero production cost (Respondendi method) before any stage 1. |
| Inconclusive (any arm disagreement that changes a count) | Adjudicate each disagreement against the held text and record why. Agreement with past reviewers is not correctness. Then apply the rows above. |

Detection is weak by design: at the admitted arm's past yield (15.5%), P(a of 2 or more) is 0.70. A 9.2% rate gives
0.41. A soft-arm rate of 5% gives P(h of 2 or more) = 0.17. Stage 0 detects only large effects; the 1-in-5 exploration
share is what measures smaller ones.

**Authorization needed:**
- the owner's answer to A-20261110-larrey-soft-probe at a $35 cap;
- an Augur review of `WAVE.json` by another model family, or the owner, bound to its hash.

Neither exists today, so nothing was launched.

## 7. After the repair (2026-11-12, evening)

### 7.1 The build defect, repaired (PR #302; Kean F-20261112-claimkit-regex-runaway)

- **Cause, found by profiling:** `build.py` `_PRECEDES` backtracked exponentially on spaced-digit runs in OCR text.
- **Fix:** a possessive digit run and atomic separator whitespace. The pattern accepts exactly the same citations.
  - The fix gives the same results as the old code on all 22,413 real pairs the old code finishes.
  - On the 130 pairs the old code cannot finish, a non-backtracking `grep -E` translation of the old pattern agrees
    with the fix on 242 of 242 windows. That translation was first checked against the old Python code on 4,365
    windows and agreed on all of them.
  - A first fix, which required a separator, was rejected: it lost 18 real citations.
- **Fresh build** (branch run 43287422922): claim-kit succeeded in 32 minutes, and larrey-triage read the new
  claim-kit summary (its hash matches).
- **Before the repair:** no build had published since 2026-11-10 10:36 UTC, and the last attempt ran into the 6-hour
  wall. The time avoided per rebuild is runner time, not a measured saving per completed dossier.
