# Nothing-held 70: text hunt results

Files: RESULTS.csv (one row per order), FETCH_URLS.txt (pages a runner should fetch), this report. Nothing in the repository was edited.

## Counts

Verdicts across all 70 orders: text_official 3, text_secondary 18, listing_only 33, identity_doubt 4, nothing_found 12.

By era: 1862-1906 (18 orders): 7 text_secondary, 5 listing_only, 1 identity_doubt, 5 nothing_found. 1907-1936 (44 orders): 3 text_official, 11 text_secondary, 22 listing_only, 3 identity_doubt, 5 nothing_found. 1937-1989 (8 orders): 6 listing_only, 2 nothing_found.

## Texts found

Official printings (3): EO 2859-A (FRUS 1918 Supplement 2, document 537, page marker 456), EO 2406 (FRUS 1916, document 9), and EO 2539 (Alaska Railroad Record vol. I no. 23, page 183; the project already holds this page as an audit admission PDF but not as text).

Secondary texts (18). Twelve Roosevelt/Taft/Wilson-era half-numbered orders and EO 171 have Wikisource pages (read on a 2022 Wikisource mirror): 106½, 171, 288½, 377½, 389½, 411½, 472½, 554½, 633½, 669½, 819½, 1324½, 1722½; also 3278-A. American Presidency Project pages exist for 6756-E, 6756-G, 7373 and 4224½. Wikisource pages carry no citation to an official printing, so these remain secondary.

## Findings that change the project's records

1. EO 4224½ is held under the wrong key. The APP page titled "Executive Order 4224 1/2" is stored as EO:4224 in the register. Re-key it to 4224½; EO 4224 then lacks its own text.
2. The APP harvest missed suffixed pages 6756-E and 6756-G (and 7373); the project index does not contain them.
3. EO 171: the earlier "web hit" was a county sheriff order. The real federal text is on Wikisource.
4. Radio-frequency "A" orders (9132-A, 9427-A, 10026-A, 10369-A, 10571-A, 10695-A) were confidential and never published in the Federal Register. APP and Wikisource hold title-only placeholders. No official text exists to find; stop searching. EO 10369-A is identified: July 1, 1952, confidential radio order. Date conflict for 9427-A: Wikisource says Feb. 24, 1944, the register says Feb. 29, 1944.
5. EO 2245½ is now identified from the WPA list entry for 2656-A: September 14, 1915 order allowing certain veterans to be appointed watchmen in the State, War and Navy building; 2656-A extended it.
6. The unnumbered order of Feb. 19, 1934 (row 107 of table 02) is described by Truman EO 9830 as concerning "negotiations for transfer"; revoked by EO 9830.
7. EO 3278-A: Truman EO 9830 calls it an "unnumbered order of June 2, 1920", so the number is a Wikisource/Lord label.

## Identity doubts

- EO:7010P is an OCR misreading of 7010-F. The WPA sequence reads A, B, C, D, E, P, G, H; the entry text (electrical employees, Miami and part of Dade County, Florida) matches APP 7010-F, and APP lists 7010-A through 7010-I with no P. Merge it into 7010F.
- EO:UN:executive-orders-01:row:71 duplicates row:12 (same date 1914-08-08, same amending order EO 8507, Public Water Reserve No. 22), following the project audit.
- EO:UNN:LORD1944:p1:1863-03-10 is a Lincoln proclamation that Wikisource labels 1-A, per the audit. The audit cites 13 Stat. 775 and the WPA line cites 12 Stat.L. 731; I did not resolve which is right.
- EO:4361½ is dated the same day as EO 4361 with no title in any index; possible duplicate or placeholder. EO 1919½ may share serial 1919 with the Arkansas National Forest order: the June 1921 Monthly Catalogue calls the Alaska townsite order of April 21, 1914 "Executive order no. 1919". EO 2069½ is the same-day pattern (same date as EO 2069), left as nothing_found.

## What remains

- 33 listing_only: mostly Wikisource index lines or WPA lines with no text. Best next leads: Civil Service Commission annual reports (2230½, 2247½, 2656-A, 2245½); Federal Register 1936 volume 1 for EO 7373 (page not found); NRA Codes volume XII p. 623 for 6786-A; Kappler for 343-A; Official U.S. Bulletin for 3001; Monthly Catalogue entries for 2703, 3493, 3733 confirm only existence.
- 12 nothing_found: date-only NARA codification rows with no subject. The NARA page executive-orders-01 was blocked by the fetch permission system; reading its Disposition column would identify most of them.
- HathiTrust full-text search could not be fetched from this environment (client error), so slip-print and bound-volume searches were not done. Wikisource, APP and FRUS were the productive sources.
- Many pages were read through a fetch tool that summarizes rather than returns raw text, so quotes in RESULTS.csv are short extracts. Printed page numbers are given only where a scan or the tool showed them.
