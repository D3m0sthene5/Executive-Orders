# Draft reference and scan requests for the early-orders archival audit

Drafts written 2026-10-18 for the owner to review and send. Nothing has been sent. Before sending:
confirm the address on each institution's current contact page, replace every `[bracket]`, and attach
the lead list the request refers to (`jobs/early-orders-hunt/output/LEADS.csv`, filtered to the era).
Reference staff answer fastest when a request names a record group and series, gives a bounded date
range, and asks a question they can answer from the finding aid.

The purpose of these requests is the **random archival audit** described in
`jobs/census-completeness/README.md`: reading a random sample of archival cells exhaustively to measure
how many orders the printed compilations missed. The sample cells should be drawn before the request is
sent and listed in it, so the audit is preregistered.

## 1. National Archives, Archives I textual reference (Washington, DC): War Department letters, 1789–1861

**To:** archives1reference@nara.gov (confirm at archives.gov/contact)
**Subject:** Reference inquiry: presidential directives in RG 107 Letters Received, 1789–1861, and reproduction of sample months

Dear Archives I Reference Staff,

I am compiling a documented census of presidential executive orders issued between 1789 and 1861, a period before the numbered series and before any official compilation. The only existing list, the 1943 Historical Records Survey *List and Index of Presidential Executive Orders, Unnumbered Series*, was built from printed sources. To measure what that list missed, I am reading a random sample of months of departmental correspondence in which presidential directions to the Secretary of War would have arrived.

Could you help me with three questions about Record Group 107, Records of the Office of the Secretary of War?

1. For Letters Received, Registered Series 1801–1870 (microfilm M221) and Unregistered Series 1789–1861 (M222), which rolls are now digitized and viewable in the National Archives Catalog, and which remain microfilm or paper only?
2. Are there separate series or files in RG 107 (or in RG 94, Records of the Adjutant General's Office) where orders received from the President, or orders issued "by command of the President," were filed or registered apart from general correspondence? A series title and date span would be enough.
3. For the rolls that are not digitized, what are the current options and costs for reproduction of specific frames or complete rolls? My sample consists of the following [N] months: [list months, e.g., March 1807, August 1832, …].

I will cite any reproductions by record group, series, roll and frame, and the resulting census will be published with the full search ledger.

Thank you for your help.

### Notes for the owner

- Three sources Lord used (GLO schedules, MR books, IR 1912) are being read in full by `early-orders-hunt`; these requests target what sits *behind* those printed derivatives, which is the only way to measure what they left out.
- The sample months for request 1 should be drawn at random from 1789–1861 before sending, recorded in `research/plans/` with the seed, and never changed afterwards.
- NARA reproduction fees and digitization-on-demand policies change; the answer to question 3 in each letter is what sets the budget.
- Founders Online (founders.archives.gov) already covers Washington, Adams, Jefferson and Madison to roughly 1836 with every known document annotated; no request is needed for those presidents before searching it.
