# Stage 19 excavator funnel — selection made before implementation

The user asked for ten radically different ideas for **each** problem, narrowing features before building. This is the recorded design choice. The project remains a legal-historical research system, not a label factory: a search result, catalog description, OCR line, AI suggestion, or formal revocation cannot by itself complete a case.

## A. Ten ways to accelerate complete EO lifecycle research and human audit

| # | Distinct concept | What it discovers or makes easier | Principal limit |
|---:|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Causal graph quarry** | Traverse already indexed predecessor, actor, source and event edges in bulk; surface likely missing transitions and quarantined contradictions. | Existing graph can omit decisive events. |
| 2 | **Clause-state compiler** | Represent each operative clause as a dated state machine; calculate candidate first loss of operation and all surviving scopes. | Clause extraction and legal effect still need source review. |
| 3 | **Reverse-citation archaeologist** | Search later orders, statutes, decisions and regulations backward for mentions of a target EO. | Citation is a lead, not proof of legal displacement. |
| 4 | **Source-custody robot** | Retrieve official scans/text once, hash them, share across all affected orders, and flag missing or conflicting representations. | Some originals are offline or restricted. |
| 5 | **Blind human review assembly line** | Generate compact, source-linked field-by-field packets with blinded proposed answers and exportable judgments. | Review quality depends on qualified humans and clear rules. |
| 6 | **Adversarial contradiction hunter** | Deliberately seek counterexamples: earlier cessation, savings, revival, independent authority, wrong dates and false successor links. | Negative search can never prove absolute absence. |
| 7 | **Active-learning queue** | Rank cases by expected causal yield, source availability, era, mechanism diversity and review uncertainty; sample rejected leads too. | Ranking must not masquerade as legal confidence. |
| 8 | **Conservation-of-clauses audit** | Require an accounted state for every original operative clause before a whole-order NO; require one current predicate for YES. | Full original footprint is often unavailable. |
| 9 | **Provenance ledger with fingerprints** | Bind each claim to exact source bytes, pages, codebook and reviewer decisions; stale reviews reopen automatically. | Hashes authenticate bytes, not legal interpretation. |
| 10 | **Population estimator, separate from case decisions** | Use stratified human samples to estimate how many current labels or early identities may be wrong, with uncertainty intervals. | Gives population quality, never certifies an individual case. |

### Selected lifecycle build

Combine **1, 5, 7, 8 and 9** now: one deterministic batch engine reads the 14,972 included identities, the reconciled legacy status overlay, Stage 16/17 claims and relationship graph, and emits a ranked causal work queue plus field-completeness packets. Each packet separates original source, operative footprint, first cessation or current predicate, later events, survival/savings, conflicts, and human review state. The packet is **ready to audit**, never auto-approved. Keep the existing Stage 17 review importer as the certified decision path; do not write human approvals from machine packets. Add **2, 3, 4 and 6** as explicit acquisition hooks and blocker codes for the next iteration. Use **10** only after a blinded human sample exists.

Why this cut: it scales immediately over the existing corpus, makes the true missing work count visible, and prevents a presumed source link from inflating a lifecycle-completion bar. A full source fetcher and legal state compiler require more reliable source coverage than currently exists.

## B. Ten ways to range for 18th- and 19th-century presidential orders

| # | Distinct concept | What it searches or compares | Principal limit |
|---:|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Catalogue federation ranger** | Send date/actor/topic leads to LOC, NARA and other public finding-aid indexes; preserve returned identifiers and URLs. | Catalogs describe collections unevenly. |
| 2 | **Lord chronology sweeper** | Convert 1,023 OCR date markers into guarded source queries; prioritize uncertain years, same-date splits and sparse eras. | An index is incomplete and may merge entries. |
| 3 | **Letterbook-neighbor walker** | Inspect nearby manuscript images once a likely letter is found, catching enclosures, commissions and multiple addressees. | Image order may not equal document order. |
| 4 | **Printer-and-broadside scout** | Search 18th-century printed ephemera for orders issued by command, including agency-signed documents. | Attribution rules matter; print may contain errors. |
| 5 | **Congressional-paper trail** | Find presidential instructions reprinted in *American State Papers*, Annals, statutes, and committee evidence. | Retrospective printing is not an autograph original. |
| 6 | **Recipient-network expedition** | Search by the named recipient or department, not only presidential name, to find dispersed papers and replies. | Correspondent archives have uneven digitization. |
| 7 | **Consequence-backtracking ranger** | Start from later litigation, pensions, land grants, or court records and trace the initiating presidential directive backward. | Consequences can outlive or misidentify the order. |
| 8 | **OCR ensemble cartographer** | Align OCR from multiple scans/transcripts and mark disagreements for human inspection. | More OCR cannot resolve an absent source. |
| 9 | **By-command attribution tribunal** | Separate presidential autograph, secretary-signed command, delegated departmental action, and editorial heading. | A legal inclusion choice remains for humans. |
| 10 | **Archive-request optimizer** | After online searching, cluster unresolved references by repository, box, roll and likely page to request a few targeted scans. | Requires archivist response or on-site access. |

### Selected early-history build

Combine **1, 2, 4, 5, 6 and 9** now: a batch ranger turns the Lord workbench's date/excerpt leads into multiple distinct catalog search routes, queries the public LOC JSON interface with bounded rate/caching, scores candidate *matches* transparently, and writes an evidence-preserving CSV/JSONL queue for manual identity and signer adjudication. It keeps **candidate**, **source located**, **document authenticated**, and **included EO** as separate states. Apply **3 and 7** to top leads after finding a manuscript or downstream case; **8** to conflicting scans; **10** when an online search is exhausted. The ranger cannot increase the historical denominator automatically.

## Shared gates and tracker choice

The headline progress measure becomes **independently reviewed whole-lifecycle evidence complete / 14,972 working identities**: original document or exact substitute; full operative footprint; dated event path or reason for continuing operation; first effective cessation with mechanism and source for a NO, or bounded current legal predicate for a YES; savings, residuals and contradictions addressed; source-claim fingerprints; and a separate independent review bound to the exact claim. Structurally complete research claims awaiting review are a distinct intermediate measure. A provisional YES/NO count remains a diagnostic, not the headline. An unknown numerator must not be guessed from sparse structured fields; this build publishes both a strict documented lower bound and an explicit unassessed remainder.
