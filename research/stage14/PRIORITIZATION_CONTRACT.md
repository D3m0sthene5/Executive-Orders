# EXECUTIVE-ORDER RESEARCH — STANDING LEVERAGE-FIRST OPERATING RULE

**Effective:** October 16, 2026. **Status:** project-level standing instruction, also saved as a user preference. **Legal cutoff unchanged:** October 14, 2026, America/New_York. **Frozen legal codebook:** 2.0.0 until a separately accepted change.

## 1. Mandatory before undertaking a large research batch

1. State the desired scientific output and the specific currently limiting prerequisite. E.g., cause of cessation, not merely present status; exact instruments rather than bibliographic entries.
2. Map dependencies upstream: complete source corpus -> identity match -> original operative scope -> later event chain -> source attribution -> legal conclusion -> independent quality check -> statistical estimate.
3. Search existing caches, source indexes, source manifests, worklists and prior negative findings before fetching any new document or investigating an individual EO.
4. Compare potential enabling tasks by **unique downstream work that can actually be unblocked** (not raw mentions), research costs, legal/error-reduction benefit, confidence, and prerequisites. A source useful for an upper bound does not necessarily establish a first cause.
5. Execute the highest-leverage enabling task first, with measured outputs and executable regression tests. A deliberate, small representative/falsification pilot may be necessary to validate a reusable rule before its mass application.
6. Once reusable infrastructure is functioning, assign bulk work by controlling source and legal subtype, NOT simply by a numerical EO-ID range. Reuse retrieved originals, source versioned text, cross-references and prior legal exceptions across both present-status and causal lanes.
7. If several cases share an inference premise that fails the first adversarial spot check, hold the entire family; do not improve throughput by weakening legal gates.
8. Record source URL, publisher, date/version, exact byte SHA where available, original-vs-editorial distinction, and scope of claim. Preserve old records as immutable inputs; make versioned overlays and lossless projections.
9. At each checkpoint report both (a) scientific completion and (b) newly available research capabilities. Count a candidate link, downloaded representation, authenticated original, and legal YES/NO as different stages.
10. Re-evaluate leverage after each enabling operation, because the bottleneck will change. Do not turn "infrastructure first" into perpetual infrastructure work if the tooling already meets downstream needs.

## 2. Hard guardrails

- Never equate an EO number, index entry, publication correction or source row to a unique signed instrument without identity evidence.
- Never promote a government editorial disposition, OCR marker, source filename, revocation citation or age-based assumption to present legal status.
- Never equate formal revocation with **first cessation**; a later event may at best supply an upper bound.
- Distinguish current applicable legal norms, residual duties, continuing legal consequences, policy similarity, and formally unrepealed status.
- Protect genuinely blind verification by giving reviewers source-only packets; the baseline answer key is not passed into those packets.
- Do not report convenience-sample agreement as national accuracy.
- If access is blocked, produce a traceable download/access manifest and state plainly that acquisition did not occur.

## 3. Default task hierarchy as of Stage14

1. **Build/reuse complete indexed and deduplicated evidence** from existing project holdings; bulk acquire genuinely missing official originals through traceable URL manifests where feasible.
2. **Fix census identity and missing-universe coverage** with Lord's unnumbered source, original-document crosswalk and commissioned archival help where warranted.
3. **Validate shared legal inference rules on diverse provisions and source strata**, quarantining risky whole-order extrapolations.
4. **Reconstruct provision-level operative histories and causal event intervals** for probability samples of YES, NO and UNKNOWN; use a historical-cohort design rather than selective NOs only.
5. **Scale instrument-level determinations** after the above gates, with agents working on legal families sharing controlling sources.
6. **Estimate historically comparable population results** only after defining a complete subpopulation, inclusion probabilities, true legal estimand, and missingness/error model.

The ranking is revisited when actual evidence changes. The aim is to maximize correct and usable research, not the number of status cells filled.
