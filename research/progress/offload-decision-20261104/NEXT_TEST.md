Current practice may already invoke maintained checks: record that, do not force an artificial script-writing baseline. Use equivalent evidence access, exact output obligations, reviewer independence and blinded controls. Preserve claim/version fingerprints. Use separate cold producer/reviewer contexts for the two arms; do not replay a learned or revealed packet sequentially in the same context. One packet gives operational evidence, not a population effect.

Producer and reviewer receipt streams remain distinct. Compute producer checks only on producer-visible artifacts, and blind reviewer checks only on that reviewer's assigned pinned cards and source allowlist.
