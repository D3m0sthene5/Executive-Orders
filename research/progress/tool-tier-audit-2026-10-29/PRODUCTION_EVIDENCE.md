## Scope and verdict

This appendix assesses demonstrated production and efficiency in **D3m0sthene5/Executive-Orders**, pinned to main **73d99f666573ee10f5b1ff2ada60f7d9331c3952**. It distinguishes accepted or filed claims, current readiness, historical isolated replays, adopted provisional statuses, and human certification.

Generic claim factories, unconsumed certificate engines, and planned runs without completion receipts receive little production credit. All ready counts mean **ready for human review**. The published human-certified whole-lifecycle count is **zero**. These rankings are analytical judgments, not a scientific reclassification of any order.

This audit read existing reports, registers, current GitHub metadata and already published data. It ran no research, checker, tracker or source-acquisition job and changed no repository scientific state. No new research was performed to write this appendix.
