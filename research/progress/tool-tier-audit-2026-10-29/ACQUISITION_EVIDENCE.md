# Acquisition and evidence infrastructure: proposed tiers

Audited read-only against main **`73d99f666573ee10f5b1ff2ada60f7d9331c3952`**, plus the explicitly separated Poggio branch **`7cc5baf4521f6a5729df8de92dee3d53e17d9060`**. The audit read the standing instructions, KEAN brief, newest handoff entries, published summaries/status receipts, and relevant primary reports. No jobs were run and no repository files were changed.

**Recommendation:** put the official-source backbone in **S**, reusable acquisition and retention in **A**, bounded specialist readers and audit inventories mostly in **B**, and source-discovery systems with limited conversion or degraded current runs in **C**. Acquisition deserves credit for preserving reviewable evidence even when it does not independently produce ready cases. Conversely, route counts, page matches, and downloaded bytes should not earn the same credit as verified document identity or integrated research outcomes.

## Important distinctions for the consolidated answer

1. **Neither corpus size nor ready gain alone is a satisfactory score.** The official-source backbone is S because it provides the evidence foundation for the entire undertaking. Early historical recovery also matters even when identities remain outside the denominator.

2. **Do not allocate the 97 transcription-dependent ready cases to a specific acquisition tool without claim-level attribution.** This is a combined current pipeline result. The current tracker also records 103 original-replacement and quotation-recheck obligations.

3. **Pinakes’ policy issue has been resolved.** The current README and shared-unlock action apply the October 21 ruling. Generated labels such as `archival_replica_scan_pending_codebook` or “page check and a codebook grade” should not be interpreted as a new owner approval requirement.

4. **Cotton’s newest failure is a reproducible operational defect; existing captures remain available.** Preserve both facts.

5. **Early Ranger’s success exit codes conceal an empty latest acquisition result.** That is a more substantive reliability concern than an old date on a completed FR acquisition output.

6. **Lewis/Clark “page confirmed” means a page names the order with a date.** It does not mean an official original has been found or an identity admitted. Lewis’s route distribution is mostly assignments to other actors.

7. **No financial or total agent-hour saving is established by these acquisition counters.** Machine runtimes, exact reuse and bounded fetch counts can support engineering judgments, but not campaign-wide cost claims.

8. **The institutional-series addition to Panizzi deserves a favorable bounded mention, not a standalone triumph.** It corrected two target routes against the native matcher, retained one existing-route control, and produced three reviewed dated-letter facts. It did not establish delivery, first cessation, whole-case readiness, or net savings.

No blanket F tier is assigned to these acquisition services. The strongest negative verdicts apply to current broken reruns or unsupported interpretations of output, while the retained evidence and transparent failure records continue to have research value.
