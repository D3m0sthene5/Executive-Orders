# Methods and governance evidence audit

Repository: `D3m0sthene5/Executive-Orders`.

Pinned main: **`73d99f666573ee10f5b1ff2ada60f7d9331c3952`**, audited October 29, 2026.

Scope: read-only examination of primary reports, current ledgers and generated summaries. No research or test runs were performed. Repository instructions, the Kean brief and the newest agent handoffs were read. This appendix records evidence and suggested judgments; the root audit owns the consolidated ranking.

The strongest distinction is **demonstrated integrity or production value versus demonstrated savings**. Several tools merit high placement because they catch false completeness or recover useful work. **None of the general acceleration mechanisms in this subset has established a large reduction in total campaign hours or tokens.** Unbuilt ideas remain **unranked**, separate from failed experiments.

## Suggested tiers and evidence

### S — integrity infrastructure: Claim-checker and readiness gate

The machinery that keeps a proposal from becoming a false completion count. Current primary output checks **510 claim lines / 509 orders**: **500 mechanical passes**, **420 structural gate passes**, **410 all-checks passes**, **3 mechanical failures**, and **7 mechanically unverified**. These are distinct from **395 ready**. Exact claim hashes, source custody, quotations, source-audit holds and completeness requirements matter.

This is essential quality infrastructure; it establishes neither legal correctness nor time savings.
