# Executive-Orders: whole-project effectiveness tier list

Assessment date: October 29, 2026. Scientific baseline: main `73d99f666573ee10f5b1ff2ada60f7d9331c3952`. Legal cutoff remains October 14, 2026.

## Judgment

The strongest demonstrated work is concentrated in evidence-ready production, exact source recovery, and safeguards that prevent false completion. The leading production families are the codification swarm, the October 28 own-terms route, Lambertini, and Rastell. Broad claims of cheap mass completion and general-purpose acceleration have performed substantially worse.

The four largest current producer-file groups contain **342 of 395 ready identities (86.6%)**: codification 129, Lambertini 93, October 28 scale batches 85, Rastell 35. This is an association with current claim files, not exclusive causal credit. Those claims also depend on shared acquisition, policy implementation, correction and review.

“Ready” means ready for human review. The current 395 comprise **298 original-supported and 97 transcription-dependent** cases; human-certified remains zero. The October 28 scale batch supplies 84 transcription-dependent cases and the spent probe supplies 13, accounting for all 97. The remaining replacement and recheck obligations remain material.

### Grading rule

These are editorial judgments of demonstrated effectiveness within each tool's role, not measured return-on-investment scores. Complete comparable cost records do not exist for most tools. Production earns credit for defensible reviewed outputs; acquisition for usable retained evidence; integrity tools for catching real defects. Leads, proposed routes, hypotheses, drafts and forecasts retain their own status.

- **S:** exceptional demonstrated contribution or indispensable, functioning evidence/integrity infrastructure.
- **A:** strong useful results, with bounded limitations.
- **B:** worthwhile specialist or supporting work; limited completion or efficiency evidence.
- **C:** mixed results, immature integration, or useful ideas with weak demonstrated conversion.
- **D:** poor conversion or a central experimental objective not achieved.
- **F:** a tested shortcut failed badly or increased measured cost.
- **Unranked:** unbuilt, newly proposed, or insufficient completion evidence. Absence of a recovered receipt does not prove that no work occurred.

All main-branch evidence is pinned to the baseline. Poggio and Mansfield are explicitly separated branch results. Grouping avoids pretending ten Early Ranger subjobs are ten independent successes. `JOB_COVERAGE.csv` maps all 66 substantive job directories to the corresponding assessment.

## What the ranking implies

Favor bounded production pools with held evidence and demonstrated conversion. Preserve exact source custody and independent review. Repair source identity, source extent, stale inputs and missing dependencies where actual production has exposed them. Keep discovery efforts for the hard historical corpus, but evaluate their conversions at the correct stage. Do not use route counts, packet counts, projected reach or modeled hours as completed research.

The best demonstrated speed improvement here is a small, specific preparation fix. The biggest production improvement is a successful ordinary research route. There is no basis in these records for a sweeping percentage reduction in the remaining project's actual cost.
