## What is good in the proposal, and what was improved

The useful idea is a queryable history of a **provision**, retaining textual versions separately from its legal operation. Renumbering cannot change a provision's identity; an amendment instruction is distinct from the norm inserted into a host order. Existing Stage 17 and Valla already provide foundations, so this work extends them rather than claiming a new lifecycle engine.

The implementation strengthens the proposal with explicit signing/publication/text dates distinct from legal commencement, source spans and hashes, independent exact record reviews, stable identities across renumbering, previous-version checks, restored ancestor amendments and dependency membership pins. A newly added, removed or moved event invalidates prior query dependencies. Before a documentary witness exists, text-as-of is withheld; held text and retrospective legal reconstruction are labeled separately. Retroactivity is not contemporaneous knowability.

Queries show local uncertainties rather than inventing uninterrupted intervals. One accepted legal-effect scope does not approve an entire order or certify a whole lifecycle. The currently reviewed inputs deliberately leave overall operative states unknown.

## Ideas that do not work automatically

- Reinstatement alone cannot restart a conditional sunset, erase an amendment or establish an uninterrupted legal episode.
- A matching quotation, lexical similarity or cross-reference is not a reviewed legal dependency or applicability judgment.
- A successful historical query over selected records is not an exhaustive search through the legal cutoff.
- Refreshing old hashes cannot replace fresh source examination. Valla's original stale refusals must remain visible.
- Ordinary CPU extraction and this deterministic query layer do not need a GPU. Local model routing would require separately measured quality and hardware evaluation; no token, monetary or speed savings are claimed here.
