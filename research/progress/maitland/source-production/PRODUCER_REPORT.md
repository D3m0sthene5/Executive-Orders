## Dates and coverage

Event dates are signing dates for textual chronology. An explicit legal effective
date is supplied only where the held source states it: EO11252 effective
1965-11-01, and EO10973 effective as of 1961-09-30 despite signing on
1961-11-03 and publication on1961-11-07. The latter requires separate valid-time
and signing/publication/knowledge-time treatment. A retrospective answer about
September30 cannot be represented as an answer knowable on September30.

For the monuments operations, EO10915, EO11963 and EO12220, no separate
commencement clause was located; `effective_date` remains null. Publication
dates remain visible as alternative research data. A reviewer can examine a new
dated interpretation, but the machine must not silently promote signing chronology
to an exact legal endpoint.

EO14252's located passage recites that EO13933 was reinstated by EO14189 while
directing D.C. enforcement activity. It is retained in the source inventory as
later corroborating context, not misclassified as a textual amendment.
The EO10915 filed claim was used as a hypothesis. Held EO10915,10973,11252,
11963 and12220 operative passages were checked. Relevant passages of EO10924,
10972,11036,11051,12148 and12163 were inspected to test stated non-events or
changes to different sections. These bounded reads are not a complete search
for every later instrument, statute, administrative implementation, judicial
limitation or surviving consequence.

No claim of cutoff-complete legal status is made. Seven selected norms are not
complete operative inventories of the listed orders. Reinstatement does not
erase the period of revocation; conditional expiry is not an established event;
restoring text does not automatically revive an exhausted duty. The source's
calendar six-month candidate for EO13933 section5 is2020-12-26, conditional on
no presidential extension. Actual expiry, extension and revival remain open.

## Source quality and review handoff

The inputs are machine-extracted official-PDF text witnesses. Their local text
hashes and exact spans were verified. Raw-PDF URLs/hashes/page locations are
retained from the existing source provenance; no fresh raw-byte custody,
source-page inspection or original authentication is asserted. Print line wraps,
soft hyphens and neighboring-document risks remain visible. Amendment recipes
target exact text witnesses; the different line wrapping of an amending quotation
does not authorize unreviewed OCR normalization.

The candidate bundle has no review ledger. The coordinator must create a
separate controlled review bundle, conduct independent source examination,
record exact canonical record hashes with separate text/legal-effect verdicts,
and preserve control adjudication and after-reveal review. Producer fields are
never independent approval. Any correction creates a new exact reviewed version.
Nothing in this handoff authorizes scientific publication or certification.

Primary official web corroboration was checked against GovInfo's EO14189 PDF
(`https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2025-02-03/pdf/2025-02231.pdf`) and
National Archives disposition tables for the1961Kennedy and1965 series. These
