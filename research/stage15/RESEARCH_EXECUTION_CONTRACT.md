## Strict research rules

- Do not use Stage 14 raw `relationships` directly for new automated work. It contains 117 already quarantined candidates, one additionally detected chronologically reversed amendment edge, and misses 513 successor references that were already present in NARA observations. Use **Stage 15 gated feed** and treat every entry as a *candidate*.
- For each proposed whole-order NO, establish every operative scope or an independently legally sufficient dispositive endpoint, and review amendment footprints, saved rights, contingent powers, statutory/regulatory successors, revival and residual obligations. An ending-instrument title or 1989 editorial note cannot prove all of that.
- For each first-cause claim, prove the order ever became operative. Compare original terms, every intermediate amendment, actual fulfillment/trigger, possible prior legal action, later revocation and later cleanup. A formal revocation creates at most a latest bound without further evidence.
- For each bibliographic admission, distinguish page appearance, publication ID, unique signed instrument, presidential signature, express presidential direction and delegated presidential statutory authority. Different signatures can be legally valid but represent different target census units.
- For statistical inference, do not extrapolate from the 2,515 completed statuses. The 120-item sampling plan includes UNKNOWNs but **has not been adjudicated**. Do not compute survival rates until original-source completeness and weighted review are sufficient.
- For historical unnumbered orders, scan/list entry ➜ original source locator ➜ document image/transcript ➜ signer/capacity ➜ document-equivalence crosswalk ➜ bibliographic inclusion decision. OCR month tokens are never counts of instruments.
- For public reports, display three independent bars: bibliography/document authentication, current legal status, and reconstructed first-cessation histories. No completion bar for a fully unknown historical all-issued denominator.
