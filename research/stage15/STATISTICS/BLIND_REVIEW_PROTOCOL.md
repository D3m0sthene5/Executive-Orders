# Blind 120-case population validity audit

This review packet is separated from the coordinator-only baseline answer key. It represents a stratified probability draw from 14,972 *bibliographic document proxies*; it cannot by itself estimate national all-history survival because the universe is incomplete.

## Case protocol

1. Recover the original instrument, provenance, author, authority, appendices, signing/effective conditions and possible multi-document alias.
2. Describe each operative command and when it began; explicitly distinguish never-activated, hortatory, completed, continuing, and genuinely invalid instruments.
3. Trace the host-amendment graph, statutes, regulations, judicial effect, saved rights, completion, property documents and relevant intervening events through the fixed cutoff.
4. Assess *currently applicable normative legal effect* separately from *enduring legal consequences*. Decide any residual ancillary effect separately.
5. Determine first substantive cessation episode with actual evidence, never infer it from a subsequent cleanup revocation.
6. Record original source URLs, saved representations, precise quotations and independent check; do not inspect the coordinator key until each conclusion has been frozen.
7. Enter HOLD whenever whole-scope negative proof, signatory authentication or a material legal bridge is missing. Do not penalize unresolved cases by turning them into NO.

The fields of `UNSCORED_120_CASE_REVIEW_FORM.csv` are intentionally empty. Send it back completed to the coordinator with evidence. The main Stage15 coordinator ZIP includes baseline outcome information and is **not an acceptable blind-review input**.
