# Inference design: duration and competing risks

**Frozen research cutoff:** 2026-10-14. No national survival estimate is licensed by the current convenience-classified statuses.

## Population

Use a clearly bounded, document-reconciled era (prefer contemporaneously numbered published EOs), then draw a stratified probability sample of **all** documents, including currently UNKNOWN; see the 120-case draw and design weights. Pre-1937 unnumbered coverage must be reported as observed lower-bound bibliographies, not a universal denominator. Consider unpublished orders under 44 USC §1505.

## Time origin and endpoint

Distinguish signing, first legal effectiveness, amendment, first cessation, last cessation, dormancy, revival, and formal revocation. Study order and provision/episode as distinct estimands. A nonoperative or never-activated clause does not contribute a traditional event-at-risk interval. A currently operative or unresolved clause with proved prior onset and no proved termination is administratively right-censored at cutoff; a clause with unproven origin or effect cannot be casually called censored.

## Survival estimators

Use issuance cohort and date at risk. Treat expirations in original terms, later-imposed terms, affirmative executive termination, statute, regulation, judicial intervention, and other termination as competing first causes where supported; use cumulative-incidence functions rather than equating 1-Kaplan-Meier with competing event risks. Record time-varying amendments, statutory transitions, revivals/multistate models and left truncation where documents enter the observable archive later than issuance. Avoid unweighted estimates from selected statuses. Stratified weights and error/nonresponse sensitivity must be included. Do not classify the date of a late housekeeping revocation as first cessation.

## Publication gate

No estimates until: identity sampling frame audited; all sample originals and amendments sourced; provision-level episodes reconstructed; competing event type and date adjudicated; missingness and source failure quantified; weighted uncertainty intervals calculated.
