## Remaining defects

Historical unnumbered issuance is seriously underrepresented. Model-reviewed outcomes lack a representative independent accuracy estimate. Later formal revocation dates cannot be substituted for first cessation. The complete Opus original-media companion has now been uploaded and its 496 PDF/image members cryptographically checked against the Opus manifest; association of any particular image with a legally dispositive original EO remains a separate research question. Earlier Stage 17 source provenance and any separately sealed originals require their own audit. Publication of a reproduction-ready database does not transform these findings into certified legal histories.

Do not use this repository's interim status coverage as the survival proportion among all American executive orders.
