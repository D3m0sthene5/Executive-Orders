## Version-by-version work

- **Stage 12:** independent census audit, duplicate/date corrections, initial census comparators, sample protocols and visual reports.
- **Stage 13:** checked provenance flags, added historical date evidence without presuming historical authentication, eight preliminary independent legal reviews and attribution challenges.
- **Stage 14:** acquired/crosswalked a source vault, built high-fanout evidence graph and document recovery queues, and prioritized research by downstream leverage.
- **Stage 15:** repaired omitted successor references, imposed chronology and legal completeness screens, designed a stratified sample and identified missing provision footprints.
- **Stage 16:** migrated to a canonical source-preserving SQLite architecture, incorporated legal-event and provision tables, applied stronger classification gates, and retained unresolved legal attribution.
- **Stage 17:** reconciled 540 additional Opus legacy-v2 decisions, preserving 531 held and 155 conflicts, added 13 scoped provision histories and 26 dated changes, and built a functioning claim-level review importer with a zero-certification baseline.

## Remaining defects

Historical unnumbered issuance is seriously underrepresented. Model-reviewed outcomes lack a representative independent accuracy estimate. Later formal revocation dates cannot be substituted for first cessation. The original-image companions for the Stage 17 Opus import and complete Opus release have not been verified or published in this repository. Publication of a reproduction-ready database does not transform these findings into certified legal histories.

Do not use this repository's interim status coverage as the survival proportion among all American executive orders.
