that (1) no firing or movement of ve­
hicles or troops, except for the necessary
road crossing, shall be permitted within
two miles of the Coachella Canal; (2)
that the representatives of the Bureau of
Reclamation shall have access to the en­
tire area for the maintenance of its ex­
isting structures or fo r the Construction
of any additional storm water control
features necessary to protect the works
o f the Bureau; and t3) there shall be no
obstruction in the present storm water
courses in the area which would inter­
fere with the present natural flow.

but not modify (1) the withdrawal for
classification and other purposes by Ex­
ecutive Order No. 6910 of November 26,
1934, as amended,-H2) the withdrawal by
Executive Order No. 6361 of October 25,
1933, for classification and pending a de­
termination as to the advisability of in­
cluding certain lands in a national mon­
ument, and (3) the withdrawal orders
made by the Secretary of the Interior for
reclamation purposes o f April 2 and 9,
1909, October 19, 1920, and June 4, 1930,
so far as they affect the above-described
lands.

months’ period following the termina­

May 27, 1941 (55 Stat. 1647). There­
upon, jurisdiction over the lands hereby
reserved shall be vested in the Depart­

ever, shall remain withdrawn from ap­
propriation as herein provided until
