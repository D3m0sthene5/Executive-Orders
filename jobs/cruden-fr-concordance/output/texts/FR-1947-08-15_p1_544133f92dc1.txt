WHEREAS by proclamation of the

dated March 16, 1911, certain land on
the Island of Hawaii, situated at Cocoanut Point at the foot of Waianuenue

WHEREAS such land is no longer

be restored to the use of the Territory
of Hawaii :
NOW, THEREFORE, by virtue of the
authority vested in me by section 91 of
the act of April 30, 1900, 31 Stat. 159, as
amended by section 7 of the act of May
27, 1910, 36 Stat. 447, and as President
of the United States, it is ordered that
the following-described land at Cocoanut

and it is hereby, restored to the juris­
