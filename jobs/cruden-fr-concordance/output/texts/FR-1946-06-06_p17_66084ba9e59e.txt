PROCLAMATION DESIGNATING NATIVE
RESERVATION

Pursuant to authority vested in the
Secretary of the Interior by section 2
of the act of May 1, 1936, 49 Stat. 1250
(U.S.C., title 48, sec. 358a), there is
hereby designated as a native reserva­
tion for the use and occupancy of the
native inhabitants of the village of
Diomede, Alaska, and vicinity, the Island
of Little Diomede, which is located in
Latitude 65e41' N. and Longitude 168° 55'
W., which includes approximately 1.43
acres previously reserved by Executive
Order No. 5289, dated March 4, 1930, for
educational purposes, together with the
waters adjacent thereto within 3,000 feet
from the shore line at mean low tide.
Little Diomede Island aggregates ap­
proximately 3,000 acres.
This order shall be subject to any valid
existing rights or claims acquired prior
to the date hereof and shall become ef­
fective only upon its approval by a m a­
jority vote of the natives residing in the
above-described area, voting in the man­
ner prescribed by the said section 2 of
the act of M ay 1, 1936.
Done in the City of Washington, D. C.,
this 22d day of April 1946.
