LIVESTOCK GRAZING ON PUBLIC LANDS
Schedule of Fees, 1975

Pursuant to the authority vested in the
Secretary of the Interior, notice is hereby
given of the schedule of fees for the 1975
fee year beginning March 1, 1975, and
ending February 29, 1976, for livestock
grazing on the public lands.
For the purpose of establishing
charges, one animal unit month (AUM)
shall be considered equivalent to graz­
ing use by one cow, five sheep, or one
horse for one month. The charge for one
horse is at twice the rate for one cow.
