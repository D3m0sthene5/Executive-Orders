Upon the written recommendation of
the Secretary of State and by virtue of
the authority vested in me by section 501
of the Second War Powers Act, 1942 (Pub­

waive compliance with the provisions of

calendar year 1942, to permit Canadian
"fishing vessels engaging in the North Pa­
cific halibut fishery only to land their
catch of halibut in ports of entry in
Alaska upon compliance with the ap­
plicable customs laws. 1 deem that such
action is necessary in the conduct of the
war.
