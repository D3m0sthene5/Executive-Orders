Subpart 2621— Indemnity Selections
§ 2 6 2 1 .0 —2

Objectives and Background.

Generally, grants made by Statehood
Acts to the various States of school sec­
tions 16 and 36, and in addition, sections
2 and 32 in ArizQna, New Mexico, and
Utah, attach to a school sections on the
date of acceptance or approval of the
plat of survey thereof. If the acceptance
or approval was prior to the granting
act, or to the date of admission of the
State into the Union, the grant attaches
either on the date of approval of the act
or the date of admission into the Union,
whichever is the later date. However, if
on the date the grant would otherwise
attach, the land is appropriated under
some applicable public land law, the
grant does not attach, and the State is
entitled to indemnity therefor as pro­
vided in the regulations in this subpart.
