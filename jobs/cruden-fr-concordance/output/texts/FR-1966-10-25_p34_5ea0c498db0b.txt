The Federal Aviation Agency is con­
sidering an amendment to Part 71 of
the Federal Aviation Regulations that
would alter the Shelbyville, Tenn.,
transition area.
The Shelbyville transition area, de­
scribed in § 71.181 (31 F.R. 2149), would
be redesignated as:
That airspace extending upward from 700
feet above the surface within a 10-mile
radius of Bomar Field (latitude 35°33'44" N.,
longitude 86° 26'33" W.); within 5 miles N
and 8 miles S of the Shelbyville VOR 272°
radial extending from the VOR to 12 miles
W; within 5 miles E and 8 miles W of the
Shelbyville VOR 196° radial extending from
the VOR to 12 miles S.
The proposed amendment would pro­
vide additional controlled airspace hav­
ing a base of 700 feet above the surface
north and east of the present transition
area. This airspace is required for the
protection of IFR aircraft departing
Bomar Field during climb from 700 to
1,200 feet above the surface.
