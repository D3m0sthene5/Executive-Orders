Statement To Accompany Amendment 71
to the Controlled Housing Rent
Regulation
It is the judgment of the Housing Ex­
pediter that the need for continuing
maximum rents in certain portions of the
Ocean County, New Jersey, DefenseRental Area— namely, the Townships of
Lacey, Union, Ocean, Stafford, Eagleswood and Little Egg Harbor; the Bor­
oughs of Point Pleasant and Point
Pleasant Beach; and certain other beach
territory—no longer exists due to the fact
that the demand for rental housing ac­
commodations has been reasonably met.
This amendment is, therefore, being
issued to decontrol said portions of said
Defense-Rental Area in accordance with
section 202 (c) of the Housing and Rent
Act of 1947, as amended.
