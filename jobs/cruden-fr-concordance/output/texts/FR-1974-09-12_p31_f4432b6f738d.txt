The purpose of this amendment to
P art 71 of file Federal Aviation reg­
ulations is to amend the description of
VOR Federal Airway No. 198 between
Greenville, Fla., and Taylor, Fla.
At the present time V-198 is described
as six miles wide from Greenville, Fla.,
to 18 miles east of Greenville, Fla. The
reduced route width was designated some
years ago to provide lateral separation
from special use airspace. Past airspace
actions have eliminated the need for
the reduced airway width and action is
taken herein to revoke the six mile route
width and replace it with the standard
eight mile route width.
Since this amendment is minor in
nature and one upon which the public
would have no particular reason to com­
ment, notice and public procedure there­
on are unnecessary.

ment Order No. 190, Rev. 9 (38 FR

the new port encompass all of the area
falling, before this consolidation, within
the port limits of Beaumont, Orange,
Port Arthur, and Sabine, Texas, and in­
clude all of Jefferson County and Orange
County, Texas.
