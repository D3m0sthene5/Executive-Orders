Notice is hereby given that pursuant to
the authority contained in section 10 of
the Migratory Bird Conservation Act of
February 18, 1929 (45 Stat. 1224; 16
U. S. C. 715i), and under authority dele­
gated by Commissioner’s Order 4 (22
F. R. 8126), it is proposed to add § 32.169
to Subpart—-Tishomingo National Wild­
life Refuge, Oklahoma, Title 50, Code of
Federal Regulations, to read as set forth
in tentative form below. The purpose of
this amendment is to provide for the
hunting of migratory game birds and
resident game birds and mammals on
certain lands of the Tishomingo National
Wildlife Refuge under certain limitations
and subject to compliance with the laws
and regulations of the State of Okla­
homa.

amendment to the Director, Bureau of
