3. The lands are located in Riverside

east in a general parallel pattern to the
Colorado River Aqueduct. This south­
easterly pattern continues along the San
Bernardino Mountains to the Mecca
Hills. Most o f the area is rough and
mountainous. The community of Desert
Hot Springs is within two miles of the
edge o f the lands. U. S. Highway 99
parallels the lands at a distance of ap­
proximately 12 miles.
