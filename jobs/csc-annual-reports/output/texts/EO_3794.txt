February 26, 1923.
Mr. Thomas S. Shelley may be reinstated with a classified status effective September
7, 1917.
Mr. Shelley, after serving many years in the classified service of the Engineer
Department, was furloughed without pay on September 30, 1911, and has been serving
since September 7, 1917, with an excepted status. ;
This order is recommended by the Secretary of War and the Civil Service Com-
mission.
