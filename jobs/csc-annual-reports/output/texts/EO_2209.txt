June 10, 1915.
Mrs. Emma L. Guckert may be reinstated as an operative in the Bureau of Engraving
and Printing, without regard to the length of time she has been separated from the
service.
This order is issued on the recommendation of the Secretary of the Treasury on
account of Mrs. Guckert’s former efficient service in the bureau, and for the further
reason that her husband, who has been employed in the bureau since July, 1901, is
sick and will probably never be able to resume his duties, and the burden of sup-
nig a family consisting of the husband, wife, and two children has fallen on Mrs.
uckert.
