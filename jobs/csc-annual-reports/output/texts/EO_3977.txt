March 25, 1924
Mr. Charles G. Keiser may be reinstated as clerk in the post office at Indian-
apolis, Ind., without regard to the time limitation prescribed by the civil-service
rules. Mr. Keiser served for 17 years in the Indianapolis post office and was
separated February 6, 1910, in connection with a reduction of force. The
Postmaster General recommends the reinstatement because of Mr. Keiser’s
good record while in the service and the conclusion reached by inspectors who
investigated the case that he was unjustly deprived of his position, the evidence
indicating that he was dropped for political reasons.
The Civil Service Commission interposes no objection to the issuance of this
order.
