August 26, 1915.
The year limit upon eligibility for reinstatement is hereby waived to permit the
reinstatement of Green A. Stephens in the postal service.
Mr. Stephens was a carrier in the San Francisco post office from June 23, 1891, to
April 12, 1905, when he was removed upon the charge of tampering with mail addressed
to a patron in his district and using a race-track admission ticket contained in a letter
so addressed. A careful reinvestigation by a post-office inspector resulted in the con-
clusion that beyond any reasonable doubt Mr. Stephens was not guilty of the charge
for which he was removed from the postal service. The Postmaster General states
that there is no question in his mind but that a distinct injustice was done in separating
Carrier Stephens from the service on the flimsy and prejudiced evidence which was
adduced in the case. He therefore recommends that an Executive order be issued
to reinstate him in the service, and the Civil Service Commission, after careful review
of the case, concurs in this recommendation.
