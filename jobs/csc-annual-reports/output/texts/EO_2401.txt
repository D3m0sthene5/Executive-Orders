June 8, 1916.
The year limit upon eligibility for reinstatement is hereby waived to permit
the reinstatement of Mr. William M. Scott in the Department of Agriculture.
Mr. Scott served in the department for eight years as a plant pathologist, re-
signing in 1912 to become the head of a research laboratory, and his reinstate-
ment is desired by the department for investigational and research work. In
view of the service Mr. Scott has rendered the department and his experience
gained since his separation which will prove valuable to the department, the
Civil Service Commission concurs with the Secretary of Agriculture in recom-
mending this order.
