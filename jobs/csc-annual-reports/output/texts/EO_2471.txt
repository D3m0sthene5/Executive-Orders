October 17, 1916. :
Mrs. George S. Ferrow may be appointed to an existing vacancy in the position of
clerk at $900 per annum in the Bureau of Navigation, Department of Commerce, with-
out reference to the civil-service rules.
This order is issued upon the recommendation of the Secretary of Commerce and the
Commissioner of Navigation, who state that Mrs. Ferrow is the widow of Mr. George S.
Ferrow who died on October 2, 1916, as a result of an accident, and who was in charge of
an important section of the Bureau of Navigation; that for many years he served in
various capacities and was at all times a faithful, efficient, and industrious clerk; that
as a result of his death his widow and two small children have been left without means
of support; that Mrs. Ferrow is fully competent to perform the duties of the position
to which it is proposed to appoint her; and that for these reasons it is believed that an
exception to the rules in her case is warranted in the interest of the service.
The Civil Service Commission does not concur in the recommendation.
