May 31, 1916.
Mrs. Ada E. B. Snyder may be appointed an operative in the Bureau of En-
graving and Printing without complying with the requirements of the Civil
Service Commission. This order is issued on the recommendation of the Sec-
retary of the Treasury, who states that Mrs. Snyder’s husband, William H. M.
Snyder, was employed in the bureau for a number of years and has recently
become insane. His wife and two children are without means of support, and
it is believed that Mr. Snyder’s record in the bureau justifies the waiving of the
rules in order that his wife may have employment to support herself and two
small children. The Civil Service Commission does not concur in the recom-
mendation.
