April 5, 1917.
The following-named persons may be permanently appointed in the competitive
classified service of the Treasury Department without reference to the civil-service
rules: Miss Margaret R. Blackford, Miss Edyth M. Branson, Miss Tillie Burchell,
Miss Catherine Easton, Mrs. Mary E. Richey, Miss Elva A. Leonard, Miss Irma E.
Dudley, Miss S. Katherine Rudasill, Miss Jessie H. Libhart, Miss Jennie B. Clark.
This order is issued upon the recommendation of the Secretary of the Treasury,
who states that the persons'named have been temporarily employed in the Treasury
Department in the absence of civil-service registers as expert calculating machine
operators, and that they have rendered very efficient and faithful service, and that
their permanent appointment will be greatly in the interests of the service.
