July 17, 1924
Mr. Frederick J. Crocker may be reinstated in his former position of chief
of the stamp gumming division of the Bureau of Engraving and Printing, from
which he was dismissed on March 31, 1922. Mr. Crocker has been thus far pre-
cluded from reinstatement by the fact that he was granted an annuity as a me-
chanic retirable at 65, which action, it appears from a review of the case, was
based on misleading information, his proper retirement age being 70. The
Treasury Department, the Department of the Interior, and the Civil Service
Commission are in agreement upon this action.
