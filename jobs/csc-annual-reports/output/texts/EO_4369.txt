January 19, 1926.
Isabel L. Towner may be reinstated to the position of assistant librarian of
the National Museum without regard to the time limit on reinstatement into
the classified service.
Miss Towner was in the classified service as librarian and cataloguer from
December 1, 1909, to July 31, 1920. Since that time she has been librarian for
the National Tuberculosis Association and the National Health Council. Her
education and extensive experience with scientific publications especially
qualify her for the position in the National Museum.
This order is issued upon the recommendation of the Secretary of the
Smithsonian Institution. The Civil Service Commission concurs.
