May 14, 1915.
Mr. Carroll W. Dunning may be reinstated in the Office of Markets and Rural
Organization in the Department of Agriculture, where his services are needed to
cooperate with the Forest Service in investigations in the marketing of woodlot
Products He served as statistician in forest products from November 1, 1911, to
arch 16, 1914, and the department states that he has had unusually valuable training
and experience at home and abroad. Since leaving the service he has been engaged
in a marketing proposition, representing on the Pacific coast a number of eastern
manufacturers, this employment being in line with his qualifications and experience.
This order is issued upon the recommendation of the Secretary of Agriculture with
the concurrence of the Civil Service Commission.
