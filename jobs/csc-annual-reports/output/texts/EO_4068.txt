August 29, 1924
Physical requirements for examination for rural carrier are hereby waived to
permit examination of Mr. William Anderson for the position of rural carrier at
Bemidji, Minn. Mr. Anderson’s application for the examination which was
held on April 26, 1924, was not accepted because it showed that he had suffered the
loss of his right foot; that he was fitted with an artificial foot and that he could
stand on his feet all day.
The regulations prohibit acceptance of an application for rural-carrier exami-
nation from anyone who has lost a foot, except in the case of honorably discharged
ex-service men who have been trained by the Veterans’ Bureau for the position of
rural carrier. The Postmaster General requested that the Civil Service Commis-
sion waive the usual physical requirements in favor of Mr. Anderson, but the
commission is without power to do so.
The Civil Service Commission does not concur in recommending this order.
For charity.
