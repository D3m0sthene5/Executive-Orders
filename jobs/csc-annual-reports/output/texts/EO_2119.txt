January 12, 1915.
The Civil Service Commission having reported that at a number of offices no eligi-
bles were secured as a result of the examination for fourth-class postmaster, under
the Executive order of May 7, 1913, providing for a competitive examination for
each post office where the postmaster was appointed otherwise than under the regula-
tions of November 25, 1912, or of January 20, 1909, that order is hereby amended by
adding the following:
The Civil Service Commission shall hold a second examination for each office
which has an annual compensation of as much as $500 and for which no eligibles
were secured as a result of the first examination. The commission may also, in its
discretion, hold a second examination for any office for which no eligibles were secured
as a result of the first examination and which has an annual compensation of between
$180 and $500.
