February 8, 1916.
Mr. Frederick H. Signor, an unclassified laborer, at $660 per annum, in
the Office of the Secretary of Commerce, may be promoted to a subclerical
position in the same office without reference to the civil-service rules. This
order is issued upon the recommendation of the Secretary of Commerce, who
states that by reason of Mr. Signor’s diligence and ability he has been enabled
to acquire special knowledge with respect to the elevator, refrigerating and
heating equipment, the switchboards, and wiring in the Commerce Building;
that it is believed he is better qualified than an eligible who could be obtained
from the Civil Service Commission; and that he is qualified to perform the
duties of the engineer of the department in the absence of that officer. Mr.
Signor has passed the skilled-laborer examination with an average of 84.50.
