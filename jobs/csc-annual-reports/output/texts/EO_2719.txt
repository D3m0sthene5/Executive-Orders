September 28, 1917.
Mr. Frank H. Schloer may be reinstated as an instrument maker at $1,600 per
annum in the Office of Public Roads and Rural Engineering, Department of Agricul-
ture, the year limit on eligibility for reinstatement being waived in view of the qualifi-
cations of Mr. Schloer as an instrument maker demonstrated by many years of service
in the Department of Agriculture, and of the difficulty of securing a sufficient number
of eligibles to meet the demands for the position.
