April 13, 1916.
Mrs. Dora M. Overby may be appointed to a position in the classified service
of the Government without regard to civil-service rules. This order is issued
on the recommendation of the Acting Secretary of the Treasury, who states
that Mrs. Overby rendered very efficient and satisfactory service under
her appointments by the Federal Reserve Organization Committee and the
Federal Reserve Board, and that, in view of her experience and capabilities
it is believed her appointment to a classified position will be in the interest of
the service. The Civil Service Commission does not concur in the recom-
mendaton.
