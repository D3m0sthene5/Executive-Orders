March 21, 1917.
Mr. William J. Martin may be appointed to a competitive position in the Treasury
Department.
This order is issued on the recommendation of the Secretary of the Treasury, who
states that Mr. Martin has rendered very efficient service for several years in an ex-
cepted position as executive clerk, has made an excellent record, has a grasp of the
many details and difficult problems connected with the work of the office, has become
eculiarly familiar with the personnel and appointments under the internal revenue
ws, and that the action proposed is desired in order to permit the extension of Mr.
Martin’s activities.
For the reasons stated by the Secretary the Civil Service Commission concurs in
the recommendation.
