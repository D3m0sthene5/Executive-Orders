March 6, 1926.
Mrs. Harriet P. Lemly, widow of Capt. Samuel C. Lemly, late Judge
Advocate General of the United States Navy, may be employed as a clerk in
the Navy Department, Washington, D. C., without regard to the provisions of
the civil service rules.
As the Civil Service Commission can properly recommend waivers only when
the procedure under the rules will not fully meet the needs of the service, it is
unable to concur in the recommendation of the Secretary of the Navy for the
issuance of this order.
Done for charity.
