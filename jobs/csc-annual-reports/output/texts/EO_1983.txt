July 6, 1914.
Mrs. Ella Riley Oliphant may be appointed to a position in the Government
Printing Office without reference to the requirements of the civil-servicerules as to ex-
amination. :
Mrs. Oliphant was temporarily employed as a sewer in the Government Printing
Office at various times during the years 1901, 1902, and 1903. Her father contracted
a disease while in the Navy from which he died. Two of her brothers served in the
Army for three years during the Civil War. She is a widow, with no income whatever.
