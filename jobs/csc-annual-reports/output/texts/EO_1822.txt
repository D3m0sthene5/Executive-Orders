September 11, 1913.
Mr. James D. Scott may be reinstated in the Railway Mail Service, without regard
to the length of time he has been out of the service.
The Postmaster General reports that he was appointed a railway mail clerk on
October 17, 1900, at $800 per annum, and promoted through the successive grades until
he received a salary of $1,100 per annum; that he resigned voluntarily, April 17,1911;
that his record as a clerk is good, and his reinstatement would be to the benefit of the
service.
The Civil Service Commission does not concur in recommending that an Executive
order be issued in this case.
