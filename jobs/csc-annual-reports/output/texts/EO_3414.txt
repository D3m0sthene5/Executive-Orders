March 1, 1921. ;
There are hereby excluded from the operation of the retirement act of May
22, 1920, noneducational employees in the Reclamation Service, intermittently
and irregularly employed, and educational employees on daily rates, similarly
employed, and the continuance of the present practice of making no deductions:
from their pay is hereby approved. This action is deemed necessary because
of the intermittent character of the employment and the exceedingly frequent:
changes in personnel.
