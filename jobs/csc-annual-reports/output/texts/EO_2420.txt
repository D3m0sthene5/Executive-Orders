July 14, 1916.
Mr. Marion T. Edwards may be appointed to the position of assistant messen-
ger qualified in the operation and repair of automobiles, in the Bureau of Stand-
ards at $720 per annum, without reference to the civil-service rules. This order
is issued upon the recommendation of the Secretary of Commerce and the Direc-
tor of the Bureau of Standards, who state that the position to which it is pro-
posed to appoint Mr. Edwards is a confidential post; that by experience and
training Mr. Edwards is especially qualified for the position; and that his ap-
pointment would be in the interests of good administration. The Civil Service
Commission does not concur in the recommendation.
