July 2, 1914.
The services of Dr. Park Howell may be continued as acting assistant surgeon in
the Public Health Service, for a period of not to exceed one year, beginning July 18,
1914, without compliance with the civil-service rules and regulations.
This order is recommended by the Secretary of the Treasury because the services
of an additional officer are needed for the above period and there is no commissioned
officer available for assignment to the duty.
