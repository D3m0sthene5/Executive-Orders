June 8, 1916.
Mrs. Katie O’Leary Jackson may be reinstated as an operative in the Bureau
of Engraving and Printing without regard to the length of time she has been
separated from the service. This order is issued on the recommendation of
the Acting Secretary of the Treasury, who states that during her former service
in the Bureau Mrs. Jackson was a valuable employee, and that it is believed
her reinstatement will be in the interest of the service. The Civil Service
Commission does not concur in the recommendation.
