June 21, 1926.
Paragraph 1, Subdivision III, Department of Commerce, Schedule B of the
civil-service rules, which provides for the appointment of clerks to commercial
attaches, Bureau of Foreign and Domestic Commerce, upon noncompetitive
examination, is revoked.
Subdivision XI, Department of Commerce, Schedule A of the civil-service
rules is amended by adding a new paragraph numbered seven, reading as
follows:
"7. Clerks to commercial attaches and trade commissioners for service
in foreign countries or in the insular possessions of the United States."
This order is recommended by the United States Civil Service Commission
with the concurrence of the Department of Commerce.
