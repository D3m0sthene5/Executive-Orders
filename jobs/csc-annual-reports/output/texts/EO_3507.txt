June 28, 1921.
Mrs. Bernice Blacksten may be appointed to a clerical position in the classified
civil service without complying with the requirements of the civil-service rules.
Mrs. Blacksten was appointed in September, 1917, to a clerical position in the
Motor Transport Corps field service of the War Department, and served in such
position until March 31, 1919, when she resigned.
Mrs. Blacksten has been serving since April 4, 1921, as a temporary clerk
in the Public Health Service, with the approval of the Civil Service Commission,
is well qualified for the duties of her position, and it is believed that her perma-
nent appointment will be in the interest of the service.
