March 22, 1917.
Otto Lewis Evans, of Amherst, Va., a law graduate of the University of Virginia,
and a practicing lawyer from 1885 to 1915, and who has served in the Department of
State since August 1916, may be appointed a law clerk in the Department of State
without reference to the requirements of the civil-service rules.
This action is based upon the recommendation of the Secretary of State, because of
jhe efficient service of Mr. Evans, and his special qualifications and experience as a
awyer.
