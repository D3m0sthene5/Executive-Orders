November 5, 1916.
The year limit upon eligibility for reinstatement is hereby waived to permit the re-
instatement of Dr. Lee F. Hawley in the Department of Agriculture.
Dr. Hawley served in the Forest Service at Madison, Wis., as chemist in forest
products, having been appointed through examination, from June 1, 1911, to Januar
31, 1913, resigning to accept private employment. The Acting Secretary of Agricul-
ture states that since Dr. Hawley ’s resignation he has been engaged in lines of chemical
research which are very closely identified with the character of the work he was doing
for the Forest Service and has gained a wealth of commercial experience and practical
knowledge which greatly enlarges his usefulness to the department and will enable
the department to continue important investigations without loss of time or efficiency.
The Civil Service Commission concurs with the Acting Scretary of Agriculture in
recommending this order.
