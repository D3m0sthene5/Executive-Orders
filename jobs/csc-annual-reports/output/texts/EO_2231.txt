August 6, 1915.
Mr. Francis J. Reinhard, of Indiana, may be appointed to the position of law clerk
in the office of the Solicitor of the Treasury, without reference to the civil-service rules.
This order is issued upon the recommendation of the Attorney General, who states
that the relationship existing between the solicitor and the man holding this position
is quite confidential; that the duties of the office require a man of considerable expe-
rience as a lawyer; that Solicitor Becker of the Treasury, who personally knows Mr.
Reinhard, is familiar with his qualifications, has confidence in him, and has recom-
mended that he be appointed, and that an effort has been made to find some one within
the civil service possessing the special qualifications needed, but without success.
The Attorney General further states that he believes the interests of the public service
would be subserved by exempting the appointment.
