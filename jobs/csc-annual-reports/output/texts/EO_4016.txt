June 3, 1924
Inasmuch as Julius G. Lay was appointed in the Consular Service on Septem-
ber 1, 1893, as vice and deputy consul general at Ottawa, having previously
served four years as clerk in that office; was promoted October 26, 1896, to consul
at Windsor; May 3, 1899, to consul general at Barcelona; October 3, 1904, to
consul general at Canton; May 24, 1906, to consul general at Cape Town; May
2, 1910, to consul general at Rio de Janeiro; June 24, 1914, to consul general at
Berlin; February 5, 1915, to consul general of class 2, under the act reorganizing
the Consular Service, and voluntarily resigned March 1, 1920, he may be rein-
stated without examination in the grade from which he resigned when a vacancy
shall occur,
