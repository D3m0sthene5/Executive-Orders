March 13, 1917.
Isaac Bassett, Thomas Harkness, and William H. Wise, guides at the Washington
Navy Yard, may be appointed as watchmen in that yard without examination under
the civil-service rules.
These men, although not employees of the Government, have served for many
oon as guides, and their accurate knowledge of employees and activities of the
ard fit them peculiarly for the duties of watchmen. The department states that it
is important that their services be utilized, and the Civil Service Commission concurs
with the department in recommending this order.
