““The persons to be appointed under this order will be bona fide students of high
schools or colleges who will be available for short periods during their vacations in
the summer months at a time when many permanent employees of the bureau are
on leave of absence. It is believed that students who acquire experience by service
in these positions will be of more value to their employers when they complete their
courses and engage in their professions and at the same time will be a help to the
bureau in its work which often comes in contact with that of manufacturing establish-
ments in electrical and mechanical lines.”
