. October 25, 1918.
Mrs. Anna I. Bennett may be appointed to the position of clerk in the classified
service without regard to the civil-service rules.
This order is issued on the recommendation of the Postmaster General, who states
that on account of Mrs. Bennett's distinct ability as a clerk, gained as the.result
of over four years of training in the Post Office Department and the difficulty that
would be experienced in replacing her by the appointment of a new and inexpe-
rienced clerk, her appointment would be in the interest of the service.
