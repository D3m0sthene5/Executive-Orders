December 3, 1924.
Mrs. Myrtle L. Davis may be appointed to a clerical position in the classifiedservice without examination. This order is issued on the recommendation of theDirector of the Veterans' Bureau, who states that Mrs. Davis was employed fromNovember 26, 1917 to September 30, 1924 (when separated at the request of theCivil Service Commission) as a result of selection from a certificate containingthe name of a Myrtle L. Davis, who in fact was another person. In view ofher long service without interruption, the fact that her record is stated to becreditable and efficient, the rather peculiar circumstances under which she wasformerly erroneously appointed, the lack of any culpability on her part, andthe apparent hardship of her discharge under such circumstances, the CivilService Commission concurs.
