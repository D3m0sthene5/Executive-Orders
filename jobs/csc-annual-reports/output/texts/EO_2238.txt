August 19, 1915.
First Sergeant William Feldman, United States Army, retired, may be admitted to
examination for the classified service. Paragraph 1, civil-service Rule V, which
provides that no person shall be admitted to examination unless he be a citizen of or
owe allegiance to the United States, is hereby waived in his favor.
Mr. Feldman is technically not a citizen of the United States, notwithstanding his
service in the Army. In view of his honorable Army service of over 25 years, he being
retired as first sergeant of Troop F, Sixth Cavalry, the commission concurs with the
Secretary of War in recommending that Mr. Feldman be admitted to examination
under waiver of the rule and with the understanding that he will without delay take
the necessary steps to acquire citizenship.
