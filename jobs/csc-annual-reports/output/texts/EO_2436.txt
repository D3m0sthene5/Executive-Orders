August 8, 1916.
The year limit on eligibility for reinstatement is hereby waived to permit
the reinstatement of Mr, Arthur 8. Hull as a fourth assistant examiner in the
Patent Office. In view of Mr. Hull's efficient service as an examiner and his
experience acquired during a service of four and one-half years, and in view
of the continuing difficulty of obtaining a sufficient number of qualified eligibles
to meet the demands of the Patent Office for examiners, the Secretary of the
Interior recommends this order and the Civil Service Commission concurs in
the recommendation.
