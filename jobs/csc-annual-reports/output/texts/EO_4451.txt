June 2, 1926.
The following Indian boarding schools in the Five Civilized Tribes, Oklahoma,
are hereby placed within the classified civil service of the United States:
Bloomfield Seminary.
Euchee Boarding School.
Eufaula Boarding School.
Jones Male Academy.
Mekusukey Male Academy.
Tuskahoma Academy.
Wheelock Female Academy.
Employees in these schools who are efficient and are occupying positions
subject to civil-service examination, whose names will be later reported to
the Civil Service Commission, may be considered as classified upon approval
of the commission, and all future vacancies must be filled in accordance with
civil-service rules.
