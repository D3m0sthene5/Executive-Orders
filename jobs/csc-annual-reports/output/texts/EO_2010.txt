August 3, 1914.
_ Mr. Fred T. Karr may be reinstated as a railway postal clerk without reference
to the year limit of eligibility.
Mr. Karr was appointed through examination on March 18, 1907, was several times
promoted, and resigned on August 2, 1912, on account of the poor condition of his
health. His reinstatement is recommended by the Postmaster General and the
General Superintendent of the Railway-Mail Service in the interest of good adminis-
tration because of his satisfactory record and because of his previous training, which
would be of value to the service. He has submitted a medical certificate showing
that he is in good physical condition. The Civil Service Commission concurs in
recommending this order.
