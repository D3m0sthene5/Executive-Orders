January 5, 1922.
Mr. George B. Hamlet, of Maryland, may be appointetl to a clerical position
in the classified service. Mr. Hamlet was appointed originally on October 3,
1882, in the Interior Department. He was classified July 16, 1883, and con-
tinued in the classified service of the United States until the date of his resig-
nation from the customs service October 16, 1913. This separation from the
classified service was due to ill health. After two years under the care of a
physician, he fully recovered his health, and on November 26, 1917, was ap-
pointed a clerk in the Bureau of Investigation, Department of Justice, where
he is now employed and where he has rendered excellent service. He has been
promoted twice and is assigned to very important work.
This order is issued upon recommendation of the Attorney Ceneral, with the
concurrence of the Civil Service Commission.
