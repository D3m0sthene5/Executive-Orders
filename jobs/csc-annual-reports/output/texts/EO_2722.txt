October 6, 1917.
Miss Lillian Schmidt, of Nebraska, may be appdinted to a permanent posi-
tion under the United States Employees Compensation Commission without
reference to the civil-service rules.
This order is issued upon the recommendation of the chairman of the com-
mission, who states that Miss Schmidt has been employed temporarily by the
commission, has proved to be of more than ordinary ability, has shown an
interest in the work, and has become one of their most valuable assistants,
and that her permanent appointment would be in the interest of the service.
