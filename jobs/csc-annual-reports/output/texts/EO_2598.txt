April 16, 1917.
Mr. Henry Fox may be reinstated as assistant engineer at $2,400 per annum in the
Engineer Department at large, Little Rock, Ark., without regard to the length of time
he has been separated from the service.
Mr. Fox served about 14 years under the Engineer Department at large in various
responsible positions and was furloughed on August 31, 1909. He has since been
engaged in important engineer work in connection with rivers, harbors, and canals.
In addition to his large experience in river and harbor work, he has passed several
high-grade examinations for responsible positions in the classified service. His
services are urgently required in connection with the completion of the survey of the
Arkansas River, a difficult work and one which can not be satisfactorily performed
except by a person having broad experience.
