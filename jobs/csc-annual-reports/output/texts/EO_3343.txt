October 20, 1920.
Mr, James J. Haney may be promoted from chief messsenger to clerk in the
classified executive service of the Department of Justice without compliance
with the civil-service rules.
This order is issued on the recommendation of the Attorney General because
of the long and faithful service of James J. Haney as assistant messenger and
chief messenger in the Department of Justice since 1876, including services for
a considerable portion of that time as personal messenger to several Attorneys
General.
