March 6, 1922.
Mr. Arthur W. Mueller may be appointed to the position of clerk in the Post
Office Department without regard to civil service rules and regulations.
The Postmaster General recommends this order in view of Mr. Mueller’s
satisfactory service in the Division of Air Mail Service, which service at the
time of Mr. Mueller’s appointment was not treated as in the classified service.
The Civil Service Commission does not concur in the recommendation.
