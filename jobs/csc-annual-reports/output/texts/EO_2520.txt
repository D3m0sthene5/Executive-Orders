January 25, 1917.
Mr. Michael Flaherty may be promoted to a subclerical position or to the position
of wireman in the Treasury Department without compliance with the requirements
of the civil-service rules.
This order is issued upon the recommendation of the Secretary of the Treasury,
who states that Mr. Flaherty has been employed as an unclassified laborer in the
electrician’s office for the last four years and has made himself particularly valuable
in that office, and that it is believed his promotion to a classified position will be in
the interests of the service. :
The Civil Service Commission does not concur in the recommendation.
