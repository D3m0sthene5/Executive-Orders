December 19, 1923
Plate printers in the Bureau of Engraving and Printing, appointed as such
under the Executive order of January 15, 1918, separated by reduction of force
as plate printers under the act of January 3, 1923, during the first 10 months
of 1923, may be transferred to other parts of the classified service subject to
Sppropriste examinations, and to the three-year and other statutory require-
ments.
This order is issued because of the abolition by law of permanent positions,
due to adoption of labor saving devices and improved methods. The Civil
Sorvies Commission concurs with the Bureau of Efficiency in recommending
e order.
