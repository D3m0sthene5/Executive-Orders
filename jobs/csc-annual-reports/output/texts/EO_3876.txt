June 20, 1923.
In consideration of the long, faithful, and meritorious service of Lawrence Fortune,
employed for more than 20 years in ‘the Bureau of Supplies and Accounts, Navy
Department, who died January 25, 1923, his widow, Mrs. Nellye C. Fortune, may
be appointed as clerk at $1,000 per annum in the Bureau of Supplies and Accounts,
Navy Department, without regard to the civil service rules or regulations. The
Civil Service Commission does not concur in the recommendation of the Acting
Secretary of the Navy for this order.
