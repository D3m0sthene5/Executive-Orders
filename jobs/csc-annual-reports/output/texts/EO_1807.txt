July 30, 1913.
Mr. Frank E. Anderson may be reinstated to an appropriate position in the Bureau
of Pensions, Department of the Interior, without reference to the period of his sepa-
ration from the service.
Mr. Anderson entered the service through open competitive examination in 1884
and was dismissed on April 30, 1897. The official who recommended his dismissal
subsequently recommended his reinstatement by Executive order, but it was not
accomplished. The Civil Service Commission reviewed his case and considered it
to be most deserving. President Roosevelt expressed the desire that the reinstatement
be made.
The Secretary of the Interior recommends the issuance of this order on account of
Mr. Anderson’s demonstrated efficiency and extended experience in the work of the
Pension Bureau, and in view of the foregoing facts the Civil Service Commission con-
curs, as the order merely waives the year limit under the reinstatement rule.
