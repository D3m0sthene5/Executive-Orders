August 10, 1921.
Mrs. Georgianna Rittman Butts may be appointed a clerk at $1,200 per annum
in the Interior Department without examination under the civil-service rules.
The Secretary of the Interior recommends her appointment in the Bureau of
Pensions in view of her qualifications as shown in her service with the French
High Commission.
The Civil Service Commission does not concur in this order.
