April 27, 1915.
Mr. Charles W. G. Brett may be transferred from the position of mechanician at
$1,200 per annum to a clerkship at the same salary, in the Bureau of the Census,
Department of Commerce, without reference to the civil-service rules.
Mr. Brett was appointed as mechanician on September 16, 1908. On March 19,
1912, he met with an accident, in the line of duty, which resulted in the loss of his
left eye. His transfer to the clerical rollisrecommended by the Director of the Census,
who states that Mr. Brett’s experience in connection with the various machines used
in the bureau, his familiarity with the matter to be tabulated, and his general educa-
tion are believed to be such as to fit him for clerical duties, and that his services have
always been satisfactory.
The Civil Service Commission does not concur in the recommendation.
