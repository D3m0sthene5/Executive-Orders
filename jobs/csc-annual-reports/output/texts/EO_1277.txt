December 23, 1910.
No officer or employee of the Government shall, directly or indirectly, instruct or
be concerned in any manner in the instruction of any person or classes of persons,
with a view to their special preparation for the examinations of the boards of examiners
for the diplomatic and consular services.
The fact that any officer or employee is found so engaged shall be considered suffi-
cient cause for his removal from the service.
