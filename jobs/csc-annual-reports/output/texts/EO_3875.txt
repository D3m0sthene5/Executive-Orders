June 20, 1923.
Mise Katie A. Guiton may be appointed to a clerical position in the classified service
without examination,
This order is issued on the recommendation of the Secretary of the Interior, who
states that Miss Guiton has served as translator for the Government in Cuba and has
been about 10 years in the Government service, occupying a temporary clerk position
in the Bureau of Pensions. The recommendation of the Secretary of the Interior
is favorably indorsed by the Commissioner of Pensions, under whom Miss Guiton is
serving.
The Civil Service Commission did not concur in recommending the issuance of
this order.
