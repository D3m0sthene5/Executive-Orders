October 26, 1927.
Civil-service Rule II, paragraph 6, is hereby amended to read as follows, by
the addition at the end thereof of the words underscored :
6. Except as provided in section 7 of this rule, a person holding a position
when it becomes classified and subject to competitive examination shall
have all the rights which he would acquire if appointed thereto upon exami-
nation under these rules, but he shall not be transferred without first passing
the examination provided by the commission; and no person shall acquire
any rights hereunder unless the Civil Service Commission is satisfied that
he was not appointed for the purpose of acquiring such rights.
