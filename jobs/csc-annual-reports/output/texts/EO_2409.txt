June 29, 1916.
Mrs. Fannie Taylor Colmesnil may be appointed to a position in the classified
service of the Government without regard to civil-service rules. This order is
issued on the recommendation of the Secretary of the Treasury, who states that
Mrs. Colmesnil rendered very efficient and satisfactory service while employed
by the Reserve Bank Organization Committee and the Federal Reserve Board,
and that, in view of her experience and ability, it is believed her appointment
to a classified position will be in the interest of the service. The Civil Service
Commission does not concur in the recommendation.
