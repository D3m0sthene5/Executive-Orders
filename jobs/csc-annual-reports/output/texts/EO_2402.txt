June 8, 1916.
Mrs. Ellen C. Watkins may be appointed as a clerk in the Government Print-
ing Office without reference to the civil-service rules. This order is issued
upon the recommendation of the Public Printer, who states that Mrs. Watkins
is the widow of the late Harry A. Watkins, who was an employee in the Gov-
ernment Printing Office for a period of 20 years, during which he rendered
faithful service. Mrs. Watkins has the qualifications of a clerk and book-
keeper. The Civil Service Commission does not concur in the recommendation
