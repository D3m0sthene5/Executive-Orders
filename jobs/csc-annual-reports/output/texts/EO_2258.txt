October 14, 1915.
Mr. William H. H. Bowen may be reinstated in the field service of tthe
Internal Revenue Service without regard to the year limit required by the civil-
service rules. This order is issued on the recommendation of the Secretary of
the Treasury with the concurrence of the Civil Service Commission. The Secre-
tary of the Treasury states that Mr. Bowen served very efficiently in the
Internal Revenue Service for 19 years and resigned from the position of in-
ternal revenue agent to accept the appointment of postmaster at Covington,
Ky., retiring from that position on September 1, 1914; that the records of the
department show that the work performed by Mr. Bowen as internal revenue
agent was of an unusually efficient character ; and the Internal Revenue Service
at this time is in need of such men as Mr. Bowen, men who are able to take
up successfully any and every phase of the internal revenue work. Mr. Bowen,
‘acting on information inadvertently given by a commissioner, failed to make
application for reinstatement in the Internal Revenue Service before the ex-
piration of the year limit, required by the rules.
