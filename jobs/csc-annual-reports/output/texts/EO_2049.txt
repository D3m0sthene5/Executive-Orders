September 17, 1914.
Miss Bessie H. Burwell, who was appointed to the position of classified laborer in
the office of the Secretary of Commerce, may be promoted to a suitable clerkship in
the classified service under the Department of Commerce without reference to the
civil-gervice rules.
In view of her efficiency and excellent record in the department, the Secretary
of Commerce recommends that an Executive order be issued authorizing her promo-
tion. The Civil Service Commission does not concur in the recommendation.
