April 18, 1917.
Mr. D. Brockman Winfrey may be appointed a clerk in the competitive classified
service of the Treasury Department without compliance with civil-service rules.
This order is issued on the recommendation of the Secretary of the Treasury, who
states that Mr. Winfrey has been serving in the excepted position of private secretary
to the Director of the Mint since November 11, 1915, and that on account of his thoroug
knowledge of the general work of the Bureau of the Mint and the efficient manner
in which he has performed his duties, it is believed that his permanent appointment
will be in the interest of the service.
