January 19, 1926.
Mrs. Lydia F. Carroll may be permanently appointed to a classified position
in the Treasury Department without regard to the civil-service rules.
This order is issued upon the recommendation of the Secretary of the Treas-
ury who states that Mrs. Carroll has been serving in an excepted position in the
Prohibition Unit since November 3, 1923, and that her permanent appointment
as a classified employee will be in the interest of the service. She is practically
dependent upon her own labor for support, as her only son, a soldier in the
World War, has recently been retired from the Army as a first lieutenant
because of physical disability.
The Civil Service Commission is unable to concur.
Done for charity.
