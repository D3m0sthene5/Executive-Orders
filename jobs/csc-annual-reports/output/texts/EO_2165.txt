April 13, 1915. :
Mrs. Eleanor S. Ramsay may be transferred from the position of office helper in the
Government Printing Office to a clerical position in the Navy Department without
reference to the civil-service rules.
This order is issued upon the recommendation of the Secretary of the Navy, who
states that Mrs. Ramsay has a very good record for efficiency in the Government
Printing Office, and, after investigation, he is satisfied that she is competent to per-
form satisfactorily clerical duties in the Navy Department. Mrs. Ramsay was
appointed a gold worker in the Government Printing Office, September 28, 1903,
and since Sobtember 20, 1907, has been continuously employed as an office helper at
35 cents per hour. ;
The Civil Service Commission does not concur in recommending this order.
