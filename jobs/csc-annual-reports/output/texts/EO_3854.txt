May 22, 1923. :
Mr. Daniel R. Edwards may be appointed a contact representative in the United
States Veterans’ Bureau without examination under the civil service rules. Mr.
Edwards is a permanently disabled beneficiary of the Veterans’ Bureau through the
lossofan arm and aleg. He hasbeen decorated with the distinguished service crossand
twice with the congressional medal of honor.
The director of the Veterans’ Bureau recommended Mr. Edwards’ appointment, as
he has been on trial and has given evidence that he can render good service. The
director regards the case as exceptional, in that the appointment would have a favor-
able psychological effect upon disabled ex-service men because of Mr. Edwards’ own
disability and his clear conception of vocational work, gained through his own training,
and because of the association he has had with welfare ex-service men’s organizations.
The Civil Service Commission concurred in this recommendation.
