August 23, 1917.
The following classified positions under the Navy Department may be filled upon
noncompetitive examination:
‘‘Aeronautical engineers, aeronautical mechanical engineers, inspectors of aero-
planes and aeroplane motors; and superintendents and foremen in Government-
owned aircraft factories.
“This order is issued upon the recommendation of the Secretary of the Navy and
the Chief of the Bureau of Construction and Repair, who states that on account of the
scarcity of men already experienced in these callings, and the demands of the present
emergency, it is deemed impracticable now to fill these positions by open competitive
examination.’
