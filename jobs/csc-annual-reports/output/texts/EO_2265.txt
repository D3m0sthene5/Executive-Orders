October 28, 1915.
Mrs. Frederick Gillman may be appointed as seamstress or flagmaker at the
navy yard, Mare Island, Cal.,, without reference to the civil-service rules or
navy yard regulations. Mrs. Gillman’s husband was a gunner on the submarine
F-}, which was lost in Honolulu Harbor, and she has a child 12 years of age
to support. This order is recommended by the Secretary of the Navy with the
concurrence of the Civil Service Commission.
