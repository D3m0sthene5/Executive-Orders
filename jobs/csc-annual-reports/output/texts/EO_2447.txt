August 31, 1916.
Mr. Henry Braxton may be promoted from the grade of messenger to a clerk-
ship in the Treasury Department without compliance with the requirements
of the Civil Service Commission relative to examinations. This order is issued
upon the recommendation of the Secretary of the Treasury, who states that
Mr. Braxton served as messenger in the office of the commissioner of railroads
from 1892 to 1899, when he was transferred to the Treasury Department, where
he has since served most efficiently in the offices of the Secretary of the Treas-
ury and the Comptroller of the Currency, rendering very valuable service, and
that it is believed his promotion will be in the interest of the service. The
Civil Service Commission does not concur in the recommendation.
