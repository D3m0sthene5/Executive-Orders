May 4, 1915.
Mr. William Leroy Fairbanks may be transferred from chief clerk of the American
Embassy at London to a clerkship in the Department of Commerce. He has served
since 1908, at first in the State Department, then in the American Legation at Peking,
and since 1910 in his present position.
The Secretary of Commerce, with the concurrence of the Civil Service Commission,
recommends the issuance of this order in view of the special qualifications of Mr.
Fairbanks for the work of the Bureau of Foreign and Domestic Commerce.
