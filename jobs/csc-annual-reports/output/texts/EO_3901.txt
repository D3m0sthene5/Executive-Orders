September 13, 1923.
The United States Coal Commission, created by the act of Congress approved
September 22, 1922 (42 Statutes at Large, p. 1023), terminates by operation of law at
the close of September 22, 1923.
It is therefore hereby ordered that the records, files, and property of the United
States Coal Commission be transferred to the Department of the Interior, the Secretary
of the Interior is hereby designated custodian of the said records, files, and property,
and under his direction and supervision the Director of the Geological Survey is
designated to certify to accounts and to perform any other administrative duties
incident to closing the work of the commission. .
