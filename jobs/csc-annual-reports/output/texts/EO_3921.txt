November 3, 1923
Mrs. Lyne Pepper Smith, widow of Col. Thomas L. Smith, may be appointed
to a clerical position in the War Department without reference to the civil-
service rules.
This order is issued for charitable purposes upon the recommendation of the
Secretary of War, who states that Colonel Smith’s death, which occurred October
31, 1922, was hastened by his devotion to duty and his arduous labor during the
World War. He was commissioned a second lieutenant in the Army: in June,
1893, and was examined for promotion to lieutenant colonel but was found
physically incapacitated for active service, because of disability incurred in line of
duty, and was retired December 5, 1915. He was one of the first retired officers to
request assignment to active duty at the outbreak of the World War. He served
until March 11, 1922, when he was again placed on the retired list as colonel.
