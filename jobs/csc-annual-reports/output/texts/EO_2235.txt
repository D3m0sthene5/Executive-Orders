August 16, 1915.
Miss Edna M. Ricketts, of Washington, D. C., may be appointed to a position in the
classified service of the Government without reference to the civil-service rules.
Miss Ricketts is the daughter of the late Thomas T. Ricketts, a veteran of Company
C, Thirty-ninth Ohio Volunteer Infantry, who was an employee in the Government
Printing Office from July, 1900, until the time of his death in April, 1913. She is an
experienced stenographer and typewriter, and is the sole support of her widowed
mother (now 74 years of age). :
The Civil Service Commission does not recommend the issuance of an order in this
case.
