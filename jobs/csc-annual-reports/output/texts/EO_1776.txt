May 7, 1913.
The list of classified positions excepted from examination under civil-service Rule
11, clause 3, is hereby amended so as to omit the position of chief post-office inspector
in the Post Office Department.
The effect of the above order was to restore the position of post-office inspector to
the competitive class. The position was transferred from the excepted to the com-
petitive class by the revision of the rules on May 6, 1896. It was placed in the
excepted class by the order of January 4, 1910.
