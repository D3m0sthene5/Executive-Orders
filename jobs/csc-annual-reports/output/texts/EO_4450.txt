June 1, 1926.
Mr. George H. McCrea may be permanently appointed as deputy collector
and inspector in the customs service of the Treasury Department without
regard to the examination requirements of the civil-service rules.
This order is recommended by the Secretary of the Treasury, who states
that Mr. McCrea has served from May 23, 1922, to the present time, with the
exception of a period of a little less than one year, under temporary appoint-
ment, that his record has been very satisfactory in the difficult and dangerous
duties to which he has been assigned, and that he appears to possess in a rare
degree the peculiar qualifications essential for their successful performance.
The Civil Service Commission submits the order without prejudice.
Done for merit demonstrated in the service.
