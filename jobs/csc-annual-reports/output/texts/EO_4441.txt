May 13, 1926.
Mr. William A. Hock may be reinstated as clerk of the fifth grade in the
post office at Newark, N. J., Without regard to the time limitation prescribed
by the civil-service rules. Mr. Hock served for 19 years in the Newark post
office and was removed June 30, 1918, upon charges which have subsequently
been found to have been unwarranted and unjustified.
This order is issued upon the recommendation of the Postmaster General to
remedy as far as possible the injustice involved in his removal.
In view of the facts presented with the recommendation and disclosed by its
own inquiry, the commission does not feel that any violence will be done the
civil-service rules by the reinstatement of Mr. Hock.
