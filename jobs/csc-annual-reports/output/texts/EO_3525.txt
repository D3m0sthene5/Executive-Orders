Schedule A, Subdivision VIII, of persons excepted from examination under
the civil-service rules, is hereby amended by the addition of a new paragraph
to be numbered 27, and to read as follows:
“27. Clerk to sign under the direction of the Secretary, in his name and for
him, his approval of all tribal deeds.”
The Secretary of the Interior believes that the position should be within the
excepted class because of the individual and confidential character of its duties.
The Civil Service Commission concurs in recommending this order.
