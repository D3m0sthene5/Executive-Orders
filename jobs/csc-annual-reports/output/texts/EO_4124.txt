January 12, 1925.
On the recommendation of the Civil Service Commission and the Postmaster
General the word "examined" is substituted for the word "appointed" in line 1
of section 7, Regulations Governing the Appointment of Postmasters of the Fourth
Class. As amended section 7 will read:
"7. No person shall be examined under the regulations of November 25, 1912,
as amended, as postmaster of an office of the fourth class where the annual
compensation is as much as $500 after he has reached his sixty-fifth birthday."
