January 5, 1917.
Mr. Frank Kearney may be promoted from the position of messenger to the $1,000
clerical grade in the classified service of the Treasury Department. This order is
issued on the recommendation of the Secretary of the Treasury, who states that Mr.
Kearney is a hard working, faithful and efficient employee, who has given more than
35 years’ service to the Government, and that on July 19, 1911, while in the per-
formance of his official duties and without negligence on his part, he received injuries
from which he will never recover; yet he performs all his duties most capably; that
there is a vacancy in the $1,000 clerical grade in the office of the Comptroller of the
Treasury for which Mr. Kearney is in every way qualified because of his long experi-
ence in that office, and it is believed that his promotion to this grade will be in the
interests of the service.
