October 22, 1913.
Mr. Edward W. Cronin may be reinstated in the Railway Mail Service without
regard to the length of his separation from the service.
r. Cronin was appointed to the Railway Mail Service on August 14, 1901, and
retained in the service until March 29, 1902, when he was dropped from the service.
His reinstatement is recommended by the Postmaster General, who states that his
separation from the service does not appear to have been warranted by the facts. The
(Civil Service Commission does not concur in recommending that an Executive order
be issued in this case. :
