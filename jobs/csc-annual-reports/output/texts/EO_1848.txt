October 29, 1913.
William A. Ryan may be appointed to the position of comptroller (in charge of all
finances and accounting) in the Reclamation Service.
This order is issued upon the recommendation of the Secretary of the Interior, with
the concurrence of the Civil Service Commission, and is based upon Mr. Ryan’s un-
usual qualifications for the position and the close personal relationship which he will
bear to the Secretary of the Interior. Mr. Ryan has been in the employ of the Inter-
state Commerce Commission during the past six years assigned to the duty of examining
the books and accounts of transportation companies, and conducted a notable investi-
gation which covered a period of three years. He is thoroughly familiar with large
financial affairs, it having been his business for several years to draft the plans for the
financing of great industrial corporations, and moreover he has an intimate personal
knowledge of irrigation experiments and projects. He has been known to the Secre-
tary of the Interior for 25 years, and knows his attitude so perfectly that he will be
able to state his policies and deal with the more than 20,000 water users without
embarrassment to the Secretary.
