September 3, 1919.
Mrs. Clara E. Graves may be appointed to a clerical position in the classified civil
service of the Department of Commerce without complying with the civil-service rules.
This order is issued upon the recommendations of the Secretary of Commerce and
the Superintendent of the Coast and Geodetic Survey, who state that Mrs. Graves is
the widow of Mr. Herbert C. Graves who died while attending an international hydro-
aphic conference in England as a representative of this country, and who was the
ont of an important division in the United States Coast and Geodetic Survey; that
Mr. Graves was a most conscientious and hard working man and that he applied him-
self diligently and untiringly to the Government’s interests; that as a result of his
death his widow and small children have been left without means of support; that
Mrs. Graves is fully competent to perform the duties of the position to which it is
proposed to appoint her. For these reasons it is believed that an exception to the
rules in her case is warranted in the interest of the service.
