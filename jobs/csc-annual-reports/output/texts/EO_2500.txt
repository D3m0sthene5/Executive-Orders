December 20, 1916.
Mr. William C. Rader may be appointed Assistant to the Director of the Bureau of
War Risk Insurance in the Treasury Department without compliance with civil-service
rules.
This order is issued on the recommendation of the Secretary of the Treasury, who
states that the position of Assistant to the Director of the Bureau of War Risk Insurance
is a technical one; that there is no person.in the Treasury Department qualified to fill
the position, and that Mr. Rader possesses the necessary qualifications, and that his
appointment will be in the interest of the service.
The Civil Service Commission does not concur in the recommendation.
