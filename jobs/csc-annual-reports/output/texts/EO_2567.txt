March 29, 1917.
Mr. Claude T. Worford may be appointed to the ositon of draftsman at $1,200 per
annum in the Office of Naval Intelligence, Navy Department, without regard to the
civil-service rules.

Efforts of the Navy Department to fill this vacancy by certification and by transfer
have failed. The work is represented as confidential and secret to the highest degree,
‘and the Secretary of the Navy states that it is imperative that the office should know
in advance the qualifications, antecedents, affiliations, and sympathies of the ap-
pointee. He states that Mr. Worford possesses the necessary qualifications, and has
to his credit four years of service in the Navy with an honorable discharge and a good
conduct medal.
The Civil Service Commission concurs with the Secretary of the Navy in recom-
mending this order.
