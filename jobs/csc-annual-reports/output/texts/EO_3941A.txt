January 15, 1924
Mr. Harvey Entriken, of Carrollton, Ga., may be appointed to a position of
private secretary in the office of the Board of United States General Appraisers
of New York without reference to the requirements of the civil-service rules.
This order is issued on the recommendation of the Secretary of the Treasury
and in view of the urgent need by one of the general appraisers for the services
of a private secretary with the qualifications possessed by Mr. Entriken.
The Civil Service Commission concurs in recommending the order.
