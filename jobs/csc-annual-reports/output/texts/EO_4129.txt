January 21, 1925.
Mrs. Eva H. Esterbrook may be appointed to a clerkship in the special agency
service of customs of the Treasury Department without compliance with therequirements of the civil-service rules.
This order is issued on the recommendation of the Secretary of the Treasury,
who states that Mrs. Esterbrook is the widow of Howard D. Esterbrook, who
served faithfully and efficiently in the Customs Service of the Treasury Depart-
ment for almost 16 years and who at the time of his death on December 9, 1924,
was agent in charge at the port of New York. The Civil Service Commission
does not concur in recommending this order.
Done for charity.
