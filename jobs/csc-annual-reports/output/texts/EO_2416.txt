July 8, 1916.
Schedule B, Subdivision IV, paragraph 1, of classified positions under the
War Department which may be filled upon noncompetitive examination is hereby
amended by the addition of the positions of inspector of aeroplanes and aero-
plane motors in the Signal Service at Large. As amended the paragraph will
read as follows:
“1. Aeronautical engineers, aeronautical mechanical engineers, instructors in
flying, aviation mechanicians, and inspectors of aeroplanes and aeroplane mo-
tors in the Aviation Section of the Signal Corps.” ]
The positions of inspector of aeroplanes and aeroplane motors belong to the
same general class as the other positions enumerated in the amended clause,
and it is deemed impracticable in the present stage of development of aviation
by the Government to subject them to open competitive examination.
