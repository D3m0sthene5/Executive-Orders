February 26, 1923.
Capt. William M. Hanson may be appointed to a position as immigrant inspector
An i classified service of the Department of Labor without regard to the civil-service
rules.
This order is recommended by the Secretary of Labor because of the need on the
Mexican border of the services of a man particularly familiar with local conditions
and methods in order to secure the administration of the immigration laws on the
border as applying to the surreptitious entry of aliens. Captain Hanson was for many
years United States marshal for the Southern District of Texas, in which capacity
he had charge of 500 miles of the border and as head of and in charge of the ranger
forces of the State of Texas he has become thoroughly familiar with the particular
problems presented.
Because of Captain Hanson’s experience, which peculiarly qualifies him for the
duties of the position of immigration inspector on the Mexican border, and because
the register of eligibles is depleted the Civil Service Commission joins with the Secre-
tary of Labor in recommending this order.
