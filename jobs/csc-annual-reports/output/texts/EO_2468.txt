October 10, 1916.
Schedule A of classified positions excepted from competitive examination under
Rule II, clause 3, is hereby amended by adding to Subdivision IX, Department of
Agriculture, a paragraph to be numbered 4, as follows:
“4. Special meteorological observers at stations in Alaska, in the West Indies, and
in portions of foreign territory bordering on the Gulf of Mexico and the Caribbean Sea.”
17147°—17 10

“The foregoing amendment is recommended by the Secretary of Agriculture and is
concurred in by the Civil Service Commission. The pay of the observers will be
small, rarely exceeding $50 a month, and the duties will require only a portion of
their time. In view of the small pay and the lack of facilities for competitive exami-
nation in these places, the positions can not be appropriately filled through compet-
itive examination.”’
