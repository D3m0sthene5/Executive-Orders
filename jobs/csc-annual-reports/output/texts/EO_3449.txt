May 3, 1921. ;
Mrs. Emily W. Cochrane, wife of John L. Cochrane, statistician and chief
of the Division of Publications of the Bureau of Mines, may be appointed in
the classified service without regard to the civil-service rules. This order is
based upon the long and efficient service of Mr. Cochrane, which has been con-
tinuous since May 1, 1907, and upon the fact that he is incapacitated, probably
permanently, from cerebral thrombosis, which is undoubtedly due in some
measure at least to his close application to his work and overtime service.
