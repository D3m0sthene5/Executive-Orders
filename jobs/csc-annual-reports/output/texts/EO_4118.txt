December 23, 1924.
Dr. Edward J. Drake may be reinstated in the grade of junior veterinarian,Department of Agriculture, without regard to the length of his separation.Doctor Drake resigned with the classified status of assistant inspector onDecember 28, 1906, and is now beyond the age limit of 45 for admission to exam-ination. While engaged recently under temporary appointment in suppressingfoot and mouth disease in California he originated an original and efficient methodof burying the carcasses of diseased animals by the use of dynamite, and he issaid to be exceptionally well-fitted for this class of work. The commissionconcurs with the department in recommending this order.
