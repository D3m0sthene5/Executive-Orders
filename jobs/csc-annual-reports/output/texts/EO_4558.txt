December 28, 1926.
Subsection (2) of section 3 of civil-service Rule X is amended by the elimina-
tion of the words, “* * * if he entered the classified service upon com-
petitive examination and the legislative service by transfer therefrom.”
As amended the subsection will read:
(2) In the legislative service.
Norp.—The amendment eliminates a restriction upon transfer of a small class of
employees who properly entered the classified service without examination, an arbitrary
distinction which appeared to, be unjust.
