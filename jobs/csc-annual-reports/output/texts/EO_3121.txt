July 19, 1919.
Mr. Samuel D. Slentz, of Bellingham, Wash., may be retained as an employee of
the United States Employees’ Compensation Commission without reference to the
civil-service rules.
This order is issued upon the recommendation of the commission, it being stated
that Mr. Slentz has been an employee of the commission since August, 1918, and has
learned its policies and practices and has proved to the satisfaction of the commission
that he has the requisite qualifications for the position.
