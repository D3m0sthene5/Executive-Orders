June 21, 1915.
Mrs. Lillian Peyton King may be appointed as clerk in the classified service of the
Treasury Department without compliance with civil-service rules.
Mrs. King is the widow of the late Gen. John Floyd King, who was for many years a
rominent Member of Congress, and recently Assistant Register of the Treasury.
he issuance of this order is recommended by the Secretary of the Treasury in recogni-
tion of Gen. King’s distinguished services in the National House of Representatives
and other public positions, and in view of the fact that his widow is entirely compe-
tent to perform the duties of a clerk, and is left without means of support.
