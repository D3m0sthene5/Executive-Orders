July 28, 1916.
Mrs. Olga S. Moncure may be reinstated in the position of topographic
draftsman in the Coast and Geodetic Survey, Department of Commerce, with-
out regard to the requirements of the civil-service rules. This order is issued
upon recommendation of the Secretary of Commerce and the superintendent
of the survey, who state that Mrs. Moncure served satisfactorily and very
efficiently in a statutory position of topographic draftsman in the Geological
Survey during a period of seven years; that upon applying for a short leave
of absence she was transferred to a position on a per diem, when actually em-
ployed, basis with the positive assurance of her superior officers that such
transfer would not deprive her of her status in the classified civil service;
that she did apply for restoration to duty prior to the expiration of the time
during which she was advised that she would be eligible for such return to duty
and then learned that she had been misinformed, and as a result of the action
of her superiors she lost her civil-service status; that she later obtained a
position of draftsman in the bureau of lands in the Philippines and while
serving in that capacity was detailed for work under the director of coast
surveys at Manila, who has certified to her faithfulness, efficiency, and atten-
tion to duty; and that in view of these circumstances it is believed that her
reinstatement in the Coast and Geodetic Survey is fully justified and desirable
in the interests of good administration and will remedy a manifest injustice
which has been done in her case.
