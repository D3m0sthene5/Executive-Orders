April 11, 1916.
Mrs. Mary A. Perry may be reinstated as a printer’s assistant in the Bureau
of Engraving and Printing without regard to the length of her separation from
the service. Mrs. Perry, after most satisfactory service, resigned from the
Bureau of Engraving and Printing on August 23, 1910, to marry. John J.
Perry, her husband, a plate printer employed in the bureau, died on September
27, 1915, leaving her with two small children to support. It is also necessary
for her to contribute to the support of a widowed mother and two minor
sisters. It is stated that she is 26 years of age, highly skilled in the work of
many divisions of the bureau, and of a type especially desirable where the
work is of an exacting mental and physical nature. The Civil Service Com-
mission concurs with the Acting Secretary of the Treasury in recommending
this order.
