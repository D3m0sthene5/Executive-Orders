August 12, 1919.
Mr. Ira F. Hoyt may be appointed to the position of passport agent, New York
City, under the Department of State without reference to the civil-service law.
This order is issued because of the difficulty of securing a competent and experi-
enced man for the position through the usual civil-service channels. Mr. Hoyt has
served for more than a year in the Division of Passport Control, Department of State,
and in the Passport Agency in New York City, and has been acting as passport agent
for the past several months. He has thus acquired the desired experience and hi
appointment would appear to be in the interest of the public service.
