March 10, 1916.
Mr. John V. Shea may be transferred from the position of bookbinder to the
clerical grade in the classified service of the Treasury Department without
examination. This order is issued on the recommendation of the Secretary of
the Treasury, who states that Mr. Shea is an exceptionally loyal and efficient
employee and has performed services which have been of great convenience
and economy to the department. The Civil Service Commission does not concur
in the recommendation.
