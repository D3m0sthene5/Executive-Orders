July 14, 1916.
Miss Ruth L. O'Donnell may be appointed to a position in the Bureau of Effi-
ciency without reference to the civil-service rules. This order is issued upon the
recommendation of the Chief of the Bureau of Efficiency, who states that Miss
O’Donnell has been employed in his office at various times during the past two
years; that her services have been most satisfactory; and that her permanent
appointment to the force of the Bureau of Efficiency would be a distinct advan-
tage to the bureau. The Civil Service Commission does not concur in the recom-
mendation.
