July 18, 1916.
Mr. Jacob P. Smith may be appointed a storekeeper-gauger in the Internal-
Revenue Service without compliance with civil-service rules. The collector of
internal revenue for the seventh district of Indiana states that Mr. Smith served
temporarily as a storekeeper-gauger in that district from September, 1915, until
June 26, 1916; that he is qualified to competently fill any position in the store-
keeper-gauger service; and that his permanent appointment will be in the inter-
est of efficient administration. The collector further states that the stress of
work at this time in his district necessitates the employment of an experienced
man. This order is issued on the recommendation of the Secretary of the Treas-
ury. Mr. Smith is on the civil-service register.
