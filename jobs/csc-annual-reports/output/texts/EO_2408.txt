June 28, 1916.
Miss Rachael C. McKeown, of Maryland, may be appointed to a position as
clerk at $900 per annum in the Bureau of Animal Industry in the Department
of Agriculture without reference to the civil-service rules. This order is issued
upon the recommendation of the Secretary of Agriculture, who states that Miss
McKeown is the daughter of Dr. Thomas H. McKeown, formerly a veterinary
inspector in the Bureau of Animal Industry, who died on April 12, 1916, as the
result of an automobile accident which occurred while he was in the discharge
of his official duties. Throughout his connection with the department his
services were satisfactory.
