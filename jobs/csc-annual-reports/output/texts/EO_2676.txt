July 31, 1917.
Mr. Harry C. Daniels, now employed as a clerk in the Solicitor’s Office, Depart-
ment of Agriculture, may be transferred and appointed to a clerkship in the Navy
Department at $1,500 per annum, without reference to the civil-service rules.
This order is issued upon the recommendation of the Secretary of the Navy and the
Chief of Naval Operations, who state that it is proposed to assign Mr. Daniels to the
position of Secretary to the Chief of Naval Operations, the duties of which are of a
confidential character and should be performed by a trained man of unquestioned
