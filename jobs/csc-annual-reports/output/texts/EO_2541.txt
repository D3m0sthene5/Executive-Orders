Schedule A, Subdivision V, paragraph 2, of the civil-service rules, which at present
excepts from examination civilian professors, instructors, and teachers in the Naval
Academy, is hereby amended to read as follows:
¢“2. In the United States Naval Academy:
‘‘(a) Professors, instructors, and teachers.
“(b) Baker helpers, coffeemen, firemen, linenman, pantrymen, dish pantrymen,
scullions, utility men, waiters, and, when promoted from the position of waiter,
head and assistant head waiter.”
The Navy Department recommended that these positions be excepted from exami-
nation for the reasons that the salaries are low; that the positions have been filled
from waiters, formerly paid from the mess fund, who have shown themselves trust-
worthy; that practically no education is required, and that the requisite knowledge
can only be acquired by actual experience in the commissary. The commission
recommended the order.
