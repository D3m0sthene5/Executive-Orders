February 11, 1916.
Mr. Monroe Ryles may be appointed as a messenger at $720 per annum in
the Federal Trade Commission without reference to the civil-service rules.
This order is issued upon the recommendation of the chairman of the Federal
Trade Commission, who states that Mr. Ryles is peculiarly qualified for the
duties to which it is proposed to assign him. The Civil Service Commission
does not concur in the recommendation.
