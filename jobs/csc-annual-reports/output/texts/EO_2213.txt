June 18, 1915.
Mrs. Mabel P. Walker may be appointed a clerk in the classified service of the
Treasury Department without compliance with civil service rules.
This order is issued on the recommendation of the Secretary of the Treasury in
view of the expert services rendered by Mrs. Walker under temporary appointment
in that department in operating the comptometer machine, whereby much time,
labor, and expense has been saved, and in view of the fact that there is no eligible
register of the Civil Service Commission for employees of this character.
