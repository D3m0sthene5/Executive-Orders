March 10, 1926.
Mrs. Maud S. Pherson may be reinstated in the classified service as a
stenographer or clerk without regard to the length of time she has been
separated therefrom.
This order is issued upon the recommendation of the Secretary of the.
Treasury, who states that Mrs. Pherson's husband, William R. Pherson, was
employed in the Interior Department from Dcember 15, 1911, to November 25,
1918, when he was transferred to the Treasury Department. His death, on
March 17, 1925, left Mrs. Pherson with three small children to support.
Mrs. Pherson was employed in the Interior Department for more than eight
years, resigning on February 13, 1919. In view of her former service and her
qualifications as ascertained by civil service examinations the Civil Service
Commission concurs in the recommendation of the Secretary of the Treasury.
For charity.
