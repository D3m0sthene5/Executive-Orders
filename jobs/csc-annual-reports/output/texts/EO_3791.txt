February 20, 1923.
Mrs. Mildred L. Jennings may be appointed to a clerical position in the General
Accounting Office without regard to the civil-service rules.
This order is recommended by the Comptroller General, who has known Mrs,
Jennings for many years, has supervised her work, and whose services, because of
her dependability and qualifications, can be used to advantage on a particular work
about which he feels some concern.
The Civil Service Commission does not concur in recommending this order.
