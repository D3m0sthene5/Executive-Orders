June 22, 1915.
Paragraph 16 of Subdivision VIII, Department of the Interior, of Schedule A
of the civil-service rules, is hereby amended by the addition of the words—
“and among the natives of Alaska.’’
As amended the paragraph will read:
“16. Special officers to assist in the suppression of the liquor traffic in the Indian
Service and among the natives of Alaska.”
This order is recommended by the Secretary of the Interior with the concurrence
of the Civil Service Commission.
