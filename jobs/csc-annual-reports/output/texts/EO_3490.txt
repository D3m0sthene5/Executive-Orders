June 13, 1921.
The year limit upon eligibility for reinstatement is hereby waived to permit
the reinstatement of Mr. Edward M. Morgan to the competitive classified service
in the post office at New York, N. Y.
Mr. Morgan was appointed a letter carrier in the New York, N. Y., post
office in 1873; promoted up through the various grades, including assistant
postmaster, and finally on December 10, 1907, appointed postmaster and served
until a short time prior to the appointment of the present postmaster on March
16, 1917.
The Postmaster General recommends this order in view of Mr. Morgan’s pre-
vious service in classified positions and the exceptional qualifications he evinced
in the performance of his duties through his entire service.
The Civil Service Commission reports that upon the facts as they appear by
the entire record in this case, the Postal Service will be benefited by the waiver
of the year limitation and his reinstatement.
