May 31, 1921.
Mr. Leroy C. A. Kaschub may be reinstated as a city letter carrier in the
classified service without regard to the length of his separation from the service.
Mr. Kaschub was appointed a substitute carrier February 14, 1908, at Meri-
den, Conn. He was promoted to regular carrier at $600 June 30, 1912, and was
promoted four times, receiving $1,100 July 1, 1916. Mr. Kaschub. was removed
from the service on June 4, 1917, for improper and abusive language in a public
place while in uniform deliv ering mail.
This order is issued for the reason that Mr. Kaschub’s dismissal does not
appear to have been sustained by the facts, as it is not believed that the evidence
in the case was sufficiently conclusive to warrant his removal. His reinstate-
ment is recommended by the Postmaster General.
The Civil Service Commission did not concur in recommending this order.
