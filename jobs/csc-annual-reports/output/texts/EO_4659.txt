June 6, 1927.
Schedule B of positions which may be filled upon noncompetitive examinations
is hereby amended so that paragraph 2 of Subdivision III, Department of
Commerce, will read as follows:
2. Commercial attachés and assistant commercial attachés, trade com-
missioners and assistant trade commissioners, district managers and
assistant district managers, experts, and commercial agents and special
agents whose initial salary is $2,400 or more, to investigate trade condi-
tions abroad and in the United States, including the insular possessions,
with the object of promoting the foreign commerce of the United States.
The above amendment based on the act of March 3, 1927, establishing the
Foreign Commerce Service of the United States, specifically sets forth the
present practice, and transfers from the noncompetitive to the competitive
class the positions of special agent and commercial agent, allocated to grades
5 and 6 (less than $2,400) of the clerical, administrative, and fiscal service.
