June 8, 1916.
Miss Nellie L. Yeatman may be appointed to a position as clerk in the
Bureau of Efficiency without reference to the civil-service rules. This order
is issued upon the recommendation of the Chief of the Bureau of Efficiency,
who states that Miss Yeatman has served temporarily in his office at various
times during the past three years and that her services have always been highly
satisfactory. Miss Yeatman is the daughter of the late Robert H. Yeatman,
who was a faithful employee in the Coast Survey and in the War and Navy
Departments for 51 years. The Civil Service Commission does not concur in
the recommendation.
