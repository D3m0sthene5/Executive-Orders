August 2, 1911.
The provisions of the Executive order of January 17, 1873, prohibiting Federal
employees from holding office under any State, Territorial, or municipal government
are hereby waived to permit Sylvester P. Bartlett, a field superintendent in the
Bureau of Fisheries, to hold the position of secretary to the Illinois State Fish Com-
mission, in view of the mutual advantages thereby secured to the State commission
and the Bureau of Fisheries. This order is recommended by the Civil Service Com-
mission.
