August 10, 1916.
Mrs. Myrtle M. Davis, of Arkansas, may be appointed in the postal service
without reference to the requirement of examination under the civil-service
rules. This order is issued at the suggestion of the Postmaster General, the
husband of Mrs. Davis having been assassinated while in the actual perform-
ance of his duties as a rural carrier on route No. 3 from England, Ark.
