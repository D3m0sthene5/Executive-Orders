February 7, 1916.
Miss Margaret S. Neate may be appointed a printer’s assistant in the
Bureau of Engraving and Printing without certification by the Civil Service
Commission. Miss Neate passed the examination for printer’s assistant in
January, 1915, but has not been reached for certification. She is the daughter of
Dr. John S. Neate, who served for 22 years in the United States Army and
rendered most valuable service in fighting yellow fever, submitting himself for
experiment, and died in 1912 from a disease believed to have been brought on
by this service. This order is issued upan the recommendation of the Secre-
tary of the Treasury. The Civil Service Commission does not concur in the
recommendation.
