February 23, 1916.
Mr. Edward Rappholt may be reinstated to the position of letter carrier in
the post office at New York City without regard to the length of his separation
from the service. Mr. Rappholt was appointed as substitute letter carrier at
St. Louis, Mo., January 2, 1902; transferred to New York City post office
August 10, 1908; to the Atlanta, Ga., post office October 16, 1912; and resigned
December 8, 1912. He made application for reinstatement in December, 1913.
His application for reinstatement was referred to the postmaster at New York
City March 11, 1914, and disapproved. This order is issued upon the recom-
mendation of the Acting Postmaster General in view of the fact that Mr. Rapp-
holt’s record as a letter carrier was good, and that the failure to approve his
request for reinstatement in the New York City post office at a time when it
could have been made in accordance with the civil-service rules was due to a
local rule rather than to a departmental rule or policy. The Civil Service Com-
mission does not concur in the recommendation.
