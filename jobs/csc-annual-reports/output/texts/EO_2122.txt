January 20, 1915.
Mr. Newton C. Llewellyn may be reinstated as a carrier in the Knoxville, Tenn.,
post office without regard to the length of his separation from the service.
Mr. Llewellyn entered the Postal Service as a substitute carrier at Knoxville on
i 18, 1895, and remained in the service until February 28, 1907, when he
resigned.
The Postmaster General states that Mr. Llewellyn’s record is good, that it is a
meritorious case, and that the department has no objection to the issuance of an order.
The Civil Service Commission does not recommend this order. .
