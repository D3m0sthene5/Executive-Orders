July 30, 1925.
Mrs Aida G. Thayer, widow of Commander Henry A. Thayer, chief of the
supply division of the Veterans' Bureau, who was killed June 16, 1925, may be
appointed to a suitable position in that bureau without regard to the civil service
rules.
The Director of the Veterans' Bureau states that Mrs. Thayer is efficient and
reliable and he recommends her appointment. The Civil Service Commission
is without authority to concur.
Done for charity.
