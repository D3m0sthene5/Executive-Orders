May 6, 1914.
Mr. Raymond Augusterfer may be promoted from the position of mechanician
(electrician) on the statutory roll of the office of the Secretary of Agriculture, at a
salary of $1,200 per annum, to the position of chief engineer on the same roll, at a
salary of $2,000 per annum, without reference to civil-service Rule XI, clause (b)
of section 2.
The Secretary of Agriculture recommends the promotion of Mr. Augusterfer, who
has been in the department since April, 1904, for the reason that he has assisted in
installing a large portion of the present equipment, and possesses an intimate knowl-
edge of the power plant and of the intricate system of connections between the various
buildings served by it. He regards the thorough practical qualifications of Mr.
Augusterfer to be of much value to the department and believes it to be in the interest
of efficient and economical service to promote Mr. Augusterfer to the position in
uestion.
3 The Civil Service Commission does not concur in the recommendation.
