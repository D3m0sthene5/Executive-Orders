June 1, 1915.
Mrs. F. Pierard may be appointed as seamstress or flagmaker at the navy yard,
Mare Island, Cal., without reference to the civil service rules or navy yard regulations.
Mis. Pierard’s husband was chief gunner’s mate on the submarine F—4, which was
lost in Honolulu Harbor, leaving her to support two small children and an invalid
sister whose husband was lost on the I'—4.
This order is recommended by the Secretary of the Navy with the concurrence of
the Civil Service Commission.
