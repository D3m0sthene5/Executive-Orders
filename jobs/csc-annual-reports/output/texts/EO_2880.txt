June 12, 1918.
Mrs. Laura E. Langley and Miss Elizabeth M. Bowling may be appointed to
the positions of telephone operator in the Department of State without reference
to civil-service rules and requirements.
Mrs, Langley and Miss Bowling have been employed temporarily upon the
State Department switchboard for the periods of nine and five minths, respec-
tively.
They have thus acquired technical proficiency and necessary knowledge of
the department and its personnel, and to replace them at this time with inex-
perienced operators would seriously affect the efficiency of the department
telephone service.
