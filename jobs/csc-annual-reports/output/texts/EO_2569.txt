March 31, 1917.
Mr. Lorraine J. Markwardt may be reinstated in the Department of Agriculture as
an engineer in forest products at $2,000 per annum without reference to the length
of his separation from the service.
This order is issued upon the representation of the department that he is unques-
tionably the best qualified man obtainable because of his experience in the department
and the character of the work upon which he has been engaged since his resignation
from the service on September 30, 1914, which will make his services of still greater
value to the Government.
The Civil Service Commission concurs in recommending the order.
