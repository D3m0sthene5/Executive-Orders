Soolion 1 of regulation VII of the regulations governing the appointment of unclassi-
fied laborers to Federal offices outside of Washington is hereby amended by inserting
the words ‘“‘and branches of the service” after the word ‘““cities.”” Asamended section
1 of regulation VII will read as follows:
“Sec. 1. If a position of laborer requires, in connection with the usual duties of mere
laborer, the performance of work of the grade done by classified employees, it should be
filled from a register for the classified service and not under these regulations. No
person who is to perform manual labor merely, in cities and branches of the service
where the labor regulations are in force, shall be appointed otherwise than in accord-
ance with these regulations.”
Regulation VIII of the regulations governing the appointment of unclassified
laborers to Federal offices outside of W. ashington is hereby amended by inserting the
words “any or all” before the word “Federal.” As amended the regulation will read
ag follows:
“The commission is authorized to extend the application of these regulations to any
or all Federal offices in other cities than those in which they are now in force.”
These amendments are proposed by the commission on the representation of the
Treasury Department that it has experienced considerable embarrassment owing to the
manner of selecting unclassified laborers and charwomen in the custodian service, and
that it wishes to bring the service to a higher degree of efficiency. It is not deemed
practicable, however, “at this time to extend the labor regulations to all branches of the
service in the smaller cities and towns of the United States.
