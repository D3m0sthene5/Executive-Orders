April 22, 1922.
John M. Masten may be appointed to a position as investigator in the Gen-
eral Accounting Office without compliance with civil-service rules. Mr. Masten
has had wide and varied experience in investigating work in the Post Office
Department, and it is believed that in view of his qualifications and experience
in this line of work it would be in the interest of the service to make this
appointment.
