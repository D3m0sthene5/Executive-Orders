February 5, 1924
Edward K. Kraemer may be reinstated to his former position of rural carrier
at Elmer, N. J., without regard to the time limitation prescribed by the civil-
service rules.
. He was appointed on September 1, 1904, and was discontinued with his route
in connection with the revision of service in Salem County on April 30, 1916.
A vacancy has existed since May 1, 1922, and Mr. Kraemer has been continu-
ously employed as temporary carrier since February 6, 1922, during the absence
of the regular carrier on account of illness and subsequent to the discontinuance
of the carrier. His services have been exceedingly satisfactory and his rein-
statement is desired in the interest of good service and because his restoration to
duty is an act of common justice, he having been arbitrarily separated from the
service,
The Civil Service Commission concurred in the view of the Postmaster Gen-
~ eral that Mr. Kraemer should be reinstated.
