August 29, 1924
Mrs. Helen C. Hunt may be permanently appointed to a clerical position in
the classified service of the Treasury Department without compliance with the
requirements of the Civil Service Commission.
This order is issued on the recommendation of the Acting Secretary of the
Treasury, who states that Mrs. Hunt has been employed on what is known as a
contract basis in the distribution unit of the Treasury Department for the last
six years, has rendered faithful, efficient, and intelligent service, and on account of
her experience it is believed her permanent appointment will be in the interests
of the service.
The Civil Service Commission does not concur in recommending this order.
Done for charity.
