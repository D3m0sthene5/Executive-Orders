June 24, 1918.
Schedule A, Subdivision V, paragraph 3 of positions excepted from the re-
quirement of examination under the civil-service rules is hereby amended to
exeept from examination all positions under the Navy Department in the Virgin
Islands. As amended, paragraph 3 will read as follows:
“3. All positions in the Island of Guam, in the Island of Samoa, and in the
Virgin Islands.”
