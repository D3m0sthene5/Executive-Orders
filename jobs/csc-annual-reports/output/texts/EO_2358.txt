April 12, 1916.
Mrs. Jennie Snodgrass Adams may be appointed to a position in the classified
service without reference to the civil-service rules. This order is issued upon
the recommendation of the Public Printer, who states that Mrs. Adams held a
classified position in the Government Printing Office for five years prior to her
. marriage and that she is still active and vigorous and capable of rendering
efficient service, and that he feels that her reentry into the classified service
would be in the public interest. Mrs. Adams is the widow of William R.
Adams, who was employed in the Government Printing Office for a period of
25 years, during all of which time he rendered faithful service. The Civil
Service Commission does not concur in the recommendation of the Public
Printer.
