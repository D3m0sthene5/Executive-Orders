December 24, 1920.
. Schedule A of positions excepted from competitive examination is hereby
amended by addition to Subdivision III, paragraph 7, of a clause to read as
follows;
“ Employees engaged in rural sanitation work financed jointly by the Treasury
Department and a State, county, or municipality, and for which at least one-
half of the expense is furnished by a State, county, or municipality.
© This order is to supersede Executive order No. 3342, issued under date of
October 20, 1920.”
