November 3, 1925.
Mrs. Stella Davis Mudd may be reinstated as an operative in the Bureau of
Engraving and Printing without regard to the length of her separation from
the service. Mrs. Mudd resigned as printer's assistant from the bureau on
February 9, 1909. Her reinstatement by Executive order is recommended by
the Secretary of the Treasury in view of the fact that Mrs. Mudd is a widow
with four minor children to support. The Civil Service Commission is unable
to concur in the recommendation.
Done for charity.
