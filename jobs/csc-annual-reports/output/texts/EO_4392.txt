March 12, 1926.
Hampton Willis may be transferred from the office of the Secretary of the
Navy to the Bureau of Construction and Repair, to the position of messenger,
without regard to civil-service rules, effective January 16, 1926.
This order is issued upon the recommendation of the Secretary of the Navy,
who states that in order to cooperate in the policy for the reduction of civilian
personnel, Mr. Willis, one of the two messengers attached to the personal office
of the Secretary, was transferred to an existing vacancy in the classified posi-
tion of messenger in the Bureau of Construction and Repair.
The Civil Service Commission submits the case without prejudice.
