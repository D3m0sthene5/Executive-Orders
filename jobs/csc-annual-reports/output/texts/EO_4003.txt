May 2, 1924
John J. Hopkins, who was appointed a parole officer at $1,500 per annum
without regard to the civil-service rules, at the McNeil Island Penitentiary,
Washington, on March 12, 1919, resigned July 20, 1922, and reappointed Sep-
tember 15, 1922, is given a classified status, it appearing that when his appoint-
ment was reported the fact that the position of parole officer was in the classified
service was overlooked. He has served approximately five years and he is
reported as being peculiarly fitted to perform the duties of the position.
The Civil Service Commission approved the issuance of this order.
