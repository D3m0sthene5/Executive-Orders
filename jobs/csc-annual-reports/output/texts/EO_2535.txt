February 21, 1917.
Mr. John E. Amis may be appointed to a clerical position in any executive depart-
ment or independent office, without reference to the civil-service rules.
Mr. Amis has been employed since September 3, 1915, as office deputy marshal in
the office of the United States marshal at Nashville, Tenn., having been appointed
without examination as the position is excepted from examination by authority of
law.
The Civil Service Commission does not recommend this order.
