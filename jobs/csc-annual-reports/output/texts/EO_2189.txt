May 4, 1915.
Schedule A, Subdivision IV, paragraph 6 of the civil-service rules excepting certain
positions from the requirement of examination is hereby amended to read as folloys:
“6. All positions on torpedo and mine planters and cable boats, both navigating
and operative, of the Quartermaster Corps of the Army.”
This amendment is recommended by the War Department and approved by the
Civil Service Commission, on representations of the department that the same reasons
exist for excepting positions on cable boats as on torpedo and mine planters. The
qualifications for employment on these vessels differ from those required in correspond-
ing positions on other vessels, and the frequent sailing and the fact that the vessels
do not sail between fixed points render it difficult to make appointments under the
civil-service rules.
