December 19, 1923
Mr. Frank E. McMillin, who served as railway mail clerk, post-office inspector,
assistant postmaster at Boston, Mass., and chief post-office inspector from
September 5, 1890, to July 31, 1909, when he resigned from the service, may be
reinstated to the position of post-office inspector.
This order was issued upon the recommendation of the Postmaster General
and was concurred in by the Civil Service Commission.
