March 24, 1925.
Paragraph 6, Subdivision XI, Schedule A, of positions excepted from exami-
nation under the civil service rules, is hereby amended to permit the appointment
of three additional assistants to the Secretary in the Office of the Secretary of
Commerce. The paragraph as amended will read as follows:
"6. Four assistants to the Secretary in the Office of the Secretary of Commerce."
This order is issued on the recommendation of the Secretary of Commerce
who states that the positions involve duties which are peculiarly personal and
confidential, and are similar to the position of Assistant to the Secretary of the
Interior which was placed under paragraph 25, Subdivision VIII, of Schedule A
by the order of April 21, 1911, and are similar generally to the position of private
secretary or confidential clerk provided in paragraphs 1 and 2 of Subdivision I of
Schedule A. The Civil Service Commission is unable to concur in the recom-
mendation for the exception of these positions from examination.
