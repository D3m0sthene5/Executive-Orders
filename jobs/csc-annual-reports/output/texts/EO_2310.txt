February 10, 1916.
Mr. Joseph W. Harris may be reinstated as fourth assistant examiner in
the Patent Office, the year limit of eligibility for reinstatement being waivea
in his case. Mr. Harris is said by the Secretary of the Interior to have proved
particularly efficient in cases involving metallurgy and electro chemistry during
his service of nearly six years in the Patent Office, and to have been contin-
uously engaged in prosecuting applications for patents from his resignation on
April 3, 1912, to the present time, thus having had an experience of nearly 10
years in patent work. In view of the technical character of the position and
the experience of Mr. Harris, and his evident qualifications for the position,
the commission joins with the Secretary of the Interior in recommending an
Executive order to permit his reinstatement.
