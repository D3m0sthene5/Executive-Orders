February 10, 1921.
Mr. Arthur Byrd may be appointed a clerk in the Navy Department without
compliance with the civil-service rules.
This order is issued in view of the long and efficient service Mr. Byrd has
rendered in the Navy Department. His duties and responsibilities compare
favorably with those of many clerks. The position which Mr. Byrd fills has
grown to one of such importance that if his service were terminated for any
cause it would be necessary to replace him with a clerk.
