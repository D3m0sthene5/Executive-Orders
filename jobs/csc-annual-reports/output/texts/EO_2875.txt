For the period of the war, positions of elevator conductor may be filled by the
appointment of women, and when so filled need not be apportioned among the
States and Territories and the District of Columbia on the basis of population,
as is provided in the case of certain minor positions mentioned in Rule VII, sec-
tion 2, of the civil-service rules. Persons so appointed shall not be eligible for
transfer or promotion to apportioned positions nor for assignment to other work.
