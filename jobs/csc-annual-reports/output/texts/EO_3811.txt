April 9, 1923.
Miss Nancy Taylor may be appointed clerk in the classified service of the War
Department without examination.
This order is issued on the recommendation of the Secretary of War, who states that
Miss Taylor has been employed 15 months in an excepted position in the War Depart-
ment and during that petiod has acquired an experience and developed a capacity in
certain special work which will make it to the interest of the department to retain her
in the classified service.
The Civil Service Commission is unable to concur in recommending this order.
