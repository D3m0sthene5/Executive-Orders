October 24, 1913.
Mr. Solon J. Boughton may be reinstated as assistant examiner in the Patent Office,
Department of the Interior, without reference to the period of his separation from the
service.
Mr. Boughton served as an assistant patent examiner from December 1, 1907, to
August 9, 1911. He resigned for the purpose of taking up his residence in Colorado
when it was found that he had contracted tuberculosis. A cure was effected within a
short time and during his period of eligibility he communicated with the Patent Office
relative to reinstatement but was ad vised to remain in Colorado and was promised that
a recommendation would be made for his reinstatement when he desired to resume his
duties. The Secretary of the Interior recommends the reinstatement on account of
Mr. Boughton’s experience and the fact that he served with great ability. The Civil
Service Commission concurs in recommending this Executive order.
