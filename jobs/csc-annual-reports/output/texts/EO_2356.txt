April 11, 1916.
The year limit upon eligibility for reinstatement is extended to permit the
reinstatement of William L. Buchanan to an appropriate position in the
Immigration Service. Mr. Buchanan was an inspector in the Immigration
Service from July 1, 1901, to November 17, 1913. The Secretary of Labor, the
Commissioner General of Immigration, and the commissioner of immigration
at Montreal recommend his reinstatement because of his high efficiency while
in the service. Satisfactory evidence of Mr. Buchanan’s exemplary conduct for
the past two years having been furnished, the Civil Service Commission con-
curs in the recommendation. ;
