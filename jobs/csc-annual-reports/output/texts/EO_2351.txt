March 30, 1916.
Mr. Herbert D. Dean may be reinstated as a fish culturist in the Bureau of
Fisheries, Department of Commerce, without regard to the length of time he has
been separated from the service. Mr. Dean entered the service of the Bureau
of Fisheries in a subordinate capacity in 1884 and in 1891, after four promo-
tions, became a superintendent of station, which position, with the exception of
five months in 1895, he held until March 11, 1912, when, at his own request, his
services were discontinued without prejudice. Mr. Dean‘s record was satis-
factory, and he was generally recognized as an efficient fish culturist and super-
intendent. This order is issued upon recommendation of the Secretary of Com-
merce, who is of the opinion that Mr. Dean’s reentrance into the fisheries service
will be beneficial thereto, and who therefore believes that an exception to the
one-year limitation in cases of separation from the service imposed by civil-
service Rule IX is justified and advisable. The Civil Service Commission does
not concur in the recommendation.
