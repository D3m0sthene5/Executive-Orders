February 17, 1917.
Mrs. Mary R. Livingston Stephenson may be appointed to a position as skilled
laborer in the Government Printing Office without reference to the civil-service rules.
This order is issued upon the recommendation of the Public Printer, who states
that Mrs. Stephenson is the widow of John K. Stephenson, a former employee of
the Government Printing Office who was a very conscientious and earnest worker,
and that through his efforts several formulas were adopted for the manufacture of
certain kinds of printing inks which are now in use in that office. He further states
that the services of Mrs. Stephenson can be utilized to advantage as a skilled laborer.
The Civil Service Commission does not concur in the recommendation.
