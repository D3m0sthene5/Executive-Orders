October 26, 1915.
The following-named persons who were formerly employees of the United
States Commission on Industrial Relations may be appointed to positions in :
the competitive classified service without reference to the civil-service rules:
William L. Soleau, Mary R. Burr, Adelaide L. Patrick, Mayme M. Davidson,
Elizabeth A. Hyde, Florence Rimsnicer, Elizabeth G. Simmons, Thomas J.
Eagan, Josephine M. Jones, Nelle B. Curry, Aloysius A. Bauer, Charles J.
Stowell, Florence A. Cooke, Minetta Hargraves, Effie M. Nelson, Inez L. Bowser,
Estelle Johnson, Margie N. Conner, Catherine M. Etter, Rosamond G. Krueger.
This order is issued upon the recommendation of the Commission on In-
dustrial Relations. In a resolution unanimously adopted by the commission
on August 11, 1915, it is stated that the employees named have been excep-
tionally faithful, that they discharged their duties with marked ability, and
have thus shown their fitness in a practical way for employment in the classi-
fied civil service of the United States. The Civil Service Commission does
not concur in the recommendation.
