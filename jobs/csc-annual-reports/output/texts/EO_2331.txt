February 28, 1916.
The following-named persons, who were formerly employees in the San Fran-
cisco, Cal., post office, may be reinstated to positions in said office without
reference to the length of their separation from the service: Miss Nellie F.
Edwards, foreman, $1,400, dead-letter division, appointed September, 1890;
Miss Mary Joesten, clerk, $900, directory division, appointed February 5, 1908;
Miss Mattie Fitzgerald, clerk, $1,200, directory division, appointed in June, 1896 ;
Miss Cyrena Sitton, clerk, $1,100, dead-letter division, appointed in May, 1904 ;
Mrs. O. L. Williams, clerk, $800, dead-letter division, transferred from the
United States Mint December 24, 1909.
These employees were all removed on April 3, 1911, and their reinstatement
is recommended by the First Assistant Postmaster General, the postmaster at
San Francisco, and other officials of the San Francisco post office. In view of
these recommendations the previous good records of these employees and the
fact that it is deemed that an injustice has been done them, the Postmaster Gen-
eral recommends the issuance of an Executive order authorizing their reinstate-
ment. The Civil Service Commission does not concur in the recommendation.
