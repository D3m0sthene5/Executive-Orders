December 18, 1915.
Mr. William S. Phelps may be reinstated to the position of examiner of mer-
chandise in the customs service at the port of New York, without reference to
the length of his separation from the service. Mr. Phelps was formerly an
examiner of merchandise in the the customs service at the port of New York,
from which position he was removed in March, 1911, for carelessness, inatten-
tion, and unsatisfactory conduct of official business. This order is issued upon
the recommendation of the Secretary of the Treasury, who states that the mat-
ter of Mr. Phelps’s removal was investigated by a committee on personnel in the
appraiser’s office at New York, and that the committee in its report, dated March
25, 1915, found that his removal was not warranted by the facts and that his
inefficiency and inattention were not established. The Civil Service Cominis-
sion does not cocur in the recommendation.
