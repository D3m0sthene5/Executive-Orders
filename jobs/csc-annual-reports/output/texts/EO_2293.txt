December 29, 1915.
Mrs. Elizabeth B. Pettus may be appointed to a clerical position in the classi-
fied service without reference to the civil-service rules. Mrs. Pettus is the
widow of Maj. Harry L. Pettus, late of the Quartermaster Corps of the Army.
The Quartermaster General states that Maj. Pettus served continuously as
assistant quartermaster, United States Volunteers, with the rank of captain,
from June 14, 1899, until honorably discharged from the service June 30, 1901 ;
that he was commissioned quartermaster in the Regular Army with the rank
of captain to take effect February 2, 1901, and was promoted to major on March
3, 1911; and that his services were of the highest order of efficiency, embracing
every feature of duty falling to the Quartermaster Department, all of which
were performed by him in a most satisfactory and efficient manner. In view of
the facts the Quartermaster General recommends the issuance of an Executive
order in this case, in which recommendation the Secretary of War concurs.
The Civil Service Commission does not concur in the recommendation.
