September 13, 1926.
Schedule A, Subdivision IV, paragraph 10, of positions excepted from the
requirement of examination under the civil-service rules is amended to read as
follows by the addition of the words italicized:
"10. Civilian professors, instructors, and teachers in the United States Mili-
tary Academy at West Point, and the position of librarian when filled by
appointment of a graduate of that academy."
The Civil Service Commission concurs with the War Department in recom-
mending this amendment.
