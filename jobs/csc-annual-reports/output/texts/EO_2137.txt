February 19, 1915.
Miss Sadie S Killeen may be appointed a clerk in the classified service of the
Treasury Department without compliance with the civil service rules.
Miss Killeen has served under temporary employment for more than nine months,
having been appointed without examination because of her expert knowledge of the
operation of a machine which was to be tested to determine its applicability to the
posting of disbursing officers’ accounts in the Office of the Treasurer of the United
States. The plan having proved satisfactory, Miss Killeen was retained to demon-
strate and assist in introducing it. Her services have been of great value, and the
installation of the machines has resulted in a large saving in expenses. The Civil
Service Commission therefore concurs with the Secretary of the Treasury in recom-
mending this order.
