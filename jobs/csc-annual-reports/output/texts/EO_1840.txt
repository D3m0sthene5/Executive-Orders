October 17, 1913.
Mr. William J. Levy may be reinstated as a clerk in the Treasury Department
without compliance with civil-service rules.
This order is issued on the recommendation of the Secretary of the Treasury in
view of Mr. Levy’s former efficient service in the Treasury Department, where he
proved to be an exceptionally able and expert technical bookkeeper. His services
in that line are especially desired now because of the new accounting system inaugu-
rated in that department. The needs of the office of the Treasurer of the United
States at the present time are unusual and extraordinary, and Mr. Levy possesses
special and peculiar qualifications as a technical bookkeeper.
