January 6, 1916.
Mr. Thomas C. Barber may be appointed as entomological assistant at. a
salary of $1,700 per annum in the Bureau of Entomology, Department of Agri-
culture, without reference to the requirements of section 1 of civil-service
rule V. Mr. Barber, who has had several years’ service in the Department of
Agriculture in positions not subject to examination and under temporary ap-
pointment, attained a rating of 85.78 in the examination for scientific assist-
ant in entomology. Mr. Barber has demonstrated his qualifications for a par-
ticular work in connection with the boll-weevil investigations, is the only eligi-
ble so qualified, and his standing on the register is such that he would soon
receive an appointment were he not in alien. The Civil Service Commission
joins with the Secretary of Agriculture in recommending Mr. Barber’s appoint-
ment by Executive order.
