April 14, 1921.
The year limit upon eligibility for reinstatement is waived to permit the re-
instatement of Hugh C. Mitchell as computer in the Coast and Geodetic Sur-
vey of the Department of Commerce. Mr. Mitchell was regularly employed in
the Coast and Geodetic Survey from August 22, 1898, to December 7, 1911, and
has since been engaged upon work in the line of his profession. This order is
issued upon the recommendation of the Secretary of Commerce in view of Mr.
Mitchell’s long, faithful, and efficient service and the present need of his serv-
ices. The Civil Service Commission concurs in the recommendation in view
of Mr. Mitchell having originally entered the service through competitive ex-
amination and in view of his having continued to the present time in work
in, the line of his profession, thereby tending to maintain his efficiency.
~ April 14, 1921.
Mrs. Martha Rich Smith may be appointed as clerk at $1,100 per annum in
the office of headquarters, United States Marine Corps, Washington, D. C.,
without reference to the civil-service rules.
This order is issued upon the recommendation of the Assistant Secretary of
the Navy, in view of the facts that Mrs. Smith was enrolled in the United
States Naval Reserve Force during the World War and performed active duty
in the Navy Department from July 11, 1918, to July 31, 1919, and was given a
temporary appo:ntment as clerk on August 1, 1919, from which position she
resigned on June 30, 1920; and that she suffered the loss of her son, Lieut.
Clarence W. Smith, United. States Marine Corps, in the World War, he being
killed in action overseas. Lieut. Smith was awarded the Croix de Guerre by
the French Government for his gallant and conspicuous conduct in action
against the enemy on the battle field of France.
The Civil Service Commission was unable to concur in recommending this
order.
