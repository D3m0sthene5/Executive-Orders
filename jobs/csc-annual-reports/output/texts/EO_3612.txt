January 12, 1922.
Mr. Paul Bazaar may be appointed as clerk in the Rochester, N. Y., office
of the United States Veterans’ Bureau, without examination under the civil
service rules.
This order is issued on the recommendation of the Director of the United
States Veterans’ Bureau. Mr. Bazaar, who suffered the loss of both hands in
the World War, has by the use of artificial hands become so rehabilitated that
he can operate a typewriter and satisfactorily perform clerical duties. The
case being exceptional, the director believes that his appointment would have
a beneficial psychological effect upon the disabled ex-service men in that vicinity.
