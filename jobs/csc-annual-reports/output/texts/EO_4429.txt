April 23, 1926.
Mrs. Martha Fleeson may be appointed as a laborer in the post office at
Boston, Mass., without regard to the requirements of the civil-service rules.
Mrs. Fleeson is the widow of Mr. James W. Fleeson, for more than 25 years
an employee of the Boston post office, who died January 18, 1926, leaving her
with a dependent daughter, 12 years old.
As the Civil Service Commission can properly recommend waivers only when
the procedure under the rules will not fully meet the needs of the service, it is
unable to concur in the recommendation of the Postmaster General for the
issuance of this order.
For charity.
