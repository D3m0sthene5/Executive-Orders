June 27, 1924
In consideration of the services of Lieut. Commander Louis Collins Scheibla,
United States Navy, who died as an officer on active duty and in the line of
duty when the seaplane, in which he was flying as an observer, crashed into the
Potomac River near Dahlgren, Va., on April 17, 1924, his widow, Mrs. Belle
Barrick Scheibla, may be employed as a file clerk, at $1,140 per annum, in the
Veterans’ Bureau, for duty in the Navy Department, for work in connection
with the administration of the act to provide adjusted compensation for veterans
of the World War, without regard to the civil-service act or rules. This action
is recommended by the Secretary of the Navy. The Civil Service Commission
does not concur in this recommendation.
For charity.
