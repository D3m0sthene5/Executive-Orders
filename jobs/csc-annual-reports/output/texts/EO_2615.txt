May 9, 1917.
Henry S. Grogan, of Minnesota, may be reinstated as a post-office inspector without
regard to the year limitation prescribed by Civil Service Rule IX.
This order is issued on the recommendation of the Postmaster General because of
Mr. Grogan’s long and efficient service in various branches of the Postal Service,
including 11 years as a letter carrier and subsequent service from 1906 to 1912 as
posto-flice inspector, superintendent of station, and assistant superintendent of mails.
The Civil Service Commission concurs in the recommendation of the Postmaster
General for an Executive order.
