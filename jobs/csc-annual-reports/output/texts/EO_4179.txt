March 20, 1925.
Mrs. Louise M. Linthicum whose name appears on the calculating machine
operator register with a rating of 90.85 but is not within reach for certification
may be appointed in the classified service without regard to her standing on the
register. Mrs. Linthicum has been serving under temporary appointment as a
comptometer operator since June 24, 1924. The Civil Service Commission is
unable to concur in the issuance of this order.
Done for charity.
