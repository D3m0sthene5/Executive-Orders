June 26, 1916.
Miss Lillian Rogenmoser, temporarily employed in the Department of State,
may be appointed to a permanent nonclerical position therein, in accordance
with Executive order No. 2383, of May 18, 1916, notwithstanding a lack of
continuous service of six months immediately preceding July 1, 1916. Miss
Rogenmoser was not eligible for permanent appointment under the order of
May 18, 1916. It was stated that she was the most adaptable to the work of
assisting in the printing of passports of any helper the lithographer of the
department had employed during the last 10 years. Her duties were described
to be the wetting down of passports to exactly the right degree and the right
temperature preparatory to the printing or lithographing, and the making of
records to correspond with those of the Treasury Department.
