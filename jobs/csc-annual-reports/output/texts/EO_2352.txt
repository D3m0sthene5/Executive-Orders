April 3, 1916.
The minimum-height requirement for the post-office service is hereby waived
in the case of Pedro D. Mora, an elevator conductor in the State, War, and Navy
Building, who desires to take the civil-service examination for the position of
clerk in the postal service. This order is issued upon the recommendation of
the Chief of Staff of the United States Army, and the statement of the Post-
master General that he has no objection to waiving the minimum-height re-
quirement in this case. The Civil Service Commission does not concur in the
recommendation.
