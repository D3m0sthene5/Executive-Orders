May 28, 1928.
The papers of the examination of Mr. George W. Sisler for postmaster at Stanwood,
lowa, may be rated by the Civil Service Commission, waiving the age limit. Mr.
Sisler was postmaster from 1911 to 1917. It is stated that he is at the present time
the choice of the majority, if not all, of the business men of the town for the office
and that he is very competent.
The Civil Service Commission concurs with the Postmaster General in recommend-
ing the issuance of this order.
