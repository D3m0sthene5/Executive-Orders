July 8, 1914.
Mrs. Mary L. Brown may be appointed without examination as a clerk in the War
Department in place of her mother, Mrs. Elizabeth F. Long. ;
Mrs. Brown has had several years of experience in the Census Office and with the
Immigration Commission, where she was unusually competent as statistical clerk and
supervising clerk. Mrs. Brown and her mother belong to a family greatly distinguished

for services rendered to the Government in its military and naval branches. In view
of these facts the Secretary of War recommends the appointment of Mrs. Brown.
