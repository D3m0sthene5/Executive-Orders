April 26, 1926.
Mr. Harry E. Randolph may be permanently appointed in the classified serv-ice of the Treasury Department without compliance with the provisions of
the civil-service rules.
Mr. Randolph served three terms as temporary customs agent at New York
during 1924. During 1925 it was found necessary in the interest of the customs
revenue to employ him repeatedly. In the opinion of the agent in charge, the
Assistant Attorney General, and the special assistants representing the Govern-
ment, the termination of Mr. Randolph's services during the pendency of certain
important cases would militate seriously against the Government's success,
and he should be permanently continued.
• This order is issued upon the recommendation of the Secretary of the Treas-
ury. In view of the representations as to its necessity, the Civil Service
Commission interposes no objection.
