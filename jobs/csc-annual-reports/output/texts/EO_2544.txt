March 15, 1917.
Mr. J. R. McArdle may be appointed in the Post Office Department subject to
such noncompetitive test of fitness as the Civil Service Commission may prescribe.
Mr. McArdle has served as private secretary from March 5, 1913, to the present.
The Postmaster General states that he is well educated, an expert stenographer and
typewriter, a good executive, has organized the work of his office in a creditable
manner, serving on the efficiency board for more than two years during which time
the board has placed promotions on a merit basis and adjusted salaries so that more
work is being done with fewer clerks and at less cost than four years ago. The
appointment is proposed to conserve the special and valuable knowledge and experi-
ence of Mr. McArdle and because the department would be unable to secure the
services otherwise of an equally valuable man.
In view of the statement of the Postmaster General as to the extent and value of
the training and services of Mr. McArdle, the commission concurs in the recom-
mendation for an Executive order.
