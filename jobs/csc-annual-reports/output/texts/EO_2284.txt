December 14, 1915.
Executive order No. 2263, of October 26, 1915, authorizing the appointment
of certain persons who were former employees of the United States Commis-
sion on Industrial Relations to positions in the competitive classified service
without reference to the civil-service rules, is hereby amended by adding the
following names: Florence Ray, Francis H. Bird, Peter A. Speck, Selig
Periman.
