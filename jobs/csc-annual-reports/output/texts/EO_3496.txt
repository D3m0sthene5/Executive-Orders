June 14, 1921.
The year limit upon eligibility for reinstatement is hereby waived to permit
the reinstatement of Mr. Bartlett S. Taylor in the Postal Service at Sharon,
Pa.
Mr. Taylor entered the service as a substitute carrier July 1, 1906, at Sharon,
Pa., and was advanced to a regular position March 1, 1907; he was promoted
through the various grades until he was receiving the maximum salary for
carriers of $1,500, when he resigned October 25, 1919, from the service in the
post office at Sharon, giving as his reason his inability to support his family,
which consists of a wife and eight children, on the salary he was receiving,
due to the increased cost of living and illness in his family.
The Postmaster General states that from the facts presented it would seem
that an Executive order reinstating Mr. Taylor would be warranted, and so
recommends. The Civil Service Commission, believing that the service would
be benefited by the reinstatement of Mr. Taylor, joins the Postmaster General
in recommending the order.
