August 3, 1922.
The Executive order of May 10, 1921, is so far waived as to permit the exami-
nation papers of Benjamin B. Gorman, an applicant for the postmastership at
Coldwater, Mich., to be rated by the Civil Service Commission, he now being
over 65 years of age. Since Mr. Gorman had previously been postmaster at
said Coldwater for 12 years, with an excellent record, this action is taken for
the good of the service on the recommendation of the Postmaster General.
