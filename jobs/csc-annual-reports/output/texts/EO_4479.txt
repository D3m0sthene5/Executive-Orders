July 15, 1926.
Oliver C. Hubbard may be permanently appointed as a compositor in the
Bureau of Engraving and Printing, without regard to his relative standing
on the register of eligibles.
This order is recommended by the Acting Secretary of the Treasury, who
states that Mr. Hubbard is a disabled veteran of the World War, has been
serving temporarily in the bureau, has demonstrated his skill, and is found
to be thoroughly qualified for the work, and that his permanent appointment
is believed to be in the interests of the service.
The Civil Service Commission is unable to concur in the issuance of this
order.
Apprcved for war service.
