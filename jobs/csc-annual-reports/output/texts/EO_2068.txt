October 28 1914.
Mrs. Addie Spofford Hess may be appointed a clerk in the Bureau of War Risk Insur-
ance of the Treasury Department without compliance with civil service rules. Mrs.
Hess's valuable experience in the Government service in filling excepted positions
particularly qualifies her for the work of the Bureau of War Risk Insurance. This
order is issued on the recommendation of the Secretary of the Treasury.
