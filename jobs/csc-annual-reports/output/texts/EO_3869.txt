June 18, 1923.
Mrs. Amelia V. Briscoe may be appointed to a clerical position in the classified
service without examination.
This order is issued on the recommendation of the Secretary of War, who states that
Mrs. Briscoe is the widow of Capt. William J. Briscoe, Philippine Scouts, who lost his
life last year in the Philippines from burns caused while fighting fire in an attempt to
save the lives of soldiers.
Mrs. Briscoe was temporarily appointed without examination in the Bureau of Pen-
sions under an act of Congress which permitted temporary appointments to be made
without regard to the civil service rules. It is reported that her work is above the
average and that she has faithfully and conscientiously discharged all the duties of the
osition.
P The Civil Service Commission did not concur in the issuance of this order.
