February 2, 1921.
Morris A. Parris may be appointed as clerk to act as confidential secretary
to the Director of the Bureau of Standards, at $1,800 per annum, without re-
gard to the requirements of the civil-service rules.
This order is issued upon the recommendation of the Director of the Bureau
of Standards and of the Secretary of Commerce, Mr. Parris having demon-
strated under temporary appointment that he possesses the peculiar qualifica-
tions and experience necessary for the duties of the position.
