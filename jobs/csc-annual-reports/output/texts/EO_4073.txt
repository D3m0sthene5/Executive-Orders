September 9, 1924
Harrie G. Thomas may be appointed an investigator in the General Accounting
Office without regard to civil-service rules.
This order is issued on the recommendation of the Comptroller General of the
United States, who states that Mr. Thomas possesses unusual aptitude and fitness
for important and particular investigational work, and that his services are ur-
gently needed.
This order is issued for the good of the service. The Civil Service Commission
does not concur in recommending this order.
