May 31, 1916.
Mrs. Maud N. Jackson may be reinstated to a position in the classified service
of the Treasury Department without regard to the length of her separation
from the service. Mrs. Jackson was originally appointed in the Bureau of En-
graving and Printing as a printer’s assistant on September 6, 1890, under her
maiden name of Maud Nichols. She served in various bureaus of the depart-
ment until May 7, 1900, when she was transferred to the office of the Super-
vising Architect and promoted to $1,000. She was promoted to $1,200 January
3, 1901, and was receiving that salary when she resigned on June 30 of that
year. This order is issued upon the recommendation of the Secretary of the
Treasury, who states that Mrs. Jackson was an especially satisfactory employee
under her former appointment, and that he believes her reinstatement will be
in the interest of the service. The Civil Service Commission does not concur
in the recommendation. !
