June 20, 1923.
Mrs. Lottie M. Ballard may be appointed to the position of printer’s assistant in
the Bureau of Engraving and Printing without regard to the civil service rules. This
order was recommended by the Secretary of the Treasury in view of the facts presented
to him by the director of the bureau.
Mrs. Ballard served from August 8, 1918, to April 23, 1919, and from August 30,
1920, to January 3, 1921, as a printer’s assistant, having been appointed under the-
Executive order of January 15, 1918, which permitted appointments in the bureau
without regard to examination. She also served temporarily from January 3, 1923, to
March 6, 1923, under section 4 of Civil Service Rule VIII. This service gave her
no status for reinstatement under the civil service rules. Her husband, who was
confined on May 23 in the Government Hospital for the Insane, leaving her without
support for herself and a young child, served for about five years as a skilled helper
at $3 per diem in the bureau.
The Civil Service Commission]did not concur in recommending this order.
