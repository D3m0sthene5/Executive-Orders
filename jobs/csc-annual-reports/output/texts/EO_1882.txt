January 24, 1914.
Mrs. Eliza Ryon may be reinstated in the classified nonapportioned force of the
Bureau of Engraving and Printing without reference to the year limitation.
This order 1s recommended by the Secretary of the Treasury in view of the fact
that Mrs. Ryon was before marriage an efficient employee. She married an employee
of the Bureau of Engraving and Printing, who is now afflicted with tuberculosis and
will not be able to resume duty. The Civil Service Commission concurs in the
recommendation.
