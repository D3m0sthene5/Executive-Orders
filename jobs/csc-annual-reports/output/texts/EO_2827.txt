March 29, 1918.
Mrs. Margaret Frazier. widow of Joseph W. Frazier, formerly employed as
a general helper at the naval torpedo station, Newport, R. I., who was killed in
the explosion at that station on January 26 1918, may be employed as a primer
worker at that station without regard to the provisions of the civil-service
rules. “
The compensation allowed Mrs. Frazier on account of the death of her hus-
band for such period as she remains a widow is said by the Secretary of the
Navy to be inadequate for her support. and he believes, in simple justice, that
she should be given further aid in the way of employment. :
