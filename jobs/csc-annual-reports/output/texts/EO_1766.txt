April 21, 1913.
Mr. Theodore T. Kirk may be appointed to the position of assistant mechanical
draftsman in the Navy Department.
Mr. Kirk was appointed a teacher in the Philippine service through examination
on August 27, 1907, and served to May 28, 1910. If he had served to August 27, 1910,
his reinstatement and transfer would be permissible under existing rules. Since his
separation from the Philippine service he has served one year and seven months as
junior engineer temporarily in the Bureau of Mines. He passed an examination for
surveyor for the Philippine service, and an examination for mechanical draftsman
held on February 3, 1913. The last examination was held for the field service and does
not render him eligible under the terms of the announcement of the examination for
certification for service in Washington.
In view of Mr. Kirk’s excellent record and demoastrated qualifications, the Civil
Service Commission recommends his appointment.
