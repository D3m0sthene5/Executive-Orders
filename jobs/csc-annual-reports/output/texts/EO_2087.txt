November 17, 1914.
Mrs. C. H. Brice may be appointed to a clerical position in the classified service
without regard to the civil-service rules. This order is issued because of the fact
that the main Suppor of Mrs. Brice and her young daughter was provided monthly
by her brother, Col. Franklin J. Moses, United States Marine Corps, who lost his life
in the line of duty on September 26,1914, in Vera Cruz. Mrs. Brice has passed a civil-
service examination for clerk. :
The Civil Service Commission does not recommend the issuance of an Executive
order in this case.
