February 9, 1916.
Mr. Edward H. Dyer, of Washington, D. C., may be appointed to a position
in the Division of Efficiency, United States Civil Service Commission, with-
out reference to the civil-service rules. Mr. Dyer was employed as an ac-
countant by the Division of Efficiency in the work of developing and install-
ing a simplified system of accounting in the Treasurer’s Office, Treasury De-
partment, and had charge of the general books until they were turned over to
the regular employees. He rendered valuable service in connection with said
work, and his appointment to a permanent position in the Division of Effi-
ciency is recommended by the chief of the division, who states that almost
every piece of work which the division undertakes involves some phase of
accounting for which Mr. Dyer is especially qualified.
