November 7, 1925.
In consideration of the services of Lieut. Commander Louis Collins Scheibla,
United States Navy, who died as an officer on active duty and in the line of
duty when the seaplane in which he was flying as an observer crashed into
the Potomac River near Dahlgren, Va., on April 17, 1924, his widow, Mrs.
Belle Barrick Scheibla, may be employed in any clerical position under the
Navy Department which she is qualified to fill, without regard to the civil
service act or rules.
Mrs. Scheibla was appointed on July 7, 1924, under the Executive order of
June 27, 1924, on the rolls of the Veterans' Bureau for duty in the Navy
Department for work in connection with the administration of the act to pro-
vide adjusted compensation for veterans of the World War. Her present
employment will soon terminate, owing to lack of work. The Secretary of
the Navy states that she has demonstrated her ability to perform satisfactorily
such clerical duties as have been assigned her, and, in view of this fact, it is
desired to retain her services under the Navy Department.
The Civil Service Commission does not concur in this recommendation.
Done for charity.
