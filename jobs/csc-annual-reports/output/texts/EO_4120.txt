January 8, 1925.
Mr. A. H. Bergthold may be appointed postmaster at Weatherford, Okla.,
without regard to the Executive order of May 10, 1921, requiring candidates to
pass a civil-service examination for presidential postmasterships.
This order is issued on recommendation of the Postmaster General, who states
that Mr. Bergthold has been acting postmaster since June 5, 1923, and his service
has been satisfactory.
The Civil Service Commission does not concur in recommending this order.
