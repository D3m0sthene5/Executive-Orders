January 12, 1926.
Mrs. Anna M. Walsh may be appointed to a clerkship in the post office at
Boston, Mass., without regard to the requirements of the civil-service rules.
Mrs. Walsh is the wife of John J. Walsh, for 13 years a clerk in the post-
office at Boston with an excellent record for efficiency, who is totally disabled
mentally to continue his work and support his wife and three infant children.
The Postmaster General recommends this order. As the Civil Service Commis-
sion can properly recommend waivers only when the procedure under the rules
will not fully meet the needs of the service, it is unable to concur.
Done for charity.
