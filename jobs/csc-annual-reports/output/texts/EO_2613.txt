May 9, 1917.
Mr. Horace M. Albright may be appointed as Assistant Director of the National
- Park Service.
This order is issued on the recommendation of the Secretary of the Interior who
states that Mr. Albright, as assistant attorney of the department, has been assisting
in the management of the national park system for several years; that he is very
familiar with the administration of the parks and with their problems; that he is
personally acquainted with the field force and with the park concessioners who are
under contract with the department to provide hotel accommodations and transporta-
tion service for the comfort and convenience of the traveling public; that he knows the
conditions under which these concessioners operate, and has drafted the contracts
under which several of the larger concessions are conducted; that he understands
thoroughly the policies that have been adopted for the development and promotion
of the national park system; that he is concerned with the preparation of legislation
relating to the national parks, reports to Congress on national park legislation, and
is charged with the duty of defending departmental estimates for appropriations for
the park system.
The Secretary also states that it is necessary that the Assistant Director of the
National Park Service be acquainted with the parks and their problems at the time
he assumes his office; that he be a lawyer in order that he may give consideration to
all legal matters relating to the National Park Service, and that Mr. Albright possesses
these qualifications.
The Civil Service Commission concurs in the recommendation for the issuance of
this order.
