April 30, 1915.
Schedule A, Subdivision IV, of the civil-service rules is hereby amended by the
addition of the paragraph as follows:
“14. Watchmen on fortifications under construction when the positions are filled
by men who have served at least two enlistments in the Army, Navy, or Marine Corps.”
This amendment, which has the effect of excepting from the requirement of exami-
nation the appointment of watchmen under the conditions mentioned, is recommended
by the War Department and the Civil Service Commission in recognition of the fact
that watchmen on fortifications under construction should be men who are alert,
vigorous, courageous, patriotic, and of unquestioned loyalty, their service being
regarded by the department as almost military in character.
