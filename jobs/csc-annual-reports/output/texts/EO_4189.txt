April 3, 1925.
Mrs. Anna T. Gibson may be appointed to a clerkship in the post office at
New York City without regard to the requirements of the civil service rules.
Mrs. Gibson is the widow of William A. Gibson, for 37 years a faithful employee
of the New York City post office who, at his death, left her with one dependent
child. This order is issued upon the recommendation of the Postmaster General.
The Civil Service Commission is unable to concur.
Done for charity.
