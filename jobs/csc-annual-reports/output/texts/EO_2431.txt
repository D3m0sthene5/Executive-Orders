August 1, 1916.
Albert W. Smith may be promoted to a subclerical position in the Treas-
ury Department without reference to the civil-service rules. This order is
issued upon the recommendation of the Secretary of the Treasury, who states
that Mr. Smith has served very satisfactorily as an unclassified laborer in the
Treasury Department since April 25, 1906, and has passed the subclerical ex-
amination, but, being from the District of Columbia, is not eligible under the
civil-service rules for promotion to an apportioned position. The Civil Service
Commission does not concur in the recommendation.
