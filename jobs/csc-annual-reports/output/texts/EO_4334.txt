November 6, 1925.
Mrs. Margaret L. Pow Ernst may be reinstated in the classified service
without regard to the length of her separation therefrom. This order is
issued upon the recommendation of the Postmaster General by reason of
Mrs. Ernst's former service of 13 years, from 1906 to 1919, as a clerk in the
Salem (Ohio) post office.
The Civil Service Commission is unable to concur in the recommendation of
the Postmaster General.
Done for charity.
