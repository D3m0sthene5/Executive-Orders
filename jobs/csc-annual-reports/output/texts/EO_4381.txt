February 8, 1926.
Mr. John G. Fagan may be appointed to the classified position of messenger
in the Department of Justice without regard to civil service rules.
Mr. Fagan has had practically continuous service either as unclassified laborer
or as messenger with an excepted status since April 13, 1905. The Attorney
General states that during his service under 10 Assistants to the Attorney
General he has won a reputation for efficiency, intelligence, courtesy, and
loyalty.
The Civil Service Commission does not recommend this order.
