April 21, 1915.
Mr. Arthur S. Kellom, formerly a letter carrier at the Chico, Cal., post office, may
be reinstated in the postal service without regard to the length of time he has been
separated from the service.
‘he reinstatement of Mr. Kellom is recommended by the Postmaster General,
who states that a careful review of the papers in this case discloses that the charges on
which the carrier was removed were not wholly true and that the action taken by the
department was based upon statements which for the most part are not borne out by
facts recently developed and were the result of prejudice on the part of the former
ostmaster and, possibly, the post-office inspector who conducted the case, and it
18 believed that an injustice was done and that the offenses charged against Carrier
Eolloms even if true, were not such as to justify so severe a punishment as that
inflicted.
