May 18, 1916.
Owing to the increased permanent clerical force provided for the Department
of State in the legislative, executive, and judicial appropriation bill, approved
May 10, 1916, the Secretary of State, without reference to the civil-service law,
may make appointments to the nonclerical grades and to the clerical grades up
to and including class 1, therein provided, from such employees who, upon
July 1, 1916, shall have served upon the temporary roll of the Department of
State for a continuous period of at least six months immediately prior thereto,
or who may have passed an appropriate examination by the Civil Service Com-
mission: Provided, That such temporary employees have satisfactorily per-
formed their respective duties and shall have attained a competent rating
by the department board of efficiency.
This order is issued upon the recommendation of the Secretary of State be-
cause of the experience acquired by the temporary employees through faithful
and efficient service in the actual work of the Department of State and to
obviate the serious interruption to the department's pressing and important
duties which would be occasioned by the loss of a large specially trained and
efficient force.
