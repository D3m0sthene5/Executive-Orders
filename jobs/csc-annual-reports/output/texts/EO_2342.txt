March 14, 1916.
Executive order No. 2331, of February 28, 1916, authorizing the appointment
of Mrs. Annie K. Campbell, a special agent in the Bureau of the Census, to
act as private secretary to the Director of the Census, may be interpreted as
authorizing Mrs. Campbell’s appointment to a clerkship with a competitive
gas status and without requiring her assignment exclusively to any spe-
cific duty.
