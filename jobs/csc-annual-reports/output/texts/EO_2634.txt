June 8, 1917.
Paragraph 1, Subdivision IV, Schedule B of classified positions which may be filled
upon noncompetitive examination under the civil-service rules, is hereby amended by
inserting after the phrase ‘instructors in flying” the words “student instructors in
flying.” As amended the paragraph will read as follows:
“1. Aeronautical engineers, aeronautical mechanical engineers, instructors in flying,
student instructors in flying, aviation mechanicians, and inspectors of airplanes and
airplane engines in the aviation section of the Signal Corps.
“This amendment also includes a change of designation previously reported by the
War Department from inspectors.of aeroplanes and aeroplane motors to inspectors of
airnlanes and airplane engines.”
