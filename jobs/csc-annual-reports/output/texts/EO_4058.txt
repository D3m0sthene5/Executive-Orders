August 6, 1924
Mrs. Cora M. Goldsworthy may be appointed to a position in the Treasury
Department without compliance with the civil-service rules.
Mrs. Goldsworthy is the widow of Benjamin Goldsworthy, who died in June,
1923, after a service of 17 years in the Bureau of Engraving and Printing.
This order is issued on the recommendation of the Secretary of the Treasury
as a matter of Executive benevolence.
The Civil Service Commission submits the case without prejudice, stating that
it is without jurisdiction.
For charity.
