October 10, 1916.
Mrs. Vera A. Hardie may be reinstated as a printer ’s assistant in the Bureau of En-
_graving and Printing without regard to the length of time she has been separated from
the service. Mrs. Hardie was appointed on November 1, 1913, and rendered very
efficient service until the date of her resignation on account of marriage on April 2, 1915.
She has an invalid husband and infant dependent upon her. The Acting Secretary
of the Treasury recommended the issuance of this order and the Civil Service Commis-
sion concurs in view of Mrs. Hardie’s good record and the short period which has
elapsed since her separation from the service.
