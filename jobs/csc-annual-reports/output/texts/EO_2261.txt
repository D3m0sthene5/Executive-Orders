October 21, 1915.
Mr. Alexander C. Downey may be appointed a clerk in the competitive
classified service of the Treasury Department without compliance with the
civil-service rules. Mr. Downey has served since September 2, 1913, in the
excepted position of private secretary to the Comptroller of the Treasury. In
addition to the duties of private secretary it appears that Mr. Downey has
been engaged on the work of passing upon the contracts of Army officers, which
is considered very complicated work. The Auditor for the War Department
states that he is in great need of a clerk capable of auditing the contracts
of Army officers and believes that Mr. Downey's experience in the Comptroller’s
office in similar duties eminently qualifies him to perform this work. This
order ‘is issued on the recommendation of the Secretary of the Treasury, the
Civil Service Commission concurring because of testimony as to Mr. Downey’s
special training.
