September 30, 1916.
Mrs. Lillian M. Watkins may be transferred from the nonapportioned to the appor-
tioned service of the Treasury Department, and promoted to the position of clerk, with-
out civil-service rules.
This order is issued upon the recommendation of the Acting Secretary of the Treas-
ury, who states that Mrs. Watkins is an efficient calculating machine operator, and that
it 18 believed her services will be of special value in an apportioned position.
The Civil Service Commission does not concur in the recommendation.
