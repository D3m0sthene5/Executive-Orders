February 11, 1916.
The temporary appointment of Miss Helen Wrenn as clerk in the Bureau of
Foreign and Domestic Commerce, Department of Commerce, may be made per-
manent without regard to the civil-service rules. This order is issued upon
recommendation of the Secretary of Commerce, who states that she was cer-
tified for temporary appointment after passing the civil-service examination
in stenography and typewriting, and that with the exception of a brief period
she has been serving in the bureau named continuously since April 1, 1915;
that she is unusually efficient and has shown special aptitude in the per-
formance of the various duties assigned to her; and that it is therefore believed
that her permanent appointment will prove of very great value to the bureau,
which is especially in urgent need of competent and trained assistants at this
time, owing to the extraordinary amount and character of work devolving
upon it.
