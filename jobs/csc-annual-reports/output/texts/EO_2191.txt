May 11, 1915.
Mrs. Laura F. Richmond may be reinstated as an operative in the Bureau of
Engraving and Printing without regard to the length of time she has been separated
from the service.
This order is issued on the recommendation of the Acting Secretary of the Treasury
in view of the efficient service rendered by the husband of Mrs. Richmond in the
Treasury Department for 28 years, and in view of the fact that Mr. Richmond is now
wholly incapacitated both mentally and physically for the performance of duty, and
also because of the satisfactory service rendered by Mrs. Richmond while a former
employee in the Bureau of Engraving and Printing where she served for a period of
seven years.
The Civil Service Commission does not concur in the recommendation.
