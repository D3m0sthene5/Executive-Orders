February 28, 1916. :
Mr. J. D. Yoakley may be reinstated to a position in the classified service
without reference to the length of his separation from the service. Mr. Yoakley
was appointed to a position in the post-office service in 1894 through the classified
civil service, having passed the prescribed examination. He remained in this
position for a period of about five years, during which time it is stated he
rendered faithful, efficient, and loyal service. His reinstatement is requested
by the Postmaster General in view of the method by which Mr. Yoakley was
separated from the service. The Postmaster General states that he is convinced
that Mr. Yoakley is well equipped for a position in the Post Office Department.
The Civil Service Commission does not concur in the request of the Postmaster
General. :
