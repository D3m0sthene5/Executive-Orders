May 2, 1921.
The year limit upon eligibility for reinstatement is waived to permit the
reinstatement of Dr. William S. Newman ag veterinary inspector, Bureau of
Animal Industry, Department of Agriculture.
Dr. Newman was regularly employed in the position of veterinary inspector
from February 15, 1908, to April 10, 1919, when he resigned. The Secretary
of Agriculture bases his recommendation for reinstatement upon the fact that
there is urgent need for the services of a veterinary inspector for assignment
to some class of work upon which Dr. Newman was previously engaged. He
was one of the best field inspectors in the service, and since his separation
from the Government service has continued in work in the line of his pro-
fession thereby maintaining his efficiency.
The Civil Service Commission concurs in the recommendation.
