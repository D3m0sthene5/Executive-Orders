February 29, 1920.
Miss Marian Kearns, of the District of Columbia, may be transferred from the
position of clerk to sign tribal deeds, $1,200 per annum, office of the Secretary,
Interior Department, to a regular clerkship. She has served efficiently in the
Department of the Interior since May 21, 1918, and has demonstrated her
ability as a clerk.
The Civil Service Commission does not concur in the recommendation for an
Executive order.
