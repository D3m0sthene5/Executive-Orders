February 1, 1926.
Mr. Aaron J. Stanton may be reinstated as stockman, technical stores (lum-
ber), at $7.28 per diem, in the supply department, navy yard, Pearl Harbor,
Hawaii, without regard to the length of his separation from the service.
Mr. Stanton resigned without prejudice as quarterman and master boat
builder in the navy yard at Puget Sound, Wash., after 27 years of service,
on May 14, 1919. No eligibles are available for appointment to this position.
The commission concurs with the Navy Department in recommending this
order.
