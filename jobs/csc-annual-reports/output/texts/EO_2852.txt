April 30, 1918.
The appointments of temporary censorship employees of postal censorship
stations are hereby exempted from the requirement of examination under the
civil-service rules. :
The Postmaster General states that the confidential and secret nature of the
work, the unusual qualifications required, the necessity of assurance of abso-
lute loyalty, the necessity for immediate services, and the temporary character
of the work make advisable the exemption of this class of employees.
The Civil Service Commission concurs with the Postmaster General in
recommending the order.
