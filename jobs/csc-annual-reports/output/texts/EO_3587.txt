December 12, 1921.
The limit of one year upon eligibility for reinstatement is hereby waived
to permit the reinstatement of Clarence Bryant to the competitive classified
service in the post office at Le Roy, N. Y.
Mr. Bryant was appointed as a clerk in the Le Roy, N. Y., post office May 1,
1900, promoted to assistant postmaster July 1, 1900, in which position he served
until he resigned on February 3, 1906. He was reappointed as assistant post-
master May 18, 1906, and resigned December 30, 1907.
This order was issued upon recommendation of the Postmaster General.
