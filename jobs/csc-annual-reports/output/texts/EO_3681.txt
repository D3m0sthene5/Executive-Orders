May 24, 1922.
The following-named employee in the Office of Public Buildings and Grounds,
is on the joint recommendation of the Secretary of War and of the Civil Service
Commission transferred from an unclassified to a classified status without
regard to the provision of the civil-service rules: John Miller, laborer.
This order is issued on account of this employee’s long, faithful, and efficient
service, and for the reason that he is unable to pass the required civil-service
examination due to lack of education.
