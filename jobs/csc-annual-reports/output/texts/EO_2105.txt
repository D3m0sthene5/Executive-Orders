December 11, 1914.
Mrs. Sallie G. Jones, who has been employed in the Indian Bureau of the Interior
Department for a period of four years, may be promoted without examination under
the civil-service rules.
Mrs. Jones has been employed in the Interior Department in various capacities since
May, 1900. Her record for efficient and faithful service is good. She was placed in
the classified service under an Executive order issued October 9, 1908, and appointed
copyist at $720 per annum. In October, 1910, she was promoted to $900, and in April,
1914, in view of the meritorious service which she was rendering, the Commissioner of
Indian Affairs promoted her to a clerical position at $1,000 per annum. However,
owing to a ruling of the Civil Service Commission that under the provisions of the
Executive order placing her in the classified service, she was not eligible for promotion
to said grade without examination, she was, on August 16, 1914, reduced to $900 per
annum.
Mrs. Jones is the widow of Robert Tyler Jones, a grandson of President Tyler, who
was born in the White House during the administration of President Tyler, and who,
it is stated, was the only male child ever born in the White House.
