March 23, 1914.
Mr. Paul F. Myers, of Princeton, N. J., may be appointed to an appropriate position
in the competitive classified service without examination.
This action is based upon the statement of the Commissioner of Internal Revenue
to the Secretary of the Treasury that his services and qualifications are needed, in
connection with the administration of the income-tax law, in the office of the Com-
missioner of Internal Revenue, in Washington, D. C.
