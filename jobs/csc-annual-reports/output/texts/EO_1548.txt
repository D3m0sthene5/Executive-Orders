June 19, 1912.
Mr. John G. Lerch may be Spoil without examination as private secretary
to Mr. lugene G. Hay, member of the Board of General Appraisers.
The position is one of a highly confidential nature, as the secretary to the general
appraiser must of necessity know in many instances in advance of the promulgation
of decisions what the decisions will be.
Mr. Lerch has had considerable previous experience in the capacity of secretary
and has passed the civil-service examination in stenography and typewriting.
The Civil Service Commission does not concur in the recommendation for an
Executive order.
