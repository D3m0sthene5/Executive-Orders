December 1, 1915.
Mr. Lawrence A. Baker may be appointed a clerk in the competitive class of
the Post Office Department without examination. The Civil Service Commission
concurs in the recommendation of the Postmaster General for the appointment.
The Postmaster General states that for the past three years the department has
been engaged in a campaign of reorganizing post offices to systematize and stand-
ardize methods, which has saved in the aggregate the services of hundreds of em-
ployees and effected many other important economies; that Mr. Baker, who was
appointed to the excepted position of confidential clerk to the First Assistant
Postmaster General July 1, 1913, has in the discharge of his duties become an
expert on reorganization of this character, and that in order to conserve the
special and valuable knowledge he has gained on this subject and utilize it to
the fullest extent in the extension of reorganization to all post offices, his ap-
pointment to a classified position is requested.
