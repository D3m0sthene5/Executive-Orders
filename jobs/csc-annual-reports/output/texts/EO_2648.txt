June 28, 1917.
Mr. H. H. Chapman may be reinstated as assistant district forester under the
Department of Agriculture without regard to the year limitation upon eligibility for
reinstatement.
Mr. Chapman worked as a student assistant in the Forest Service as early as 1899.
In July, 1904, he was appointed forest assistant through appropriate examination,
and continued in this position until 1906, when he was appointed instructor in the
Yale Forest School, and the following year became an assistant professor. At various
intervals or during summer vacations between 1906 and 1910 he was employed by the
department as a collaborator. His resignation from the Forest Service was effected
in February, 1914. Since his separation from the service, his work has been along
lines tending to enhance his value to the Government. He is considered one of the
best foresters in the country, and in the opinion of the department the best interests
of the service will be met by his reemployment.
The commission concurs in recommending the order.
