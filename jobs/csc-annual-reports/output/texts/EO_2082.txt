November 11, 1914.
Mrs. Annie Day may be appointed to an appropriate position in the classified service
at the Government Printing Office without regard to the civil-service rules. Mrs.
Day is the widow of Joseph P. Day, a former employee of the Government Printing
Office, who in 1908 received an injury in line of duty which hastened his death and
for which he received no compensation, as the law granting such payments for injuries
received in the line of duty did not become effective until five weeks thereafter. Mrs.
Day, with three small children, is now in destitute circumstances, and the Public
Printer recommends that he.be given authority to appoint her to a position in his
office. The Civil Service Commission is unable to concur in his recommendation.
