February 6, 1914.
Mrs. Rose Tyler, who has passed the required tests for appointment under the labor
regulations and is well qualified, may be appointed a charwoman without reference
to her relative standing among the competitors. She has been deserted by her hus-
band, who is a Spanish War veteran, and is now required to support herself and four
little children, one of whom is blind.
The Civil Service Commission does not recommend this order.
(It will be noted that this order waives the labor regulations and not the civil-service
rules.)
