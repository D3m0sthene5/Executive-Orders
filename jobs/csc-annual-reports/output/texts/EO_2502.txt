December 28, 1916.
Miss Anne M. Roden may be permanently appointed in the classified service of the
Treasury Department without compliance with the civil-service requirements.
This order is issued upon the recommendation of the Secretary of the Treasury, who
states that Miss Roden is temporarily employed, with the concurrence of the Civil
Service Commission, in the office of the Auditor for the War Department, where her
services have proven most valuable as a calculating machine operator, and it is be-
lieved that her permanent appointment will be greatly in the interest of the service.
