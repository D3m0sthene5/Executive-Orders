March 29, 1916.
Mr. Charles H. Slack may be reinstated to the position of compositor at 50
cents per hour in the Government Printing Office without reference to the
period of his separation from the service. Mr. Slack was appointed in the
Government Printing Office December 3, 1901, and resigned September 23, 1904.
A certificate was requested for his reinstatement within the year of his eligi-
bility and was issued by this office, but it was returned unused by the Public
Printer February 27, 1906.
This order is issued on the recommendation of the Public Printer with the
concurrence of the Civil Service Commission because of Mr. Slack’s efficiency
while in the service, and because of the fact that since leaving the service he
has been continuously engaged in work that would maintain and add to that
efficiency.
