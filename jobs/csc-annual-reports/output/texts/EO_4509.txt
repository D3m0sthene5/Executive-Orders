September 15, 1926.
Mr. Ernest H. Hubbard served as office deputy United States marshal,
Indian Territory, from July 1, 1900, to November 17, 1907, and again as chief
deputy marshal, eastern district of Oklahoma, from April 15, 1908, until June
1, 1916, the position meanwhile having been classified by order of March 2,
1909, and excepted by act of October 22, 1913.
The classified status which Mr. Hubbard retained until his separation in
1916 is hereby restored to him in his present excepted position of chief deputy
marshal. This action is taken upon the recommendation of the Acting Attorney
General. The Civil Service Commission deems the order unobjectionable.
