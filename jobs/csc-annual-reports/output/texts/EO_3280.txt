June 10, 1920.
The heads of the executive departments and independent establishments are
directed to furnish the United States Civil Service Commission, at such times
and in such form and manner as it may prescribe, information to complete or
to supplement its records, to enable it to carry out the requirements of the retire-
ment act approved May 22, 1920, and to enable it to prepare and keep timely
statistical and other information which experience has shown to be of value to
the President, the Congress, executive officials, and the public.
