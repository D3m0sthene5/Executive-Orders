December 1, 1917,
The Postmaster General is hereby authorized, without regard to the require-
ments of the civil-service rules, to appoint as temporary carrier on a rural route,
during the absence of the regular carrier on military duty, the person upon
whom" the support of the dependents of the regular carrier devolves, the Post-
master General having ascertained that such person is competent and qualified
to perform the duties involved.
