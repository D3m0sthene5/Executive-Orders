March 31, 1925.
Mrs. C. W. Salter may be appointed keeper of the Turkey Point Light Station
without reference to the provisions of the civil service rules. Mrs Salter is left
with three children without means of support by the death of her husband,
Clarence W. Salter, for many years a keeper in the Lighthouse Service. This
order is issued upon the recommendation of the Assistant Secretary of Commerce.
The Civil Service Commission submits the order without prejudice but is without
authority to recommend its issuance.
Done for charity.
