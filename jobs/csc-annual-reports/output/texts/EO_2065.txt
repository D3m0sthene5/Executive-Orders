October 27, 1914.
Mrs. Margaret A. Hampton may be transferred from the position of clerk at $1,000
per annum in the Census Bureau, Department of Commerce, to the position of clerk at
$1,200 per annum in the Children’s Bureau, Department of Labor, without regard to
the requirements of the civil-service rules.
Mrs. Hampton was appointed temporary clerk in the Thirteenth Census on April 1,
1910, and served until July 1, 1912, when she received a permanent appointment as
clerk at $1,000 per annum through Executive order. She has rendered excellent
service with the Census Bureau, and her transfer to the Children’s Bureau is recom-
mended by the Secretary of Labor and the chief of the Children’s Bureau.
The Civil Service Commission does not concur in the recommendation.
