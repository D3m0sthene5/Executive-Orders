June 27, 1924
In consideration of the faithful, meritorious, and valuable services of Boat-
swain Arthur Dietrich, who was killed in line of duty at the U. S. Naval Air
Station, Anacostia, D. C., on March 3, 1924, after serving in the Navy contin-
uously since October 6, 1909, his widow, Mrs. Bessie Vivian Dietrich, may be
employed as messenger under the Navy Department without regard to the civil
service act or rules. This action is recommended by the Secretary of the Navy.
The Civil Service Commission does not concur in this recommendation.
Issued for charity.
