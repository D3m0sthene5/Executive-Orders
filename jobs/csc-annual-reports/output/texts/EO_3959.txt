February 16, 1924
Mr. Louis Marcks may be reinstated as a clerk or carrier in the post-office
service without regard to the time limitations of the civil-service rules. This
order is issued on recommendation of the Civil Service Commission with the
approval of the Postmaster General to remedy, as far as possible, the injustice
‘involved in his removal on November 4, 1915, from the position of carrier in
the St. Louis, Mo., post office, where he had served since 1887.
