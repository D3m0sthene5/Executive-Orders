December 1, 1925.
Mr. William A. Rogers may be reinstated as guard in the United States
penitentiary service without regard to the length of his separation from the
service.
This order is issued upon the recommendation of the Attorney General and
the warden of the United States penitentiary, Atlanta, Ga., who state that,
in view of the excellent service and extended experience of Mr. Rogers, his
reinstatement would be greatly in the interest of the service.
The Civil Service Commission concurs.
