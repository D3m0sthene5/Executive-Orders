May 16, 1921.
Mr. Albert A. Hamilton may be reinstated without regard to the year limi-
tation prescribed by the civil-service rules. Mr. Hamilton was a railway mail
clerk from April 27, 1889, to August 31, 1919, when he resigned. Application
for his reinstatement made within the year limit was not approved by the Post
Office Department because he was within 10 years of the retirement age for
railway mail clerks. The Postmaster General recommends the reinstatement
because of Mr. Hamilton’s good record, his long service, and the value of his
experience,
The Civil Service Commission reports that upon the facts as they appear.
by the entire record in this case, the postal service will probably be benefited
by the waiver of the year limitation. :
