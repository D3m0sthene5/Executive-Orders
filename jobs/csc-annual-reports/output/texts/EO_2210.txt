June 15, 1915.
The year limit upon eligibility is hereby waived to permit the reinstatement of
Mr. William R. Houston as a clerk in the Railway Mail Service.
Mr. Houston, who is a son of Gen. Sam Houston, governor of Tennessee, commander
in chief of the Texan army of independence, president of the republic of Texas, gover-
nor of the State of Texas, and United States Senator from that State, was employed
in the Railway Mail Service from July 20, 1882, to May 24, 1902, when he resigned
with a fair record.
The Postmaster General recommends and the Civil Service Commission offers no
objections to this order.
