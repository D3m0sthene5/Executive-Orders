November 23, 1925.
Mrs. Myrtle L. Clayton may be appointed as a printer's assistant in the
Bureau of Engraving and Printing without compliance with the provisions of
the civil-service rules.
This appointment is recommended by the Secretary of the Treasury in view
of the fact that Mrs. Clayton is the widow of Percy B. Clayton, who served
in the bureau as an electrician to the time of his death on October 24, 1925,
leaving Mrs. Clayton with two small children and without means of support.
As the Civil Service Commission can properly recommend waivers only when •
the procedure under the rules will not fully meet the needs of the service,
it is unable to concur.
For charity.
