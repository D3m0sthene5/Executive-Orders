June 17, 1918.
Mr. Y. Tsenshan Wang may be appointed a geologic aid in the Geological Survey
for three months, beginning July 1.
This appointment is authorized upon the request of the Chinese minister as a matter
of courtesy, and in accordance with the practice of the Geological Survey of maintain-
ing friendly relations with the scientists of other nations,
It is recommended by the Secretary of the Interior and the Director of the Geolog-
ical Survey.
Mr. Wang was employed last year in a similar capacity under Executive order
dated March 30, 1912.
<The Civil Service Commission joins with the Secretary of the Interior in recom-
mending the issuance of the order.
