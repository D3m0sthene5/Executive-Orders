February 2, 1926.
Mr. Hugh S. Hanna may be reinstated in the Department of Labor as editor
in chief, $3,800 per annum, Bureau of Labor Statistics, without regard to the
length of his separation from the service.
Mr. Hanna resigned from the position of expert with a classified status,
Bureau of Labor Statistics, on March 19, 1918, since which time he has been
engaged in work similar to that required in the position to be filled and has had
constant contact with the Bureau of Labor Statistics.
The Civil Service Commission recommends the reinstatement of Mr. Hanna
which is desired by the department as in the interests of the service.
