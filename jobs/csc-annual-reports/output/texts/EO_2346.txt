March 21, 1916.
Mrs. May G. Walker may be appointed to a clerical position in the classified
service of the Government without compliance with the requirements of the
Civil Service Commission. This order is issued on the recommendation of the
Secretary of the Treasury, who states that Mrs. Walker was employed with
the Federal reserve bank organization committee, and the records show that
she was graded as one of the best clerks on the staff. Mrs. Walker has also
had service in the Census Office.
