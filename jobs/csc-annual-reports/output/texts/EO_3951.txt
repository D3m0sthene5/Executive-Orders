January 30, 1924
Assistant inspectors of hulls and assistant inspectors of boilers, Steamboat
"Inspection Service, Department of Commerce, whose services were dispensed
with July 31, 1921, in response to a call of the President for economy in the
Federal service, and who were given to understand that they would receive
first consideration for suitable vacancies in similar positions, may be reinstated
to appropriate positions in the classified service without regard to the time
limitation prescribed in Rule IX of the Civil Service Rules.
The Civil Service Commission concurs with the Department of Commerce
in this recommendation.
