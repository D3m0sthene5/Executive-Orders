January 15, 1918.
During the continuance of the present war the Secretary of the Treasury
is authorized to make appointments to the various positions in the Bureau of
Engraving and Printing without regard to the requirements of the Civil Service
Commission. This order is issued on the recommendation of the Secretary of
the Treasury, who states that the department has experienced great difficulty
in securing efficient help for the Bureau of Engraving and Printing from the
Civil Service Commission and that this action will be greatly in the interest of
the service. This is purely an emergency measure and will be revoked as soon
as the necessity for it is past.
