November 9, 1914.
Mr. William W. Lemmond may be transferred after reinstatement in the Depart-
ment of Justice to the position of law clerk at $2,400 per annum in the office of the
Judge Advocate General, War Department, without reference to the requirement of
six months’ service immediately preceding transfer.
Mr. Lemmond was a clerk in the classified service of the War Department from
December 12, 1903, to February 25, 1907, when he was transferred to the Attorney
General's Office. May 16, 1911, he was promoted to the position of assistant attorney,
which he held until August 31, 1914, when he resigned. This order is issued upon
the recommendation of the Secretary of War, with the concurrence of the Civil Service
Commission, in view of the statement of the Judge Advocate General that he believes
Mr. Lemmond to be especially qualified for the position of law clerk by reason of his
education, training, and experience in legal work in the- Department of Justice.
