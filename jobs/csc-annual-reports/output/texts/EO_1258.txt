October 25, 1910.
Mr. W. E. S. Griswold, of New York, may be appointed to the position of Secretary
of the commission to investigate questions pertaining to the issuance of stocks and
bonds by railroad corporations authorized under section 16 of the act to create a
commerce court, approved on June 18, 1910. This order is issued with the concur-
rence of the Civil Service Commission, as the secretary is to be the administrative
head of such commission whose force is now being initially organized.
