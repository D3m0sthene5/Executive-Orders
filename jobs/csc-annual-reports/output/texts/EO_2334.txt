March 6, 1916.
Mr. Carl G. Lines may be appointed as electrician at $1,200 per annum in
the Bureau of the Census, Department of Commerce, without reference to the
civil-service rules. Mr. Lines was appointed as electrician at $4 per diem in
the Bureau of the Census on March 2, 1910, and served until January 10, 1912,
under the provisions of the Executive order of December 29, 1909. He was
reappointed April 23, 1914, by certification for temporary appointment by the
Civil Service Commission and has served continuously since that date. In
addition to performing the duties of an electrician in connection with the
bureau’s machines, Mr. Lines has frequently been called upon to perform the
work of a general mechanic, and his services in both respects have been highly
satisfactory. This order. is issued upon the recommendation of the Secretary
of Commerce, who states that in view of the valuable experience and training
which has been acquired by ‘Mr. Lines during his temporary employment in the
bureau he feels that an exception to the usual procedure is justified and ad-
visable. The Civil Service Commission does not concur in the recommendation.
