July 27, 1921.
The Executive order of May 10, 1921, prescribing examinations for the
appointment of presidential postmasters, is hereby amended by striking out in -
the second paragraph the words “ such vacancy ” and substituting therefor the
words ‘“ the date of examination.” As amended, the paragraph will read:
“No person who has passed his sixty-fifth birthday, or who has not actually
resided within the delivery of such office for two years next preceding the date
of examination shall be given the examination herein provided for.”
