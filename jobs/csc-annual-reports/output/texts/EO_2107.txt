December 18, 1914.
Schedule B of classified positions which may be filled upon noncompetitive exami-
nation is hereby amended by adding to Subdivision I, Interior Department, the fol-
lowing paragraph:
“6. One superintendent of irrigation in the Indian Service, who shall be compe-
tent to pass upon water rights.”’
The duties of this employee will be wholly legal in cases which heretofore have
been usually handled by attorneys in the Department of Justice. These duties
relate to the legal rights of the Indians to the use of water, and the employee must have
had wide experience in prosecuting cases of water rights before the courts and be
able to protect the rights of the Indians.
This amendment is recommended by the Secretary of the Interior, with the con-
currence of the Civil Service Commission.
