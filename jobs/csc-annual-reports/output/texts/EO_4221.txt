May 11, 1925.
Schedule B of the civil service rules, positions subject to noncompetitive
examination, is hereby amended by adding a third paragraph to Subdivision III,
Department of Commerce, to read as follows:
"3. Special agents employed in collecting cotton statistics."
Special agents so employed shall be continued in office, without examination,
so long as their services are efficient and their conduct satisfactory.
This order is recommended by the Secretary of Commerce and the Civil Service
Commission.
