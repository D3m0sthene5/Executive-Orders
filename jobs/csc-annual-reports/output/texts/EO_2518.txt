January 19, 191%.
Mr. Austin F. Hawes may be reinstated as extension specialist in forestry at a
salary of $2,760 per annum in the States Relations Service of the Department of
Agriculture without regard to the year limit of the reinstatement rule.
Mr. Hawes was appointed a forest assistant in 1903 through examination under the
civil-service rules, and served until 1906, when he became State forester of Con-
necticut. In 1909 he became State forester of Vermont and professor of forestry at
the University of Vermont. The Department of Agriculture states that it is believed
that his reinstatement would be in the interests of good administration. The Civil
Service Commission concurs in recommending this order.
