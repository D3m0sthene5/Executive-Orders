April 26, 1916.
Mrs. Grace Porter Hopkins may be appointed to a position in the United
States Employment Service of the Department of Labor without reference to
the civil-service rules. Her appointment is recommended by the Secretary of
Labor. It is stated that Mrs. Hopkins has the requisite qualifications for such
a position, and also has had such practical experience as would make her
services desirable. The Civil Service Commission does not concur in the
recommendation.
