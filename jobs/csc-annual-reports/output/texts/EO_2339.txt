March 10, 1916.
Mrs. Katherine F. Rogers may be reinstated in the classified service of the
Treasury Department without regard to the length of time she has been sepa-
rated therefrom. This order is issued on the recommendation of the Secretary
of the Treasury, who states that Mrs. Rogers served very efficiently in the
Treasury Department for 10 years, resigning her position in 1910, and that he
believes on account of her experience and her high efficiency her services will
be of real value to the department. Mrs. Rogers is the daughter of the late
Mr. James N. Fitzpatrick, for many years executive clerk in the Division of
Appointments of the department and later assistant chief of that division, and
one of the most efficient and valuable employees the department ever had. Mr.
Fitzpatrick has recently died, and the burden of the support of his widow and
herself falls mainly upon Mrs. Rogers. In view of the facts the Civil Service
Commission concurs in the recommendation for an Executive order.
