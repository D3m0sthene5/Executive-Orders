August 2, 1916.
The year limit upon eligibility for reinstatement is hereby waived to permit
the reinstatement of Ernest Szemelenyi as a clerk at $1,200 per annum in the
Patent Office. Mr. Szemelenyi served from 1881 to 1909 as a translator in the
Patent Office. The Commissioner of Patents, who has been personally cogni-
zant of the character of his work for 20 years, states that his acquaintance
with foreign languages and the practical arts and science combined gives him
a range of ability for handling certain business of the Patent Office which is
unique; that no one has ever been able to distribute foreign patents among
the different divisions as acceptably as Mr. Szemelenyi did while he was so
employed ; and that he desires to have the appointment authorized by Execu-
tive order because of the needs of the office and because it is impossible to get
anyone to do the work which Mr. Szemelenyi can do within the salary which the
office can pay. The Civil Service Commission concurs with the Secretary of
the Interior in recommending this Executive order.
