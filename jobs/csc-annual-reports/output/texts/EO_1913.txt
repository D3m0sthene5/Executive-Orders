April 13, 1914.
Dr. W. R. P. Thompson, of Houston, Tex., may be appointed to a position, in the
Public Health Service, to be stationed at some point within the United States, with-
out regard to the civil-service rules and regulations. ;
Dr. Thompson is a physician and surgeon of character, skill, learning, and expe-
rience. He was acting assistant surgeon in the Public Health Service, stationed at
foreign ports in Mexico from 1907 to November 1, 1913.
