October 16, 1913.
Mr. Sam W. Smiley may be reinstated as a clerk in the Railway Mail Service without
reference to the period which has elapsed since his separation.
Mr. Smiley was severely injured in a wreck in May, 1895, in the line of duty, which
necessitated his leaving the service. He applied for reinstatement as soon as he
recovered from his injuries but was not reinstated. Itappears that his present physical
condition is good.
The Civil Service Commission concurs with the Postmaster General in recommend-
ing this order.
