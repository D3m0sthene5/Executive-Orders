October 1, 1913.
Mr. Leroy E. Reed may be appointed to a competitive classified position in the
Department of Justice.
Mr. Reed has twice passed the stenographer and typewriter examination and held
a competitive classified position in the Department of Justice from July 9, 1910, to
February 1, 1912, when he accepted a position in the State Department regarded by
that department as not subject to the civil-service rules. He was subsequently given
a competitive classified status under an Executive order and on April 1, 1913, was
appointed confidential clerk to the Attorney General. This order is therefore issued
to restore Mr. Reed to a competitive classified status in the Department of Justice.
