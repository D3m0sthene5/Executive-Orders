May 16, 1921, :
The year limit upon eligibility for reinstatement is waived to permit the
continuance in the service of Mr. Matthew W. McCullough as carrier in the
rural delivery service, Post Office Department.
This order is issued on the recommendation of the Postmaster General, in
which the Civil Service Commission concurs, because of Mr. McCullough’s serv- -
ice as a regular rural carrier from April 1, 1901, to October 15, 1915, and his
faithful and efficient service as a temporary carrier since June 3, 1918, when :
he was appointed because there were no eligibles and has not been replaced
because no eligibles have been secured.
