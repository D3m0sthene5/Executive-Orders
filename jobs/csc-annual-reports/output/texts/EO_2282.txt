December 14, 1915.
Mrs. Margaretta G. H. Neale may be transferred from the position of special
agent in the Bureau of the Census to a clerical position in the Department of
Commerce without reference to the civil-service rules. This order is issued
upon recommendation of the Secretary of Commerce, who states that there has
existed in the Division of Publications since September 7, 1915, a vacancy which
the division has been trying to fill by selection from the editorial-clerk register
of the Civil Service Commission, and that of the 12 eligibles certified by the
commission, 11 have declined to accept appointment at the stipulated salary,
and that the papers of the other eligible, who made a very low rating, did not
indicate that he has had the necessary experience or possesses qualifications
which in the opinion of the division would make him a useful employee. The
secretary further states that Mrs. Neale was temporarily employed in a clerical
capacity in the Bureau of the Census during the Twelfth and Thirteenth
Decennial Census periods; that since February 12, 1915, she has been serving
as a special agent in connection with the census of manufactures; and that in
view of the efficiency and aptitude displayed by her in the performance of the
various duties assigned her, he believes that she is exceptionally capable and is
better qualified than would be an untrained eligible who might eventually be
obtained through civil-service channels for filling the vacancy in question.
