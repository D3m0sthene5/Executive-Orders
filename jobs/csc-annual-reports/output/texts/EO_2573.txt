April 3, 1917.
Mr. Gilbert J. Murray may be appointed Assistant to the Director of the Bureau of
War Risk Insurance in the Treasury Department without complaince with civil-
service rules. ein
This order is issued on the recommendation of the Secretary of the Treasury, who
states that the position of Assistant to the Director of the Bureau of War Risk Insur-
ance is a technical one; that there is no person in the Treasury Department qualified
to fill the position, and that Mr. Murray possesses the necessary qualifications, and
that his appointment will be in the interest of the service.
