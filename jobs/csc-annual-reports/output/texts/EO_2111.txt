December 28, 1914.
Civil service-Rule XI, paragraph 2, is hereby amended by adding a new clause (d)
to read as follows: :
“(d) No employee shall be promoted during probation except upon approval of
the commission previously obtained.”
In making certification from a register to fill a position, the salary of which is
materially lower than that usually paid persons appointed from the same register,
the commission stipulates that the person should not be promoted until after six
months’ service without its approval. Promotions during probation are infrequent,
and most of them made are small or to the next higher class and are therefore not
objectionable. However, occasionally a person appointed at the usual entrance
salary is immediately thereafter promoted to a much higher salary, for which his name
would not have been certified, because other eligibles are on the register with much
higher ratings. Such cases tend to bring disrepute upon the merit system, though
at times there are extenuating circumstances. such as previous service in the depart-
ment.
