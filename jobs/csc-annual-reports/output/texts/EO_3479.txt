June 7, 1921.
The year limit upon eligibility for reinstatement is hereby waived to permit
the reinstatement of William G. Kimberlin as carrier, Rural Delivery Service,
Post Office Department.
This order is issued upon the recommendation of the Postmaster General, and
concurred in by the Civil Service Commission.
Note.—The Postmaster General stated that Mr. Kimberlin served as rural
carrier at McCordsville, Ind., from August 1, 1902, to April 17, 1917, when he
was removed from the service; that he found upon examination that the charges
upon which Kimberlin was removed were trivial and in no manner susceptible
of construction justifying the action taken; that the commission itself recom: -
mended a suspension of but 10 days for the gravest of the charges, and believed -
that a grave injustice was done a faithful employee who had rendered efficient
service for nearly 15 years.
