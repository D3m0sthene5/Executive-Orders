April 17, 1926.
Capt. William T. Rigby, commissioner for the Vicksburg National Military
Park, is hereby excluded from the operation of the retirement act.
The national military parks on the battle fields of Gettysburg, Chickamauga,
Vicksburg, and Shiloh were formerly each in charge of three commissioners,
veterans of the Civil War. An act of August 24, 1912, provided that vacancies
occurring by death or resignation in the membership of the several commissions
be left unfilled.
Captain Rigby is the last surviving member of the last existing commission.
He desires to remain and the War Department desires to retain him. He is 84
years old and will have no successor.
The Civil Service Commission recommends this action.
