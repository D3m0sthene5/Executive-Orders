July 20, 1925.
Joseph Antenone may be reinstated as a laborer in the custodian service at
Portland, Oreg., without regard to the length of time he has been separated
from the service.
This order is issued on the recommendation of the Secretary of the Treasury,
who states that Mr. Antenone served as a laborer in the custodian service at
Portland, Oreg., from February 3, 1906, to December 31, 1918, and again tempo-
rarily from January 1, 1921, to April 30, 1925. In view of his satisfactory record,
it is believed his reinstatement would be in the interest of the service.
The present custodian employed Mr. Antenone temporarily from 1921 to 1925
because he believed he was unjustly deprived of his permanent position in 1918.
The Civil Service Commission concurs in recommending this order.
