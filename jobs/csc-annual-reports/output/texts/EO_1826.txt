September 17, 1913.
Miss Emily W. Matter, of the District of Columbia, now temporarily employed in
the same position through certification by the Civil Service Commission, may be
appointed permanently to the position of clerk at $720 per annum in the Coast and
Geodetic Survey. :
The department in recommending this action stated that Miss Matter had demon-
strated her fitness during temporary appointment, that the department had been
unable to make selection from six certifications because of declinations and of the fact
that many of the eligibles were found unsuitable.
