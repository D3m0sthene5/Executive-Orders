August 16, 1917.
Schedule A, Subdivision IX, section 1, of positions excepted from examination
under the civil service rules in the Department of Agriculture, is hereby amended by
the addition of a new clause (g) as follows:
“(g) Agents employed in the field, to be known as emergency demonstration agents,
whose a are made for the same purpose and in the same manner as those
appointed under clause (a), except that they are not cooperatively financed, appoint-
ments of such agents to terminate not later than the beginning of the fiscal year follow-
ing the termination of the present war between the United States and Germany.”
The Department of Agriculture, through the States Relations Service, planned to
make about 4,000 emergency appointments of county agents, home demonstration
agents, agents of boys’ and girls’ clubs, and agents for city work under the recently
enacted food legislation. It estimated that 75 per cent of these appointments would
come within the provisions of clause (a) of section 1, of Subdivision IX, of Schedule A,
which excepts from examination “agents employed in field positions the work of
which is financed jointly by the department and cooperating persons or organiza-
tions outside the Federal service.”” The remainder of these appointments would
not come within the provisions of the above exception, since they will not be in the
beginning cooperatively financed. The department proposed to obtain cooperation
as rapidly as possible. The department could not forecast the number of cases in
which it will be able ultimately to obtain cooperation or for what length of time these
Sppoinimenss will be made. The duties of the appointees will be the same as those
of agents appointed under clause (a). The State and municipal authorities will be
consulted in their selection and will have joint direction of their activities. The
sole difference between them and the agents under clause (a) is that they will not be
cooperatively financed.
