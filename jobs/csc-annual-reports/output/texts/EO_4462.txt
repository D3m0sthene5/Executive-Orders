June 25, 1926.
The time limit on reinstatement under the civil-service rules is hereby waived
to permit the reinstatement of Mrs. Ursula B. Lees to a clerical position in the
Department of Justice.
Mrs. Lees had been a classified employee for approximately 13 years at
the time of her resignation from the Treasury Department on May 19, 1911.
Since January 16, 1920, she has been employed in a position excepted from
examination in the Department of Justice. She is a graduate of Cornell Uni-
versity with post graduate library work at the Iowa State University. Her
record with the Department of Justice is satisfactory and the character of her
work is excellent.
The Civil Service Commission does not recommend this order.
Done for charity.
