January 11, 1924
The provisions of the Executive order of January 17, 1873, prohibiting
Federal employees from holding office under any State, territorial, or municipal
overnment, are hereby waived to permit C. C. Crabbe, attorney general of the
tate of Ohio, to hold the position of Special Assistant to the Attorney General
of the United States, in view of the mutual advantages thereby secured to the
Government of the United States and the State of Ohio. This order is recom-
mended by the Attorney General of the United States.
