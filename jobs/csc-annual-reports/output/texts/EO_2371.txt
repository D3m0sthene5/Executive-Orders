April 28, 1916.
Miss Ella Lee, of Mississippi, may be appointed to a clerical position in the
Postal Service, with a view to her appointment in the post office at Jackson,
Miss., without reference to the requirements of the civil-service rules. Miss
Lee is the daughter of the late Robert C. Lee, who for many years was United
States district attorney for the southern district of Mississippi, and whose
death about a year ago, together with the recent death of her brother, renders
it necessary for Miss Lee to secure employment in order to provide a livelihood
for herself and mother. It is stated that Miss Lee is a graduate of the high
school of Jackson and well qualified to fill a clerical position in the Postal Serv-
ice, and that the postmaster will appoint her to such a position if authorized to
do so.
