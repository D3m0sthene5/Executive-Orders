July 19, 1916.
Mr, Arthur E. Wilson may be permanently appointed to a clerical position in
the office of the Surgeon General, Public Health Service, without reference to
civil-service rules. This order is issued on the recommendation of the Secretary
of the Treasury, who states that Mr. Wilson was appointed a temporary clerk
at $1,200 per annum on January 20, 1916, in the Surgeon General's office, to fill
a vacancy caused by the resignation of a regular employee on account of illness;
that he has given complete satisfaction and has exhibited special capacity for
the particular duties assigned to him, which renders his services of peculiar
value; and that it is believed Mr. Wilson’s permanent appointment will be in the
interest of the service. The Civil Service Commission does not concur in the
recommendation.
