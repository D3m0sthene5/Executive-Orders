August 6, 1924
The Executive order of January 17, 1873, is further modified as follows:
“Officers and employees of the United States Veterans’ Bureau serving in a
medical capacity and on a part-time basis may, with the consent of the Director
of the United States Veterans’ Bureau, hold State, county, or municipal positions
in which they are employed in a medical capacity.
“Officers and employees of the United States Veterans’ Bureau may, with the
consent of the Director of the United States Veterans’ Bureau, accept appoint-
ments under State, county, or municipal authority, as deputy sheriffs.”
