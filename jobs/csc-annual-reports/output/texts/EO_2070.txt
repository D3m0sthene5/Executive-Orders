October 29, 1914.
Edward A. Gisburne may be appointed to a classified position without reference to
his physical condition after recommendation by some appointing officer and such
noncompetitive examination as the Civil Service Commission may require.
Mr. Gisburne was a messenger boy in the classified service of the Navy Department
from September 14, 1908, to February 7,1910, when he resigned to enlist in the Navy.
He was honorably discharged from the Navy on August 29, 1914, by reason of disability
resulting from wounds incurred in the line of duty, and was awarded a medal of honor
for attempting to save a comrade while wounded himself. Under the law heis entitled
to preference in original appointment if found, upon examination, to possess the
required business capacity.
This action is taken upon the request of the postmaster at Boston, Mass., and the
Executive order is recommended by the Civil Service Commission.
