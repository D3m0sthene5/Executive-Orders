March 25, 1915.
The provisions of the Executive order of January 17, 1873, prohibiting Federal
employees from holding office under any State, Territorial, or municipal g government,
are hereby waived to permit Mark Eisner, who has been appointed collector of internal
revenue for the third district of the State of New York, to actin the capacity of delegate
to the constitutional convention of the State of New York, he having been duly elected
thereto before his appointment as collector. Mr. Eisner is a man who would be of
great value in the constitutional convention, and his services in the convention will
not interfere with his work as collector of internal revenue. This order is recom-
mended by the Acting Secretary of the Treasury.
