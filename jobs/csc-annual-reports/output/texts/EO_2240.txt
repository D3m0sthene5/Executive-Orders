August 25, 1915.
Mr. C. Virgil Hinkle may be appointed to a competitive classified position in the
postal service without reference to the civil-service rules.
Mr. Hinkle was employed as a clerk in the Conway, Ark., post office from July 1,
1898, to June 28, 1913, when he was removed for insubordination, mistreatment of
registered mail, and failure to comply with request of post-office inspectors to make
good losses of registered mail for which, from circumstantial evidence, it was believed
he wasresponsible. The assistant postmaster in office at the time of the embezzlement
has confessed to rifling the mail in question.
The Conway post office became classified on December 1, 1910, but Mr. Hinkle was
not given a competitive classified status.
This order is recommended by the Postmaster General, with the concurrence of the
Civil Service Commission.
