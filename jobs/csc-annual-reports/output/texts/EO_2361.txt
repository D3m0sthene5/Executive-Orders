April 13, 1916.
Mr. G. Howard Ritter may be appointed to a position in the classified service
without reference to the civil-service rules. Mr. Ritter was appointed clerk
to the Commissioner of Fisheries at $1,600 per annum on June 2, 1913, and
resigned April 15, 1915. He has also been temporarily employed in the Bureau
of the Census since that time. The appointment of Mr. Ritter to a position in
the classified service is recommended by Dr. E. L. Jones, superintendent of the
Coast and Geodetic Survey. The Civil Service Commission does not concur in
the recommendation.
