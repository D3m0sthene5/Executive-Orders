Positions of guard in the office of the superintendent, State, War, and Navy Depart»
ment Buildings, may, during the remainder of the fiscal year 1923 and during the
fiscal year 1924, be filled by the superintendent without compliance with the civil-
service law and rules when filled by suitably qualified retired enlisted men from the
Army, Navy, or Marine Corps, or retired firemen or policemen of the District of
Columbia Government who have been retired therefrom under honorable conditions.
The excessive turnover in the guard force, due to inadequate salaries under exist-
ing conditions, and the desirability of assigning to the temporary frame buildings
as guards men who have had training and experience in the Fire Department indicate
the desirability of the exception proposed. It is anticipated that the reclassifica-
Hon fel; which becomes effective oo 1, 1924, will take care of the situation after
at date.
The Civil Service Commission concurs with the Secretary of State and the Acting
Secretaries of War and Navy in recommending this order.
