July 25, 1914.
Clause (c) of section 1 of Rule VII is hereby amended by adding the following
sentence:
A probationer separated from the service without delinquency or misconduct may
be restored to the register of eligibles in the discretion of the commission for the
remainder of his period of eligibility.
The practice indicated has been in effect for more than 15 years and for a number
of years was included in the civil service rules.
