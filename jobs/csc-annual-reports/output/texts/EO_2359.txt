April 12, 1916.
Mrs. Mary E. Cline may be reinstated as an operative in the Bureau of
Engraving and Printing without regard to the length of time she has been
separated from the service. This order is issued on the recommendation of the
Acting Secretary of the Treasury, who states that Mrs. Cline was employed
in the bureau from 1894 to 1901, and again temporarily in the year 1908, and
that she rendered efficient service under these appointments. The Civil Service
Commission does not concur in the recommendation.
