February 10, 1916.
Mrs. Ethel Robards may be appointed to a clerical position in the Navy De-
partment without regard to the requirement of examination under the ecivil-
service rules. Mrs. Robards is the widow of Capt. Frank F. Robards, United
States Marine Corps, who was killed on December 16, 1915, in the line of
duty at Port au Prince, Haiti, by his horse stumbling and throwing him
violently to the ground. Capt. Robards served throughout the Spanish-Ameri-
can War in the Second Texas Volunteer Infantry. He was appointed second
lieutenant in the United States Marine Corps July 23, 1900; promoted to first
lieutenant March 3, 1903, and to captain May 13, 1908. By his death Mrs.
Robards was left with a child 6 years old and without means of support except
her widow’s pension of $20 a month. In view of the fact that Capt. Robards
lost his life while on foreign service and in the line of duty, the commission
concurs with the Secretary of the Navy in recommending an Executive order
to permit her appointment.
