February 28, 1916.
Mrs. Annie K. Campbell, a special agent in the Bureau of the Census, may
be appointed without examination under the civil-service rules to act as private
secretary to the Director of the Census. The Secretary of Commerce states
that Mrs. Campbell has had 20 years of practical business experience, is an
accomplished stenographer and typewriter, and a trained correspondent, and
that her experience has covered both commercial work and the technical side
of manufacturing in both domestic and foreign trade. She has been serving
since April 5, 1915, as a special agent in connection with the census of manu-
factures, and has had experience in that work both in the field and at the central
office. In securing her permanent services the Secretary of Commerce states
that he is sure the department will benefit by her very unusual experience and
by her exceptional ability. In view of these facts and the exception of private
secretaries allowed by the civil-service rules to certain other heads of bureaus,
the commission concurs with the Secretary of Commerce in recommending
the issuance of this order.
