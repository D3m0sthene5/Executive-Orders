June 20, 1917.
Mr. John J. Crowley may be appointed Assistant to the Director of the Bureau of
War-Risk Insurance in the Treasury Department, without regard to civil-service
requirements. :
This order is issued on the recommendation of the Secretary of the Treasury, who
states that Mr. Crowley is a life and accident insurance expert and that his services
are necessary in connection with the new branch of the Bureau of War-Risk Insurance
which has to do with the insurance of masters, officers, and crews of American mer-
chant vessels against loss of life or personal injury by the risks of war.
