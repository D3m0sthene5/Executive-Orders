February 1, 1915.
Paragraph 5, of Subdivision I, of Schedule A, of classified positions excepted from
examination under Rule II, clause 3, is hereby amended to read as follows:
“5. Chinese, Japanese, and Hindu interpreters.’
Chinese and Japanese interpreters are now excepted and this amendment excepts
Hindu interpreters, much the same reasons for exemption applying to all these posi-
tions.
This order is recommended by the Department of Labor and the Civil Service
Commission.
