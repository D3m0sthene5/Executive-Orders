November 11, 1914.
Horatio Whitted may he appointed to an appropriate position in the classified
service without regard to the civil-service rules. Mr. Whitted was appointed as a
la’ orer at $660 per annum on May 9, 1901, and assigned to the General Land Office,
where he still is employed. It appears that, although his principal duties were of the
grade performed by classified employees, his name was erroneously omitted from the
list of such employees who were classified by the Executive orders of January 12, 1905,
and March 30, 1905, and this order is issued on the recommendation of the Acting
Secretary of the Interior to correct the error. The Civil Service Commission does
not concur in this recommendation.
