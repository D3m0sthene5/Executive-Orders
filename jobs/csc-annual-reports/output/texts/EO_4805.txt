February 10, 1928.
There are hereby excluded from the operation of the retirement act of July 3,
1926, because of their intermittent service on a piece-work basis, garment
makers (bundle hands) employed by the naval clothing factory, naval supply
depot, Brooklyn, N. Y. No record is kept of the hours of labor performed by
these employees and their service is intermittent and uncertain in tenure.
This order is recommended by the Navy Department and the Civil Service
Commission.
