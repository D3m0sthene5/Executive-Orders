November 21, 1914.
The two sons of Hon. Julian Uribe Uribe, first vice president of the Colombian
House of Representatives, may be employed on irrigation projects in the United
States without regard to the provisions of the civil-service rules, for the purpose of
learning through such employment the theory and practice of irrigation with a view
ultimately to introducing this branch of science into the Colombian Republic.
This order is issued in accordance with the principles of international comity and
is recommended by the Acting Secretary of the Department of the Interior and by
the Secretary of State. The Civil Service Commission concurs with the Department
of the Interior and the Department of State in recommending the issuance of the order.
