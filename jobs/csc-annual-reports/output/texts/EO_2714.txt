September 27, 1917.
Mr. Nathan Comstock may be reinstated in the Patent Office as an examiner without
regard to the year limitation upon reinstatement contained in Civil Service Rule IX.
Mr. Comstock entered the service as fourth assistant examiner through appropriate
competitive examination on August 15, 1904, and resigned on October 26, 1915, since
when he has been engaged in patent business. On account of the demand for patent
examiners and the long experience of Mr. Comstock the Commission in accordance
with its practice in similar cases joins with the Secretary of the Interior in recom-
