August 13, 191%.
Robert Gaines, of Raleigh, N. C., may be appointed to the position of messenger
in ihe Navy Department at $840 per annum, without reference to the civil-service
rules. :
This order is issued upon the recommendation of the Secretary of the Navy, who
states that he has recently lost his personal messenger on account of his having received
a clerical appointment in the War Department, and that it is necessary, especially
at this time, that the position be filled by a person of known integrity, and that he
possesses somewhat higher qualifications than those of a mere messenger. He states
that Robert Gaines is personally known to him as a man who can fill the requisites of
the position.
