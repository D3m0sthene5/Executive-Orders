September 8, 1915.
Mr. Clifford B. Potter may be reinstated in the postal service without reference to
the length of his separation from the service.
Mr. Potter was appointed a substitute carrier in the Jersey City, N. J., post office
on September 15, 1896, through examination. On January 11, 1900, he was made a
regular carrier at $600 and was promoted four times, reaching $1,200, the highest grade,
onJuly 1,1908. Heresigned on March 31,1912. The Postmaster General recommends
the issuance of this order, as it appears that Mr. Potter's resignation was tendered
because his application for leave on account of illness was refused, which request, in
the judgment of the Postmaster General’s office, should have been granted. The
Civil Service Commission does not recommend the issuance of an order authorizing
the reinstatement of Mr. Potter.
