November 26, 1918.
Mrs. Clara E. Werres may be appointed to the position of minor clerk at $900 per
annum in the Government Printing Office without reference to the civil-service
rules. This order is issued upon the recommendation of the Public Printer, who
states that Mrs. Werres is the widow of John P. Werres, late an employee in the
Government Printing Office; that she has been employed for several months in said
office as a skilled laborer, being detailed to minor clerical work in the accounting
division; and that her services have been satisfactory in every particular.
