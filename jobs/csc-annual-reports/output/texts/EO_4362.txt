December 28, 1925.
Mrs. James E. Maynard may be appointed as a skilled laborer in the Govern-
ment Printing Office without regard to the provisions of the civil-service rules.
Mr. James E. Maynard, her husband, a Spanish war veteran, after faithfu1.
and efficient service in the Government Printing Office from 1900 until Novem-
ber, 1925, was retired for total disability. He is now in a hospital and Mrs.
Maynard is in urgent need of employment for the support of herself and
11-year-old son.
Done for charity.
