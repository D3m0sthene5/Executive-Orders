May 2, 1925.
Mrs. Bessie L. Dudley may be appointed in the classified service of the Treas-
ury Department without regard to her standing on the eligible register. This
order is issued upon the recommendation of the Secretary of the Treasury, who
states that she is the daughter of a former officer in the United States Revenue
Cutter Service and a widow with a family of small children to support.
The Civil Service Commission submits the order without prejudice but is
without authority to recommend its issuance.
Done for charity.
