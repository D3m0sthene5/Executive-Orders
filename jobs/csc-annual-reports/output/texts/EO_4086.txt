October 14, 1924.
Candidates for appointment to the diplomatic or consular branch of the ForeignService, who were designated for examination and had already taken part ofthe examination prior to the issue of the Executive order of June 7, 1924, pre-scribing rules and regulations for administering the Foreign Service on an inter-changeable basis, may take the remainder of the examination and if successfulbe certified as eligible for appointment regardless of the fact that at the time oftheir completion of their examination they may have attained the maximum agelimit of 35 years established by paragraph 17 of the order.
