December 18, 1915.
Miss Harriet M. Sweet may be appointed to a clerical position on the per-
manent roll of the Treasury Department without compliance with the require-
ments of the civil-service rules. This order is issued on the recommendation of
the Secretary of the Treasury, who states that Miss Sweet, during the Pan
American Financial Conference, rendered exceptional service to the oflice of the
Secretary General of that conference; that her familiarity with the Latin-
American correspondence and the details of the office have made her services of
great value; that the place could not be adequately filled by selection from the
register of eligibles; and that without her services the work of that office
would be severely handicapped. In view of the representation of the Secretary
of the Treasury the Civil Service Commission concurs in his recommendation
for an Executive order.
