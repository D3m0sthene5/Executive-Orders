December 24, 1924.
Mrs. Effie A. Deane may be appointed to a position in the Government serv-ice without compliance with the civil-service rules.
Mrs. Deane is the widow of William C. Deane, who died in September, 1924,after a service of 28 years in the Bureau of Engraving and Printing.
This order is issued on the recommendation of the Secretary of the Treasuryas a matter of executive benevolence.
The Civil Service Commission submits the case without prejudice, statingthat it is without jurisdiction.
For charity.
