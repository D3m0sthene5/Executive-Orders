May 29, 1918.
Persons to be employed in Canada by the Ordnance Department, United
States Army, may be appointed without regard to registers of eligibles when in
the opinion of the Civil Service Commission such action is necessary. This
order is recommended by the Acting Chief of Ordnance with the concurrence
of the Civil Service Commission, and it extends to all appeintments under the
Ordnance Department in Canada the provisions of the order of December 5,
1917, which was limited in its application to positions below the grade of in-
speetor. The exemption is deemed advisable in view of the uncertain tenure of
the positions and of the difficulty of administering United States civil-service
laws in another country. .
