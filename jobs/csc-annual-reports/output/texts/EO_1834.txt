October 1, 1913.
Mrs. Mary D. Hanna may be appointed to a clerkship in the classified service subject
to such tests of fitness as the Civil Service Commission may require.
Mrs. Hanna is the widow of Capt. Guy B. G. Hanna, Coast Artillery, who met his
death while in the line of duty, by the premature explosion of a service charge during
night target practice. In view of the tragic circumstances of the case and the fact that
it 18 now necessary for Mrs. Hanna to earn her own livelihood the Secretary of War
recommended the issuance of an Executive order waiving the requirement of exami-
nation under the civil-service rules to permit her appointment. The Civil Service
Commission recommended that the appointment be permitted, subject to appropriate
tests of fitness,
