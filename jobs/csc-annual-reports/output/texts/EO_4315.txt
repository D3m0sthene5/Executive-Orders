September 29, 1925.
Mrs. Belle M. Kerr may be appointed to a clerical position in the Postal
Service at Washington, D. C., without regard to the provisions of the civil-
service rules. This order is issued upon the recommendation of the Postmaster
General. The Civil Service Commission can not concur, as it is limited in its
recommendations to those required by the interests of the public service.
Mrs. Kerr is a widow of James S. Kerr, who served for 20 years in the
Washington, D. C., post office and whom the Postmaster General states was a
very faithful and earnest employee. He left two of his own children and an
adopted child, and the widow found it necessary to work in order to support
herself and the children.
Mrs. Kerr is believed to be fully qualified for post-office clerk.
Done for charity.
