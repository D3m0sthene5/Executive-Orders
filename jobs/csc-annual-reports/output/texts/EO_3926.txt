November 17, 1923
Mrs. Blanche N. Bailey may be appointed to a clerical position in the Depart-
ment of Agriculture without examination under the civil-service rules.
Mrs. Bailey is the widow of Mr. Robert Victor Bailey who entered the depart-
ment through the stenography and typewriting examination on January 4, 1902,
and by many promotions, due to faithful and efficient service, reached the
osition of assistant to chief of bureau. He died on October 30, 1923, leaving
rs. Bailey and two children destitute. Granted as a matter of charity.
