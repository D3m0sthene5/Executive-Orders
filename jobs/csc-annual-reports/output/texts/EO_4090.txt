October 21, 1924.
Mrs. Emma J. Smith may be appointed to a clerical position in the classified
service of the Treasury Department without compliance with the requirements
of the civil service act and rules.
This order is issued on the recommendation of the Secretary of the Treasury,
who states that Mrs. Smith was employed on what is known as a contract basis in
the distribution unit of the Treasury Department for almost seven years and was
an efficient employee. The Civil Service Commission is unable to concur in this
recommendation.
Done for charity.
