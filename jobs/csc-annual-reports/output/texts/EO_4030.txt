June 19, 1924
The provisions of the acts of June 22, 1906, and October 6, 1917, which require
an employee to have served for a term of three years in an executive department
or independent establishment at Washington, D. C., before transfer to another
department or establishment, having been repealed by the classification act of
March 4, 1923, the similar provision in the civil-service rules governing transfer
is hereby revoked.
Employees will be permitted to remain in the positions to which they have
been allocated in accordance with the classification aet of 1923 and receive the
compensation attaching to such allocations, although contrary to existing provi-
sions of the civil-service rules, but shall not thereby be given any different status
for promotion or transfer than they had acquired under the civil-service rules
prior to such allocation.
