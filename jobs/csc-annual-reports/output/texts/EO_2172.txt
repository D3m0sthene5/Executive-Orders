April 17, 1915.
Samuel Z. C. Westerfield, of Nebraska, now a messenger with the British Arbitra-
tion Claims Commission, may be appointed without examination to an assistant
messengership in the Department of State. The position now filled by Mr. Wester-
field is abolished by legislation effective July 1, and because of his efficiency it is
deemed in the interest of the public service to continue him in the employ of the
Department of State.
