February 21, 1917.
Schedule A, excepting from examination certain positions under the civil-service
rules, is hereby amended by striking out paragraph 4 of Subdivision III, Treasury
Department, which reads as follows:
‘4. Storekeepers and gaugers whose compensation does not exceed $3 per diem
when actually employed and whose aggregate compensation shall not exceed $500
per annum.
“This exception from the requirement of examination shall not apply to the fifth
internal-revenue district of North Carolina.”
This order was recommended by the commission in view of the decision of the
Treasury Department to make no further appointments under this provision of the
rules. The department’s reasons for this action are shown in the following quotation
from Treasury Decision 2438:
“It has been brought to the attention of this office that a too fine division of duties
imposed upon internal-revenue officers, such as storekeepers, storekeeper-gaugers,
etc., assigned to distilleries has resulted in limiting the work performed by said
officers and for that reason an excessive number is frequently assigned to duty, whereas
with a notice of assignment of a broader character it would appear that a less number
of officers in many cases could easily perform all necessary services under the direction
of collectors.
“With a view to correcting this condition the Secretary of the Treasury will be
requested to change the designations of all officers now in commission as storekeepers
or as gaugers to storekeeper-gaugers. When this action is taken and new commissions
are issued, collectors will be notified and the officers will be required to furnish bonds
as storekeeper-gaugers. In the event that any officers refuse to file new bonds report
should be made to that effect. No officer who refuses to qualify by giving bond under
the changed designation will be assigned to duty, and collectors will not recommend
such assignments after opportunity has been afforded for such qualifications.
‘After the receipt hereof collectors will not recommend for assignment storekeeper-
gaugers appointed under civil-service rule 2, section 3, Schedule A, and hereafter
no more storekeeper-gaugers will be appointed under said rule.”
