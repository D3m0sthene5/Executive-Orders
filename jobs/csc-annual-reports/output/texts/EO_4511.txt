September 20, 1926.
Thomas Rogers may be appointed to a clerical position in the Postal Service
without regard to the civil-service rules.
Mr. Rogers, a full-blooded Ankara Indian, attended for six years the Indian
State Normal Training School at Santee, Nebr. ; enlisted in a North Dakota
company; went to France; was many times cited for bravery; took an impor-
tant part in scouting, capturing at night bare-handed and alone many sentinels
who were taken back to the American camp for questioning; and was honorably
discharged July 5, 1919.
The Acting Postmaster General recommends the appointment of Mr. Rogers
under an Executive order. As the Civil Service Commission can properly rec-
ommend a waiver only when the procedure under the rules will not fully meet
the needs of the service, it is unable to concur.
