April 23, 1915.
Fred Gilmore, of the District of Columbia, and Robert Taylor, of the District of
Columbia, unclassified laborers at $660 per annum in the Post Office Department,
may be appointed classified laborers at $660 per annum in the same department
without reference to civil-service rules.
The above order was issued on request of the Postmaster General, who stated
that these men were highly desirable as messengers, entirely trustworthy, and had
rendered excellent service, having been directly connected with the office of the
Postmaster General since the beginning of the present administration.
