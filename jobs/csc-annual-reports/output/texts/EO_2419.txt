July 14, 1916.
Mrs. Olivia Blanchard may be appointed to a position in the classified service
under the Department of Labor without reference to the civil-service rules. This
order is issued upon the recommendation of the Secretary of Labor, who states
that the department contemplates the establishment of a branch of the United
States Employment Service in the city of New Orleans, La., and that it is neces-
sary that a woman employee be assigned thereto to attend to applications of
women for employment or help, and it is believed that a woman as favorably
known in New Orleans as Mrs. Blanchard is would possess advantages calcu-
lated to promote the interests of the new employment service, and that her
acquaintance would make her services desirable in the labors of woman’s and
girl’s division of said service involving vocational direction as to industrial
training of girls. The Civil Service Commission does not concur in the recom-
mendation.
