February 14, 1917. :
Mrs. Nora A. Fennell may be reinstated as an operative in the Bureau of Engraving
and Printing without regard to the length of time she has been separated from the
service. Mrs. Fennell served as a printer’s assistant and operative in the Bureau of
Engraving and Printing continuously from March 12, 1900, to April 16, 1903, when
she resigned“to be married. It appears from the statement of the Secretary of the
Treasury that Mrs. Fennell is a widow with three children dependent upon her for
support, and that she is still a young woman in excellent health and capable of ren-
dering in the future, as she rendered in the past, efficient service. The register of
eligibles for original appointment to the position of printer’s assistant is practically
exhausted, for which reason and in view of the other facts above an the com-
mission, concurs with the Secretary of the Treasury in a recommendation for an Execu-
' tive order. :
