May 7, 1913.
Mr. Romus F. Stuart, of Indiana, who entered the classified service by virtue of a
competitive examination as post-office inspector, may be reinstated without regard to
the year limitation as prescribed by the civil-service rules.
This order is issued on the recommendation of the Postmaster General, in view of the
fact that his separation from the service was without any fault, delinquency, or mis-
conduct on his part whatsoever.
