March 26, 1924
Mrs. Florence R. Dowling, widow of Chief Boatswain James Dowling, who
served 42 years in the United States Navy with an excellent record and who
died recently, leaving Mrs. Dowling with three daughters dependent upon her
for support, may be appointed to any clerical or stenographic position under
the Navy Department which she is qualified to fill without regard to civil-service
rules and regulations. This action is recommended by the Acting Secretary of
the Navy. The Civil Service Commission does not concur.
Done for charity.
