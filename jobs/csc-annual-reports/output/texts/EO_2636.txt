June 12, 1917.
Mrs. E. Anna Draper and Mrs. Katherine Gaines may be appointed as clerks in
the Treasury Department for service in the Bureau of War Risk Insurance, without
regard to civil-service requirements.
This order is issued on the recommendation of the Secretary of the Treasury, who
states that, owing to the technical nature of the work of the Bureau of War-Risk
Insurance, there is need for especially trained clerks, and that Mrs. Draper and Mrs.
Gaines have the necessary qualifications to fill the positions to which it is desired to
appoint them.
