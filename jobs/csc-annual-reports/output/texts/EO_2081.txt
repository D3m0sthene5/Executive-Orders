November 11, 1914.
Mrs. Minnie D. Middleton, of the District of Columbia, who served as a clerk in the
War Department for a period of 12 years, may be reinstated in the classified
service and appointed to a clerkship at $900 in the Department of State, notwithstand-
ing the lapse of more than one year since her separation from the service. Mrs.
Middleton’s service in the War Department was faithful and efficient. She is at
present employed upon the temporary force of the State Department dealing with
the relief of American citizens abroad, and this order is issued upon the recommenda-
tion of the Secretary of State, who considers it in the interest of the public service.
The Civil Service Commission does not concur in this recommendation.
