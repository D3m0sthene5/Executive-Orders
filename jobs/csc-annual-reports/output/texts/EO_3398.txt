February 2, 1921.
Schedule A, Subdivision III, paragraph 9, of positions excepted from the re-
quirement of examination of the civil-service rules is hereby amended by strik-
ing out the words, “And one examiner of tea.” As amended the paragraph will
read as follows:
“9. One examiner of tobacco in the customs service at the port of Chicago.”
In line with the action of the President in connection with fourth-class post-
masters, in 1913, the present incumbent of the position of tea examiner at
Chicago does not by this order acquire a competitive classified status.
