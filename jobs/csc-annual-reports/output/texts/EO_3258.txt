April 13, 1920.
The veteran preference statutes shall apply in the selection of persons for ap-
pointment as postmaster at offices of the first, second, and third class. When
the highest eligible certified to the Postmaster General by the Civil Service Com-
mission is not a veteran, but a veteran is among those certified as eligible, the
Postmaster General may submit to the President for nomination the name of
either the highest eligible or the veteran obtaining the highest eligible rating
as the best interests of the service may require.
