May 5, 1923.
Mr. Henry T. Ellington may be reinstated to the position of clerk in the Railway
Mail Service without regard to the length of his separation from the service.
Mr. Ellington was removed from the position of clerk in the Railway Mail Service
on May 17, 1916, charged with embezzlement. The Postmaster General states that
he was tried and acquitted of the charge, that a most careful investigation has con-
vinced him that Mr. Ellington’s removal was an injustice and that he was entirely
innocent of the charges made against him, and that his reinstatement by Executive
order would serve the public interest better than the appointment of a new clerk.
The Civil Service Commission concurs in recommending this order.
