December 17, 1917.
Mr. O. C. Husting, of Mayville, Wis., may be appointed to an appropriate
position in the competitive classified service without examination,
This action is based upon the statement of the Secretary of the Treasury
that his services and qualifications are needed, in connection with the adminis-
tration of the customs law, in the office of the Collector of Customs for the
thirty-seventh collection district.
