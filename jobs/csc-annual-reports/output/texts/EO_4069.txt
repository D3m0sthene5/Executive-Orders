August 29, 1924
Mrs. Bertha E. Barrick may be appointed a clerk in the Treasury Department
without complying with civil-service requirements.
This order is issued on the recommendation of the Acting Secretary of the
Treasury, who states that Mrs. Barrick is the widow of Henry L. Barrick, who
died on June 6, 1924, after many years of faithful and efficient service in the
Treasury and who at the time of his death was assistant director of customs.
Mrs. Barrick was at one time employed as a clerk in the statistical division
9 ihe customs bureau at Manila, Philippine Islands, but has lost her civil-service
status.
The Civil Service Commission does not concur in this recommendation.
For charity.
