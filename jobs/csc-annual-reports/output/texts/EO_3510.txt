July 5, 1921.
Mrs. Margaret I. Paulsen, who was employed as a skilled laborer in the
Government Printing Office from October 26, 1918, to December 17, 1919, may
be reinstated without regard to the civil service rules.
The Civil Service Commission does not recommend the issuance of this order.
NoteE.—The commission was not advised of the reasons for the issuance of
the above order.
