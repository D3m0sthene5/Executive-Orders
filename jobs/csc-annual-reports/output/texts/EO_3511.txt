July 5, 1921.
Mr. Thomas E. Wall, formerly a post-office inspector, who entered the post
office service in 1889 and who served until June 30, 1915, may be reinstated
as post-office inspector without regard to the year limitation prescribed by
the civil-service rules.
Mr, William C. Ela, formerly post-office inspector, who entered the post office
service on September 1,.1900, and served continously until June 30, 1915, when
he resigned, may be reinstated as post-office inspector without regard to the
year limitation as prescribed by the civil service rules.
The Civil Service Commission reports that upon the facts appearing in the
entire record in these cases, the postal service will probably be benefited by
the waiver of the year limitation, and, therefore, concurs in the recommenda-
tion of the Postmaster General that the one year limitation be waived in these
cases.
