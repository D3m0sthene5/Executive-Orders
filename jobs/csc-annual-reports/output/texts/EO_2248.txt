September 23, 1915.
Mr. Shelly Inch may be appointed a clerk in the post office at Placerville, Cal.,
without reference to civil-service rules. This order is issued on the recommen-
dation of the Postmaster General, who states that Mr. Inch was appointed post-
master at Placerville in 1898 and after serving 16 years in that capacity he was
recommended by his successor for appointment as a temporary clerk, in which
capacity he has been employed since the new postmaster took charge of the
office. In the opinion of the Postmaster General Mr. Inch is thoroughly quali-
fied for the position. The Civil Service Commission ‘does not concur in the
recommendation for the issuance of this order.
