December 30, 1914.
Mr. Calvin Satterfield, of Pennsylvania, may be appointed to the position of Chief
of the Division of Accounts, Department of Justice, without reference to the civil-
service rules.
This order is issued upon the recommendation of the Attorney General, who states
that the position of Chief of the Division of Accounts is highly confidential, and
requires a peculiar order of talent; that a diligent effort has been made within the
classified service to find some one possessing the required qualifications, but without
success. The Attorney General believes that Mr. Satterfield, who is known to him
personally, is especially qualified for the position, and that the interests of the public
would be subserved by exempting the appointment.
The Civil Service Commission does not concur in the recommendation.
