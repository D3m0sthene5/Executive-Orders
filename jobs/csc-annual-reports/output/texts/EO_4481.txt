July 19, 1926.
Mr. Victor Shepherd may be transferred from the position of foreman, which
is excepted from examination, to the position of clerk in the office of the
chief of quartermaster, Panama Canal Zone, without examination, under the
civil-service rules. Mr. Shepherd's transfer is recommended by the chief of
office of the Panama Canal because of his experience with the particular duties
of the position, which can be tested only with difficulty. Mr. Shepherd is
a Spanish-American War veteran and has been employed almost continuously
since 1906.
The Civil Service Commision recommends approval of this order.
