February 20, 1924
Mrs. Olive Crook Henderson, widow of Mr. John A. Henderson, who died
suddenly at his desk in the office of the War Labor Board as the result of heart
failure, believed to have been accelerated by the nervous strain resulting from
the character of his work, may be appointed to an appropriate clerical position
in the classified civil service of the United States.
Having sole regard to the requirements of the service, the Civil Service Com-
mission finds itself unable to recommend a waiver of the rules in this case. The
order is granted as a matter of Executive benevolence.
