October 6, 1915.
J. Williar Sheetz may be appointed to a clerkship in the classified service of
the Treasury Department without compliance with ecivil-service rules. This
order is issued on the recommendation of the Secretary of the Treasury, who
states that Mr. Sheetz has served for a number of years as private secretary
to the Director of the Mint, has rendered very efficient service, and on account
of his knowledge of the mint work it is desired to use his services in a classified
position. The Civil Service Commission concurs in recommending this order.
