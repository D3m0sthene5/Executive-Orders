June 19, 1928.
Mr. George E. Strong, of Kansas, may be appointed to the position of chief clerk and
pdminisisive assistant, Department of Justice, without reference to the civil service
rules.
This order is issued upon the recommendation of the Attorney General, who states
that the position of chief clerk is highly confidential and requires a peculiar order of
talent; that a diligent effort has been made within the classified service to find some
one possessing the required qualifications, but without success. The Attorney General
believes that Mr. Strong is especially qualified for the position and that the interests
of the service would be promoted by exempting the appointment.
