May 20, 1921.
Mr. James W. Wells may be reinstated as assistant outside superintendent,
at $12.88 per d‘em, in the Hull Division, Navy Yard, New York, N. Y., the
year limit on reinstatements being waived.
With the concurrence of the Civil Service Commission this order is issued
at the request of the Secretary of the Navy, who states that Mr. Wells had
served for approximately 25 years in the navy yard, New York, and was
discharged on February 20, 1914, because the position which he held was
abolished, this action being taken over the protest of the industrial manager
of the yard. As the position has been reestablished and as it is considered
that Mr. Wells is best qualified to fill it, his reinstatement is authorized.
