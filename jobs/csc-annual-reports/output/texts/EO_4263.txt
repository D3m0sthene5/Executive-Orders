July 6, 1925.
Mrs. Sophie A. Benton may be appointed as printer's assistant in the Bureau
of Engraving and Printing without reference to the civil-service act and rules.
This order is issued on the recommendation of the Secretary of the Treasury.
Mrs. Benton is the widow of Benjamin F. Benton, who served as a plate printer
in the Bureau of Engraving and Printing from July 19, 1904, to March 30, 1924,
when he was killed in an automobile accident, leaving his widow with four small
children and without adequate provision for their care. The Civil Service
Commission is unable to concur in this order.
Done for charity.
