August 23, 1921.
Mrs. Edith L. F. Richter may be appointed to a classified position at $720 per
annum in the Treasury Department with the restriction that she shall not be
promoted beyond $900 per annum without the prior approval of the commission.
The Secretary of the Treasury recommends the appointment and states:
“ Mrs. Richter has made an excellent record for industry and efficiency in a
temporary position which she has been holding in the Treasury Department;
and she is the widow of Charles W. Richter, who served in a subclerical capacity
in the Treasury Department for almost 22 years and died leaving his widow
with five minor children to support.”
The Civil Service Commission submits the case without prejudice stating
that it is without jurisdiction.
