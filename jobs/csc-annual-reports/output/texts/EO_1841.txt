October 21, 1913.
Mr. Monte C. Price may be reinstated in the Postal Service without regard to the
length of his separation from the service.
Mr. Price entered the Government service as a railway postal clerk August 27,
1886, and was appointed a post-office inspector at $1,200 per annum, with $4 per diem,
on October 2, 1893; promoted to $1,400 June 1, 1894; to $1,600 November 1, 1895;
both with per diem allowances; and on October 1, 1906, was appointed a city inspector
at $2,000 per annum, without per diem. His resignation as an inspector was accepted
January 21, 1907, effective January 31, 1907. The chief inspector of the Post Office
Department states that Mr. Price’s record as an inspector is good, and that he would
not hesitate to recommend his reinstatement. The Postmaster General has no objec-
tion to the reinstatement. The Civil Service Commission does not recommend that
an order be issued authorizing the reinstatement of Mr. Price.
