February 15, 1905.
The following amendment was approved:
The requirement as to United States citizenship may be waived for applicants for
examination for the positions of officers of ships of the Coast and Geodetic Survey
on duty in the Philippine Islands under such regulations as may be provided by the
United States Civil Service Commission.
It was found by experience that citizens of the United States were not always
available to fill casual vacancies in positions on the vessels of the Coast and Geodetic
Survey, stationed in the Philippine Islands. The amendment was therefore recom¬
mended to meet these unusual conditions, with the understanding that the appoint¬
ment of aliens should be confined to cases where properly qualified Americans are
not to be had.
