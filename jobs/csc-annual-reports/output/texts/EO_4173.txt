March 14, 1925.
Mrs. Virginia R. Guilfoyle, who served three years as yeoman (F) in the Naval
Reserve Force during the war and who was very seriously injured in an auto-
mobile accident in 1923 in which her husband, a member of an Army Reserve
Unit, was killed, leaving her with two small children dependent upon her for sup-
port, may be appointed to any clerical position under the Navy Department
which she is qualified to fill, without regard to civil service rules or regulations.
This action is recommended by the Secretary of the Navy. The Civil Service
Commission does not concur.
Done for charity.
