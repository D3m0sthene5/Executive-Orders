May 9, 1917.
Schedule B, Subdivision IV, paragraph 2, of the civil-service rules, which permits
.appointments of retired noncommissioned officers of the Signal Corps as military
storekeepers in the Signal Service at large, is hereby amended to read as follows:
“2. Positions of military storekeeper in the Signal Service at large, the Medical
Department at large, and the Quartermaster Corps of the Army, when filled by re-
tired noncommissioned officers of those branches of the service.
“The War Department states that the Surgeon General reports that the Medical
Department will soon be called upon to establish a number of additional supply
depots, and that it will be difficult, if not impossible, to find competent civilian em-
ployees to perform this work. The department also states that the Quartermaster
General hasrecommended the extension of the provision to the Quartermaster Corps.”
