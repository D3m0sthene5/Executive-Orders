October 29, 1913.
Mr. John H. Jenkins may be reinstated to the position of immigrant inspector in the
Immigration Service without regard to the length of his separation from the service.
This order is issued upon the recommendation of the Secretary of Labor, who, after a
thorough investigation of the matter, is satisfied that Mr. Jenkins’ dismissal from the
service was not warranted by the facts. The Civil Service Commission does not concur
in recommending the issuance of an Executive order in this case.
