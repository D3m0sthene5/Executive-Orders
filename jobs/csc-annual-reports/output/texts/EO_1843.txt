October 22, 1913.
Mrs. Mary R. Geddings may be appointed to a position in the classified service with
out reference to the requirement of examination under the civil-service rules.
Mrs. Geddings is the widow of Surg. Henry D. Geddings, of the Public Health Serv-
ice, who was a commissioned medical officer in that service for about 25 years, and
who rendered distinguished service in matters relating to sanitation and public health.
He also rendered valuable service in several epidemics of yellow fever at a time when
few medical men were immune to that disease and its cause was but little known.
The Secretary of the Treasury states that it would be a fitting testimonial to the serv-
ices of Surg. Geddings to waive the civil-service rules to permit the appointment of
Mrs. Geddings in the classified service. The Civil Service Commission does not
recommend that an Executive order be issued in this case.
