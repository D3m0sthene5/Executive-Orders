June 1, 1925.
Under the authority provided in subsection (d) section 18 of the act approvedMay 24, 1924, the President of the United States, in the exercise of his discretion,and acting in the interest of the United States, hereby orders the retention onactive duty for a period of one year from July 1, 1925, of John G. Foster ofVermont, commissioned Foreign Service Officer, class one.
