November 20, 1925.
The regulations for the appointment of unclassified laborers are hereby
amended by the addition to Regulation II, section 1, of the following:
"Whenever citizen eligibles are not obtained as a result of duly announced
examinations, aliens may be employed temporarily without regard to the regu-
lations. In all such cases, however, if citizen eligibles later are available,
places temporarily filled by aliens shall be immediately vacated and filled by
citizen eligibles. In no circumstances shall alien labor be engaged under wage
rate less than the prevailing rate for like labor paid to citizen labor in the
immediate vicinity."
As amended, section 1, of Regulation II, will read as follows:
"SECTION 1. Applicants must be citizens of the United States, physically
and mentally qualified, of good character and habits, and not less than
18, nor more than 50, years of age, except for the position of boy laborer, for
which applicants must be not less than 14, nor more than 18 years of age.
These age limitations shall not apply to persons honorably discharged from
the Army or Navy or Marine Corps, who may be admitted to examination
without reference to age.
"Whenever citizen eligibles are not obtained as a result of duly announced
examinations, aliens may be employed temporarily without regard to the regu-
lations. In all such cases, however, if citizen eligibles later are available,
places temporarily filled by aliens shall be immediately vacated and filled by
citizen eligibles. In no circumstances shall alien labor be engaged under wage
rate less than the prevailing rate for like labor paid to citizen labor in the
immediate vicinity."
