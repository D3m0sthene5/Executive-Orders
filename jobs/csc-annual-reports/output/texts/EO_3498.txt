June 16, 1921.
The year limit upon eligibility for reinstatement is hereby waived to permit
the reinstatement of William H. Styles as postal clerk in the Post Office De-
partment.
This order is issued upon the recommendation of the Postmaster General,
who states that Mr. Styles was forced to resign from the Railway Mail Service
September 28, 1911, because of injuries received in a wreck while in the per-
formance of his official duties, and has rendered very satisfactory service as a
substitute clerk for the past three years.
The Civil Service Commission concurs in recommending the order, believing
the service will be benefited by the reinstatement.
