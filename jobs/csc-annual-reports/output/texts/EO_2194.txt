May 13, 1915.
Michael L. Eidsness, jr., of Iowa, a minor clerk of class D ($900) in the Post Office
Department, may be promoted to the position of clerk without further civil service
examination.
This order was issued upon the recommendation of the Postmaster General, who
stated that while Mr. Eidsness was not able to pass the first-grade examination he had
a very exceptional record in the Post Office Department and was engaged on clerical
work of high order; accomplished without apparent effort 50 per cent more work than
any of the clerks with whom he was associated. Mr. Eidsness made three attempts
to pass the first-grade examination, but on account of lack of early education was unable
to pass.
