February 28, 1924
De Warren B. Barnett and Charles F. Cortelyou may be appointed in the
special agency service of the customs of the Treasury Department without re-
gard to civil-service rules.
This order is issued on the recommendation of the Secretary of the Treasury,
who states that Messrs. Barnett and Cortelyou possess unusual aptitude and
fitness for investigational work requiring powers of keen observation, shrewd
and accurate deduction, and mental alertness of the highest order; also that
they have a comprehensive and exact knowledge of the customs laws, regula-
tions, and procedure.
The Civil Service Commission did not concur in the recommendation of this
order. Issued for the good of the service.
