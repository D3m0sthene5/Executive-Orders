November 22, 1907.
When transfers or promotions are properly applied for and are otherwise per¬
missible, the Civil Service Commission is hereby authorized, in its discretion,
looking to the good of the public service only, to waive requirements for exam¬
ination and to substitute for such examination so waived such other tests of
fitness and capacity as the Commission may decide.
In each case where such waiver is made the reasons therefor shall be stated
at length by the Commission and made matter of record in its minutes.
This order was recommended by the Commission for the reason that it was
believed that the service would be benefited if the discretion of waiving ex¬
aminations in certain cases of transfer or promotion, where such examinations
would ordinarily be required by the rules, should be placed in the hands of the
Commission.
