March 10, 1918.
Mrs. Mabel P. LeRoy, of Michigan, appointed by the Secretary of the Interior
February 26, 1910, to the position of clerk to sign land patents, is hereby designated
as required by law to sign said patents.
March 18, 1913. ®
Mr. Edward H. Hayden may be appointed to any position in the classified civil
service of the Treasury Department without compliance with the civil-service rules.
This order was issued on the statement of the Secretary of the Treasury that he
deemed it important that he should have in addition to his private secretary and
stenographer, a confidential clerk attached to his immediate staff in whom he had
complete confidence. He stated that he was unable to find any one in the classified
service who could meet this peculiar need. :
