September 19, 1921.
Mr. Artemus A. Rowe, formerly a post-office inspector, who entered the
Postal Service in May, 1890, and who served continuously until July 31, 1914,
when he was separated, may be re nstated to the position of post-office inspector
without regard to the time limitation. :
This action is taken on the recommendation of the Postmaster General, who
states that on account of Mr. Rowe's 24 years’ experience and interest in the
Postal Serv'ece it is his opinion that he would be a valuable acquisition to the
inspection force. :
