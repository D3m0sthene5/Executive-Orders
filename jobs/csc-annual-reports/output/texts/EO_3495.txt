June 14, 1921.
The year limit upon eligibility for reinstatement is hereby waived to permit
the reinstatement of William R. Dalby as carrier, rural-delivery service, Post
Office Department.
This order is issued upon the recommendation of the Postmaster General,
and concurred in by the Civil Service Commission.
Notre.—Dalby was removed as a rural carrier at Muncie, Ind., on November
22, 1919. The Postmaster General stated that upon examination he found
the charges were technical and trivial and did not justify the action taken,
which resulted in grave injustice to a faithful employee, who had given more
than 18 years of the best part of his life for the Postal Service, and who, by
reason of his age (60 years) was debarred from taking the prescribed examina-
tion and thereby qualifying for appointment in the regular way.
