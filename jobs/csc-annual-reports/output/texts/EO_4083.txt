October 6, 1924.
Schedule A, Section VIII, paragraph 8, of the civil service rules, which excepts
from the requirement of examination physicians employed in the Indian Service
and receiving not more than $720 per annum salary is hereby amended by in-
creasing the compensation permitted to $1,000 per annum. As amended the
paragraph will read as follows:
"8. Physicians employed in the Indian Service and receiving not more than
$1,000 per annum salary, who may lawfully perform their official duties in
connection with their private practice, such employment, however, to be subject
to the approval of the commission."
This amendment is recommended by the Commissioner of Indian Affairs, who
states that the medical services required at some of the reservations where the
medical work is not sufficient to provide a regular full time physician can not be
procured for $720 per annum. The Civil Service Commission concurs in the
recommendation.
