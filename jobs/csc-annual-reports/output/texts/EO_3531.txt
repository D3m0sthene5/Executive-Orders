August 9, 1921.
The first sentence of paragraph 2, Subdivision IV of Schedule A, of posi-
tions excepted from the requirement of examination under the civil service
rules, is hereby amended to increase the mraximum to be paid to cable seamen
who may be appointed without examination from $60 per month to $1,020 per
annum. As amended, the paragraph will read as follows:
“2. All telegraph operators and telegraph linemen receiving a monthly com-
pensation of $60 or less and cable seamen receiving not more than $1,020 per
annum, serving on military telegraph systems or at military stations, and who
perform their duties in connection with their private business or with other
employment, such duties requiring only a portion of their time. Appointment
to such positions shall be subject to noncompetitive examination as to practical
skill in the work required therein by a signal officer or acting signal officer,
whose certificates as to the professional fitness of the appointee shall be for-
warded to the Secretary of War and a duplicate thereof to the Civil Service
Commission.”
This amendment is based upon the representations of the War Department
that the increased salary is necessary.
