March 26, 1924
Mrs. Minnie Richards may be appointed to a clerical position in the Navy
Department without examination under the civil-service rules.
This order is issued upon the recommendation of the Acting Secretary of the
Navy in consideration of the faithful, meritorious, and valuable services of A.
Webster Richards, who died September 23, 1923, leaving his widow with three
small daughters and an aged mother to support. It was found that Mrs. Richards
has the education and experience which would apparently well qualify her for
clerical work.
The Civil Service Commission does not coneur in recommending this order.
Done for charity.
