June 20, 1917. :
Mr. Charles H. Kesler may be reinstated as an assistant examiner in the Patent
Office, Department of the Interior, without reference to the length of time he has been
separated from the service.
r. Kesler was appointed an assistant examiner through examination on July 1,
1911, and served until September 13, 1915, when he resigned. After his resignation
he was engaged in work in the office of a patent attorney. The Secretary of the
Interior recommends the reinstatement as distinctly in the interest of good adminis-
tration in view of Mr. Kesler’s satisfactory service and an experience of nearly six
years in patent matters.
The Civil Service Commission concurs in recommending this order in view of
the foregoing statement and the fact that the number of eligibles for the position of
assistant examiner is hardly equal to the demand.
