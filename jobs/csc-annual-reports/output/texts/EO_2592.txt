April 11, 191%.
It is hereby ordered that Henry J. Cantey, a laborer in the unclassified service, at
a salary of $660 per annum, in the Bureau of Labor Statistics, Department of Labor,
may be promoted to the position of assistant messenger in the classified service, in
the same bureau, at a salary of $720 per annum, without reference to civil-service
rules.
It is stated by administrative officials of the Department of Labor that Mr. Cantey
possesses qualifications of unusual value in the work of the Bureau of Labor Statistics.
He performs labor which demands a degree of skill and judgment beyond that usually
demanded from those in the grade he occupies. He has been with the Bureau of
Labor Statistics nearly 10 years, during all of which time his record has been excellent.
