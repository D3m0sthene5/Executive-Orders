December 16, 1915.
Mrs. Arabella N. McDonald may be reinstated as an operative in the Bureau
of Engraving and Printing without regard to the length of time she has been
separated from the service. This order is issued upon the recommendation of
the Secretary of the Treasury, who states that Mrs. McDonald has held six
temporary appointments in the bureau since her permanent appointment ter-
minated in 1898; that her services have been satisfactory and it is believed her
permanent reinstatement will be in the interest of the service. The Civil Serv-
ice Commission does not concur in the recommendation.
