April 14, 1917.
Dr. Katherine M. Herring, of Vineland, N. J., may be appointed to a position in
the classified service without reference to the civil-service rules.
Dr. Herring has served under various temporary appointments during the past
three years in the Children’s Bureau and the Immigration Service in the Department
of Labor, and the Secretary of Labor states that she has been extremely energetic
and conscientious and has shown proficiency of a very marked degree. He also states
that by reason of her hearty sympathy with the purposes of the employment service
and her thorough knowledge of its work, Miss Herring should prove a valuable acqui-
sition to this force. ;
