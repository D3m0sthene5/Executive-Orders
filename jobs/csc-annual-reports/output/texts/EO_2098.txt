November 28, 1914.
Miss Laura E. Scott may be appointed to a clerical position in the classified service
of the Department of State without examination under the civil-service rules.
This action is based upon the recommendation of the Acting Secretary of State
because of Miss Scott’s efficient service with several international arbitration com-
missions, the exceptional qualifications she has evinced in the performance of her
duties as confidential clerk to the Acting Secretary of State, and his representation
that the loss of the services of Miss Scott, who has been with him in the capacity of
confidential clerk for several years, would be a great inconvenience.
The Civil Service Commission concurs in the recommendation of this order.
