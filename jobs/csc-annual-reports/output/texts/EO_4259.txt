July 1, 1925.
The height requirement for the Post Office Service and for the position of guard
in the Immigration Service is waived in favor of Mr. Angelo L. Manghise who
on account of his height was refused enlistment in the Army, Navy, and Marine
Corps but who served 18 months as an ambulance driver for the American Red
Cross in France during the World War.
The Civil Service Commission was unable to recommend the issuance of this
order.
