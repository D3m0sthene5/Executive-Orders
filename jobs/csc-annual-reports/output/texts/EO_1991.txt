July 9, 1914.
The Executive order of January 17, 1873, prohibiting certain civil officers from
accepting offices under the authority of States or Territories, is hereby amended so
as to permit the acceptance, with the approval of the Secretary of the Interior, by
employees of the Reclamation Service and of the National Park Service, of appoint-
ments as deputy State fish or game wardens under the laws of the States or Terri-
tories wherein such employees may be on duty, provided that the service as deputy
warden shall be without compensation and shall in no manner interfere or be incon-
sistent with the duties of such employees in the Government service wherein they
are employed.
