July 30, 1917.
Mr. Benjamin Grady may be appointed as expert (with inventive ability) in the
Bureau of the Census, Department of Commerce, without regard to. the requirements
of the civil-service rules.
This order is issued upon recommendation of the Secretary of Commerce and the
Director of the Census, who state that there is immediate need in the mechanical
laboratory of the bureau for the services of a person with inventive ability to assist in
the devising of machinery, the application of mechanical principles, and in experi-
mentation; that it is not deemed practicable to obtain a competent person through
the usual civil-service channels; that Mr. Grady is a person of known and tried me-
chanical ability with inventive talent; and that the bureau will regard itself as
fortunate in securing, under present conditions, the services of a person with Mr.
Grady’s qualifications. For these reasons it is believed that an exception to the
rules in this instance is fully justified in the interests of the service.
