June 17, 1918.
Mr. James H. Shirley may be reinstated to an appropriate position in the service
under the Post Office Department without regard to the length of his separation from
the service.
Mr. Shirley entered the Railway Mail Service as a result of an open competitive
examination and served from June 21, 1899, until August 31, 1906, in that service and
in the Columbia, S. C., post office, resigning on the latter date. On August 1, 1907, he
was appointed postal clerk on the Isthmus of Panama without examination and with-
out a competitive status for transfer, although at the time he could have been rein-
stated to a competitive position in the post office service. His preparations to acquire
a competitive status by examination on the Isthmus were interrupted by malaria
followed by a paralysis of the lower limbs which necessitated his resignation and
return to the States for medical treatment. He hassince entirely recovered his health.
The Civil Service Commission joins with the Postmaster General in recommending an
Executive order to permit the reinstatement of Mr. Shirley.
