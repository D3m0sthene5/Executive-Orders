August 20, 1913.
Mr. Charles M. McCabe may be reinstated as a clerk in the Postal Service without
reference to the year limitation of the reinstatement rule.
Mr. McCabe was removed from the Nashville, Tenn., post, office on September 10,
1898, notice of his removal alleging ‘‘inefficiency.”” No charge of inefficiency had
been preferred against him and he was allowed no opportunity to reply to the charge
as required by the rules then in effect. Mr. McCabe as a member of the board of civil
service examiners for the Nashville post office rendered assistance to the commission
in connection with investigations involving the collector of internal revenue, who was
removed by the President for violation of the civil service rules. The Postmaster
General states that it appears that Mr. McCabe was removed on political grounds, and
the issuance of an Executive order for his reinstaterient seems to be justified by the
facts in the case.
In view of the facts stated above the Civil Service Commission concurs in recom-
mending this order.
