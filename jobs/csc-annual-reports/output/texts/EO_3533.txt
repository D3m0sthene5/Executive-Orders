August 11, 1921.
Mrs. Nellie J. Latta (formerly Nellie W. Jenkins) may be reinstated to the
position of clerk in the classified service without regard to the civil-service rules.
Mrs. Latta, who is now serving as temporary clerk in the Post Office Depart-
ment, is the widow of Albert E. Latta, a former employee of that Department,
who after 24 years of faithful service was dropped from the rolls September 30,
1918, on accounty of insanity, and died in March, 1919, leaving Mrs. Latta in
destitute circumstances.
Mrs. Latta before her marriage served in the Post Office Department from
March 1, 1899, to November 24, 1903, as Miss Nellie W. Jenkins, and was again
appointed in a temporary Dosition October 1, 1919, in which position she is now
serving.
