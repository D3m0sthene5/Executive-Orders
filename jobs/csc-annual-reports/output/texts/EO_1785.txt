June 9, 1913.
Dr. Park Howell may be appointed as acting assistant surgeon in the Public Health
Service, for a period not to exceed one year, without compliance with the civil-service
rules and regulations.
This order is recommended by the Secretary of the Treasury because the work to
which Dr. Howell is to be assigned demands the services of an additional officer and
there is no commissioned officer available for assignment to the duty. The Civil
Sopvios Commission does not concur in the recommendation for the issuance of the
order.
