December 29, 1915.
Mrs. Marian K. Ferguson may be appointed to a clerkship in the Navy Depart-
ment without reference to the civil-service rules. Mrs. Ferguson is the wife of
William J. Ferguson, who enlisted in the Navy at the outbreak of the Spanish
American War, and who advanced rapidly and was about to achieve the rating
of chief machinist when he was stricken with paralysis, and upon the recom-
mendation of a board of medical survey was discharged because of his unfit-
ness for further service. This order is issued upon the recommendation of the
Secretary of the Navy, who states that during the organization of the Federal
Reserve Board, Mrs. Ferguson was employed temporarily by the organization
committee ; that the quality of her work has been heartily commended by the
Comptroller of the Currency and the counsel of the Federal Reserve Board;
and that he is of the opinion that she is fully qualified for a clerical position.
The Civil Service Commission does not concur in the recommendation.
