June 19, 1916.
Section 7, Civil Service Rule X, is hereby amended by striking therefrom the
following words :
“ Money-order assorter and skilled laborer acting as money-order assorter in
the office of the Auditor for the Post Office Department.”
Section 7 of Rule X permits the transfer of operatives and other employees
in the Bureau of Engraving and Printing to certain positions in other parts of
the department, among them those mentioned above, without examination and
without charge to the apportionment, for the performance of certain work.
As the audit of money orders is now done by labor-saving devices, the part of
the rule quoted is no longer in effect.
