June 30, 1919.
Mrs. Josephine Glascott may be appointed to a clerical position in the classified
chil service of the Treasury Department without complying with the civil-service
rules.
This order is issued on the recommendation of the Secretary of the Treasury, who
states that in view of the exceptional and satisfactory service performed by Mrs.
Glascott during her temporary employment in the Treasury Department he believes
her permanent appointment would be in the interest of the service. It is stated that
Mrs. Glascott is entirely dependent upon her own efforts for support.
