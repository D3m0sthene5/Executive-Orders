July 14, 1922.
The rule prohibiting the promotion of messenger girls to apportioned posi-
tions is waived to permit the promotion of Miss Elsie M. Robertson in the
Department of Agriculture from messenger girl to clerk.
Miss Robertson’s services have been highly satisfactory. The Secretary of
Agriculture recommends the issuance of an Executive order permitting the
promotion for exceptional reasons.
The Civil Service Commission did not concur in recommending this order.
