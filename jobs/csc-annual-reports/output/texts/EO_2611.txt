May 7, 1917. :
The appointment of Mr. Edgar Mabie as superintendent of the Bureau of War Risk
Insurance in the Treasury Department is hereby authorized without compliance
with civil-service rules.
This order is issued on the recommendation of the Secretary of the Treasury, who
states that Mr. Mabie has had 14 years’ experience in one of the large insurance com-
panies in New York City, passing on applications for insurance, examining policies,
and having supervision of general office detail, and in view of the technical character
of the work in the Bureau of War Risk Insurance, for which Jedially trained assistants
are required, it is believed his appointment will be greatly in the interests of the
service.
