April 6, 1925.
Mr. Roy A. Finney may be appointed in any department, establishment, or
office in the classified service of the United States, in any position for which his
qualifications fit him, and where there is need of his services, witliout reference
to the requirements of the civil service rules.
Mr. Finney is an honorably discharged sailor from the United States Navy,
where he served two enlistments. Following his discharge from the naval service
he was injured while employed in private industry. He passed the examination
for post-office clerk at Newark, N. J., on July 31, 1920, with an average of 80.30.
His physical condition necessitated cancellation of his eligibility.
Mr. Finney has endeavored to pass examinations for the departmental service
where physical disability is not a bar, and was a competitor in six such examina-
tions. The Civil Service Commission does not recommend this order.
Done for charity.
