July 9, 1921.
Mr. LaClare Chamberlin may be reinstated to the position of city letter
carrier in the post office at Santa Barbara, Calif., without regard to the length
of his separation from the classified service.
Mr. Chamberlin served as a carrier in the Akron, Ohio, post office from
1907 to 1913. He has been serving temporarily in the Santa Barbara post
office and in view of his being above the age limit for appointment through
examination and of his efficiency and loyalty his reinstatement by Executive
order is recommended by the Postmaster General.
The commission concurs with the Postmaster General in the issuance of
this order.
