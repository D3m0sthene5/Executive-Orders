May 14, 1915.
Mr. Bernard R. Jett may be transferred in the Office of the Secretary of the Interior
from wireman on the per diem roll to electrician at $1,000 per annum.
Mr. Jett is employed on the ‘‘ Repair of Buildings” roll in a position for many years
not regarded by the department as classified. He was one of a small number whose
retention and reemployment without a competitive status were authorized by an
executive order of June 17, 1909. He has been employed intermittently since Sep-
ember 9, 1902, has passed the examination for electrician, and by reason of extensive
and varied electrical work has become familiar with the complicated systems of light-
ing, being one of the few persons who will be able to assist materially in the prepara-
tion of long deferred electrical plans for the several buildings. The action proposed
will also save several hundred dollars annually by permitting the continuous employ-
ment of skilled mechanics who must otherwise be laid off when their services can not
well be spared.
In view of these facts the commission concurs with the department in recommending
the transfer of Mr. Jett by executive order.
