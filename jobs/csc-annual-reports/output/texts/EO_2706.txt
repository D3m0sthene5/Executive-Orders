September 22, 1917.
Mrs. Mabel Money Kitchin, of Mississippi, may be appointed as assistant in the
library of the Army Medical Museum, without reference to the civil-service rules.
Mrs. Kitchin has been employed in the Library of Congress for the past year; she
has a working knowledge of the French, German, Italian and Spanish languages,
and it is stated that she is peculiarly fitted for the place to which it is proposed to
appoint her.
