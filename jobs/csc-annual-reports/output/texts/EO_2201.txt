May 25, 1915.
The year limit upon eligibility for reinstatement is hereby waived to permit the
reinstatement of Mr. Albert J. Phillips as associate chemist at $2,000 per annum in
the service of the Bureau of Standards, Department of Commerce, at Pittsburgh, Pa.
Mr. Phillips was in the service from August 13, 1906, to June 15, 1913, when he
resigned. He is especially recommended by the Director of the Bureau of Standards
on account of his satisfactory and efficient service. Difficulty has been experienced
in obtaining a suitably qualified person for filling the position. :
The Civil Service Commission concurs in recommending this Executive order
because of Mr. Phillips long and efficient service, the comparatively short period of
his separation from the service, and the need of an employee with his training, which
it has been difficult to meet through the usual procedure. 3
