April 9, 1923.
Mrs. Maude Morrison may be appointed to a clerical position in the Treasury Depart-
ment without regard to the requirements of the civil-service rules. This order is
issued upon recommendation of the Secretary of the Treasury, who states that Mrs.
Morrison is the wife of George R. Morrison, who served for 17 years in the Treasury
Department and 4 years in the Marine Corps of the United States, and whose failing
health caused his retirement from the service upon a small annuity.
The Civil Service Commission did not concur in recommending this order.
