July 18, 1921.
The year limitation upon eligibility for reinstatement is hereby waived to
permit the reinstatement of Mr. Harry B. Hall to the position of post-office
inspector.
Mr. Hall was first appointed in the postal service in 1888; on August 13,
1898, he was appointed a post-office inspector as the result of passing the post
office inspector examination; was promoted up through the various grades
including the position of inspector in charge and finally on August 27, 1913,
was separated from the service.
The Postmaster General recommends this order in view of Mr. Hall's ripe
experience and the exceptional qualifications he evinced in the performance of
his duties throughout his entire service.
The Civil Service Commission concurs in recommending this order.
