October 14, 1915.
Miss Maude H. Bundy may. be appointed to a position in the Government
Printing Office without reference to the civil-service rules. Miss Bundy is the
daughter of the late Alvin A. Bundy, a Civil War veteran, and a former em-
ployee of the Government Printing Office. He was appointed to a position
in that office on May 4, 1881, and continued in such employment until the
time of his death, August 29, 1910. The reports show that his record was
good. In consideration of the long and faithful service of Mr. Bundy in the
military and civil service of the Federal Government, the Public Printer recom-
mends that an Executive order be issued authorizing her appointment. The
Civil Service Commission does not concur in the recommendation.
