April 9, 1923.
Mr. Green A. Stephens may be reinstated in an appropriate position in the Post
Office Service on condition that he waive his claim to annuity upon retirement.
Mr. Stephens was removed from the position of letter carrier on April 12, 1905, on charges
which upon reinvestigation were found to be untrue. Hisreinstatement wasauthorized
by an Executive order of August 26, 1915, but, for private reasons, it was impossible
for him to reenter the service at that time. The Civil Service Commission does not
recommend this order.
