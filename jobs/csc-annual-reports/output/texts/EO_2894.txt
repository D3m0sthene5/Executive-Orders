June 25, 1918.
It is hereby ordered that the experiment station at American University,
Washington, D. C., which station has been established under the supervision of
the Bureau of Mines, Interior Department, for the purpose of making gas in-
vestigations for the Army, under authority of appropriations made for the
Ordnance and Medical Departments of the Army, together with the personnel
thereof, be, and the same is hereby, placed under the control of the War Depart-
ment for operation under the Director of Gas Service of the Army.
