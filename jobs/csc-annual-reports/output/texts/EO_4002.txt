April 30, 1924
Mrs. Agnes E. Mahoney may be reinstated in a suitable vacancy in the Depart-
ment of Agriculture without regard to the length of her separation from the
service.
Mrs. Mahoney entered the Department of Agriculture on November 1, 1918,
* through open competitive examination, and resigned on April 12, 1919, having
married. The Secretary of Agriculture states that her services were satisfactory
and that he would be glad to reinstate her in the first suitable vacancy. The
Civil Service Commission does not recommend the reinstatement of Mrs.
Mahoney.
Done as charity.
