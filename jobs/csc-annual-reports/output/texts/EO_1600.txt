September 7, 1912.
Mr. James B. Brunemer may be appointed a watchman in the Government Printing
Office. He is a veteran of the Civil War and served as a watchman in the Post Office
Department and later as a clerk in the Treasury and Interior Departments. He is
eligible for reinstatement under the rules in any of these departments because of his
former service therein; but not in the Government Printing Office. This order is
issued upon the recommendation of the Public Printer, with the concurrence of the
Civil Service Commission, as there are no eligible applicants among the veterans
employed in the Government Printing Office. This action merely permits reinstate-
ment in an office other than one for which the applicant is already eligible.
