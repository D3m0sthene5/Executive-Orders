May 2, 1924
Civil Service Rule X, section 3, is hereby amended to allow retransfer under
certain conditions of persons who left the classified service to ‘enter the service
of a State, county, municipality, or foreign government.
As amended the section will read as follows:
“SeEctioN 3. Any person may be retransferred to a position in which he was
formerly employed or to any position to which transfer could be made therefrom
if since his transfer he has served continuously and satisfactorily under any of
the following conditions:
“(1) In the executive or judicial civil service of the United States or of its
insular possessions. :
“(2) In the legislative service if he entered the classified service upon com-
petitive WRB and the legislative service by transfer thereform.
“(3) In the service of a State, county, municipality, or foreign government
in a position in which he has acquired valuable training and experience.”
Such retransfer may be made without compliance with clauses (b), (¢), (d),
and (e) of section 8 of this rule.
