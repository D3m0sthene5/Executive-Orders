April 26, 1916.
Mrs. D. C. McCann, of California, may be appointed to a position in the
United States Employment Service of the Department of Labor without ref-
erence to the civil-service rules. Her appointment is recommended by the Sec-
retary of Labor. It is stated that Mrs. McCann possesses qualifications which
eminently fit her for a position in the employment service. The Civil Service
Commission does not concur in the recommendation,
