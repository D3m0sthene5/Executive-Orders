May 1, 1913.
Mrs. Mary E. Netherland may be transferred from clerk to sign tribal deeds, $1,200
per annum, in the office of the Secretary, Department of the Interior, to a regular
clerkship.
She has served efficiently three years in her present position and has demonstrated
her ability as a clerk.
The commission concurs with the Secretary of the Interior in recommending her
appointment by Executive order.
