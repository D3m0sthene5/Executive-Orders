May 11, 1915.
Miss Agnes Deery may be appointed as a nurse in the Indian Service without
reference to the requirement of examination under the civil-service rules. . This action,
based upon the recommendation of the Secretary of the Interior, is in fulfillment of
an agreement between representatives of the Departments of the Interior and War in
October, 1912, when it was understood that Miss Deery was eligible for transfer.
Miss Deery served from June 12, 1902, to May 7, 1914, at the Apache Indian Hos-
pital at Fort Sill, Okla., with distinguished faithfulness and success in the positions
of matron and nurse. The Apache prisoners having been removed to the Mescalero
Agency, N. Mex., it is desired to appoint Miss Deery as nurse in the Indian Service.
The Civil Service Commission concurs in the recommendation for this order.
