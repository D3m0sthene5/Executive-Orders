September 4, 1913.
Mr. Pat O'Keefe may be appointed a deputy collector of internal revenue without
examination under the civil-service rules.
The commission was not consulted regarding this appointment. The reasons stated
for the order are that Mr. O’ Keefe possesses excellent qualifications for work of this
character, particularly in view of the income-tax provisions of the new tariff bill, and
it was desirable that the collector of internal revenue for the State of Texas, where Mr.
