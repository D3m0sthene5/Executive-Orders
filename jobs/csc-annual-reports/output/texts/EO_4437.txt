May 1, 1926.
Mr. Michael A. McNamara may be reinstated in the Post Office Service in
Bethlehem, Pa., without regard to the requirements of the civil-service rules.
This order is issued upon the recommendation of the Postmaster General by
virtue of Mr. McNamara's previous service over a period of more than 11 years
and of the fact that his period of eligibility for reinstatement under the civil-
service rules has expired and that he is beyond the age limit for admission to
the clerk-carrier examination.
The Civil Service Commission submits the case without prejudice.
