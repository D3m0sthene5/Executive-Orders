January 8, 1923.
Mrs. Frank L. Scott may be appointed to a clerical position in the classified service
of the War Department without regard to the civil-service rules.
This order is recommended by the Chief of Finance, under whom Mrs. Scott served
under temporary appointment. She has performed her duties in a satisfactory manner
and by her general aptitude has become a valuable asset to the service. Her husband
is confined in St. Elizabeths Hospital suffering from paresis and his recovery is hope-
less, this condition doubtless having resulted largely from his service as a soldier in
the Tropics and to overwork during the late war. While in the Tropics he contracted
a fever, which caused a generally weakened condition of his system. During the
war he organized an important unit of the War Department in Washington and gave
attention to his work without regard to hours. Mrs. Scott has been left solely upon
her own resources, as her husband receives no pension or other remuneration.
The Secretary of War, while reluctant to recommend an Executive order, states
that favorable action will be agreeable to him.
The Civil Service Commission does not concur in recommending this order.
