June 22, 1925.
Miss Helen A. Clark may be appointed without examination to a position in
the classified service with such status as she would have acquired if originally
appointed through the typist examination. This order is issued on the recom-
mendation of the Director of the Veterans' Bureau, who states that Miss .Clark
was appointed effective April 28, 1919, as a result of selection from a certificate
containing the name of Mrs. Helen A. Clark, who, in fact, was another. person.
In view of her long service without interruption, the fact that her record is stated

was formerly erroneously appointed, the lack of any culpability on her part, and
the apparent hardship of her discharge under such circumstances, the Civil
