February 4, 1915.
Mr. Ralph Izard, who was dismissed from the position of Chinese inspector in the
Immigration Service February 3, 1903, may be reinstated to the position of immigrant
inspector in the Immigration Service, Department of Labor, without regard to the
length of his separation from the service.
This order is issued upon the recommendation of the Secretary of Labor, who states
that he made a thorough investigation of the case and arrived at the conclusion that
Mr. Izard was improperly dismissed from the service.
