February 26, 1919.
In view of the pressing importance and specialized character of the work to be
Poptormd by the Industrial Board of the Department of Commerce, which was created
y the Secretary of Commerce on February 18, 1919, with my approval, given under
date of February 13, 1919, said industrial board is authorized to employ, for the period
of its existence, without reference to the requirements of the civil-service laws and
rules, such persons as in the judgment of those in responsible charge are best adapted
to its work, it being understood that all possible use will be made of the registers of
the Civil Service Commission. :
