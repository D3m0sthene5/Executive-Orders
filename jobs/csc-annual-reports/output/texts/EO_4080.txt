October 1, 1924
Mrs. S. Virginia Page may be appointed to a clerical position in the classified
service of the Treasury Department without compliance with the requirements of
the civil service act and rules.
This order is issued on the recommendation of the Secretary of the Treasury,
who states that Mrs. Page has been employed on what is known as a contract basis
in the distribution unit of the Treasury Department for almost seven years and
has rendered faithful service.
The Civil Service Commission does not concur in recommending this order.
Done for charity.
