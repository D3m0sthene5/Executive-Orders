February 23, 1916.
The Executive order of February 14, 1912, as amended by orders of May 5
and May 26, 1914, permitting employees in the executive civil service perma-
nently residing in certain incorporated municipalities adjacent to the District
of Columbia to become candidates for or hold municipal office therein, is hereby
further amended so as to include the incorporated municipality of Capitol
Heights, Md., among those named in the order. The first paragraph of the
order of February 14, 1912, as amended, will read as follows: :
“ Employees of the executive civil service permanently residing in the fol-
lowing incorporated municipalities adjacent to the District of Columbia will not
be prohibited from becoming candidates for or holding municipal office in such
corporations :
“In Maryland.—Takoma Park, Kensington, Garrett Park, Chevy Chase, Glen
Echo, Hyattsville, Mount Ranier, Somerset, Capitol Heights.
“In Virginia.—Falls Church, Vienna, Herndon.”
This order is recommended by the Civil Service Commission for the reason
that the same conditions exist in Capitol Heights, Md., as in the other munici-
palities named in the original order and its amendments.
