August 22, 1925.
Employees appointed without examination under exceptions contained in
war emergency Executive orders, to positions which were then and are now in
the classified service, and who are serving in classified positions on the date
of this order, are hereby included in the classified service; and unskilled laborers
still in service who were appointed under such orders are hereby included in the
service with the same status as though they had been employed under the labor
regulations: Provided, That this order shall not apply to employees on the
Isthmus of Panama or to employees on a consulting basis.
