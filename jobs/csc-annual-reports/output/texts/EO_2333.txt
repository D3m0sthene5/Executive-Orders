March 6, 1916.
Mrs. Elizabeth L. Blackistone may be appointed to a position in the classified
service without reference to the civil-service rules. Mrs. Blackistone has had
temporary service in the Bureau of Pensions, the Bureau of Immigration, the
Vocational Educational Committee, and with the Federal Reserve Board. Her
appointment is recommended by the Secretary of Labor, who states that he
finds upon investigation that she is qualified to undertake work in several
branches of the Immigration Service in which women are employed and are
now needed. The Civil Service Commission does not concur in the recom-
mendation.
