December 7, 1914.
Miss Louisa W. Happersett may be appointed to a position in the classified civil
service without reference to the civil-service rules.
This order is issued upon the recommendation of the Secretary of War, who states
that the Quartermaster General, the Surgeon General, and the Judge Advocate
General of the Army unite in requesting the issuance of an Executive order. Miss
Happersett is the daughter of the late John C. G. Happersett, major and surgeon,
United States Army, who rendered highly efficient military service during a period
of 32 years, including the 4 years of the Civil War. The Surgeon General and the
Judge Advocate General call special attention to the services rendered by Maj.
Happersett and his family during the yellow-fever epidemic at Fort Brown, Tex.,
in 1882.
The Civil Service Commission does not concur in recommending the order.
