November 30, 1908.
Schedule A, Subdivision V, paragraph 4 of the civil-service rules, is hereby
amended to read as follows :
4. All employees on star routes and in post-offices having no city free deliv¬
ery service, other than postmasters of the fourth class, in Maine, New Hamp¬
shire, Vermont, Massachusetts, Rhode Island, Connecticut, New York, New
Jersey, Pennsylvania, Ohio, Indiana, Illinois, Wisconsin, and Michigan.
Fourth-class postmasters having been classified in certain States and time
being necessarily required before any positions in such offices can be filled
by examination and certification from the Civil Service Commission, any
vacancy in such post-offices occurring prior to the time at which the Civil
Service Commission shall certify to the Post-Office Department that it is able
to hold examinations and make certifications to such positions may be filled by
the Post-Office Department without examination.
The classification was restricted to the Northern and Eastern States because
that section of the country is more densely populated and throughout this terri¬
tory the commission has the apparatus of postal examinations and organization
perfected and less difficulty has been experienced in getting eligibles for the
rural carrier service in this section than elsewhere. The order affects 15,488
of the 55,318 fourth-class postmasters.
