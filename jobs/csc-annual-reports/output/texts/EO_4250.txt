June 10, 1925.
Mr. Joseph H. McGrath, who is serving as a janitor at $1,080 per annum in
the Federal Building, Lowell, Mass., and as a member of the License Com-
mission of Lowell, Mass., at $850 per annum, is hereby excepted from the opera-
tion of the Executive order of January 17, 1873, relative to the holding of State
or municipal office of persons in the Federal Service.
This order was recommended by the Secretary of the Treasury.
The Civil Service Commission was unable to concur in the order.
