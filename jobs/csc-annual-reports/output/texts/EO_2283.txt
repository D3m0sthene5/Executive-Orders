December 14, 1915.
Miss Emma I. Windridge, who has passed the civil-service examination in
stenography and typewriting, may be appointed to a clerkship in the War
Department without reference to the civil-service rules. Miss Windridge is the
daughter of Charles E. Windridge, formerly a clerk in the War Department.
Her father was appointed to a position in the Post Office Department in Novem-
ber, 1902, where he served until April, 1905, when he was appointed -to a posi-
tion in the Navy Department. He resigned in November, 1905, to accept a clerk-
ship in the War Department, which he held until the time of his death, Sep-
tember 14, 1915. Mr. Windridge had an excellent record while employed in the
‘War Department. The Secretary of War recommends the issuance of an order
in this case. The Civil Service Commission does not concur in the recommen-
dation.
