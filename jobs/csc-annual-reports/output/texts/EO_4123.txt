January 12, 1925.
Miss Violet Hawkesworth, who has been employed upon clerical work in theVeterans' Bureau since February 1, 1922, may be appointed to a clerkship in
the Veterans' Bureau without regard to the civil-service rules.
Miss Hawkesworth qualified for appointment as trained nurse at a hospital,
but not as clerk. She has rendered satisfactory service as clerk. She has an
invalid, dependent mother which prevents her from accepting appointment as
nurse. Her appointment by Executive order is recommended by the Director
of the Veterans' Bureau.
Having sole regard to the requirements of the service, the Civil Service Com-
mission finds itself unable to recommend a waiver of the rules in this case. The
order is granted as a matter of Executive benevolence.
