September 18, 1913.
Mr. William B. Green may be reinstated as a compositor in the Weather Bureau
without reference to the time limit imposed by the civil-service rules.
Mr. Green’s services as a compositor were terminated on December 31, 1910, after
more than 20 years’ service. The reason given at the time was that a reduction of the
force was necessary because of the transfer of certain work from the Weather Bureau
to the Government Printing Office. The Secretary of Agriculture with the concur-
rence of the Civil Service Commission recommends the reinstatement because there
is reason to believe that Mr. Green was unfairly dealt with because of certain testimony
he had given in a case which resulted in the reprimand of the Chief of the Weather
Bureau.
