October 28, 1918.
In view of conditions existing in Washington, D. C., due to the influenza epidemic
and the inadequate housing facilities, the Civil Service Commission is authorized
as a war measure to certify to the executive departments and independent establish-
ments in the District of Columbia for probational (or permanent) appointment the
names of persons who, by reason of having two or more members of their family in
the service, would otherwise be barred from consideration for such appointment.
