August 17, 1921.
Mr. Mason Bragg may be reinstated in the postal service at Beckley, W. Va,
without regard to the length of his separation from the service.
Mr. Bragg was removed from the position in the Beckley post office on
August 7, 1917, charged with disloyalty. The case has since been reinvestigated
and it has been ascertained that an injustice was done Mr. Bragg in authorizing
his removal. His reinstatement as a clerk 5 the post office is recommended by
the Postmaster General.
The Civil Service Commission does not concur in the recommendation.
