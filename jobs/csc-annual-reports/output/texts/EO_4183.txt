Section 3, Subdivision VII, Schedule A, which provides:
"All employees on star routes and in post offices of the third and fourth
classes, other than postmasters of the fourth class, except those in Alaska,
Guam, Hawaii, Porto Rico, and Samoa,"
is amended to read as follows:
"3. All employees on star routes and in post offices of the third and fourth
classes except village delivery carriers who were regularly employed in a
first or second class post office upon the relegation of the office below the
second class and postmasters of the fourth class other than those in Alaska,
Guam, Hawaii, Porto Rico, and Samoa."
The amendment preserves the rights of village carriers in post offices upon the
relegation of the offices below the second class.
This order is recommended by the Civil Service Commission with the con-
currence of the Postmaster General.
