September 18, 1925.
Mrs. Ida Diamond may be appointed to the classified position of guide in the
Bureau of Engraving and Printing without compliance with the requirements
of the civil-service rules.
This order is issued on the recommendation of the Secretary of the Treasury,
who states that Mrs. Diamond is the widow of Constantine Diamond, a veteran
of the Civil War, and that she has been employed with the exception of a little
more than two years in the Bureau of Engraving and Printing in the unclassi-
fied positions of charwoman and forewoman of charwomen from May 7, 1898,
to the present time.
The Civil Service Commission reports that it is without authority to make
an affirmative recommendation, but states that in view of her long and faithful
service her appointment by Executive order would not do violence to the spirit
of the civil-service rules.
Done for charity.
