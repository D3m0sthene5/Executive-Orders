Friday, N ovem b er 9, 1962 FEDERAL REGISTER 10927
Executive Order 11061  
MRS. ANNA ELEANOR ROOSEVELT
As a mark of respect to the memory of Mrs. Anna Eleanor Roose­
velt, it is hereby ordered, pursuant to the provisions o f Section 4 o f 
Proclamation 3044 of March 1, 1954, that until interment the flag o f 
the United States shall be flown at half-staff on all buildings, grounds 
and naval vessels o f the Federal Government in the District o f Co­
lumbia and throughout the United States and its Territories and pos­
sessions. I  also direct that the flag shall be flown at half-staff for the 
same length of time at all United States embassies, legations, consular 
offices, and other facilities abroad, including all military facilities and 
naval vessels and stations.
