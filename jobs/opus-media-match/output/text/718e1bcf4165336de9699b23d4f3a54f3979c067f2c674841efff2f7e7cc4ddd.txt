RECOMMENDATIONS OF THE 
PRESIDENTIAL CLEMENCY BOARD 
DENYING CLEMENCY FORWARDED 
BY THE ATTORNEY GENERAL 
Attached you will find the second submission from the Attorney 
General on behalf of the Presidential Clemency Board recom­
mending the denial of clemency in 435 cases, 17 civilian and 
418 military. The Board suggested, and I concur, that no 
warrants or other formal documents be executed by the 
President upon reviewing and approving these cases. Instead, 
the President's approval should be signified in a memorandum 
to me. A draft of such a memorandum is attached. 
This procedure will confirm the President's approval of the 
Board's recommendations, but will avoid any formal entry into 
the records of these applicants. The Board has regularly 
assured all potential applicants that the program is entirely 
voluntary and that they would suffer no penalty or adverse 
consequences merely by applying to the Board. Through the 
suggested procedure, these individuals will not suffer any 
additional disadvantage for having availed themselves, 
albeit unsuccessfully, of the President's offer of clemency. 
Copies of the original staff summaries for each case remain 
available should you wish to review them.

SUBJECT: Recommendations of the Presidential 
Clemency Board Forwarded by the 
Attorney General 
Attached is the ninth collection of recommendations. totaling 
110 individuals, transmitted on behalf of the Presidential 
Clemency Board by the Attorney General. The recommendations 
in this transmittal fall into six categories, reflected in the tabs 
attached to the Attorney General's letter to you. Each of the 
formal documents is virtually the same as those in the previous 
transmittals by the Presidential Clemency Board, except that the 
formal documents which are attached for your signature reflect 
(1) certification by the Attorney General instead of by the 
Presidential Clemency Board of the satisfactory completion of 
any prescribed period of alternative service and (2) certification 
by the Attorney General instead of by the Chairman of the 
Presidential Clemency Board of the number of applications 
being forwarded. 
The recommendations transmitted herein exclude cases of known 
felons. Copies of the original staff summaries for each case 
remain available should you wish to review them. 
In accordance with past practice the lists indicating the period 
of alternative service recommended for each applicant should 
not be released publicly in order to preserve the privacy of the 
individuals concerned.
