Presidential Documents
Title 3— THE PRES IDENT
Executive Order 1 1 233 
ADLAI E. STEVENSON
As a mark of respect to the memory of Adlai E. Stevenson, it is 
hereby ordered, pursuant to the provisions of Section 4 of Procla­
mation 3044 of March 1, 1954, that until interment the flag of the 
United States shall be flown at half-staff on all buildings, grounds and 
naval vessels of the Federal Government in the District of Columbia 
and throughout the United States and its Territories and possessions. 
I  also direct that the flag shall be flown at half-staff for the same length 
of time at all United States embassies, legations, consular offices, and 
other facilities abroad, including all military facilities and naval 
vessels and stations.
