I. OPZEGGING
De Secretaris-Generaal van de Caraïbische Organisatie heeft, in overeenstem-
ming met artikel IV, lid 3, van het Statuut, van de volgende leden van de Organi-
satie een mededeling van uittreding ontvangen:
De Regering van de Verenigde Staten van Amerika heeft, in over-
eenstemming met artikel V, lid 3, van de Overeenkomst, van de
volgende Staten een kennisgeving van opzegging ontvangen:
i) De tekst van de kennisgeving van de Regering van de Federatie luidde als
volgt:
" As you will readily understand, the future of the Federation of The
West Indies, following the Jamaica referendum decision of the 19th September,
1961, is in the balance and, in consequence, certain difficulties are being expe-
rienced which make it doubtful whether the Federal Government would be able,
much longer, to continue meeting its financial obligations as a Member of the
Organization. Accordingly, to avoid any embarrassment to the Organization and
to Member Governments, I am directed to give you notice under the terms
of paragraph 3 of Article IV of the Statute of the Caribbean Organization that
The West Indies hereby elects to cease to be a member of the Organization effec-
tively one year from the date of the receipt of this letter by you or (sc one year)
from the 31st December 1961, whichever is the later.

De Vierde Zitting van de Carai'bische Raad werd van 23 tot 27
September 1963 te San Juan (Puerto Rico) gehouden. Tijdens deze
Zitting bevestigde de Raad de resolutie welke was aangenomen tijdens
zijn Derde Zitting, en drong er op aan dat de Regeringen van de
Staten voor wie de Overeenkomst was ondertekend, zo spoedig moge-
lijk de noodzakelijke stappen zouden ondernemen om het doel van
die resolutie te verwezenlijken.
De Vijfde Zitting van de Carai'bische Raad werd van 26 november
tot 4 december 1964 te Willemstad (Curasao) gehouden. Tijdens
deze Zitting werd de volgende resolutie aangenomen:
"The Caribbean Council, at its Fifth Meeting taking into consider-
ation the implications of the decision of three Members of the
Caribbean Organization to withdraw, and the financial consequence
thereof:
Decides that the operations of the Central Secretariat shall be
terminated not later than December 31, 1965;
Requests that the Parties Signatory to the Agreement Establishing
the Caribbean Organization take such steps as are necessary to termi-
nate the Agreement on December 31, 1965, or as soon thereafter
as possible;
Recommends that a Working Group comprising all Members,
including the withdrawing Members, the Special Observers, and all
other interested democratic countries in the area, convene at the
earliest possible date to develop a formula or a series of formulae
to permit and provide for regional cooperation in the social, eco-
nomic, educational, and cultural fields;
Recommends further that before April 15, 1965, a meeting of
the above-mentioned governments be convened at the highest possible
level to decide the form and nature of regional cooperation.".
Uitgegeven de zevenentwintigste december 1966.
