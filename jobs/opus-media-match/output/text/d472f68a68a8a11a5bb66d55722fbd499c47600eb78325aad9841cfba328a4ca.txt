IMMEDIATE RELEASE MAY 26, 1945

EXECUTIVE ORDER

REINSTATING CERTAIN FORMER FOREIGN SERVICE OFFICERS

By virtue of and pursuant to the authority vested in me by
section 12 of the act of February 23, 1931, 46 Stat. 1207, 1208, it
4s ordered that Ellis 0. Briggs and Loy W. Henderson, who resigned their
positions as officers in the Foreign Service of the United States on,»
April 22, 1944 and July 8, 1943 to accept appointment as Ambassador X
Extraordinary and Plenipotentiary of the United States to the Dominican
Republic, and as Envoy Extraordinary and Minister Plenipotentiary of
the United States to Iraq, respectively, be and they are hereby,
reinstated in the Foreign Service of the United States in Class I,
their reinstatement to be effective upon the date their nominations to

positions in such class are confirmed by the Senate.

HARRY S. TRUMAN

THE WHITE HOUSE,

May 26, 1945.
