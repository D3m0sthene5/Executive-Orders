Presidential Documents
Title 3—THE PRESIDENT

Executive Order 11039
EXTENSION OF THE PRESIDENT'S COMMISSION ON CAMPAIGN COSTS

By virtue of the authority vested in me as President of the United
States, it is ordered that Executive Order No. 10974 of November 8,
1961, be, and it is hereby, amended, effective as of July 1, 1962, as
follows:

(1) By inserting before the period at the end of the first sentence
of Section 5 a comma and the following: “and from any thereto cor-
responding appropriation available for the fiscal year 1963”,

(2) By substituting for the last sentence of the order the following:
The Commission shall terminate as of November 1, 1962.”

Joun F, Kexxepy

‘Tum Warre House,
August 3, 1962.
