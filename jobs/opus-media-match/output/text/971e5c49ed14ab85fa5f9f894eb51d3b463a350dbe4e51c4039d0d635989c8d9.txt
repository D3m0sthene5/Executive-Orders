THE PRESIDENT 3879
Executive Order 1 1 333
PARTIALLY SUSPENDING SECTION 6374 OF TITLE 1 0 OF THE UNITED 
STATES CODE, RELATING TO RETIREMENT OF BRIGADIER GENERALS 
OF THE MARINE CORPS
By virtue of the authority vested in me by section 6386 (c) of title 10 
of the United States Code, I hereby suspend until June 29, 1968, the 
provisions of section 6374 of title 10 of the United States Code, but 
only with respect to a brigadier general on the active list of the Marine 
Corps whose second failure of selection for promotion to the grade 
of major general occurred during the same fiscal year as his first failure 
of selection for such promotion.
