THE PRESIDENT 14277
Executive Order 11481
THE HONORABLE EVERETT McKINLEY DIRKSEN
In further respect to the memory of the Honorable Everett McKinley 
Dirksen, late Minority Leader of the Senate of the United States, I  
direct that on the day of his interment, September 11, 1969, the flag 
of the United States shall be flown at half-staff on all buildings, 
grounds and naval vessels of the Federal Government in the District 
of Columbia and throughout the United States and its Territories 
and possessions.
I  also direct that the flag shall be flown at half-staff for the same 
length of time at all United States embassies, legations, consular 
offices, and other facilities abroad, including all military facilities 
and naval vessels and stations.
T h e W h ite H ouse,
September 10,1969.
