This appropriation provides for the Federal share of the 
annual expenses of the Commission and a portion of the 
Federal share of the costs of the water quality program. 
The compact provides that the amount required to balance 
the Commission's current expense budget shall be appor­
tioned equitably among the signatory parties by unani­
mous vote of the Commission. The amount recommended 
for the Federal contribution of the Commission’s annual 
expense budget for 1969 is approximately 24% , exclu­
sive of the the water quality program.
