AGREED MINUTE TO THE TREATY ON THE
FINAL SETTLEMENT WITH RESPECT TO
GERMANY OF 12 SEPTEMBER 1990

Any questions with respect to the application of the
word “deployed” as used in the last sentence of
Paragraph 3 of Article § will be decided by the
Government of the united Germany in a reasonable and
responsible way taking into account the security
interests of each Contracting Party as set forth in the
preasble.
