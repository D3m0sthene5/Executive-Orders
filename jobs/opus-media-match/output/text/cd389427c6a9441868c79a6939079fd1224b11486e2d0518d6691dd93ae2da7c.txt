SUBJECT: Commissioned Corps Deployments 
 
1. PURPOSE:  The purpose of this Directive is to optimize the use of  officers by the Department of 
Health and Human Services (HHS or Departme nt), through its Operating Divisions and Staff 
Divisions (OPDIVs/STAFFDIVs) or non-HHS orga nizations, during periods when the Corps is 
activated for deployment while ensuring that t hese organizations do not experience a shortage of 
officers to meet their mission.  In doing so, this Directive implements the regulation: 
 
1-1. Defining the term Commissioned Corps deployment (Corps deployment); and 
 
1-2. Establishing the authorities for activating the Commissioned Corps of the U.S. Public 
Health Service (Corps) for deployment and in releasing officers from their assigned duties 
to respond to such deployments. 
 
2. APPLICABILITY: 
 
2-1. This Directive applies to all Regular and Reserve Corps officers whether serving on 
extended active-duty or in a reserve component with the exception of Section 2-2. 
 
2-2. This Directive does not apply to: 
 
a. Periods when the Corps has been declared a branch of the Armed Forces or 
when individuals or groups of officers  have been militarized by Executive Order 
(42 USC 217); and 
 
b. Officers who are detailed to a branch of the Armed Forces ( 42 USC 215(a)).  The 
Armed Forces include the U.S. Army, U. S. Navy, U.S. Air Force, U.S. Marine 
Corps, and U.S. Coast Guard.

6. POLICY: 
 
6-1. Corps deployment is defined as a directed, temporary assignment of officers from their 
assigned duties within HHS OPDIVs/STAFFDIVs and non-HHS organizations, as 
applicable, authorized by the President or Secr etary in response during a time of war or 
in response to: 
 
a. A national emergency as declared by the President; 
 
b. A public health emergency as declar ed by the President or Secretary;  
 
c. An urgent public health need: 
 
(1) A critical staffing shortage of health  care/public health personnel within a 
facility or program administered by  an OPDIV/STAFFDIV or non-HHS 
organization that threatens the health and safety of the affected 
population; 
 
(2) A crisis response  is a situation presenting a significant threat to the 
public health of a State, Tribe, or local community, and available 
resources are inadequate to respond; or 
 
d. A National Special Security Event  declared by the Secretary of Homeland 
Security, is of national significance, requires special security, and is coordinated 
at the national response level. 
 
6-2. To ensure that the Department, th rough its OPDIVs/STAFFDIVs, or non-HHS 
organizations, do not experience a shortage of  officers to meet their mission, officers 
shall not be released from their regularly assigned duties to respond to the activities 
listed in Section 6-1 unless: 
 
a. Authorized to do so by the Secretary; or 
 
b. If detailed outside HHS, pursuant to  the detail agreement between HHS and the 
non-HHS organization. 
 
6-3. Nothing in this Directive shall be construed to limit:   
 
a. An OPDIV/STAFFDIV or non-HHS or ganization Head or designee from 
authorizing officers to perform duties in furtherance of their organization’s 
statutory and/or departmental missio n except under circumstances determined 
by the Secretary. 
 
b. The participation of officers in educat ion and/or training activities related to 
Corps deployment(s) and/or force readiness under policies established by the 
Assistant Secretary of Health (ASH). 
 
c. The participation of officers in the Corp s’ leave program and/or outside activities 
program in accordance with applicable policies and procedures and/or detail 
agreement if detailed to a non-HHS organization.

7. RESPONSIBILITIES: 
 
7-1. The authority to activate the Corps and release officers for Corps deployment(s) shall 
reside with the President and the Secretary wi th the exception that further delegation of 
the Secretary’s authority may be made to the Deputy Secretary or ASH under 
circumstances determined by the Secretary. 
 
7-2. The authority to promulgate policies implementing this Directive shall reside with the 
ASH without further delegation.  Consistent with this Directive and other regulations or 
issuances by the Secretary or ASH, t he ASH through the Surgeon General or 
designee(s) shall exercise field command of officers during deployments. 
 
8. HISTORICAL NOTES:  This is the first Directive on Corps deployments.
