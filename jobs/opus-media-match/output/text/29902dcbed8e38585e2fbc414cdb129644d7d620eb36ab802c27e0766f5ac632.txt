THE PRESIDENT 19063
EXECUTIVE ORDER 11620
The Honorable Hugo L . Black
As a mark of respect to the memory of the Honorable Hugo L. Black, 
former Associate Justice of the.United States, it is hereby ordered, pur­
suant to the provisions of Section 4 of Proclamation 3044 of March 1, 
1954, as amended, that until interment the flag of the United States shall 
be flown at half-staff on all buildings, grounds and naval vessels of the 
Federal Government in the District of Columbia and throughout the 
United States and its Territories and possessions. I also direct that the 
flag shall be flown at half-staff for the same length of time at all United 
States embassies, legations, consular offices, and other facilities abroad, 
including all military facilities and naval vessels and stations.
T h e W h ite H o u se,
September 25, 1971.
