THE PRESIDENT 20261
Executive Order 11857 May 7, 1975
Amending Executive Order Nos. 11803,1  11837,2  and 11842 3  To Pro­
vide Authority To Increase the Number of Members of the Presiden­
tial Clemency Board
By virtue of the authority vested in me as President of the United States  
by Section 2 of Article II of the Constitution of the United States, Section  
1  of Executive Order No. 11803 of September 16, 1974, is hereby-  
amended as follows:
By adding the following at the end of the last sentence, “The President  
may appoint such additional members to the board as he shall from time  
to time determine to be necessary to carry out its functions.”
