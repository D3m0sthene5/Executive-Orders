Title 3—The President
EXECUTIVE ORDER 11823
Abolishing the National Commission for Industrial Peace

By virtue of the authority vested in me as President of the United
States of America by the Constitution and laws of the United States,
including the Federal Advisory Gommittee Act (5 U.S.C. App. I), and
section 301 of title 3 of the United States Code, it is hereby ordered as
follows:

Szcrion 1. The National Commission for Industrial Peace is hereby
abolished and Executive Order No. 11710 of April 4, 1973, and Execu-
tive Order No. 11729 of July 12, 1973, are hereby revoked.

Src. 2. The Secretary of the Treasury shall be responsible for closing
the affairs of the Commision, and for the completion of any functions
that remain to be performed under the Federal Advisory Committee Act
with respect to the Commission and its report. In preparing the report
required by section 6(b) of that Act, the Secretary shall consult with the
Director of the Federal Mediation and Conciliation Service.
