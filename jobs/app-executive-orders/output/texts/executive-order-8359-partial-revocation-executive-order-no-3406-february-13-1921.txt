ALASKA

By virtue of the authority vested in me as President of the United States, Executive Order No. 3406 of February 13, 1921, withdrawing lands in Alaska for lighthouse purposes, is hereby revoked so far as it affects the parcel of land designated in that order as Parcel 26 "Mitkof Island, Wrangell Strait, Shown on U.S. Coast and Geodetic Survey Chart No. 8170-Sheet No. 15", and now designated as U.S. Survey No. 1626.

FRANKLIN D. ROOSEVELT

The White House,

March 2, 1940.
