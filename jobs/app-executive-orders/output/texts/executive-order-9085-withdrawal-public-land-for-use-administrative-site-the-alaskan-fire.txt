ALASKA

By virtue of the authority vested in me as President of the United States, it is ordered that the following-described public land in Alaska be, and it is hereby, withdrawn and reserved, subject to valid existing rights, for the use of the Alaskan Fire Control Service, General Land Office, Department of the Interior, as a site for a patrol station.

Copper River Meridian

T. 4 N., R. 2 W., sec. 23, E½SW¼ (unsurveyed); containing 80 acres.

FRANKLIN D ROOSEVELT

The White House,

March 4, 1942.
