Fourth Executive Order
DEER FLAT RESERVATION
IDAHO
That part of Executive Order No. 1032, of February 25, 1909, reserving Deer
Flat reservoir site, Idaho, together with the smallest legal subdivisions of land
adjoining the flow lines thereof, for use by the Department of Agriculture as a
preserve and breeding ground for native birds, is hereby revoked in so far as it
affects the following described lands as indicated upon the attached diagram forming
a part hereof:
BOISE MERIDIAN
In T. 3 N., R. 3 W., Lots 16, 17, 19 and 22, Sec. 30, Lots 1 and 2 Sec. 33.
