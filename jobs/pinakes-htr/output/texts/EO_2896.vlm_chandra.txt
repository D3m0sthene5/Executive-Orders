Executive Order
UTAH - P. 6 La. 10 f. 28
It is hereby ordered that the withdrawal until July 1, 1918, in aid of legislation, made by Executive Order of March 4, 1917 (No. 2542-A), under the Act of Congress approved June 25, 1910 (36 Stat., 847), as amended by the Act of August 24, 1912 (37 Stat., 497), be extended to March 5, 1919, in so far as it affects the hereinafter described lands in the State of Utah, in aid of the legislation proposed in pending S. Bills 2088 and 4103, 65th Congress.
If said legislation be not enacted before the adjournment of the last session of the Sixty-fifth Congress, and no other direction is given regarding the disposition of such lands, they will, on March 5, 1919, become subject to disposal under any law then applicable thereto without further order.
Such lands are described as follows:
In T. 14 N., R. 4 W., W½ SW¼ Sec. 5, all Sec. 6, N½, N¼ SW¼ and NE¼ SE¼ Sec. 7, E½ NE¼ Sec. 20;
In T. 14 N., R. 5 W., Lots 1, 2, 3, 5, 6, 7, SW¼ NE¼, W½ SE¼, SE¼ NW¼ and E½ SW¼ Sec. 1, NE¼ NW¼, NE¼ and N¼ SE¼ Sec. 12;
In T. 13 N., R. 17 W., SW¼ and SW¼ SE¼ Sec. 5, NE¼ SE¼ Sec. 8, NW¼ SW¼, S½ SW¼ and SW¼ SE¼ Sec. 9, W½ NE¼, SE¼ NE¼ and SE¼ Sec. 17;
In T. 13 N., R. 18 W., W½ Sec. 1, NE¼ NE¼ Sec. 9, N½ Sec. 10, NE¼ SE¼, W½ SE¼, E½ SW¼ and NW¼ Sec. 11, E½, E¼ W½ and W½ NW¼ Sec. 12, SE¼ SE¼ Sec. 23, S½ NW¼ and SW¼ Sec. 24, W½ NW¼ and NW¼ SW¼ Sec. 25, E½ NE¼ and SE¼ Sec. 26, Lot 4 Sec. 30;
In T. 13 N., R. 19 W., E½ Sec. 11, W½ and SE¼ Sec. 15, Lots 1, 2 and NE¼ Sec. 21, W½ NW¼ Sec. 22, S½ Sec. 27, SE¼ Sec. 28, Lots 1, 2, 3, 4, SE¼ SE¼, N½ SE¼ and NE¼ Sec. 33, all Sec. 34;
In T. 4 N., R. 5 E., S½ SW¼ Sec. 10, W½ NW¼ and NW¼ SW¼ Sec. 24;
In T. 5 N., R. 5 E., S½ SW¼, SW¼ SE¼ Sec. 26, N½ NW¼, SW¼ NW¼, W½ SW¼, NE¼ SW¼, N½ SE¼ and SE¼ SE¼ Sec. 34;
