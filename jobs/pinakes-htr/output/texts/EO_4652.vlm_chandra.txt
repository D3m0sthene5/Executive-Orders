Executive Order
CALIFORNIA AND NEVADA
Under authority of the act of Congress approved June 25, 1910 (36 Stat., 847), as amended by the act of August 24, 1912 (37 Stat., 497), it is hereby ordered that the following described lands in the States of California and Nevada be, and they are hereby withdrawn from settlement, location, sale or entry, subject to prior rights and the conditions and limitations of said acts, for use by the Department of Commerce as air mail beacon sites and landing fields:
California
In T. 9 N., R. 2 W., S. B. M., NW¼ Sec. 20;
In T. 12 N., R. 7 E., S. B. M., S½ SE¼ Sec. 11;
In T. 13 N., R. 8 E., S. B. M., W½ Sec. 1;
In T. 15 N., R. 11 E., S. B. M., Sec. 4 except 4.16 acres in patented mineral entry;
In T. 16 N., R. 12 E., S. B. M., NE¼, S½ NW¼, S½ Sec. 21, N½ NE¼, N½ NW¼ and SW½ NW¼ Sec. 28;
In T. 16 N., R. 13 E., S. B. M., E½ Sec. 10 and W½ Sec. 11.
Nevada
In T. 25 S., R. 59 E., M. D. M., S½ SE¼ Sec. 11, NE¼, E½ NW¼, SW¼ NW¼, SW¼, N½ SE¼ and SW¼ SE¼ Sec. 14;
In T. 23 S., R. 61 E., M. D. M., NE¼ NW¼ Sec. 17.
