SPECIAL RULE

James M. Cooper and Ralston Williams may be employed by the board of medical officers appointed to investigate the origin and spread of typhoid fever in U.S. military camps in 1898 until the completion of the report of the board.

Theodore Roosevelt

WHITE HOUSE.
Approved February 5, 1904.
