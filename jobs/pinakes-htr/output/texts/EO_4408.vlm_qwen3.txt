Executive Order

It is hereby ordered, under authority contained in Section 5 of the Act of
February 8, 1887 (24 Stat. L., 388) that the trust period on the following allotments,
made to Grande Ronde Indians in Oregon, be, and the same is hereby, extended
for a further period of ten years, from present date of expiration.
