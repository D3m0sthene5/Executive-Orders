EXECUTIVE ORDER
AUTHORIZING THE ⟦ALIEN⟧ PROPERTY CUSTODIAN
TO SELL AT PRIVATE SALE CERTAIN SHARES
OF STOCK OF THE WESTERN MARYLAND RAIL-
WAY COMPANY
By virtue of the authority vested in me by an act of
Congress entitled "An act to ⟦confine,⟧ regulate, and punish ⟦trad-⟧
⟦ing⟧ with the enemy and for other purposes", approved October 6,
1917, known as the "Trading with the Enemy Act", and the ⟦amend-⟧
⟦ments⟧ to such Act embodied in "An act making appropriations to
supply urgent deficiencies in the appropriations for the fiscal
year ending June 30th, 1918, and prior fiscal years on account
of war expenses and for other purposes", approved March ⟦20th,⟧
1918, and the amendment to such act embodied in "An Act making
appropriations to supply deficiencies in appropriations for the
fiscal year ending June 30th, 1919, and prior fiscal years on
account of war expenses, and for other purposes", approved
November 4th, ⟦1919,⟧ and all other amendments thereto, to make
certain orders such as that herein contained, I hereby, in the
public interest, make the following determination, order, rule
and regulations:
The ⟦Alien⟧ Property Custodian is hereby authorized to
sell at private sale at such time as to him or his authorized
agent may seem advisable without public or other advertisement
⟦15,000⟧ shares of First Preferred Stock and ⟦65,000⟧ shares of
Common Stock of the Western Maryland Railway Company, a ⟦corpor-⟧
⟦ation⟧ incorporated under the laws of the States of Maryland and
Pennsylvania, of $100.00 par value, heretofore required to be
conveyed, transferred, assigned and delivered to the ⟦Alien⟧
Property Custodian as the property of the ⟦Deutsche⟧ Bank of
Berlin.
⟦My⟧ ⟦reasons⟧ for the foregoing determination and order are:
First: That no dividends have been paid upon either the common or preferred stock of said corporation since the ⟦seizure⟧ of the stock herein ordered to be sold and that there has been such a variation and fluctuation in the earnings applicable to ⟦said⟧ stock over a period of several years as to preclude its classification as a proper investment for Trust funds.
Second: That the Deutsche Bank to whose credit said stock is held has requested the Alien Property Custodian to sell said stock and to accept an offer of ⟦$4,010,000.net,⟧ by the Bank of Manhattan Company, New York City, which offer appears to be an advantageous one for said stock. The investment of the proceeds from said sale in Liberty Bonds or Certificates of Indebtedness of the United States Government would result in a fair income from the investment and would prevent a possible loss of income through a decline of earnings of the corporation and would conserve the principal by reason of decline in the market price.
Third: That the sale of said stock will result in the realization in tangible form of an increase in principal by virtue of the appreciation of the market value of the stock since its ⟦seizure⟧ by the Alien Property Custodian.
