EXECUTIVE ORDER.
The provisions of an Executive Order approved September 14,
⟦1915,⟧ and ⟦extended⟧ June 19, 1916 and July 9, 1917, to permit positions
of ⟦which⟧ in the State, War and Navy Department Building to be filled,
without compliance with the Civil Service law and rules during the
fiscal years 1916, 1917 and 1918, when filled by men who have served
at least two enlistments in the Army, Navy or Marine Corps, and who have
been discharged as non-commissioned officers with character excellent,
are hereby modified so as to permit such positions to be filled without
compliance with the Civil Service law and rules during the period of
the war when filled by men who have served in the Army, Navy or
Marine Corps and who have been honorably discharged therefrom, with
character excellent.
The White House.
November, 1917.
