Erecutive Order.
It is hereby ordered, under authority conferred by the 5th section of
the act of February 8, 1887 (24 Stat. L., 388), that the trust period on the
allotments of Indians on the Yankton Sioux Reservation, South Dakota,
which expires during the calendar years 1916 and 191g, be, and is hereby,
extended for a period of ten years from the dates of expiration, with the
exception of the following:
