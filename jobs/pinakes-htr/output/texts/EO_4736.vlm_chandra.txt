4736
Executive Order
Mrs. Alice R. Ball may be reinstated to an appropriate subclerical position in the Bureau of Engraving and Printing without regard to the length of her separation from the service. Mrs. Ball, as Alice R. Pope, served from 1912 to 1917 in the Bureau of Engraving and Printing, having been appointed thru competitive examination. Her husband, Marion J. Ball, a baker, was killed in an automobile accident on July 24, 1927, leaving her without means of support and with a young son and aged mother dependent upon her. The Secretary of the Treasury recommends her appointment. The Civil Service Commission is unable to concur.
For charity.
CALVIN COOLIDGE
THE WHITE HOUSE,
October 4 1927.
[No. 4736]
Class. Civ Serv. - Mftd, Reg as to
