Executive Mansion
January 2° - 1874.

By virtue of the authority granted
by section sixteen of the act entitled
"An act to regulate the diplomatic
and Consular systems of the United
States", approved August 18, 1856, the
Tariff of Fees prescribed by me on
October 1, 1870 is hereby amended
so that hereafter a single fee of twenty
five cents shall be charged for the
services required in sealing ears
coming from countries adjacent to the
United States, as described in Fees
108 and 109, instead of a fee of twenty
five cents for each of those services
as heretofore.

[signature]
