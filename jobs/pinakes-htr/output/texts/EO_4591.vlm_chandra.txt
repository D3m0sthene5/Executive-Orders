Executive Order
CALIFORNIA
Executive order of December 1, 1913, creating Public Water Reserve No. 13, California No. 1, is hereby revoked as to S. \frac{1}{4} NE. \frac{1}{4} and N. \frac{1}{4} SE. \frac{1}{4}, Sec. 22, T. 27 N., R. 1 E., S. B. M., and the tract described is hereby restored to entry.
CALVIN COOLIDGE
