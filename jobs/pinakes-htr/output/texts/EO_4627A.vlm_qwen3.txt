EXECUTIVE ORDER.

AUTHORIZING THE ALLEN PROPERTY CUSTODIAN TO SELL
CERTAIN SECURITIES UPON THE NEW YORK STOCK EXCHANGE

By virtue of the authority vested in me by an
Act of Congress entitled "An act to define, regulate, and punish trad-
ing with the enemy and for other purposes", approved October 4, 1917,
known as the "Trading with the Enemy Act", and the amendments to such
Act embodied in "An act making appropriations to supply urgent deficien-
cies in the appropriations for the fiscal year ending June 30th, 1918,
and prior fiscal years on account of war expenses and for other purposes",
approved March 26th, 1918, and the amendment to such act embodied in "An
Act making appropriations to supply deficiencies in appropriations for
the fiscal year ending June 30th, 1919, and prior fiscal years on account
of war expenses, and for other purposes", approved November 4th, 1919,
and all other amendments thereto, to make certain orders such as that
herein contained, I hereby, in the public interest, make the following
determination, order, rule and regulation:

The Allen Property Custodian is hereby authorized
to sell upon the New York Stock Exchange, at such time as to him or to his
authorized agent may seem advisable, without public or other advertisement,
Two thousand One hundred Eleven (2111) shares of Second Preferred stock
and Three thousand Four hundred (3400) shares of Common stock of Western
Maryland Railway Company, a corporation organized under the laws of Mary-
land and Pennsylvania, of One Hundred Dollars ($100.00) par value, hereto-
fore required to be conveyed, transferred, assigned and delivered to the
Allen Property Custodian as the property of the Deutsche Bank of Berlin

- 1 -

- 2 -

By reasons for the foregoing determination

and order are:

First: That, considering the earning capacity and
immediate prospects of the Western Maryland Railway Company, it is
believed the current prices at which the Second Preferred and Common
shares are selling represent their fair market value. No dividends
have been paid on either class of stock since seizure by the Alien
Property Custodian.

Second: That the sale of said stock at current prices
will result in realization in tangible form of a substantial increase
in principal by virtue of the appreciation in the market value of the
stock since seizure, and the investment of the proceeds in Liberty Loan
Bonds or Certificates of Indebtedness of the United States Government
would insure a return on the fund.

Third: That the Deutsche Bank, Berlin, Germany, for
whose account said stock is held has consented to and requested the Alien
Property Custodian to negotiate the sale of the shares at the prevailing
market prices.

Fourth: That, as the stock is listed on the New York
Stock Exchange and has an active and established market, a public or
other advertisement of the sale of the said stock or attempted sale at
auction would be a useless formality involving unnecessary expense and
would serve no useful purpose.

THE WHITE HOUSE,
