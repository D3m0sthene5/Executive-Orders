EXHIBIT A
AUTHORIZING THE ALLIED PROPERTY CUSTODIAN TO SELL,
CERTAIN PROPERTY UPON THE NEW YORK STOCK EXCHANGE

- 2 -
My reasons for the foregoing determination and order
are:
First: That the dividend record of the corporation over a period of several years is not such as to justify its classification as an investment stock and that the income return upon the investment, based on the current market quotations, is too small to warrant the retention of the principal in a security, the market value of which fluctuates so widely.
Second: That the sale of the said stock and the investment of the proceeds in Liberty Bonds or Certificates of Indebtedness of the United States Government would insure a fair income upon the investment and conserve the principal, preventing possible depreciation by reason of a decline in the market price.
Third: That the sale of the said stock will result in the realization in tangible form of a great increase in principal by virtue of the appreciation of the market value of the stock since the time of its issue and since its seizure by the Alien Property Custodian.
Fourth: That a public or other advertisement of the sale of the said stock or attempted sale at auction, would be a useless formality involving unnecessary expenses and inconvenience, and would cause such a delay as to imperil the realization of a price equal to that now prevailing on the open market.
(L. B. ...)
THE WHITE HOUSE,
March 20, 1925.
