Executive Order
CALIFORNIA Pet lands of WS
The classification contemplated by the Act of Congress approved March 3, 1919 (40 Stat., 1316), having been effected, it is hereby ordered that Executive Order of May 4, 1919, No. 3085, temporarily withdrawing certain lands in California in aid thereof, be and the same is hereby revoked.
The greater portion of the area so withdrawn has been included in the Modoc National Forest by Proclamation of even date herewith, and the remaining lands are described as follows:
