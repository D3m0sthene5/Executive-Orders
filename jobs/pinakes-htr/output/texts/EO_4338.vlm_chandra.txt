Executive Order
WYOMING
Under authority of the act of Congress approved June 25, 1910 (36 Stat., 847), as amended by the act of August 24, 1912 (37 Stat., 497), it is hereby ordered that the following described lands in the State of Wyoming be, and they are hereby temporarily withdrawn, subject to the conditions of said acts, pending determination as to the suitability of the area for a federal game refuge:
In T. 43 N., R. 7½ W., 6th P.M., SW¼ NW¼, SW¼ and S½ SE¼ Sec. 4, N½ and SE¼ Sec. 5, E½ NE¼ Sec. 6, N½, NE¼ SE¼ Sec. 9, W½ NW¼ and NW¼ SW¼ Sec. 10;
In T. 44 N., R. 75 W., SE¼ Sec. 31, and SW¼ SW¼ Sec. 32.
