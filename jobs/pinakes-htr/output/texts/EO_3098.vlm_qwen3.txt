Executive Order.
ARIZONA

It is hereby ordered that Executive Order of September 16, 1918 (No. 2964), temporarily withdrawing, under the Act of Congress approved June 25, 1910 (36 Stat., 847) as amended, the therein described lands in Arizona be, and the same is hereby, revoked in so far as it affects the following described lands:

In T. 15 N., R. 3 E., NW¼ SW¼, S½ S½ Sec. 1, Secs. 2, 3 and 4, NE¼ Sec. 9, Secs. 10, 11, 12, 13 and 14;

In T. 16 N., R. 3 E., NW¼ SW¼, S½ SW¼ Sec. 15, NE¼ and S½ Sec. 16, NW¼ and S½ Sec. 17, Secs. 18, 19, 20 and 21, W½ and SW¼ SE¼ Sec. 22, SW¼ and SW¼ SE¼ Sec. 26, NW¼ NE¼, S½ NE¼, NW¼ and S½ Sec. 27, Secs. 28 and 29, N½ and SE¼ Sec. 30, E½ Sec. 31, Secs. 32, 33, 34 and 35;

Gila & Salt River Meridian.

WOODROW WILSON

THE WHITE HOUSE,
19 June, 1919.
