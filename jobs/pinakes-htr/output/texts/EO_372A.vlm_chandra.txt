In accordance with the provisions of Sections 2250 and 2252 of the Revised Statutes of the United States, and by virtue of the authority therein given, it is hereby ordered that:
The Bent land district in the State of Colorado, with office at Lamar, Colorado, be discontinued and the lands, business and archives of said land district and office transferred to and consolidated with the Arkansas Valley land district, with office at Pueblo, Colorado.
THE WHITE HOUSE.
March 1, 1905.

WAR DEPARTMENT,
WASHINGTON,
November 20, 1905.
THE PRESIDENT:
I have the honor to recommend that all the lands included within the limits of the military reservation of Fort St. Michael, Alaska, declared by Executive Order of October 20, 1897 (G. O. 59, Headquarters of the Army, October 20, 1897), reduced by Executive Order of April 15, 1899 (G. O. 77, Headquarters of the Army, April 20, 1899), and further reduced by Executive Order of October 27, 1900 (G. O. 132, Headquarters of the Army, November 5, 1900), except the lands hereinafter described, which are to be retained for military purposes, be placed under the control of the Secretary of the Interior for disposition under the Act of July 5, 1894 (25 Stat. L., 103), or as may be otherwise provided by law, said lands having become useless for military purposes.
The lands to be held in reservation for military purposes are bounded and described as follows:

Co.'s hotel building on the south shore of the eastern end of St. Michael Island, and is marked by a post of lava (one foot square, marked on the faces with the initials U. S. N. The northern boundary of the reservation is Morton Sound, the southern boundary is the Bay of St. Michael, and the reservation includes all land between high and low water mark and all tidal lands lying between the eastern and western boundary lines extended. All bearings are true.
Also a strip of land, fifty feet wide, along the pipe line extending from the reservation for Wireless Telegraph Station to the reservation for the post of Fort St. Michael; the center line of said strip being the center line of the said pipe line.
TARGET RANGE (FOR COLLECTIVE FIRE). S. N. R., one letter on each face) bearing on the N. Wireless pole 90° E. of magnetic meridian and on the comb of the Quartermaster's Depot, 20° 42' E. of magnetic meridian. Magnetic variation 21° 30' 40" E.
In order that this action may not operate as a surprise upon parties having possession of sites within the reservation, for business purposes, under licenses from the War Department, and in order to give such parties opportunity to take steps to present their papers for the purpose of obtaining title to their holdings from the Interior Department, it is recommended that the transfer herein recommended take effect on November 1, 1906.
Very respectfully,
W. L. King, Secretary of War.
