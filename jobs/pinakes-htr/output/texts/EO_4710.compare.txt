Executive Order
ALASKA
Under authority of the act of Congress approved June 25, 1910 (36 Stat., 847),
as amended by the act of August 24, 1912 (37 Stat., 497), it is hereby ordered that
the tracts of land in Alaska, lying within the following described boundaries, and
containing approximately six acres, be, and the same are hereby, temporarily ⟦with-⟧
⟦drawn⟧ from settlement, location, sale or entry, except as provided by said acts, for
classification, subject to the right of way of the Glacier Highway:
Tract A: Beginning at corner No. 1, meandering Mendenhall
River a post 4" square set in a mound of rock and scribed F. S. C-1;
thence N. 56° 30' W. 81 feet, S. 54° 30' W. 100 feet, S. 31° 00' W. 100
feet, S. 2° 30' E. 67 feet to center of Glacier Highway at the south end
of Mendenhall Bridge; thence along the center of the Glacier Highway
S. 72° 30' E. 100 feet, S. 51° 30' E. 100 feet, N. 8° 00' W. 50 feet to
corner No. 2 on approximately the north side of the Glacier Highway,
a post 4" square set in a mound of rock and scribed F. S. C-2; thence
N. 9° 30' E. approximately 215 feet to corner No. 1, the place of
beginning:
Tract B: Beginning at the south end of Mendenhall Bridge at the
center of the Glacier Highway at a point identical with the meander
corner at the end of the Mendenhall Bridge described in field notes of
Tract "A"; thence S. 72° 30' E. 100 feet, S. 51° 30' E. 100 feet, S.
34° 00' E. 100 feet, S. 19° 00' E. 100 feet, S. 2° 00' W. 100 feet, S.
16° 00' W. 100 feet, S. 14° 00' W. 100 feet, S. 13° 00' E. 100 feet, S.
44° 00' E. 100 feet, S. 52° 30' E. 100 feet, S. 64° 00' E. 100 feet, S.
74° 30' E. 100 feet, N. 88° 00' E. 100 feet, N. 81° 00' E. 100 feet, N.
79° 30' E. 100 feet, N. 81° 00' E. 100 feet, N. 84° 00' E. 100 feet, N.
76° 30' E. 100 feet, S. 5° 00' E. 30 feet to corner No. 3 on the south
edge of Glacier Highway right-of-way, a post approximately 4" square
set in a mound of rock and scribed F. S. C-3; thence S. 5° 00' E. 225
feet to corner No. 4, an alder tree approximately 10" in diameter
blazed and scribed F. S. C-4; thence meandering the bank of ⟦Menden-⟧
⟦hall⟧ River 1,865 feet to center of Glacier Highway at the south end of
Mendenhall Bridge, the place of beginning.
This order will continue in full force and effect unless and until revoked by the
President or by act of Congress.
CALVIN COOLIDGE
