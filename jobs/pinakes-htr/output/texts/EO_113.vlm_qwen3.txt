TELEGRAM RECEIVED.  Ext. 2 149  Feb 8 1899
From Executive Mansion,
Feb 8, 1899.
Received 11.50 P.M.

The Members of the Cabinet,

The President directs that the
Flags in all the Departments
be half mast ed at noon
today during the removal
of the remains of the
Late Major General and
Secretary of War John A.
Tewain to Arlington Cemetery.

J. C. Porter,
Copy to the President.
