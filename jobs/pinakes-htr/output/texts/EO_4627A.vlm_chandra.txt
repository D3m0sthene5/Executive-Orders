EXECUTIVE ORDER.
713
DEPARTMENT OF JUSTICE
AUTHORIZING THE ALLEN PROPERTY CUSTODIAN TO SELL
CERTAIN SECURITIES UPON THE NEW YORK STOCK EXCHANGE
-
[model output cut here: it began to repeat itself]

- 2 -
By reasons for the foregoing determination
and order are:
First: That, considering the earning capacity and
immediate prospects of the Western Maryland Railway Company, it is
believed the current prices at which the Second Preferred and Common
shares are selling represent their fair market value. No dividends
have been paid on either class of stock since seizure by the Alien
Property Custodian.
Second: That the sale of said stock at current prices
will result in realization in tangible form of a substantial insurance
in principal by virtue of the appreciation in the market value of the
stock since seizure, and the investment of the proceeds in Liberty Loan
Bonds or Certificates of Indentures of the United States Government
would insure a return on the fund.
Third: That the Deutsche Bank, Berlin, Germany, for
whose account said stock is held has consented to and requested the Alien
Property Custodian to negotiate the sale of the shares at the prevailing
market prices.
Fourth: That, as the stock is listed on the New York
Stock Exchange and has an active and established market, a public or
other advertisement of the sale of the said stock or attempted sale or
motion would be a useless formality involving unnecessary expense and
would serve no useful purpose.
THE WRITING HOUSE,
