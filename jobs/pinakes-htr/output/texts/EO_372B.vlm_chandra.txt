WAR DEPARTMENT.
November 20, 1905.
SECRETARY OF WAR----
Recommends that all lands included within the limits of the U. S. Military Reservation of Fort St. Michael, Alaska -- except those herein described, which are to be retained for military purposes -- be placed under the control of the Secretary of the Interior for disposition under the Act of July 4th, 1884 (23 Stat. 14, 102), or as may be otherwise provided by law, and that such transfer of such lands take effect on May 1, 1905.
THE WHITE HOUSE.
Nov 22 1905
The within recommendations are approved.
The Secretary of the Interior will cause this action to be noted on the files of the General Land Office.
J. Roosevelt
