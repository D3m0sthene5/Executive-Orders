Executive Order.

The area of land hereinafter described, to be known as Fort William D.
Davis Reservation, consisting of two parcels, situated in the Canal Zone, is
hereby set apart and assigned to the uses and purposes of a military reserva-
tion, under the jurisdiction of the Secretary of War; but said area shall be
subject to the civil control and jurisdiction of the Governor of the Panama
Canal in conformity with the Panama Canal Act.
