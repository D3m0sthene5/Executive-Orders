EXECUTIVE ORDER

AUTHORIZING THE ALIEN PROPERTY CUSTODIAN
TO SELL AS PRIVATE SALE CERTAIN SHARES
OF STOCK OF THE SCHNITZ & HENDERSON CO. (CARTY).

By virtue of the authority vested in me by an act of
Congress entitled "An act to define, regulate, and punish trading
with the enemy and for other purposes", approved October 6,
1917, known as the "Trading with the Enemy Act", and the amendments
to such act embodied in "An act making appropriations to
supply urgent deficiencies in the appropriations for the fiscal
year ending June 30th, 1918, and prior fiscal years on account
of war expenses and for other purposes", approved March 25th,
1918, and the amendment to such act embodied in "An act making
appropriations to supply deficiencies in appropriations for
the fiscal year ending June 30th, 1919, and prior fiscal years
on account of war expenses, and for other purposes", approved
November 4th, 1918, and all other amendments thereto, to make
certain orders such as that herein contained, I hereby, in the
public interest, make the following determination, order, rule
and regulations:

The Alien Property Custodian is hereby authorized to
sell at private sale without public or other advertisement three
thousand (3000) shares of the preferred capital stock and accumulated
dividends of the Schnitz & Henderson, Company, Philadelphia,
Pennsylvania, of a par value of $100.00 per share, heretofore required
to be canceled, transferred, and made no solicitation to the Alien
Property Custodian or the property of said licensing, canceled,
for Three Hundred Twenty-five Thousand ($325,000.00) dollars when

the following term, viz., One Hundred Fifty Thousand ($150,000.00)
Dollars in cash and the remainder, One Hundred Seventy-five Thousand
($175,000.00) Dollars, represented by a note payable July 1, 1926,
with interest at five (5%) per cent; the said stock to be held as
security by the Alien Property Custodian until the said note is paid.
By reasons for the foregoing determination and order are:
First: That the Schmitz & Coartin Company is a comparatively
small concern. It has outstanding $100,000.00 of common stock di-
vided into one thousand (1000) shares of $100.00 par value each,
and $500,000.00 of preferred stock divided into three thousand (3000)
shares of a like par value. The Alien Property Custodian holds the
entire issue of the preferred stock, but no common. Voting privi-
leges are in the common stock exclusively.
Second: That no dividends have ever been paid to the Alien
Property Custodian on the preferred stock so that ", is placed in
the position of holding: the entire issue of preferred stock of the
company without receiving any return thereon, and without the pos-
sibility of a voice in the management of the concern.
Third: That the stock is held for account of the estate
and the heirs of the estate have signified their willingness to ac-
cept this price and have requested: the Alien Property Custodian to
negotiate the sale on the terms as hereinbefore stated.
Fourth: That considering, the current position: and indicated
earning ability of the Company, it would appear the offer of Three
Hundred Twenty-five Thousand ($325,000.00) Dollars for the three
thousand (3000) shares of preferred stock and accumulated dividends
represents a fair market value. Also, that in view of the fact
that the common and preferred issues are closely held, there is
little public interest in the affairs of the company and the shares
have no established market. It is therefore believed that ad-
vertisement and public sale of the stock would involve unneces-
sary expense and would not result advantageously to the trust.
