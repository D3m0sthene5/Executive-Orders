EXECUTIVE ORDER
The Executive Order of February 9, 1905, providing for the transfer
of " the duties pertaining to the office of Commissioner of Railroads and
now devolving upon the fecretary of the Interior, together with all
records and files of said office, " to the Department of Commerce and
Labor, is hereby annulled.
Theodore Roosevelt
The White House
March 31, 1905
