Executive Order.

It is hereby ordered, under authority contained in section 5 of the act of
February 8, 1887 (24 Stat. L., 388–389), that the trust periods on the allotments of the Cheyenne and Arapaho Indians in Oklahoma, which trust expires during the calendar year 1917, be, and is hereby, extended for a period of 10 years from the dates of expiration, with the exception of the following:
