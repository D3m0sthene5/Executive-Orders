Executive Order
It is hereby ordered, under authority contained in Section 5 of the Act of
February 8, 1887 (24 Stat. L., 388), that the trust period on the following
allotments, made to the Eastern Shawnee Indians in Oklahoma be, and the same
is hereby, extended for a period of ten years:
