DEPT OF AGRIC. WALS & FLEET

Executive Order

NEVADA

It is hereby ordered that Executive Order No. 4042 dated July 2, 1924, with-
drawing the public lands in T. 17 N., R. 25 E., M. D. M., Nevada, pending a
resurvey, be and the same is hereby revoked.

All of said lands were withdrawn by the Secretary of the Interior July 2, 1902,
and August 26, 1902, under second form, act of June 17, 1902 (32 Stat., 388), which
was changed to first form of withdrawal, act of June 17, 1902, supra, by the Secre-
tary of the Interior February 6, 1908, August 13, 1908, and July 9, 1910. There-
fore they are not subject to appropriation.
