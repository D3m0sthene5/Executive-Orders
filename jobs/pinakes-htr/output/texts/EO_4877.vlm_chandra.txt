Executive Order
The Executive Order dated December 22, 1924, (No. 4114) establishing the McClellan National Forest, Alabama, pursuant to the provisions of Section 9 of the Act of Congress approved June 7, 1924, (43 Stat., 655) on a portion of the Camp McClellan Military Reservation, Alabama, is hereby revoked.
CALVIN COOLIDGE
THE WHITE HOUSE,
May 4, 1928.
[No. 4877]
DEPARTMENT OF STATE
