CHIEF CLERK
MAY
EXECUTIVE ORDER.
In accordance with the provisions of Sections 2248 and 2252 of the Revised Statutes of the United States, and by virtue of the authority therein given, it is hereby ordered that:
The Ironton land district in the State of Missouri, with office at Ironton, Missouri, be discontinued, and the lands, business and archives of said district be transferred to and consolidated with the Springfield land district, with office at Springfield, Missouri.
THE WHITE HOUSE,
Mary 11, 1905.
J. Roosevelt
