Executive Order
CALIFORNIA
Under authority of the act of Congress approved June 25, 1910 (36 Stat., 847), as amended by the act of August 24, 1912 (37 Stat., 497), it is hereby ordered that the following described tract in the State of California, be, and the same is hereby, temporarily withdrawn subject to the provisions and conditions of said acts, for classification and pending enactment of legislation for its proper disposition:
In T. 22 S., R. 29 E., M. D. M., Lot 1 Sec. 14.
CALVIN COOLIDGE
THE WHITE HOUSE,
August 22, 1925.
[No. 4287]
DEPARTMENT OF STATE.
September 9, 1925
