Executive Order

CALIFORNIA AND NEVADA

Under authority of the act of Congress approved June 25, 1910 (36 Stat., 847), as amended by the act of August 24, 1912 (37 Stat., 497), and subject to the conditions therein expressed, and on the recommendation of the Secretary of Agriculture, it is hereby ordered that the public lands within such of the following areas described in the act of Congress approved February 20, 1925, Public No. 428, as are not now parts of any national forest be, and the same are hereby, temporarily withdrawn in aid of the classification contemplated by said act, subject to valid claims and the provisions of existing withdrawals affecting certain of such lands:
