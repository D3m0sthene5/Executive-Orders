Executive Order

COLORADO

Under authority of the Act of Congress approved June 25, 1910 (36 Stat., 847), as amended by the Act of August 24, 1912 (37 Stat., 497), and on the recommendation of the Secretary of Agriculture, it is hereby ordered that the following described lands in the State of Colorado be temporarily withdrawn, subject to the conditions, provisions and limitations of said acts, and the provisions of the Act of Congress approved September 8, 1916 (Public No. 282), for classification and pending determination as to the advisability of including within the Colorado and Pike National Forests such of the lands as may be found to be chiefly valuable for the production of timber or the protection of stream flow:
