2. It is further ordered that the following lands included within the
Camp John Hay Military Reservation, situated in the city of Baguio,
Island of Luzon, sub Province of Benguet, Mountain Province, Philippine
Islands, reserved by Executive Orders of October 10, 1903, (General
Orders No. 48, War Department, November 3, 1903) and enlarged by
Executive Orders of January 23, 1907, (General Orders No. 22, War
Department, January 30, 1907) having become useless for military pur-
poses, are hereby restored to the Government of the Philippine Islands
to be administered for the benefit of the inhabitants thereof, under the
provisions of section 12 of the Act of Congress approved July 1, 1902.
(32 Stat. L., 691)
