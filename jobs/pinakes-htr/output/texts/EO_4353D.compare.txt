EXECUTIVE ORDER
Authorizing the Alien Property Custodian to sell certain
property upon the New York Stock Exchange.
By virtue of the authority vested in me by an Act of Congress
entitled "An Act to define, regulate, and punish trading with the
enemy and for other purposes," approved October 6, 1917, known as the
"Trading with the Enemy Act," and the amendments to such act embodied
in "An Act making appropriations for the fiscal year ending June 30th,
1918, and prior fiscal years on account of war expenses and for other
purposes," approved March 28th, 1918, and the amendment to such act
embodied in "An Act making appropriations to supply deficiencies in
appropriations for the fiscal year ending June 30th, 1918, and prior
fiscal years on account of war expenses, and for other purposes,"
approved November 4th, 1918, and all other amendments thereto, to make
certain orders such as that herein contained, I hereby, in the public
interest, make the following determination, order, rule and ⟦regulations:⟧
The Alien Property Custodian is hereby authorized to sell upon
the New York Stock Exchange, without previous advertising, at the price
bid for the same, as if, and when offered, the following securities
in such lots, and at such times as to him, or his authorized ⟦agent⟧ may
seem advisable, viz:
72 shares of the United Drug Company common stock, held in trust 464,
Report 1018, as the property of Julius ⟦Elkan.⟧
⟦The⟧ reasons for the foregoing determination and order are:
1st, That the beneficiary under this trust has requested the sale of
this property.
2nd, That the sale of the said stock and the investment of the proceeds
in Liberty Bonds or Certificates of Indebtedness of the United
States Government would insure a fair income upon the investment
and would prevent a possible loss of income through a decline of
earnings of the corporation and consequent reduction of the
dividend, and would conserve the principal, preventing a possible
depreciation thereof by reason of a decline in the market price.
3rd. That the sale of the said stock will result in the realization
in tangible form of a great increase in principal by virtue
of the appreciation of the market value of the stock since
its original purchase, and since its seizure by the Alien
Property Custodian.
4th. That a public or other advertisement of the sale of the said
stock or attempted sale at auction would be a useless ⟦formal-⟧
⟦ity⟧ involving unnecessary expense and inconvenience and would
cause such delay as to imperil the realization of a price
equal to that now prevailing on the open market.
The White House,
December 9, 1925.
