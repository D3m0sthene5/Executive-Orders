EXECUTIVE ORDER
APPOINTMENT OF MRS. CHARLES BROADY
The Public Printer under date of January 24, 1906,
reported that he had authorized the temporary appointment
of Mrs. Charles Broady in the Government Printing Office,
basing the action on a note received from the President,
a copy of which is given below:
"Mrs. Charles Broady of 43 Bates Street, Washing-
ton, D.C., is the widow of a soldier who served five
years in the Regular Army and resigned to accept a
position under the civil government at Manila and died
of cholera while in the service. She has one child, a
girl ten years of age, and no means of support.
Dear Mrs. Stiilings:
If this case is as deserving as it seems,
could the woman be given at least a temporary place?
(signed) T. Roosevelt
