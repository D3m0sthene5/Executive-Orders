AUTHORIZING THE ALIEN PROPERTY CUSTODIAN
TO SELL CERTAIN PROPERTY AT PRIVATE SALE.
SEP 3 1924
DEPARTMENT OF STATE

By virtue of the authority vested in me by an Act of Congress
entitled "An Act to define, regulate and punish trading with the enemy
and for other purposes", approved October 6, 1917, known as the "Trading
with the Enemy Act", and the amendment to such act embodied in
"an act making appropriations to supply urgent deficiencies in the
appropriations for the fiscal year ending June 30, 1918, and prior
fiscal years on account of war expenses and for other purposes", ap-
proved March 26, 1918, and the amendment to such act embodied in "An
act making appropriations to supply deficiencies in appropriations for
the fiscal year ending June 30, 1919, and prior fiscal years on account
of war expenses, and for other purposes", approved November 4, 1918,
to make certain orders such as that herein contained, I hereby, in
the public interest, make the following determination, order, rule and
regulation:

The Alien Property Custodian is hereby authorized to sell at pri-
vate sale, at such time and place as to him or to his authorized agent
may seem advisable, without public or other advertisement, for and in
consideration of the sum of Fifty Three Thousand Three Hundred Thirty
Three Dollars and Thirty Four cents ($55,333.54), certain interests in
real estate now being held and administered by him as such Alien Proper-
ty Custodian, and heretofore, under requirement, conveyed, transferred,
assigned and delivered to him as the property of CARL HAGUS and FRIEDRICH
HAGUS, residing in Germany, whom, after investigation, he did determine

to be enemies within the purview of said act as amended, which said.

Together with all the right, title and interest of the said Carl

My reasons for the foregoing determination, order, rule and
regulation are:

(a) An advantageous offer for the purchase of said real
estate has been received by the Alien Property Custodian, who holds the
assent of the alien interests to the sale;

(b) The real estate in question being divided into one-
fourth interests, it is advisable, for the purpose of administration,
that the alien holdings be liquidated by sale to an American citizen
who holds an undivided one-fourth interest;

(c) Investment of the proceeds of the sale will accrue
to the benefit of the respective trusts;

(d) Liquidation is necessary, in order to provide for the
payment of claims under the Act of March 4, 1923.

(e) An attempted sale at public auction would not be of any
advantage and would involve unnecessary expense, delay and inconvenience,
which should be avoided.
