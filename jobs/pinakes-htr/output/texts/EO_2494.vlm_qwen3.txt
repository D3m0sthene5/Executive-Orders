Executive Order.

It is hereby ordered, under authority contained in Section 5 of the Act
of February 8, 1887 (24 Stat. L., 388-89), that the trust periods on the allot-
ments of the Absentee Shawnee and Citizen Pottawatomie Indians in Okla-
homa, which trust expires during the calendar year, 1917, be, and is hereby,
extended for a period of ten years from the dates of expiration, with the
exception of the following:
