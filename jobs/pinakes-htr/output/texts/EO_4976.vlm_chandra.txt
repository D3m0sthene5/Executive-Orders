Executive Order
The Executive Order dated April 10, 1925 (No. 4194) establishing the Tobyhanna National Forest, Pennsylvania, pursuant to the provisions of Section 9 of the Act of Congress approved June 7, 1924 (43 Stat. 655) on a portion of the Tobyhanna Military Reservation, Pennsylvania, is hereby revoked.
CALVIN COOLIDGE
THE WHITE HOUSE,
October 10, 1928.
