Executive Order
BOATHOUSE SITE
ALASKA
Under authority of the act of Congress approved June 25, 1910 (36 Stat., 847), as amended by the act of August 24, 1912 (37 Stat., 497), and subject to the conditions therein expressed, and on the recommendation of the Secretary of Agriculture, it is hereby ordered that the tract of unsurveyed land lying within the following described boundaries, containing approximately 0.23 acres, be, and the same is hereby, temporarily withdrawn, for use by the Forest Service as a boathouse site in connection with the administration of the Chugach National Forest, Alaska:
Beginning at Corner No. 1, established on the north shore of Eyak Lake, near Cordova, approximately in latitude 60° 33′ N., longitude 145° 41′ W., an iron pipe 5′ long set 3′ in the ground and marked A. S. 1, whence a spruce tree 4″ diameter bears N. 12° W. 42 links distant marked A. S. W. 1 in blaze; a hemlock tree 8″ diameter bears N. 34° E. 47 links distant marked A. S. W. 1 in blaze; Corner No. 2 of the Russian Greek Mission church site (Survey No. 656), bears S. 41° W., 9.49 chains distant; thence N. 1° E. along mean high water line of Eyak Lake 1.52 chains to Corner No. 2, an iron pipe 5′ long set 3′ in ground and marked A. S. 2, whence a hemlock tree 4″ diameter bears S. 76° E. 35 links distant marked A. S. W. 2 in blaze, a hemlock tree 5″ diameter bears S. 5° E. 52 links distant marked A. S. W. 2 in blaze; thence S. 84° E. 1.
