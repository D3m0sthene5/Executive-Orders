Executive Order
NEVADA
Under authority conferred on me by law, it is hereby ordered that the tract of unsurveyed land in Nevada which, when surveyed, will probably be described as the S½ NE¼ and N½ SE¼, Sec. 4, T. 19 S., R. 63 E., M. D. M., containing 160 acres, be, and it is hereby withdrawn from settlement, location, sale or entry, subject to any prior valid claim legally initiated and maintained, for use by the Department of Commerce as an air mail beacon site.
CALVIN COOLIDGE
THE WHITE HOUSE,
August 10, 1927.
[No. 4702]
Chief, Division of Publications.
