1904
Executive Order.
It is hereby ordered that the following-described lands in the State of Washington, aggregating 4,629.27 acres, be, and they are hereby, withdrawn from settlement, entry, or other disposal and set aside as a reservation for the Kalispel Indians in that State:
TOWNSHIP 33 NORTH, RANGE 44 EAST, WILLAMETTE MERIDIAN.
Sec. 5, Lots 4, 5, 6, SW \frac{1}{4} NW \frac{1}{4}, E \frac{1}{2} SW \frac{1}{4};
Sec. 6, Lots 1, 6, 7;
Sec. 8, Lots 1, 2, 3, 4, SW \frac{1}{4} NE \frac{1}{4}, E \frac{1}{2} NW \frac{1}{4}, E \frac{1}{2} SW \frac{1}{4}, W \frac{1}{2} SE \frac{1}{4}, SE \frac{1}{4} SE \frac{1}{4};
Sec. 17, W \frac{1}{2}, SE \frac{1}{4}, W \frac{1}{2} NE \frac{1}{4}, SE \frac{1}{4} NE \frac{1}{4};
Sec. 18, Lots 1, 6, 7, 12;
Sec. 19, Lots 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8, 9, 12;
Sec. 20, N \frac{1}{2}, SW \frac{1}{4}, W \frac{1}{2} SE \frac{1}{4};
Sec. 29, All of fractional section.
Sec. 30, Lots 1, 6, 7;
Sec. 32, Lots 1, 2, 4, 5, 6, 7, N \frac{1}{2} NE \frac{1}{4}, SE \frac{1}{4} NE \frac{1}{4};
Sec. 33, Lot 1, S \frac{1}{2} NW \frac{1}{4}, N \frac{1}{2} SW \frac{1}{4}, SE \frac{1}{4} SW \frac{1}{4}.
TOWNSHIP 34 NORTH, RANGE 44 EAST, WILLAMETTE MERIDIAN.
Sec. 20, S \frac{1}{2} SE \frac{1}{4}, S \frac{1}{2} SW \frac{1}{4};
Sec. 29, All except SE \frac{1}{4} SE \frac{1}{4};
Sec. 30, Lots 1, 6, 7, 12;
Sec. 31, Lots 1, 6, 7, 12;
Sec. 32, NW \frac{1}{4} NE \frac{1}{4}, N \frac{1}{2} NW \frac{1}{4}, SW \frac{1}{4} NW \frac{1}{4}, W \frac{1}{2} SW \frac{1}{4}.
This withdrawal is subject to any existing valid rights or claims of any persons.
WOODROW WILSON
THE WHITE HOUSE,
March 23, 1914.
