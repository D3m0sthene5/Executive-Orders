Executive Order
CALIFORNIA

as amended by the act of August 24, 1912 (37 Stat., 497), it is hereby ordered
that the following described tract in the State of California, be, and the same is
hereby, temporarily withdrawn subject to the provisions and conditions of said
acts, for classification and pending enactment of legislation for its proper dis-
position:
