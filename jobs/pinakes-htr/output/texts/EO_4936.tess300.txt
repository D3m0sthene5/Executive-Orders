Executive Order
CALIFORNIA
It is hereby ordered that Executive order dated February 16, 1927, withdrawing
certain lands in California hereinafter described for use and benefit of the U. S..
Navy for the development of and use as an ammunition depot be and the same is
hereby revoked.
And it is hereby ordered, pursuant to Public Resolution No. 29 of February
14, 1920 (41 Stat. 434), as amended January 21 and December 28, 1922 (42 Stat.
358, 1067), that the said lands subject to valid rights, shall be opened only to entry
under the homestead and desert-land laws by qualified ex-service men of the war
with Germany, under the terms and conditions of such resolutions, aud the regula-
tions issued thereunder, for a period of ninety-one days, beginning with the sixty-
third day from and after the date hereof, and thereafter to appropriation under any
public land law applicable thereto by the general public.
Subsequent to the date hereof, and prior to the date of restoration to general
disposition as herein provided no right may be acquired to the released land by
settlement in advance of entry, or otherwise, except strictly in accordance herewith.
