A circular ink stamp from the Department of State, dated May 11, 1906.
A rectangular ink stamp from the Chief Clerk's office, dated May 10, 1906, with the Department of State noted below.
EXECUTIVE ORDER.
In accordance with the provisions of Sections 2250 and 2252 of the Revised Statutes of the United States, and by virtue of the authority therein given, it is hereby ordered that:
The Akron land district in the State of Colorado, with office at Akron, Colorado, be discontinued and the lands, business and archives of said district be transferred to and consolidated with the Sterling land district, with office at Sterling, Colorado.
THE WHITE HOUSE,
May 6, 1905.
J. Roosevelt
