Executive Order
HARNEY NATIONAL FOREST
SOUTH DAKOTA
Under authority of the act of Congress approved June 4, 1897 (30 Stat. 11, 34-36), and on the recommendation of the Secretary of Agriculture, it is hereby ordered that the boundaries of the Harney National Forest be modified by excluding therefrom the following described lands in the State of South Dakota:
Black Hills Meridian
In T. 3 S., R. 5 E., Sec. 36;
In T. 4 S., R. 5 E., N\frac{1}{2} NE\frac{1}{4}, SE\frac{1}{4} NE\frac{1}{4}, NW\frac{1}{4} and SW\frac{1}{4} SE\frac{1}{4} Sec. 36;
In T. 3 S., R. 6 E., Secs. 16 and 36;
In T. 4 S., R. 6 E., Sec. 16.
CALVIN COOLIDGE
The White House,
April 27, 1928.
