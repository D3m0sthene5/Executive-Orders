Executive Order.
...
It is hereby ordered that veterans of the Spanish-American war employed in the District of Columbia in or under the several Executive Departments and independent Government establishments, who desire to attend the unveiling of the Rough Riders' monument at Arlington on Friday, April 12, 1907, be excused from duty at three p. m. on that day.
THEODORE ROOSEVELT
THE WHITE HOUSE,
April 11, 1907.
