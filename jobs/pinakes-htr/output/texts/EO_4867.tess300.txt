Minnesota
‘It has been found by further examination in the field that changes are desirable
in Executive Order No. 4758, dated November 14, 1927, which. withdrew certain

from settlement, entry, or other disposition in order to carry into effect the provisions
of the act of Congress dated May 22,1926 (44 Stat. 617). The order in question
is, therefore, revoked and under the authority of the act of Congress approved June
25, 1910 (36 Stat. 847), as amended by the act of August 24, 1912 (37 Stat. 497),
it is hereby ordered that the lands described below be withdrawn from settlement,
entry or other disposition, subject to any valid prior existing rights of any person
