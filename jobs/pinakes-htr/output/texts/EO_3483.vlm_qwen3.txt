Executive Order

LEADVILLE NATIONAL FOREST,
COLORADO.

Under authority of the act of Congress approved June 4, 1897 (30 Stat.,
II, 34-36), and on the recommendation of the Secretary of Agriculture, it is
hereby ordered that the boundaries of the Leadville National Forest, Colorado,
as defined by proclamation of March 3, 1913, be modified by excluding there-
from the E. ¼ SE. ¼, Sec. 21, and SW. ¼ NW. ¼ and NW. ¼ SW. ¼, Sec.
22, T. 2 S., R. 80 W., 6th P. M.

WARREN G HARDING

THE WHITE HOUSE,
June 10, 1921.
