Executive Order

It is hereby ordered that the following described lands situated in the
State of New Mexico, which belong to or may hereafter be acquired by the
United States, are hereby withdrawn from settlement and sale and are set
apart for the use and occupancy of the Navajo and such other Indians as
the Secretary of the Interior may see fit to settle thereon:
Township 15 N., Range 10 W.
S.½, Sec. 1; W.½ and SE.½, Sec. 3; all of Sec. 11; E.½, Sec. 15; N.½ and
SW.½, Sec. 21; W.½ and SE.½, Sec. 31.
