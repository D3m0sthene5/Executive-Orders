A circular ink stamp from the Department of State. The outer ring contains the text 'CHIEF CLERK' at the top and 'DEPARTMENT OF STATE' at the bottom. In the center, the date 'NOV 1 1924' is stamped.
EXECUTIVE ORDER
By virtue of the power and authority vested in me by "An Act to define, regulate and punish trading with the enemy, and for other purposes", known as the "Trading with the Enemy Act", approved October 3, 1917, and amendments thereto, I hereby make the following order, rule and regulations:
I hereby vest in the Alien Property Custodian, and in the event of his death, resignation, absence or disability, in the General Counsel, and in the event of his death, resignation, absence or disability during his period of authority, then in the Managing Director of the office of the Alien Property Custodian, all power and authority conferred upon me by the provisions of the Act approved October 3, 1917, known as the "Trading with the Enemy Act", as amended, pertaining to the deposit, transfer, and payment of moneys, and interest which may have accrued thereon, which may be or have been deposited with the Treasurer of the United States, and to receive and deliver property held under and by virtue of said laws. Provided, however, that all claims for amounts in excess of $10,000 shall be approved by the President with the Attorney General.
