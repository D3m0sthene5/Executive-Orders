2443
Executive Order
UTAH
Under authority of the Act of Congress approved June 25, 1910 (36 Stat., 847), as amended by the Act of August 24, 1912 (37 Stat., 497), and subject to the conditions therein expressed, it is hereby ordered that the lands in the State of Utah hereinafter described be, and the same are hereby temporarily withdrawn from settlement, location, sale, entry, and other forms of disposal, until March 5, 1917, in aid of legislation proposed in pending S. Bills 6376-77-78-79-80-82-83,—64th Congress.
If said legislation be not enacted before the adjournment of the last session of the Sixty-fourth Congress, and no other direction is given regarding the disposition of such lands, they will, on March 5, 1917, become subject to disposal under any law then applicable thereto without further order.
Such lands are described as follows:
In T. 14 N., R. 4 W., W½ SW¼ Sec. 5, all Sec. 6, N½, N½ SW¼ and NE¼ SE¼ Sec. 7, E½ NE¼ Sec. 20;
In T. 14 N., R. 5 W., Lots 1, 2, 3, 5, 6, 7, SW¼ NE¼, W½ SE¼, SE¼ NW¼ and E½ SW¼ Sec. 1, NE¼ NW¼, NE¼ and N½ SE¼ Sec. 12;
In T. 13 N., R. 17 W., SW¼ and SW¼ SE¼ Sec. 5, NE¼ SE¼ Sec. 8, NW¼ SW¼, S½ SW¼ and SW¼ SE¼ Sec. 9, W½ NE¼, SE¼ NE¼ and SE¼ Sec. 17;
In T. 13 N., R. 18 W., W½ Sec. 1, NE¼ NE¼ Sec. 9, N½ Sec. 10, W½ SE¼, E½ SW¼ and NW¼ Sec. 11, E½, E½ W½ and W½ NW¼ Sec. 12, Lot 4 Sec. 30;
In T. 13 N., R. 19 W., E½ Sec. 11, W½ and SE¼ Sec. 15, Lots 1, 2 and NE¼ Sec. 21, W½ NW¼ Sec. 22, S½ Sec. 27, SE¼ Sec. 28, Lots 1, 2, 3, 4, SE¼ SE¼, N½ SE¼ and NE¼ Sec. 33, all Sec. 34;
In T. 2 N., R. 3 E., NE¼ NE¼, E½ SE¼ and W½ SW¼, or Lots 6 and 7, Sec. 28, S½ SE¼ and SE¼ SW¼ Sec. 30;
In T. 2 N., R. 4 E., Lot 2 Sec. 30;
In T. 4 N., R. 5 E., S½ SW¼ Sec. 10, W½ NW¼ and NW¼ SW¼ Sec. 24;
