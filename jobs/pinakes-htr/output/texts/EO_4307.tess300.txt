Executive Order
TONGASS NATIONAL FOREST —
ALASKA .
‘Under authority of the act of Congress approved June 4, 1897 (30 Stat., 11
‘at 34 and 36), and on the recommendation of the Secretary of Agriculture, it is
hereby ordered that the tract of land, in Alaska, lying within the following described .
boundaries, heretofore occupied for fish cannery purposes, be, and the same is
hereby, excluded from the’ Tongass National Forest and restored to entry under
the applicable public:land laws: \
Beginning at corner No. 1, identical with Forest Service Monument, and
consisting of a 4’’ x 6” spruce post scribed F S M on north face and S U 1 on east
face, spiked to cross beam of boardwalk at the line of mean high tide on the west
shore of the. entrance to Hidden Inlet, Alaska, approximately in latitude 54° 58’ |
20’ N., longitude 130° 21’ 46’’ W., whence a cedar tree 30’’ diameter blazed and
marked FSM/W bears N. 67° W. 0.26 chains distant, Southwest corner of boat
gridiron owned by A. & P. Products Company bears N. 45° E. 0.14 chains distant;
thence following the mean high tide of Pearse Canal to corner No. 2, a 3” x'4’’
éedar stake scribed S U 2 set at mean high tide line, whence Easterly wireless pole .
bears N. 52° E. 0.76 chains distant, Southeast corner of residence bears N. 73° W.;
thence north 3.00 chains to corner No. 3 unmarked; thence west 15.90 chains to
corner No. 4 unmarked; thence south 9.00 chains to corner No. 1, the place of
beginning, containing approximately 6.80 acres.
