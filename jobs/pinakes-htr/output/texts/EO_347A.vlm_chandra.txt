EXECUTIVE ORDER
It is hereby ordered that the Executive order of December 9, 1852, reserving for light-house purposes, among other lands, Lot 2 of Section 12, Township 47 North, Range 19 West, Michigan, be, and it is, hereby, canceled so far as it relates to the tract above described.
J. Roosevelt
The White House
August 19, 1905.
