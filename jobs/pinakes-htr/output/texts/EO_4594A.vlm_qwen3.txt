DIVISION OF PUBLICATIONS

EXECUTIVE ORDER
AUTHORIZING THE ALIEN PROPERTY CUSTODIAN TO SELL
ALIEN PROPERTY UPON THE NEW YORK STOCK EXCHANGE.
FEB 24 1927
Department of State

By virtue of the authority vested in me by an
Act of Congress entitled "An act to define, regulate, and punish trading
with the enemy and for other purposes", approved October 6, 1917,
known as the "Trading with the Enemy Act", and the Amendments to such
Act embodied in "An act making appropriations to supply urgent deficiencies
in the appropriations for the fiscal year ending June 30th, 1919,
and prior fiscal years on account of war expenses and for other purposes",
approved March 20th, 1919, and the amendment to such act embodied in "An
Act making appropriations to supply deficiencies in appropriations for
the fiscal year ending June 30th, 1919, and prior fiscal years on account
of war expenses, and for other purposes", approved November 4th, 1919,
and all other amendments thereto, to make certain orders such as that
herein contained, I hereby, in the public interest, make the following
determination, order, rule and regulations:

The Alien Property Custodian is hereby authorized
to sell upon the New York Stock Exchange, at such time as to him or to his
authorized agent may seem advisable, without public or other advertisement,
Four thousand Two (4002) shares Common stock of the "Black Ballony Company,
incorporated under the laws of the State of Indiana, of One Hundred Dollars
($100.00) par value, heretofore required to be conveyed, transferred, as-
signed and delivered to the Alien Property Custodian as the property of
Deutsche Bank of Berlin

-1-

- 2 -

My reasons for the foregoing determination
and order are:

First: That, considering the earning capacity and
immediate prospects of the Rhine Railway Company, the current prices
at which the common shares are selling is believed to represent their
fair market worth. No dividends have been paid on the common shares
since seizure by the Alien Property Custodian.

Second: That the sale of said stock at current prices
will result in realization in tangible form of an increase of more than
600 per cent in principal by virtue of the appreciation in the market
value of the stock since seizure, and the investment of the proceeds
in Liberty Loan bonds or Certificates of Indebtedness of the United
States Government would insure a return on the fund.

Third: That the Deutsche Bank, Berlin, Germany, for
whose account said stock is held has requested the Alien Property
Custodian to negotiate the sale.

Fourth: That, as the stock is listed on the New York
Stock Exchange and has an active and established market, a public or
other advertisement of the sale of the said stock or attempted sale at
auction would be a useless formality involving unnecessary expense and
would serve no useful purpose.
