AUGUST 31, 1874.

It appearing to me from their trial at Washington and at the city of
New York that the further extension of the civil-service rules will pro-
mote the efficiency of the public service, it is ordered that such rules be,
and they are hereby, extended to the several Federal offices at the city and
in the customs district of Boston, and that the proper measures be taken
for carrying this order into effect.

U. S. GRANT.
