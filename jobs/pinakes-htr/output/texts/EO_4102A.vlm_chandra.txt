By virtue of the power and authority vested in me
by "An Act to define, regulate and permit trading with the
energy, and for other purposes", known as the "Trading with the
Enemy Act", approved October 6, 1917, and amendments thereto,
I hereby make the following order, rules and regulations:
I hereby authorize the Alien Property Custodian
to make and approve all acts done or to be done under
the provisions of the Act approved October 6, 1917, known as
the "Trading with the Enemy Act", as amended, which were
judicial in character and which pertain to deposits, transfer,
and payment of property, and interest which may have accrued thereon,
heretofore deposited with the Treasurer of the United States.
The White House
November 12, 1924.
Edwin L. Dwyer
4102-1A
NATIONAL ARCHIVES
WASHINGTON, D. C.
ORIGINAL EXECUTIVE ORDER
