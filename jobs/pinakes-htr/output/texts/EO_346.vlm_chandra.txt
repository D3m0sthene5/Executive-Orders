A circular ink stamp located in the upper right corner of the document. The text 'DEPARTMENT OF STATE' is curved along the top inner edge, and 'AUG 1905' is printed in the center.
EXECUTIVE ORDER.
Mr. Ferdinand Schwarz, who lost two fingers on his right hand in the line of duty while serving temporarily as an unclassified laborer in the Government Printing Office, may be permanently appointed as an unclassified laborer in that office.
J. Roosevelt
The White House.
August 3, 1905.
