Executive Order.

eral Land Office, tracts of land in the following described places in the
District of Alaska, not to exceed forty (40) acres at each place, be, and
they are hereby, reserved and set apart for educational purposes, subject to
any valid claims thereto existing at the date of this order, viz.:
