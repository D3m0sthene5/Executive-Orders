Executive Order
transferring to the service and jurisdiction of the War Department
and the Navy Department certain vessels, equipment and personnel
of the United States Coast and Geodetic Survey.
In accordance with the authority vested in me by the “Act to temporarily
increase the commissioned and warrant and enlisted strength of the Navy and
Marine Corps and for other purposes”, approved May 22, 1917, I, Woodrow
Wilson, President of the United States of America, do hereby declare that a
national emergency exists and do direct that there be transferred to the service
and jurisdiction of the Navy Department for temporary use the following
vessels, including equipment and personnel other than commissioned officers
thereof: SURVEYOR, ⟦ISTS,⟧ BACHE.
