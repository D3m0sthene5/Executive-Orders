Executive Order

The Executive Order of March 12, 1927, discontinuing certain land offices and consolidating certain land districts at the close of business June 30, 1927, is hereby amended by eliminating the consolidation of the Lander district with the Evanston district in Wyoming, and substituting a consolidation of the Lander district with the Cheyenne district, with transfer of the records and business to Cheyenne.

CALVIN COOLIDGE

THE WHITE HOUSE,
March 21, 1927.

The President,
The White House.

My dear Mr. President:

I have the honor to submit herewith for your signature
draft of Executive order amending Executive order of March 12,
1927, discontinuing certain land offices and consolidating
certain land districts, so as to provide for the transfer of
the Lander, Wyoming, business to Cheyenne, Wyoming, instead
of to Evanston, Wyoming.

This draft of proposed order is submitted in compliance
with a wire dated March 18 from the Secretary, from Denver,
Colorado, reading as follows: "Consolidate Lander land office
with Cheyenne and transfer records there."

Very truly yours,

[illegible signature]

Acting Secretary.
