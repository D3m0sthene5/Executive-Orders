Feb 8, 1899.
Received 11:50 P.M.
The Members of the Cabinet,
The President directs that the
Flags on all the Departments
be half masted at noon
today during the removal
of the remains of the
late Major General and
Secretary of War John A.
Towbin to Arlington Cemetery.
J. K. Porter
Acting Secy to the President.
