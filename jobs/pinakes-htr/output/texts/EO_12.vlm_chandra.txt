Executive Mansion
January 2o - 1894.
By virtue of the authority granted by section sixteen of the act entitled "An act to regulate the diplomatic and consular systems of the United States", approved August 18, 1856, the tariff of fees prescribed by me on October 1, 1870 is hereby amended so that hereafter a single fee of twenty five cents shall be charged for the services required in sealing cards coming from countries adjacent to the United States, as described in fees 108 and 109, instead of a fee of twenty five cents for each of those services as heretofore.
