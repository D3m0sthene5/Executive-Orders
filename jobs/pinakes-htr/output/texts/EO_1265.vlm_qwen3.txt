Executive Order

It is hereby ordered that the following described lands in the State of
Nevada be, and the same are hereby, transferred from the Mono National
Forest to the Toiyabe National Forest:

IN TOWNSHIP 9 NORTH, RANGE 33 EAST:
Sections 1, 2, 3, 10, 11, 12, 13, 14, and 15;

IN TOWNSHIP 10 NORTH, RANGE 33 EAST:
Sections 8, 9, 10, 11, 14, 15, 16, 17, 20, 21, 22, 23, 24, 25, 26, 27, 34,
35, and 36;

IN TOWNSHIP 9 NORTH, RANGE 34 EAST:
Sections 1 to 18 both inclusive, and sections 21, 22, 23, and 24;

IN TOWNSHIP 10 NORTH, RANGE 34 EAST:
Sections 19, 20, 29, 30, 31, 32, 33, 34, 35, and 36; all Mt. Diablo
Meridian.

WM H TAFT

THE WHITE HOUSE,
Nov 25 1910.
