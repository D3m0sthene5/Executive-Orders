Executive Order

SEQUOIA NATIONAL FOREST
CALIFORNIA

Subject to the provisions of the act of June 11, 1906 (34 Stat., 233), Pember-
ton Beaty on December 20, 1918, made homestead entry for the SE. ¼ NE. ¼
SW. ¼, E. ½ SE. ¼ SW. ¼, Sec. 22, and the NW. ¼ NE. ¼, E. ½ NW. ¼,
Sec. 27, T. 25 S., R. 34 E., M. D. M., within the Independence, California, land
district. The SE. ¼ NE. ¼ SW. ¼, and the E. ¼ SE. ¼ SW. ¼, of said entry
lie within the Sequoia National Forest, Proclamation of February 14, 1908.*
On the recommendation of the Secretary of Agriculture, it is hereby ordered
that said SE. ¼ NE. ¼ SW. ¼, and E. ¼ SE. ¼ SW. ¼, be and the same are
hereby excluded from said National Forest.

WARREN G HARDING

THE WHITE HOUSE,
January 12, 1922.
