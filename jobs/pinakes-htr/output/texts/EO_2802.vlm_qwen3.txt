Executive Order.

Modification of Alaskan withdrawal No. 1.

Upon the recommendation of the Secretary of the Interior, Alaskan Withdrawal No. 1 of December fifth, nineteen hundred and eleven as amended by Executive Order, dated August twenty-fourth, nineteen hundred and twelve, is hereby modified so as to permit the following homestead entries to be patented if all else be found regular.

Seward Base and Meridian.

Juneau serial 02766 of George J. Laughlin, for S½ SW¼ Sec. 1, NW¼ and N½ SW¼ Sec. 12, T. 1 N., R. 1 W.;

Juneau serial 01777 of Alfred F. Johnston for lots 2, 3, 4, and N½ NW¼ Sec. 34, T. 1 N., R. 1 W.;

Juneau serial 02756 of John Nash for lot 1, Sec. 1, T. 1 S., R. 1 W., SW¼, S½ NW¼ and NE¼ NW¼, Sec. 36 T. 1 N., R. 1 W.;

Juneau serial 02889 of Joseph S. Hofman for lots 4, 5, and E½ NE¼ Sec. 1, T. 1 S., R. 1 W., and SE¼ SE¼ Sec. 36, T. 1 N., R. 1 W.

WOODROW WILSON

THE WHITE HOUSE,
8 February, 1918.
