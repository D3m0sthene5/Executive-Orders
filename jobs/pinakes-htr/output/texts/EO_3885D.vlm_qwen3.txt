Department of State
Washington
August 6, 1925.

ORDER

By order of the President, the order issued on
August 4, 1925 closing the Executive Departments on
Tuesday, August 7, 1925, at and after one o'clock p.m.
and on Wednesday, Thursday and Friday, August 8, 9
and 10, 1925, shall apply to all Executive establishments and those shall be closed accordingly on the
said days.
