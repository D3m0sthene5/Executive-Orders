Executive Order
WASHINGTON NATIONAL FOREST
WASHINGTON
It is hereby ordered that on and after July 1, 1908, the boundary of the Washington National Forest, shall be as indicated on the attached diagram. The remaining portions of said Forest shall continue reserved and constitute the Chelan, Snoqualmie, and Wenatchee National Forests, and part of the Rainier National Forest, as directed by my several orders of this date. It is not intended by this order to release any land from reservation or to reserve any land not heretofore embraced in a National Forest.
THEODORE ROOSEVELT
THE WHITE HOUSE,
June 18, 1908
