Executive Order
FLORIDA
Under authority of the act of Congress approved June 25, 1910 (36 Stat., 847), as amended by the act of August 24, 1912 (37 Stat., 497), it is hereby ordered that Lots 1 and 2, Sec. 25, T. 31 S., R. 39 E., Tal. M., Florida, containing 51.97 acres, be, and they are hereby, temporarily withdrawn subject to the provisions and conditions of said acts, for classification and pending enactment of legislation for their proper disposition.
CALVIN COOLIDGE
THE WHITE HOUSE,
April 14, 1925.
