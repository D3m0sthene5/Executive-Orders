Executive Order.

In accordance with the provisions of Sections 3141 and 3142 of the Revised
Statutes, and by virtue of the authority thereby vested in and conferred upon
the President, it is hereby ordered that the following changes in Internal Reve-
nue Collection Districts shall be made and shall take effect July 1, 1920:

VIRGINIA

1. All of the territory now included in the Sixth Internal Revenue Collec-
tion District of Virginia, to wit:
That part of the State of Virginia comprised in the counties of Albemarle,
Allegheny, Amherst, Appomattox, Augusta, Bath, Bedford, Bland, Bote-
tourt, Buchanan, Buckingham, Campbell, Carroll, Charlotte, Clark, Craig,
Cumberland, Dickinson, Floyd, Fluvanna, Franklin, Frederick, Giles,
Grayson, Greene, Halifax, Henry, Highland, Lee, Madison, Montgomery,
Nelson, Page, Patrick, Pittsylvania, Pulaski, Rappahannock, Roanoke,
Rockbridge, Rockingham, Russell, Scott, Shenandoah, Smyth, Tazewell,
Warren, Washington, Wise, and Wythe shall be transferred to, and made
a part of, the Second Internal Revenue Collection District of Virginia.

2. The Sixth Internal Revenue Collection District of Virginia shall be
abolished.

3. The Second Internal Revenue Collection District of Virginia shall there-
after be known as the Internal Revenue Collection District of Virginia.

WOODROW WILSON

THE WHITE HOUSE,
11 March, 1920.
