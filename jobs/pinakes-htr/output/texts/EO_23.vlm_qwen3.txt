By the President of the United States.

Executive Orders.

In conformity with the Executive order directing
the organization of a Board, to be composed of one person,
to be named by the head of each of the Executive
Departments which may have articles and materials
to be chartered at the World's Industrial and Cotton
Centenary Exposition, I hereby direct the persons who
have been so designated, viz: Major and First Lieu-
tenant George W. Stiles, of the War
Department, and Charles E. Hill,
of the Department of State, to serve as members of said Board.
