Executive Order.
Pursuant to the authority contained in Section 179 of the Revised
Statutes, George Uhler, Supervising Inspector General, Steamboat Inspection
Service, is hereby designated and authorized to act as Supervising
Inspector of Steam Vessels, Seventh District, Steamboat Inspection Service,
Cincinnati, Ohio, pending the regular filling of the position named.
THEODORE ROOSEVELT
THE WHITE HOUSE,
July 3, 1908.
[No. 911.]
