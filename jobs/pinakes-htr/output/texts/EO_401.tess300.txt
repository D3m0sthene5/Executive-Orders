No. M1118 1906-E0-401
EXECUTIVE ORDER
WAIVING NONCOMPETITIVE EXAMINATION IN THE CASE OF THE RE-
INSTATEMENT OF HOWARD H. BELDEN
Under date of December 11, 1905, the President ap-
proved an order permitting the appointment of Mr. Howard
H. Belden in any of the Executive Departments upon his pass-
ing a satisfactory noncompetitive examination.
The Treasury Department in a letter of February 3, 1906,
enclosed an affidavit giving a statement of the services of
Mr. Belden on the Isthmus of Panama, and on this affidavit
appeared the following endorsement by the President.
"Waive all examination in view of his service
in the Spanish War, of his satisfactory record in office,
and of facts above set forth. Signed T. Roosevelt.”
This endorsement was regarded by the commission as an
Executive Order waiving the requirement of noncompetitive
examination in Mr. Belden's case.
