WYOMING

1910 (36 Stat., 847), as amended by the act of August 24, 1912 (37 Stat., 497), that

they are hereby temporarily withdrawn subject to the conditions of said acts, until
March 5, 1927, in aid of the legislation proposed in pending H. R. 9640:
