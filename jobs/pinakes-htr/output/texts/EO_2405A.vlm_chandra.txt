EXECUTIVE ORDER.
The provisions of an Executive order approved September 14, 1915, to permit positions of watchman in the State, War and Navy Department Building to be filled without compliance with the civil service law and rules during the fiscal year 1916, when filled by men who have served at least two enlistments in the Army, Navy, or Marine Corps and who have been discharged as non-commissioned officers with character "excellent", are hereby extended to permit the filling of these positions in the same manner during the fiscal year 1917.
THE WHITE HOUSE,
19 June, 1916.
