A circular ink stamp from the Department of State. The text "MAR 29 1906" is in the center, with "DEPARTMENT OF STATE" curved along the bottom edge.
EXECUTIVE ORDER.
In pursuance of the Act of Congress approved June 28, 1894 (28 Stat. 96), making a legal public holiday the first Monday of September in each year, being celebrated and known as Labor's Holiday, employes of the Washington Navy Yard will be excused from work on Monday, September 4, 1905.
Theodore Roosevelt
The White House,
August 31, 1905.
