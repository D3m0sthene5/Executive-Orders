Executive Order.
IT IS HEREBY ORDERED that the following-described tracts of land in Honolulu, Territory of Hawaii, heretofore by proclamations of November 2, 1898, and of November 10, 1899, issued in pursuance of Joint Resolution of July 7, 1898 (30 Statutes at Large, 750), reserved from the public domain of Hawaii and placed under the Navy Department, be, and the same are hereby, transferred from the control of the Navy Department and placed under the control of the War Department:
TRACT No. 1.
Esplanade lots Nos. 63, 64, 65, 66, 67, and 68, beginning at the north corner of Alakea street and Allen street as shown on Government surveys, registered map No. 1867, and running by true bearings
N 30° 25' W 200 feet along the northeast side of Allen street; thence
N 59° 35' E 150 feet along the southeast side of Kilauea street; thence
S 30° 25' E 200 feet along lot 62 and 69; thence
S 59° 35' W 150 feet along the northwest side of Alakea street to the initial point;
Containing 30,000 square feet, more or less.

S 77° 52' W 576.3 feet; thence
N 5° 35' W 475.1 feet along Ala Moana; thence
N 54° 34' E 348.98 feet along Richards street to the initial point;
Containing 5.69 acres, more or less.
