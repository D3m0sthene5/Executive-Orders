OF THE CIVIL SERVICE
Within one year of the civil service rules approved March 26, 1903, to take effect on and after April 15, 1903, shall be construed to give a right of permanent appointment to any person serving under a temporary or emergency appointment under these rules, sections 13, 14, 15, or 16 of the existing rules.
Theodore Roosevelt
WHITE HOUSE.
Approved March 31, 1903.

EXECUTIVE ORDER.
