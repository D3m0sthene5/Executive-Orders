2478

Executive Order

COLORADO

It is hereby ordered that the lands hereafter described be, and the same are hereby temporarily withdrawn from settlement, location, sale, entry, and other forms of disposal in aid of the legislation proposed in pending H. R. 10124:

In T. 4 N., R. 72 W., that part of Sec. 31 lying south and west of the main hydrographic divide east of Cow Creek;

In T. 5 N., R. 72 W., Secs. 6, 7 and N½ Sec. 18;

In T. 4 N., R. 73 W., that part of Sec. 3 lying west of the main hydrographic divide east of Cow Creek, Secs. 4, 5, 6, 9, those portions of Secs. 10, 15, 22, 23, 24 and 25 lying south and west of said divide, Secs. 26, 27, 34, 35 and that part of Sec. 36 lying south and west of said divide;

In T. 5 N., R. 73 W., S½ Sec. 1, SE¼ SW¼ and SE¼ Sec. 2, SE¼ Sec. 10, Secs. 11 and 12, N½ of Secs. 13 and 14, Secs. 15 to 22, inclusive, and 27 to 34, inclusive;

In T. 5 N., R. 74 W., E½ SE¼ Sec. 11, S½ Sec. 12, N½ and N½ S½ Sec. 13, E½ NE¼ and NE¼ SE¼ Sec. 14;

Sixth Principal Meridian.

WOODROW WILSON

THE WHITE HOUSE,

22 October, 1916.
