EXECUTIVE ORDER.
In pursuance of the Act of Congress approved June 28, 1894
(28 Stat. 96), making a legal public holiday the first Monday
of September in each year, being celebrated and known as Labor's
Holiday, ⟦employees⟧ of the Washington Navy Yard will be excused
from work on Monday, September 4, 1905.
The White House,
August 31, 1905.
