Willamette Meridian.
It appearing that a portion of the lands excluded from the Paulina
National Forest as aforesaid is embraced in existing withdrawals that
should not now be revoked or modified, and it further appearing proper
and necessary in the interest of equal opportunity and good administration
that all of such excluded lands subject to disposition should be restored to
settlement in advance of entry, it is hereby ordered, pursuant to the authority
reposed in me by the Act of September 30, 1913 (38 Stat., 113), that
such lands, subject to valid rights and the provisions of existing ⟦with-⟧
⟦drawals,⟧ shall be open to actual settlers only under the provisions of the
homestead laws from and including nine o'clock, a. m., standard time, on
the 9th day of May, 1914, until and including the 7th day of June, 1914,
and thereafter to entry and disposition under any and all of the public
land laws applicable thereto.
