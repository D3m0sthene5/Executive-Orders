EXECUTIVE ORDER.
Nothing contained in the civil service rules approved
March 20, 1903, to take effect on and after April 15, 1903,
shall be construed to give a right of permanent appointment
to any person serving under a temporary or emergency appoint-
ment under Rule VIII, sections 13, 14, 15, or 16 of the existing
rules.
Theodore Roosevelt
WHITE HOUSE.
Approved March 31, 1903.
