Executive Order

COLVILLE NATIONAL FOREST
Washington

Under authority of the act of Congress approved June 4, 1897 (30 Stat., 34-36),
and on the recommendation of the Secretary of Agriculture, it is hereby ordered
that the boundaries of the Colville National Forest be modified by excluding there-
from the following described lands not in public ownership in the State of Washington:
In T. 35 N., R. 27 E., W. M., NE¼ NW¼, SW¼ SW¼ Sec. 15, E½ NE¼
NE¼ Sec. 21, NW¼ NW¼, N½ SW¼ NW¼ Sec. 22, and the land in Secs. 29 and
33 embraced in homestead entry surveys Nos. 60, 61 and 136.

CALVIN COOLIDGE

THE WHITE HOUSE,
September 27, 1927.
