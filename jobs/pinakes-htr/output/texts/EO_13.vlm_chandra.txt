Of a public obsequies
Washington, March, 9 1874.
1874
2
3
It is with deep regret that the President announces to the People of the United States the death of Mildred Fillmore, one of his honored predecessors who died at Buffalo, New York last evening.
The long continued and useful public service and eminent fidelity of character of the deceased Ex President will be remembered beyond the days of mourning in which a nation will be honored by the court which is thus announced.

As a mark of respect to his memory,
it is ordered that the Executive Clamours
and the several Departments at Washington,
be drafted in mourning until the close of
the day on which the funeral shall take
place, and that all business be suspended
on the day of the funeral.
13
It is further ordered that the War
and Navy Departments cause suitable
military and naval honors to be paid on
the occasion to the memory of the eminent
citizen whose life is now closed.
By the President
H. W. H. L. H.
Secretary of State.
