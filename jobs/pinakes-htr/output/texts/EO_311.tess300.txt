CIS TRANSCRIPTION OF NATIONAL ARCHIVES MICROFILM PUBLICATION
No. M1118 1905-E0-311
EXECUTIVE ORDER
The Executive Order of March 18, 1905, amending Executive Order of March
14,1905,creating and appointing a Consultative Board of Architects to secure
uniformity and harmony in the public buildings hereafter to be constructed
in the District of Columbia, by striking out the words "the members of the
board will serve without compensation" and inserting the following:
"Each member of the board shall receive while he is engaged
in the discharge of the duties imposed by the Executive Order,
ten dollars per day, and shall be paid in addition thereto such
actual expense of traveling and subsistence as he may have incur-
red while engaged in the business of the board.
“The money for the per diems and traveling expenses
shall be paid by the officer engaged in the construction of the
building and shall be charged by him to the incidental expenses
of said construction."
is hereby annulled.
Theodore Roosevelt
The White House
March 27, 1905
