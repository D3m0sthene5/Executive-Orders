Executive Order
ARIZONA - Pub Lands of US
Under authority of the Act of Congress approved June 25, 1910 (36 Stat., 847), as amended by the Act of August 24, 1912 (37 Stat., 497), and subject to the conditions therein expressed, it is hereby ordered that the following described lands in the State of Arizona be, and the same are hereby, temporarily withdrawn from settlement, location, sale or entry for classification, subject to the provisions of existing withdrawals or reservations for public purposes, pending determination as to the advisability of including such lands within adjoining National Forests:
No. 3098
In T. 15 N., R. 3 E., Secs. 1, 2, 3, 4, 9 to 16, inclusive, and 22 to 27, inclusive;
In T. 16 N., R. 3 E., Secs. 10 to 15, inclusive, NE¼ and S½ Sec. 16, NW¼ and S½ Sec. 17, Secs. 18 to 36, inclusive;
In T. 13 N., R. 4 E., Secs. 1 and 12;
In T. 14 N., R. 4 E., Secs. 1 to 5, inclusive, 8 to 16, inclusive, 22 to 26, inclusive, 35 and 36;
In T. 15 N., R. 4 E., Secs. 1 to 30, inclusive, E¼ Sec. 31, Secs. 32 to 36, inclusive;
All T. 16 N., R. 4 E.;
In T. 13 N., R. 5 E., Secs. 1 to 17, inclusive, N¼ Sec. 18, N¼ and SE¼ Sec. 20, Secs. 21 to 27, inclusive, E¼ Sec. 28, Secs. 34, 35 and 36;
All Ts. 14 and 15 N., R. 5 E.;
All Ts. 13 and 14 N., R. 6 E.;
Gila and Salt River Meridian.
WOODROW WILSON
THE WHITE HOUSE,
16 September, 1918.
