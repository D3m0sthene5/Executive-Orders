Executive Order

It is hereby ordered, under authority contained in the Act of February 8,
1887 (24 Stat. L., 388-389), that the trust period on the allotments made to
the Indians on the Nez Perce Reservation in Idaho, which trust period expires
during the calendar year 1920, be, and is hereby extended for a period of ten
years, with the following exceptions:
