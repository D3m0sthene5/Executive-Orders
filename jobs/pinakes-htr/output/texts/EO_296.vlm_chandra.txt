EXECUTIVE ORDER.
The Secretary of State, the Attorney General, and the Secretary of Commerce and Labor, are each hereby authorized and directed to appoint from among the officers of their respective departments some person qualified to act as a member of a commission to investigate and report upon the subject of the naturalization of aliens. The commission hereby authorized shall, upon its organization, proceed to investigate the subject of the naturalization of aliens under existing laws, and shall prepare a draft of a proposed law covering the entire subject of naturalization, together with such recommendations as such commission may consider proper to submit. Said commission is hereby required to report the result of its work to the President (or to the Attorney General) not later than the fifteenth day of November, 1905.
THEODORE ROOSEVELT.
THE WHITE HOUSE
March 1, 1905.
