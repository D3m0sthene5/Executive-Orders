Executive Order
SANTA BARBARA NATIONAL FOREST
CALIFORNIA
It is hereby ordered that on and after July 1, 1910, the Santa Barbara and San Luis National Forests, California, be consolidated under the name of Santa Barbara National Forest.
WM H TAFT
THE WHITE HOUSE
June 8, 1910
