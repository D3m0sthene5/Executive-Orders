764
Executive Order.
By virtue of the authority vested in me by Section 3141, Revised Statutes of the United States, it is hereby ordered that the State of Mississippi, now a part of the Collection District of Louisiana, be transferred to and made a part of the Collection District of Alabama.
This order to become effective April 1, 1908.
THEODORE ROOSEVELT
THE WHITE HOUSE,
February 28, 1908.
[No. 764.]
