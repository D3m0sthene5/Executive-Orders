Executive Order
IDAHO - Pt. lands of ID
Under authority of the Act of Congress approved June 25, 1910 (36 Stat., 847), as amended by the Act of August 24, 1912 (37 Stat., 497), and subject to the conditions therein expressed, the hereinafter described lands in the State of Idaho are hereby temporarily withdrawn for classification and pending enactment of appropriate legislation for their proper disposition:
In T. 13 S., R. 23 E., E\frac{1}{4} of Secs. 1 and 12, NE\frac{1}{4} and S\frac{1}{4} Sec. 13, Secs. 24, 25 and 36;
In T. 12 S., R. 24 E., Sec. 17, E\frac{1}{4} Sec. 18, NE\frac{1}{4} and S\frac{1}{4} Sec. 19, Secs. 20, 21, S\frac{1}{4} Sec. 24, NE\frac{1}{4} and S\frac{1}{4} Sec. 26, S\frac{1}{4} Sec. 27, Secs. 28, 29, 30 and 31;
In T. 13 S., R. 24 E., Secs. 6, 7, 18, 19, 30 and 31;
In T. 12 S., R. 25 E., S\frac{1}{4} Sec. 19;
In T. 13 S., R. 25 E., W\frac{1}{4} Sec. 20;
Boise Base and Meridian.
WOODROW WILSON
THE WHITE HOUSE,
22 February, 1921.
