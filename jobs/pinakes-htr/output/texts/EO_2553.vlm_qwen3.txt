Executive Order

Wireless Telegraph Naval Reservation.

It is hereby ordered that the tract of land in the Territory of Alaska hereto-
fore represented in Survey No. 484, accepted by the Commissioner of the General
Land Office March 16, 1914, shown by accompanying diagram, containing 8,39
acres on Woody Island, near the town of Kodiak, Alaska, in approximate
latitude 57° 47' N. and longitude 152° 22' W., be and the same is hereby
reserved, subject to any vested rights existing, for the use of the United States
Navy, in connection with the U. S. Wireless Telegraph Service.

Description.

Beginning at point for meander corner No. 1, on St. Paul Harbor; running
thence S. 34° 57' E. 2.77 chains; thence S. 54° 54' W. 2.17 chains; thence
S. 41° 41' E. 5.34 chains; thence N. 88° 10' E. 2.79 chains; thence with shore of
small lake N. 7° 00' E. 4.30 chains; thence N. 39° 03' E. with same shore 4.53
chains; thence N. 43° 02' W. 0.91 chains; thence N. 47° 14' E. 2.82 chains;
thence N. 23° 27' W. with same shore, 4.17 chains; thence N. 64° 54' W. 1.51
chains; thence with shore of St. Paul Harbor S. 39° 45' W. 5.00 chains; thence
with shore S. 53° 07' W. 5.94 chains to point of beginning.

WOODROW WILSON

THE WHITE HOUSE,
21 March, 1917.
