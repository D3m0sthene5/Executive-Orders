Executive Order
Lewis J. Pettijohn is hereby designated Acting Farm Loan Commissioner of the
Federal Farm Loan Board for a period of ten days from the date hereof, or until
the return of Honorable Albert C. Williams, Farm Loan Commissioner, to
Washington.
THE WHITE HOUSE,
April 26, 1927.
