Executive Order

SANTA BARBARA NATIONAL FOREST
CALIFORNIA

It is hereby ordered that on and after July 1, 1910, the Santa Barbara
and San Luis National Forests, California, be consolidated under the name
of Santa Barbara National Forest.

THE WHITE HOUSE
June 8, 1910

WM H TAFT

[No. 1209.]
