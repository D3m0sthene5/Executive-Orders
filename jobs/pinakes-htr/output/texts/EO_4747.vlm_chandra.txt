Executive Order
ALASKA
By virtue of the authority vested in me by sections 2380 and 2381, Revised Statutes, Lots 3 and 4 and NE¼ SE¼ Sec. 13, T. 7 S., R. 5 E., F. M., Alaska, are hereby withdrawn for townsite purposes.
This order will continue in full force and effect unless and until revoked by the President or by act of Congress.
CALVIN COOLIDGE
THE WHITE HOUSE,
October 24, 1927.
[No. 4747]
DEPARTMENT OF STATE.
November 1, 1927
A true copy.
L. W. Boyer
Acting Chief, Division of Publications.
