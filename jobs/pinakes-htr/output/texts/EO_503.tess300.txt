CIS TRANSCRIPTION FROM NATIONAL ARCHIVES MICROFILM PUBLICATION
No. M1118 1906-E0-503
THE WHITE HOUSE
September 18, 1906
Enlisted men of the Marine Corps regularly detailed as
messmen in a command, not to exceed in number in one for every
twenty men, shall receive the same compensation in addition
to their monthly pay as is now or may hereafter be allowed
to enlisted men of the Navy under like circumstances: Pro-
vided, however, That marines must serve as messmen a whole
month ro be entitled to this extra compensation, and shall
not be paid for service as such for fractional parts of a
month.
Theodore Roosevelt
