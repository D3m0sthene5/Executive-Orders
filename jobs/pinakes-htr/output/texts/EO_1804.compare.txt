1804
Executive Order
I, Woodrow Wilson, President of the United States, by virtue of the
authority vested in me by Sections 3141 and 3142, Revised Statutes of the
United States, hereby order that the Fourth Internal Revenue Collection
District of Iowa, embracing the Counties of Adair, Adams, Appanoose,
Audubon, Cass, Cedar, Clarke, Clinton, Dallas, Davis, Decatur, Des Moines,
Fremont, Guthrie, Henry, Harrison, Iowa, Jackson, Jasper, Jefferson,
Johnson, Keokuk, Lee, Louisa, Lucas, Madison, Mahaska, Marion, Mills,
Monroe, Montgomery, Muscatine, Page, Polk, Pottawattamie, Poweshiek,
Ringgold, Scott, Shelby, Taylor, Union, Van Buren, Wapello, Warren,
Wayne and Washington, is hereby consolidated with the Third Internal
Revenue Collection District of Iowa, the consolidated district to be known
as the Third District of Iowa, and Louis Murphy is hereby designated as
Collector for the consolidated district.
This order to take effect August 1, 1913.
WOODROW WILSON
THE WHITE HOUSE,
July 24, 1913.
