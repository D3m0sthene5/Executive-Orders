Executive Order
ALASKA
By virtue of the authority vested in me by Secs. 2380 and 2381 U. S. Revised Statutes the following described lands are hereby withdrawn for townsite purposes: NE¼ NE¼, S½ NE¼, E½ SW¼, N½ SE¼, SW¼ SE¼, Lot 1, Sec. 35, T. 5 S., R. 4 E., and all legal subdivisions which will on survey lie wholly or in part within one-half mile of the shore of Salchaket Lake in T. 6 S., R. 4 E., T. 5 S., R. 5 E., and T. 6 S., R. 5 E., F. M., Alaska.
— This order will continue in full force and effect unless and until revoked by the President or by act of Congress.
CALVIN COOLIDGE
THE WHITE HOUSE,
July 4, 1927.
[No. 4683]
DEPARTMENT OF STATE.
