CHELAN NATIONAL FOREST
WASHINGTON
It is hereby ordered that on and after July 1, 1908, the land within the boundary shown on the attached diagram heretofore set apart, reserved, and proclaimed as a part of the Washington National Forest, shall be known as the Chelan National Forest. It is not intended by this order to release any land from reservation or to reserve any land not heretofore embraced in a National Forest.
THEODORE ROOSEVELT
THE WHITE HOUSE,
June 18, 1908
