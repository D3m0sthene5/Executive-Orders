Executive Order

Chugach National Forest
Alaska

Under authority of the act of Congress approved June 4, 1897 (30 Stat., 34-36), and on the recommendation of the Secretary of Agriculture, it is hereby ordered that the tract of land on the east side of Evans Island, in Alaska, lying within the hereinafter described boundaries, heretofore occupied for fish cannery purposes, be, and the same is hereby, excluded from the Chugach National Forest and restored to entry under the applicable public land laws:

Beginning at Corner No. 1 and M. C. on the north shore of Evans Bay, Prince William Sound, approximately in latitude 60° 4' N., longitude 148° 3' W., a squared hemlock tree 18 inches in diameter marked S U - I, whence a spruce tree 8 inches in diameter bears N. 43° E. 21 links distant marked W - S U I in blaze; a spruce tree 6 inches in diameter bears S. 89° E. 20 links distant marked W - S U I in blaze, and a water tank bears N. 25° E. 12.12 chains; thence along mean high water line of Evans Bay to Corner No. 2 and M. C. unmarked; thence east 3.25 chains to Corner No. 3 unmarked; thence south 5.53 chains to Corner No. 1 and M. C., the place of beginning, containing approximately 6.90 acres.
