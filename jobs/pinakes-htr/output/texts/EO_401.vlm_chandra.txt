EXECUTIVE ORDER
FOR A NONCOMPETITIVE EXAMINATION IN THE CASE OF THE RE-
APPOINTMENT OF HOWARD R. FELDEN.
By order of December 11, 1905, the President ap-
pealed an order permitting the appointment of Mr. Howard
Felden in any of the Executive Departments upon his pass-
ing a satisfactory noncompetitive examination.
The Treasury Department in a letter of February 7, 1906,
submitted an affidavit giving a statement of the services of
Felden on the Islands of Panama, and on this affidavit
issued the following endorsement by the President:
"Whole life examination in view of his service
in the Spanish War, of is satisfactory record in office,
and of facts above set forth. Signed T. Roosevelt."
This endorsement was regarded by the Commission as an
executive order waiving the requirement of noncompetitive
examination in Mr. Felden's case.

EXECUTIVE ORDER
WAIVING NONCOMPETITIVE EXAMINATION IN THE CASE OF THE RE-
INSTATEMENT OF HOWARD H. BELDEN
Under date of December 11, 1905, the President approved an order permitting the appointment of Mr. Howard H. Belden in any of the Executive Departments upon his passing a satisfactory noncompetitive examination.
The Treasury Department in a letter of February 3, 1906, enclosed an affidavit giving a statement of the services of Mr. Belden on the Isthmus of Panama, and on this affidavit appeared the following endorsement by the President.
"Waive all examination in view of his service in the Spanish War, of his satisfactory record in office, and of facts above set forth. Signed T. Roosevelt."
This endorsement was regarded by the commission as an Executive Order waiving the requirement of noncompetitive examination in Mr. Belden's case.
