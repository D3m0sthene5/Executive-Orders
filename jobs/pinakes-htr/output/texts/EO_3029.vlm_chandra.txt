3029
Executive Order
The following-named officers, who were transferred to the service and jurisdiction of the War Department by Executive Order of September 24, 1917, are returned to their former status in the service and jurisdiction of the Coast and Geodetic Survey, Department of Commerce, to take effect on the dates specified after their names:
1st Lieut. Harold W. Pease, Coast Artillery--December 28, 1918,
1st Lieut. Isaiah M. Dailey, Coast Artillery--December 28, 1918,
2nd Lieut. Rowland K. Bennett, Coast Artillery--December 28, 1918,
2nd Lieut. John W. Cox, Coast Artillery--December 20, 1918.
WOODROW WILSON
THE WHITE HOUSE,
25 January, 1919.
[No. 3029]
