Executive Order

CALIFORNIA. — TAKEN, 13, 1923

Under authority of the act of Congress approved June 25, 1910 (36 Stat., 847), as amended by the act of August 24, 1912 (37 Stat., 497), the following described public lands in the State of California are hereby temporarily withdrawn, subject to the conditions, provisions, and limitations of said acts, for the purpose of classifying said lands and pending enactment of appropriate legislation for their proper disposition:

Mt. Diablo Meridian

In T. 27 N., R. 6 E., NE¼ NW¼ Sec. 4;
In T. 28 N., R. 6 E., W½ SW¼ and SE¼ SW¼ Sec. 33;
In T. 26 N., R. 7 E., SE¼ Sec. 6;
In T. 27 N., R. 7 E., S½ NE¼ Sec. 22 and S½ NW¼ Sec. 23;
In T. 29 N., R. 7 E., NW¼ NE¼, N½ NW¼ and SE¼ NW¼ Sec. 21, NE¼ NW¼, NE¼ SW¼ and NW¼ SE¼ Sec. 29, NE¼, NE¼ NW¼ and NE¼ SE¼ Sec. 34.

CALVIN COOLIDGE

THE WHITE HOUSE,
August 13, 1923.
