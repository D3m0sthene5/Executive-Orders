AN ORDER REJECTING THE BID OF MONSANTO CHEMICAL WORKS OF
$605,000 for 747 SHARES, OF THE PAR VALUE OF $200 EACH, OF THE HEYDEN
CHEMICAL WORKS, A CORPORATION OF THE STATE OF NEW JERSEY, CONSTITUTING
ALL OF THE OUTSTANDING CAPITAL STOCK OF SAID CORPORATION (THREE REMAINING
SHARES BEING TREASURY STOCK); AND ALL THE RIGHTS, INTERESTS AND BENEFITS
CREATED IN FAVOR OF CHEMISCHE FABRIK VON HEYDEN A.G. OF RADERBEUL, GERMANY,
BY A CERTAIN AGREEMENT DATED JUNE 7, 1906, BETWEEN SAID CHEMISCHE FABRIK
VON HEYDEN A.G. AND THE HEYDEN CHEMICAL WORKS; AND CERTAIN PATENTS AND
TRADEMARKS HERETOFORE HELD BY THE SAID CHEMISCHE FABRIK VON HEYDEN A.G.,
AND FR. VON HEYDEN NACHFOLGER, OF RADERBEUL, GERMANY, OFFERED FOR SALE
AT PUBLIC AUCTION ON FEBRUARY 14, 1919, PURSUANT TO THE "TRADING WITH
THE ENEMY ACT AND AMENDMENTS THEREOF.
By virtue of the authority vested in the President by "An Act to define, regulate and punish trading with the enemy, and for other purposes," approved October 6, 1917, known as the "Trading with the Enemy Act", and the amendment to such Act embodied in "an Act making appropriations to supply urgent deficiencies in appropriations for the fiscal year ended June 30, 1918, and prior fiscal years on account of war expenses and for other purposes", approved March 26, 1918, and the amendment to such Act embodied in "An Act making appropriations to supply deficiencies in appropriations for the fiscal year ended June 30, 1919, and prior fiscal years on account of war expenses and for other purposes," approved November 4, 1918, to make certain orders such as that herein contained, which authority has been delegated to me by the Executive Order No. 3016 and signed by the President under the third day of December, 1918, I hereby, in the public interest, make the following determination and order:
3016-B

The Alien Property Custodian having offered at public sale at the office of the company at Garfield, in the State of New Jersey, at three o'clock P.M. on the 14th day of February, 1919, the following property to-wit:
(a) Seven hundred and forty seven (747) shares of the par value of Two Hundred ($200) Dollars each, of the capital stock of The Heyden Chemical Works, a corporation organized and existing under the laws of the State of New Jersey, aggregating in par value One Hundred Forty Nine Thousand and Four Hundred ($149,600) Dollars, and constituting all of the outstanding capital stock of said corporation, and, with three (3) shares of said stock held in the treasury of said corporation, all of the authorized capital stock thereof.
(b) All of the rights, interests and benefits created in favor of said Chemische Fabrik von Heyden A.G., of Radebeul, Germany by a certain agreement in the German language, dated June 7, 1906, between said Chemische Fabrik von Heyden, A.G. and The Heyden Chemical Works, a corporation of the State of New Jersey, and also all rights and interest to any sum or sums of money hereafter accruing in favor of or becoming due or payable to said Chemische Fabrik von Heyden A.G. under the terms of said agreement, and also all claims and demands whatsoever created or existing in favor of, or conferred upon said Chemische Fabrik von Heyden A.G. against the said The Heyden Chemical Works, all of which rights, interest, benefits, claims and demands have been heretofore required by the Alien Property Custodian to be transferred, assigned, delivered and paid over to him.
(c) All those certain letters patent of the United States, specified in Schedule "A" hereto annexed, and every right, title and interest with respect thereto, heretofore held by said Chemische Fabrik von Heyden A.G., of Radebeul, Germany, all of which patents have been heretofore required by the Alien Property Custodian to be conveyed, transferred, assigned and delivered to him.
(d) All those certain registered trademarks specified in Schedule "B" hereto annexed and every right title and interest with

respect thereto, heretofore held by said Chemische Fabrik von Heyden A.G., of Radebeul, Germany, all of which trademarks have been heretofore required by the Alien Property Custodian to be conveyed, transferred, assigned and delivered to him.

At which sale the highest bid received for said property was the sum of $605,000 made by Monsanto Chemical Works.
It is therefore Ordered that the Alien Property Custodian be, and he is authorized, and shall have the right to reject all bids at said sale including the bid of the said Monsanto Chemical Works, and to resell such property in accordance with the provisions of the said "Trading with the Enemy Act", as amended, and the executive orders and proclamations issued pursuant thereto
The reason for the foregoing order is that the price bid for said property by the said Monsanto Chemical Works, to-wit: the sum of $605,000 is inadequate and not the fair value of the property offered for sale.
Done in the District of Columbia, this 14th day of March, in the year of our Lord One Thousand Nine Hundred and Nineteen.
