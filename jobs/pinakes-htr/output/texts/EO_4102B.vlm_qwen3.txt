CHIEF CLERK
NO7 4 1924
DEPARTMENT OF STATE

EXECUTIVE ORDER

By virtue of the power and authority vested in me
by "An Act to define, regulate and punish traitors with the enemy,
and for other purposes", known as the "Traitor with the Enemy
Act", approved October 6, 1917, and amendments thereto, I
hereby issue the following order, rule and regulation:

I hereby vest in the Alien Property Custodian,
and in the event of his death, resignation, absence or
disability, in the General Counsel, and in the event of
his death, resignation, absence or disability during his
period of authority, then in the Acting Director of the
Office of the Alien Property Custodian, all power and authority
conferred upon me by the provisions of the Act approved October
6, 1917, known as the "Traitor with the Enemy Act", as amended,
pertaining to the deposit, transfer, and payment of moneys, and
interest which may have accrued thereon, which may be or have
been deposited with the Treasurer of the United States, and to
receive and deliver property held under and by virtue of said Act.

Provided, however, that all claims for amounts in excess of
$10,000 shall be approved by the Executive and the Attorney General.
Any order of payment of money, and interest, which may be executed
by said officials shall constitute a ratification of all previous
orders.
