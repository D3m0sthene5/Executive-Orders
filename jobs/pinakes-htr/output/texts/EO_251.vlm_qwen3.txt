EXECUTIVE ORDER

Mr. Matthew J. McCarthy may be transferred from
the excepted position of special deputy surveyor at
the customs port of Syracuse, New York, to the non-
excepted position of deputy surveyor and inspector
at the same port without examination under the civ-
il-service rules.

Theodore Roosevelt

WHITE HOUSE.
February 8, 1904.
