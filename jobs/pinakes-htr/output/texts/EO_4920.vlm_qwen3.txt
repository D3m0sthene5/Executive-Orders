Executive Order

UTAH

Under authority conferred on me by law, it is hereby ordered that the following described tracts of public land in Utah, aggregating 366.54 acres, be and they are hereby withdrawn from settlement, location, sale or entry, subject to prior valid claims legally initiated and maintained, for use by the Department of Commerce in the maintenance of air navigation facilities:

Salt Lake Meridian

In T. 29 S., R. 11 W., NE¼ SW¼ Sec. 20;
In T. 30 S., R. 12 W., SE¼ NW¼ Sec. 1 and NW¼ NE¼ Sec. 22;
In T. 31 S., R. 12 W., SE¼ SW¼ Sec. 5;
In T. 32 S., R. 12 W., SW¼ SW¼ or Lot 4, Sec. 30;
In T. 33 S., R. 12 W., SW¼ NW¼ or Lot 2, Sec. 18;
In T. 34 S., R. 12 W., NW¼ NW¼ or Lot 4 Sec. 6 and NW¼ NW¼ or Lot 1, Sec. 30.
