EXECUTIVE ORDER.
In accordance with the provisions of Sections
2250 and 2252 of the Revised Statutes of the United States,
and by virtue of the authority therein given, it is hereby
ordered that:
The Akron land district in the State of Colorado,
with office at Akron, Colorado, be discontinued and the
lands, business and archives of said district be transferred
to and consolidated with the Sterling land district, with
office at Sterling, Colorado.
THE WHITE HOUSE,
May 6, 1905. J. ROOSEVELT
