Contolidating present Collection Districts and creating new Districts,
The attached Exeeutive Order abolishes io Internal Revenue Collection
Districts, consolidating them with other Districts, and creates 10 new Districts
in states which do not now have separate Collection Distriets. This leaves
the number of Districts the same as at present 64. Dt gives each state, with
the single exception of Nevada, at feast one separate Collection District of
its own,
With the number of tax returns increasing from joo,000 ten years ago
to over 15,000,000 for 1919, With he many complicated tax laws requiring so
much personal explanation and servive, the old order of Internal Revenue
policy based on conditions existing many years ago ne longer legitimately
performs its expected functions, It has become imperative that each state
have its own revenue service and Chat as rapidly as possible the head collec-
tion offices be decentralized so that they shall become clearing and account-
ing places for Washington, and thus permit the opening of permanent personal
service division headquarters in every important city.
This program is designed to accomplish this by creating the new state
districts, by abolishing districts where changed revenue conditions logically
permit such action and in such consolidated districts open up the personal
service division headquarters to more efficiently serve taxpayers.
The following changes are provided for, effective as of August 1, 1919.
