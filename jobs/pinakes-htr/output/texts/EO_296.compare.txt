EXECUTIVE ORDER.
The Secretary of State, the Attorney General, and the Secretary of
Commerce and Labor, are each hereby authorized and directed to appoint,
from among the officers of their respective departments, some person
qualified to act as a member of a commission to investigate and report
upon the subject of the naturalization of aliens. The commission hereby
authorized shall, upon its organization, proceed to investigate the ⟦sub-⟧
⟦ject⟧ of the naturalization of aliens under existing laws, and shall ⟦pre-⟧
⟦pare⟧ a draft of a proposed law covering the entire subject of ⟦naturaliza-⟧
⟦tion,⟧ together with such recommendations as such commission may consider
proper to submit. Said commission is hereby required to report the result
of its work to the President (or to the Attorney General) not later than
the fifteenth day of November, 1905.
THEODORE ROOSEVELT.
March 1, 1905.
