EXECUTIVE ORDER.
Paragraph 463 of the Consular Regulations of ⟦1906,⟧ is
hereby amended to read as follows:
"463. All circulars issued over the signature of a consular officer must be submitted
to the Department of State and receive its
approval before published."
White House
June 6, 1903.
