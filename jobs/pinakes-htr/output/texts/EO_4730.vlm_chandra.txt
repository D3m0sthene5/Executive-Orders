4730
Executive Order
By virtue of the authority vested in me by the Act of Congress approved August 1, 1914, making appropriations for the sundry civil expenses of the Government for the fiscal year ended June 30, 1915, the following ports of entry in customs collection district #38 (Michigan), with headquarters at Detroit, Michigan, are hereby abolished, effective thirty days from the date of this order:
Alpena, Charlevoix, Detour, Escanaba, Houghton, Mackinaw, Manistee, Marine City, Marquette, Muskegon, St. Clair, and St. Joseph.
CALVIN COOLIDGE
THE WHITE HOUSE,
September 30, 1927.
