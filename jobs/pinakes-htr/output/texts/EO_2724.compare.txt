Executive Order
NEW MEXICO.
Under authority of the Act of Congress, approved June 25, 1910 (36 Stat., 847) as amended by the Act of August 24, 1912 (37 Stat., 497), it is hereby ordered that the following described land be, and the same is, hereby reserved and withdrawn from all forms of entry or disposal whatever, to be utilized as a Rifle Range by Company “B” First New Mexico Infantry.
Description
SE¼ Sec. 20, SW¼ Sec. 21, NW¼, N½ SW¼, SE¼ SW¼ Sec. 28, N½ of NE¼ Sec. 29, and NE¼ NW¼ Sec. 33, T. 21 S., R. 26 E., New Mexico Principal Meridian.
WOODROW WILSON
THE WHITE HOUSE,
6 October, 1917.
[No. 2724.]
