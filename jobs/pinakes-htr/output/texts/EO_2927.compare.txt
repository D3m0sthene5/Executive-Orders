Executive Order:
It is hereby ordered, under authority contained in the Act of February 8,
1887 (24 Stat. 388-389), that the trust period on the allotments of the Prairie
Band of ⟦Potawatomi⟧ Indians in Kansas, which trust period expires during the
calendar year 1918, be, and is hereby, extended for a period of ten years, with
the exception of the following:
