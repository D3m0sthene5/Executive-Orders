Executive Order

CALIFORNIA

Under authority of the act of Congress approved June 25, 1910 (36 Stat., 847),
as amended by the act of August 24, 1912 (37 Stat., 497), and subject to the con-
ditions therein expressed, it is hereby ordered that Lot 1 Sec. 4, T. 22 S., R. 39
E., M. D. M., containing 39.80 acres, be, and the same is hereby, temporarily
withdrawn for classification under the act of March 3, 1925 (43 Stat., 1133).

THE WHITE HOUSE,
October 2, 1925.

[No. 4316]

DEPARTMENT OF STATE.

October 14, 1925
