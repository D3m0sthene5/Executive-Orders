Executive Order
WASHINGTON
It is hereby ordered, under authority of the act of Congress approved June 25, 1910 (36 Stat., 847), as amended by the act of August 24, 1912 (37 Stat., 497), and subject to the conditions therein expressed, that the NE \frac{1}{4} NW \frac{1}{4} Sec. 2, T. 39 S., R. 22 E., W. M., in the State of Oregon, containing 40 acres, be, and the same is, hereby temporarily withdrawn in aid of legislation to authorize the use of such land for a fish hatchery.
