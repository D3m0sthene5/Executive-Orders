TONTO NATIONAL FOREST
ARIZONA
Under authority of the act of Congress approved June 4, 1897, (30 Stat. 11, 34-36), and on the recommendation of the Secretary of Agriculture, it is hereby ordered that the boundaries of the Tonto National Forest, in Arizona, be modified by excluding therefrom the following described land:
In T. 10 N., R. 10 E., G. & S. R. M., NE\frac{1}{4} SE\frac{1}{4} SW\frac{1}{4}, W\frac{1}{4} SE\frac{1}{4} SW\frac{1}{4}, S\frac{1}{4} SW\frac{1}{4} SW\frac{1}{4}, NE\frac{1}{4} SW\frac{1}{4} SW\frac{1}{4}, S\frac{1}{4} S\frac{1}{4} NW\frac{1}{4} SW\frac{1}{4} SW\frac{1}{4} Sec. 4, E\frac{1}{4} E\frac{1}{4} SE\frac{1}{4} SE\frac{1}{4} SE\frac{1}{4}, SE\frac{1}{4} SE\frac{1}{4} NE\frac{1}{4} SE\frac{1}{4} SE\frac{1}{4} Sec. 5, those portions of the NW\frac{1}{4} NE\frac{1}{4} NW\frac{1}{4} and NE\frac{1}{4} NW\frac{1}{4} NW\frac{1}{4} of Sec. 9 not included in homestead entry survey No. 127, and that part of the NW\frac{1}{4} NW\frac{1}{4} NW\frac{1}{4} of said Sec. 9 lying north of a line beginning at Corner No. 1 of H. E. S. No. 127, thence along boundary of Payson ranger station N. 68° 10' W. approximately 3.70 chains; thence N. along boundary of said ranger station approximately 1.90 chains; thence N. 68° W. approximately 5.20 chains to intersection with west boundary of section, containing approximately 85 acres.
The land excluded from the national forest is hereby reserved for townsite purposes under Section 2380 to be disposed of under Sections 2382 to 2389, inclusive, United States Revised Statutes.
