⟦By⟧ reasons for the foregoing determination and
order are:
⟦Firsts⟧
That the earnings of the corporation over a period
of several years would indicate that there is no reason to anticipate
an increase in the dividend rate and that the income return upon the
investment based upon the current market quotations is too small to
warrant the retention of the principal in a security the market value
of which fluctuates so widely.
⟦Seconds:⟧
That the sale of the said stock and the investment
of the proceeds in Liberty Bonds or Certificates of Indebtedness
of the United States Government would insure a fair income upon the
investment and conserve the principal, preventing possible depreciation
by reason of a decline in the market price.
⟦Thirds⟧
That the sale of the said stock will result in the
realization in tangible form of a great increase in principal by
virtue of the appreciation of the market value of the stock since
the time of its issue and since its seizure by the Alien Property
Custodian.
Fourth:
That a public or other advertisement of the sale
of the said stock or attempted sale at auction, would be a useless
formality involving unnecessary expense and inconvenience, and would
cause such a delay as to impair the realization of a price equal
to that now prevailing on the open market.
