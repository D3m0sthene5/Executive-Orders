EXECUTIVE ORDER

Authorizing the Alien Property Custodian to sell certain property upon the New York Stock Exchange.

By virtue of the authority vested in me by an Act of Congress entitled "An Act to define, regulate, and punish trading with the enemy and for other purposes," approved October 6, 1917, known as the "Trading with the Enemy Act," and the amendments to such act embodied in "An Act making appropriations for the fiscal year ending June 30th, 1918, and prior fiscal years on account of war expenses and for other purposes," approved March 28th, 1918, and the amendment to such act embodied in "An Act making appropriations to supply deficiencies in appropriations for the fiscal year ending June 30th, 1919, and prior fiscal years on account of war expenses, and for other purposes," approved November 4th, 1918 and all other amendments thereto, to make certain orders such as that herein contained, I hereby, in the public interest, make the following determination, order, rule and regulations:

The Alien Property Custodian is hereby authorized to sell upon the New York Stock Exchange, without previous advertising, at the price bid for the same, as, if, and when offered, the following securities in such lots, and at such times as to him, or his authorized agent may seem advisable, viz:

12 shares Baltimore and Ohio Preferred Stock
22 shares Baltimore and Ohio Common Stock
held in Trust 1711, Report 40759, as the property of: The Anglo Austrian Bank.

By reasons for the foregoing determination and order are:

1st To partially reimburse Trust 290, on account of over-payment under Claim 22732, amounting to $3,021.64.

2nd. That a public or other advertisement of the sale of the said stock or attempted sale at auction would be a useless formality involving unnecessary expense and inconvenience and would cause such a delay as to impair the realization of a price equal to that now prevailing on the open market.

The White House,
December 31, 1925.
