Executive Order
HARNEY NATIONAL FOREST
SOUTH DAKOTA
Under authority of the act of Congress approved June 4, 1897 (30 Stat. 11,
34-36), and on the recommendation of the Secretary of Agriculture, it is hereby
ordered that the boundaries of the Harney National Forest be modified by excluding
therefrom the following described lands in the State of South Dakota:
Black Hills Meridian
In T. 3 S., R. 5 E., Sec. 36;
In T. 4 S., R. 5 E., ⟦N½⟧ ⟦NE¼,⟧ ⟦SE¼⟧ ⟦NE¼,⟧ ⟦NW¼⟧ and ⟦SW¼⟧ ⟦SE¼⟧ Sec. 36;
In T. 3 S., R. 6 E., Secs. 16 and 36;
In T. 4 S., R. 6 E., Sec. 16.
CALVIN COOLIDGE
THE WHITE HOUSE,
