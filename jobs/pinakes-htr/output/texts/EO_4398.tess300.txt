Executive Order
It is hereby ordered, under authority contained in Section 5 of the Act of
February 8, 1887 (24 Stat. 389), that the trust period on allotments made to the
Indians of the Kiowa, Comanche, Apache and Wichita Reservation in Oklahoma,
which trust expires during the calendar year, 1926, be, and is hereby extended for
a period of ten years, with the following exceptions:
