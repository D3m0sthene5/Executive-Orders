Executive Order.
In conformity with the Executive order directing the organization of a Board to be composed of one person to be named by the head of each of the Executive Departments which may have articles and materials to be stored at the World's Industrial and Cotton Centennial Exhibition, I hereby direct the persons who have been so designated, viz: Sillinger and Brewster Kennedy, Secy. of War; John H. Coffey, Head of the War Dept.; President of the Board; Charles E. Hill, Secretary of State;

and that the said Board shall organize
members of the Agricultural Department, of the Bureau of Agriculture, of the Smithsonian Institution, of the Department of State, in the city of Washington, at noon, on the 1st day of May 1861, and then proceed to organize said Board, and said Board when so organized shall immediately proceed to the discharge of its duties.
I also appoint Mr. J. L. Hamilton as the Secretary of said Board.
By the order of the President, this thirtieth day of April, 1861, in the presence of the Secretary of the Navy, the Secretary of the Treasury, and the Secretary of the War Department.
Charles A. Sumner
By the President,
