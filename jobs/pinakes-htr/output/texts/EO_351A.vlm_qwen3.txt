EXECUTIVE ORDER

It is hereby ordered that the hereinafter described tract of
land on Gravina Island, District of Alaska, and the small island
in front of such tract known as East Clump or False Island, be,
and they are hereby, reserved as a site for a light-house depot,
to wit:

Beginning at a point on the high water line of the easterly
shore of Gravina Island, from which point a cedar 18 inches in
diameter bears N. 40 degrees W., distant 310 feet, thence S. 55
degrees W. 2640 feet to a point, thence N. 35 degrees W. 4000 feet
to a point, thence N. 55 degrees E. 2640 feet, more or less to a
point on the high water line of the easterly shore of said island,
thence southerly along the high water line to the point of begin-
ning, together with the tide lands in front of and abutting on the
above described tract, also the small island known as East Clump
or False Island, in front of said tract, containing in all 278
acres, more or less.

Theodore Roosevelt

The White House,
September 16, 1905.
