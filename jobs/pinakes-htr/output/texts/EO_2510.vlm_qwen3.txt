Executive Order.

Upon the recommendation of the Secretary of the Interior, with the concurrence of the Department of Justice, Petroleum Reserve No. 30, California No. 15, dated November 28, 1913, is hereby vacated and revoked as to the following-described lands, which were patented in 1876, under the grant in aid of the construction of the Southern Pacific Railroad:

Sec. 11, all;
Sec. 13, NW.¼ NE.¼, S.½ NE.¼, NW.¼;
