Executive Order.
I. With the consent of the government of the Territory of Hawaii, it is hereby ordered that the following described parcel of ground contiguous to the military reservation of Fort Ruger, situate at Diamond Head, on the Island of Oahu, Territory of Hawaii, as described in Paragraph III of Executive Order dated June 26, 1911, published in General Orders, No. 101, War Department, July 25, 1911, be, and it is hereby, withdrawn and set aside for military purposes, and made a part of said military reservation of Fort Ruger:
That certain piece or parcel of land situate at Kaalawai, Kona, Oahu, Territory of Hawaii, more particularly described as follows:
Beginning at a point S. 87° 18' W. 32.0 feet distant from a star on a stone at an angle in the Kapahulu-Waialae Nui boundary, as shown on Government Survey Registered Map No. 1502, said star being the initial point of the description of the main reservation of Fort Ruger as contained in Paragraph III of said Executive Order of June 26, 1911, and said star on stone being S. 81° 43' 04" E. (true) 6634.82 feet from Government Survey Triangulation Station Leahi (said station being referred to as Diamond Head Station in Paragraph III of said Executive Order of June 26, 1911.) From the above-described point of beginning running by true bearings as follows:
1. S. 22° 58' E. 129.3 feet along a 30-foot road reservation; thence
