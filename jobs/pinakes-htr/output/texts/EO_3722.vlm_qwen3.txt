Executive Order

It is hereby ordered that the trust periods remaining on any parts of the following allotments and homesteads of Santee Indians, in Nebraska, which trust would expire during the calendar years 1922, 1923, and 1924, be, and the same are hereby extended for periods of ten years from the dates of expiration.

Campbell, Theresa, Allotment,   130
Johnson, Henry, Homestead,     318
Haines, Mary, Allotment,       469
Crow, George, Homestead,       482
Saul, Moses, Allotment,        499
Frazier, Albert E., Homestead, 669
Blacksmith, Stephen, Homestead, 690

WARREN G HARDING

THE WHITE HOUSE,
