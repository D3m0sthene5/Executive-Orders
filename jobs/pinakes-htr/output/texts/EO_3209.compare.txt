Executive Order.
It is hereby ordered that Lot 1 Sec. 17, Lots 1 and 2 Sec. 18, Lots 1 and 2 Sec. 20, Lots 1 and 2 Sec. 21, Lot 1 Sec. 22, Lot 1 Sec. 27, Lots 1, 2, 5, 6 and 7 Sec. 28, the reef in Sec. 17 and the reef in Sec. 28, T. 168 N., R. 33 W., 5th P. M., Minnesota, containing 72.78 acres, be and the same are hereby temporarily withdrawn from settlement, entry, sale or other forms of disposal and reserved pending the establishment of the International Boundary Line through the Lake of the Woods, between the United States and Canada.
WOODROW WILSON
THE WHITE HOUSE,
3 January, 1920.
