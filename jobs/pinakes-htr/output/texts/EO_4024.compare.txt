Executive Order
It is hereby ordered, under authority contained in Section 5 of the Act of
February 8, 1887 (24 Stat. L., 388), that the trust period on the allotments
made to Indians on the Umatilla Reservation, Oregon, which trust period
expires during the calendar year 1924, be, and is hereby, extended for a
period of ten years from the date of expiration, with the exception of the
following:
