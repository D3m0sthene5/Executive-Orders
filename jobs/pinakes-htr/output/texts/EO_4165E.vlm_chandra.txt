AUTHORIZING THE ALIEN PROPERTY CUSTODIAN TO SELL

My reasons for the foregoing determination
and order are:
First: That there has been such a variation in the earnings applicable to the common stock of the corporation over a period of several years as to preclude its classification as a proper investment for trust funds.
Second: That the sale of the said stock and the investment of the proceeds in Liberty Bonds or Certificates of Indebtedness of the United States Government would insure a fair income upon the investment and conserve the principal, preventing possible depreciation by reason of a decline in the market price.
Third: That the sale of the said stock will result in the realization in tangible form of a great increase in principal by virtue of the appreciation of the market value of the stock since its original purchase, and since its seizure by the Alien Property Custodian.
Fourth: That a public or other advertisement of the sale of the said stock or attempted sale at auction would be a useless formality involving unnecessary expense and inconvenience and would cause such a delay as to imperil the realization of a price equal to that now prevailing on the open market.
THE WHITE HOUSE,
March 9, 1925.
