Executive Order
CALIFORNIA.
Pat Lando
Under authority of the Act of Congress approved June 25, 1910 (36 Stat., 847), as amended by the Act of August 24, 1912 (37 Stat., 497), and on the recommendation of the Secretary of Agriculture, it is hereby ordered that the lands in California hereinafter listed, within the area described in the Act of Congress approved March 3, 1919 (Public 330), be, and the same are, hereby temporarily withdrawn subject to the conditions, provisions, and limitations of said acts, and to the provisions of existing withdrawals and reservations, for classification and pending determination as to the advisability of including within the Modoc National Forest such of the lands as may be found to be available for the production of timber, protection of stream flow, or regulation and improvement of the grazing thereon:
