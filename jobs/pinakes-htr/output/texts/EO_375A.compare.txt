EXECUTIVE ORDER
It is hereby ordered that lots 8 and 9 of fractional section 36, township 29 south, range 10 east, Mount Diablo Meridian, California, containing an aggregate of 52.83 acres, be, and they are hereby, reserved for light-house purposes.
Theodore Roosevelt
The White House,
November 27, 1905.
