Executive Order
Wireless Telegraph Naval Reservation.
It is hereby ordered that the tract of land in the Territory of Alaska heretofore represented in Survey No. 484, accepted by the Commissioner of the General Land Office March 16, 1914, shown by accompanying diagram, containing 8.39 acres on Woody Island, near the town of Kodiak, Alaska, in approximate latitude 57^{\circ} 47' N. and longitude 152^{\circ} 22' W., be and the same is hereby reserved, subject to any vested rights existing, for the use of the United States Navy, in connection with the U. S. Wireless Telegraph Service.
Description.
Beginning at point for meander corner No. 1, on St. Paul Harbor; running thence S. 34^{\circ} 57' E. 2.77 chains; thence S. 54^{\circ} 54' W. 2.17 chains; thence S. 41^{\circ} 41' E. 5.34 chains; thence N. 88^{\circ} 10' E. 2.79 chains; thence with shore of small lake N. 7^{\circ} 00' E. 4.30 chains; thence N. 39^{\circ} 03' E. with same shore 4.53 chains; thence N. 43^{\circ} 02' W. 0.91 chains; thence N. 47^{\circ} 14' E. 2.82 chains; thence N. 23^{\circ} 27' W. with same shore, 4.17 chains; thence N. 64^{\circ} 54' W. 1.51 chains; thence with shore of St. Paul Harbor S. 39^{\circ} 45' W. 5.00 chains; thence with shore S. 53^{\circ} 07' W. 5.94 chains to point of beginning.
WOODROW WILSON
THE WHITE HOUSE,
21 March, 1917.
