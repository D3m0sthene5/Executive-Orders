EXECUTIVE ORDER.
The provisions of an Executive Order approved September 14, 1916, and continued June 19, 1916 and July 9, 1917, to permit positions of machinists in the State, War and Navy Department Building to be filled without compliance with the Civil Service law and rules during the fiscal years 1916, 1917 and 1918, when filled by men who have served at least two enlistments in the Army, Navy or Marine Corps, and who have been discharged as non-commissioned officers with character excellent, are hereby modified so as to permit such positions to be filled without compliance with the Civil Service law and rules during the period of the war when filled by men who have served in the Army, Navy or Marine Corps and who have been honorably discharged therefrom, with character excellent.
Margaret Wilson
The White House,
November, 1917.
THE NATIONAL ARCHIVES
WASHINGTON, D.C.
