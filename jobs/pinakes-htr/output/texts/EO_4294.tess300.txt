Executive Order
BOATHOUSE SITE
ALASKA
Under authority of the act of Congress approved June 25, 1910 (36 Stat., 847),
as amended by the act of August 24, 1912 (37 Stat., 497), and subject to the condi-
_ tions therein expressed, and on the recommendation of the Secretary of Agriculture,
it is hereby ordered that the tract of unsurveyed land lying within the following
described boundaries, containing approximately 0.23 acres, be, and the same is —
hereby, temporarily withdrawn, for use by the Forest Service as a boathouse site.
in connection with the administration of the Chugach National Forest, Alaska:
Beginning at Corner No. 1 , established on the north shore of Eyak Lake,
near Cordova, approximately in latitude 60° 33’ N., longitude 145° 41’ W., an
iron pipe 5’ long set 3’ in the ground and marked A. S. 1, whence a spruce
tree 4’ diameter bears N. 12° W. 42 links distant marked A. S. W. 1 in blaze;
