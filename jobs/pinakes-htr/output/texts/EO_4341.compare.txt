Executive Order
APACHE NATIONAL FOREST
NEW MEXICO
Under authority of the act of Congress approved June 4, 1897 (30 Stat., 11 at
34 and 36), it is hereby ordered that the SE¼ NW¼ SE¼, Sec. 8, T. 5 S., R. 16 W.,
N. M. P. M.; be, and the same is hereby excluded from the Apache National
Forest in New Mexico; and the land so eliminated from the national forest is
hereby reserved for townsite purposes under authority of Sec. 2380 U. S. R. S., to
be disposed of under Secs. 2382 to 2386 U. S. R. S.
