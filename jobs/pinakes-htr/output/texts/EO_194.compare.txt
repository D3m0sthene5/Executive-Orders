EXECUTIVE ORDER.
⟦—ooo—⟧
Paragraph 660 of the Consular Regulations of 1896 in
regard to the shipment of gold and silver bullion to the
United States is hereby amended by the addition of the
following words:
Collectors of Customs have however, authority
to accept entry of gold and silver bullion on an oath of
one of the principal officers of the importing bank or firm
that such bullion is imported as money or medium of exchange.
J. Roosevelt
WHITE HOUSE,
November 6, 1902.
