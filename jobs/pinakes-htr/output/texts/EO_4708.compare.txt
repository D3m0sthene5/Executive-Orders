Executive Order
SAN BERNARDINO NATIONAL FOREST
CALIFORNIA
Under authority of the act of Congress approved June 4, 1897 (30 Stat.,
11, 34-36), and on the recommendation of the Secretary of Agriculture, it is hereby
ordered that the ⟦N½⟧ ⟦SE¼⟧ Sec. 14, T. 7 S., R. 4 E., S. B. M., California, be, and
the same is hereby, excluded from the San Bernardino National Forest, and restored
to the public domain subject to disposition under the acts of January 12, 1891 (26
Stat., 712), and March 1, 1907 (34 Stat., 1022).
