Executive Order
MALHEUR NATIONAL FOREST
OREGON
a Under authority of the act of Congress approved June 4, 1897 (30 Stat., 11, 34-36), it is hereby ordered that the boundaries of the Malheur National Forest, in Oregon, as defined by proclamation of June 13, 1911, be modified by excluding therefrom the W½ NW¼ and W½ SW¼ Sec. 33, T. 9 S., R. 31 E., W. M., containing 160 acres, to permit adjustment of an equitable claim thereto, and the said tract of land is hereby restored to entry only under the provisions of the act of January 27, 1922 (42 Stat., 359).
CALVIN COOLIDGE
