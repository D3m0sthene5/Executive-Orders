Executive Order
It is hereby ordered that the public lands in the following areas, in the
State of Wyoming, which have been selected by the Secretary of Agriculture
in accordance with the provisions of the Act of March 4, 1913 (37 Stat., 847),
be, and the same are hereby reserved and set apart for use by the Department
of Agriculture as an Elk Refuge and as an addition to a purchase area
for such purpose under the Act of August 10, 1912 (37 Stat., 293), and so
indicated upon the attached diagram forming a part hereof:
