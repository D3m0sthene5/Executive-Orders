Executive Order

It is hereby ordered, under authority contained in the Act of February 8, 1887 (24 Stat. L. 388-89), that the trust period on the allotments of the Modoc Indians in Oklahoma, which trust expires during the calendar year 1916, be, and is hereby, extended for a period of ten years, with the exception of the following:
