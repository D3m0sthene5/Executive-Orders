Executive Order
FLORIDA
Under authority contained in the act of Congress approved June 25, 1910
(36 Stat., 847), as amended by the act of August 24, 1912 (37 Stat., 497), and
subject to the conditions, provisions and limitations of said acts, it is hereby
ordered that all unsurveyed islands and shore lands belonging to the United
States situated on meandered lakes in Secs. 1, 2, 11, 12, 13, 14, 23, 24, 25, 26,
and 36, T. 18 S., R. 19 E.; Secs. 6, 7, 18, 19, 29, 30, 31, 32, 33 and 34, T. 18 S.,
R. 20 E.; Secs. 2, 3, 4, 5, 7, 8, 9, 10, 11, 15, 16, 17, 18, 21, 22, 23, 25, 26, 27, 34,
35 and 36, T. 19 S., R. 20 E.; Secs. 1, 2, 3, 10, 11, 12, 13, 14, 15, 22, 23 and 24,
T. 20 S., R. 20 E., in the State of Florida, be and the same are hereby withdrawn
from settlement, location, sale, entry and all forms of appropriation, subject to
any valid existing legal rights in and to the same, for classification and in aid of
legislation.
CALVIN COOLIDGE
THE WHITE HOUSE,
January 14, 1926.
