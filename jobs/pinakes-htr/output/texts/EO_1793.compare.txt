Executive Order
BOULDER CREEK ADMINISTRATIVE SITE
(Near Helena National Forest)
MONTANA.
Under authority of the Act of Congress approved June 25, 1910
(36 Stat., 847), as amended by the Act of August 24, 1912 (37 Stat., 497),
and on the recommendation of the Secretary of Agriculture, it is hereby
ordered that the following described tract of unsurveyed land, containing
40 acres, be temporarily withdrawn from settlement, location, sale or entry,
except as provided in said Acts, and be reserved for use by the Forest
Service as a nursery site in connection with the administration of the
Helena National Forest:
In T. 7 N., R. 3 W., M. P. M., Beginning at a point whence the
corner common to the southeast corner of Sec. 36, T. 7 N., R. 4 W., and
the northeast corner Sec. 1, T. 6 N., R. 4 W., M. P. M., bears S. 45° W.,
⟦6.28⟧ chs.,
Thence North 20 chains,
Thence East 20 chains,
Thence South 20 chains,
Thence West 20 chains, to the place of beginning. Magnetic
variation 21° E. This area would by protraction of the public survey in
T. 7 N., R. 4 W., M. P. M., probably be the NE. ⟦¼⟧ SW. ⟦¼⟧ Sec. 31, T.
7 N., R. 3 W., M. P. M.
WOODROW WILSON
THE WHITE HOUSE,
3 July, 1913.
