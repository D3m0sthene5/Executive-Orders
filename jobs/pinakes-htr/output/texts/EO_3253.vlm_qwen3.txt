Executive Order

REJECTING THE BID OF BAUSCH & LOMB OPTICAL COMPANY FOR CERTAIN
LETTERS PATENT OF THE UNITED STATES AND DAMAGES FOR PAST INFRINGE-
MENTS THEREOF OFFERED FOR SALE IN CONNECTION WITH THE OFFERING
FOR SALE OF CERTAIN SHARES OF THE CAPITAL STOCK OF C. P. GOERZ AMERI-
CAN OPTICAL COMPANY, A NEW YORK CORPORATION, AT PUBLIC AUCTION
ON THE 5TH DAY OF MARCH, 1920, PURSUANT TO THE “TRADING WITH THE
ENEMY ACT” AND AMENDMENTS THEREOF.

By virtue of the authority vested in me by “An act to define, regulate and
punish trading with the enemy, and for other purposes”, approved October 6,
1917, known as the “Trading with the Enemy Act”, and the amendment to
such act embodied in “An act making appropriations to supply urgent defi-
ciencies in appropriations for the fiscal year ending June 30, 1918, and prior
fiscal years on account of war expenses and for other purposes”, approved
March 28, 1918, and the amendment to such act embodied in “An act making
appropriations to supply deficiencies in appropriations for the fiscal year end-
ing June 30, 1919, and prior fiscal years on account of war expenses and for
other purposes”, approved November 4, 1918, to make certain orders such as
that herein contained, and all other powers and authorities me hereto enabling,
I do hereby make the following determination and order;

WHEREAS, the Alien Property Custodian offered for sale at auction in the
City of New York, State of New York, at 11:00 o’clock A. M. on the 5th day of
March, 1920, certain shares of the capital stock of C. P. Goerz American Optical
Company, a New York corporation, and other property, said shares and other
property being described as follows:

ITEM I. 549 shares of the common capital stock of C. P. Goerz American
Optical Company, a New York Corporation, of the par value of $100 each,
out of a total of 600 shares of the common capital stock of said corporation,
all of which are issued and outstanding.

Goerz American Optical Company to manufacture, sell and use, without the

ITEM V. All damages and profits recoverable at law or in equity from any and every person, firm, corporation or government for every past infringement of each and all of the letters patent enumerated in Items IV and VI hereof, except such infringements as arose from the use or manufacture by or for the United States of any of the inventions described in and covered by said letters patent enumerated in Items IV and VI.

ITEM VI. Letters patent of the United States No. 784,487, dated March 7, 1905, entitled Tangent-scale Telescope for guns, and No. 784,852, dated March 14, 1905, entitled Panorama-Telescope, subject however to the right of the United States to manufacture in its own shops and have manufactured elsewhere in the United States, to use and to sell panoramic sights possessing any or all of the features covered by and described in these two letters patent, as more particularly provided by and specified in an agreement dated April 20, 1909, between the said Optische Anstalt C. P. Goerz Optical Company (sic) and the United States of America.

AND WHEREAS, the highest bid received for the property described as Items IV, V and VI was a bid of one thousand three hundred dollars ($1,300) for the whole property described by said Items, which bid was made by Bausch & Lomb Optical Company.

The Alien Property Custodian is hereby authorized and shall have the right to reject all bids made at said sale for the property described in Items IV, V and VI, including the bid of the said Bausch & Lomb Optical Company.

My reason for the foregoing determination and order is that the above stated price bid for the said property is inadequate and not the fair value thereof.

Done in the District of Columbia this 27th day of March, in the year of our Lord one thousand nine hundred and twenty, and in the year of the independence of the United States, the one hundred forty-fourth.

WOODROW WILSON
