Executive Order

HOLY CROSS NATIONAL FOREST
COLORADO

It is hereby ordered that on and after this date, the land within the
boundary shown on the attached diagram, heretofore a part of the Holy
Cross National Forest, shall be known as the Holy Cross National Forest.
The remaining portions of the Holy Cross National Forest shall continue
reserved and constitute the Sopris National Forest in Colorado.  It is not
intended by this order to release any land from reservation or to reserve
any land not heretofore embraced in a National Forest.

WM H TAFT

THE WHITE HOUSE,
April 26, 1909.
