Executive Order
TONGASS NATIONAL FOREST
ALASKA
Under authority of the act of Congress approved June 4, 1897 (30 Stat., 34-36), and on the recommendation of the Secretary of Agriculture, it is hereby ordered that the tract of land on Chichagof Island, in Alaska, lying within the hereinafter described boundaries, heretofore occupied for fish cannery purposes, be, and the same is hereby, excluded from the Tongass National Forest and restored to entry under the applicable public land laws:
Beginning at corner No. 1 M. C., on southwest shore of Port Althorp, approximately in latitude 58° 07' 45" N.; longitude 136° 20' W., a hemlock post 5 inches square, 4 feet high, scribed M. C. 1 T. & M. Elim. and set in mound of stone on prominent rock bluff and about 10 feet directly above line of mean high tide, from which a 12 inch spruce bears S. 75° W. 45 links distant; a 10 inch hemlock bears N. 76° W. 33 links distant, both trees are blazed on side facing corner and scribed M. C. 1 T. and M. Elim. B. T., a small island near head of Port Althorp bears S. 62° E.; thence along mean high water line of Port Althorp to corner No. 2 M. C., not set, 16.23 chains north and 11.12 chains west of corner No. 1, on smooth bed rock, under bluff; thence due west up steep bluff 46 links, establish witness corner, a hemlock tree 10 inches in diameter blazed on four sides and scribed on east face W. M. C. 2 and on south face T. & M. Elim. from which a 15 inch spruce bears N. 35° W. 11 links distant, a 9 inch spruce bears S. 7° E. 11 links distant, both trees scribed W. M. C. 2 T. & M. Elim. B. T.; continue west 8.88 chains to corner No. 3, not set; thence south 16.23 chains to corner No. 4, not set; thence east 20.00 chains to corner No. 1 M. C. place of beginning, containing approximately 18.38 acres.
