COTTONWOOD ADMINISTRATIVE SITE
(Near the La Sal National Forest)
UTAH
Under authority of the Act of Congress approved June 25, 1910 (36 Stat., 847), and on the recommendation of the Secretary of Agriculture, it is hereby ordered that the following described tract of unsurveyed land, containing 160 acres, be temporarily withdrawn from settlement, location, sale, or entry, except as provided in said Act, and be reserved for use by the Forest Service as a Ranger Station in the administration of the La Sal National Forest.
