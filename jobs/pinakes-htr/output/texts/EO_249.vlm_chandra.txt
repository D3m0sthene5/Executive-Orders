A circular ink stamp located in the upper right corner of the page. The text around the inner border reads "BUREAU OF ROLLS & LIBRARY" at the top and "DEPARTMENT OF STATE" at the bottom. In the center, the date "FEB 6 1904" is stamped.
SPECIAL RULE
R.C.H.
James M. Cooper and Ralston Williams may be employed by the board of medical officers appointed to investigate the origin and spread of typhoid fever in U. S. military camps in 1898 until the completion of the report of the board.
Theodore Roosevelt
WHITE HOUSE.
Approved February 5, 1904.
