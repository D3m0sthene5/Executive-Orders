By reasons for the foregoing determination
and order are:
First: That, considering the earning capacity and
immediate prospects of the B'nai B'rith Company, the current prices
at which the common shares are selling is believed to represent their
fair market worth. No dividends have been paid on the common shares
since seizure by the Alien Property Custodian.
Second: That the sale of said stock at current prices
will result in realization in tangible form of an increase of more than
400 per cent in principal by virtue of the appreciation in the market
value of the stock since seizure, and the investment of the proceeds
in Liberty Loan Bonds or Certificates of Indebtedness of the United
States Government would insure a return on the fund.
Third: That the Deutsche Bank, Berlin, Germany, for
whose account said stock is held has requested the Alien Property
Custodian to negotiate the sale.
Fourth: That, as the stock is listed on the New York
Stock Exchange and has an active and established market, a public or
other advertisement of the sale of the said stock or attempted sale or
notice would be a useless formality involving unnecessary expense and
would serve no useful purpose.
THE WHITE HOUSE,
February 24, 1927.
