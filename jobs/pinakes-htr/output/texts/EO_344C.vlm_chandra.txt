EXECUTIVE ORDER.
In accordance with the provisions of Sections 2248 and 2252 of the Revised Statutes of the United States, and by virtue of the authority therein given, it is hereby ordered that:
The Ashland and Eau Claire land districts in the State of Wisconsin, with offices at Ashland and Eau Claire, respectively, be discontinued, and the lands, business and archives of said districts be transferred to and consolidated with the Wausau land district, with office at Wausau, Wisconsin.
THE WHITE HOUSE,
July 31, 1905.
J. Roosevelt
