DEPT. OF STATE
EXECUTIVE ORDER.
In accordance with the provisions of Section 2252 of the Revised Statutes of the United States, and by virtue of the authority therein given, it is hereby ordered that:
The Marysville land district in the State of California, with office at Marysville, California, be discontinued, and the lands, business and archives of said district be transferred to and consolidated with the Sacramento land district, with office at Sacramento, California.
THE WHITE HOUSE,
May 6, 1906.
J. Roosevelt
