EXECUTIVE ORDER
(Amending Executive Order of March 14, 1905.)
The Executive Order of March 14th, 1905, heretofore issued appointing a Consultative Board of architects to secure uniformity and harmony in the public buildings Hereafter to be constructed, is hereby amended by striking out the words "The members of the board will serve without compensation" and inserting therein the following:
"Each member of the board shall receive while he is engaged in the discharge of the duties imposed by the Executive Order, ten dollars per day, and shall be paid in addition thereto such actual expense of traveling and subsistence as he may have incurred while engaged in the business of the board.
The money needed for the per diems and traveling expenses shall be paid by the officer engaged in the construction of the building and shall be charged by him to the incidental expenses of said construction.
Theodore Roosevelt
THE WHITE HOUSE
March 18, 1905.
