BUREAU OF ROLLS & LIBRARY
MAY 11 1906
DEPARTMENT OF STATE

EXECUTIVE ORDER.

In accordance with the provisions of Section 2252
of the Revised Statutes of the United States, and by virtue
of the authority therein given, it is hereby ordered that:
The Marysville land district in the State of
California, with office at Marysville, California, be dis-
continued, and the lands, business and archives of said
district be transferred to and consolidated with the Sacra-
mento land district, with office at Sacramento, California.

THE WHITE HOUSE,
May 10, 1905.
J. ROOSEVELT

CHIEF CLERK
