Executive Order
The Executive Order dated December 22, 1924, (No. 4115) establishing the
Jackson National Forest, South Carolina, pursuant to the provisions of Section 9
of the Act of Congress approved June 7, 1924, (43 Stat. 655) on a portion of the
Camp Jackson Military Reservation, South Carolina, is hereby revoked.
THE WHITE HOUSE,
May 17, 1928.
