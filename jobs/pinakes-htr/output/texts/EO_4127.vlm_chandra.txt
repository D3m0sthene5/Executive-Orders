Executive Order
FLORIDA
Under authority of the act of Congress approved June 25, 1910 (36 Stat., 847), as amended by the act of August 24, 1912 (37 Stat., 497), it is hereby ordered that the following described lands in the State of Florida, containing .119.66 acres, be, and they are hereby, temporarily withdrawn subject to the provisions and conditions of said acts, for classification and pending enactment of legislation for their proper disposition:
In T. 22 S., R. 17 E., Tal. M., SE¼ NE¼ and SE¼ SE¼ Sec. 13.
In T. 22 S., R. 18 E., Tal. M., NW¼ NW¼ Sec. 18.
