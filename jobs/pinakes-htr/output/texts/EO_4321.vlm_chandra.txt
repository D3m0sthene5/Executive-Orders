Executive Order
Alabama, Florida and Mississippi.
Under authority of the act of Congress approved June 25, 1910 (36 Stat., 847), as amended by the act of August 24, 1912 (37 Stat., 497), it is hereby ordered that Executive order of July 3, 1925, No. 4262, withdrawing certain lands in Alabama, Florida and Mississippi, be revoked in so far as it applies to lands within national forests.
CALVIN COOLIDGE
THE WHITE HOUSE,
October 13, 1925.
[No. 4321]
DEPARTMENT OF STATE.
October 23, 1925
A true copy.
