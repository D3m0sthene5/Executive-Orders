Executive Order.

Consular officers are hereby forbidden to use in their correspondence regarding private or unofficial business matters, paper bearing any heading or device indicating their official position, and they are also forbidden to sign themselves as consuls when acting in an unofficial capacity.

The foregoing prohibition does not apply to authentications and other notarial acts the legal validity of which requires the official signature and seal.

THEODORE ROOSEVELT

WHITE HOUSE,
December 5, 1903.

[No. 240.]
