Executive Order

COTTONWOOD ADMINISTRATIVE SITE
(Near the La Sal National Forest)
UTAH

Under authority of the Act of Congress approved June 25, 1910 (36
Stat., 847), and on the recommendation of the Secretary of Agriculture, it
is hereby ordered that the following described tract of unsurveyed land,
containing 160 acres, be temporarily withdrawn from settlement, location,
sale, or entry, except as provided in said Act, and be reserved for use by
the Forest Service as a Ranger Station in the administration of the La Sal
National Forest.
Township 33 S., Range 21 E., S. L. M., Utah, Sections 5 and 8.
Beginning at Corner No. 1, whence the Forest Service Monument
bears North 40° East, 3 chains;
