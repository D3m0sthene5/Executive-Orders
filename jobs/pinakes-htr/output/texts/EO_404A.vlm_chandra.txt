EXECUTIVE ORDER
100-10000
In accordance with the provisions of Section 2253 of the Revised Statutes of the United States, and by virtue of the authority herein given, it is hereby ordered that the boundary lines of the Bismarck and Fargo land districts in the State of North Dakota be changed and re-established as hereinafter described, and that the lands included in the following described boundaries be detached from the Bismarck district and attached to, and made a part of, the Fargo land district, and that the archives, records and business pertaining to these lands be transferred from the land office at Bismarck, North Dakota, to the land office at Fargo, North Dakota:
Beginning at a point where the line between ranges seventy-one and seventy-two west of the fifth Principal Meridian intersects the fifteenth standard parallel north, thence south along said range line to the southern boundary of the State of North Dakota; thence east along the boundary line to its intersection with the ninth Guide Meridian, between ranges sixty-six and sixty-seven, west of the fifth Principal Meridian, thence north along the guide meridian to its intersection with the twelfth standard parallel north; thence west along the parallel to the point of beginning.
Theodore Roosevelt
the White House,
January 27, 1906
