Executive Order

It is hereby ordered under authority contained in Section 5 of the Act of
February 8, 1887 (24 Stat. L., 388-389), that the trust periods on allotments of
the Absentee Shawnee and Citizen Potawatomi Tribes in Oklahoma, the title to
which has not passed out of the United States, which would otherwise expire dur-
ing the calendar year 1927, be, and the same are hereby extended for a further
period of ten years, excepting only in the following instances:
