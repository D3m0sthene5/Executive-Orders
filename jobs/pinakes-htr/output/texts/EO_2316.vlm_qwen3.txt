Executive Order.

1. It is hereby ordered that the Camp Eldridge Military Reservation,
situated in the municipality of Los Banos, Province of Laguna, Island of
Luzon, Philippine Islands, as reserved by Executive Order dated September
1, 1903 (General Orders, No. 31, War Department, October 13, 1903), modified
by Executive Order dated December 6, 1901 (General Orders, No. 188, War
Department, December 14, 1901), and enlarged by a tract purchased for a
target range for the reservation in 1911 (Transfer Certificate of Title No. 288,
dated December 16, 1914), be, and the same is, hereby further enlarged by
reserving, subject to private rights, if any there be, all lands included within
the metes and bounds described as follows:

Beginning at point marked 1 on Bureau of Lands plan 11 10019, which
point bears S. 4 03' W., 598.68 m. from B. L. L. M. No. 1, Los Banos, thence;
S. 48 48' W. 79.14 m. to point 2; S. 25° 22' E. 16.31 m. to point 3;
