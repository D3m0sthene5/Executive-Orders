Executive Order 5258. January 9, 1930
Withdrawal of Public Lands for Claasification
North Dakota
Under authority of the act of Congress approved June 25, 1910 (36 Stat. 847),
as amended by the act of August 24, 1912 (37 Stat. 497), and subject to the condi-
tions therein expressed, it is hereby ordered that the public lands within the following
described areas be, and the same are hereby temporarily withdrawn for classification
and in aid of legislation, subject to prior valid rights:
