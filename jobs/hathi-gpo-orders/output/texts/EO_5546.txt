Executive Order 5546. January 31, 1931
Executive Order
Extension of Trust Period on Allotments of Indians on the
Cheyenne River Reservation, South Dakota
It is hereby ordered, under authority contained in the act of June 21, 1906
(34 Stat. 325-326), that the trust period on allotments made to Indians of the Chey-
enne River Reservation, S. Dak., which trust period expires diuing the calendar
year 1931, be, and the same is hereby, extended for a period of 10 years.
HERBERT HOOVER
The White House,
January 31 f 1931.
[No. 5546]
