Executive Order 5358. June 3, 1930
Executive Order
Disbursement of Funds, Committee on the Administration and
Conservation of the Public Domain
Funds made available in the Interior Department Appropriation Act, approved
May 14, 1930, for the study of and report on the public domain, will be disbursed by
the chief disbursing officer of the Interior Department, on the certificate and with
the approval of the executive secretary of the Committee on the Administration and
Conservation of the Public Domain.
HERBERT HOOVER
The White House,
June 5, 1930.
