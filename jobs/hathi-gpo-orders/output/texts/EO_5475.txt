Executive Order
Lake Charles, Louisiana, Designated a Customs Port of Entry
By virtue of the authority vested in me by the act of Congress approved August 1,
1914 (38 Stat. 623), making appropriations for the sundry civil expenses of the
Government for the fiscal year ended June 30, 1915, the customs station of Lake
Charles, La., is hereby designated a customs port of entry in Customs Collection
District No. 21 (Sabine) with headquarters at Port Arthur, Tex., effective 30 days
from the date of this order.
HERBERT HOOVER
The White House,
November 5, 1930,
