Executive Order 5577. March 13, 1931
Executive Order
Oakland, California, Abolished as a Customs Port of Entry
Executive Order No. 5011, dated December 15, 1928, designating Oakland,
Calif., a customs port of entry in Customs Collection District No. 28 (San Francisco),
with headquarters at San Francisco, Calif., and including within its port limits
Oakland, Alameda, Berkeley, Emeryville, and San Leandro, is hereby revoked, and,
effective immediately, the said places are restored to and included within the port
limits of San Francisco, Calif.
HERBERT HOOVER
The White House,
March 13, 1931.
[No. 5577]
