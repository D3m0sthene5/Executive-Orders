Executive Order

ALLOWANCE TO RURAL CARRIERS FOR EQUIPMENT MAINTENANCE.

In accordance with the provisions of Section 9 of the Independent Offices Appro-
priation Act, 1934, the amount to be paid to carriers in the Rural Mail Delivery
Service for equipment maintenance is fixed, for the period from July 1 to September
30, 1933, at 1.176 cents per mile per day for each mile or major fraction of a mile

scheduled.
FRANKLIN D. ROOSEVELT.

THe Wuite Houvss,
June 16, 1933.

[No. 6176]
