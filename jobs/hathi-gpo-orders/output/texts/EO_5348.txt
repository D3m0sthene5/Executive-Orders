Executive Order 5348. May 12, 1930
Executive Order
Appointment of Mrs. Helen Hall York
Mrs. Helen Hall York may be appointed a clerk in the classified service of the
Treasury Department without compliance with the requirements of the civil service
rules.
Mrs. York is the widow of former Prohibition Agent Lamar W. York, who met
his death while in the performance of his oflScial duties at Washington, D. C, on April
