Executive Order

NATIONAL RECOVERY REVIEW BoarD

By virtue of the authority vested in me under the provisions of title 1 of the
National Industrial Recovery Act of June 16, 1933 (ch. 90, 48 Stat. 195), and pursuant
to Executive order dated March 7, 1934, creating the National Recovery Review
Board, the Federal Emergency Administration of Public Works is hereby directed to
allot to the said National Recovery Review Board from the moneys heretofore ear-
marked for the National Recovery Administration, the sum of fifty thousand dollars
($50,000) to carry out its functions.

FRANKLIN D. ROOSEVELT.
Tae Waite Hovuss,
March 9, 1984.
