Executive Order

WITHDRAWAL OF PuBLic LANDS FOR RESURVEY
COLORADO

By virtue of the authority vested in me by the act of June 25, 1910 (ch. 421,
36 Stat. 847), as amended by the act of August 24, 1912 (ch. 369, 37 Stat. 497),
and subject to the conditions, provisions, and limitations of said acts, it is hereby
ordered that the public lands in T. 1 S., R. 84 W. of the sixth principal meridian,
Colorado, be, and the same are hereby, withdrawn from settlement, location, sale,
entry, and all forms of appropriation, pending a resurvey of said township under
the act of March 3, 1909 (ch. 271, 35 Stat. 845).

This order shall continue in full force and effect unless and until revoked by the

President or by an act of Congress.
FRANKLIN D. ROOSEVELT.
Tue Wuite Hovuss,
September 6, 1933.
