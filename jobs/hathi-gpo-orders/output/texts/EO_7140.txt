Executive Order

AUTHORIZING THE APPOINTMENT OF Mrs. RutH Hampton ss ASSISTANT
Director, Division OF TERRITORIES AND ISLAND POSSESSIONS, WITH-
ouT REGARD TO CIVIL-SERVICE RULES

By virtue of and pursuant to the authority vested in me by the provisions of
paragraph Eighth of subdivision SECOND of section 2 of the Civil Service Act of
January 16, 1883 (22 Stat. 403, 404), it is hereby ordered that Mrs. Ruth Hampton,
of Illinois, may be appointed as Assistant Director, Division of Territories and Island
Possessions, Department of the Interior, without compliance with the civil-service
Tules and without being accorded a classified status.

FRANKLIN D ROOSEVELT
Tue Waite Houses,

August 18, 1936.
