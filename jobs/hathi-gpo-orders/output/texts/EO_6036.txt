Executive Order 6036. February 18, 1933
(Cxetutttie #ttiet
Appointment of Mr. Edmond M. Martin
Edmond M. Martin, who, since June 28, 1929, has been serving in a position in
the Post Office Department excepted from civil-service requirements, may be ap-
pointed in the classified service without compliance with civil-service rules.
This order is issued upon the recommendation of the Postmaster General.
HERBERT HOOVER
The White House,
February 18, 1933.
[No. 6036]
