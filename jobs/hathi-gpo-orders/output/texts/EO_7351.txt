Executive Order

AUTHORIZING THE APPOINTMENT OF Mrs. LucittE Coy DaILEY TO A
PosITION IN THE CLASSIFIED CiviL SERVICE WiTHOUT REGARD TO
THE CIvIL SERVICE RULES

By virtue of and pursuant to the authority vested in me by the provisions of
paragraph Eighth of subdivision SECOND of section 2 of the Civil Service Act of
January 16, 1883 (22 Stat. 403, 404), it is hereby ordered that Mrs. Lucille Coy Dailey
may be appointed to a position in the classified civil service of the Government
without compliance with the requirements of the Civil Service Rules.

Mrs. Dailey is the widow of Ernest E. Dailey, radioman, first class, United
States Navy, who lost his life in the U. S.S. MACON disaster on February 12, 1935.
Upon her husband’s death Mrs. Dailey was left without adequate means of continued
support.

FRANKLIN D ROOSEVELT
THe Waite Hovss,
April 21, 1936.
