Executive Order

RENTAL AND SUBSISTENCE ALLOWANCES OF OFFICERS FOR FISCAL YEAR
1935

By virtue of and pursuant to the authority vested in me by sections 5 and 6 of
the act of June 10, 1922 (ch. 212, 42 Stat. 628), as amended by section 3 of the act of
March 20, 1933 (ch. 3, 48 Stat. 13), as amended by section 21 of the act of March
28, 1934 (Public, No. 141, 73d Cong.), it is ordered that the following rates be, and
they are hereby, made effective for the fiscal year ending June 30, 1935, for the rental
and subsistence allowances of officers of the various services entitled thereto:

For one subsistence allowance, 57¢ per day.
For one rental allowance for one room, $19 per month.

FRANKLIN D. ROOSEVELT.

Tue Waite Hovss,
June 30, 19384.
