The regulations for the appointment of unclassified laborers are hereby amended
so that Regulation VIII, Reinstatement, shall read as follows:
REGULATION VIII .—REINSTATEMENT
A person separated without delinquency or misconduct from an unclassified
position may be reinstated to a similar position under any department or inde-
pendent establishment subject to physical examination and .to the following
Umitations :
(a) Unless otherwise provided hereinafter, an unclassified laborer may
be reinstated only upon certificate of the Commission and upon requisition
made within one year from the date of separation: Provided j That the
Commission may authorize waiver of the one-year limit herein prescribed
in cases when in its opinion the circumstances are unusual, urgent, or in
the interest of the public service, under the following time Umitations: Two
years where service has been two years but less than three years; three
years where service has been three years but less than four years; four
years where service has been four years but less than five years; and five
years where service has been five years or more.
(b) A person honorably released from the active military or naval
service of the United States after service in the Civil War, or the war with
