OREGON.
Under authority of the act of Congress approved June 25, 1910 (36 Stat. 847),
as amended by the act of August 24, 1912 (37 Stat. 497), and in conformity with
the conditions, limitations, and exceptions of said acts, it is hereby ordered that,
subject to valid existing rights, the NWK NEJ^ Sec. 13, T. 4 S., R. 1 E., Willam-
ette meridian, Oregon, be, and it is hereby temporarily withdrawn from settlement,
location, sale or entry, for classification and in aid of legislation.
This order shall continue in full force and effect unless and until revoked by
the President or by act of Congress.
HERBERT HOOVER
The White House,
Oct. 8, 1929.
