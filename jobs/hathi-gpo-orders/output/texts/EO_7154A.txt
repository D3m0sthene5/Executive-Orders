Executive Order

EsTaBLISHING Des Lacs Migratory WATERFOWL REFUGE

NORTH DAKOTA

By virtue of and pursuant to the authority vested in me as President of the United
States, and in order to effectuate further the purposes of the Migratory Bird Conser-
vation Act (45 Stat. 1222), it is ordered that the following-described lands, acquired
or to be acquired by the United States, in Burke and Ward Counties, North Dakota,
consisting of 24,100 acres, more or less, be, and they are hereby, reserved and set apart
for the use of the Department of Agriculture, subject to valid existing rights, as a
refuge and breeding ground for migratory birds and other wildlife: Provided, that any
private lands within the areas described shall become a part of the refuge hereby
established upon the acquisition of title or lease thereto by the United States:
