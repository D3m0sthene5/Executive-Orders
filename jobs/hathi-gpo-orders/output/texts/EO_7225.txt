Executive Order

AUTHORIZING PROCUREMENT OFFICERS AND EMPLOYEES OF THE TREASURY
DEPARTMENT TO CERTIFY CERTAIN VOUCHERS

By virtue of and pursuant to the authority vested in me by the Emergency
Relief Appropriation Act of 1935, approved April 8, 1935 (Public Resolution No. 11—
74th Congress), I hereby authorize the Director of Procurement of the Treasury
Department and such officers and employees under his direction as he may designate
in writing, to certify and approve vouchers and schedules of disbursements charge-
able to any appropriation-allocation under the Emergency Relief Appropriation Act
of 1935: Provided, that such vouchers are for (a2) payment for materials, supplies,
and equipment, including transportation charges thereon and overhead expenses
pertaining thereto, purchased by the Secretary of the Treasury through the Pro-
curement Division pursuant to section II (A) (2) of Executive Order No. 7034 of
May 6, 1935, or (6) for reimbursing, at prices fixed by the Director of Procurement,
the Work Relief Supply Fund established pursuant to section 1 of Executive Order
No. 7151 of August 21, 1935, for such purchases, including transportation and over-
head expenses thereon: And provided further, that such vouchers have attached
thereto documentary evidence that the materials, etc., so purchased have been duly
received by the requisitioning department, independent establishment, or other
agency whose appropriation-allocations are chargeable therefor.

FRANKLIN D ROOSEVELT
Tue Waits Hovss,
November 15, 1936.
