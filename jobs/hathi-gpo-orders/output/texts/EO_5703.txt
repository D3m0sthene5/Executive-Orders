Executive Order 5703. September 1, 1931
Executive Order
Power-Site Restoration No. 463
Wyoming
So much of the orders of April 12, 1910, creating Temporary Power-Site With-
drawal No. 137, and July 2, 1910, creating Power-Site Reserve No. 137, as affects
the lands hereinafter described is hereby revoked:
Sixth Principal Meridian
T. 39 N., R. 94 W., sec. 7, lot 3.
HERBERT HOOVER
The White House,
September 1, 1931.
[No. 5703]
