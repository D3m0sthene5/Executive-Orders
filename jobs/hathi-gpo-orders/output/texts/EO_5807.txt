Executive Order 5807. February 25, 1932
Cxetuttbe dirtier
Withdrawal of Public Lands for Resurvey
colorado
Under authority contained in the act of June 25, 1910 (36 Stat. 847), as
amended by the act of August 24, 1912 (37 Stat. 497), and subject to the conditions,
provisions, and limitations of said acts, it is hereby ordered that the public lands in
township 1 1 south, range 96 west of the sixth principal meridian, Colorado, be, and
the same are hereby, withdrawn from settlement, location, sale, entry, and all forms
of appropriation, pending a resurvey of said township under the act of March 3,
1909 (35 Stat. 845).
