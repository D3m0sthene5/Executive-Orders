Executive Order 5585. March 30, 1931
Executive Order
Withdrawal of Public Lands for Resurvey
Galifomia
Under authority contained in the act of June 25, 1910 (3&Stat. 847), as amended
by the act of August 24, 1912 (37 Stat. 497), and subject to the conditions, provisions,
and limitations of said acts, it is hereby ordered that the public lands in Ts. 18 S.,
Rs. 3 and 4 E., Mount Diablo meridian, Galifomia, be, and the same are hereby,
withdrawn from settlement, location, sale, entry, and all forms of appropriation,
pending a resurvey of said townships under the act of March 3, 1909 (35 Stat. 845).
This order shall continue in full force and effect unless and until revoked by
the President or by an act of Congress.
HERBERT HOOVER
The White House,
March SO, 1931.
