Executive Order

AUTHORIZATION TO APPOINT GEORGE S. RIDNER

By virtue of and pursuant to the authority vested in me by the provisions of the
last sentence of the eighth paragraph of subdivision second of section 2 of the Civil
Service Act of January 16, 1883 (ch. 27, 22 Stat. 403, 404), it is hereby ordered that
George S. Ridner may be appointed in the Treasury Department to a position as
investigator, Alcohol Tax Unit, Bureau of Internal Revenue, without compliance
with the requirements of the civil-service rules.

Mr. Ridner was formerly employed in the Treasury Department in the enforce-
ment of prohibition laws and was removed from the service as a result of conviction
on charges of extortion. These charges were reinvestigated and the conclusion
reached by officials of the Treasury Department, the judge before whom the case
was tried, the prosecuting attorney, and officials of the Department of Justice,
that Mr. Ridner had been unjustly and wrongfully convicted. He was granted
& full and unconditional pardon on January 12, 1924. In view of Mr. Ridner’s
previous experience, he will be assigned to investigative duties in connection with
violations of internal-revenue laws relating to liquor.

This order is recommended by the Secretary of the Treasury.

FRANKLIN D. ROOSEVELT.
Tae Waite Hovsz,
October 1241934.
