Executive Order 5245. December 23, 1929
To Permit Approval of an Application for Right of Way for a Gas Pipe Line
Within Public Water Reserve No. 36
Wyoming
Upon recommendation of the Secretary of the Interior, Executive order of
August 2, 1916, creating Public Water Reserve No. 36, in Wyoming, is hereby
modified to the extent of authorizing him to approve an application filed by the
Uinta Pipe Line Company, pursuant to the provisions of the act of Congress approved
475
24-519 O - 74 - vol. I - 31

EO 5246 Executive Orders
February 25, 1920 (41 Stat. 437), for right of way for a pipe line to convey gas
crossing certain lands within said reserve.
HERBERT HOOVER
The White House,
December 23, 1929.
