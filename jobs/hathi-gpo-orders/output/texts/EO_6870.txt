AUTHORIZATION TO APPOINT Mrs. Era I. SEXTON

By virtue of and pursuant to the authority vested in me by the provisions of the
last sentence of the eighth paragraph of subdivision second of section 2 of the Civil
Service Act of January 16, 1883 (ch. 27, 22 Stat. 403, 404), it is hereby ordered that
Mrs. Era I. Sexton may be appointed as inspectress of customs, district of San
Antonio, Texas, without compliance with the requirements of the civil-service rules.

Mrs. Sexton is the widow of Harry L. Sexton, collector of customs, who was
burned to death in an airplane accident while in the performance of his official duties,
leaving his widow with three young children without adequate means of support,
and is represented as being qualified to perform the duties of the position designated.

This order is recommended by the Secretary of the Treasury.

FRANKLIN D. ROOSEVELT.
Tue Waite Hovss,
October 10, 1934.
