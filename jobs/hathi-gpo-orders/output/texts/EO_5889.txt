Withdrawal of Public Lands for use in Connection with San
Carlos Indian Irrigation Project, Arizona
Under authority of the act of Congress approved June 25, 1910 (36 Stat. 847-
848), as amended by the act of August 24, 1912 (37 Stat. 497), it is hereby ordered
that the following-described tracts of public lands in New Mexico be, and they are
hereby, withdrawn from settlement, location, sale, or entry, except as provided in
said acts, for use in coimection with the San Carlos Indian Irrigation Project, subject
to any valid rights or claims initiated prior to the date hereof:
