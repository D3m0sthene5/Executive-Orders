Executive Order 5657. June 24, 1931
Executive Order
Amending Schedule A of the Civil-Service Rules
Schedule A of the civil-service rules. Subdivision II — State Department, para-
graph 2, is hereby amended to read :
"Assistants to the Legal Adviser."
HERBERT HOOVER
The White House,
June 24,1931.
