Appointment of Miss Ajlice W. Goodwin
Miss Alice W. Goodwin, who, since September 8, 1924, has been serving in a
position in the Post Office Department excepted from civil-service requirements,
may be appointed in the classified service without compliance with civil-service rules.
This order is issued upon the recommendation of the Postmaster General.
HERBERT HOOVER
The White House,
February 18, 1933,
[No. 6031]
