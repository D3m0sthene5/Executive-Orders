Industries, Inc., is hereby amended so as to correct the errors in the names of two of
the persons designated therein as members of the first Board of Directors by changing
the names ‘Honorable John B. Miller” and ‘Dr. M. L. Brittain”, as they appear
in said order, to ‘‘Honorable John D. Miller’? and ‘‘Dr. Marion Luther Brittain’’,

respectively.
