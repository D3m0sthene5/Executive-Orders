Executive Order

EXCLUDING CERTAIN TRACTS OF LAND FROM TonaGass NATIONAL FOREST
AND RESTORING THEM TO ENTRY

ALASKA

By virtue of and pursuant to the authority vested in me by the act of June 4,
1897 (30 Stat. 11, 36; 16 U.S. C. 473), and upon the recommendation of the Secretary
of Agriculture, it is ordered that the following-described tracts of land in Alaska,
occupied as homesites and identified by elimination surveys, plats and field notes of
which are on file in the General Land Office, Washington, D. C., be, and they are
hereby, excluded from the Tongass National Forest and restored to entry under the
applicable public-land laws:

Homesite no. 254, Mountain Point group, Revillagigedo Island, 1.59 acres;
approximate latitude 55°17'53’’ N., longitude 131932’ W.

Homesite no. 362, on Seclusion Harbor, Kuiu Island, 3.38 acres; approximate
latitude 56°33/30’’ N., longitude 133°52’ W.

Homesite no. 373, lot no. 27, Mountain Point group, Revillagigedo Island,
1.05 acres; approximate latitude 55°17'45’’ N., longitude 131°32’02’’ W.

Homesite no. 374, Mountain Point group, Revillagigedo Island, 0.71 of an acre;
approximate latitude 55°17/30’’ N., longitude 131°32’05’’ W.

Homesite no. 376, Mountain Point group, Revillagigedo Island, 0.90 of an acre;
approximate latitude 55°17’53’’ N., longitude 131°32’ W.

Homesite no. 475, Mountain Point group, Revillagigedo Island, 0.74 of an acre;
approximate latitude 55°17’30’’ N., longitude 131°32/05’’ W.

FRANKLIN D ROOSEVELT
Tue Waite Hovssz,

August 19, 1935.
