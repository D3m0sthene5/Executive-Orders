Executive Order

WITHDRAWAL OF LAND FOR USE BY THE WASHINGTON-ALASKA
MILITARY CABLE AND TELEGRAPH SYSTEM

ALASKA

By virtue of the authority vested in me by the act of June 25, 1910 (ch. 421,
36 Stat. 847), as amended by the act of August 24, 1912 (ch. 369, 37 Stat. 497),
and subject to the conditions of said acts, it is hereby ordered that a tract of land
in Alaska, designated as U.S. Survey No. 1079, trade and manufacturing site of the
Marconi Wireless Telegraph Co. of America, containing 20.55 acres, be, and the
same is hereby, withdrawn and reserved, subject to any valid existing rights, for
use by the Washington-Alaska Military Cable and Telegraph System.

This order shall continue in full force and effect unless and until revoked by the

President or by act of Congress.
FRANKLIN D. ROOSEVELT.

Tae Wuite Hovss,
September 6, 1988.
