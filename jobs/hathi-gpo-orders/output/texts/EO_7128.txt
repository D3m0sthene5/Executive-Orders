WITHDRAWAL OF PuBLic LANDS
ALASKA

By virtue of and pursuant to the authority vested in me by the act of June 25,
1910 (ch. 421, 36 Stat. 847), as amended by the act of August 24, 1912 (ch. 369, 37
Stat. 497), and subject to the conditions therein expressed, to valid existing rights, and
to the provisions of existing withdrawals, it is ordered that the public lands in the fol-
lowing-described areas in Alaska be, and they are hereby, temporarily withdrawn from
settlement, location, sale, entry, or other form of appropriation and reserved to con-
serve the public interests, and in aid of such legislation as may hereafter be proposed
or recommended:
