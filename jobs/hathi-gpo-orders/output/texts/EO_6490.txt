Ae Executive Order

REvocaTION OF WITHDRAWAL OF Pusiic LANDS PENDING RESURVEY
NEW MEXICO

By virtue of the authority vested in me by the act of June 25, 1910 (ch. 421,
36 Stat. 847), as amended by the act of August 24, 1912 (ch. 369, 37 Stat. 497),
Executive Order No. 5315, dated March 26, 1930, withdrawing public lands in T. 16
N., R. 11 E. of the New Mexico principal meridian, New Mexico, pending resurvey,
is hereby revoked.

FRANKLIN D. ROOSEVELT.
Tue Wuite Hovsr,
December 12, 1938.
