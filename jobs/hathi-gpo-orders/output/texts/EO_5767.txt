Appointment of Mrs. Hallie D. Stotler
Mrs. Hallie D. Stotler may be appointed to the position of clerk in the Bureau
of Prohibition, Department of Justice, without compliance with the requirements of
the civil-service rules.
This order is based on a recommendation of the Attorney General, who states
that Mrs. Stotler is the widow of a prohibition officer who met his death in line of
duty. The Civil Service Commission concurs in the recommendation of the
Attorney General.
HERBERT HOOVER
The White House,
December SO, 19S1.
