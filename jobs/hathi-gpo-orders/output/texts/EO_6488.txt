AMENDMENT OF SCHEDULE A, SUBDIVISION XIII, oF THE
CiviL-SERVICE RULES

By virtue of the authority vested in me by the provisions of the last sentence
of the eighth paragraph of subdivision second of section 2 of the Civil Service Act
of January 16, 1883 (ch. 27, 22 Stat. 403, 404), schedule A, subdivision XIII, of the
civil-service rules is hereby amended by the addition of a paragraph reading as
follows:

«2. One position of Director of Information and one position of Administrative
Assistant in the Office of the Secretary.”’

FRANKLIN D. ROOSEVELT.
Tae Waits Hovss,
December 11, 1988.
