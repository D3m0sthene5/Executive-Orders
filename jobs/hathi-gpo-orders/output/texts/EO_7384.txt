by the act of June 25, 1910, ch. 421, 36 Stat. 847, as amended 
by the act of August 24, 1912, ch. 369, 37 Stat. 497, it is 
ordered as follows:
S e c t io n 1. Executive Order No. 5089 of April 9, 1929, tem­
porarily withdrawing certain public lands in Colorado for 
classification and in aid of legislation, is hereby revoked 
as to the following-described land: _
Sixth  P rincipal  M eridian  
, T. 1 S., R. 78 W., sec. 3£, N E & S W & , 40 acres.
