Executive Order 5537. January 21, 1931
Executive Order
Potash Restoration No. 9
California
So much of the order of February 21, 1913, creating Potash Reserve No. 2,
California No. 1, as affects the lands hereinafter described is hereby revoked:
Mount Diablo Meridian
T. 25 S., R, 43 E., sec. 31, lots 4 and 9.
HERBERT HOOVER
The White House,
January 21, 19S1,
