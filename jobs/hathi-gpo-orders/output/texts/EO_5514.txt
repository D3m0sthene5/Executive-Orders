Executive Order 5514. December 15, 1930
Executive Order
Christmas, 1930
It is hereby ordered that the several executive departments and independent
Government establishments in the District of Columbia, including the Government
Printing Office and the Navy Yard and stations, be closed on Wednesday, December
24, 1930, and all clerks and other employees in the Federal service in the District of
Columbia, except those who may for special pubUc reasons be excepted from the pro-
visions of this order, or those whose absence from duty would be inconsistent with the
provisions of existing law, are hereby excused from duty on that day.
This order is not to be deemed as establishing a precedent.
HERBERT HOOVER
The White House,
December 15, 1930.
