Executive Order

EXECUTIVE CoUNCIL.

By virtue of the authority vested in me by the Acts of Congress respectively
entitled, ‘‘AN ACT For the relief of unemployment through the performance of
useful public work, and for other purposes’’, approved March 31, 1933 (Public No.
5—73rd Congress) and ‘‘AN ACT To encourage national industrial recovery, to
foster fair competition, and to provide for the construction of certain useful public
works, and for other purposes’, approved June 16, 1933 (Public No. 67—73rd
Congress), and in order to provide for the orderly presentation of business and to
coordinate inter-agency problems of organization and work of the new governmental
agencies, therefore:

I hereby appoint Colvin W. Brown to exercise temporarily the office of Assistant
Secretary of the Executive Council as created by Executive order of July 11, 1933,
issued pursuant to the authority of the above entitled Acts.

FRANKLIN D. ROOSEVELT.
Tue Waite House,

July 25, 1933,
