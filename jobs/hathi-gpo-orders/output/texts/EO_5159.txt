Executive Order 5159. July 18, 1929
Executive ®rber
By virtue of the authority vested in me by the Act of Congress approved
August 1, 1914, making appropriations for the sundry, civil expenses of the Gov-
ernment for the fiscal year ended June 30, 1915, Reidsville, North Carolina, is
hereby created a port of entry in Customs Collection District No. 15 (North
Carolina) with headquarters at Wilmington, North Carolina, effective thirty days
from the date of this order.
HERBERT HOOVER
The White House,
July 18, 1929.
