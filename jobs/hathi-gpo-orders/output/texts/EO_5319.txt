Executive Order
Los Angeles Water Supply
California

following described tracts of public lands in California be, and they are hereby, with-
drawn from settlement, location, sale, or entry, except as provided in said acts, in aid
of proposed legislation authorizing the sale of these lands to the city of Los Angeles
to protect and augment said city's water supply system :
