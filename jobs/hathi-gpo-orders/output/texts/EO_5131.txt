Executive Order 5131. May 31, 1929
Executive ^rbet
Mr. George Henry Malone, yeoman, 1st class, U. S. Navy, may be appointed
to the position of supervising clerk, OflSce of the Captain of the Yard, Naval Sta-
tion, Cavite, P. I., to fill an existing vacancy, without regard to the Civil Service
Act or Rules.
This action is taken in view of the inability of the U. S. Civil Service Com-
mission to supply local American eligibles thru the Philippine Civil Service Bureau.
The Bureau of Navigation, Navy Department, will direct his discharge from the
Navy.
This Order is issued upon recommendation of the Secretary of the Navy.
HERBERT HOOVER
The White House,
May 31, 1929.
