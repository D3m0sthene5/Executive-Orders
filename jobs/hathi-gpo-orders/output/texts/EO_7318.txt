Executive Order

AUTHORIZING THE APPOINTMENT OF Louis HUNTER GWINN TO THE
Position oF Customs AGENT WiTHouT REGARD TO CiviL SERVICE
RULES
By virtue of and pursuant to the authority vested in me by the provisions of

paragraph Eighth of subdivision SECOND of section 2 of the Civil Service Act of

January 16, 1883 (22 Stat. 403, 404), it is hereby ordered that Louis Hunter Gwinn

may be appointed a Customs Agent in the Customs Service of the Treasury Depart-

ment without compliance with the requirements of the Civil Service Rules.
This order is recommended by the Secretary of the Treasury, who states that Mr.

Gwinn’s appointment as a Customs Agent will be in the interest of the service.

FRANKLIN D ROOSEVELT
Tae Wuire Hovse,
March 18, 1936.
