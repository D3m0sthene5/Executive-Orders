Executive Order 5530. January 9, 1931
Executive Order
Two Harbors, Minnesota, Abolished as a Customs Port of Entry
By virtue of the authority vested in me by the act of Congress approved August
1, 1914 (38 Stat. 623), making appropriations for the sundry civil expenses of the
Government for the fiscal year ended June 30, 1915, Two Harbors, Minn., is hereby
abolished as a customs port of entry in Customs Collection District No. 36 (Duluth
and Superior), with headquarters at Duluth, Minn., and Superior, Wis., effective 30
days from the date of this order.
HERBERT HOOVER
The White House,
January 9y 1931.
