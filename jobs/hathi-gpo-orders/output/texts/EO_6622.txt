Executive Order

AMENDMENT OF EXECUTIVE ORDER No. 6440 or NovemBeER 18, 1933,
AS AMENDED BY EXECUTIVE ORDER No. 6554 or January 10, 1934

Executive Order No. 6440 of November 18, 1933, as amended by Executive
Order No. 6554 of January 10, 1934, relating to rates of compensation of Government
employees in emergency agencies not subject to the Classification Act of 1923 and
acts amendatory thereof, is hereby amended so as to exclude and exempt from the
provisions thereof all employees of the Federal Civil Works Administration assigned
to duty without the District of Columbia or in the field service within the District of
Columbia, and all other employees of the said Administration except the clerical
and administrative personnel employed in the central office in the District of
Columbia.

FRANKLIN D. ROOSEVELT.

THe Wuite House,

March 1, 1934.
