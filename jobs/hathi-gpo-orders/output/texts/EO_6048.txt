Appointment of Harry E. Timmis
Harry E. Timmis, who has been serving in a position under the Department of
Commerce excepted from civil-service requirements, may be appointed in the classi-
fied service without compliance with civil-service rules.
This order is issued upon recommendation of the Secretary of Commerce.
