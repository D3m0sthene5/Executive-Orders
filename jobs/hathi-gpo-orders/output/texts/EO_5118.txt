Executive Order 5118. May 16. 1929
Executive (^bet
LOOKOUT STATION
OREGON
Under authority of the act of Congress approved June 25, 1910 (36 Stat. 847),
as amended by the act of August 24, 1912 (37 Stat. 497), and subject to the condi-
tions therein expressed, it is hereby ordered that the SEJi SWJi Sec. 21, T. 29 S.,
R. 11 W., W. M., Oregon, containing 40 acres, be and it is hereby temporarily
withdrawn from settlement, location, sale or entry, and reserved for use by the
State of Oregon as a lookout station, the timber thereon to remain subject to sale
by the United States in accordance with the provisions of the act of June 9, 1916
(39 Stat. 218).
This order shall remain in full force and effect unless and until revoked by the
President or by act of Congress.
HERBERT HOOVER
The White House,
May 16, 1929.
