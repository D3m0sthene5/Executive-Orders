AUTHORIZING AND DESIGNATING THE FEDERAL EMERGENCY RELIEF
ADMINISTRATOR TO ACQUIRE PROPERTY FOR CERTAIN PURPOSES

By virtue of and pursuant to the authority vested in me by title II of the National
Industrial Recovery Act, approved June 16, 1933 (48 Stat. 195), I hereby authorize
and designate the Federal Emergency Relief Administrator, and the Director of the
Land Program as he may be authorized by such Administrator, to acquire by pur-
chase or by the exercise of the power of eminent domain any real or personal property
or any interest therein, in connection with the construction or carrying on of any
project or program financed by allocations, allotments, or transfers made, or to be
made, to the Federal Emergency Relief Administration under the authority and in
accordance with the provisions of the said National Industrial Recovery Act or acts
supplementing the appropriations heretofore made therefor, and to sell any security
acquired or any property so constructed or acquired or to lease any such property
with or without the privilege of purchase, and to administer or to direct the admin-
istration of such property.

FRANKLIN D ROOSEVELT

THE Wuitt House,

March 6, 1986.
