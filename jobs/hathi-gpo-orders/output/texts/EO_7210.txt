AMENDMENT OF SCHEDULE A, Civit-SERVICE RULES

By virtue of and pursuant to the authority vested in me by the provisions of
paragraph Eighth of subdivision SECOND of section 2 of the Civil Service Act of
January 16, 1883 (22 Stat. 403, 404), schedule A of the civil-service rules is hereby
amended by adding thereto a new subdivision reading as follows:

“XXV. Unitep States High ComMIssioNER TO THE PHILIPPINE IsLANDS

“1, Junior messengers and under clerks with a salary range of $180 to $250
per annum in the office of the United States High Commissioner to the Philippine
Islands.”
