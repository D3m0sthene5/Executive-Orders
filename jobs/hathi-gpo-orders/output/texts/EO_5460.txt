Executive Order 5460. October 8, 1930
Executive Order
Petroleum Restoration No, 59
Colorado
So much of the order of October 25, 1918, creating Petroleum Reserve No. 61 ,
Colorado No. 2, as affects the lands hereinafter described, is hereby revoked, and all
such lands not otherwise reserved or withdrawn are hereby restored to the public
domain and to disposition under the laws applicable thereto.
Sixth Principal Meridian
T. 5 N., R. 91 W., sec. 20, lots 9, 10, 11, 12, 13, and 14;
sec. 29, lots 3, 4, 5, 6, 10, 11, 12, and 13;
sec. 31, all;
sec. 32, lots 1, 2, 3, and 4, tracts 56 and 57.
HERBERT HOOVER
The White House,
October 8, 1930,
[No. 5460]
