Executive Order 5293. March 5, 1930
Executive Order
Public Water Restoration No. 63, Montana No. 11
So much of the order of withdrawal dated November 10, 1917, creating Public
Water Reserve No. 61, as affects the land hereinafter listed is hereby revoked, and all
such land not otherwise reserved or withdrawn is hereby restored to the public
domain and shall become subject to disposition under the laws applicable thereto
upon such date and after such notice as may be hereafter determined and annoimced«
Montana Meridian
T. 1 S., R. 3 W., sec. 6, SE. Ji.
HERBERT HOOVER
The White House,
March 5, 1930.
