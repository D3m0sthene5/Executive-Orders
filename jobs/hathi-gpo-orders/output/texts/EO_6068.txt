Appointment of Mrs. Daphne Mary Byrne
Mrs. Daphne Mary Byrne may be appointed as clerk- typist at $1,260 per annum
in the classified service under the Veterans' Administration. This order is issued
upon the recommendation of the Administrator of Veterans' Aifairs. He states that
Mrs. Byrne is the widow of Joseph V. Byrne, an employee of the Veterans' Administra-
tion who died December 30, 1932. He also states that Mr. Byrne enhsted August 3,
1917, and was discharged September 30, 1919, that he served overseas and suffered
from a service-connected disability, resulting in his death.
HERBERT HOOVER
The White House,
March 5, 1933.
