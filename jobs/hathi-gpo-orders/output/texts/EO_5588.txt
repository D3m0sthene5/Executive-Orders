Executive Order 5588. March 31, 1931
Executive Order
Amendment of Schedule A of the Civil Service Rules in Relation to Appoint-
ment of Private Secretaries without Examination under Rule II, Section 3
The following clauses of Schedule A are revoked: XIV-l, XVI-1, XVII-1,
XX-l, XXI-1, XXII-1; and subdivision I is amended by the addition of clause 17
as follows:
17. One private secretary or confidential clerk to the head of each independent
establishment, to the assistant to the head of each independent establishment, and to
each member of commissions or boards; when such heads, assistants, or members are
appointed by the President.
HERBERT HOOVER
The White House,
March Sly 19S1.
