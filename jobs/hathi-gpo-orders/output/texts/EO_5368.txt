Executive Order 5368. June 16, 1930
Executive Order
Revocation of Withdrawal of Public Lands for Resurvey
Michigan
It is hereby ordered that Executive Order No. 4921 dated June 29, 1928, with-
drawing among other lands the public lands in T. 25 N., R. 7 E., Michigan meridian,
Michigan, pending a resurvey, be, and the same is hereby, revoked as to said
township.
All of said lands are within the limits of Huron National Forest, having been
reserved for forest purposes by proclamations of February 11, 1909, and July 30,
1928, which remain in full force and effect as to said lands.
HERBERT HOOVER
The White House,
June 16, 1930.
[No. 5368]
