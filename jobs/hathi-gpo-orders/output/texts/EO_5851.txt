Appointment of Mrs. Nesta Stephens Long
Mrs. Nesta Stephens Long may be appointed a clerk in the Customs Service in
the district of Arizona for a period of one year without regard to her standing on the
civil-service junior stenographic register.
Mrs. Long is the wife of Carroll Robert Long who has served as a clerk in the
office of the Collector of Customs at Nogales, Ariz., since July 11, 1927, and is forced
to give up his position temporarily on account of serious illness. Mrs. Long passed
