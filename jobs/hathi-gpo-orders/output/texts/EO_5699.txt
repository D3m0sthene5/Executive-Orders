Executive Order 5699. August 31, 1931
Executive Order
Revocation of Withdrawal of Public Lands for Resurvey
Oregon
It is hereby ordered that Executive Order No. 5115, dated May 15, 1929, with-
drawing the public lands in T. 18 S., R. 9 W., Willamette meridian, Oregon, pending
a resurvey, be, and the same is hereby, revoked.
And it is hereby ordered, pursuant to PubUc Resolution 85 of June 12, 1930 (46
Stat. 580), that, subject to vaUd rights, the unreserved public lands in said township,
if otherwise subject to disposition, shall be opened, under the terms and conditions of
such resolution and the regulations issued thereunder, to entry under the homestead
or desert land laws only, by quaUfied ex-service men for whose service recognition is
granted by the resolution, for a period of 91 days beginning with the date of the official
filing of the plat of resurvey of said township, and thereafter to appropriation by the
general public under any public land law applicable thereto.
