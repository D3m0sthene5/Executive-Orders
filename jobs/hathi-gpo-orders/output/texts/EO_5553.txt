Executive Order 5553. February 9, 1931
Executive Order
Appointment of Mrs. Nixon S. Plummet
Mrs. Nixon S. Plummer may be appointed to an appropriate position in the
Federal Trade Commission without regard to the civil service rules.
This order is issued upon the recommendation of the chairman of the com-
mission, who states that because of the illness of her husband Mrs. Plummer is
compelled to support herself and small daughter and that she has the education and
experience that will enable the commission to use her services to advantage.
HERBERT HOOVER
The White House,
February 9, 1931.
[No. 5553]
