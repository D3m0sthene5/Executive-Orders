Executive Order 5805. February 23, 1932
Cxecttttbe 0ttitx
Withdrawal of Public Lands for Resurvey
new mexico
Under authority contained in the act of June 25, 1910 (36 Stat. 847-848), as
amended by the act of August 24, 1912 (37 Stat. 497), and subject to the conditions,
provisions, and limitations of said acts, it is hereby ordered that the public lands in
the following-described townships in New Mexico be, and the same are hereby,
withdrawn from settlement, location, sale, entry, and all forms of appropriation,
pending a resurvey of said townships under the act of March 3, 1909 (35 Stat. 845) :
New Mexico Principal Meridian
T. 3 S., R. 18 E.;
T. 2S., R. 19 E.;
T. 4 S., R. 21 E.;
Tps. 6 S., Rs. 21 and 22 E.
This order shall continue in full force and effect unless and until revoked by
the President or by an act of Congress.
HERBERT HOOVER
The White House,
February 2S, 1932.
