Exemption of Persons from Compulsory Retirement for Age
By virtue of the authority vested in me by section 204 of Part II of the act
entitled "AN ACT Making appropriations for the Legislative Branch of the Govern-
ment for the fiscal year ending June 30, 1933, and for other purposes,*' approved
June 30, 1932, the following-named persons who, during the current month, reach
the retirement age prescribed for automatic separation from the service are hereby
exempted from the provisions of that section.
