KNOXVILLE, TENNESSEE, ABOLISHED AS A CUSTOMS PorT oF ENTRY

By virtue of the authority vested in me by section 1 of the act of August 1, 1914
(ch. 223, 38 Stat. 609, 623; 19 U.S.C., sec. 2), Knoxville, Tennessee, is hereby
abolished as a customs port of entry in customs collection district no. 43 (Tennessee),
with headquarters at Memphis, Tennessee, effective as of 30 days from the date of
this order.
FRANKLIN D. ROOSEVELT.
THe Waite Hovsz,

September 13, 1938.
