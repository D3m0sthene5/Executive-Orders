Executive Order 5406. July 25, 1930
Executive Order
Restoring Portion of Upolu Point Military Reservation to the
Territory of Hawaii
Pursuant to the authority vested in me by section 91 of the act of Congress
approved April 30, 1900 (31 Stat. 159), entitled "An act to provide a government
for the Territory of Hawaii", as amended by section 7 of the act of Congress approved
May 27, 1910 (36 Stat. 447), it is hereby ordered that the tract of land known as the
Upolu Airplane Landing Field, Island of Hawaii, Territory of Hawaii, set aside for

And provided that the land so restored to the Territory of Hawaii shall be at all

available for the taking off and landing of airplanes or airships belonging to the
United States without charge, such reserved privilege to include such temporary
occupation for operation and maintenance of Government airplanes or airships as
may from time to time be deemed necessary by competent military authority.
