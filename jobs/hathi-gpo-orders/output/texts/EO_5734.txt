Executive Order 5734. October 17, 1931
Executive Order
Extension of Trust Period on Allotments of the Warm Springs
Reservation, Oreg.
It is hereby ordered, under authority contained in section 5 of the act of Febru-
ary 8, 1887 (24 Stat. 388-389), that the trust period on allotments made in favor of
Indians of the Warm Springs Reservation, Greg., which trust period expires during
the calendar year 1931, be, and the same is hereby, extended for a period of 10 years.
HERBERT HOOVER
The White House,
October 17, 1931,
[No. 5734]
