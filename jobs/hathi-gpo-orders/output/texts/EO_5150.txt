Executive Order 5150. July 5, 1929
Executive ®tbet
Mrs. Mae A. Krigbaum may be reinstated as an operative in the Bureau of
Engraving and Printing without regard to the length of her separation from the
service. She was appointed and served thru competitive examination as printer's
assistant, from Jime, 1902, to February, 1905. She is the widow of Samuel Elrig-
baum who died December 19, 1928, after serving as plate printer in the Bureau
for over twenty-six years, leaving his widow with a house and a small insurance
which is nearly gone, and three dependent children of 18, 16, and 12 years, the
oldest, a boy, being in poor health.
The Secretary of the Treasury recommends this order.
HERBERT HOOVER
The White House,
July 5, 1929.
