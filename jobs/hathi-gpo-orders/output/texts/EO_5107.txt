Executive Order 5107. ^ May 8, 1929
Executive 0rbet
Schedule A of positions excepted from the requirement of examination under
the civil service rules is hereby amended by the addition of a new subdivision to be
numbered XX, said amendment being prepared and recommended by the Civil
Service Commission in compliance with the official request of the Chairman of the
United States Employees' Compensation Commission:
XX. UNITED STATES EMPLOYEES' COMPENSATION COMMISSION
1. One private secretary or confidential clerk to each member of the
United States Employees' Compensation Commission.
HERBERT HOOVER
The White House,
May 8, 1929,
[No. 5107]
