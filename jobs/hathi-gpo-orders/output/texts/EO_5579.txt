Warning is given to all persons not to commit any of the acts herein enumeratedi
under the penalties prescribed by sections 106, 107, and 145 of chapter 4, title 18,

Bird Conservation Act of Febmary 18, 1929, United States Code, Supplement III,
title 16, chapter 7a (45 Stat. 1222).
This refuge shall be known as the Crescent Lake Wild life Refuge.
