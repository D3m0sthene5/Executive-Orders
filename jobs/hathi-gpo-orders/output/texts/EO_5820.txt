Revocation of Withdrawal of Pubuc Lands Pending Resurvby
arkansas
It is hereby ordered that Executive Order No. 5116, dated May 16, 1929, which
withdrew the public lands in T. 1 N., R. 26 W., fifth principal meridian, Arkansas,
pending resurvey, be, and the same is hereby, revoked.
All the lands in this township are included in the Ouachita National Forest.
HERBERT HOOVER
The White House,
March 16, 19S2.
