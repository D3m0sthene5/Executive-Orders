Executive Order 5173. August 9, 1929
iBrecutive ®rber
It is hereby ordered, under authority contained in Section 5 of the Act of
February 8, 1887 (24 Stats. 388), that the trust period on the allotments of Indians
on the Yankton Sioux Reservation, South Dakota, which expires during the calen-
dar year 1929, be, and is hereby extended for a further period of ten years.
HERBERT HOOVER
The White House,
August 9, 1929.
