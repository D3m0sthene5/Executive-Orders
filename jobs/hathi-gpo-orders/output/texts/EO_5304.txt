Executive Order 5304. March 14, 1930
Executive Order
Withdrawal of Public Lands for Resurvey
Colorado
Under authority contained in the act of June 25, 1910 (36 Stat. 847), as amended
by the act of August 24, 1912 (37 Stat. 497), and subject to the conditions, provisions,
and limitations of said acts, it is hereby ordered that the public lands in T. 50 N.,
R. 11 E., New Mexico principal meridian, Colorado, be, and the same are hereby
withdrawn from settlement, location, sale, entry, and all forms of appropriation,
pending a resurvey of said township under the act of March 3, 1909 (35 Stat. 845).
