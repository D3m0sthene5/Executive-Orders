Executive Order 5287. February 25, 1930
Executive Order
Withdrawal of Public Lands for Resurvey
Nevada
Under authority contained in the act of June 25, 1910 (36 Stat. 847), as amended
by the act of August 24, 1912 (37 Stat. 497), and subject to the conditions, provisions,
and limitations of said acts, it is hereby ordered that the public lands in T. 41 N.,
R. 21 E., Mt. Diablo meridian, and in the fractional west halves of sections 6, 7, 18,
and 19, T. 41 N., R. 22 E., Mt. Diablo meridian, Nevada, be, and the same are
hereby, withdrawn from settlement, location, sale, entry, and all forms of appro-
priation, pending a resurvey of said T. 41 N., R. 21 E. under the act of March 3,
1909 (35 Stat. 845).
This order shall continue in full force and effect imless and until revoked by the
President, or by an act of Congress.
HERBERT HOOVER
The White House,
February 25, 1930.
