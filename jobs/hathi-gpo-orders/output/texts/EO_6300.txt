Executive Order

CHINCOTEAGUE, VIRGINIA, ABOLISHED AS A Customs Port or ENTRY

By virtue of the authority vested in me by section 1 of the act of August 1, 1914
(ch. 223, 38 Stat. 609, 623; 19 U.S.C., sec. 2), Chincoteague, Virginia, is hereby
abolished as a customs port of entry in Customs Collection District No. 14 (Virginia),
with headquarters at Norfolk, Virginia, effective as of 30 days from the date of this

order.
FRANKLIN D. ROOSEVELT.

Tue Waite Hovssz,
September 30, 1933.
