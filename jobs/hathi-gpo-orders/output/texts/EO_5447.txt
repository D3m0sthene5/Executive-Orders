Executive Order 5447. September 22, 1930
Executive Order
Reservation of Lots in Seward for the Department of Agriculture
Alaska
Under authority of the act of March 12, 1914 (38 Stat. 305), it is hereby ordered
that lots 1, 2, and 3, block 12, of the Federal Addition to the town-site of Seward,

Alaska, containing 5,000 square feet each, according to the plat thereof approved
May 22, 1916, be, and the same are hereby, reserved and set aside for the use of the
Department of Agriculture.
This order shall continue in full force and effect unless and until revoked by the
President or by act of Congress.
