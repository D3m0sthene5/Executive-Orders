Exemption of Oliver M. Maxam from Compulsory Retirement
FOR Age
By virtue of the authority vested in me by section 204 of Part II of the act
entitled "AN ACT Making appropriations for the Legislative Branch of the Gov-
ernment for the fiscal year ending June 30, 1933, and for other purposes," approved
Jime 30, 1932, Oliver M. Maxam, Chief of the Division of Operations, United States
Coast Guard, Treasury Department, who, during the current month, reaches the
retirement age prescribed for automatic separation from the service, is hereby
exempted from the provisions of that section.
HERBERT HOOVER
The White House,
January 19, 19SS,
