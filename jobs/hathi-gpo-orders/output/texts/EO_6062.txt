Appointment of Ruby Mae Webb
Ruby Mae Webb may be appointed to a position as rural letter carrier in the
classified service without compliance with the civil-service rules.
The Postmaster General, who concurs in the issuance of this order, states that
Mrs. Webb is the widow of John G. Webb, who served as a rural letter carrier at
Saginaw, Tex., from April 9, 1920, to January 15, 1933, when he was drowned after
rescuing from drowning two other persons; that in addition to his wife he is survived
