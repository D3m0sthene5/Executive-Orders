AMENDMENT TO TREASURY Decision 4359 TO PERMIT INSPECTION OF RETURNS BY
SpeciaL CoMMITTEE TO INVESTIGATE FOREIGN AND Domestic, OCEAN AND

Arr Mar. Contracts
Treasury DEPARTMENT,
Washington, D. C.

To Collectors of Internal Revenue and Others Concerned:

Treasury Decision 4359 (being regulations prescribed by the Secretary and
approved by the President, applicable to the inspection of returns under the Revenue
Act of 1932 and prior Revenue Acts, and incorporated as part of article 421 of Income
Tax Regulations 77), is amended by inserting between paragraphs 13 and 14 thereof
& new paragraph numbered 13(a) reading as follows:

“*13(a). Notwithstanding any other provisions of these regulations, returns may
be inspected by the Special Committee to Investigate Foreign and Domestic, Ocean
and Air Mail Contracts, appointed under Senate Resolution 349, Seventy-second
Congress, to the same extent and in the same manner as by a select committee of the
Senate specially authorized to investigate returns by a resolution of the Senate.”

Tuomas HEwWEs,
Acting Secretary of the Treasury.

Approved:

Frankuin D. RoosEvett.
The White House,
August 3, 1933.
