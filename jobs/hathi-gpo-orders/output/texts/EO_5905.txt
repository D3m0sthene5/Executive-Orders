Appointment of Mrs. Sarah L. Curtiss
Mrs. Sarah L. Curtiss may be appointed to a clerkship at $1,260 per annum in
the Veterans' Administration without regard to the civil-service requirements.
Mrs. Curtiss is the widow of Dr. William W. Curtiss, formerly a commissioned
officer in the Public Health Service and an employee of the Veterans' Administration.
This order is issued upon the recommendation of the Administrator of Veterans'
Affairs.
HERBERT HOOVER
The White House,
August 18, 1932.
