Executive Order

AMENDMENT OF EXECUTIVE ORDER No. 7129, or Avcust 6, 1935,
AUTHORIZING THE ACQUISITION OF LaNnpD ON ISLE ROYALE FOR
EMERGENCY CONSERVATION WORK
Executive Order No. 7129 of August 6, 1935 is hereby amended by striking out

the words “from the appropriation made by the said Deficiency Act for carrying into

effect the provisions of the said act of March 31, 1933.” at the end of the fourth para-
graph of the said order and inserting in lieu thereof the words “from funds appro-

priated or made available by the said Emergency Relief Appropriation Act of 1935.”

FRANKLIN D ROOSEVELT

Tue WuiteE Hovss,
Sept. 25 1935
