Executive Order

APPOINTMENT OF Davip H. Morton

David H. Morton may be appointed as personal secretary and law clerk in the
United States Customs Court, New York, without reference to the requirements of
the civil service rules. This action is desired by Judge Brown of the court.

FRANKLIN D. ROOSEVELT.
Tue Waite Hovse,
October 6, 1988.
