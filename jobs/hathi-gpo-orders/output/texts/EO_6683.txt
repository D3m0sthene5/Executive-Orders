Executive Order

AMENDMENT OF EXECUTIVE ORDER No. 6474, or DEcEMBER 4, 1933,
AND OF EXECUTIVE ORDER No. 6576, or JANUARY 25, 1934

Paragraph (3) of Executive Order No. 6474, of December 4, 1933, relating to the
establishment of the Federal Alcohol Control Administration, etc., as amended by
Executive Order No. 6576, of January 25, 1934, is hereby amended to read as follows:

‘“‘(3) The following-named persons are hereby designated and appointed as the

present members of the Federal Alcohol Control Administration established under
the provisions of said title:

Joseph H. Choate, Jr.
Harris E. Willingham

W. A. Tarver
Willard L. Thorp
John E. Dalton
Frederic P. Lee”
FRANKLIN D ROOSEVELT
Tur Waite Hovst,
April 19, 1934.
