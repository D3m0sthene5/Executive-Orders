Executive Order 5177. August 23, 1929
Biecutive ®rbet
NEW MEXICO
It is hereby ordered that Executive Order No. 4506 dated September 11, 1926,
withdrawing the public lands in T. 17 N., E. 10 E., N.M.P.M., New Mexico,
pending a resurvey, be and the same is hereby revoked.
And it is hereby ordered, pursuant to Public Resolution No. 29 of February
14, 1920 (41 Stat. 434), as amended January 21 and December 28, 1922 (42 Stat.
358, 1067), that the above described lands, if otherwise subject to disposition,
subject to valid rights, shall be opened to entry only by qualified ex-service men of
the World War under the homestead or desert land laws under the terms and con-
ditions of such resolutions and regulations issued thereunder, for a period of 91
days beginning with the official filing of the plat of resurvey, and thereafter to
appropriation by the general public under any public land law applicable thereto.
