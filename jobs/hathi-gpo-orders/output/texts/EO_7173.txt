ENLARGING Rep Rock Lakes MIGRATORY WATERFOWL REFUGE
MONTANA

By virtue of and pursuant to the authority vested in me as President of the
United States, and in order to effectuate further the purposes of the Migratory Bird
Conservation Act (45 Stat. 1222), it is ordered that the following-described lands,
consisting of 37,000 acres, more or less, acquired or to be acquired by the United
States, in Beaverhead County, Montana, be, and they are hereby, reserved and set
apart for the use of the Department of Agriculture, subject to valid existing rights,
as an addition to the Red Rock Lakes Migratory Waterfowl Refuge established by
Executive Order No. 7023, of April 22, 1935: Provided, That any private lands within
the areas described shall become a part of the refuge upon the acquisition of title or
lease thereto by the United States:

and other wildlife: Provided, That any private lands within the areas described shall
become a part of the refuge hereby established upon the acquisition of title or lease
thereto by the United States:
