Executive Order 5681. August 12, 1931
Executive Order
Withdrawal of Public Lands for Resurvey
California
Under authority contained in the act of June 25, 1910 (36 Stat. 847), as amended
by the act of August 24, 1912 (37 Stat. 497), and subject to the conditions, provisions,
and linutations of said acts, it is hereby ordered that the public lands in T. 9 S., R.
3 E., San Bernardino meridian, Calif omia, be, and the same are hereby, withdrawn
from settlement, location, sale, entry, and all forms of appropriation, pending a
resurvey of said township under the act of March 3, 1909 (35 Stat. 845).
