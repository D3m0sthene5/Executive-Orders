Revocation op Withdrawal op Public Lands Pending Resurvey
minnesota
It is hereby ordered that Executive Order No. 5309, dated March 24, 1930,
which withdrew the public lands in T. 62 N., R. 2 E., of the fourth principal meridian,
Minnesota, pending resurvey, be, and the same is hereby, revoked.
All the lands in this township are within the limits of the Superior National
Forest.
HERBERT HOOVER
The White House,
April 28, 1982,
