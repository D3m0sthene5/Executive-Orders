Executive Order 6037. February 18, 1933
Cxetttttoe (Bthtx
Appointment of Miss Alice Mummenhoff
Miss Alice Mummenhoff, who, since March 5, 1923, has been serving in a position
in the Post Office Department excepted from civil-service requirements, may be
appointed in the classified service without compliance with civil-service rules.
This order is issued upon the recommendation of the Postmaster General.
HERBERT HOOVER
The White House,
February 18, 1933.
