Executive Order 5081. March 22, 1929
JBtecutive ®rber
By virtue of the authority vested in me by the Act of Congress approved
August 1, 1914, making appropriations for the sundry civil expenses of the Govern-
ment for the fiscal year ended June 30, 1915, Roche Harbor is hereby abolished as
a port of entry in Customs Collection District No. 30 (Washington) with head-
quarters at Seattle, Washington, effective thirty days from the date of this Order.
HERBERT HOOVER
The White House,
March 22, 1929.
