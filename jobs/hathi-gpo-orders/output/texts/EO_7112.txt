Executive Order

Pusiic WATER RESERVE No. 158
MONTANA

By virtue of and pursuant to the authority vested in me by the act of June 25,
1910 (ch. 421, 36 Stat. 847), as amended by the act of August 24, 1912 (ch. 369, 37
Stat. 497), it is ordered that the following-described public land in the State of
Montana be, and it is hereby, temporarily withdrawn from settlement, location,
sale, or entry and reserved for public use in accordance witb the provisions of sec-
tion 10 of the act of December 29, 1916 (39 Stat. 862, 865):

Montana MERIDIAN
T.45S., R. 17 E., sec. 4, SWYNEX, NEYSW%, NWYSEX.

FRANKLIN D ROOSEVELT

Tue Waits Hovse,
July 24, 1936.
