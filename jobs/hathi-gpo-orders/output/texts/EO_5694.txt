Executive Order 5694. August 25, 1931
Executive Order
Withdrawal of Public Land in Aid of Legislation
Oregon
Under authority of the act of Congress approved June 25, 1910 (36 Stat. 847),
as amended by the act of August 24, 1912 (37 Stat. 497), it is hereby ordered that the
NE. Vi NE. % and SE. }i NE. }i sec. 10, T. 3 S., R. 6 W., Willamette meridian, Oregon,
