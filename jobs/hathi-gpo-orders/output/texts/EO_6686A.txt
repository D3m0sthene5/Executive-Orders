Executive Order

AUTHORIZATION To Appoint Miss HELEN Wooprow Bones

By virtue of the authority vested in me by the provisions of the last sentence of
the eighth paragraph of subdivision second of section 2 of the Civil Service Act of
January 16, 1883 (ch. 27, 22 Stat. 403, 404), it is hereby ordered that Miss Helen
Woodrow Bones may be appointed to an appropriate position in the Department
of State without compliance with the requirements of the civil-service rules.

FRANKLIN D ROOSEVELT
Tue Wuire Hovse,
April 21, 1934.

[No. 6686-A]
