Executive Order 5356. May 28, 1930
Executive Order
Extension of Trust Period on Certain Allotments Made to Members
of the Prairie Band of Potawatomi Indians in Kansas
It is hereby ordered, under authority contained in section 6 of the act of February
8, 1887 (24 Stat. 389), that the trust period on allotments made to members of the
Prairie Band of Potawatomi Indians in Kansas, which trust period expires during
the calendar year 1930, be, and is hereby, extended for a period of ten years, with
