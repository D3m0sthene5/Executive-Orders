Executive Order

WITHDRAWAL OF PusBtic LANDS FOR RESURVEY

NEW MEXICO

By virtue of the authority vested in me by the act of June 25, 1910 (ch. 421,
36 Stat. 847), as amended by the act of August 24, 1912 (ch. 369, 37 Stat. 497),
and subject to the conditions, provisions, and limitations of said acts, it is hereby
ordered that the public lands in T. 10 S., R. 30 E. of the New Mexico principal
meridian, New Mexico, be, and the same are hereby, withdrawn from settlement,
location, sale, entry, and all forms of appropriation, pending a resurvey of said
township under the act of March 3, 1909 (ch. 271, 35 Stat. 845).

This order shal] continue in full force and effect unless and until revoked by the

President or by an act of Congress.
FRANKLIN D. ROOSEVELT.
Tue Wuitt Hovse,
September 6, 1933.
