Appointment of Martha Owczarzak
Mrs. Martha Owczarzak of Bay City, Michigan, may be appointed a charwoman
in the Custodian Service, Post Office, Bay City, Michigan, without regard to her stand-
ing on the Civil Service register for classified laborer, (female).
Mrs. Owczarzak is the wife of Frank Owczarzak who has had both of his legs
amputated. They have four children dependent upon them for support.
This Order is recommended by the Secretary of the Treasury.
HERBERT HOOVER
