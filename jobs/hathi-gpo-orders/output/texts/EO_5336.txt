Executive Order 5336. April 23, 1930
Executive Order
Coal Land Restoration, Utah No. 48
So much of the order of withdrawal of July 7, 1910, made for the purpose of coal
land classification, namely Utah No. 1, as affects the lands hereinafter described is
hereby revoked for the reason that the Director of the Geological Survey has classified
these lands. This revocation does not affect withdrawals or reservations other than
as above set forth.
