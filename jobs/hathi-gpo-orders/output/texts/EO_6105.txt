Executive Order

APPOINTMENT OF Miss JEssiz B. SAUNDERS

Miss Jessie B. Saunders may be appointed to an appropriate position in the
Bureau of the Budget without regard to the civil-service rules.
This order is recommended by the Director of the Bureau of the Budget.

FRANKLIN D ROOSEVELT
Tag Waits Hovsz,
April 6, 1988.
