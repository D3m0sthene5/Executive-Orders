Executive Order 6054. February 28, 1933
Cxecuttoe ©rber
Withdrawal of Public Lands for Resurvey
colorado
Under authority contained in the act of June 25, 1910 (36 Stat. 847-848), as
amended by the act of August 24, 1912 (37 Stat. 497), and subject to the conditions,
provisions, and limitations of said acts, it is hereby ordered that the public lands in
T. 1 S., R. 75 W. of the sixth principal meridian, Colorado, be, and the same are
hereby, withdrawn from settlement, location, sale, entry, and all forms of appropria-
tion, pending a resurvey of said township under the act of March 3, 1909
(35 Stat. 845).
This order shall continue in full force and effect imless and until revoked by the
President or by an act of Congress,
HERBERT HOOVER
The White House,
February 28, 19SS.
