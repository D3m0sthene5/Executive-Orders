WITHDRAWAL OF PusBLic LANDS FOR RESURVEY
NEW MEXICO

By virtue of the authority vested in me by the act of June 25, 1910 (ch. 421,
36 Stat. 847), as amended by the act of August 24, 1912 (ch. 369, 37 Stat. 497), and
subject to the conditions, provisions, and limitations of said acts, it is hereby ordered
that the public lands in T. 12 S., R. 8 W. of the New Mexico principal meridian, New
Mexico, be, and they are hereby, withdrawn from settlement, location, sale, entry,
and all forms of appropriation, pending a resurvey of said township under the act of
March 3, 1909 (ch. 271, 35 Stat. 845).

This order shall continue in full force and effect unless and until revoked by the
President or by an act of Congress.

FRANKLIN D. ROOSEVELT.
THe Wuite House,

December 15, 1988.
