Executive Order

AMENDMENT OF EXECUTIVE ORDER No. 6433-A, oF NOVEMBER 17, 1933

WHEREAS the last paragraph of Executive Order No. 6433-A, dated November
17, 1933, creating the National Emergency Council, abolishes, effective 30 days
from the date of said order, the volunteer field agencies established under and for
the purpose of effectuating the legislation under the authority of which said order

was issued; and

WHEREAS it is desirable and necessary to defer to a later date the abolition
of such volunteer field agencies;

NOW, THEREFORE, it is hereby ordered that the effective date for the
abolition of the aforesaid volunteer field agencies be, and hereby is, deferred to

January 16, 1934.
FRANKLIN D. ROOSEVELT.

Tue Wuite House,
December 16, 1933.
