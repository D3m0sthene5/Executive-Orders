Executive Order 12944 of December 28, 1994
Adjustments of Certain Rates of Pay
By the authority vested in me as President by the Constitution and the laws of the United States of America, it is hereby ordered as follows:
Section 1. Statutory Pay Systems. The rates of basic pay are adjusted.
Sec. 5. Superseded Orders. Executive Order No. 12826 of December 30, 1992, and Executive Order No. 12886 of December 23, 1993, are superseded.
Sec. 6. Executive Orders Nos. 9001-9003 are hereby revoked.
Sec. 7. Section 3 of Executive Order 11111 is revoked insofar as it relates to the Panama Canal.
Sec. 8. Executive Order No. 10077 of September 7, 1949, which transfers the administration of the Island of Guam and revokes Executive Order No 108-A of December 23, 1898, is hereby amended by changing the effective date.
Sec. 9. I hereby revoke Executive Order 12345 and Executive Order 12346.
Sec. 10. The Council established by Executive Order 12500 is hereby abolished.
Sec. 11. Executive Order 12944 is cited here and must not point at itself; it is hereby amended.
THE WHITE HOUSE, December 28, 1994.
