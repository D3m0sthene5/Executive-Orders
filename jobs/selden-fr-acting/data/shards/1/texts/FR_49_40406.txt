[CGD 80-024]
Lifesaving Equipment fo r G reat Lakes 
Vessels, Emergency Position  
Indicating Radio Beacons
a g e n c y: Coast Guard, DOT.
ACTIO N: Final rules.
SUMMARY: These rules require that small 
passenger vessels, other inspected Great 
Lakes vessels, as well as certain 
coastwise vessels operating on the 
Great Lakes, be equipped with 
Emergency Position Indicating Beacons 
(EPIRB). The purpose of these beacons 
is to provide a radio signal that will 
alert potential rescuers to a casualty 
and to provide a beacon to assist in 
locating the casualty. The need for this 
action arises from several Great Lakes 
vessel casualties in which such a 
beacon would probably have prevented 
some of the loss of life.
EFFECTIVE DATE: These rules become 
effective on March 29,1985.
FOR FURTHER INFOR M ATIO N CONTACT:
Mr. Steams Whitney, Office of 
Merchant Marine Safety, (202) 426-1444.
