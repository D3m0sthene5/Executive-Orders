EXTENDING BOUNDARIES OF CHEQUAMEGON 
NATIONAL FOREST; PARTIALLY REVOKING 
EXECUTIVE ORDER OF JULY 21, 1871,
WHICH WITHDREW LANDS FOR LIGHT­
HOUSE PURPOSES
By virtue of the authority vested in 
the President by section 24 of the act of 
March 3, 1891 (26 Stat. 1103; 16 U. S. C. 
471), and the act of June 4,1897 (30 Stat.
34, 36; 16 U. S. C. 473), and pursuant to 
Executive Order No. 10355 of May 26, 
1952, it is ordered as follows:
The boundaries of the Chequamegon 
National Forest are hereby extended to 
include the following-described public 
lands in Wisconsin, and, subject to valid 
existing rights, the lands are hereby 
made a part of the said national forest 
and hereafter shall be subject to all laws 
and regulations applicable thereto:
Beginning at a point on the west shore of  
lot 2, sec. 12, T. 52 N., R. 5 W., from which a 
point on the south boundary of lot 1, sec. 12  
is due north approximately 530 feet, and  
from which the present boathouse bears  
north 160 feet, thence
East 200 feet;
North 200 feet;
West 340 feet, approximately, to shore line;
Southerly along shore line to point of  
beginning.
The tract described contains 0.902 
acre.
The Executive order of July 21, 1871, 
which withdrew certain public lands in 
Wisconsin for lighthouse purposes is 
hereby revoked so far as its affects the 
lands.
F red G. Aandahl,
Assistant Secretary of the Interior.
