In the matter of amendment of Part  
3 of the Commission’ s rules and regula­

therein.

editorial in nature, and, therefore, prior  
publication of Notice of Proposed Rule

unnecessary, and the amendments may  
become effective immediately.

issued pursuant to authority contained  
m sections 4 (i), 5 (d) (1) and 303 C r)
