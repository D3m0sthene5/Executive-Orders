§  37.7 Retired employees,  (a) To be 
eligible for life insurance as a retired 
employee, an insured employee must be 
entitled to immediate annuity under a 
system legally established for the retire­
ment of civilian employees of the Fed­
eral or District of Columbia Govern­
ments, must have had at least 15 years 
of creditable service or be retired for 
disability, and must have met all re­
quirements for annuity (including filing 
of application  where necessary),

service allowable under the provisions of

ice, honorable active service performed

States.
