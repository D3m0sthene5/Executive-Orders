BILLING CODE 1505-01-M
INTERSTATE COMMERCE  
COMMISSION
[Ex Parte No. 346 (Sub-No. 8)
49 CFR Part 1039
Rail Carriers; Exemption From  
Regulation; Boxcar Traffic
AGENCY: Interstate Commerce 
Commission.
ACTION: Modification of final rules 
(exemption).
SUMMARY: In a decision issued May 2, 
1983, the Commission adopted a 
regulation, to be codified at 49 CFR 
1039.14, partially exempting railroad 
boxcar service from regulation. A notice 
of the decision was published May 6, 
1983 at 48 FR 20412. The Commission 
has issued a further decision in this 
proceeding in which it denies petitions 
for reconsideration. The Commission 
also extended the effective date of the 
exemption to January 1,1984, in order to 
allow time for consideration of petitions 
for stay by the Commission and by the 
reviewing court before the exemption 
becomes effective. The car hire portion 
of the exemption, as it pertains to Class 
III carriers, was further extended to July
1,1984, in order to afford these carriers 
adequate time to negotiate bilateral car 
hire agreements.
DATES: The exemption is effective on 
January 1,1984, except that the car hire 
portion of the exemption, as it applies to 
cars owned or leased by Class III 
carriers 49 CFR 1039.14(c), is effective 
July 1,1984.
FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT:  
Louis E. Gitomer, (202) 275-7245 
or
Thomas Gire, (202) 275-7759.

Additional information is contained in 
the Commission decision. To purchase a 
copy of the full decision, write to T.S. 
InfoSystems, Inc., Room 2227, Interstate 
Commerce Commission, Washington,
DC 20423, or call 289-4357 (D.C. 
Metropolitan area) or toll free (800) 424- 
5403.
