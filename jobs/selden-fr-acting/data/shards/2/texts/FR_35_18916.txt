The areas described above aggregate

The lands are located 20 miles north  
of Deming, N. Mex., on the east slope  
of Cooks Range. The lands are moun­
tainous and vary in elevation from 5,400  
feet to 7,450 feet. The soils are shallow,  
coarse textured, sandy loams with scat­
