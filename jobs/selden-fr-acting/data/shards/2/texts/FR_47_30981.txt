AGENCY: Federal Communications 
Commission.
a c t io n: Final rule.
s u m m a r y: This action denies a joint 
petition for rule making to change three 
existing FM stations in Puerto Rico 
(WRFE, Aguado; WREI, Quebradillas; 
and WBRQ, Cidra) from Class A  to 
Class B channels; to make a new Class 
A  assignment (Channel 249A, Lajas); 
and to require four existing Class B 
stations to change their channels 
(WNIK, Arecibo; WMLD, Manati; 
W IOA, Mayaguez; and WERR, Utuado). 
Instead the action grants an assignment 
of Class B Channel 279 to Lajas, Puerto 
Rico. This channel can also be applied 
for at Cabo Rojo pursuant to the “15 
mile rule,” Section 73.203(b) of the 
Commission’s Rules, as requested by 
David Ortiz Radio Corp. through the 
filing of a counterproposal for Cabo 
Rojo.
DATE: Effective August 31,1982.
ADDRESS: Federal Communications 
Commission, Washington, D.C. 20554.
