(4) Except for the security of the

thereof, the use of firearms for any pur­

within the Kenai National Moose Range.
(5) No hunting in the withdrawn area 
is to be permitted, and the regulations 
of the Pish and Wildlife Service shall be 
observed.
(6) The area is to be returned to the 
Kenai National Moose Range when it is 
no longer needed by the Department of 
the Army.
