4 of the Administrative Procedure Act 
(5 U. S. C. 1003) that the Secretary of 
Agriculture, pursuant to section 8 of the 
Plant Quarantine Act of 1912, as 
amended (7 U. S. C. 161) is considering 
amending §§ 301.38-6, 301.38-7, and
301.38- 9 of the regulations supplemental 
to the quarantine relating to black stem 
rust (7 CFR 301.38-6, 301.38-7, 301.38-9) 
in the following respects:
1. Amend §§ 301.38-6 and 301.38-9 to 
eliminate the requirements that each 
regulated interstate shipment of bar­
berry, mahonia, and mahoberb^ps 
plants, seeds, or fruits shall be accom­
panied by a permit.
2. Amend §§ 301.38-6 (b) (2) and
301.38- 7 Kb) to permit unconditional 
movement from the eradication States to 
non-eradication States of seeds and 
fruits obtained from rust-resistant plants 
of barberry, mahonia' and mahoberberis 
growing in such States.

ception whereby nurseries may be issued 
permits to move rust-resistant barberry,
