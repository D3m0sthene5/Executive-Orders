By virtue of the authority contained in  
the Act of May 31, 1938 (52 Stat. 593;  
48 U.S.C. 353a), it is ordered as follows:
1. Executive Order No. 5289 of March  
4, 1930, withdrawing lands in Alaska for  
educational purposes, is hereby revoked  
so far as it affects the following-de­
scribed lands:
K o k r i n e s

the State of Alaska shall have a preferred  
right to select the land as provided by

CFR 2222.9.
