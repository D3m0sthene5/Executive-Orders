National Wildlife Refuge and the Jo r­
nada Experimental Range shall be sub­
ject to and in conformity with

the interested Federal agencies.

way No. 70 may be used for grazing pur­

by the act of June 26, 1936, 49 Stat. 1976

ary 20, 1942, reserving public lands for

eral bombing range, as amended by

29, 1942, reserving public lands for the

9526 of February 28, 1945, and by Public 
Land Order No. 473 of April 30,1948.

tember 27, 1943, reserving public lands

No. 473 of April 30, 1948.
5. Public Land Order No. 703 of March 
8,1951, reserving public lands for the use

tary purposes.

the Department of Agriculture, and any 
other Federal agency according to their 
respective interests of record.
