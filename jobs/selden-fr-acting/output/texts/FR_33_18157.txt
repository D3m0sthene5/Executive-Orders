PA R T 153— MAIL  D EP O SIT  A N D 
C O L L E C T IO N
m . In Part 153 a new §  153.8 is added 
reading as follows:
§ 153.8 Local ordinances and State laws.
Postmasters are expected to report to 
regional offices any contemplated action 
by local or State authorities to enact or 
consider enactment of local ordinances or 
State laws which would adversely affect 
operating costs or performance of postal 
service.
