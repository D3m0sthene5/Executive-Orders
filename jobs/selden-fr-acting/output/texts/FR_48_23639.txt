Regulation
In consideration of the foregoing, Part

Regulations, is amended by adding a  
new § 165.T1128 to read as follows:
§ 165.T1128 Safety Zone: San Pedro Bay,  
California.
(a) Location. The waters bounded by  
the following coordinates are a safety '  
zone: 33°43'15" N., 118°06'52" W.;  
33°43'15" N., 118°06'39" W.; 33°43'03'' N.,  
118°06'39" W.; 33°43'03" N., 118°06'52"
W.
(b) R egulations. (1) In accordance  
with the general regulations in § 165.23  
of this part, entry into this zone is  
prohibited unless authorized by the  
captain of the port.
