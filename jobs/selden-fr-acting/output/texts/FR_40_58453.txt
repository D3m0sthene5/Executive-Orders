• Purpose. These regulations amend the 
lifesaving equipment requirement's by in­
corporating the current Coast Guard .speci­
fication requirements for inflatable life- 
rafts in the inspection requirements for 
vessels. •
On August 1, 1975, there were pub­
lished in the Federal R egister (40 FR 
32339) proposed rules concerning the

if they were to continue to be used in

1976, and on other certificated vessels

the marking requirements.

proposal. The proposed regulations are
