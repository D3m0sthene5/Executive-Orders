§ 3.1609 Forfeiture.
(a) Forfeiture of benefits for fraud 
by a veteran during his lifetime will not 
preclude payment of burial allowance 
if otherwise in order. No benefits will be 
paid to a claimant who participated in 
the fraud which caused the forfeiture 
by the veteran. (38 U.S.C. 3503(c))

based on a period of service commencing 
prior to the date of commission of the 
offense where either the veteran or

versive activities, unless the offense was 
pardoned by the President of the United 
States prior to the date of the veteran’ s 
death. (38 U.S.C. 3504(c) (2), 3505(a) )
