Pursuant to the provisions of the

seq.), and the applicable rules of prac­
tice and procedure governing the for­
mulation of marketing agreements and

notice is hereby given of a public hear­
ing to be held in the Century Room,  
LaSalle Hotel, La Salle and Madison  
Streets, Chicago, Illinois, beginning at  
10:00 a. m., c. d. s. t., on May 9, 1955,  
for the purpose of receiving evidence  
with respect to amending the tentative  
marketing agreement and the order, as  
amended, regulating the handling of  
milk in the Chicago, Illinois marketing  
area.
Evidence will be received at the hear­
ing with respect to only the price for  
Class I milk. References to the price  
for Class II milk contained herein are to  
be considered only as relating to changes  
which conform order provisions to pro­
posed changes in the Class I price.

have been submitted are as follows:

That such Class I price differentials

percent. The adjustments resulting  
from the above computations shall not  
exceed 24 cents per hundredweight.”

(1) to make Class I price differentials  
$1.10 per hundredweight for August,  
September, October and November, and  
90 cents per hundredweight for all other
