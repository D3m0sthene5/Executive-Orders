§ 14.663 Banks or trust companies  
acting as guardians for veterans.  
Banks or trust companies, corporate en­
tities, acting as guardians for claimants 
may be represented before adjudicating 
agencies as authorized representatives 
of claimants by an officer or employee 
thereof, including a regularly employed 
attorney, if such employee or attorney 
represents the corporation in its fidu­
ciary capacity hut no fee may be allowed 
for such services under 5  14.646.
This regulation is effective August 31, 
1954.
