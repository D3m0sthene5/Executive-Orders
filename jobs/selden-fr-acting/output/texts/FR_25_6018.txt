ords shall be processed in accordance

set forth in Part 41 of this title, as im­
plemented by the Manual of the Medical

this section). If in processing such a  
request for medical records, it appears  
that the interests of the United States  
may be involved, then such requests shall 
be referred to the Judge Advocate  
General.
