in the Pacific Ocean and lying off the

recreational purposes or protection of 
waterfowl and other native birds, is 
hereby revoked in its entirety.

withdrew the unsurveyed island known

8. The Executive Order of January 29,

affects the rocky islets off Cape Blanco 
in Oregon.
