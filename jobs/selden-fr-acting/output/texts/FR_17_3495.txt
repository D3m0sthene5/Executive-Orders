Pursuant to the authority contained in 
the applicable provisions of the Agri­

Burley, flue-cured, fire-cured, dark air- 
cured, and Virginia sun-cured tobacco 
for the 1952-53 marketing year.

52 (16 F. R. 4920, 5037, Burley and flue- 
cured), (16 F. R. 5517, fire-cured, dark 
air-cured, and Virginia sun-cured).
