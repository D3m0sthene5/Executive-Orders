By virtue of the authority vested in

Alaska. The soil consists of Tanana silt 
loam, which supports a cover of white 
spruce, and aspen of fair size. Under­
story plants include blueberry, lingen- 
berry and various other shrubs, grasses and forbs.

he State of Alaska shall have a preferred
