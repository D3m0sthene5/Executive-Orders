CLERKS — POST-OFFICE SERVICE. June 8, 1907. Schedule A, of classified positions excepted from examination under Rule IT, section 3, is hereby amended by the addition to Subdivision V, relating to the Post-Office Department, the following paragraphs: 7. Clerks In charge of contract stations, appropriated for as such and so re- ported. 8. Clerks in charge of stations, whose condensation does not exceed $500 per annum. Persons now employed or hereafter appointed within the limits of this exception may be promoted but may not be transferred or assigned to any other position which Is competitive. It had been the practice of the Post-Office Department to regard clerks in charge of contract stations and clerks in charge of stations at post-offices as excepted from the requirements of examination. Many of these clerks at the time of appointment were given a limited sum to include salary and maintenance of station.
