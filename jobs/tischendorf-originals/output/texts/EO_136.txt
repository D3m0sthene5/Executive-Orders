The  United  States  Civil  Service  CommisBion  is  directed  to  render  such  assistance as  may  be  practicable  to  the  civil-service  board  created  under  the  act  of  the  United States  Philippine  Commiesion  for  the  establishment  and  maintenance  of  an  honest and  efficient  civil  service  in  the  Philippine  Islands,  and  for  that  purpose  to  conduct examinations  for  the  civil  service  of  the  Philippine  Islands  upon  the  request  of  the civil-service  board  of  said  islands,  under  such  regulations  as  may  be  agreed  upon  by the  said  board  and  the  said  United  States  Civil  Service  Commission.
