The greater part of the land herein reserved has been 
withdrawn for reclamation purposes in connection with the 
North Platte and Casper-Alcova Projects, and is primarily 
under the jurisdiction of the Department of the Interior. 
The reservation of such land as a wildlife refuge is subject 
.to the use thereof by the said Department for irrigation and 
incidental purposes.
The reservation made by this order supersedes as to any 
of the above-described lands affected thereby the temporary 
withdrawal for classification and other purposes made by 
Executive Order No. 6910 of November 26,1934, as amended.
This refuge shall be known as the Pathfinder Wildlife 
Refuge.
Executive Order No. 4860 iff April 19, 1928, establishing 
the Pathfinder Reservation reserving certain lands within a 
reservoir site in Wyoming as the Pathfinder Bird Refuge is 
hereby revoked.
