An  executive  order  issued  April  13,  1920,  extends  to  presi- dential postmasterships  the  principle  of  military  preference  en- acted by  Congress  for  all  non-presidential  positions,  but  in  a much  more  limited  form.     Tlie  order  provides : The  Veteran  Preference  Statutes  shall  apply  in  the  selec- tion of  persons  for  appointment  as  postmaster  at  offices  of  the first,  second,  and  third  class.  When  the  highest  eligible  cer- tified to  the  Postmaster  General  by  the  Civil  Service  Commis- sion is  not  a  veteran  but  a  veteran  is  among  those  certified  as eligible,  the  Postmaster  General  may  submit  to  the  President for  nomination  the  name  of  either  the  highest  eligible  or  the veteran  obtaining  the  highest  eligible  rating  as  the  best  inter- ests of  the  service  may  require.
