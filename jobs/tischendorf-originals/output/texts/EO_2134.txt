This order is issued upon the recommendation of the Secretary of the Navy, who states that he is informed by the superintendent that on accoimt of the special experi- ence in electrical work gained by Mr. Hands since his employment in August, 1906, he is better qualified to perform the duties of the position of skilled laborer than an eligible who would be secured from the skilled-laborer roister of the Civil Service Commission; that he has made a study of electrical work, and in addition is familiar with the building and operation of the electrical force.
