Mr. Paterson resigned the position of assistant engineer in the Geological jSurvey on December 31, 190G, aud although eligible for reinstatement in the Department of the Interior, paragraph 8 of civil-service Rule X, prohibiting transfers within six months after reinstatement, would have made him ineli- gible for Immediate transfer to the War Department. Mr. Frank Huntington may be appointed to a suitable position In the Depart- ment of Agriculture, in order that his long editorial experience in the handling of technical or scientific articles may be utilized. Mr. Huntington was a clerk in the Census Office, and his transfer to the De- partment of Agriculture was prohibited by the law requiring three years' service in one Department before transfer to another. The Department stated that it was in need immediately of editorial assistance in the Office of Experiment Stations in connection with the preparation and publication of technical reports of irrigation and drainage Investigations; that Mr. Huntington was a man of long editorial experience in the handling of technical and scientific articles; that he hail acquitted himself most creditably in this capacity, and that by reason of his wide exi>erience he would be able to render far more efficient and helpful service than any man on the eligible register. % August 12, 1907.
