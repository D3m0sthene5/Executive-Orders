ASSISTANCE TO POBTO BICAN CIVIL SERVICE COMMISSION. September 27, 1907. The United States Civil Service Commission is directed to render such assist- ance as may be practicable to the Porto Rican civil service commission, created under the insular legislative act of March 14, 1907, and officers and employees in the Federal service in Porto Rico shall facilitate the holding of examinations and other work of the Porto Rican civil service commission and render such other assistance as may be practicable. The United States Civil Service Com- mission may, in its discretion, authorize the transfer of employees from the civil service of Porto Rico to that of the United States, subject to the limitations respecting transfer within the Federal civil service. This order is similar to that signed by President McKinley on November 20, 11)00, in regard to the Philippine service. WAIVES OF EXAMINATION IN PROMOTION OB TRANSFER, November 22, 1907. When transfers or promotions are properly applied for and are otherwise per- missible, the Civil Service Commission is hereby authorized, in its discretion, looking to the good of the public service only, to waive requirements for exam- ination and to substitute for such examination so waived such other tests of fitness and capacity as the Commission may decide. In each case where such waiver is made the reasons therefor shall be stated at length by the Commission and made matter of record in its minutes. This order was recommended by the Commission for the reason that it was believed that the service would be benefited if the discretion of waiving ex- aminations in certain cases of transfer or promotion, where such examinations would ordinarily be required by the rules, should be placed in the hands of the Commission.
