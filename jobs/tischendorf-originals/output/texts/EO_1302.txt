1024 LAKE TRAVERSE INDIAN RESERVE. It is hereby ordered, under authority contained in the act of February 8, 1887 (24 Stat., 388-389), that the trust period on allotments made to the Indians of the Grand Portage Reservation in Minnesota, which trust period expires during the cal- endar year 1922, be, and the same is hereby, extended for a period of ten years from the date of expiration.
