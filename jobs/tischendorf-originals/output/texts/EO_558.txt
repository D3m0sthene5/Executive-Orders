In reporting leave of absence granted to officers of the Navy, the report should not be forwarded to the department until after the leave has expired and the officer has returned to his station and duty. The report should show the exact number of days absent. No report should be made for a fraction of a day. The time required for an officer to travel to and from the place where his leave is to be spent is leave, and should be included in the report of the number of days absent. The date from which an officer’s leave begins should be reported to the department. If an officer is on duty outside the continental limits of the United States and is granted leave for the purpose of visiting the United States, his leave begins from the date of his ar- rival in the United States and expires on the date of his de- parture therefrom, and the date of his arrival in, and de- parture from, the United States must be reported to the de- partment, Leave of absence will be granted in terms of months and days, as ‘‘one month,’’ one month and ten days.’’ A leave of absence commences on the day following that on which an officer departs from his station or duty. The day of departure, whatever the hour, is counted as a day of duty; the day of return as a day of absence. Leave for one month, commencing on the first day of a calendar month, will expire on the last day ef the month, whatever its number of days. Commencing on an intermediate day, the leave will expire on the day pre- ceding the same day of the next month. Officers of the Navy will keep an account of all leave of absence granted them (exclusive of sick leave) in accordance with the provisions of Chapter 35 of the Navy Regulations, and will furnish a statement of such leave to the pay officers having their accounts, in order to insure their receiving the proper rate of pay when on leave, and to relieve the Auditor for the Navy Department from the necessity of checking their accounts when overpaid by the paymaster, which is frequently done when that officer is not aware of the total amount of leave to which the officer is entitled.
