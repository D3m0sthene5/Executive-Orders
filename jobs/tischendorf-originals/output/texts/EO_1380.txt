By  virtue  of  the  authority  vested  in  me,  I  hereby  establish  the  following  Order  in the  Canal  Zone: Sec.  1.  Every  vessel  propelled  in  whole  or  in  part  by  machinery  shall  be  deemed a  steam  vessel  within  the  meaning  of  this  order. Sec.  2.  All  steam  vessels  navigating  the  waters  of  the  Canal  Zone,  except  public vessels  of  the  United  States  or  other  countries,  shall  be  subject  to  the  provisions  of this  order. Sec.  3.
