CIRCULAR NO. %2 Extension of Period of Eligibility for Reinstatement of Persons who Served with War Organizations during the War with Germany. (Superseding Circular No. 60.) The attention of all officers of the Department is called to the following Executive Order: "EXECUTIVE ORDER "In the discretion of the Civil Service Com- mission, the time during which persons formerly in the competitive classified service served with organizations doing distinctive war service shall not count against their period of eligibility under civil service Rule IX for reinstatement, nor against their eligibility under civil service Rule X for transfer, provided they have had continuous service. (Signed) Warren G. Harding THE WHITE HOUSE, May 31, 1921."
