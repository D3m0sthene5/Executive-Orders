APPOINTMENTS AUTHORIZED BY THE COMMISSION WITH- OUT EXAMINATION UNDER SECTION 10 OF RULE II. Francis A. Wildman. (Minute 4, Oct. 15, 1914.) The War Department requested approval of the appointment of Francis A. Wildman as aviation expert at $3,600 per annum. It was stated that Mr. Wildman had been employed by the Curtiss Aeroplane Co. for several years and was probably the best flying-boat instructor in the world, having a record of over a thousand hours in the air and having taught several hundred students to operate the flying boat. The commis- sion was of the opinion that the qualifications for the position were so rare that it would be impracticable to hold a competitive examination. Frederick G. Erauss.
