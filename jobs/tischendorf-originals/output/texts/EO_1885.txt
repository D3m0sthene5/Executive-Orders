J In  construing  and  carrying  out  the  foregoing  order,  I  direct  that  it  be  done  with the  following  considerations  in  view: — I  have  deemed  it  advisable  for  reasons  of  efficiency,  economy  and  good  administra- tion to  have  all  the  activities  connected  with  the  Panama  Canal  under  the  supervision of  one  Cabinet  officer. The  troops  which  will  be  stationed  on  the  Canal  Zone  for  the  protection  of  the Panama  Canal  are  under  the  Secretary  of  War;  the  Panama  Canal  Act  provides  that in  time  of  war  an  Officer  of  the  Army  shall  upon  the  order  of  the  President  have exclusive  authority  over  the  operation  of  the  Panama  Canal  and  the  Government  of the  Canal  Zone;  the  construction  of  the  Canal  has  been  successfully  carried  on  under the  supervision  of  the  Secretary  of  War;  the  logical  conclusion  is,  therefore,  that  the supervision  of  the  operations  of  the  Panama  Canal  under  the  permanent  organization should  be  under  the  Secretary  of  War.
