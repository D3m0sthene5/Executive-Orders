Gentlemen i On behalf of the United States Forest Service I want to express my appreciation of the opportunity you have afforded for telling the public something of the purposes of the service and of the manner in which it is undertaking to ac complish them. The fundamental purpose of the Forest Service is the insurance of continuous production of timber on the twenty-seven million acres of mountain land in California set aside by congress with this end in view. To guarantee continuous production of timber on this vast area, the primary requisite is reasonable safety from fire. Because the typical California forest is not • totally wiped out with every fire which runs over it, many are led to suppose that surface fires do little or no damage.
