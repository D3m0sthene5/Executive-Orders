The effect of this amendment to the rules was to provide that the Commission might waive the requirement of six months* service preceding transfer in meri- torious cases. The reason for this was that in a large number of cases persona had been separated from the Government Printing Office and from the Bureau of Pensions through no fault of their own, but because of the necessary reduc- tion of force. There was a need for such employees in some of the other De- partments, but because they had already been separated from the service they could not be transferred under the old rule until they had been reinstated and served a period of six months after reinstatement. C. CEBT1FICATION. January 27, 1908. (For this order see page 67 of this volume.) Rule VII, in regard to certification, was entirely rewritten under date of January 27, 1908. The changes embodied in this order were the provision to authorize the Commission to Issue a blanket certificate containing the names of sufficient eligibles to fill several vacancies when a requisition for a number of vacancies was made at one time Instead of issuing separate certificates for each vacancy, but preserving the exact rights of each applicant for consideration in groups of three; and the provision that an eligible who had been three times considered might subsequently be selected, subject to the approval of the Com- mission, if the condition of the register had not changed so as to place him In other resi>ects beyond the reach of certification.
