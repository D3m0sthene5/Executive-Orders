The following is published for the information and guidance of all concerned: EXECUTIVE MANSION, WASHINGTON, D. C., December 29, 1898. The Executive Officers and Public Printer: The President directs that the several Executive Departments and the Government Printing Office be closed at 11 o’clock on the 31st instant, on account of the funeral of the late Senator Morrill of Ver- mont, which will be held in the Senate Chamber on Saturday next at 12 o’clock. (Signed) J. A. PORTER, Secretary to the President. In accordance with the above, the Department will close at 11 o’clock Saturday, the 31st instant. By order of the Secretary. CU osm ‘ Chief Clerk.
