Hospital  Fund.— Twenty  cents  per  month  is  deducted  from  the  pay  of  each  enlisted  man  of  the  Navy,  to  he applied  to  the  fund  for  naval  hospitals,  (Sec.  4808,  R.  S.) 24.  Travel  Allowance  on  Discharge.— Enlisted  men  of  the  Navy  discharged  by  reason  of  expiration  of  enlistment shall  be  furnished  at  time  of  discharge,  in  lieu  of  transportation  and  subsistence,  travel  allowance  of  4 cents  per  mile  for travel  performed  within  the  United  States  to  place  of  enlistment,  as  provided  by  Art  802,  N R.,  1909. 25.  Navy  Mail  Clerks.— Enlisted  men  detailed  as  Navy  mail  clerks  in  accordance  with  the  act  of  May  27, 1908,  receive, in  addition  to  the  pay  of  their  rating,  the  following  monthly  compensation:  On  vessels  having  complements  of  650  or  more and  on  receiving  ships,  $25;  on  vessels  and  in  flotillas  having  a complement  of  more  than  249  and  less  than  650,  $20;  on  ves- sels and  in  flotillas  having  a complement  of  more  than  124  and  less  than  250,  $15;  on  vessels  having  complements  of  more than  34  and  less  than  125  (except  station  tugs  and  barges,  but  including  torpedo  boats  and  destroyers  acting  singly  and  not attached  to  regularly  organized  flotillas),  $5. (а)  The  additional  pay  as  navy  mail  clerk  is  not  dependent  upon  the  filing  of  a bond  (Comp.  Dec.,  Mar  1,  1910.) (б)  Navy  mail  clerks  are  entitled  to  the  additional  compensation  therefor  only  while  in  performance  of  this  special duty,  and  are  not  entitled  to  the  additional  pay  if  absent  from  the  vessel  under  treatment  in  hospital  ashore  or  on  leave  of absence.  (Comp.  Dec.,  Mar.  16,  1909,  and  Apr.  4,  1910.) (c)  Navy  mail  clerks  are  entitled  to  additional  compensation  from  date  of  designation  by  the  Postmaster-General, provided  they  enter  upon  their  duties  on  or  prior  to  such  date  and  take  the  required  oath  of  office  subsequent  to  designa- tion and  prior  to  separation  from  the  position.  (Comp.  Dec.,  Oct  19,  1909.) (d)  Assistant  Navy  Mail  Clerks. — Enlisted  men  designated  as  assistant  Navy  mail  clerks  receive  $15  per  month  in addition  to  the  pay  of  their  rating.  (Act  June  27,  1908;  General  Orders,  Nos,  74,  June  27,  1908;  79,  Nov.  18,  1908;  Art.  441, N.  R.,  1909.) (e)  Mates  Eligible  as  Mail  Clerks.— (For  mates  as  mail  clerks,  see  Note  6,  Table  4.) 26.  Messmen  Branch. — “ On  and  after  March  1,  1908,  the  classification  and  pay  of  mess  attendants  in  the  Navy  who are  citizens  of  the  United  States  shall  be  as  follows:  Mess  attendants,  first  class,  $30;  mess  attendants,  second  class,  $25; mess  attendants,  third  class,  $20.” On  and  after  March  1,  1908,  all  stewards  and  cooks  in  the  messmen  branch  who  are  citizens  of  the  United  States  and who  hold  or  may  receive  certificates  of  qualification  as  stewards  or  cooks  shall  receive  $5  per  month  additional  to  the  pay of  their  rating  while  holding  such  certificate,  such  additional  pay  to  be  of  a permanent  character  as  regular  pay.  (Exec, Ord.,  Jan.  28,  1908;  Art.  1134,  par.  (12),  N.  R.,  1909.) “Certificates  of  qualification  heretofore  or  hereafter  issued  by  the  Bureau  of  Navigation  shall  remain  in  force  for  a period of  two  years  from  date  unless  sooner  revoked  in  the  discretion  of  the  commanding  officer  for  cause,  and  shall  be  renewed by  the  commanding  officer  at  the  expiration  of  each  two  years  for  a similar  term,  provided  the  continued  good  performance of  duty  of  the  cook  or  steward  so  warrants.”  (Gen.  Ord.  No.  62,  Feb.  7, 1908.) 27.  Retired  Pay. — “The  pay  of  all  commissioned,  warrant,  and  appointed  officers  and  enlisted  men  of  the  Navy  now on  the  retired  list  shall  be  based  on  the  pay,  as  herein  provided  for,  of  commissioned,  warrant,  and  appointed  officers  and enlisted  men  of  corresponding  rank  and  service  on  the  active  list;  and  all  pay  herein  pro\ided  shall  remain  in  force  until changed  by  act  of  Congress.”  (Act  of  May  13, 1908.) (a)  An  enlisted  man  on  the  retired  list  is  entitled  to  the  full  amount  of  75  cents  a month  for  each  good-conduct  medal held,  in  addition  to  75  per  cent  of  the  pay  of  which  he  was  in  receipt  at  the  date  of  retirement.  (Comp.  Dec.,  Oct.  27, 1902;  Dec.  11,  1902;  May  14,  1909.) 28.  Death  Gratuity.— Payment  of  death  gratuity  to  widow  or  previously  designated  beneficiary  of  deceased  enlisted men  of  the  active  list  is  authorized  under  the  same  conditions  as  in  the  case  of  officers.  (See  par.  3,  “General  Remarks.”) 29.  Divers,  Service  as.— Enlisted  men  of  the  navy,  not  under  instruction  or  diving  for  practice,  shall,  when  emx)loyed in  submarine  diving,  receive  extra  compensation  therefor,  to  be  charged  to  the  bureau  for  which  the  service  is  performed, at  the  rate  of  $1.20  per  hour  for  the  actual  time  so  employed  under  water.  (Art.  1133,  N.  R.,  1909.) 30.  Honorable  Discharge  Gratuity. — If  any  enlisted  man,  being  honorably  discharged,  shall  reenlist  for  four  years within  four  months  thereafter,  he  shall,  on  presenting  his  honorable  discharge,  or  on  accounting  in  a satisfactory  manner for  its  loss,  be  entitled  to  pay  during  the  said  four  months  equal  to  that  to  which  he  would  have  been  entitled  if  he  had been  employed  in  actual  service.  (Art.  1072,  par.  (1),  N.  R.,  1909.) (a)  The  honorable  discharge  gratuity  is  computed  at  the  rate  of  base  pay  plus  permanent  additions  thereto,  but  not including  transient  additions  which  require  a special  detail,  such  as  gun  pointer,  messman,  etc.  (Comp.
