Navy  Mail  Clerks. — Enlisted  men  detailed  as  Navy  mail  clerks  in  accordance  with  the  act  of  May  27, 1908,  receive  in  a<idi- tion  to  the  pay  of  their  rating,  the  following  monthly  compensation:  On  vessels  having  complements  of  650  or  more  and  on  receiv- ing ships,  $25;  on  vessels  and  in  flotnias  having  a complement  of  more  than  249  and  less  than  650,  $20;  on  vessels  and  in  flotillas having  a complement  of  more  than  124  and  less  than  250,  $15;  on  vessels  having  complements  of  more  than  34  and  less  than  125 (except  station  tugs  and  barges,  hut  including  torpedo  boats  and  destroyers  acting  singly  and  not  attached  to  regularly  organized flotillas)  $5. (а)  The  additional  pay  as  Navy  mail  clerk  is  not  dependent  upon  the  filing  of  a bond.  (Comp.  Dec.,  Mar.  1, 1910.) (б)  Navy  mafl.  clerks  are  entitled  to  the  additional  compensation  therefor  only  while  in  performance  of  this  special  duty,  and are  not  entitled  to  the  additional  pay  if  absent  from  the  vessel  under  treatment  in  hospital  ashore  or  on  leave  of  absence.  (Comp. Dec.,  Mar.  16,  1909,  and  Apr.  4,  1910.) (c)  Navy  mail  clerks  are  entitled  to  additional  compensation  from  date  of  designation  by  the  Postmaster  General,  provided they  enter  upon  their  duties  on  or  prior  to  such  date  and  take  the  required  oath  of  office  subsequent  to  designation  and  prior  to separation  from  the  position.  (Comp.  Dec.  , Oct.  19,  1909.) (e)  Mates  Eligible  as  Mail  Clerks. — (For  mates  as  mail  clerks,  see  Note  6,  Table  4.) if  ) The  appointment  of  an  assistant  mail  clerk  for  duty  on  a flagship  is  not  revoked  by  reason  of  the  temporary  transfer  of  the flag  to  another  vessel. An  assistant  mail  clerk  is  not  entitled  to  the  additional  pay  provided  for  assistant  mail  clerks  for  postal  duties  performed  on board  a vessel  other  than  the  one  for  which  he  was  designated. (Art.  R-4427 (5),N.  R.,  1913.) Stewards  and  cooks  in  the  messmen  branch  who  are  citizens  of  the  United  States  and  who  hold  or  may  receive  certificates  of qualification  as  stewards  or  cooks  shall  receive  $5  per  month  additional  to  the  pay  of  their  rating  while  holding  such  certificate, such  additional  pay  to  be  of  a permanent  character  as  regular  pay.  (Exec.  Ord.,  Jan.  28,  1908;  Art.  R-4427 (32-a), N.  R.,  1913.) “Certificates  of  qualification  issued  by  the  Bureau  of  Navigation  shall  be  renewed  by  the  commanding  officer  at  the  expira- tion of  each  two  years  for  a similar  term,  provided  the  continued  good  performance  of  duty  of  the  cook  or  steward  so  warrants.” (Gen.  Ord.  No.  62,  Feb.  7,  1908;  and  Art.  R-4427  (32-b),  N.  R.,  1913.) 27.  Retired  Pay. — “The  pay  of  all  commissioned,  warrant,  and  appointed  officers  and  enlisted  men  of  the  Navy  now  on the  retired  fist  shall  be  based  on  the  pay,  as  herein  provided  for,  of  commissioned,  warrant,  and  appointed  officers  and  enlisted men  of  corresponding  rank  and  service  on  the  active  list;  and  all  pay  herein  provided  shall  remain  in  force  until  changed  by act  of  Congress.”  (Act  of  May  13,  1908.) (a)  An  enUsted  man  on  the  retired  list  is  entitled  to  the  full  amount  of  75  cents  a month  for  each  good-conduct  medal  held, in  addition  to  75  per  cent  of  the  pay  of  which  he  was  in  receipt  at  the  date  of  retirement.  (Comp.  Dee.,  Oct.  27,  1902;  Dec.  11, 1902;  May  14, 1909.) 28.  Death  Gratuity.— Payment  of  death  gratuity  to  widow,  children,  or  dependent  relative  of  enlisted  men  of  the  active  list is  authorized  under  the  same  conditions  as  in  the  case  of  officers.  (See  par.  4,  “ General  Remarks.”) 29.  Divers,  Service  as. — Enlisted  men  of  the  Navy,  not  under  instruction  or  diving  for  practice,  shall  when  employed  in submarine  diving,  receive  extra  compensation  therefor,  to  be  charged  to  the  bureau  for  which  the  service  is  performed,  at  the  rate of  $1.20  per  hour  for  the  actual  time  so  employed  under  water.  (Art.  R-4427  (33),  N.  R.,  1913.) 30.  Honorable  Discharge  Gratuity. — That  the  term  of  enlistment  of  all  enlisted  men  of  the  United  States  Navy  other  than those  who  are  enlisted  during  minority  shall  be  four  years. That  the  term  of  enlistment  of  any  enlisted  man  in  the  Navy  may,  by  his  voluntary  written  agreement,  under  such  regula- tions as  may  be  prescribed  by  the  Secretary  of  the  Navy  with  the  approval  of  the  President,  be  extended  for  a period  of  either one,  two,  three,  or  four  full  years  from  the  date  of  expiration  of  the  then  existing  four-year  term  of  enlistment,  and  subsequent  to said  date  such  enhsted  men  as  extend  the  term  of  enlistment  as  authorized  in  this  section  shall  be  entitled  to  and  shall  receive the  same  pay  and  allowances  in  all  respects  as  though  regularly  discharged  and  reenhsted  immediately  upon  expiration  of  their term  of  enlistment,  and  such  extension  shall  not  operate  to  deprive  them  upon  discharge  at  the  termination  thereof  of  any  right, privilege,  or  benefit  to  which  they  would  be  entitled  at  the  expiration  of  a four-year  term  of  enlistment. That  section  1573  of  the  Revised  Statutes  of  the  United  States  as  amended  by  section  16  of  an  act  entitled  “An  act  to  reor- ganize and  increase  the  efficiency  of  the  personnel  of  the  Navy  and  Marine  Corps  of  the  United  States,”  approved  March  3,  1899, be,  and  the  same  is  hereby,  amended  to  read  as  follows:  “If  any  enlisted  man  or  apprentice,  being  honorably  discharged,  shall reenlist  for  four  years  witiiin  four  months  thereafter,  he  shall,  on  presenting  his  honorable  discharge  or  on  accounting  in  a satis- factory manner  for  its  loss,  be  entitled  to  a gratuity  of  four  months’  pay  equal  in  amount  to  that  which  he  would  have  received if  he  had  been  employed  in  actual  service:  Provided,  That  any  enlisted  man  in  the  Navy  whose  term  of  enlistment  has  been  ex- tended for  an  aggregate  of  four  years  shall,  after  the  expiration  of  the  preceding  four-year  term  of  enlistment  upon  which  the  exten- sion is  made  and  if  otherwise  entitled  to  an  honorable  discharge,  be  paid  the  gratuity  above  provided:  And  provided,  That  any man  who  has  received  an  honorable  discharge  from  his  last  term  of  enhstment,  or  who  has  received  a recommendation  for  reen- listment upon  the  expiration  of  his  last  term  of  enhstment,  who  reenlists  for  a term  of  four  years  within  four  months  from  the  date of  his  discharge,  shall  receive  an  increase  of  $1.36  per  month  to  the  pay  prescribed  for  the  rating  in  which  he  serves  for  each  suc- cessive reenlistment:  And  provided  further.  That  an  extension  of  the  period  of  enlistment  as  hereinbefore  authorized,  aggregating four  years,  shall  be  held  and  considered  as  equivalent  to  continuous  service  with  respect  to  all  rights,  privileges,  and  benefits granted  for  such  service  pursuant  to  law.”  (Act  Aug.  22, 1912.) (a)  The  honorable  discharge  gratuity  is  computed  at  the  rate  of  base  pay  plus  permanent  additions  thereto,  but  not  including transient  additions  which  require  a special  detail,  such  as  gun  pointer,  messman;  etc.  (Comp.  Dec.,  Mar.  27,  1903;  June  19,  1906.) (&)  An  enlisted  man  who  extends  his  enhstment  for  one  year  in  accordance  with  the  provision  authorizing  the  extension  of terms  of  enhstment  which  appear  in  the  act  making  appropriations  for  the  naval  service  for  the  fiscal  year  ending  June  30, 1913, approved  August  22,  1912,  is  entitled  to  the  continuous  service  pay  of  $1.36  per  month  authorized  by  section  1573,  Revised  Stat- utes, but  is  not  entitled  to  the  payment  of  the  four  months’  gratuity  pay,  since  the  payment  of  this  gratuity  is  limited  to  those whose  terms  of  enlistment  are  extended  for  an  aggregate  of  four  years.  (Comp.  Dec.,  Oct.  11,  1912.) (c)  The  cost  of  transportation  of  remains  should  be  included  in  the  sum  to  be  deducted  from  six  months’  gratuities  for  “ex- penses of  interment.”  (Comp.  Dec.,  Sept.  1, 1911.)
