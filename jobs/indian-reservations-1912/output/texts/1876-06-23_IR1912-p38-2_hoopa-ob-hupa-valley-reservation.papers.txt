By virtue of power vested in me by an act of Congress approved
April 8, 1864, and acting under instructions from the Interior De-
partment, dated at Washington City, D. C, April 26, 1864, concern-
ing the location of four tracts of land for Indian reservations in the
State of California, I do hereby proclaim and make known to all
concerned that I have this day located an Indian reservation, to be
known and called by the name and title of the Hoopa Valley Reser-
vation, said reservation being situated on the Trinity River, in Kla-
math County, Cal.j to be described by such metes and bounds as may
hereafter be established by order of the Interior Department, subject
to the approval of the President of the United States. Settlers in
Hoopa valley are hereby notified not to make any further improve-
ments upon their places, as they will be appraised and purchased as
soon as the Interior Department may direct.
Austin Wiley.
Superintendent Indian Affairs for the State of California.
FoffT Gaston, Cal., August 21, 1864-.
