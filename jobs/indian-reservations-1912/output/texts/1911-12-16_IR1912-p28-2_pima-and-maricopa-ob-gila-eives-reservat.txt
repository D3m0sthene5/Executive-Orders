The White House, December 16, 1911.
Under authority of the act of Congress approved June 25, 1910
(36 Stat., 847), and on the recommendation of the Secretary of the
Interior, it is hereby ordered that all of township 5 south, range 7
east, Gila and Salt River meridian, Arizona, except such portions
thereof as have been heretofore reserved and set aside as an addition
to the Gila River Indian Resen'ation, be temporarily withdrawn
from settlement, location, sale, or entry, except as provided in said
act, and be reserved for classification.
Wm. H. Taft.
