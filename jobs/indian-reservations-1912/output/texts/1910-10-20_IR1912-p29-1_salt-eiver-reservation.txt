Thu White House, October 20, 1910.
It is hereby ordered that the following-described lands in the State
of Arizona, viz, all of sections 1 and 12 in township 1 north, range 4
east of the Gila and Salt River meridian, be, and the same are hereby,
withdrawn from settlement, entry, and sale, and set apart as an addi-
tion to the Salt River Indian Reservation: Provided, That nothing
herein shall affect any existing valid rights of any person to the
lands described.
Wm. H. Tait.
