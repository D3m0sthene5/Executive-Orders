Executive Mansion, July 26, 1876.
The military reservation in California known as Camp Wright,
embracing the west half of section
1 and the east half of section
2,
township 22 north, range 13 west, and containing
1 mile square of
land, be the same more or less, having been, with its buildings, im-
provements, etc., relinquished by the War Department, the Execu-
tive order of April 27, 1869, creating said military reservation
is
hereby revoked, and the said tract of land, with its buildings, im-
provements, etc.,
ishereby withheld from public sale, and reserved
for the use and occupancy of the Indians located on the Round
Valley Reservation, as an extension thereof, until otherwise ordered.
U. S. Grant.
