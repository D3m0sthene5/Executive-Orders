Executive Mansion,
Washington, March 1^, 1887.
It is hereby ordered that the lands embraced in section 23, town-
ship 7 south, range 2 east, San Bernardino meridian, California, be,
and the same hereby are, withdrawn from sale and settlement and
set apart for the use and occupation of the Mission Indians as an
addition to the Coahuila Reservation.
Grover Cleveland.
