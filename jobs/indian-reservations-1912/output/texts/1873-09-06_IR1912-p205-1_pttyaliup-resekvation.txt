Executive Mansion, September 6, 1873.
Agreeable to the recommendation di the Acting Secretary of the
Interior, it is hereby ordered that the Puyallup Reservation, in
Washington Territory, be so extended as to include within its limits
all that portion of section 34, township 21 north, range 3 east not
already included within the reservation.
U. S. Grant.
