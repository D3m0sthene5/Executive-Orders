The White House,
Afril 24, 1912.
It is hereby ordered that the north half of the northeast quarter of
section twenty-nine, township eight south, range twenty-one east of'
the Mount Diablo meridian, be, and the same is hereby, withdrawn
from settlement, entry sale, or other disposition, for Indian use, sub-.
ject to any valid existing rights of any persons thereto.
Wm. H,. Taft.
