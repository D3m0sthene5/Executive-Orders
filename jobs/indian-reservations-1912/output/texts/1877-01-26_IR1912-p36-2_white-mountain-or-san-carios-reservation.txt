Executive Mansion, January 26, 1877.
It
ishereby ordered that all that portion of the White Mountain
Indian Reservation in Arizona Territory lying within the, following-
described boundaries, viz, commencing at
apoint known as corner
1
of survey made by Lieut. E. D. Thomas, Fifth Cavalry, in March,
1876, situated northeast of and 313 chains from flagstaff of Camp
Apache, magnetic variation 13° 48' east; thence south 68° 34' west,
360 chains, to corner II, post in monument of stones, variation 13°
45' east; thence south 7° 5' west, 240 chains to corner III, post in
monument of stones, variation 13° 43' east; thence north 68° 34' east
360 chains to corner IV, post in monument of stones, magnetic varia-
tion 13° 42' east; thence north 7° 15' east, 240 chains to place of
beginning, comprising 7,421.14 acres, be restored to the public
domain.
U. S. Grant.
