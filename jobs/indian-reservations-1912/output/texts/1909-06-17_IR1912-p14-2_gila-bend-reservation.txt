The White House, June 17, 1909.
It is hereby ordered that the following lands in Arizona, being a
part of those withdrawn by Executive order of December twelve,
eighteen hundred and eighty-two, for use of the Papago Indians, be,
and hereby are, restored to the public domain: Section sixteen and
sections nineteen to thirty-six, inclusive, in township five south of
range five west of the Gila and Salt River meridian.
Wm. H. Taft.
