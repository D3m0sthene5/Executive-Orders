Department of the Interior,
Washington, D. C, April 9, 1872.
Sir: I have the honor to submit herewith a communication, dated
the 8th instant, from the Commissioner of Indian Affairs, and accom-
panying papers, representing the necessity for the setting apart, by
Executive order, of a tract of country therein described for certain
bands of Indians in Washington Territory not parties to any treaty.
The recommendation of the commissioner in the premises is ap-
proved, and I respectfully request that the President direct that the
tract of country designated upon the inclosed map be set apart for the
Indians referred to and such others as this department may see fit to
settle thereon.
I am, sir, very respectfully, your obedient servant,
