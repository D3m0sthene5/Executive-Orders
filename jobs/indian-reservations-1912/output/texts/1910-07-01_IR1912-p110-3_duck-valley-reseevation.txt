The White House, July 1st, 1910.
It
ishereby ordered that the following-described lands in the State
of Idaho, viz
: All that part of T. 15 S., R.
4E., B. M., lying and
being west of
aline formed by extending the east boundary line of
the Duck Valley Indian Reservation through the said township, be,
and the same are hereby, withdrawn from settlement, entry, and^sale,
and set apart as an addition to the Duck Valley Indian Reservation
:
Provided, That nothing herein shall affect any existing valid rights
of any person to the lands described.
Wm. H. Taft.
