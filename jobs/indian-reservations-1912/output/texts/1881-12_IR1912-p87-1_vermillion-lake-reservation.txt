Executive Mansion, December £0, 1881.
It is hereby ordered that the following-described land in Minne-
sota, viz : That portion of the southeast quarter of section 23, lying
east of Sucker Bay; the southwest quarter, and lot , being the
most southerly lot in the southeast quarter of section 24, ai^d frac-
tional sections 25, 26, and that portion of section 35 north of Ver-
million Lake, all in township 62 north, range 16 west, fourth prin-
cipal meridian, Minnesota, be, and the same are hereby, withdrawn
from sale or settlement and set apart as a reservation for Indian pur-
poses, for the use of the Boise Fort Band of Chippewa Indians, to
be known as the " Vermillion Lake Indian Reservation."
Chester A. Arthur.
