Executive Mansion, Novemher ^4, 1874.
All orders establishing and setting apart the Tulerosa Valley, inNew Mexico — described as follows : Beginning at the headwaters of
the Tulerosa River and its tributaries in the mountains, and extend-
ing down the same 10 miles on each side for a distance of 30 miles—
as an Indian reservation, are hereby revoked and annulled; and the
said described tract of country is hereby restored to the public
domain.
U. S. Grant.
