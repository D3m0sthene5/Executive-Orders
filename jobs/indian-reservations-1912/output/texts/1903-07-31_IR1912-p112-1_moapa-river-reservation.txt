White House, July 31st, 1903.
It is hereby ordered that the following-described tracts of land be,
and the same are hereby, withdrawn from sale and settlement and
set apart as an addition to the Moapa River or Pai-Ute Indian Res-
ervation in southeastern Nevada, for the use of the Pai-Ute Indians:
Lot 4, containing 38.75 acres, and lot 5, containing 11.62 acres, in
section 36, township 14 south, range 65 east.
Lot 3, containing 39.20 acres, and lot 4, containing 13.71 acres, in
section 31, township 14 south, range 66 east.
Theodore Roosevelt.
