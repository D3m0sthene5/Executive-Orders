Executive Mansion, September 29, 1877.
It is hereby ordered that the following-described lands in Califor-
nia, to wit, all the even-numbered sections, and all the unsurveyed
portions of township 4 south, range 4 east ; township 4 south, range 5
east; and township 6 south, range 4 east, San Bernardino meridian,
excejjtin^ sections 16 and 36, and excepting also any tract or tracts
the title to which has passed out of the United States Government,
be, and the same hereby are, withdrawn from sale and settlement,
and set apart as a reservation for Indian purposes for certain of the
Mission Indians. '
E. B. Hayes.
