The White House, December 19, 1906.
It is hereby ordered that the following described lands in New
Mexico, namely, township sixteen north, range one east, Jemez me-
ridian, excepting any tract or tracts the title to which has passed out
of the United States Government, or to which valid legal rights have
attached, be, and the same are hereby, withdrawn from sale and set-
tlement and set apart as a reservation for the use and benefit of the
Indians of the Jemez Pueblo.
Theodoke Roosevelt.
