Executive Mansion, January
8,1900.
It
ishereby ordered that the tract of country lying west of the
Navajo and Moqui Eeservations, in the Territory of Arizona, em-
braced within the "following-described boundaries, viz, beginning at
the southeast corner of the Moqui Reservation and running due west
to the Little Colorado River
; thence down that stream to the Grand
Canyon Forest Reserve; thence north on the line of that reserve to
the northeast corner thereof; thence west to the Colorado River;
thence up that stream to the Navajo Indian Reservation, be, andihe
same
ishereby, withdrawn from sale and settlement until further
ordered.
William McKinlet.
