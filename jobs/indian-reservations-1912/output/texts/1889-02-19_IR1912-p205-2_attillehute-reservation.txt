Executive Mansion, February 19, 1889.
It is hereby ordered that the following-described tracts of land sit-
uate in Washington Territory, viz, lots 3, 4, 5, and 6, section 21 ;
lots 10, 11, and 12, and the southwest quarter of the southwest quar-
ter section 22; fractional section 27; and lots 1, 2, and 3, section 28,
all in township 28 north, of range 15 west, be, and the same are
hereby, withdrawn from sale and settlement and set apart for the
permanent use and occupation of the Quillehute Indians: Pro'vided,
That this withdrawal shall not affect any existing valid rights of
any party.
Grover Cleveland.
