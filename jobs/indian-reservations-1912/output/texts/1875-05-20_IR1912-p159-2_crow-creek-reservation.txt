Executive Mansion, May 20, 1875.
It
ishereby ordered that that portion of the public domain in the
Territory of Dakota lying south of an east and west line from the
northwest corner of the Yankton Indian Reservation to the ninety-
ninth degree of longitude, and between said longitude and the Mis-
souri River on the west and the Yankton Indian Reservation on the
east, be, and the same hereby
is,withdrawn from sale and settlement
and set apart for the use of the several tribes of Sioux Indians as an
addition to their present reservation in said Territory.
U. S. Grant.
