Executive Mansion, May W, 1873.
It is hereby ordered that the tract of country above described be
withheld from entry and settlement as public lands, and that the
same be set apart as a reservation for the Mescalero Apache Indians,
as recommended by the Secretary of the Interior and Commissioner
of Indian Affairs.
U. S. Grant.
