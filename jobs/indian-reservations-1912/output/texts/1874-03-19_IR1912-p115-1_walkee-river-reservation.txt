Executive Mansion, March 19, 1874.
It is hereby ordered that the reservation situated on Walker River,
Nevada, as surveyed by Eugene Monroe, December, 1864, and indi-
cated by red lines on the above diagram in accordance with the fif-
teen courses and distances thereon given, be withdrawn from public
sale or other disposition and set apart for the use of the Pah-Ute
Indians residing thereon.
U. S. Grant.
