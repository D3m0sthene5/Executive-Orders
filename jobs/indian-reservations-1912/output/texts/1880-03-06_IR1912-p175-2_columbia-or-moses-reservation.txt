Executive Mansion, March 6, 1880.
It is hereby ordered that the tract of country in Washington Ter-
ritory lying within the following-described boundaries, viz: Com-
mencing at a point where the south boundary line of the reservation
created for Chief Moses and his people by Executive order dated
April 19, 1879, intersects the Okinakane River; thence down said
river to its confluence with 'the Columbia River; thence across and
down the east bank of said Columbia River to a point opposite the
river forming the outlet to Lake Chelan ; thence across said Columbia
River and along the south shore of said outlet to Lake Chelan;
thence following the meanderings of the south bank of said lake to
the mouth of Shehekin Creek; thence up and along the south bank of
said creek to its source; thence due west to the forty-fourth degree
of longitude west from Washington; thence north along said degree
to the south boundary of the reservation created by Executive order
of April 19, 1879 ; thence along the south boundary of said reserva-
tion to the place of beginning, be, and the same is hereby, withdrawn
from sale and settlement and set apart for the permanent use 'ind
occupancy of Chief Moses and his people, and such other friendly
Indians as may elect to settle thereon with his consent and that of
the Secretary of the Interior, as an addition to the reservation set
apart for said Chief Moses and his people by Executive order dated
April 19, 1879.*^
R. B. Hates.
