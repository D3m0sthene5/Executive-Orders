Executive Mansion, August 25, 1877.
It is hereby ordered that the following lands in California, to wit,
all the even-numbered sections and all the unsurveyed portions of
township 2 south, range 1 east ; township 2 south, range 2 east ; town-
ship 3 south, range 1 east; and township 3 south, range 2 east, San
Bernardino meridian, excepting sections 16 and 36, and excepting
also all tract or tracts the title to which has passed out of the United
States Government, be, and the same hereby are, withdrawn from sale
and settlement, and set apart as a reservation for Indian purposes.
E. B. Hayes.
