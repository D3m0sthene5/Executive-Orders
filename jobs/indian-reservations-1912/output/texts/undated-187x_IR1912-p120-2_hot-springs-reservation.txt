Executive Mansion, April 9, 187^.
It is hereby ordered that the following-described tract of country
in the Territory of New Mexico be, and the same is hereby, with-
drawn from sale and reserved for the use and occupation of such
Indians as the Secretary of the Interior may see fit to locate thereon,
as indicated in this diagram, viz :
Beginning at the ruins of an ancient pueblo in the valley of the
Canadja Alamosa Kiver, about 7 miles above the present town of
Capada Alamosa, and running thence due east 10 miles; thence due
north 25 miles ; thence due west 30 miles ; thence due south 25 miles ;
thence due east 20 miles to the place of beginning.
U. S. Grant.
