The White House, September 1, 1911.
Executive order of December 19, 1906, withdrawing township 16
north of range 1 east, Jemez meridian, for the benefit of the Indians
of the Jemez Pueblo, is hereby amended to read as follows :" It is hereby ordered that the following described lands in New
Mexico, namely, township 16 north, range 1 east, New Mexico prin-
cipal meridian, excepting any tract or tracts the title to which hay
passed out of the United States Government, or to which valid legal
rights have attached, be, and the same are, hereby withdrawn from
fiale and settlement, and set apart as a reservation for the use and
benefit of the Indians of the Jemez Pueblo."
Wm. H. Taft.
