ExECUTi^nE Mansion, March 29, 1884..
It is hereby ordered that the tract of country in the Territory of
Dakota withdrawn from sale and settlement and set apart for the
use and occupancy of the Turtle Mountain Band of Chippewa In-
dians by Executive order dated December 21, 1882, except townships
162 and 163 north, range 71 west, be, and the same is hereby, restored
to the mass of the public domain.
Chester A. Arthur.
