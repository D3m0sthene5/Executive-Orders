Executive Mansion, Novemher 8, 1873.
It is hereby ordered that the following tract of country in the
Territory of Idaho be, and the same is hereby, withdrawn from sale
and set apart as a reservation for the Coeur d Alene Indians in said
Territory, viz:
Beginning at a point on the top of the dividing ridge between Pine
and Latah (or Hangmans) Creeks, directly south of a point on said
last-named creek 6 miles above the point where the trail from Lewis-
ton to Spokane Bridge crosses said creelv; thence in a northeasterly
direction in a direct line to the Cfpur d'Alene Mi'^sion on the Cceur
d'Alene River (but not to include the lands of said mission) ; thence
in a westerly direction in a direct line to the point where the Spokane
River heads in or leaves the Cneur d'Alene Lakes; then down along
the center of the channel of said Spokane River to the dividing line
between the Territories of Idaho and Washington as established by
the act of Congress organizing a Territorial government for the
Territory of Idaho; thence south along said dividing line to the top
of the dividing ridge between Pine and Latah (or Hangmans)Creeks; thence along the top of the said ridge to the place of
beginning.
U. S. Grant.
