ExEcfuTTVE Mansion, Washington., July 23, 1880.
The Executive order dated December 5, 1872, creating the " Fort
Harney Military Reservation,'' in Oregon, is hereby canceled, and the
lands embraced therein and as shown on the accompanying plat are
hereby made subject to the Executive order dated September 12,
1872, establishing the Malheur Indian Reservation. The Secretary
of the Interior will cause the same to be noted in the General Land
Office.
R. B. Hates.
