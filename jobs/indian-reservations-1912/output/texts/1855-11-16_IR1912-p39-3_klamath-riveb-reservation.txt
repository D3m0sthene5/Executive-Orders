Sir : I have the honor to submit herewith the report from the Com-
missioner of Indian Affairs of the 10th instant, and its accompanying
papers, having relation to two of the reservations in California for
Indian purposes, authorized by the act of 3d March last.
The precise limits of but one of the reservations, viz, a strip of ter-
ritory commencing at the Pacific Ocean and extending 1 mile in
width on each side of the Klamath Eiver, are given, no suflicient data
being furnished to justify any definite action on the other.
T recommend your approval of the proposed Klamath [Eiver]
Reservation, with the provision, however, that upon a survey of the
tract a sufficient quantity be cut off from the upper end thereof to
bring it within the limit of 25,000 acres authorized by law.
