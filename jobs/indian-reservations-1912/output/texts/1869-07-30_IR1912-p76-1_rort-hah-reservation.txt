Executive Mansion, July 30, 1869.
The within recommendation of the Secretary of the Interior is
hereby approved, and within the limits of the tract reserved by Ex-
ecutive order of June 14, 1867, for the Indians of southern Idaho
will be designated a reservation provided for the Bannocks by the
second article of the treaty with said tribe of July 3, 1868.
U. S. Grant.
