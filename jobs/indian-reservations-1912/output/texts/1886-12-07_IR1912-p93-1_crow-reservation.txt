Executive Mansion,
Washington, December 7, 1886.
The within request is approved and the reservations are m^de- and
proclaimed accordingly : Provided, That the l3 Indian families herein
referred to shall not be disturbed, but shall be allowed to remain
where they are now located, and to retain their present allotments of
land, land be permitted the free and unrestricted enjoyment thereof,
unless they shall voluntarily release or abandon the same.
The Secretary of the Interior will cause the proper notation to be
made in the General Land Office.
Grover Cleveland.
