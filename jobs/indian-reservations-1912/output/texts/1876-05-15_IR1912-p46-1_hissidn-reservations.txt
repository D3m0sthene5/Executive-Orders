It is hereby ordered that the following-described lands in San Ber-
nardino County, Cal., viz :
Portrero. — Township 2 south, range 1 east, section 36 ;
Mission. —Township 2 south, range 3 east, sections 12, 13, and 14 ;
Aqua Galienta. —Township 4 south, range 4 east, section 14, and
east half of southeast quarter and northeast quarter of section 22;
Torros. —Township 7 south, range 7 east, section 2 ;
Tillage. —Township 7 south, range 8 east, section 16 ;
Cahezons. —Township 7 south, range 9 east, section 6;
Village.—Township 5 south, range 8 east, section 19 ;
Village.—Township 5 south, range 7 east, section 24,
be, and the same hereby are, withdrawn from sale and set apart as
reservations for the permanent use and occupancy of the Mission
Indians in southern California, in addition to the selections noted
and reserved under Executive order dated 27th December last.
U. S. Grant.
