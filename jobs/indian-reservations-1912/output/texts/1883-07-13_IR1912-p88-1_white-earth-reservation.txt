Executive Mansion, July 13, 1883.
It is hereby ordered that the Executive order dated March 18, 1879,
withdrawing from sale and settlement and setting apart certain de-
scribed lands north of and adjoining the White Earth Reservation
in the State of Minnesota, as a reservation for Indian purposes, be,
and the same is hereby, canceled, the lands embraced within said
reservation not being required for the purposes for which they were
set apart.
Chester A. Arthur.
