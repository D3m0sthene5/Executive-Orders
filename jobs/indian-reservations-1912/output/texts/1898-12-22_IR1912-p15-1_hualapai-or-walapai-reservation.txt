Executive Mansion, December 22, 1898.
It is hereby ordered that section 10, township 23 north, range 13
west, Arizona, be, and the same is hereby, set apart as a reservation
for Indian school purposes for the Hualapai Indians, to be known
as the " Hualapai Indian School Eeserve."
William McKinlet.
