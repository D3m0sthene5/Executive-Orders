Executive Mansion, March 20, 1884.
It
ishereby ordered that the lands embraced within the three exist-
ing Executive additions to the Great Sioux Reservation, in Dakota,,
cast of the Missouri River, viz, the one opposite the Standing Rock
Agency, the one opposite the mouth of Grand River and the site of
the old Grand River Agency, and the one opposite the mouth of Big
Cheyenne River and the Cheyenne River Agency, be, and the same
are htreby, restored to the mass of the public domain, the same being
no longer needed for the purpose for which they were withdrawn
from sale and settlement.
Chester A. Arthur.
