Department of the Interior,
Washington, D. C, July SO, 1873.
Respectfully submitted to the President, with the recommendation
that all that portion of the valley of the Gila River in the Territory
of Arizona hitherto included in the San Carlos division of the White
Mountain Indian Reservation as established by Executive order dated
December 14, 1872, lying east of and above the site of old Camp Good-
win, be restored to the public domain, as recommended by the Acting
Commissioner of Indian Affairs.
B. R. CowEN, Acting Secretary.
