Executive Mansion, May 21, 1883.
It is hereby ordered thai the Malheur Indian Reservation, in the
State of Oregon, except a tract of 320 acres, described in an Executive
order dated September 13, 1882, as " the north half of the late mili-
tary post reserve of Camp Harney, as established by Executive order
of December 5, 1872," be, and the same is hereby, restored to the
public domain, the same being no longer required for the purposes
for which it was set apart.
Chester A. Arthur.
