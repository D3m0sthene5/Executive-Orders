Executive Mansion, May 15, 1884..
It is hereby ordered that the tract of country in the Territory of
New Mexico set apart as a reservation for the Jicarilla Apache In-
dians by Executive order dated September 21, 1880, be, and the same
hereby
is,restored to the public domain.
Chester A. Arthur.
