The White House, April 13, 1912.
It is ordered that the NE. \ of the SW. \ of sec. 31, T. 19 N., R.
23 W., M. P. M., in the Flathead Indian Reservation, Montana, be,
and the same is hereby, withdrawn from settlement, location, sale,
and entry, and reserved for examination and classification, subject to
all of the provisions, limitations, exceptions, and conditions contained
in the act of June 25, 1910 (36 Stat., 847).
Wm. H. Taft.
