Department of the Interior,
Washington., D. 0. FebrnoTy 26, 1866.
Sir : I have the honor to submit herewith a letter addressed to this
department by the Commissioner of Indian Affairs, requesting the
reservation from preemption or sale of townships 31 and 32 north,
range 5 west, and townships 31 and 32 north, range 6 west of the
sixth principal meridian, in Nebraska Territory, until the action of
Congress be had, with a view to the setting apart of these townships
as a reservation for the Santee Sioux Indians now at Crow Creek,
Dakota, and recommend that you direct those lands to be withdrawn
from market and held in reserve for the purpose indicated.
I am, sir, very respectfully, your obedient servant,
James Harlan, Secretary.
The President of the United States.
