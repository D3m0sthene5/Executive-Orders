Executive Mansion,
Washington, D. C, November 19, 1892.
It is hereby ordered that the Executive order of May 17, 1884, by
President Chester A. Arthur, withdrawing from sale and settlement
and setting apart as a reservation for Indian purposes certain lands
in the Territories of Utah and Arizona, be, and the same hereby
is,
modified so that all the lands described in said order which lie west
of the one hundred and tenth degree of west longitude and within the
Territory of Utah be, and the same hereby are, restored to the public
domain, freed from the reservation made by said order.
Benj. Harrison.
