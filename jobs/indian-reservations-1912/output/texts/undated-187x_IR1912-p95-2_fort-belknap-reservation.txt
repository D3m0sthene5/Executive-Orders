Executive Mansion, August 19, 187!^.
It is hereby ordered that all that tract of country in Montana
Territory set apart by Executive order dated July 5, 1873, and not
embraced within the tract set apart by act of Congress approved
April 15, 1874, for the use and occupation of the Gros Ventre, Pie-
gan, Blood, Blackfeet, River Crow, and other Indians, comprised
within the following boundaries, viz :
Commencing at a point on the south bank of the Missouri River
opposite the mouth of the Marias River; thence along the main
channel of the Marias River to Birch Creek; thence up the main
channel of Birch Creek to its source; thence west to the summit of
the main chain of the Rocky Mountains; thence along said summit
in a southerly direction to a point opposite the source of the Medi-
cine or Sun River; thence easterly to said source, and down the
south bank of said Medicine or Sun River to the south bank of the
Missouri River; thence down the south bank of the Missouri River
to the place of beginning, be, and the same is hereby, restored to the
public domain.
U. S. Grant.
