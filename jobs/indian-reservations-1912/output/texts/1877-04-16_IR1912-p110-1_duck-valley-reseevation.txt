Executive Mansion, April 16, 1877.
It is hereby ordered that the following-described tract of country,
situated partly in the Territory of Idaho and partly in the State of
Nevada, be. and the same hereby
is,withdrawn from the public do-
main, to wit
: Commencing at the one hundredth milepost of the sur-
vey of the north boundary of Nevada
; thence due north to the inter-
section of the north boundary of township 16 south of Boise base
line in Idaho
; thence due west to
apoint due north of the one hun-
dred and twentieth milepost of said survey of the north boundary
of Nevada; thence due south to the ninth standard parallel north
of the Mount Diablo base line in Nevada
; thence due east to
apoint
due south of the place of beginning
; thence north to the place of be-
ginning. And the above-named tract of land
ishereby set apart
as
a reservation for the Western Shoshone Indians, subject to such
modifications of boundary as
alocation of limits shall determine.
K. B. Hayes.
