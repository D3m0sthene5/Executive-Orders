The White House, May 29, 1912.
It is hereby ordered that the SW. J of the NE. i, the SE. I of the
NW. i, the NW. i of the SE. J, and the NE. i of the SW. i of sec.
8, and the S. 4 of the SW. I of sec. 9, T. 11 S., R. 19 W. of the Salt
Lake meridian, in the county of Tooele, State of Utah, be, and the
same are hereby, reserved from settlement, entry, sale, or other dis-
posal and set aside for school, agency, and other necessary uses for
the benefit of Indians on the public domain in the State of Utah,
subject, however, to any valid existing rights of any persons thereto.
Wm. H. Taft.
