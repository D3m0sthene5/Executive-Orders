A NO claim needs every clause decided as terminated on or before 2026-10-14, each with its own exact first-end date, actor, mechanism and a held source quoted exactly (an express revocation or supersession by a later order whose text is held; the order's own terms; expiry of the statute it rests on with the statute text held; performance shown by a held source). A later cleanup revocation is the formal disposition, not by default the first end: a first end needs its own reason and source. Typical defects that reviewers reject on NO orders: an end date that does not match the cited source (signing versus effective date; a year typed wrong); an earlier end (an earlier revocation, a partial revocation of the part this order relies on, an own-terms end) not considered; a held Federal Register "Revoked by" or "Superseded by" note not reconciled; an extension or amendment not listed as a later event. If the held evidence shows the order was in force on 2026-10-14, do not submit a NO claim: block the member, give the evidence in its blocker and assessment row, and say so in your report (the ledger is changed only by a separate owner route). Where only a bound is supported (for example a formal revocation date with the first end unresolved), block and record the bound in the assessment row.
