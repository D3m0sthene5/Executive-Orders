Fu el U se Prohibitions
By the authority vested in me as President by the Constitution and statutes of 
the United States of America, and in order to eliminate unnecessary adminis­
trative burdens and costs associated with the conversion of certain Federal 
powerplants to alternative sources of fuel, as required by the Powerplant and 
Industrial Fuel Use Act of 1978 (92 Stat. 3289), it is hereby ordered that 
Executive Order No. 12217 is revoked.
THE WHITE HOUSE,
