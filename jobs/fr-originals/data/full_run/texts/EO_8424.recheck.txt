EXECUTIVE ORDER
Amending Paragraph 7, Subdivision I,
Schedule A of the Civil Service
Rules
By virtue of and pursuant to the au­
thority vested in me by paragraph
Eighth, subdivision SECOND, section
2 of the Civil Service Act (22 Stat. 404),
it is ordered that paragraph 7, Subdivi­
sion I, Schedule A of the Civil Service
Rules be, and it is hereby, amended to
read as follows:
“7. Any person employed in a foreign
country or in the Virgin Islands, or in
Puerto Rico when public exigency war­
rants, or in any island possession of the
United States in the Pacific ocean (ex­
cept the Hawaiian Islands), or United
States citizens employed in the Philip­
pine Islands, when in the opinion of the
Civil Service Commission it is not prac­
ticable to treat the position as in the
competitive classified service; but this
paragraph shall not apply to any person
employed in Canada or Mexico in the
service of the Immigration and Naturali­
zation Service, Department of Labor, or
to any person employed in any foreign
country by the Bureau of Customs of
the Treasury Department.”
Franklin D Roosevelt
The White House,
May 28, 1940.
