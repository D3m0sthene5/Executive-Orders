Executive  O rder
DESIGNATING PORT ST. JO E , FLORIDA, AS A CUSTOMS PORT OF ENTRY
By virtue of and pursuant to the authority vested in me  
by the act of August 1, 1914, 38 Stat. 609, 623 (U. S. C., title  
19, sec. 2), it is ordered that the port of Port St. Joe, Florida,  
be, and it is hereby, designated as a customs port of entry  
in Customs Collection District No. 18 (Florida), with head­
quarters at Tampa, Florida.
This order shall become effective thirty days from the date  
hereof.
