“7. Any person employed in a foreign 
country or in the Virgin Islands, or in 
Puerto Rico when public exigency war­
rants, or in any island possession of the 
United States in the Pacific Ocean (ex­
cept the Hawaiian Islands), or in the 
Philippine Islands, when in the opinion 
of the Civil Service Commission it is not 
practicable to treat the position as in 
the competitive classified service; but 
this paragraph shall not apply to any 
person employed in Canada or Mexico 
in the service of the Immigration and 
Naturalization Service, Department of 
Justice, or to any person employed in any 
foreign country by the Bureau of Cus­
toms of the Treasury Department.”

make material disclosure—Old and used  
as unused or new.  Representing, in 
connection with offer, etc., in commerce, 
of hats, (a) that hats composed in whole 
or in part of used or secondhand ma­
terials are new or are composed of new 
materials by failure to stamp on the 
sweat bands thereof, in conspicuous and 
legible terms which cannot be removed 
or obliterated without mutilating the 
sweat bands, a statement that said prod­
ucts are composed of secondhand or used 
materials, or (b) representing in any 
manner that hats made in whole or in 
part from old, used or secondhand ma­
terials are new or are composed of new 
materials, prohibited; subject to the pro­
vision, however, that if sweat bands are 
not affixed to such hats then such stamp­
ing must appear on the bodies of such 
hats in conspicuous and legible terms 
which carihot be removed or obliterated
