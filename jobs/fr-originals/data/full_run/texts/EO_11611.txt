THE PRESIDENT 13889
EXECUTIVE ORDER 11611
Inspection of Income, Excess-Profits, Estate, and G ift T ax Returns by  
the Committee on Internal Security, House of Representatives
By virtue of the authority vested in me by section 55(a) of the Internal 
Revenue Code of 1939, as amended (26 U.S.C. (1952 Ed.) 5 5 (a)), 
and by section 6103(a) of the Internal Revenue Code of 1954, as 
amended (26 U.S.C. 6103(a)), I do hereby order that any income, 
excess-profits, estate, or gift tax return for the years 1950 to 1971, 
inclusive, shall, during the Ninety-second Congress, be open to inspec­
tion by the Committee on Internal Security, House of Representatives, or 
any duly authorized subcommittee thereof, for the purpose of carrying 
on those investigations authorized by clause 11 of Rule XI of the Rules 
of the House of Representatives. Such inspection shall be in accordance 
and upon compliance with the rules and regulations prescribed by the 
Secretary of the Treasury in Treasury Decisions 6132 and 6133, relating 
to the inspection of returns by committees of the Congress, approved 
by the President on May 3, 1955.
