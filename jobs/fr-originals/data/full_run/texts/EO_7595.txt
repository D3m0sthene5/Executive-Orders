ESTABLISHING MATIA ISLAND MIGRATORY BIRD REFUGE
Washington
By virtue of and pursuant to the authority vested in me 
as President of the United States and by the act of June 
25, 1910, ch. 421, 36 Stat. 847, as amended by the act of 
August 24 1912, ch. 369, 37 Stat. 497, and in order to effectu­
ate further the purposes of the Migratory Bird Conservation 
Act (45 Stat. 1222), it is ordered that all that area of unsur­
veyed land, comprising approximately 145 acres, known as 
Matia Island, situated in T. 38 N., R 1  W., Williamette 
Meridian, San Juan County, Washington (the approximate 
geographic position of which island is in latitude 48° 44'48" 
N., and longitude 122°50' W., from Greenwich), be, and it 
is hereby reserved and set apart for the use of the Depart­
ment of Agriculture, subject to valid existing rights, as 
a refuge and breeding ground for migratory birds and other 
wildlife.
The Executive order of July 15, 1875, reserving certain 
public lands for lighthouse purposes, is hereby revoked in 
so far as it applies to the above-described land.
This refuge shall be known as the Matia Island Migratory 
Bird Refuge.
