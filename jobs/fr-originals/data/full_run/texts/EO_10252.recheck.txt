EXECUTIVE ORDER 10252
Attaching the Canal Zone to the In­
ternal Revenue Collection District
of Florida
By virtue of the authority vested in
me by section 3650 (a) of the Internal
Revenue Code, it is ordered that the
Canal Zone be, and it is hereby, attached
to and made a part of the Internal Reve­
nue Collection District of Florida for all
purposes authorized by the internal
revenue laws of the United States.
This order shall be effective as of
January 1, 1951.
Harry S. Truman
The White House,
June 9, 1951.
