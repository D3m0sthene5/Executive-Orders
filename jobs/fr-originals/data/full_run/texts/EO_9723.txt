By virtue of the authority vested in me 
by the Constitution and the statutes, and 
as President of the United States and 
Commander in Chief of the Army and 
Navy, it is hereby ordered as follows: 
Executive Order No. 9205 of July 25, 
1942, is revoked, and the President’ s 
War Relief Control Board established 
by that order is hereby terminated. The 
Secretary of State is authorized and di­
rected to liquidate all of the activities 
and obligations and wind up all of the 
affairs of the Board as rapidly as prac­
ticable, and to utilize therefor such of 
the personnel, property, records, and 
unexpended appropriations of the Board 
as may be necessary.
This order shall become effective at 
the close of business May 15, 1946.
