Presidential Documents
Title 3— THE PRESIDENT
Executive Order 11038
CONTINUING IN EFFECT EXECUTIVE ORDER NO. 10945 1  OF MAY 24,  
1961, RELATING TO THE ADMINISTRATION OF THE EXPORT CON­
TROL ACT OF 1949, AS AMENDED
By virtue of the authority vested in me by the Export Control Act 
of 1949, as amended, and as President of the United States it is 
ordered that:
Effective July 1, 1962, Executive Order No. 10945 of May 24, 1961, 
and all delegations, redelegations, rules, regulations, orders, licenses, 
and other forms of administrative action under said order which were 
in effect on June 30,1962, are continued in full force and effect.
J o h n F .  Ke n n e d y
T h e W h i t e H o u s e,
J u ly  23,1962.
