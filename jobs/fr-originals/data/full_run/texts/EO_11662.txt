Presidential Documents
Title 3— The President
EXECUTIVE ORDER 11662
Further Providing for the Administration of the Disaster Relief Act of
1970, as Amended
By virtue of the authority vested in me by the Disaster Relief Act of  
1970, as amended, Section 301 of title 3 of the United States Code, and  
as President of the United States, Executive Order No. 11575 of  
December 31, 1970, is amended by substituting “Disaster Relief Act of  
1970, as amended” for “Disaster Relief Act of 1970.” Any reference in  
any other order to the Disaster Relief Act of 1970 shall, after the date of  
this order, be deemed to refer to the Disaster Relief Act of 1970, as  
amended.
T h e W h it e H ouse ,
March 29,1972.
