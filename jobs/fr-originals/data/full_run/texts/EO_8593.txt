EXECUTIVE ORDER
P rescribing  R egulations  G overning the 
Enforcement  of the N eutrality  of 
the U nited S tates
WHEREAS, under the treaties of the  
United States and the law of nations it  
is the duty of the United States, in any  
war in which the United States is a neu­
tral, not to permit the commission of  
unneutral acts within the jurisdiction of  
the United States;
AND WHEREAS, a proclamation was  
issued by me on the fifteenth day of  
November1  declaring the neutrality of  
the United States of America in the war  
now existing between Italy, on the one  
hand, and Greece, on the other hand:  
NOW, THEREFORE, in order to make  
more effective the enforcement of the  
provisions of said treaties, law of nations,  
and proclamation, I hereby prescribe that  
the provisions of my Executive Order No.  
8233* of September 5, 1939, prescribing  
regulations governing the enforcement  
of the neutrality of the United States,  
apply equally in respect to Greece.
F ranklin  D R oosevelt
