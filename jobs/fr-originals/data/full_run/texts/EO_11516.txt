Presidential Documents
Title 3— THE PRESIDENT
Executive Order 11516
AMENDING EXECUTIVE ORDER NO. IT248, PLACING CERTAIN POSI­
TIONS IN LEVELS IV AND V OF THE FEDERAL EXECUTIVE SALARY 
SCHEDULE
By virtue of the authority vested in me by section 5317 of title 5  
of the United States Code, as amended, section 2 of Executive  
Order No. 11248 1  of October 10, 1965, as amended, placing certain  
positions in level Y of the Federal Executive Salary Schedule, is  
further amended by deleting “ (24) Director, Office of Minority Busi­
ness Enterprise, Department of Commerce”, and inserting in lieu  
thereof the following:
(24) Assistant to the Secretary, Department of Commerce.
T he  W h ite H ouse,
March 19, 1970.
