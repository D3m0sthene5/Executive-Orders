By virtue of the authority vested in me 
by the Civil Service Act (22 Stat. 403) 
and by section 1753 of the Revised Stat­
utes of the United States, it is hereby 
ordered as follows:
1. All existing reemployment rights to 
positions in the Federal service acquired 
under authority of Executive Order No. 
8973 of December 12,1941, Executive Or­
der No. 9067 of February 20, 1942, or
Directive No. X as amended by Directive 
No. XVI of the War Manpower Commis­
sion (7 F. R. 7298,11050), or under regu­
lations of the Civil Service Commission 
issued pursuant thereto, shall expire at 
the end of the six months’  period fol­
lowing the date of this order unless ap­
plication for reemployment under such 
rights shall have been made before the 
end of such six months’  period.
2. This order shall not affect reem­
ployment rights heretofore or hereafter 
acquired under Executive Order No. 9711 
of April 11, 1946, No. 9721 of May 10, 
1946, No. ^862 of May 31, 1947, or No. 
9932 of February 27, 1948.
3. The Civil Service Commission is au­
thorized to prescribe such rules and regr 
ulations and to establish such procedures 
as may be necessary for the administra­
tion of this order.
