Title 3— THE PRESIDENT
Executive Order 11319
AMENDING EXECUTIVE ORDER NO. 10707, ESTABLISHING A SEAL  
FOR THE UNITED STATES COAST GUARD
By virtue of the authority vested in me as President of the United  
States, and upon the recommendation of the Secretary of the Treasury,  
Executive Order No. 107071  of May 6,1957, is amended by substituting  
for the second paragraph of the order the following:
On a white disk the shield of the Coat of Arms of the United  
States (paly of thirteen pieces argent and gules a chief azure)  
between the motto “SEMPER PARATUS” in red;  circumscribed  
by a white annulet edged and inscribed “UNITED STATES  
COAST GUARD 1790” in blue all in front of two crossed anchors  
with stock, arms, and flukes in slight perspective in gold; super­
imposed upon a light blue disk with gold rope rim.
T h e W h ite H ouse,
December 9 , 1966.
