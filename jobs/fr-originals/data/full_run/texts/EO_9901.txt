under the Emergency Relief Appropria­

funds allocated to Puerto Rico under the 
Emergency Relief Appropriation Act of

Rico Reconstruction Administration,

without additional compensation, and to 
exercise and discharge the functions, 
duties, and authority conferred upon the 
Puerto Rico Reconstruction jAdministra-

Orders No. 7057 of May 28, 1935, No. 
7180 of September 6,1935, as amended by 
No. 7554 of February 17, 1937, and No.

§ 27.1 Exclusion from provisions of  
Federal Employees Pay Act and Clas­
sification Act.  In accordance with the 
provisions of section 1  and section 2 of 
Public Law 330, 80th Congress, ap­
proved August 4, 1947, the following 
positions, in addition to those specifi­
cally excluded by section 1  and section 
2 of such law, are excluded from the 
provisions of the Federal Employees Pay 
Act of 1945 (Public Law 106, 79th Con­
gress), as amended, and the Classifica­

Interns in clinical psychology at St. 
Elizabeths Hospital, second year ap­
proved post graduate training (pre-doc- 
toral) and fifth year approved post 
graduate training (post-doctoral).
§  27.2 Maximum stipends prescribed.  
In accordance with the provisions of sec­

approved August 4, 1947, the following 
maximum stipends (including overtime 
pay, maintenance allowances, and other 
payments in money or kind) for student 
nurses, medical or dental interns, resi- 
dents-in-training, student dietitians, 
student physical therapists, and student 
occupational therapists, except as other­
wise provided in § 27.3 are hereby pre­
scribed:
