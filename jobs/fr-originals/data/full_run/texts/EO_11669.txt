THE PRESIDENT 9013
E X E C U T IV E  O R D E R  11669
J. Edgar Hoover
As a mark of respect to the memory of J. Edgar Hoover, it is hereby 
ordered, pursuant to the provisions of Section 4 of Proclamation 3044 
of March 1, 1954, as amended, that until interment the flag of the 
United States shall be flown at half-staff on all buildings, grounds and 
naval vessels of the Federal Government in the District of Columbia and 
throughout the United States and its Territories and possessions. I also 
direct that the flag shall be flown at half-staff for the same length of time 
at all United States embassies, legations, consular offices, and other 
facilities abroad, including all military facilities and naval vessels and 
stations.
T h e W h ite H o u se,
May 2 , 1972.
