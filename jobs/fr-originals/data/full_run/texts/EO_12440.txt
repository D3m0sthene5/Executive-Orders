Executive Order 12440 of September 8, 1983
President’s Commission on Industrial Competitiveness
By the authority vested in me as President by the Constitution and law s of the  
United States of A m erica, including the Federal, Advisory Committee A ct, as  
amended (5 U.S.C. App. I), and in order to increase the membership of the  
President’s Commission on Industrial Competitiveness, it is hereby ordered  
that the second sentence of Section 1(a) of Executive Order No. 12428 of June  
28,1983 is hereby amended to provide as follows:
“The Commission shall be composed of no more than thirty members appoint­
ed or designated by the President.”.
