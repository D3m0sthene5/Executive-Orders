Executive Order
AMENDAIENT OF PARAGRAPH 6, SUBDIVISION IX, SCHEDULE A OF
THE CIVIL SERVICE RULES
By virtue of and pursuant to the authority vested in me
by paragraph Eighth, subdivision SECOND, section 2 of the
Civil Service Act (22 Stat. 404), it is ordered that paragraph
6 of Subdivision IX of Schedule A of the Civil Service Rules
be, and it is hereby, amended to read as follows:
“6. Two assistants to the Secretary in the office of the
Secretary of Agriculture.”
Franklin D Roosevelt
The White House,
September 6, 1937.
