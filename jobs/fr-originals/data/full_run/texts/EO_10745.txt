WHEREAS section 4731 (g) of the In­

OPIATE.— The word “opiate”, as used in  
this part shall mean any drug (as defined

321) found by the Secretary or his delegate,  
after due notice and opportunity for public  
hearing, to have an addiction-forming or ad-  
diction-sustaining liability similar to mor­
phine or cocaine, and proclaimed by the  
President to have been so found by the  
Secretary or his delegate, * *■■•*;
AND WHEREAS the Secretary of the  
Treasury; after due notice and oppor­
tunity for public hearing, has found that 
each of the following-named drugs has  
an addiction-forming or addiction-sus­
taining liability similar to morphine, and 
that in the public interest this finding  
should be effective immediately:  
l-(2 Morpholinoethyl) -4-carbethoxy-  
4-phenylpiperidine.
d-2, 2-Diphenyl-3 -methyl-4 -morpho-  
lino-butyryl-pyrrolidine.

that the Secretary of the Treasury has  
found that each of the aforementioned  
drugs has an addiction-forming or ad­
diction-sustaining liability similar to  
Morphine and that in the public interest 
this finding should be effective im­
mediately.
