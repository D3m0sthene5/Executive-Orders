EXECUTIVE ORDER
Excepting Appointments in the United
States Coronado Exposition Commis­
sion From the Requirements of the
Civil Service Act and Rules
By virtue of and pursuant to the au­
thority vested in me by the provisions of
paragraph Eighth of subdivision SEC­
OND of section 2 of the Civil Service Act
(22 Stat. 403, 404), it is hereby ordered
that appointments to positions under
the United States Coronado Exposition
Commission established by the act of
July 17, 1939, Public, No. 186, 76th Con­
gress, may be effected without regard to
the competitive requirements of the
Civil Service Act and Rules.
Franklin D Roosevelt
The White House,
October 21,1939.
