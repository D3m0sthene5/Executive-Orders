Executive Order
reservation of land for river and harbor purposes
Minnesota
By virtue of and pursuant to the authority vested in me as
President of the United States and by the act of June 25,
1910, 36 Stat. 847, as amended by the act of August 24, 1912,
37 Stat. 497, it is hereby ordered as follows:
Section 1. The Executive Order of March 13, 1854, as
modified by the Executive Order of January 11, 1855, reserv­
ing and setting apart fractional section 20, township 49 north,
range 13 west, fourth principal meridian, for military pur­
poses, is hereby revoked.
Section 2. The land described in section 1 hereof is hereby
temporarily withdrawn from settlement, location, sale, or
entry and reserved for the use erf the War Department for
river and harbor purposes.
Section 3. This order shall continue in force until revoked
by the President or by act of Congress.
Franklin D Roosevelt
The White House,
April 6, 1938.
