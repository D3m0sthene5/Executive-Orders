venes a selection board under chapter 543

for recommendation for promotion to the  
next higher grade. The promotion zone in  
each grade shall be Composed of that num ber

eration, who are eligible for selection for pro­
motion to the next higher grade and who  
have not previously failed of such selection,  
that m ust either be selected for promotion

m aintain '  a  flow of promotion consistent

suspended until June 30 of the fiscal year 
following that in which the national 
emergency proclaimed by Proclamation 
No. 2914 of December 16, 1950, shall end.
