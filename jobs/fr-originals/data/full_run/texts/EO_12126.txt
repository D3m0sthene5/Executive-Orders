Executive Order 12126 of March 29, 1979
Correction in Executive Order No. 12107
[FR Doc. 79-10111

By the authority vested in me as President by the Constitution of the United 
States of America, and in order to delete an erroneous reference to a revoked 
Executive order in Executive Order No. 12107 of December 28,1978, relating to 
the Civil Service Commission and Labor-Management in the Federal Serv­
ice, the number “ 11512” is deleted from Section 2-101(b) of that Order.
THE WHITE HOUSE,
