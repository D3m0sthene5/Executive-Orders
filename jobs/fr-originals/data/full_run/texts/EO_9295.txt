(1) Any manufacturer of Schedule I

Production Board Order L-170, except

tary of Agriculture or of the Special War 
Board Assistant to the Secretary of 
Agriculture.(2) Manufacturers, mail order houses, 
distributors, or dealers may accept trans­
fers of any of the Schedule I equipment 
listed in this paragraph (a) which is au­
thorized for transfer by subparagraph
(1) of this paragraph 'a) and may trans­

authorized for transfer by subparagraph
(1) of this paragraph (a) to any other

transfers and such transfers may be
