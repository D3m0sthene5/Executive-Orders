Title 3— THE PRESIDENT
Executive Order 11069
AMENDING EXECUTIVE ORDER NO. 110171  SO AS TO DESIGNATE  
THE SECRETARY OF COMMERCE AS A MEMBER OF THE RECREA­
TION ADVISORY COUNCIL
By virtue of the authority vested in me as President of the United  
States, Section 1(a)  of Executive Order No. 11017 of April 27, 1962,  
which established the Recreation Advisory Council, is hereby amended  
by inserting “the Secretary of Commerce,” immediately after “the  
Secretary of Defense,”.
