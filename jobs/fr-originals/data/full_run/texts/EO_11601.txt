By virtue of the authority vested in me by section 301 of title 3 of the 
United States Code, and as President of the United States, section 1  of 
Executive Order No. 113901  of January 22, 1968, is amended by 
adding thereto the following:
“ (15) The authority vested in the President by section 6386(c) of 
title 10, United States Code, to suspend, during a war or national emer­
gency, certain provisions of law relative to the mandatory retirement or 
separation of officers of the Navy and Marine Corps.”
