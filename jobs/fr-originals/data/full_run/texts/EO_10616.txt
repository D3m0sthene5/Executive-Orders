By virtue of the authority vested in me 
by section 426 (c) of the Officer Per­
sonnel Act of 1947, as amended by sec­
tion 1  (h) of the act of June 30, 1951, 
6 5  Stat. 109, it is ordered as follows:
1 . The operation of those provisions 
of Title HI of the Officer Personnel Act 
of 1 9 4 7 , as amended, which relate to the 
service-in-grade requirements of officers 
of the Marine Corps of the grade of 
brigadier general for eligibility for con­
sideration by a selection board for tem­
porary promotion to the grade of major 
general is hereby suspended until June 
3 0  of the fiscal year following that in 
which the national emergency pro­
claimed by Proclamation No. 2914 of 
December 16,1950, shall end.
2 . This order shall become effective 
on July 1 , 1955.
