This Administration is determined to continue to develop a m ore com ­
prehensive, integrated national energy policy to meet the emerging energy 
challenge. M any steps have been taken toward that end, including meas­
ures to increase domestic production o f all form s o f energy without 
violating our natural environment, to conserve the energy w e produce, 
to better utilize our current resources, and to use our vast scientific and 
technological capacities to develop new sources and new forms o f 
energy. I have now  determined that in order to protect and prom ote the 
interests o f the people o f the U nited States as energy users, and to co­
ordinate the policies o f the executive branch in this area, it is necessary 
to establish a Special Committee on Energy and a N ational Energy 
O ffice.

Committee on Energy. T h e D irector o f the National Energy O ffice shall

Sec. 2. There is hereby established in the Executive O ffice o f the Presi­

Functions of the Director
Sec. 3. (a )1 . T he D irector shall advise the President, through the

grams, activities, and related matters.
(b ) T h e D irector shall recommend policies and guidelines pertaining

Branch. T o  the maximum extent permitted by law, Federal officers and
