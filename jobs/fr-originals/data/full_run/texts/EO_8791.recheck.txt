EXECUTIVE ORDER
Withdrawing Public Lands for Use of
the Department of the Navy
CALIFORNIA
By virtue of the authority vested in
me as President of the United States, it
is ordered that, subject to valid existing
rights and withdrawals, the following-
described public lands be, and they are
hereby, withdrawn from all forms of ap­
propriation under the public-land laws,
including the mining laws, and reserved
for the use of the Department of the
Navy in connection with a Marine Corps
combat and training area:
San Bernardino Meridian
T. 15 S., R. 2 W.,
sec. 11, lot 1, NE14SW14,
sec. 12, Ny2NEV4, NE^NWft,
containing 199.04 acres.
This order supersedes, as to any of the
above-described lands affected thereby,
the temporary withdrawal for classifica­
tion and other purposes, made by Execu­
tive Order No. 6910 of November 26,1934,
as amended.
Franklin D Roosevelt
The White House,
June 14, 1941.
