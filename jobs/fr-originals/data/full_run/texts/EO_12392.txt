Executive Order 12392 of November 15, 1982
International Financial Institutions
By the authority vested in me as President by the Constitution and statutes of  
the United States of America, including Section 301 of Title 3 of the United  
States Code, and in order to assign to the Secretary of the Treasury the  
authority to make payments to certain international financial institutions, it is  
hereby ordered that the functions vested in the President by Section 129 of  
Public Law 97-276 (October 2, 1982) are delegated to the Secretary of the  
Treasury.
THE WHITE HOUSE,
