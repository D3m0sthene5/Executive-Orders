Designating  Fairbanks , Alaska, as a Cus­

By virtue of and pursuant to the au­
thority vested in me by the act of August 
1, 1914, 38 Stat. 609, 623 (U. S. C., title 
19, sec. 2), it is ordered as follows:
1. Fairbanks, Alaska, is hereby desig­
nated as a customs port of entry in Cus­
toms Collection District No. 31 (Alaska), 
with headquarters at Juneau, Alaska.
2. Seward, Alaska, is hereby discontin­
ued as a customs port of entry in Cus­
toms Collection District No. 31 (Alaska).
3. This order shall become effective 
thirty days from the date hereof.
Franklin  D  Roosevelt
