Executive Order 12442 of September 21, 1983
Presidential Commission on Indian Reservation Economies
By the authority vested in me as President by the Constitution and laws of the 
United States of America, including the Federal Advisory Committee Act, as 
amended (5 U.S.C. App. I), it is hereby ordered as follows:
Section 1. Section 2(d) of Executive Order No. 12401 'is hereby amended to 
provide as follows:
“The Commission shall submit its final report to the President and the 
Secretary of the Interior by November 30,1984.”.
Sec. 2. Section 4(b) of Executive Order No. 12401 is hereby amended to 
provide as follows:
“The Commission shall, unless sooner extended, terminate 30 days after it 
transmits its final report to the President.”.
THE WHITE HOUSE,
