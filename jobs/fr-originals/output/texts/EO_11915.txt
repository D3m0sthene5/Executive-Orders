THE PRESIDENT 19195
Executive Order 11915 • M ay 10, 1976
Abolishing the Energy Research and Development Advisory Council
By virtue of the authority vested in me by the Constitution and Statutes of the 
United States, and as President of the United States of America, the Energy Research 
and Development Advisory Council established June 29, 1973, is hereby abolished and 
Section 2(4)  of Executive Order No. 11827 of January 4, 1975, is hereby rescinded.
T h e W h ite H o use,
May 10,  1976.
