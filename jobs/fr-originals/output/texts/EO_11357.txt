Executive Order 11357
ADMINISTRATION OF THE NATIONAL TRAFFIC AND MOTOR VEHICLE  
SAFETY ACT THROUGH THE NATIONAL HIGHWAY SAFETY BUREAU  
AND ITS DIRECTOR
By virtue of the authority vested in me as President of the United 
States by Section 201 of the Highway Safety Act of 1966, as amended 
(80 Stat. 735, 943), and by Section 3(f)(3) of the Department of 
Transportation Act (80 Stat. 932), it is hereby ordered that the pro­
visions of the National Traffic and Motor Vehicle Safety Act of 1966, 
as amended (80 Stat. 718, 943), shall be carried out through the 
National Highway Safety Bureau and the Director thereof.
