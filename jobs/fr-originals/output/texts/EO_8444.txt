It is unlawful for any person to hunt, 
trap, capture, wilfully disturb, or kill 
any bird or wild animal of any kind 
whatsoever within the limits of the 
refuge, or to enter thereon for such pur­
poses, except under such rules or regu­
lations as may be prescribed by the Sec­
retory of the Interior.

5, 1935, withdrawing for classification 
and other purposes all vacant, unre­
served, and unappropriated public lands

as amended, the public lands in the 
above-described area.
