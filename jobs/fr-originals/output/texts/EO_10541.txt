THE QUETICO-SUPERIOR COMMITTEE
By virtue of the authority vested in  
me as President of the United States,  
the existence of the Quetico-Superior  
Committee, which was created by Execu­
tive Order No. 6783 of June 30,1934, and  
the existence of which has been ex­
tended from time to time, the last ex­
tension being made to June 30, 1954, by  
Executive Order No. 10134 of June 28,  
1950, is hereby further extended for a  
period of four years from June 30, 1954,  
to June 30, 1958.
