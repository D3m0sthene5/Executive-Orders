EXECUTIVE ORDER 9718
T ermination  op the  P etroleum  A d m inis­

By virtue of the authority vested in me 
by the Constitution and statutes, includ­
ing Title I of the First War Powers Act, 
1941, and as President of the United 
States, it is hereby ordered as follows :
The Petroleum Administration for 
War, established by Executive Order No. 
92761  of December 2 T ,  1942, together with 
the offices of Petroleum Administrator 
and Deputy Petroleum Administrator, is 
terminated. The Secretary of the In­
terior, acting through such agency or 
agencies of the Department of the In­
terior; as he shall designate, is authorized 
and directed to wind up the affairs of the 
Administration, and to utilize therefor 
so much of the personnel, records, prop­
erty, and funds of thé Administration as 
may be necessary.
All provisions of prior Executive orders 
which are in conflict with this order are 
amended accordingly.
This order shall be effective as of the 
close of business May 8, 1946.

such date shall be deemed to be March 
31, 1947.
