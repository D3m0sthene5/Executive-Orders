EXECUTIVE ORDER
Modifying Executive Order of Decem­
ber 12, 1917, Creating Power Site Re­
serve No. 661, Willamette River
Tributaries, Oregon
MODIFICATION NO. 418
By virtue of the authority vested in
me by the act of June 25, 1910, c. 421,
36 Stat. 847, as amended by the act of
August 24, 1912, c. 369, 37 Stat. 497, it
is ordered that the Executive order of
December 12, 1917, creating Power Site
Reserve No. 661, be, and it is hereby,
modified to the extent necessary to per­
mit the County of Lane, Oregon, to con­
struct a road over lot 5, sec. 35, T. 16 S., R.
2 E., Willamette meridian, Oregon, as
shown on a map on file in the General
Land Office, Department of the Interior,
and bearing the title
COUNTY ROAD LOCATION
IN SECTION 35, T 16 S. R. 2 E. W. M.
