THE PRESIDENT 10917
Executive Order 11299
PLACING AN ADDITIONAL POSITION IN LEVEL V OF THE FEDERAL  
EXECUTIVE SALARY SCHEDULE
B y virtue of the authority vested in me by subsection (f ) of Section  
303 of the Government Employees Salary Reform Act of 1964, and as  
President of the United States, Section 2 of Executive Order No. 11248  
of October 10,1965, as amended, is further amended by adding thereto  
the following:
(9 ) Director, Urban Transportation Administration, Department  
of Housing and Urban Development.
