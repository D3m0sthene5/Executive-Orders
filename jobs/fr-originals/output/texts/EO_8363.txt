EXECUTIVE ORDER
Amendment  op Executive  O rder N o. 8283 
of November 9,1939, Amending  Section 
6 of Civil  Service Rule  n
By virtue of the authority vested in me 
by the Civil Service Act (22 Stat. 403), it 
is ordered that the penultimate para­
graph of Executive Order No. 8283 of
November 9, 1939, be, and it is hereby, 
amended to read*as follows:
“The second proviso  of the said section 
6 of Civil Service Rule n  as herein 
amended shall also apply to the incum­
bents of positions heretofore or here­
after covered into the classified (competi­
tive) service by section 1  of Executive 
Order No. 7916 of June 24, 1938, and sec­
tion 3 of that order is modified accord­
ingly, effective as of November 9, 1939.”
This order is recommended by the Civil 
Service Commission.
Franklin  D R oosevelt

Revocation  of E xecutive  O rder N o. 5346 
of M ay 9, 1930, W ithdrawing  P ublic  
Lands
MINNESOTA
By virtue of the authority vested in me 
by the act of June 25, 1910, c. 421, 36 
Stat. 847, Executive Order No. 5346 of 
May 9, 1930, withdrawing public lands in 
Minnesota pending a resurvey, is hereby 
revoked.
This order shall become effective upon 
the date of the official filing of the plat 
of the resurvey of the lands involved.
Franklin  D R oosevelt
