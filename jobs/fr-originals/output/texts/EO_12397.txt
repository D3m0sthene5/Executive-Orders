Federal Register 
Vol. 47, No. 249

Presidential Documents
Title 3—
The President
Executive Order 12397 of December 23, 1982
National Commission on Social Security Reform
By the authority vested in me as President by the Constitution and laws of the  
United States of America, and specifically the Federal Advisory Committee  
Act, as amended (5 U.S.C. App. I), it is hereby ordered that Section 2(b) of  
Executive Order No. 12335, establishing the National Commission on Social  
Security Reform, is hereby amended to provide as follows:
“The Commission shall make its report to the President by January 15,1983.’’.
THE WHITE HOUSE,  
D e cem b e r 23, 1982.
