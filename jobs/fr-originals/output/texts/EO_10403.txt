The lands hereby reserved as an ad­
dition to the Port Missoula District of 
the Lolo National Forest shall be admin­
istered by the Secretary of Agriculture 
under such rules and regulations and in 
accordance with such general plans as 
may be jointly approved by the Secretary 
of Agriculture and the Secretary of the 
Army for the use, occupation, and pro­
tection of such lands and for the sale of 
products therefrom: Provided, that 
such lands shall remain subject to the 
unhampered use of the Department of 
the Army for purposes of national de­
fense.
The rules and regulations applicable 
to the military establishment shall con­
tinue in full force and effect at all times 
and under all conditions upon all lands 
hereby added in the Port Missoula Dis­
trict of the Lolo National Forest except 
as otherwise provided by agreement be­
tween the Secretary of the Army and the 
Secretary of Agriculture.
