Seal for the Office of Administration
By the authority vested in me as President by the Constitution of the  
United States of America, it is hereby ordered as follows:
1-101. There is established for the Office of Administration in the Execu­
tive Office of the President an official seal described as follows:
On a blue seal, the Arms of the United States proper above the inscrip­
tion OFFICE OF ADMINISTRATION,” in gold raised letters, all within a  
white border edged gold and inscribed “EXECUTIVE OFFICE OF THE  
PRESIDENT OF THE UNITED STATES,” in blue raised letters. Dark blue  
suSSestcd by the Seal of the President denotes the direct organizational link  
with the Presidential office.
1-102. The seal shall be of the design which is attached hereto and made  
a part of this Order.
