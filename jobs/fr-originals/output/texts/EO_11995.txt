Title 3—The President
Executive Order 11995 June 8, 1977
Relating to Certain Positions in Level V of the Executive Schedule
By virtue of the authority vested in me by Section 5317 of Title 5 of the United  
States Code, and as President of the United States of America, Section 2 of Executive  
Order No. 11861, as amended, placing certain positions in level V  of the Executive  
Schedule, is further amended by adding thereto “ (13): Executive Director, Federal  
Personnel Management Systems Study, United States Civil Service Commission.” .
