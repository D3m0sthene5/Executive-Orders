Executive Order
ABOLISHING VINEYARD HAVEN, MASSACHUSETTS, AS A CUSTOMS
PORT OF ENTRY
By virtue of and pursuant to the authority vested in me
by the act of August 1, 1914, 38 Stat. 609, 623 (TJ. S. C.,
title 19, sec. 2), it is ordered that Vineyard Haven, Massa­
chusetts, be, and it is hereby, abolished as a customs port of
entry in Customs Collection District No. 4 (Massachusetts),
as a customs port of entry, effective immediately.
Franklin D Roosevelt
The White House,
May 18, 1937.
