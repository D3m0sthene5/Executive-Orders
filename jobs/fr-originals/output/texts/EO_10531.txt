MISCELLANEOUS AMENDMENTS
The regulations issued by Commodity 
Credit Corporation and the Commodity 
Stabilization Service published in 18 
P. R. 3517 and containing the instruc­
tions and requirements with respect to 
the 1953 Cotton Loan Program are 
hereby amended as follows:
1. Section 427.416 (formerly § 607.416) 
is amended to provide for carrying loans 
in a past-due status through July 31, 
1955, and to provide that CCC will pur­

not been repaid as of August 1, 1955, so 
that the amended section reads as
