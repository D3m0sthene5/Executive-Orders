EXECUTIVE ORDER
Appointment of Admiral William D.
Leahy, Governor of the Territory of
Puerto Rico, As Administrator of
the Puerto Rico Reconstruction Ad­
ministration
By virtue of and pursuant to the au­
thority vested in me under the Emer­
gency Relief Appropriation Act of 1935
(49 Stat. 115) and the act entitled “An
Act to provide that funds allocated to
Puerto Rico under the Emergency Relief
Appropriation Act of 1935 may be ex­
pended for permanent rehabilitation, and
for other purposes”, approved February
11, 1936 (49 Stat. 1135), I hereby appoint
Admiral William D. Leahy, U. S. Navy
(Retired), Governor of the Territory of
Puerto Rico, as Administrator of the
Puerto Rico Reconstruction Administra­
tion, vice Harold L. Ickes, resigned, to
serve without additional compensation,
and to exercise and discharge the func­
tions, duties, and authority conferred
upon the Puerto Rico Reconstruction Ad­
ministration and the Administrator by
Executive Orders No. 7057 of May 28,
1935; No. 7180 of September 6, 1935, as
amended by No. 7554 of February 17,
1937; and No. 7689 of August 12,1937.*
The said Executive Orders are amend­
ed accordingly.
Franklin D Roosevelt
The White House,
November 30, 1939.
