EXECUTIVE ORDER
Revocation of Executive Order No. 5633
of May 28, 1931, Withdrawing Public
Lands
CALIFORNIA
By virtue of the authority vested in me
by the act of June 25, 1910, ch. 421, 36
Stat. 847, Executive Order No. 5633 of
May 28, 1931, withdrawing public lands
in California pending a resurvey, is here­
by revoked.
This order shall become effective upon
the date of the official filing of the plat
of the resurvey of the lands involved.
Franklin D Roosevelt
The White House,
Jvly 19,1939.
