Executive Order 12141 of June 5, 1979  
Independent Water Project Review
By the authority vested in me as President by the Constitution and statutes of  
the United States of Am erica, and in order to provide for an orderly implemen­
tation of the independent review  of Federal w ater resource programs and  
projects, subsections (b) and (c) of Section 1-105 of Executive Order No. 12113  
of January 4,1979, are hereby amended to read as follows:
“(b) After O ctober 1, 1979, an agency shall not submit to the Council, during  
any calendar quarter, more than one-third of the total reports, proposals, and  
plans scheduled for review  during any one fiscal year.
“(c) The Chairman of the Council, or his designee, shall transmit the results of  
the im partial'technical review  to the appropriate agency head. The transmittal
