Presidential Documents
Title 3— THE PRESIDENT
Executive Order 10959
AUTHORIZING THE APPOINTMENT  
OF MR. MAURICE L. KOWAL TO A  
COMPETITIVE POSITION WITHOUT  
REGARD TO THE CIVIL SERVICE  
RULES AND REGULATIONS
By virtue of the authority vested in 
me by section 2 of the Civil Service Act 
of January 16, 1883 (22 Stat. 403, 404), 
it is hereby ordered that Mr. Maurice L. 
Kowal may be appointed to a competitive 
position in the classified civil service of 
the United States without regard to the 
competitive provisions of the Civil 
Service Rules and regulations.
J o h n P. K e n n e d y
T h e W h it e H o u s e,
August 17, 1961.
