of commerce and national defense, and  
for other purposes”, ^approved June 6,

Over Certain Foreign Merchant Ves­
sels”, be, and it is hereby, amended to  
read as follows:
(c) To document any or all of such  
vessels under the laws of the United  
States or of any other country of the  
Western Hemisphere.
