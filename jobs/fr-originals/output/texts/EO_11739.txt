Salary Limitation
S e c. 4. Notwithstanding the rates of basic pay or salaries established  
by sections 1, 2, and 3 of this order, under section 5308 of title 5 of the  
United States Code no rate of basic pay or salary may be paid which is  
in excess of the rate now or hereafter provided for level V  of the Execu­
tive Schedule.
Effective Date
S e c. 5. This order shall take effect as of the first day of the first  
applicable pay period beginning on or after October 1, 1973.
