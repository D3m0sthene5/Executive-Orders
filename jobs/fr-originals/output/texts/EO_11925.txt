Executive Order 11925 June 29, 1976
\
Promotion of Navy and Marine Corps Officers From Below the Appropriate
Promotion Zone
By virtue of the authority vested in me by section 5711(b) of Title 10 of the  
United States Code, and as President of the United States of America and Commander  
in Chief of the Armed Forces, Executive Order No. 11554 of August 29, 1970, as  
amended, is further amended by deleting “June 30, 1976” and inserting in lieu thereof
