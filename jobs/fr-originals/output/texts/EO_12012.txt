Title 3—The President
Executive Order 12012 October 3, 1977
White House Fellowships
By virtue of the authority vested in me by the Constitution and statutes of the  
United States of America, and as President of the United States of America, Executive  
Order No. 11183, as amended, is further amended as follows :
Section  1. The word “young” is deleted wherever it appears in the preamble and  
in Section 2(a).
Sec. 2. Section 2(b)(2) is amended to read :
“(2)  Are presently early in their chosen careers and show exceptional promise of  
future development;”.
Sec . 3. Section 2(b)  (4) is deleted, the word “and” is added at the end of Section  
2(b) (3),  and Section 2(b)  (5) is redesignated as Section 2(b) (4).
Sec. 4. Section 2 (c) is amended by adding “age” after “national origin,”.
