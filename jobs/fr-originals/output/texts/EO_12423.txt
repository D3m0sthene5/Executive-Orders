Title 3—
The President
Executive Order 12423 of May 26, 1983
Foreign Assistance and Arms Control
By the authority vested in me as President by the Constitution and statutes of 
the United States of America, including Section 621 of the Foreign Assistance  
Act of 1961, as amended (22 U.S.C. 2381), and Section 2778 of Title 22 of the  
United States Code, it is hereby ordered as follows:
Section 1. Section l-301(b) of Executive Order No. 12163, as amended, is  
further amended by deleting “and 635(g)” and inserting in lieu thereof “635(g),  
and 636(i)”.
Sec. 2. Section 1(d) of Executive Order No. 11958, as amended, is further  
amended to provide as follows: “Those under Sections 22(a), 29 and 30 of the  
Act to the Secretary of Defense.”.
