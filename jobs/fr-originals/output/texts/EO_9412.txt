WHEREAS the continuous operation of 
transportation service in the Nation is 
necessary for the movement of troops, 
materials of war, necessary passenger 
traffic, and supplies and food for the 
armed forces and the civilian popula­
tion, and is otherwise essential to the 
successful prosecution of the war; and
WHEREAS the continuous operation 
of some  transportation systems is 
threatened by strikes called to com­
mence on December 30, 1942*
NOW, THEREFORE, by virtue of th* 
authority vested in me by the Constitu­
tion and laws of the United States, in­
cluding. the Act of August 2 9 -, 1916, 39 
Stat. 645, and as President of the United 
States and Commander in Chief of the 
Army and Navy, I  hereby order:
I. Possession and control of all 
common earners by railroad, express 
companies terminal companies and 
associations, sleeping, parlor and rail­
road-owned or controlled private car 
companies Call hereinafter referred to 
as carriers), located in the continental 
United States, together with any and all 
appurtenances and; facilities used in con­
nection therewith, su re hereby taken and 
assumed,, through the Secretary of War*, 
as of seven o'clock p. m., on the twenty-

called interurbans* or local public transit 
systems whether or not the same be 
owned or controlled by any of the 
systems of transportation taken here­
under; but if and when the Secretary 
finds it necessary or appropriate to carry 
out the purposes o f this order, he may, 
by subsequent order, take and assume 
possession, control an$ operation of all 
or any part of any transportation
system, including, subways and tunnels, 
and any transportation system so taken 
shall be deemed a carrier for the pur­
poses of this order,
2. The Secretary of War is directed to 
manage and operate or arrange for the

riers taken under this order in such 
manner as he deems necessary to assure 
to the fullest possible extent continuous 
and uninterrupted transportation serv­
ice.
3. In  carrying out this» order the Secre­

of such public or private instrumentali­

may -delegate such o f his authority as he

All Federal agencies shall comply with 
the directives of the Secretary hereunder 
and shall cooperate to the fullest extent 
of their authority with the Secretary in 
carrying out the purposes of this order.
