Executive Order 12536 of October 9, 1985
B o a r d  o f  th e  F o r e ig n  S e r v ic e
By the authority vested in me as President by the Constitution and laws of the  
United States of America, including section 153 of Public Law  99-93, it is  
hereby ordered that Section 9(e) of Executive Order No. 12293, as amended,  
relating to the appointment of the Chairman of the Board of the Foreign  
Service, is revoked, and that Section 9(f) of that Order is redesignated as  
Section 9(e).
TH E W H IT E H O U SE ,
