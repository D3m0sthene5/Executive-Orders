Base Production Control Level for M arginal Properties

m arginal oil w ells, it is hereby ordered as follows:
1-101. For purposes o f the pricing regulations adopted pursuant to the Emer­
gency Petroleum A llocation A c t o f 1973, with respect to months commencing

1-102. For purposes o f this Order, the term “ m arginal properties” has the same

1-103. The Secretary o f Energy m ay, pursuant to Executive Order N o. 11790, as
