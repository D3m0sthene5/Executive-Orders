By virtue of and pursuant to the au­
thority vested in me by the act of June 
20, 1936, 49 Stat. 1554, it is ordered that 
during the fiscal year 1941 the grades and 
ratings of the enlisted men of the Regular 
Army shall be as set forth herein, and 
that the number of enlisted men in the 
several grades and ratings shall not ex­
ceed the number specified herein.
