THE PRESIDENT £ 20171
Executive Order 11502
ENLARGING THE MEMBERSHIP OF THE PRESIDENT’S COMMITTEE ON  
THE NATIONAL MEDAL OF SCIENCE
By virtue of the authority vested in me as President of the United 
States, Executive Order No. 11287 of June 28, 1966, entitled “Award 
and presentation of the National Medal of Science,” is hereby amended 
as follows-: _
(1) By substituting for the words “one ex officio member” in sub­
section (a) of section 2 the following : “two ex officio members”.
(2) By substituting, for subsection (c) of section 2 the following: 
“ (c) The following shall be ex officio members of the Committee: 
“ (1) The President’ s Science Adviser.
“ (2) The President of the National Academy of Sciences.”
