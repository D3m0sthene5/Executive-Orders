such mine or mines prevailing prior to  
the taking of possession thereof.

EXECUTIVE ORDER 9759
S uspending  C ertain  S tatutory  P rovi ­
sio ns R elating  to E mployment  in  the  
Canal Z one
By virtue of the authority vested in  
me by section 2 of the War Department  
Civil Appropriation Act, 1947 (Public Law
374, 79th Congress), section 107 of the  
Naval Appropriation Act, 1947 (Public  
Law 492, 79th Congress), and section 6  
of the Military Appropriation Act, 1947  
(Public Law 515, 79th Congress), relat­
ing to certain kinds of employment in the  
Canal Zone, and deeming such course to  
be in the public interest, I hereby sus­
pend, from and including the effective  
dates of Jthe said Acts, compliance with  
the provisions of the said sections during  
the continuance of any of the wars in  
which the United States is now engaged  
or of the present national emergency.
