THE PRESIDENT 13367
Title 3—The President
EXECUTIVE ORDER 11608
Termination of Federal Field Committee for Development P lanning
in Alaska
By virtue of the authority vested in me as President of the United 
States, Executive Order No. 11182 of October 2, 1964, as amended, is 
revoked and the Federal Field Committee for Development Planning in 
Alaska established thereby is abolished. Executive Order No. 11386 of 
December 28, 1967, to the extent that it pertains to the Federal Field 
Committee for Development Planning in Alaska, is revoked. The Secre­
tary of Commerce shall make such arrangements as may be necessary 
relating to the termination of the Committee.
