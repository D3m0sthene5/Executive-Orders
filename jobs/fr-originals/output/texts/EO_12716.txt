Executive Order 12716 of May 24, 1990
Extending the National Commission on Superconductivity
By the authority vested in me as President by the Constitution and laws of the 
United States of America, including the Omnibus Trade and Competitiveness 
Act of 1988 (P.L. 100-418,102 Stat. 1107) and section 301 of title 3 of the United 
States Code, and to give the National Commission on Superconductivity 
additional time to complete its report, it is hereby ordered that Section 3- 
401(5) of Executive Order No. 12661, as amended, is further amended by 
deleting the date “March 31, 1990,“ and inserting in lieu thereof the date 
“August 7, 1990,” and that the first sentence of Section 3-401(7) of the same 
Executive order is amended to read “The Commission shall disband no later 
than August 7,1990.”
THE WHITE HOUSE, 
M a y  24, 1990.
