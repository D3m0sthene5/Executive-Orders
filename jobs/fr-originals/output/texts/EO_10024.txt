Subject to valid existing rights and to 
the provisions of existing withdrawals, 
the following-described land is hereby 
opened to location and entry under'the 
mining laws of the United States:
San B ernardino  M eridian
T. 4 N., R. 14 W.,
Sec. 15, Ny2
The area described contains 320 acres. 
This order shall not become effective 
to changer the status of the land until 
10:00 a. m. on the thirty-fifth day after 
the date of this order, at which time the 
land shall, subject to the provisions of 
this order, become subject to disposal 
under the mining laws of the United 
States.
