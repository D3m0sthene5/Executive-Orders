Executive Order
ESTABLISHING THE LACASSINE MIGRATORY WATERFOWL REFUGE
Louisiana
By virtue of and pursuant to the authority vested in me as
President of the United States and in order to effectuate
further the purposes of the Migratory Bird Conservation Act
(45 Stat. 1222), it is ordered that the lands acquired or to
be acquired by the United States within the following-
described area, comprising approximately 33,400 acres, in
Cameron Parish, Louisiana, be, and they are hereby, reserved
and set apart, subject to existing valid rights, for the use
of the Department of Agriculture as a refuge and breeding
ground for migratory birds and other wildlife: Provided, that
any private lands within the area described shall become a
part of the refuge hereby established upon the acquisition
of title thereto or lease thereof by the United States:
