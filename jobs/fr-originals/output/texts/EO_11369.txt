Presidential Documents
Title 3— THE PRESIDENT
Executive Order 11369
PLACING ADDITIONAL POSITIONS IN LEVEL V OF THE FEDERAL  
EXECUTIVE SALARY SCHEDULE
By virtue of the authority vested in me by section 5317 of title 5 of  
the United States Code, as amended, and as President of the United  
States, section 2 of Executive Order No. 11248 of October 10,1965, as  
amended, is further amended by adding thereto the following:
(15) Administrator, Social and Rehabilitation Service, Depart­
ment of Health, Education, and Welfare.
(16) Chief, Children’s Bureau, Social and Rehabilitation Service,  
Department of Health, Education, and Welfare.
T h e W h ite H ouse,
August 29, 1967.
