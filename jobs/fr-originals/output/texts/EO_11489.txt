Presidential Documents
Title 3— THE PRESIDENT
Executive Order 11489
AMENDING EXECUTIVE ORDER NO. 11248, PLACING CERTAIN POSI­
TIONS IN LEVELS IV AND V OF THE FEDERAL EXECUTIVE SALARY  
SCHEDULE
By virtue of the authority vested in me by section 5317 of title 5 of  
the United States Code, as amended, section 1 of Executive Order No.  
112481  of October 10, 1965, as amended, placing certain positions in  
level IV of the Federal Executive Salary Schedule, is further amended  
by deleting “ (1) Special Assistant to the Secretary (for Enforcement),  
Treasury Department”, and inserting in lieu thereof the following:
(1) Special Assistant to the Secretary (Congressional Relations),  
Treasury Department.
T h e W h ite H ouse,
October 27, 1969.
