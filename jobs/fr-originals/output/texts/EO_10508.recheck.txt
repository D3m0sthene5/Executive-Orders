Excusing Federal Employees From Duty

as President of the United States, it is

days; and each such one-half day shall
be considered a holiday within the mean­

9, 1952, and of all statutes so far as they
relate to the compensation and leave of
employees of the United States.
Dwight D. Eisenhower
The White House,
December 14, 1953.
