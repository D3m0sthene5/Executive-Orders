WITHDRAWAL OF PUBLIC LAND FOR THE USE, POSSESSION, AND 
CONTROL OF THE TENNESSEE VALLEY AUTHORITY
Alabama
By virtue of and pursuant to the authority vested in me  
by the act of June 25,1910, ch. 421, 36 Stat. 847, as amended  
by the act of August 24, 1912, ch. 369, 37 Stat. 497, and by  
section 7 (b) of the act of May 18, 1933, 48 Stat. 58, 63, it  
is ordered as follows:
S e c t io n 1. Executive Order No. 6964 of February 5, 1935,  
as amended, temporarily withdrawing all public lands in  
certain states for classification and other purposes, is hereby  
revoked in so far as it affects the following-described tract  
of public land in Alabama:
Huntsville  M eridian
T. 4 S., R. 7 E., sec. 28, N W ^ N W ^ , 40 acres.
S e c t io n 2. The tract of land described in section 1 of this 
order is hereby temporarily withdrawn from settlement,  
location, sale, or entry and, subject to valid existing rights,  
reserved for the use, possession, and control of the Ten­
nessee Valley Authority.
S e c t io n 3. The reservation made by section 2 of this  
order shall remain in force until revoked by the President  
or by act of Congress.
