Presidential Documents
The President
Executive Order 12617 of December 17, 1987
President’s Advisory Committee on Mediation and Conciliation
By the authority vested in me as President by the Constitution and laws of the 
United States of America, including the Federal Advisory Committee Act as 
amended (5 U.S.C. App. 1), and in order to extend the life of the President’s 
Advisory Committee on Mediation and Conciliation, it is hereby ordered that 
Section 4(b) of Executive Order No. 12462 of February 17,1984, as amended, is 
further amended to read: “The Committee shall terminate 60 days after the 
Committee submits its report to the President and the Director of the Federal 
Mediation and Conciliation Service, or no later than September 30, 1988, 
unless sooner extended.”
TH E W H ITE HOUSE,
