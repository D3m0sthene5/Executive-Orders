THE PRESIDENT 4647
Executive Order 12116 of January 19, 1979  
Issuance of Food Stamps by the Postal Service
By the authority vested in me as President of the United States of America by  
Section 11 (k) of the Food Stamp Act of 1977 (91 Stat. 974; 7 U.S.C. 2020(k)J, the  
United States Postal Service is hereby granted approval for post offices in all  
or part of any State to issue food stamps to eligible households, upon request  
by the appropriate State agency, as defined in Section 3(n) of the Food Stamp  
Act of 1977 (91 Stat. 960; 7 U.S.C. 2012(n)J.
