EXECUTIVE ORDER
Excluding Certain Lands From the Man­
istee National Forest
MICHIGAN
By virtue of the authority vested in
me by the act of June 4, 1897, 30 Stat.
11, 36 (U.S.C., title 16, sec. 473), and
upon the recommendation of the Secre­
tary of Agriculture, it is ordered that the
following-described tract of land in
15 F.R. 2817, 3652.
Michigan be, and it is hereby, excluded
from the Manistee National Forest:
Sections 2 and 3, and the east half of sec­
tion 4, township 20 north, range 12 west of
the Michigan Meridian.
Franklin D Roosevelt
The White House,
March 14,1942.
