THE PRESIDENT 10881
EXECUTIVE ORDER 11773
Revoking the Authority of the Department of Agriculture To Inspect 
Income Tax Returns
By virtue of the authority vested in me as President of the United States 
of America, Executive Order No. 11697 of January 17,1973, and Execu­
tive Order No. 11709 of March 27, 1973, are hereby revoked and the 
authority conferred upon the Department of Agriculture by those Orders 
to inspect income tax returns is hereby revoked.
T h e W h ite H ouse ,
March 21, 1974.
