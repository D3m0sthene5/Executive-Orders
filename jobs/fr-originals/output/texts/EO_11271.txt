THE PRESIDENT 2991
Executive Order 11271
FLEET ADMIRAL CHESTER W. NIMITZ
As a mark of respect to the memory of Fleet Admiral Chester W.  
Nimitz, it is hereby ordered, pursuant to the provisions of Section 4  
of Proclamation 3044 of March 1, 1954, that until interment the flag  
of the United States shall be flown at half-staff on all buildings,  
grounds, and naval vessels of the Federal Government, in the District  
of Columbia and throughout the United States and its Territories and  
possessions.
