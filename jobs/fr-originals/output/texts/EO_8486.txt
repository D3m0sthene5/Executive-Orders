the Secretary of the Interior to with­

§  29.301 (p) Pursuant to authority 
conferred upon the Secretary of Agricul­
ture by The Tobacco Inspection Act (49 
Stat. 731; 7 U.S.C., Supp. V, secs. 511- 
511q), the tobacco market at Shelbyville, 
Kentucky is designated as a market at 
which transactions in tobacco are subject 
to the provisions of The Tobacco Inspec­
tion Act.
Effective thirty days from this date, 
no tobacco shall be offered for sale at 
auction on said market until such to­
bacco shall have been inspected and cer­
tified by an authorized representative 
of the United States Department of Agri­
culture according to standards estab­
lished under The Tobacco Inspection 
Act: Provided, however.  That the re­
quirement of inspection and certification 
may be suspended at any time when it 
is found impracticable to provide inspec­
tion or when the quantity of tobacco 
available for inspection is not sufficient
