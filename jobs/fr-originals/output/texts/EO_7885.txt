To Collectors of Customs and Others Concerned:
1. The following changes of designation have been made 
in the Customs Patrol Service:
Captain, U. S. Customs Patrol—in lieu of Chief Customs 
Patrol Inspector.
Lieutenant, U. S. Customs Patrol—in lieu of Assistant to 
Chief Customs Patrol Inspector.
Sergeant, U. S. Customs Patrol—in lieu of Zone Sergeant.
Corporal, U. S. Customs Patrol—in lieu of Patrol Unit 
Leader.

of the Customs Patrol Service to the Customs Agency 
Service (T. D. 48481), Article 1326 (g) of the Customs 
Regulations of 19371  is hereby amended by deleting those 
portions thereof entitled “Marks of Rank—Patrol unit 
leader”, “Same—Assistant to chief customs patrol inspec­

tuting therefor the following language:
