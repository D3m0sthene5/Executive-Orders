Executive Order 12390 of October 25, 1982
Delegation to the Secretary of State
By the authority vested in me as President of the United States of America by  
Section 301 of Title 3 of the United States Code, in order to assign certain  
functions to the Secretary of State, it is hereby ordered as follows:
Section 1. The functions vested in the President by Section 306 of the  
Supplemental Appropriations Act of 1982 (Public Law 97-257) are delegated to  
the Secretary of State.
Sec. 2. Before making the determination and providing the written reports  
referred to in that Section 306, the Secretary of State shall confer with the  
Secretary of the Treasury and, as appropriate, with the Secretary of Agricul­
ture and the heads of other interested Executive agencies.
Sec. 3. Similar functions vested in the President by Section 205 of Public Law  
97-216 were delegated to the Secretary of State. The performance by the  
Secretary of State of those functions is confirmed and ratified.
