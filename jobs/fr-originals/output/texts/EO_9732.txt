Washington, Tuesday, June 4, 1946
The P residen t
EXECUTIVE ORDER 9732
R elating  to the  A dministration  of the
Act of F ebruary  22,1935, as A mended
By virtue of the authority vested in 
me by the act of February 22, 1935, en­
titled “An Act to regulate interstate and 
foreign commerce in petroleum and its 
products by prohibiting the shipment in 
such commerce of petroleum and its 
products produced in violation of State 
law, and for other purposes,” (49 Stat. 
30), as amended, it is ordered as follows:
Executive Order No. 7756 of December 
1, 1937, issued pursuant to the said act 
of February 22, 1935, is hereby amended 
by substituting the words “Oil and Gas 
Division” for the words “Petroleum Con­
servation Division” occurring in the 
order and in its title.
The regulations approved October 27, 
1942, as amended, governing the admin­
istration and enforcement of the said 
act of February 22,1935, as amended, are 
hereby amended by substituting the 
words “Oil and Gas Division” for the 
words “Petroleum Conservation Divi­
sion” wherever the latter words appear 
in such regulations, as amended.
