ESTABLISHING BITTER LAKE MIGRATORY WATERFOWL REFUGE
New Mexico
By virtue of and pursuant to the authority vested in me  
as President of the United States and by the act of June 25,  
1910, ch. 421, 36 Stat. 847, as amended by the act of Au­
gust 24, 1912, ch. 369, 37 Stat. 497, and in order to effectuate 
further the purposes of the Migratory Bird Conservation Act  
(45 Stat. 1222), it is ordered that all lands owned or con­
trolled by the United States within the following-described  
area, comprising 21,8.69 acres, more or less, in Chaves County, 
New Mexico, be, and they are hereby, reserved and set apart  
for the use of the Department of Agriculture, subject to  
valid existing rights, as a refuge and breeding ground for  
migratory birds and other wildlife: Provided, that any pri­
vate lands within the area described shall become a part of  
the refuge hereby established upon the acquisition of title  
thereto or lease thereof by the United States:
