by Proclamation No. 2416 of July 25, 
1940;1  and

the boundaries of the said refuge, and 
havé determined that certain adjust­

will become desirable from time to time 
in the interests of the Government and 
of the public; and
WHEREAS pursuant to such studies

the Authority have recommended the

a certain tract of land hereinafter de­

that provision be made for the elimina­

the internal management of the Gov­
