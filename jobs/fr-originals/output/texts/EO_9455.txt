By virtue of the authority vested in me 
by sections 55 (a), 508, 603, 1204, and  
729 (a) of the Internal Revenue Code  
(53 Stat. 1, 29, 111, 171; 54 Stat. 974, 989), 
It is hereby ordered that income, excess-  
profits, declared value excess-profits, and 
capital stock tax returns made under the  
Internal Revenue Code for the years 1939 
to 1944, inclusive, shall be open to in­
spection by the Select Committee to In­
vestigate the Federal Communications  
Commission, House of Representatives,  
or any duly authorized subcommittee  
thereof, for the purpose of carrying out  
the provisions of House Resolution 21  
(Seventy-eighth Congress, first session),  
passed January 19, 1943, and subject to  
the conditions stated in the Treasury  
decision relating to the inspection of such 
returns by that Committee, approved by  
me this date.1
This order is supplemental to Execu­
tive Order 9448, dated June 8,1944.
This order shall be published in the
