By virtue of the authority vested in me 
by sections 55 (a) and 603 of the Inter­
nal Revenue Code (53 Stat. 29, 111), it is 
hereby ordered that corporation statisti­
cal transcript cards prepared by the 
Bureau of Internal Revenue from cor­
poration income and declared value ex­
cess profits tax returns made under the 
Internal Revenue Code, as amended, for 
any taxable year ending after June 30, 
1943, and before July 1, 1944, shall be 
open to inspection by the Office of Price 
Administration; such inspection to be in 
accordance and upon compliance with 
the rules and regulations prescribed by 
the Secretary of the Treasury in Treas­
ury Decision 5173, approved October 26, 
1942, relating to the inspection of simi­
lar cards by the Office of Price Admin­
istration, and the amendments approved 
March 7, 1944, and this date.1

NOW, THEREFORE, by virtue of the 
power and authority vested in me by the 
Constitution and laws of the United 
States, including section 9 of the Selec­
tive Training and Service Act of 1940 as 
amended by section 3 of the War Labor 
Disputes Act, the Act of August 29,1916, 
39 Stat. 645, and the First War Powers 
Act, 1941, as President of the United 
States and Commander in Chief of the 
Army and Navy, it is hereby ordered as 
follows:
1. The Director of the Office of De­
fense Transportation is authorized and 
directed, through or with the aid of any 
public officers, Federal agencies, or other 
government instrumentalities that he 
may designate, to take possession and 
assume control of the said transportation

real and personal property and other

the operation of such transportation sys­

or to arrange for the operation of the 
said transportation system, plants and 
facilities in such a manner as he may 
deem necessary for the successful prose­
cution of the war,; and do anything that 
he may deem necessary to carry out the 
provisions and purposes of this order.
