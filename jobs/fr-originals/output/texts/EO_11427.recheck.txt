Executive Order 11427
TERMINATING THE MARITIME ADVISORY COMMITTEE
By virtue of the authority vested in me as President of the United
States, the Maritime Advisory Committee established by Executive
Order No. 11156 of June 17,1964, is hereby terminated and that order
is revoked.
The W hite House,
September J, 1968.
