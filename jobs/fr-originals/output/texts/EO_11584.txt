Presidential Documents
Title 3—The President
EXECUTIVE ORDER 11584
Inspection of Income, Excess-Profits, Estate, and Gift Tax Returns by  
the Senate Committee on Government Operations
By virtue of the authority vested in me by section 55(a) of the 
Internal Revenue Code of 1939, as amended (26 U.S.C. (1952 Ed.) 
55(a) ), and by section 6103(a) of the Internal Revenue Code of 1954 
(26 U.S.C. 6103 (a)),  it is hereby ordered that any income, excess- 
profits, estate, or gift tax return for the yearsl950 to 1971, inclusive, 
shall, during the Ninety-second Congress, be open to inspection by the 
Senate Committee on Government Operations or any duly authorized 
subcommittee thereof, in connection with its studies of the operation of 
Government activities at all levels with a view to determining the 
economy and efficency of the Government. Such inspection shall be 
in accordance and upon compliance with the rules and regulations 
prescribed by the Secretary of the Treasury in Treasury Decisions 6132 
and 6133, relating to the inspection of returns by committees of the 
Congress, and approved by the President on May 3, 1955.
T h e W h it e H o u s e,
March 3, 1971.
