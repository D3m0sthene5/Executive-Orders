WHEREAS section 2470 (a) (2) of the 
Internal Revenue Code, as amended, pro­

“Additional rate on coconut oil.  There  
shall be imposed (in addition to the tax im­
posed by the preceding paragraph) a tax of  
2 cents per pound, to be paid by the proc­

coconut oil' or of any combination or mix­

coconut oil with respect to tfrhich oil there

ing, except that the tax imposed by this  
sentence shall not apply when it is estab­

scribed by the Commissioner with the ap­
proval of the Secretary, that such coconut  
oil (whether or not contained in such a com­

duction of the Philippine Islands or any

produced wholly from materials the growth  
or production of the Philippine Islands or  
any possession of the United States * *
WHEREAS section 505 (b) of the Phil­
ippine Trade Act of 1946 (Public Law 371, 
79th Congress) provides as follows:
" Suspension of Section 2470  (o) (2) of 
Internal Revenue Code.  Whenever the Pres­
ident, after consultation with the President  
of the Philippines, finds that adequate sup­
plies of neither copra nor coconut oil, the-  
productT5f the Philippines, are readily avail­
able for processing in the United States, he*

bo readily available”;
AND WHEREAS I have consulted with 
the President of the Philippines concern- 
ing the supplies of copra and coconut oil, 
the product of the Philippines, which are 
available for processing in the United 
States:
