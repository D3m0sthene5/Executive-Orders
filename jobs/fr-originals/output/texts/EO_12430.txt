Flue-Cured Tobacco; 1982-83 Average  
Market Price and 1983-84 Penalty Rate

Section 314 of the Agricultural 
Adjustment Act of 1938 provides that 
marketing quota penalties shall be 
assessed whenever a kind of tobacco is 
marketed in excess of the marketing 
quota for the farm on which such 
tobacco is produced. The rate of penalty 
per pound of a kind of tobacco as 
prescribed by section 314 of the 1938 Act 
is 75 percent of the previous year’s 
average market price for such tobacco.
Since the 1982-83 average market 
price producers received for flue-cured 
tobacco and the rate of penalty reflect 
only mathematical computations which 
are required to be made in accordance 
with a statutory formula, it has been 
determined that no further public 
rulemaking is required. Accordingly, this 
final rule shall become effective upon 
date of publication in the Federal 
Register.

Lemons Grown in California and  
Arizona; Limitation of Handling
a g e n c y: Agricultural Marketing Service, 
USDA.
a c t i o n: Final rule.
s u m m a r y: This regulation establishes 
the quantity of fresh Califomia-Arizona 
lemons that may be shipped to market 
during the period July 10-16,1983. Such 
action is needed to provide for orderly 
marketing of fresh lemons for the period 
due to the marketing situation 
confronting the lemon industry.
