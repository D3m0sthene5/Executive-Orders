By virtue of the authority vested in 
me by the Constitution and statutes of 
the United States, it is hereby ordered 
as follows:
1. The name of the Economic Defense 
Board, established by Executive Order 
No. 88391  of July 30, 1941, is changed to 
the Board of Economic Warfare.

30,1941, No. 89002  of September 15,1941, 
No. 8926 s  of October 28, 1941, and No. 
89424 of November 19, 1941 are amended 
accordingly.
