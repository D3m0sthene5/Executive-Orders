By virtue of the authority vested in  
me by sections 55 (a ), 508, 603, 729 (a ), 
and 1204 of the Internal Revenue Code of 
1 9 3 9  (53 Stat. 29, 111, 171; 54 Stat. 989, 
1008; 55 Stat. 722; 26 U. S. C. 55 (a ), 508,

come, excess-profits, declared-value ex­

Senate Committee on Government Oper­
ations, or any duly authorized subcom­

studies of the operation of Government  
activities at all levels with a view to de­
termining the economy and efficiency of 
the Government, such inspection to be in 
accordance and upon compliance with  
the rules and regulations prescribed by  
the Secretary of the Treasury in Treas­
ury Decisions 6132 and 6133, relating to 
the inspection of returns by committees  
of the Congress, approved by me on May 
3,1955. —^

filing for publication in the Federal
