Title 3— Executive Order 12627 of February 29, 1988
Amending the President’s Commission on PrivatizationThe President
By virtue of the authority vested in me as President by the Constitution and  
laws of the United States of America, and in accordance with the Federal  
Advisory Committee Act, as amended (5 U.S.C. App. I), it is hereby ordered  
that section 2(d) of Executive Order No. 12607 of September 2, 1987, entitled  
“President’s Commission on Privatization” is amended by deleting “March 1,  
1988” and inserting in lieu thereof “March 31,1988.”
THE WHITE HOUSE,
