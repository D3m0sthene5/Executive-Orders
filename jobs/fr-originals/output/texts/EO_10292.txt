fication.  (a) The Universal Military

provides that every male citizen of the

that persons who on June 19,1951, were,

cally provided; others are authorized to

and Service Act, as amended, in which

free society the obligations and privi­
leges of serving in the armed forces and  
the reserve components thereof should  
be shared generally, in accordance with  
a system of selection which is fair and  
just, and which is consistent with the  
maintenance of an effective national  
economy.”
