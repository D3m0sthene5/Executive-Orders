Exemption erf Kenneth H . Tuggle From Mandatory Retirement
Kenneth H. Tuggle, Commissioner of the Interstate Commerce Com­
mission, will, during June, 1974, become subject to mandatory retirement 
for age under the provisions of section 8335 of title 5 of the United States 
Code unless exempted therefrom by Executive order.
In my judgment, the public interest requires that Commissioner Tuggle 
be exempted from such mandatory retirement.
NOW , THEREFORE, by virtue of the authority vested in me by 
subsection (c) of section 8335 of title 5 of the United States Code, I 
hereby exempt Kenneth H. Tuggle from mandatory retirement until 
December 31, 1975.
T h e W h it e H o u s e,
June 25, 1974.
