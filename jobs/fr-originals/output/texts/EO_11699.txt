THE PRESIDENT 2323
EXECUTIVE ORDER 11699
Amending Executive Order No. 11248, Placing Certain Positions in 
Levels IV  and V  of the Federal Executive Salary Schedule
By virtue of the authority vested in me by section 5317 of title 5 
of the United States Code, as amended, section 1  of Executive Order No. 
11248 1  of October 10, 1965, as amended, placing certain positions in 
level IV  of the Federal Executive Salary Schedule, is further amended 
by deleting “ (1) Special Assistant to the Secretary (Congressional 
Relations), Treasury Department.” , and inserting in lieu thereof the 
following:
(1) Assistant to the Secretary for Legislative Affairs, Department of 
the Treasury.
T h e W h it e H o u s e,
January 22, 1973.
