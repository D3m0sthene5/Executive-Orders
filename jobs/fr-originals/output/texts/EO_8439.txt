By virtue of and pursuant to the 
authority vested in me by section 1752 
of the Revised Statutes of the United
States (U.S.C., title 22, sec. 132), it is 
ordered that Chapter IX of the Foreign 
Service Regulations of the United States 
be, and it is hereby, amended as follows:
1. The subheading “Federal Security 
Agency” is placed before section IX-8 in 
lieu of “Treasury”, and the subheading 
“Treasury” is placed before section 3X-9.
2. The words “by the Secretary of the 
Treasury” are deleted from section IX-8.
