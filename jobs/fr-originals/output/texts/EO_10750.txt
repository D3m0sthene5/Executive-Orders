§  221.13 Payment.  The charges as  
fixed in §  221.12 shall become due on  
April 1  of each year, and are payable on  
or before* that date. To all charges  
assessed against lands in non-Indian  
ownership and Indian lands under lease  
to non-Indian lessees which are not paid  
on or before July 1  of each year, there  
shall be added a penalty of one-half of  
1  percent per month or fraction thereof  
from the due date, April 1, so long as  
the delinquency continues. No water  
shall be delivered until such charges  
have been paid; except that Indian  
water users who are financially unable  
to pay the assessment on the due date  
may be furnished water, provided the  
Superintendent of the reservation certi­
fies to the Project Engineer that such  
Indian is not financially able to pay the  
assessment, or has made satisfactory ar­
rangement to pay the assessments from  
proceeds of crops or from other sources. 
Penalty interest charges shall not be  
assessed against lands owned by an In­
dian water user, nor against Indian lands 
under lease to an Indian lessee.
