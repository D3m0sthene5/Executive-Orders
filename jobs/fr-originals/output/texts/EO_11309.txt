Title 3— THE PRESIDENT
Executive Order 11309
AMENDING EXECUTIVE ORDER NO. 11215, RELATING TO THE  
PRESIDENT’S COMMISSION ON THE PATENT SYSTEM
By virtue of the authority vested in me as President of the United  
States, it is ordered that Section 4(a)  of Executive Order No. 112151  
of April 8, 1965, entitled “Establishing the President’ s Commission  
on the Patent System”, is hereby amended by deleting “18 months”  
and inserting in lieu thereof “20 months”.

October 6, 1966.
