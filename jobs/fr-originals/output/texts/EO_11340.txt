Title 3— THE PRESIDENT
EFFECTIVE DATE OF DEPARTMENT OF TRANSPORTATION ACT
By virtue of the authority vested in me as President of the United 
States by Section 15 of the Department of Transportation Act (Public 
Law 89-670, approved October 15,1966; 80 Stat. 950) April 1,1967, is 
hereby prescribed as the date on which the Department of Transporta­
tion Act shall take effect.
