Executive Order 14432 of September 29, 2026 
Streamlining Access to Government Services Through Amer-
ica.gov 
By the authority vested in me as President by the Constitution and the 
laws of the United States of America, it is hereby ordered: 
Section 1. Purpose. For generations, Americans seeking essential services 
from the Federal Government have been required to navigate a system orga-
nized around the structure of Government rather than the needs of the 
people. Americans must search across numerous executive departments and 
agency (agency) websites, interpret complicated Government terminology, 
frequently provide the same information, and repeatedly prove their identities 
to a Government that may already possess the information necessary to 
serve them. The American people deserve better. 
Advances in technology now make possible a fundamentally different rela-
tionship between the people and the Federal Government, defined by sim-
plicity, speed, accuracy, security, and respect for the individual. The same 
spirit of American innovation that can accelerate scientific discovery can 
also help Americans complete essential interactions with the Federal Govern-
ment. 
This order therefore directs the establishment of America.gov as the unified 
digital front door to the Federal Government for every individual in the 
United States seeking Federal information or services. America.gov will not 
be merely another website, search engine, or directory of Government links. 
It will be a secure, intelligent, and service-oriented point of entry through 
which an individual may sign in, communicate in plain language, receive 
accurate answers, and, where authorized and technically available, complete 
Government transactions without being required to navigate the websites 
of multiple agencies. America.gov will enable the Federal Government to 
do its own paperwork so that the American people may get on with their 
lives. 
Sec. 2. Policy. It is the policy of the United States to: 
(a) provide every individual in the United States seeking Federal informa-
tion or services with a single, secure, conversational point of entry through 
which the citizen may discover, access, and, where authorized, complete 
covered Federal transactions; 
(b) establish common standards of quality, simplicity, accessibility, inter-
operability, security, reliability, and design across participating Federal dig-
ital services and public-facing websites; 
(c) preserve each agency’s custody and control of its records, systems, 
statutory responsibilities, and adjudicatory authority and ensure that unifying 
access to Federal services does not create a centralized Federal system 
of records concerning the American people; 
(d) protect personal information through data minimization, secure authen-
tication, auditable authorization, and disclosure practices consistent with 
applicable law; 
(e) ensure that super intelligence used in connection with America.gov 
is accurate, reliable, and transparent; and 
(f) preserve existing lawful methods by which Americans may obtain 
Federal services, including in-person, telephone, mail, and agency-specific

Sec. 3. Definition. The term ‘‘covered services’’ means public-facing Federal 
services that serve more than 100,000 users in a 12-month period and 
can be accessed or applied for online. The term excludes tax filing services 
provided by the Internal Revenue Service and services provided by the 
Department of War and elements of the Intelligence Community. The Director 
of the Office of Management and Budget (OMB) may, by memorandum, 
add or exclude ‘‘covered services.’’ 
Sec. 4. Establishing America.gov. The Administrator of General Services, 
in coordination with the Administrator of the National Design Studio and 
the Director of OMB, shall: 
(a) establish and operate America.gov as the single point of entry for 
Americans to access covered services online; and 
(b) take such steps as are necessary to integrate Login.gov with America.gov, 
including using Login.gov as the authentication service for America.gov. 
Sec. 5. Implementing America.gov. (a) Within 90 days of the date of this 
order, the Director of OMB shall issue a memorandum to the heads of 
agencies on implementation of this order. 
(b) Heads of agencies shall: 
(i) identify covered services in their respective agencies; 
(ii) integrate the provisions of such services with America.gov in a secure 
and privacy-preserving manner, including by making the public Applica-
tion Programming Interfaces, dashboards, and digital forms that already 
support services accessible through the America.gov platform; 
(iii) complete integration with Login.gov consistent with the requirements 
of section 1523(b)(1)(D) of title 6, United States Code, and OMB Memo-
randum M–26–18; and 
(iv) provide, on an ongoing basis, the Administrator of General Services 
and the Director of OMB with historical and current usage and performance 
data for covered services. 
Sec. 6. General Provisions. (a) Nothing in this order shall be construed 
to impair or otherwise affect: 
(i) the authority granted by law to an executive department or agency, 
or the head thereof; or 
(ii) the functions of the Director of the Office of Management and Budget 
relating to budgetary, administrative, or legislative proposals. 
(b) This order shall be implemented consistent with applicable law and 
subject to the availability of appropriations. 
(c) This order is not intended to, and does not, create any right or benefit, 
substantive or procedural, enforceable at law or in equity by any party 
against the United States, its departments, agencies, or entities, its officers, 
employees, or agents, or any other person. 
(d) Nothing in this order shall be construed to require the disclosure 
of information that is prohibited by law, or to authorize any person other 
than the originating agency to access records maintained by that agency 
about an individual, except as provided by the Privacy Act of 1974 (5 
U.S.C. 552a) or other applicable law.
