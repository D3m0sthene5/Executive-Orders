as follows:

§ 236.1 Eligible pro&uoer.  Any per­
son, partnership, association, or corpo­
ration producing or harvesting hay and 
pasture seed in 1943, as landowner, land­
lord, tenant, or custom harvester, on a 
farm on which no deductions have been 
or will be computed under the 1943 
Agricultural Conservation Program for 
failure to meet 90 percent of the 1943
