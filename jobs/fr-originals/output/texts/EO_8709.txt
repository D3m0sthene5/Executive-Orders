EXECUTIVE ORDER
Changing the N ame op the W yoming 
National F orest to B ridger N ational 
F orest
WYOMING
By virtue of the authority vested in  
me by the act of June 4, 1897, 30 Stat.  
34, 36, and upon the recommendation  
of the Secretary of Agriculture, it is or­
dered that the name of the Wyoming  
National Forest, as presently defined by  
Executive Order No. 873 of July 1, 1908,  
Executive Order No. 3842 of May 14,1923,  
and Proclamation No. 1529 of July 10,
1919 (41 Stat. 1761), bf, and it is hereby,  
changed to Bridger National Forest, in  
honor of Jim Bridger, who was long asso­
ciated with the early exploration and  
development of the region in which this  
forest is located.
F ranklin D R oosevelt
