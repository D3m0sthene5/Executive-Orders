Title 3— The President
Executive Order 11978 •  M arch 18, 1977
Relating to Trade and Other Transactions Involving Southern Rhodesia
By virtue of the authority vested in me by Section 5 of the United Nations Par­
ticipation Act of 1945, as amended (22 U.S.C. 287c), and Section 301 of Title 3 of 
the United States Code, and as President of the United States o f  America, Section 4 
of Executive Order Nb. 11419 of July 29, 1968, is hereby amended by adding thereto 
the following subsection :
“ (c) The Secretary of the Treasury may exempt from the provisions of this 
Order, and Executive Order No. 11322, as amended, any shipment of chromium in 
any form which is in transit to the United States on March 18, 1977.” .
