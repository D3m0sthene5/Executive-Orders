The Honorable Tom C. Clark
As a mark of respect to the memory of the Honorable Tom C. Clark, former  
Associate Justice of the Supreme Court of the United States, it is hereby ordered,  
pursuant to the provisions of Section 4 of Proclamation 3044 of March 1, 1954, as  
amended, that until interment, the flag of the United States shall be flown at half-  
staff on all buildings, grounds and naval .vessels of the Federal Government in the  
District of Columbia and throughout the United States and its Territories and pos­
sessions. I also direct that the flag shall be flown at half-staff for the same length  
of time at all United States embassies, legations, consular offices, and other facilities  
abroad, including all military facilities and naval vessels and stations.
T h e W h it e H o u s e,
June 14, 1977.
