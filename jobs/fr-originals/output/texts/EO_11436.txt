Title 3— THE PRESIDENT
Executive Order 11436
SUSPENDING THE PROVISIONS OF SECTION 6372 OF TITLE 10 OF THE  
UNITED STATES CODE, WHICH RELATE TO THE RETIREMENT OR RE­
TENTION ON THE ACTIVE LIST OF CERTAIN REAR ADMIRALS IN THE  
NAVY
By virtue of the authority vested in me by section 6386 (c) of title 10 
of the United States Code, I hereby suspend the provisions of section 
6372 of title 10 of the United States Code.
