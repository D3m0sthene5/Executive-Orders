ujider authority o f the Act o f M arch 20, 1922 (42 Stat. 465),

W hoever begins such operations must, on demand, ex­
hibit to the Forest Officer in  charge satisfactory evidence

w ith the follow ing requirements:
1. Only so much o f the surface o f the land shall be used

right to so occupy, use, or disturb such land shall be recog­
nized unless the recorded owner o f the reserved m ineral 
rights, or his legally authorized representative, shall have 
applied fo r and received from  the Forest Supervisor a per­
m it authorizing such use or occupancy, fo r which perm it 
advance payment shall be made annually at the rate o f $6 
per acre or fraction thereof.
A ll buildings, camps, equipment, and other structures shall

pletion or abandonment o f the operation, which shall be 
construed as being the date when payment of the perm it 
charges fo r the land term inates. Otherwise such buildings,
