EXECUTIVE ORDER
Authorizing E xcepted A ppointments  to 
Meet P ublic E xigency
By virtue of and pursuant to the  
authority vested in me by the provisions  
of paragraph Eighth of subdivision SEC­
OND of Section 2 of the Civil Service Act  
(22 Stat. 403, 404), it is hereby ordered  
that, subject to appropriate noncompeti­
tive tests of fitness, the Civil Service Com-  
missicfn is authorized to permit an im­
mediate appointment without regard to  
the competitive requirements of the Civil  
Service Rules in any case in which it  
appears that a public exigency exists  
which is directly connected with the neu­
trality of the United States or the pre­
paredness program of the Federal Gov­
ernment. This authority may be used  
only under most unusual and compelling  
circumstances, and the person or persons  
so appointed will not thereby acquire a  
classified civil service status.
F ranklin  D R oosevelt

EXECUTIVE ORDER
Amending P aragraph 7, S ubdivision  I, 
S chedule A op the C ivil S ervice 
R ules
By virtue of and pursuant to the au­
thority vested in me by paragraph  
Eighth, subdivision SECOND, section 2  
of the Civil Service Act (22 Stat. 404),  
it is ordered that paragraph 7, Subdivi­
sion I, Schedule A of the civil service  
rules be, and it is hereby, amended to  
read as follows:
“7. Any person employed in a for­
eign country or in the Virgin Islands, or  
in Puerto Rico when public exigency  
warrants, or in any island possession of  
the United States in the Pacific ocean
(except the Hawaiian Islands), or  
United States citizens employed in a con­
fidential capacity in the Philippine Is­
lands, when in the opinion of the Civil  
Service Commission it is not practicable  
to treat the position as in the competi­
tive classified service;  but this para­
graph shall not apply to any person em­
ployed in Canada or Mexico in the serv­
ice of the Immigration and Naturaliza­
tion Service, Department of Labor, or to  
any person employed in any foreign  
country by the Bureau of Customs of  
the Treasury Department.”
F ranklin  D  Roosevelt

Pursuant to the authority vested in  
the Secretary of Agriculture under Sec­
