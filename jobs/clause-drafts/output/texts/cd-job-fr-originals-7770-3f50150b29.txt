Executive  O rder
establishing  hazen bay migratory waterfowl  refuge 
Alaska
By virtue of and pursuant to the authority vested in me 
as President of the United States, and by the act of June 25, 
1910, ch. 421, 36 Stat. 847, as amended by the act of August 
24,1912, ch. 369, 37 Stat. 497, and in order to effectuate fur­
ther the purposes of the Migratory Bird Conservation Act 
(45 Stat. 1222), it is ordered that Nunivakchak and Krigegak 
Islands, lying in Hazen Bay, north of Nelson Island near the 
north entrance to Baird Inlet, off the west coast of Alaska 
(the approximate geographic position of which islands is in 
latitude 60°51' N., and longitude 164°42' W., from Green­
wich) , be, and they are hereby, withdrawn from settlement, 
location, sale, or entry, and reserved and set apart, subject 
to valid existing rights, for the use of the Department of 
Agriculture as a refuge and breeding ground for migratory 
birds and other wildlife.
This refuge shall be known as the Hazen Bay Migratory 
Waterfowl Refuge.
F ranklin  D  R oosevelt
