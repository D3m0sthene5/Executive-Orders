EXECUTIVE ORDER 10514
R evo catio n of  E xecutive  O rder  N o.
2414 o f Ju n e 30, 1916
By virtue of and pursuant to the au­
thority vested in me by the act of March  
12,1914, 38 Stat. 305, and as President of 
the United States, and upon the recom­
mendation of the Secretary of the In­
terior, it is ordered that Executive Order  
No. 2414 of June 30, 1916, prescribing  
regulations for hospital service in con­
nection with the construction'and opera­
tion of the Government railway in  
Alaska, now the Alaska Railroad, be, and  
it is hereby, revoked.
This order shall become effective at  
the close of business on December 31,  
1953.
