By virtue of and pursuant to the au­
thority vested in me by the provisions of  
paragraph Eighth of subdivision SEC­
OND of section 2 of the Civil Service Act  
(22 Stat. 403, 404), it is hereby ordered  
that, subject to the establishment before

qualifications, the initial appointment to  
the position of Assistant to the Assistant  
Administrator in Charge of the Coopera­
tion and Inspection Branch of the Wage  
and Hour Division in the Department of  
Labor may be effected without compli­
ance with the competitive requirements  
of the Civil Service Rules: Provided,  
that the person so appointed shall not  
thereby acquire a competitive classified  
civil-service status.

and Standards Section.
