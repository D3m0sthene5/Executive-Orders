By virtue of the authority vested in me 
by section 2 of the Civil Service Act.(22 
Stat. 404) and section 1753 of the Re­
vised Statutes of the United States, it is 
hereby ordered as follows:
Any person who was appointed to a 
position as Fingerprint Classifier in the 
Federal Bureau of Investigation, De­
partment of Justice, between the dates 
of August 21, 1939, and June 3, 1941, and 
who was not eligible to acquire a com­
petitive classified civil-service status be­
cause his selection from the certificate 
of eiigibles issued by the Civil Service 
Commission was not in accordance with 
the provisions of Civil Service Rule VII, 
may now acquire a competitive classi­
fied status: Provided, (a) that immedi­
ately following the date of his entry on 
duty in the Fingerprint Classifier posi­
tion such person had at least one year 
of continuous satisfactory service; (b) 
that he voluntarily left the service with­
out prejudice; and (c) that he is a citi­
zen of the United States and is not dis­
qualified by any provision of section 3 
of Civil Service Rule V or any other pro­
vision of the Civil Service Rules, other 
than Rule VII, or any provision of the 
Civil Service Act or of any statute or 
Executive order.
