By virtue of the authority vested in me 
under the Emergency Relief Appropria­
tion Act of 1935 (49 Stat. 115, 118) and 
the act entitled “An Act to provide that 
funds allocated to Puerto Rico under 
thé Emergency Relief Appropriation Act 
of 1935 may be expended for permanent 
rehabilitation, and for other purposes”, 
approved February 11, 1936 (49 Stat. 
1135); I  hereby appoint Benjamin W. 
Thoron, Director, Division of Territories 
and Island Possessions, Department of 
the Interior, as Administrator of the 
Puerto Rico Reconstruction Administra­
tion, vice Guy J . Swope, resigned, to 
serve without additional compensation, 
and to exercise and discharge the func­
tions, duties, and authority conferred 
upon the Puerto Rico Reconstruction 
Administration and the Administrator 
by Executive Orders No. 7057 of May 28, 
1935, No. 7180 of September 6, 1935, as 
amended by No. 7554 of February 17, 
1937, and No. 7689 of August 12, 1937.
The said Executive orders are hereby 
amended accordingly.
