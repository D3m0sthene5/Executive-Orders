CANAL ZONE
By virtue of the authority vested in me  
by section 5 of title 2 of the Canal Zone  
Code, approved June 19, 1934, and as  
President of the United States, Executive 
Order No. 2987 of November 4,1918, plac­
ing certain land and water areas in  
Gatun Lake, Canal Zone, under the con­
trol of the Secretary of the Navy for use  
as a Naval Air Station, is hereby revoked  
and such land returned to the control and  
jurisdiction of the Governor of The  
Panama Canal.
