EXECUTIVE ORDER 10198
Exemption  op P aul A. W alker P rom 
Compulsory R etirement  for A ge
WHEREAS Paul A. Walker, a member 
of the Federal Communications Corn- 
mission, will, during the month of Janu­
ary 1951, become subject to compulsory 
retirement for age under the provisions 
of the Civil Service Retirement Act of 
May 29, 1930, as amended, unless ex­
empted therefrom by Executive order; 
and
WHEREAS, in my judgment, the pub­
lic interest requires that the said person 
be exempted from such compulsory re­
tirement as provided below:
NOW, THEREFORE, by virtue of the 
authority vested in me by section 204 
of the act of June 30, 1932, 47 Stat. 404 
(5 U. S. C. 715 a), I hereby exempt the 
said Paul A. Walker from compulsory 
retirement for age for an indefinite pe­
riod of time not extending beyond the 
expiration of his present term of office 
as a member of the Federal Communi­
cations Commission.
Harry S. T ruman
