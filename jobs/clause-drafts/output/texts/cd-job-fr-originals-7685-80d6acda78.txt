REVOCATION OF EXECUTIVE ORDER NO. 4 6 9 9  OF AUGUST 1, 1 9 2 7 , 
WITHDRAWING PUBLIC LANDS
California
By virtue of and pursuant to the authority vested in me 
by the act of June 25, 1910, ch. 421, 36 Stat. 847, as amended 
by the act of August 24, 1912, ch. 369, ¿7 Stat. 497, Execu­
tive Order No. 4699 of August 1, 1927, withdrawing public 
lands in California pending resurvey, is hereby revoked.
This order shall become effective upon the date of the 
official filing of the plat of the resurvey of the said lands.
