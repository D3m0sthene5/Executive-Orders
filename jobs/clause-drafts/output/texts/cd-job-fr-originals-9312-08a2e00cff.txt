Powers Act, 1941, approved December 18,

and as Commander in Chief of the Army 
and Navy and as President of the United 
States, it is hereby ordered as follows:
1. The Office of War Information will

Parts of the foreign propaganda program

authority, functions and duties of the

establishing the Office of Strategic Serv­
