There are transferred from the War

the site of any of the said facilities, in­

maintenance of such facilities, and (4) 
so much of the funds available or to be 
made available for the use of the War 
Department in the operation and main­
tenance of such facilities as the Direc­
tor of the Bureau of the Budget shall 
determine to relate to the said functions.
This order shall become effective on
