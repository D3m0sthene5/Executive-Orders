EXECUTIVE ORDER 10895
EXTENSION OF THE PROVISIONS  
FOR ROTATION OF CIVILIAN EM­
PLOYEES OF THE DEFENSE ESTAB­
LISHMENT ASSIGNED TO DUTY  
OUTSIDE THE UNITED STATES
By virtue of the authority vested in me 
by section 1586(f) of title 10 of the United 
States Code, and as President of the  
United States, and having determined  
that such action is necessary in the na­
tional interest, it is ordered as follows:  
Se c t io n 1. Assignment of an employee  
to duty in the State of Alaska or Hawaii  
under regulations prescribed pursuant to  
section 1586 of title 10 of the United  
States Code shall be held and considered, 
for the purposes of that section, to be an  
assignment to duty outside the United  
States.
Sec. 2. The Secretary of Defense shall  
from time to time, and at least annually,  
consider the need for continuing this  
order in effect, and he shall recommend  
the revocation thereof at such time as he 
may deem such action advisable.
