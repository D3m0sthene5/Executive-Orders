EXECUTIVE ORDER
R evoking the D esignation of P eskan, 
Montana, as a Customs  P ort of E ntry
By virtue of the authority vested in 
me by section 1 of the act of August 1, 
1914, 38 Stat. 609, 623 (U.S.C. title 19, 
sec. 2), it is ordered that the designa­
tion of Peskan, Montana, as a customs 
port of entry in Customs Collection Dis­
trict No. 33 (Montana and Idaho), be, 
and it is hereby, revoked.
This order shall become effective at 
the close of business on September 15, 
1941.
F ranklin D R oosevelt 
T he W hite  H ouse,
Sept.  3, 1941.
