Executive Order 11286
DESIGNATING THE DEPARTMENT OF COMMERCE AS THE DEPARTMENT  
THROUGH WHICH THE UNITED STATES SHALL PARTICIPATE IN THE  
INTER-AMERICAN CULTURAL AND TRADE CENTER IN DADE COUNTY,  
FLORIDA
By virtue of the authority vested in me by Section 301 of Title 3 
of the United States Code and in conformity with the first section of 
the Act of February 19, 1966 (80 Stat. 5; Public Law 89-355), I  
hereby designate the Department of Commerce as the Department 
through which the United States shall, under that Act, participate in 
the Inter-American Cultural and Trade Center (Interama) in Dade 
County, Florida. In  carrying out that function, the Department is 
authorized to exercise the authority vested in me by the first section 
of the Act of February 19,1966.
