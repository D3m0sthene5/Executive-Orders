(1) Income (including income of per­
sonal holding companies and unjust en­
richment income), excess-profits, capital 
stock, estate, and gift tax returns under 
the Internal Revenue Code and returns 
of employment tax on employers under 
Subchapter C of Chapter 9 of such Code.
(2) (a) Income, excess-profits, and
