EXECUTIVE ORDER
P lacing the G oethals M emorial C om­
mission  U nder Tim J urisdiction  and 
Control of the W ar D epartment
Under and by virtue of the authority 
vested in me by the act of August 24, 
1935, c. 636, 49 Stat. 743, the Goethals 
Memorial Commission, appointed by me 
under the authority of the said Act, is 
hereby placed under the jurisdiction and 
control of the War Department, its func­
tions to be performed under the direction 
and supervision of the Secretary of War.
This order shall become effective on 
July 1, 1939.
