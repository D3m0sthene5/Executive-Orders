Executive Order 12238 of September 12, 1980
Public International Organizations Entitled To Enjoy Privileges, 
Exemptions, and Immunities
By the authority vested in me by Section 1 of the International Organizations  
Immunities Act (59 Stat. 669; 22 U.S.C. 288), and having found that the United  
States participates in the International Maritime Satellite Organization pursu­
ant to Title V of the Communications Satellite Act of 1962, as amended (47  
U.S.C. 751 e t seq.), the International Maritime Satellite Organization is hereby  
designated as a public international organization entitled to enjoy the privi­
leges, exemptions, and immunities conferred by the International Organiza­
tions Immunities Act.
THE WHITE HOUSE,
