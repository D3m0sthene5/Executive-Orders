EXECUTIVE ORDER 11734
Revising the Membership of the President’s Committee on the National
Medal of Science
By virtue of the authority vested in me as President of the United 
States, subsection (c ) of Section 2 of Executive Order No. 11287 of 
June 28, 1966, as amended, is hereby amended by deleting “ (1) The 
President’s Science Adviser,”  and by inserting in lieu thereof “ (1) The 
Science Adviser.”
