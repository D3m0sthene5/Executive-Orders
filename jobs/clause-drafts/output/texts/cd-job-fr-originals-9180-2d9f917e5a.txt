EXECUTIVE ORDER 9180
Authorizing  the  S ecretary  o f th e I n ­
terior  to  E nter  I n t o C ontracts  for  
the  D isposal  o f Y ucca  G row ing o n 
the  P ublic  D om ain
By virtue of the authority vested in  
me as President of the United States,  
and in order to expedite the prosecu­
tion of the war effort, it is ordered as  
follows;
The Secretary of the Interior is hereby  
authorized to enter into contracts,  
through the Commissioner of the Gen­
eral Land Office, for the disposal of  
yucca growing on the public domain, un­
der such terms and conditions as he may  
deem proper whenever he finds that the  
materials or products to be made from  
such yucca are substitutes, in whole or  
in part, for any material which has been  
or hereafter may be designated as stra­
tegic or critical, or both, or is otherwise  
essential to the prosecution of the war,
F rank lin D  Roosevelt  
T h e W h it e H o u se,
June 5, 1942.
