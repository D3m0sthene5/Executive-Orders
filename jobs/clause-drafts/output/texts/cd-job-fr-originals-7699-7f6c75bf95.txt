ABOLISHING NOME, ALASKA, AS A CUSTOMS PORT OF ENTRY
By virtue of and pursuant to the authority vested in me by  
the act of August 1, 1914, 38 Stat. 609, 623 (U. S. C., title 19,  
sec. 2), it is ordered that Nome, Alaska, be, and it is hereby,  
abolished as a customs port of entry in Customs Collection  
District No. 31 (Alaska), effective thirty days from the date  
of this order.
