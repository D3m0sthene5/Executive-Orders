By virtue of the authority vested in me 
by the act of June 25,1910, c. 421, 36 Stat. 
847, Executive Order No. 6667 of April 5, 
1934, withdrawing public lands in Colo­
rado pending a resurvey, is hereby re­
voked.
This order shall become effective upon 
the date of the official filing of the plat of 
the resurvey of the lands involved.
