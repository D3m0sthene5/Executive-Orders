By virtue of and pursuant to the authority vested in me 
by section 91 of the act of April 30, 1900, 31 Stat. 141, 159, 
as amended by section 7 of the act of May 27, 1910, 36 Stat. 
443, 447, it is ordered that the following-described portion of 
Waikalua-Waho Beach Reserve, Waikalua-Waho, Kaneohe, 
Koolaupoko, Oahu, Territory of Hawaii, be, and it is hereby, 
reserved and set aside for the use of the Lighthouse Service, 
Department of Commerce:
Beginning at a galvanized iron spike driven in root of 
pine tree at the West corner of this parcel of land and on 
the South boundary of Land Court Application 726, the 
coordinates of said point of beginning referred to Govern­
ment Survey Trig. Station “Puu Pahu” being 3205.60 feet 
South and 2135.75 feet East, as shown on Government Sur­
vey Registered Map 2585, and running by azimuths measured 
clockwise from true South:
L 237°00' 91.00 feet along Land Court Application 726 
to a pipe;
2. Thence along seashore at highwater mark, the direct 
azimuth and distance being: 318°06' 40.60 feet to a pipe;
3. 57°00' 76.85 feet along government land to a pipe;
4. 120°00' 45.00 feet along reserve for 8-foot walk to 
the point of beginning.
The above-described tract contains an area of 3365 square 
feet, more or less.
