WHEREAS, in my judgment, the 
public interest requires that Arthur A . 
Quinn, Comptroller of Customs with 
headquarters at New York, New York, 
be exempted from compulsory retirement 
for age as provided below:
NOW, THEREFORE, by virtue of the 
authority vested in me by section 204 
of the Act of June 30, 1932, 47 Stat. 404 
(5 U. S. C. 715a), it is ordered that the 
said Arthur A. Quinn be, and he hereby 
is, exempted from compulsory retirement 
for age under the provisions of the Civil 
Service Retirement Act of May 29, 1930, 
as amended, for an indefinite period of 
time, not extending beyond June 30, 
1951.
