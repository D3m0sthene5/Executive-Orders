S ec. 12 (a) Commutation of expenses — 
general. In lieu of the payment of actual 
expenses of transportation, packing, 
crating, drayage, and unpacking of 
household goods and personal effects in

within the continental United States, re­
imbursement shall be made to the em­

Schedule A which is attached to and 
made a part of these regulations. The 
amount payable shall be the product of 
the applicable rate and the net weight of 
household goods and personal effects 
actually shipped by carrier for the em­
ployee (within the weight limitation pre­
scribed by section 16 hereof). Govern­
ment bills of lading shall not be used.
