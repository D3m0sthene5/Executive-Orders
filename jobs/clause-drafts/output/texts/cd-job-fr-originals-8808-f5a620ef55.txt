EXECUTIVE ORDER  
American  D efense  S ervice M edal
By virtue of the authority vested in me  
as President of the United States, and as  
Commander-in-Chief of the Army and  
Navy of the United States, it is hereby  
ordered that the American Defense Serv­
ice Medal, including suitable appurte­
nances, be established and that the said  
medal may be awarded, under such regu­
lations as the Secretary of War, the  
Secretary of the Navy, and the Secretary  
of the Treasury may prescribe, to per­
sonnel of the Army, Navy, Marine Corps,  
and Coast Guard of the United States  
serving during the limited emergency  
proclaimed by me on September 8, 1939 1 
to exist, or during the unlimited emer­
gency proclaimed by me on May 27,1941.*
F ranklin  D R oosevelt  
T he  W hite  H ouse ,
June 28, 1941.
