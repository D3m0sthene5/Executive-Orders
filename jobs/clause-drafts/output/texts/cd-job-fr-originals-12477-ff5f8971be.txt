Executive Order 12477 of May 23, 1984
Adjustments of Certain Rates of Pay
By the authority vested in me as President by the Constitution and laws of the 
United States of America, and in accordance with section 202 of the Omnibus 
Budget Reconciliation Act of 1983, it is hereby ordered as follows:
Section 1. Executive Order No. 12456 of December 30, 1983, is amended by 
replacing the schedules attached thereto with the corresponding new sched­
ules attached hereto.
Sec. 2. The adjustments of rates of pay made by section 1 of this Order are 
effective on the first day of the first applicable pay period beginning on or 
after January 1,1984.
