By virtue of the authority vested in me 
by section 1  of the Act of August 1, 1914, 
38 Stat. 609, 623 (U.S.C. title 19, sec. 2), 
it is ordered that the limits of the customs 
port of entry of Charleston, South Caro­
lina (the headquarters port of Customs 
Collection District Number 16), be, and 
they are hereby, extended to include all 
points in Charleston Harbor, and all 
points on the Ashley and Cooper Rivers 
and their tributaries within ten miles 
from the corporate limits of the city of 
Charleston.
This order shall become effective thirty 
days from the date hereof.
