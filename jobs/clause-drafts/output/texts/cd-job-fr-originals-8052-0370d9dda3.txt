EXECUTIVE ORDER
Designating  the H onorable A ngel R. de 
Jesus as Acting  Judge of the D istrict 
Court of the U nited  S tates for 
Puerto R ico
By virtue of and pursuant to the au­
thority vested in me by section 41 of the 
act entitled “An Act to provide a civil 
government for Porto Rico, and for 
other purposes” , approved March 2, 1917 
(39 Stat. 965, U. S. C., title 48, sec. 863), 
I  hereby designate and authorize the 
Honorable Angel R. de Jesus, Associate 
Justice of the Supreme Court of Puerto 
Rico, to perform and discharge the du­
ties of the Judge o f the District Court 
of the United States for Puerto Rico and 
to sign all necessary papers and records 
as Acting Judge o f the said Court, with­
out extra compensation, in the case of 
absence, illness, or other legal disability 
of the Judge thereof during the current 
calendar year.
Franklin  D  Roosevelt 
T he W hite  H ouse
February 23, 1939.
