By virtue of and pursuant to the au­
thority vested in me by paragraph 
Eighth, subdivision SECOND, section 2 
of the Civil Service Act (22 Stat. 403, 
404), it is ordered that paragraph 6, 
Subdivision VII, Schedule A of the Civil 
Service Rules be, and it is hereby, 
amended to read as follows:
“6. Temporary clerks, carriers, and 
laborers required for part-time or in­
termittent work in the Postal Service 
in connection with the holiday or sea­
sonal business from November 15, 1938, 
to January 15, 1939.”
