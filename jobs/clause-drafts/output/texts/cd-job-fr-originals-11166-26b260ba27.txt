Executive Order 11166
SETTING ASIDE FOR THE USE OF THE UNITED STATES CERTAIN PUBLIC  
LANDS AND OTHER PUBLIC PROPERTY LOCATED AT THE MAKUA  
MILITARY RESERVATION, HAWAII
B y virtue of the authority vested in me by section 5(d) of the Act 
of March 18,1959, providing for the admission of the State of Hawaii 
into the Union (73 Stat. 5), and as President of the United States, it 
is hereby ordered as follows—
1. All lands and other property hereinafter described, being lands 
and property which were ceded to the United States by the Republic 
of Hawaii under the joint resolution of annexation, approved Ju ly 7, 
1898 (30 Stat. 750), or which have been acquired in exchange for lands 
or properties so ceded, are hereby set aside for the use of the United 
States* in fee simple subject to valid existing rights—
