By virtue of the authority vested in 
me by section 1  of the act of August 1, 
1914, 38 Stat. 609, 623 (U. S. C., title 
19, sec. 2), Executive Order No. 83231  of

limits of Customs Collection District No. 
42 (Kentucky) to include the State of 
Tennessee; and extending the limits of 
Customs Collection District No. 45 (St. 
Louis) to include the State of Arkansas,
