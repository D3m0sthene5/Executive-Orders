of October 14,1947, as amended by Exec­

authority vested in me by section 1  of the

by the said act of March 3, 1913, and as 
President of the United States, I hereby 
further extend the provisions of the said 
Executive Order No. 9898 of October 14, 
1947, as amended by Executive Order No. 
9926 of January 17, 1948, to July 1, 1950.
This order shall become effective on July 1, 1949.
