By virtue of the authority vested in me 
by section 303 of the Foreign Service Act 
of 1946, approved August 13,1946 (Public 
Law 724, 79th Congress), and section 202 
of the Revised Statutes (5 U. S. C. 156), 
and as President of the United States, I 
hereby authorize and direct the Secre­
tary of State, in the interest of the in­
ternal management of the Government, 
to exercise the authority vested in the 
President by section 411 of the Foreign 
Service Act of 1946 to classify into four 
classes the positions which are to be oc­
cupied by chiefs of mission.
This order shall,become effective on 
November 13, 1946.
