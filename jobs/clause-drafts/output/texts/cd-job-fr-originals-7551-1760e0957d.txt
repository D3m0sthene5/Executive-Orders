AMENDMENT OF PARAGRAPH 1, SUBDIVISION VI, SCHEDULE A, 
CIVIL SERVICE RULES
By virtue of and pursuant to the authority vested in me 
by paragraph Eight, subdivision SECOND, section 2 of the 
Civil Service Act (22 Stat. 403, 404), it is ordered that para­
graph 1, Subdivision VI, Schedule A of the Civil Service 
Rules be, and it is hereby, amended by revoking subdivi­
sions (c), (d), and (e) thereof, so that the paragraph, as 
amended, shall read as follows:
“ 1. (a) The Director of the Bureau of Prisons and not 
more than three Assistant Directors.
“ (b) Members of the Board of Parole.”
