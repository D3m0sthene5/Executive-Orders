EXECUTIVE ORDER 10405
Exemption of T heodore M arks F rom 
Compulsory R etirement  for A ge
WHEREAS Theodore Marks, Associ­
ate Chief of the Veterans Employment 
Service, Department of Labor, during 
the month of November 1952 will become 
subject to compulsory retirement for 
age under the provisions of the Civil 
Service Retirement Act of May 29, 1930, 
as amended, unless exempted therefrom 
by Executive order; and WHEREAS, in my judgment, the pub­
lic interest requires that the said person 
be exempted from such compulsory re­
tirement as provided below:
NOW, THEREFORE, by virtue of the 
authority vested in me by section 204 
of the act of June 30, 1932, 47 Stat. 404 
(5 U. S. C. 715a),J hereby exempt the 
said Theodore Marks from compulsory 
retirement for age for an indefinite pe­
riod of time.
H arry S. T ruman
The W hite H ouse,
November 6, 1952.
