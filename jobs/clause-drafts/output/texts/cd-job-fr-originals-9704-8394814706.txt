EXECUTIVE ORDER 9704
Functions of the A merican B attle 
Monuments  C ommission
By virtue of the authority vested in 
me by the Constitution and Statutes, 
including Title I of the First War Powers 
Act, 1941, and as President of the United 
States and Commander in Chief of the 
Army and the Navy, it is hereby or­
dered that the American Battle Monu­
ments Commission shall control as to 
materials and design, and provide regu­
lations for and supervise the erection 
of, all memorial monuments and build­
ings in American cemeteries located 
outside of the United States and its 
territories and possessions, and shall 
also, when authorized by any foreign 
country or political subdivision thereof 
in which the American armed forces 
have served, control as to materials and 
design, and provide regulations for the 
erection of, all memorial monuments 
and buildings located in, and commemo­
rating the services of the American 
armed forces in, any such foreign coun­
try or political subdivision.
Harry S. T ruman
The W hite  H ouse,
March 14, 1946.
