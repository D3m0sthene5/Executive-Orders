Under and by virtue of the authority  
vested in me as President of the United  
States, and in order to remove any  
doubts which might otherwise exist and  
to insure the effective utilization of all  
existing productive facilities, it is hereby  
ordered that Executive Order No. 325A  
of May 18, 1905, be, and the same is  
hereby, suspended for the period of the  
war and for six months thereafter to the  
extent necessary to permit officers and  
agencies of the Federal Government  
charged with the purchase or procure­
ment of articles necessary in the conduct  
of the war to procure, directly or indi­
rectly, through any contractor or sub­
contractor or otherwise, articles of any  
kind produced in any Federal, State or  
territorial prison, provided such articles  
are not produced pursuant to any con­
tract or other arrangement under which  
prison labor is hired out to, or employed  
or used by, any private person, firm or  
corporation.
