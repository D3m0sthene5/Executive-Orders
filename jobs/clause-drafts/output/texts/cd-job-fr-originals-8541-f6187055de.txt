EXECUTIVE ORDER
D esignation  op  the  A ssistant  S ecretary  
of C ommerce  and  th e S olicito r of 
Commerce  to A ct as S ecretary  of 
C ommerce
By virtue of the authority vested in me 
by section 179 of the Revised Statutes of 
the United States (U.S.C., title 5, sec. 6), 
I hereby authorize and direct the Assist­
ant Secretary of Commerce to perform 
the duties of the Secretary of Commerce 
in case of the absence, sickness, resigna­
tion, or death of the Secretary of Com­
merce and of the. Under Secretary of 
Commerce; and I  hereby further author­
ize and direct the Solicitor of Commerce 
to perform the duties of the Secretary of 
Commerce in case of the absence, sick­
ness, resignation, or death of the Secre­
tary of Commerce, the Under Secretary 
of Commerce, and the Assistant Secre­
tary of Commerce.
This order supersedes Executive Order 
No. 7900 of May 28,1938, entitled “Desig­
nating John Monroe Johnson and Rich­
ard C. Patterson, Jr., to Act as Secretary 
of Commerce.”
