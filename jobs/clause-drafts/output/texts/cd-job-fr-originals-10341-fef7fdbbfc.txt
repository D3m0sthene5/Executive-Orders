By virtue of the authority vested in me  
by section 2152 of title 18 of the United  
States Code and section 4 of the Air  
Commerce Act of 1926 (44 Stat. 570; 49

space reservations, established by Execu­

of April 2,1941, are hereby discontinued:
1. Rose Island Naval Defensive Sea  
Area.
2. Rose Island Naval Airspace Reser­
vation.
3. Tutuila Island Naval Defensive Sea  
Area.
4. Tutuila Island Naval Airspace Res­
ervation.
