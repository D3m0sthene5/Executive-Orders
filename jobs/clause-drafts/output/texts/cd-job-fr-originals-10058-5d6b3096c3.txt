Hawaii, were set aside for the use of the  
United States Navy Department for mili­
tary purposes by Executive Order No. 361,

the Territory of Hawaii;

the act of April 30, 1900, 31 Stat. 159, as  
amended by section 7 of the act of May  
27, 1910, 36 Stat. 447, it is ordered as  
follows:«

Keaukaha, Waiakea, South Hilo, County
