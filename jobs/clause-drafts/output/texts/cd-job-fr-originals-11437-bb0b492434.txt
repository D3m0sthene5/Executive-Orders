Executive Order 11437
SUSPENDING THE PROVISIONS OF SECTION 57511b) OF TITLE 10 OF THE  
UNITED STATES CODE, RELATING TO SERVICE-IN-GRADE REQUIRE­
MENTS FOR PROMOTION OF OFFICERS OF THE NAVY AND MARINE  
CORPS
By virtue of the authority vested in me by section 5785(b) of title 
10 of the United States Code, I hereby suspend the provisions of sec­
tion 5751(b) of title 10 of the United States Code.
Executive Order No. 11232 of July 8, 1965, Executive Order No. 
11352 of May 22,1967, and Executive Order No. 11361 of July 6,1967, 
are hereby revoked.
T h e W h i t e H o u s e,
December 2 , 1968.
