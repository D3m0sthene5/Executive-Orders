Executive Order 11873 July 21,1975
Amending Executive Order No. 11868,1  on the President’s Commission
on Olympic Sports
By virtue of the authority vested in me as President of the United  
States, Section 2 of Executive Order No. 11868 of June 19, 1975, is  
hereby amended to read as follows:
“ Se c. 2. The Commission shall consist of a Chairman and thirteen  
other members, who shall be appointed by the President. In addition,  
the Speaker of the House of Representatives and the President Pro  
Tempore of the Senate are requested to appoint four members of the  
House of Representatives and four members of the Senate, respectively,  
to serve as members of the Commission.”
T h e W h it e H o u s e,
July 21, 1975.
