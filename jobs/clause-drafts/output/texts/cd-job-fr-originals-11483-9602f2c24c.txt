Executive Order 11483
INSPECTION OF INCOME TAX RETURNS BY THE SELECT COMMITTEE  
ON CRIME, HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES
By virtue of the authority vested in me by section 6103(a) of the  
Internal Revenue Code of 1954, as amended (26 U.S.C. 6103(a)), it  
is hereby ordered that any income tax return for the years 1960 to 1970,  
inclusive, shall, during the Ninety-first Congress, be open to inspection  
by the Select Committee on Crime, House of Representatives, or any  
duly authorized subcommittee thereof, in cbnnection with its study  
and investigation of all aspects of crime in the United States, pursuant  
to House Resolution 17, 91st Congress, agreed to May 1?  1969. Such  
inspection shall be in accordance and upon compliance with the rules  
and regulations prescribed by the Secretary of the Treasury in Treas­
ury Decision 6132, relating to the inspection of returns by committees  
of the Congress, approved by the President on May 3,1955.
