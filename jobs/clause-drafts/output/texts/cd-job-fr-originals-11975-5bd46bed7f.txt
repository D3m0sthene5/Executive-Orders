Executive Order 11975 March 7, 1977
Abolishing the President’s Economic Policy Board, and for Other Purposes
By virtue of the authority vested in me by the Constitution and statutes of the  
United States of America, including the' International Economic Policy Act of 1972, 
as amended (22 U.S.C. 2841 et seq.),  and the Council on Wage and Price Stability  
Act (88 Stat. 750, 12 U.S.C. 1904 note), and as President of the United States of  
America, in order to permit a new designation of the Chairman of the Council on  
Wage and Price Stability and to abolish the Economic Policy Board, it is hereby  
ordered as follows:
Section 1. Executive Order No. 11808, as amended, is hereby revoked.
Sec. 2. The Secretary of the Treasury shall continue as Chairman of the Council  
on International Economic Policy (22 U.S.C. 2844 and 2848).
