By virtue of the authority vested in me  
by section 210 (b) of the Public Health  
Service Act (58 Stat. 687) as amended by  
Public Law 425, 80th Congress, approved  
February 28,1948, it is hereby ordered as  
follows:
Commissioned officers of the Regular  
Corps of the Public Health Service in the  
nurse, dietitian, and therapist categories  
shall be permanently promoted to the  
full and senior grades only if vacancies  
exist in such grades, and commissioned  
officers of the Regular Corps of the Pub­
lic Health Service in the pharmacist and  
sanitarian categories shall be perma­
nently promoted to the senior grade only  
if vacancies exist in such grade.
