By virtue of the authority vested in

of the United States, it is hereby ordered  
that Executive Order No. 90171  of Janu­
ary 12, 1942, entitled “Establishment of  
the National War Labor Board”, be, and  
it is hereby, amended so as to provide  
for the appointmènt of associate mem­

Such associate members shall be au­

Board.

of service in like manner as regular mem­
