Executive Order 12031 December 21, 1977
Exemption of Jerome K. Kuykendall from Mandatory Retirement
Jerome K. Kuykendall, Chairman, Indian Claims Commission, will become sub­
ject to mandatory retirement for age on December 31, 1977, under the provisions of  
Section 8335 of Title 5 of the United States Code unless exempted by Executive order.
In my judgment, the public interest requires that Jerome K. Kuykendall be  
exempted from such mandatory retirement.
NOW, THEREFORE, by virtue of the authority vested in me by subsection (c)  
of Section 8335 of Title 5 of the United States Code, I hereby exempt Jerome K.  
Kuykendall from mandatory retirement until September 30, 1978.
T h e W h i t e H o u s e,
December 21, 1977.
