EXECUTIVE ORDER 
Control op C ivil  A viation
By virtue of the authority vested in me 
by section 1  of the act of August 29, 1916, 
3 9  Stat. 645 (U.S.C., title 10, sec. 1361), 
and as President of the United States, 
it is hereby ordered as follows:
1 . In the administration of the statutes 
relating to civil aviation the Secretary 
of Commerce is directed to exercise his 
control and jurisdiction over civil avia­
tion in accordance with requirements for 
the successful prosecution of the war, as 
may be requested by the Secretary of 
War.
2 . The Secretary of War is authorized 
and directed to take possession and as­
sume control of any civil aviation system., 
or systems, or any part thereof, to the 
extent necessary for the successful prose­
cution of the war.
