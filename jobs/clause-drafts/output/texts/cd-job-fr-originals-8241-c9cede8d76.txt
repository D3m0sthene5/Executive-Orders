WASHINGTON
By virtue of the authority vested in 
me by the act of June 25, 1910, ch. 421, 
36 Stat. 847, Executive Order No. 6774 
of June 30, 1934, withdrawing public 
lands in Washington pending a resurvey, 
is hereby revoked;
This order shall become effective upon 
the date of the official filing of the plat 
of the resurvey of the lands involved.
