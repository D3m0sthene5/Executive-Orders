EXECUTIVE ORDER
AM ENDING SUBDIVISION IV OF SCHEDULE A 
OF THE CIVIL SERVICE RULES
By virtue of the authority vested in 
me by paragraph Eighth, Subdivision 
SECOND, section 2 of the Civil Service 
Act (22 Stat. 404), it is ordered that Sub­
division IV of Schedule A of the Civil 
Service Rules be, and it is hereby, 
amended by adding thereto the following 
paragraph, numbered 13:
13. During the emergency declared by 
the President to exist on May 27, 1941,1  
all positions in the War Department on 
the Isthmus of Panama.
