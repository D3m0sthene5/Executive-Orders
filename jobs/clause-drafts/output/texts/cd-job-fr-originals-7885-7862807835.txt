PLACING CERTAIN LAND UNDER THE CONTROL AND JURISDICTION OP 
THE SECRETARY OF THE TREASURY
Alaska
By virtue of the authority vested in me as President of the 
United States it is ordered that Block 27, in the Townsite of 
Anchorage, Alaska, be, and it is hereby, placed under the con­
trol and jurisdiction of the Secretary of the Treasury for use 
as a site for Federal buildings.
