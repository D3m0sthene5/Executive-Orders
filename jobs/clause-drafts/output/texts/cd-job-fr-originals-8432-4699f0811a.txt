By virtue of the authority vested in me 
by the act of June 25, 1910, c. 421, 36 
Stat. 847, Executive Order No. 5208 of 
October 12, 1929, withdrawing public 
lands in Nevada pending a resurvey, and 
heretofore partially revoked, is hereby 
revoked as to the remainder of the lands 
affected thereby.
This order shall become effective upon 
the date of the official filing of the plat 
of the resurvey of the lands involved.
