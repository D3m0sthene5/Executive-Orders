AMENDING PARAGRAPH 5, SUBDIVISION XI, SCHEDULE A OF THE  
CIVIL SERVICE RULES
By virtue of and pursuant to the authority vested in me by 
section 6 of the Civil Service Act f22 Stat. 403, 406), it is 
ordered that paragraph 5, Subdivision XI, Schedule A of the 
Civil Service Rules, be, and it is hereby, amended to read as 
follows:
“5. Seamen, deck-hand, fireman, and employees in the 
mess department on vessels of, in addition to lamplighters in, 
the Lighthouse Service.”
The present incumbents of the positions placed in the 
competitive classified civil service by this order may acquire a 
competitive classified status in accordance with the provisions 
of section 6 of Civil Service Rule n, as amended.
