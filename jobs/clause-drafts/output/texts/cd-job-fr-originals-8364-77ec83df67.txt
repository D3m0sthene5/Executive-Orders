EXECUTIVE ORDER
Revocation  of E xecutive  O rder N o. 5346 
of M ay 9, 1930, W ithdrawing  P ublic  
Lands
MINNESOTA
By virtue of the authority vested in me 
by the act of June 25, 1910, c. 421, 36 
Stat. 847, Executive Order No. 5346 of 
May 9, 1930, withdrawing public lands in 
Minnesota pending a resurvey, is hereby 
revoked.
This order shall become effective upon 
the date of the official filing of the plat 
of the resurvey of the lands involved.
Franklin  D R oosevelt 
The W hite H ouse,
March 4, 1940.
