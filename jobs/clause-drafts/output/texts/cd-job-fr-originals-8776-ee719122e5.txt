CALIFORNIA
By virtue of the authority vested in me 
by the act of June 25,1910, c. 421,36 Stat. 
847, as amended by the act of August 24, 
1912, c. 369, 37 Stat. 497, it is ordered as 
follows:
SECTION 1. Executive Order No. 6910 
of November 26, 1934, as amended, tem­
porarily withdrawing all public lands in 
California and certain other States for 
classification and other purposes, is 
hereby revoked as to the following- 
described tract of public land in Cali­
fornia:
Mount Diablo Meridian 
T. 9 N., R. 7 W., sec. 3, N E ^ S E ft, 40 acres.
SECTION 2. Subject to valid existing 
rights, the tract of land described in sec­
tion 1  of this order is hereby temporarily
withdrawn from settlement, location, 
sale, or entry, and reserved and set apart 
under the jurisdiction of the Department 
of the Interior for use as a radio relay 
station site for Federal and State cooper­
ative forest-protection work.
