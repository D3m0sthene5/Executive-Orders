EXECUTIVE ORDER 9344
Changing  the  Nam e of th e Al le y D w e l l­
ing  A uth o r ity to  N ational  C apital  
Housing  A u th o r ity
By virtue of the authority vested in me 
by the District of Columbia Alley Dwell­
ing Act, approved June 12,1934 (48 Stat. 
930), as amended, and as President of the 
United States, it is hereby ordered as fol­
lows:
1 . The name of the Alley Dwelling Au­
thority, designated by Executive Order 
No. 6868 of October 9, 1934, to carry out 
the provisions of the said District of 
Columbia Alley Dwelling Act, is changed 
to “National Capital Housing Authority.”
2. The said Executive Order No. 6868, 
as amended by Executive Orders No. 
7784-A of January 5,1938,1  and No. 8033 
of January 11,1939,2  is modified accord­
ingly.
3 . This order shall become effective on 
June 1 , 1943.
F r a n k lin D R oosevelt  
T he W hite  H ouse ,
May 21, 1943.
