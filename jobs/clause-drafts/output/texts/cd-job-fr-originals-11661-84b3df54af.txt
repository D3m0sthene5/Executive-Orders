EXECUTIVE ORDER 11661
Relating to Facilitating Coordination of Federal Education Programs
By virtue of die authority vested in me as the President of the United 
States, Executive Order No. 11260 of December 11, 1965, which 
amended Executive Order No. 11185, of October 16, 1964, relating to 
facilitating coordination of Federal education programs, is hereby 
revoked, and Executive Order No. 11185 shall, until otherwise provided, 
be deemed to be in effect as originally issued.
