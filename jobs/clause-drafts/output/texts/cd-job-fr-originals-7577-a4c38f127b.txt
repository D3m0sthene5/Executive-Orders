“ Thorough consideration was given by the Committee

to the acceptance of presents, decorations, orders, medals,

testim onials, or other things, when presented to or con­

sentatives of foreign governments. In  view of the atti­
tude of the Congress and the policy of the Government,  
Am erican diplom atic and consular officers are hereby

present, decoration, m edal, order, testim onial, or other 
thing th at m ay be tendered to them by any foreign king,
