AMENDING THE TARIFF OF UNITED STATES CONSULAR FEES
By virtue of and pursuant to the authority vested in me 
by section 1745 of the Revised Statutes, as amended (U. S. C., 
title 22, sec. 127), it is ordered that the Tariff of United 
States Consular Fees be, and it is hereby, amended as follows: 
Immediately following paragraph nine of Item 8 of the 
Tariff of United States Consular Fees, the following para­
graph is prescribed:
“The taking of an application for, and issuance of, a
travel certificate for use in China ---------------------$1.00”
