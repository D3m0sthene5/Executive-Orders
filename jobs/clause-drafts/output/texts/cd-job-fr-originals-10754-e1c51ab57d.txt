By virtue of the authority vested in me 
as President of the United States, it is 
ordered that the second and third sen­
tences of section 1 (a) of Executive Order 
No. 10655 of January 28, 1956 (21 F. R. 
665), be, and they are hereby, amended 
to read as follows:
"The aforementioned members of the 
Committee shall be designated by the 
respective heads of the member agencies. 
The President shall designate a repre­
sentative who shall be an additional and 
non-voting member of the Committee 
and shall also be Chairman of the Com­
mittee.”
