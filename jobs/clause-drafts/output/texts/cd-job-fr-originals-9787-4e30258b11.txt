Executive Order No. 7747 of November 
20,1937, as aménded by Executive Order 
No. 8536 of September 6, 1940, is hereby 
amended to read as follows;
“By virtue of and pursuant to the 
authority vested in me by the provisions 
of section 44 of the Criminal Code, as 
amended (18 U. S. C. 96), the area of 
water surrounding San Clemente Island, 
California, extending from low-water 
mark out for a distance of three hundred 
yards beyond low-water mark, except in 
Wilson Cove, where it is to extend one 
huhdred yards beyond low-water mark, 
and including that part of Pyramid Cove 
lying north of a line between a point 
one thousand yards south of China Point 
light and a point three hundred yards 
south of Whitewashed Rock, is hereby 
established as a defensive sea area for 
purposes of national defense, subject to 
the uses reserved for the Department of 
Commerce by Executive Order No. 6897 of 
November 7, 1934. The said area shall 
be known as the San Clemente Island 
Naval Defensive Sea Area.
“At no time shall vessels or other craft 
be navigated within the defensive sea 
area above defined except such as are 
authorized by the Secretary of the Navy.
“Any person violating the provisions of 
this order shall be subject to the penalties 
provided by law.”
