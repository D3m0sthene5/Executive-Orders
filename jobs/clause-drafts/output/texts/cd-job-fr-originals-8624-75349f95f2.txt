EXECUTIVE ORDER
M aking C ertain C hanges in  the  O r­
ganization  of C ustoms  C ollection  
District  No. 26 (A rizona)
By virtue of the authority vested in 
me by section 1 of the act of August 1, 
1914, 38 stat. 609, 623 (U.S.C., title 19, 
sec. 2), it is ordered that the following 
changes be, and they are hereby, made 
in the organization of Customs Collection 
District No. 26 (Arizona):
1. The designation of the town of Ajo, 
Arizona, as a customs port of entry is 
revoked.
2. The town of Sonoyta, Arizona, is 
designated as a customs port of entry.
This order shall become effective as of 
January 1, 1941.
