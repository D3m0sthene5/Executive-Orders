By virtue of and pursuant to the au­
thority vested in me by the act of March 
26, 1934, 48 Stat. 466 (U.S.C., title 5, 
sec. 118c), the list of basic rates of 
exchange established by section 4 of 
Executive Order No. 79721  of September 
15, 1938, as amended, prescribing regu­
lations for the payment of losses sus­
tained by officers, enlisted men, and 
employees of the United States in for­
eign countries on account of the appre­
ciation of foreign currencies in their 
relation to the American dollar, is 
hereby amended, effective January 1, 
1941, by the inclusion under the Nether­
lands possessions of Surinam, at the 
basic rate of 40.19 cents to the florin.
