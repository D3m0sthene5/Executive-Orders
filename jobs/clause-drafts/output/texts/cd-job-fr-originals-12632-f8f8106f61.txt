Executive Order 12632 of March 23, 1988
Exclusions From the Federal Labor-Management Relations 
Program
By virtue of the authority vested in me as President by the Constitution and 
law s of the United States of America, including Section 7103(b) of Title 5 of 
the United States Code, and in order to exempt certain agencies Or subdivi­
sions thereof from coverage of the Federal Labor-M anagement Relations 
Program, it is hereby ordered as follows:
Section 1 . Determinations.  The agencies or subdivisions thereof set forth in 
Section 3 of this Order are hereby determined to have as a primary function 
intelligence, counterintelligence, investigative, or national security work. It is 
also hereby determined that Chapter 71 of Title 5 of the United States Code 
cannot be applied to these agencies or subdivisions in a manner consistent 
with national security requirements and considerations. These agencies or 
subdivisions thereof are hereby excluded from coverage under Chapter 71 of 
Title 5 of the United States Code.
