ALASKA
By virtue of the authority vested in me 
by section 2380 of the Revised Statutes of 
the United States, it is ordered that the 
following-described public land in Alaska 
be, and it is hereby, withdrawn from set­
tlement, location, sale, or entry, and 
reserved for town-site purposes, to be 
hereafter disposed of under applicable 
town-site laws:

vey No. 1991 Unalaska Townsite, in lati­
tude 53°52' N., and longitude 166°32' W.;

No. 4, U. S. Survey No. 1991;

No. 4, U. S. Survey No. 1946;
