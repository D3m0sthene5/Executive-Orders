EXECUTIVE ORDER 10229
Exemption  of H ugh  H. B ennett  P rom 
Compulsory  R etirement  for A ge
WHEREAS Hugh H. Bennett, Chief of 
the Soil Conservation Service of the U. S. 
Department of Agriculture, will, during 
the month of April 1951 become subject 
to compulsory retirement for age under 
the provisions of the Civil Service Retire, 
ment Act of May 29, 1930, as amended, 
unless exempted therefrom by Executive 
order; and
WHEREAS, in my judgment, the pub­
lic interest requires that the said person 
be exempted from such compulsory re­
tirement as provided below:
NOW, THEREFORE, by virtue of and 
pursuant to the authority vested in me 
by section 204 of the act of June 30, 
1932, 47 Stat. 404 (5 U. S. C. 715a), I 
hereby exempt the said Hugh H. Bennett 
from compulsory retirement for age for 
a period of one year ending April 30, 
1952.
