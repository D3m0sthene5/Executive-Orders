By virtue of and pursuant to the au­
thority vested in me by section 11 (b) of 
the act of June 15, 1943 (Public Law 74, 
78th Congress), as amended by the act of 
March 4,1944 (Public Law 248,78th Con­
gress) , student nurses transferred to any 
Federal hospital in the continental 
United States, exclusive of Alaska, pursu­
ant to subsections (e) and (f) of section 
2 of the said act of June 15,1943, shall be 
paid a stipend at the monthly rate of 
sixty dollars for that period of training 
requisite to graduation: Provided, that 
the period of training in no case shall 
extend beyond the period required for 
graduation by the institution from which 
the student nurse was transferred, but 
may be terminated at any time prior 
thereto as the interests of the service 
may require.
