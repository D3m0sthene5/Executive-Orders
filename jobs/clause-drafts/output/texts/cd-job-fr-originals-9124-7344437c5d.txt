IDAHO AND UTAH
By virtue of the authority vested in  
me by the act of Congress approved June  
A* 1897, 30 Stat. 11, 36 (U.S.C., title 16,  
sec. 473), and upon the recommendation  
of the Secretary of Agriculture, it is or­
dered that "the following-described na­
tional-forest lands, in the States of  
Idaho and Utah, be and they are hereby,  
transferred from the Cache National  
Forest to the Caribou National Forest:
All lands of the Malad Division of the  
Cache National Forest, situated within  
townships 11, 12, 13, 14, 15 and 16 south,  
ranges 35, 36, 37 and 38 east of the Boise  
Meridian, Idaho, and within townships 14 
and 15 north, ranges 2 and 3 west of  
the Salt Lake Meridian, Utah, as fixed  
and defined by Proclamation No. 1397,  
dated October 9, 1917.
It is not intended by this order to give  
a national-forest status to any publicly-  
owned lands which have not heretofore  
had such status, or to remove any pub-  
licly-bwned lands from a national-forest  
status.
This order shall befcome effective July  
1, 1942.
