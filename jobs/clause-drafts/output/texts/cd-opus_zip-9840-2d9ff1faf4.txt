By virtue of the authority vested in
me by the Constitution and laws of the
United States and as President of the
United States and Commander in Chief
of the Army and Navy of the United
States, it is ordered that Executive Order
No. 9492 of October 24, 1944, as amended
by Executive Orders No. 9629 of Septem­
ber 25, 1945, No. 9714 of April 20, 1946,
and No. 9792 of October 23, 1946, pre­
scribing regulations governing non­
military and non-naval transportation
on Army and Navy air transports, be,
and it is hereby, further amended by
substituting the words “two years and
eight months” for the words “two and
one-half years.”
