By virtue of the authority vested in
me by section 1 of the act of August 1,
1914, 38 Stat. 609, 623 (U.S.C., title 19,
sec. 2), it is ordered that the following
changes be, and they are hereby, made
in the customs field organization:
1. Customs Collection District No. 48
(Utah and Nevada) is abolished.
2. The designation of Salt Lake City,
Utah, as a customs port of entry is
revoked.
3. The limits of Customs Collection
District No. 28 (San Francisco) are ex­
tended to include the States of Utah
and Nevada.
Tiiis order shall become effective
thirty days from the date hereof.
