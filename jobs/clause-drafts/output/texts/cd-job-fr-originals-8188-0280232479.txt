By virtue of and pursuant to the au­
thority vested in me by the act of June  
25, 1910, c. 421, 36 Stat. 847, Executive  
Order No. 4539 of November 6,1926, with­
drawing public lands in Utah pending a

voked, is hereby revoked as to the re­
mainder of the lands affected thereby.
This order shall become effective upon  
the date of the official filing of the plat
