EXECUTIVE ORDER
Designating  D el B onita , M ontana , as a 
Customs  P ort of E ntry
By virtue of and pursuant to the au­
thority vested in me by the act of August 
1, 1914, 38 Stat. 609, 623 (U. S. C., title 
19, sec. 2), it is ordered that Del Bonita, 
Montana, be, and it is hereby, designated 
as a' customs port of entry in Customs 
Collection District No. 33 (Montana and 
Idaho), with headquarters at Great Falls, 
Montana.
This order shall become effective thirty 
days from the date hereof.
Franklin  D R oosevelt
T he W hite  H ouse,
Aug. 9, 1938.
