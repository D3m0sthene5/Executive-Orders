REVOCATION OF EXECUTIVE ORDER n o. 5603 OF APRIL 20, 1931,  
WITHDRAWING PUBLIC LANDS
Wyoming
By virtue of and pursuant to the authority vested in me 
by the act of June 25, 1910, ch. 421, 36 Stat. 847, as amended 
by the act of August 24, 1912, ch. 369, 37 Stat. 497, Execu­
tive Order No. 5603 of April 20, 1931, withdrawing public 
lands in Wyoming, pending resurvey, is hereby revoked.
This order shall become effective upon the date of the 
official filing of the plats of the resurvey of the lands now 
subject to said Executive Order Nq. 5603.
Franklin  D R oosevelt
T he W hite  H ouse ,
July 9, 1937.
