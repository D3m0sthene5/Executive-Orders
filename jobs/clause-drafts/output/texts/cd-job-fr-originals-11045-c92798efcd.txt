Executive Order 11045
DISCONTINUING THE GUAM ISLAND NAVAL DEFENSIVE SEA AREA  
AND GUAM ISLAND NAVAL AIRSPACE RESERVATION
By virtue of the authority vested in me as President of the United  
States, it is ordered as follows:
S ection  1. The Guam Island Naval Defensive Sea Area and the  
Guam Island Naval Airspace Reservation, heretofore existing under  
the provisions of Executive Order No. 8683 of February 14, 1941, as  
amended, are hereby discontinued.
S ec. 2. To the extent not heretofore rendered inapplicable, the  
following are hereby revoked:
(1) Executive Order No. 8683 of February 14, 1941.
(2) Executive Order No. 8729 of April 2, 1941.
(3) Executive Order No. 10341 of April 8, 1952.
