Executive Order 11549
REVOKING EXECUTIVE ORDER NO. 10361 OF JUNE 11, 1952,  
ESTABLISHING THE WHITTIER DEFENSIVE SEA AREA, ALASKA
By virtue of the authority vested in me by Section 2152 of Title 18,  
United States Code, and as President of the United States, Executive  
Order No. 103611  of June 11,1952, establishing the Whittier Defensive  
Sea Area, Alaska, is hereby revoked.

July 28, 1970.
