EXECUTIVE ORDER 9104
Withdrawing Public Lands for Use of
the War Department as an Aerial Gun­
nery Range
ARIZONA
By virtue of the authority vested in me
by the act of July 9, 1918, c. 143, 40 Stat.
845, 848 (U.S.C., title 10, sec. 1341), it is
ordered that, subject to valid existing
rights and to power site classification No.
239, the public lands in the following-
described areas be, and they are hereby
withdrawn from all forms of appropria­
tion under the public-land laws, includ­
ing the mining laws, and reserved for
the use of the War Department as an
aerial gunnery range:
