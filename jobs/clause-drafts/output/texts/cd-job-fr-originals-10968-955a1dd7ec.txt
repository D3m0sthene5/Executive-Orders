Executive Order 10968
AMENDMENT OF EXECUTIVE ORDER  
NO. 10858,1  RELATING TO THE  
PRESIDENT’S COMMITTEE FOR  
TRAFFIC SAFETY
By virtue of the authority vested in me 
as President of the United States, it is 
ordered that Executive Order No. 10858, 
dated January 13, 1960, and headed 
“The President’ s Committee for Traffic

“(b> The President’ s Committee for

other fields as the President may from

serve as ex officio members of the Com­
