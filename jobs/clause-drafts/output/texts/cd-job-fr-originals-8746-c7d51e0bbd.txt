By virtue of the authority vested in me  
by section 5 (b) of the Act of October 6,  
1917 (40 Stat. 411), as amended, and by  
virtue of all other authority vested in me,  
I, FRANKLIN D. ROOSEVELT, PRESI­
DENT of the UNITED STATES OP  
AMERICA, do hereby amend Executive  
Order No. 83891  of April 10, 1940, as  
amended, so as to extend all the pro­
visions thereof to, and with respect to,  
property in which Greece or any national 
thereof has at any time on or since April  
28, 1941, had any interest of any nature  
whatsoever, direct or indirect; except  
that, in defining “Greece” and “national”  
of Greece, the date “April 28, 1941” shall  
be substituted for the dates appearing in  
the definitions of countries and nationals  
thereof.
