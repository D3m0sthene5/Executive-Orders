By virtue of the authority vested in 
me by the act of January 28, 1915, 38
Stat. 800 (U.S.C., title 14, sec. 1), the 
United States Coast Guard for the Dis­
trict of Honolulu, Territory of Hawaii, 
together with its organization, per­
sonnel, and equipment, is hereby trans­
ferred from the servioe and jurisdiction 
of the Secretary of the Treasury to the 
service and jurisdiction of the Secretary 
of the Navy, to operate as a part of the 
Navy and subject to the control thereof 
during the unlimited national emer­
gency proclaimed by me on May 27,1941.
