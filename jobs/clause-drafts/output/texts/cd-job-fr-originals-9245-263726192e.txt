By virtue of the authority vested in me 
by the Constitution and statutes of the 
United States, particularly the First War 
Powers Act, 1941 (Public Law 354—77th 
Congress), it is hereby ordered that the 
functions, powers, and duties of the 
United States High Commissioner to the 
Philippine Islands, together with the per­
sonnel, records, property, and funds of 
the office of the High Commissioner, be, 
and they are hereby, transferred to the 
Secretary of the Interior.
