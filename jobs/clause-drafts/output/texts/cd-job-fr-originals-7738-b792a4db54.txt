AMENDMENT OF PARAGRAPH 6, SUBDIVISION VH, SCHEDULE A, CIVIL  
SERVICE RULES
By virtue of and pursuant to the authority vested in me 
by paragraph Eighth, subdivision SECOND, section 2 of the 
Civil Service Act (22 Stat. 403, 404), it is ordered that para­
graph 6, Subdivision VII, Schedule A of the Civil Service 
Rules be, and it is hereby, amended to read as follows:
“6. Temporary clerks, carriers, and laborers required for 
part-time or intermittent work in the Postal Service in con­
nection with the holiday or seasonal business from November 
15, 1937, to January 15, 1938.”
