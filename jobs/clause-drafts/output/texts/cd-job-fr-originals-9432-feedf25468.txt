EXECUTIVE ORDER 9432
Designation  op U nder S ecretary and 
Assistant  S ecretaries  of the  I nterior  
to A ct as S ecretary  of the  I nterior
By virtue of and pursuant to the au­
thority vested in me by section 179 of the  
Revised Statutes of the United States  
(5 U.S.C., sec. 6), I hereby authorize and  
direct the Under Secretary of the In­
terior to perform the duties of the Secre­
tary of the Interior during the absence  
or sickness of the Secretary of the In­
terior; and I hereby further authorize  
and direct Assistant Secretary of the In­
terior Michael W. Straus or his succes­
sors, and in his absence or sickness  
Assistant Secretary of the Interior Oscar  
L. Chapman or his successors, to perform 
the duties of the Secretary of the In­
terior during the absence or sickness of  
both the Secretary and the Under Secre­
tary of the Interior.
This order supersedes Executive Order  
No. 7277 of January 17, 1936.
