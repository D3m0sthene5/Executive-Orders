By virtue of the authority vested in me 
by sections 55 (a ), 508, 603, 729 (a ), and

1008; 55 Stat. 722; 26 U. S. C. 55 (a ), 508,

Congress, be open to inspection by the

Congress, agreed to January 30; 1957, to

and regulations prescribed by the Secre­

Congress, approved by me on May 3, 
1955.
