EXECUTIVE ORDER
Revoking the  D esignation  of M olson , 
Washington , as a C ustoms  P ort of 
E ntry
By virtue of the authority vested in me  
by section 1 of the act of August 1, 1914,  
38 Stat. 609, 623 (U.S.C., title 19, sec. 2),  
it is ordered that the designation of  
Molson, Washington, as a customs port of  
entry in Customs Collection District No.  
30 (Washington), be, and it is hereby,  
revoked.
This order shall become effective at the  
close of business January 31,1941.
