Executive Order 11318
DESIGNATING THE EUROPEAN SPACE RESEARCH ORGANIZATION AS  
A PUBLIC INTERNATIONAL ORGANIZATION ENTITLED TO ENJOY  
CERTAIN PRIVILEGES, EXEMPTIONS, AND IMMUNITIES
B y virtue o f the authority vested in me by Sections 1 and 11 of the 
International Organizations Immunities Act (59 Stat. 669; 22 U.S.C. 
288), as amended by Public Law 89-353 (80 Stat. 5), I  hereby desig­
nate the European Space Research Organization (E S R O ) as a public 
international organization entitled to enjoy those privileges, exemp­
tions, and immunities provided for by the International Organizations 
Immunities Act which are described in paragraphs 6 and 7 of the 
Agreement between the United States and the European Space 
Research Organization effected by an Exchange of Notes at Paris, 
dated November 28, 1966, a copy of which paragraphs are annexed 
hereto and made a part of this Order.
