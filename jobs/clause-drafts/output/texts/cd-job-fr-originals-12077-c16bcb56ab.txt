Executive Order 12077 August 18,1978
Exemption of Roy T. Sessums From Mandatory Retirement
*
Roy T. Sessums, Member, Mississippi River Commission, will become 
subject to mandatory retirement for age on August 31, 1978, under the 
provisions of Section 8335 of Title 5 of the United States Code unless 
exempted by Executive order.
In my judgment, the public interest requires that Roy T. Sessums be 
exempted from such mandatory retirement.
NOW, THEREFORE, by the authority vested in me by subsection (c) of 
Section 8335 of Title 5 of the United States Code, I hereby exempt Roy T. 
Sessums from mandatory retirement until October 1, 1978.
T h e W h it e H o u s e,
August 18, 1978.
