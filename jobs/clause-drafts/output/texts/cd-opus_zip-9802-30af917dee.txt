By virtue of the authority vested in me
by section 124 (h) of the Internal Reve­
nue Code, Executive Order No. 9491 of
October 20, 1944, entitled “Regulations
Governing the Issuance of Payment Cer­
tificates under Section 124 (h) of the
Internal Revenue Code,” is amended by
adding the following at the end thereof:
“5. Terminal Date for Filing Applica­
tion. A payment certificate shall be
issued only If application therefor is filed
in conformity with the provisions of
these regulations on or before December
31,1946.”
