EXECUTIVE ORDER
Revoking  the  D esignation  of  L ancaster , 
M innesota , as a C usto m s P ort of

By virtue of the authority vested in me 
by section 1  of the act of August 1, 1914, 
38 Stat. 609, 623 (U.S.C., title 19, sec. 2), 
it is ordered that the designation of Lan­
caster, Minnesota, as a customs port of 
entry in Customs Collection District No. 
34 (Dakota), be, and it is hereby, revoked.
This order shall become effective at the 
close of business on the thirtieth day 
from the date hereof.
