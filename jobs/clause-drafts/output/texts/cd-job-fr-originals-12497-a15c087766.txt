Executive Order 12497 of December 29, 1984
President’s Advisory Committee on Mediation and Conciliation
By the authority vested in me as President by the Constitution and laws of the  
United States of Am erica, including the Federal Advisory Committee A ct, as  
amended (5 U.S.G. App. I), and in order to extend the life of the President’s  
Advisory Committee on Mediation and Conciliation, it is hereby ordered that  
Section 4(b) of Executive Order No. 12462 of February 17,1984, is amended to  
read: “The Committee shall terminate on September 30, 1985, unless sooner  
extended.”
