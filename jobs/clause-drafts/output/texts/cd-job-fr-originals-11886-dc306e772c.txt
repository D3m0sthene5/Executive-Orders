Executive Order 11886 October 17, 1975
Abolishing the Culebra Island Naval Defensive Sea Area Established  
by Executive Order No. 86841
By virtue of the authority vested in me by section 2152 of Title 18 of  
the United States Code, and as President of the United States of America,  
and as Commander-in-Chief of the Armed Forces, in order to abolish the  
Ciilebra Island Naval Defensive Sea Area, since the United States Navy  
ceased using targets on the island of Culebra, Puerto Rico on July 1,  
1975, and terminated all weapons training activities in the vicinity of  
Culebra on September 30, 1975, and since the Culebra Island Naval  
Airspace Reservation has, at the request of the United States Navy, been  
revoked by the Federal Aviation Administration (14 CFR Part 73.71;  
40 FR 45804), Executive Order No. 8684 of February 14, 1941, as  
amended, is hereby revoked.
T h e W h it e H o u se,
October 17, 1975.
