E xecutive  O rder
ENLARGING THE HART MOUNTAIN ANTELOPE REFUGE 
OREGON
By virtue of and pursuant to the authority vested in me 
as President of the United States it is hereby ordered as 
follows:
1. All private lands acquired or leased by the United States 
since the issuance of Executive Order No. 75231  of December 
21, 1936, establishing the Hart Mountain Antelope Refuge, 
within the area described in section 2 of that order are hereby 
included in and made a part of the said refuge.
2. All private lands within the said area hereafter acquired 
or leased by the United States shall be included in and become 
a part of the said refuge when so acquired or leased.
