Executive Order 11531
DELEGATING AUTHORITY OF THE PRESIDENT UNDER SECTION
4102(a)(2)(B) OF TITLE 5, UNITED STATES CODE, TO DESIGNATE U S.
MARSHALS AND U.S. ATTORNEYS FOR TRAINING
By virtue of the authority vested in me by Section 301 of Title 3 of 
the United States Code, I hereby delegate to the Attorney General 
the authority vested in me by Section 4102(a) (2) (B) of Title 5, United 
States Code, to designate individuals appointed by the President as 
United States Attorneys and United States Marshals for training 
under Chapter 41 of Title 5, United States Code.
T h e W h it e H o u se,
May 26, 1970.
