AUTHORIZING THE APPOINTMENT OF DR. WINFRED OVERHOLSER AS
SUPERINTENDENT OF ST. ELIZABETHS HOSPITAL WITHOUT RE­
GARD, TO CIVIL SERVICE RULES
By virtue of and pursuant to the authority vested in me 
by the provisions of paragraph Eighth of subdivision SEC­
OND of section 2 of the Civil Service Act (22 Stat. 403, 404), 
it is hereby ordered that Dr. Winfred Overholser may be 
appointed Superintendent of St. Elizabeths Hospital, De­
partment of the Interior, without compliance with the re­
quirements of the Civil Service Rules.
This order is recommended by the Secretary of the 
Interior.
