EXECUTIVE ORDER 10369
F urther  D esignation  P ursuant  to
Section  103 (a) of  the  R enegotiation
A ct of 1951
By virtue of the authority vested in 
me by the Renegotiation Act of 1951, 65 
Stat. 7, hereinafter referred to as the Act, 
and as President of the United States, it 
is ordered as follows:
Section  1. The Bureau of Reclamation, 
which exercises functions having a di­
rect and immediate connection with the 
national defense, is hereby designated, 
pursuant to section 103 (a) of the Act, 
as an agency of the Government included 
within the definition of the term “De­
partment” for the purposes of Title I of 
the Act.
S ec. 2. In accordance with section 102 
of the Act, the provisions of Title I  of the 
Act shall be applicable to all contracts 
with the Bureau of Reclamation, and re­
lated subcontracts, to the extent of the 
amounts received or accrued on or after 
the first day of July, 1952, whether such 
contracts or subcontracts were made on, 
before, or after that date.
