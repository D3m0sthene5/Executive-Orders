By virtue of the authority vested in me 
by section 1  of the act of July 9, 1918, 
c. 143, 40 Stat. 845, 848 (U.S.C., title 10, 
sec. 1341), it is ordered that, subject to 
valid existing rights, all the public lands 
within the following-described areas be, 
and they are hereby, withdrawn from all 
forms of appropriation under the public- 
land laws, including the mining laws, and 
reserved for the use of the War Depart­
ment in connection with aerial gunnery 
ranges:
