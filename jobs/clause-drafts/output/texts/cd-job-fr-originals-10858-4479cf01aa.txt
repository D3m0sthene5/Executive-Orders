Sec, 3. (a) The Chairman of the Com­

(b) The Committee shall meet an­

(c) Members of the Committee shall 
serve without compensation.
Sec. 4. The Secretary of Commerce

subject to the availability of appropria­

supplies, and services as may be neces­
sary for the operations of the Committee.
