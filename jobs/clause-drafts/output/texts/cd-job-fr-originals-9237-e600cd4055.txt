By virtue of the authority vested in me 
by paragraph Eight, subdivision SECOND, 
section 2 of the Civil Service Act (22 
Stat. 403, 404), it is hereby ordered that 
temporary clerks, carriers, and laborers 
required for part-time or intermittent 
work in the Postal Service in connection 
with the holiday or seasonal business 
from November 15, 1942, to January 15, 
1943, may be appointed without compli­
ance with the requirements of the Civil 
Service Rules.
This order is recommended by the Post­
master General.
