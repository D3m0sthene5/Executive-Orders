By virtue of the authority vested in me 
by the Constitution and statutes and as 
President of the United States, it is 
ordered that section 3 of Executive Order 
No. 10479 of August 13, 1953, entitled 
“Executive Order Establishing the Gov­
ernment Contract Committee” shall be, 
and it is hereby, amended (1) by striking 
from the preamble of the said section the 
word “fourteen” and inserting in lieu 
thereof the word “fifteen”, and (2) by 
striking from paragraph (b) of the said 
section the word “Eight” and inserting 
in lieu thereof the word “Nine.”
