EXECUTIVE ORDER
R evoking  the  D esignation  of U nalaska, 
Alaska, as a C ustoms  P ort of E ntry
By virtue of the authority vested in me  
by section 1 of the Act of August 1,  
1914, 38 Stat. 609, 623 (U.S.C. title 19,  
sec. 2), it is ordered that the designation  
of Unalaska, Alaska, as a customs port  
of entry in Customs Collection District  
No. 31 (Alaska), be, and it is hereby,  
revoked.
This order shall become effective at  
the close of business January 15, 1941.
