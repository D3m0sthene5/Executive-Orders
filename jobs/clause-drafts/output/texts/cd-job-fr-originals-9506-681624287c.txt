EXECUTIVE ORDER 9506
Modifying  the  C onditions  U pon  W hich  a 
Classified  S tatus M ay B e. Granted
By virtue of the authority vested In 
me by section 1753 of the Revised Stat­
utes and by section 2 of the Civil Service 
Act (22 Stat. 403), it is hereby ordered 
as follows:
Hereafter no person shall be accorded 
a classified civil service status on the 
basis of his name having been reached 
on a civil service register unless he shall 
have been regularly selected for appoint­
ment from a certificate issued by the Civil 
Service Commission for probational ap­
pointment and shall have entered on 
duty pursuant to selection from such cer­
tificate.
