By virtue of and pursuant to the authority vested in me as 
President of the United States, Executive Order No. 3206 of 
December 30,1919, as amended, creating the Federal Board of 
Surveys and Maps, is hereby further amended by adding the 
Division of Maps and Charts, The National Archives, to the 
list of organizations entitled to membership on the said Board.
