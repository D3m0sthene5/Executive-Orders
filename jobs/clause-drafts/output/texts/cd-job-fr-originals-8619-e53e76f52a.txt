EXECUTIVE ORDER
D esignating  the  D irector  of S elective  
Service to P erform  Certain  F unctions  
and D uties  U nder the  S elective 
T raining  and S ervice A ct of 1940
Under and by virtue of the authority 
vested in me by the Selective Training 
and Service Act of 1940 (Public No. 783, 
76th Congress), the Director of Selective 
Service is hereby designated, authorized, 
and empowered to perform all of the 
functions and duties vested in the Presi­
dent by the said Act that relate to ap­
peals from the determinations of boards 
of appeal. The provisions of the Selec­
tive Service Regulations relating to such 
appeals are modified accordingly.
