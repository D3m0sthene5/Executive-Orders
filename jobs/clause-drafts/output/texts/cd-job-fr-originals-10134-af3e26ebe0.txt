EXECUTIVE ORDER 10134
F urther  E xtension  of the  E xistence  
O F THE QUETICO-SUPERIOR COMMITTEE
By virtue of the authority vested in 
me as President of the United States, the 
existence of the Quetico-Superior Com­
mittee, which was created by Executive 
Order No. 6783 of June 30,1934, and the 
existence of which has been extended 
to June 30,1950 by Executive Orders No. 
7921 of June 30,1938, No. 9213 of August 
4,1942, and No. 9741 of June 25, 1946, is 
hereby further extended for a period of 
four years from June 30,1950 to June 30, 
1954.
