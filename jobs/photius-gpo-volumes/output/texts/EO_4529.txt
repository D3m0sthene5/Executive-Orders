Executive Order
WASHINGTON
Under authority of the act of Congress approved June 25, 1910 (36 Stat .
, 847) ,
as amended by the act of Congress approved August 24, 1912 (37 Stat .
, 497), it is
hereby ordered that Executive Order No. 4104 , dated November 20, 1924, with￾drawing, among other lands, all of Sec . 8, T. 34 N. , R. 2 W. , W. M., Washington ,
for the use of the Navy Department as sites for naval radio compass stations, be
revoked in so far as said order applies to Goose Island . Said island was reserved
for military purposes by Executive order of January 9, 1889, and transferred to the
Interior Department for disposition under the act of July 5, 1884 (23 Stat .
, 103),
by Executive order of August 13 , 1923, which order will now be carried out .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
October 25, 1926 .
