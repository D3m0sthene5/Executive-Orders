Executive Order
It is hereby ordered under authority contained in the Act of June 21 , 1906 (34
Stat . 325-326 ) that the trust periods on allotments of the Omaha and Winnebago
Tribes in Nebraska , the title to which has not passed out of the United States ,
which would otherwise expire during the calendar year 1927, be and the same are
hereby extended for a further period of ten years .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
December 4, 1926 .
