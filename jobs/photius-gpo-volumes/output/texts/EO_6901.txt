Executive Order
WITHDRAWAL OF PUBLIC LAND FOR WILD -LIFE ADMINISTRATIVE SITE
ALASKA
By virtue of and pursuant to the authority vested in me by the act of June 25 ,
1910 (ch . 421 , 36 Stat . 847)
, as amended by the act of August 24, 1912 (ch. 369 , 37
Stat . 497 ), and subject to the conditions therein expressed and to valid existing
rights , it is hereby ordered that the tract of public land in Alaska lying within the
following-described boundaries and indicated upon the diagram hereto annexed and
forming a part hereof be, and the same is hereby , reserved and set apart for the joint
use and occupancy of the Department of Agriculture and the Alaska Game Commis￾sion as a headquarters site in connection with the administration of Alaska game laws :

Mountain bears N. 20° E., approximately 4½ miles distant ; and the south corner of
schoolhouse bears N. 59 ° W. , 683 ft. distant .
