Executive Order
NEW MEXICO
Under authority contained in the act of Congress approved June 25 , 1910 (36
Stat . 847) , as amended by the act of August 24, 1912 (37 Stat . 497), and subject to
all the conditions , provisions and limitations of said acts , and upon the recommen￾dation of the Secretary of the Interior, it is hereby ordered that all the public lands
in New Mexico , east of the east boundary of Section 33, in Township 26 South of
Range 3 East ; east of the east boundary of Sections 5, 8 , 17 , 20 , 29 and 32, in
Township 27 South of Range 3 East ; east of the east boundary of Section 5, and
east of the east boundary of the Santa Teresa Grant , in Township 28 South of
Range 3 East ; east of the east boundary of Sections 2, 11 and 14 , and east of the
east boundary of the Santa Teresa Grant in Township 29 South of Range 3 East ;
and all of Township 29 South of Range 4 East, all of the New Mexico Principal
Meridian , which may be found to lie west of the true boundary line between the
State of New Mexico and the State of Texas, as defined in the decree of the United
States Supreme Court dated April 9, 1928 (48 Sup. Ct. Rep . 344), in the case of
the State of New Mexico v . State of Texas , be and they are hereby withdrawn from
settlement , location, sale, entry and all forms of appropriation , subject to any valid
existing rights in and to the same , for classification and in aid of legislation.
This order shall continue in full force and effect unless and until revoked by
the President, or by act of Congress .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
June 4, 1928
