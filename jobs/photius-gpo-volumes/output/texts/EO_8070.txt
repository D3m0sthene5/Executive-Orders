Executive Order
PROPERTY OF
APR 151939
YTRVARD LAW LIBRARY
EXEMPTION OF SIMS ELY FROM COMPULSORY RETIREMENT FOR AGE
WHEREAS in my judgment , the public interest requires that Sims Ely, City
Manager of Boulder City , Nevada , who, during the current month, will reach the
retirement age prescribed for automatic separation from the service , applicable to
him, be exempted from compulsory retirement for age for a period of one year :
NOW, THEREFORE , by virtue of and pursuant to the authority vested in me
by section 204 of the act of June 30, 1932 , 47 Stat . 382 , 404 ( U. S. C. , title 5, sec .
715a ), I hereby exempt the said Sims Ely from compulsory retirement for age for a
period of one year ending March 31 , 1940 .
THE WHITE HOUSE,
March 21 , 1939.
