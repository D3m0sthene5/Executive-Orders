Executive Order
ALASKA
Under and pursuant to the provisions of the act of Congress of March 12, 1914
(38 Stat . 305) entitled "An Act to authorize the President of the United States to
locate , construct and operate railroads in the Territory of Alaska and for other
purposes , " it is hereby ordered that lot 14 Sec . 3, and lot 2 Sec . 10 , T. 1 S. , R. 1
W. , F. M. , Alaska , withdrawn by Executive Order No. 1967-A approved June 23 ,
1914, and reserved for townsite purposes under said act , be eliminated from such
order .
These tracts of land are hereby restored to the public domain and will be made
subject to entry under the homestead laws applicable to Alaska , under the terms
and conditions of Public Resolution No. 29 , approved February 14 , 1920 (41 Stat .
434) and Public Resolution No. 36, approved June 21 , 1922 (42 Stat . 358 ) , on a
date to be hereafter fixed by the Commissioner of the General Land Office .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
May 8, 1928.
