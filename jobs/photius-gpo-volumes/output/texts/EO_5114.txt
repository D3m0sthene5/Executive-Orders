Executive Order
Under the provisions of Section 3 of the Federal Farm Loan Act, approved
July 17 , 1916 , Horace Paul Bestor , who has been duly appointed a member of the
Federal Farm Loan Board , is hereby designated Farm Loan Commissioner of said
Board until otherwise directed .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
May 15, 1929.
