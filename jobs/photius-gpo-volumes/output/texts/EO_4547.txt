Executive Order
The composition of the Board of Surveys and Maps created by Executive
Order No. 3206, of December 30 , 1919 , is hereby amended by the addition of the
Aeronautics Branch , Department of Commerce , Bureau of Foreign and Domestic
Commerce , Department of Commerce , Geographic Section, Department of State ,
and the Division of Maps and Charts , Library of Congress .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
November 29 , 1926 .
