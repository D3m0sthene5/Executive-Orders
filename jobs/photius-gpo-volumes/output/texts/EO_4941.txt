Executive Order
OREGON
Under authority of the act of Congress approved June 25, 1910 (36 Stat . 847),
as amended by the act of August 24, 1912 (37 Stat. 497) , it is hereby ordered that
lot 7 Sec . 35 , T. 22 S. , R. 7 W., W. M. , Oregon , containing 49.76 acres , be and it is
hereby temporarily withdrawn from settlement , location, sale or entry , and reserved
for use by Douglas County , Oregon , as a primary lookout station, subject to the
right of the United States to sell the timber thereon in accordance with the provi￾sions of the act of June 9, 1916 (39 Stat . 218) .
This order shall remain in full force and effect unless and until revoked by the
President or by act of Congress .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
July 27, 1928.
