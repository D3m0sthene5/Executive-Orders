Executive Order
AUTHORIZING THE APPOINTMENT OF MASTER SERGEANT MORRIS SWETT ,
UNITED STATES ARMY , RETIRED , TO THE CIVILIAN POSITION OF
JUNIOR LIBRARIAN IN THE FIELD ARTILLERY SCHOOL, FORT SILL ,
OKLAHOMA , WITHOUT COMPLIANCE WITH CIVIL SERVICE RULES
By virtue of and pursuant to the authority vested in me by the provisions of
paragraph Eighth of subdivision SECOND of section 2 of the Civil Service Act of
January 16 , 1883 , (22 Stat. 403 , 404), it is hereby ordered that Master Sergeant
Morris Swett, United States Army , Retired , may be appointed as junior librarian ,
P- 1 , at $2000 per annum, in the Field Artillery School , Fort Sill , Oklahoma, without
compliance with the requirements of the Civil Service Rules .
FRANKLIN D ROOSEVELT
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
Sept 30, 1939
