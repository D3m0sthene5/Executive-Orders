Executive Order
By virtue of the authority vested in me by the Act of Congress approved
August 1, 1914, making appropriations for the sundry civil expenses of the
Government for the fiscal year ended June 30, 1915, the ports of Kenosha and
Kewaunee , in customs collection district #37 ( Wisconsin)
, with headquarters at
Milwaukee , Wisconsin , are hereby discontinued, effective thirty days from the date
of this order .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
February 5, 1927.
