Executive Order
Extension of Trust Period on Allotments of Red Lake Indians
of Minnesota
It is hereby ordered under authority contained in section 5 of the act of February
8, 1887 (24 Stat . 389-390 ), that the trust period on the allotments of the following
named members of the Red Lake Chippewa Tribe of Indians of Minnesota , on the
ceded part of the Red Lake Reservation, be, and is hereby , extended for a period of
10 years from the date of expiration.
