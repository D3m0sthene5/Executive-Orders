PROPERTY OF
Executive Order
CT 9-1940
D
LAWUBRARY
EXEMPTION OF SAMUEL J. GOMPERS FROM COMPULSORY RETIREMENT
FOR AGE
WHEREAS, in my judgment, the public interest requires that Samuel J. Gompers ,
Chief Clerk , Department of Labor , who was exempted from compulsory retirement
for age for periods totaling two years by Executive Orders No. 7958 of August 19 ,
1938 , and No. 8252 of September 14, 1939 , be further exempted from such compulsory
retirement for a period of one year :
NOW, THEREFORE , by virtue of and pursuant to the authority vested in me
by section 204 of the act of June 30, 1932, 47 Stat. 382, 404 (U. S. C. , title 5, sec. 715a )
,
I hereby further exempt the said Samuel J. Gompers from compulsory retirement for
age for a period of one year ending September 30, 1941 .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
September 18 , 1940.
FRANKLIN D ROOS
