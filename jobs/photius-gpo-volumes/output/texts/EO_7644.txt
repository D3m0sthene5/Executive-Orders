Executive Order
AUTHORIZING TRANSFER OF CERTAIN EMPLOYEES OF THE GOVERNMENT￾OPERATED STAR ROUTE SERVICE BUREAU , POST OFFICE DEPART￾MENT , TO POSITIONS IN THE POSTAL SERVICE
By virtue of and pursuant to the authority vested in me by the provisions of
paragraph Eighth of subdivision SECOND of section 2 of the Civil Service Act
(22 Stat . 403 , 404) it is hereby ordered that, subject to such noncompetitive tests
of fitness as the Civil Service Commission may prescribe , the following-named non￾classified employees of the Government-Operated Star Route Service , Bureau of
the Second Assistant Postmaster General, who have long been engaged in the opera￾tion of certain Star Routes which are again being placed under contract operation,
may be transferred and appointed to appropriate classified positions in the Postal
Service without regard to the provisions of the Civil Service Act and rules :
