Executive Order
Mrs. Bessie H. Smith may be reinstated in the Bureau of Engraving and Print￾ing, as an operative, without regard to the length of time she has been separated
from the service .
Mrs. Smith was employed in the Bureau from 1904 to 1907, when she resigned
to marry . She has four children aged eighteen , seventeen , fifteen , and four , and
she and her family , including her mother , are entirely dependent upon the earnings
of the two older children .
This order is recommended by the Secretary of the Treasury . The Civil Ser￾vice Commission is unable to concur .
For charity .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
March 31 , 1927.
