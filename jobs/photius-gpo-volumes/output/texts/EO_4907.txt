Executive Order
Mrs. Victoria A. Hulbert may be employed as a clerk at Headquarters , United
States Marine Corps , Washington , D. C. , without regard to the civil service act or
rules.
Mrs. Hulbert is the widow of Lieutenant Henry Lewis Hulbert , United States
Marine Corps , who met his death in action while on duty in France during the
period of the World War.
This order is issued upon the recommendation of the Secretary of the Navy.
The Civil Service Commission does not concur .
For Charity .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
June 8, 1928.
