Executive Order
AMENDMENT TO THE CIVIL SERVICE RULES
Subdivision IX Schedule A of the Civil Service rules is hereby amended and
an additional paragraph to be known as No. 6 is added reading as follows :
" Two assistants to the Secretary in the Office of the Secretary of Agriculture ."
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
HERBERT HOOVER
September 30, 1929.
