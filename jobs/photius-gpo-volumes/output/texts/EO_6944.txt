Executive Order
TRANSFER OF LANDS FROM THE UINTA NATIONAL FOREST TO THE
WASATCH NATIONAL FOREST
UTAH
By virtue of and pursuant to the authority vested in me by the act of June 4, 1897
(30 Stat . 11 , 36 ; U. S. C. , title 16, sec . 473), and upon the recommendation of the
Secretary of Agriculture , it is ordered that all lands in the Uinta National Forest
lying to the east of the following-described line be, and they are hereby , transferred
to the Wasatch National Forest :
Beginning at the point on the boundary of the Uinta National Forest as described
by proclamation of July 30 , 1929 (46 Stat. 3003 ), where said boundary intersects the
west boundary of T. 2 N. , R. 9 W. , Uinta special meridian ; thence in a southeasterly
direction along the hydrographic divide between Duchesne River on the east and
Soapstone Creek and Wolf Creek on the west to the point where said hydrographic
divide intersects the said Uinta National Forest boundary near the southeast corner
of sec . 13 , T. 1 N. , R. 9 W. , Uinta special meridian.
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
January 9, 1935.
FRANKLIN D ROOSEVELT
