Executive Order
Isabel L. Towner may be reinstated to the position of Assistant Librarian of
the National Museum without regard to the time limit on reinstatement into the
classified service .
Miss Towner was in the classified service as librarian and cataloguer from
December 1 , 1909 , to July 31 , 1920. Since that time she has been librarian for the
National Tuberculosis Association and the National Health Council . Her educa￾tion and extensive experience with scientific publications especially qualify her for
the position in the National Museum .
This order is issued upon the recommendation of the Secretary of the Smith￾sonian Institution . The Civil Service Commission concurs .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
January 19 , 1926 .
