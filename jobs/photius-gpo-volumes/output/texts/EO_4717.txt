Executive Order
Mrs. Maysie Lawrence Reeves may be reinstated as an operative in the Bureau
of Engraving and Printing without regard to the length of time she has been sepa￾rated from the service .
This order is recommended by the Acting Secretary of the Treasury , who states
that Mrs. Reeves , during her former employment in the Bureau of Engraving and
Printing from 1912 to 1921 , rendered excellent service and that , owing to the
illness of her husband, his support as well as that of herself and four minor children
falls upon Mrs. Reeves .
The Civil Service Commission is unable to concur in recommending this order .
For charity .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
September 12 , 1927.
