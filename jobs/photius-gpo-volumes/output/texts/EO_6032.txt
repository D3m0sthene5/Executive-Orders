Executive Order
APPOINTMENT OF MR. ISAAC GREGG
Isaac Gregg , who , since June 13 , 1924 , has been serving in a position in the
Post Office Department excepted from civil -service requirements , may be appointed
in the classified service without compliance with civil-service rules .
This order is issued upon the recommendation of the Postmaster General .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
February 18 , 1933.
