Executive Order
By virtue of the authority vested in me as President of the United States of
America , and pursuant to the provision of the Act of Congress approved March 4,
1927, entitled "An Act To authorize the sale , under the provisions of the Act of
March 12, 1926, (Public , Numbered 45 ) of surplus War Department real property "
(Public No. 796-69th Congress ), the Secretary of Agriculture having made applica￾tion to the Secretary of War for the transfer thereof, and given specific reasons
therefor within ninety days after the approval of the above -mentioned Act of
Congress approved March 4, 1927, and such action having received my approval ,
IT IS HEREBY ORDERED that that portion of the Fort Matanzas Military Reserva￾tion in the State of Florida , as below described , be hereby transferred to the Depart￾ment of Agriculture as a refuge for birds :
The entire group of marsh islands lying in the Matanzas River
south of the main channel thereof near the mouth of the said river ,
in the SE14 of Section 14 and the NE14 of Section 23, Township 9
South, Range 30 East , about 15 miles to the southward of Saint
Augustine , Florida , as set apart for military purposes by Executive
Order No. 2353 , dated April 3, 1916 , EXCEPTING THEREFROM an area
of one ( 1 ) acre comprising within such area the site of the old fortifi￾cation which is situated on a marsh island south of the present main

Saint Augustine , and about one mile from Matanzas Inlet, which area
was specifically designated a national monument by Proclamation No.
1713 , dated October 15 , 1924 .

Executive Order
WHEREAS , by certain leases executed under dates of June 15 and July 5, 1918 ,
the cities of Oakland and Alameda, municipal corporations of the State of Califor￾nia , leased to the United States Shipping Board Emergency Fleet Corporation , now
known as the United States Emergency Fleet Corporation , for a term of 25 years ,
certain premises known as Government Island , comprising approximately 110 acres ,
situated in the estuary between said cities ;
WHEREAS , by the terms of each of said leases the United States Emergency
Fleet Corporation is given the right to assign its interests therein to the United
States ;
WHEREAS , under date of November 1, 1922, the United States Emergency
Fleet Corporation did assign its interests in said leases to the United States ;
WHEREAS, since such assignment the administration of federal activities on
said Island has been under the control of the Department of Agriculture ; and
WHEREAS , it is now desired that the administrative control over Government
Island be vested in the War Department ;
NOW , THEREFORE , by virtue of the authority vested in me, as President of the
United States of America , it is hereby ordered that the duties , rights, powers and
responsibilities of the United States under said leases be , and the same are, hereby
transferred to the jurisdiction and control of the War Department ; provided, that
the federal agencies now occupying Government Island shall not be disturbed
except by mutual agreement between such agencies and the War Department .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
August 10 , 1927.
