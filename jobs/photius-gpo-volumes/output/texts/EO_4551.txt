Executive Order
There are hereby excluded from the operation of the Retirement Act of July 3 ,
1926, noneducational employees in the Indian Irrigation Service intermittently and
irregularly employed , and educational employees on daily rates similarly employed .
This Order is deemed desirable because of the intermittent character of the employ￾ment and the exceedingly frequent changes in personnel .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
December 8 , 1926.
