Executive Order
It is hereby ordered that two small islands in the Columbia River , one described
as Lot 8, Sec . 21 , and Lot 7, Sec . 28, containing 3.03 acres , and the other as Lot 5 ,
Sec . 33 , containing 5.20 acres , in T. 7 N. , R. 31 E., W. M. , Washington , as segregated
by the broken line upon the diagram hereto attached and made a part of this order ,
be and the same are hereby reserved and set apart for the use of the Department
of Agriculture as a refuge and breeding ground for native birds .
It is unlawful for any person to hunt , trap , capture , wilfully disturb or kill any
bird of any kind whatever, or take the eggs of such bird within the limits of this
refuge , except under such rules and regulations as may be prescribed by the Secre￾tary of Agriculture .
Warning is expressly given to all persons not to commit any of the acts herein
enumerated , under the penalties prescribed by Sec . 84 of the U. S. Penal Code ,
approved March 4, 1909 (35 Stat.
, 1088 ), as amended by the act approved April
15, 1924 (43 Stat .
, 98) .
This refuge to be known as Columbia River Bird Refuge .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
August 28 , 1926 .
