Executive Order
ADMINISTRATION OF THE EMERGENCY CONSERVATION WORK
By virtue of the authority vested in me by the act of Congress entitled " AN
ACT For the relief of unemployment through the performance of useful public work,
and for other purposes " , approved March 31 , 1933 (Public , No. 5-73d Cong .)
,
and supplementing Executive Order No. 6101 , dated April 5, 1933 , it is hereby
ordered that :
(1) The enrollment of 25,000 veterans of the World War be undertaken as soon
as possible under supervision and such regulations as may be deemed necessary and
desirable by the Director of the Emergency Conservation Work .
(2) These men shall be enrolled under practically the same conditions as apply
to the original enrollment formerly authorized .
(3) The veterans contingent shall be made a separate part of the organization
and a distinctive part of the enterprise .
(4) The enrollment of this special veterans contingent shall proceed as rapidly
as possible and their assignment to proper work camps shall be made in accordance
with the regular program .
(5) Actual enrollment will not proceed until full plans to govern same have
been agreed on and made public .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
May 11 , 1933.
FRANKLIN D ROOSEVELT
