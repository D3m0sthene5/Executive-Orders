Executive Order
Mr. Ralph W. Osler may be appointed to the classified position of Special
Agent in the Internal Revenue Service without regard to the civil service rules .
This Order is issued on the recommendation of the Secretary of the Treasury ,
who states that Mr. Osler possesses qualifications for the position of Special Agent
and that his appointment would be in the interest of the service .
The Civil Service Commission is unable to concur in this recommendation .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
January 29, 1927 .
