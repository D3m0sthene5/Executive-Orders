Executive Order
Pursuant to the authority contained in Section 179 of the Revised Statues
(U. S. C. Title 5, Section 6), I hereby authorize and direct Ephraim F. Morgan ,
Solicitor for the Department of Commerce , to perform the duties of the Secretary of
Commerce during the absence of the Secretary of Commerce and the Assistant
Secretaries of Commerce appointed under the Act of February 14 , 1903 , as amended
by the Act of March 4, 1913, and the Act of May 20, 1926 .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
December 17 , 1927.
[No. 4784]
CALVIN COOLIDGE
