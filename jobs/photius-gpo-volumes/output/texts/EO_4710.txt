Executive Order
Mrs. Helen M. Tullis may be appointed to a clerkship in the post office at
Denver, Colorado , without regard to the requirements of the civil service rules .
Mrs. Tullis is the widow of Joseph T. Tullis, for nineteen years a faithful
employee of the Post Office Department , whose death on May 19 , 1927 , in an
automobile accident in the direct line of his duty , left Mrs. Tullis with one dependent
child and without adequate means of support.
The Postmaster General recommends the issuance of this order . The Civil
Service Commission is unable to concur .
For charity .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
August 31 , 1927 .
[No. 4713]

Executive Order
By virtue of authority vested in me by the Panama Canal Act , approved
August 24, 1912, as amended by the Act approved December 29 , 1926 , the follow￾ing regulations are hereby established to govern the granting of leaves of absence to
the Judge of the District Court of the Canal Zone , the District Attorney of the
Canal Zone , and the Marshal of the Canal Zone :
1. Leave of absence allowed the above officials by the said Act shall
be reckoned by calendar years .
2. Should any one of such offices be held by more than one person in
any one calendar year , the leave authorized shall be prorated to such per￾sons in proportion to the length of time they have held the office in that
calendar year .
3. Unused leave of absence in any one calendar year may be accumu￾lated and allowed to such officer in the next calendar year, provided no
leave in excess of 120 days shall be taken in any one calendar year .
4. The above officials will submit applications for leave to the Secre￾tary of War through the Governor of the Panama Canal , and shall report
to the Governor of the Panama Canal their time of departure on such leave
and of return to duty therefrom .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
August 29, 1927 .
[No. 4711]
CALVIN COOLIDGE
