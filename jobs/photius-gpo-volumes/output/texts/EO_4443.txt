Executive Order
AUTHORIZING THE ALIEN PROPERTY CUSTODIAN
TO SELL CERTAIN PROPERTY AT PRIVATE SALE
By virtue of the authority vested in me by an Act of Congress entitled " An
Act to define , regulate , and punish trading with the enemy and for other purposes " ,
approved October 6, 1917, known as the " Trading with the Enemy Act ", and the
amendments to such act embodied in " An act making appropriations to supply
urgent deficiencies in the appropriations for the fiscal year ending June 30, 1918, and
prior fiscal years on account of war expenses , and for other purposes " , approved
March 28, 1918 , and the amendment to such act embodied in "An act making appro￾priations to supply deficiencies in appropriations for the fiscal year ending June 30 ,
1919 , and prior fiscal years on account of war expenses , and for other purposes " ,
approved November 4, 1918, to make certain orders such as that herein contained,
I hereby, in the public interest, make the following determination, order , rule and
regulation :
The Alien Propery Custodian is hereby authorized to sell at private sale , at such
time and place as to him or to his authorized agent may seem advisable , without
public or other advertisement , for and in consideration of the sum of ONE HUNDRED
THIRTY THOUSAND ONE HUNDRED ($ 130,100.00 ) DOLLARS , certain real estate
now being held and administered by him as such Alien Property Custodian , and
heretofore , upon requirement, conveyed , transferred, assigned and delivered to
him as the property of WALDEMAR LEOPOLD VON BREDOW , residing in Germany ,
whom, after investigation , he did determine to be an enemy within the purview of
said Act as amended , which said real estate is situate in the City of San Francisco ,
County of San Francisco , State of California , and described as follows : to -wit:
The Northwest corner of Fourth and Bryant Streets , commencing at
the intersection of the Southwesterly line of Fourth Street with the
Northwesterly line of Bryant Street, running thence Northwesterly
along the Southwesterly line of Fourth Street 150 feet ; thence at a right
angle Southwesterly 300 feet ; thence at a right angle Southeasterly 150
feet to the Northwesterly line of Bryant Street; thence at a right angle
Northeasterly along the Northwesterly line of Bryant Street 300 feet
to the intersection of the Northwesterly line of Bryant Street with the
Southwesterly line of Fourth Street and the point of commencement .
Being part of 100 Vara Block 3761 .
My reasons for the foregoing determination , order, rule and regulation are :
(a) An advantageous offer for the purchase of said real estate has been received
by the Alien Property Custodian .
(b) The Alien has filed a petition with the Alien Property Custodian , requesting
that this property be sold at a consideration of not less than $ 120,000.00 .
(c) An attempted sale at public auction would be a useless formality and would
involve unnecessary expenses , delay and inconvenience , which should be avoided .
CALVIN COOLIDGE
THE WHITE HOUSE,
May 18, 1926 .
