Executive Order
By virtue of the authority vested in me by the Act of Congress approved August
1, 1914, making appropriations for the sundry civil expenses of the Government for
the fiscal year ended June 30, 1915, Akron , Ohio, is hereby created a port of entry
in customs collection district No. 41 , (Ohio), with headquarters at Cleveland , Ohio ,
effective thirty days from the date of this order .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
February 25, 1927.
[No. 4597]
CALVIN COOLIDGE
