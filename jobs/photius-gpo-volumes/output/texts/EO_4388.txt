Executive Order
Under authority contained in the act of Congress approved June 25, 1910 (36
Stat .
, 847) , as amended by the act of August 24 , 1912 (37 Stat .
, 497) , and subject
to the conditions , provisions and limitations of said acts , it is hereby ordered that
the public lands in Secs . 7, 8, 17, 18 , 19 , 30 and 31 , T. 19 S. , R. 27 E., Florida , be
and the same are hereby withdrawn from settlement , location , sale , entry and all
forms of appropriation , for classification and in aid of legislation , subject to any
vested rights in and to the same .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
March 6, 1926 .
