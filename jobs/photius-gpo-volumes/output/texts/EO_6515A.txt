Executive Order
AUTHORIZING CHARLES E. WYZANSKI , JR.
, TO ACT AS SECRETARY
OF LABOR
By virtue of the authority vested in me under the provisions of section 179 of
the Revised Statutes of the United States (5 U.S.C. , sec . 6), I hereby authorize and
direct Charles E. Wyzanski , Jr., Solicitor of Labor , to perform the duties of Secretary
of Labor during the absence of the Secretary of Labor and the Assistant Secretary
of Labor .
FRANKLIN D. ROOSEVELT .
THE WHITE HOUSE,
December 19, 1933
