Executive Order
REVOCATION OF EXECUTIVE ORDER NO . 6237 , OF JULY 28, 1933
It is hereby ordered that Executive Order No. 6237, of July 28, 1933, allocating
funds for the purchase of lands necessary for the completion of the Great Smoky
Mountains National Park, be, and it is hereby, revoked .
FRANKLIN D. ROOSEVELT .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
January 24, 1934.
