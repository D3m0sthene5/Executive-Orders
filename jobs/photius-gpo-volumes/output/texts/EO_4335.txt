Executive Order
Amending Section 9 of the Canal Zone Vehicle License Regulations
By virtue of the authority vested in me by law , I hereby order that Section 9
of the Executive Order of April 14 , 1921 , entitled, " Relating to Licensing of Vehicles ,
Road Rules , Use of Lights , Tags, Signals, and Speed Regulations in the Canal
Zone," be amended to read as follows , and that the Amendment shall take effect
thirty days from and after its publication in The Panama Canal Record :
" Sec . 9. Whenever any motor vehicle licensed in the Canal Zone shall ,
before the period for which the license is taken expires , be sold or transferred by
the licensee to some other person who may desire to continue the use and oper￾ation of such motor vehicle over the streets and roads of the Canal Zone ,
such transferee , within 10 days after such sale or transfer, by application to
the Executive Secretary, or the official acting under his authority , and upon
submission of satisfactory evidence of such purchase or transfer, shall have
the license transferred to him by the Executive Secretary, or the official acting
under his authority , authorizing such transferee to operate such motor vehicle
under such license , upon the payment of transfer fee of $1.00 to the Executive
Secretary , or the official acting by his authority , and executing such transfer
on forms provided for that purpose in accordance with rules and regulations
prescribed by the Governor of The Panama Canal. The license issued to
vendor shall be suspended unless the provisions of this section shall be complied
with within ten days from the date of sale , and the operation of the motor
vehicle under such license in the Canal Zone is prohibited ; providing , however,
that the license may be transferred from the vendor to the vendee after such
suspension upon compliance with the foregoing provisions .
" No new license shall issue to a different owner for a motor vehicle there￾tofore licensed in the Canal Zone without satisfactory proof of sale and regis￾tration by the Executive Secretary or the official acting by his authority .
The fee for such registration shall be $ 1.00, but this shall not be collected at
the time of sale if the license is transferred in accordance with the provisions
of this section ."
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
November 6 , 1925 .
