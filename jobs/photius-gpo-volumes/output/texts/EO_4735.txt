Executive Order
ILLINOIS
It is hereby ordered that an Executive order dated June 13 , 1895, withdrawing
an island , known as " Maple Island " , in the Mississippi River in Secs . 19 and 30,
T. 5 N. , R. 9 W. , 3rd P. M. , Illinois, for use in connection with the improvement
of the navigation of the Mississippi River, be and the same is hereby revoked.
And it is hereby ordered , pursuant to Public Resolution No. 29 of February 14 ,
1920 (41 Stat.
, 434), as amended January 21 , and December 28 , 1922 (42 Stat .
358 , 1067)
, that the above described land subject to valid rights , shall be opened
only to entry under the homestead laws by qualified ex-service men of the War with
Germany, under the terms and conditions of such resolutions , and the regulations
issued thereunder, for a period of 91 days, beginning with the sixty -third day from
and after the date hereof , and thereafter to appropriation under any public land
law applicable thereto by the general public .
Subsequent to the date hereof , and prior to the date of restoration to general
disposition as herein provided no right may be acquired to the released land by
settlement in advance of entry, or otherwise , except strictly in accordance herewith .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
October 3, 1927 .
