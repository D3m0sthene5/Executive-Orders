Executive Order
EXEMPTION OF ULYSSES S. SHEWMAKER FROM COMPULSORY RETIREMENT
FOR AGE
WHEREAS, in my judgment , the public interest requires that Ulysses S.
Shewmaker , administrative assistant in the Publications Section of the Division of
Bookkeeping and Warrants , Treasury Department, who during the month of Septem￾ber 1938, will reach the retirement age prescribed for automatic separation from the
service , applicable to him, be exempted from compulsory retirement for age for a period
of one year :
NOW, THEREFORE , by virtue of and pursuant to the authority vested in me
by section 204 of the act of June 30, 1932, 47 Stat. 382, 404 (U. S. C., title 5, sec. 715a)
,
I hereby exempt the said Ulysses S. Shewmaker from compulsory retirement for age
for a period of one year ending September 30 , 1939 .
FRANKLIN D ROOSEVELT
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
July 14 , 1938
