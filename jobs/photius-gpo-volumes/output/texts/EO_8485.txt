PROPERTY OF
Executive Order
4UG8-1940
HARVARD LAW
AUTHORIZING THE APPOINTMENT OF PERES D. ZIEGLER TO A POSITION AS RURAL
LETTER CARRIER WITHOUT REGARD TO THE REQUIREMENTS OF THE CIVIL
SERVICE RULES
By virtue of and pursuant to the authority vested in me by the provisions of
paragraph Eighth of subdivision SECOND of section 2 of the Civil Service Act (22
Stat . 403, 404 ), it is hereby ordered that Peres D. Ziegler may be appointed to a position
as rural letter carrier in the Postal Service without compliance with the requirements
of the Civil Service Rules .
Mr. Ziegler is a former member of the crew of the U. S. S. PANAY and was injured
in line of duty in the bombing and sinking of that vessel .
FRANKLIN D ROOSEVELT
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
July 16, 19
