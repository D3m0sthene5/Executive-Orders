Executive Order
Under the authority provided in Subsection (d) Section 18 of the Act approved
May 24, 1924, the President of the United States , in the exercise of his discretion ,
and acting in the interest of the United States, hereby orders the retention on
active duty for a period of one year from July 1 , 1926, of John G. Foster of Vermont ,
commissioned Foreign Service Officer , class one .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
June 4, 1926.
[No. 4452]
CALVIN COOLIDGE
