Executive Order
It is hereby ordered that all positions of laborer in post offices of the first and
second classes be included within the classified service , except positions of cleaners
and employees engaged in charwork ; and that , in accordance with the usual
practice , the occupants of said positions who are not already classified , be given
a classified status .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
September 30 , 1927 .
