Executive Order
The Civil Service Rule prescribing citizenship as a pre -requisite for appoint￾ment may be waived in the case of Mr. Joseph Roith, in order that he may be per￾mitted to continue in employment as ordnanceman in the Naval Gun Factory , Navy
Yard, Washington , D. C.
This order is issued upon the recommendation of the Secretary of the Navy
who states that Mr. Roith was born in Bavaria , Germany , on July 12 , 1886, and ,
when five years old, came to this country with his father and mother who settled
in Bloomer , Wisconsin , and still live there ; that Mr. Roith enlisted in the United
States Marine Corps on October 18, 1905, and served four years, being honorably
discharged on October 18 , 1909; that he re-enlisted on January 15 , 1910 for four
years and was appointed corporal on March 3 , 1910 , and honorably discharged on
January 14 , 1914. He has been continuously employed at the Naval Gun Factory ,
Navy Yard , Washington , D. C. since July 9 , 1914 , and is now engaged in handling
and loading certain pyrotechnic materials, work for which no other employee of the
Naval Establishment is qualified, by reason of his knowledge and training . On
August 22 , 1924 , while Mr. Roith was engaged in handling certain pyrotechnic
material , he was severely burned and injured in a minor explosion. Mr. Roith
remained out of the military service in the late war because of the fact that his
services at the Naval Gun Factory could not be replaced without substantial mate￾rial loss to the adequate and effective operation thereof .
The Civil Service Commission does not concur in this recommendation .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
April 23, 1926.
