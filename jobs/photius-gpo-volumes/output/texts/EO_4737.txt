Executive Order
ALASKA
Executive Order No. 4605 of March 8, 1927 is hereby vacated in so far as it
pertains to the lands situated at Cape Fanshaw , Alaska , thereby placed under the
control of the Secretary of the Interior for disposition in accordance with the pro￾visions of the act of July 5, 1884 (23 Stat .
, 103 ), and it is hereby ordered that such
lands shall remain part of the Tongass National Forest as contemplated by the
proclamation of September 10 , 1907, subject to the laws applicable to lands so
situated.
This order shall continue in full force and effect unless and until revoked by
the President or by act of Congress .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
October 5, 1927 .
