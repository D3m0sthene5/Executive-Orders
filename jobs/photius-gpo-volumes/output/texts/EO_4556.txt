Executive Order
NEW MEXICO
Under authority contained in the act of Congress approved June 25, 1910 (36
Stat .
, 847) , as amended by the act of August 24, 1912 (37 Stat.
, 497), and subject
to the conditions, provisions and limitations of said acts , it is hereby ordered that
the public lands in Ts. 12, 13 and 14 S. , R. 19 E., and T. 14 S., R. 20 E., N. M. P. M. ,
New Mexico , be and the same are hereby withdrawn from settlement , location , sale ,
entry and all forms of appropriation pending a resurvey of said townships under the
act of Congress approved March 3, 1909 (35 Stat .
, 845), as amended by the Joint
Resolution of June 25, 1910 (36 Stat .
, 884) ; this withdrawal to remain in full force
and effect from the date hereof until the resurvey is duly approved and accepted
and the approved plats thereof are officially filed in the proper district land office ,
unless another date be fixed by the Commissioner of the General Land Office ,
whereupon this withdrawal will become of no effect .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
December 18, 1926 .
