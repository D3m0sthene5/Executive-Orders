Executive Order
Mrs. Dorothy Potter Benedict may be appointed to a clerical position in the
classified service without examination under the civil service rules .
Mrs. Benedict is the widow of Major Charles C. Benedict, United States Air
Service, who was killed last year in an aviation accident .
This order is recommended by the Secretary of War , who states that during
the war Mrs. Benedict made a fine record with the American Red Cross Service
overseas and in the aerodromes at the front , showing keen intelligence and marked
administrative ability .
As the Civil Service Commission can properly recommend waivers only when
the procedure under the rules will not fully meet the needs of the service , it is
unable to concur .
For charity .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
March 15, 1926 .
