Executive Order
Mrs. Cathrine E. Greenwald, widow of Mark H. Greenwald , employed in the
Interstate Commerce Commission from 1908 to November 28, 1926, may be
appointed to a clerical position in the classified service without reference to the civil
service rules . The services of Mr. Greenwald were terminated by death and he left
his widow with three minor children and without means of support. This Order is
recommended by the Secretary of the Interstate Commerce Commission .
The Civil Service Commission does not feel warranted in recommending the
Order as demanded by the interests of the service .
Done for charity .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
January 10 , 1927 .
CALVIN COOLIDGE
