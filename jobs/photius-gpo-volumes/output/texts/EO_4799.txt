Executive Order
Mrs. Iva C. D. Tupper may be reinstated to a clerical position in the Depart￾ment of Commerce without regard to the length of her separation from the service .
Mrs. Tupper is the widow of Mr. Arthur W. Tupper who served diligently and
well as an engineer in the Bureau of Lighthouses from 1911 to his death, on
June 8, 1926. He left his widow without adequate means of support and with three
minor children, two boys aged 19 and 10, and a girl 16 years old.
The Secretary of Commerce recommends the reinstatement of Mrs. Tupper
because he regards her as fully competent , and because of the diligence , talents,
versatility , and the valuable services of Mr. Tupper in the Bureau of Lighthouses .
The Civil Service Commission is unable to concur.
For charity .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
January 25, 1928.
