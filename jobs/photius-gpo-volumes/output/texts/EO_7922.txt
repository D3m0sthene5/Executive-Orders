Executive Order
DIRECTING THE SECRETARY OF THE TREASURY TO ASSEMBLE
ANNUALLY A COAST GUARD PERSONNEL BOARD
By virtue of and pursuant to the authority vested in me by section 2 of the
act of January 12 , 1938 , 52 Stat . 4, I hereby authorize and direct the Secretary of
the Treasury to assemble annually a Coast Guard Personnel Board , as provided in
the said section .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
June 30 , 1938 .
[No. 7922]
FRANKLIN D
