Executive Order
Mrs. Dorothy Thelma Bishop , widow of Walter C. Bishop , first class radio man,
United States Navy, who lost his life in the line of duty in the recent Submarine
disaster to the S-4, may be appointed to an appropriate position in the Veterans '
Bureau without regard to the examination requirements of the civil service rules .
As the Civil Service Commission can properly recommend waivers only when
the procedure under the rules will not fully meet the needs of the service , it is
unable to concur in the recommendation of the Director of the Veterans ' Bureau
for the issuance of this order .
For charity .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
January 21 , 1928.
