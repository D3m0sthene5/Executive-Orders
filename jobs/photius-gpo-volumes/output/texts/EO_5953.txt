Executive Order
EXTENSION OF TRUST PERIOD ON INDIAN RESERVATION ALLOTMENTS
It is hereby ordered , under authority contained in section 5 of the act of
February 8, 1887 (24 Stat. 388 , 389 ), and the act of June 21 , 1906 (34 Stat . 325 ,
326 )
, that the period of trust covering allotments made in favor of Indians on the
following-named reservations , or on other reservations not specifically named herein ,
which trust will expire during the calendar year 1933 , be, and the same is hereby ,
extended for a period of 10 years from the respective dates of expiration thereof :
Round Valley Reservations , Calif .
White Earth Reservation, Minn.
Flathead Reservation, Mont .
Santee Reservation, Nebr .
Devils Lake and Standing Rock Reservations , N. Dak.
Kiowa Reservation , Okla .
Pine Ridge Reservation , S. Dak .
Shoshone or Wind River Reservation, Wyo .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
November 23 , 1932.
