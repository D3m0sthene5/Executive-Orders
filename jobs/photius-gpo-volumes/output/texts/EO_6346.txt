Executive Order
IN THE MATTER OF THE APPLICATION OF THE GREENSBORO LUMBER
COMPANY AND H. A. TAYLOR, FOR CERTAIN EXEMPTIONS FROM
THE CODE OF FAIR COMPETITION FOR THE LUMBER AND TIMBER
PRODUCTS INDUSTRIES
A Code of Fair Competition for the Lumber and Timber Products Industries
has been heretofore approved by me . After such approval, and in accordance with
the provisions of my further Executive order dated July 15 , 1933 , hearings have been
granted by the Administrator to the named applicants, allegedly directly affected
by said code , who have claimed that applications thereof have been unjust to them
and have applied for an exemption therefrom with reference to the minimum wage
provided in the said code .
It appearing to me on the basis of the showing made at the hearings granted the
applicants above mentioned as set forth in the report thereon , dated September 15 ,
1933, rendered to me by the Administrator , which is hereby adopted and approved ,
that no case of injustice and extreme hardship requiring special treatment has been
made out by the above applicants :
Now, therefore , I, Franklin D. Roosevelt, President of the United States , pur￾suant to the authority and discretion vested in me under title I of the National Indus￾trial Recovery Act, approved June 16 , 1933 , and otherwise , and in accordance with
the provisions of my Executive order dated July 15 , 1933 , providing for hearing on
the application of codes under certain circumstances , do order that the application
for exemption by the above -named applicants be, and it is hereby , denied .
Approval recommended :
HUGH S. JOHNSON ,
Administrator .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
October 20, 1933.
