Executive Order
I. WHEREAS , by Executive Order No. 2565, dated March 28, 1917, certain lands ,
containing a net area of 1510 acres , more or less, at Waimanalo , District of Koolau￾polo , Island of Oahu, Territory of Hawaii , were withdrawn and set aside for mili￾tary purposes , excepting therefrom all tracts of land , described by Land Commission
Awards and Royal Patent Grants , Grants and Patents that were awarded by the
authorities of Hawaii ;
WHEREAS, the description of the reservation as given in said Executive Order
No. 2565, dated March 28 , 1917, has been found to be inaccurate and a resurvey has
been made of the reservation ;
NOW THEREFORE , pursuant to the authority vested in me by Section 91 of the
Act of Congress approved April 30, 1900 (31 Stat. 141 ), entitled, "An Act to pro￾vide a government for the Territory of Hawaii ," as amended by Section 7 of the
Act of Congress approved May 27, 1910 (36 Stat . 443), it is hereby ordered that
the description contained in said Executive Order No. 2565, dated March 28, 1917 ,
be , and the same is hereby amended to read as follows :
