Executive Order
ARVARD LAWLIBRARY
EXEMPTION OF ALES HRDLICKA FROM COMPULSORY RETIREMENT FOR AGE
WHEREAS, in my judgment , the public interest requires that Ales Hrdlicka ,
Curator of Physical Anthropology in the United States National Museum , Smith￾sonian Institution , who was exempted from compulsory retirement for age for periods
totaling two years by Executive Orders No. 8058 of February 23, 1939 , and No. 8368
of March 7, 1940, be further exempted from such compulsory retirement for a period
of one year :
NOW , THEREFORE , by virtue of and pursuant to the authority vested in me
by section 204 of the act of June 30, 1932, 47 Stat. 382 , 404 (U. S. C., title 5, sec. 715a ),
I hereby further exempt the said Ales Hrdlicka from compulsory retirement for age
for a period of one year ending March 31 , 1942 .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
March 12, 1941 .
FRANKLIN D ROOSEVELT
