Executive Order
WYOMING
The withdrawal made by Executive order of August 3, 1914 ( No. 2008 ), under
the act of June 25, 1910 ( 36 Stat . 847), as amended , of a tract in Secs . 17 and 20,
T. 55 N. , R. 105 W. , 6th P. M. , described by metes and bounds containing 3.01
acres , for use by the Forest Service as Crandal Right of Way in connection with
the administration of the Shoshone National Forest, Wyoming , is hereby revoked .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
February 26, 1927.

CALVIN COOLIDGE
