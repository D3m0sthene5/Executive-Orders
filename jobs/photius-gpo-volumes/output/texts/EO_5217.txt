ORDER OF RESTORATION
PUBLIC WATER RESTORATION NO . 61
NEW MEXICO NO . 6
So much of the orders of withdrawal dated April 30, 1919 , creating Public
Water Reserve No. 63 , February 11 , 1918 , creating Public Water Reserve No. 53 ,
and February 8, 1923, creating Public Water Reserve No. 86, as affects the land
hereinafter listed is hereby revoked , and all such land not otherwise reserved or
withdrawn is hereby restored to the public domain and shall become subject to
disposition under the laws applicable thereto upon such date and after such notice
as may be hereafter determined and announced .
NEW MEXICO
Gila and Salt River Meridian
Lands affected in Public Water Reserve No. 63 :
T. 13 S., R. 20 W. , Sec . 19 , lot 4 .
T. 13 S. , R. 21 W. , Sec . 24 , E of SE1 .
