JUL 221941
Executive Order
CARVARD LAWLBRARY
EXEMPTION OF HUBERT K. BISHOP FROM COMPULSORY RETIREMENT
FOR AGE
WHEREAS, in my judgment, the public interest requires that Hubert K.
Bishop , Chief, Division of Construction , Public Roads Administration , Federal
Works Agency, who was exempted from compulsory retirement for age for a period
of one year by Executive Order No. 8413 of May 18 , 1940, be further exempted from
such compulsory retirement for a period of one year :
NOW, THEREFORE , by virtue of and pursuant to the authority vested in me
by section 204 of the act of June 30, 1932 , 47 Stat. 382, 404 (U. S. C. , title 5, sec. 715a ),
I hereby further exempt the said Hubert K. Bishop from compulsory retirement for
age for a period of one year ending June 30 , 1942 .
FRANKLIN D ROOSEVELT
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
June 30 , 1
