Executive Order
CALIFORNIA
The Executive order of October 28, 1925, withdrawing Lot 6 Sec . 6, Lots 1 and
2 Sec. 7, T. 7 N., R. 1 E. , H. M., California , under the provisions of the act of
Congress approved June 25, 1910 (36 Stat. 847)

by the Little River Redwood Company , for right of way for a tramway crossing
certain lands covered by the said reserve .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
CALVIN COOLIDGE
