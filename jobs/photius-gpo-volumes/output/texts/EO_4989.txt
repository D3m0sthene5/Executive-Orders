Executive Order
WYOMING
The Executive order of April 19 , 1912 , as adjusted July 24 , 1913 , creating
Public Water Reserve No. 3 , Wyoming No. 2, under the provisions of the act of
Congress approved June 25, 1910 (36 Stat . 847) , as amended , is hereby modified to
the extent of authorizing the Secretary of the Interior to approve the application
by the Union Pacific Railroad Company for right of way for a water pipe line cross￾ing certain lands covered by said water reserve .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
November 3 , 1928 .
