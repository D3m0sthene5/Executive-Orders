Executive Order
AUTHORIZING THE APPOINTMENT OF MRS . HILDA MCTEER TO A POSITION
IN THE CLASSIFIED SERVICE OF THE GOVERNMENT WITHOUT REGARD
TO CIVIL-SERVICE RULES
By virtue of and pursuant to the authority vested in me by the provisions of
paragraph Eighth of subdivision SECOND of section 2 of the Civil Service Act of
January 16 , 1883 (22 Stat. 403, 404 ), it is hereby ordered that Mrs. Hilda McTeer may
be appointed to a position in the classified service of the Government without com￾pliance with the requirements of the civil -service rules .
Mrs. McTeer is the widow of John H. McTeer , who had long and meritorious
service in the Treasury Department , and who died on July 18 , 1935, leaving his
widow and a minor child without adequate means of support. Mrs. McTeer has
also served a number of years under temporary appointments in the Treasury Depart￾ment and other branches of the Government service .
This order is recommended by the Secretary of the Treasury .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
February 28 , 1936 .
