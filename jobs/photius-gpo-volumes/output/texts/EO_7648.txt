Executive Order
WAIVING PROVISIONS OF EXECUTIVE ORDER OF JANUARY 17, 1873 ,
AS TO DOUGLAS ARMSTRONG
By virtue of and pursuant to the authority vested in me by section 1753 of the
Revised Statutes of the United States (U. S. C. , title 5, sec . 631), the provisions of
the Executive Order of January 17, 1873 , as amended , prohibiting , with certain excер￾tions , officers and employees of the United States from holding any office under any
state , territorial , or municipal government , are hereby waived as to Douglas Arm￾strong , Director of Police for the Municipality of St. Croix , Virgin Islands , so as to
permit him to be appointed to and hold the office of United States Marshal for the
Virgin Islands .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
June 28 , 1937.
[No. 7648]
FRANKLIN
