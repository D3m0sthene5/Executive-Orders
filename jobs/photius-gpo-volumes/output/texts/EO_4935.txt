Executive Order
Miss Madge Herron may be appointed to an appropriate position in the
Department of Commerce without regard to the provisions of the civil service rules .
This Order is issued upon the recommendation of the Secretary of Commerce .
As the Civil Service Commission can properly recommend waivers only when
the procedure under the rules will not meet the needs of the service , it does not
concur .
For charity .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
July 16 , 1928 .
