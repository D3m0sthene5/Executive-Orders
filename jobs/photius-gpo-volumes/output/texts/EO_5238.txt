Executive Order
Christmas , 1929
It is hereby ordered that the several executive departments and independent
Government establishments in the District of Columbia , including the Government
Printing Office and the Navy Yard and Stations, be closed on Tuesday, December 24,
1929 , and all clerks and other employees in the Federal service in the District of
Columbia , except those who may for special public reasons be excepted from the
provisions of this order , or those whose absence from duty would be inconsistent with
the provisions of existing law, are hereby excused from duty on thatday .
This order is not to be deemed as establishing a precedent .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
December 12 , 1929.
