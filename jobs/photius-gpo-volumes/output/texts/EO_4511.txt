Executive Order
Thomas Rogers may be appointed to a clerical position in the Postal Service
without regard to the civil service rules .
Mr. Rogers , a full -blooded Arikara Indian , attended for six years the Indian
State Normal Training School at Santee , Nebraska ; enlisted in a North Dakota
company; went to France ; was many times cited for bravery ; took an important
part in scouting , capturing at night bare -handed and alone many sentinels who were
taken back to the American camp for questioning ; and was honorably discharged
July 5 , 1919.
The Acting Postmaster General recommends the appointment of Mr. Rogers
under an executive order. As the Civil Service Commission can properly recom￾mend a waiver only when the procedure under the rules will not fully meet the
needs of the service , it is unable to concur .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
September 20 , 1926.
