Executive Order
By virtue of the authority vested in me by the Act of Congress approved
August 1 , 1914 , making appropriations for the sundry civil expenses of the Govern￾ment for the fiscal year ended June 30, 1915, Mahukona is hereby abolished as a
port of entry in Customs Collection District No. 32 (Hawaii ) in which Honolulu
is the headquarters port, effective November 1 , 1925 .
