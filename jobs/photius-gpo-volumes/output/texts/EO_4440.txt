Executive Order
MICHIGAN AND WISCONSIN
Pursuant to the authority conferred upon me by the act of Congress approved
June 25, 1910 (36 Stat .
, 847) , as amended by the act of Congress approved August
24 , 1912 (37 Stat.
, 497), it is hereby ordered that , all public lands of the United
States as hereinafter indicated are withdrawn from settlement , location, sale , entry
and all forms of appropriation subject to valid existing rights in and to the same
pending classification and legislation :
All islands in the Menomonee River opposite T. 37 N., R. 28 W. , Mich. M. ,
Michigan , and T. 36 N. , Rs . 21 and 22 E. , 4th P. M., Wisconsin .
CALVIN COOLIDGE
THE WHITE HOUSE,
May 11 , 1926.
