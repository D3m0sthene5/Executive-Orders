Executive Order
Mr. Charles E. Margerum may be reinstated as a material engineer in the
Naval Gun Factory , Navy Yard, Washington , D. C., without regard to the time
limit on reinstatement under the civil service rules .
Mr. Margerum was employed in the field service of the Navy Department from
August 16 , 1917, until November 18, 1922. The Secretary of the Navy in recom￾mending this order states that he rendered particularly valuable and efficient
service and that his reinstatement would be in the interest of the Government.
The Civil Service Commission does not concur , as it can properly recommend
waivers only when the needs of the service can not be met by the procedure under
the rules .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
July 6 , 1928.
