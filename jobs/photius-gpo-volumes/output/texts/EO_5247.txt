Executive Order
Retention of Edwin V. Morgan in the Foreign Service
Under the authority provided in subsection (d)
, section 18, of the act approved
May 24 , 1924 , the President of the United States , in the exercise of his discretion ,
and acting in the interest of the United States , hereby orders the retention on active
duty for such time as may be deemed necessary not in excess of five years from Febru￾ary 22 , 1930 , of Edwin V. Morgan , of New York, ambassador extraordinary and
plenipotentiary to Brazil.
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
December 23, 1929 .
HERBERT HOOVER
