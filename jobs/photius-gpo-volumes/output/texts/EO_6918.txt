Executive Order
AMENDMENT OF EXECUTIVE ORDER No. 3206 , OF DECEMBER 30, 1919 ,
CREATING THE BOARD OF SURVEYS AND MAPS
Executive Order No. 3206, of December 30, 1919 , creating the Board of Surveys
and Maps , as amended , is hereby further amended by adding the Tennessee Valley
Authority to the list of organizations entitled to membership on said Board .
THE WHITE HOUSE,
December 12 , 1934 .
FRANKLIN D ROOSEVELT
