Executive Order
ESTABLISHING LOWER SOURIS MIGRATORY WATERFOWL REFUGE
NORTH DAKOTA
By virtue of and pursuant to the authority vested in me as President of the
United States , and in order to effectuate further the purposes of the Migratory Bird
Conservation Act (45 Stat . 1222 ), it is ordered that the following -described lands,
consisting of 40,000 acres , more or less , acquired or to be acquired by the United
States , in Bottineau and McHenry Counties , North Dakota , be, and they are hereby ,
reserved and set apart for the use of the Department of Agriculture , subject to valid
existing rights , as a refuge and breeding ground for migratory birds and other wild￾life : Provided , That any private lands within the areas described shall become a part
of the refuge hereby established upon the acquisition of title or lease thereto by the
United States :
