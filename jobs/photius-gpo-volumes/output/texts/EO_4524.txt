Executive Order
OREGON
The classification contemplated by the act of Congress , approved March 4,
1925 (43 Stat .
, 1282), having been effected in part, it is hereby ordered that
Executive order of May 8, 1925, No. 4220 , temporarily withdrawing the public
lands within certain areas in Oregon in aid thereof, be, and the same is hereby ,
revoked as to such portions of the following areas as have not been included
within the Whitman National Forest under the provisions of said act :
Willamette Meridian
In T. 10 S., R. 37 E., Sec . 1 , N /½ Sec . 2, NW1/4 and S½ Sec . 5, all Secs . 6, 7,
8, NW14 and S½ Sec . 9 , W/½ Sec . 15 , all Secs . 16 to 22 , inclusive , 26 to 36 ,
inclusive :
In T. 11 S. , R. 37 E., Secs . 1 to 30 , inclusive , 34 , 35 and 36 ;
In T. 11 S. , R. 38 E., Secs . 1 to 30, inclusive, NE4 Sec . 32 , all Secs . 33 , 34
and 35;
In T. 10 S. , R. 39 E., NW14 Sec . 3, all Secs . 4 to 9 , inclusive , 17 , 18 , 19, 20,
29, 30 , 31 and 32 ;
In T. 11 S. , R. 39 E., Secs . 5 to 9, inclusive, S1/½ Sec . 10, Secs . 13 to 28 ,
inclusive, W½ NW4 Sec . 29 , N½ SW4 Sec . 30; all Secs . 33, 34 , 35 and 36 ;
In T. 12 S. , R. 39 E., Secs . 1, 2, 3, 10, 11 , 12, E1/½ Sec . 13 ;
In T. 11 S. , R. 40 E., Secs . 16 to 21 , inclusive , 28 to 33 , inclusive .
And it is further ordered , pursuant to Public Resolution No. 29 of February 14 ,
1920 (41 Stat .
, 434) , as amended January 21 and December 28, 1922 (42 Stat .
,
358 , 1067), that the unreserved public land lands in the above described areas ,
subject to valid rights, shall be opened to entry only under the homestead or desert
land laws by qualified ex-service men of the War with Germany , under the terms
and conditions of said resolutions and the regulations issued thereunder , for a
period of ninety -one days , beginning with the sixty-third day from and after the
date hereof , and thereafter if unentered to appropriation under any public land law
applicable thereto by the general public .
Subsequent to the date hereof and prior to the date of restoration to general
disposition as herein provided no right may be acquired to such land by settlement
in advance of entry, or otherwise except strictly in accordance herewith .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
October 18 , 1926.
