Executive Order
By virtue of the authority vested in me by the Act of Congress approved
August 1 , 1914, making appropriations for the sundry civil expenses of the Govern￾ment for the fiscal year ended June 30, 1915, the customs station of Charlotte , N. C. ,

with headquarters at Wilmington , N. C., effective thirty days from the date of
this order.
