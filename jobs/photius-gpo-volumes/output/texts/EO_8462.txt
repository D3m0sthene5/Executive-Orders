PROPERTY OF SEP
9-1940
Executive Order
AUTHORIZING THE APPOINTMENT OF WILFORD S. ALEXANDER AS DISTRICT
SUPERVISOR IN THE ALCOHOL TAX UNIT, BUREAU OF INTERNAL
REVENUE , WITHOUT REGARD TO THE CIVIL SERVICE RULES
By virtue of the authority vested in me by paragraph Eighth of subdivision
SECOND of section 2 of the Civil Service Act (22 Stat. 403 , 404), it is hereby ordered
that Wilford S. Alexander may be appointed as District Supervisor in the Alcohol Tax
Unit of the Bureau of Internal Revenue without compliance with the requirements of
the Civil Service Rules .
This order is recommended by the Secretary of the Treasury .
THE WHITE HOUSE,
June 29, 1940.
