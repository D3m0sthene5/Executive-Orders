Executive Order
WHEREAS, by Executive Order No. 4671, dated June 20, 1927 , there was placed
under the control of the Secretary of the Navy for the use of the Navy Department
for coal storage and other naval purposes a certain tract of land comprising part of
the Norfolk Army Supply Base, Norfolk, Virginia ; and
WHEREAS, certain courses in the description of the said tract of land as contained
in said Executive Order were found after its issuance to have been in error ; and
WHEREAS, Executive Order No. 4716 , dated September 12, 1927, was issued ,
superseding said Executive Order No. 4671, dated June 20, 1927, and correcting the
courses found to have been in error therein ; and
WHEREAS , certain other courses and distances in the description of the tract of
land as given in said Executive Orders are approximate only and not definite; and
WHEREAS, it is desired that the said tract of land to be held for the use of the
Navy be described by definite courses and distances
NOW, THEREFORE , in order that said tract may be more definitely described ,
said Executive Order No. 4716 , dated September 12, 1927, is amended to read as
follows :
Under the authority of an Act of Congress approved July 11 , 1919 , (41 Stat .
131, 132) it is hereby ordered that the following described tract of land at the
Norfolk Army Supply Base, at Norfolk, Virginia , which is no longer needed for
military purposes , be and hereby is placed under the control of the Secretary of the
Navy for the use of the Navy Department for coal storage and other naval purposes ,
to wit:
Beginning at a steel bolt located a distance of 210 feet north of and normal
to the base line between the main tracks connecting the Norfolk Army
