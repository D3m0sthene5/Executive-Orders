Executive Order
Mrs. James E. Golladay , widow of Mr. Golladay , who served in the Interior
Department for twenty -eight years preceding his death, may be appointed to an
appropriate clerical position without reference to the civil service rules .
The Civil Service Commission cannot recommend the appointment as required
by the interests of the service .
For charity .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
CALVIN COOLIDGE
February 21 , 1929.
