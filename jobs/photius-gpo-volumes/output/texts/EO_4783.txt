Executive Order
Abraham Krotoshinsky may be appointed to an appropriate position in the
executive civil service , preferably in the southwestern part of the United States or
Saranac Lake region , New York , or some other locality favorable to his complete
recovery from his present physical disability .
Abraham Krotoshinsky was inducted into the service September 30, 1917, by
Local Board No. 13 , Bronx , N. Y. He was assigned to Company K, 307th Infan￾try. He was overseas from April 7, 1918, to May 30, 1919, and was honorably
discharged June 26 , 1919 , as private in Company K, 307th Infantry. His charac￾ter was noted as excellent and he was given the distinguished service cross in Order
No. 139 , which stated as follows :
" He was on liaison duty with a battalion of the 308th Infantry which was
surrounded by the enemy north of the forest de La Buinonne of the Argonne
Forest . After patrols and runners had been repeatedly shot down while attempt￾ing to carry back word of the battalion's position and condition , he volunteered
for the mission and successfully accomplished it . "
The Civil Service Commission will interpose no objection to the order .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
December 16 , 1927 .
