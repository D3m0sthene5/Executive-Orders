Executive Order
AMENDMENT OF EXECUTIVE ORDER No. 6657-A DATED MARCH 27, 1934
Section 3 (c ) of Executive Order No. 6657-A of March 27, 1934 , prescribing regu￾lations for payment of losses sustained by officers , enlisted men , and employees of the
United States in foreign countries due to appreciation of foreign currencies in their
relation to the American dollar , is hereby amended to read as follows:
" (c) In case of employees traveling in foreign countries under official orders ,
not employed in or on assignment or detail to a post of duty in a foreign country ,
no part of the employee's salary not converted for expenditure abroad shall be
included in the loss referred to for the purposes of these regulations . "
This order shall be retroactive to April 1, 1934, the effective date of the order
which it amends .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
June 30 , 1934.
