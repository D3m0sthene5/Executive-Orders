Executive Order
COLORADO
Under authority contained in the act of June 25, 1910 (36 Stat . 847 ), as
amended by the act of August 24, 1912 (37 Stat. 497), and subject to the conditions ,
provisions and limitations of said acts , it is hereby ordered that the public lands in
Ts . 1 and 2 S. , R. 86 W. , and Ts. 2, 3 and 4 S. , R. 87 W. , 6th P. M., Colorado , be
and the same are hereby withdrawn from settlement , location, sale, entry and all
forms of appropriation , pending a resurvey of said townships under the act of
March 3 , 1909 (35 Stat. 845 ) .
This order shall continue in full force and effect unless and until revoked by the
President , or by an act of Congress .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
July 26, 1929.
