Executive Order
James M. E. Brown may be reinstated to the position of guard in the Treasury
Department without regard to the time limit on reinstatement under the civil
service rules .
Mr. Brown served in the Government Printing Office in various positions for
more than forty -one years , being dropped on account of reduction of force , March
27, 1923. This order is issued upon the recommendation of the Secretary of the
Treasury . The Civil Service Commission concurs .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
January 2, 1929 .
