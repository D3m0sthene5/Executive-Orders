Executive Order
CHANGING LOCATION OF THE LAND OFFICE FOR THE LINKTON LAND
DISTRICT
OREGON
By virtue of and pursuant to the authority vested in me by section 2251 of the
Revised Statutes (title 43, U. S. C., sec . 126), it is hereby ordered that the location of
the land office for the Linkton land district in the State of Oregon be changed from
Lakeview to Klamath Falls .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
April 10, 1935.
FRANKLIN D ROOSEVELT
