Executive Order
Classification of Attorney Positions in the Veterans ' Administration by
Revocation of Paragraph 1 of Section IX, Schedule B, Civil - Service Rules
Schedule B of the civil -service rules is hereby amended by eliminating para￾graph 1 of Section IX, reading as follows :
"Attorneys of all grades ."
The effect of this order is to place within the competitive classified service all
positions of attorney in the Veterans ' Administration .
Effective October 1 , 1931 , all employees of the Veterans ' Administration who
are occupying positions of attorney of any grade , and who are certified by the Ad￾ministrator of Veterans ' Affairs as capable and efficient , will be included, with their
positions , in the classified civil service .
This order will be effective as of October 1 , 1931 .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
June 3, 1931 .
