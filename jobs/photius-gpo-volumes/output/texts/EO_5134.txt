Executive Order
Mrs. Mary V. Kehoe may be appointed to an appropriate position in the
General Accounting Office without regard to the examination requirements of the
civil service rules . She is the widow of George D. Kehoe, who died February 9 ,
1929, after many years of honorable and faithful service in the Government
Printing Office , Treasury Department , and General Accounting Office .
This order is recommended by the Comptroller General .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
June 12 , 1929 .
[No. 5134]
HERBE
