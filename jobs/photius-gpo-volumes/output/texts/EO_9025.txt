PROPERTY OF
Executive Order
FEB 19:042
AUTHORIZING THE APPOINTMENT OF GEORGE HOWE TO THE POSITION OF
SUPERVISING ARCHITECT IN THE PUBLIC BUILDINGS ADMINISTRATION ,
FEDERAL WORKS AGENCY, WITHOUT REGARD TO THE CIVIL SERVICE
RULES
By virtue of the authority vested in me by the provisions of paragraph Eighth,
subdivision SECOND , section 2 of the Civil Service Act (22 Stat . 403 , 404) , it is hereby
ordered that George Howe may be appointed to the classified position of Supervising
Architect in the Public Buildings Administration , Federal Works Agency, without
compliance with the requirements of the Civil Service Rules .
FRANKLIN D ROOSEVELT
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
January 16, 1
