Executive Order
By virtue of the authority vested in me by the Act of Congress approved
August 1 , 1914, making appropriations for the sundry civil expenses of the Govern￾ment for the fiscal year ended June 30 , 1915, the limits of the port of Wilmington ,
Delaware , a Port of Entry in Customs Collection District No. 11 (Philadelphia ) ,
with headquarters at Philadelphia , Pennsylvania , are hereby extended to include
the towns of New Castle , Newport , and Claymont , in the State of Delaware , and
Carney's Point and Deepwater Point , in the State of New Jersey , effective
September 1 , 1926 .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
August 12 , 1926,
