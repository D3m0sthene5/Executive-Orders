Executive Order
Mrs. Edna May may be appointed as an Operative in the Bureau of Engraving
and Printing without compliance with the requirements of the civil service rules .
This order is recommended by the Secretary of the Treasury , who states that Mrs.
May is the widow of Ellwood D. May, a former plate printer in the Bureau , who
died in August , 1928, leaving his widow without adequate means to support herself ,
two minor children , and her mother.
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
June 29 , 1929.
