Executive Order
LAWRENCEBURG , INDIANA , DESIGNATED AS A CUSTOMS PORT OF ENTRY
By virtue of the authority vested in me by section 1 of the act of August 1 , 1914
(ch. 223 , 38 Stat . 609, 623 ; 19 U.S.C. , sec. 2 ), Lawrenceburg, Indiana , is hereby
designated as a customs port of entry in customs collection district no . 40 (Indiana ),
with headquarters at Indianapolis , Indiana , effective as of this date .
There shall be included within the limits of this port the adjoining town of
Greendale , Indiana .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
March 7, 1934 .
