Executive Order
John G. Dudley may be reinstated as Statistical and Research Agent and Legal
Advisor in the Department of Commerce without regard to the length of his sep￾aration from the service . From 1898 until 1911 Mr. Dudley was in the competitive
classified service .
For more than six years Mr. Dudley , serving in an excepted position , has
answered all inquiries and supplied all information concerning the activities of the
United States Food Administration and allied organizations, the records of which
were placed in the custody of the Department of Commerce by the Executive Order
of December 31 , 1927.
This order is issued upon the recommendation of the Secretary of Commerce
who states that no other person available possesses such an intimate knowledge of
the affairs of those agencies and that Mr. Dudley's appointment will be in the
interest of the service .
The Civil Service Commission concurs .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
May 3, 1928.
