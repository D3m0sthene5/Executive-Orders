Executive Order
By virtue of the authority vested in me as President of the United States of
America , and pursuant to the provisions of Section three of the Act of Congress
approved June 7, 1926 (Public No. 345-69th Congress )
, entitled " An Act To
authorize the disposition of lands no longer needed for naval purposes ," the Sec￾retary of State by letter of July 21 , 1926, having made application to the Secretary
of the Navy for the transfer thereof and given specific reasons therefor, within
ninety days after the approval of the above mentioned Act of Congress , and such
action having received my approval , it is hereby ordered that the lot of land No. 1 of
No. 98, Yamatecho , Yokohama , Japan , upon which the United States holds a super￾ficies of nine hundred and ninety nine years , be and the same is hereby transferred to
the Department of State for use in connection with the proposed housing of the
officers and staff of the American Consulate at Yokohama .
