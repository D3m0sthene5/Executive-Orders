Executive Order
FLORIDA
Executive Order No. 3509 of July 5, 1921 , issued under authority of the act of
June 25 , 1910 (36 Stat .
, 847), as amended by the act of August 24, 1912 (37 Stat .
,
497)
, withdrew temporarily all the public lands in T. 28 S., Rs . 26 and 27 E. , Tal .
M. , Florida , from settlement , location , sale or entry , pending examination and sur￾vey, and in contemplation of legislation . The purposes of the withdrawal having
been accomplished by the survey of the land and the enactment of legislation, it is
hereby ordered that the Executive order be, and the same is hereby , revoked as to
all public lands in the townships mentioned outside of those described in the act of
Congress approved February 27 , 1925 (43 Stat .
, 1012) .
The lands covered by this order shall become subject to settlement , entry, or
other form of disposal only upon such date and under such regulations as the Sec￾retary of the Interior shall prescribe .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
CALVIN COOLIDGE
April 20, 1926 .
