Executive Order
Under authority of the Act of Congress , approved June 10 , 1922, the following
change is made in Executive Order No. 3694 of June 19, 1922, effective on and
after July 1 , 1926 :
Table I of said Executive Order is hereby amended by striking out the words-
"B. Special-Alaska , Europe and South America " and substituting therefor
the following :
" B. -Special-Alaska , Europe , South America , and men absent from their ships
on temporary duty not involving travel . "
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
July 8 , 1926 .
