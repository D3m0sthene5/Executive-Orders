JAN 141941
Executive Order
AUTHORIZING THE APPOINTMENT OF MARY ELIZABETH HUBER TO A CLASSI￾FIED POSITION IN THE FRANKLIN D. ROOSEVELT LIBRARY , HYDE PARK ,
NEW YORK , WITHOUT REGARD TO THE CIVIL SERVICE RULES
By virtue of and pursuant to the authority vested in me by the provisions of
paragraph Eighth of subdivision SECOND of section 2 of the Civil Service Act (22
Stat . 403, 404), it is hereby ordered that Mary Elizabeth Huber may be appointed to
the classified position of under clerk￾typist in the Franklin D. Roosevelt Library at
Hyde Park, New York , without compliance with the requirements of the Civil
Service Rules .
FRANKLIN D ROOSEVELT
THE WHITE HOUSE,
December 11 , 1940
