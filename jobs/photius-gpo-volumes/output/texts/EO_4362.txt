Executive Order
Mrs. James E. Maynard may be appointed as a skilled laborer in the Govern￾ment Printing Office without regard to the provisions of the civil service rules .
Mr. James E. Maynard , her husband, a Spanish War Veteran, after faithful
and efficient service in the Government Printing Office from 1900 until November ,
1925, was retired for total disability . He is now in a hospital and Mrs. Maynard
is in urgent need of employment for the support of herself and eleven year old son .
Done for charity .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
December 28 , 1925.
