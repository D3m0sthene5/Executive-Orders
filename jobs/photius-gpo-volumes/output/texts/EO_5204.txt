Executive Order
Mrs. Estelle Roberts , widow of Mr. Earl A. Roberts , formerly Immigration
Patrol Inspector at Detroit , Michigan , who was killed while in the performance of
his duties by a rum runner, may be appointed matron in the Immigration Service
without regard to the civil service rules . This action is recommended by the
Secretary of Labor .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
October 9, 1929.
