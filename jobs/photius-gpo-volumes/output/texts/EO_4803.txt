Executive Order
Mrs. Reberta Estell Jacobs , a widow whose son, Charles A. R. Jacobs , left his
position in the Government Printing Office to enlist in the Marine Corps in 1917
and who was killed in action in France , may be appointed to a suitable position in
the Government Printing Office in accordance with the recommendation of the
Public Printer .
The Civil Service Commission cannot certify that the interests of the service
demand the waiver of the civil service rules .
For charity .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
February 3, 1928 .
[No. 4803 ]
CALVIN CO
