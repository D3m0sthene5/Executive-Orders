Executive Order
Dr. Howard M. Batchelder of Sterling, Colorado, may be reinstated to a position
in one of the divisions of the Bureau of Animal Industry , Department of Agricul￾ture, without regard to civil service requirements .
Dr. Batchelder resigned from the position of veterinary inspector March 24,
1917, to enter the military service but, being prevented by age, he engaged exten￾sively in raising foodstuffs during the war . He was prevented from accepting
reinstatement near the close of his eligibility by the fatal illness of his wife . He has
had approximately eighteen years of service with the Department of Agriculture .
This order is recommended by the Secretary of Agriculture . The Civil Service
Commission concurs .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
March 13, 1928 .
