Executive Order
By virtue of the authority vested in me as President of the United States of
America , and pursuant to the provisions of Section 6 of the Act of Congress
approved March 12, 1926, entitled "An Act Authorizing the use for permanent
construction at military posts of the proceeds from the sale of surplus War
Department real property , and authorizing the sale of certain military reservations ,
and for other purposes " , (Public No. 45-69th Congress ) , the Secretary of Com￾merce having made application to the Secretary of War for the transfer thereof
and given specific reasons therefor, within ninety days after the approval of the
above mentioned Act of Congress and such action having received my approval , it
is hereby ordered that all of the Fort Andrew Military Reservation situated on
Gurnet Point on the north side of the entrance to Plymouth Harbor , near the town
of Plymouth , in Plymouth County , State of Massachusetts, except that part
thereof herein transferred to the Treasury Department , be and the same is hereby
transferred to the Department of Commerce for use as a lighthouse reservation,
the property so transferred being more particularly described as follows :
Starting at a stone south 43 degrees East 35 feet from the South￾east corner of the barn of Mr. F. Holmes, formerly the property of
Mr. Leander Lovell , as the Northwest corner of the tract ; thence
North 65 degrees 30 minutes East 792 feet , more or less (for the last
200 feet or more along a stone fence ) to the edge of the bluff, and
thence on the same line 274 feet , more or less , to the low water of
Cape Cod Bay , as the Northwest boundary . Starting again at the
aforesaid Northwest corner the line runs South 2 degrees 3 minutes
East about 356 feet , more or less , to the bluff (the first 150 feet or
more along a stone fence) ; thence on the same line 300 feet , more or
less , to the low water of Cape Cod Bay , and thence eastward and
northward along the low water line of Cape Cod Bay to the extremity
of the first described line ; exclusive of the lighthouse tract located
within the said area and conveyed to the United States of America by
Hannah Thomas and John Thomas by deed dated November 10 , 1802 ;
being all the land acquired by the United States of America by deed
from Leander Lovell dated June 7, 1870, recorded in Book 365 , pages
247-248 , of the Plymouth County Registry of Deeds , Massachusetts ;
excepting and reserving that portion thereof and the right to use the
roadway and well, hereinafter described and transferred to the Treas￾ury Department .
And the Secretary of Treasury having made application to the Secretary of
War for the transfer thereof and given specific reasons therefor, within ninety days
after the approval of the above mentioned Act of Congress , and such action having
received my approval, it is hereby ordered that the portion of the Fort Andrew
Military Reservation, in the State of Massachusetts, hereinafter described , be and
the same is hereby transferred to the Treasury Department for use as a Coast
Guard Station :
Starting at a stone South 43 degrees East 35 feet distant from
the Southeast corner of the barn of Mr. F. Holmes , formerly owned
by Mr. Leander Lovell , as the Northwest corner of the tract ; thence
North 65 degrees 30 minutes East and distant 792 feet , more or less ,
to the crest of the bluff ; thence along the crest of the bluff, in a south￾erly direction a distance of 100 feet , more or less , to the intersection
of the crest of the bluff and a line parallel to and 100 feet distant from
the last mentioned course ; thence South 65 degrees 30 minutes West
along a line parallel to and 100 feet distant from the first mentioned
course a distance of 338 feet , more or less , to a post; thence South 42
degrees West a distance of 171 feet , more or less , to a post ; thence
North 85 degrees West a distance of 165 feet , more or less , to a stone
fence ; thence North 2 degrees 3 minutes West along said stone fence
a distance of 102 feet , more or less , to the point of beginning ;
Together with the common use of the roadway along the last men￾tioned course but one, and with the common use of the well on the
Parade Ground of Fort Andrew .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
CALVIN COOLIDGE
February 23, 1927
