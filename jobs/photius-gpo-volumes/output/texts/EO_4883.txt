Executive Order
Mrs. Anna E. Sill may be appointed to an appropriate position in the classified
or unclassified civil service without reference to the examination requirements of
the civil service rules or the labor regulations .
Mrs. Sill is the wife of a retired veteran nearly seventy-five years old. She is
recommended for special consideration by Major General Lejeune, Colonel U. S.
Grant, 3rd.
, Brigadier General F. R. Keefer , and Major J. A. Ulio. The Civil
Service Commission is unable to concur.
For charity .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
May 16, 1928 .
