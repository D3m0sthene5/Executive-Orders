Executive Order
APPOINTMENT OF COLLECTORS OF COTTON STATISTICS
By virtue of and pursuant to the authority vested in me by the provisions of
paragraph Eighth of subdivision SECOND of section 2 of the Civil Service Act
(22 Stat . 403 , 404), Executive Order No. 6141 , of May 23, 1933 , amending schedule
B of the civil -service rules , is hereby amended so as to permit the appointment under
the provisions of paragraph 6 , subdivision I, schedule A of the civil -service rules of
any person employed by the Bureau of the Census , Department of Commerce , as
collector of cotton statistics where the aggregate compensation is not more than $540
per annum.
This order is intended to have the effect of placing appointments to the positions
involved within the terms of the above -mentioned provisions of the civil-service rules ,
which have existed for many years , governing similar part-time and intermittent
employment in the Federal Government.
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
February 27 , 1936 .
FRANKLIN D ROOSEVEL
