Executive Order
Mrs. Berenice B. Shelton may be appointed to an appropriate clerical position
in the Veterans ' Bureau without regard to the requirements of the civil service
rules .
Mrs. Shelton is the widow of General George Henry Shelton , of distinguished
war service in France , who died November 2 , 1920, leaving his widow and minor
daughter without adequate means of support.
The Director of the Veterans ' Bureau recommends the order . The Civil
Service Commission submits the case without prejudice .
In recognition of the distinguished service of Gen. Shelton .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
March 12 , 1928 .
