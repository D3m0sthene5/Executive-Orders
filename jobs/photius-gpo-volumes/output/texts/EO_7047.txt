Executive Order
MODIFICATION OF EXECUTIVE ORDER No. 6957 , OF FEBRUARY 4, 1935 ,
WITHDRAWING PUBLIC LANDS
ALASKA
By virtue of and pursuant to the authority vested in me by the act of June 25,
1910 (ch . 421 , 36 Stat . 847), as amended by the act of August 24 , 1912 (ch . 369 ,
37 Stat . 497 ), it is ordered that Executive Order No. 6957, of February 4, 1935,
withdrawing certain public lands in Alaska , be, and it is hereby, modified so as to
permit settlement upon the said lands .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
May 20, 1935.
FRANKLIN D ROOSEVE
