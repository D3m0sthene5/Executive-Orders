Executive Order
Mrs. Stella Davis Mudd may be reinstated as an operative in the Bureau of
Engraving and Printing without regard to the length of her separation from the
service . Mrs. Mudd resigned as printer's assistant from the Bureau on February 9 ,
1909. Her reinstatement by executive order is recommended by the Secretary of
the Treasury in view of the fact that Mrs. Mudd is a widow with four minor
children to support . The Civil Service Commission is unable to concur in the
recommendation .
Done for charity .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
November 3, 1925.
