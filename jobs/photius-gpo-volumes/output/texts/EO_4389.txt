Executive Order
Mrs. Harriet P. Lemly , widow of Captain Samuel C. Lemly , late Judge
Advocate General of the United States Navy, may be employed as a clerk in the
Navy Department , Washington , D. C. , without regard to the provisions of the
civil service rules .
As the Civil Service Commission can properly recommend waivers only when
the procedure under the rules will not fully meet the needs of the service , it is
unable to concur in the recommendation of the Secretary of the Navy for the
issuance of this Order .
Done for charity .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
March 6, 1926 .
