Executive Order
REVOCATION IN PART OF EXECUTIVE ORDER NO . 5603 , OF APRIL 20,
1931 , WITHDRAWING PUBLIC LANDS
WYOMING
By virtue of and pursuant to the authority vested in me by the act of June 25 ,
1910, ch . 421 , 36 Stat . 847 , as amended by the act of August 24, 1912, ch . 369, .37
Stat. 497, Executive Order No. 5603, of April 20, 1931, withdrawing , together with
other lands , public lands in T. 43 N. , R. 84 W. of the sixth principal meridian,
Wyoming , pending a resurvey , is hereby revoked as to the said township .
This order shall become effective upon the date of the official filing of the plat
of resurvey of said township .
THE WHITE HOUSE,
January 15, 1936.
FRANKLIN D ROOSEVELT
