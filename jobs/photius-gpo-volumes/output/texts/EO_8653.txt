Executive Order
AUTHORIZING THE APPOINTMENT OF MRS . FLORENCE BANKHEAD AS CHIEF ,
NATIONAL MEMORIALS AND HISTORIC SITES SECTION , NATIONAL
PARK SERVICE, WITHOUT REGARD TO CIVIL SERVICE RULES
By virtue of and pursuant to the authority vested in me by the provisions of
paragraph Eighth of subdivision SECOND of section 2 of the Civil Service Act of
January 16 , 1883 (22 Stat . 403 , 404 ), it is hereby ordered that Mrs. Florence Bank￾head of Alabama , may be appointed as Chief , National Memorials and Historic Sites
Section , National Park Service , Department of the Interior , without compliance
with the requirements of the Civil Service Rules .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
January 28 , 1941 .
FRANKLIN D ROOSEVEL
