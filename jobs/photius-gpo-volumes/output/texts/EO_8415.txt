JUN 13 1940
Executive Order
AUTHORIZING THE APPOINTMENT OF LYMAN A. FILLMEN TO A CLASSIFIED
POSITION IN THE SMITHSONIAN INSTITUTION WITHOUT REGARD TO
THE CIVIL SERVICE RULES
By virtue of the authority vested in me by the provisions of paragraph Eighth ,
subdivision SECOND , section 2 of the Civil Service Act (22 Stat. 403, 404), it is
hereby ordered that Lyman A. Fillmen may be appointed to a classified position of
instrument maker in the Smithsonian Institution without compliance with the
requirements of the Civil Service Rules .
Mr. Fillmen has performed highly creditable and capable service for nine years
in the private laboratories of the Smithsonian Institution , and it is desirable that he
be appointed to the above -mentioned technical position, which is in the competitive
classified service .
This order is recommended by the Secretary of the Smithsonian Institution .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
May 20, 1940.
