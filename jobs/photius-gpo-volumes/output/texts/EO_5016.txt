Executive Order
Paragraph 52 of Chapter XX of Instructions to Diplomatic Officers is amended
to read as follows :
" XX- 52 Drafts to be drawn at five days after sight.
All drafts drawn by diplomatic officers on the Secretary of State must
be drawn at five days after sight, acceptance waived, in order to give
sufficient time to the accounting officers to adjust the accounts before the
day of payment . Drafts for disbursements made for objects not expressly
authorized by law nor by instructions must not be drawn until notice is
received from the Department of State that the accounts and vouchers
have been examined and approved."
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
December 21 , 1928 .
