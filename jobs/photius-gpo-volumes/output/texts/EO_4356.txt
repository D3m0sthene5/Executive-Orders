Executive Order
By virtue of the authority vested in me by the Act of Congress approved August
1, 1914, making appropriations for the sundry civil expenses of the Government
for the fiscal year ended June 30, 1915, Boothbay is hereby abolished as a port of
entry in customs collection district No. 1 (Maine and New Hampshire ) , effective
January 1 , 1926 .
By virtue of the same authority the port limits of Bath are hereby extended
to include Boothbay and Wiscasset , with headquarters at Portland , Maine , effective
January 1 , 1926 .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
December 15, 1925 .
