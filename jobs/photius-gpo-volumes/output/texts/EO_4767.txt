Executive Order
By authority of the provisions of Section 2 of the Act of Congress approved
September 21 , 1922 , I designate Judge James W. Blackburn as Special Judge of
the Canal Zone until January 1, 1929 , to act during the absence or illness of the
District Judge of the Canal Zone , and in all cases where said District Judge may
be disqualified .
THE WHITE HOUSE,
CALVIN COOLIDGE
November 25, 1927 .
