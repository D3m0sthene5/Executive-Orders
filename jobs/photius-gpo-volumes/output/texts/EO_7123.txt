Executive Order
8/9/35
APPOINTING MEMBERS OF THE NATIONAL ADVISORY COMMITTEE OF THE
NATIONAL YOUTH ADMINISTRATION
By virtue of and pursuant to the authority vested in me under the Emergency
Relief Appropriation Act of 1935 , approved April 8 , 1935 (Public Resolution No. 11,
74th Congress ), I hereby appoint the following persons to serve as members of the
National Advisory Committee of the National Youth Administration , established
by Executive Order No. 7086 of June 26 , 1935 :
A. A. Berle, Jr.
Mary McLeod Bethune
Selma M. Borchardt
Frank L. Boyden
Howard S. Braucher

Dr. David deSola Pool
Agnes Samuelson
Mae K. Sargent
M. W. Thatcher
Florence Thorne
Dr. David Weglein
Owen D. Young
Dr. George F. Zook
