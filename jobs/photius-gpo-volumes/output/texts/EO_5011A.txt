Executive Order
Mr. Stephen H. Hastings may be reinstated as senior agronomist , Department
of Agriculture , Bureau of Plant Industry , without regard to the time limit on rein￾statement under the civil service rules .
Mr. Hastings was employed as agent and farm superintendent, Department of
Agriculture , from November 22, 1906, to March 15, 1920. After resigning from the
service of the Department of Agriculture he operated about 30,000 acres of land in
the Salt River Valley in the capacity of Ranch Manager for the Southwest Cotton
Company at Phoenix , Arizona .
The Secretary of Agriculture recommends this order in view of Mr. Hastings '
background of training and experimental work with the Department , his experience
with the Southwest Cotton Company , his intimate knowledge of the work, and the
confidence reposed in him by the Department . The Civil Service Commission
concurs .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
December 3, 1928 .
