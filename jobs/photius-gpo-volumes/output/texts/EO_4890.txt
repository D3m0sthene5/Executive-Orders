Executive Order
Mr. Harry W. Walker may be appointed postmaster at Simsbury , Connecticut ,
without regard to the Executive Order of November 5, 1926 , requiring candidates
to pass a civil service examination for Presidential postmasterships .
Mr. Walker has been postmaster at West Simsbury with a civil service status
since March 2 , 1925.
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
May 26, 1928 .
