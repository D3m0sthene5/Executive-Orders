Executive Order
WITHDRAWING LAND FOR ARMY RADIO STATION
ALASKA
It is hereby ordered that the land hereinafter described be, and the same is
hereby , withdrawn subject to valid existing rights as provided by the act of June 25 ,
1910 (36 Stat. 847-848 ), as amended by the act of August 24, 1912 (37 Stat. 497 ) ,
for the use of the War Department as a site for the Army radio station at Point
Barrow, Alaska :

the northerly extension of the easterly line of the Presbyterian Mission Property
and 30 ft.
