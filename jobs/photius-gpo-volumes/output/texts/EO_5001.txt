Executive Order
In order that the intent of the regulations governing the payment of additional
compensation to flying personnel of the Army , Navy, Marine Corps , Coast Guard
and the National Guard may be insured of correct interpretation , paragraph 10 of
the Executive Order dated March 10, 1927 , is hereby amended as follows :
10. For personnel of the Army, Navy , Marine Corps , or Coast Guard , or of the
National Guard when participating in exercises or performing duties provided for
by Sections 94, 97 and 99 of the National Defense Act as amended , who are
