PROPERTY
OF
Executive Order
SEP 9-1940
AUTHORIZING THE APPOINTMENT OF NEWTON B. DRURY TO THE POSITION
OF DIRECTOR OF THE NATIONAL PARK SERVICE WITHOUT REGARD
TO THE CIVIL SERVICE RULES
By virtue of the authority vested in me by the provisions of paragraph Eighth
of Subdivision SECOND of section 2 of the Civil Service Act (22 Stat . 403, 404 ), it
is hereby ordered that Newton B. Drury may be appointed to the position of Director
of the National Park Service , Department of the Interior , without regard to the
competitive requirements of the Civil Service Act and Rules .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
July 9 , 1940 .
FRANKLIN D ROO
