Executive Order
Mrs. Lizzie A. Haley may be reinstated as an operative in the Bureau of
Engraving and Printing of the Treasury Department without regard to the length
of time she has been separated from the classified service .
Mrs. Haley , under her maiden name of Lizzie A. Dwyer , was appointed through
a competitive examination and served in that Bureau from March 7, 1908, to Octo￾ber 26, 1909 , when she resigned to marry . Her husband, Joseph D. Haley , was
also employed under that Bureau , from 1905 to 1923, when he was separated by a
necessary reduction of force . He has recently died leaving his widow with three
small children and without means of support.
This order is recommended by the Secretary of the Treasury . As the Civil
Service Commission may properly recommend waivers only when the procedure
under the rules will not fully meet the needs of the service , it is unable to concur .
For charity .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
February 25 , 1927 .
