Executive Order
COLORADO
Under authority contained in the act of June 25, 1910 (36 Stat. 847), as amended
by the act of August 24, 1912 (37 Stat . 497), and subject to the conditions , pro￾visions and limitations of said acts , it is hereby ordered that the public land in
T. 1 N. , R. 75 W. , 6th P. M., Colorado , be and the same is hereby withdrawn
from settlement , location , sale , entry and all forms of appropriation , pending a
resurvey of said township under the act of March 3, 1909 (35 Stat. 845 ), as amended
by the Joint Resolution of June 25, 1910 (36 Stat. 884 ) .
This order shall continue in full force and effect unless and until revoked by
the President or by an act of Congress .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
September 7, 1928 .
