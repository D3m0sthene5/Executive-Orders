Executive Order
IDAHO
Under authority of the act of Congress approved June 25, 1910 (36 Stat . 847 ),
as amended by the act of August 24 , 1912 (37 Stat . 497), the NE and N SE
Sec . 25, T. 9 S. , R. 13 E., B. M., Idaho , are hereby temporarily withdrawn from all
forms of appropriation , pending classification and in aid of legislation .
This order shall remain in full force and effect unless and until revoked by the
President or by act of Congress .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
May 21, 1928 .
[No. 4885]
CALVIN COOLIDGE
