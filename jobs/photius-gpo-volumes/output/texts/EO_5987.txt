Executive Order
REVOCATION OF WITHDRAWAL OF PUBLIC LANDS
CALIFORNIA
It is hereby ordered that Executive Order No. 4652, dated May 18, 1927 ,
withdrawing certain public lands for use by the Department of Commerce as air
mail beacon sites and landing fields , be, and the same is hereby , revoked in so far
as it affects the following-described lands in California :
SAN BERNARDINO MERIDIAN
T. 13 N. , R. 8 E., sec . 1, W. ½/.
T. 15 N., R. 11 E., sec . 4 except 4.16 acres in patented mineral entry.
T. 16 N., R. 12 E., sec . 21, NE . ¼, S. ½ NW. 4, and S. 2;
sec . 28 , N. ½ NE . ¼, N. ½ NW. 4, and SW . ¼ NW. 4.
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
January 4, 1933 .
