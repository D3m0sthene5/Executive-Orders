Executive Order
ORANGE , TEXAS , ABOLISHED AS A CUSTOMS PORT OF ENTRY
By virtue of and pursuant to the authority vested in me by the act of August 1 ,
1914 (38 Stat. 609, 623 ; 19 U. S. C., sec . 2), Orange , Texas, is hereby abolished as a
customs port of entry in customs collection district no . 21 (Sabine) with headquarters
at Port Arthur, Texas , effective 30 days from the date of this order .
FRANKLIN D ROOSEVELT
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
June 7, 1935
