Under and pursuant to the provisions of section 18 , paragraph (k)
, of the act
of Congress approved May 24, 1924, entitled " An Act For the reorganization and
improvement of the Foreign Service of the United States , and for other purposes ” ,
which paragraph reads as follows :
" (k) The President is authorized from time to time to establish , by
Executive order, a list of places in tropical countries which by reason

the following place is hereby established as an unhealthful post for the purposes of

Lagos, Nigeria .
