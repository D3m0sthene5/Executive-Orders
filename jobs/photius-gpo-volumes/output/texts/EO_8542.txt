OCT9-1940
Executive Order
IF
VARD LAW LIBRAR
AUTHORIZING THE APPOINTMENT OF HAROLD AMBROSE TO A CLASSIFIED
POSITION IN THE POST OFFICE DEPARTMENT WITHOUT REGARD TO THE
CIVIL SERVICE RULES
By virtue of and pursuant to the authority vested in me by the provisions of para￾graph Eighth of subdivision SECOND of section 2 of the Civil Service Act (22 Stat .
403 , 404), it is hereby ordered that Harold Ambrose, who, since June 12 , 1933 , has
been serving in a position in the Post Office Department excepted from civil service
requirements , may be appointed to a classified position in the Post Office Department
without compliance with the requirements of the Civil Service Rules .
FRANKLIN D ROOSEVELT
THE WHITE HOUSE,
September 18 , 1940.
