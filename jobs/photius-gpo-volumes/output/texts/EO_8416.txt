Executive Order
JUN 141940
AUTHORIZING THE APPOINTMENT OF WILLIAM T. GEURTS TO A POSITION
AS SENIOR MEDIATOR, MARITIME LABOR BOARD , WITHOUT REGARD
TO THE CIVIL SERVICE RULES
By virtue of the authority vested in me by the provisions of paragraph Eighth of
subdivision SECOND of section 2 of the Civil Service Act (22 Stat . 403 , 404), it is
hereby ordered that William T. Geurts may be appointed to a position as Senior
Mediator , Maritime Labor Board , without regard to his standing on the register of
civil service eligibles .
This action is taken upon the recommendation of the Chairman of the Maritime
Labor Board , who states that Mr. Geurts has performed effective and efficient service
since his temporary appointment on May 2, 1939, under authority of section 2 of
Civil Service Rule VIII , and that it would be in the interest of efficient administration
to continue his services .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
May 22, 1940.
FRANKLIN D ROOSEVELT
