Executive Order
AMENDING CODE OF FAIR COMPETITION FOR THE ALCOHOLIC BEVERAGES
IMPORTING INDUSTRY
WHEREAS the Federal Alcohol Control Administration has submitted for my
approval amendments to the Code of Fair Competition for the Alcoholic Beverages
Importing Industry and has rendered its report and recommendations and findings
thereon ; and
WHEREAS due notice of public hearing on said proposed amendments has been
given and a public hearing thereon has been held pursuant to such notice ;
NOW, THEREFORE , by virtue of and pursuant to the authority vested in me
by Title I of the National Industrial Recovery Act of June 16 , 1933 (ch . 90, 48 Stat.
195), and in order to effectuate the purposes of said Title, I hereby approve said
amendments and modify my previous approval of the Code of Fair Competition
for the Alcoholic Beverages Importing Industry to include an approval of said Code
in its entirety as hereby amended .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
April 19 , 1935.
FRANKLIN D ROOSEVELT
