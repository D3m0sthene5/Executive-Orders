Executive Order
The Executive Order of March 12, 1927, discontinuing certain land offices and
consolidating certain land districts at the close of business June 30, 1927, is hereby
amended by eliminating the consolidation of the Lander district with the Evanston
district in Wyoming , and substituting a consolidation of the Lander district with
the Cheyenne district , with transfer of the records and business to Cheyenne .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
March 21 , 1927.
