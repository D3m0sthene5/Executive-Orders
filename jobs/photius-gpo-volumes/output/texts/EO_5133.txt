Executive Order
The provisions of the Executive Order of January 17, 1873 , prohibiting federal
employees from holding office under any state, territorial or municipal government ,
are hereby waived to permit Honorable Percy W. Phillips , Member of the United
States Board of Tax Appeals, to teach in the Summer School of the University of
North Carolina .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
June 7, 1929 .
[No. 5133 ]
HERBERT HOO
