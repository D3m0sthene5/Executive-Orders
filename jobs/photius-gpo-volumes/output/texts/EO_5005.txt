Executive Order
Louisiana
Under authority of the act of Congress approved June 25, 1910 (36 Stat . 847) ,
as amended by the act of August 24, 1912 (37 Stat . 497 ), it is hereby ordered that
the following described tracts of public lands in Louisiana , namely, the NE14 NW4
Sec . 17, T. 10 N., R. 7 E., Lot 3 Sec . 10 , T. 11 N. , R. 6 E. , and that part of the
SW14 SW4 Sec . 3, T. 11 N. , R. 6 E., lying south and west of the Boeuf River, con￾taining 89.75 acres , be , and the same are hereby withdrawn from settlement ,
location , sale or entry , except as provided in said acts , in aid of legislation and of
Mississippi flood control projects .
This order shall continue in full force and effect unless and until revoked by
the President, or by act of Congress .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
December 5, 1928 .
