Executive Order
Mrs. Ruth McElroy Hill may be reinstated to an appropriate position in the
Post Office Department without regard to the time limit on reinstatement under
the civil service rules .
Mrs. Hill resigned August 15, 1924, after more than four years ' service in the
Treasury Department, General Accounting Office , and the Post Office Department .
This order is recommended by the Postmaster General, who states that she rendered
efficient service and is exceptionally qualified for the work she performed .
The Civil Service Commission cannot concur, its recommendations for waivers
being limited to those cases where the needs of the service cannot be fully met
under the rules .
For charity .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
September 22 , 1928 .
