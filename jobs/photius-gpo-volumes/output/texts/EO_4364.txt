Executive Order
Under authority of "An Act to authorize the President of the United States
to make withdrawals of public lands in certain cases " , approved June 25, 1910
(36 Stat.
, 847) , there are hereby with drawn from settlement , location , sale or
entry, islands known as Cove Rock or Table Rock, and all other islands and rocks
situated in the Pacific Ocean within one half mile from the shore and lying opposite
fractional sections 35 and 36, township 28 south, range 15 west of the Willamette
Meridian , Oregon .
There are also hereby withdrawn from settlement, location, sale or entry, under
the authority of said act, the islands situated in the Pacific Ocean and locally
known as Haystack rock or rocks , Bird rocks , Gull rocks , the Needles , Arch Cape
rock , Jockey Cap rock , Seal rocks upon which native birds breed , and all other
islands and rocks lying off the coast west of townships 4 and 5 north , range 10
west of the Willamette Meridian , Oregon .
These withdrawals are made pending the passage of legislation to provide for
the permanent reservation of the islands and rocks , in whole or in part, for recrea￾tional purposes or for the creation of permanent reservations of such rocks or
islands as have long been occupied by breeding waterfowl and other native birds .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
January 7, 1926
