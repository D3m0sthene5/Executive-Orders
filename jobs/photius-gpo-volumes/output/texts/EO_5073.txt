Executive Order
Mrs. Lee H. Garby , widow of Dr. Carl D. Garby , engaged in chemical work in
the Departments of Navy, War, and Agriculture from January 12, 1916 , to July 10 ,
1928 , may be appointed to an appropriate position in the classified service without
regard to the provisions of the civil service rules .
This order is recommended by the Secretary of Agriculture . The Civil Service
Commission is unable to concur .
For charity.
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
March 2 , 1929.
CALVIN COOLIDGE
