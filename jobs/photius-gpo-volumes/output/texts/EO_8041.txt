Executive Order
PROPERTY OF
FEB 281939
1 WEIBRARY
EXEMPTION OF HENRY CLARKE HILL FROM COMPULSORY RETIREMENT
FOR AGE
WHEREAS, in my judgment, the public interest requires that Henry Clarke Hill ,
Warden of the United States Northeastern Penitentiary , Lewisburg , Pennsylvania ,
who , during the month of February , 1939 , will reach the retirement age prescribed for
automatic separation from the service , applicable to him , be exempted from com￾pulsory retirement for age for a period not in excess of one year from March 1, 1939 :
NOW , THEREFORE , by virtue of and pursuant to the authority vested in me
by section 204 of the act of June 30, 1932 , 47 Stat. 382 , 404 (U. S. C. , title 5, sec . 715a),
I hereby exempt the said Henry Clarke Hill from compulsory retirement for age for
a period not in excess of one year from March 1 , 1939 .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
January 25, 1939 .
FRANKLIN D ROOSEVEL
