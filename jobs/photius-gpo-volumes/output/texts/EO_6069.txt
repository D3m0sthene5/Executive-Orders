Executive Order
APPOINTMENT OF JOHN M. HOOE
Mr. John M. Hooe may be permanently appointed as marine gasoline engineer
(qualified as airplane pilot) on the force of the Collector of Customs at Buffalo, N. Y. ,
without compliance with the requirements of the civil -service rules .
This order is recommended by the Secretary of the Treasury , who states that
Mr. Hooe has been serving under temporary appointment in an essential position
since March 19, 1932 , in the absence of qualified civil -service eligibles , and that his
permanent appointment will be in the interest of the service .
The Civil Service Commission concurs in recommending the Order .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
March 3 , 1933 .
