REVOCATION IN PART OF EXECUTIVE ORDERS Nos. 5789 AND 5792 , OF
FEBRUARY 2, 1932, WITHDRAWING PUBLIC LANDS
CALIFORNIA AND NEVADA
By virtue of and pursuant to the authority vested in me by the act of June 25 ,
1910 (ch . 421 , 36 Stat. 847 ) , as amended by the act of August 24, 1912 (ch . 369 , 37
Stat. 497 ), it is ordered that Executive Orders Nos . 5789 and 5792, dated February 2 ,
1932 , withdrawing public lands in California and Nevada , be, and they are hereby ,
revoked as to the following -described areas :
