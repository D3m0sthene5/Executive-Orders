Executive Order
Partial Restoration of Public Lands on Amaknak Island
Alaska
It is hereby directed that Executive Order No. 5243 of December 19 , 1929 , restor￾ing a portion of Amaknak Island , of the Aleutian group, Alaska , to the public domain ,
be, and the same is hereby , amended so as to revoke Executive order of April 2, 1901 ,
withdrawing certain lands on the island for public purposes , and to exclude from the
restoration the lands on the island set apart for lighthouse purposes by Executive
order of January 13 , 1899 , amended by Executive order of June 10, 1902, and the
lands reserved for the use of the Navy Department as a wireless station by Executive
Order No. 1456 of January 6, 1912 .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
October 1 , 1930
HERBERT HOOVE
