Executive Order
REVOCATION OF WITHDRAWAL OF PUBLIC LANDS PENDING RESURVEY
NEW MEXICO
It is hereby ordered that Executive Order No. 5555, dated February 11 , 1931 ,
which withdrew the public lands in T. 15 N. , R. 18 W. of the New Mexico principal
meridian, New Mexico , pending a resurvey , be, and the same is hereby , revoked .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
July 3, 1933.
[No. 6193]
FRANKLIN D.
