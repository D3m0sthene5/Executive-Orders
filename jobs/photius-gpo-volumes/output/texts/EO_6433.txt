Executive Order
AUTHORIZATION TO APPOINT MRS . JOSEPHINE B. WALES
By virtue of the authority vested in me by the provisions of the last sentence
of the eighth paragraph of subdivision second of section 2 of the Civil Service Act
of January 16 , 1883 (ch . 27, 22 Stat . 403 , 404 ), it is hereby ordered that Mrs. Josephine
B. Wales may be appointed to a position in the classified civil service , without com￾pliance with the requirements of the civil -service rules .
This order is recommended by the Civil Service Commission .
THE WHITE HOUSE,
November 17 , 1933 .
[No. 6433 ]
FRANKLIN D. ROOSEVEL
