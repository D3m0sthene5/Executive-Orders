Executive Order
ALASKA ROAD COMMISSION RESERVE
Pursuant to authority conferred upon me by the Act of Congress approved
June 25 , 1910 (36 Stat. 847 ), as amended by the Act of Congress approved August
24 , 1912 (37 Stat. 497) entitled "An Act to authorize the President of the United
States to make withdrawals of public lands in certain cases, " it is hereby ordered
that the following described lands adjoining the Signal Corps Reservation at Fort
Egbert and the townsite of Eagle , Alaska , be, and the same are hereby , withdrawn
from settlement , location , sale or entry , and reserved for the use of the Alaska
Road Commission in the construction and maintenance of military and post roads ,
bridges and trails in Alaska :
Beginning at Corner Post No. 4 of the Signal Corps Reservation ,
and extending thence north 68 deg . 3' east along the boundary of said
reservation 300 feet ; thence north 21 deg . 57' west 55 feet ; thence north
68 deg . 3' east 189.5 feet ; thence south 21 deg . 57' east 225 feet to the
boundary of the townsite of Eagle ; thence south 68 deg . 3 ' west along
said boundary 489.5 feet ; thence north 21 deg . 57' west 170 feet to the
place of beginning. Area 2.16 acres , more or less .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
November 12, 1925 .
