Executive Order
ALASKA
By virtue of the authority vested in me by sections 2380 and 2381 , Revised
Statutes , Lots 3 and 4 and NE4 SE4 Sec . 13 , T. 7 S. , R. 5 E., F. M. , Alaska , are
hereby withdrawn for townsite purposes .
This order will continue in full force and effect unless and until revoked by
the President or by act of Congress .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
October 24, 1927.
