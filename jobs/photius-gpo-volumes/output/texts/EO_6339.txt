Executive Order
MICHIGAN CITY, INDIANA , ABOLISHED AS A CUSTOMS PORT OF ENTRY
By virtue of the authority vested in me by the act of August 1, 1914 (ch . 223 ,
38 Stat . 609 , 623 ; 19 U.S.C. , sec. 2), Michigan City , Indiana , is hereby abolished
as a customs port of entry in customs collection district no . 39 (Chicago ), with
headquarters at Chicago , Illinois, effective as of 30 days from the date of this order.
THE WHITE HOUSE,
October 14 , 1933.
[No. 6339 ]
FRANKLIN D
