Executive Order
AUTHORIZING THE APPOINTMENT OF WILLIAM J. SHAW TO THE POSITION
OF INSPECTOR OF CUSTOMS WITHOUT REGARD TO CIVIL-SERVICE
RULES
By virtue of and pursuant to the authority vested in me by the provisions of
paragraph Eighth of subdivision SECOND of section 2 of the Civil Service Act of
January 16 , 1883 (22 Stat. 403, 404), it is hereby ordered that William J. Shaw may
be appointed an inspector in the Customs Service of the Treasury Department at
San Francisco, California , without compliance with the requirements of the civil￾service rules .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
November 20, 1935 .
[No. 7231]
FRANKLIN
