Executive Order
It is hereby ordered , under authority contained in the Act of March 2, 1917
(39 Stat . L. , 969-976 ), that the period of trust on lands held in trust for the use and
benefit of the following named bands or villages of Mission Indians in California ,
which trust expires during the calendar year 1928, be , and is hereby, extended for
a period of ten years from the date of expiration thereof :
Campo
Augustine
Cuyapipe
Inaja
Laguna
La Posta
Manzanita
Mesa Grande
Pala
Ramona
Santa Ysabel
Sycuan
Temecula
San Manuel
This order of extension does not apply to allotments where trust patents have
been issued to individual allottees .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
November 23, 1927 .
