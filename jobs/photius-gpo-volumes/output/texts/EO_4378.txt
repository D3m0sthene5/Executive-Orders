Executive Order
Michigan
Under authority of the act of Congress approved June 25, 1910 (36 Stat. 847 ),
as amended by the act of August 24, 1912 (37 Stat. 497), it is hereby ordered that
the public lands in the following described areas in the State of Michigan be , and
they are hereby temporarily withdrawn from settlement , location , sale or entry ,
subject to any valid existing claims initiated under the public land laws , and sub￾ject to the conditions of said acts , in aid of pending legislation embodied in House
Bill No. 7482, 69th Congress , to wit :
In T. 15 N. , R. 18 W. , Mich. M. ,
Sec . 18 , lots 3, 4 and SE14 of SW 14 ;
Sec . 19 , NW14 , lots 2, 3, and NW14 of SW1/4;
Sec . 30, lots 1 and 2 .
In T. 15 N. , R. 19 W. , Mich . M. ,
Sec . 13 , all of fractional section ;
Sec . 24, lots 1 , 2 , 3 and SE14 of SE14 ;
Sec . 25 , NE14 , N1/2 of SE14 , SE14 of SW4 .
If such legislation be not enacted before the adjournment of the last session
of the 69th Congress , and no other direction is given regarding the disposition of
such lands , they will on March 5, 1927, become subject to disposal under the
method prescribed by existing law without further order .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
February 2 , 1926 .
