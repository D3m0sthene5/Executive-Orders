NATIONAL RECOVERY REVIEW BOARD

National Industrial Recovery Act of June 16 , 1933 (ch . 90, 48 Stat. 195 ), and pursuant

Board , the Federal Emergency Administration of Public Works is hereby directed to
allot to the said National Recovery Review Board from the moneys heretofore ear￾marked for the National Recovery Administration , the sum of fifty thousand dollars
