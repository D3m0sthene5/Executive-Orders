Executive Order
Appointment of Mrs. Helen Hall York
Mrs. Helen Hall York may be appointed a clerk in the classified service of the
Treasury Department without compliance with the requirements of the civil service
rules .
Mrs. York is the widow of former Prohibition Agent Lamar W. York, who met
his death while in the performance of his official duties at Washington , D. C., on April
12, 1930 , leaving Mrs. York with three small children and without adequate means of
support .
This order is recommended by the Secretary of the Treasury .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
May 12 , 1930 .
