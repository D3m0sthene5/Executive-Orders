Executive Order
Mrs. Evalyn B. Memminger may be appointed to a position in the Customs
Service without regard to the civil service rules and regulations .
As the Civil Service Commission can properly recommend waivers only when
the procedure under the rules will not fully meet the needs of the service , it is
unable to recommend this order .
For charity .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
March 16 , 1926 .
