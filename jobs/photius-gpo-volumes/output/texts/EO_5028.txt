Executive Order
It is hereby ordered , under authority contained in the act of June 21 , 1906 (34
Stats . 325-326)
, that the trust period on the allotments of the Rosebud Indians in
South Dakota, which would otherwise expire during the calendar year 1929, be ,
and is hereby, extended for a further period of ten years.
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
January 16, 1929 .
