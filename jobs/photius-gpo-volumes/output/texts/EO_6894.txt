Executive Order
AUTHORIZATION TO APPOINT MRS . HELEN K. STEBBINS
By virtue of and pursuant to the authority vested in me by the provisions of
the last sentence of the eighth paragraph of subdivision second of section 2 of the
Civil Service Act of January 16 , 1883 (ch . 27, 22 Stat . 403 , 404), it is hereby ordered
that Mrs. Helen K. Stebbins may be appointed to the position of senior stenographer ,
CAF-3 , $ 1,620 per annum base pay, in the Navy Yard, New York, N. Y., without
compliance with the requirements of the civil -service rules .
Mrs. Stebbins is the widow of Thomas C. Stebbins , chief pharmacist's mate ,
United States Navy, who met his death in line of duty at the Navy Yard , Washing￾ton, D. C. , on October 4, 1934, and is represented as being qualified to perform the
duties of the position designated .
This order is recommended by the Secretary of the Navy .
FRANKLIN D ROOSEVELT
THE WHITE HOUSE,
