Executive Order
;
Mrs. Charlotte Steele Gardner , widow of Arthur N. Gardner , deceased , an
associate cartographic engineer in the Geological Survey , who served in that office
for about twenty -six years , may be appointed to an appropriate clerical position in
the Employees ' Compensation Commission without regard to the examination
requirements of the civil service rules .
The Civil Service Commission is unable to concur in the recommendation of
the Employees ' Compensation Commission .
For charity .
9
THE WHITE HOUSE,
April 28 1928.
[No. 4868]
CALVIN COOLIDGE
