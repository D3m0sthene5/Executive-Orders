Executive Order
REINSTATEMENT OF FORMER FOREIGN SERVICE OFFICER
By virtue of and pursuant to the authority vested in me by section 12 of the act
of February 23 , 1931 , 46 Stat. 1207, 1208 , it is ordered that George T. Summerlin,
who was promoted from his position as a Foreign Service officer of Class I in the Foreign
Service of the United States on March 12 , 1925, to be Envoy Extraordinary and
Minister Plenipotentiary to Honduras , and who was subsequently appointed Envoy
Extraordinary and Minister Plenipotentiary to Venezuela and Panama, respectively ,
be, and he is hereby , reinstated in the position of Foreign Service officer of Class I,
his reinstatement to be effective , after his nomination as a Foreign Service officer of
Class I shall have been confirmed by the Senate , upon the date of his taking the oath
of office as such officer .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
July 19, 1937.
FRANKLIN D ROOSEVELT
