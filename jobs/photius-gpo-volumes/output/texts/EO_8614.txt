JAN 141941
Executive Order
AUTHORIZING INITIAL APPOINTMENTS TO THE POSITIONS OF DIRECTOR
AND ASSISTANT DIRECTOR OF THE BUREAU OF WATER CARRIERS
OF THE INTERSTATE COMMERCE COMMISSION WITHOUT COMPLIANCE
WITH THE CIVIL SERVICE RULES
By virtue of and pursuant to the authority vested in me by the provisions of
paragraph Eighth of subdivision SECOND of section 2 of the Civil Service Act of
January 16 , 1883 (22 Stat . 403 , 404 ) it is hereby ordered that, subject to the establish￾ment before the Civil Service Commission of the requisite qualifications in each case ,
the initial appointments to the positions of Director and Assistant Director of the
Bureau of Water Carriers of the Interstate Commerce Commission may be made
without compliance with the competitive provisions of the Civil Service Act and
Rules .
This order is recommended by the Interstate Commerce Commission.
FRANKLIN D ROOSEVELT
THE WHITE HOUSE,
December 18 , 1940.
