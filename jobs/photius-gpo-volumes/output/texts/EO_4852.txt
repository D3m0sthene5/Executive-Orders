Executive Order
The Executive Orders, dated April 10, 1925 (No. 4199), and June 5, 1925 (No.
4248 )
, establishing as national forests , pursuant to the provisions of Section 9 of
the Act of Congress approved June 7, 1924 (43 Stat. 655 ), certain described areas
within the respective military reservations of Camp Dix, New Jersey , and Camp
Knox, Kentucky , are hereby rescinded and the said national forests are restored to
their former status as parts of the said military reservations and are no longer
subject to use and administration for national forest purposes .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
April 6, 1928
