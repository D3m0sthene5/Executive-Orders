Executive Order
1
Mrs. Mary C. Duff may be appointed as clerk in the Philadelphia , Pennsylvania ,
post office without regard to the requirements of the civil service rules .
Mrs. Duff is the wife of Francis Duff , who for a number of years was an efficient
and faithful employee of the Philadelphia post office and while in the service sus￾tained an injury which has totally incapacitated him for work . Mrs. Duff has passed
the required examination for post office clerk but her name has not yet been reached
for certification for appointment.
This order is recommended by the Postmaster General. The Civil Service Com￾mission cannot concur, its recommendations for waivers being limited to those cases
where the needs of the service cannot be met by the procedure under the rules .
For charity .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
CALVIN COOLIDGE
September 12, 1928.
