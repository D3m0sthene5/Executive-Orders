Executive Order
Mrs. Grace S. E. McComb may be employed as a clerk in the Navy Depart￾ment , Washington , D. C. , without regard to the civil service act or rules .
Mrs. McComb's husband was formerly a Lieutenant Commander in the U. S.
Navy, and met his death in line of duty , in a plane crash at the Naval Air Station ,
Pearl Harbor , T. H., November 26 , 1927.
This Order is issued upon recommendation of the Secretary of the Navy .
The Civil Service Commission is unable to concur in this recommendation .
Done to honor the memory of a sailor and officer of the Navy .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
February 13 , 1928 .
