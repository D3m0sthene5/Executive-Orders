Executive Order
DESIGNATION OF MRS. LEAFIE E. DIETZ TO SIGN LAND PATENTS
Mrs. Leafie E. Dietz , of Colorado, appointed by the Secretary of the Interior
January 6, 1931 , to the position of clerk to sign land patents, is hereby designated
as required by law to sign my name to said patents .
FRANKLIN D ROOSEVELT
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
March 18, 1933 .
