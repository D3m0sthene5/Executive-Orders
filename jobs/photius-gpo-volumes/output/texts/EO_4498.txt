Executive Order
Pursuant to the authority contained in Section 179 of the Revised Statutes, I
hereby authorize and direct William P. MacCracken , Jr. , Assistant Secretary of
Commerce , appointed under the authority of the Act of May 20, 1926, to perform
the duties of the Secretary of Commerce during the absence of the Secretary of
Commerce, and the Assistant Secretary of Commerce appointed under the Act of
February 14 , 1903 , as amended by the Act of March 4, 1913. During the absence
of the Secretary of Commerce , and of the Assistant Secretaries of Commerce ap￾pointed under the Act of February 14 , 1903 (as amended by the Act of March 4,
1913) , and the Act of May 20 , 1926, I hereby authorize and direct Stephen B. Davis ,
Solicitor for the Department of Commerce, to perform the duties of the Secretary
of Commerce .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
