FLORIDA
It is hereby ordered that Executive order of August 26, 1880 , reserving a right
of way at Haulover Canal , Florida , connecting the waters of Indian River and
Mosquito Lagoon for a distance of at least 1000 feet on each side of the center

(10 Stat .
