Executive Order
ESTABLISHING LOSTWOOD MIGRATORY WATERFOWL REFUGE
NORTH DAKOTA
By virtue of and pursuant to the authority vested in me as President of the
United States , and in order to effectuate further the purposes of the Migratory Bird
Conservation Act (45 Stat . 1222 ), it is ordered that the following-described lands,
acquired or to be acquired by the United States , in Burke and Mountrail Counties,
North Dakota , consisting of 33,045 acres , more or less , be , and they are hereby,
reserved and set apart for the use of the Department of Agriculture , subject to valid
existing rights, as a refuge and breeding ground for migratory birds and other wild
life : Provided , That any private lands within the areas described shall become a part
of the refuge hereby established upon the acquisition of title or lease thereto by the
United States :
FIFTH PRINCIPAL MERIDIAN
T. 158 N. , R. 91 W. , secs . 3 to 10 , inclusive ;
secs . 17 to 20, inclusive.
T. 159 N. , R. 91 W. , secs . 3 to 10 , inclusive ;
secs . 15 to 18 , inclusive ;
secs . 20 , 21 , and 22 ;
sec . 26 , NWSW ;
secs . 27 , 28 , and 29;
secs . 32 , 33 , and 34 .
T. 160 N. , R. 91 W. , secs . 19 to 23 , inclusive ;
secs . 25 to 35 , inclusive.
T. 158 N. , R. 92 W. , secs . 13 and 24.
T. 159 N. , R. 92 W. , secs . 11 to 14 , inclusive .
This refuge shall be known as the Lostwood Migratory Waterfowl Refuge .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
September 4, 1935.
