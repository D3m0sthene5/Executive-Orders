ROOSEVELT NATIONAL FOREST
COLORADO
In order to avoid the confusion arising from the fact that one of the several
national forests in the State of Colorado bears the name of that State, it is hereby
ordered that the name of the " Colorado National Forest , " as defined by proclama￾tion of June 12 , 1917 (40 Stat . 1671 ) , and subsequently modified by acts of Congress ,
be changed to " Roosevelt National Forest " in honor of Theodore Roosevelt, twenty￾sixth President of the United States .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
March 28 , 1932.
