Executive Order
Mr. Samuel Hunter Griffin may be reinstated as a clerk in the Bureau of Yards
and Docks , Navy Department , without regard to the length of his separation from
the service .
This order is issued upon recommendation of the Secretary of the Navy .
Mr. Griffin was originally employed under the Naval Establishment on July 3 ,
1893 , and rendered excellent services for twenty-eight years .
The Civil Service Commission is unable to concur in this recommendation .
For good of service .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
October 14, 1927.
CALVIN COOLIDGE
