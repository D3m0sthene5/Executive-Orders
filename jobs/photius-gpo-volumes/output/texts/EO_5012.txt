Executive Order
Miss Flora Wilson may be appointed to an appropriate position in the classi￾fied service without regard to the civil service rules .
Miss Wilson is the daughter of the late James Wilson , Secretary of Agriculture
for sixteen years . She is a graduate of the Iowa Agricultural College, and in
addition to the experience as official hostess for her father while he was Secretary
of Agriculture she has traveled and studied extensively in Europe . She has been
a frequent contributor to leading magazines and newspapers . Her education and
experience seem to fit her for capable and efficient service .
The Civil Service Commission is without authority to recommend this order .
For charity .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
December 18 , 1928.
