Executive Order
ESTABLISHING LACREEK MIGRATORY WATERFOWL REFUGE
SOUTH DAKOTA
By virtue of and pursuant to the authority vested in me as President of the
United States , and in order to effectuate further the purposes of the Migratory Bird
Conservation Act (45 Stat . 1222 ), it is ordered that the following-described lands,
acquired or to be acquired by the United States, in Bennett County , South Dakota ,
consisting of 9,362 acres , more or less , be, and they are hereby , reserved and set
apart for the use of the Department of Agriculture , subject to valid existing rights,
as a refuge and breeding ground for migratory birds and other wildlife : Provided ,
that any private lands within the areas described shall become a part of the refuge
hereby established upon the acquisition of title or lease thereto by the United States :
