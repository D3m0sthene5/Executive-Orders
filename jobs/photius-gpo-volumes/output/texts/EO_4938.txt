Executive Order
NEW MEXICO
It is hereby ordered that Executive Order No. 4232, dated May 25, 1925,
withdrawing the public lands in T. 18 N., R. 12 E. , N. M. P. M., New Mexico ,
pending a resurvey, be and the same is hereby revoked .
All of said lands are in the Santa Fe National Forest , having been reserved for
forest purposes by proclamations of January 11 , 1892 , May 27 , 1898 and January
28 , 1909 and Executive order of April 6, 1915, which remain in full force and effect
as to said lands .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
July 23, 1928 .
