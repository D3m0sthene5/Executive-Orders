Executive Order
California
It is hereby ordered that Executive Order No. 4323 , dated October 15 , 1925,
withdrawing the public lands in T. 15 S. , R. 4 E., S. B. M. , California , pending a
resurvey , be and the same is hereby revoked .
All of said lands are in the Cleveland National Forest having been reserved for
forest purposes by Proclamation of February 14, 1907, which remains in full force
and effect .
THE WHITE HOUSE,
March 21 , 1928 .
