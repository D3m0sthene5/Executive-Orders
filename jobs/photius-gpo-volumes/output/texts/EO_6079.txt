Executive Order
DESIGNATION OF MRS . HELEN V. MCLEOD TO SIGN LAND PATENTS
Mrs. Helen V. McLeod , of the General Land Office , Department of the Interior ,
is hereby designated to sign land patents during the absence of the clerk regularly
designated for that purpose .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
March 18 , 1933 .
FRANKLIN D ROOSEVELT
