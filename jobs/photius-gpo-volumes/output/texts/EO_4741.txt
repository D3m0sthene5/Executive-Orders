Executive Order
ALASKA
ELIMINATING TRACT FROM EXECUTIVE ORDER
No. 1967 -A
Under and pursuant to the provisions of the act of Congress approved March
12, 1914 (39 Stat.
, 305) entitled "AN ACT to authorize the President of the United
States to locate , construct and operate railroads in the Territory of Alaska and for
other purposes ," it is hereby ordered that Lot 10, Sec . 3 , T. 1 S. , R. 1 W. , F. M. ,
Alaska , withdrawn by Executive Order No. 1967 -A, dated June 23, 1914, and
reserved for townsite purposes under said act be eliminated from said order .
This tract of land is hereby restored to the public domain and will be made
subject to entry under the homestead laws applicable to Alaska and under the terms
and conditions of Public Resolution No. 29, approved February 14, 1920 (41 Stat .
,
434), and Public Resolution No. 36, approved January 21 , 1922 (42 Stat .
, 358 )
,
on a date to be hereafter fixed by the Commissioner of the General Land Office .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
October 10, 1927 .
