Executive Order
Withdrawing certain lands in Box Elder and Weber Counties , Utah , for the
Bear River Migratory Bird Refuge
Under authority of the Act of Congress approved June 25, 1910 (36 Stat . 847 ) ,
as amended by the Act of August 24, 1912 (37 Stat. 497), and on the recommenda￾tion of the Secretary of Agriculture , it is hereby ordered that all public lands ,
whether surveyed or unsurveyed , in the following described areas in Utah , be and
the same are hereby temporarily withdrawn from settlement , location, sale or entry,
subject to the conditions, provisions, and limitations of said acts in order that the
Department of Agriculture may select certain lands therein for use in connection
with the establishment of the Bear River Migratory Bird Refuge authorized by the
Act of April 23 , 1928 -Public No. 304-70th Congress :
In Township 7 North, Ranges 2 and 3 West ; in Township 8 North, Ranges 2,
3, 4 and 5 West ; in Township 9 North , Ranges 2, 3, 4 and 5 West, and in Town￾ship 10 North , Ranges 3, 4, and 5 West, Salt Lake Meridian .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
July 23, 1928 .
