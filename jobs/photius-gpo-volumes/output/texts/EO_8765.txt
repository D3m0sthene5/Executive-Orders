JUN 251941
Executive Order
AUTHORIZING THE APPOINTMENT OF ALEXANDER M. KNAUSS TO THE
POSITION OF JUNIOR HISTORICAL AIDE AT THE VANDERBILT MANSION
NATIONAL HISTORIC SITE , NATIONAL PARK SERVICE , DEPARTMENT OF
THE INTERIOR, WITHOUT REGARD TO THE CIVIL SERVICE RULES
By virtue of the authority vested in me by the provisions of paragraph Eighth
of subdivision SECOND of section 2 of the Civil Service Act (22 Stat . 403, 404), it
is hereby ordered that Alexander M. Knauss may be appointed to the position of
Junior Historical Aide at the Vanderbilt Mansion National Historic Site, National
Park Service , Department of the Interior , without compliance with the requirements
of the Civil Service Rules .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
June 3, 1941 .
