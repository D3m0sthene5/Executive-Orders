Executive Order
Mrs. Constance Ely may be appointed to an appropriate position in the
Department of Commerce without regard to the provisions of the civil service rules .
She had short previous service in the War Department .
The Acting Secretary of Commerce desires to appoint her in that Department .
The Civil Service Commission is unable to concur .
For charity .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
February 28 , 1928.
