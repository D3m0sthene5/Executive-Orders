Executive Order
By virtue of the authority vested in me as President of the United States of
America , and pursuant to the provisions of Section three of the Act of Congress
approved June 7, 1926 (Public No. 345-69th Congress ), entitled "An Act To au￾thorize the disposition of lands no longer needed for naval purposes ," the Secretary
of Commerce by letters of February 26, 1926 , and June 17 , 1926, having made
application to the Secretary of the Navy for the transfer thereof and given specific
reasons therefor, within ninety days after the approval of the above mentioned Act
of Congress , and such action having received my approval, it is hereby ordered that
the portion of the Long Point Military Reservation , Provincetown , Massachusetts ,
hereinafter described , be and the same is hereby transferred to the Department of
Commerce for use as a lighthouse reservation :
Beginning at a stone post set in the ground 358 feet North 55° 15 ′
East from Wood End Light Tower , thence South 22 ° 4′ West 348
feet to a stone bound , thence continuing South 22 ° 4' West 200 feet ,
more or less , to low water line, thence westerly 202 feet , more or
less , by low water line to a point 168 feet , more or less , South 22° 4 ′
West from a stone post, thence North 22° 4′ East 168 feet , more
or less , to said stone post, thence continuing North 22° 4' East
348 feet to a stone post, thence continuing North 22° 4′ East 88 feet ,
more or less , to high water line, thence Easterly 402 feet , more or less,
by said high water line to a point 144 feet , more or less , North 22 ° 4 ′
East from the stone bound first above mentioned , thence South 22 ° 4 ′
West 144 feet, more or less , to the point of beginning , meaning to
convey a strip of land 400 feet wide from low water line on southerly
side of Wood End to high water line on the Provincetown Harbor
side of said point , together with a right of way for vehicles and foot
people by the most desirable way from the station to and from
Provincetown .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
CALVIN COOLIDGE
September 20, 1926 .
