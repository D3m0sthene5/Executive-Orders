Executive Order
Appointment of Mrs. Mabel V. Birch
Mrs. Mabel V. Birch may be appointed to a classified position in the Treasury
Department without compliance with the requirements of the civil service rules .
Mrs. Birch is the widow of Platt H. Birch , who served very efficiently in the
Treasury Department from February 6 , 1919 , to the date of his death on January 3 ,
1930. Before his appointment in the Treasury , Mr. Birch served in other branches
of the Government from May , 1911 , to February , 1919. Mrs. Birch and three minor
children have been left without adequate means of support.
This order is recommended by the Secretary of the Treasury .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
February 21 , 1930 .
