Executive Order
Civil Service Rule II , paragraph 6, is hereby amended to read as follows , by
the addition at the end thereof of the words underscored :
6. Except as provided in section 7 of this rule, a person holding a
position when it becomes classified and subject to competitive examina￾tion shall have all the rights which he would acquire if appointed
thereto upon examination under these rules, but he shall not be
transferred without first passing the examination provided by the
Commission ; and no person shall acquire any rights hereunder unless
the Civil Service Commission is satisfied that he was not appointed
for the purpose of acquiring such rights .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
October 26 , 1927.
