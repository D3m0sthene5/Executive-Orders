Executive Order
Pursuant to provisions of The Alaska Railroad act of March 12, 1914 (38
Stat .
, 305), and a Joint Resolution (H. J. 96 ), approved March 3, 1927 (Private
Resolution No. 6, 69th Congress , 2nd Session ), and upon the recommendation of
the Secretary of the Interior, authority is hereby given the General Manager of
The Alaska Railroad to enter into agreements with surgeons for their services
under its hospital regulations, including a division of their fees earned and collected
in private cases and other cases not entitled to free treatment, and to pay from
hospital receipts or other funds of the railroad the amounts due under such agree￾ments and any similar agreements heretofore existing .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
CALVIN COOLIDGE
March 19, 1927
