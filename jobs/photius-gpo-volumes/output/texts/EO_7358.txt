Executive Order
REINSTATEMENT OF CERTAIN FORMER FOREIGN SERVICE OFFICERS
By virtue of and pursuant to the authority vested in me by Section 12 of the
Act of February 23 , 1931 , 46 Stat . 1207, 1208, it is ordered that Earl L. Packer and
Vinton Chapin , who resigned their positions as officers in the Foreign Service of the
United States on May 1 , 1929 , and April 15 , 1932 , respectively, to accept appoint￾ments to positions in the Department of State, be, and they are hereby , reinstated in
the Foreign Service of the United States in the classes indicated below, their rein￾statement to be effective upon the date their nominations to positions in such classes
are confirmed by the Senate :
Earl L. Packer , of Utah , Foreign Service officer , Class IV.
Vinton Chapin , of Massachusetts, Foreign Service officer , Class VI .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
May 5, 1936 .
[No. 7358]
FRANKLIN
