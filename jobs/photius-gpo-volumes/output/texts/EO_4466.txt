Executive Order
Effective July 1 , 1926, the heads of departments and establishments as defined
in the Subsistence Expense Act of 1926 , approved June 3 , 1926 (Public No. 328 ) ,
may, in directing travel , fix subsistence allowances at rates not exceeding those
provided in that Act, subject to the provisions of section 13 thereof, and may
operate under their respective existing lawful regulations relating to per diem
allowance and subsistence expenses , so far as applicable, until such time as standard
regulations are approved .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
June 29, 1926 .
