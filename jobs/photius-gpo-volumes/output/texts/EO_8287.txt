Executive Order
EXEMPTION OF ARTHUR H. CHASE FROM COMPULSORY RETIREMENT
FOR AGE
WHEREAS, in my judgment , the public interest requires that Arthur H. Chase ,
senior ordnance engineer , Navy Department , who was exempted from compulsory
retirement for age for a period of one year by Executive Order No. 8011 of November
19 , 1938, be further exempted from such compulsory retirement for a period of one
year :
NOW, THEREFORE , by virtue of and pursuant to the authority vested in me
by section 204 of the act of June 30, 1932, 47 Stat . 382, 404 (U. S. C. , title 5, sec .
715a), I hereby further exempt the said Arthur H. Chase from compulsory retirement
for age for a period of one year ending November 30, 1940 .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
November 18 , 1939.
[No. 8287 ]
