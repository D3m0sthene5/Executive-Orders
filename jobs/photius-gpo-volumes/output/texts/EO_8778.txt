Executive Order
EXEMPTION OF PERCY NICHOLLS FROM COMPULSORY RETIREMENT FOR AGE
WHEREAS, in my judgment, the public interest requires that Percy Nicholls ,
heat transmission engineer , Bureau of Mines , Department of the Interior , who was
exempted from compulsory retirement for age for a period of one year by Executive
Order No. 8401 of May 7, 1940, be further exempted from such compulsory retire￾ment for a period of one year :
NOW, THEREFORE , by virtue of and pursuant to the authority vested in
me by section 204 of the act of June 30, 1932 , 47 Stat . 382, 404 (U. S. C. , title 5,
sec . 715a)
, I hereby further exempt the said Percy Nicholls from compulsory retire￾ment for age for a period of one year ending June 30, 1942.
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
June 11 , 1941 .
[No. 8778]
FRANKLIN D ROOS
