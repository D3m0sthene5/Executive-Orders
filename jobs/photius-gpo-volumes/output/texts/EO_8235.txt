Executive Order
AUTHORIZING THE APPOINTMENT OF MRS . COEY CUSTER JONES TO A
CLASSIFIED POSITION IN THE PUBLIC HEALTH SERVICE , FEDERAL
SECURITY AGENCY, WITHOUT REGARD TO THE CIVIL SERVICE RULES
By virtue of and pursuant to the authority vested in me by the provisions of
paragraph Eighth , subdivision SECOND , section 2 of the Civil Service Act (22
Stat. 403, 404 )
, it is hereby ordered that Mrs. Coey Custer Jones may be appointed
to an appropriate classified position in the Public Health Service , Federal Security
Agency , without compliance with the requirements of the Civil Service rules .
Mrs. Jones is the widow of Surgeon Roy R. Jones , a commissioned medical
officer of the Public Health Service , who died on June 13 , 1939, leaving his widow
and three children without adequate means of continued support.
This order is recommended by the Surgeon General of the Public Health Service .
FRANKLIN D ROOSEVELT
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
Sept 6, 1939 .
