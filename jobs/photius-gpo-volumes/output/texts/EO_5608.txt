Executive Order
Designation of Sasabe , Arizona , as a Customs Port of Entry
By virtue of the authority vested in me by the act of Congress approved August
1, 1914 (38 Stat. 623), making appropriations for the sundry civil expenses of the
Government for the fiscal year ended June 30, 1915 , the customs station of Sasabe ,
Ariz .
, is hereby designated a customs port of entry in Customs Collection District
No. 26 (Arizona ) with headquarters at Nogales , Ariz .
, effective 30 days from the date
of this order.
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
HERBERT HOOVER
April 22 , 1931 .
