Executive Order
Mrs. Marian H. Boothe may be reinstated in the classified service as a stenog￾rapher and typewriter under the Navy Department , without regard to the length
of her separation from the service .
She served as a stenographer and typewriter in the Interior Department from
1918 to 1922 when she resigned , having married Ensign Eaton Alexander Boothe
who met his death in the line of duty , as a Lieutenant in the United States Navy,
September 7, 1927, in an airplane accident near Palmdale , California .
This order is recommended by the Secretary of the Navy. The Civil Service
Commission submits the case without prejudice .
For charity .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
March 2 , 1928.
