Executive Order
Mrs. Jessie H. Collier may be appointed to an appropriate position in or under
the Post Office Department without regard to the requirements of the civil service
rules .
Mrs. Collier is the widow of Darsie A. Collier , forty years an employee of the
Post Office Department, who died on February 26 , 1926 , leaving her without adequate
means of support.
This order is issued upon the recommendation of the Postmaster General .
The Civil Service Commission is unable to concur.
For charity .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
March 20, 1928 .
[No. 4836]
CALVIN COOLIDGE
