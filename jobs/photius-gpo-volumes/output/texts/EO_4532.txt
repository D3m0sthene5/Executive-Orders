Executive Order
NEVADA
Under authority of the act of Congress approved June 25 , 1910 (36 Stat .
, 847) ,
as amended by the act of August 24, 1912 (37 Stat .
, 497), it is hereby ordered that
the SE14 NE14 Sec . 12 , T. 24 N. , R. 30 E., M. D. M., in Nevada , containing 40
acres , be and the same is hereby temporarily withdrawn , subject to the provisions
and conditions of said acts, pending archaeological examination by the Smithsonian
Institution of the cave located therein.
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
October 29, 1926.
