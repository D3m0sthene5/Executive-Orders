Executive Order
Colorado
Under authority contained in the act of Congress approved June 25, 1910 (36
Stat .
, 847 )
, as amended by the act of August 24, 1912 (37 Stat.
, 497) , and subject
to the conditions , provisions and limitations of said acts , it is hereby ordered that
the public lands in T. 49 N. , R. 12 E. , N. M. P. M., Colorado , be and the same are
hereby withdrawn from settlement , location, sale , entry and all forms of appropri￾ation, pending a resurvey of said township under authority contained in the act of
Congress approved March 3 , 1909 (35 Stat.
, 845)
; this withdrawal to remain in
full force and effect from the date hereof until the resurvey is duly approved and
accepted and the approved plat thereof is officially filed in the proper United States
local land office , unless another date be fixed by the Commissioner of the General
Land Office , whereupon this withdrawal will become of no effect .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
October 15, 1925.
