Executive Order
Mrs. Martha Fleeson may be appointed as a laborer in the Post Office at Boston,
Massachusetts, without regard to the requirements of the civil service rules .
Mrs. Fleeson is the widow of Mr. James W. Fleeson, for more than 25 years an
employee of the Boston Post Office , who died January 18 , 1926, leaving her with a
dependent daughter, 12 years old .
As the Civil Service Commission can properly recommend waivers only when
the procedure under the rules will not fully meet the needs of the service , it is unable
to concur in the recommendation of the Postmaster General for the issuance of this
Order .
For charity .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
April 23, 1926
[No. 4431]
CALVIN COOLIDGE
