Executive Order
Mrs. Arlene K. Mix may be employed as a clerk in the Headquarters ,
U. S. Marine Corps, Washington , D. C., without regard to the Civil Service
Act or rules .
This order is issued upon recommendation of the Secretary of the Navy .
Mrs. Mix' husband was formerly a Master Technical Sergeant in the U. S.
Marine Corps, and met his death in line of duty in an aeroplane accident in
the month of April, 1926.
The Civil Service Commission is unable to concur in this recommendation .
Done for charity .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
January 12, 1927.
CALVIN COOLIDGE
