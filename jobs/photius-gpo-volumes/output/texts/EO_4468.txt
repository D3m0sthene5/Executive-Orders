Executive Order
Nevada
Under authority contained in the act of Congress approved June 25 , 1910
(36 Stat .
, 847 ) , as amended by the act of August 24, 1912 (37 Stat .
, 497), and sub￾ject to the conditions , provisions and limitations of said acts , it is hereby ordered
that the public lands in Ts . 36 , 37 , 38 and 39 N. , R. 48 E., M. D. M. , Nevada , be
and the same are hereby withdrawn from settlement, location , sale , entry and all
forms of appropriation pending a resurvey of said townships under the act of Con￾gress approved March 3, 1909 (35 Stat .
, 845 )
, as amended by the Joint Resolution
of June 25 , 1910 (36 Stat.
, 884 )
; this withdrawal to remain in full force and effect
from the date hereof until the resurvey is duly approved and accepted and the
approved plats thereof are officially filed in the proper United States district land
office , unless another date be fixed by the Commissioner of the General Land Office ,
whereupon this withdrawal will become of no effect .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
June 30 , 1926.
