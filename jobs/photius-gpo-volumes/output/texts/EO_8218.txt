Executive Order
SEP 5-1939
HARVARD LAW LIBRARY
EXEMPTION OF HERBERT VANSANT FROM COMPULSORY RETIREMENT
FOR AGE
WHEREAS, in my judgment, the public interest requires that Herbert Vansant ,
senior civil engineer , Engineer Department at Large, War Department , who , during
the month of August , 1939 , will reach the retirement age prescribed for automatic
separation from the service , applicable to him, be exempted from compulsory retire￾ment for age for a period of one year:
NOW , THEREFORE , by virtue of and pursuant to the authority vested in me
by section 204 of the act of June 30, 1932 , 47 Stat . 382 , 404 (U. S. C. , title 5 , sec .
715a ), I hereby exempt the said Herbert Vansant from compulsory retirement for
age for a period of one year ending August 31 , 1940 .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
Aug 7th , 1939.
FRANKLIN D ROOSEVELT
