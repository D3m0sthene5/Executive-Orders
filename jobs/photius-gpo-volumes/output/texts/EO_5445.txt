Extension of Port Limits of Marshfield , Oregon
By virtue of the authority vested in me by the act of Congress approved August
1, 1914 (38 Stat . 623), making appropriations for the sundry civil expenses of the
Government for the fiscal year ended June 30, 1915, the limits of the port of Marsh￾field , Oreg .
