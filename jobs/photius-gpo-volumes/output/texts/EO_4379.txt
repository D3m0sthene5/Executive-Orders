Executive Order
Mrs. Mabel A. Murphy may be reinstated as an operative in the Bureau of
Engraving and Printing without regard to the length of time she has been separated
from the service .
This order is issued upon the recommendation of the Secretary of the Treasury ,
who states that her husband , formerly employed by the Bureau of Engraving and
Printing , has made application for retirement on account of total disability and
leaves Mrs. Murphy with two daughters to support with no resources to speak of.
She was employed in the Bureau of Engraving and Printing and the Office of the
Treasurer of the United States from July 13 , 1899 , to November 7, 1907 .
As the Civil Service Commission can properly recommend waivers only when
the procedure under the rules will not fully meet the needs of the service , it is
unable to concur .
Done for charity .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
February 4, 1926 .
