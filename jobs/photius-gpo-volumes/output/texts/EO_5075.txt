Executive Order
Mrs. William M. Stuart (nee Sarah V. Kenner ) may be appointed to an
appropriate clerical position in the Federal Service without regard to the length of
her separation therefrom.
Mrs. Stuart was in the competitive classified service from July 1 , 1902 , to
December 31 , 1918 .
For charity .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
March 4, 1929 .
