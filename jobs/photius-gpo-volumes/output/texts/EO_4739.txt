Executive Order
By virtue of the authority vested in me as President of the United States of
America , and pursuant to the provisions of Section 6 of the Act of Congress approved
March 12, 1926, entitled " An Act authorizing the use for permanent construction
at military posts of the proceeds from the sale of surplus War Department real
property, and authorizing the sale of certain military reservations , and for other
purposes , " (Public No. 45-69th Congress )
, the Secretary of Commerce having made
application to the Secretary of War for the transfer thereof and given specific
reasons therefor, within ninety days after the approval of the above mentioned
Act of Congress , and such action having received my approval , it is hereby ordered
that the portion of the Fort Barrancas Military Reservation , in the State of Florida ,
hereinafter described , be and the same is hereby transferred to the Department of
Commerce for use as a lighthouse reservation :
From initial point on eastern boundary line of Fort Barrancas
Military Reservation (marked by a large square granite stone , near the
shore of Pensacola Bay , northeast from the Quartermaster Wharf )
the azimuth and distance to the initial point of the parcel is 270° 15 ′
03 " , 6612 feet .
From Pensacola Lighthouse the azimuth and distance to the ini￾tial point of the parcel is 270° 15 ′ 03 ″, 600 feet .
From the initial point on the western boundary of the parcel the
line extends true south to the shore of Pensacola Bay , about 1625 feet .
From the initial point last above described the line extends true
north 705 feet to a point which marks the northwest corner of the
parcel . Thence 90° 15 ′ 03 ″ true, 2400 feet to a point which marks the
northeast corner of the parcel. Thence true South to the shore of
Pensacola Bay about 1070 feet .
Thence along the shore of Pensacola Bay as the same winds and
turns to the point where the first course intersects the shore line; con￾taining approximately 75 acres , more or less .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
October 5, 1927 .
