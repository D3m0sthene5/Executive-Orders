Executive Order
WASHINGTON
Under authority contained in the act of June 25, 1910, (36 Stat . 847) , as
amended by the act of August 24, 1912, ( 37 Stat. 497), and subject to the condi￾tions , provisions and limitations of said acts , it is hereby ordered that the public
lands in Secs . 6, 8 and 18 , T. 29 N. , R. 38 E., all of T. 30 N. , R. 38 E., and Secs .
20 , 24 and 30, T. 32 N. , R. 39 E., W. M. , Washington , be, and the same are hereby
withdrawn from settlement , location , sale , entry and all forms of appropriation
pending a resurvey of said townships under the act of March 3, 1927 (44 Stat .
1370) .
This order shall continue in full force and effect unless and until revoked by
the President or by an act of Congress .
THE WHITE HOUSE,
September 27 , 1927.

CALVIN COOLIDGE
