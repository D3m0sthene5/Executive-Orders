Executive Order
Alexander Scruton may be permanently appointed as deputy collector and
inspector of customs without compliance with the requirements of the civil serv￾ice rules .
This order is recommended by the Secretary of the Treasury , who states that
Mr. Scruton has filled temporary assignments as deputy collector and inspector of
customs in the absence of a Civil Service register , that his services have been
very satisfactory and that his permanent appointment will be in the interest of the
service .
In view of the highly successful performance of his work in a difficult position
for more than three years , during which time frequent examinations have failed
to secure sufficient eligibles to meet the needs of the service , the Commission sub￾mits the case without prejudice .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
December 5, 1927 .
