Executive Order
The Executive Order of March 30, 1928 relating to the Assignment of Frequen￾cies to Government Radio Stations is hereby amended to permit the additional use
of frequencies now assigned by that Order to the Army as follows :
(a) The frequencies 4030 , 8060, 12090, 16120, 20150 , now assigned to Ketch￾ikan, Los Angeles , Monterey , Cal.
, are also assigned to the Philippine
Department for communication with the American Forces in China ,
Hawaiian Department and the United States .
(b) The frequencies 4080 , 8160, 12240, 16320 , 20400, now assigned to Fort
Lawton #1 , are also assigned to the Hawaiian Department for communica￾tion with the Philippine Department , the American Forces in China , the
Panama Canal Department and the United States .
(c) The frequencies 4365, 8730, 13095, 17460, now assigned to Seward #1 , are
also assigned to the Panama Canal Department for communication with
the Hawaiian Department and the United States .
(d) The frequencies 4430, 8860, 13290, 17720, now assigned to Fairbanks , Fort
Lincoln , Fort Riley, Boston, are also assigned to the American Forces in
China for communication with the Philippine Department , Hawaiian
Department and the United States .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
June 4, 1928 .
