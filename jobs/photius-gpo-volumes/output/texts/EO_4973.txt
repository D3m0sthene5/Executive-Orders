Executive Order
TONGASS NATIONAL FOREST
ALASKA
Under authority of the act of Congress approved June 4, 1897 (30 Stat . 34-36 ),
and on the recommendation of the Secretary of Agriculture , it is hereby ordered
that the tract of land on Sarkar Cove, in Alaska , lying within the hereinafter described
boundaries , heretofore occupied as a dwelling site and fishing headquarters , be , and
the same is hereby, excluded from the Tongass National Forest and restored to
entry under the applicable public land laws :
Beginning at corner No. 1 , approximately in latitude 55° 57′ N. ,
longitude 133 ° 15 ′ W. , a 4 × 4 × 5′ spruce post set 24 " in the ground
at mean high tide line marked HS 1 MC; thence west along the shore
of Sarkar Cove 3.00 chains ; thence S. 71 ° W. 2.20 chains to corner
No. 2 on a large marble boulder ; thence N. 83 /½° W. 6.27 chains to
corner No. 3 , a 4 × 4 × 5' spruce post set 24" in the ground marked
HS 3 MC on said Cove ; thence north 3.03 chains to corner No. 4, a
4 × 4 × 5′ spruce post set 24" in the ground marked HS 4 ; thence east
11.26 chains to corner No. 5, a 4 × 4 × 5' spruce post set 24 " in the
ground marked HS 5; thence south 3.03 chains to corner No. 1 , place
of beginning , containing approximately 3.7 acres .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
September 28 , 1928 .
