Executive Order
APPOINTMENT OF MRS. MARY A. MCCLUNG
Mrs. Marý A. McClung may be appointed to an appropriate position in the
Department of Agriculture without regard to the Civil Service rules .
Mrs. McClung has been employed in an excepted position in a confidential

This order is recommended by the Secretary of Agriculture .
