PROPERTY OF
Executive Order JUL 191908
HARVARD LAWURD
AUTHORIZING THE APPOINTMENT OF MRS . ELEANOR J. TOWNSEND TO A
CLASSIFIED POSITION IN THE SECURITIES AND EXCHANGE COMMISSION
WITHOUT REGARD TO THE CIVIL SERVICE RULES
By virtue of and pursuant to the authority vested in me by the provisions of
paragraph Eighth , subdivision SECOND , section 2 of the Civil Service Act (22 Stat .
403 , 404), it is hereby ordered that Mrs. Eleanor J. Townsend may be appointed to a
classified position in the Securities and Exchange Commission without compliance
with the requirements of the Civil Service rules .
This order is recommended by the Chairman of the Securities and Exchange
Commission .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
June 25, 1938.
FRANKLIN D ROOSEV
