Executive Order
Paragraph 121 of the Consular Regulations of 1896 , providing for the acknowl￾edgment of all instructions on Form 7a, is canceled .
Paragraph 221 of the Consular Regulations concerning Form 126, is hereby
canceled .
In Paragraph 604 of the Consular Regulations of 1896, in the section entitled
"At inland consulates " , section 5, relating to the passport book , is canceled .
Paragraph 533, Tariff of Fees , Item No. 42, second paragraph, is amended to
read as follows :
" In connection with any service rendered outside of the consular office
at the request of private individuals , the exact amount of the expenses
actually and necessarily incurred by the person rendering the service shall be
collected from the persons for whom the service is performed in addition to the
fee or fees prescribed therefor, but no amount in excess of the fee or fees pre￾scribed and such actual and necessary expenses shall be charged or accepted " .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
August 8, 1927.
