Executive Order
Mr. James W. Girard may be reinstated as Logging Engineer , $ 3800 per
annum , in the Forest Service , without regard to the time limit on eligibility for
reinstatement fixed by the rules . In view of the high standing, peculiar fitness ,
and need of the service for a man of his qualifications , the Commission concurs in
the recommendation of the Secretary of Agriculture that the reinstatement of
Mr. Girard be authorized .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
March 2 , 1929 .
