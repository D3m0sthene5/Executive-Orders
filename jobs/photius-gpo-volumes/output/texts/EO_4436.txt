Executive Order
Ouachita National Forest
Arkansas
Under authority of the Act of Congress of June 4, 1897 (30 Stat .
, 11), and upon
recommendation of the Secretary of Agriculture , it is hereby ordered that the
Arkansas National Forest , Arkansas , fixed and defined by proclamation dated
December 18, 1907 , and modified by proclamations dated February 27, 1909 ,
September 26 , 1910 , February 23 , 1916 , October 19 , 1920 , and Executive Order of
April 21 , 1914 , is hereby designated and shall hereafter be known as the Ouachita
National Forest.
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
April 29, 1926.
