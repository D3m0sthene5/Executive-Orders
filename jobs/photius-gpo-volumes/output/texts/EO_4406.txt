Executive Order
It is hereby ordered , under authority contained in Section 5 of the Act of
February 8, 1887 (24 Stat. L., 388) that the trust period on the allotments of
Indians on the Yankton Sioux Reservation , South Dakota , which expires during
the calendar year 1926, be , and is hereby extended for a further period of ten years .
THE WHITE HOUSE
March 30, 1926 .
