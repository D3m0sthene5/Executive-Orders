Executive Order
WITHDRAWAL OF PUBLIC LANDS FOR RESURVEY
ARIZONA
By virtue of and pursuant to the authority vested in me by the act of June 25 ,
1910 (ch . 421 , 36 Stat. 847), as amended by the act of August 24, 1912 (ch . 369 , 37
Stat. 497)
, and subject to the conditions therein expressed and to all valid existing
rights , it is ordered that the public lands in township 12 north , range 3 east of the
Gila and Salt River meridian, Arizona , be, and they are hereby , withdrawn from
settlement , location, sale , or entry , pending a resurvey of said township under the
act of March 3 , 1909 (ch . 271 , 35 Stat . 845) .
This order shall continue in full force and effect unless and until revoked by the
President or by act of Congress .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
April 7, 1934 .
FRANKLIN D. ROOSEVELT .
