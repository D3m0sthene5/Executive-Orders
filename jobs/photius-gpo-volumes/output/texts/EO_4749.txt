Executive Order
ARIZONA
It is hereby ordered that Executive orders of withdrawal of June 24, 1924 ,
and February 12, 1927 (No. 4583), be and they are hereby revoked in so far as
they affect the whole or any part of the following described tract of public land,
namely , NE14 Sec . 6 , T. 5 S. , R. 10 E., G. and S. R. M. , Arizona , and the said tract
is hereby restored to entry .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
October 28, 1927 .
