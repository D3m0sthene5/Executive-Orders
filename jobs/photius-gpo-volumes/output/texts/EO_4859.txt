Executive Order
Mrs. Roy K. Jones , widow of Lieutenant Commander Roy K. Jones , United
States Navy, who met his death in line of duty when the submarine S-4 sank on
December 17 , 1927, off the coast of Provincetown , Massachusetts , may be appointed
to an appropriate position in the Navy Department without regard to the exami￾nation requirements of the civil service rules .
As the Civil Service Commission can properly recommend waivers only when
the procedure under the rules will not fully meet the needs of the service , it is
unable to concur in the recommendation of the Secretary of the Navy for the
issuance of this order .
For patriotism .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
April 17 , 1928 .
