Executive Order
1
Mr. Arthur J. Dahn may be reinstated as clerk in the Railway Mail Service
without regard to the time limit on reinstatement under the civil service rules .
Mr. Dahn was seriously injured in line of duty in a railroad wreck May 29 , 1918 .
As a result of this wreck he was incapacitated for service for several years .
This order is recommended by the Postmaster General . The Civil Service
Commission concurs .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
January 19 , 1929 .
