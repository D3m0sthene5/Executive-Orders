Executive Order
PARTIAL REVOCATION OF WITHDRAWAL OF PUBLIC LANDS
CALIFORNIA
It is hereby ordered that Executive Order No. 5326, dated April 14, 1930, with￾drawing all unreserved islands , rocks , and pinnacles situated in the Pacific Ocean off
the coast of California for classification and in aid of legislation, be, and the same is
hereby , revoked in so far as it affects all the islands , rocks , and pinnacles situated in
the Pacific Ocean south of the mouth of Carmel River and in the vicinity of Point
Lobos in T. 16 S. , R. 1 W. , Mount Diablo meridian, California .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
December 23, 1932 .
