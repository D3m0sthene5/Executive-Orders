Executive Order
Mrs. Mary P. Towle may be appointed as clerk in the Boston post office with￾out regard to the requirements of the civil service rules . Mrs. Towle is the widow
of Charles E. Towle , for twenty -six years an employee of the Boston post office ,
who died December 1 , 1927, leaving her with limited means and with a daughter
who is not entirely self-supporting .
This order is issued on recommendation of the Acting Postmaster General .
The Civil Service Commission is unable to concur .
For charity .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
March 7, 1928.
