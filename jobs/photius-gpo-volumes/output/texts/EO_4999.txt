Executive Order
Mrs. Genevieve G. Meehan may be appointed to a clerical position in the class￾ified service under the Federal Trade Commission without regard to the civil
service rules .
Mrs. Meehan is the widow of Charles V. Meehan , formerly Judge of the
Municipal Court of the District of Columbia , who at death left her without adequate
resources . She has rendered several years of satisfactory and efficient service along
statistical lines in various branches of the government.
This order is issued upon the recommendation of the Federal Trade Commission .
The Civil Service Commission cannot concur, its recommendations for waivers being
limited to those cases where the needs of the service cannot be met under the usual
procedure under the rules .
For charity .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
November 21 , 1928 .
