Executive Order
Federal Addition to the Townsite of Seward , Alaska.
By virtue of authority vested in me by the provisions of the act of Congress
approved March 12, 1914 (38 Stat . 305), lots 11 and 12, block 8, in the Federal
Addition to the townsite of Seward , Alaska , are hereby reserved and set aside for
the use of the Forest Service of the United States Department of Agriculture .
This order shall continue in full force and effect unless and until revoked by
the President or by act of Congress .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
April 1 , 1927.
CALVIN COOLIDGE
