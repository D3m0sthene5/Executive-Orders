Executive Order
In accordance with the provisions of Section 179 of the Revised Statutes , as
amended by the act approved August 5, 1882 (22 Stat . 238), the Assistant Secretary
of War appointed under the Act of Congress approved July 2, 1926, the Chief of
Staff, United States Army, the Deputy Chief of Staff, United States Army, and
The Adjutant General , United States Army, each in order of precedence as given
herein , is authorized and directed to perform the duties of Secretary of War during
illness or temporary absence from the seat of Government of the Secretary of War,
whenever during such illness or absence the Assistant Secretary of War appointed
under the Act of March 5, 1890 (26 Stat . 17), as amended , is also absent .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
July 14, 1926 .
