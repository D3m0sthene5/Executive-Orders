Executive Order
Mrs. Edith M. White may be appointed as clerk in the Boston , Massachu￾setts , post office without regard to the requirements of the civil service rules .
Mrs. White is the widow of Walter H. White , a former employee of the Boston
post office who died December 15 , 1927, leaving her with five small children and
without adequate means of support. Mr. White was a veteran of the World War.
This order is recommended by the Postmaster General .
The Civil Service Commission can not certify that the needs of the service
require a waiver of the rules to permit Mrs. White's appointment but submits the
case without prejudice .
For charity .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
November 10 , 1928.
