Executive Order
Mr. Thomas Howard Bailey may be employed as watchman in the Office
of the Director of Public Buildings and Public Parks of the National Capital
without regard to the Civil Service Act and Rules .
Mr. Bailey was injured in a powder explosion in the line of his duty in a
turret of the United States Steamship Trenton . The Secretary of the Navy,
in recommending this Order says :
Mr. Bailey was jammed in the rear access door, pinned there
by reason of the fact that his leg was doubled up in the pile of
unconscious men around the hand rammer, and , although the skin
from his face and hands was hanging in shreds from severe burns ,
he succeeded in getting the nozzle of the hose and passing it into
the turret, thereafter keeping the kinks out of it where it passed
over the edge of the door . Although so seriously wounded , he
repeatedly refused assistance for himself until several of the other
men had been cleared away and passed to safety. He was
honorably discharged on February 6, 1925, on account of physical
disability incurred in the line of duty . This disability also prevents
Mr. Bailey from securing sufficiently remunerative commercial
employment .
The Civil Service Commission concurs in recommending the Order in recognition
of the heroic conduct of Mr. Bailey .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
March 29 , 1926.
