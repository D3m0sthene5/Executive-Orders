Executive Order
AUTHORIZATION TO APPOINT MRS. JEAN SPRINGSTEAD WHITTEMORE
By virtue of the authority vested in me by the provisions of the last sentence of
the eighth paragraph of subdivision second of section 2 of the Civil Service Act of
January 16 , 1883 (ch . 27, 22 Stat . 403, 404), it is hereby ordered that Mrs. Jean
Springstead Whittemore may be appointed to the position of collector of customs for
Puerto Rico without compliance with the requirements of the civil-service rules .
This order is recommended by the Secretary of the Treasury .
FRANKLIN D. ROOSEVELT .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
October 26, 1933.
