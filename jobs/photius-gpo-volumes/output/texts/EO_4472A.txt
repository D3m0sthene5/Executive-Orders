Executive Order
Under the authority provided in Subsection (d) Section 18 of the Act approved
May 24 , 1924, as amended by the Act of July 3, 1926, the President of the United
States , in the exercise of his discretion, and acting in the interest of the United
States , hereby orders the retention on active duty until Dec. 31 , 1926, of William
W. Russell , of the District of Columbia , Envoy Extraordinary and Minister
Plenipotentiary to Siam.
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
July 3, 1926 .
