Executive Order
Mrs. Julia H. Hutchinson , widow of George T. Hutchinson , an employee of the
Bureau of Engraving and Printing, from April 18 , 1906, until his death on April
21 , 1926 , may be reinstated to the position of operative in the Bureau of Engraving
and Printing , without regard to the length of time she has been separated from the
service .
This Order is recommended by the Secretary of the Treasury who states that
Mrs. Hutchinson served in the Bureau from August 27, 1906 , to November 20 , 1914 ,
and that in view of her former experience , her services can be utilized at this time.
As the Civil Service Commission can properly recommend waivers only when
the procedure under the rules will not fully meet the needs of the service , it is
unable to concur .
Done for charity .
THE WHITE HOUSE,
July 3, 1926 .
