Executive Order
RETENTION OF EDWIN SHEDDAN CUNNINGHAM IN THE FOREIGN SERVICE
Under the authority provided in subsection (d) of Section 26 of the Act approved
February 23 , 1931 , (46 Stat 1207) and Section 204 of the Act of June 30, 1932 , the
President of the United States , in the exercise of his discretion, and acting in the
interest of the United States , hereby orders the retention on active duty for such
time as may be deemed necessary not in excess of five years from July 6, 1933 , of
Edwin Sheddan Cunningham, of Tennessee , a Foreign Service Officer of Class I.
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
March 3, 1933.
