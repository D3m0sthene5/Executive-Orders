Executive Order
Mrs. Susan T. Waite may be reinstated to a permanent clerkship in the Bureau
of Labor Statistics , Department of Labor , without regard to the time limit on rein￾statement under the civil service rules .
Mrs. Waite is at the head of the register for statistical clerk but may not be
certified for permanent appointment because of the law of the apportionment . In
addition to some two years of temporary employment she served as a permanent
clerk in the Pension Bureau from December 16, 1901 , to June 1, 1907. Her hus￾band , who was a Government employee for more than twenty -four years, is now in
a hospital a hopeless invalid and she is the sole support of two minor children .
This Order is recommended by the Secretary of Labor. The Civil Service
Commission interposes no objection .
Done for charity .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
October 23, 1926.
