Executive Order
The following persons , now employed in the Customs Service in foreign countries ,
may be transferred to classified clerical positions in the Customs Service :
David L. Brown
Roy V. Fox
Hanna Hegeler
Michael Donahue
L. J. Eckstrom
Carmen D'Agostino
Myra A. Sharp
Wilson C. Beers
Ella M. Jacobson
This order is recommended by the Secretary of the Treasury , who believes the
experience and knowledge of these persons of customs matters in foreign countries
may be used to advantage in the United States or wherever they may be employed .
The Civil Service Commission does not concur.
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
April 25, 1928.
