Executive Order

X
Mrs. Josephine Robbins may be employed in a clerical capacity under the
Naval Establishment without regard to the civil service rules . Mrs. Robbins
served during the World War as a yeoman (F) for more than a year and a half .
She has three children dependent upon her for support and is without means or
income .
This order is recommended by the Secretary of the Navy. The Civil Service
Commission does not concur, as it can properly recommend waivers only when the
needs of the service can not be met by the procedure under the civil service rules .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
November 2 , 1928.
