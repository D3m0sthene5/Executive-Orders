Executive Order
ADDITIONAL COMPENSATION OF ENLISTED MEN OF THE ARMY FOR SPECIAL
QUALIFICATIONS IN THE USE OF ARMS
By virtue of the authority vested in me under section 18 of the act of June 10,
1922 (ch . 212 , 42 Stat . 625 , 632 ), it is hereby ordered that enlisted men of the Army ,
after having established their special qualifications in the use of the arm or arms
which they may be required to use according to standards of proficiency which have
been or may be prescribed from time to time by the Secretary of War, and subject to
the provisions of sections 2 and 3 of title II of the act of March 20, 1933 (Public ,
No. 2 , 73d Cong .), shall , for such periods of time and under such conditions as have
