Executive Order
By virtue of the authority vested in me as President of the United States of
America , and pursuant to the provisions of Section 6 of the Act of Congress approved
March 12 , 1926, entitled "An Act authorizing the use for permanent construction
at military posts of the proceeds from the sale of surplus War Department real
property , and authorizing the sale of certain military reservations , and for other
purposes " , (Public -No . 45-69th Congress ) the Secretary of Commerce having
made application to the Secretary of War for the transfer thereof and given spe￾cific reasons therefor, within ninety days after the approval of the above -mentioned
Act of Congress , and such transfer having received my approval , it is hereby ordered
that the portion of the Fort Pike Military Reservation in the State of Louisiana
hereinafter described , be, and the same is hereby , transferred to the Department of
Commerce for use as a lighthouse reservation :
All that Portion of Fort Pike Military Reservation , Louisiana ,
between Great Rigolet and Lake Pontchartrain lying north of an east
and west line cutting through the point of land in Section 24 , Township
10 South , Range 14 East and Section 19 , Township 10 South, Range
15 East , the said east and west line being drawn one-quarter of a mile
south of the center of West Rigolet's Lighthouse (East of the Missis￾sippi River , Louisiana) as indicated on map attached hereto and made
by reference a part hereof .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
September 15 , 1926.
