Executive Order
Colorado
Under authority contained in the act of June 25, 1910 (36 Stat .
, 847) , as
amended by the act of August 24, 1912 (37 Stat .
, 497), and subject to the condi￾tions , provisions, and limitations of said acts , it is hereby ordered that the public
lands in T. 1 S. , R. 71 W. , 6th P. M., Colorado, be and the same are hereby with￾drawn from settlement , location, sale , entry and all forms of appropriation , pending
a resurvey of said township ; this withdrawal to remain in full force and effect
from the date hereof until the resurvey is duly approved and accepted , and the
approved plat thereof is officially filed in the proper United States district land
office, unless another date be fixed by the Commissioner of the General Land Office ,
whereupon this withdrawal will become of no effect .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
July 15, 1926.
