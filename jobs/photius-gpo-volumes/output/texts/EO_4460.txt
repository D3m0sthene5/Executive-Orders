Executive Order
Wyoming
Under authority contained in the act of Congress approved June 25, 1910 (36
Stat .
, 847 )
, as amended by the act of August 24, 1912 (37 Stat .
, 497) , and subject
to the conditions , provisions and limitations of said acts , it is hereby ordered that
the public lands in Ts. 42, 43 and 44 N., R. 89 W. , and T. 44 N. , Rs . 90 and 91 W. ,
6th P. M. , Wyoming , be and the same are hereby withdrawn from settlement ,
location , sale , entry and all forms of appropriation pending a resurvey of said town￾ships under the act of Congress approved May 29, 1908 (35 Stat .
, 465 ) ; this with￾drawal to remain in full force and effect from the date hereof until the resurvey
is duly approved and accepted , and the approved plats thereof are officially filed
in the proper United States district land office , unless another date be fixed by
the Commissioner of the General Land Office, whereupon this withdrawal will
become of no effect .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
June 18 , 1926 .
[No. 4460]
CALVIN COOLIDGE
GOVERNMENT PRINTINGOFFICE
