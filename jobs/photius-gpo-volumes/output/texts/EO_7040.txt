Executive Order
APPOINTMENT OF EXECUTIVE OFFICER OF THE RURAL ELECTRIFICATION
ADMINISTRATION
By virtue of and pursuant to the authority vested in me under the Emergency
Relief Appropriation Act of 1935 , approved April 8, 1935 (Public Resolution No. 11 ,
74th Cong .), I hereby appoint Perry R. Taylor , of Pennsylvania , as Executive Officer
of the Rural Electrification Administration established by Executive Order No. 7037,
of May 11 , 1935 .
The said Executive Officer shall exercise and perform the functions and duties
prescribed for the Rural Electrification Administration in the said Executive order
pending the appointment of the Administrator of the said Administration by and
with the advice and consent of the Senate .
This appointment shall terminate upon the appointment and qualification of
the Administrator .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
May 15, 1935 .
FRANKLIN D ROOSEVE
