Executive Order
By virtue of the authority vested in me by the Act of Congress approved
August 1 , 1914 , making appropriations for the sundry civil expenses of the
Government for the fiscal year ended June 30, 1915, the port limits of Boston ,
Massachusetts , the headquarters port of Customs Collection District No. 4 (Mas￾sachusetts ) are hereby extended to include Braintree , Weymouth and Hingham ,
and the waters adjacent thereto , effective thirty days from the date of this order .
