Executive Order
UTAH
Under authority contained in the act of June 25, 1910 ( 36 Stat . 847 )
, as
amended by the act of August 24, 1912 (37 Stat. 497), and subject to the condi￾tions , provisions and limitations of said acts , it is hereby ordered that the public
lands in T. 10 S. , R. 20 E., S. L. M. , Utah , be and the same are hereby withdrawn
from settlement , location , sale , entry and all forms of appropriation pending a
resurvey of said township under the act of March 3, 1909 ( 35 Stat . 845), as
amended by the joint resolution of June 25, 1910 (36 Stat . 884 ) .
This order shall continue in full force and effect unless and until revoked by
the President, or by act of Congress .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
October 21 , 1927.
