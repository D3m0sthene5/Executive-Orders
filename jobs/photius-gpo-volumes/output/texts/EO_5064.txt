Executive Order
Miss Elizabeth Gibson may be employed as a clerk or typist under the Navy
Department or Naval Establishment without regard to the Civil Service Act or
Rules .
This Order is issued upon recommendation of the Secretary of the Navy .
Miss Gibson's father who died on May 10 , 1911 , was a Rear Admiral in the United
States Navy and served both in the Civil War and the Spanish-American War .
The Civil Service Commission is unable to concur in this recommendation .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
February 28 , 1929 .
