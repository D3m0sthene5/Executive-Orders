Executive Order
By authority of the provisions of Section 2 of the Act of Congress approved
September 21 , 1922 , I designate Honorable James W. Blackburn as Special Judge
of the Canal Zone until January 1, 1927, to act during the absence or illness of the
District Judge of the Canal Zone , and in
all
cases where said District Judge may
be disqualified .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
January 20 ,
1926 .
