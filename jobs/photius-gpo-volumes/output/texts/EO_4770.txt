Executive Order
Mrs. Mary Hazard Teele may be appointed to a clerical position in the
Department of Agriculture without examination under the civil service rules .
Mrs. Teele is the widow of Mr. Ray P. Teele , Agricultural Economist , who
was employed in the Department of Agriculture almost continuously since 1899 .
Mr. Teele died suddenly August 31, 1927, while on a special field assignment to the
Interior Department in connection with an investigation of reclamation projects in
Indian Reservations . Mrs. Teele has no adequate means of support and the Sec￾retary of Agriculture recommends her appointment .
The Civil Service Commission is unable to concur .
Done for 1
charity .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
November 28 , 1927 .
