AMENDMENT TO EXECUTIVE ORDER NO . 7060 , OF JUNE 5, 1935, PRESCRIB￾ING RULES AND REGULATIONS RELATING TO PROCEDURE FOR EMPLOY￾MENT OF WORKERS UNDER THE EMERGENCY RELIEF APPROPRIATION
ACT OF 1935
AMENDMENT OF SECTION 5 OF REGULATION NO . 2
Section 5 of regulation no . 2, as contained in Executive Order No. 7060 , of June
5 , 1935 , prescribing rules and regulations relating to procedure for employment of
workers under the Emergency Relief Appropriation Act of 1935 , is hereby amended to
read as follows :
" Section 5. Only persons certified for assignment to work by the United States
Employment Service shall be employed on projects : Provided , That for the purpose
of effectuating the purposes of paragraph I (C) of Executive Order No. 7034, of May
6, 1935, the Works Progress Administrator , or representatives specifically designated
by him , are hereby authorized , in their discretion , to modify this requirement in
connection with any project or portion thereof ."
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
August 5, 1935.
FRANKLIN D ROOSEVELT
