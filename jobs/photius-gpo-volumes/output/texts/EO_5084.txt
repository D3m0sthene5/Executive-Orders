Executive Order
The maximum age limit of fifty years for admission to the general clerical
examination in the Eleventh United States Civil Service District which closes
April 5, 1929, is hereby waived to permit the examination of Mrs. Lessie L. Schaefer
of Pendleton , Oregon .
It is stated that Mrs. Schaefer is in good health , brilliant , thoroughly fitted and
experienced in clerical work , and that it is necessary that she be self-supporting .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
March 28, 1929 .
