Executive Order
WHEREAS , by Executive Order No. 4987 , dated November 2, 1928, a portion
of the tract of land containing thirteen and one-third acres at Rockaway Point ,
Long Island , New York , is reserved and set apart for the use of the War Department
in connection with the Fort Tilden Military Reservation , New York ; and
WHEREAS , it has been determined by recent survey of the land that the
description thereof as set forth in said Executive Order is erroneous ; and
WHEREAS , it is desired that said Executive Order be amended so as to
accurately describe said land ,
NOW , THEREFORE , it is hereby ordered that the said Executive Order No.
4987, dated November 2, 1928, be and the same is hereby amended with respect
to the description of the land , so as to read as follows :-
" Beginning at a point described as Monument #124 located on
the southerly line of Rockaway Beach Boulevard (running East &
West ) running 331.2 feet North 69 ° 49 ′ 38 ″ East; thence 1383.6 feet
South 24 ° 00′ 38 ″ East ; thence 196.5 feet South 65° 59′22 ″ West to
Army Reservation Boundary ; thence 1411.9 feet North 29 ° 24 ′ 47.7 ′′
West across Monument #120 to place of beginning at Monument
#124 ."
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
February 16 , 1929 .
[No. 5044]
CALVIN C
