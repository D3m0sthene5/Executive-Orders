Executive Order
IDAHO
It is hereby ordered that Executive orders of withdrawal of March 22, 1916
(No. 2348), and January 28 , 1921 (No. 3393), under the act of June 25, 1910 ( 36
Stat . 847), as amended , be and they are hereby revoked in so far as they affect the
following described lands in Idaho :
Boise Meridian
In T. 10 N. , R. 13 E., that portion of the E½ Sec . 6 lying south and west
of the north bank of Valley Creek , NE14 , E2 NW14 Sec . 20, N½
and N½ S½ Sec . 21 , lots 2 , 3, 6, 7 , W½ NW , NW14 SW4 and S½
SW4 Sec . 22 , lot 2 and NW14 NE4 Sec . 27 ;
In T. 8 N. , R. 14 E., those portions of Secs . 4 and 5 lying west of the east
bank of Salmon River, E1/2½ Sec . 6, those portions of E1/2 Sec . 8, NW4
Sec. 9 and E½ Sec . 17 lying west of the east bank of said river , and
those portions of the E1/2 of Secs . 20 and 29 lying west of the east banks
of said river and Lake Creek ;
In T. 9 N. , R. 14 E., those portions of E½ of Sec . 18 and N12 NE4 Sec .
19 lying west of the east bank of Salmon River, and those portions of
the E12 of Secs . 30 and 31 and of W12 Sec . 32 lying west of the east
bank of said river .
And it is hereby ordered , pursuant to Public Resolution No. 29 of February
14 , 1920 (41 Stat. 434) , as amended January 21 and December 28, 1922 (42 Stat .
358 , 1067) , that the above described area, subject to valid rights and the provisions
of existing withdrawals for power and stock driveway purposes , shall be opened
only to entry under the homestead or desert land laws by qualified ex-service men
of the War with Germany , under the terms and conditions of such resolution and the
regulations issued thereunder , for a period of 91 days beginning with the 63rd day
from and after the date hereof , and thereafter to appropriation under any public
land law applicable thereto by the general public .
Subsequent to the date hereof and prior to the date of restoration to general
disposition as herein provided, no right may be acquired to the land by settlement
in advance of entry , or otherwise except strictly in accordance herewith.
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
July 13, 1928 .
