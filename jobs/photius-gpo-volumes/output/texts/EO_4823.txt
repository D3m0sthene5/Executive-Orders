Executive Order
By virtue of the authority vested in me by the Act of Congress approved
August 1 , 1914, making appropriations for the sundry civil expenses of the Govern￾ment for the fiscal year ended June 30, 1915, Fairport , Lorain and Put-in-Bay are
hereby abolished as ports of entry in customs collection district #41 (Ohio), with
headquarters at Cleveland , Ohio, effective thirty days from the date of this order .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
March 5, 1928.
