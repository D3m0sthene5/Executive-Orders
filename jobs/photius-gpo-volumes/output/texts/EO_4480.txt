Executive Order
Mrs. Mary Graham Byrd may be reinstated in the Bureau of Engraving and
Printing , without regard to the length of time she has been separated from the
service .
This order is recommended by the Secretary of the Treasury , who states that
Mrs. Byrd served in the Bureau of Engraving and Printing from January 5 , 1882,
to December 18 , 1918, and that on account of her previous experience her services
can be used at the present time.
As the Civil Service Commission can properly recommend waivers only when
the procedure under the rules will not fully meet the needs of the service , it is
unable to concur .
Done for charity .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
July 17 , 1926 .
