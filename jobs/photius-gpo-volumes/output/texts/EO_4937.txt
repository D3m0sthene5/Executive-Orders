Executive Order
CALIFORNIA
It is hereby ordered that Executive order of December 22, 1924, No. 4117,
withdrawing for classification under the act of June 25, 1910 (36 Stat . 847) , as
amended , a tract of 168.78 acres in California , be and it is hereby revoked, the
said tract being described as follows :
In T. 23 N. , R. 15 W. , M. D. M. , Lots 2, 3 ,
4 and SW NE Sec . 20.
And it is hereby ordered , pursuant to Public Resolution No. 29 of February
14, 1920 (41 Stat . 434), as amended January 21 and December 28, 1922 (42 Stat .
358 , 1067) , that the above described area , subject to any valid right, shall be opened
to entry under the homestead or desert land laws by qualified ex-service men of
the war with Germany , under the terms and conditions of such resolution and the
regulations issued thereunder, for a period of 91 days beginning with the 63rd day
from and after the date hereof , and thereafter if unentered to appropriation under
any public land law applicable thereto by the general public .
Subsequent to the date hereof and prior to the date of restoration to general
disposition as herein provided , no right may be acquired to the land by settlement
in advance of entry , or otherwise except strictly in accordance herewith .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
July 23, 1928.
