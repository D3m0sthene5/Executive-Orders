Executive Order
Mr. Ernest H. Hubbard served as Office Deputy United States Marshal ,
Indian Territory , from July 1 , 1900, to November 17, 1907, and again as Chief
Deputy Marshal , Eastern District of Oklahoma , from April 15, 1908, until June
1 , 1916 , the position meanwhile having been classified by Order of March 2, 1909,
and excepted by Act of October 22, 1913.
The classified status which Mr. Hubbard retained until his separation in 1916 is
hereby restored to him in his present excepted position of Chief Deputy Marshal .
This action is taken upon the recommendation of the Acting Attorney General .
The Civil Service Commission deems the order unobjectionable .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
September 15, 1926 .
