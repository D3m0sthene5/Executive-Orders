Executive Order
Appointment of Miss Patia Smith
Miss Patia Smith may be appointed to an appropriate position in the Depart￾ment of Commerce without reference to the provisions of the civil service rules .
Miss Smith is the daughter of Curtis M. Smith, a former employee of the Bureau
of Standards, who was seriously injured in an explosion which occurred while he
was on duty in the dynamometer laboratory of the Bureau of Standards in Sep￾tember , 1923. As a result of his heroic action in preventing loss of life and further
damage to Government property by closing the liquid ammonia valves of the alti￾tude chamber and pumping out the ammonia coils to a vacuum, Mr. Smith was
seriously injured and incapacitated for duty for some time prior to his death on
March 15, 1929 .
This order is issued upon the recommendation of the Secretary of Commerce .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
June 23, 1930.
