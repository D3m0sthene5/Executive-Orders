Executive Order
ALASKA
By virtue of the authority vested in me by Secs . 2380 and 2381 U. S. Revised.
Statutes the following described lands are hereby withdrawn for townsite purposes :
NE4 NE4 , S½ NE14 , E2 SW14, N1/2 SE14, SW14 SE14, Lot 1 , Sec . 35 , T. 5
S. , R. 4 E. , and all legal subdivisions which will on survey lie wholly or in part
within one-half mile of the shore of Salchaket Lake in T. 6 S. , R. 4 E., T. 5 S. , R.
5 E., and T. 6 S. , R. 5 E., F. M., Alaska .
This order will continue in full force and effect unless and until revoked by the
President or by act of Congress .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
July 4, 1927 .
