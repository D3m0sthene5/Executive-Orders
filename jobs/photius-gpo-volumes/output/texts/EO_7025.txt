Executive Order
APPOINTMENT OF CHARLES EDISON AS A MEMBER OF THE NATIONAL
INDUSTRIAL RECOVERY BOARD
By virtue of the authority vested in me by Title I of the National Industrial
Recovery Act, approved June 16 , 1933 , and to effectuate the purposes of said Act,
I hereby appoint Charles Edison as a member of the National Industrial Recovery
Board to fill the vacancy caused by the retirement of A. D. Whiteside , resigned ; and
I hereby direct that said Charles Edison be given leave of absence without pay from
his position as State Director for the National Emergency Council for the State of
New Jersey during the period of his service as a member of said National Industrial
Recovery Board .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
April 29 , 1935.
