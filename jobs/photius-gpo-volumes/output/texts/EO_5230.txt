Executive Order
Amendment of Schedule A, Positions Excepted from Examination under the
Civil Service Rules
Schedule A, positions excepted from the requirement of examination under the
civil service rules , is hereby amended by the addition of a new subdivision, to be
numbered XXI , as follows :
XXI . FEDERAL FARM BOARD
1. One private secretary or confidential clerk to each Member of
the Federal Farm Board .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
November 25, 1929 .
