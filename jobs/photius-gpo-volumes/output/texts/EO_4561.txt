Executive Order
Mrs. Louie E. Hough , who was employed as copyist and clerk in the Patent
Office from November 8, 1907, to August 22, 1917, may be reinstated in a clerical
position in the Patent Office without regard to the length of her separation from
the service .
This order is issued on the recommendation of the Secretary of Commerce , who
states that Mrs. Hough's familiarity with the work of the Patent Office would make
her of greater value than an appointee having no experience in that office .
As the Civil Service Commission must be governed in its recommendations by
the demands of the service , it is unable to concur .
Done for charity .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
January 7, 1927.
