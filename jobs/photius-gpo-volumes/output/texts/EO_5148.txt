Executive Order
It is hereby ordered under authority contained in the act of June 21 , 1906
(34 Stat . L. 326), that the trust period on allotments made to the Indians of the
Omaha Reservation in Nebraska , which period expires during the calendar year
1929 , be, and is hereby extended for a period of ten years .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
July 3, 1929 .
