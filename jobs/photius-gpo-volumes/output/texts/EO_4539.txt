Executive Order
Utah
Under authority contained in the act of June 25, 1910 (36 Stat .
, 847), as
amended by the act of August 24, 1912 (37 Stat.
, 497)
, and subject to the condi￾tions , provisions and limitations of said acts , it is hereby ordered that the public
lands in the surveyed portions of T. 5 S. , Rs . 3 and 4 W. , S. L. M., Utah , be and
the same are hereby withdrawn from settlement , location , sale , entry and all forms
of appropriation pending a resurvey of said surveyed portions of the townships
under the act of March 3 , 1909 (35 Stat .
, 845), as amended by the joint resolution
of June 25, 1910 (36 Stat .
, 884) ; this withdrawal to remain in full force and effect
from the date hereof until the resurvey is duly approved and accepted , and the
approved plats thereof are officially filed in the proper United States district land
office , unless another date be fixed by the Commissioner of the General Land Office ,
whereupon this withdrawal will become of no effect .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
November 6, 1926 .
