Executive Order
EXEMPTION OF APPOINTMENT OF SECRETARY TO THE AVIATION COMMIS￾SION CREATED BY THE ACT OF JUNE 12, 1934 , PUBLIC , No. 308-
SEVENTY- THIRD CONGRESS
By virtue of the authority vested in me by the provisions of the last sentence of
the eighth paragraph of subdivision second of section 2 of the Civil Service Act of
January 16 , 1883 (ch . 27, 22 Stat . 403 , 404 ), the position of secretary to the aviation
commission created by the act of June 12 , 1934, Public , No. 308-Seventy-third
Congress , is placed in the unclassified service , and appointment thereto may be made
at the discretion of the aviation commission .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
June 30 , 1934 .
[No. 6772]
FRANKLIN D
