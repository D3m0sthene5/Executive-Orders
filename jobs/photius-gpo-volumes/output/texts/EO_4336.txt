Executive Order
In consideration of the services of Lieutenant Commander Louis Collins
Scheibla , United States Navy, who died as an officer on active duty and in the line
of duty when the seaplane , in which he was flying as an observer , crashed into the
Potomac River near Dahlgren , Virginia , on April 17, 1924 , his widow , Mrs. Belle
Barrick Scheibla, may be employed in any clerical position under the Navy Depart￾ment which she is qualified to fill, without regard to the Civil Service Act or Rules .
Mrs. Scheibla was appointed on July 7, 1924, under the Executive Order of
June 27, 1924, on the rolls of the Veterans' Bureau for duty in the Navy Depart￾ment for work in connection with the administration of the Act to provide adjusted
compensation for veterans of the World War . Her present employment will soon
terminate , owing to lack of work . The Secretary of the Navy states that she has
demonstrated her ability to perform satisfactorily such clerical duties as have been
assigned her , and , in view of this fact, it is desired to retain her services under the
Navy Department .
The Civil Service Commission does not concur in this recommendation .
Done for charity .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
