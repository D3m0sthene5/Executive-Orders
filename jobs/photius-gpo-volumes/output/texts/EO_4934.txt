Executive Order
Mrs. Theraesa L. Irwin may be appointed to a clerkship in the Boston, Massa￾chusetts , post office , without regard to the requirements of the civil service rules .
Mrs. Irwin is the widow of George C. Irwin, for more than seventeen years an
employee of the Boston post office , who died on December 26, 1923, leaving her
without funds and making it necessary that she earn a livelihood for herself and a
minor child.
This Order is issued upon the recommendation of the Postmaster General. As
the Civil Service Commission is limited in its recommendations for waivers to those
cases where the needs of the service can not be met by the regular procedure under
the rules , it does not concur .
For charity .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
July 16, 1928 .
