Executive Order
AMENDMENT OF EXECUTIVE ORDER NO . 6657-A, DATED MARCH 27, 1934
Section 1 (e) of Executive Order No. 6657-A, of March 27, 1934, prescribing
regulations for payment of losses sustained by officers , enlisted men, and employees
of the United States in foreign countries due to appreciation of foreign currencies
in their relation to the American dollar , is hereby amended to read as follows :
" (e) while attached to and serving on board United States vessels stationed in
foreign waters for not less than 60 consecutive days .”
This order shall be effective this date.
FRANKLIN D. ROOSEVELT.
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
October 22, 1934 .
