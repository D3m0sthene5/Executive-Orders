Executive Order
AUTHORIZING THE ALIEN PROPERTY CUSTODIAN
TO SELL CERTAIN PROPERTY AT PRIVATE SALE
By virtue of the authority vested in me by an Act of Congress entitled "An
act to define , regulate , and punish trading with the enemy and for other purposes ” ,
approved October 6, 1917, known as the " Trading with the Enemy Act " , and the
amendment to such Act embodied in " An Act making appropriations to supply
urgent deficiencies in the appropriations for the fiscal year ending June 30, 1918,
and prior fiscal years on account of war expenses and for other purposes " , approved
March 28, 1918, and the amendment to such Act embodied in " An Act making
appropriations to supply deficiencies in appropriations for the fiscal year ending
June 30, 1919 , and prior fiscal years on account of war expenses , and for other pur￾poses " , approved November 4, 1918, to make certain orders such as that herein
contained , I hereby , in the public interest , make the following determination, order,
rule and regulation :
The Alien Property Custodian is hereby authorized to sell at private sale , at
such time and place as to him or to his authorized agent may seem advisable , with￾out public or other advertisement , for and in consideration of the sum of FIVE
THOUSAND FOUR HUNDRED AND EIGHTY-FIVE DOLLARS and
SEVENTY -TWO CENTS ($ 5,485.72 ), the six-sevenths interest in certain real estate
now being held and administered by him as such Alien Property Custodian , and
heretofore , upon requirement , conveyed , transferred , assigned and delivered to him
as the property of Louis F. Kloz , Frieda Kloz , Friedrick L. Kloz, Albert J. Kloz ,
Bertha Volz , Ernest P. Volz , Frederick W. Rabel , Louise D. Iselabold , Eugene
Rabel , Carl Ludwig , Dorothea L. Richter , Marie E. Hiller, Rosalie S. Bensich,
August Weigle , Marie Weigle , Catherine Weigle and Emilie E. Tucht , residing in
Germany , whom, after investigation , he did determine to be enemies within the
purview of said Act as amended , which said real estate is situate in the City of
Long Island , County of Queens , State of New York , and described as follows :
to-wit:
All that certain lot of land situate in the late Village of Astoria , now
Long Island City, in Queens County , and State of New York , known
and distinguished on a certain map of property situate in the Fourth
Ward of Long Island City, Queens County , New York , belonging to
Francis Brill , Esquire , surveyed and drawn March 1880 by P. G. Van
Alst , City Surveyor and intended to be filed in the Office of the Clerk
of the County of Queens by the number ( 5) in Block number One
hundred and twenty ( 120 ), and bounded and containing as follows ,
to -wit : BEGINNING at a point on the Southeasterly side of
Lathrop Street (
formerly Third Avenue), as laid down on said map,
distant Two hundred (200) feet Northeasterly from the corner formed
by the intersection of the Southeasterly side of said Lathrop Street
and the Northwesterly side of Jamaica Avenue as laid down on said
map, and running thence Northeasterly along said Lathrop Street
Twenty -five (25) feet ; thence Southeasterly and parallel with said
Jamaica Avenue Ninety -seven (97) feet seven and one-quarter (714)
inches to the centre line of the block between said Lathrop Street and
Rapelye Avenue as laid down on said map ; thence southwesterly
along the centre line of the said block ; and parallel with said Lathrop
Street Twenty -five (25) feet , and thence Northwesterly and parallel
with said Jamaica Avenue Ninety -seven (97) feet , seven and one-quarter
(714) inches to the place of beginning .

My reasons for the foregoing determination, order, rule, and regulation are :
(a) An advantageous offer for the purchase of said real estate has been received
by the Alien Property Custodian .
(b) Because there are thirty -four persons each owning a small fractional interest
in the property and it is necessary to convert the same into cash in order to make
distribution .
(c) Because the Americans holding a one -seventh (1/7) interest request that
the sale be made in order that they can obtain their equity in cash from the
property .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
December 30 , 1927 .
[No. 4790]
CALVIN COOLIDGE

Executive Order
WHEREAS the corporate life of the United States Grain Corporation expired
according to the terms of its charter on August 14 , 1922 ; and
WHEREAS since the expiration of said charter the officers of said corporation
have been collecting its assets and liquidating its obligations , and there remains
nothing further to be done by said corporation and its officers , except to pay the
remaining assets thereof to its stockholder , United States of America ; and
WHEREAS said corporation has fully accounted for all its acts and for all the
funds which have come into its hands, showing the disposition thereof and the
balance on hand;
NOW, THEREFORE , I , CALVIN COOLIDGE , President of the United States , pur￾suant to the power and authority conferred on me by law, and as holder of all the
issued and outstanding capital stock of the United States Grain Corporation , do
hereby approve , ratify , confirm and adopt all the acts of the said United States
Grain Corporation and of the officers and directors thereof, and I do hereby approve,
ratify , confirm and adopt the final accounting of the property and funds of said
corporation and the disposition thereof as shown by the audited accounts submitted
by Edward M. Flesh , President , Treasurer and Liquidating Trustee of said corpo￾ration ; and I do order and direct that Edward M. Flesh, as Liquidating Trustee of
said corporation, pay the balance in its hands , amounting to the sum of $ 115,419.98 ,
into the Treasury of the United States, in full and final settlement , liquidation and
dissolution of said corporation and in full and final payment to its stockholder ,
United States of America .
And I do further order and direct that all the records , books of account and
files of the United States Food Administration and of the United States Wheat
Director heretofore held in the custody of said corporation , pursuant to Executive
Order , and all the records , books of account and files of said corporation be trans￾ferred and delivered to and shall be preserved by the Department of Commerce as
custodian thereof, until the completion of a building to be used for the permanent
housing of Government records , when the Department of Commerce shall deliver
all said records , books of account and files into said building and into the custody
of the official in charge thereof.
In Witness Whereof, I have hereunto set my hand and caused the
seal of the United States to be hereunto affixed .
[SEAL ]
DONE this 31st day of December , in the year of our Lord one thousand
nine hundred and twenty -seven and of the Independence of the
United States of America the one hundred and fifty -second .
By the President :
FRANK B. KELLOGG
Secretary of State
