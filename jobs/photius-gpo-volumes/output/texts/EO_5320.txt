Executive Order
Extension of the Limits of the Port of Niagara Falls , New York ,
to Include Lewiston , New York
By virtue of the authority vested in me by the act of Congress approved August
1, 1914, making appropriations for the sundry civil expenses of the Government for
the fiscal year ended June 30, 1915, the limits of the port of Niagara Falls , N. Y., are
hereby extended to include, in addition to Niagara Falls , Lewiston , N. Y. , in Customs
Collection District No. 9 (Buffalo ) with headquarters at Buffalo , N. Y. , effective
thirty days from the date of this order .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
April 7, 1930 .
