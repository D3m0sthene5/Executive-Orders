Executive Order
COLORADO
Under authority contained in the act of Congress approved June 25, 1910
(36 Stat .
, 847)
, as amended by the act of August 24, 1912 (37 Stat .
, 497) , and sub￾ject to the conditions , provisions and limitations of said acts, it is hereby ordered
that the public lands in T. 44 N. , R. 15 W. , N. M. P. M., Colorado, be and the same
are hereby withdrawn from settlement , location , sale , entry and all forms of appro￾priation, pending the resurvey of said township ; this withdrawal to remain in full
force and effect from the date hereof until the resurvey is duly approved and accepted
and the approved plat thereof is officially filed in the proper U. S. local land office ,
unless another date be fixed by the Commissioner of the General Land Office ,
whereupon this withdrawal will become of no effect .
THE WHITE HOUSE,
CALVIN COOLIDGE
October 21 , 1925 .
