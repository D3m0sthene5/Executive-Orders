Executive Order
Mrs. R. L. Riter may be reinstated as a clerk in the postal service without
regard to the time limit on reinstatement under the civil service rules .
Mrs. Riter was employed as a post office clerk at El Paso, Texas, from May
16 , 1906 , to October 2, 1909. She is the widow of Roscoe L. Riter who at the
time of his death , March 27, 1926, had a record of more than twenty -one years'
faithful service in the post office at El Paso and Fort Bliss , Texas. She is without
means of support and has three children dependent upon her.
For charity .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
June 29, 1928 .
