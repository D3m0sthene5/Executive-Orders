Executive Order
WAIVER OF CIVIL SERVICE RULE II TO PERMIT THE APPOINTMENT OF
MR . ROBERT K. MCQUEEN IN THE WAR DEPARTMENT
By virtue of the authority vested in me by the provisions of the last sentence of
the eighth paragraph of subdivision second of section 2 of the Civil Service Act of
January 16 , 1883 (ch . 27, 22 Stat. 403, 404), it is hereby ordered that Mr. Robert K.
McQueen may be appointed to the position of lockmaster in the Engineer Depart￾ment at large , War Department, without compliance with the requirements of Civil
Service Rule II .
This order is issued upon the recommendation of the Acting Secretary of War .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
January 12 , 1934 .
[No. 6555]
FRANKLIN
