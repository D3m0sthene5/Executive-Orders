Executive Order
Utah
Under authority contained in the act of Congress approved June 25, 1910 (36
Stat.
, 847)
, as amended by the act of August 24, 1912 (37 Stat .
, 497), and subject
to the conditions , provisions , and limitations of said acts , it is hereby ordered that
the public lands in T. 1 S. , R. 2 E., S. L. M. , Utah , be and the same are hereby
withdrawn from settlement , location , sale , entry and all forms of appropriation ,
pending a resurvey of said township under the act of Congress approved March 3 ,
1909 (35 Stat.
, 845 ) , as amended by the joint resolution of June 25, 1910 (36 Stat .
,
884 ) ; this withdrawal to remain in full force and effect from the date hereof until
the resurvey is duly approved and accepted , and the approved plat thereof is offi￾cially filed in the proper United States district land office , unless another date be
fixed by the Commissioner of the General Land Office , whereupon this withdrawal
will become of no effect .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
April 19, 1926.
