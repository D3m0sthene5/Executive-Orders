Executive Order
Jeremiah Regan, Fred Eugene Potter, and Patrick Reddington , employed as
chauffeurs at the Marine Corps Garage , Washington , D. C., are hereby included
within the classified civil service without regard to the civil service act or rules .
These employees entered the service of the U. S. Marine Corps as laborers ,
June 7 , 1913 , November 1 , 1917, and July 20, 1918, respectively , and have rendered
faithful and efficient service . They have qualified thru examination for the
position of chauffeur but their names have never been reached for certification .
This order is issued upon recommendation of the Secretary of the Navy.
The Civil Service Commission is unable to concur .
THE WHITE HOUSE,
December 29 , 1927 .
