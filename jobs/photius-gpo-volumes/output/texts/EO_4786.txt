Executive Order
ARIZONA
Pursuant to the authority conferred upon me by the act of Congress approved
June 25, 1910 (36 Stat . 847), as amended by the act of Congress approved August
24 , 1912 (37 Stat . 497), entitled "An act to authorize the President of the United
States to make withdrawals of public lands in certain cases " , and subject to the
conditions, provisions and limitations of said acts , it is hereby ordered that the S½
of the NE14 , and the SE14 of Sec . 23, and the NE14 of Sec . 26 , T. 7 S. , R. 25 E.,
G. and S.R.M. , Arizona , be and the same are hereby withdrawn from settlement ,
location , sale or entry , for use of the National Guard of Arizona as a rifle range :
Provided , That this withdrawal is subject to the existing pipe line right of way of
the Gila Valley Electric , Gas and Water Company across the SW14 of the NE14 ,
and the E½ of the NE 14 of said section 26, and the existing telephone line right
of way of the Department of Agriculture , Forest Service , across the SE14 of the
SE14 of said section 23, and the N1/2 of the NE14 , and the SW14 of the NE4 of
said section 26 , and subject to any other valid existing rights .
This order shall continue in full force and effect unless and until revoked by
the President or by act of Congress .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
December 17 , 1927 .
