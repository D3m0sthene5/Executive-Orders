Executive Order
NEW MEXICO
Under authority of the act of Congress approved June 25, 1910 (36 Stat .
, 847),
as amended by the act of August 24, 1912 ( 37 Stat .
, 497), certain public lands in
the State of New Mexico as described in Executive Order No. 4554 approved
December 17 , 1926, and by Executive Order No. 4559 approved December 30 , 1926 ,
were thereby temporarily withdrawn from settlement , location, sale or entry, sub￾ject to any prior withdrawal of the lands or any valid existing claims initiated
under the public land laws, and subject to the condition of said acts , in aid of
contemplated legislation ,
The legislation contemplated was effected by act of Congress approved March
2, 1927 ( Public No. 68, 69th Congress ), by which certain of the withdrawn lands
were granted to the State of New Mexico for the use and benefit of the New
Mexico College of Agriculture and Mechanic Arts .
It is hereby ordered that the said Executive orders of December 17, 1926 , and
December 30 , 1926, be and the same are hereby revoked as to the following described
lands which lands were not included in the grant made by the act of March 2, 1927 :
New Mexico Principal Meridian
In T. 20 S. , R. 1 W. , Secs . 2, 16 , 32 and 36 , and that part of Secs . 30 and 31
lying south and west of the Rio Grande River.
In T. 20 S. , R. 1 E., Secs . 1 to 5, inclusive ; N1/2 NE4 Sec . 8 ; N½ and SE14
Sec . 9 ; Secs . 10 to 13 , inclusive ; N1/2, SE4, N12 SW1/4 Sec . 14; NE14 , E2 NW/4
Sec . 15 ; NE14 , N½ NW4 Sec . 24; all of Secs . 16 , 32 and 36 .
In T. 20 S. , R. 2 E., Secs . 13 and 24 .
In T. 21 S. , R. 1 W. , W1/2 Sec . 12 .
In T. 21 S. , R. 1 E., Secs . 2, 16, 24, 25, 30, 31 , 32 and 36, and SW4 SW4
Sec . 29 .
In T. 21 S. , R. 2 E., Secs . 19 , 29 , 30 , 31 , and S½ Sec . 20 .
In T. 20 S. , R. 2 W. , W½ , W½ E½, NE4 NE14 Sec . 13 ; W/2, W½ E2
Sec . 24 .
And it is further ordered , pursuant to Public Resolution No. 29 of February
14 , 1920 (41 Stat.
, 434 ), as amended by Resolutions Nos . 36 and 79 , approved Jan￾uary 21 and December 28, 1922, respectively , (42 Stat .
, 358, 1067), that the unre￾served public lands in the above described areas , subject to valid rights , be opened
to entry only under the homestead and desert -land laws by qualified ex-service men
of the War with Germany , under the terms and conditions of said resolutions and
the regulations issued thereunder , for a period of ninety -one days beginning with
the sixty-third day from and after the date of this order , and thereafter any of said
lands remaining unentered will be subject to appropriation by the general public
under any public land law applicable thereto .
Subsequent to the date hereof and to the date of restoration to general dispo￾sition as herein provided, no right may be acquired to the restored lands by settle￾ment in advance of entry , or otherwise , except strictly in accordance herewith .
CALVIN COOLIDGE
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
May 13, 1927
