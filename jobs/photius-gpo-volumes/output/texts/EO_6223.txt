Executive Order
ADMINISTRATION OF THE EMERGENCY CONSERVATION WORK
By virtue of the authority vested in me by the Act of Congress entitled " AN
ACT For the relief of unemployment through the performance of useful public work,
and for other purposes ", approved March 31 , 1933 , (Public No. 5-73rd Congress ),
the sum of $ 171,638.75 is hereby allocated from the appropriation for “ National
Industrial Recovery " , contained in the Fourth Deficiency Act, fiscal year 1933 ,
approved June 16 , 1933 , and shall be transferred by the Treasury Department to the
credit of the Department of Agriculture for completing the acquisition of land for
the National Arboretum , including the costs incident to the purchase of the lands .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
July 27, 1933.
[No. 6223]
FRANKLIN D.
