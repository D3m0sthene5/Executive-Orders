Executive Order
CLASSIFICATION OF MISS PAULA GILLESPIE
Miss Paula Gillespie, who has been employed in the office of the Assistant
Secretary of the Navy , as senior clerk -stenographer , without a competitive classified
status since April 22, 1930 , is hereby classified .
This order is issued upon the recommendation of the Assistant Secretary of the
Navy.
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
January 18 , 1933 .
