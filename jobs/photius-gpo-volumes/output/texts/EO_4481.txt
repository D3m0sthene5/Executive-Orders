Executive Order
Mr. Victor Shepherd may be transferred from the position of foreman, which
is excepted from examination , to the position of clerk in the office of the chief of
Quartermaster, Panama Canal Zone , without examination, under the civil service
rules . Mr. Shepherd's transfer is recommended by the Chief of Office of the
Panama Canal because of his experience with the particular duties of the position ,
which can be tested only with difficulty . Mr. Shepherd is a Spanish-American
War veteran and has been employed almost continuously since 1906 .
The Civil Service Commission recommends approval of this order .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
CALVIN COOLIDGE
July 19, 1926.
