Executive Order
Mrs. Jeannette Warren may be employed as clerk under the Naval Establish￾ment without regard to the civil service act or rules .
This Order is issued upon recommendation of the Secretary of the Navy .
Mrs. Warren's husband was formerly a Gunner's Mate , first class , United States
Navy, and met his death in line of duty in an airplane accident at the Naval Air
Station , Pensacola, Florida .
The Civil Service Commission is unable to concur in this recommendation , as
it can recommend waivers only when procedure under the rules will not meet the
needs of the service .
Done for charity .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
December 18 , 1926.
