Executive Order
By virtue of the authority vested in me by the Act of Congress , approved
August 1 , 1914, making appropriations for the sundry civil expenses of the Govern￾ment for the fiscal year ended June 30, 1915, the customs station of Baudette ,
Minnesota , is hereby created a port of entry in Customs Collection District No.
36 , (Duluth and Superior)
, with headquarters at Duluth , Minnesota , effective
thirty days from this date .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
April 19 , 1926.
