Executive Order
Fixing the Salary of the War Claims Arbiter
Pursuant to and under the authority of the provisions of section 3 (a) of the
Settlement of War Claims Act of 1928, the salary of Judge James Waldron Remick
as War Claims Arbiter is hereby fixed at $ 10,000 per annum, effective as of the date
on which he takes office .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
January 25, 1930 .
HERBERT HOO
