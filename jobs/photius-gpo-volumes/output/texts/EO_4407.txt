Executive Order
It is hereby ordered under authority contained in the Act of June 21 , 1906
(34 Stat . 325-326 ), that the trust period on the allotments of the Ponca Indians
in Nebraska , which trust expires during the calendar year 1926, be, and is hereby
extended for a further period of ten years .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
March 30 , 1926 .
CALVIN COOLIDGE
