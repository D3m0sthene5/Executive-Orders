Executive Order
Mrs. Ruth M. Cranford may be reinstated to an appropriate position in the
Post Office Service without regard to the length of her separation, on the basis of
her former service as a printer's assistant in the Bureau of Engraving and Printing .
Mrs. Cranford , nee Murray, is the widow of Edward B. Cranford , for twenty
years an employee in the Railway Mail Service and Post Office Department and
who at his death , in 1925, left her with two dependent children and without adequate
means of support.
This order is issued on the recommendation of the Postmaster General . The
Civil Service Commission is unable to concur .
Done for charity .
THE WHITE HOUSE
February 23, 1927 .
