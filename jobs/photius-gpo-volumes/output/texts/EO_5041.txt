Supplemental Executive Order
Withdrawing Additional Lands in Utah for the Bear River Migratory
Bird Refuge
It appearing that certain lands in addition to those withdrawn by Executive
Order of July 23 , 1928 , No. 4939 , should be withdrawn for the classification con￾templated , it is hereby ordered, under authority of the act of Congress approved
June 25 , 1910 (36 Stat . 847), as amended by the act of August 24, 1912 (37 Stat.
497) , and on the recommendation of the Secretary of Agriculture , that the public
lands in T. 10 N. , R. 2 W., S. L. M., Utah, be and they are hereby temporarily
withdrawn from settlement , location , sale or entry, subject to the conditions ,
provisions and limitations of said acts, pending determination as to their suitability
for use in connection with the establishment of the Bear River Migratory Bird
Refuge authorized by the act of April 23 , 1928 (45 Stat . 448) .
This order shall continue in full force and effect unless and until revoked by
the President or by act of Congress .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
February 9 , 1929 .
[No. 5041 ]
CALVIN COOL
