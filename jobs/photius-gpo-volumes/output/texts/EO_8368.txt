Executive Order
VASD LAWUBRARY
EXEMPTION OF ALES HRDLICKA FROM COMPULSORY RETIREMENT FOR
AGE
WHEREAS , in my judgment , the public interest requires that Ales Hrdlicka ,
Curator of Physical Anthropology in the United States National Museum , Smith￾sonian Institution , who was exempted from compulsory retirement for age for a period
of one year ending March 31 , 1940, by Executive Order No. 8058 of February 23 ,
1939 , be further exempted from such compulsory retirement for a period of one year :
NOW , THEREFORE , by virtue of and pursuant to the authority vested in me
by section 204 of the act of June 30, 1932 , 47 Stat . 382 , 404 (U. S. C., title 5, sec .
715a ), I hereby further exempt the said Ales Hrdlicka from compulsory retirement for
age for a period of one year ending March 31, 1941.
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
March 7, 1940.
FRANKLIN D ROOSEV
