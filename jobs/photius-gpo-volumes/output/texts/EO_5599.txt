Executive Order
Inclusion of Captain Washington C. McCoy in the Classified Service
It is hereby directed that Capt . Washington C. McCoy , master of the U.S.M.V.
Kilkenny , Bureau of Navigation , be placed in the classified service .
This order is issued on the recommendation of the Commissioner of Navigation ,
concurred in by the Secretary of Commerce , in view of the splendid record of Captain
McCoy , his exceptional ability as a navigator , faithful attention to duty , and interest
in the service , for many years .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
April 13, 1931 .
HERBERT HOOV
