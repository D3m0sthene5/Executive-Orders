Executive Order
ILLINOIS
Under the authority of the act of Congress approved June 25, 1910 ( 36 Stat .
847), as amended by the act of August 24 , 1912 (37 Stat. 497), and subject to the
conditions therein expressed , it is hereby ordered that the following described tracts
of public land in the State of Illinois, be and they are hereby withdrawn from all
forms of appropriation , except under the isolated tract law , section 2455 U. S.
Revised Statutes as amended :
In T. 36 N. , R. 13 E., 3rd P. M., the fractional NE14 and the fractional SW14
of Sec . 13 lying north of the Indian boundary line, containing 2.21 and 1.28 acres ,
respectively .
This order shall continue in full force and effect until the said lands have been
disposed of in the prescribed manner , or until the order is revoked by the President
or by act of Congress .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
CALVIN COOLIDGE
December 15, 1928 .
