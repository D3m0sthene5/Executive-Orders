Executive Order
Modification of Reservoir - Site Reserve No. 16
Deschutes River , Oregon
The Executive order of March 28 , 1924, creating Reservoir -Site Reserve No. 16
affecting lands adjacent to Odell Lake in the headwaters of the Deschutes River , is
hereby modified to the extent of authorizing the Federal Power Commission to issue
a license to the Odell Lake Co. (Inc. ), of Crescent Lake , Oreg .
, for a minor power
project on the following described land , on condition that whenever the land is
required in connection with the development of Odell Lake Reservoir Site the licensee
agrees at its own expense to move its project works downstream a distance such
that they will not interfere with the construction and operation of a dam and other
structures for the utilization of the Odell Lake Reservoir Site .
WILLAMETTE MERIDIAN
T. 23 S. , R. 6 E., (unsurveyed ) sec. 25 ;
(unsurveyed ) sec . 26.
