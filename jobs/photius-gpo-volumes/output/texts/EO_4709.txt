Executive Order
By virtue of the authority vested in me by the Act of Congress approved
August 1, 1914, making appropriations for the sundry civil expenses of the Govern￾ment for the fiscal year ended June 30, 1915, the Executive Order of November 10 ,
1925 , making Coos County , N. H., a part of customs collection district No. 1 ,
(Maine and New Hampshire)
, with headquarters at Portland , Me.
, is hereby
revoked ; and in lieu thereof it is hereby ordered that said Coos County be made a
part of customs collection district No. 2, (Vermont)
, with headquarters at St.
Albans , Vt .
, effective thirty days from the date of this order.
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
August 19, 1927 .
