Executive Order
ALASKA
Under authority of the act of Congress , approved March 12 , 1914 (38 Stat .
, 305) ,
it is hereby ordered that Lots 7 and 8, Block 53, in the townsite of Nenana, Alaska ,
containing 9,500 square feet and 7,566 square feet , respectively, according to the
plat approved February 3, 1917, be reserved for the use of The Alaska Railroad .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
December 21 , 1925.
