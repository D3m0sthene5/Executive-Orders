Executive Order
NEW MEXICO
Pursuant to the authority conferred upon me by the Act of Congress approved
June 25, 1910 (36 Stat . 847), as amended by the Act of Congress of August 24,
1912 (37 Stat . 497)
, entitled : "An Act to authorize the President of the United
States to make withdrawals of public lands in certain cases", it is hereby ordered
that the lots 1 to 16, inclusive, Sec . 7, T. 24 S. , R. 7 W., N. M. P. M., New Mexico ,
containing 620.68 acres , be withdrawn from settlement , location , sale or entry and
reserved for the use of the New Mexico National Guard as a site for a target range .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
March 15, 1926.
