Executive Order
In accordance with the provisions of Sections 3141 and 3142 of the Revised
Statutes , and by virtue of the authority thereby vested in and conferred upon the
President , it is hereby ordered that the following changes in Internal Revenue Col￾lection Districts shall be made and shall take effect February 1 , 1927 :
Michigan
1. The Fourth Internal Revenue Collection District of Michigan
shall be abolished .
2. The counties of Alger , Allegan, Antrim, Baraga, Barry , Benzie,
Berrien, Cass , Charlevoix , Chippewa , Delta , Dickinson , Eaton ,
Emmet , Gogebic , Grand Traverse, Houghton , Ionia , Iron,
Kalamazoo , Kalkaska , Kent , Keweenaw, Lake , Leelanau , Luce,
Mackinac , Manistee, Marquette , Mason , Mecosta, Menominee,
Missaukee , Montcalm , Muskegon, Newaygo , Oceana , Ontona￾gon , Osceola , Ottawa , St. Joseph , Schoolcraft, Van Buren , and
Wexford , of said State, which now comprise the Fourth Dis￾trict, shall be consolidated with and made a part of the First
Internal Revenue Collection District of Michigan .
3. The First Internal Revenue Collection District of Michigan
shall thereafter be known as the Internal Revenue Collection
District of Michigan .
It is hereby further ordered , by virtue of the authority above referred to :
1. That Fred L. Woodworth , of Detroit , Michigan , now Collector
of the First Internal Revenue Collection District of Michigan ,
be, and he hereby is, designated Collector of Internal Revenue
for the said consolidated Internal Revenue Collection District
of Michigan .
2. That this change shall take effect February 1 , 1927 .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
CALVIN COOLIDGE
January 12, 1927 .
