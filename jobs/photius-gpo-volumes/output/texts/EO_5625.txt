Executive Order
Saturday Working Hours , Field Service , Department
of the Interior
It is hereby ordered that four hours , exclusive of time for luncheon , shall constitute
a day's work on Saturdays for all clerks and other employees of the field service of
the Department of the Interior ; and all Executive or other orders in conflict herewith
are hereby revoked .
It is provided, however , that this order shall not apply to any bureau or office ,
or to any of the clerks or other employees thereof , that may for special public reasons
be excepted therefrom by the head of said department , or where the same would
be inconsistent with the provisions of existing law.
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
May 18 , 1931 .
