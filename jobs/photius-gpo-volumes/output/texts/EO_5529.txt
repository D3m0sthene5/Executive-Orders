Executive Order
Designation of Mrs. Leafie E. Dietz to Sign Land Patents
Mrs. Leafie E. Dietz , of Colorado , appointed by the Secretary of the Interior
January 6, 1931, to the position of clerk to sign land patents , is hereby designated as
required by law to sign my name to said patents .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
January 8 , 1931 .
