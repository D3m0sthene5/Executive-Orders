Executive Order
Mr. Aaron J. Stanton may be reinstated as stockman , technical stores (lumber) ,
at $7.28 per diem , in the Supply Department , Navy Yard, Pearl Harbor , T. H.,
without regard to the length of his separation from the service .
Mr. Stanton resigned without prejudice as quarterman and master boat builder
in the Navy Yard at Puget Sound, Washington , after twenty -seven years of service ,
on May 14 , 1919. No eligibles are available for appointment to this position .
The Commission concurs with the Navy Department in recommending this
order .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
February 1 , 1926.
