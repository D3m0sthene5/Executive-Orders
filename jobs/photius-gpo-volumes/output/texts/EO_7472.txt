Executive Order
MODIFICATION OF EXECUTIVE ORDER NO. 7140 TO PERMIT MRS . RUTH
HAMPTON, ASSISTANT DIRECTOR, DIVISION OF TERRITORIES AND
ISLAND POSSESSIONS , DEPARTMENT OF THE INTERIOR , TO ACQUIRE
A CLASSIFIED CIVIL SERVICE STATUS
By virtue of and pursuant to the authority vested in me by paragraph Eighth of
subdivision SECOND of section 2 of the Civil Service Act (22 Stat. 403 , 404), Execu￾tive Order No. 7140 of August 13, 1935, authorizing the appointment of Mrs. Ruth
Hampton to the position of Assistant Director , Division of Territories and Island
Possessions , Department of the Interior , without compliance with the civil service
rules , is hereby modified so as to permit Mrs. Hampton to acquire a classified civil
service status as a result of her appointment to such position .
FRANKLIN D ROOSEVELT
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
October 15, 1936 .
