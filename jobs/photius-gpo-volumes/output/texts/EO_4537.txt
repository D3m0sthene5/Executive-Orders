Executive Order
By virtue of the authority vested in me as President of the United States of
America , and pursuant to the provisions of Section three of the Act of Congress
approved June 7, 1926 (Public No. 345-69th Congress ), entitled " An Act To
authorize the disposition of lands no longer needed for naval purposes ," the Secre￾tary of Commerce by letter of June 17, 1926, having made application to the Secre￾tary of the Navy for the transfer thereof and given specific reasons therefor, within
ninety days after the approval of the above mentioned Act of Congress, and such
action having received my approval, it is hereby ordered that the portion of the
Naval Reservation , Blythe Island , Georgia , hereinafter described , be and the same
is hereby transferred from the control and jurisdiction of the Navy Department to
the control and jurisdiction of the Department of Commerce for lighthouse purposes :
Beginning at a point at the edge of the marsh on the west bank
of Turtle River in Latitude 31 ° 9′ 45 ″ North , thence West, true, a dis￾tance of 900 feet , more or less , to a point where the marsh land joins
the high land, thence South, true, 900 feet, thence South 22 ° East,
true, 3100 feet, more or less, to the edge of the marsh at Turtle River,
thence along the edge of the marsh on the West bank of Turtle River
3900 feet , more or less , in a general direction of North 4° West to the
point of beginning, containing 82 acres , more or less ; also a perpetual
easement for the beams of light of Turtle River Upper Range Rear
Light across the marsh at the South end of Blythe Island , as shown
on Coast & Geodetic Survey Chart No. 447.
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
November 4, 1926 .
