Executive Order
WAIVER OF THE EXAMINATION REQUIREMENTS OF CIVIL SERVICE RULE IX
TO PERMIT THE APPOINTMENT OF BERNARD J. KINNAHAN TO A CLASSI￾FIED POSITION IN THE POST OFFICE DEPARTMENT
By virtue of and pursuant to the authority vested in me by the provisions of
paragraph Eighth , subdivision SECOND , section 2 of the Civil Service Act (22 Stat .
403 , 404), it is hereby ordered that the examination requirements of Civil Service
Rule IX may be waived to permit the appointment of Bernard J. Kinnahan to an
appropriate position in the classified service in the Post Office Department.
This order is recommended by the Postmaster General , who states that since
June 10 , 1930, Mr. Kinnahan has rendered efficient service in the Post Office Depart￾ment in a position excepted from civil service requirements .
FRANKLIN D ROOSEVELT
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
December 28 , 193
