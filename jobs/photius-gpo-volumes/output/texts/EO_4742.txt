Executive Order
Mr. W. S. FitzSimons may be reinstated to the position of associate engineer
in the Engineer Department at Large , without regard to the length of his separa￾tion from the service . Mr. FitzSimons resigned without prejudice from the
position of assistant engineer on August 31 , 1920, after almost continuous service
which began in 1902 .
The Secretary of War recommends this order on the ground that there is no
employee available for transfer and no eligible on the register with the requisite
qualifications .
The case is submitted without recommendation by the Civil Service Com￾mission .
For the good of the service .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
October 12 , 1927.
