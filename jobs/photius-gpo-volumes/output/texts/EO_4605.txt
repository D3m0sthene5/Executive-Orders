Executive Order
Under authority of Section 1 of the Act of July 5, 1884 (23 Stat . 103), it is
hereby ordered that the lands comprised within the following military reservations
in the Territory of Alaska , which were reserved for military purposes by Executive
Orders hereinafter designated , the same having become useless for military purposes ,
be and are hereby placed under the control of the Secretary of the Interior for
disposition as provided in the above Act or as may be otherwise provided by law :
Cape Fanshaw . Situated at Cape Fanshaw , Alaska , at the junction of
Frederick Sound and Fanshaw Bay, nearly due east of Sitka , Alaska . Re￾served by Executive Order of February 26 , 1907 .
Tolovana . Located in Latitude 64° 50′ N .; Longitude 149° 55′ W. , on
the Tanana River . Reserved by Executive Order of May 4, 1908.
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
CALVIN COOLIDGE
March 8, 1927
