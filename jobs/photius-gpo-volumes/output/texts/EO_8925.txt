PROPERTY OF
Executive Order
NOV 18 1941
EXEMPTION OF DR. LAWRENCE W. WHITE FROM COMPULSORY
RETIREMENT FOR AGE
WHEREAS , in my judgment , the public interest requires that Dr. Lawrence W.
White , senior medical officer , Office of Indian Affairs , Department of the Interior ,
who , during the month of February , 1942, will reach the retirement age prescribed
for automatic separation from the service , applicable to him, be exempted from com￾pulsory retirement for age for a period of one year :
NOW , THEREFORE , by virtue of and pursuant to the authority vested in me
by section 204 of the act of June 30 , 1932 , 47 Stat. 382 , 404 (U. S. C., title 5, sec. 715a) ,
I hereby exempt the said Dr. Lawrence W. White from compulsory retirement for
age for a period of one year ending February 28 , 1943 .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
Oct. 25, 1941 .
FRANKLIN D ROOSEVELT
