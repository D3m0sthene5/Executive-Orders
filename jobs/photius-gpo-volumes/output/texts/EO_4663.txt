COLORADO
It is hereby ordered that Executive Order No. 4412 , dated April 7, 1926,
withdrawing the public lands in T. 2 N. , R. 75 W. , 6th P. M., Colorado , pending
a resurvey , be and the same is hereby revoked .

by proclamations of May 17, 1905, March 2, 1907, September 27, 1907, and Execu￾tive order effective July 1 , 1908, therefore , they are not subject to appropriation .
