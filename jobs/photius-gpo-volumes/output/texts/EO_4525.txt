Executive Order
By virtue of the authority vested in me as President of the United States of
America , and pursuant to the provisions of Section 6 of the Act of Congress
approved March 12 , 1926 , (44 Stat . 203 , 206) entitled "An Act authorizing the use
for permanent construction at military posts of the proceeds from the sale of sur￾plus War Department real property , and authorizing the sale of certain military
reservations , and for other purposes , " the Secretary of Commerce having made
application to the Secretary of War for the transfer thereof and given specific
reasons therefor , within ninety days after the approval of the above -mentioned Act
of Congress , and such transfer having received my approval , it is hereby ordered
that the portion of the Saint Joseph's Bay Military Reservation , in the State of
Florida , hereinafter described , be, and the same is hereby, transferred to the
Department of Commerce for use as a lighthouse reservation :
Lots 8 and 9 of Section 20, in Township 9 South, Range 11 West,
and Lots 1 , 2 and 3 in Section 29 , Township 9 South, Range 11 West,
in Calhoun County , Florida , as shown in Volume 8, Florida Plats ,
United States General Land Office .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
October 21 , 1926.
