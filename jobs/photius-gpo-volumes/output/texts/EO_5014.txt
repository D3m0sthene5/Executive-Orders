Meals En Route .
--Charges for meals taken en route to or from official station
will be allowed only in accordance with the following table :
Breakfast when arrival is after 8:00 A. M.
Dinner when arrival is after 1:00 Р. М.
Supper when arrival is after 6:00 P.M.
Breakfast when departure is before 8:00 А. М.
Dinner when departure is before 1:00 P. М.
Supper when departure is before 7:00 Р. М.
