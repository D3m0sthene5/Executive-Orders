Executive Order
ALASKA ROAD COMMISSION RESERVE
Pursuant to authority conferred upon me by the act of Congress approved
June 25, 1910 (36 Stat . 847), entitled " An Act to authorize the President of the
United States to make withdrawals of public lands in certain cases " , as amended
by act of Congress approved August 24, 1912 (37 Stat . 497 ), it is hereby ordered
that the following described lands and buildings thereon , forming part of that
portion of the old military reservation of Fort St. Michael , Alaska , which was
transferred to the Department of the Interior by Executive Order No. 4131 ,
approved January 22, 1925, be, and the same are hereby, withdrawn from settle￾ment , location , sale or entry , and reserved for the use of the Alaska Road
Commission in the construction and maintenance of roads, bridges and trails in
Alaska .
Beginning at the intersection of the East side of Fort St. Michael
wharf with mean high water line of St. Michael Bay; thence N. 23 °
40 ' W. 150 feet ; thence S 66 ° 20 ′ W 200 feet ; thence S 23 ° 40 ′ Ε 150
feet more or less , to the mean high water line ; thence northeasterly
along the high water line to the point of beginning ; with the buildings
thereon located , namely, Storehouse No. 2 , and the wharf extending
therefrom into St. Michael Bay .
This order shall continue in full force and effect unless and until revoked by
the President or by act of Congress .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
November 19 , 1927 .
