Executive Order
APPOINTMENT OF MRS . ADELLE B. FREEMAN
Mrs. Adelle B. Freeman may be appointed a junior bacteriologist in the Depart￾ment of Agriculture without reference to the civil -service rules .
Mrs. Freeman is the widow of George F. Freeman, whose death was the result of
an accident in the performance of his official duties while serving in charge of the
Department of Agriculture Puerto Rico Experiment Station . Mrs. Freeman is a
graduate of the Kansas State Agricultural College with the degree of bachelor of
science .
This order is recommended by the Secretary of Agriculture .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
February 21 , 1933 .
