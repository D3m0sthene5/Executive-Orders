Executive Order
Oliver C. Hubbard may be permanently appointed as a compositor in the
Bureau of Engraving and Printing, without regard to his relative standing on the
register of eligibles .
This Order is recommended by the Acting Secretary of the Treasury , who
states that Mr. Hubbard is a disabled veteran of the World War, has been serving
temporarily in the Bureau , has demonstrated his skill and is found to be thoroughly
qualified for the work, and that his permanent appointment is believed to be in the
interests of the service .
The Civil Service Commission is unable to concur in the issuance of this Order .
Approved for war service .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
July 15, 1926.
