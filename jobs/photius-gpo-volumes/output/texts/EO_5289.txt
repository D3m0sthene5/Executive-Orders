Executive Order
Reservation of Lands in Native Villages for Educational Purposes
Alaska

Office , tracts of land in the following described places in Alaska , upon which the

in each place , be, and they are hereby , reserved and set apart for the use of the
Office of Education , subject to any valid claims thereto existing at the date of this
