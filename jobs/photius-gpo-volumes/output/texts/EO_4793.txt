Executive Order
Thomas Baine, a deputy collector in the Internal Revenue District of Alabama ,
may be restored to the classified service without regard to the length of time he
has been separated therefrom. Mr. Baine was continuously employed in the com￾petitive classified service as an Inspector under the Post Office Department from
July 1, 1902, to September 8, 1916, when he resigned the position he was then
holding as Post Office Inspector in Charge . On December 24, 1917, he was ap￾pointed as a deputy collector of Internal Revenue and he has served continuously
as such since that date .
This order is recommended by the Secretary of the Treasury and is issued in
recognition of Mr. Baine's long and meritorious service with the Government .
The Civil Service Commission does not concur in the recommendation.
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
January 13 , 1928 .
