Executive Order
By virtue of the authority vested in me by law, Paragraph 12 of the Executive
Order of February 2, 1914, ( No. 1888 ) , providing conditions of employment for the
permanent force for The Panama Canal, is hereby amended so as to authorize pay￾ment to certain hourly and per diem employees for emergency work in excess of
eight hours on Sundays and authorized holidays , on the same basis as now auth￾orized or may hereafter be authorized for employees of the Naval establishment
in the United States. As amended , the paragraph will read as follows :
12. Employees above the grade of laborer , appointed with rates of pay per
hour or per day, will not be employed over eight hours in any one calendar day,
except in case of emergency . The time such employees work over eight hours in
one calendar day, and time worked on Sundays and regularly authorized holidays ,
including January 1st, February 22d , May 30th , July 4th, Labor Day , Thanksgiving
Day, and December 25th , shall be considered overtime for which time and one-half
will be allowed. Such employees who work on the days prior and subsequent to
the holidays specifically named above will be allowed their regular pay for eight
hours for such days, in addition to pay for any work performed ; Provided , That
payment to such employees for emergency work in excess of eight hours on Sundays
and authorized holidays shall be made on the same basis as now authorized or may
hereafter be authorized for employees of the Naval establishment in the United
States .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
June 17, 1926 .
