Executive Order
8/16/35
WITHDRAWAL OF PUBLIC LANDS
ALASKA
By virtue of and pursuant to the authority vested in me by the act of June 25,
1910 (ch . 421 , 36 Stat . 847), as amended by the act of August 24, 1912 (ch . 369 , 37
Stat. 497 ), and subject to the conditions therein expressed , to valid existing rights, and
to the provisions of existing withdrawals , it is ordered that the public lands in the fol￾lowing -described areas in Alaska be, and they are hereby , temporarily withdrawn from
settlement, location , sale , entry, or other form of appropriation and reserved to con￾serve the public interests , and in aid of such legislation as may hereafter be proposed
or recommended :
SEWARD MERIDIAN
T. 16 N. , R. 1 W. , secs . 3 , 4, 5, 6 , and 7.
T. 16 N. , R. 2 W. , secs . 1 , 2, 11 , and 12 .
This order shall remain in full force and effect unless and until revoked by the
President or by act of Congress .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
August 6, 1935.
FRANKLIN D ROOSEVELT
