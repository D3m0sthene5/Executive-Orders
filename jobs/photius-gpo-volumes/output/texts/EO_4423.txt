Executive Order
Wyoming
Under authority contained in the act of Congress approved June 25 , 1910
(36 Stat .
, 847) , as amended by the act of August 24, 1912 (37 Stat .
, 497)
, and
subject to the conditions , provisions and limitations of said acts , it is hereby ordered
that the public lands in T. 41 N. , Rs . 89 and 90 W. , 6th . P. M. , Wyoming , be and
the same are hereby withdrawn from settlement , location , sale , entry and all forms
of appropriation pending a resurvey of said townships under the act of Congress
approved May 29 , 1908 (35 Stat .
, 465 ) ; this withdrawal to remain in full force and
effect from the date hereof until the resurvey is duly approved and accepted , and
the approved plats thereof are officially filed in the proper United States district
land office , unless another date be fixed by the Commissioner of the General Land
Office , whereupon this withdrawal will become of no effect .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
April 19 , 1926.
