Executive Order
OF
SEP 5-1939
HARVARD LAW LIBRARY
EXEMPTION OF MRS . FRANCES S. NICHOLS FROM COMPULSORY RETIREMENT
FOR AGE
WHEREAS, in my judgment, the public interest requires that Mrs. Frances
S. Nichols , Editorial Assistant, Bureau of American Ethnology , Smithsonian Institu￾tion, who , during the month of August, 1939 , will reach the retirement age prescribed
for automatic separation from the service , applicable to her , be exempted from
compulsory retirement for age for a period of one year :
NOW, THEREFORE , by virtue of and pursuant to the authority vested in me
by section 204 of the act of June 30, 1932 , 47 Stat. 382 , 404 (U. S. C., title 5, sec.
715a), I hereby exempt the said Mrs. Frances S. Nichols from compulsory retirement
for age for a period of one year ending August 31 , 1940.
FRANKLIN D ROOSEVELT
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
July 17, 1939 .
