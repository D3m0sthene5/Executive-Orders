Executive Order
Mrs. Grace G. Baldwin may be appointed to an appropriate clerical position in
the Department of Commerce without regard to the length of her separation from
the Federal Service .
Mrs. Baldwin was first appointed to the Government Service as a copyist in the
Post Office Department on April 3 , 1891 , from which Department she resigned in
1910. She is the wife of Albertus H. Baldwin , formerly Director of the Bureau of
Foreign and Domestic Commerce of the Department of Commerce , and Commer￾cial Attache in the service of that Department at London , who is now confined to a
sanitarium completely incapacitated for work of any kind . This order is issued upon
the recommendation of the Secretary of Commerce , who states that Mrs. Baldwin's
services can be used to advantage in his Department .
The Civil Service Commission is unable to concur.
For charity .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
October 16 , 1928.
