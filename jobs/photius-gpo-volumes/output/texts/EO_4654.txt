Executive Order
STATE OF WASHINGTON
Under authority contained in the act of June 25, 1910 (36 Stat .
, 847 ), as
amended by the act of August 24, 1912 (37 Stat.
, 497), and subject to the conditions ,
provisions and limitations of said acts , itis hereby ordered that two small unsurveyed
islands in T. 34 N. , R. 1 E. , W. M., Washington , one in Sec . 22 a few hundred feet
off shore from Rosario Beach , Deception Pass State Park, and the other about one￾half mile easterly of Deception Pass and about one-quarter mile south of Fidalgo
Island, approximately in Sec . 25, be and the same are hereby withdrawn from
settlement , location , sale , entry and all forms of appropriation , subject to any valid
existing rights in and to the same for classification and in aid of legislation .
This order shall continue in full force and effect unless and until revoked by
the President or by an act of Congress .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
May 23 , 1927.
