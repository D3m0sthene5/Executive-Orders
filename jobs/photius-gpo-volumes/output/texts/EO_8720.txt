AUTHORIZING THE APPOINTMENT OF WATSON B. MILLER TO THE POSITION
OF ASSISTANT ADMINISTRATOR OF THE FEDERAL SECURITY AGENCY
WITHOUT REGARD TO CIVIL SERVICE RULES
By virtue of the authority vested in me by the provisions of paragraph Eighth
of subdivision SECOND of section 2 of the Civil Service Act (22 Stat . 403, 404),
it is hereby ordered that Watson B. Miller may be appointed to the position of
Assistant Administrator of the Federal Security Agency without compliance with the
competitive provisions of the Civil Service Act and Rules : Provided , that the said
Watson B. Miller shall establish before the Civil Service Commission requisite quali￾fications for such position.
This order is recommended by the Federal Security Administrator .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
March 22, 1941 .
