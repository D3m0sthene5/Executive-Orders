Executive Order
Mr. William A. Rogers may be reinstated as guard in the United States Pen￾itentiary Service without regard to the length of his separation from the service .
This order is issued upon the recommendation of the Attorney General and the
Warden of the United States Penitentiary , Atlanta , Georgia , who state that , in

The Civil Service Commission concurs .
