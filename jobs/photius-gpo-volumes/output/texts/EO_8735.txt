PROPERTY OF
Executive Order
MAY 12 1941
IRVED LAW LIBRARY
AUTHORIZING CERTAIN EMPLOYEES IN THE OFFICE OF THE ADMINISTRATOR ,
FEDERAL SECURITY AGENCY , TO ACQUIRE A COMPETITIVE CLASSIFIED
STATUS WITHOUT REGARD TO THE CIVIL SERVICE RULES
By virtue of the authority vested in me by paragraph Eighth of subdivision
SECOND of section 2 of the Civil Service Act (22 Stat. 403 , 404), it is hereby ordered
that the following-named persons , now occupying attorney positions in the Office of
the Administrator , Federal Security Agency , may acquire a competitive classified
status without compliance with the requirements of the Civil Service Rules :
Edward K. Adelsheim
Joseph L. Maguire
Henry J. Meloy
Bernard M. Niezer
Richard M. Siegel
This order is issued upon recommendation of the Federal Security Administrator ,
with the concurrence of the Civil Service Commission.
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
April 14 , 1941 .
[No. 8735]
FRANKLIN
