Executive Order
The maximum age limit for entrance to the open competitive examination for
postmaster at the post office at The Dalles , Oregon , is hereby waived to permit
Mr. Albert S. Roberts to take the pending open competitive examination for that
position .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
February 13 , 1928 .
