Executive Order
PROPERTY OF
NOV 1-1938
EXEMPTION OF MISS ADA TANNER FROM COMPULSORY RETIREMENT
FOR AGE
WHEREAS , in my judgment, the public interest requires that Miss Ada Tanner ,
junior administrative assistant in the Office of the Treasurer of the United States ,
who, during the current month, will reach the retirement age prescribed for auto￾matic separation from the service , applicable to her, be exempted from compulsory
retirement for age for a period of one year :
NOW , THEREFORE , by virtue of and pursuant to the authority vested in
me by section 204 of the Act of June 30, 1932 , 47 Stat. 382, 404 (U. S. C. , title 5,
sec . 715a ) , I hereby exempt the said Miss Ada Tanner from compulsory retirement
for age for a period of one year ending October 31 , 1939 .
FRANKLIN D ROOSEVELT
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
October 12 , 1938.
