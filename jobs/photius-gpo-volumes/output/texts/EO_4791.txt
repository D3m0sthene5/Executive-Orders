Executive Order
WHEREAS the corporate life of the United States Grain Corporation expired
according to the terms of its charter on August 14, 1922 ; and
WHEREAS since the expiration of said charter the officers of said corporation
have been collecting its assets and liquidating its obligations , and there remains
nothing further to be done by said corporation and its officers , except to pay the
remaining assets thereof to its stockholder, United States of America ; and
WHEREAS said corporation has fully accounted for all its acts and for all the
funds which have come into its hands , showing the disposition thereof and the
balance on hand;
Now, THEREFORE , I, CALVIN COOLIDGE , President of the United States , pur￾suant to the power and authority conferred on me by law, and as holder of all the
issued and outstanding capital stock of the United States Grain Corporation , do
hereby approve , ratify , confirm and adopt all the acts of the said United States
Grain Corporation and of the officers and directors thereof, and I do hereby approve ,
ratify , confirm and adopt the final accounting of the property and funds of said
corporation and the disposition thereof as shown by the audited accounts submitted
by Edward M. Flesh , President , Treasurer and Liquidating Trustee of said corpo￾ration ; and I do order and direct that Edward M. Flesh , as Liquidating Trustee of
said corporation, pay the balance in its hands , amounting to the sum of $ 115,419.98 ,
into the Treasury of the United States , in full and final settlement , liquidation and
dissolution of said corporation and in full and final payment to its stockholder ,
United States of America .
And I do further order and direct that all the records , books of account and
files of the United States Food Administration and of the United States Wheat
Director heretofore held in the custody of said corporation , pursuant to Executive
Order , and all the records , books of account and files of said corporation be trans￾ferred and delivered to and shall be preserved by the Department of Commerce as
custodian thereof, until the completion of a building to be used for the permanent
housing of Government records , when the Department of Commerce shall deliver
all said records , books of account and files into said building and into the custody
of the official in charge thereof.
Ju Witness Whereof, I have hereunto set my hand and caused the
seal of the United States to be hereunto affixed .
[SEAL ]
DONE this 31st day of December , in the year of our Lord one thousand
nine hundred and twenty -seven and of the Independence of the
United States of America the one hundred and fifty -second .
By the President :
FRANK B. KELLOGG
Secretary of State
