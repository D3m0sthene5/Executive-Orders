Executive Order
Miss Barbara Davis may be appointed to a suitable position in the Depart￾ment of Commerce without regard to the provisions of the civil service rules .
Miss Davis is a daughter of the late Dr. William H. Davis , who at the time
of his death , January 8, 1929, was chief statistician in the Bureau of the Census .
This order is issued upon the recommendation of the Secretary of Commerce , who
believes it to be an appropriate expression of appreciation of meritorious service
rendered by her father . The Civil Service Commission cannot concur , its recom￾mendations for waivers being limited to those cases where the needs of the service
cannot be met by the usual procedure under the civil service rules .
THE WHITE HOUSE,
February 26, 1929 .
