Executive Order
Mrs. Dawn J. Sutton may be appointed to an appropriate position in the Post
Office Department or the field service thereof without regard to the civil service rules .
Mrs. Sutton is the widow of Ernest R. Sutton , for more than fifteen years an
employee of the Post Office Department , who died on December 27, 1926, leaving
her with a young daughter who is not self-supporting .
This order is issued upon the recommendation of the Postmaster General. The
Civil Service Commission is unable to concur.
For charity .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
March 20, 1928 .
[No. 4835]
