Executive Order
Withdrawal of Public Lands for Classification
California
Under authority of the act of Congress approved June 25, 1910 (36 Stat. 847),
as amended by the Act of August 24, 1912 (37 Stat. 497 )
, and subject to the condi￾tions therein expressed , it is hereby ordered that the unreserved public lands within

for classification , subject to prior valid rights :

In Tps . 7, 11 and 12 N., R. 1 E.;
In T. 6 N. , R. 1 W.;
In Tps . 4 and 5 S. , R. 1 E.;
In T. 5 S. , R. 2 E.;
In Tps . 3 and 4 S., R. 1 W.;

President or by act of Congress .
