Executive Order
Mrs. Teresa C. Suit may be appointed to an appropriate position in the
Government Printing Office without regard to examination requirements under the
civil service rules .
Mrs. Suit is the widow of Ralph M. Suit who was employed in the Government
Printing Office from October 12 , 1916 , to December 23, 1927. She has four small
children and is without income for their support.
This order is recommended by the Public Printer. As the Civil Service
Commission can properly recommend waivers only when required by the needs of
the service , it does not concur .
For charity .
THE WHITE HOUSE,
July 13 , 1928.
