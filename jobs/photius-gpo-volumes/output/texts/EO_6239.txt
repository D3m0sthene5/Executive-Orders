Executive Order
ORGANIZATION OF EXECUTIVE AGENCIES
Under the provisions of section 22 of Executive Order No. 6166, dated June 10 ,
1933 , issued pursuant to section 16 of the act of March 3, 1933 (Public , No. 428 ,
47 Stat. 1517 ), the effective date for the abolition of the Federal Coordinating Service ,
as directed in section 17 of said Executive order, is deferred to October 10 , 1933 :
Provided , That such abolition may be made effective , in whole or in part, between
August 10 , 1933, and October 10, 1933, by order of the President.
FRANKLIN D. ROOSEVELT .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
August 2 , 1933 .
