Executive Order
AUTHORIZATION TO APPOINT MISS MAUDE MCGAUGH
By virtue of the authority vested in me by the provisions of the last sentence of
the eighth paragraph of subdivision second of section 2 of the Civil Service Act of
January 16 , 1883 (ch . 27 , 22 Stat . 403, 404), it is hereby ordered that Miss Maude
McGaugh may be appointed to a position in the classified service without compliance
with the requirements of the civil -service rules .
This order is recommended by the Administrator of Veterans ' Affairs .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
November 29 , 1933.
