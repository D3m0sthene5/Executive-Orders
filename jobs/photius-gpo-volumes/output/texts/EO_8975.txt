Executive Order
PROPERTYOF JAN9
3942
AUTHORIZING THE ATTORNEY GENERAL TO MAKE APPOINTMENTS IN THE
ALIEN PROPERTY DIVISION WITHOUT REGARD TO THE REQUIREMENTS
OF THE CIVIL SERVICE ACT AND RULES
By virtue of the authority vested in me by section 2 of the Civil Service Act
(22 Stat . 404), the Attorney General of the United States is hereby authorized to make
appointments to positions in the Alien Property Division , Department of Justice ,
including the position of head of the Division , without regard to the requirements of the
Civil Service Act and Rules .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
December 13, 1941 .
FRANKLIN D ROOSEVELT
