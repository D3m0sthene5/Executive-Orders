Executive Order
RESCINDING THE EXECUTIVE ORDER RESTRICTING THE TRANSPORTATION
OF PASSENGERS FROM CERTAIN PORTS IN THE ORIENT
TO A UNITED STATES PORT.
Whereas it appears from reports received that the unusual prevalence of epidemic
cerebrospinal meningitis infection in the Orient has subsided and the danger of
introducing this disease into the United States now can be controlled satisfactorily
through the exercise of ordinary quarantine procedures at United States ports of
arrival ;
Therefore , Executive Order No. 5143, dated June 21 , 1929 , " Restricting for
the Time Being the Transportation of Passengers From Certain Ports in the Orient
to a United States Port " , hereby is rescinded .
THE WHITE HOUSE
March 3, 1933 .
