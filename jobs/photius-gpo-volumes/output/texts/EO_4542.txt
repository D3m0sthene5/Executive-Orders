Executive Order
ALASKAN TIMBER RESERVE NO . 1 REDUCED
It is hereby ordered that Executive Order No. 2589 dated April 11 , 1917 ,
reserving the timber on the following described area in Alaska under the act of
March 12 , 1914 (38 Stat .
, 305), for use in connection with the location, construction
and operation of the Government railroad in Alaska , which was partially revoked
by my order of November 26, 1924, No. 4107, be and the same is hereby revoked
in so far as it affects the timber on the remainder of such area :
A tract of land five miles wide on each side of the right of way of the
Government railroad , commencing at Seward, on Resurrection Bay , and
extending along the right of way of said railroad to the left limit of Knik
River.
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
November 13 , 1926 .
