Executive Order
New Mexico
Under authority contained in the act of Congress approved June 25, 1910
(36 Stat.
, 847 ), as amended by the act of August 24, 1912 (37 Stat .
, 497) , and sub￾ject to the conditions , provisions and limitations of said acts , it is hereby ordered
that the public lands in T. 1 N. , R. 19 E., and T. 4 N. , R. 16 E. , N. M. P. M. , New
Mexico , be and the same are hereby withdrawn from settlement , location , sale , entry
and all forms of appropriation pending a resurvey of said townships under the act
of Congress approved March 3, 1909 (35 Stat .
, 845 )
, as amended by the joint res￾olution of June 25, 1910 (36 Stat.
, 884) ; this withdrawal to remain in full force and
effect from the date hereof until the resurvey is duly approved and accepted and
the approved plats thereof are officially filed in the proper United States district
land office , unless another date be fixed by the Commissioner of the General Land
Office , whereupon this withdrawal will become of no effect .
THE WHITE HOUSE,
April 19, 1926 .
