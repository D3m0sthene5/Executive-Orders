PROPERTY OF
Executive Order
JUL 22 1941
VARD LAW IBRARY
EXEMPTION OF CLARENCE H. BRANSCOMBE FROM COMPULSORY RETIRE￾MENT FOR AGE
WHEREAS, in my judgment, the public interest requires that Clarence H.
Branscombe , assistant supervising engineer , Public Buildings Administration , Federal
Works Agency, who , during the month of August, 1941 , will reach the retirement age
prescribed for automatic separation from the service , applicable to him, be exempted
from compulsory retirement for age for a period of one year .
NOW, THEREFORE , by virtue of and pursuant to the authority vested in me
by section 204 of the act of June 30, 1932, 47 Stat . 382 , 404 , (U. S. C., title 5, sec .
715a), I hereby exempt the said Clarence H. Branscombe from compulsory retirement
for age for a period of one year ending August 31, 1942 .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
June 7, 1941 .
FRANKLIN D ROOSE
