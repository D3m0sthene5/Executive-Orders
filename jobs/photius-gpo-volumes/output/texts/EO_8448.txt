PROPERTY OF
Executive Order JUL 23 1940
VDLAWHBRARY
EXEMPTION OF MAITLAND S. WRIGHT FROM COMPULSORY RETIREMENT
FOR AGE
WHEREAS, in my judgment , the public interest requires that Maitland S.
Wright , Chief , Supply Unit, Department of State, who , during the month of July ,
1940 , will reach the retirement age prescribed for automatic separation from the
service , applicable to him, be exempted from compulsory retirement for age for a
period of one year :
NOW , THEREFORE , by virtue of and pursuant to the authority vested in me
by section 204 of the act of June 30, 1932 , 47 Stat . 382 , 404 ( U. S. C., title 5, sec .
715a), I hereby exempt the said Maitland S. Wright from compulsory retirement
for age for a period of one year ending July 31 , 1941 .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
June 18 , 1940.
FRANKLIN D ROOSEV
