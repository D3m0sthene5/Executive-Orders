Executive Order
Wichita , Kansas , Designated a Customs Port of Entry
By virtue of the authority vested in me by the act of Congress approved August
1, 1914 (38 Stat. 623), making appropriations for the sundry civil expenses of the
Government for the fiscal year ending June 30, 1915, Wichita , Kans.
, is hereby
designated a port of entry in Customs Collection District No. 45 (St. Louis )
, with
headquarters at St. Louis , Mo., effective 30 days from the date of this order.
THE WHITE HOUSE,
September 25, 1930.
