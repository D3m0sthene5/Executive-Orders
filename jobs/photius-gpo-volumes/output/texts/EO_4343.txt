Executive Order
By virtue of the authority vested in me by the Act of Congress approved
August 1, 1914, making appropriations for the sundry civil expenses of the
Government for the fiscal year ended June 30, 1915, the port limits of Los Angeles ,
the headquarters port of Customs Collection District No. 27 (Los Angeles ) are
hereby extended to include Long Beach, Calif .
, effective November 15 , 1925 .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
November 16 , 1925 .
