Executive Order
PUERTO RICO ATTACHED TO INTERNAL REVENUE COLLECTION DISTRICT
OF MARYLAND .
By virtue of the authority vested in me by Section 3141 of the Revised Statutes of
the United States , as amended by the Act of February 27 , 1877 , 19 Stat. L. , 248 , it is
hereby ordered that Puerto Rico be attached to and made a part of the Internal
Revenue Collection District of Maryland for all purposes authorized by the internal
revenue laws of the United States .
This order will be effective on and after July 28 , 1933 .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
July 11 , 1933.
[No. 6201 ]
