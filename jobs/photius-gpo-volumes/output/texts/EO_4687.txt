Executive Order
It is hereby ordered under authority contained in the Act of March 2, 1917
(39 Stat . L., 969-976 ), that the period of trust on lands held in trust for the use
and benefit of the Potrero (La Piche or La Jolla) and Rincon bands or villages of
Indians in California , which trust expires during the calendar year 1927, be, and is
hereby extended for a period of ten years from the date of expiration thereof .
THE WHITE HOUSE,
July 11 , 1927.
[No. 4687]
CALVIN COOL
