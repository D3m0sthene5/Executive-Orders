Executive Order
By virtue of the authority vested in me by the Act of Congress approved
August 1 , 1914, making appropriations for the sundry civil expenses of the Govern￾ment for the fiscal year ended June 30, 1915, the customs station of Fabens , Texas ,
in customs collection district #24 (El Paso) is hereby created a customs port of
entry in the said district , with headquarters at El Paso , effective thirty days from
the date of this order .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
May 1 , 1928 .
[No. 4869]
CALVIN COOLIDGE
