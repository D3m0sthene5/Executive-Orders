Executive Order
Mr. Hugh S. Hanna may be reinstated in the Department of Labor as Editor￾in-Chief, $3800 per annum , Bureau of Labor Statistics , without regard to the length
of his separation from the service .
Mr. Hanna resigned from the position of expert with a classified status, Bureau
of Labor Statistics , on March 19, 1918, since which time he has been engaged in work
similar to that required in the position to be filled and has had constant contact
with the Bureau of Labor Statistics .
The Civil Service Commission recommends the reinstatement of Mr. Hanna
which is desired by the Department as in the interests of the service .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
February 2 , 1926.
