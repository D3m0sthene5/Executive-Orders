Executive Order
By virtue of the authority vested in me as President of the United States of
America , and pursuant to the provisions of Section 6 of the Act of Congress approved
March 12, 1926, entitled " An Act authorizing the use for permanent construction
at military posts of the proceeds from the sale of surplus War Department real
property , and authorizing the sale of certain military reservations , and for other
purposes ," the Secretary of Commerce having made application to the Secretary
of War for the transfer thereof and given specific reasons therefor, within ninety
days after the approval of the above mentioned Act of Congress , and such action
having received my approval , it is hereby ordered that the portion of the Fort
Jackson Military Reservation , in the State of Louisiana , hereinafter described , be
and the same is hereby transferred to the Department of Commerce for use as a
lighthouse reservation :
Beginning at the center of Fort Jackson Light , which is located
77 ° East 2640 feet from the center of Fort Jackson ; thence true East
25 feet to the initial point on the eastern boundary of the tract of land
hereby transferred; thence true South 200 feet to a point which marks
the southeast corner of the tract ; thence true West 50 feet to the
point which marks the southwest corner of said tract ; thence true
North approximately 645 feet to the shore of the Mississippi River ,
which marks the northwest corner of said tract ; thence easterly along
the shore of the Mississippi River, approximately 51 feet to a point
which marks the northeast corner of said tract; thence true South
parallel with and 50 feet east of the western boundary of said tract to
the point of beginning.
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
January 28, 1927 .
