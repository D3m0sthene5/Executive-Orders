PROPERTY OF
JUL 181939
DLAWIERARY
REINSTATEMENT OF PAUL H. ALLING , A FORMER FOREIGN SERVICE OFFICER
By virtue of and pursuant to the authority vested in me by section 12 of the act
of February 23 , 1931 , 46 Stat. 1207 , 1208 , it is ordered that Paul H. Alling , who
resigned his position as an officer in the Foreign Service of the United States on
November 30, 1930, to accept an appointment to a position in the Department of
State, be, and he is hereby , reinstated in the Foreign Service of the United States in
Class IV, his reinstatement to be effective upon the date his nomination to a position
in such class is confirmed by the Senate .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
June 12 , 1939.
FRANKLIN D ROOSEV
