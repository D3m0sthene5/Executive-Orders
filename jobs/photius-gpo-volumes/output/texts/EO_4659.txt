Executive Order
Schedule B of positions which may be filled upon noncompetitive examinations
is hereby amended so that paragraph 2 of Subdivision III , Department of Commerce ,
will read as follows :
2. Commercial attaches and assistant commercial attaches , trade
commissioners and assistant trade commissioners , district managers
and assistant district managers , experts , and commercial agents and
special agents whose initial salary is $2400 or more , to investigate
trade conditions abroad and in the United States , including the in￾sular possessions , with the object of promoting the foreign commerce
of the United States .
The above amendment based on the Act of March 3, 1927 , establishing the
Foreign Commerce Service of the United States , specifically sets forth the present
practice , and transfers from the noncompetitive to the competitive class the posi￾tions of special agent and commercial agent , allocated to Grades 5 and 6 (less than
$2400) of the Clerical , Administrative and Fiscal Service .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
June 6, 1927 .
