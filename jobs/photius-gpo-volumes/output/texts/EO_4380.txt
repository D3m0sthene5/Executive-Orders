Executive Order
Mrs. Stella Davis Mudd may be reinstated as telephone switchboard operator
in the Naval Powder Factory at Indian Head , Maryland , without regard to the
length of her separation from the service .
Mrs. Mudd's husband was formerly an employee of the Naval Powder Factory
at Indian Head and his sudden death , while not incurred in the line of duty , left
her a widow with four minor children to support.
This order is issued upon recommendation of the Secretary of the Navy in lieu
of an Executive Order of November 3 , 1925 , which was not utilized , authorizing
the reinstatement of Mrs. Mudd as an operative in the Bureau of Engraving and
Printing.
The Civil Service Commission is unable to concur in the recommendation .
Done for charity .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
February 5, 1926.
