Executive Order
4
DESIGNATING THE CHAIRMAN OF THE BOARD OF GOVERNORS OF THE
FEDERAL RESERVE SYSTEM
By virtue of and pursuant to the authority vested in me by Section 10 of the
Federal Reserve Act, (38 Stat . 260) , as amended by Section 203 (a) of the Act of
August 23 , 1935 , (49 Stat. 704 ), I hereby designate Marriner S. Eccles as Chairman
of the Board of Governors of the Federal Reserve System, to serve as such for a term
of four years .
THE WHITE HOUSE
February 3, 1936.
