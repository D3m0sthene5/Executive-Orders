Executive Order
Schedule A of the civil service rules , positions excepted from examination, is
hereby amended by adding an eighth paragraph to Subdivision XI , Department of
Commerce, to read as follows :
8. Caretakers and light attendants employed in connection with emergency
landing fields and other air navigation facilities under the Air Commerce Act
of 1926. Continuance of this exception beyond a period of three years from
the date of its adoption shall be dependent upon the joint agreement of the
Department of Commerce and the Civil Service Commission .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
November 3, 1926.
[No. 4536]
CALVIN
