Executive Order
TRANSFER OF LANDS FROM THE TUSAYAN TO THE PRESCOTT NATIONAL
FOREST AND FROM THE PRESCOTT TO THE TONTO NATIONAL FOREST
ARIZONA
By virtue of and pursuant to the authority vested in me by the act of June 4,
1897 (30 Stat . 11 , 36, U. S. C., title 16, sec . 473 ), and on the recommendation of the
Secretary of Agriculture , it is ordered that all lands in the Tusayan National Forest
as defined by proclamation of June 28, 1910 (36 Stat . 2709), lying to the west and
south of the following -described land be, and they are hereby, transferred to and
made a part of the Prescott National Forest as defined by proclamation of August
14 , 1923 (43 Stat . 1923 ):

the Atchison -Topeka and Santa Fe Railroad intersects said section line ; thence in a
