Executive Order
ENFORCEMENT OF THE NORTHERN PACIFIC HALIBUT ACT
By virtue of and pursuant to the authority vested in me by section 7 of the
Northern Pacific Halibut Act (47 Stat. 142), I hereby designate such vessels of the
Coast Guard as the Commandant of the Coast Guard shall assign for that purpose
to patrol the territorial waters of the United States , as defined in the said act, for the
enforcement of the said act and the Convention for the Preservation of the Halibut
Fisheries of the Northern Pacific Ocean and the Bering Sea .
FRANKLIN D ROOSEVELT
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
October 24, 1935
