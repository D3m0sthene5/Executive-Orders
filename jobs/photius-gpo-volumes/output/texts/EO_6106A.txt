Executive Order
RELIEF OF UNEMPLOYMENT THROUGH THE PERFORMANCE OF USEFUL
PUBLIC WORK
By virtue of the authority vested in me by the Act of Congress entitled " An
Act for the relief of unemployment through the performance of useful public work,
and for other purposes ," approved March 31 , 1933 (Public No. 5-73rd Congress ),
supplementing Executive Order No. 6101 , dated April 5, 1933 , it is hereby ordered
that :
(1 ) The Chief of Finance of the Army is designated , empowered , and directed
to receive , expend , and account for
all

relief
