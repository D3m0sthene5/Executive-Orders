Executive Order
APPOINTMENT OF MRS . RUTH BASCOT DARTT
Mrs. Ruth Bascot Dartt may be appointed to an existing vacancy in the rating
of junior clerk, Grade CAF-2, at $ 1,440 per annum , Headquarters , United States
Marine Corps , Washington , D.C., without regard to the civil -service act or rules .
Mrs. Dartt is the widow of Lieut . Veryl H. Dartt, United States Marine Corps ,
who was killed in line of duty on April 18 , 1930, in Nicaragua .
This order is issued upon the recommendation of the Secretary of the Navy.
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
April 4, 1932 .
