Executive Order
Paragraph 22 , Subdivision VIII of Schedule A of the civil service rules which
permits appointments of scouts , buffalo keepers , assistant buffalo keepers and park
rangers in the National Parks without examination is hereby revoked .
This order is recommended by the Secretary of the Interior and the Civil
Service Commission .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
May 19, 1926.
