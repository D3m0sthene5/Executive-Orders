Executive Order pes30
CHANGE OF NAME OF CUSTOMS PORT OF NYANDO , NEW YORK
150
By virtue of the authority vested in me by section 1 of the act of August 1, 1914
(ch . 223 , 38 Stat. 609 , 623 ; 19 U.S.C. , sec . 2), it is hereby ordered that the name of
the customs port of Nyando , New York, in customs collection district no . 7 (St.
Lawrence ), with headquarters at Ogdensburg , New York, be, and the same is hereby ,
changed to Rooseveltown , New York, effective as of this date .
FRANKLIN D. ROOSEVELT .
