Executive Order
Alaskan Timber Reserve No. 1 Reduced .
Under authority of the act of Congress approved March 12 , 1914 (38 Stat .
305 )
, it is hereby ordered that Executive Order No. 2217 of June 22, 1915, creating
Alaskan Timber Reserve No. 1 reserving the timber on certain lands in Alaska for
the use of the United States in connection with the location , construction , and
operation of the Government railroad in Alaska , which was reduced by Executive
Orders No. 2810 of February 16, 1918, No. 3033 of February 5, 1919 and No. 4107 ,
on November 26, 1924, is hereby revoked in so far as it affects the timber on the
remainder of the area included in the original order .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
April 27, 1928 .
