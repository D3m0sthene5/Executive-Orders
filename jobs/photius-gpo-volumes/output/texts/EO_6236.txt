Executive Order
APPOINTMENT OF HENRY ROE CLOUD
Henry Roe Cloud may be appointed without competitive civil -service examina￾tion Superintendent of the Haskell Institute , Lawrence , Kansas . This institution
is an important nonreservation Indian school . Mr. Cloud is a Winnebago Indian .
He has had long and varied experience in Indian education ; he has been a field repre￾sentative in the Indian Service; and he is eminently fitted for the superintendency .
This order is issued upon the recommendation of the Secretary of the Interior .
THE WHITE HOUSE,
July 28 , 1933 .
