Executive Order
The Executive Order of June 22, 1928, amending previous executive orders
requiring candidates for appointment to presidential postmasterships to pass a civil
service examination , is hereby waived to permit the nomination of Ward M. Parker
as postmaster at Venice, Florida, without examination .
This order is issued upon the recommendation of the Postmaster General . The
Civil Service Commission reports that , as a result of three examinations announced
for filling this position, only one eligible has been secured and that further exami￾nations would probably not result in securing enough eligibles for a complete certi￾fication of three names to the Postmaster General .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
January 15, 1929 .
