GROPERTY OF
Executive Order
FEB 191940
HARVARD LAW:
EXEMPTION OF HENRY CLARKE HILL FROM COMPULSORY RETIREMENT
FOR AGE
WHEREAS, in my judgment, the public interest requires that Henry Clarke
Hill , Warden of the United States Penitentiary , Lewisburg, Pennsylvania , who
was exempted from compulsory retirement for age for a period not in excess of one
year from March 1, 1939, by Executive Order No. 8041 of January 25, 1939 , be
further exempted from compulsory retirement for a period not in excess of six months
from March 1 , 1940 :
NOW, THEREFORE , by virtue of and pursuant to the authority vested in me
by section 204 of the act of June 30, 1932, 47 Stat. 382 , 404 (U. S. C., title 5, sec.
715a) , I hereby further exempt the said Henry Clarke Hill from compulsory retire￾ment for age for a period not in excess of six months from March 1 , 1940 .
FRANKLIN D ROOSEVELT
THE WHITE HOUSE,
January 22, 194
