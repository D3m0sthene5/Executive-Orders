Executive Order
Mr. George H. McCrea may be permanently appointed as deputy collector
and inspector in the Customs Service of the Treasury Department , without regard
to the examination requirements of the civil service rules .
This Order is recommended by the Secretary of the Treasury who states that
Mr. McCrea has served from May 23, 1922, to the present time, with the exception
of a period of a little less than one year, under temporary appointment, that his
record has been very satisfactory in the difficult and dangerous duties to which he
has been assigned , and that he appears to possess in a rare degree the pecular qual￾ifications essential for their successful performance . The Civil Service Commission
submits the Order without prejudice .
Done for merit demonstrated in the service .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
June 1 , 1926.
