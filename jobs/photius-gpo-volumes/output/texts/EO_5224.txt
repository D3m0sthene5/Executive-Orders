Executive Order
[Appointment of Mrs. Alice Tasker]
Mrs. Alice Tasker , formerly a matron in the New York Office of the Veterans '
Bureau from December 15 , 1921 , to February 8, 1924, when she was discharged
without prejudice for continued absence due to injury incurred in an accident in
the subway at 42nd Street, New York, may be reinstated in an appropriate position
in any part of the service . The Director of the Veterans ' Bureau states that her
services were satisfactory during her employment under his office .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
November 15, 1929.
