Executive Order
NEW MEXICO
Under authority contained in the act of June 25, 1910, (36 Stat .
, 847) , as
amended by the act of August 24, 1912 (37 Stat.
, 497) , and subject to the conditions ,
provisions and limitations of said acts , it is hereby ordered that the public lands in
T. 17 N. , R. 10 E., N. M. P. M. , New Mexico , be and the same are hereby with￾drawn from settlement , location, sale , entry and all forms of appropriation pending
a
resurvey of said township under the act of Congress approved March 3, 1909
(35 Stat .
, 845), as amended by the Joint Resolution of June 25, 1910 (36 Stat .
,
884)
; this withdrawal to remain in full force and effect from the date hereof until
the resurvey is duly approved and accepted and the approved plat thereof is offici￾ally filed in the proper United States district land office , unless another date be fixed
by the Commissioner of the General Land Office , whereupon this withdrawal will
become of no effect .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
September 11 , 1926 .
