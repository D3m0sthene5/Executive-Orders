Executive Order
Mrs. Lulu M. Davis may be reinstated as an operative in the Bureau of
Engraving and Printing without regard to the length of time she has been separated
from the service .
Mrs. Davis was employed as a classified printer's assistant in the Bureau from
December 17 , 1906 , to September 14 , 1915 , and is the widow of August Davis , a
plate printer in the Bureau for nearly 22 years prior to his death on June 18 , 1922 .
This order is recommended by the Secretary of the Treasury .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
July 8, 1929 .
