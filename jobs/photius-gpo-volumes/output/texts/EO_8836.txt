EXEMPTION OF PAUL BARTSCH FROM COMPULSORY RETIREMENT FOR AGE
WHEREAS , in my judgment, the public interest requires that Paul Bartsch ,
Curator of Mollusks and Cenozoic Invertebrates in the United States National
