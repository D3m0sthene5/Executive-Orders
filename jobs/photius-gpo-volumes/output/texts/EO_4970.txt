Executive Order
By virtue of the authority vested in me by the Act of Congress , approved
August 1 , 1914, making appropriations for the sundry civil expenses of the
Government for the fiscal year ended June 30, 1915 , TULSA, OKLAHOMA , is
hereby created a port of entry in customs collection district No. 45 (St. Louis) ,
with headquarters at St. Louis , Mo., effective thirty days from date .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
September 28 , 1928 .
