Executive Order
Civil Service Rule VII , Section 2, is hereby amended by striking out the fifth
paragraph which reads as follows :
" In the General Accounting Office : Operative for the audit of accounts
and vouchers of the Postal Service by means of labor -saving devices ."
Section 6 of Rule XI, restricting transfers and promotions of operatives in the
General Accounting Office , is hereby revoked .
These amendments have the effect of placing in the apportioned service the
position of operator in the General Accounting Office .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
August 14, 1928 .
