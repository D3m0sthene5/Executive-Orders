Executive Order
CALIFORNIA
It is hereby ordered under authority of the act of Congress approved June 25 ,
1910 ( 36 Stat . 847) as amended by the act of August 24 , 1912 (37 Stat . 497) , that
the following described area in the State of California be, and the same is hereby ,
temporarily withdrawn from settlement , location , sale or entry , subject to prior
claims legally initiated and maintained and to the conditions and limitations of said
acts, for classification and pending determination as to the advisability of adding
same to the Pinnacles National Monument :
MT. DIABLO BASE AND MERIDIAN.
Sections 20 to 29, inclusive , W , and W + E , Section 33 , and Sections 35 and
36 , T. 16 S. R. 7 E.
Section 1 , E , and E NW of Section 2, E of Section 9, E of Section 11 ,
Sections 12 to 15, inclusive , and Sections 22 to 24, inclusive , T. 17 S. , R. 7 E.
This order shall continue in full force and effect unless and until revoked by
the President or by act of Congress .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
February 2 , 1929 .
