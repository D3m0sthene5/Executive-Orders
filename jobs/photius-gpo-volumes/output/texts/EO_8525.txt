PROPERTY OF
Executive Order
521 1940
DLAWEIBRARY
EXEMPTION OF HARRY T. EDWARDS FROM COMPULSORY RETIREMENT FOR
AGE
WHEREAS , in my judgment, the public interest requires that Harry T. Edwards ,
senior technologist , Bureau of Plant Industry , Department of Agriculture , who was
exempted from compulsory retirement for age for a period of one year ending October
31 , 1940 , by Executive Order No. 8259 of September 21 , 1939 , be further exempted
from such compulsory retirement for a period of one year :
NOW , THEREFORE , by virtue of and pursuant to the authority vested in
me by section 204 of the act of June 30, 1932 , 47 Stat . 382, 404 (U. S. C., title 5,
sec. 715a) , I hereby further exempt the said Harry T. Edwards from compulsory
retirement for age for a period of one year ending October 31 , 1941 .
FRANKLIN D ROOSEVELT
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
August 26, 1940
