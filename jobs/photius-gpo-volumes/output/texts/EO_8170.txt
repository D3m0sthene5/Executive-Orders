Executive Order
PROPERTY OF
JUL 181939
RVOED LAW LUBRARY
AUTHORIZING THE APPOINTMENT OF A. SIDNEY JOHNSON TO THE POSITION
OF DEPUTY COMMISSIONER OF CUSTOMS WITHOUT REGARD TO CIVIL
SERVICE RULES
By virtue of and pursuant to the authority vested in me by the provisions of
paragraph Eighth , subdivision SECOND , section 2 of the Civil Service Act (22 Stat .
403 , 404 ), it is hereby ordered that A. Sidney Johnson may be appointed a deputy
commissioner of customs in the Bureau of Customs, Treasury Department , at
Washington , D. C. , without compliance with the requirements of the Civil Service
Rules .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
June 15, 1939 .
[No. 8170]
FRANKLIN
