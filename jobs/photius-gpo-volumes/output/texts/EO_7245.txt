Executive Order
ESTABLISHING WAUBAY MIGRATORY WATERFOWL REFUGE
SOUTH DAKOTA
By virtue of and pursuant to the authority vested in me as President of the
United States , and in order to effectuate further the purposes of the Migratory Bird
Conservation Act (45 Stat. 1222 ), it is ordered that the following -described lands ,
consisting of 2,200 acres , more or less , acquired or to be acquired by the United
States , and certain adjoining public lands, in Day County , South Dakota , be, and
they are hereby , reserved and set apart for the use of the Department of Agricul￾ture , subject to valid existing rights , as a refuge and breeding ground for migratory
birds and other wildlife : Provided , That any private lands within the areas described
shall become a part of the refuge hereby established upon the acquisition of title
thereto or lease thereof by the United States :
