Executive Order
WHEREAS, pursuant to a decree of the Circuit Court of the United States
for the Eastern District of New York, rendered August 10, 1910, in settlement of a
suit brought by the United States against the Neponsit Realty Company , et al .
, the
United States acquired a tract of land containing 13-15 acres at Rockaway Point,
Long Island , New York ; and
WHEREAS, the said tract of land has heretofore been used for Coast Guard
purposes ; and
WHEREAS, a portion of the said tract, hereinafter described , is desired by the
War Department for use in connection with Fort Tilden , Harbor Defenses of Sandy
Hook, and the Secretary of the Treasury consents to the transfer thereof to the
jurisdiction and control of the War Department ; and
WHEREAS , the land in question has never been impressed with any specific
use either by Executive Order or by Act of Congress ,
NOW , THEREFORE , it is hereby ordered that the following described tract
of land be and the same is hereby reserved and set apart for the use of the War
Department in connection with the Fort Tilden Military Reservation , New York ,
to wit:
Beginning at a point described as monument #124 located on the south￾erly line of Rockaway Beach Boulevard (running East and West) running
331 feet North 69 ° 49 ′ 38 ″ East; thence 1279 feet South 34° 26′ 47.7 " East ;
thence 230 feet South 60° 35 ' 12.3" West ; thence 1332 feet North 29 ° 24 '
47.7 " West across monument #120 to place of beginning at monument #124 .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
November 2 , 1928.
