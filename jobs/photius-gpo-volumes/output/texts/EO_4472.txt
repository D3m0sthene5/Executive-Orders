Executive Order
In view of the provision in Section 11 of the Act of May 24, 1924, "An Act for
the reorganization and improvement of the Foreign Service of the United States ,
and for other purposes " , that the provisions of Sections 8 and 10 of the Act of
April 5, 1906 , relative to official fees and the method of accounting therefor shall
include both branches of the Foreign Service, fees collected by secretaries in the
diplomatic service in the performance of official services will , on and after September
30 , 1926 , be collected in accordance with the rates stipulated in the Tariff of Con￾sular Fees .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
July 3, 1926 .

CALVIN COOLIDGE
