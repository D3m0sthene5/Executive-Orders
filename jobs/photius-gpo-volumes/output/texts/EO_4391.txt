Second Executive Order
It is hereby ordered that the Strawberry Valley Reservation , Utah , for the
protection of native birds , created by Executive Order No. 1032, dated February
25 , 1909 , be , and the same is , hereby modified in area by adding thereto adjoining
tracts of land so that the reservation as enlarged will embrace the following legal
subdivisions :
In township three south, range eleven , the southeast quarter of section seven ,
the south half of sections eight and nine, and the southwest quarter of section
ten; the west half , and the southeast quarter of section fifteen , all of sections six￾teen and seventeen , and the east half of sections eighteen and nineteen ; all of
sections twenty , twenty -one, twenty -two , twenty -seven , twenty -eight , twenty￾nine, and the east half of section thirty; the east half , and the southwest quarter
of section thirty -one , and all of sections thirty-two , thirty-three and thirty-four ,
in said township .
In township three south, range twelve, the southeast quarter of section thirty-six .
In township four south, range twelve, the northeast quarter of section one .
In township four south, range eleven , all of sections three , four , five and six ;
the east half of section eight , all of section nine and the west half of section ten ,
all west of the Uinta Meridian , Utah, as segregated by the broken line upon the
diagram hereto attached and made a part of this order.
All of the lands enumerated are within a withdrawal for reclamation purposes
and their additional reservation for a bird refuge , which is hereby ordered , is
subject to their use by the Bureau of Reclamation , including leasing for grazing,
under the provisions of the act approved June 17, 1902 (32 Stat .
, 388), and acts
amendatory thereof, and to any other valid existing rights , and are set apart for
the use of the Department of Agriculture as a preserve and breeding ground for native birds .
It is unlawful for any person to hunt , trap , capture, wilfully disturb or kill
any native bird of any kind whatever or take the eggs of such birds within the
limits of this reservation, except under such rules and regulations as may be
prescribed by the Secretary of Agriculture . Warning is expressly given to all
persons not to commit any of the acts herein enumerated under the penalties
provided by Section 84 , U. S. Criminal Code, approved March 4, 1909 (35 Stat .
,
1088) .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
March 11 , 1926 .
