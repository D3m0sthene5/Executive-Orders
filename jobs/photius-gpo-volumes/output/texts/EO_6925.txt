Executive Order
RESTORATION TO ENTRY OF CERTAIN TRACTS OF LAND IN TONGASS
NATIONAL FOREST
ALASKA

1897 (ch . 2 , 30 Stat . 11 , 34, 36), and upon the recommendation of the Secretary of
Agriculture , it is ordered that the following -described tracts of land in Alaska , occupied
as home sites and identified by elimination surveys, plats and field notes of which are
on file in the General Land Office , Washington , D. C., be , and they are hereby ,
excluded from the Tongass National Forest and restored to entry under the applicable
public-land laws :
