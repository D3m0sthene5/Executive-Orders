Executive Order
APPOINTMENT OF MISS MARY STEWART
Miss Mary Stewart, who on July 18, 1929, was appointed Assistant Director of
Education in the Indian Office , Department of the Interior , under Civil Service Rule
II, section 10 , without competitive examination , with the approval of the Civil Service
Commission , because there were no eligibles at that time possessing her peculiar
qualifications , is hereby placed in the classified service .
This order is issued upon the recommendation of the Secretary of the Interior .
THE WHITE HOUSE,
February 16, 1933.
