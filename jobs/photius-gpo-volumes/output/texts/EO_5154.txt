Executive Order
Mrs. Birdie E. Gordon may be reinstated as an operative in the Bureau of
Engraving and Printing , without regard to the length of time she has been separated
from the service .
Mrs. Gordon was employed in the Bureau as a classified printer's assistant and
operative from September 13, 1915, to October 16 , 1923 , when she resigned because
of the illness of her husband who was suffering from tuberculosis and has recently
died , leaving her dependent upon her own efforts for support .
This Order is recommended by the Secretary of the Treasury .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
HERBERT HOOVER
July 9 19
