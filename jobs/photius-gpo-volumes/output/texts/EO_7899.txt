Executive Order
AUTHORIZING THE APPOINTMENT OF MISS AMY G. MAHER TO A POSITION
IN THE SOCIAL SECURITY BOARD WITHOUT REGARD TO CIVIL SERVICE
RULES
By virtue of and pursuant to the authority vested in me by the provisions of
paragraph Eighth of subdivision SECOND of section 2 of the Civil Service Act of
January 16 , 1883 , (22 Stat. 403, 404), it is hereby ordered that Miss Amy G. Maher
may be appointed to a position as technical adviser in the Social Security Board
without compliance with the requirements of the civil service rules .
This order is recommended by the Social Security Board .
FRANKLIN D ROOSEVELT
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
May 26, 1938 .
