Executive Order
Executive Order No. 3471 , issued May 21 , 1921 , authorizing the Civil Service
Commission to extend for single periods of a year or less the employments of
qualified persons engaged on activities growing out of the World War which were
temporary and diminishing in nature, is hereby revoked.
Employees now in the service whose retention has been authorized under the
Order of May 21 , 1921, are hereby covered into the classified service .
This order is recommended by the War Department and concurred in by the
Civil Service Commission .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
December 8 , 1928 .
