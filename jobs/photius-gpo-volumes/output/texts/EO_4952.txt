Executive Order
Mrs. Gladys Evert may be appointed to an appropriate position in the classi￾fied service without regard to the civil service rules .
Mrs. Evert is the widow of Lieutenant Paul Evert , who died in flight while pilot￾ing one of the United States Army entries in the National Elimination Balloon Race .
She is a graduate of Lawrence University , Appleton , Wisconsin , with a Bachelor of
Arts degree . This order is issued upon the recommendation of the Acting Secretary
of War that Mrs. Evert be made eligible for appointment thru executive order to an
appropriate position in the classified civil service .
The Civil Service Commission submits the case without prejudice. It is unable
to concur in view of the existence of an eligible register .
For patriotism .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
August 20, 1928.
