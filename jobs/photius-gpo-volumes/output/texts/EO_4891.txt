Executive Order
The positions of skilled laborer and tide observer now held by William C.
Meyer and Frederick A. Kummell , respectively, in the Coast and Geodetic Survey
of the Department of Commerce shall be included with their occupants in the
classified service from the date of this order .
Mr. Kummell entered the service in 1899 and Mr. Meyer in 1900, in the Coast
and Geodetic Survey , without examination, after the position of tide observer had
been classified and accordingly all their subsequent service has been technically
irregular . The order is issued so that they may no longer be held as having an
irregular status as neither they nor the Department of Commerce was primarily
responsible for the irregularity involved in their appointment.
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
May 26, 1928.
