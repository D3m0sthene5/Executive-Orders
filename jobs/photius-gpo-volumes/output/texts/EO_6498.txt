Executive Order
EXTENSION OF TRUST PERIOD ON INDIAN LANDS EXPIRING DURING
THE YEAR 1934
By virtue of the authority vested in me by section 5 of the act of February 8 ,
1887 (ch . 119, 24 Stat . 388 , 389 ), and by the acts of June 21 , 1906 (ch . 3504, 34 Stat.
325, 326 ), and March 2, 1917 (ch . 146, 39 Stat. 969 , 976 ), it is hereby ordered that
the period of trust applying to any Indian lands , whether of a tribal or individual
status , upon which the period of trust expires during the calendar year 1934, be, and
the same is hereby, extended for a period of 10 years from the date on which any
such trust would otherwise expire .
This order is not intended to apply in cases where Congress has specifically
reserved to itself authority to extend the period of trust on tribal or individual
Indian lands .
THE WHITE HOUSE,
December 15, 1933 ,
[No. 6498]
FRANKLIN D
