Executive Order
IDAHO
Under authority of the act of Congress approved June 25, 1910 (36 Stat . 847)
,
as amended by the act of August 24, 1912 (37 Stat . 497 ), the following described
lands in Twin Falls County , State of Idaho , are hereby temporarily withdrawn ,
subject to the conditions of said acts , for the use of the State of Idaho as a rifle
range for the Idaho National Guard :
SW4 SW4 , SE4 SW4 , SW14 SE14 and SE14 SE4 Sec . 25, T. 9 S. ,
R. 13 E., B. M., Idaho .
This order shall continue in full force and effect unless and until revoked by
the President or by act of Congress .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
November 4, 1927.
[No. 4753]
CALVIN COOLIDGE
