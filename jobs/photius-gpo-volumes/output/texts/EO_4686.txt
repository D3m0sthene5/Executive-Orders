Executive Order
WHEREAS, By Executive Order dated October 31 , 1910, a certain tract of land
containing two acres , more or less, of the military reservation of Fort Ruger , sit￾uated on Diamond Head , Island of Oahu, Territory of Hawaii , was transferred to
the Department of Commerce and Labor , and set aside for a lighthouse reservation ;
and
WHEREAS, a right-of-way for the main highway , known as the Diamond
Head Road , through the military reservation of Fort Ruger , has been returned to
the Territory of Hawaii for road purposes ; and
WHEREAS, the Department of Commerce desires the transfer to it of a narrow
strip of land between the new highway and the present Diamond Head Lighthouse
Reservation ;
NOW , THEREFORE , It is hereby ordered that the following described portion
of the military reservation of Fort Ruger , situated at Diamond Head , Island of
Oahu , Territory of Hawaii , be , and the same is hereby transferred to the Depart￾ment of Commerce , and set aside for a lighthouse reservation , viz :
Beginning at a point , which is the northwest corner of the present Light￾house Reservation , marked by the U. S. Military Reservation concrete monu￾ment #32 , and which is 10.87 feet distant and bears 107° 27′ 06 ′′ (true ) from
Territorial Trig. Station "Kaauau ", (said Trig. Station being marked by a
concrete monument) thence :-
1. 190 ° 19′55 ″-25.77 feet more or less to the south boundary of the
proposed Diamond Head Road , thence :
