Executive Order
By virtue of the authority vested in me by the Act of Congress approved
August 1 , 1914, making appropriations for the sundry civil expenses of the
Government for the fiscal year ended June 30, 1915, the customs station of ROMA
is hereby created a port of entry in Customs Collection District No. 23 (San
Antonio ), with headquarters at San Antonio , Texas, effective this date .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
March 14 , 1928.
