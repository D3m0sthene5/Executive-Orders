Executive Order
EXCLUDING A CERTAIN TRACT OF LAND FROM TONGASS NATIONAL FOREST
AND RESTORING IT TO ENTRY
ALASKA
By virtue of and pursuant to the authority vested in me by the act of June 4,
1897 , 30 Stat. 11 , 36 (16 U. S. C. , sec . 473 )
, and upon the recommendation of the
Secretary of Agriculture , it is ordered that the following-described tract of land in
Alaska , occupied as a homesite , be , and it is hereby, excluded from the Tongass
National Forest and restored to entry under the applicable public -land laws :
COPPER RIVER MERIDIAN
T. 51 S. , R. 68 E. , sec . 6, NE4SW4SE4SW4 , NSE4SW4SE4SW¼ , fractional
NWSE SE4SW¼ , fractional N2SW SEXSEXSW ,
aggregating 5.30 acres .
THE WHITE HOUSE,
December 21 , 1935.
