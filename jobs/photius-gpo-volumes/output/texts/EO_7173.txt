Executive Order
ESTABLISHING WHITE RIVER MIGRATORY WATERFOWL REFUGE
ARKANSAS
By virtue of and pursuant to the authority vested in me as President of the
United States , and in order to effectuate further the purposes of the Migratory Bird
Conservation Act (45 Stat . 1222), it is ordered that the following-described lands ,
consisting of 110,000 acres , more or less , acquired or to be acquired by the United
States , in Arkansas , Desha, Monroe , and Phillips Counties, Arkansas , be , and they
are hereby , reserved and set apart for the use of the Department of Agriculture ,
subject to valid existing rights, as a refuge and breeding ground for migratory birds
and other wildlife : Provided , That any private lands within the areas described shall
become a part of the refuge hereby established upon the acquisition of title or lease
thereto by the United States :
