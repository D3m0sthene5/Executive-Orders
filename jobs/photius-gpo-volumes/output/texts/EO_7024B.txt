Executive Order
ORDER NO. 3 UNDER ACT OF FEBRUARY 22, 1935
WHEREAS, by virtue of and pursuant to the authority vested in me by the
Act of Congress entitled "An Act to regulate Interstate and Foreign Commerce in
petroleum and its products by prohibiting the shipment in such commerce of petro￾leum and its products produced in violation of State law, and for other purposes " ,
Public No. 14, 74th Congress , approved February 22, 1935, and the Executive Order
of the President of the United States , No. 6979 (February 28, 1935), I am authorized
to designate an agency to execute the powers and functions vested in me by the Act
of Congress and Executive Order hereinbefore mentioned ; and
WHEREAS, I find that it is necessary and appropriate for the enforcement of
the provisions of said Act to establish an administrative and investigative agency
in connection with tenders presented to the Federal Tender Board No. 1 ;
NOW, THEREFORE , it is ordered :
ARTICLE I
I hereby create and designate an Agency to be known as the " Federal Petroleum
Agency No. 1 " , hereinafter designated as the "Agency ", which shall exercise all
duties and functions pertaining or incidental to investigations provided for by my
Order No. 1 approved and promulgated March 1 , 1935 .
ARTICLE II
Hereafter all applications for tenders shall be referred by the "Board " to the
"Agency " for investigation and report. Representatives of the "Agency " are
authorized to appear before the " Board " to present evidence and examine witnesses
whenever it appears necessary or advisable .
