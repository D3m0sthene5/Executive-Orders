Executive Order
CODE OF FAIR COMPETITION FOR THE SHOVEL , DRAGLINE AND
CRANE INDUSTRY

the provisions of title I of the National Industrial Recovery Act, approved June 16 ,
1933 , for my approval of a Code of Fair Competition for the Shovel, Dragline and
Crane Industry, and hearings having been held thereon and the Administrator having
rendered his report containing an analysis of the said code of fair competition together
with his recommendations and findings with respect thereto , and the Administrator
having found that the said code of fair competition complies in
