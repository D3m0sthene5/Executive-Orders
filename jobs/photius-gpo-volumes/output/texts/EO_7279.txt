Executive Order
REVOCATION IN PART OF EXECUTIVE ORDER NO . 5341 , OF MAY 2 , 1930 ,
WITHDRAWING PUBLIC LANDS
ARIZONA
By virtue of and pursuant to the authority vested in me by the act of June 25 ,
1910 , ch . 421 , 36 Stat . 847 , as amended by the act of August 24, 1912 , ch . 369 , 37
Stat. 497, Executive Order No. 5341 , of May 2 , 1930, withdrawing , together with
other lands , public lands in T. 14 N. , R. 3 W. of the Gila and Salt River meridian ,
Arizona , pending a resurvey , is hereby revoked as to the said township .
This order shall become effective upon the date of the official filing of the plat of
resurvey of said township .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
January 25, 1936 .
FRANKLIN D ROOSEVELT
