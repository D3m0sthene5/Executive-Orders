Executive Order
By virtue of the authority vested in me by the Act of Congress approved Aug￾ust 1 , 1914 , making appropriations for the sundry civil expenses of the Government
for the fiscal year ended June 30, 1915, the Customs station of Oroville , Washing￾ton, is hereby created a port of entry in Customs Collection District No. 30
(Washington) with headquarters at Seattle , Washington, effective thirty days from
the date of this order .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
October 11 , 1929.
