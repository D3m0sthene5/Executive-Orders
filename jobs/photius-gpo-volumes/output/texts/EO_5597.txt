Executive Order
Public Water Restoration No. 68
New Mexico
So much of Executive order of February 8, 1923, creating Public Water Reserve
No. 86 , as affects the land hereinafter listed is hereby revoked , and all such land not
otherwise reserved or withdrawn is hereby restored to the public domain and shall
become subject to disposition under the laws applicable thereto upon such date and
after such notice as may be hereafter determined and announced .
