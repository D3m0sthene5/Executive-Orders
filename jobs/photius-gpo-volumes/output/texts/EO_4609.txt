Executive Order
Joseph T. O'Regan may be appointed to a classified position in the Bureau of
Engraving and Printing without compliance with the requirements of the civil
service rules .
Mr. O'Regan is a World War veteran and has been receiving vocational train￾ing in the Bureau of Engraving and Printing where his services can be utilized
permanently in the position of skilled helper. This Order is suggested by the
Secretary of the Treasury . The Civil Service Commission is unable to concur .
Done for veteran relief.
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
March 10 , 1927 .
