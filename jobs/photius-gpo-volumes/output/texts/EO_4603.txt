Executive Order
Mrs. Clotilde Woods Holderby may be employed as a clerk at Headquarters ,
U. S. Marine Corps , Washington , D. C. , without regard to the civil service act or
rules . Mrs. Holderby's husband , Lieutenant Andrew R. Holderby , U. S. M. С. ,
lost his life in a fire at the Marine Barracks , Quantico , Virginia , February 5 , 1927 ,
leaving Mrs. Holderby one minor child to support. The Secretary of the Navy
recommends the Order .
The Civil Service Commission is unable to concur in this recommendation .
For charity .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
March 5, 1927 .
