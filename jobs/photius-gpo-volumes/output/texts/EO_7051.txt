Executive Order
AUTHORIZING THE APPOINTMENT OF GUY C. HANNA TO A POSITION IN
THE TREASURY DEPARTMENT WITHOUT REGARD TO CIVIL-SERVICE
RULES
By virtue of and pursuant to the authority vested in me by the provisions of
paragraph Eighth of subdivision SECOND of section 2 of the Civil Service Act of
January 16 , 1883 (22 Stat. 403, 404), it is hereby ordered that Guy C. Hanna may
be appointed as non-attorney member of the Committee on Enrollment and Dis￾barment, Treasury Department , without compliance with the requirements of the
civil-service rules and without being accorded a classified status .
This order is recommended by the Secretary of the Treasury .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
May 21, 1935 ,
[No. 7051]
FRANKL
