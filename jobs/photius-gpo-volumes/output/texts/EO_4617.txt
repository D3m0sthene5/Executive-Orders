Executive Order
Mrs. Alice K. McCracken may be employed as a clerk under the Navy Depart￾ment , without regard to the civil service act or rules .
This order is issued upon recommendation of the Secretary of the Navy. Mrs.
McCracken's husband, Lt. Comdr . John J. McCracken , U. S. N. , died at Marble￾head , Massachusetts , June 29 , 1926, as a result of disabilities incurred in line of
duty , leaving Mrs. McCracken seven young children to support.
The Civil Service Commission is unable to concur in this recommendation .
For charity .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
March 19, 1927.
