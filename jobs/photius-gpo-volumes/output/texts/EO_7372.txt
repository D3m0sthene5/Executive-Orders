Executive Order
AMENDMENT OF SCHEDULE A OF THE CIVIL SERVICE RULES
By virtue of and pursuant to the authority vested in me by the provisions of
paragraph Eighth of subdivision SECOND of section 2 of the Civil Service Act of
January 16 , 1883 (ch . 27, 22 Stat . 403, 404 ), Section 2 of Executive Order No. 7228
of November 18 , 1935, is hereby amended by changing the number of the new sub￾division added by said order to Schedule A of the Civil Service Rules from XXV
to XXVI .
THE WHITE HOUSE,
May 18 , 1936 .
