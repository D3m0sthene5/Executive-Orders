Executive Order
Withdrawal of Public Lands for Resurvey
Arizona
Under authority contained in the act of June 25, 1910 (36 Stat. 847 ) , as amended
by the act of August 24, 1912 (37 Stat. 497), and subject to the conditions , provisions ,
and limitations of said acts , it is hereby ordered that the public lands in T. 1 S. , R.
2 W. , T. 3 S. , R. 4 W. , and T. 2 N. , R. 4 W., Gila and Salt River meridian , Arizona , be ,
and the same are hereby, withdrawn from settlement , location, sale , entry, and all
forms of appropriation , pending a resurvey of said townships under the act of March 3 ,
1909 (35 Stat . 845) .
This order shall continue in full force and effect unless and until revoked by the
President, or by an act of Congress .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
March 5, 1931 .
