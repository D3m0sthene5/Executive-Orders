Executive Order
AUTHORIZING WILLIS R. GREGG TO ACT AS SECRETARY OF AGRICULTURE
By virtue of the authority vested in me under the provisions of section 179
of the Revised Statutes of the United States (5 U.S.C. , sec. 6), I hereby authorize
and direct Willis R. Gregg, Chief of the Weather Bureau of the Department of
Agriculture , to perform the duties of Secretary of Agriculture during the absence
of the Secretary of Agriculture and the Assistant Secretary of Agriculture .
FRANKLIN D. ROOSEVELT .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
February 9, 1934 .
