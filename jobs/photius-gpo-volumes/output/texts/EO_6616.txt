Executive Order
WITHDRAWAL OF PUBLIC LANDS FOR USE AS AN ADDITION TO THE CORDOVA
AVIATION LANDING FIELD
ALASKA
By virtue of the authority vested in me by the act of June 25, 1910 (ch . 421 , 36
Stat. 847)
, as amended by the act of August 24, 1912 (ch. 369 , 37 Stat . 497), and
subject to the conditions therein expressed and to all valid existing rights , it is ordered
that the public lands in the Territory of Alaska included within the following-described
boundaries be, and they are hereby, withdrawn from settlement , location , sale , or
entry and reserved for use as an addition to the Cordova Aviation Landing Field ,
Alaska :
Beginning at corner no . 4 of U.S. Survey No. 1668 , which is seventy -five hun￾dredths (0.75) chains south of witness corner MC and forty-six and seventeen
