Executive Order
Prescribing fees and mileage to be charged for attempting to serve and for return
on process or any order, judgment, or decree of any court.
By authority of law, the following Executive Order is made and declared effec￾tive on the date of the signing thereof, to wit:
That Section 813 of the Code of Civil Procedure of the Canal Zone shall be
amended by adding thereto the following provisions for fees to be charged for
return on and mileage in attempts to serve process , or any order , judgment or
decree of any court in civil cases :
(a) For each return, one dollar .
(b) For mileage going one way in attempting to serve or execute any
process , order , judgment or decree of any court, for each mile traveled one
way, ten cents .
(c) No such fees shall be charged against the United States or The
Panama Canal or an officer thereof sued in his official capacity .
CALVIN COOLIDGE
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
June 23, 1928
