DESIGNATING THE ELECTRIC HOME AND FARM AUTHORITY , A CORPORA￾TION ORGANIZED UNDER THE LAWS OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA ,
AS AN AGENCY OF THE UNITED STATES
I hereby designate the Electric Home and Farm Authority , a corporation
organized under the laws of the District of Columbia , as an agency of the United
States , to continue , insofar as permitted by its certificate of incorporation, the func￾tions of, and to carry on the business hitherto engaged in by, Electric Home and Farm
Authority , Inc. , a corporation organized under the laws of the State of Delaware.
The direction included in Executive Order No. 6514 , of December 19 , 1933 ,
that the outstanding stock of the Electric Home and Farm Authority , Inc., a corpora￾tion organized under the laws of Delaware, be voted by the Directors of the Tennes￾see Valley Authority , is hereby rescinded , and I hereby direct that the outstanding
stock of the said Electric Home and Farm Authority , Inc., be voted jointly by the
trustees of the Electric Home and Farm Authority , a corporation organized under
the laws of the District of Columbia .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
August 12, 1935.
FRANKLIN D ROOSEVELT
