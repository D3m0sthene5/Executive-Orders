DLAW PRARY
AUTHORIZING THE APPOINTMENT OF ARCHIE L. HARDY TO AN APPROPRIATE
CLERICAL POSITION IN THE SOCIAL SECURITY BOARD , FEDERAL SECURITY
AGENCY , WITHOUT COMPLIANCE WITH THE REQUIREMENTS OF THE
CIVIL SERVICE RULES
By virtue of the authority vested in me by the provisions of paragraph Eighth
of subdivision SECOND of section 2 of the Civil Service Act (22 Stat . 403, 404), it
is hereby ordered that Archie L. Hardy may be appointed to an appropriate clerical
position in the Social Security Board of the Federal Security Agency without com￾pliance with the requirements of the Civil Service Rules .
This action is taken upon the recommendation of the Federal Security Admini￾strator in order to permit Mr. Hardy's reemployment in the Social Security Board
under a civil -service status which he would have acquired as of August 19, 1939 , had
he not resigned on July 4, 1939, to accept temporary employment in the Department
of Labor .
THE WHITE HOUSE,
March 2, 1940 .
