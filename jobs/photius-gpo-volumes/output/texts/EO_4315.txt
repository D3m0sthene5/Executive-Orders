Executive Order
Mrs. Belle M. Kerr may be appointed to a clerical position in the Postal
Service at Washington , D. C., without regard to the provisions of the civil service
rules . This order is issued upon the recommendation of the Postmaster General .
The Civil Service Commission cannot concur as it is limited in its recommendations
to those required by the interests of the public service .
Done for charity .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
September 29 , 1925.
[No. 43151
CALVIN COOLIDGE
GOVERNMENT PRINTINGOFFICE
