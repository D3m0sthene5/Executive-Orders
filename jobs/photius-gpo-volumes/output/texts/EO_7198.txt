Executive Order
DESIGNATION OF UNDER SECRETARY TO BE ACTING SECRETARY OF THE
INTERIOR
By virtue of and pursuant to the authority vested in me by section 179 of the
Revised Statutes of the United States (5 U. S. C. , sec. 6), I hereby authorize and
direct Charles West, Under Secretary of the Interior , to perform the duties of the
Secretary of the Interior during the absence or sickness of the Secretary of the
Interior.
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
September 26 , 1935 .
[No. 7198]
FRANKLIN
