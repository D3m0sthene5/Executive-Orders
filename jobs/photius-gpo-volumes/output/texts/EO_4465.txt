Executive Order
The Consular Regulations are hereby amended as follows :
Paragraph 518 is amended to read as follows :
518. President to prescribe fees for official services .-The President is author￾ized to prescribe from time to time the rates or tariffs of fees to be charged by
consular officers for official services , and such rates or tariffs are required to be
reported annually to Congress .-R. S. 1745; 34 Stat . L. 101 , Secs . 7, 8 , 9.
Paragraph 520 is amended to read as follows :
520. American vessels not required to pay .-By law no fees named in the tariff
of consular fees prescribed by the President can be collected by consular officers
from regularly documented American vessels and seamen for official services to
them. Consular agents , however , who are compensated by fees , must furnish the
masters of every such vessel with an itemized statement of such services performed
on account of said vessel , with the fee so prescribed for each service , and also make
a detailed report of such services and fees , under such regulations as the Secretary
of State may prescribe , and they will be allowed such compensation for said ser￾vices as they would have received prior to the passage of said law . Such services
will not be compensated , unless they have been necessarily rendered .-23 Stat . L.
56; 18 Op . Att. Gen. 111 , 234 .
Paragraph 521 is canceled and the following substituted :
521. Other services for which no fee is to be charged . Services required to be
performed by consular officers for which no fee may be charged are as follows :
(a) Passports for certain persons . Consular officers are not permitted to make
a charge for the issuance of passports to officers or employees of the United States
proceeding abroad in the discharge of their official duties, or to members of their
immediate families, or to seamen , or to widows, children , parents, brothers, and
sisters of American soldiers , sailors , or marines , buried abroad whose journey is
undertaken for the purpose and with the intent of visiting the graves of such sol￾diers , sailors , or marines , provided the facts are made a part of the application for
the passport .—41 Stat . L. 750 .
(b) Visas of passports for foreign officials . Consular officers are not permitted
to make a charge for the execution of the application for a visa or for the visa of
a passport for any officer of any foreign government, or members of his immediate

Paragraph 522 is amended to read as follows :
522. No additional charges allowed .--When a fee is fixed in the tariff of fees for
any particular act or service , no additional fee is to be demanded for signature,
attestation or affixing the seal of office ; nor may any subordinate in the consulate
charge a fee for any assistance rendered by him in connection with any service
performed by the principal officer and for which the latter has charged a fee .
In regard to the verification of invoices, a penalty is provided for charging for
blank forms , advice, or for clerical services in the preparation of the declaration
or certificate, or for receiving any fee greater than that authorized by law; and if
a consular officer collects or knowingly allows to be collected any other or greater
fees than are allowed by law for any service, he is liable in treble the amount of

3
the unlawful charge (plus the costs of recovery) besides the liability to refund it.
In such case the Secretary of the Treasury is authorized to retain the amount of
the overcharge out of the compensation of the consular officer .-R. S. Secs . 1716 ,
1723. Provision has been made by law for the refundment at the Treasury of
fees and charges erroneously collected by consular officers from the regularly docu￾mented vessels of the United States when the claim shall have been made within
one year from the date of collection .-23 Stat . L. 59 , Sec. 26 .
Paragraph 523 is amended to read as follows :
523. Consul liable for fees not collected .-If a consular officer omits to collect
any fees which he is required to collect for a service, he is liable to the United
States therefor as if he had collected them, unless they shall be remitted , on good
cause shown, by the Secretary of the Treasury . Receipts must be furnished by all
consular officers to owners , agents , consignees , masters , or commanders of all ves￾sels from whom fees or charges or extra wages or arrears of wages of seamen shall
be collected.-R. S. Secs . 1724 , 4213 .
Paragraph 524 is amended to read as follow :
524. Tonnage and other fees charged against the Treasury. The tonnage fees
provided for in fees 16 and 17 of the Tariff of the United States Consular Fees are
not charged by consular officers against regularly documented ' American vessels
and are not charged by consular agents against the Treasury for such vessels run￾ning regularly , making weekly or monthly trips , or otherwise , to or between foreign
ports, for more than four trips in a year . These fees are chargeable , however , by
consular agents against the Treasury for each vessel on the first four trips in each
calendar year . They are chargeable at the foreign port where the principal offices
of the steamship company or owners are located, but are not to be charged at
other consular offices on the route, whether within or without the same allegiance .
Consular fees other than tonnage fees are charged by consular agents against
the Treasury for all services performed for regularly documented American vessels ;
and when a vessel makes a special trip to a foreign port other than that to which
it runs on its regular trips , a consular agent at the port thus visited is entitled to
receive tonnage fees for the special trip .-R. S. Sec. 1720; 1 Comp . Dec. 374 .

For convenience fees are numbered in three groups , each group beginning
with number 1 at the commencement of each calendar year and continuing con￾secutively throughout the year .
Fees for the certification of consular invoices constitute the first group, and
must be numbered in a separate consecutive series of numbers , corresponding num￾bers being placed on the invoices or certificates .
Fees for landing certificates constitute the second group, and must be num￾bered consecutively in a separate series , corresponding numbers being placed on
the certificates at the point indicated , or just beneath the fee stamp .
All other fees collected constitute the third group, and must be numbered
consecutively in a separate series , corresponding numbers being placed on the
documents at the point indicated , or just beneath the fee stamp .
Paragraph 531 is canceled and the following substituted therefor :
531. Return offees . Every consular officer in charge of an office shall , at the
close of each quarter or fractional part of a quarter during which he has been in
charge , make a return of all fees collected by him during such period, which return
shall be a full and perfect transcript of the record of fees which he is required to
keep . These returns must be rendered on the prescribed forms . If no fees are
collected in any period for which return is to be made that fact should be reported
on the prescribed form . The statute declares a consular officer guilty of embezzle￾ment, and disqualifies him from holding any office of trust or profit under the
United States, who wilfully neglects to render true and just quarterly accounts
and returns of the business of his office , and of moneys received by him for the
use of the United States, or who neglects to pay over any balance of such moneys
due to the United States at the expiration of any quarter before the expiration of
the next succeeding quarter .-R. S. Sec . 1734 .
Paragraph 532 is canceled and the following substituted therefor :
532. Oath to return offees . Every consular officer shall make oath to the return
of fees collected by him in which he shall swear that it is a full and perfect tran￾script of the record of fees which he is by law required to keep; that the same is
true and correct and contains a full and accurate statement of all fees received by
him as such consular officer during the period of time therein mentioned . This
oath may be taken before any person having authority to administer oaths at the
post or place where the consular officer is located. When no fees have been col￾lected the form on which the return is made must , nevertheless , be sworn to .
Paragraph 533 is amended to read as follows :
533. Tariff of United States Consular Fees .-The Tariff of United States Con￾sular Fees prescribed by the President under authority of Section 1745 of the
Revised Statutes as amended by the Acts of April 5, 1906, and June 4, 1920, to be
charged by consular officers for official services , is supplied to all consular offices by
the Department . All charges for such services must be in strict accordance with
this tariff , and be collected in the coin of the United States or at its representative
value in exchange . No fee or compensation will be collected for any service not
covered by this tariff .
