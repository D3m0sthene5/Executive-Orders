Executive Order
SIOUX CITY, IOWA, ABOLISHED AS A CUSTOMS PORT OF ENTRY
By virtue of and pursuant to the authority vested in me by the act of August
1 , 1914, 38 Stat . 609 , 623 , 19 U. S. C., sec. 2, Sioux City , Iowa , is hereby abolished
as a customs port of entry in Customs Collection District No. 44 (Iowa ), with head￾quarters at Des Moines , Iowa , effective thirty days from the date of this order .
FRANKLIN D ROOSEVELT
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
December 29 , 1934.
