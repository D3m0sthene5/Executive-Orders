Executive Order
Mrs. Lydia F. Carroll may be permanently appointed to a classified position in
the Treasury Department without regard to the civil service rules .
This order is issued upon the recommendation of the Secretary of the Treasury
who states that Mrs. Carroll has been serving in an excepted position in the Prohi￾bition Unit since November 3, 1923, and that her permanent appointment as a
classified employee will be in the interest of the service . She is practically dependent
upon her own labor for support, as her only son , a soldier in the World War , has
recently been retired from the Army as a first lieutenant because of physical disa￾bility .
The Civil Service Commission is unable to concur.
Done for charity .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
January 19, 1926.
