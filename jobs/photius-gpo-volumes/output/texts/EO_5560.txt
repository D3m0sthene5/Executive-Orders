Executive Order
Amendment of Schedule A, Positions Excepted from Examination
under the Civil Service Rules
Schedule A, positions excepted from the requirement of examination under the
civil service rules, is hereby amended by the addition of a new subdivision, to be
numbered XXII , as follows :
XXII . FEDERAL POWER COMMISSION
1. One private secretary or confidential clerk to each member of the Federal
Power Commission .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
February 16, 1931 .
