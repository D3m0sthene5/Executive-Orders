Executive Order
AUTHORIZING THE ALIEN PROPERTY CUSTODIAN
TO SELL CERTAIN PROPERTY AT PRIVATE SALE
By virtue of the authority vested in me by an Act of Congress entitled " An
act to define , regulate , and punish trading with the enemy and for other purposes " ,
approved October 6, 1917 , known as the " Trading with the Enemy Act" , and the
amendment to such Act embodied in " An Act making appropriations to supply
urgent deficiencies in the appropriations for the fiscal year ending June 30, 1918 ,
and prior fiscal years on account of war expenses and for other purposes " , approved
March 28 , 1918, and the amendment to such Act embodied in " An Act making
appropriations to supply deficiencies in appropriations for the fiscal year ending
June 30, 1919 , and prior fiscal years on account of war expenses , and for other
purposes " , approved November 4, 1918, to make certain orders such as that herein
contained , I hereby , in the public interest , make the following determination, order ,
rule and regulation :
The Alien Property Custodian is hereby authorized to sell at private sale , at
such time and place as to him or to his authorized agent may seem advisable ,
without public or other advertisement , for and in consideration of the sum of
Fifty-two thousand five hundred ($52,500.00 ) dollars, certain real estate delivered
to him as the property of David Herrmann , Julius Herrmann , Hermine Lowenthal ,
David Mayer , Gustav Mayer, Julius Mayer , Sophie Mayer , Karoline Stern, Fannie
Oettinger , Isaac Landauer , Julius Landauer , Theodor Landauer , Albert Landauer ,
Gustav Landauer , Willy Landauer , Anna Eschelbacher, Siegfried Spiegal, Simon
Herrmann , Josef Herrmann , Karoline Blumenfeld , Alice Schwab, David Gutmann ,
Siegfried Gutmann , Theodor Gutmann , Alfred Gutmann , Hedwig Mayer , Karoline
Frakenbacher, Jette Gunzenhauser , Sophie Ullman and Ricke Fromm , residing in
Germany, whom, after investigation , he did determine to be enemies within the
purview of the said Act as amended , which said real estate is situate in the City of
New York, County of Bronx , State of New York , and described as follows , to - wit ;
All that certain piece or parcel of land , beginning at a point in the easterly
line of Ogden Avenue , distant 95 feet southerly along the same from the south￾easterly corner of East 165th Street and Ogden Avenue, as said street and
avenue are legally opened , thence easterly parallel with East 165th Street,
152.72 feet to Nelson Avenue, thence southerly along the westerly line of Nelson
Avenue , 100.27 ; thence westerly parallel with East 165th Street, 145.31 feet
to Ogden Avenue ; thence northerly along the easterly line of Ogden Avenue ,
100 feet to the point of beginning .
ALSO , all that certain piece or parcel of land , beginning at a point in the
westerly line of Woodycrest Avenue , distant southerly along the same , 95.74
feet from the southwesterly corner of Woodycrest Avenue and East 165th
Street , as said avenue and street are legally opened ; thence westerly parallel
with East 165th Street , 167.04 feet to the easterly line of Nelson Avenue ;
thence southerly along the same , 100.27 feet ; thence easterly parallel with East
165th Street , 161.95 feet to the westerly line of Woodycrest Avenue, and thence
northerly along the same 100.78 feet to the point of beginning.
My reasons for the foregoing determination , order, rule and regulation are :
(a) An advantageous offer for the purchase of the said real estate has been
received by the Alien Property Custodian :
(b) An attempted sale at public auction would be a useless formality and would
involve unnecessary expenses , delay and inconvenience , which should be avoided :
(c) The property is vacant, unimproved , producing no income and is subject
to heavy taxation .
(d) The Attorneys in Fact for the Alien Enemies have given their assent
that this offer be accepted and the property sold.
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
December 19 , 1925.
