Executive Order
REINSTATEMENT OF JAMES J. MURPHY , JR.
, A FORMER FOREIGN SERVICE
OFFICER
By virtue of and pursuant to the authority vested in me by section 12 of the act
of February 23 , 1931 , 46 Stat . 1207 , 1208 , it is ordered that James J. Murphy , Jr., who
resigned his position as an officer in the Foreign Service of the United States on
March 23 , 1931 , to accept an appointment to a position in the Department of State ,
be, and is hereby , reinstated in the Foreign Service of the United States in Class III ,
his reinstatement to be effective upon the date of his nomination to a position in such
class is confirmed by the Senate .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
July 25, 1939 .
FRANKLIN D ROOSEVE
