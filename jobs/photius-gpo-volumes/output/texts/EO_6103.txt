Executive Order
EXEMPTION OF BANKING EMERGENCY EMPLOYES FROM CIVIL SERVICE
RULES
In view of the emergency and temporary nature of appointments in the Office
of the Comptroller of the Currency , under the provisions of the Act of Congress
approved March 9th , 1933 (To provide relief in the existing national emergency in
banking , and for other purposes )
, such appointments may be made without regard
to Civil Service requirements .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
April 5, 1933.
FRANKLIN D ROOSEVELT
