JUN 21 1941
Executive Order
VARD LAV
EXEMPTION OF EDWARD P. DAVIS FROM COMPULSORY RETIREMENT FOR AGE
WHEREAS, in my judgment, the public interest requires that Edward P. Davis ,
topographic engineer , Geological Survey , Department of the Interior , who , during
the month of June, 1941 , will reach the retirement age prescribed for automatic
separation from the service , applicable to him, be exempted from compulsory retire￾ment for age for a period of one year :
NOW, THEREFORE , by virtue of and pursuant to the authority vested in me
by section 204 of the act of June 30, 1932, 47 Stat. 382, 404 (U. S. C. , title 5, sec.
715a), I hereby exempt the said Edward P. Davis from compulsory retirment for age
for a period of one year ending June 30, 1942 .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
May 27, 1941 .
FRANKLIN D ROOSEVELT
