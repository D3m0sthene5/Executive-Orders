Executive Order
By virtue of the authority vested in me as President of the United States of
America , and pursuant to the provisions of Section three of the Act of Congress
approved June 7, 1926 (Public No. 345-69th Congress )
, entitled "An Act To
authorize the disposition of lands no longer needed for naval purposes , " the Sec￾retary of Commerce by letter of July 19 , 1926, having made application to the
Secretary of the Navy for the transfer thereof and given specific reasons therefor ,
within ninety days after the approval of the above mentioned Act of Congress , and
such action having received my approval , it is hereby ordered that all of that
certain piece or parcel of land situate and being in the City of Alpena , State of
Michigan , hereinafter described , be and the same is hereby transferred to the
Department of Commerce for use by the Bureau of Fisheries as a hatchery site for
the propagation of white fish , lake trout and other species of that region , subject to
any rights which the City of Alpena may have in the improvements now located
thereon :
Beginning at a stake on the westerly line of River Street (now
Park Place) extended , 236 feet southeasterly from the most northerly
corner of Lot 1 in Block 3 of the Village Plat (now City of Alpena ) ;
thence southeasterly along the extended westerly line of Park Place
45 feet ; thence southwesterly parallel with First Street 140 feet to the
extended alley line in said Block 3 ; thence northwesterly 45 feet on
said extended alley line ; thence northeasterly 140 feet to place of
beginning , said property lying southeasterly and adjacent to the
Bingham lot .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
October 21 , 1926.
