Executive Order
APPOINTMENT OF MEMBERS OF THE PRISON INDUSTRIES REORGANIZATION
BOARD
I hereby appoint as members of the Prison Industries Reorganization Board ,
created by Executive Order of September 26 , 1935, establishing the Prison Industries
Reorganization Administration , the following :
Joseph N. Ulman , Chairman
Louis N. Robinson
Linton N. Collins
Gustav Peck
James P. Davis
Until further order members of the Board shall be paid necessary expenses
incurred while in attendance at and in travelling to and from meetings of the Prison
Industries Reorganization Board , and Louis N. Robinson shall be compensated for
his services in attendance at meetings of the said Board at the rate of $25.00 per diem .
FRANKLIN D ROOSEVELT
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
September 28 , 1935.
