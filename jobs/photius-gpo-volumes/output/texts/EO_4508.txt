Executive Order
By virtue of the authority vested in me as President of the United States of
America , and pursuant to the provisions of Section 6 of the Act of Congress
approved March 12, 1926 , entitled " An Act authorizing the use for permanent
construction at military posts of the proceeds from the sale of surplus War Depart￾ment real property , and authorizing the sale of certain military reservations , and for
other purposes ," the Secretary of Commerce having made application to the Sec￾retary of War for the transfer thereof and given specific reasons therefor, within
ninety days after the approval of the above mentioned Act of Congress , and such
action having received my approval , it is hereby ordered that the portion of the
Three Tree Point Military Reservation , in the State of Washington hereinafter
described , be and the same is hereby transferred to the Department of Commerce
for use as a lighthouse reservation :
Commencing at a point 1169.8 feet south and 2516.6 feet west from
the southeast corner of the Three Tree Point Military Reservation , Wash￾ington, in Sections 10 , 11 , 14 and 15 Township 9 North , Range 7 West
Willamette Meridian ; thence , running south 10 degrees west 132 feet ;
thence , west 99 feet ; thence north 26 degrees west , 264 feet ; thence , south
65 degrees 42 minutes East 260.7 feet to the point of beginning , containing
in all approximately 0.65 acre .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
September 14 , 1926 .
