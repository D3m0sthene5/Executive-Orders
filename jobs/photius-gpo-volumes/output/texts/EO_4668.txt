Executive Order
Mrs. Alma M. Key may be appointed as an operative in the Bureau of Engrav￾ing and Printing of the Treasury Department without compliance with the exami￾nation requirements of the civil service rules .
Mrs. Key is the widow of Emmett M. Key, who was for a number of years
employed in the Bureau of Engraving and Printing and who at the time of his
death on May 11, 1927, was Assistant Foreman of the Electrolytic Plate Making
Section .
This order is recommended by the Secretary of the Treasury , who states that
Mr. Key was connected with the Electrolytic Section from the time of its inception ,
and during his employment and association with the process he proved to be a very
loyal and valuable employee , that many of the mechanical time -saving devices
which are at the present time in use in the manufacture of electrolytic plates were
originated by him and that his determined efforts and ever loyal cooperation assisted
greatly in the present success of the Electrolytic Section .
For charity .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
June 17 , 1927.
