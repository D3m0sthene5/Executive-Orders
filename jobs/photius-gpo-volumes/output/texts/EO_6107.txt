Executive Order
EXEMPTION OF REUBEN PERRY FROM COMPULSORY RETIREMENT FOR AGE
By virtue of the authority vested in me by section 204 of part II of the act
entitled " AN ACT Making appropriations for the Legislative Branch of the Govern￾ment for the fiscal year ending June 30 , 1933 , and for other purposes " , approved
June 30, 1932 , Reuben Perry , superintendent of the Albuquerque School , New
Mexico, Indian Service , Department of the Interior , who during the current month
reaches the retirement age prescribed for automatic separation from the service , is
hereby exempted from the provisions of that section until June 30, 1933 .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
April 11 , 1933.
[No. 6107]
FRANKLIN
