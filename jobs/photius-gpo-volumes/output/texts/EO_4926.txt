Executive Order
Mr. Harry M. Vogel may be appointed to a subclerical or an unclassified
position under the Office of the Director of Public Buildings and Public Parks of
the National Capital without regard to the civil service rules or the labor regulations .
This order is issued upon the recommendation of the Acting Director , Public
Buildings and Public Parks of the National Capital .
THE WHITE HOUSE,
July 6, 1928 .
