Executive Order
ARIZONA
Under authority of the act of Congress approved June 25 , 1910 (36 Stat .
, 847 )
,
as amended by the act of August 24, 1912 (37 Stat .
, 497) , it is hereby ordered that
the public lands within the following described tracts in Arizona , be, and the same
are hereby , temporarily withdrawn subject to the provisions and conditions of
said acts , for classification, pending examination by the Smithsonian Institution
of certain Indian ruins located thereon :
In T. 9½ N. , R. 2 E., unsurveyed, what will probably be when surveyed ,
Sec . 25;
In T. 10 N., R. 2 E., unsurveyed , what will probably be when surveyed , Sec . 36 .
CALVIN COOLIDGE
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
September 23 , 1925 .
