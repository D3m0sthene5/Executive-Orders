Executive Order
AUTHORIZATION TO APPOINT MISS WILL HARRIS
By virtue of the authority vested in me by the provisions of the last sentence of
the eighth paragraph of subdivision second of section 2 of the Civil Service Act of
January 16 , 1883 (ch . 27, 22 Stat . 403, 404), it is hereby ordered that Miss Will Harris
may be appointed in the classified service without compliance with the requirements
of the Civil Service rules .
This order is recommended by the Secretary of State .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
Jan. 31 , 1934 .
FRANKLIN D ROOSEVELT
