Executive Order
Mr. Clifford C. Marsh may be reinstated as Assistant Postmaster at Yellowstone
Park , Wyoming, without regard to the time limit fixed by the civil service rules .
Mr. Marsh was a classified employee from 1893 to 1914 and has been employed
temporarily and satisfactorily during the past seven years at the post office .
The Department and the Civil Service Commission concur in this order .
CALVIN COOLIDGE
THE WHITE HOUSE,
February 27, 1929 .
