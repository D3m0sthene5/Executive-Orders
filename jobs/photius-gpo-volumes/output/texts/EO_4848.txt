Executive Order
LOOKOUT STATION
OREGON
Under authority of the act of Congress approved June 25, 1910 (36 Stat . 847),
as amended by the act of August 24, 1912 (37 Stat . 497), and subject to the condi￾tions therein expressed , it is hereby ordered that the NWA NW /4 Sec. 9 , T. 7 S. ,
R. 7 W. , W. M. , Oregon , containing 40 acres , be and it is hereby temporarily with￾drawn from settlement , location, sale or entry , and reserved for use by the State
of Oregon as a lookout station, subject also to the right of the United States to
sell the timber thereon in accordance with the provisions of the act of June 9, 1916
(39 Stat . 218 ).
This order shall remain in full force and effect unless and until revoked by the
President or by act of Congress .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
