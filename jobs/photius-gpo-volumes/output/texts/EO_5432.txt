Executive Order
Public Water Restoration No. 65 , California
So much of the order of withdrawal dated January 24, 1914, creating Public
Water Reserve No. 14, as affects the land hereinafter listed is hereby revoked , and
all such land not otherwise reserved or withdrawn is hereby restored to the public
domain and shall become subject to disposition under the laws applicable thereto
upon such date and after such notice as may be hereafter determined and announced .
SAN BERNARDINO MERIDIAN
T. 3 S. , R. 5 E., sec . 18 , SE . 4 SW. 4.
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
August 28 , 1930 .
