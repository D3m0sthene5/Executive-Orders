Executive Order
Mrs. Loretta V. Mann , who served in the Bureau of Engraving and Printing
and in the Government Printing Office from 1907 to 1916 , may be appointed to an
appropriate position without regard to the time limit for reinstatement . She is the
widow of Howard Q. Mann , formerly a principal clerk in the Geological Survey ,
who left her with three children without means of support.
The Civil Service Commission cannot recommend the appointment as required
by the interests of the service .
For charity.
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
February 21 , 1929 .
[No. 5051 ]
CALVIN COO
