Executive Order
Temporarily Changing the Order of Precedence of the Assistant
Secretaries of War
Under the authority of sections 177 and 179 of the Revised Statutes , the Assistant
Secretary of War appointed under the act of Congress approved July 2, 1926, is hereby
authorized and directed, pending further orders , to perform the duties of Secretary
of War during the illness or temporary absence from the seat of government of the
Secretary of War.
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
HERBERT HOOVER
April 22, 1930 .
