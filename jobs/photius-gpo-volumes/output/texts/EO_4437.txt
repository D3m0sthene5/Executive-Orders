Executive Order
Mr. Michael A. McNamara may be reinstated in the Post Office Service in
Bethlehem, Pennsylvania , without regard to the requirements of the civil service
rules .
This Order is issued upon the recommendation of the Postmaster General by
virtue of Mr. McNamara's previous service over a period of more than eleven
years and of the fact that his period of eligibility for reinstatement under the civil
service rules has expired and that he is beyond the age limit for admission to the
clerk carrier examination .
The Civil Service Commission submits the case without prejudice .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
May 1 , 1926
