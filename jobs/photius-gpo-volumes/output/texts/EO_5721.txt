Addition to Upper Mississippi River Wild Life and Fish Refuge
It is hereby ordered that the following-described vacant public lands, which are
situated on either side of or upon islands in the Mississippi River, between Rock
Island, Ill., and Wabasha, Minn., and are subject to overflow by said river and have
been determined by the Secretary of Agriculture to be suitable for the purposes
defined by the act of June 7, 1924 (U. S. Code, title 16, ch. 8, sec. 721; 43 Stat. 650),
entitled “An act to establish the Upper Mississippi River Wild Life and Fish Refuge,”
be, and the same are hereby, reserved and set apart, subject to existing valid rights,
