Executive Order
Under the provisions of the Act approved March 3, 1926, making an appropria￾tion for such expenses as the President may deem necessary in connection with the
participation by the United States in the work of the Preparatory Commission which
is to meet at Geneva, Switzerland , in 1926 , for the purpose of making preliminary
studies on the Reduction and Limitation of Armaments , which appropriation reads
as follows ,
"For the expenses of participation by the United States as the President
may , in his discretion, determine , in the work of the preparatory commission ,
which is to meet at Geneva, Switzerland , in 1926, for the purpose of making
preliminary studies and preparations for a conference on the reduction and
limitation of armaments ; and for each and every purpose connected there￾with , including compensation of employees , travel , subsistence expenses
(notwithstanding the provisions of any other Act) ; and such other expenses
as the President shall deem proper, to be expended under the direction of the
Secretary of State, to remain available until June 30, 1927 , $50,000 ."
expenditures for the following are hereby authorized to be incurred or to be author￾ized by the Secretary of State in his discretion but not including any expense for
transportation or subsistence for the families of members or employees of the
delegation:
Compensation of clerks , messengers and other employees .
Messenger service .
Courier service .
Office supplies and equipment.
Entertainment as approved by the Head of the American Representation .
Printed forms , letter heads and invitations .
Stationery .
Cleaning and toilet supplies .
Newspapers and publications .
Books , maps and charts .
Telegrams .
Telephone service .
Postage .
Freight , express , drayage and customs charges .
Rent of offices .
Purchase and rent of office furntiure and equipment .
Registration of telegraphic address .
Printing at home and abroad .
Local transportation , taxicabs , cab hire, et cetera , not including purchase or
maintenance of automobiles .
Travel in accordance with the provisions of the " regulations to govern travel
of diplomatic and consular officers " including travel expenses of Ambas￾sador Houghton , London to Washington and return , under telegraphic
orders of February 27, 1926 , and Minister Gibson , Berne to Washington
and return , under telegraphic orders of February 10 , 1926 .
Subsistence , actual and necessary , while en route, during the continuance of
the conference , or prior thereto while engaged on work in preparation for
the conference . Subsistence shall include meals , lodging , baths, laundry ,
tips to hotel employees and dining car and restaurant waiters, drinking
water . The maximum of such subsistence , which is to be fixed by the
Secretary of State, shall not exceed the following :
For the Head of the Representation and Advisers $ 15.00 each a day.
For Technical Assistants and the Secretary to the Delegation $ 12.00
each a day .
For Clerks and other employees $8.00 each a day.
Such other miscellaneous items as may from time to time become necessary ,
except where expenditures for any such items are specifically prohibited
by law.
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
April 14, 1926.
CALVIN COOLIDGE
