Executive Order
There are hereby excluded from the operation of the Retirement Act of July 3 ,
1926, because of their intermittent service on a piece work basis , garment makers
(bundle hands) employed by the Naval Clothing Factory , Naval Supply Depot ,
Brooklyn , New York . No record is kept of the hours of labor performed by these
employees and their service is intermittent and uncertain in tenure .
This order is recommended by the Navy Department and the Civil Service
Commission .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
February 10, 1928 .
