Executive Order
AGRICULTURAL RANGE EXPERIMENT STATION ENLARGED
ARIZONA
Under authority of the act of Congress approved June 25, 1910 (36 Stat .
, 847 ) ,
as amended by the act of August 24, 1912 ( 37 Stat.
, 497), and on the recommenda￾tion of the Secretary of Agriculture , it is hereby ordered that the public lands
