Executive Order
Mrs. Mary E. Kelly may be reinstated in a clerical position in the classified
service without regard to the time limit on reinstatements under the civil service
rules .
Mrs. Kelly is the widow of John J. Kelly who at the time of his death , Septem￾ber 5, 1928 , had been an employee of the Government Printing Office for more than
thirty years . His death left her without means of support for herself and her ten￾year old daughter . Mrs. Kelly was employed in the classified service for approxi￾mately twenty years , her last service as a permanent employee terminating by
resignation on April 9, 1918 .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
February 23, 1929 .

CALVIN COOL
