Executive Order
Mrs. Lillian Capron may be appointed as welfare worker in the War Depart￾ment without regard to the examination requirements of the civil service rules .
Mrs. Capron is the widow of Captain Allyn A. Capron , killed in action in Cuba
during the Spanish War. The outstanding service of Mrs. Capron with the Red
Cross and as a welfare worker at home and abroad , both in peace and in war , from
the relief work in Cuba in 1899 thru the World War , seems to constitute a basis
for her appointment purely on the ground of merit altho the Civil Service Com￾mission cannot certify that the needs of the service cannot be met in the manner
provided by the civil service rules .
The Secretary of War recommends the order .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
November 26 , 1927.
