Executive Order
Corrected Copy
Under the authority of the Act approved July 11 , 1919, (41 Stat . 131 , 132 ), it
is hereby ordered that the following lands at the Norfolk Supply Base of the Army
at Hampton Roads , Virginia, which are needed no longer for military purposes , be
and are hereby placed under the control of the Secretary of the Navy for the use
of the Navy Department for coal storage and other naval purposes :
Beginning at a point marked by a steel bolt at Station 117 plus 81.53 at
a distance of 210 feet north of Base Line ; thence NORTH 22 ° 11 ′ east
132.99 feet to a steel bolt ; thence north 22 ° 11 ′ east 38.57 feet ; thence north
57 °33 ′ west 216.45 feet ; thence north 51 ° 35 ′ west 276.83 feet ; thence north
37°46′30 ″ east 86.40 feet ; thence north 54 °02′30 ″ west 89.15 feet ; thence
north 50°06′ east 166.42 feet ; thence north 25° 51 ′ WEST 329.97 feet ; thence
north 1 ° 38′30 ″ east 188.78 feet ; thence north 26°43′30 ″ east 133.75 feet ;
thence north 8 °47′30″ east 369.85 feet ; thence north 26°45′30″ west 1,192.20
feet ; all on the easterly boundary line of the War Department property ;
thence north 79 °44′ west 468.05 feet ; thence north 89 °52 ′ west 539.95 feet ;
thence south 77 ° 19 ′ west 496.4 feet ; thence north 22 ° 13′30 ″ west 63.4 feet
to a stone monument on the northerly boundary line of the War Department
property ; thence south 86° 00′30 ″ west 220 feet , approximately ; thence south
30 ° 39′30 ″ east 2,935 feet , approximately ; thence south 39°50′30 ″ west 430
feet, approximately ; thence south 82°21 ′ west 260 feet , approximately ;
thence south 7° 39 ′ east 55 feet to the northerly boundary line of property
transferred to the United States Shipping Board ; thence north 82° 21 ′ east
1,668 feet, approximately, to the point or place of beginning, as shown on
Bureau of Yards and Docks , Navy Department , Drawing No. 101,422 .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
CALVIN COOLIDGE
September 12 , 1927
