Executive Order
Section 3 , Subdivision VII , Schedule A, which provides :
"All employees on star routes and in post offices of the
third and fourth classes , (except village delivery carriers who
were regularly employed in a first or second class post office
upon the relegation of the office below the second class ) and
postmasters of the fourth class other than those in Alaska ,
Guam , Hawaii , Porto Rico , and Samoa ."
is amended to read as follows :
3. All employees on star routes and in post offices of the
third and fourth classes , except village delivery carriers, and
postmasters of the fourth class other than those in Alaska ,
Guam , Hawaii , Porto Rico , and Samoa .
This order is recommended by the Civil Service Commission with the
concurrence of the Postmaster General .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
CALVIN COOLIDGE
January 26, 1926 .
