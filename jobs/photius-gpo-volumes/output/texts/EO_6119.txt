Executive Order
WITHDRAWAL OF PUBLIC LANDS FOR RESURVEY
CALIFORNIA
Under authority contained in the act of June 25, 1910 (36 Stat. 847-848 )
, as
amended by the act of August 24, 1912 (37 Stat. 497), and subject to the conditions ,
provisions , and limitations of said acts , it is hereby ordered that the public lands
in the following townships in California be, and the same are hereby , withdrawn
from settlement ,
location, sale, entry, and all forms of appropriation, pending a
resurvey of said townships under the act of March 3, 1909 (35 Stat. 845 ) :
T. 22 N. , R.
SAN BERNARDINO MERIDIAN

T. 21 N. , R. 11 E., secs . 6, 7, 16 , 17, 20, 21, 22, 26, 27, 28 , 34, 35 , and 36 .

President or by an act of Congress .
