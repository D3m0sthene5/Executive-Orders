Executive Order
AUTHORIZATION TO APPOINT JOB K. JONES, ALLAN B. WALSH, ET AL.
By virtue of the authority vested in me by the provisions of the last sentence
of the eighth paragraph of subdivision second of section 2 of the Civil Service Act
of January 16 , 1883 (ch . 27, 22 Stat . 403 , 404), it is hereby ordered that the
following -named persons may be appointed to appropriate positions in the Bureau
of Internal Revenue of the Treasury Department without compliance with the
requirements of the civil -service rules :
Job K. Jones
Allan B. Walsh
B. Fritz Smith
Arthur D. Condon
Phelps F. Ferris
Herbert W. Huber
Joseph P. Russell
Leon Chatelain , Jr.
George R. O'Neil
Daniel M. Dwyer
Gale P. Kyle
Ernie G. Carroll
Glenn A. Ruggles
Spaulding F. Glass
George Young
M. S. Zimmerman
J. O. Rhyne
Paul R. Younts
Hubert F. Teitman
Phillip N. Davison
Sam L. Connor
Arthur W. Almand
Arthur R. Carnduff
L. Robert Driver
This order is recommended by the Acting Secretary of the Treasury .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
November 17, 1933 .
FRANKLIN D. ROOSEVELT .
