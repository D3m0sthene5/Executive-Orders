Executive Order
LOOKOUT STATION
WASHINGTON
Under authority of the act of Congress approved June 25, 1910 ( 36 Stat . 847 ) ,
as amended by the act of August 24, 1912 (37 Stat. 497 )
, and subject to the con￾ditions therein expressed , it is hereby ordered that Lot 1 (NE14 NE14 ) Sec . 24 , T.
36 N. , R. 42 E., W. M. , Washington , containing 39.24 acres , be and it is hereby
temporarily withdrawn from settlement , location , sale or entry, and reserved for
use by the State of Washington as a lookout station .
This order shall remain in full force and effect unless and until revoked by the
President or by act of Congress .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
May 10, 1929 .
