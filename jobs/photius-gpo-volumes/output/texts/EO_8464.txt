PROPERTY OF

ARVARD LAWLIBRARY
Executive Order
AUTHORIZING THE APPOINTMENT OF MRS . GERTRUDE S. COOPER AS
SUPERINTENDENT OF THE VANDERBILT MANSION NATIONAL HISTORIC
SITE , NATIONAL PARK SERVICE , DEPARTMENT OF THE INTERIOR ,
WITHOUT REGARD TO THE CIVIL SERVICE RULES
By virtue of and pursuant to the authority vested in me by the provisions of
paragraph Eighth of subdivision SECOND of section 2 of the Civil Service Act of
January 16 , 1883 (22 Stat. 403, 404), it is hereby ordered that Mrs. Gertrude S. Cooper
may be appointed to the position of Superintendent of the Vanderbilt Mansion
National Historic Site, National Park Service , Department of the Interior , without
compliance with the requirements of the Civil Service Rules .
FRANKLIN D ROOSEVELT
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
June 29, 194
