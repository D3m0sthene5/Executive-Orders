Executive Order
Mrs. Anna M. Walsh may be appointed to a clerkship in the post office of
Boston , Massachusetts , without regard to the requirements of the civil service
rules .
Mrs. Walsh is the wife of John J. Walsh , for thirteen years a clerk in the post
office of Boston with an excellent record for efficiency , who is totally disabled
mentally to continue his work and support his wife and three infant children.
The Postmaster General recommends this order. As the Civil Service Commission
can properly recommend waivers only when the procedure under the rules will not
fully meet the needs of the service , it is unable to concur .
Done for charity .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
January 12, 1926.
