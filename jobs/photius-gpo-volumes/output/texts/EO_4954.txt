Executive Order
William A. Marshall may be reinstated to an appropriate position in the
General Accounting Office without regard to the time limit on reinstatement under
the civil service rules .
Mr. Marshall was employed in the Treasury Department and in the General
Accounting Office for approximately three years as a messenger , having been dis￾continued December 31 , 1922 , on account of failing health. He is now physically fit to
resume work and has awife and six small children
to
support .

efficient ,

exemplary
