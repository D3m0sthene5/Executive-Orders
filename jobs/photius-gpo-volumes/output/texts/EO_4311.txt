Executive Order
NEW MEXICO
Under authority contained in the act of Congress approved June 25 , 1910 (36
Stat .
, 847)
, as amended by the act of August 24, 1912 (37 Stat .
, 497) , and subject
to the conditions , provisions and limitations of said acts , it is hereby ordered that
the public lands in Ts . 1 and 2 S. , R. 18 E., and T. 3 S. , R. 24 E., N. M. P. M. ,
New Mexico , be and the same are hereby withdrawn from settlement , location ,
sale , entry and all forms of appropriation , pending the resurvey of said townships ;
this withdrawal to remain in full force and effect from the date hereof until the
resurvey is duly approved and accepted and the approved plats thereof are officially
filed in the proper U. S. local land office , unless another date be fixed by the Com￾missioner of the General Land Office , whereupon this withdrawal will become of
no effect .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
CALVIN COOLIDGE
September 25, 1925
