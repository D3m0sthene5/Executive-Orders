Executive Order
FLORIDA
Under authority contained in the act of June 25, 1910 (36 Stat. 847), as amended
by the act of August 24 , 1912 (37 Stat . 497) , and subject to the conditions , pro￾visions and limitations of said acts , it is hereby ordered that the public land in Ts .
14 and 15 S. , R. 25 E. , Tal . M. , Florida , be and the same is hereby withdrawn from
settlement , location , sale , entry and all forms of appropriation , pending a resurvey
of said townships under the act of March 3, 1909 (35 Stat . 845) , as amended by
the Joint Resolution of June 25, 1910 (36 Stat . 884) .
This order shall continue in full force and effect unless and until revoked by
the President or by an act of Congress .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
January 18, 1929 .
