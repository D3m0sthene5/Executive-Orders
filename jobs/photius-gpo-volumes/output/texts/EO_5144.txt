Executive Order
NEW MEXICO
Under authority contained in the act of June 25, 1910 (36 Stat . 847 ) , as
amended by the act of August 24, 1912 (37 Stat. 497)
, and subject to the condi￾tions, provisions and limitations of said acts , it is hereby ordered that the public

the President , or by an act of Congress .
THE WHITE HOUSE
