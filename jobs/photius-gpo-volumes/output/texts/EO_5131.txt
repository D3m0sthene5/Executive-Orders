Executive Order
Mr. George Henry Malone , yeoman, 1st class, U. S. Navy, may be appointed
to the position of supervising clerk , Office of the Captain of the Yard , Naval Sta￾tion , Cavite , P. I., to fill an existing vacancy , without regard to the Civil Service
Act or Rules .
This action is taken in view of the inability of the U. S. Civil Service Com￾mission to supply local American eligibles thru the Philippine Civil Service Bureau .
The Bureau of Navigation , Navy Department , will direct his discharge from the
Navy .
This Order is issued upon recommendation of the Secretary of the Navy .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
