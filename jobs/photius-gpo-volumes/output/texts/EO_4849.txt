Executive Order
Mrs. Earl W. Boone , widow of Earl W. Boone, formerly chief electrician's mate
in the United States Navy, who met his death in line of duty when the submarine
S-4 sank , on December 17 , 1927, off the coast of Provincetown , Massachusetts,
may be appointed as a telephone operator under the Naval Establishment , without
regard to the examination requirements of the civil service rules .
As the Civil Service Commission can properly recommend waivers only when
the procedure under the rules will not fully meet the needs of the service , it is
unable to concur in the recommendation of the Secretary of the Navy for the
issuance of this order .
For patriotic sacrifice .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
April 2 1928 .
