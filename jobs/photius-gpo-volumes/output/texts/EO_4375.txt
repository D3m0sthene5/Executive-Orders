Executive Order
Alabama
Under authority of the act of Congress approved June 25 , 1910 (36 Stat .
, 847)
,
as amended by the act of August 24, 1912 (37 Stat .
, 497), it is hereby ordered that
the public lands in the following described areas in the State of Alabama be , and
they are hereby temporarily withdrawn from settlement , location , sale or entry ,
subject to the conditions of said acts, in aid of pending legislation embodied in
H. R. Bill No. 7104, 69th Congress , to wit :
All of Secs . 56-A, 57 and 58 , T. 2 N. , R. 1 E., St. Stp . M. , embracing a total
of 575.69 acres .
If such legislation be not enacted before the adjournment of the last session of
the Sixty-ninth Congress , and no other direction is given regarding the disposition
of such lands , they will on March 5 , 1927, become subject to disposal under the
method prescribed by existing law without further order .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
January 30 , 1926.
