Executive Order
Oliver H. Clarke may be reinstated to an appropriate position in the Office of
the Supervising Architect , Treasury Department , without regard to the time limit
upon his eligibility for reinstatement .
This Order is recommended by the Secretary of the Treasury who states that
Mr. Clarke served in the Office of the Supervising Architect for a number of years ,
that his training and experience make him unusually well qualified for the position
of Associate Architect , and that in view of the large building program now under
way his appointment will be in the interest of the service .
The Civil Service Commission is unable to concur .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
February 26 , 1929 .
[No. 5059 ]
