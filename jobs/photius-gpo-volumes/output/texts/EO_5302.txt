Executive Order
Extension of Trust Period on Certain Allotments Made to Indians
of the Rosebud Reservation , South Dakota
It is hereby ordered , under authority contained in the act of June 21 , 1906
(34 Stat . 325-326), that the trust period on the allotments of the Rosebud Indians
in South Dakota , which would otherwise expire during the calendar year 1930 , be ,
and is hereby extended for a further period of ten years , with the exception of the
following :
