Executive Order
PUBLIC WATER RESERVE NO. 145
CALIFORNIA

act of Congress approved August 24, 1912 (37 Stat. 497), it is hereby ordered that
the lands hereinafter described be, and the same are hereby , in so far as title thereto
remains in the United States, withdrawn from settlement , location , sale , or entry,
and reserved for municipal water-supply purposes :
