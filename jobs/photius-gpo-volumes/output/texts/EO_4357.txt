Executive Order
Mrs. Margaret C. Gates, a Spanish War emergency clerk , who served more
than seven years in the Post Office Department and was classified as a permanent
clerk by the act of April 28, 1902, may be reinstated to a position with minor
clerical status in the classified civil service without regard to the time limit on
reinstatement .
Mrs. Gates is in need of employment by reason of the death, at a military
training camp , of her nephew who was her principal support and by the exhaustion
of his insurance .
As the Civil Service Commission can properly recommend waivers only when
the procedure under the rules will not fully meet the needs of the service , it is
unable to concur .
For charity .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
December 15, 1925 .
