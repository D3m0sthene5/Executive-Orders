Executive Order
VETERANS REGULATION NO . 1 (E)
ENTITLEMENT TO PENSION
By virtue of the authority vested in me under sections 1 and 4 of title I of the
act entitled "An act to maintain the credit of the United States Government ",
approved March 20, 1933 (Public , No. 2, 73d Cong.)
, the following regulation
amending Veterans Regulation No. 1 (d) is hereby prescribed :
1. Veterans Regulation No. 1 (d) is hereby canceled effective March 28, 1934 .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
April 6, 1934 .
FRANKLIN D. ROOSEVELT.
Done in triplicate .
