Executive Order
Mrs. Josephine Jackley may be appointed a clerk in the Treasury Department
without compliance with the civil service rules .
This order is recommended by the Secretary of the Treasury , who states that
Mrs. Jackley is the widow of Dano M. Jackley , who served as a prohibition agent
for seven years and who was killed at Branchville , Maryland , on May 14, 1929, in
the performance of his official duty while engaged in the pursuit of an automobile
suspected of transporting liquor .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
October 17 , 1929.
