PROPERTY OF
Executive Order
SEP 9-1940
AUTHORIZING THE APPOINTMENT OF LELAND L. TOLMAN AND H. FRED
MARTIN , JR.
, TO LEGAL POSITIONS IN THE ADMINISTRATIVE OFFICE
OF THE UNITED STATES COURTS WITHOUT COMPLIANCE WITH THE
PROVISIONS OF THE CIVIL SERVICE RULES
By virtue of and pursuant to the authority vested in me by the provisions of
paragraph Eighth , subdivision SECOND , section 2 of the Civil Service Act (22 Stat .
403, 404), it is hereby ordered that Leland L. Tolman and H. Fred Martin, Jr., may
be appointed to legal positions in the Administrative Office of the United States
Courts without compliance with the requirements of the Civil Service Rules .
This order is recommended by the Director of the Administrative Office of the
United States Courts .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
July 1 , 1940.
FRANKLIN D ROOSEVELT
