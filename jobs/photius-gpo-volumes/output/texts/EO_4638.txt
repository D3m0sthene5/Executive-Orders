Executive Order
New Mexico
Under authority contained in the act of Congress approved June 25, 1910 (36
Stat . 847 )
, as amended by the act of August 24, 1912 ( 37 Stat. 497), and subject
to the conditions, provisions and limitations of said acts , it is hereby ordered that
the public lands in T. 20 N. , Rs . 6 and 7 E., N. M. P. M., New Mexico , be and the
same are hereby withdrawn from settlement , location , sale , entry and all forms of
appropriation , pending a resurvey of said townships under the act of Congress
approved March 3, 1909 ( 35 Stat . 845), as amended by the Joint Resolution of June
25 , 1910 (36 Stat. 884) .
This order shall continue in full force and effect unless and until revoked by
the President or by act of Congress .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
April 26, 1927.
CALVIN COOLIDGE
