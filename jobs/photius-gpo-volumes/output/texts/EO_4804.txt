Executive Order
Mrs. Rose Pauly may be appointed to an appropriate position in the classified
service of the Treasury Department without compliance with the requirements of
the civil service rules .
Mrs. Pauly is the widow of George Pauly who served as a pressman in the
Bureau of Engraving and Printing from March 13, 1903, to February 13 , 1925,
when he was retired on account of disability . Mr. Pauly died on July 11 , 1927,
leaving Mrs. Pauly with two minor children and herself to support.
This Order is recommended by the Secretary of the Treasury as an especially
meritorious and charitable case .
The Civil Service Commission is unable to concur.
For charity .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
February 10, 1928 .
