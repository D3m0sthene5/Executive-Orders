Executive Order
Mrs. Jeannette J. Donahue, widow of Mr. Peter A. Donahue who died April
13 , 1928 , after many years of service in the Immigration Service , may be reinstated
to an appropriate position in the Department of Labor without regard to the time
limit upon reinstatement .
The Secretary of Labor recommends this order. The Civil Service Commission
is unable to concur .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
March 2, 1929 .
[No. 5069 ]
CALVIN COO
