Executive Order
Mrs. Rowena M. Busbey may be employed as a clerk at Headquarters , U. S.
Marine Corps , Washington , D. C. , without regard to the Civil Service Act or rules .
Mrs. Busbey is the widow of Lieutenant Horace Carleton Busbey, U. S. Marine
Corps , who met his death in line of duty in the crash of a Fokker plane, which
occurred near File, Virginia , on June 30 , 1928 .
This order is issued upon recommendation of the Secretary of the Navy.
The Civil Service Commission, having sole regard to the requirements of the service ,
is unable to concur .
For patriotism .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
August 9, 1928.
