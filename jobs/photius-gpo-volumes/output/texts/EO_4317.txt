Executive Order
NEW MEXICO
Pursuant to the authority conferred upon me by the act of Congress approved
June 25, 1910 (36 Stat .
, 847) , as amended by the act of Congress of August 24,
1912 (37 Stat .
, 497), entitled, "An Act to authorize the President of the United
States to make withdrawals of public lands in certain cases " , it is hereby ordered
that Lots 2 and 3, SE14 NW14 and SW4 NE14 Sec . 4, T. 23 S. , R. 18 W. ,
N. M. P. M., New Mexico , containing 159.90 acres be withdrawn from settlement ,
location , sale or entry and reserved for the use of the War Department for an
airplane landing field .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
October 2, 1925 .
