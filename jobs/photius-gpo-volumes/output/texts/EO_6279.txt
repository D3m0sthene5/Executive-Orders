Executive Order
STURGEON BAY, WIS .
, ABOLISHED AS A CUSTOMS PORT OF ENTRY
By virtue of the authority vested in me by section 1 of the act of August 1, 1914
(ch . 223, 38 Stat . 609, 623 ; 19 U.S.C., sec. 2)
, Sturgeon Bay, Wis .
, is hereby abol￾ished as a customs port of entry in customs collection district no . 37 (Wisconsin ),
with headquarters at Milwaukee , Wis .
, effective as of 30 days from the date of this
order .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
September 12 , 1933 .
