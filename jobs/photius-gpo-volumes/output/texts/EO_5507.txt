Executive Order
Paducah , Kentucky , Abolished as a Customs Port of Entry
By virtue of the authority vested in me by the act of Congress approved August
1, 1914 (38 Stat . 623 ), making appropriations for the sundry civil expenses of the
Government for the fiscal year ended June 30, 1915, Paducah, Ky.
, is hereby abolished
as a customs port of entry in Customs Collection District No. 42 (Kentucky ), with
headquarters at Louisville , Ky.
, effective 30 days from the date of this order .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
December 8 , 1930.
