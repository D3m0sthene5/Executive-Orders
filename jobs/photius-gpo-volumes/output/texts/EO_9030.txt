PROPERTY OF
Executive Order
FEB 181942
EXEMPTION OF WALTER C. MENDENHALL FROM COMPULSORY
RETIREMENT FOR AGE
WHEREAS, in my judgment , the public interest requires that Walter C.
Mendenhall , Director , Geological Survey, Department of the Interior , who was exempted
from compulsory retirement for age for a period of one year by Executive Order No.
8628 of January 4, 1941 , be further exempted from such compulsory retirement for a
period of one year :
NOW, THEREFORE , by virtue of and pursuant to the authority vested in me
by section 204 of the act of June 30 , 1932 , 47 Stat . 382 , 404 (U. S. C., title 5, sec .
715a) , I hereby further exempt the said Walter C. Mendenhall from compulsory
retirement for age for a period of one year ending Februrary 28, 1943 .
FRANKLIN D ROOSEVELT
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
January 20, 1942
