Executive Order
AMENDMENT OF SUBDIVISION XIII , SCHEDULE A, CIVIL SERVICE RULES
By virtue of and pursuant to the authority vested in me by the provisions of
paragraph Eighth of subdivision SECOND of section 2 of the Civil Service Act of
January 16, 1883 (22 Stat . 403 , 404), Subdivision XIII of Schedule A of the Civil
Service Rules is amended by adding thereto the following paragraph:
"4. One special assistant to the Secretary in the Office of the Secretary . "
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
June 19, 1936.
[No. 7393]
FRANKLIN
