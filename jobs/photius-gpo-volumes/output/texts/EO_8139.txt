Executive Order
PROPERTY OF JUN151980
HARVARD LAWLIBRARY
EXEMPTION OF JAMES E. HARPER FROM COMPULSORY RETIREMENT
FOR AGE
WHEREAS, in my judgment, the public interest requires that James E. Harper ,
Chief, Division of Appointments , Treasury Department , who , during the month of
June , 1939 , will reach the retirement age prescribed for automatic separation from the
service , applicable to him, be exempted from compulsory retirement for age for a
period of one year :
NOW, THEREFORE , by virtue of and pursuant to the authority vested in me
by section 204 of the act of June 30, 1932, 47 Stat. 382 , 404 (U. S. C., title 5, sec .
715a), I hereby exempt the said James E. Harper from compulsory retirement for age
for a period of one year ending June 30, 1940 .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
May 19, 1939 .
FRANKLIN D ROOSEVELT
