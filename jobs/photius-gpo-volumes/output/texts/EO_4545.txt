Executive Order
Pursuant to the Act of Congress approved January 31, 1922 (42 Stat. 360),
entitled "An Act to provide for exchange of Government lands for privately
owned lands in the Territory of Hawaii ", and extended by Act of Congress of
March 3, 1925 (43 Stat. 1115), the following described parcels of land and rights of
way have been acquired from the Samuel M. Damon Estate , adjoining the Fort
Shafter Military Reservation on the west , by deed dated the 16th day of January ,
1926, and recorded in Book 809 on pages 181-184 , Bureau of Conveyances ,
Territory of Hawaii , and are hereby set aside for military purposes and made a
part of the Fort Shafter Military Reservation .
