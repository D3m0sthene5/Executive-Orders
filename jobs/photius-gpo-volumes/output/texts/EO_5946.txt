Executive Order
EXEMPTION OF PERSONS FROM COMPULSORY RETIREMENT FOR AGE
By virtue of the authority vested in me by section 204 of Part II of the act
entitled "AN ACT Making appropriations for the Legislative Branch of the
Government for the fiscal year ending June 30, 1933 , and for other purposes , "
approved June 30, 1932, the following-named persons who , during the current
month, reach the retirement age prescribed for automatic separation from the
service are hereby exempted from the provisions of that section .
Name
Thomas W. Maxwell
Cornelius T. Finehout
Charles E. Molster
Department
Post Office Department
Treasury Department
Department of Commerce
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
November 16 , 1932 .
