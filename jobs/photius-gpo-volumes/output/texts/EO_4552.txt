Executive Order
Paragraph 534, Article XXVII , of the Consular Regulations , is hereby amended
to read as follows :
" 534. General Accounts . Consular officers are required to keep separate general
accounts of all public or other moneys coming into their possession in their official
capacity from any source and for any purpose whatever and to render accounts of
the same in such manner, on such forms, and at such times , as may be prescribed
by the Comptroller General of the United States or the Department of State.
Consular officers shall keep a general cash book and such other account books , as
may be required according to the needs of their offices , to show all details of receipts
and expenditures , to ensure accuracy , and to enable principal officers to exercise
complete control over the funds of their offices . "
Paragraph 539, Article XXVII , of the Consular Regulations , is hereby canceled .
In Paragraph 432 , Article XXIV , of the Consular Regulations , the paragraph
reading :
" After being prepared the inventory must be copied in the ' Miscellaneous
Record Book '
, sufficient space being left at the end of each section to permit of future
entries upon supplementary inventories . Having once been recorded in correct
form it is not necessary to recopy it in succeeding volumes of the ' Miscellaneous
Record Book '
, but a reference should always be made in the current volume to
the one in which the inventory appears " ,
is canceled .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
December 11 , 1926 .
