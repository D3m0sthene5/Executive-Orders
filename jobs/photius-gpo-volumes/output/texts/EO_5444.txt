Executive Order
Lawrence , Massachusetts , Designated a Customs Port of Entry
By virtue of the authority vested in me by the act of Congress approved August
1 , 1914 (38 Stat. 623), making appropriations for the sundry civil expenses of the
Government for the fiscal year ended June 30, 1915 , Lawrence, Mass .
, is hereby
designated a customs port of entry in Customs Collection District No. 4 (Massa￾chusetts ) with headquarters at Boston , Mass .
, and the townships of Lawrence,
Methuen , and Andover are included within the limits of said port, effective 30 days
from the date of this order .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
September 16, 1930 .
