Executive Order
WYOMING
Executive Orders Nos . 2905 and 3394, dated July 8, 1918 , and January 28 ,
1921 , respectively, withdrawing certain lands in Wyoming in aid of legislation to
extend the boundaries of the Yellowstone National Park, are hereby revoked in so
far as they affect Lot 4 Sec . 31 , T. 45 N. , R. 113 W. , and the NE4 NE4 Sec . 36 ,
T. 45 N. , R. 114 W. , 6th P. M. , containing 85.43 acres , and the said land is hereby
restored to entry .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
April 25, 1927 .
