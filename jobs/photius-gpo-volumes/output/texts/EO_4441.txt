Executive Order
Mr. William A. Hock may be reinstated as clerk of the fifth grade in the post
office at Newark , New Jersey , without regard to the time limitation prescribed by
the Civil Service Rules . Mr. Hock served for 19 years in the Newark post office
and was removed June 30 , 1918 , upon charges which have subsequently been found
to have been unwarranted and unjustified .
This order is issued upon the recommendation of the Postmaster General to
remedy as far as possible the injustice involved in his removal.
In view of the facts presented with the recommendation and disclosed by
its
own inquiry ,
the Commission does not feel that any violence will
be
done the Civil
Service Rules by the reinstatement
of Mr. Hock . CALVIN COOLIDGE
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
May 13 ,1926 .
