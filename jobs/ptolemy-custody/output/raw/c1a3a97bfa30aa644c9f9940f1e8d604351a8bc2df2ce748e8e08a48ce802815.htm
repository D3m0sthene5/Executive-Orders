DEPARTMENT OF THE INTERIOR

Bureau of Land Management

43 CFR Public Land Order 7028

[OR-943-4210-06; GP4-014; OR-46603 (WASH)]

 

Partial Revocation of Executive Order No. 7693 Dated August 19, 
1937; Washington

AGENCY: Bureau of Land Management, Interior.

ACTION: Public Land Order.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: This order revokes an Executive order insofar as it affects 
11,725.30 acres of lands withdrawn for the Department of Agriculture 
for the Northeast Washington Project, LA-WA 2. The lands are no longer 
needed for the purpose for which they were withdrawn. The lands have 
been conveyed out of Federal ownership and will not be restored to 
surface entry.

EFFECTIVE DATE: March 17, 1994.

FOR FURTHER INFORMATION CONTACT:
Donna Kauffman, BLM Oregon/Washington State Office, P.O. Box 2965, 
Portland, Oregon 97208-2965, 503-280-7162.
