DEPARTMENT OF STATE ,
Washington , January 28 , 1873 .
Inquiries having been made from various quarters as to the application
of the Executive order issued on the 17th January , relating to the hold-
ing of State or municipal offices by persons holding civil offices under
the Federal Government , the President directs the following reply to be
made :

city , or of a town councilman of a town or village , or of appointments
