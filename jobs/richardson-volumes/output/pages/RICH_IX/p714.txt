CIVIL SERVICE .-EXECUTIVE ORDER .
EXECUTIVE MANSION , November 2 , 1896 .
The regulations of the Navy Department governing the employment
of labor at navy -yards having been adopted by the Civil Service Com-
mission as a regulation of the Commission July 29 , 1896 , under the au-
thority conferred by clause 1 , Rule I , of the revised civil - service rules of
May 6 , 1896 , it is hereby ordered that no modification of the existing
regulations shall be made without the approval of the Civil Service
Commission . GROVER CLEVELAND .
