AMENDMENT OF CIVIL - SERVICE RULES .
EXECUTIVE MANSION , June 18, 1889 .
Departmental Rule X , Customs Rule VII , Postal Rule VII , and Rail-
way Mail Rule VI are hereby amended by adding to each of said rules ,
at the end thereof , the following :
Provided , That certification may be made , subject to the other conditions of this
rule , for the reinstatement of any person who served in the military or naval service
of the United States in the late War of the Rebellion , and was honorably discharged
therefrom , without regard to the length of time he has been separated from the
service .
BENJ . HARRISON .
