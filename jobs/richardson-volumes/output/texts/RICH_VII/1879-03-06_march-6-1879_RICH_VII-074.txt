MARCH 6 , 1879 .
SIR : Your letter of the 26th ultimo , inclosing a draft of modification of
the civil - service rules , was duly received , and the rules have been con-
sidered and approved by the President . You may therefore act upon
them .
Very respectfully ,
JOHN SHERMAN ,
Secretary .
