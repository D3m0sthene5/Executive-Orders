GENERAL ORDERS , NO . 102 .
WAR DEPARTMENT ,
ADJUTANT - GENERAL'S OFFICE ,
Washington , October 10 , 1873 .
The President of the United States commands it to be made known
that all soldiers who have deserted their colors , and who shall , on or
before the 1st day of January , 1874 , surrender themselves at any military
station , shall receive a full pardon , only forfeiting the pay and allowances
due them at the time of desertion , and shall be restored to duty without
trial or punishment on condition that they faithfully serve through the
term of their enlistment .
By order of the Secretary of War :
E. D. TOWNSEND ,
Adjutant - General .
