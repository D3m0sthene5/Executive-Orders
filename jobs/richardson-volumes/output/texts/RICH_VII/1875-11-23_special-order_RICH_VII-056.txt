SPECIAL ORDER .
NAVY DEPARTMENT ,
Washington , November 23 , 1875 .
The President of the United States announces the death of Vice-
President Henry Wilson in the following order :
[For order see preceding page .]
In pursuance of the foregoing order , it is hereby directed that upon
the day following the receipt of this the ensign at each United States
naval station and of each United States naval vessel in commission be
hoisted at half - mast from sunrise to sunset , and that thirteen guns be
fired at sunrise , nineteen minute guns at meridian , and a national salute
at sunset at each United States naval station and on board flagships and
vessels acting singly , at home or abroad .
The officers of the Navy and Marine Corps will wear the usual badge
of mourning for three months . GEO . M. ROBESON ,
Secretary of the Navy .
