SPECIAL ORDER .
NAVY DEPARTMENT ,
Washington , August 2 , 1875 .
The President of the United States announces the death of ex - Presi-
dent Andrew Johnson in the following order :
[For order see preceding page .]
In pursuance of the foregoing order , it is hereby directed that the en-
sign at each naval station and of each vessel of the United States Navy
in commission be hoisted at half - mast from sunrise to sunset , and that a
gun be fired at intervals of every half hour from sunrise to sunset at each
naval station and on board of flagships and of vessels acting singly , on
Tuesday , the 3d instant , the day of the funeral , where this order may be
received in time , otherwise on the day after its receipt .
The officers of the Navy and Marine Corps will wear the usual badge
of mourning attached to the sword hilt and on the left arm for the period
of thirty days . DANIEL ΑΜΜΕΝ ,
Acting Secretary of the Navy .
