WASHINGTON , May 29 , 1874 .
The Civil Service Commission , at its sessions at Washington , having
recommended certain rules * to be prescribed by the President for the
government of the Light - House Service of the United States , these rules
as herewith published are approved , and their provisions will be enforced
by the proper officers . U. S. GRANT .
