GENERAL ORDER .
NAVY DEPARTMENT ,
Washington , October 9 , 1869 .
The death of ex - President Franklin Pierce is announced in the follow-
ing order of the President of the United States :
[For order see preceding page .]
In pursuance of the foregoing order , it is hereby directed that twenty-
one guns be fired , at intervals of one minute each , at the several navy-
yards and stations , on the day of the funeral where this order may be
received in time , otherwise on the day after its receipt , commencing at
noon , and also on board the flagships in each fleet . The flags at the
several navy - yards , naval stations , marine barracks , and vessels in com-
mission will be placed at half-mast from sunrise to sunset on the day
when the minute guns are fired .
All officers of the Navy and Marine Corps will wear the usual badge
of mourning attached to the sword hilt and on the left arm for thirty
days.
GEO . M. ROBESON ,
Secretary of the Navy .
