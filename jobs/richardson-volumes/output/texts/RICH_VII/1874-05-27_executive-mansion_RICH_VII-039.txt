EXECUTIVE MANSION ,
Washington , D. C. , May 27 , 1874 .
SIR : * The President directs me to say that the several Departments of
the Government will be closed on the 30th instant , in order to enable the
*Addressed to the heads of the Executive Departments , etc.
employees to participate in the decoration of the graves of the soldiers
who fell during the rebellion .
I am , sir , your obedient servant ,
О. Е. ВАВСOCK ,
Secretary .
