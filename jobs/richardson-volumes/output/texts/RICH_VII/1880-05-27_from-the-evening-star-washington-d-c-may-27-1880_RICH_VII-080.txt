[From the Evening Star , Washington , D. C. , May 27, 1880.]
EXECUTIVE MANSION ,
Washington , D. C. , May 27 , 1880 .
DEAR SIR :* I am directed by the President to say that the several
Departments of the Government will be closed on Saturday , the 29th
instant , in remembrance of those who fell in defense of the nation , and
to enable the employees to participate in the commemorative ceremonies
of the day .
Very respectfully , your obedient servant ,
W. K. ROGERS,
Private Secretary .
