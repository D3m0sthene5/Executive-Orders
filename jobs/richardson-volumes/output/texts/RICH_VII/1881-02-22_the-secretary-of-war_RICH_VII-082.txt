The SECRETARY OF WAR :
EXECUTIVE MANSION ,
Washington , February 22 , 1881 .
In view of the well -known fact that the sale of intoxicating liquors
in the Army of the United States is the cause of much demoralization
among both officers and men , and that it gives rise to a large proportion
of the cases before general and garrison courts - martial , involving great
expense and serious injury to the service—
It is therefore directed , That the Secretary of War take suitable steps ,
as far as practicable consistently with vested rights , to prevent the sale of
intoxicating liquors as a beverage at the camps , forts , and other posts
of the Army . R. B. HAYES .
