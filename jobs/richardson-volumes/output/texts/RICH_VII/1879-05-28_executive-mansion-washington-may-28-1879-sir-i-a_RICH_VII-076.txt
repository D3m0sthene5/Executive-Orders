EXECUTIVE MANSION , Washington , May 28 , 1879 . SIR : * I am directed by the President to say that the several Depart- ments of the Government will be closed on Friday , the 30th instant , in remembrance of those who fell in defense of the nation , and to enable the employees to participate in the commemorative ceremonies of the day . Very respectfully , your obedient servant , W. K. ROGERS , Private Secretary . * Addressed to the heads of the Executive Departments , etc.
