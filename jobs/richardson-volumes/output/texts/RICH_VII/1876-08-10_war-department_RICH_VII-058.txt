WAR DEPARTMENT ,
Washington City , August 10 , 1876 .
By direction of the President , General W. T. Sherman and Brigadier-
General M. C. Meigs , Quartermaster - General United States Army , are
appointed members of the commission to examine the whole subject
of reform and reorganization of the Army of the United States , " as pro-
vided by section 4 , act approved July 24 , 1876 , " making appropriations
for the support of the Army for the fiscal year ending June 30 , 1877 ,
and for other purposes . "
J. D. CAMERON , Secretary of War .
