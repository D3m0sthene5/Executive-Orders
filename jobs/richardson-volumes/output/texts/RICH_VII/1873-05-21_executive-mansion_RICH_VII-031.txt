EXECUTIVE MANSION ,
Washington , D. C. , May 21 , 1873 .
SIR : * The President directs me to say that the several Departments
of the Government will be closed on the 30th instant , in order to enable
the employees of the Government to participate , in connection with the
Grand Army of the Republic , in the decoration of the graves of the sol-
diers who fell during the rebellion .
I am, sir , your obedient servant ,
О. Е. ВАВСоск,
Secretary .
