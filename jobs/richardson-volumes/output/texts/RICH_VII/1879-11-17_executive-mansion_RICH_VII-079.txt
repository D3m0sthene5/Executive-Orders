EXECUTIVE MANSION ,
Washington , November 17 , 1879 .
DEAR SIR : † I am directed by the President to say that the several
Departments of the Government will be closed on Wednesday , the 19th
instant , to enable the employees to participate in the ceremonies attend-
ing the unveiling of the statue of the late General George H. Thomas .
Very truly , yours ,
W. K. ROGERS , Private Secretary .
