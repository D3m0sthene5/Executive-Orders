GENERAL ORDERS , No. 83 .
HEADQUARTERS OF THE ARMY ,
ADJUTANT - GENERAL'S OFFICE ,
Washington , December 24 , 1869 .
Brevet Major - General A. H. Terry , in addition to his duties as com-
mander of the Department of the South , is , by order of the President of
the United States , appointed to exercise the duties of commanding gen-
eral of the District of Georgia , as defined by the act of Congress approved
December 22 , 1869 .
By command of General Sherman :
E. D. TOWNSEND ,
Adjutant - General .
