EXECUTIVE MANSION , May 28, 1895.
To the Heads of the Executive Departments :
As a mark of respect to the memory of the Hon . Walter Q. Gresham ,
late Secretary of State , the President directs that the several Executive
Departments and the Government Printing Office , in the city of Wash-
ington , be closed on Wednesday , the 29th day of May , 1895 , the day of
the funeral .
HENRY T. THURBER ,
Private Secretary.
