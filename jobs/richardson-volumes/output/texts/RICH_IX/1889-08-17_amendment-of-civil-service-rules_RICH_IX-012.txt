AMENDMENT OF CIVIL - SERVICE RULES .
EXECUTIVE MANSION , August 17 , 1889.
Clause 5 of Railway Mail Rule II is hereby amended by adding thereto
the following clauses :
(d) Clerks employed exclusively as porters in handling mail matter in bulk , in
sacks , or pouches , and not otherwise .
(e ) Clerks employed exclusively on steamboats .
Approved :
BENJ . HARRISON .
