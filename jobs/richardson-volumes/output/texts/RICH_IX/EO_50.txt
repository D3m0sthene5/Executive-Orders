AMENDMENT OF CIVIL - SERVICE RULES .
EXECUTIVE MANSION , November 2, 1894 .
Departmental Rule VII , clause 1 , is hereby amended by inserting at
the end of line 6 the following :
Vacancies in places authorized to be filled by noncompetitive examination may
be filled without examination for a period not exceeding thirty days , until a regular
appointment can be made upon certification made by the Commission .
Every such appointment and the reasons therefor shall be at once reported to the
Commission .
Approved : GROVER CLEVELAND .
