AMENDMENTS OF CIVIL - SERVICE RULES .
EXECUTIVE MANSION , May 13 , 1896 .
The civil - service rules are hereby amended as follows :
Rule III , clause 2 ( a ) , is amended by adding after the words " the
light - house service " the words " the life- saving service . "
Paragraph ( b ) of the same rule and clause is amended by striking out
after the words " who are in the service of the Treasury Department in
any capacity " the words " except those in the life - saving service ."
Approved :
GROVER CLEVELAND .
