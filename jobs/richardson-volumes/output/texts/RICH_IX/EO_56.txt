AMENDMENT OF CIVIL - SERVICE RULES .
Departmental Rule VII is hereby amended by adding thereto the fol-
lowing section , to be numbered 9:
The Commission shall certify for transfer and reappointment to any classified non-
excepted place in the departmental service , upon the requisition of the head of a
Department , any person who at the time of making such requisition is holding an
office outside the classified service in any Executive Department at Washington to
which he was appointed from a classified place in the departmental service ; and
upon the requisition of any head of Department the Commission shall certify for
reinstatement in the classified service of said Department any such officer who within
one year next preceding the date of the requisition , by the abolition of his office or
otherwise , has without delinquency or misconduct been separated from said office :
Provided , That this section shall not authorize the reappointment to the classified
service of any such officer or ex -officer who was appointed to his office from an
excepted place , unless his appointment to such excepted place was by promotion
from a nonexcepted place .
Approved , December 15 , 1894 . GROVER CLEVELAND .
