AMENDMENT OF CIVIL -SERVICE RULES .
Special Departmental Rule I is hereby amended to except from exami-
nation in the Department of the Treasury , in the Bureau of Printing and
Engraving , forty -three compositors and eight pressmen now temporarily
employed under authority of the sundry civil act of March 2 , 1895 , such
employment to cease prior to March 14 , 1896. Vacancies occurring in
this force shall be filled only by competitive examination under the civil-
service rules .
Approved , September 16 , 1895 . GROVER CLEVELAND .
