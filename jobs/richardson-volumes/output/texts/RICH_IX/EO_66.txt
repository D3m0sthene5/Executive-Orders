AMENDMENT OF CIVIL - SERVICE RULES .
EXECUTIVE MANSION , May 24 , 1895 .
Special Departmental Rule No. 1 , clause 3 , is hereby amended by add-
ing to the places excepted from examination in the Department of the
Interior the following :
In the Bureau of Education : Specialist in foreign educational systems and spe-
cialist in education as a preventive of pauperism and crime .
Approved : GROVER CLEVELAND .
