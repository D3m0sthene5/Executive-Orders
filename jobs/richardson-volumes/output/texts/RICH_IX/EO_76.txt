AMENDMENT OF CIVIL -SERVICE RULES .
Special Departmental Rule I is hereby amended by adding to the list
of places excepted from examination in the Treasury Department—
In the Bureau of Immigration : One statistician and stenographer , with power to act
as immigrant inspector .
Approved , July 30 , 1895 . GROVER CLEVELAND .
