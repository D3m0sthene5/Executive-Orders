AMENDMENT OF CIVIL - SERVICE RULES .
SEPTEMBER 2 , 1890 .
Special Departmental Rule No. 1 is hereby amended by adding to the
places excepted from examination therein the following :
In the Post -Office Department , office of the Postmaster - General : Stenographer as
confidential clerk to the chief post-office inspector .
BENJ . HARRISON .
