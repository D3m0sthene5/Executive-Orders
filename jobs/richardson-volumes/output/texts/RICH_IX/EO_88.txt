AMENDMENT OF CIVIL -SERVICE RULES .
EXECUTIVE MANSION , March 28, 1896 .
So much of the Executive orders heretofore issued under General Rule
III , section 2 , clause (c) , as provides for the appointment of members of
the board of pension appeals in the Department of the Interior by non-
competitive examination is hereby revoked , and these places will hereafter
be treated as subject to competitive examination .
Approved : GROVER CLEVELAND .
