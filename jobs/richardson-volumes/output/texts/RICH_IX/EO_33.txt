AMENDMENT OF CIVIL -SERVICE RULES .
Special Customs Rule No. 1 is hereby amended by adding to the places
excepted from examination therein the following :
In the customs district of Boston , office of the collector : One superintendent of
warehouses .
In the customs district of Philadelphia , office of the collector : Five chiefs of
division .
Approved , June 21 , 1894 . GROVER CLEVELAND .
