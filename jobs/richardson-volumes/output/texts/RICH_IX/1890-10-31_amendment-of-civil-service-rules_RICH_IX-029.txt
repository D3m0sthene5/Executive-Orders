AMENDMENT OF CIVIL - SERVICE RULES .
OCTOBER 31 , 1890 .
Section 7 of Railway Mail Rule IV is hereby amended by inserting in
line 7 , after the word " days ," the following : " or until the emergency
ceases . "
BENJ . HARRISON.
