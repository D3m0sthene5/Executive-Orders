AMENDMENT OF CIVIL - SERVICE RULES .
EXECUTIVE MANSION , December 2, 1895 .
So much of Executive orders heretofore issued under General Rule III ,
section 2 , clause (c) , as provides for the appointment of special agents
in the Department of Labor by noncompetitive examination is hereby
revoked .
Approved : GROVER CLEVELAND .
