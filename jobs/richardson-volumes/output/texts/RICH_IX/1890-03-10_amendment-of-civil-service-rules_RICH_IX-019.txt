AMENDMENT OF CIVIL -SERVICE RULES .
Special Customs Rule No. 1 is hereby amended by adding thereto the
following :
In the customs district of New York : Detectives employed exclusively as such .
Approved , March 10 , 1890 . BENJ . HARRISON .
