AMENDMENT OF CIVIL -SERVICE RULES .
EXECUTIVE MANSION , July 15, 1895.
Special Departmental Rule I is hereby amended by striking out the
whole of the paragraph in section 3 , Department of the Interior , relating
to the Geological Survey and substituting in lieu thereof the following :
In the Geological Survey : Professional experts and special agents employed for
short periods at per diem salaries and paid only when actually employed .
Approved : GROVER CLEVELAND .
