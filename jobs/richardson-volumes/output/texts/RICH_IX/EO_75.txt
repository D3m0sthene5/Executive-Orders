AMENDMENT OF CIVIL - SERVICE RULES .
EXECUTIVE MANSION , July 15, 1895 .
Executive orders heretofore issued designating the places to be filled
by noncompetitive examination under clause (c) of General Rule III are
hereby amended so as to include among those places in the Department of
the Interior , in the Geological Survey , the editor and the photographer .
Approved : GROVER CLEVELAND .
