AMENDMENT OF CIVIL -SERVICE RULES .
NOVEMBER 24 , 1891 .
Special Departmental Rule No. 1 is hereby amended so as to include
among the places excepted from examination the following :
In the Department of the Treasury , in the Bureau of Statistics : One confidential
clerk to the Chief of the Bureau .
BENJ . HARRISON .
