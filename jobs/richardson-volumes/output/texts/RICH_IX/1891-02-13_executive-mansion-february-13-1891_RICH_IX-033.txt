EXECUTIVE MANSION , February 13 , 1891 .
To the Heads of the Executive Departments :
In token of respect to the memory of Admiral David D. Porter, who
died this morning , the President directs that the national flag be dis-
played at half- mast upon all public buildings throughout the United
States until after his funeral shall have taken place , and that on the day
of the funeral public business in the Departments at Washington be
suspended . E. W. HALFORD , Private Secretary .
