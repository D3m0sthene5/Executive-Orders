EXECUTIVE OFFICE ,
Washington , D. C. , May 31, 1865 .
To -morrow , the 1st of June , being the day appointed for special humilia-
tion and prayer in consequence of the assassination of Abraham Lincoln ,
late President of the United States , the Executive Office and the various
Departments will be closed during the day .
ANDREW JOHNSON ,
President of the United States .
