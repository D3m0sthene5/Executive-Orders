EXECUTIVE MANSION , February 1 , 1864 .
Ordered, That a draft for 500,000 men , to serve for three years or during
the war , be made on the 10th day of March next for the military service
of the United States , crediting and deducting therefrom so many as may
have been enlisted or drafted into the service prior to the 1st day of March
and not heretofore credited .
ABRAHAM LINCOLN .
