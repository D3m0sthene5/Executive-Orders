Lieutenant -General WINFIELD Scott :
WASHINGTON , October 14 , 1861 .
The military line of the United States for the suppression of the insur-
rection may be extended so far as Bangor , in Maine . You and any officer
acting under your authority are hereby authorized to suspend the writ of
habeas corpus in any place between that place and the city of Washington .
ABRAHAM LINCOLN .
By the President :
WILLIAM H. SEWARD ,
Secretary of State .
