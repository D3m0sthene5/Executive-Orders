WAR DEPARTMENT ,
Washington City , April 29 , 1865 .
The Executive order of January 20 , 1865 , prohibiting the exportation
of hay , is rescinded from and after the 1st day of May , 1865 .
By order of the President :
EDWIN M. STANTON ,
Secretary of War .
