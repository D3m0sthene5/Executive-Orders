619322Executive Order 505 — Authorizing Transfer of Julius H. Hammond, Clerk in General Land Office, to Competitive Service1906President of the United States

Mr. Julius H. Hammond, of Colorado, depositary acting for the Commissioner as receiver of public moneys and also as confidential clerk, General Land Office, Interior Department, may be transferred with his position to the competitive, classified service.

Theodore Roosevelt.

The White House,

September 20, 1906.
