1910 CSR 112

649155Executive Order 1174 — Authorizing Reinstatement of Henry Bartalot as Marine Engineer in Quartermaster's Department at Large Without Regard to Civil Service Rules1910President of the United States

Henry Bartalot, formerly employed as a marine engineer in the Quartermaster's Department at Large, may be reinstated as a marine engineer without regard to the length of separation from the service.

Wm. H. Taft.

The White House,

March 7, 1910.
