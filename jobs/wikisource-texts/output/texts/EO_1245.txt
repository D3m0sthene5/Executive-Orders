649804Executive Order 1245 — Authorizing Placement of Twenty-Nine Laborers on Classified List in Customs Service Without Regard to Civil Service Rules1910President of the United States

The following-named laborers in the Customs Service at New York, N. Y., may be placed on the list of classified laborers at that port:

Matthew F. Mannix, Charles Williams, Edward J. Kennedy, Charles P. Klausman, John J. McAdams, John F. Daly, Leo Steiner, Anthony J. O'Donnell, John F. Meagher, Edward McCann, Alfredo Sulla, Thomas F. McNamara, Nathaniel J. De La Mater, John A. Holmes, Joseph F. Worth, James H. Hunt, Vinton Giles, Jesse T. Moir, William Fitzgerald, George Lander, John Veit, John McAdams, Michael Rudden, M. Harry Isaacs, Harry Gerstman, Henry Quinn, Frederick H. Schiele, Patrick C. Driscoll, Frank Scharf.

Wm. H. Taft.

The White House,

September 14, 1910.
