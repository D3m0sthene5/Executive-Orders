Signed by President Theodore Roosevelt Saturday, June 16, 1906

1906 CSR 102

619218Executive Order 460 — Authorizing Transfer of Cressy L. Wilbur to Chief Statistician in Bureau of the Census Without Regard to Civil Service Rules1906President of the United States

Dr. Cressy L. Wilbur, expert special agent of the Bureau of the Census, may be transferred to the position of chief statistician for vital statistics in the same Bureau.

Theodore Roosevelt.

The White House,

June 16, 1906.
