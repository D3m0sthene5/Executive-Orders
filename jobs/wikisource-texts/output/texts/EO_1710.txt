1330323Executive Order 1710 — Authorizing Reinstatement of David C. Hanawalt in Bureau of Animal Industry Without Regard to Civil Service Rules1913President of the United States

Dr. David C. Hanawalt, who served as a veterinary inspector in the Bureau of Animal Industry from March 1, 1900, to October 15, 1910, when he resigned voluntarily, may be reinstated, the year limit fixed in the rules being waived, a comparatively short period having elapsed since his resignation.

Wm. H. Taft.

The White House,

February 20, 1913.
