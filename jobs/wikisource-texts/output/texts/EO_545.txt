619432Executive Order 545 — Authorizing Reinstatement of David Robinson as Immigrant Inspector Without Regard to Civil Service Rules1907President of the United States

Mr. David Robinson may be reinstated to the position of immigrant inspector, on the ground of his efficiency and familiarity with immigration matters.

Theodore Roosevelt.

The White House,

January 12, 1907.
