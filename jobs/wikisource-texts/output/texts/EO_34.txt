Signed by President Grover Cleveland Thursday, June 21, 1894

609565Executive Order 34 — Amending Civil Service Special Indian Rule No. 1 Regarding Exceptions from Examination1894President of the United States

Special Indian rule No. 1 is hereby amended by adding to the places excepted from examination therein the following:

Kindergarten teachers, to be employed as such, not exceeding twenty in number.

Approved June 21, 1894.

Grover Cleveland.
