Signed by President Theodore Roosevelt Wednesday, February 10, 1909

592295Executive Order 1025 — Authorizing Promotion of James J. Smith to Day Inspector in the Customs Service at the Port of New York1909President of the United States

Mr. James J. Smith may be promoted from night inspector to day inspector in the Customs Service at the port of New York.

Theodore Roosevelt.

The White House

February 10, 1909.
