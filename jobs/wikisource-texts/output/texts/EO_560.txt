624788Executive Order 560 — Authorizing Appointment of Elizabeth M. Leech as Clerk Without Regard to Civil Service Rules1907President of the United States

Miss Elizabeth M. Leech may be appointed to a clerical position in the classified civil service without compliance with the civil-service rules.

Theodore Roosevelt.

The White House,

February 8, 1907.
