616113Executive Order 343 — Authorizing Permanent Appointment of Gertrude Weaver as Clerk in Signal Service Without Examination1905President of the United States

Mrs. Gertrude Weaver, now a temporary clerk, may be permanently appointed as a clerk in the Signal Service at large, without examination under the civil service rules.

Theodore Roosevelt.

White House,

July 21, 1905.
