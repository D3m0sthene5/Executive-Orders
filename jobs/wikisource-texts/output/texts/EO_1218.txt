Amends Executive Order 209, March 20, 1903.

Signed by President William H. Taft Friday, July 1, 1910

1910 CSR 108

648548Executive Order 1218 — Amending Civil Service Rules to Except Specialist in Bureau of Education from Examination1910President of the United States

Schedule A, Section VII, Civil Service Rules, relating to the Department of the Interior, is hereby amended by the addition of a paragraph, as follows:

One specialist in higher education in the Bureau of Education.

Wm. H. Taft.

The White House,

July 1, 1910.
