Signed by President Theodore Roosevelt Thursday, August 20, 1903

606405Executive Order 232 — Authorizing Appointment of Dr. William A. White as Superintendent of the Government Hospital for the Insane Without Examination1903President of the United States

Dr. William A. White may be appointed to the vacancy now existing in the position of superintendent of the Government Hospital for the Insane without examination under the civil-service rules.

Theodore Roosevelt.

White House,

August 20, 1903.
