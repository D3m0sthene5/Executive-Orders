Signed by President Theodore Roosevelt Sunday, August 9, 1903

606401Executive Order 229 — Authorizing Appointment of Julia W. Wellborn as Clerk in the Geological Survey Without Examination1903President of the United States

Mrs. Julia W. Wellborn may be permanently appointed Clerk in the Geological Survey without examination under the civil service rules.

Theodore Roosevelt.

White House,

August 9, 1903.
