826615Executive Order 1391 — Authorizing Transfer of Charles B. Bateman to Mechanical Position in Classified Service1911President of the United States

Charles B. Bateman may be transferred from the navy-yard service to any classified mechanical position in the departments at Washington for which his experience of 14 years as a machinist qualifies him.

Wm. H. Taft.

The White House,

August 2, 1911.
