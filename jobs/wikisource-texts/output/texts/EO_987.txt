591984Executive Order 987 — Authorizing Appointment of Dell A. Baker as Deputy Collector in Office of Collector of Customs at Port of New York1908President of the United States

Mr. Dell A. Baker may be appointed to the position of deputy collector in the office of the collector of customs at the port of New York.

Theodore Roosevelt.

The White House

December 3, 1908.
