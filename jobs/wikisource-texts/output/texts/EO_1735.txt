1330360Executive Order 1735 — Authorizing Appointment of Thomas Butler as Laborer in Classified Service Without Examination1913President of the United States

Thomas Butler of the District of Columbia may be appointed without examination to the position of laborer in the classified civil service.

Wm. H. Taft.

The White House,

March 3, 1913.
