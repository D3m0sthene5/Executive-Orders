Revokes Executive Order 370, November 15, 1905. See also Executive Order 394, January 12, 1906.

Signed by President Theodore Roosevelt Friday, January 12, 1906

1905 CSR 103

616452Executive Order 393 — Repealing Executive Order of November 15, 1905, Regarding Appointments to Isthmian Canal Commission Positions1906President of the United States

Executive Order of November 15, 1905, as to appointments to positions in the service of the Isthmian Canal Commission upon the Isthmus of Panama, is hereby repealed.

T. Roosevelt.

The White House,

January 12, 1906.
