Signed by President William H. Taft Saturday, June 26, 1909

General Orders, No. 136, War Dept., July 6, 1909

1585931Executive Order 1097 — Amending Executive Order of May 12, 1909 Regarding Military Reservation on Shaw Island in Washington1909President of the United States

The Executive Order of May 12, 1909, amending Executive Order of July 2, 1875, declaring the military reservation on the western side of Shaw Island, Washington, is hereby amended so that the reference bearing, “N. 18° 55′ W.,” of the ﬁr tree from the concrete monument “located in the northwest corner of Hoffman’s ﬁeld alongside the trail leading from Parks' Cove
to O'Hara's Cove," shall read, “N. 18° 55′ E.”

Wm. H. Taft.

The White House,

June 26, 1909.
