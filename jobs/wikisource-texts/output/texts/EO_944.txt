636220Executive Order 944 — Authorizing Continued Employment of Three Clerks in Isthmian Canal Service Without Examination1908President of the United States

George Shaffer, Olof Ekedahl, and Willoughby Culbertson may be continued on clerical duties in the Isthmian Canal Service without examination under the civil-service rules.

Theodore Roosevelt.

The White House,

September 30, 1908.
