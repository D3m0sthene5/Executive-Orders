616217Executive Order 354 — Authorizing Transfer of W. E. Hall to Clerk in Treasury Department Without Regard to Civil Service Rules1905President of the United States

Mr. W. E. Hall may be transferred from the Isthmian Canal Service to a clerkship, Class D, Treasury Department, without regard to the limitations contained in civil service Rule X, requiring absolute appointment or service of six months next preceding the transfer.

Theodore Roosevelt.

White House,

September 26, 1905.
