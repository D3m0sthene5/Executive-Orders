625847Executive Order 673 — Revoking Executive Order of June 11, 1907, Regarding Transfer of George S. Fox to United States1907President of the United States

The Executive Order of June 11, 1907, providing for the transfer of Mr. George S. Fox from the Isthmian Canal Service to the Federal classified service, is hereby revoked.

Theodore Roosevelt.

The White House,

August 3, 1907.
