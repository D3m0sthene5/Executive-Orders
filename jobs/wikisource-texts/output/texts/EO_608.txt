625784Executive Order 608 — Authorizing Transfer of Jake T. Patrick to Competitive Position in Internal Revenue Service Without Regard to Civil Service Rules1907President of the United States

Mr. Jake T. Patrick, a storekeeper-gauger, excepted from examination, in the eighth Kentucky district, Internal-Revenue Service, may be transferred to a competitive position in that district.

Theodore Roosevelt.

The White House,

April 3, 1907.
