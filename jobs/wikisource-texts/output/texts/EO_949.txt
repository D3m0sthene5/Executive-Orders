Amends Executive Order 209, March 20, 1903. Also see Executive Order 1339, April 21, 1911.

Signed by President Theodore Roosevelt Wednesday, October 7, 1908

1908 CSR 123

635939Executive Order 949 — Amending Civil Service Rules Regarding Retransfers to Positions Previously Held1908President of the United States

Section 3 of Civil Service Rule X is hereby amended by adding after the word "executive" the words "or judicial," and by striking out after the word "clauses" the expression "(a)," so that the section as amended will read as follows:

Any person may be retransferred to a position in which he was formerly employed, or to any position to which transfer could be made therefrom, if since his transfer he has been continuously in the executive or judicial civil service of the United States or of its insular possessions. Such transfer may be made without compliance with clauses (b), (c), (d), and (e) of section 8 of this rule.

Theodore Roosevelt.

The White House,

October 7, 1908.
