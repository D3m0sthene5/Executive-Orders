591662Executive Order 1151 — Amending Civil Service Rules to Except Chief Post-Office Inspector from Examination1910President of the United States

The list of classified positions excepted from examination under Civil Service Rule II, clause 3, is hereby amended so as to include the position of chief post-office inspector in the Post-Office Department.

Wm. H. Taft.

The White House,

January 4, 1910.
