1909 CSR 117

591881Executive Order 1050 — Making Six Employees of the Department of Justice Eligible for Transfer to Competitive Service1909President of the United States

The two employees under the appropriation "Enforcement of antitrust laws" and the four under the appropriation "Five Civilized Tribes" in the Department of Justice, who have been commended by the Attorney-General for retention in the public service, may be transferred to the competitive classified service after appropriate noncompetitive examination and approval by the Civil Service Commission.

Theodore Roosevelt.

The White House

March 3, 1909.
