Amends Executive Order 209, March 20, 1903. Superseded by Executive Order 632, May 10, 1907.

Signed by President Theodore Roosevelt Wednesday, April 26, 1905

1905 CSR 75

615089Executive Order 320 — Amending Civil Service Rules to Except Certain Firemen of War Department from Examination1905President of the United States

Section III of Schedule A, relating to the War Department, is hereby amended by the addition of the following clause, to be numbered 6:

All firemen employed on torpedo planters.

Theodore Roosevelt.

White House,

April 26, 1905.
