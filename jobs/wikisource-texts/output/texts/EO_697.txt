626549Executive Order 697 — Authorizing Appointment of William Stimpel as Disciplinarian at Carlisle, Pennsylvania Indian School After Examination1907President of the United States

Mr. William Stimpel may be appointed disciplinarian at the Carlisle, Pa., Indian School upon noncompetitive examination showing him to be qualified by experience for the position.

Theodore Roosevelt.

The White House,

October 5, 1907.
