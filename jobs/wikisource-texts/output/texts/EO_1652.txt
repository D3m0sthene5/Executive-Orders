1329606Executive Order 1652 — Authorizing Appointment of George H. Hamilton as Assistant Postmaster Without Regard to Civil Service Rules1912President of the United States

Mr. George H. Hamilton may be appointed to the vacancy existing in the position of assistant postmaster at Newark, Ohio, without reference to the civil-service rules. 

Wm. H. Taft.

The White House,

November 29, 1912.
