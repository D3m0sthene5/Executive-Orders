619411Executive Order 523 — Authorizing Reinstatement of Albert M. West as Scientific Assistant in Department of Agriculture Without Regard to Civil Service Rules1906President of the United States

Mr. Albert M. West, of Massachusetts, may be reinstated to the position of scientific assistant in the Department of Agriculture without regard to the one year limitation of civil service Rule IX, as it appears that the qualifications which he possesses are of an unusual and expert character.

Theodore Roosevelt.

The White House,

November 5, 1906.
