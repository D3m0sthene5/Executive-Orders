Signed by President William H. Taft Tuesday, February 15, 1910

1910 CSR 112

649153Executive Order 1162 — Authorizing Reinstatement of John G. Curran in Assistant Custodian and Janitor Service Without Regard to Civil Service Rules1910President of the United States

John G. Curran, long employed in the public building at Cleveland, Ohio, as assistant custodian-janitor, may be reappointed in the Assistant Custodian and Janitor Service under the designation of assistant custodian, United States Post-Office, Custom-House, and Court-House Building at Cleveland, Ohio, notwithstanding he has been more than one year separated from the service because of the abandonment of the old building.

Wm. H. Taft.

The White House,

February 15, 1910.
