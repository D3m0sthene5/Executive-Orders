626834Executive Order 766 — Authorizing Appointment of Charles L. Babcock as Immigrant Inspector in Immigration Service Without Examination1908President of the United States

Mr. Charles L. Babcock may be appointed as an immigrant inspector for confidential duty in the Immigration Service in the Department of Commerce and Labor without examination under the civil-service rules.

Theodore Roosevelt.

The White House,

March 4, 1908.
