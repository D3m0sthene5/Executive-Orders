Signed by President Theodore Roosevelt Wednesday, January 10, 1906

619445Executive Order 392 — Authorizing Appointment of E. M. LeBoiteaux in Bureau of the Census After Noncompetitive Examination1906President of the United States

Mr. E. M. Le Boiteaux may be employed in the Bureau of the Census upon experimental work in developing tabulating machinery, as authorized in the latest act appropriating for executive, legislative, and judicial expenses, subject to noncompetitive examination by the Civil Service Commission.

Theodore Roosevelt.

The White House,

January 10, 1906.
