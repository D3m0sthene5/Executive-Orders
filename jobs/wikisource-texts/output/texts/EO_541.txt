Signed by President Theodore Roosevelt Monday, December 31, 1906

620545Executive Order 541 — Authorizing Transfer of Joseph J. Harvey to Subclerical Position in Civil Service1906President of the United States

Mr. Joseph J. Harvey, a messenger in the Spanish Treaty Claims Commission, may be transferred or appointed to any other subclerical position in the executive civil service.

Theodore Roosevelt.

The White House,

December 31, 1906.
