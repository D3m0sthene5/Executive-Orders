625789Executive Order 620 — Authorizing Appointment of Frederick L. Dunlap as Associate Chemist in Department of Agriculture Without Examination1907President of the United States

Frederick L. Dunlap, of the State of Michigan, may be appointed to the position of associate chemist and member of the food and drug inspection in the classified service of the Department of Agriculture without examination and certification by the Civil Service Commission.

Theodore Roosevelt.

The White House,

April 25, 1907.
