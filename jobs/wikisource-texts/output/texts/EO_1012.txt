Signed by President Theodore Roosevelt Saturday, January 23, 1909

592007Executive Order 1012 — Authorizing Continuance of William H. Baldwin as Special Examiner in Corporations Bureau1909President of the United States

Mr. William H. Baldwin may be continued as a special examiner in the Bureau of Corporations, Department of Commerce and Labor, as long as the department may regard his services necessary and in the interest of good administration.

Theodore Roosevelt.

The White House

January 23, 1909.
