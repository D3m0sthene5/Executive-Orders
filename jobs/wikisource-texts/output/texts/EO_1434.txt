826631Executive Order 1434 — Authorizing Reinstatement of Richard W. Barkley as Examiner in Patent Office Without Regard to Civil Service Rules1911President of the United States

Mr. Richard W. Barkley may be reinstated as an examiner in the Patent Office, the year limit upon eligibility for reinstatement being waived in his case.

Wm. H. Taft.

The White House,

November 17, 1911.
