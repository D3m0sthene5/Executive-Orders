625968Executive Order 691 — Classifying R. D. Scarlett and John M. Newton as Clothing Examiners in Quartermaster's Department, Philadelphia, Without Regard to Civil Service Rules1907President of the United States

R. D. Scarlett and John M. Newton, who have been serving in the quartermaster's department at large, Philadelphia, as clothing examiners since February, 1904, are hereby included within the classified service.

Theodore Roosevelt.

The White House,

September 23, 1907.
