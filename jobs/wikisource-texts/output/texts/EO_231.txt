Signed by President Theodore Roosevelt Monday, August 17, 1903

606403Executive Order 231 — Authorizing Employment of Allen Hazen as Engineer in Washington Filtration Plant for Two Years1903President of the United States

Allen Hazen, who has been employed temporarily as expert and consulting engineer in connection with the preparation of plans, estimates, and specifications for the Washington filtration plant, may be continued during the construction and initial operation of the plant, not to exceed twenty-four months.

Theodore Roosevelt.

White House,

August 17, 1903.
