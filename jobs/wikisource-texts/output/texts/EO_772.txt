626844Executive Order 772 — Authorizing Appointment of Wilhelm Lutz to Position in Immigration Bureau Without Regard to Civil Service Rules1908President of the United States

Mr. Wilhelm Lutz, assistant chief of police of Porto Rico, may be appointed to a position in the Immigration Bureau of the United States for the reasons set forth in the letter of Governor Post.

Theodore Roosevelt.

The White House,

March 21, 1908.
