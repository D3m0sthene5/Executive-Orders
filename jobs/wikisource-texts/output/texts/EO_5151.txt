625798Executive Order 5151 — Amending Executive Order No. 1713 of February 24, 1913, Reserving Certain Described Islands in Washington for Public Purposes1929President of the United States

Executive Order No. 1713, dated February 24, 1913, which reserved certain unsurveyed islands in Ts. 36 and 37 N., R. 2 W., W. M., Washington, for public purposes, is hereby revoked and set aside in so far as it applies to the following described islands; viz: 

An island locally known as "Sheep Island " situated in the NW ¼ Sec. 9, T. 36 N., R. 2 W., W. M.

Two islands known as "Double Island" in the E ½ Sec. 17, T. 36 N., R. 2 W., W.M.

Herbert Hoover

The White House,

July 30, 1929.
