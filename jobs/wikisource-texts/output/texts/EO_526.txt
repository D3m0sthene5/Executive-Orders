619413Executive Order 526 — Authorizing Appointment of Theodore C. M. Schindler as Special Agent in Bureau of Corporations Without Examination1906President of the United States

Mr. Theodore C. M. Schindler may be appointed special agent in the Bureau of Corporations without examination under the civil service rules.

Theodore Roosevelt.

The White House,

December 6, 1906.
