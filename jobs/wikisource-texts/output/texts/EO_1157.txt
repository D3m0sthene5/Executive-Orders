1910 CSR 112

649144Executive Order 1157 — Authorizing Reinstatement of John N. Parsons as Letter Carrier in Postal Service Without Regard to Civil Service Rules1910President of the United States

Upon the recommendation of the Postmaster General; with the concurrence of the Civil Service Commission, John N. Parsons may be reinstated as a letter carrier in the postal service with a view to his promotion to the position of post-office inspector, the one-year limitation of the reinstatement rule being waived because of his excellent record as carrier and his efficient service as postmaster at Yonkers, N. Y.

Wm. H. Taft.

The White House,

January 27, 1910.
