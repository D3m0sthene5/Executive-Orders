Amends Executive Order 209, March 20, 1903

Signed by President Theodore Roosevelt Monday, August 10, 1903

1903 CSR 197

609251Executive Order 230 — Amending Civil Service Rules Regarding Exceptions from Examination1903President of the United States

Schedule A, of the Civil Service Rules, defining the positions excepted from examination under Rule II, clause 3, is amended by striking out clause 13 of Subdivision VI, Department of the Interior, which reads:

One clerk at each pension agency to act for the agent during his absence or disability.

Theodore Roosevelt.

White House,

August 10, 1903.
