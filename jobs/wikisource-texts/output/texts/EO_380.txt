616421Executive Order 380 — Authorizing Appointment of Mary E. Owings to Position in Government Printing Office Without Examination1905President of the United States

Mrs. Mary E. Owings may be appointed to a position in the Government Printing Office without examination under the civil service rules.

Theodore Roosevelt.

The White House,

December 11, 1905.
