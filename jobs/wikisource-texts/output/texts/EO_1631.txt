1329593Executive Order 1631 — Authorizing Appointment of De Lorma A. Morrow as Special Agent in Bureau of Corporations Without Regard to Civil Service Rules1912President of the United States

Mr. De Lorma A. Morrow may be appointed a special agent in the Bureau of Corporations without regard to the provisions of the civil-service rules.

Wm. H. Taft.

The White House,

October 28, 1912.
