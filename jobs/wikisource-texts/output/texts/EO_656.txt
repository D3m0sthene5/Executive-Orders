1907 CSR 125

625829Executive Order 656 — Authorizing Permanent Appointment of Mary A. Greenstreet as Clerk in War Department Without Regard to Civil Service Rules1907President of the United States

Mrs. Mary A. Greenstreet, now serving under temporary appointment in the War Department by certification from the Civil Service Commission, may be selected from the clerk register of eligibles for permanent appointment.

Theodore Roosevelt.

The White House,

June 18, 1907.
