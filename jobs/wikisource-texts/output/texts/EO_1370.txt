820442Executive Order 1370 — Authorizing Reinstatement of I. C. Faulk as Storekeeper-Gauger in Internal Revenue Service Without Regard to Civil Service Rules1911President of the United States

Mr. I. C. Faulk may be reinstated as storekeeper-gauger in the Internal Revenue Service, twenty-third district of Pennsylvania, without regard to the length of his separation from the service.

Wm. H. Taft.

The White House,

June 9, 1911.
