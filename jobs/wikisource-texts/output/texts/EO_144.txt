Amends Executive Order 119, May 29, 1899.

Signed by President Theodore Roosevelt Monday, November 18, 1901

610556Executive Order 144 — Amending Civil Service Rules to Make Certain United States Army Positions Subject to Classification1901President of the United States

Section 8 of Civil-Service Rule III is hereby amended by striking out all of clauses (g), (h), (i), and (j) thereof and re-lettering the succeeding clauses accordingly.

Approved.

Theodore Roosevelt.

White House,

November 18, 1901.
