Amends Executive Order 94, January 2, 1897.

Signed by President Theodore Roosevelt Tuesday, January 21, 1902

610662Executive Order 156 — Amending Civil Service Rules Regarding Age Limitations in Coast and Geodetic Survey1902President of the United States

Section 4 of Civil-Service Rule V is hereby amended by inserting the following at the end of that part of the tabular statement therein under the heading "Departmental Service:"

Watch officer in the Coast and Geodetic Survey
2136

Chief engineer in the Coast and Geodetic Survey
2545

Also by inserting the following in the tabular statement therein, after the word "aid" in line 11, "deck officer and junior captain's clerk." 

Approved.

Theodore Roosevelt.

White House,

January 21, 1902.
