624794Executive Order 562 — Authorizing Appointment of Mrs. George H. Hildreth as Clerk in Bureau of Pensions Without Examination1907President of the United States

Mrs. George H. Hildreth may be appointed to a clerical position in the Bureau of Pensions without examination under the civil-service rules.

Theodore Roosevelt.

The White House,

February 13, 1907.
