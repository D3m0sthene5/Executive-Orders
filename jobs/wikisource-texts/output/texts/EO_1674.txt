1330279Executive Order 1674 — Authorizing Reinstatement of Ralph D. Converse as Immigrant Inspector in Department of Commerce and Labor Without Regard to Civil Service Rules1913President of the United States

Dr. Ralph D. Converse may be reinstated to the position of immigrant inspector, Department of Commerce and Labor, without reference to the period of his separation from the service.

Wm. H. Taft.

The White House,

January 1, 1913.
