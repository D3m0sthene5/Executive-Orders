Amended Executive Order 3091, May 24, 1919.

Signed by President Woodrow Wilson Tuesday, February 3, 1920

602402Executive Order 3222 — Applying Executive Order No. 3091 of May 24, 1919, to the Panama Canal1920President of the United States

Under authority of the Act of Congress, Chapter 78, Section 2, approved May 20, 1918, Executive Order No. 3091, dated May 24, 1919, is hereby amended so as to include The Panama Canal.

Woodrow Wilson 

The White House,

3 February, 1920.
