650399Executive Order 1278 — Authorizing Appointment of Florence G. Miller as Clerk in Classified Service Without Regard to Civil Service Rules1911President of the United States

Mrs. Florence G. Miller, widow of the late Lieut. Edward Y. Miller, United States Army, may be appointed a clerk in the classified service of the United States, subject to such tests of fitness as the Civil Service Commission may require.

Wm. H. Taft.

The White House,

January 4, 1911.
