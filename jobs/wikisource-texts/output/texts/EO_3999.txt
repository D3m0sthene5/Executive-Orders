Signed by President Calvin Coolidge Friday, April 25, 1924

585742Executive Order 3999 — Withdrawing Lands in Arizona for Classification and Pending Legislation for its Disposition1924President of the United States

It is hereby ordered, under authority of the act of Congress approved June 25, 1910 (36 Stat, 847), as amended by the act of August 24. 1912 (37 Stat. 497), that the following described tract of land in Arizona be, and the same is hereby, temporarily withdrawn subject to the conditions of said acts, for classification and pending enactment of legislation for its proper disposition: In T. 30 N., R. 2 E., G. & S. R. M., E½ SE¼ NE¼ NE¼ and NE¼ SE¼, NE¼ Sec. 11, NW¼ NE¼ NW¼, NE¼ NW¼ NW¼, S½ NW¼ NW¼, NW¼, SW¼ NW¼ and NW¼ NE¼ SW¼ NW¼ Sec. 12. 67.5 acres.

Calvin Coolidge

The White House,

April 25, 1924.
