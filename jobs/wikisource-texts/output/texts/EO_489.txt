1906 CSR 104

619309Executive Order 489 — Authorizing Permanent Appointment of Florence E. Broady in Government Printing Office Without Examination1906President of the United States

Mrs. Florence E. Broady may be permanently appointed to a position in the Government Printing Office without examination under the civil service rules.

Theodore Roosevelt.

The White House,

July 30, 1906.
