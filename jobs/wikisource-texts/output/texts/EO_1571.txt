827032Executive Order 1571 — Authorizing Promotion of Richard T. Underwood to Clerk in Post Office Department Without Examination1912President of the United States

Mr. Richard T. Underwood may be promoted from assistant messenger to clerk in the Post Office Department without examination.

Wm. H. Taft.

The White House,

July 27, 1912.
