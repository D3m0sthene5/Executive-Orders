619415Executive Order 528 — Authorizing Appointment of William W. Gillespie as Deputy Collector in Customs Service Without Examination1906President of the United States

Mr. William W. Gillespie may be appointed as deputy collector in the Customs Service at Stamford, Conn., without examination.

Theodore Roosevelt.

The White House,

December 8, 1906.
