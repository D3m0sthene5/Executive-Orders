606556Executive Order 255 — Authorizing Appointment of Francis Walker as Special Agent in Bureau of Corporations Without Examination1904President of the United States

Mr. Francis Walker, of Massachusetts, may be appointed a special agent in the Bureau of Corporations, at $2,000 per annum, without examination under the Civil-Service rules.

Theodore Roosevelt.

White House,

February 25, 1904.
