1330362Executive Order 1730 — Authorizing Appointment of Col. Heber M. Creel as Supervisor of Indian Schools Without Regard to Civil Service Rules1913President of the United States

Col. Heber M. Creel of North Dakota may be appointed a supervisor of Indian schools without regard to the civil-service rules.

Wm. H. Taft.

The White House,

March 3, 1913.
