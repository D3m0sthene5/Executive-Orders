1330317Executive Order 1698 — Authorizing Appointment of Cyrus F. Adams to Competitive Position Without Regard to Civil Service Rules1913President of the United States

Mr. Cyrus F. Adams may be appointed to a competitive position in the classified civil service without compliance with the civil-service rules.

Wm. H. Taft.

The White House,

February 7, 1913.
