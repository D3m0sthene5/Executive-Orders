1329628Executive Order 1671 — Authorizing Appointment of W. D. Lundy as Clerk Without Regard to Civil Service Rules1912President of the United States

Mr. W. D. Lundy may be appointed to a clerical position without regard to the length of his separation from the service.

Wm. H. Taft.

The White House,

December 31, 1912.
