Signed by President Theodore Roosevelt Thursday, September 4, 1902

611626Executive Order 189 — Authorizing Appointment of David Lynch for Duty in the Signal Service Without Examination Under Civil Service Rules1902President of the United States

David Lynch may be appointed cable engineer for duty in the signal service in Manila, P. I., without examination under the civil-service rules.

Theodore Roosevelt

White House, September 4, 1902.
