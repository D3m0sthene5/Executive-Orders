619426Executive Order 540 — Authorizing Transfer of W. B. Johnson to Clerk in Department of the Interior Without Examination1906President of the United States

Mr. W. B. Johnson may be transferred without examination from the position of compositor in the Government Printing Office to the position of clerk in the Land Service of the Department of the Interior.

Theodore Roosevelt.

The White House,

December 31, 1906.
