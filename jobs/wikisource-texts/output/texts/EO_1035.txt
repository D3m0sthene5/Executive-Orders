Signed by President Theodore Roosevelt Saturday, February 27, 1909

592303Executive Order 1035 — Authorizing Transfer of Roger H. Adams to Competitive Position in United States1909President of the United States

Mr. Roger H. Adams, a clerk in the Isthmian service, may be transferred to a competitive position in the United States, subject to such examination as the Civil Service Commission may prescribe.

Theodore Roosevelt.

The White House

February 27, 1909.
