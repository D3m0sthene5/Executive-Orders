Signed by President Theodore Roosevelt Friday, August 14, 1908

2 Canal Record 11, September 9, 1908.

636459Executive Order 928 — Amending Penal Code of Canal Zone Regarding Punishment for Grand Larceny1908President of the United States

Under authority vested in me by law, it is ordered:

Section 344 of the Penal Code of the Canal Zone is amended to read as follows:

"Section 344. Grand larceny is punishable by imprisonment in the penitentiary not exceeding ten years."

Theodore Roosevelt.

The White House,

Washington, D. C., August 14, 1908.
