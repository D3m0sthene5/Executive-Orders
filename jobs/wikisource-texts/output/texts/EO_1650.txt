1329604Executive Order 1650 — Authorizing Appointment of William R. Carpenter as Clerk in Post Office Department Without Regard to Civil Service Rules1912President of the United States

Mr. William R. Carpenter, of Illinois, may be appointed to a clerical position in the competitive classified service of the Post Office Department.

Wm. H. Taft.

The White House,

November 27, 1912.
