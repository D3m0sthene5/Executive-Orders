Signed by President Theodore Roosevelt Wednesday, February 17, 1909

592299Executive Order 1028 — Authorizing Reinstatement of R. Barnard Talcott as Assistant Engineer in Office of Supervising Architect of the Treasury1909President of the United States

Mr. R. Barnard Talcott maybe reinstated as assistant chief mechanical and electrical engineer, in the office of the Supervising Architect of the Treasury, without compliance with the civil-service rules.

Theodore Roosevelt.

The White House

February 17, 1909.
