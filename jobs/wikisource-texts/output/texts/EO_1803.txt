1913 CSR 113

2000328Executive Order 1803 — Amending Civil Service Rules to Except Private Secretary to Public Printer from Examination1913President of the United States

Schedule A, Subdivision I, paragraph 1 of the civil-service rules excepting positions named from the requirement of examination is hereby amended by the addition of the following words:

* * * and one to the Public Printer.

As amended the exception contained in paragraph 1 will read as follows:

Two private secretaries or confidential clerks to the head of each of the executive departments and one to each assistant head and one to the Public Printer.

Woodrow Wilson.

The White House

July 23, 1913.
