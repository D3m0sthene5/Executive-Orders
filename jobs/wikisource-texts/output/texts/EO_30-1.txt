Signed by President Grover Cleveland Friday, June 16, 1893

609873Executive Order 30-1 — Transferring Functions of Preparing and Recording Pardons to the Department of Justice1893President of the United States

I hereby direct that all warrants of pardons and commutations of sentences heretofore prepared at the Department of State on the requisition of the Attorney General, be prepared and recorded in the Department of Justice under the seal of that Department and countersigned by the Attorney General.

Grover Cleveland.

Executive Mansion,

Washington, June 16, 1893.
