External Information about this EO  U.S. Official Bulletin No. 13, p. 4, May 24, 1917

550949Executive Order 2621 — German Boats1917President of the United States

In accordance with a request of the Secretary of the Navy, concurred in by the United States Shipping Board, the Secretary of the Treasury is hereby directed to transfer the German vessel Atlas, now at San Francisco, to the commandant of the twelfth naval district, for use in harbor patrol.

Woodrow Wilson 

The White House,

May 16, 1917.
