Signed by President Theodore Roosevelt Friday, March 8, 1907

1907 CSR 117

625553Executive Order 583 — Authorizing Appointment of C. F. House as Clerk in Naval Training Station, North Chicago, Illinois, Without Regard to Civil Service Rules1907President of the United States

Mr. C. F. House may be appointed as clerk at $1,000 per annum, Naval Training Station, Great Lakes, North Chicago, Ill., without compliance with the civil-service rules.

Theodore Roosevelt.

The White House,

March 8, 1907.
