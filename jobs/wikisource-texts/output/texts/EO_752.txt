626825Executive Order 752 — Authorizing Reinstatement and Transfer of Douglas B. Thompson to Departmental Service Without Regard to Civil Service Rules1908President of the United States

Mr. Douglas B. Thompson, who was separated from the service on the Isthmus of Panama by reason of illness, may be reinstated with the privilege of transfer to the departmental service if it should appear that any Department desires to appoint him in spite of his physical disablement.

Theodore Roosevelt.

The White House,

February 7, 1908.
