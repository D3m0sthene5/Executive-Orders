636230Executive Order 957 — Authorizing Appointment of Conrad Henzman as Messenger in New York Military Headquarters Without Examination1908President of the United States

Mr. Conrad Henzman, a retired first sergeant, with thirty years' experience in the army, may be appointed without examination as a messenger at military headquarters, New York City.

Theodore Roosevelt.

The White House,

October 23, 1908.
