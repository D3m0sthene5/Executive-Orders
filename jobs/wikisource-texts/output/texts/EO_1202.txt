648578Executive Order 1202 — Authorizing Appointment of French Government Engineers in Engineer Department at Large Without Examination1910President of the United States

The engineers detailed by the French Government to observe certain public works in the United States may be employed as junior engineers in the Engineer Department at Large, without examination under civil-service rules.

Wm. H. Taft.

The White House,

May 6, 1910.
