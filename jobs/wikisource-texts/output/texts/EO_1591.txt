827046Executive Order 1591 — Authorizing Retention of William W. Dyar in Forest Service With Alternate Appropriation1912President of the United States

Mr. William W. Dyar, an expert in an excepted position in the Forest Service, Department of Agriculture, may be retained in the service with his present excepted status and paid from the appropriation for law clerks.

Wm. H. Taft.

The White House,

August 26, 1912.
