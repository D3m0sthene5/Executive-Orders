649159Executive Order 1191 — Authorizing Appointment of Collinson Lewis Warwick as Nautical Expert in Coast and Geodetic Survey Without Examination1910President of the United States

Mr. Collinson Lewis Warwick may be appointed nautical expert in the Coast and Geodetic Survey without examination under the civil-service rules, it appearing that his services are essential in the prosecution of the work of the Coast Pilot Division.

Wm. H. Taft.

The White House,

April 18, 1910.
