613490Executive Order 215 — Authorizing Appointment of Albert U. Wyman as Clerk in Office of the Treasurer Without Examination1903President of the United States

White House, April 29, 1903.

Mr. Albert U. Wyman may be appointed clerk at $1,600 per annum in the office of the Treasurer of the United States without examination under the civil-service rules.

Theodore Roosevelt.
