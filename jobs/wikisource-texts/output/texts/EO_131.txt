Amends Executive Order 119, May 19, 1899.

Signed by President William McKinley Friday, June 29, 1900

610454Executive Order 131 — Amending Civil Service Rules to Except all Paymasters' Clerks from Examination1900President of the United States

Rule VI is hereby amended as follows:

Strike out section 27 and insert in lieu thereof the following:

All paymasters' clerks.

Approved.

William McKinley.

Executive Mansion,

June 29, 1900.
