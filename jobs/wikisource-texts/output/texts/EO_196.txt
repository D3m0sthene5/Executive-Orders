613498Executive Order 196 — Authorizing Appointment of Charles W. Newton as Physician in Western Navajo Agency Without Examination1902President of the United States

White House, December 27, 1902.

Dr. Charles W. Newton may be permanently appointed physician at the Western Navajo Agency, Ariz., without examination other than such evidence of his qualifications as a practicing physician as may be satisfactory to the United States Civil Service Commission.

Theodore Roosevelt.
