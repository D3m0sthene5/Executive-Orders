827025Executive Order 1569 — Authorizing Reinstatement of Isaac D. Laferty in Bureau of Pensions Without Regard to Civil Service Rules1912President of the United States

Mr. Isaac D. Laferty may be reinstated in the Bureau of Pensions, Department of the Interior, without reference to the time limit of eligibility for reinstatement under the civil-service rules.

Wm. H. Taft.

The White House,

July 23, 1912.
