Signed by President William H. Taft Tuesday, June 7, 1910

656081Executive Order 1208 — Directing Officials in all Branches of Government to Provide Tariff Board Access to Needed Information1910President of the United States

To facilitate the investigations being conducted by the Tariff Board, and to avoid unnecessary duplication of work, officials in all branches of the Government service are hereby directed to accord to the members of said Board or its duly accredited representatives, access to any appropriate data or information in their possession needed by said Board in the discharge of its duties.

Wm. H. Taft.

The White House,

June 7, 1910.
