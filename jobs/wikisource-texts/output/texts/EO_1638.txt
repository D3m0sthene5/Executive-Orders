1329599Executive Order 1638 — Authorizing Appointment of Mrs. Robert L. Bowman as Matron in Indian Service Without Examination1912President of the United States

Mrs. Robert L. Bowman may be appointed to the position of matron in the Indian Service without examination under the civil-service rules.

Wm. H. Taft.

The White House,

November 1, 1912.
