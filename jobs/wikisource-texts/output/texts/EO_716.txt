Signed by President Theodore Roosevelt Thursday, November 28, 1907

General Order No. 57, Navy Dept., December 9, 1907

628912Executive Order 716 — Adjusting Rules for Extra Pay for Navy Gun Pointers1907President of the United States

The White House,

November 28, 1907.

The extra pay allowed gun pointers in accordance with the Executive order of July 25, 1903, shall be allowed a gun pointer regularly detailed as a gun pointer at a gun of the class at which he qualified only as long as he remains qualified: Provided, This order shall be construed as affecting only gun pointers who qualify subsequent to the date of this order.

Theodore Roosevelt.
