Amends Executive Order 253 , February 17, 1904. This paragraph formerly excepted only commissioners. Amended by Executive Order 1189, April 12, 1910.

Signed by President William H. Taft Tuesday, February 8, 1910

591664Executive Order 1163 — Amending Civil Service Rules to Except Statutory Places of Secretary in National Military Parks from Examination1910President of the United States

Schedule A, Section IV, paragraph 5, of positions under the War Department excepted from examination under Rule II, clause 3, is hereby amended to read as follows:

All commissioners and statutory places of secretary for the national military parks.

Wm. H. Taft.

The White House,

February 8, 1910.
