Executive Order 3154 (1919)

by President of the United States
Dissolving Committee on Public Information and Transferring Assets to Council of National Defense

Executive Order 3155

Obsoletes Executive Order 2594 of April 14, 1917, which created the Committee on Public Information.

Signed by President Woodrow Wilson Thursday, August 21, 1919

596932Executive Order 3154 — Dissolving Committee on Public Information and Transferring Assets to Council of National Defense1919President of the United States

Under the provisions of section 4 of the act making appropriations for sundry civil expenses of the Government for the fiscal year ending June 30, 1920, and for other purposes, approved July 19, 1919, it is hereby ordered and directed that the chairman of the Committee on Public Information relinquish and transfer to the Council of National Defense all papers, files, records, public property, assets, and liabilities, and all effects of whatsoever kind, and that upon such relinquishment and transfer the Committee on Public Information shall cease and be at an end.

Woodrow Wilson.

The White House,

August 21, 1919.
