Signed by President William H. Taft Friday, April 16, 1909

645365Executive Order 1064 — Authorizing Appointment of Frances M. Marsh as Clerk in State Department Without Examination1909President of the United States

Miss Frances M. Marsh, of Pennsylvania, may be appointed to a clerkship in the Department of State, without examination under the civil-service act and rules. This order is made at the request and upon recommendation of the Secretary of State, who has knowledge of Miss Marsh's fitness from her clerical services under him while a Senator in Congress.

Wm. H. Taft.

The White House,

April 16, 1909.
