826990Executive Order 1542 — Authorizing Reinstatement of Arthur S. Blum as Photographer in Forest Service Without Regard to Civil Service Rules1912President of the United States

Mr. Arthur S. Blum may be reinstated as photographer in the Forest Service, Washington, D. C, without regard to the length of his separation from the service.

Wm. H. Taft.

The White House,

June 5, 1912.
