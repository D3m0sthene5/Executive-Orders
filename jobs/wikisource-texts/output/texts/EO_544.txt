619434Executive Order 544 — Authorizing Promotion of Marshall M. Pool to Clerk in United States Army Office Without Examination1907President of the United States

Mr. Marshall M. Pool may be promoted to a clerical position in the office of the purchasing commissary, United States Army, at Kansas City, Mo., without examination.

Theodore Roosevelt.

The White House,

January 12, 1907.
