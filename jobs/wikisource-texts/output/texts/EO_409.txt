Indian Key National Wildlife Refuge was revoked on June 26, 1950.

Signed by President Theodore Roosevelt Saturday, February 10, 1906

620125Executive Order 409 — Establishing Indian Key Reservation, Tampa Bay, Florida as Preserve and Breeding Ground for Birds1906President of the United States

It is hereby ordered that Indian Key, an island in Tampa bay, Florida, containing ninety acres, and located in sections 10 and 15, township 32 south, range 16 east, as the same appears upon the official plat of survey of said township on file in the General Land Office, be, and it is hereby reserved and set apart for the use of the Department of Agriculture, as a preserve and breeding ground for native birds. This reservation to be known as Indian Key Reservation.

Theodore Roosevelt.

The White House,

February 10, 1906.
