Signed by President Theodore Roosevelt Thursday, June 18, 1908

658459Executive Order 820 — Establishing Columbia National Forest in Washington from Portion of Rainier National Forest1908President of the United States

It is hereby ordered that on and after July 1, 1908, the land within the boundary shown on the attached diagram heretofore set apart, reserved, and proclaimed as part of the 
Rainier National Forest, shall be known as the Columbia National Forest. It is not intended by this order to release any land from reservation or to reserve any land not heretofore embraced in a National Forest.

Theodore Roosevelt.

The White House,

June 18, 1908.
