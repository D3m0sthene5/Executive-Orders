Executive Order 538 (1906)

by President of the United States
Amending Tariff of Consular Fees to Exempt Affidavit Fees for Owners of Sealskin Garments Entering from Canada

Amends Executive Order 496, August 13, 1906. Exemption discontinued by Executive Order 5110, May 13, 1929.

Signed by President Theodore Roosevelt Friday, December 21, 1906

State Department Circular, December 22, 1906

631662Executive Order 538 — Amending Tariff of Consular Fees to Exempt Affidavit Fees for Owners of Sealskin Garments Entering from Canada1906President of the United States

The following is a description by the Department of State of the change defined in the order, not the actual order text.

Fee No. 31 of Tariff of Fees amended by Executive order of December 21, 1906, by addition of the following words:

"No fee for the affidavit of temporary stay of owner of sealskin garment entering the United States from Canada."
