Signed by President Theodore Roosevelt Friday, May 12, 1905

1905 CSR 87

615386Executive Order 323 — Authorizing Appointment of Carriage Driver for Treasury Secretary Without Examination1905President of the United States

An appointment to the vacancy now existing in the position of laborer in the Bureau of Engraving and Printing for assignment to the duties of driver of the carriage of the Secretary of the Treasury may be made without examination under the civil service rules or labor regulations.

Theodore Roosevelt.

White House,

May 12, 1905.
