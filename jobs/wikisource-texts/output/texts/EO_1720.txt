1330344Executive Order 1720 — Authorizing Reinstatement of Ralph Hoagland as Laboratory Inspector in Bureau of Animal Industry Without Regard to Civil Service Rules1913President of the United States

Mr. Ralph Hoagland may be reinstated to the position of laboratory inspector in the Bureau of Animal Industry, Department of Agriculture, without regard to the length of time he has been separated from the service.

Wm. H. Taft.

The White House,

February 26, 1913.
