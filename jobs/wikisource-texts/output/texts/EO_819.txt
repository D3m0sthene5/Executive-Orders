636192Executive Order 819 — Authorizing Appointment of Andrew Christensen as Special Agent in General Land Office Without Regard to Civil Service Rules1908President of the United States

Mr. Andrew Christensen, a former employee of the classified service, may be appointed as a special agent of the General Land Office without reference to the limitations of the reinstatement and transfer rules.

Theodore Roosevelt.

The White House,

June 15, 1908.
