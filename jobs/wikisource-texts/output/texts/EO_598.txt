625581Executive Order 598 — Authorizing Reinstatement of Mahlon Pursel as Clerk in War Department Without Regard to Civil Service Rules1907President of the United States

Mr. Mahlon Pursel may be reinstated as a clerk in the Adjutant-General's Office, War Department, without reference to the time limit of eligibility for reinstatement under the civil-service rules.

Theodore Roosevelt.

The White House,

March 23, 1907.
