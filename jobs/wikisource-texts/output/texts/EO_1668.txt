1329622Executive Order 1668 — Authorizing Transfer of Horace P. De Hart to Position in Interior Department Without Regard to Civil Service Rules1912President of the United States

Mr. Horace P. De Hart may be transferred from the excepted position of confidential clerk to the Assistant Secretary of the Interior to an appropriate competitive position in that department.

Wm. H. Taft.

The White House,

December 19, 1912.
