618918Executive Order 406 — Authorizing Appointment of Charles H. Ingram as Clerk in War Department Without Examination1906President of the United States

Charles H. Ingram, who resigned his position in the Treasury Department on August 7, 1903, may be appointed to a clerical position in the War Department without examination under the civil-service rules.

Theodore Roosevelt.

The White House,

February 3, 1906.
