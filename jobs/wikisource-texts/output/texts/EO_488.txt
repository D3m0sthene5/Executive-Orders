1906 CSR 104

619306Executive Order 488 — Authorizing Appointment of Tony Verrosso as Unclassified Laborer Without Regard to Laborer Regulations1906President of the United States

Mr. Tony Verrosso, a veteran of the war with Spain, and eligible under the labor regulations, may be appointed an unclassified laborer, without regard to the order of certification prescribed by the regulations.

Theodore Roosevelt.

The White House,

July 30, 1906.
