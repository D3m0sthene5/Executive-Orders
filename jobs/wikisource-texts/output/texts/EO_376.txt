616386Executive Order 376 — Authorizing Appointment of Edna M. Peltz as Clerk in Pension Office Without Examination1905President of the United States

Miss Edna M. Peltz may be permanently appointed to a clerical position in the Pension Office without examination under the civil service rules.

Theodore Roosevelt.

The White House,

December 6, 1905.
