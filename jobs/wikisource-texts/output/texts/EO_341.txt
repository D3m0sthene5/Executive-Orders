1905 CSR 90

616109Executive Order 341 — Authorizing Employment of Allen Hazen as Engineer in Washington Filtration Plant for Seven Additional Months1905President of the United States

The temporary employment of Mr. Allen Hazen as expert consulting engineer at $275 per month, in connection with the constructing and initial operation of the Washington filtration plant, may be continued for such period of seven months from August 31, 1905, as may be necessary for the initial operation of that plant.

Theodore Roosevelt.

White House,

July 13, 1905.
