613488Executive Order 212 — Authorizing Transfer of Exum L. Holland to Inspector in Customs Service Without Regard to Civil Service Rules1903President of the United States

White House, March 26, 1903.

Mr. Exum L. Holland may be transferred from the position of clerk in the Railway Mail Service to that of night inspector in the Customs Service at the port of New Orleans, La., notwithstanding the provision of section 2 of Rule X limiting transfers to positions in which the same line of work is performed.

Theodore Roosevelt.
