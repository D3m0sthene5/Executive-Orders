Amends Executive Order 1096, June 25, 1909.

Signed by President William H. Taft Friday, July 16, 1909

645537Executive Order 1111 — Ordering that Executive Orders of June 8 and June 25, 1909, are Not Applicable on the Isthmus of Panama1909President of the United States

It is hereby ordered that Executive Order No. 1084, made June 8, 1909, and Executive Order No. 1096, made June 25, 1909, shall have no application to employes upon the Isthmus of Panama.

Wm. H. Taft.

The White House,

July 16, 1909.
