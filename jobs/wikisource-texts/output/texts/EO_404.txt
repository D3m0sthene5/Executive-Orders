618913Executive Order 404 — Authorizing Appointment of Charles S. Brown as Inspector in Government Printing Office Subject to Noncompetitive Examination1906President of the United States

Mr. Charles S. Brown may be appointed as an inspector in the Government Printing Office, subject to his passing a noncompetitive examination before the Civil Service Commission.

Theodore Roosevelt.

The White House,

January 26, 1906.
