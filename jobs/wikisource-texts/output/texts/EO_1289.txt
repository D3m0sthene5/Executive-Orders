Amends Executive Order 1268, December 2, 1910. Amended by Executive Order 1307, February 21, 1911.

Signed by President William H. Taft Thursday, January 26, 1911

1910 CSR 109

648568Executive Order 1289 — Amending Civil Service Rules to Except Navy Positions on Guam from Examination1911President of the United States

Schedule A of classified positions excepted from examination under Civil Service Rule II, clause 3, is hereby amended by the addition of a new paragraph to Subdivision V, Navy Department, to be numbered 3, and to read as follows:

All positions in the island of Guam.

Wm. H. Taft.

The White House,

January 26, 1911.
