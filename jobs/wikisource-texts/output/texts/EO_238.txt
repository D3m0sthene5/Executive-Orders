Signed by President Theodore Roosevelt Monday, November 2, 1903

606411Executive Order 238 — Authorizing Appointment of Mrs. Roy L. Quackenbush as Clerk in Post Office Department Without Examination1903President of the United States

Mrs. Roy L. Quackenbush, who has been employed temporarily as a clerk in the Post-Office Department, may be given permanent appointment without examination.

Theodore Roosevelt.

White House,

November 2, 1903.
