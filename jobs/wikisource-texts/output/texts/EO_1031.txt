Signed by President Theodore Roosevelt Thursday, February 25, 1909

592301Executive Order 1031 — Authorizing Employment of F. N. Clark in Interstate Commerce Commission1909President of the United States

Mr. F. N. Clark may be employed as a clerk in the Interstate Commerce Commission without reference to the civil-service rules.

Theodore Roosevelt.

The White House

February 25, 1909.
