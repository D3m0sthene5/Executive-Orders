1910 CSR 116

650411Executive Order 1310 — Authorizing Reinstatement of Nathan C. Grover as Chief Engineer in United States Geological Survey Without Regard to Civil Service Rules1911President of the United States

Mr. Nathan C. Grover may be reinstated as chief engineer of the land classification board of the United States Geological Survey without regard to the length of time that he has been separated from the service.

Wm. H. Taft.

The White House,

February 27, 1911.
