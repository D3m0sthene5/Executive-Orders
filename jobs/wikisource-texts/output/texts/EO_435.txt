619105Executive Order 435 — Authorizing Appointment of Charles P. Galpin as Classified Laborer in Bureau of Pensions Without Examination1906President of the United States

Mr. Charles P. Galpin may be appointed to the position of classified laborer in the Bureau of Pensions without examination under the civil-service rules.

Theodore Roosevelt.

The White House,

March 31, 1906.
