649809Executive Order 1263 — Authorizing Permanent Appointment of William J. Meyers as Statistician in Interstate Commerce Commission Without Examination1910President of the United States

Mr. William J. Meyers may be permanently appointed as a statistician, at $5,000 per annum, in the Interstate Commerce Commission, without examination under the civil-service rules.

Wm. H. Taft.

The White House,

November 4, 1910.
