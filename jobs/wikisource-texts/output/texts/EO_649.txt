Revoked by Executive Order 673, August 3, 1907.

Signed by President Theodore Roosevelt Tuesday, June 11, 1907

1907 CSR 124

625821Executive Order 649 — Authorizing Transfer of George S. Fox to Clerical Position in United States Without Regard to Civil Service Rules1907President of the United States

Mr. George S. Fox, bookkeeper at $1,500 per annum in the Isthmian service, may be transferred to a classified clerical position in the United States.

Theodore Roosevelt.

The White House,

June 11, 1907.
