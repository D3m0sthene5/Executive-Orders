1910 CSR 107

648531Executive Order 1179 — Amending Civil Service Rules To Except Temporary Histopathologist from Examination1910President of the United States

Schedule A, Subdivision VII, of the civil-service rules, relating to the Department of the Interior, is hereby amended by the addition of the following paragraph:

One histopathologist temporarily engaged in research work at the Government Hospital for the Insane.

Wm. H. Taft.

The White House,

March 23, 1910.
