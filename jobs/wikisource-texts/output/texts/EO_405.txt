Signed by President Theodore Roosevelt Thursday, February 1, 1906

1906 CSR 97

618916Executive Order 405 — Authorizing Reinstatement of Mary H. Donnelly as Clerk in Office of Chief of Ordnance Without Regard to Civil Service Rules1906President of the United States

Miss Mary H. Donnelly may be reinstated as a clerk in the office of the Chief of Ordnance without reference to the year limit contained in the reinstatement rule.

Theodore Roosevelt.

The White House,

February 1, 1906.
