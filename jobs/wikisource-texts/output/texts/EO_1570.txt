827028Executive Order 1570 — Authorizing Reinstatement of James D. Goldsby in Classified Service Without Regard to Civil Service Rules1912President of the United States

Mr. James D. Goldsby, who held a competitive classified position as superintendent of Westport Station in the post office at Kansas City, Mo., and other assignments in the Kansas City post office from July 2, 1900, to April 18, 1910, when he resigned, may be reinstated without reference to the time limit upon eligibility for reinstatement.

Wm. H. Taft.

The White House,

July 25, 1912.
