636236Executive Order 968 — Authorizing Appointment of Hilworth T. B. Jones as Translator in Office of Naval Intelligence Without Regard to Civil Service Rules1908President of the United States

Mr. Hilworth T. B. Jones may be appointed as translator at $1,400 per annum, Office of Naval Intelligence, Navy Department.

Theodore Roosevelt.

The White House,

November 11, 1908.
