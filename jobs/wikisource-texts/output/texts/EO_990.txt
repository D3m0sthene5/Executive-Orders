Amended Executive Order 772, March 21, 1908.

Signed by President Theodore Roosevelt Thursday, December 10, 1908

591988Executive Order 990 — Authorizing Appointment of Wilhelm Lutz to Position in Customs Service1908President of the United States

The executive order of March 21, 1908, permitting the appointment of Mr. Wilhelm Lutz, assistant chief of police of Porto Rico, to a position in the Immigration Bureau of the United States, is hereby amended to permit his appointment to a position in the Customs Service of the United States.

Theodore Roosevelt.

The White House

December 10, 1908.
