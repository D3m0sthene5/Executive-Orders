649818Executive Order 1273 — Authorizing Reinstatement of Edward K. Ketchum as Letter Carrier in Philadelphia Post Office Without Regard to Civil Service Rules1910President of the United States

Mr. Edward K. Ketchum may be reinstated as a letter carrier in the Philadelphia, Pa., post office, without reference to the year limitation of the civil-service rules.

Wm. H. Taft.

The White House,

December 16, 1910.
