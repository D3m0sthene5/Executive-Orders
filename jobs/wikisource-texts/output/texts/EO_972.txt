636238Executive Order 972 — Authorizing Reinstatement of George F. Cooke as Clerk in Post Office Without Regard to Civil Service Rules1908President of the United States

Mr. George F. Cooke may be reinstated to a clerkship of not exceeding $1,000 in any post-office, without reference to the time limitation of the reinstatement rule.

Theodore Roosevelt.

The White House,

November 13, 1908.
