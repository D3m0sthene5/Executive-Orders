Amends Executive Order 1353, May 11, 1911.

Signed by President William H. Taft Monday, June 12, 1911

IV PCR 347, June 28, 1911

1326403Executive Order 1372 — Amending Effective Date of Collection of Distillation Tax1911President of the United States

By virtue of the authority vested in me, the order of May 13, 1911, providing 
for the collection of a distillation tax in the Canal Zone, is hereby amended to as to be 
effective August 13, 1911.

Wm. H. Taft.

The White House, June 12, 1911.
