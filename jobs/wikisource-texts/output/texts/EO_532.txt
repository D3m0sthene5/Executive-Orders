619422Executive Order 532 — Authorizing Transfer of Kate Jones to Clerk in Department of the Interior Without Examination1906President of the United States

Mrs. Kate Jones, of Tennessee, may be transferred from classified laborer with clerical duties in the Patent Office, Department of the Interior, to clerk in the Knoxville pension agency under the same Department without examination.

Theodore Roosevelt.

The White House,

December 14, 1906.
