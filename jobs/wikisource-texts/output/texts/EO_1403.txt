Signed by President William H. Taft Tuesday, August 22, 1911

1912 CSR 142

826625Executive Order 1403 — Authorizing Appointment of Edwin L. Williams as Clerk in Post Office Department Without Regard to Civil Service Rules1911President of the United States

Mr. Edwin L. Williams, formerly clerk to the Committee on the Post Office and Post Roads of the House of Representatives, may be appointed to a clerical position in the Post Office Department.

Wm. H. Taft.

The White House,

August 22, 1911.
