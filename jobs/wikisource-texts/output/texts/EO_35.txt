Signed by President Grover Cleveland Friday, November 2, 1894

609567Executive Order 35 — Amending Civil Service Rules Regarding Exceptions from Examination in Bureau of Statistics1894President of the United States

Special departmental rule No. 1 is hereby amended by adding to the places therein excepted from examination in the Department of the Treasury the following:

In the Bureau of Statistics: One expert in mechanical designs and in diagraming commercial and financial facts.

Approved November 2, 1894.

Grover Cleveland.
