Revokes Executive Order 562, February 13, 1907.

Signed by President Theodore Roosevelt Saturday, February 16, 1907

1907 CSR 115

624796Executive Order 564 — Authorizing Appointment of Mrs. George H. Hildreth as Clerk in Classified Service Without Examination1907President of the United States

Mrs. George H. Hildreth may be appointed to a clerical position in the classified service without examination under the civil-service rules.

The Executive order of February 13, permitting Mrs. Hildreth's appointment in the Bureau of Pensions is revoked.

Theodore Roosevelt.

The White House,

February 16, 1907.
