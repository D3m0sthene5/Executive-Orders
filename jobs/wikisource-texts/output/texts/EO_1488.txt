826950Executive Order 1488 — Authorizing Reinstatement of Edward W. Cady as Examiner in Patent Office Without Regard to Civil Service Rules1912President of the United States

Mr. Edward W. Cady may be reinstated as an examiner in the Patent Office, the year limit upon eligibility for reinstatement being waived in his case.

Wm. H. Taft.

The White House,

February 23, 1912.
