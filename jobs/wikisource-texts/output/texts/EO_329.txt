615719Executive Order 329 — Authorizing Appointment of Albert C. Muhse as Special Agent in Bureau of Corporations Without Examination1905President of the United States

Mr. Albert C. Muhse may be appointed special agent of the Bureau of Corporations, Department of Commerce and Labor, at a salary of $2,000 per annum, without examination under the civil service rules.

Theodore Roosevelt.

White House,

June 3, 1905.
