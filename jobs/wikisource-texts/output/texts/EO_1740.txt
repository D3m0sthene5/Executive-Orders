1330358Executive Order 1740 — Authorizing Appointment of Catherine Branan as Charwoman in Treasury Department Without Regard to Civil Service Rules1913President of the United States

Mrs. Catherine Branan may be appointed a charwoman in the Treasury Department without reference to the civil-service rules.

Wm. H. Taft.

The White House,

March 3, 1913.
