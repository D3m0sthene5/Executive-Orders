Amends Executive Order 1002 of January 6, 1909. Amended by Executive Order 1285, January 16, 1911.

Signed by President William H. Taft Friday, June 11, 1909

2 Canal Record 347, June 30, 1909

566366Executive Order 1085 — Amending Executive Order Providing that Railroad Cars in Canal Zone Be Equipped with Safety Appliances1909President of the United States

Under authority vested in me by law, it is ordered:

Paragraph 6 of Executive Order No. 1002, dated January 6, 1909, is hereby amended as follows:

This order shall take effect not later than six months from the date of the promulgation thereof, except for the use of old French dump cars in use on the Panama Canal, including also the couplers of locomotives necessary to operate them, until July 1, 1910, after which date it shall likewise apply to this equipment.

Wm. H. Taft.

The White House,

June 11, 1909.
