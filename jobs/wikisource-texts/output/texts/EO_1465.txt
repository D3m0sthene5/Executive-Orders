Amended by Executive Order 2699, September 17, 1917, and Executive Order 2809, February 15, 1918

Signed by President William H. Taft Wednesday, January 17, 1912

584161Executive Order 1465 — Reserving Land in Utah for School, Agency, and Other Uses for Benefit of Indians1912President of the United States

It is hereby ordered that the SW. ¼ of the NE. ¼ and the NW. ¼ of the SE. ¼ of sec. 11, T. 5 S., R. 8 W., Salt Lake meridian, in the county of Tooele, State of Utah, be, and the same hereby are, reserved from settlement, entry, sale, or other disposal and set aside for school, agency, and other necessary uses for the benefit of Indians on the public domain in the State of Utah, subject, however, to any valid existing rights of any persons thereto.

Wm. H. Taft.

The White House,

January 17, 1912.
