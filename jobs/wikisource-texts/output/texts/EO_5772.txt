625717Executive Order 5772 — Modifying Executive Order No. 5327 of April 15, 1930 to Permit for a Railroad Right-of-way Across Lands Previously Withdrawn Temporarily in Utah1932President of the United States

Upon recommendation of the Secretary of the Interior, Executive Order No. 5327 of April 15, 1930, temporarily withdrawing certain lands in Utah and other States on account of their possible oil-shale value, is hereby modified to the extent of authorizing the Secretary to approve application Salt Lake City 049347 of the Uintah Railway Co. for a railroad right of way traversing certain of the withdrawn lands, the application having been filed pursuant to the act of March 3, 1875 (18 Stat. 482-483).

Herbert Hoover

The White House,

January 11, 1932.
