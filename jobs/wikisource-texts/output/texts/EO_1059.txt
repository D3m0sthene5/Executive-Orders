592316Executive Order 1059 — Authorizing Reinstatement of Nannie M. Wayland to Position in Classified Service in Treasury Department1909President of the United States

Mrs. Nannie M. Wayland may be reinstated in the classified service in the Treasury Department without regard to the length of tune that she has been separated from the service.

Wm. H. Taft.

The White House,

April 7, 1909.
