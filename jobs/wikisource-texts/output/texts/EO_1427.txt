Signed by President William H. Taft Saturday, October 28, 1911

1912 CSR 142

826629Executive Order 1427 — Authorizing Reinstatement of Edwin H. Peery as Clerk in Office of the Judge Advocate General Without Regard to Civil Service Rules1911President of the United States

Mr. Edwin H. Peery may be reinstated to the position of clerk, class 4, in the Office of the Judge Advocate General without regard to the length of his separation from the service. 

Wm. H. Taft.

The White House,

October 28, 1911.
