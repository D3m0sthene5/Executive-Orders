606614Executive Order 265 — Authorizing Appointment of Clarence Reeder as Secretary to the Director of the Geological Survey Without Examination1904President of the United States

Clarence Reeder may be appointed as private secretary to the Director of the Geological Survey, without examination under the civil-service rules.

Theodore Roosevelt.

White House,

June 24, 1904.
