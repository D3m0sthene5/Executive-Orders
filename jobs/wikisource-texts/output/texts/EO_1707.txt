John Fitzgerald was a winner of the Congressional Medal of Honor.

Signed by President William H. Taft Thursday, February 20, 1913

1913 CSR 123

1330321Executive Order 1707 — Authorizing Promotion and Appointment of John Fitzgerald as Merchandise Sampler in Treasury Department Without Regard to Civil Service Rules1913President of the United States

The Secretary of the Treasury is hereby authorized to promote and appoint Mr. John Fitzgerald, now an opener and packer in the office of the appraiser of merchandise at New York, to the position of merchandise sampler in the same office, without compliance with the civil-service rules.

Wm. H. Taft.

The White House,

February 20, 1913.
