Amends Executive Order 119, May 29, 1899.

Signed by President William McKinley Tuesday, June 6, 1899

610417Executive Order 120 — Amending Civil Service Rules to Reinsert Position Excepted from Examination1899President of the United States

Rule VI is hereby amended by inserting after clause No. 33 the following as clause No. 34 and renumbering the succeeding clauses to the end:

One assistant postmaster, or the chief assistant to the postmaster of whatever designation, at each post-office.

Approved.

William McKinley.

Executive Mansion,

June 6, 1899.

Note.—This clause was in the draft of amendments to the civil-service rules of May 29, 1899, and was intended to be approved by me, but was omitted inadvertently in the copy sent to the printer. This exception had existed prior to that date.
