649799Executive Order 1235 — Authorizing Reinstatement of Robert T. Frazier in Patent Office Without Regard to Civil Service Rules1910President of the United States

Mr. Robert T. Frazier may be reinstated in the examining corps of the Patent Office without regard to the length of time he has been separated from the service.

Wm. H. Taft.

The White House,

August 8, 1910.
