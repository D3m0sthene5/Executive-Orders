Signed by President Theodore Roosevelt Tuesday, February 19, 1907

1907 CSR 115

625521Executive Order 566 — Authorizing Appointment of Robert L. Slagle as Clerk in Post Office Department Without Regard to Civil Service Rules1907President of the United States

Mr. Robert L. Slagle, of Tennessee, who is now serving as an assistant messenger in the Post-Office Department, may be appointed to a clerical position on the permanent roll of that Department.

Theodore Roosevelt.

The White House,

February 19, 1907.
