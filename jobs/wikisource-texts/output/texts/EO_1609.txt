827058Executive Order 1609 — Authorizing Reinstatement of George S. Livingston in Pension Office Without Regard to Civil Service Rules1912President of the United States

Mr. George S. Livingston may be reinstated to an appropriate position in the Pension Office without regard to the length of his separation from the service.

Wm. H. Taft.

The White House,

September 17, 1912.
