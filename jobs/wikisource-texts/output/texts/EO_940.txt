636216Executive Order 940 — Authorizing Appointment of Albert W. Pontius as Clerk in Department of State Without Examination1908President of the United States

Mr. Albert W. Pontius, of Minnesota, may be appointed a clerk, class three, in the Department of State without examination under the civil-service rules.

Theodore Roosevelt.

The White House,

September 25, 1908.
