Amends Executive Order 209, March 20, 1903.

Signed by President Theodore Roosevelt Saturday, September 29, 1906

1906 CSR 94

618575Executive Order 508 — Amending Civil Service Rules to Except Private Secretary in Customs Office at Port of New York from Examination1906President of the United States

Schedule A of the civil-service rules is hereby amended by adding to Section II, Treasury Department, paragraph 2, the following:

One private secretary in the office of the naval officer of customs at the port of New York.

Theodore Roosevelt.

The White House,

September 29, 1906.
