Amends Executive Order 209, March 20, 1904. Amended by Executive Order 289, January 5, 1905. Superseded by Executive Order 394, January 12, 1906.

Signed by President Theodore Roosevelt Tuesday, November 15, 1904

614004Executive Order 273 — Amending Civil Service Rules Regarding Positions Excepted from Examination for Isthmian Canal Commission Personnel1904President of the United States

Schedule A of the civil-service rules is hereby amended by adding at the end thereof a new section, reading:

VIII.—Isthmian Canal Commission.

Secretary.

Assistant secretary.

Executive secretary to the governor of the Canal Zone.

Chief clerk.

Treasurer.

One deputy treasurer.

One chief of materials and supplies.

Auditor.

Two deputy auditors.

One disbursing officer for the Canal Zone.

One collector of revenue.

Chief engineer of the Commission.

Division engineers.

Resident engineers.

Hospital attendants.

Chief sanitary officer.

Assistant sanitary officers.

Director of hospitals.

Superintendents of canal hospitals, one for each hospital.

Chief quarantine officer.

Two assistant quarantine officers.

Sanitary officer at Colon.

Sanitary officer at Panama.

Theodore Roosevelt.

White House,

November 15, 1904.
