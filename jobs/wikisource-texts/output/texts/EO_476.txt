619230Executive Order 476 — Authorizing Appointment of Ralph Modjeski as Consulting Engineer in Ordnance Department After Noncompetitive Examination1906President of the United States

Ralph Modjeski may be appointed as consulting engineer in the Ordnance Department at large, for duty at the Rock Island Arsenal, during the period of construction of the Rock Island bridge and viaduct, subject to noncompetitive examination by the Civil Service Commission.

Theodore Roosevelt.

The White House,

July 9, 1906.
