Matthew Henson accompanied Robert Peary on his expedition to the North Pole.

Signed by President William H. Taft Wednesday, February 26, 1913

1913 CSR 124

1330342Executive Order 1721 — Authorizing Appointment of Matthew A. Henson to Any Suitable Position in Classified Service Without Regard to Civil Service Rules1913President of the United States

Mr. Matthew A. Henson may be appointed to any suitable position in the classified service without compliance with the civil-service rules.

Wm. H. Taft.

The White House,

February 26, 1913.
