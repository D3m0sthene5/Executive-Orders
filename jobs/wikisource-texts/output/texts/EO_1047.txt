Signed by President Theodore Roosevelt Tuesday, March 2, 1909

592305Executive Order 1047 — Authorizing Appointment of John M. Longan to Clerical Position in Classified Service1909President of the United States

Mr. John M. Longan, of Massachusetts, may be appointed to a clerical position in the classified service without reference to the requirements of the civil-service rules.

Theodore Roosevelt.

The White House

March 2, 1909.
