1905 CSR 94

616424Executive Order 381 — Classifying Thirteen Special Agents in the Bureau of the Census1905President of the United States

The thirteen special agents in the Bureau of the Census, whose right to classification was the same as that of the employees who were classified by act of Congress, may be transferred to competitive positions in that office; or elsewhere subject to the limitations of the civil service rules.

Theodore Roosevelt.

The White House,

December 14, 1905.
