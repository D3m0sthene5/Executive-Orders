614820Executive Order 309 — Authorizing Appointment of Mrs. M. G. Lauxmann as Writer in Coast and Geodetic Survey Without Examination1905President of the United States

Mrs. M. G. Lauxmann may be permanently appointed as writer in the Coast and Geodetic Survey without examination under the civil service rules.

Theodore Roosevelt.

White House,

March 18, 1905.
