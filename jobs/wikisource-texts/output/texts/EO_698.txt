Amends Executive Order 496, August 13, 1906.

Signed by President Theodore Roosevelt Monday, October 14, 1907

State Department Circular, November 30, 1907

629191Executive Order 698 — Amending Tariff of Consular Fees to Specify Passport Extension Fee as $1.001907President of the United States

Fee No. 8 of the tariff of American consular fees which took effect November 1, 1906, is hereby amended to read as follows:

"Fee No. 8: Issuing a passport (Form No. 9) or extending a passport, $1.00."

Theodore Roosevelt.

The White House,

October 14, 1907.
