615084Executive Order 317 — Authorizing Reinstatement of S. F. Scott, Jr., in Post Office Department Without Regard to Civil Service Rules1905President of the United States

S. F. Scott, jr., may be reinstated in the Post-Office Service without regard to the time limit upon eligibility for reinstatement.

Theodore Roosevelt.

White House,

March 30, 1905.
