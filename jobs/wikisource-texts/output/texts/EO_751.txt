626827Executive Order 751 — Authorizing Reinstatement of S. Baillio to Norfolk Navy Yard Without Regard to Civil Service Rules1908President of the United States

Mr. S. Baillio may be reinstated in the Norfolk Navy-Yard without regard to the length of his separation therefrom.

Theodore Roosevelt.

The White House,

February 7, 1908.
