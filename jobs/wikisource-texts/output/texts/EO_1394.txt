1912 CSR 141

826617Executive Order 1394 — Authorizing Appointment of Interstate Commerce Commission Secretary Without Regard to Civil Service Rules1911President of the United States

An appointment may be made to the position of secretary of the Interstate Commerce Commission to succeed Mr. E. A. Moseley, who occupied this position from the date of its classification until his death, which recently occurred.

Wm. H. Taft.

The White House,

August 9, 1911.
