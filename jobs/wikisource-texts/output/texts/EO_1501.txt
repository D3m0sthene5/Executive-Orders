826955Executive Order 1501 — Authorizing Reinstatement of Charles W. Rider in Post Office Department Without Regard to Civil Service Rules1912President of the United States

Mr. Charles W. Rider, who resigned from the Department of Commerce and Labor on August 22,1908, may be reinstated in the Post Office Department without regard to the length of his separation or the provisions of the civil-service rules requiring reinstatement in the same department.

Wm. H. Taft.

The White House,

March 23, 1912.
