626553Executive Order 700 — Authorizing Continued Employment of John V. Davis a Automatic Scale Mechanician Without Regard to Civil Service Rules1907President of the United States

John V. Davis, automatic scale mechanician, may be continued in the service for such further period as may be necessary for the completion of the work to which he is now assigned without compliance with the civil-service rules.

Theodore Roosevelt.

The White House,

October 21, 1907.
