2000326Executive Order 1784 — Amending Civil Service Rules Regarding Excepted Positions for Clerks to Heads of Bureaus Appointed by the President1913President of the United States

Schedule A, Subdivision I, paragraph 2 of the civil-service rules is hereby amended by striking out the words: "and confirmed by the Senate."

As amended clause 2 will read as follows:

One private secretary or confidential clerk to each of the heads of bureaus appointed by the President in the executive departments, if authorized by law.

Woodrow Wilson.

The White House

June 6, 1913.
