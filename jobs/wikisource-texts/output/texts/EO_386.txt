Signed by President Theodore Roosevelt Tuesday, December 19, 1905

1905 CSR 95

616432Executive Order 386 — Authorizing Appointment of Nevil Monroe Hopkins as Electrical Engineer in Navy Department After Noncompetitive Examination1905President of the United States

Nevil Monroe Hopkins may be appointed as electrical engineer in the Bureau of Yards and Docks, Navy Department, subject to noncompetitive examination by the Civil Service Commission.

Theodore Roosevelt.

The White House,

December 19, 1905.
