Signed by President Theodore Roosevelt Thursday, April 5, 1906

620542Executive Order 437 — Authorizing Employment of Jacob S. McClure in Classified Service1906President of the United States

Jacob S. McCIure, a veteran of the Civil War, who was dropped from the position of laborer at the Topeka, Kans., pension agency when his position was abolished on June 30, 1905, may be reemployed in a classified position.

Theodore Roosevelt.

The White House,

April 5, 1906.
