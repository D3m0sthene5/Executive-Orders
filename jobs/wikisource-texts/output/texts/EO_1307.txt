Amends Executive Order 1289, January 26, 1911.

Signed by President William H. Taft Tuesday, February 21, 1911

1910 CSR 109

648570Executive Order 1307 — Amending Civil Service Rules to Except Navy Positions on Samoa from Examination1911President of the United States

Schedule A, Subdivision V, paragraph 3, of the civil-service rules is hereby amended by the addition at the end thereof of the following words:

"and the island of Samoa."

As amended the paragraph will read as follows and will except from the requirement of examination

"All positions in the island of Guam and in the island of Samoa."

Wm. H. Taft.

The White House,

February 21, 1911.
