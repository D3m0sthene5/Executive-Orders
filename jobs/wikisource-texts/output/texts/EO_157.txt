Amends Executive Order 119, May 29, 1899.

Signed by President Theodore Roosevelt Thursday, January 23, 1902

610664Executive Order 157 — Amending Civil Service Rules to Except Four Interior Department Special Inspectors from Examination1902President of the United States

Section 43 of Civil-Service Rule VI is hereby amended by adding thereto the following: "and four special inspectors."

The section as amended shall read as follows:

One special land inspector, and four special inspectors.

Approved.

Theodore Roosevelt.

White House,

January 23, 1902.
