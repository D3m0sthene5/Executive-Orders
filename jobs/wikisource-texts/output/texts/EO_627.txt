625801Executive Order 627 — Authorizing Appointment of Claude I. Parker as Librarian or Clerk in General Land Office Without Regard to Civil Service Rules1907President of the United States

Claude I. Parker, of Louisiana, may be appointed librarian for the law library or to a clerical position in the General Land Office.

Theodore Roosevelt.

The White House,

May 6, 1907.
