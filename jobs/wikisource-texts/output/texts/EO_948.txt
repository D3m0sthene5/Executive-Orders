636222Executive Order 948 — Authorizing Appointment of Melvin W. Sheppard as Night Inspector in Customs Service Without Regard to Civil Service Rules1908President of the United States

Mr. Melvin W. Sheppard may be appointed a night inspector in the customs service at the port of Philadelphia without a compliance with the provisions of the civil service rules and regulations.

Theodore Roosevelt.

The White House,

October 2, 1908.
