619214Executive Order 451 — Authorizing Reinstatement of Mrs. M. C. Kratz to Treasury Department Without Regard to Civil Service Rules1906President of the United States

Mrs. M. C. Kratz may be reinstated in the Treasury Department, it appearing that she was removed through inadvertence.

Theodore Roosevelt.

The White House,

May 29, 1906.
