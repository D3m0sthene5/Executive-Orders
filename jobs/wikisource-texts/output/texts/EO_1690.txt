1330300Executive Order 1690 — Authorizing Appointment of Mrs. C. E. Jones as Clerk in Classified Service Without Regard to Civil Service Rules1913President of the United States

Mrs. C. E. Jones may be appointed to a clerical position in the competitive classified service without compliance with the provisions of the civil-service rules.

Wm. H. Taft.

The White House,

January 24, 1913.
