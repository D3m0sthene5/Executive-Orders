Signed by President Theodore Roosevelt Tuesday, December 22, 1908

591994Executive Order 994 — Authorizing Appointment of Clare Kiggins to Position in Classified Service Upon Passing Examination1908President of the United States

Miss Clare Kiggins may be appointed to a position in the departmental classified service upon passing such appropriate noncompetitive examination for the place to be filled as the Commission may prescribe.

Theodore Roosevelt.

The White House

December 22, 1908.
