Signed by President Theodore Roosevelt Thursday, December 7, 1905

617078Executive Order 377-B — Authorizing Isthmian Canal Commission to Hold Quarterly Session of January 1, 1906, in Washington, D. C.1905President of the United States

The quarterly session of the Isthmian Canal Commission for the 1st of January next may be held at the office of the Commission in Washington, D. C., instead of at the office of the governor of the Canal Zone on the Isthmus of Panama, as provided in Executive order of April 1, 1905, in view of the necessity of the Commission for considering the report of the advisory board of engineers and the impossibility of leaving so soon for the Isthmus.

Theodore Roosevelt.

The White House, December 7, 1905.
