625810Executive Order 638 — Authorizing Appointment of Maude Ward Rockwood as Clerk in War Department Without Examination1907President of the United States

Mrs. Maude Ward Rockwood may be appointed to a clerical position in the classified service of the War Department without examination under the civil-service rules.

Theodore Roosevelt.

The White House,

May 24, 1907.
