Amends Executive Order 209, March 20, 1903.

Signed by President Theodore Roosevelt Tuesday, June 13, 1905

1905 CSR 76

616028Executive Order 334 — Amending Civil Service Rules to Except Certain Construction Workers from Examination1905President of the United States

Section I of Schedule A was amended by the addition of the following clause, to be numbered 12:

Mechanics and skilled tradesmen or laborers, employed upon construction or repair work in the field services, under such restrictive conditions that, in the opinion of the Commission, they can not, as a class, be appointed from registers of eligibles.

Theodore Roosevelt.

White House,

June 13, 1905.
