Amends Executive Order 750, February 6, 1908.

Signed by President Theodore Roosevelt Tuesday, March 31, 1908

1 Canal Record 266, April 22, 1908

586472Executive Order 774 — Jury Trial Amendments1908President of the United States

Under authority vested in me by law, it is ordered:

Executive Order No. 750, dated February 6, 1908, providing for a system of jury trials in criminal prosecutions in the Canal Zone, wherein the penalty of death or imprisonment for life may be inflicted, is hereby modified by striking out of line 1 and 2, section 2, and out of line 4 and 5 of the same section, the words "collector of revenue" and inserting in lieu thereof the words "tax collector."

Theodore Roosevelt.

The White House

March 31, 1908.
