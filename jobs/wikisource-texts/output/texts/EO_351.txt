616670Executive Order 351 — Prescribing Procedure for Removal of Officers and Employees Serving on the Isthmus of Panama1905President of the United States

The procedure required by civil service Rule XII, clause 2, need not be observed in removals of officers or employees serving on the Isthmus of Panama. In such removals, however, the reasons therefor shall be made a part of the records of the office of administration, Isthmian Canal affairs, and the Civil Service Commission shall, upon request, be furnished with copies or the originals thereof.

Theodore Roosevelt.

White House,

September 8, 1905.
