1330330Executive Order 1711 — Authorizing Reinstatement of John S. Beach in Pension Office Without Regard to Civil Service Rules1913President of the United States

John S. Beach of New York may be reinstated as a clerk in the office of the disbursing clerk for the payment of pensions, Pension Office, without regard to the length of time he has been separated from the service.

Wm. H. Taft.

The White House,

February 21, 1913.
