614510Executive Order 293 — Authorizing Transfer of Stephen W. Fuller to Messenger in Department of Commerce and Labor Without Regard to Civil Service Rules1905President of the United States

Stephen W. Fuller, a messenger boy in the Library of Congress, may be transferred to the position of messenger in the Bureau of Manufactures of the Department of Commerce and Labor.

Theodore Roosevelt.

White House,

February 6, 1905.
