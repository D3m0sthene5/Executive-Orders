Amends Executive Order 299, March 3, 1905. Amended by Executive Order 1121, August 6, 1909.

Signed by President Theodore Roosevelt Tuesday, November 24, 1908

1908 CSR 126

635962Executive Order 976 — Amending Civil Service Rules Regarding Exceptions from Examination for Special Agents of General Land Office1908President of the United States

Paragraph 11 of Subdivision VI of Schedule A of classified positions excepted from examination under Rule II, clause 3, is hereby amended by striking out the word "five" and inserting in lieu thereof the word "three." As amended the paragraph will read:

Three special agents of the General Land Office to investigate fraudulent entries and other matters of a criminal nature.

Theodore Roosevelt.

The White House,

November 24, 1908.
