615713Executive Order 328 — Authorizing Appointment of William J. Humphreys as Professor of Meteorological Physics in Weather Bureau Without Examination1905President of the United States

William J. Humphreys may be appointed professor of meteorological physics in the Weather Bureau, Department of Agriculture, without examination under the civil service rules.

Theodore Roosevelt.

White House,

May 22, 1905.
