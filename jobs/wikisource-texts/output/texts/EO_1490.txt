1912 CSR 144

826952Executive Order 1490 — Authorizing Appointment of William Langley as Clerk in Census Bureau Without Regard to Civil Service Rules1912President of the United States

Mr. William Langley, a special agent in the Bureau of the Census from October 1, 1902, to January 15, 1912, may be appointed as a clerk in the classified service of the Census Bureau.

Wm. H. Taft.

The White House,

February 29, 1912.
