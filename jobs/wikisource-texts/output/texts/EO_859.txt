Signed by President Theodore Roosevelt Friday, June 26, 1908

1202657Executive Order 859 — Modifying Lemhi National Forest1908President of the United States

It is hereby ordered that on and after July 1, 1908, the land within the boundaries shown on the attached diagram, heretofore set apart, reserved, and proclaimed as part of the Lemhi and Sawtooth National Forest, shall be known as the Lemhi National Forest, shall continue reserved and constitute a part of the Salmon National Forest. It is not intended by this order to release any land from Reservation or to reserve any land not heretofore embraced as a National Forest.

Theodore Roosevelt.

The White House,

June 26, 1908.
