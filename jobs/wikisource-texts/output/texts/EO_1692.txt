1913 CSR 122

1330306Executive Order 1692 — Authorizing Placement of Mrs. Mabel P. LeRoy in Classified Service in Interior Department Without Regard to Civil Service Rules1913President of the United States

The position held by Mrs. Mabel P. LeRoy, appropriated for in the office of the Secretary of Interior in the legislative, executive, and judicial appropriation act, approved August 23, 1912, namely, female clerk to be designated by the President to sign land patents, $1,200, is hereby placed in the competitive classified service.

Wm. H. Taft.

The White House,

January 27, 1913.
