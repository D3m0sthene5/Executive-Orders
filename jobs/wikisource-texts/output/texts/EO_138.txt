Signed by President William McKinley Monday, March 25, 1901

605873Executive Order 138 — Providing that Fees be Collected for Bills of Health Issued to Foreign War Vessels1901President of the United States

Consular officers will hereafter collect any fees for bills of health and supplemental bills of health issued foreign war vessels. The tariff of Consular fees is amended accordingly.

William McKinley.

Executive Mansion,

Washington, D. C., March 25, 1901.
