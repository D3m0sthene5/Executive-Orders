Signed by President Theodore Roosevelt Friday, June 26, 1908

1334504Executive Order 842 — Clearwater National Forest, Idaho1908President of the United States

It is hereby ordered that on and after July 1, 1908, the land within the boundaries shown on the attached diagram, heretofore set apart, reserved, and proclaimed as parts of the Coeur D'Alene and Bitter Root National Forests, shall be known as the Clearwater National Forest. It is not intended by this order to release any land from reservation or to reserve any land not heretofore embraced in a National Forest.

Theodore Roosevelt.

The White House,

June 26, 1908.
