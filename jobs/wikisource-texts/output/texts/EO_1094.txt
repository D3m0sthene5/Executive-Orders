Amends Executive Order 209, March 20, 1903.

Signed by President William H. Taft Wednesday, June 23, 1909

591641Executive Order 1089 — Amending Civil Service Rules to Except War Department Cable Electricians from Examination1909President of the United States

Paragraph 2, Subdivision III, Schedule A, of the civil-service rules, relating to the War Department, is amended by adding the words "and cable electricians." The paragraph as amended will read:

All cable engineers and cable electricians.

Wm. H. Taft.

The White House,

June 23, 1909.
