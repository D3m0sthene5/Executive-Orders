1329624Executive Order 1667 — Authorizing Promotion of Thomas E. Price to Clerk in Post Office Department Without Examination1912President of the United States

Mr. Thomas E. Price may be promoted from the position of messenger to that of clerk in the Post Office Department without examination.

Wm. H. Taft.

The White House,

December 19, 1912.
