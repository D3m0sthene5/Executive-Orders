1912 CSR 143

826927Executive Order 1457 — Authorizing Reinstatement of Paul Brosig as Draftsman in Isthmian Canal Commission Without Regard to Civil Service Rules1912President of the United States

Mr. Paul Brosig may be reinstated in the classified service as a draftsman at $1,800 per annum for duty with the Fortification Division of the Isthmian Canal Commission.

Wm. H. Taft.

The White House,

January 9, 1912.
