625540Executive Order 580 — Authorizing Appointment of James G. Massey as Clerk in Department of the Interior Without Regard to Civil Service Rules1907President of the United States

James G. Massey, of Maryland, may be appointed to a clerical position in the Department of the Interior without reference to civil-service rules.

Theodore Roosevelt.

The White House,

March 5, 1907.
