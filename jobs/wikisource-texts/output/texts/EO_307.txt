614816Executive Order 307 — Authorizing Appointment of Sydnie Kennedy as Chief of Division in Isthmian Canal Service Without Examination1905President of the United States

Mr. Sydnie Kennedy may be appointed as chief of division, clerk, and stenographer, at $1,800 per annum, in the Isthmian Canal Service without examination under the civil service rules.

Theodore Roosevelt.

White House,

March 15, 1905.
