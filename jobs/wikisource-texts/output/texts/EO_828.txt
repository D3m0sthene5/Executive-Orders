636196Executive Order 828 — Authorizing Reinstatement of Frank E. Elder in Government Printing Office Without Regard to Civil Service Rules1908President of the United States

Mr. Frank E. Elder may be reinstated in the Government Printing Office without regard to the fact that he has been separated from that office for more than a year.

Theodore Roosevelt.

The White House,

June 19, 1908.
