Signed by President Woodrow Wilson Saturday, December 8, 1917

U.S. Official Bulletin No. 180, p. 5, December 10, 1917

601550Executive Order 2771 — Returning Aaron L. Shalowitz to Service in Department of Commerce1917President of the United States

Second Lieut. Aaron L. Shalowitz, Coast Artillery, United States Reserve Corps, who was transferred to the jurisdiction of the War Department in accordance with Executive Order No. 2707, of September 24, 1917, is hereby returned to the service and jurisdiction of the Department of Commerce.

Woodrow Wilson 

The White House,

December 8, 1917.
