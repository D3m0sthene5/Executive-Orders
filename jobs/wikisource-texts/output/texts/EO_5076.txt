625154Executive Order 5076 — Designating Mrs. Helen V. McLeod, Clerk in the Interior Department, to Sign President's Name to Land Patents1929President of the United States

Mrs. Helen V. McLeod, of the General Land Office, Department of the Interior, is hereby designated to sign my name to patents during the absence of the clerk regularly designated for that purpose.

Herbert Hoover

The White House,

March 7, 1929.
