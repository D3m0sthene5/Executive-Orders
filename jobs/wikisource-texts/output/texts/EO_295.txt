614512Executive Order 295 — Authorizing Reinstatement of George F. Otis as Clerk in Quartermaster's Department Without Regard to Civil Service Rules1905President of the United States

George F. Otis may be reinstated to the position of clerk in the Quartermaster's Department at large without regard to the provision of Rule IX, limiting the period of eligibility for reinstatement to one year from date of separation.

Theodore Roosevelt.

White House,

February 15, 1905.
