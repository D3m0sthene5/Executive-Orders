613502Executive Order 201 — Authorizing Appointment of F. A. Collins as Private Secretary to the Public Printer Without Examination1903President of the United States

White House, January 30, 1903.

Mr. F. A. Collins may be appointed to the vacancy now existing in the position of private secretary to the Public Printer without examination under the civil-service rules.

Theodore Roosevelt.
