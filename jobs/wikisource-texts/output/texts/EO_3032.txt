Signed by President Woodrow Wilson Saturday, January 25, 1919

601932Executive Order 3032 — Revoking Executive Order of April 9, 1917, Placing Control of the Panama Canal and the Canal Zone Under Army Control1919President of the United States

By virtue of the power and authority vested In and conferred upon me by the Panama Canal act approved August 24, 1912 (37 Statutes at Large, 560, 569), the Executive order, dated April 9, 1917, placing the Panama Canal and all its adjuncts, appendants, and appurtenances, including the entire control and government of the Canal Zone, under the jurisdiction and authority of the officer of the Army commanding the United States' troops stationed in the Canal Zone, is hereby terminated and shall be no longer in force.

Woodrow Wilson 

The White House,

25 January, 1919.
