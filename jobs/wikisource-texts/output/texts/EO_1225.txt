Amends Executive Order 209, March 20, 1903.

Signed by President William H. Taft Tuesday, July 12, 1910

1910 CSR 108

648551Executive Order 1225 — Amending Civil Service Rules to Except Certain Laborers in Customs Service, District of Hawaii, from Examination1910President of the United States

Schedule A, Section III, of the Civil Service Rules relating to the Treasury Department is hereby amended by adding the following paragraph:

Laborers, at $480 per annum, in the Customs Service, District of Hawaii, who are to perform the duties of opener and packer.

Wm. H. Taft.

The White House,

July 12, 1910.
