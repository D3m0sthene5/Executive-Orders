Signed by President Theodore Roosevelt Monday, June 2, 1902

611362Executive Order 174 — Authorizing Appointment of Assistant Commissioner of Immigration in New York Without Examination1902President of the United States

An appointment to the vacancy now existing in the position of Assistant Commissioner of Immigration at the port of New York may be made without examination under the civil-service rules.

Theodore Roosevelt.

White House,

June 2, 1902.
