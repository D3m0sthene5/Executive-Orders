1912 CSR 134

820898Executive Order 1516 — Amending Civil Service Rules Regarding Exceptions from Examination for Inspectors in Interior Department1912President of the United States

Schedule A, Subdivision VIII, paragraph 2 of the civil-service rules, making exceptions from the requirement of examination in the Interior Department, is amended to read as follows:

Inspectors whose duties are of a confidential nature in the office of the Secretary of the Interior and who are appropriated for by the Congress.

Wm. H. Taft.

The White House,

April 10, 1912.
