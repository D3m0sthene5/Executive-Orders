606551Executive Order 250 — Revoking Special Civil Service Rule of July 3, 1902, Pertaining to Persons Formerly Employed in Military Government of Cuba1904President of the United States

The special rule of July 3, 1902, rendering certain persons who were in the employ of the United States in the military government of Cuba eligible to retention and employment in positions in the classified service of a grade corresponding to that which they then held, is hereby revoked.

Theodore Roosevelt.

White House,

February 5, 1904.
