1913 CSR 125

1330348Executive Order 1724 — Authorizing Appointment of Seward Charles in Office of the Supervising Architect in the Treasury Department Without Regard to Civil Service Rules1913President of the United States

Mr. Seward Charles may be appointed a structural engineer draftsman in the Office of the Supervising Architect in the Treasury Department without regard to his standing on the eligible register of the Civil Service Commission for said position.

Wm. H. Taft.

The White House,

February 27, 1913.
