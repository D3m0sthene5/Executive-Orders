1907 CSR 116

625533Executive Order 577 — Authorizing Reinstatement of Lillie L. Wright as Clerk in Patent Office Without Regard to Civil Service Rules1907President of the United States

Mrs. Lillie L. Wright, formerly a clerk in the Patent Office, may be reinstated without regard to the length of time she has been separated from the service.

Theodore Roosevelt.

The White House,

March 4, 1907.
