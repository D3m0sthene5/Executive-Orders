Authorizing Retention of War Department Laborers in San Francisco

Signed by President Theodore Roosevelt Friday, November 13, 1908

1908 CSR 130

636177Executive Order 971 — Authorizing Retention of War Department Laborers in San Francisco1908President of the United States

D. L. Howard, John Thompson, and William Lafaire, who were employed in the Quartermaster's Department at San Francisco, Cal., incident to the earthquake and fire of April, 1906, as unskilled laborers for the performance of unclassified duties, without reference to the labor regulations, and who are still in the service, may be retained and shall have the same status as laborers selected from the labor registers.

The retention of any other laborers employed under the same circumstances may be authorized by the Civil Service Commission.

Theodore Roosevelt.

The White House,

November 13, 1908.
