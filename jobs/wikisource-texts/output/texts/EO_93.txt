Amends Executive Order 89, May 6, 1896.

Signed by President Grover Cleveland Wednesday, December 23, 1896

610201Executive Order 93 — Amending Civil Service Rules To Include Certain Employees of the Penitentiary Service in the Departmental Services1896President of the United States

Amendment to Rule III.

Amend clause 2 (b) of Rule III by adding at the end thereof the following:

And all officers and employees in the penitentiary service who are by law subject to classification.

Approved.

Grover Cleveland.

Executive Mansion,

December 23, 1896.
