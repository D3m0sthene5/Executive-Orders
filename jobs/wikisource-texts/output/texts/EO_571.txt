625530Executive Order 571 — Authorizing Transfer of John E. Kidwell, Accountant in Isthmian Canal Service, to Classified Clerical Position in United States1907President of the United States

Mr. John E. Kidwell, accountant (pay clerk), at $2,200 per annum, in the Isthmian Service, may be transferred to a classified clerical position in the United States.

Theodore Roosevelt.

The White House,

February 21, 1907.
