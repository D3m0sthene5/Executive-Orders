Amends Executive Order 119, May 29, 1899.

Signed by President William McKinley Saturday, March 2, 1901

610546Executive Order 137 — Amending Civil Service Rules to Except Chinese Interpreters in Treasury Department from Examination1901President of the United States

Rule VI of the Civil-Service Rules is hereby amended by adding to the positions therein excepted from the requirements of examination, under the Treasury Department, in paragraph 12, the positions of Chinese interpreters. As amended the paragraph will read:

(a) All shipping commissioners.

(b) All Chinese interpreters.

Approved.

William McKinley.

Executive Mansion,

March 2, 1901.
