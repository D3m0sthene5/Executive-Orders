1330378Executive Order 1734 — Authorizing Appointment of Jesse L. Suter as Clerk in Classified Service Without Regard to Civil Service Rules1913President of the United States

Mr. Jesse L. Suter may be appointed to a clerical position in the classified service without reference to the civil-service rules.

Wm. H. Taft.

The White House,

March 3, 1913.
