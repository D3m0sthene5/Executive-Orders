1330350Executive Order 1726 — Authorizing Transfer of Aaron Burnett in Treasury Department Without Regard to Civil Service Rules1913President of the United States

Aaron Burnett, now a laborer in the Treasury Department, may be transferred to any subclerical classified grade in that department without compliance with civil-service rules.

Wm. H. Taft.

The White House,

February 28, 1913.
