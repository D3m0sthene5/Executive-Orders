1329602Executive Order 1651 — Authorizing Transfer of George W. Baptist to Classified Position in Custom House Without Regard to Civil Service Rules1912President of the United States

Mr. George W. Baptist, who has been employed as a laborer in the custom-house at New York since September 1, 1899, may be transferred to a classified position of the subclerical grade.

Wm. H. Taft.

The White House,

November 27, 1912.
