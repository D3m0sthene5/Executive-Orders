Authorizing Reemployment of John Moynihan as Laborer in Post Office at Kansas City, Missouri, Without Regard to Civil Service Rules

1330302Executive Order 1688 — Authorizing Reemployment of John Moynihan as Laborer in Post Office at Kansas City, Missouri, Without Regard to Civil Service Rules1913President of the United States

Mr. John Moynihan may be reemployed as an unclassified laborer in the post office at Kansas City, Mo., notwithstanding the fact that he has been separated from the service for more than one year.

Wm. H. Taft.

The White House,

January 24, 1913.
