649157Executive Order 1177 — Authorizing Reinstatement of James A. Compton, William E. Dulin, and George P. Meyer in Pension Office Without Regard to Civil Service Rules1910President of the United States

James A. Compton, William E. Dulin, and George P. Meyer may be reinstated in the Pension Office without regard to the fact that they have been separated from the service for more than one year.

Wm. H. Taft.

The White House,

March 15, 1910.
