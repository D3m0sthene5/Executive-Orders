Signed by President Theodore Roosevelt Monday, December 28, 1903

606415Executive Order 242 — Authorizing Reinstatement of Mrs. Frank L. Harrigan as Machine Feeder in Government Printing Office Without Regard to Civil Service Rules1903President of the United States

Mrs. Frank L. Harrigan may be reinstated to the position of ruling machine feeder in the Government Printing Office without regard to the provisions of Rule IX limiting the period of eligibility for reinstatement to one year from the date of separation.

Theodore Roosevelt.

White House,

December 28, 1903.
