Signed by President Theodore Roosevelt Tuesday, March 2, 1909

592307Executive Order 1045 — Authorizing Appointment of James Mullenbach to Inspector in Immigration Service Without Examination1909President of the United States

Mr. James Mullenbach may be appointed inspector in charge of information and distribution work, Immigration Service, Chicago, Ill., without examination under the civil-service rules, in view of his peculiar qualifications for the work involved.

Theodore Roosevelt.

The White House

March 2, 1909.
