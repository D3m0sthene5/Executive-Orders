625527Executive Order 572 — Authorizing Reinstatement of Frank O'Neill as Stock Examiner in Department of Agriculture Without Regard to Civil Service Rules1907President of the United States

Mr. Frank O'Neill may be reinstated to the position of stock examiner in the Department of Agriculture without regard to the one-year limitation of civil-service Rule IX.

Theodore Roosevelt.

The White House,

February 21, 1907.
