616117Executive Order 344 — Authorizing Employment of Charles W. Spicer in Bureau of the Census Without Examination1905President of the United States

Mr. Charles W. Spicer may be employed in the Bureau of the Census, without examination, upon experimental work in developing tabulating machines as authorized in the latest act appropriating for executive, legislative, and judicial expenses.

Theodore Roosevelt.

White House,

July 27, 1905.
