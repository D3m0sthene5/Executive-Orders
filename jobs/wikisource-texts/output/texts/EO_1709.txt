1912 CSR 135

822144Executive Order 1709 — Amending Civil Service Rules Regarding Exceptions from Examination in Treasury Department1913President of the United States

Paragraph 5, Subdivision III, of Schedule A of the civil service rules relating to the Treasury Department is hereby amended to read as follows:

One private secretary or confidential clerk to the superintendent in each mint and in the assay office at New York.

This paragraph heretofore read:

One private secretary or confidential clerk to the superintendent, one cashier in each mint, and one cashier in the assay office at New York.

Wm. H. Taft.

The White House,

February 20, 1913.
