Amends Executive Order 209, March 20, 1903. Revoked by Executive Order 1371, June 12, 1911.

Signed by President Theodore Roosevelt Thursday, June 25, 1908

1908 CSR 124

635941Executive Order 832 — Amending Civil Service Rules Regarding Exceptions from Examination for Paymasters in New York Customs District1908President of the United States

Schedule A, Subdivision II, paragraph 4, of the civil-service rules, which at present excepts from the requirement of examination:

In the New York customs district: One paymaster; stitch counters,

is hereby amended to read as follows:

In the New York customs district: Two paymasters; stitch counters.

Theodore Roosevelt.

The White House,

June 25, 1908.
