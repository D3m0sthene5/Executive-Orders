611624Executive Order 188 — Directing that Raplie M. Cheshire be Recertified for Appointment1902President of the United States

The Civil Service Commission is authorized and directed to reissue certificate No. 991, as of date September 26, 1901, containing the name of Raplie M. Cheshire, appointment under said certificate having been declined by him under a mistake.

Theodore Roosevelt.

White House, September 4, 1902.
