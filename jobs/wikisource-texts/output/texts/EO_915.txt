1908 CSR 134

636202Executive Order 915 — Authorizing Reinstatement of Herman Gluck in Internal Revenue Service Without Regard to Civil Service Rules1908President of the United States

Mr. Herman Gluck, whose services as a deputy collector of internal revenue at New York were discontinued on March 1, 1907, may be reinstated without regard to the expiration of his year of eligibility.

Theodore Roosevelt.

The White House,

July 16, 1908.
