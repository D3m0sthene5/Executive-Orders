1905 CSR 75

615390Executive Order 322 — Amending Civil Service Rules to Except Certain Law Officers in Bureau of Insular Affairs from Examination1905President of the United States

Schedule A of the civil service rules is hereby amended by the addition to Section III, War Department, of a paragraph which shall be numbered 6, and shall read as follows:

One law officer in the Bureau of Insular Affairs.

Theodore Roosevelt.

White House,

May 12, 1905.
