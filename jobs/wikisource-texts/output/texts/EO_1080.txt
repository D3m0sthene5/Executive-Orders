Signed by President William H. Taft Friday, May 28, 1909

General Orders, No. 114, War Dept., June 11, 1909

1588021Executive Order 1080 — Reducing Portion of Crow Creek Forest Reserve Transferred to War Department for Military Purposes1909President of the United States

The Executive Order of October 9, 1903, transferring to the War Department for use as a military reservation the tracts of public land in the State of Wyoming known as the “Crow Creek Forest Reserve," originally declared as a forest reserve by Proclamation dated October 10, 1900, with the understanding that the use of the lands for the purposes of a military reservation shall not interfere with the object for which the forest reserve was established, is hereby amended so as to exclude from reservation for military purposes the southwest quarter of section six, township fourteen north, range seventy-one west, and the south half of the northeast quarter and the north half of the southeast quarter of section seventeen, township fifteen north, range seventy-one west; sixth principal meridian.

Wm. H. Taft.

The White House,

May 28, 1909.
