1330327Executive Order 1708 — Authorizing Promotion and Transfer of Martin J. Murphy to Clerk in Treasury Department Without Regard to Civil Service Rules1913President of the United States

The Secretary of the Treasury is hereby authorized to promote, transfer, and appoint Mr. Martin J. Murphy, now a sugar sampler in the office of the appraiser of merchandise at New York, to the position of clerk in that office, without compliance with the civil-service rules.

Wm. H. Taft.

The White House,

February 20, 1913.
