827008Executive Order 1561 — Authorizing Reinstatement of Leroy A. McGee as Post Office Inspector Without Regard to Civil Service Rules1912President of the United States

Mr. Leroy A. McGee may be reinstated to the position of post-office inspector without reference to the time limit upon eligibility for reinstatement.

Wm. H. Taft.

The White House,

July 10, 1912.
