1910 CSR 112

649141Executive Order 1159 — Authorizing Reinstatement of Nannie M. Wayland as Clerk Without Regard to Civil Service Rules1910President of the United States

Mrs. Nannie M. Wayland may be reinstated in the clerical grade in any department without regard to the length of time that she has been separated from the service.

Wm. H. Taft.

The White House,

January 27, 1910.
