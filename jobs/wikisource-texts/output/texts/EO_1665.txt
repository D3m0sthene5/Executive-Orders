1329618Executive Order 1665 — Authorizing Appointment of Charles R. Pickard in Competitive Classified Service Without Regard to Civil Service Rules1912President of the United States

Mr. Charles R. Pickard may be appointed to an appropriate position in the competitive classified service without compliance with the provisions of the civil-service rules.

Wm. H. Taft.

The White House,

December 17, 1912.
