Signed by President William H. Taft Monday, July 31, 1911

1912 CSR 141

826610Executive Order 1388 — Authorizing Appointment of Thomas Richardson Without Examination1911President of the United States

Mr. Thomas Richardson, who has been serving as postmaster at Port Gibson, Miss., since July 12, 1898, may be appointed to an appropriate position in the competitive classified service at Washington where his postal experience will be available, without reference to the requirement of examination under the civil-service rules.

Wm. H. Taft.

The White House,

July 31, 1911.
