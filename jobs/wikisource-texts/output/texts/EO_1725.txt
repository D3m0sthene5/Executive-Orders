1330354Executive Order 1725 — Authorizing Transfer of Alexander J. Stormont to Clerk in Classified Service Without Examination1913President of the United States

Alexander J. Stormont, clerk to the Secretary of War, may be transferred to a clerkship in the competitive classified service, without examination.

Wm. H. Taft.

The White House,

February 28, 1913.
