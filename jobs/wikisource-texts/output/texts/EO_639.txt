625815Executive Order 639 — Authorizing Appointment of Clifford Rose as Private Secretary to the Public Printer Without Examination1907President of the United States

Mr. Clifford Rose may be appointed as private secretary to the Public Printer without examination under the civil-service rules.

Theodore Roosevelt.

The White House,

May 28, 1907.
