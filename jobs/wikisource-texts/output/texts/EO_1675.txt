1330283Executive Order 1675 — Authorizing Reinstatement of James E. Taylor in Internal Revenue Service Without Regard to Civil Service Rules1913President of the United States

Mr. James E. Taylor may be reinstated to an appropriate position in the Internal Revenue Service without reference to the length of time he has been separated from the service.

Wm. H. Taft.

The White House,

January 3, 1913.
