1912 CSR 147

827001Executive Order 1549 — Authorizing Transfer of M. M. Dodge to Naturalization Examiner Without Regard to Civil Service Rules1912President of the United States

Mr. M. M. Dodge may be transferred, before the completion of his probationary period, from the position of clerk in the War Department to naturalization examiner at Pittsburgh, Pa.

Wm. H. Taft.

The White House,

June 19, 1912.
