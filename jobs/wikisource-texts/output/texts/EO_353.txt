616214Executive Order 353 — Authorizing Reinstatement of Mary E. Enright in Bureau of Engraving and Printing Without Regard to Civil Service Rules1905President of the United States

Mrs. Mary E. Enright may be reinstated in the Bureau of Engraving and Printing without regard to the time limit of eligibility for reinstatement.

Theodore Roosevelt.

White House,

September 22, 1905.
