636234Executive Order 963 — Authorizing Appointment of Charles W. Edwards as Classified Laborer in Treasury Department Without Regard to Civil Service Rules1908President of the United States

Mr. Charles W. Edwards, an unclassified laborer in the office of the Register of the Treasury, may be promoted or appointed to the position of classified laborer in the service of the Treasury Department.

Theodore Roosevelt.

The White House,

October 28, 1908.
