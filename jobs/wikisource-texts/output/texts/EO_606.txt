Signed by President Theodore Roosevelt Monday, April 1, 1907

626878Executive Order 606 — Appointing George W. Goethals, U.S. Army, as Chairman of Isthmian Canal Commission1907President of the United States

Lieutenant-Colonel George W. Goethals, U. S. A., is hereby appointed chairman of the Isthmian Canal Commission effective this date, vice John F. Stevens, resigned.

Chairman Goethals will receive the same compensation that was fixed in the Executive Order of March 4, 1907, appointing him a member of the Isthmian Canal Commission.

Theodore Roosevelt.

The White House,

April 1, 1907.
