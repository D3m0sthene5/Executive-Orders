1908 CSR 134

636204Executive Order 914 — Authorizing Appointment of Four People as Experts in Liquor Suppression Among Indians Without Regard to Civil Service Rules1908President of the United States

W. S. Lowe, Grosvenor A. Porter, T. E. Brentz, and E. E. Kinnett, of Oklahoma, may be appointed without reference to the civil-service rules as experts in the suppression of the liquor traffic among the Indians.

Theodore Roosevelt.

The White House,

July 16, 1908.
