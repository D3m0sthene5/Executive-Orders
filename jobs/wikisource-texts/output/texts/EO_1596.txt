827050Executive Order 1596 — Authorizing Reinstatement of G. M. Davison as Storekeeper-Gauger in Internal Revenue Service Without Regard to Civil Service Rules1912President of the United States

Mr. G. M. Davison maybe reinstated as storekeeper-gauger in the Internal-Revenue Service without regard to the length of his separation from the service.

Wm. H. Taft.

The White House,

September 2, 1912.
