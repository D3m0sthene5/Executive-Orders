1330334Executive Order 1715 — Authorizing Appointment of John J. Farrell as Letter Carrier in Brooklyn, New York, Post Office Without Regard to Civil Service Rules1913President of the United States

Mr. John J. Farrell may be appointed a letter carrier in the Brooklyn, N. Y., post office without reference to the civil-service rules.

Wm. H. Taft.

The White House,

February 25, 1913.
