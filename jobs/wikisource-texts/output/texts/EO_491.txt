Signed by President Theodore Roosevelt Monday, August 6, 1906

619311Executive Order 491 — Authorizing Appointment of Julia Lynah McCoy in Treasury Department Without Examination1906President of the United States

Mrs. Julia Lynah McCoy may be appointed to a position in the Treasury Department without examination under the civil service rules.

Theodore Roosevelt.

The White House,

August 6, 1906.
