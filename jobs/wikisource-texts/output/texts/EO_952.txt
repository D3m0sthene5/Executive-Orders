636226Executive Order 952 — Authorizing Appointment of John A. Flickinger as Watchman Without Regard to Civil Service Rules1908President of the United States

Mr. John A. Flickinger, formerly an eligible on the watchman register and three times certified for appointment may be appointed as a watchman in the classified civil service.

Theodore Roosevelt.

The White House,

October 17, 1908.
