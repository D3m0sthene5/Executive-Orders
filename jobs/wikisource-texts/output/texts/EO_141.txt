Signed by President Theodore Roosevelt Thursday, October 31, 1901

611428Executive Order 141 — Authorizing Appointment of Clerk in Pension Agency in Philadelphia Without Examination1901President of the United States

Special Rule.

An appointment to the vacancy now existing in the position of the clerk designated to sign checks for the pension agent at the pension agency at Philadelphia, Pa., may be made without examination under the civil-service rules.

Theodore Roosevelt.

White House,

October 31, 1901.
