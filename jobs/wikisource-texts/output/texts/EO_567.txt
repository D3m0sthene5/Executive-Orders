625523Executive Order 567 — Authorizing Reinstatement of James H. Adams in Classified Service Without Regard to Civil Service Rules1907President of the United States

Mr. James H. Adams, who was appointed in the classified service of the Treasury Department through examination and served with slight interruptions from December 12, 1892, to February 23, 1904, may be reinstated in the classified service without regard to the provisions of the civil-service rules.

Theodore Roosevelt.

The White House,

February 20, 1907.
