Signed by President Theodore Roosevelt Saturday, January 30, 1909

592016Executive Order 1017 — Authorizing Reinstatement of Daniel F. Dolan as Post-Office Inspector with Suspension of Sixty Days1909President of the United States

Mr. Daniel F. Dolan may be reinstated as a post-office inspector and will be suspended for sixty days from the date of his reinstatement.

Theodore Roosevelt.

The White House

January 30, 1909.
