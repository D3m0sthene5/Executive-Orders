Signed by President Woodrow Wilson Thursday, November 22, 1917

U.S. Official Bulletin No. 179, p. 8, December 8, 1917

601546Executive Order 2759 — Reserving Sister Islands in Green Bay, Wisconsin for Lighthouse Purposes1917President of the United States

It is hereby ordered that two unsurveyed islands containing about 2½ acres known as Sister Islands, situated in Green Ray in approximate sec. 30, T. 32 N., R. 28 E., 4th P. M., Wisconsin, be, and the same are hereby reserved for lighthouse purposes.

Woodrow Wilson 

The White House,

November 22, 1917.
