Signed by President Theodore Roosevelt Saturday, June 23, 1906

620106Executive Order 466 — Ordering Four Hour Workdays on Saturdays During Summer for Government Printing Office Workers1906President of the United States

It is hereby ordered that on Saturdays during July, August and September, until further notice, four hours, exclusive of time for luncheon, shall constitute a day's work for skilled mechanics, laborers, and employees in the Classified Civil Service at the Government Printing Office, Washington: Provided, that if any skilled mechanic, laborer or employee in the Classified Civil Service shall work more than four hours on any Saturday during the months aforesaid, he shall be paid for such extra work at the rate of fifty per centum more than his regular and normal compensation and no more. 

Theodore Roosevelt.

The White House, June 23, 1906.
