2001230Executive Order 1808 — Amending Civil Service Rules to Except Temporary Detective Employees in Internal Revenue Service from Examination1913President of the United States

Schedule A, Subdivision III, paragraph 14, of the civil-service rules, making exceptions from the requirement of examination in the Treasury Department, is amended to read as follows:

Informers and posse men, and special employees employed temporarily for detective work in the Internal Revenue Service, under the appropriation for detecting and bringing to trial and punishment persons violating the internal-revenue laws.

Woodrow Wilson.

The White House

August 2, 1913.
