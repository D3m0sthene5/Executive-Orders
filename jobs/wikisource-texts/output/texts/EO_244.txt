606546Executive Order 244 — Authorizing Appointment of Mrs. G. W. Shipman as Clerk in Office of the First Assistant Postmaster-General Without Examination1904President of the United States

Mrs. G. W. Shipman may be appointed a clerk in the Office of the First Assistant Postmaster-General without examination under the Civil-Service rules.

Theodore Roosevelt.

White House,

January 7, 1904.
