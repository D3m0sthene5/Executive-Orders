Signed by President Theodore Roosevelt Thursday, May 16, 1907

Published: General Orders, No. 118, War Dept., May 24, 1907  

618080Executive Order 636 — Rescinding Transfer Prescribed by Executive Order No. 372-B of November 20, 1905, as Amended, of Certain Lands Comprising Fort St. Michael Military Reservation, Alaska1907President of the United States

The Executive Order of November 20, 1905, as amended by Executive Order of June 8, 1906 (General Orders, No. 114, War Department, June 20, 1906), providing for the transfer to the Interior Department, under the act of July 5, 1884 (28 Stat. L., 103), to take effect on November 1, 1907, of all the lands comprised within the military reservation of Fort St. Michael, Alaska, except the limited areas therein described as to be held in reservation for military purposes, is hereby rescinded, and the lands affected thereby will be continued in reservation for military purposes.

Theodore Roosevelt

The White House,

May 16, 1907.
