Signed by President Theodore Roosevelt Tuesday, July 16, 1907

1907 CSR 126

625836Executive Order 667 — Authorizing Employment of Aksel Hansen in Bureau of the Census Without Examination1907President of the United States

Mr. Aksel Hansen may be employed in the Bureau of the Census upon experimental work in developing tabulating machinery, as authorized by the act of February 3, 1905, appropriating for executive, legislative, and judicial expenses, without examination under the civil-service rules.

Theodore Roosevelt.

The White House,

July 16, 1907.
