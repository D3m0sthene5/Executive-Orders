1330346Executive Order 1723 — Authorizing Reinstatement of Frank P. Caviezel in Internal Revenue Service Without Regard to Civil Service Rules1913President of the United States

Mr. Frank P. Caviezel may be reinstated as special employee in the Internal Revenue Service without reference to the period of his separation from the service.

Wm. H. Taft.

The White House,

February 27, 1913.
