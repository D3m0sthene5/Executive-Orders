Signed by President Franklin D. Roosevelt Friday, April 6, 1934

1329312Executive Order 6668 — Veterans Regulation No. 1(e); Entitlement to Pension1934President of the United States

Veterans Regulation No. 1(e)

Entitlement to Pension

By virtue of the authority vested in me under sections 1 and 4 of title I of the act entitled “An act to maintain the credit of the United States Government”, approved March 20, 1933 (Public No. 2, 73d Congress), the following regulation amending Veterans Regulation No. 1(d) is hereby prescribed:

Veterans Regulation No. 1(d) is hereby cancelled effective March 28, 1934.

Franklin D. Roosevelt

The White House,

April 6, 1934.

[Filed 4–??–34; 8:45 am]
