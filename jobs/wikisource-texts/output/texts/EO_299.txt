1905 CSR 73

614790Executive Order 299 — Amending Civil Service Rules to Except Five Special Agents in Interior Department from Examination1905President of the United States

Schedule A of the civil service rules is hereby amended by the addition to Section VI, relating to the Department of the Interior, of a paragraph (to be numbered 11) to read as follows:

Five special agents of the General Land Office to investigate fraudulent entries and other matters of a criminal nature.

Theodore Roosevelt.

White House,

March 3, 1905.
