625963Executive Order 688 — Authorizing Appointment of Felix Neumann as Clerk in Smithsonian Institution Without Examination1907President of the United States

Mr. Felix Neumann may be appointed as a clerk in the Bureau of International Exchanges, Smithsonian Institution, without examination under the civil-service rules.

Theodore Roosevelt.

The White House,

September 9, 1907.
