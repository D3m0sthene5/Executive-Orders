Signed by President Theodore Roosevelt Friday, January 4, 1907

General Orders, No. 8, War Dept., January 15, 1907   

577016Executive Order 543 — Revoking the Military Reservation of Camp Hartshorne in the Philippine Islands1907President of the United States

The Military Reservation of Camp Hartshorne at Laguan, Island of Laguan. Samar, Philippine Islands, made by Executive Order of July 31, 1903 (General Orders No. 4, War Department. August 22, 1903), having become useless for military purposes, is hereby restored to the control of the government of the Philippine Islands to be administered for the benefit of the inhabitants thereof.

Theodore Roosevelt

The White House,

January 4, 1907.
