Signed by President William McKinley Thursday, December 29, 1898

1720543Executive Order 109 — Closing Executive Departments for Funeral of Senator Morrill1898President of the United States

The president directs that the several executive departments of the government and the printing office be closed at 11 o'clock on the 31st inst., on account of the funeral of the late Senator Morrill of Vermont, which will be held in the senate chamber on Saturday next at 12 o'clock.

J. A. Porter, Secretary to the President.
