1330293Executive Order 1685 — Authorizing Appointment of Monico Lopez as Messenger in Office of the Chief of the Quartermaster Corps, United States Army, Without Regard to Civil Service Rules1913President of the United States

Monico Lopez may be appointed a messenger in the office of the Chief of the Quartermaster Corps, United States Army, without reference to the civil-service rules.

Wm. H. Taft.

The White House,

January 21, 1913.
