Signed by President Theodore Roosevelt Saturday, September 26, 1908

1908 CSR 136

636218Executive Order 941 — Authorizing Transfer of Charles H. Cameron to Chinese Inspector in Immigration Service Without Examination1908President of the United States

Mr. Charles H. Cameron, a mounted deputy collector and inspector in the customs service, may be transferred to the position of Chinese inspector in the Immigration Service, Department of Commerce and Labor, without examination under the civil-service rules.

Theodore Roosevelt.

The White House,

September 26, 1908.
