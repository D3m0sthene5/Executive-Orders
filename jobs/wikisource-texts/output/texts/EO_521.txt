Signed by President Theodore Roosevelt Tuesday, October 30, 1906

1906 CSR 105

619409Executive Order 521 — Authorizing Promotion of Paul A. Hines Without Examination1906President of the United States

Mr. Paul A. Hines, a clerk in the office of the Secretary of Agriculture, may be promoted as a clerk without examination under the civil service rules.

Theodore Roosevelt.

The White House,

October 30, 1906.
