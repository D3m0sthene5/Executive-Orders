Signed by President William H. Taft Monday, August 21, 1911

1912 CSR 141

826623Executive Order 1402 — Authorizing Transfer of Norris R. Welsh to Department of Agriculture Without Regard to Civil Service Rules1911President of the United States

Mr. Norris R. Welsh, a skilled laborer in the Government Printing Office, at $780 per annum, may be transferred to a position in the Division of Publications, Department of Agriculture, at a salary of $720 per annum—a reduction of $60.

Wm. H. Taft.

The White House,

August 21, 1911.
