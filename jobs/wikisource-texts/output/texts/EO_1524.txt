1912 CSR 146

826980Executive Order 1524 — Authorizing Appointment of Homer C. Poundstone in Civil Service Commission Without Examination1912President of the United States

Commander Homer C. Poundstone, United States Navy, retired, may be employed on the rolls of the United States Civil Service Commission without examination under the civil-service rules.

Wm. H. Taft.

The White House,

April 30, 1912.
