1905 CSR 85

614812Executive Order 305 — Authorizing Appointment of Dr. Cyrus Adler as Assistant Secretary in Smithsonian Institution Without Examination1905President of the United States

Dr. Cyrus Adler may be appointed as assistant secretary, in charge of the library and exchanges, Smithsonian Institution, at a compensation of $2,700 per annum, without examination under the civil service rules.

Theodore Roosevelt.

White House,

March 14, 1905.
