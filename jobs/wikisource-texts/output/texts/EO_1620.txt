827067Executive Order 1620 — Authorizing Reinstatement of John K. Robinson as Printer in Weather Bureau Without Regard to Civil Service Rules1912President of the United States

Mr. John K. Robinson may be reinstated as a printer in the Weather Bureau without reference to the period of his separation from the service.

Wm. H. Taft.

The White House,

October 4, 1912.
