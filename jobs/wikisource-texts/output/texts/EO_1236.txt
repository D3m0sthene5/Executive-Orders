649801Executive Order 1236 — Authorizing Placement of John J. Scully as Classified Laborer in Customs Service Without Regard to Civil Service Rules1910President of the United States

John J. Scully, a laborer in the Customs Service at New York, N. Y., may be placed on the list of classified laborers at that port.

Wm. H. Taft.

The White House,

August 8, 1910.
