1330315Executive Order 1696 — Authorizing Reinstatement of Will S. Cox as Railway Mail Clerk Without Regard to Civil Service Rules1913President of the United States

Mr. Will S. Cox, who was removed on March 11, 1907, from the position of railway mail clerk, may be reinstated without regard to the fact that he has been separated more than one year.

Wm. H. Taft.

The White House,

February 3, 1913.
