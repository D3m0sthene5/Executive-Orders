614007Executive Order 280 — Authorizing Appointment of James Rankin Young as Superintendent of Dead Letter Office Without Examination1904President of the United States

Mr. James Rankin Young, of Pennsylvania, may be appointed as Superintendent of the Dead-Letter Office without examination under the civil service rules.

Theodore Roosevelt.

White House,

November 30, 1904.
