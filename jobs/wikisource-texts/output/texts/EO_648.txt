625819Executive Order 648 — Authorizing Permanent Appointment of Samuel H. Cunningham as Clerk in Bureau of Animal Industry Without Regard to Civil Service Rules1907President of the United States

Mr. Samuel H. Cunningham, who is temporarily employed as a clerk in the Bureau of Animal Industry, Department of Agriculture, as a result of examination, may be permanently appointed without reference to his standing on the eligible register.

Theodore Roosevelt.

The White House,

June 11, 1907.
