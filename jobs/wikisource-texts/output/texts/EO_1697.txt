Amends Executive Order 1268, December 2, 1910.

Signed by President William H. Taft Tuesday, February 4, 1913

1912 CSR 135

822140Executive Order 1697 — Amending Civil Service Rules Regarding Exceptions from Examination in Navy Department1913President of the United States

Schedule A, Subdivision V, of the civil service rules is amended by adding the following paragraph, to be numbered 5:

Artisan and supervisory artisan positions at the naval stations at Cavite, Olongapo, and Guantanamo.

Wm. H. Taft.

The White House,

February 4, 1913.
