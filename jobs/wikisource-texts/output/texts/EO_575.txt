EXECUTIVE ORDER
(EO 575)

It is hereby ordered that the hereinafter described lands
at Cape Spencer, District or Alaska, be, and they are hereby, re-
served and set apart for light-house purposes, viz:
All that portion of the head land or which Cape Spencer,
(Entrance to Cross Sound, Alaska), forms the southerly extremity
lying south of the parallel 58 degrees 14 minutes north latitude,
together with all outlying islands and including all land on both
sides of the inlet known as Dick's Arm and south of said parallel.
(Signed) Theodore Roosevelt

THE WHITE HOUSE,
March 2d, 1907.
