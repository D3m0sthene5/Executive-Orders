626839Executive Order 768 — Authorizing Transfer of Philip W. Reinhard, Jr., Customs Clerk, to Competitive Service Without Regard to Civil Service Rules1908President of the United States

Mr. Philip W. Reinhard, jr., of New York, confidential clerk to the collector of customs at New York, may be transferred from the excepted to the competitive class because of his faithful service during the past nine years, it being desirable that he continue in the performance of the responsible duties, classified in character, which he has been performing.

Theodore Roosevelt.

The White House,

March 6, 1908.
