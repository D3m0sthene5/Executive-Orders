826984Executive Order 1533 — Authorizing Reinstatement of Walter L. Barnum as Clerk in Classified Service Without Regard to Civil Service Rules1912President of the United States

Mr. Walter L. Barnum may be reinstated to an appropriate clerical position in the classified service without regard to the length of his separation therefrom.

Wm. H. Taft.

The White House,

May 27, 1912.
