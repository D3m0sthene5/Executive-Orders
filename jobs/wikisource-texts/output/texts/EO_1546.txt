1912 CSR 147

826999Executive Order 1546 — Authorizing Reinstatement of Oscar Wenderoth as Supervising Architect in Treasury Department Without Regard to Civil Service Rules1912President of the United States

Mr. Oscar Wenderoth may be reinstated in the classified service to the position of Supervising Architect without regard to the length of time that he has been separated from the service.

Wm. H. Taft.

The White House,

June 15, 1912.
