Signed by President Theodore Roosevelt Wednesday, December 23, 1908

2 Canal Record 155, January 13, 1909.

636461Executive Order 996 — Appointing H. A. Gudger as Chief Justice of the Supreme Court of the Canal Zone1908President of the United States

Under authority vested in me by law, and until otherwise provided by law or ordered, H. A. Gudger is appointed Chief Justice of the Supreme Court of the Canal Zone, at a salary of six thousand five hundred dollars per annum.

Effective January 4, 1909.

Theodore Roosevelt.

The White House,

December 23, 1908.
