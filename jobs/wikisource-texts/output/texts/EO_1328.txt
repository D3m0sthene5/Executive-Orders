Amends Executive Order 1268, December 2, 1910.

Signed by President William H. Taft Monday, April 3, 1911

1911 CSR 109

820372Executive Order 1328 — Amending Civil Service Rules to Except Certain Marine Corps Clerks from Examination1911President of the United States

Schedule A of the civil-service rules is hereby amended by the insertion of a new paragraph under Subdivision V, Navy Department, to be numbered 4, and to read as follows:

One clerk actually on duty with each assistant paymaster of the United States Marine Corps.

Wm. H. Taft.

The White House,

April 3, 1911.
