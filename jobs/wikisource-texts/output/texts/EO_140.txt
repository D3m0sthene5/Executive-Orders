Amends Executive Order 94, January 2, 1897. Amended by Executive Order 152, December 23, 1901.

Signed by President William McKinley Wednesday, July 31, 1901

610553Executive Order 140 — Amending Civil Service Rules Regarding Age Limitations in Post Office Service1901President of the United States

Rule V, section 4, is hereby amended as follows: In the table of age limits, the twenty-seventh line, reading "Letter carriers * * * 21—40," is stricken out; in the twenty-eighth line, the words "other" and "No limit" are stricken out, and in lieu of the words "No limit" thus stricken out the number "45" is inserted.

As amended, the part of the table of age limits in section 4 of Rule V, which is affected by this amendment, reads as follows:

Minimum.Maximum.

Post-Office Service:

All positions
1845

Approved.

William McKinley.

Executive Mansion,

July 31, 1901.
