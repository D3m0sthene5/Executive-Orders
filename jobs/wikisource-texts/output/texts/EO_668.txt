625839Executive Order 668 — Authorizing Appointment of Margaret F. Sterne as Clerk in Treasury Department Without Regard to Civil Service Rules1907President of the United States

Miss Margaret F. Sterne may be appointed clerk in the Treasury Department without compliance with the civil-service rules.

Theodore Roosevelt.

The White House,

July 25, 1907.
