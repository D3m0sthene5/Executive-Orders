Amends Executive Order 209, March 20, 1903.

Signed by President Theodore Roosevelt Wednesday, November 23, 1904

616478Executive Order 277 — Amending Civil Service Rules to Make Treasury Department Cashiers and Deputies Subject to Examination1904President of the United States

This is the Civil Service Commission's description of the Executive Order, not the order text itself.

The President promulgated an order amending Schedule A by striking out paragraphs 1 and 2 and part of paragraph 3 of section 2, which had the effect of restoring to the competitive service the positions of deputy or assistant collector, deputy naval officer, deputy surveyor, and cashier in the Customs Service.
