826932Executive Order 1462 — Authorizing Appointment of Florence E. Mole as Clerk Without Regard to Civil Service Rules1912President of the United States

Mrs. Florence E. Mole, who has been postmaster at Rehoboth, Del., for several years, may be appointed to the position of clerk at $900 in the office of the collector of internal revenue at Baltimore, Md., without compliance with the civil-service rules. She does not have a classified competitive civil-service status.

Wm. H. Taft.

The White House,

January 12, 1912.
