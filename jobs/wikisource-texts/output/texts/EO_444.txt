619115Executive Order 444 — Authorizing Permanent Appointment of George M. Shafer as Watchman in Department of the Interior Without Regard to Civil Service Rules1906President of the United States

George M. Shafer, a civil-war veteran, may be permanently appointed as a watchman in the Department of the Interior without reference to the requirements of the civil-service rules.

Theodore Roosevelt.

The White House,

May 1, 1906.
