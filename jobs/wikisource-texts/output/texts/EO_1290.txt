Amends Executive Order 1180, March 23, 1910.

Signed by President William H. Taft Monday, January 30, 1911

1910 CSR 107

648525Executive Order 1290 — Amending Civil Service Rules Regarding Noncompetitive Examinations of Miners1911President of the United States

Schedule B of classified positions which may be filled upon noncompetitive examination is hereby amended by adding to Subdivision I, Interior Department, a paragraph as follows:

Miners whether employed in rescue or first-aid work at rescue stations, on rescue cars, or at experimental mines, under the Bureau of Mines, provided that should the Civil Service Commission at any time have reason to believe that the privilege so afforded is abused it may revoke it.

Wm. H. Taft.

The White House,

January 30, 1911.
