616430Executive Order 385 — Authorizing Reinstatement of Richard H. Williams as Inspector of Hulls of Steam Vessels Without Regard to Civil Service Rules1905President of the United States

Richard H. Williams may be reinstated as an inspector of hulls of steam vessels without regard to the limitation of one year prescribed by the civil service rules.

Theodore Roosevelt.

The White House,

December 18, 1905.
