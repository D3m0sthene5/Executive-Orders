626863Executive Order 786 — Authorizing Reinstatement of John Krey as Assistant Engineer in Engineer Department of the United States Army Without Regard to Civil Service Rules1908President of the United States

Mr. John Krey may be reinstated as an assistant engineer in the Engineer Department of the United States Army in the discretion of the Secretary of War and should the needs of the service so require, without reference to the time limit of the civil-service rules.

Theodore Roosevelt.

The White House,

April 23, 1908.
