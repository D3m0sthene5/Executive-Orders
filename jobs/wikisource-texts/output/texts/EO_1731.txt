1330368Executive Order 1731 — Authorizing Appointment of Katharine T. Gerald in Bureau of Pensions Without Examination1913President of the United States

Mrs. Katharine T. Gerald may be appointed to a classified position in the Bureau of Pensions without examination.

Wm. H. Taft.

The White House,
