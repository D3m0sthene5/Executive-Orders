Amends Executive Order 1198, April 30, 1910.

Signed by President William H. Taft Saturday, February 18, 1911

828328Executive Order 1303 — Appointment of Commander Richard H. Jackson to Wireless Telegraphy Board1911President of the United States

Executive Order No. 1198, dated April 30, 1910, is so far amended that Commander Richard H. Jackson, U. S. N., is hereby appointed a member of the Wireless Board referred to therein, vice Commander Chester M. Knepper, U. S. N., relieved.

Wm. H. Taft.

The White House,

February 18, 1911.
