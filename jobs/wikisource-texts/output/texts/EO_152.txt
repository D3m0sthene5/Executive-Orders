Amends Executive Order 140, July 31, 1901. Amended by Executive Order 153, December 27, 1901.

Signed by President Theodore Roosevelt Monday, December 23, 1901

610622Executive Order 152 — Amending Civil Service Rules Regarding Age Limitations in Post Office Service1901President of the United States

Section 4 of Civil-Service Rule V is hereby amended by striking out from the tabular statement therein the following under the heading of "Post-Office Service:" 

All positions
1845

And inserting in lies thereof the following:

Rural letter carrier
1755

All other positions
1845

Approved.

T. Roosevelt.

White House,

December 23, 1901.
