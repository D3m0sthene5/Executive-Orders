Signed by President Theodore Roosevelt Friday, December 18, 1908

591992Executive Order 993 — Authorizing Promotion of Herman W. Kuchmeister to Day Inspector1908President of the United States

Mr. Herman W. Kuchmeister, an assistant weigher in the Customs Service at Boston, may be promoted to day inspector without further examination.

Theodore Roosevelt.

The White House

December 18, 1908.
