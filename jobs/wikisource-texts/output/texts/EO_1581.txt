827039Executive Order 1581 — Authorizing Appointment of Sadie A. Wright as Charwoman Without Regard to Civil Service Commission Regulations1912President of the United States

Mrs. Sadie A. Wright may be appointed a charwoman in any of the executive departments without compliance with the labor regulations of the Civil Service Commission.

Wm. H. Taft.

The White House,

August 23, 1912.
