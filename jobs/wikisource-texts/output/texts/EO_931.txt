636210Executive Order 931 — Authorizing Appointment of Harry H. Weddle as Inspector in Charge in Immigration Service Without Examination1908President of the United States

Mr. Harry H. Weddle, of California, may be appointed as inspector in charge at San Diego, Cal., in the Immigration Service without examination under the civil-service act and rules.

Theodore Roosevelt.

The White House,

August 27, 1908.
