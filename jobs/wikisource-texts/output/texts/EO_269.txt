Signed by President Theodore Roosevelt Tuesday, August 9, 1904

1905 CSR 82

614000Executive Order 269 — Authorizing Promotion of Ludwick C. Young to Clerk in Census Office Without Examination1904President of the United States

Ludwick C. Young, laborer in the Census Office, may be promoted to clerk without examination under the civil service rules in view of his former service as a clerk in that Office and as recommended by the Director of the Census.

Theodore Roosevelt.

White House,

August 9, 1904.
