613500Executive Order 197 — Authorizing Reinstatement of Stanislaus M. Hamilton as Clerk in Department of State Without Regard to Civil Service Rules1903President of the United States

White House, January 7, 1903.

The provisions of Civil Service Rule IX are hereby waived so far as may be necessary to permit the reinstatement to the position of clerk of class 2 in the Department of State of Stanislaus M. Hamilton, who resigned the position of clerk of class 3 in that Department on April 1, 1901.

Theodore Roosevelt.
