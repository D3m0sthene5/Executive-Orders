Signed by President Theodore Roosevelt Wednesday, November 8, 1905

Published: General Orders, No. 9, Navy Dept., November 9, 1905  

616005Executive Order 366-B — Authorizing Additional Compensation for Enlisted Men of the Navy Serving With Submarine Torpedo Boats1905President of the United States

Besides the $5 per month extra pay allowed them for submarine service, enlisted men serving with submarine torpedo boats, and having been reported by their commanding officers to the Navy Department as qualified for submarine torpedo boat work, shall receive $1 additional pay for each day during any part of which they shall have been submerged in a submarine torpedo boat while under way: Provided, however, That such further additional pay shall not exceed $15 in any one calendar month.

Theodore Roosevelt

The White House,

November 8, 1905.
