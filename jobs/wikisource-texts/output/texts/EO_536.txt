1906 CSR 106

619417Executive Order 536 — Authorizing Reinstatement of Alfred W. Brown in Customs Service Without Regard to Civil Service Rules1906President of the United States

Alfred W. Brown, formerly appraiser of merchandise at the port of Boston, Mass., may be reinstated in the classified Customs Service at that port without compliance with the civil service rules.

Theodore Roosevelt.

The White House,

December 11, 1906.
