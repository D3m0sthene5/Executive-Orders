626585Executive Order 719 — Authorizing Transfer of William McCambridge to Competitive Position in Interstate Commerce Commission Without Examination1907President of the United States

Mr. William McCambridge, of Illinois, may be transferred without examination from the excepted to the competitive class in the Interstate Commerce Commission.

Theodore Roosevelt.

The White House,

December 13, 1907.
