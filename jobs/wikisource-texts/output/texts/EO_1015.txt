Signed by President Theodore Roosevelt Wednesday, January 27, 1909

592009Executive Order 1015 — Authorizing Reinstatement of Edwin W. Hutcheson in Classified Service Upon Passing Examination1909President of the United States

Mr. Edwin W. Hutcheson may be reinstated in the classified service in any department or independent executive establishment, subject to his passing an appropriate examination.

Theodore Roosevelt.

The White House

January 27, 1909.
