1330286Executive Order 1676 — Authorizing Reinstatement of William B. Kilpatrick to Competitive Service Without Regard to Civil Service Rules1913President of the United States

Mr. William B. Kilpatrick may be restored to the competitive service without regard to the year limitation upon reinstatement.

Wm. H. Taft.

The White House,

January 8, 1913.
