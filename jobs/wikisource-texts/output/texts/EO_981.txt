Signed by President Theodore Roosevelt Wednesday, November 25, 1908

591982Executive Order 981 — Authorizing Appointment of Stephen C. Stuntz as Soil Bibliographer in Soils Bureau1908President of the United States

Mr. Stephen C. Stuntz may be appointed a soil bibliographer in the Bureau of Soils, Department of Agriculture, without reference to the provisions of the civil-service act and rules.

Theodore Roosevelt.

The White House

November 25, 1908.
