Signed by President Theodore Roosevelt Wednesday, December 9, 1908

591986Executive Order 991 — Authorizing Appointment of Georgia M. Sargent to Position in Classified Service1908President of the United States

Mrs. Georgia M. Sargent, widow of the late Commissioner-General of Immigration, Frank P. Sargent, may be appointed to a position in the classified service without examination under the civil-service rules.

Theodore Roosevelt.

The White House

December 9, 1908.
