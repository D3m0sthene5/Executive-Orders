619117Executive Order 448 — Authorizing Appointment of Harry E. Burns as Fireman Without Regard to Civil Service Rules1906President of the United States

Harry E. Burns may be appointed as fireman in any Department at Washington. Mr. Burns was honorably discharged with an excellent record as fireman in the United States Navy, where he showed himself to be a brave man. Mr. Burns has been twice certified under the civil service rules for the position of fireman outside of the District of Columbia.

Theodore Roosevelt.

The White House,

May 21, 1906.
