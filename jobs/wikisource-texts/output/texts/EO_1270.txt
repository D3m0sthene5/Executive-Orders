649813Executive Order 1270 — Authorizing Reinstatement of Charles M. Irelan as Clerk in War Department Without Regard to Civil Service Rules1910President of the United States

Mr. Charles M. Irelan may be reinstated as a clerk at $900 per annum in the office of the Chief of Engineers, War Department, notwithstanding the fact that somewhat more than a year elapsed between the date of his separation and the date of request for a certificate for his reinstatement.

Wm. H. Taft.

The White House,

December 12, 1910.
