Signed by President William H. Taft Wednesday, January 24, 1912

1912 CSR 143

826935Executive Order 1466 — Authorizing Reinstatement of Pembrook B. Banton as Clerk in Isthmian Canal Commission Without Regard to Civil Service Rules1912President of the United States

Mr. Pembrook B. Banton may be reinstated as a clerk at $900 per annum in the service of the Isthmian Canal Commission when he shall have demonstrated his fitness by passing an examination deemed appropriate by the Civil Service Commission; and he may be subsequently transferred to any position for which he is qualified and for the performance of whose duties he is not incapacitated by physical disability.

Wm. H. Taft.

The White House,

January 24, 1912.
