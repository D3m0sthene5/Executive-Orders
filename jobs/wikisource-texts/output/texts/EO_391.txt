618891Executive Order 391 — Authorizing Permanent Appointment of Ralph Whitman as Draftsman in Isthmian Canal Commission Without Regard to Civil Service Rules1906President of the United States

Mr. Ralph Whitman may be permanently appointed to the position of engineer draftsman in the office of the Isthmian Canal Commission, where he is at present temporarily employed.

Theodore Roosevelt.

The White House,

January 8, 1906.
