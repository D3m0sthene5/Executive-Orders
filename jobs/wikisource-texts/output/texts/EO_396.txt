Authorizing Reinstatement of Jeannette L. Cavanagh as Classified Laborer in Department of Agriculture Without Regard to Civil Service Rules

Undated endorsement on a letter from Senator Knute Nelson to the President; the appointment itself occurred on January 22, 1906.

Signed by President Theodore Roosevelt January 1906

1906 CSR 96

618909Executive Order 396 — Authorizing Reinstatement of Jeannette L. Cavanagh as Classified Laborer in Department of Agriculture Without Regard to Civil Service Rules1906President of the United States

This is the Civil Service Commission's description of the order, not the actual text, which took the form of an endorsement of a Senator's letter.

By direction of the President the Commission allowed the reinstatement of Mrs. Jeannette L. Cavanagh to the position of classified laborer in the Department of Agriculture.
