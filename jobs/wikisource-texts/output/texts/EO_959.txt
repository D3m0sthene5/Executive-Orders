636232Executive Order 959 — Authorizing Reinstatement of Mary A. Eldridge as Clerk in War Department Without Regard to Civil Service Rules1908President of the United States

Mrs. Mary A. Eldridge may be reinstated to a clerical position in the office of the Auditor for the War Department on the basis of her former service in the clerical grade in the Patent Office.

Theodore Roosevelt.

The White House,

October 26, 1908.
