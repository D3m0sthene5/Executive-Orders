Amends Executive Order 209, March 20, 1903.

Signed by President William H. Taft Tuesday, June 29, 1909

591644Executive Order 1102 — Amending Civil Service Rules Regarding Exceptions for Temporary Acting Assistant Surgeons1909President of the United States

Paragraph 7, Subdivision II, Schedule A, of the civil-service rules, relating to the Treasury Department, is amended by adding the words "at stations or localities where, in the opinion of the Commission, the establishment of registers is impracticable." The paragraph as amended will read:

 Any local physician employed for temporary duty as acting assistant surgeon in the Public Health and Marine-Hospital Service at stations or localities where, in the opinion of the Commission, the establishment of registers is impracticable.

Wm. H. Taft.

The White House,

June 29, 1909.
