1329772Executive Order 1575 — Authorizing Reinstatement of George H. Getz as to Pension Office Without Regard to Civil Service Rules1912President of the United States

Mr. George H. Getz may be reinstated to an appropriate position in the Pension Office without regard to the length of his separation from the service.

Wm. H. Taft.

The White House,

August 10, 1912.
