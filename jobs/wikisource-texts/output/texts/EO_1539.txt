Executive Order 1539 (1912)

by President of the United States
Deep Creek Band - Reserving Lands in Tooele County, Utah, for Use by Indians on Public Domain

Superseded by Executive Order 1903, March 23, 1914. Also see Executive Order 1465, January 17, 1912.

Signed by President William H. Taft Wednesday, May 29, 1912

1328977Executive Order 1539 — Deep Creek Band - Reserving Lands in Tooele County, Utah, for Use by Indians on Public Domain1912President of the United States

It is hereby ordered that the SW. ¼ of the NE. ¼, the SE. ¼ of the NW. ¼, the NW. ¼ of the SE. ¼, and the NE. ¼ of the SW. ¼ of sec. 8, and the S. ½ of the SW. ¼ of sec. 9, T. 11 S., R. 19 W. of the Salt Lake meridian, in the county of Tooele, State of Utah, be, and the same are hereby, reserved from settlement, entry, sale, or other disposal and set aside for school, agency, and other necessary uses for the benefit of Indians on the public domain in the State of Utah, subject, however, to any valid existing rights of any persons thereto.

Wm. H. Taft.

The White House,

May 29, 1912.
