Granddaughter of President John Tyler

Signed by President Theodore Roosevelt Tuesday, June 11, 1907

1907 CSR 124

625823Executive Order 652 — Authorizing Appointment of Mattie R. Tyler as Clerk in Post Office at Norfolk, Virginia, Without Regard to Civil Service Rules1907President of the United States

It is hereby ordered, That Miss Mattie R. Tyler be appointed a clerk at $800 per annum in the post-office at Norfolk, Va., and that in her case the usual civil-service requirements as regards eligibility be waived.

Theodore Roosevelt.

The White House,

June 11, 1907.
