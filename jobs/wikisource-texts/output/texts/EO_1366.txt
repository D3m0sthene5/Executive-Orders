820438Executive Order 1366 — Authorizing Appointment of George E. Pickett as Clerk in War Department Without Regard to Civil Service Rules1911President of the United States

Mrs. George E. Pickett may be appointed to a clerkship in the War Department without regard to the provisions of the civil-service rules.

Wm. H. Taft.

The White House,

May 31, 1911.
