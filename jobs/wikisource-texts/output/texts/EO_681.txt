625855Executive Order 681 — Authorizing Appointment of W. Dawson Johnston as Compiler of Statistics in Bureau of Education Without Regard to Civil Service Rules1907President of the United States

Mr. W. Dawson Johnston, of Vermont, who has been employed in the Library of Congress for several years, may be transferred to the position of collector and compiler of statistics, for duty also as librarian, in the Bureau of Education, Department of the Interior, without reference to the civil-service rules.

Theodore Roosevelt.

The White House,

August 14, 1907.
