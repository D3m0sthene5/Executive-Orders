625803Executive Order 5152 — Approving Recommendation for Reinstatement of Mrs. Lulu M. Davis in the Bureau of Engraving and Printing Without Regard to Length of Separation from Service1929President of the United States

Mrs. Lulu M. Davis may be reinstated as an operative in the Bureau of Engraving and Printing without regard to the length of time she has been separated from the service.

Mrs. Davis was employed as a classified printer's assistant in the Bureau from December 17, 1906, to September 14, 1915, and is the widow of August Davis, a plate printer in the Bureau for nearly 22 years prior to his death on June 18, 1922.

This order is recommended by the Secretary of the Treasury.

Herbert Hoover

The White House,

July 8, 1929.
