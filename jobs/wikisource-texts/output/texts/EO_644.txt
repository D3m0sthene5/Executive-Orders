625817Executive Order 644 — Authorizing Appointment of Terence V. Powderly as Chief of Division of Information in Bureau of Immigration and Naturalization Without Examination1907President of the United States

Mr. Terence V. Powderly, of Washington, D. C., may be appointed chief of the Division of Information, in the Bureau of Immigration and Naturalization, Department of Commerce and Labor, without examination under the civil-service rules.

Theodore Roosevelt.

The White House,

June 5, 1907.
