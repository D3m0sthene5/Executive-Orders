616210Executive Order 352 — Authorizing Appointment of John B. Goff and D. P. Wells as Assistant Forest Rangers in Forest Service Without Examination1905President of the United States

Mr. John B. Goff and Mr. D. P. Wells may be appointed as assistant forest rangers, Forest Service, Department of Agriculture, without examination under the civil service rules.

Theodore Roosevelt.

White House,

September 19, 1905.
