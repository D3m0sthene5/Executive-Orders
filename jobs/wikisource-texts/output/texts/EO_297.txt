614559Executive Order 297 — Authorizing Reinstatement of John Nolan as Carrier in Postal Service Without Regard to Civil Service Rules1905President of the United States

Mr. John Nolan may be reinstated as a carrier in the Postal Service without regard to the limitations prescribed by the civil service rules.

Theodore Roosevelt.

White House,

March 2, 1905.
