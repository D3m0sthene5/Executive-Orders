Signed by President William McKinley Monday, October 10, 1898

605557Executive Order 108 — Amending Consular Regulations Pertaining to Accounts of Consular Agents1898President of the United States

Paragraph 576 of the Consular Regulations is hereby amended so as to read as follows:

Consular Agents will be governed by the foregoing requirements in relation to official services and will render their quarterly reports in accordance with the prescribed forms to the principal Consular Officer who will transmit the same to the Auditor for the State and other Departments.

The amounts which may be found due at the Treasury on account of services rendered to American vessels and seamen will in all cases be sent by Treasury Warrant to the address of and payable to the order of the officer entitled thereto.

Forms Nos. 190 and 191 are established in full force and authority as parts of the Consular Regulations of September 30, 1898.

William McKinley.

Executive Mansion,

Washington, October 10, 1898.
