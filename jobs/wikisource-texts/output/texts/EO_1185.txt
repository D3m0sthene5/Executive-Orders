Amends Executive Order 748, February 4, 1908. Superseded by Executive Order 1709, February 13, 1913.

Signed by President William H. Taft Monday, April 4, 1910

1910 CSR 107

648535Executive Order 1185 — Amending Civil Service Rules Regarding Exceptions from Examination in Treasury Department1910President of the United States

Paragraph 6 of Subdivision III of Schedule A of classified positions excepted from examination under the Treasury Department, which reads: "One private secretary or confidential clerk to the superintendent, and one cashier in each mint or assay office," is hereby amended to read as follows: "One private secretary or confidential clerk to the superintendent and one cashier in each mint, and one cashier in the assay office at New York."

Wm. H. Taft.

The White House,

April 4, 1910.
