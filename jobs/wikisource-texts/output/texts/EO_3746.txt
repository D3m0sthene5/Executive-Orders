Signed by President Warren G. Harding Saturday, October 7, 1922

605398Executive Order 3746 — Directing All Requests and Petitions Under Tariff Act be Referred to United States Tariff Commission1922President of the United States

It is ordered, that all requests, applications, or petitions for action or relief under the provisions of Sections 315, 316, and 317 of Title III of the Tariff Act approved September 21, 1922, shall be filed with or referred to the United States Tariff Commission for consideration and for such investigation as shall be in accordance with law and the public interest, under rules and regulations to be prescribed by such Commission.

Warren G. Harding

The White House,

October 7, 1922.
