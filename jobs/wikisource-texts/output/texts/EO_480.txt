1906 CSR 94

618569Executive Order 480 — Classifying Certain Laborers in the Bureau of Fisheries1906President of the United States

Laborers appointed without examination to statutory positions in the field service of the Bureau of Fisheries, who have been assigned to fish-cultural duties, and whose names appear on the list furnished the Civil Service Commission, are hereby classified, and may be transferred and promoted in accordance with the civil service rules and regulations.

Theodore Roosevelt.

The White House,

July 17, 1906.
