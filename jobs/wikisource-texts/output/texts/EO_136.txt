Amends Executive Order 128, March 29, 1900.

Signed by President William McKinley Tuesday, February 26, 1901

610544Executive Order 136 — Amending Civil Service Rules Regarding Age Limitations in Light-House Service1901President of the United States

Rule V, clause 4, in the table fixing age limitations, under the heading "Departmental Service," is hereby amended by striking out the word "light-ships" and inserting in lieu thereof the words "light-house tenders and light vessels;" so that as amended the clause will read:

Keeper, assistant keeper, and officers of light-house tenders and light vessels in the Light-House Service.

Approved.

William McKinley.

Executive Mansion,

February 26, 1901.
