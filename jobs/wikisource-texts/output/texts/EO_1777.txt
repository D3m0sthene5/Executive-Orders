Signed by President Woodrow Wilson Wednesday, May 7, 1913

1913 CSR 113

2000322Executive Order 1777 — Amending Civil Service Rules to Omit Chief Post-Office Inspector from Excepted Positions1913President of the United States

The list of classified positions excepted from examination under civil-service Rule II, clause 3, is hereby amended so as to omit the position of chief post-office inspector in the Post Office Department.

Woodrow Wilson.

The White House

May 7, 1913.
