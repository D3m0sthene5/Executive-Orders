625544Executive Order 581 — Authorizing Appointment of John C. Eversman, Sr. to Competitive Position in Pension Office Without Regard to Civil Service Rules1907President of the United States

Mr. John C. Eversman, sr., a special examiner in the Pension Office, may be appointed or transferred to a competitive position in that office.

Theodore Roosevelt.

The White House,

March 7, 1907.
