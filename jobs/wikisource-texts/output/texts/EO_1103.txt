Signed by President William H. Taft Tuesday, June 29, 1909

591887Executive Order 1103 — Authorizing Classification of Certain Unclassified Watchmen in the Customs Service1909President of the United States

The unclassified laborers employed in the Customs Service at the port of New York whose names appear upon a list of persons recommended for classification as watchmen by the Treasury Department with the approval of the Civil Service Commission may be classified as watchmen at $960 per annum, but shall not be eligible for transfer or promotion beyond the watchman force without examination. This order is made upon the recommendation of the Civil Service Commission.

Wm. H. Taft.

The White House,

June 29, 1909.
