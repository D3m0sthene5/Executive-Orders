Amends Executive Order 209, March 20, 1903.

Signed by President Theodore Roosevelt Thursday, March 30, 1905

1905 CSR 74

615082Executive Order 316 — Civil Service Rules Amended to Except Certain Interior Department Positions from Examination1905President of the United States

Schedule A of the civil service rules is hereby amended by adding to Division VI the following:

Consulting engineers of the Reclamation Service under the Geological Survey.

One confidential clerk and one record clerk to the Superintendent of the Government Hospital for the Insane.

Theodore Roosevelt.

White House,

March 30, 1905.
