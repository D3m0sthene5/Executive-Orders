Subdivision II was originally added to Schedule A in late 1909 for excepted positions resulting from Executive Order 950.

Signed by President William H. Taft Saturday, August 24, 1912

1912 CSR 134

821763Executive Order 1582 — Amending Civil Service Rules Regarding State Department Positions1912President of the United States

Subdivision II of Schedule A relating to the State Department is hereby amended to read as follows:

Eight officers to aid in important drafting work.

Assistant solicitors.

Those 14 persons now employed in the Department of State under the item "Advancement of commercial and other interests as provided in the act approved August 5, 1909" contained in the act approved March 4, 1911, may be appointed to competitive classified positions in that department upon meeting such tests of fitness as the Civil Service Commission may prescribe.

Wm. H. Taft.

The White House,

August 24, 1912.
