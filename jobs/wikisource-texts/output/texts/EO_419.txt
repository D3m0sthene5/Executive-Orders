618932Executive Order 419 — Authorizing Appointment of Katherine Moynihan in Post Office Department Without Examination1906President of the United States

Mrs. Katherine Moynihan may be appointed to a position in the Post-Office Department without examination under the civil-service rules.

Theodore Roosevelt.

The White House,

February 27, 1906.
