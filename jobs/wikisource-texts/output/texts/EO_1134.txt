Signed by President William H. Taft Tuesday, October 19, 1909

General Orders, No. 221, War Dept., November 4, 1909

1585888Executive Order 1134 — Transferring Military Reservation in Sangley Point, Luzon, Philippine Islands, to Navy Department1909President of the United States

The military reservation of about 2 acres, situated within the limits of the naval reservation on Sangley Point, Island of Luzon, Philippine Islands, which military reservation was made by Executive Order of March 25, 1904, as amended by Executive Order of September 23, 1904 (General Orders No. 158, War Department, September 30, 1904), and has become useless for the purposes for which reserved, is hereby transferred to the Navy Department to become a part of said naval reservation.

Wm. H. Taft.

The White House,

October 19, 1909.
