625834Executive Order 662 — Authorizing Transfer of Horace W. Mitchell to Clerk in Marine Corps Office Without Regard to Civil Service Rules1907President of the United States

Mr. Horace W. Mitchell may be transferred from the excepted position of clerk at $1,400 per annum in the pay department, Marine Corps, Philippine Islands, to the position of clerk at $1,400 per annum in the office of the assistant paymaster, U. S. Marine Corps, Berkeley, Cal., a competitive classified position, without reference to the civil-service rules.

Theodore Roosevelt.

The White House,

July 4, 1907.
