Signed by President Theodore Roosevelt Thursday, August 29, 1907

General Orders No. 189, War Dept., September 11, 1907

629311Executive Order 685 — Reserving Additional Lands for Fort Sill Military Reservation, Oklahoma Territory1907President of the United States

It is hereby ordered that the public lands, hereinafter described, be withdrawn from sale or other disposition and reserved and set apart for military purposes as a portion of the Fort Sill Military Reservation, Oklahoma Territory, viz:

Lots 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, and 7, the SE. ¼ of the SE. ¼, the W. ½ of the SE. ¼, and the E. ½ of the SW. ¼; section 18, township 2 north, range 11 west, Indian meridian, Oklahoma Territory. Said lands contain, in the aggregate, 372.45 acres.

T. Roosevelt.

The White House,

August 29, 1907.
