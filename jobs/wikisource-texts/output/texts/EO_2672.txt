Amends Executive Order 2319, February 16, 1916

Signed by President Woodrow Wilson Monday, July 23, 1917

U.S. Official Bulletin No. 68, p. 4, July 30, 1917

597451Executive Order 2672 — Restoring to Entry and Settlement Certain Lands Reserved for Townsite Purposes by Executive Order of Feb. 16, 19161917President of the United States

It is hereby ordered that the lands hereinafter described be, and the same are hereby, eliminated from the operation of Executive Order No. 2319, dated February 16, 1916, withdrawing and reserving said lands for townsite purposes under the act of Congress approved March 12, 1914 (38 Stat., 305); and that the same be restored to entry and settlement on such date hereafter as may be fixed by the Secretary of the Interior.

Description.—Entire sections 3, 4, and 9, and the NE. ¼ and the SE. ¼ and the SW. ¼ of section 10, T. 17 N., R. 1 W., Seward meridian, Alaska.

This order shall not affect any other lands withdrawn by said Executive Order No. 2319.

Woodrow Wilson.

The White House,

23 July, 1917.
