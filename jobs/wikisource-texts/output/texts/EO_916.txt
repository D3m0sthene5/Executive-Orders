636206Executive Order 916 — Authorizing Reinstatement of George Terwilliger in Government Printing Office Without Regard to Civil Service Rules1908President of the United States

George Terwilliger may be reinstated in the Government Printing Office without regard to the length of his separation from the service.

Theodore Roosevelt.

The White House,

July 16, 1908.
