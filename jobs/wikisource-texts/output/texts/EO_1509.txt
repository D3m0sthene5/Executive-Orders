826965Executive Order 1509 — Authorizing Appointment of Andrew M. Latham as Clerk in Department of Agriculture Without Regard to Civil Service Rules1912President of the United States

Mr. Andrew M. Latham may be appointed to the position of clerk in the Department of Agriculture without reference to the provisions of the civil-service rules.

Wm. H. Taft.

The White House,

March 30, 1912.
