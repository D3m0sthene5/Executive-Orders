Amends Executive Order 209, March 20, 1903. Amended by Executive Order 733, January 13, 1908, and Executive Order 1408, September 4, 1911.

Signed by President Theodore Roosevelt Tuesday, October 31, 1905

1905 CSR 76

616257Executive Order 364 — Amending Civil Service Rules to Except Certain Temporary Workers in Bureau of Fisheries from Examination1905President of the United States

Schedule A, of positions excepted from examination under Rule II, clause 3, is amended as follows:

IX. Department of Commerce and Labor.

All persons temporarily connected with the field operations of the Bureau of Fisheries, who are paid from lump appropriations for miscellaneous expenses. No person employed in a position, specifically provided for by statute at any station, shall be regarded as excepted from examination hereunder.

Theodore Roosevelt.

White House,

October 31, 1905.
