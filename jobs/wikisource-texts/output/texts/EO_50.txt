Signed by President Grover Cleveland Friday, November 2, 1894

609614Executive Order 50 — Amending Civil Service Rules to Allow Temporary Appointments for Certain Vacancies Pending Regular Appointments1894President of the United States

Departmental Rule VII, clause 1, is hereby amended by inserting at the end of line six the following:

Vacancies in places authorized to be filled by noncompetitive examination may be filled without examination, for a period not exceeding thirty days, until a regular appointment can be made upon certification by the Commission.

Every such appointment, and the reasons therefor, shall be at once reported to the Commission.

Approved.

Grover Cleveland.

Executive Mansion,

November 2, 1894.
