Amended by Proclamation 1107, December 16, 1910. Revoked by Executive Order 3317, August 7, 1920.

Signed by President William H. Taft Monday, April 26, 1909

651981Executive Order 1069 — Establishing Sopris National Forest, Colorado, from Portion of Holy Cross National Forest1909President of the United States

It is hereby ordered that on and after this date, the land within the boundary shown on the attached diagram, heretofore a part of the Holy Cross National Forest, shall be known as the Sopris National Forest. It is not intended by this order to release any land from reservation or to reserve any land not heretofore embraced in a National Forest.

Wm. H. Taft.

The White House,

April 26, 1909.
