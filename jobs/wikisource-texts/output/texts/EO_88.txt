Signed by President Grover Cleveland Saturday, March 28, 1896

610089Executive Order 88 — Amending Civil Service Rules Making Board of Pension Appeals in the Department of the Interior Subject to Competitive Examination1896President of the United States

Special Order Under General Rule III.

So much of Executive orders heretofore issued under General Rule III, section 2, clause (c), as provides for the appointment of members of the Board of Pension Appeals in the Department of the Interior by noncompetitive examination is hereby revoked, and these places will hereafter be treated as subject to competitive examination.

Approved.

Grover Cleveland.

Executive Mansion,

March 28, 1896.
