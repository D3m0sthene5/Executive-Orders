626546Executive Order 696 — Authorizing Transfer of Charles E. Knight to Shipping Commissioner in San Francisco Without Regard to Civil Service Rules1907President of the United States

Charles E. Knight, at present a bank examiner, may be transferred from his position and appointed shipping commissioner at the port of San Francisco, Cal., another position under the Government service without examination and certification under the civil-service rules. There is now no register of eligibles for this position.

Theodore Roosevelt.

The White House,

September 28, 1907.
