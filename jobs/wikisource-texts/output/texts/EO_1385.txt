826608Executive Order 1385 — Authorizing Reinstatement of Charles L. Dooley Without Regard to Civil Service Rules1911President of the United States

Mr. Charles L. Dooley, formerly an immigrant inspector in the Immigration Service, who served faithfully for more than three years, may be reinstated without regard to the fact that he has been separated from the service for more than a year.

Wm. H. Taft.

The White House,

July 14, 1911.
