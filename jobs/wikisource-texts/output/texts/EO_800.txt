636181Executive Order 800 — Authorizing Employment of John M. Jones as Tariff Expert in Interstate Commerce Commission Without Examination1908President of the United States

Mr. John M. Jones may be employed as an expert familiar with modern tariff and traffic conditions in the Interstate Commerce Commission without examination under the civil-service rules. This employment may be terminated at any time in the discretion of the Interstate Commerce Commission.

Theodore Roosevelt.

The White House,

May 26, 1908.
