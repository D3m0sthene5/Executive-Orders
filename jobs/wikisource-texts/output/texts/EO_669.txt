625841Executive Order 669 — Authorizing Appointment of Alice V. Fought as Clerk in Treasury Department Without Regard to Civil Service Rules1907President of the United States

Miss Alice V. Fought may be appointed a clerk in the Treasury Department without compliance with civil-service rules.

Theodore Roosevelt.

The White House,

July 25, 1907.
