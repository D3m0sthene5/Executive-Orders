1907 CSR 131

626561Executive Order 710 — Authorizing Appointment of Joseph B. Dalton as Skilled Laborer in Bureau of the Census Without Examination1907President of the United States

Joseph B. Dalton may be appointed to the position of skilled laborer in the Bureau of the Census without examination under the civil-service rules.

Theodore Roosevelt.

The White House,

November 9, 1907.
