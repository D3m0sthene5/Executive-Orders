636198Executive Order 830 — Authorizing Appointment of Basil Miles as Superintendent in Post Office Department Without Examination1908President of the United States

Mr. Basil Miles may be appointed as superintendent of the division of foreign mails in the Post-Office Department without examination under the civil-service rules.

Theodore Roosevelt.

The White House,

June 24, 1908.
