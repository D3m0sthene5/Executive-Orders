1907 CSR 128

625957Executive Order 686 — Authorizing Appointment of Four Employees as Clerks in War Department Without Regard to Civil Service Rules1907President of the United States

Mrs. Etta A. Giers and Mrs. Lyda M. Stansell, widows of deceased clerks in the War Department, Mrs. Ella Laing, an excepted employee of the army transport service, and Miss Emma Washburn, a temporary employee in the Quartermaster-General's Office, may be appointed to clerical positions in the War Department without reference to the civil-service rules.

Theodore Roosevelt.

The White House,

September 2, 1907.
