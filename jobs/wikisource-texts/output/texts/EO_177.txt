Amends Executive Order 89, May 6, 1896.

Signed by President Theodore Roosevelt Thursday, July 3, 1902

611135Executive Order 177 — Amending Civil Service Rules Regarding Recommendations for Promotion1902President of the United States

In the exercise of the power vested in the President by the Constitution, and by virtue of the seventeen hundred and fifty-third section of the Revised Statutes, and of the civil-service act approved January 16, 1883, Civil-Service Rule XI is hereby amended by adding at the end thereof the following section:

No recommendation for the promotion of any employee in the classified service shall be considered by any officer concerned in making promotions except it be made by the officer or officers under whose supervision or control such employee is serving; and such recommendation by any other person, with the knowledge and consent of the employee, shall be sufficient cause for debarring him from the promotion proposed, and a repetition of the offense shall be sufficient cause for removing him from the service.

Approved.

Theodore Roosevelt.

White House,

July 3, 1902.
