649148Executive Order 1162 — Authorizing Reinstatement of John H. Dickinson as Inspector in Quartermaster's Department of the Army Without Regard to Civil Service Rules1910President of the United States

John H. Dickinson, formerly employed as an inspector in the Quartermaster's Department of the Army, who was dismissed more than one year ago on account of alleged irregularities which were afterwards found to be groundless, may be reinstated as an inspector without regard to the provision of the civil-service rules limiting reinstatement to one year after separation.

Wm. H. Taft.

The White House,

February 4, 1910.
