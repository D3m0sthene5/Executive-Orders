614319Executive Order 287 — Amending Civil Service Rules to Except from Examination a Consulting Architect in the War Department for Work on the United States Military Academy1904President of the United States

Schedule A, classified positions excepted from examination under Rule II, clause 3, is hereby amended by adding, under the subheading "War Department," a clause as follows:

Consulting architect, for work of reconstructing United States Military Academy, at West Point, New York.

Theodore Roosevelt.

White House,

December 24, 1904.
