Signed by President Theodore Roosevelt Wednesday, November 9, 1904

613943Executive Order 272 — Authorizing Appointment of Luther Conant, Jr., as Special Examiner in Bureau of Corporations Without Examination1904President of the United States

Mr. Luther Conant, jr., may be appointed as a special examiner in the Bureau of Corporations, at $2,500 per annum, without examination under the civil service rules.

Theodore Roosevelt.

White House,

November 9, 1904.
