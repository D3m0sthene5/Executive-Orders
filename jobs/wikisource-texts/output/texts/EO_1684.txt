1330291Executive Order 1684 — Authorizing Appointment of Mrs. Ollie M. Croghan as Clerk in Department of Justice Without Regard to Civil Service Rules1913President of the United States

Mrs. Ollie M. Croghan may be appointed to a position in the clerical grade in the Department of Justice.

Wm. H. Taft.
