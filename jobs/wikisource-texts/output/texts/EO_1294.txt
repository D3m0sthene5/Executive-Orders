1910 CSR 116

650403Executive Order 1294 — Authorizing Appointment of Edith F. Spofford as Clerk in Bureau of Mines Without Regard to Civil Service Rules1911President of the United States

Mrs. Edith F. Spofford may be appointed from the Library of Congress, the employees of which are not classified, to a clerical position in the Bureau of Mines, to take charge of the library of that bureau.

Wm. H. Taft.

The White House,

January 30, 1911.
