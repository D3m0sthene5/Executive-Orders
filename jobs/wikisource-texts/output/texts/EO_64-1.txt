Amends Executive Order 64, May 24, 1895. The date of the order is for the approval; the letterhead has May 16.

Signed by President Grover Cleveland Friday, May 24, 1895

609785Executive Order 64-1 — Amending Civil Service Rules to Except from Examination the Chief of the Dairy Division in Department of Agriculture1895President of the United States

Executive Mansion,

Washington, D. C., May 16, 1895.

Special departmental rule No. 1 is hereby amended as follows: 

Include among the places excepted from examination therein the following: 

6. In the Department of Agriculture: The chief of the dairy division.

Approved, May 24, 1895. 

Grover Cleveland, President.
