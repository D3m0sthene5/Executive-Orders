592319Executive Order 1063 — Authorizing Appointment of Archibald S. Pinkett as Clerk in State Department Without Examination1909President of the United States

Mr. Archibald S. Pinkett, of Pennsylvania, may be appointed to a clerkship in the Department of State without examination under the civil-service act and rules. This order is made at the request and upon the recommendation of the Secretary of State, who has knowledge of Mr. Pinkett's fitness from his clerical service under the Secretary while he was a Senator in Congress.

Wm. H. Taft.

The White House,

April 16, 1909.
