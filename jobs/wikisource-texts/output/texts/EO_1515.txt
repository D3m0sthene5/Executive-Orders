826972Executive Order 1515 — Authorizing Appointment of Margaret C. Leonard as Clerk in War Department Without Regard to Civil Service Rules1912President of the United States

Miss Margaret C. Leonard may be appointed as a clerk in the War Department without reference to the civil-service rules involved.

Wm. H. Taft.

The White House,

April 9, 1912.
