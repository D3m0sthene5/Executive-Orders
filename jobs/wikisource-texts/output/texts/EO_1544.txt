826997Executive Order 1544 — Authorizing Appointment of May Stanley in Classified Service Without Examination1912President of the United States

Mrs. May Stanley may be appointed to a position in the classified service without examination under the civil-service rules.

Wm. H. Taft.

The White House,

June 11, 1912.
