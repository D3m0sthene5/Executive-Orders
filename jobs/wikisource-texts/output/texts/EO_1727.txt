1330352Executive Order 1727 — Authorizing Transfer of William F. Smith to Classified Position Without Regard to Civil Service Rules1913President of the United States

Mr. William F. Smith, now assistant chief messenger, unclassified, in the office of the Secretary of the Treasury, may be transferred to the classified executive civil service without compliance with the civil-service rules.

Wm. H. Taft.

The White House,

February 28, 1913.
