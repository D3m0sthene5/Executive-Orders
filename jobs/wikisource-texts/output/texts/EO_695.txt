626544Executive Order 695 — Authorizing Transfer of Charles M. Irelan to Clerk in War Department Without Regard to Civil Service Rules1907President of the United States

Charles M. Irelan may be transferred from the Patent Office to a clerical position in the War Department without reference to the six months' limitation upon transfers after reinstatement.

Theodore Roosevelt.

The White House,

September 28, 1907.
