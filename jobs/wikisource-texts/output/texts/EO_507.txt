619324Executive Order 507 — Authorizing Reinstatement of Joseph Tyssowski in Interior Department Without Regard to Civil Service Rules1906President of the United States

By reason of his special knowledge of the public land laws and his long practice therein, Joseph Tyssowski may be reinstated in the classified service of the Interior Department, notwithstanding the time limit.

Theodore Roosevelt.

The White House,

September 26, 1906.
