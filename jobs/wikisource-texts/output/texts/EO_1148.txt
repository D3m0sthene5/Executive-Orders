Supplements Executive Order 1100, June 28, 1909; these two employees were inadvertently omitted from the list associated with that order.

Signed by President William H. Taft Tuesday, December 21, 1909

591977Executive Order 1148 — Permanent Appointment of Two Temporary Unclassified Laborers in the Customs Service in New York1909President of the United States

Messrs. Fred. Glavin and Fred. Liese, temporary unclassified laborers in the Customs Service at the port of New York, N. Y., who were appointed without reference to the laborer register, may be given the status of permanent unclassified laborers. This order is made upon the suggestion of the Treasury Department and the recommendation of the Civil Service Commission.

Wm. H. Taft.

The White House,

December 21, 1909.
