1909 CSR 116

591877Executive Order 1033 — Making Ten Employees of the Spanish Treaty Claims Commission Eligible for Transfer to Competitive Service1909President of the United States

The ten employees of the Spanish Treaty Claims Commission, who have been specially commended for retention in the public service by the president of that commission, may be transferred to the competitive classified service, no such transfer to be made until after appropriate noncompetitive examination and approval by the Civil Service Commission.

Theodore Roosevelt.

The White House

February 26, 1909.
