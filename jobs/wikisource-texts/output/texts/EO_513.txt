619407Executive Order 513 — Authorizing Retention of Joe Osscar as Engineer in Leech Lake Indian Agency Without Examination1906President of the United States

Joe Osscar may be retained as engineer at the Leech Lake Indian Agency, Minn., without examination under the civil service rules.

Theodore Roosevelt.

The White House,

October 5, 1906.
