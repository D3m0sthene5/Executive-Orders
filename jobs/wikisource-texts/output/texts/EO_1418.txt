Signed by President William H. Taft Monday, October 2, 1911

1912 CSR 142

826627Executive Order 1418 — Authorizing Appointment of Edwin M. Hamilton as Lumberman in Indian Service Without Regard to Civil Service Rules1911President of the United States

Edwin M. Hamilton of New York may be appointed lumberman in the Indian Service at large, without reference to paragraph (a), civil service rule No. IX, relating to reinstatement, he having resigned February 28, 1909, from the position of supervisor in the Forest Service, in charge of the Black Hills National Park, with headquarters at Deadwood, S. Dak.

Wm. H. Taft.

The White House,

October 2, 1911.
