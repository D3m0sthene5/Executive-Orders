Amends Executive Order 127, March 16, 1900.

Signed by President William McKinley Tuesday, April 17, 1900

1901 CSR 296

610448Executive Order 129 — Amending Civil Service Rules Regarding Transfer from Excepted to Non-Excepted Positions1900President of the United States

Section 4 of Rule X of the Civil Service Rules, as amended March 16, 1900, is hereby further amended by inserting in line 4, after the word "entered," the words "or held;" so that the second clause of the proviso to said section of said rule shall read: "or which he entered or held in accordance with the provisions of these rules at a time when said position was subject to competitive examination, and has since served continuously therein," etc.

William McKinley.

Executive Mansion,

April 17, 1900.
