General Orders, No. 14, War Dept., January 25, 1910 External Information about this EO  

576780Executive Order 1154 — Military Reservations Restored to Government of the Philippine Islands1910President of the United States

The following military reservations, made by Executive Order dated September 1, 1903 (General Orders No. 34, War Department, October 13, 1903), having become useless for military purposes, and having been abandoned as military stations, they are hereby restored to the control of the Government of the Philippine Islands, under the provisions of Section 12 of the Act of Congress approved July 1, 1902, entitled ‘‘An act temporarily to provide for the administration of the affairs of civil government in the Philippine Islands, and for other purposes,’’ (32 Stat. L., 691), namely:

The reservation at Bacon, Province of Sorsogon, Island of Luzon, comprising an area of 32.50 hectares (about 80.30 acres).

The reservation at Nueva Caceres. Province of Camarines Sur, Island of Luzon, comprising an area of about 87.01 hectares (about 215 acres).

The reservation near Solomague, Province of Ilocos Sur, Island of Luzon, comprising an area of about 107 acres.

Wm. H. Taft

The White House,

January 13, 1910.
