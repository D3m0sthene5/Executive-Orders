1913 CSR 123

1330308Executive Order 1693 — Authorizing Appointment of Laura A. Thompson as Clerk in Children's Bureau Without Regard to Civil Service Rules1913President of the United States

Miss Laura A. Thompson may be appointed as clerk (librarian-translator) at $1,800 per annum in the Children's Bureau without reference to the civil-service rules.

Wm. H. Taft.

The White House,

January 27, 1913.
