1907 CSR 132

626586Executive Order 722 — Authorizing Extended Temporary Employment of Emily G. Stickney as Clerk Examiner in Bureau of Chemistry Without Regard to Civil Service Rules1907President of the United States

The temporary appointment of Miss Emily G. Stickney as a clerk examiner in the Bureau of Chemistry, Department of Agriculture, which will expire by limitation on December 31, 1907, may be extended for such periods as may be necessary to make an appointment from the editorial clerk examination, which is to be held on January 8 and 9, 1908.

Theodore Roosevelt.

The White House,

December 17, 1907.
