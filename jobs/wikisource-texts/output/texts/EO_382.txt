1905 CSR 94

616426Executive Order 382 — Authorizing Transfer of Katherine S. Todd to Clerk in Office of Commissioner of Internal Revenue Without Regard to Civil Service Rules1905President of the United States

Miss Katherine S. Todd may be transferred from the Bureau of Engraving and Printing to the position of clerk in the office of the Commissioner of Internal Revenue without regard to the restrictions of section 7 of civil service Rule X.

Theodore Roosevelt.

The White House,

December 14, 1905.
