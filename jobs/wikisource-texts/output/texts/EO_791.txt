626865Executive Order 791 — Authorizing Appointment of Katherine J. Fenton as Draftsman in Departments of the Washington, D.C. Without Regard to Civil Service Rules1908President of the United States

The appointment of Mrs. Katherine J. Fenton to a position as departmental clerk copyist draftsman, or copyist topographic draftsman, in the departments at Washington is hereby permitted without compliance with the civil-service rules.

Theodore Roosevelt.

The White House,

May 1, 1908.
