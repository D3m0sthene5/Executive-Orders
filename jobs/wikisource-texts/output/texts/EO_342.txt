616111Executive Order 342 — Authorizing Appointment of Henry Summers as Clerk in New York Pension Agency Without Examination1905President of the United States

Mr. Henry Summers may be appointed clerk in the New York pension agency without examination under the civil service rules.

Theodore Roosevelt.

White House,

July 20, 1905.
