619107Executive Order 436 — Authorizing Reinstatement of Joseph F. Laporte to Post Office Service Without Regard to Civil Service Rules1906President of the United States

Mr. Joseph F. Laporte may be reinstated in the post-office service without regard to the time limit upon eligibility for reinstatement.

Theodore Roosevelt.

The White House,

April 4, 1906.
