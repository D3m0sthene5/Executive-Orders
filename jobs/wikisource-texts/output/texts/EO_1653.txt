1329611Executive Order 1653 — Authorizing Reinstatement of W. B. Johnson in General Land Office Without Regard to Civil Service Rules1912President of the United States

Mr. W. B. Johnson may be reinstated to an appropriate position in the service of the General Land Office without reference to the fact that he has been separated for more than a year.

Wm. H. Taft.

The White House,

December 3, 1912.
