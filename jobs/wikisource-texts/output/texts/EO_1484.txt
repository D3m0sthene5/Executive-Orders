826946Executive Order 1484 — Authorizing Appointment of Embree E. Hoss, Jr., as Inspector in Post Office Without Examination1912President of the United States

Mr. Embree E. Hoss, jr., may be appointed without examination under the civil-service rules as a post-office inspector.

Wm. H. Taft.

The White House,

February 19, 1912.
