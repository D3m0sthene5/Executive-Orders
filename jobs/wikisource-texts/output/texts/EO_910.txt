636200Executive Order 910 — Authorizing Reinstatement of Joe M. Johnson in Government Printing Office Without Regard to Civil Service Rules1908President of the United States

Mr. Joe M. Johnson may be reinstated in the Government Printing Office without regard to the length of his separation therefrom.

Theodore Roosevelt.

The White House,

July 2, 1908.
