625586Executive Order 599 — Authorizing Reinstatement of F. T. Quinlan as Inspector of Furs in Quartermaster's Department in Philadelphia Without Regard to Civil Service Rules1907President of the United States

Mr. F. T. Quinlan may be reinstated as an inspector of furs in the quartermaster's department at Philadelphia, Pa.

Theodore Roosevelt.

The White House,

March 25, 1907.
