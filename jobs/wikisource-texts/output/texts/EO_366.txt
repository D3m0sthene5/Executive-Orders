616266Executive Order 366 — Authorizing Appointment of Edward McCauley as Chief of Redemption Division in Post Office Department Without Examination1905President of the United States

Edward McCauley may be appointed chief of the Redemption Division, office of the Third Assistant Postmaster-General, Post-Office Department, without examination, in view of his experience as chief clerk of the Census Bureau.

Theodore Roosevelt.

White House,

October 31, 1905.
