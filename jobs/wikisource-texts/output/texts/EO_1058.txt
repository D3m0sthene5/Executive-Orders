Signed by President William H. Taft Tuesday, April 6, 1909

592313Executive Order 1058 — Authorizing Reinstatement of Leonard J. Garver to Position in Classified Service1909President of the United States

Mr. Leonard J. Garver, who was regularly in a classified clerical position in the Forestry Service from July 23, 1904, to June 30, 1905, and who served continuously in the office of the Secretary of the United States Senate from June 30, 1905, to March 31, 1909, may be reinstated in an appropriate position in the classified service.

Wm. H. Taft.

The White House,

April 6, 1909.
