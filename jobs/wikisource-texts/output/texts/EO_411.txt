1906 CSR 98

618924Executive Order 411 — Authorizing Promotion of Joseph Fought to Clerk in Treasury Department Without Regard to Civil Service Rules1906President of the United States

Mr. Joseph Fought may be promoted from classified laborer to clerk in the Treasury Department in view of his former service as a clerk in the War Department and his gallant record as a soldier in the civil war.

Theodore Roosevelt.

The White House,

February 13, 1906.
