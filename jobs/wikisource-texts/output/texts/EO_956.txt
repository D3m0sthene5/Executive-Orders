636228Executive Order 956 — Authorizing Appointment of Frederick Koch as Disciplinarian at Indian School Upon Noncompetitive Examination1908President of the United States

Mr. Frederick Koch may be appointed disciplinarian at the Carlisle, Pa., Indian School, upon noncompetitive examination showing him to be qualified by experience for the position.

Theodore Roosevelt.

The White House,

October 21, 1908.
