Signed by President Theodore Roosevelt Monday, January 11, 1909

592004Executive Order 1005 — Authorizing Appointment of Grace W. Nettleton to Clerkship in Treasury Department1909President of the United States

Mrs. Grace W. Nettleton, whose name stands third on the editorial clerk register from Connecticut, and second on the clerk register from the same State, may be appointed to a clerkship in the Treasury Department.

Theodore Roosevelt.

The White House

January 11, 1909.
