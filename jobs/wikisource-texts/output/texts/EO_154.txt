Signed by President Theodore Roosevelt Saturday, January 18, 1902

611369Executive Order 154 — Authorizing Appointment of Assistant Commissioner of Immigration in New York Without Examination1902President of the United States

An appointment to the vacancy now existing in the position of Assistant Commissioner of Immigration, Port of New York, may be made without examination under the civil service laws.

Theodore Roosevelt.

White House,

January 18, 1902.
