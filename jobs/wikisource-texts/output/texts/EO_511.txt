Signed by President Theodore Roosevelt Wednesday, October 3, 1906

1979747Executive Order 511 — Excusing United States Spanish War Veterans Federal Employees in Washington from Work for Spanish War Veterans' Parade1906President of the United States

It is hereby ordered that the members of the United Spanish War Veterans employed in the several executive departments, the government printing office and the navy
yard and station at Washington, D. C., be excused from duty on October 9, 1906, to enable them to participate in the parade of the organization arranged for that day.

Theodore Roosevelt.

The White House,

October 3, 1906.
