1904 CSR 90

606605Executive Order 264 — Authorizing Appointment of Dr. William L. Ralph as Curator in National Museum Without Examination1904President of the United States

An exception to the requirements of the civil-service rules is hereby made to permit the appointment, without examination, of Dr. William L. Ralph as curator of the section of birds' eggs in the National Museum, at a salary of $100 per month.

Theodore Roosevelt.

White House,

June 23, 1904.
