614807Executive Order 302 — Authorizing Appointment of Donald G. Mitchell as Supervisor in Interior Department Without Examination1905President of the United States

Mr. Donald G. Mitchell, jr., who was employed on October 1, 1901, with the approval of the contractor, to supervise the construction of the buildings comprising the extension to the Government Hospital for the Insane, may be retained and appointed as supervisor of buildings and grounds without examination under the civil service rules.

Theodore Roosevelt.

White House,

March 10, 1905.
