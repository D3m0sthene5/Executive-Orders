Signed by President William H. Taft Monday, February 13, 1911

General Orders, No. 39, War Dept., March 22, 1911

1328033Executive Order 1297 — Reserving lands in Idaho for Protection of Water Supply of Boise Barracks, Fort Boise1911President of the United States

It is hereby ordered that the following-described lands in Idaho, be and the same are hereby reserved for military purposes, subject to private rights, if any there be, for the protection of the water supply of Boise Barracks, Fort Boise, viz:

In T. 3 N., R. 3 E., B. M.

Lot 4 and the SE. ¼ of the NE. ¼ sec. 5 and lots 1 and 2, sec. 6.

In T. 4 N., R. 3 E., B. M.

The SE. ¼ sec 8; the S. ½ sec 9; all of sec. 17; the NW. ½, the E. ½, and the SW. ¼ sec. 20; all of sec. 21; the W. ½ sec. 22; the W. ½ sec. 27; all of secs. 28, 29, and 30; all of sec. 31, except 20.516 acres embraced in "Sunnyside Lode" patented to W. P. Richards and David Gekeler, and all of sec. 32; the N. ½ of the NW. ¼ and the SW. ¼ of the NW. ¼ sec 33.

Wm. H. Taft.

The White House,

February 13, 1911.
