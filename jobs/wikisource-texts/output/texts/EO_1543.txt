Signed by President William H. Taft Thursday, June 6, 1912

1912 CSR 146

826994Executive Order 1543 — Authorizing Appointment of Margaret A. Hampton as Clerk in Classified Service Without Regard to Civil Service Rules1912President of the United States

Mrs. Margaret A. Hampton, a clerk at $1,000 per annum on the temporary force of the Thirteenth Census, may be appointed as a clerk at $1,000 per annum in the classified service.

Wm. H. Taft.

The White House,

June 6, 1912.
