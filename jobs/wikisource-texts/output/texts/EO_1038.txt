Revoked by Executive Order 3421, March 26, 1921. Also see Executive Order 4131, January 22, 1925 (re-established), and Public Land Order 1152, May 27, 1955 (20 FR 3701) (re-revoked).

Signed by President Theodore Roosevelt Saturday, February 27, 1909

641411Executive Order 1038 — Establishing Fire Island Reservation, Cook Inlet, Alaska, as Preserve and Breeding Ground for Alaska Moose1909President of the United States

It is hereby ordered that Fire Island, located approximately in latitude sixty-one degrees, fifteen minutes North, longitude one hundred and fifty degrees, fifteen minutes West from Greenwich, and near the head of Cook Inlet, south coast of Alaska, be and the same is hereby reserved and set apart for the use of the Department of Agriculture as a preserve and breeding ground for the Alaska moose.

Theodore Roosevelt.

The White House

February 27, 1909.
