649811Executive Order 1264 — Authorizing Reinstatement of Mary A. Wells as Clerk in Bureau of the Census Without Regard to Civil Service Rules1910President of the United States

Mrs. Mary A. Wells may be reinstated as a clerk in the Bureau of the Census without reference to the year limitation of the reinstatement rule.

Wm. H. Taft.

The White House,

November 7, 1910.
