619419Executive Order 531 — Authorizing Permanent Appointment of Three Watchmen in Department of the Interior Without Regard to Civil Service Rules1906President of the United States

Philip M. Earle and Isaac Pearson, of the District of Columbia, and George F. Miller, of New York, veterans of the civil war, who have served satisfactorily as watchmen in the Department of the Interior, under temporary appointment, may be permanently appointed watchmen without reference to the civil service rules.

Theodore Roosevelt.

The White House,

December 14, 1906.
