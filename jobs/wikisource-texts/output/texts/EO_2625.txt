External Information about this EO  U. S. Official Bulletin No. 18, p. 4, May 31, 1917

550973Executive Order 2625 — German Boats1917President of the United States

In accordance with a request of the Secretary of the Navy, concurred in by the United States Shipping Board, the Secretary of the Treasury is hereby directed to transfer the following German vessels to the Navy for use as colliers and cargo carriers:

Hohenfelde.
Kiel.
Breslau.
Frieda Leonhardt.
Rudolf Blumberg.
Saxoma.
Nicaria.
Vogesen.

Woodrow Wilson 

The White House,

May 22, 1917.
