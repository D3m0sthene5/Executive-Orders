Signed by President Theodore Roosevelt Monday, June 12, 1905

Published: General Orders, No. 102, War Dept., June 29, 1905  

616010Executive Order 330-A — Approval of War Secretary Recomendation to Reserve Grass Island, Gray's Harbor, Washington, for Military Purposes1905President of the United States

This is the War Department's description of the order, not the actual order text.

The President of the United States, by order dated June 12, 1905, reserved for purposes of the defense of Grays Harbor, Washington, all of Grass Island, unsurveyed, situate in said harbor in section 18, township 16 north, range 11 west, Willamette meridian, Washington.

Theodore Roosevelt

The White House,

June 12, 1905.
