827036Executive Order 1573 — Authorizing Appointment of Mingo Sanders as Messenger in Classified Service Without Regard to Civil Service Rules1912President of the United States

Mingo Sanders may be appointed as messenger in the classified service without reference to the provisions of the civil-service rules.

Wm. H. Taft.

The White House,

July 31, 1912.
