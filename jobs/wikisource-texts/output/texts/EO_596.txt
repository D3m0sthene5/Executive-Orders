1907 CSR 118

625573Executive Order 596 — Authorizing Appointment of Frederick M. Kerby as Clerk in Department of the Interior Without Regard to Civil Service Rules1907President of the United States

Frederick M. Kerby, of the District of Columbia, may be appointed to a clerical position in the Department of the Interior, without reference to civil-service rules.

Theodore Roosevelt.

The White House,

March 19, 1907.
