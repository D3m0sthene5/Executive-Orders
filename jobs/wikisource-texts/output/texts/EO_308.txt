Signed by President Theodore Roosevelt Wednesday, March 15, 1905

1905 CSR 86

614818Executive Order 308 — Authorizing Appointment of W. G. Tucker as Clerk in Isthmian Canal Service Without Examination1905President of the United States

Mr. W. G. Tucker may be appointed as clerk at $1,500 per annum, Isthmian Canal Commission, without examination under the civil service rules.

Theodore Roosevelt.

White House,

March 15, 1905.
