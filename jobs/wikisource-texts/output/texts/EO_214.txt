613470Executive Order 214 — Requiring Executive Departments to Furnish Information to Secretary of the Interior for Preparation of Official Register1903President of the United States

White House, March 31, 1903.

The heads of the Executive Departments and independent offices are directed to furnish to the Secretary of the Interior, for the preparation of the Official Register, under the act approved January 12, 1895, the date of the appointment of each officer and employee and his age and length of service under the United States, and a statement whether the position occupied by him is subject to appointment by the President, or is excepted, competitive, unclassified, temporary, or subject to contract.

Approved, March 31, 1903.

Theodore Roosevelt.
