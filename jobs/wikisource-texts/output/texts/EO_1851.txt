Signed by President Woodrow Wilson Wednesday, October 29, 1913

1913 CSR 114

2001251Executive Order 1851 — Amending Civil Service Rules Regarding Positions Excepted from Examination for Interstate Commerce Commission Personnel1913President of the United States

Schedule A, Subdivision XII, relating to the Interstate Commerce Commission, is hereby amended as follows:

The word “three” in the first paragraph is stricken out and the word “five” substituted therefor.

New paragraphs are added as follows:

One director under said act.

One supervisor of land appraisals under said act.

One chief accountant under said act.

As amended the Subdivision will read as follows:

XII. INTERSTATE COMMERCE COMMISSION.

Five experts to be members of an advisory board under the act of March 1, 1913, providing for the valuation of the property of carriers.

Five members of a board of engineers under said act.

One director under said act.

One supervisor of land appraisals under said act.

One chief accountant under said act.

Woodrow Wilson.

The White House

October 29, 1913.
