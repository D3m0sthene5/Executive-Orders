616382Executive Order 374 — Authorizing Appointment of Marie L. Eastwood in Bureau of the Census Without Regard to Civil Service Rules1905President of the United States

Mrs. Marie L. Eastwood may be appointed to a permanent position in the Bureau of the Census, in view of her former satisfactory service therein.

Theodore Roosevelt.

White House,

November 27, 1905.
