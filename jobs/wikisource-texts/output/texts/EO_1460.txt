826930Executive Order 1460 — Authorizing Reinstatement and Transfer of Charles S. Heinline to Clerk in Indian Field Service Without Regard to Civil Service Rules1912President of the United States

Mr. Charles S. Heinline may be nominally reinstated in the Government Printing Office and immediately transferred to the position of clerk at $1,200 a year in the Indian Field Service with headquarters at Spokane, Wash.

Wm. H. Taft.

The White House,

January 11, 1912.
