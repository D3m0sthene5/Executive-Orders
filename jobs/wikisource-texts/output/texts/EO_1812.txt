Signed by President Woodrow Wilson Saturday, August 9, 1913

564289Executive Order 1812 — Appointing Richard Lee Metcalfe to the Isthmian Canal Commission1913President of the United States

Richard Lee Metcalfe is hereby appointed a member of the Isthmian Canal Commission at the rate of Fourteen Thousand Dollars ($14,000.00) per annum, effective this date.

Commissioner Metcalfe will be allowed the use of a furnished dwelling house on the Isthmus of Panama and will be allowed and paid his actual and necessary expenses while away from the Isthmus on official business.

Woodrow Wilson.

The White House

August 9, 1913.
