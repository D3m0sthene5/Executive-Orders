625975Executive Order 690 — Authorizing Reinstatement of Rachel F. Sullivan as Clerk in Boston Post Office Without Regard to Civil Service Rules1907President of the United States

Miss Rachel F. Sullivan may be reinstated as a clerk in the Boston post-office without reference to the time limitation of the reinstatement rule.

Theodore Roosevelt.

The White House,

September 23, 1907.
