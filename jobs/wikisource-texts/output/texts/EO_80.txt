Signed by President Grover Cleveland Monday, September 16, 1895

609923Executive Order 80 — Amending Civil Service Rules to Except Certain Compositors and Pressmen in Bureau of Engraving and Printing from Examination1895President of the United States

Amendment to Special Departmental Rule No. 1.

Special departmental Rule 1 is hereby amended to except from examination in the Department of the Treasury, in the Bureau of Engraving and Printing, 43 compositors and 8 pressmen now temporarily employed under authority of the sundry civil act of March 2, 1895, such employment to cease prior to March 14, 1896. Vacancies occurring in this force shall be filled only by competitive examination under the civil-service rules.

Approved September 16, 1895.

Grover Cleveland.
