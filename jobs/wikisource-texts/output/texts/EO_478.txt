619232Executive Order 478 — Authorizing Appointment of William E. DeRiemer as Clerk in Smithsonian Institution Without Regard to Civil Service Rules1906President of the United States

Mr. William E. De Riemer, who has been employed almost continuously for seven years in connection with exposition work in which the National Museum has participated, may be appointed as a clerk at a compensation not exceeding $900 per annum in the Smithsonian Institution or its bureaus.

Theodore Roosevelt.

The White House,

July 14, 1906.
