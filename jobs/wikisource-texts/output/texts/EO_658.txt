Signed by President Theodore Roosevelt Monday, June 24, 1907

625830Executive Order 658 — Authorizing Reinstatement of John S. Drukker as Pressman in Bureau of Engraving and Printing Without Regard to Civil Service Rules1907President of the United States

Mr. John S. Drukker, formerly a temporary employee in the Bureau of Engraving and Printing, may be reinstated as a pressman in the Bureau without reference to the civil-service rules.

Theodore Roosevelt.

The White House,

June 24, 1907.
