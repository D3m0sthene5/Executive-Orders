616393Executive Order 379 — Authorizing Appointment of Howard H. Belden Upon Passing Noncompetitive Examination1905President of the United States

Mr. Howard H. Belden may be appointed in any of the Executive Departments upon his passing a satisfactory noncompetitive examination.

Theodore Roosevelt.

The White House,

December 11, 1905.
