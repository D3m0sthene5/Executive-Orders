Amends Executive Order 209, March 20, 1903.

Signed by President Theodore Roosevelt Thursday, January 25, 1906

1906 CSR 91

618551Executive Order 403 — Amending Civil Service Rules to Except Mounted Inspectors in the Customs Service from Examination1906President of the United States

Schedule A of the civil-service rules is hereby amended by adding under II, Treasury Department, a new clause to be numbered 13, to read as follows:

"13. Mounted inspectors in the customs service on the Mexican border."

T. Roosevelt.

The White House,

January 25, 1906.
