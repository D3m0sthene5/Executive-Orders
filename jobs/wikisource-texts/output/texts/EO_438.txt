619109Executive Order 438 — Authorizing Reinstatement of Benjamin B. Cox to Post Office Service Without Regard to Civil Service Rules1906President of the United States

Mr. Benjamin B. Cox may be reinstated in the post-office at Mobile, Ala., without regard to the provisions of civil service Rule IX.

Theodore Roosevelt.

The White House,

April 9, 1906.
