1329576Executive Order 1625 — Authorizing Reinstatement of Charles A. Bronson as Assistant Postmaster Without Regard to Civil Service Rules1912President of the United States

Mr. Charles A. Bronson may be reinstated as assistant postmaster at Albion, N. Y., without reference to the time limit upon eligibility under the reinstatement rule.

Wm. H. Taft.

The White House,

October 17, 1912.
