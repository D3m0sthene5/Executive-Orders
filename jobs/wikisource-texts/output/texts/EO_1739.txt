1330364Executive Order 1739 — Authorizing Reinstatement of H. Norman Fleming as Clerk in Pension Office Without Regard to Civil Service Rules1913President of the United States

Mr. H. Norman Fleming may be reinstated as a clerk in the Pension Office without regard to the civil-service rule fixing one year as the period of eligibility.

Wm. H. Taft.

The White House,

March 3, 1913.
