Amends Executive Order 209, March 20, 1903. Amended by Executive Order 1268, December 2, 1910.

Signed by President Theodore Roosevelt Thursday, September 3, 1908

1908 CSR 124

635944Executive Order 933 — Amending Civil Service Rules Regarding Exceptions from Examination for Civilian Teachers in Military Academies1908President of the United States

The civil-service rules, Schedule A, Section III, War Department, is hereby amended by adding a new paragraph, to be numbered 11, and to read as follows:

Civilian professors, instructors, and teachers in the U. S. Military Academy at West Point and in the U. S. Naval Academy at Annapolis.

Theodore Roosevelt.

The White House,

September 3, 1908.
