Amends Executive Order 209, March 20, 1903. Amended by Executive Order 1163, February 8, 1910.

Signed by President Theodore Roosevelt Wednesday, February 17, 1904

1904 CSR 80

613808Executive Order 253 — Amending Civil Service Rules to Except Commissioners of National Military Parks from Examination1904President of the United States

Schedule A is hereby amended by adding under the subheading "War Department" a clause as follows:

All commissioners for the national military parks.

Theodore Roosevelt.

White House,

January 27, 1904.
