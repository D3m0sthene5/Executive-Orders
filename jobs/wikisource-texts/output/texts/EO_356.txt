616175Executive Order 356 — Authorizing Appointment of Mrs. M. C. Miller to a Clerkship in the Classified Service Without Examination Under Civil-Service Rules1905President of the United States

Mrs. M. C. Miller may be appointed to a clerkship in the classified service without examination under the civil service rules.

Theodore Roosevelt

The White House,

October 7, 1905.
