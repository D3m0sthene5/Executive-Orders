Amends Executive Order 209, March 20, 1903.

Signed by President William H. Taft Friday, December 10, 1909

1909 CSR 115

591660Executive Order 1146 — Amending Civil Service Rules to Except Chief Law Officer in the Reclamation Service from Examination1909President of the United States

The list of classified positions excepted from examination under Civil Service Rule II, clause 3, is hereby amended so as to include, in the Department of the Interior, chief law officer in the Reclamation Service.

Wm. H. Taft.

The White House,

December 10, 1909.
