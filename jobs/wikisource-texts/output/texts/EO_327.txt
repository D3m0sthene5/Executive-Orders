615710Executive Order 327 — Authorizing Reinstatement of Eleanor Relyea Without Regard to Civil Service Rules1905President of the United States

Mrs. Eleanor Relyea may be reinstated in the Departmental Service without reference to the civil service rules.

Theodore Roosevelt.

White House,

May 20, 1905.
