649171Executive Order 1214 — Authorizing Reinstatement of Louise M. Foster as Laborer in Department of Agriculture Without Regard to Civil Service Rules1910President of the United States

Mrs. Louise M. Foster may be reinstated as an unclassified laborer in the Department of Agriculture, the year limit of the labor regulations being waived.

Wm. H. Taft.

The White House,

June 22, 1910.
