Signed by President Theodore Roosevelt Friday, December 11, 1908

591990Executive Order 992 — Authorizing Transfer of Walter R. Gallaher to Classified Clerical Position in United States1908President of the United States

Walter R. Gallaher, a clerk at $1,800 per annum in the Isthmian service, may be transferred to a classified clerical position in the United States.

Theodore Roosevelt.

The White House

December 11, 1908.
