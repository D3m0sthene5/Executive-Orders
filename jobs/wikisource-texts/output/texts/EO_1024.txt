Signed by President Theodore Roosevelt Wednesday, February 10, 1909

592293Executive Order 1024 — Authorizing Reinstatement of Charles B. Brewer as Ship Drafts in the Bureau of Construction and Repairs, Navy Department1909President of the United States

Mr. Charles B. Brewer may be reinstated as a ship draftsman in the Bureau of Construction and Repairs, Navy Department, without reference to the length of time he has been separated from the service.

Theodore Roosevelt.

The White House

February 10, 1909.
