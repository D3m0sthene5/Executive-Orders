Signed by President Theodore Roosevelt Thursday, January 17, 1907

General Orders, No. 18, War Dept., January 26, 1907

622824Executive Order 551 — Addition to the Fort Lincoln Military Reservation, Burleigh County, North Dakota1907President of the United States

It is hereby ordered that lots two (2), three (3), and four (4) of section ten (10), township 137 north, range 80 west of 5th principal meridian, situated in Burleigh County, North Dakota, aggregating about 32.50 acres, be reserved from sale and set apart for military purposes as an addition to the military reservation of Fort Lincoln, North Dakota.

Theodore Roosevelt.

The White House,

January 17, 1907.
