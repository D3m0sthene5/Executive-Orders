1329616Executive Order 1662 — Authorizing Appointment of Alice Engle as Clerk in Department of Agriculture Without Regard to Civil Service Rules1912President of the United States

Miss Alice Engle may be appointed to a clerical position in the Department of Agriculture without regard to the civil-service rules.

Wm. H. Taft.

The White House,

December 14, 1912.
