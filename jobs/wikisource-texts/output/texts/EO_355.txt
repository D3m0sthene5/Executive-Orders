616174Executive Order 355 — Authorizing Appointment of Miss Marjorie H. Young as Clerk in the United States Pension Agency Without Examination Under Civil-Service Rules1905President of the United States

Miss Marjorie H. Young may be retained and appointed as a clerk in the United States pension agency at Pittsburg, Pa., without examination under the civil service rules.

Theodore Roosevelt

The White House,

October 6, 1905.
