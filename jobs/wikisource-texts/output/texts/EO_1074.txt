Signed by President William H. Taft Friday, May 21, 1909

586036Executive Order 1074 — Revoking Executive Order of January 19, 1909, Which Appointed the Council of Fine Arts1909President of the United States

By reason of the provisions of the Act of March 4, 1909 (Sundry Civil), Executive Order issued under date of January 19, 1909, appointing the Council of Fine Arts, and requiring plans to be submitted to such Council, etc., is hereby revoked.

Wm. H. Taft.

The White House,

May 21, 1909.
