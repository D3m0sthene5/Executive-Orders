616071Executive Order 336 — Authorizing Permanent Appointment of James W. Plake as Copyist in Office of Indian Affairs Without Regard to Civil Service Rules1905President of the United States

James W. Plake, an Indian, may be permanently appointed as copyist in the Office of Indian Affairs.

Theodore Roosevelt.
