2 Canal Record 155, January 13, 1909.

636463Executive Order 997 — Appointing Wesley M. Owen as Associate Justice of the Supreme Court of the Canal Zone1908President of the United States

Under authority vested in me by law, and until otherwise provided by law or ordered, Wesley M. Owen, is appointed an Associate Justice of the Supreme Court of the Canal Zone, at a salary of six thousand dollars per annum.

Effective January 4, 1909.

Theodore Roosevelt.

The White House,

December 23, 1908.
