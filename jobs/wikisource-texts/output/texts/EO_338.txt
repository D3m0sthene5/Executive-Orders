616076Executive Order 338 — Authorizing Permanent Appointment of Abram B. Frisbie as Watchman in Interior Department Without Examination1905President of the United States

Abram B. Frisbie may be permanently appointed to the position of watchman in the Interior Department, at the discretion of the Secretary of the Interior, without examination under the civil service rules.

Theodore Roosevelt.

White House,

July 10, 1905.
