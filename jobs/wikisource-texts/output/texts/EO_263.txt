606600Executive Order 263 — Authorizing Appointment of Samuel D. Amen as Internal Revenue Agent Without Regard to Civil Service Rules1904President of the United States

An exception to the requirements of the civil-service rules is hereby made to permit the appointment of Mr. Samuel D. Amen to the position of internal-revenue agent.

Theodore Roosevelt.

White House,

June 17, 1904.
