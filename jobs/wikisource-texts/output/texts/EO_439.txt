Signed by President Theodore Roosevelt Thursday, April 12, 1906

1984263Executive Order 439 — Reserving Lands in Mississippi as Target Range for Revenue Cutter Service1906President of the United States

It is hereby ordered that sections 23 and 26, township 9 south, range 7 west, Mississippi, containing a total of 307.54 acres, be and they are hereby reserved as a target range for the Revenue-Cutter Service.

Theodore Roosevelt.

The White House,

April 12, 1906.
