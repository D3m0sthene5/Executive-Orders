1911 CSR 111

820412Executive Order 1342 — Authorizing Reinstatement of Ralph R. Mulcare as Letter Carrier Without Regard to Civil Service Rules1911President of the United States

Mr. Ralph R. Mulcare may be reinstated as letter carrier in the post office at Brooklyn, N. Y., without regard to the year limit prescribed by the civil-service rules.

Wm. H. Taft.

The White House,

April 24, 1911.
