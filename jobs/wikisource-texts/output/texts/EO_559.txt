624785Executive Order 559 — Authorizing Appointment of Norman Oswell Cully as Messenger at Naval Academy Without Examination1907President of the United States

Mr. Norman Oswell Cully may be appointed as messenger to the superintendent of the Naval Academy without examination under the civil-service rules.

Theodore Roosevelt.

The White House,

February 6, 1907.
