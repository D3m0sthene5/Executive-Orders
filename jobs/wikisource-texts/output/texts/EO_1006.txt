Signed by President Theodore Roosevelt Monday, January 11, 1909

592002Executive Order 1006 — Authorizing Reinstatement of Fannie C. Chase as Skilled Laborer in Government Printing Office1909President of the United States

Miss Fannie C. Chase may be reinstated as a skilled laborer in the Government Printing Office without reference to the time limit prescribed by the civil-service rules.

Theodore Roosevelt.

The White House

January 11, 1909.
