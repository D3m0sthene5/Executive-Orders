636208Executive Order 917 — Authorizing Reinstatement of William E. Shields in Government Printing Office Without Regard to Civil Service Rules1908President of the United States

Mr. William E. Shields may be reinstated in the Government Printing Office without regard to the length of his separation from the office.

Theodore Roosevelt.

The White House,

July 18, 1908.
