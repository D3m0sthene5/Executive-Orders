Signed by President William H. Taft Friday, July 30, 1909

651983Executive Order 1117 — Authorizing Leave for Grand Army of the Republic Members Employed by Government to Attend National Encampment in Salt Lake City1909President of the United States

It is hereby ordered that the employees of the Executive Departments, the Government Printing Office, and the Navy Yard and Station, who are members of the Grand Army of the Republic, be granted such portions of their annual leave as they may desire, not exceeding fourteen days, to enable them to attend the National Encampment of the Grand Army of the Republic at Salt Lake City, beginning on the 9th and ending on the 14th of August.

Wm. H. Taft.

The White House,

July 30, 1909.
