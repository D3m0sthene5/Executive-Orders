Signed by President Theodore Roosevelt Wednesday, September 9, 1908

1908 CSR 135

636212Executive Order 935 — Authorizing Appointment of Four People in Bureau of the Census Without Regard to Civil Service Rules1908President of the United States

Frank Dingman, C. W. G. Brett, Raymond Bartlett, and Charles F. Young may be appointed under the appropriation "Tabulating statistics, Census Office," in the Bureau of the Census, without reference to the civil-service rules.

Theodore Roosevelt.

The White House,

September 9, 1908.
