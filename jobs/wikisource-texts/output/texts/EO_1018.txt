Amends Executive Order 209, March 20, 1903.

Signed by President Theodore Roosevelt Wednesday, February 3, 1909

591634Executive Order 1018 — Amending Civil Service Rules to Except National Bank Examiners and Receivers from Examination1909President of the United States

Schedule A of classified positions excepted from examination under Rule II, clause 3, is hereby amended by the addition to Subdivision II, relating to the Treasury Department, of a paragraph to read as follows:

 National-bank examiners and receivers under the office of the Comptroller of the Currency.

Theodore Roosevelt.

The White House

February 3, 1909.
