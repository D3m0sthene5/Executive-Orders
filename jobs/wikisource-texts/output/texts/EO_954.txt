Amends Executive Order 209, March 20, 1903.

Signed by President Theodore Roosevelt Wednesday, October 21, 1908

1908 CSR 125

635949Executive Order 954 — Amending Civil Service Rules Regarding Exceptions from Examination for Civilian Instructors in Revenue Cutter Service1908President of the United States

Schedule A, Section II, of the civil-service rules, is hereby amended by the addition of a new paragraph, to be numbered 12, and to read as follows:

 Civilian instructors in the U. S. Revenue-Cutter Service.

Theodore Roosevelt.

The White House,

October 21, 1908.
