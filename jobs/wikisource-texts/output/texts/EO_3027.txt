Signed by President Woodrow Wilson Saturday, January 25, 1919

602051Executive Order 3027 — Revoking Executive Orders Establishing Defensive Sea Areas1919President of the United States

All Executive Orders heretofore issued for the establishment of Defensive Sea Areas are hereby revoked.

Woodrow Wilson 

The White House,

25 January, 1919.
