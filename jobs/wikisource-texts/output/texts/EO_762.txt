626832Executive Order 762 — Authorizing Appointment of Edward J. Tupper as Deputy Collector and Inspector of Customs in Stamford, Connecticut, Without Regard to Civil Service Rules1908President of the United States

Mr. Edward J. Tupper may be appointed deputy collector and inspector of customs at Stamford, Conn., without compliance with the civil-service rules or the Executive order of January 17, 1873.

Theodore Roosevelt.

The White House,

February 22, 1908.
