616380Executive Order 373 — Authorizing Appointment of S. H. Alexander as Clerk in Post Office Department Without Regard to Civil Service Rules1905President of the United States

Mr. S. H. Alexander, of North Carolina, who is now serving as a classified laborer in the Post-Office Department, may be appointed to a clerical position on the permanent roll of that Department.

Theodore Roosevelt.

White House,

November 23, 1905.
