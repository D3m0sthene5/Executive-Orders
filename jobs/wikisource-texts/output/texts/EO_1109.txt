Signed by President William H. Taft Friday, July 9, 1909

591647Executive Order 1109 — Amending Civil Service Rules to Except Superintendent of Construction, Corregidor, Philippine Island, from Examination1909President of the United States

Upon the recommendation of the Civil Service Commission, Schedule A of classified positions excepted from examination under Rule II, clause 3, is hereby amended by adding to Subdivision III, relating to the War Department, a paragraph to read as follows:

Superintendent of Construction, Quartermaster's Department at Large, Corregidor, Philippine Island.

Wm. H. Taft.

The White House,

July 9, 1909.
