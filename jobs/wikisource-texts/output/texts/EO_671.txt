625845Executive Order 671 — Authorizing Appointment of Harry T. Paterson as Junior Civil Engineer in Engineer Department at Large Without Regard to Civil Service Rules1907President of the United States

Mr. Harry T. Paterson, who was appointed through competitive examination and served for several years in the Reclamation Service, may be appointed as a junior civil engineer in the Engineer Department at large without reference to the limitations respecting reinstatement and transfer.

Theodore Roosevelt.

The White House,

July 29, 1907.
