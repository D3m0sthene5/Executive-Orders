613504Executive Order 202 — Authorizing Reinstatement of Oscar Wenderoth as Senior Draftsman in Treasury Department Without Regard to Civil Service Rules1903President of the United States

White House, February 6, 1903.

Mr. Oscar Wenderoth may be reinstated to the position of senior architectural draftsman in the office of the Supervising Architect of the Treasury Department without regard to the provision of Rule IX, limiting the period of eligibility for reinstatement to one year from the date of separation.

Theodore Roosevelt.
