619424Executive Order 539 — Authorizing Transfer of William A. Woodruff to Clerk in Treasury Department Without Regard to Civil Service Rules1906President of the United States

Mr. William A. Woodruff, now a hospital attendant in the Public Health and Marine-Hospital Service of the United States at Ellis Island, New York, may be transferred to a clerical position in the Treasury Department at Washington, D. C, without compliance with the civil service rules.

Theodore Roosevelt.

The White House,

December 24, 1906.
