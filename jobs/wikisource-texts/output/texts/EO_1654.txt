1329609Executive Order 1654 — Authorizing Reinstatement of Louis H. Eberlein in Customs Service Without Regard to Civil Service Rules1912President of the United States

Mr. Louis H. Eberlein may be reinstated to an appropriate classified position in the Customs Service at New York without regard to the length of time he has been separated from the service. 

Wm. H. Taft.

The White House,

December 3, 1912.
