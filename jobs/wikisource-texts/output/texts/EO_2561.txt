Signed by President Woodrow Wilson Monday, March 26, 1917

597030Executive Order 2561 — Increasing Authorized Enlisted Strength of Marine Corps1917President of the United States

By virtue of the authority vested in the President by the Act approved August 29, 1916, entitled "An Act Making appropriations for the Naval Service for the fiscal year ending June thirtieth, nineteen hundred and" seventeen, and for other purposes," it is hereby directed that the authorized enlisted strength of the Marine Corps be increased to seventeen thousand four hundred men.

Woodrow Wilson.

The White House,

March 26, 1917.
