1329596Executive Order 1636 — Authorizing Appointment of E. O. Merchant as Special Agent in Bureau of Corporations Without Regard to Civil Service Rules1912President of the United States

Mr. E. O. Merchant may be appointed a special agent in the Bureau of Corporations without regard to the civil-service rules.

Wm. H. Taft.

The White House,

October 29, 1912.
