1330298Executive Order 1689 — Authorizing Transfer of John Garcin in Forest Service Without Regard to Civil Service Rules1913President of the United States

Mr. John Garcin may be transferred from the position of clerk at $2,000 per annum, Customs Service, New Orleans, La., to the position of clerk at $1,200 per annum Forest Service, Flagstaff, Ariz., without reference to the requirement of six months' actual service next preceding transfer.

Wm. H. Taft.

The White House,

January 24, 1913.
