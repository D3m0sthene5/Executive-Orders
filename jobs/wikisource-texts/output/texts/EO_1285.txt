Amends Executive Order 1002, January 6, 1909, and Executive Order 1085, June 11, 1909.

Signed by President William H. Taft Monday, January 16, 1911

IV PCR 179, February 1, 1911

1214571Executive Order 1285 — Authorizing Use of French Dump Cars in Canal Zone Without Safety Appliances1911President of the United States

Under authority vested in me by law, it is ordered:

The Isthmian Canal Commission is hereby authorized to use six-meter French dump cars for the purpose of obtaining gravel from the bed of the Chagres River until July 1, 1911, without equipping such cars with the various safety appliances required by the provisions of Executive Order No. 1002, dated January 6, 1909.

Wm. H. Taft.

The White House, January 16, 1911.
