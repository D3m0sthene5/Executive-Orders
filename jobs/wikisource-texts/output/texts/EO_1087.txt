Signed by President William H. Taft Tuesday, June 15, 1909

592330Executive Order 1087 — Authorizing Reinstatement of Hattie M. Miller as Classified Laborer in Patent Office1909President of the United States

Miss Hattie M. Miller, of Pennsylvania, may be reinstated in the Patent Office as a classified laborer at $600, without regard to the length of time since her separation from the service, September 30, 1907.

Wm. H. Taft.

The White House,

June 15, 1909.
