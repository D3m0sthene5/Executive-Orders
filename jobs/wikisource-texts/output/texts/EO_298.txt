Amends Executive Order 209, March 20, 1903. Also see Executive Order 299, March 3, 1905.

Signed by President Theodore Roosevelt Friday, March 3, 1905

1905 CSR 73

614779Executive Order 298 — Amending Civil Service Rules to Make Certain Interior Department Positions Subject to Examination1905President of the United States

Schedule A of the civil service rules is hereby amended by striking out paragraphs 4 and 5 of Section VI, relating to the Department of the Interior, which are as follows:

Special agents employed, as necessity for their employment may arise, for the purpose of protecting public lands.

Inspectors of surveyors-general and district land offices.

The succeeding paragraphs will be renumbered accordingly.

Theodore Roosevelt.

White House,

March 3, 1905.
