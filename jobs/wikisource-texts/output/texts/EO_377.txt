616388Executive Order 377 — Authorizing Reappointment of Emily L. Genella in Bureau of Engraving and Printing Without Regard to Civil Service Rules1905President of the United States

Mrs. Emily L. Genella may be reappointed in the Bureau of Engraving and Printing without reference to the year limit imposed by the civil service rules.

Theodore Roosevelt.

The White House,

December 9, 1905.
