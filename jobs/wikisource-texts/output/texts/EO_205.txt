613476Executive Order 205 — Authorizing Appointment of Translator in Bureau of Insular Affairs Without Examination1903President of the United States

White House, March 7, 1903.

The vacancy in the position of clerk qualified as translator of the English, Spanish, and Tagalog languages, in the Bureau of Insular Affairs of the War Department, may be filled without examination under the civil-service rules.

Theodore Roosevelt.
