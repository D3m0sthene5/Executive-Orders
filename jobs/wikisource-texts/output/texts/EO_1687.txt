1330295Executive Order 1687 — Authorizing Reinstatement of George J. Bunce to Subclerical Position in Mint and Assay Service Without Regard to Civil Service Rules1913President of the United States

Mr. George J. Bunce may be reinstated in a subclerical position in the mint and assay service of the United States without reference to the period of his separation from the service.

Wm. H. Taft.

The White House,

January 24, 1913.
