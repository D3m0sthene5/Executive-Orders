826621Executive Order 1396 — Authorizing Reinstatement of Randolph Fortune as Messenger in War Department Without Regard to Civil Service Rules1911President of the United States

Randolph Fortune, who served as assistant messenger and messenger in the War Department from September 4, 1890 to February 28, 1910, when he resigned to accept a position in the legislative service, may be reinstated to a messenger position in the War Department without regard to the length of his separation from that department.

Wm. H. Taft.

The White House,

August 9, 1911.
