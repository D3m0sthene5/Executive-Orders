Signed by President William H. Taft Thursday, August 5, 1909

591940Executive Order 1119 — Authorizing Classification of Certain Unclassified Laborers in the Customs Service1909President of the United States

The unclassified laborers employed in the Customs Service at the port of New York, whose names appear upon a list of persons recommended for classification as classified laborers by the Treasury Department, may be given a status as classified laborers, on the recommendation of the Civil Service Commission, in view of the classified duties performed by them.

Wm. H. Taft.

The White House,

August 5, 1909.
