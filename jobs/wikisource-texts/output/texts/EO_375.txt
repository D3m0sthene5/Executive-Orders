Amends Executive Order 209, March 20, 1903.

Signed by President Theodore Roosevelt Monday, November 27, 1905

1905 CSR 78

616384Executive Order 375 — Amending Civil Service Rules to Except Cooks from Examination1905President of the United States

Schedule A of the civil service rules is hereby amended by the addition to section 1 of a paragraph which will be numbered 13, and shall read as follows:

Cooks, when in the opinion of the Commission it is not expedient to make appointment upon competitive examination.

Theodore Roosevelt.

White House,

November 27, 1905.
