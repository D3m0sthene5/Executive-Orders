619099Executive Order 425 — Authorizing Reinstatement of Coila B. Ashby as Clerk in Interior Department Without Regard to Civil Service Rules1906President of the United States

Mrs. Coila B. Ashby may be reinstated as clerk in the Interior Department without regard to the year limitation of the reinstatement rule.

Theodore Roosevelt.

The White House,

March 1, 1906.
