650409Executive Order 1309 — Authorizing Reinstatement of William E. Lewis in Government Printing Office Without Regard to Civil Service Rules1911President of the United States

Mr. William E. Lewis may be reinstated in the Government Printing Office without regard to the length of his separation from that office.

Wm. H. Taft.

The White House,

February 25, 1911.
