Signed by President Woodrow Wilson Wednesday, December 12, 1917

U.S. Official Bulletin No. 189, p. 4, December 20, 1917

601601Executive Order 2772 — Reserving Lands in New Mexico for Military Purposes1917President of the United States

All the lands in T. 22 S., R. 9 W., N. M. P. M., New Mexico, the title to which has not passed out of the United States, are hereby temporarily withdrawn from settlement, filing, selection, entry, or sale, and reserved for military purposes.

Woodrow Wilson 

The White House,

December 12, 1917.
