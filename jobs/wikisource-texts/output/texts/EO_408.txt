618920Executive Order 408 — Authorizing Reinstatement of Clayton G. Coleman as Special Agent in General Land Office Without Regard to Civil Service Rules1906President of the United States

Clayton G. Coleman may be reinstated to the position of special agent of the General Land Office without reference to the year limit imposed by the civil-service rules.

Theodore Roosevelt.

The White House,

February 9, 1906.
