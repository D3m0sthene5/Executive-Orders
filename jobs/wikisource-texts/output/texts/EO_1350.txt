Authorizing Appointment of John Roche and Patrick McKeefrey as Watchmen in Customs Service Without Regard to Civil Service Rules

Signed by President William H. Taft Wednesday, May 10, 1911

1911 CSR 111

820417Executive Order 1350 — Authorizing Appointment of John Roche and Patrick McKeefrey as Watchmen in Customs Service Without Regard to Civil Service Rules1911President of the United States

John Roche and Patrick McKeefrey, laborers in the Customs Service at the port of New York, may be appointed to the classified position of customs watchmen at that port.

Wm. H. Taft.

The White House,

May 10, 1911.
