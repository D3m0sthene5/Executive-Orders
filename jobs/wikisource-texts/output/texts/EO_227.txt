Signed by President Theodore Roosevelt Thursday, July 30, 1903

606399Executive Order 227 — Authorizing Appointment of Luther M. Walter as Law Clerk in Interstate Commerce Commission Upon Noncompetitive Examination1903President of the United States

Luther M. Walter may be appointed to the position of law clerk in the Interstate Commerce Commission upon appropriate noncompetitive examination.

Theodore Roosevelt.

White House,

July 30, 1903.
