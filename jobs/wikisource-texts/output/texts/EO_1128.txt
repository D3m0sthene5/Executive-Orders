Amends Executive Order 209, March 20, 1903.

Signed by President William H. Taft Friday, September 24, 1909

591655Executive Order 1128 — Amending Civil Service Rules to Except Employees at Leprosy Investigation Station, Molokai, Hawaii, from Examination1909President of the United States

Schedule A of classified positions excepted from examination under Civil Service Rule II, clause 3, is hereby amended by the addition to Subdivision II, relating to the Treasury Department, of a paragraph to read as follows:

 All persons actually employed in the Public Health and Marine-Hospital Service at the leprosy investigation station, Molokai, Hawaii.

Wm. H. Taft.

The White House,

September 24, 1909.
