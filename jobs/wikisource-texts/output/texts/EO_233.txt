607028Executive Order 233 — Authorizing Transfer of W. E. Lackland to Post-Office at Prescott, Arizona, Without Regard to Civil Service Rules1903President of the United States

The requirement that a person proposed for transfer must have actually served in the classified service at least six months next preceding the transfer may be waived in the case of the transfer of W. E. Lackland from the Treasury Department to the Post-Office at Prescott, Arizona.

Theodore Roosevelt.

White House,

August 26, 1903.
