625538Executive Order 578 — Authorizing Appointment of George W. Pitts as Clerk in Treasury Department Without Regard to Civil Service Rules1907President of the United States

George W. Pitts may be appointed a clerk in the Treasury Department without compliance with the civil-service rules.

Theodore Roosevelt.

The White House,

March 4, 1907.
