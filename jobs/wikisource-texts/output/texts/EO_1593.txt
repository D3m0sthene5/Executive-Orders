Covers certain employees who were no longer exempt after the changes in Executive Order 1592, August 26, 1912.

Signed by President William H. Taft Monday, August 26, 1912

1912 CSR 140

826572Executive Order 1593 — Authorizing Classification of Up to 115 Experts and Agents in Agriculture Department1912President of the United States

Not exceeding 115 persons appointed as experts and agents without examination under Subdivision IX, section 1 of Schedule A, of the civil-service rules, and who now are engaged upon work similar to that of competitive employees, and who are especially recommended by the Secretary of Agriculture because of their proved capacity for efficient service may be transferred to appropriate competitive positions in the Department of Agriculture.

Wm. H. Taft.

The White House,

August 26, 1912.
