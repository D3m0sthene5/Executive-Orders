Authorizing Appointment of 25 Clerks in Post Office Department for Employees Previously Transferred from Bureau of Printing and Engraving

Signed by President William H. Taft Thursday, April 28, 1910

1910 CSR 110

648575Executive Order 1195 — Authorizing Appointment of 25 Clerks in Post Office Department for Employees Previously Transferred from Bureau of Printing and Engraving1910President of the United States

Twenty-five positions of clerk, at $900, in the office of the Auditor for the Post Office Department may, as vacancies occur therein, be filled and refilled by promotion on noncompetitive clerk examination from among the employees heretofore transferred from the Bureau of Engraving and Printing who have had seven years of service, at least two years of which shall have been in the office of the Auditor for the Post Office Department.

Wm. H. Taft.

The White House,

April 28, 1910.
