Signed by President Theodore Roosevelt Friday, January 22, 1909

General Orders, No. 22, War Dept., February 4, 1909

1588822Executive Order 1011 — Re-establishing Fort Keogh Military Reservation in Montana1909President of the United States

The Executive Order, dated May 28, 1908 (No. 803) placing the military reservation of Fort Keogh, Montana, under the control of the Secretary of the Interior for disposition as provided for by act of July 5, 1884 (23 Stat. L., 103) or as may be otherwise provided by law, is hereby vacated, and the lands included in said military reservation, which was declared by Executive Order, dated March 14, 1878 (G. O., No. 7, Department of Dakota, March 22, 1878) and reduced by act of Congress, approved July 30, 1890 (26 Stat. L., 292) are hereby placed under the control of the War Department for military purposes.

Theodore Roosevelt.

The White House

January 22, 1909.
