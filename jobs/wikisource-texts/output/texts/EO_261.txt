606589Executive Order 261 — Authorizing Appointment of I. B. Conklin as Clerk in Washington Navy Yard Without Examination1904President of the United States

Mr. I. B. Conklin may be appointed as special laborer (clerk) at $3.28 per diem in the office of the paymaster, navy-yard, Washington, D. C, without examination under the civil-service rules.

Theodore Roosevelt.

White House,

May 26, 1904.
