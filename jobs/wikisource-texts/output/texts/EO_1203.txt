649161Executive Order 1203 — Authorizing Appointment of Helen M. Bullis as Special Inspectress in Immigration Service Without Examination1910President of the United States

Miss Helen M. Bullis may be appointed special inspectress in the Immigration Service, Department of Commerce and Labor, without examination under the civil-service rules.

Wm. H. Taft.

The White House,

May 11, 1910.
