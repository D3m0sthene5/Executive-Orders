636190Executive Order 811 — Authorizing Reinstatement of William J. Dow as Private Secretary to Public Printer Without Regard to Civil Service Rules1908President of the United States

Mr. William J. Dow may be reinstated in the Government Printing Office and appointed as private secretary to the Public Printer without reference to the time limitation prescribed by the civil-service rules.

Theodore Roosevelt.

The White House,

June 9, 1908.
