Revoked Executive Order 2472, October 17, 1916

Signed by President Woodrow Wilson Monday, July 23, 1917

U.S. Official Bulletin No. 68, p. 4, July 30, 1917

597456Executive Order 2671 — Revoking Executive Order of October 17, 1916 which Reserved Certain Lands in Arizona for Townsite Purposes1917President of the United States

It is hereby ordered that Executive Order No. 2472, issued October 17, 1916, reserving for townsite purposes the S. ½ NW. ¼ SW. ¼, sec. 14, in T. 13 N., R. 1 E., Gila and Salt River base and meridian, be and the same is hereby revoked.

Woodrow Wilson.

The White House,

23 July, 1917.
