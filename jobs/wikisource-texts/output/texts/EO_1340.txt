Amends Executive Order 209, March 20, 1903.

Signed by President William H. Taft Friday, April 21, 1911

1911 CSR 110

820379Executive Order 1340 — Amending Civil Service Rules to Except Assistant to Secretary of the Interior from Examination1911President of the United States

Schedule A, Subdivision VIII, of the civil-service rules is hereby amended by the addition of the following paragraph to be numbered 25:

The assistant to the Secretary in the office of the Secretary of the Interior.

Wm. H. Taft.

The White House,

April 21, 1911.
