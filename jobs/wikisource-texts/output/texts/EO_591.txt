625564Executive Order 591 — Authorizing Appointment of Dr. A. Brown in Department of Agriculture Without Regard to Civil Service Rules1907President of the United States

Dr. A. Brown may be appointed in the Department of Agriculture without examination on account of his special ability along the line of work in execution of the pure-food and drugs law.

Theodore Roosevelt.

The White House,

March 15, 1907.
