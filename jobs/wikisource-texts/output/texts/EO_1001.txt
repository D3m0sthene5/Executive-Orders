Signed by President Theodore Roosevelt Friday, January 1, 1909

592000Executive Order 1001 — Authorizing Admittance of W. W. Adams to Examination Without Reference to Civil Service Rules1909President of the United States

Mr. C. M. Huntington may be admitted to examination without reference to the provisions of the civil-service rules disqualifying him for examination.

Theodore Roosevelt.

The White House

January 1, 1909.
