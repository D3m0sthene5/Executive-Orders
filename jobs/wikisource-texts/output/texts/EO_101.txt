Amends Executive Order 89, May 6, 1896.

Signed by President William McKinley Tuesday, July 27, 1897

610301Executive Order 101 — Amending Civil Service Rules Regarding Removal from Service1897President of the United States

Amendment to Rule II.

Amend Rule II by adding a new paragraph, to read as follows:

No removal shall be made from any position subject to competitive examination except for just cause and upon written charges filed with the head of the Department, or other appointing officer, and of which the accused shall have full notice and an opportunity to make defense.

Approved.

William McKinley.

Executive Mansion,

July 27, 1897
