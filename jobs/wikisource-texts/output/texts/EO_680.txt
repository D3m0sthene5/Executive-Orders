1907 CSR 128

625851Executive Order 680 — Authorizing Appointment of Chemist in Bureau of Standards Without Regard to Civil Service Rules1907President of the United States

The vacancy in the position of chemist at $4,000 per annum in the Bureau of Standards, created by the resignation of Dr. William A. Noyes, may be filled without examination and certification under the civil-service rules.

Theodore Roosevelt.

The White House,

August 12, 1907.
