616253Executive Order 363 — Authorizing Appointment of Charles Denby as Chief Clerk of the State Department Without Examination1905President of the United States

Mr. Charles Denby may be appointed chief clerk of the State Department without examination under the civil service rules.

Theodore Roosevelt.

White House,

October 24, 1905.
