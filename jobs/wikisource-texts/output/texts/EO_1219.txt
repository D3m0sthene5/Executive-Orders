648940Executive Order 1219 — Authorizing Appointment of Editor in Bureau of Education Without Examination1910President of the United States

The present vacancy in the position of editor in the Bureau of Education may be filled by appointment without regard to the requirement of examination.

Wm. H. Taft.

The White House,

July 1, 1910.
