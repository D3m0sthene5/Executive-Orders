Signed by President William H. Taft Monday, November 22, 1909

592354Executive Order 1138 — Authorizing Reinstatement of Fred V. Murphy as Architectural Draftsman in Office of Supervising Architect of the Treasury1909President of the United States

Mr. Fred V. Murphy may be reinstated as an architectural draftsman in the office of the Supervising Architect of the Treasury, the time limit for reinstatement being hereby waived.

Wm. H. Taft.

The White House,

November 22, 1909.
