1912 CSR 143

826939Executive Order 1469 — Authorizing Reinstatement of John La Fon, Jr., as Lumberman in Forest Service Without Regard to Civil Service Rules1912President of the United States

Mr. John La Fon, jr., may be reinstated as lumberman in the Forest Service of the Department of Agriculture, the year limitation on eligibility for reinstatement being waived to permit his reemployment. 

Wm. H. Taft.

The White House,

February 7, 1912.
