619437Executive Order 555 — Authorizing Appointment of James Campbell as Deputy Surveyor of Customs Without Regard to Civil Service Rules1907President of the United States

James Campbell, assistant appraiser of merchandise at the port of Baltimore, Md., may be appointed to the position of deputy surveyor of customs at that port without compliance with the civil-service rules.

Theodore Roosevelt.

The White House,

January 30, 1907.
