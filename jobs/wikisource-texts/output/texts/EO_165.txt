John P. Green, Classified as Postage Stamp Agent Without Examination or Certification in Regard to Civil Service Rules

Signed by President Theodore Roosevelt Friday, February 7, 1902

611366Executive Order 165 — John P. Green, Classified as Postage Stamp Agent Without Examination or Certification in Regard to Civil Service Rules1902President of the United States

John P. Green, of Ohio, appointed August 27, 1897, to position of postage-stamp agent at $2,500 per annum in the Post-Office Department under an exception from examination, may be treated as occupying a competitive position without examination or certification by the United States Civil Service Commission.

Theodore Roosevelt.

White House,

February 7, 1902.
