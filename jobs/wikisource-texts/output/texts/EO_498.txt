619314Executive Order 498 — Authorizing Reinstatement of E. J. Mooklar as Clerk in Naval Station at Tutuila, Samoa Without Regard to Civil Service Rules1906President of the United States

Mr. E. J. Mooklar may be reinstated as clerk to the commandant of the naval station at Tutuila, Samoa, without regard to the provisions of the civil service rules limiting eligibility for reinstatement to a period of one year from the date of separation.

Theodore Roosevelt.

The White House,

August 14, 1906.
