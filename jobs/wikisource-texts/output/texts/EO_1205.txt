Amends Executive Order 1195, April 26, 1910.

Signed by President William H. Taft Friday, May 13, 1910

General Orders, No. 103, War Dept., June 6, 1910

655525Executive Order 1205 — Amending Executive Order of April 26, 1910, Reserving Land in Beaver Dam, Alaska, for Use by Army Signal Corps in Operation of Telegraph Lines1910President of the United States

It is hereby ordered that the executive order of April 26, 1910, reserving a tract of public land, situate at Beaver Dam, Alaska, for the use of the Signal Corps, United States Army, in the operation and maintenance of military telegraph lines in Alaska, be, and the same is hereby, amended so as to give the latitude and longitude of said reserved tract, respectively, as follows: Latitude, 61° 13′; longitude, 145° 28′.

Wm. H. Taft.

The White House,

May 13, 1910.
