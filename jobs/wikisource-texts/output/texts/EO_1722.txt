1330337Executive Order 1722 — Authorizing Appointment of Charles A. Gibson in Bureau of Plant Industry Without Regard to Civil Service Rules1913President of the United States

Mr. Charles A. Gibson may be appointed to an appropriate position in the Bureau of Plant Industry of the Department of Agriculture without reference to the provisions of the civil-service rules.

Wm. H. Taft.

The White House,

February 26, 1913.
