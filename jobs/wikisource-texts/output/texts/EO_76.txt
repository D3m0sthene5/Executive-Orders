Signed by President Grover Cleveland Tuesday, July 30, 1895

609913Executive Order 76 — Amending Civil Service Rules Regarding Exceptions from Examination in the Bureau of Immigration1895President of the United States

Amendment to Special Departmental Rule No. 1.

Special departmental Rule I is hereby amended by adding to the list of places excepted from examination in the Treasury Department:

In the Bureau of Immigration: One statistician and stenographer, with power to act as immigrant inspector.

Approved July 30, 1895.

Grover Cleveland.
