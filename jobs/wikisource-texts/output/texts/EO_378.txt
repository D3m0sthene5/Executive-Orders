Signed by President Theodore Roosevelt Thursday, December 7, 1905

1905 CSR 78

616415Executive Order 378 — Amending Civil Service Rules to Make Treasury Department Assistant Assayer Subject to Examination1905President of the United States

Schedule A of classified positions excepted from examination under Rule II, clause 3, is hereby amended by inserting after the word "coiner," in paragraph 8 of Section II, the word "and," and striking out after the word "refiner," the words "one assistant assayer." As amended the paragraph will read:

One private secretary or confidential clerk to the superintendent, one cashier, one deposit weigh clerk, one assistant coiner, and one assistant melter and refiner, in each mint or assay office.

Theodore Roosevelt.

The White House,

December 7, 1905.
