649179Executive Order 1223 — Authorizing Reinstatement of Capt. J. W. Powell as Clerk in Government Printing Office Without Regard to Civil Service Rules1910President of the United States

Capt. J. W. Powell may be reinstated to a clerical position in the Government Printing Office, without regard to the length or cause of his separation therefrom.

Wm. H. Taft.

The White House,

July 2, 1910.
