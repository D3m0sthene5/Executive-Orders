606576Executive Order 260 — Authorizing Reinstatement of Katherine C. Masterson in Government Printing Office Without Regard to Civil Service Rules1904President of the United States

Miss Katherine C. Masterson may be reinstated in the Government Printing Office without regard to the year limitation of Civil-Service Rule IX, relating to reinstatements.

Theodore Roosevelt.

White House,

May 18, 1904.
