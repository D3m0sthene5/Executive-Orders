Signed by President Theodore Roosevelt Saturday, June 21, 1902

611353Executive Order 175 — Authorizing Transfer of Alexander C. Caine as Disbursing Clerk in Justice Department Without Regard to Civil Service Rules1902President of the United States

The vacancy now existing in the position of clerk of class 4, for designation as disbursing clerk in the Department of Justice, may be filled by the transfer of Mr. Alexander C. Caine from the excepted position of examiner which he now holds in the same Department.

Theodore Roosevelt.

White House,

June 21, 1902.
