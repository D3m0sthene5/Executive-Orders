827048Executive Order 1594 — Authorizing Reinstatement of Lillian E. Clark as Clerk in Office of Indian Affairs Without Regard to Civil Service Rules1912President of the United States

Mrs. Lillian E. Clark, widow of F. A. P. Clark, who was carpenter at the Santa Fe School, New Mexico, and who while in the line of duty fell from a scaffold through no fault of his own and through no fault of the Government and in said accident lost his life on October 15, 1911, may be reinstated to a classified clerical position in the Office of Indian Affairs, the requirement of the civil service rule relative to reinstatement being hereby waived, it appearing that Mrs. Clark was a classified employee in the Indian Service until June 31, 1906.

Wm. H. Taft.

The White House,

August 31, 1912.
