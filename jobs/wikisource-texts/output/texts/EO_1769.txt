2000290Executive Order 1769 — Amending Civil Service Rules Regarding Positions Excepted from Examination for Interstate Commerce Commission Personnel1913President of the United States

Schedule A of the civil-service rules specifying classified positions which may be filled without examination or upon noncompetitive examination is hereby amended by adding at the end thereof a new subdivision to be numbered XII, as follows:

XII. INTERSTATE COMMERCE COMMISSION.

Three experts to be members of an advisory board under the act of March 1, 1913, providing for the valuation of the property of carriers.

Five members of a board of engineers under said act.

Woodrow Wilson.

The White House

April 23, 1913.
