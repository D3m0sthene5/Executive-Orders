Signed by President Theodore Roosevelt Tuesday, June 30, 1908

2248694Executive Order 867 — Crater National Forest, Oregon and California1908President of the United States

It is hereby ordered that on and after July 1, 1908, the land within the boundaries shown on the attached diagram, heretofore set apart, reserved, and proclaimed as the Ashland National Forest, parts of the Cascade and Siskiyou National Forests, and a part of the Klamath National Forest in California, shall be known as the Crater National Forest. It is not intended by this order to release any land from reservation or to reserve any land not heretofore embraced in a National Forest. 

Theodore Roosevelt.

The White House,

June 30, 1908.
