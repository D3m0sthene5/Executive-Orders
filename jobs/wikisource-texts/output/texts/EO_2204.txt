Signed by President Woodrow Wilson Tuesday, May 25, 1915

506370Executive Order 2204 — Rent, Fuel, and Electric Current Regulations for Employees of the Panama Canal1915President of the United States

Executive Order.

By virtue of the authority vested in me, it is hereby ordered that the Executive Order of January 15, 1915, relative to charges for rent, fuel and electric current furnished employees of the Panama Canal and the Panama Railroad Company on the Isthmus of Panama, is modified by suspending from the operation thereof so much as relates to rent, fuel, and lights during the period of actual construction of the Panama Canal but not later than June 30, 1916.

Woodrow Wilson 

The White House,

May 25, 1915.
