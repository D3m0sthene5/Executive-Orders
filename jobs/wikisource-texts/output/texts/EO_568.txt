625525Executive Order 568 — Authorizing Appointment of Gilman B. Howe as Clerk in Bureau of the Census Without Regard to Civil Service Rules1907President of the United States

Mr. Gilman B. Howe may be appointed as clerk in the Bureau of the Census without compliance with the civil-service rules.

Theodore Roosevelt.

The White House,

February 20, 1907.
