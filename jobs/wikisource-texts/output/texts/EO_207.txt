613480Executive Order 207 — Authorizing Appointment of Horatio C. Pollock as Clerk at Military Headquarters in Philippine Islands Without Examination1903President of the United States

White House, March 9, 1903.

Mr. Horatio C. Pollock may be appointed to the vacancy now existing in the position of clerk at the military headquarters in the Philippine Islands without examination under the civil-service rules.

Theodore Roosevelt.
