Executive Order 938 (1908)

Authorizing Appointment of Drs. Hopkins and Cunningham as Physicians on Isthmus of Panama Without Regard to Civil Service Rules

Signed by President Theodore Roosevelt Tuesday, September 15, 1908

1908 CSR 136

636214Executive Order 938 — Authorizing Appointment of Drs. Hopkins and Cunningham as Physicians on Isthmus of Panama Without Regard to Civil Service Rules1908President of the United States

Doctors Hopkins and Cunningham may be appointed as physicians to teach microscopic work in connection with tropical diseases at the Ancon Hospital, on the Isthmus of Panama, without reference to the civil-service rules.

Theodore Roosevelt.

The White House,

September 15, 1908.
