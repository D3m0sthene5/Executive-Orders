Amends Executive Order 89, May 6, 1896.

Signed by President William McKinley Tuesday, April 18, 1899

610330Executive Order 115 — Amending Civil Service Rules to Except One Foreman of Job Division in Government Printing Service from Examination1899President of the United States

Civil-service Rule VI is hereby amended by adding thereto the following:

GOVERNMENT PRINTING SERVICE.

One foreman of job division. 

Approved. 

William McKinley.

Executive Mansion,

April 18, 1899.
