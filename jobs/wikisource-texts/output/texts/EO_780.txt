626847Executive Order 780 — Authorizing Appointment of Harry A. Strohm as Special Agent in Office of the Third Assistant Postmaster General Without Examination1908President of the United States

Mr. Harry A. Strohm, of Indiana, a third-class postmaster, who has had nine years' experience, may be appointed as a special agent of the Division of Classification in the Office of the Third Assistant Postmaster-General without examination under the civil-service rules.

Theodore Roosevelt.

The White House,

April 13, 1908.
