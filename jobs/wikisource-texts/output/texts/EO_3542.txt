Revokes Executive Order 3450, May 3, 1921

Signed by President Warren G. Harding Wednesday, August 31, 1921

585767Executive Order 3542 — Revoking Executive Order of May 3, 1921, Temporarily Withdrawing Lands in New Mexico to Determine Advisability for Inclusion in National Monument1921President of the United States

It is hereby ordered that Executive order of May 3, 1921 (3,450), temporarily withdrawing the following described lands in New Mexico for classification and pending determination as to the advisability of reserving said lands for national monument purposes, be revoked:

New Mexico principal meridian.

In T. 20 N., R. 8 E., those portions of secs. 13, 24, and 25 not in private grants;
In T. 20 N., R. 9 E., sec. 18, W. ½ NE. ¼, NW. ¼, and S. ½ sec. 19, and those portions of secs. 28, 29, 30, and 33 not in private grants.

Warren G. Harding

The White House,

August 31, 1921.
