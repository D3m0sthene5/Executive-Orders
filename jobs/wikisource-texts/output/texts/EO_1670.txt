1329626Executive Order 1670 — Authorizing Reinstatement of Samuel E. Fouts as Assistant Examiner in Patent Office Without Regard to Civil Service Rules1912President of the United States

Mr. Samuel E. Fouts may be reinstated as an assistant examiner in the Patent Office, Department of the Interior, without reference to the period of his separation from the service.

Wm. H. Taft.

The White House,

December 30, 1912.
