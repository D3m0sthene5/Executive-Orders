Amends Executive Order of January 19, 1905.

Signed by President Theodore Roosevelt Thursday, September 26, 1907

General Orders, No. 205, War Dept., October 3, 1907

627044Executive Order 693 — Redefining Boundaries of Camp Keithley Military Reservation, Mindanao, Philippine Islands1907President of the United States

It is hereby ordered that the military reservation of Camp Keithley, Mindanao, Philippine Islands, reserved, under the name of "Camp Marahui" by executive order of January nineteenth, nineteen hundred and five (General Orders, Numbered Twelve, War Department, Washington, January twenty-eighth, nineteen hundred and five), be modified so as to reserve all lands, and those only, included within metes and bounds described as follows:

Beginning at station 1 on map of Proposed Brigade Post on Lake Lanao, Mindanao, P. I., tracing of which is filed in office of the Chief Engineer Officer, Philippines Division, said station being a stone monument on the shore of Lake Lanao, near the mouth of the Agus River; thence N. 52° 4′ E., 6,530 feet to station 2; thence N. 4° 50′ E., 17,938 feet to station 3 on Mt. Wheeler; thence S. 61° 00′ W., 24,663 feet to station 4 on Lions Paw; thence S. 56° 21′ W., 17,071 feet to station 5 on Umbrella Tree; thence S. 8° 49′ W., 15,161 feet to station 6 on Flake Hill; thence S. 77° 56′ E., 865 feet to station 7 on Gurayn Point; thence N. 81° 10′ E., 23,595 feet to station 8 on Kailaden Point; thence N. 51° 45′ E., 13,542 feet to station 9 on shore line of Lake Lanao; thence following shore line of Lake Lanao to station 1 or point of beginning, N. 47° 22′ W., 3,827 feet. Area 17,076 acres, more or less. All bearings are magnetic. Magnetic deviation 1° 35′ E., for October, 1906.

Theodore Roosevelt.

The White House, September 26, 1907.
