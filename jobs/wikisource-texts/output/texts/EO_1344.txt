1911 CSR 111

820415Executive Order 1344 — Authorizing Reinstatement of Leroy T. Steward as Superintendent at Chicago Post Office Without Regard to Civil Service Rules1911President of the United States

Mr. Leroy T. Steward may be reinstated as superintendent of city delivery at the Chicago post office without reference to the year limitation prescribed by the civil-service rules.

Wm. H. Taft.

The White House,

April 29, 1911.
