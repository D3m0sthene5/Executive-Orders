Revokes Executive Order 1540, May 29, 1912.

Signed by President Woodrow Wilson Friday, July 18, 1913

582074Executive Order 1798 — Revoking Executive Order of May 29, 1912, Which Reserved Lands for Walapai Indians1913President of the United States

It is hereby ordered that Executive Order No. 1540, dated May 29, 1912, reserving certain tracts of land in Arizona from settlement, entry, sale or other disposition for use of the Walapai Indians be, and the same is hereby, revoked.

Woodrow Wilson.

The White House,

July 18, 1913.
