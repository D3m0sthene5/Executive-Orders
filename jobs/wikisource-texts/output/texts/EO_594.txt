625570Executive Order 594 — Authorizing Appointment of Charles P. Galpin as Clerk in Pension Office Without Examination1907President of the United States

Mr. Charles P. Galpin, a messenger in the Pension Office, may be appointed to a clerical position in that office without examination.

Theodore Roosevelt.

The White House,

March 18, 1907.
