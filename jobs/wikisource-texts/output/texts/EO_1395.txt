Signed by President William H. Taft Wednesday, August 9, 1911

1912 CSR 141

826619Executive Order 1395 — Authorizing Reinstatement of Lilly Dent Rogers as Clerk in Treasury Department Without Regard to Civil Service Rules1911President of the United States

Mrs. Lilly Dent Rogers may be reinstated as a clerk of class 1 in the National Bank Redemption Agency in the Treasurer's Office, Treasury Department, without regard to the length of time since her separation from the service.

Wm. H. Taft.

The White House,

August 9, 1911.
