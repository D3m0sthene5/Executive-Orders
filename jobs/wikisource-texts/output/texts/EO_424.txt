619097Executive Order 424 — Authorizing Appointment of Robert Lee Gray as Assistant Messenger in Department of Justice Without Examination1906President of the United States

Robert Lee Gray may be appointed as assistant messenger in the Department of Justice without examination under the civil-service rules.

Theodore Roosevelt.

The White House,

March 1, 1906.
