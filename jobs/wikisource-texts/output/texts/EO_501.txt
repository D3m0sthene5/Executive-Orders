Signed by President Theodore Roosevelt Friday, September 7, 1906

1906 CSR 105

619320Executive Order 501 — Authorizing Appointment of Alice A. Masters as Clerk in Bureau of the Census Without Examination1906President of the United States

Miss Alice A. Masters may be appointed as a clerk in the Census Bureau without examination under the civil service rules.

Theodore Roosevelt.

The White House,

September 7, 1906.
