619216Executive Order 453 — Authorizing Reinstatement of Jesse F. Gates as Classified Laborer in Treasury Department Without Regard to Civil Service Rules1906President of the United States

Mr. Jesse F. Gates may be reinstated as a classified laborer in the Treasury Department on account of his services in the Spanish war, in which he was severely wounded.

Theodore Roosevelt.

The White House,

June 6, 1906.
