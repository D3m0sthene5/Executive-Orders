Authorizing Permanent Appointment of Dollie A. Slayton as Clerk in Bureau of the Census Without Regard to Civil Service Rules

Signed by President Theodore Roosevelt Monday, October 16, 1905

1905 CSR 92

616243Executive Order 360 — Authorizing Permanent Appointment of Dollie A. Slayton as Clerk in Bureau of the Census Without Regard to Civil Service Rules1905President of the United States

Miss Dollie A. Slayton, of Montana, who is now serving in a temporary position in the Bureau of the Census as the result of certification, may be retained and appointed to a clerical position on the permanent roll of that Bureau.

Theodore Roosevelt.

White House,

October 16, 1905.
