Signed by President Grover Cleveland Monday, December 2, 1895

609929Executive Order 83 — Amending Civil Service Rules to Exclude Department of Labor Special Agents from Noncompetitive Appointment1895President of the United States

Special Order Under General Rule III.

So much of Executive orders heretofore issued under General Rule III, section 2, clause (c), as provides for the appointment of special agents in the Department of Labor by noncompetitive examination is hereby revoked.

Approved.

Grover Cleveland.

Executive Mansion,

December 2, 1895.
