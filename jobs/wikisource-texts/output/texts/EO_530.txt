Amends Executive Order 469, June 27, 1906

Signed by President Theodore Roosevelt Wednesday, December 12, 1906

620553Executive Order 530 — Amending Consular Service Regulation of June 27, 1906, Governing Appointment and Promotion in the Consular Service1906President of the United States

Let student interpreters be included under paragraph 2 sub-division (a) and under paragraph 11, on the same footing as vice consuls, deputy consuls and consular agents.

Theodore Roosevelt.

The White House,

December 12, 1906.
