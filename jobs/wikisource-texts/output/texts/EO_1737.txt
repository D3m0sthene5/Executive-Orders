1330374Executive Order 1737 — Authorizing Reinstatement of Fred Roege as Assistant Postmaster Without Regard to Civil Service Rules1913President of the United States

Mr. Fred Roege may be reinstated as assistant postmaster in the post office at Pensacola, Fla., without regard to the length of time that he has been separated from the service.

Wm. H. Taft.

The White House,

March 3, 1913.
