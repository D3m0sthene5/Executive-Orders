Amends Executive Order 328-B, May 24, 1905

Signed by President William H. Taft Thursday, November 23, 1911

General Orders No. 163, War Department, 1911

1327113Executive Order 1438 — Restoring Certain Lands Between Hot Springs and Fort Gibbon, Alaska, to Public Domain1911President of the United States

Under authority of the act of Congress approved July 5, 1884, entitled "An
act to provide for the disposal of abandoned and useless military reservations"
(23 Stat. L., 103), that portion of the right of way for military telegraph lines
in Alaska reserved by Executive order dated May 24, 1905 (G. O. No. 83. War
Department, June 5, 1905), which is located between Hot Springs and Fort
Gibbon, Alaska, is hereby placed under the control of the Secretary of the
Interior for disposition under said act of Congress, or as may be otherwise
provided by law.

Wm. H. Taft.

The White House, November 23, 1911.
