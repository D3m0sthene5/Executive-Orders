826958Executive Order 1506 — Authorizing Reinstatement of Charles W. Rider as Storekeeper-Gauger in Internal Revenue Service Without Regard to Civil Service Rules1912President of the United States

Mr. J. C. Wilson may be reinstated as storekeeper-gauger in the Internal Revenue Service without regard to the length of time that he has been separated from the service.

Wm. H. Taft.

The White House,

March 26, 1912.
