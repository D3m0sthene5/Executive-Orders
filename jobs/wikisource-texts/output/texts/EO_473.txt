Signed by President Theodore Roosevelt Saturday, June 30, 1906

1906 CSR 102

619225Executive Order 473 — Authorizing Transfer of Charles N. Twadell to Competitive Position in Bureau of Pensions Without Regard to Civil Service Rules1906President of the United States

Mr. Charles N. Twadell may be transferred from a position of special examiner in the Bureau of Pensions, which is excepted from examination under Schedule A, to a similar position which is not excepted.

Theodore Roosevelt.

The White House,

June 30, 1906.
