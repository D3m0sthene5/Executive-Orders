Amends Executive Order 89, May 6, 1896. Amended by Executive Order 128, March 29, 1900, and Executive Order 140, July 31, 1901.

Signed by President Grover Cleveland Saturday, January 2, 1897

610294Executive Order 94 — Amending Civil Service Rules Regarding Age Limitations1897President of the United States

Amendment to Rule V.

Amend Rule V, section 4, prescribing age limitations for the classified service, by striking out the table after the tenth line and substituting therefor the following:
