636179Executive Order 798 — Authorizing Continued Employment of Richard H. Taylor as Inspector in Department of Commerce and Labor Without Regard to Civil Service Rules1908President of the United States

Mr. Richard H. Taylor, of California, may be continued as an immigrant inspector for assignment to special investigations of a confidential character in the Department of Commerce and Labor so long as the department may regard such services necessary and in the interest of good administration.

Theodore Roosevelt.

The White House,

May 23, 1908.
