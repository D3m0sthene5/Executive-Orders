636183Executive Order 805 — Authorizing Employment of Charles Lutz in Charge of Statistics and Accounts in Interstate Commerce Commission Without Examination1908President of the United States

Mr. Charles Lutz may be employed in charge of statistics and accounts under the Interstate Commerce Commission without examination under the civil-service rules. This employment may be terminated at any time in the discretion of the Interstate Commerce Commission.

Theodore Roosevelt.

The White House,

June 2, 1908.
