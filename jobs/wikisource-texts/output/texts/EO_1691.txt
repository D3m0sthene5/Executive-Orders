1330304Executive Order 1691 — Authorizing Promotion of Gertrude Printz to Clerk Treasury Department Without Regard to Civil Service Rules1913President of the United States

Miss Gertrude Printz, now an employee of the subclerical grade in the Treasury Department, may be promoted to the grade of clerk in that department without compliance with civil-service rules.

Wm. H. Taft.

The White House,

January 24, 1913.
