Signed by President Theodore Roosevelt Wednesday, February 17, 1909

592297Executive Order 1029 — Authorizing Promotion of John Hunter, Peter J. Kaster, and Joseph Stangle to Assistant Weigher in the Customs Service at the Port of New York1909President of the United States

John Hunter, Peter J. Kaster, and Joseph Stangle may be promoted from the position of classified laborer to that of assistant weigher in the Customs Service at the port of New York, the provisions of the promotion regulations which operate against such promotions being hereby waived.

Theodore Roosevelt.

The White House

February 17, 1909.
