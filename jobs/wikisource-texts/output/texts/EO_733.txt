1907 CSR 107

624341Executive Order 733 — Amending Civil Service Rules to Except Certain Shipping Commissioners of Department of Commerce and Labor from Examination1908President of the United States

Schedule A of classified positions excepted from examination under Rule II, clause 3, is hereby amended by adding to Subdivision IX, relating to the Department of Commerce and Labor, the following paragraph:

Shipping commissioners whose compensation for the fiscal year ending June 30, 1907, was, as shown by the records in the Department of Commerce and Labor, $2,500 or over.

Theodore Roosevelt.

The White House,

January 13, 1908.
