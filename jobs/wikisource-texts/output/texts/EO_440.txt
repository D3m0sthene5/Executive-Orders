619111Executive Order 440 — Authorizing Transfer of Harry H. Brewster to Finance Clerk in Post Office Service Without Regard to Civil Service Rules1906President of the United States

Harry H. Brewster, money order and stamp clerk at the South St. Joseph, Mo., post-office, which is unclassified, may be transferred to the position of finance clerk in the St. Joseph, Mo., post-office, without reference to the requirements of the civil-service rules.

Theodore Roosevelt.

The White House,

April 14, 1906.
