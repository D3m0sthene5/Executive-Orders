827060Executive Order 1612 — Authorizing Appointment of R. A. Kirk as Deputy Collector of Internal Revenue Without Regard to Civil Service Rules1912President of the United States

Mr. R. A. Kirk may be appointed a deputy collector of internal revenue without examination under the civil-service rules.

Wm. H. Taft.

The White House,

September 21, 1912.
