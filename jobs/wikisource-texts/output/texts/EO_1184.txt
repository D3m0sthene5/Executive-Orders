Providing that Moneys Paid for Liquor Licenses in Canal Zone be Collected by Collector of Revenues

Signed by President William H. Taft Saturday, April 2, 1910

III Canal Record 267, April 20, 1910

565911Executive Order 1184 — Providing that Moneys Paid for Liquor Licenses in Canal Zone be Collected by Collector of Revenues1910President of the United States

The provisions of the second paragraph of the Executive Order of March 13, 1907, which fix the duties of the district tax collectors of the Canal Zone, shall not require the collection by them of moneys to be paid for liquor licenses under the regulation respecting the sale of intoxicating liquors in the Canal Zone, approved by the Isthmian Canal Commission April 27, 1907; but such moneys shall be collected by the Collector of Revenues or his deputy or assistant.

Wm. H. Taft.

The White House,

April 2, 1910.
