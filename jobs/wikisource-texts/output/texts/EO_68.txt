Signed by President Grover Cleveland Thursday, July 11, 1895

609827Executive Order 68 — Amending Civil Service Rules Regarding Temporary Procedures for Promotion in Customs Districts1895President of the United States

In the exercise of the power vested in him by the Constitution, by the seventeen hundred and fifty-third section of the Revised Statutes, and the act entitled "An act to regulate and improve the civil service of the United States," approved January 16, 1883, the President hereby makes and promulgates the following rule to cancel and be in lieu of Customs Rule V of the revised civil-service rules:

Customs Rule V.

Until promotion regulations have been applied to a classified customs district the following promotions may be made therein at any time after absolute appointment:

(a)
Any employee in any grade, upon any test of fitness determined upon by the nominating officer, to any vacant place in the class next above the one in which be may be serving, except to the positions of weigher and gauger.

(b)
Any employee in any grade may be promoted or transferred to a vacancy in the lowest class of the grade of examiner after passing the examiner examination, to a vacancy in the lowest class of the grade of weigher after passing the weigher examination, to a vacancy in the lowest class of the grade of gauger after passing the gauger examination, or to a vacancy in the lowest class of any other grade than the one in which he may be serving upon passing the examination provided for that grade.

Approved July 11, 1895.

Grover Cleveland.
