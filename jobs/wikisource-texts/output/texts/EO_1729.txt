1330356Executive Order 1729 — Authorizing Appointment of Louise F. Bradley as Charwoman in State, War, and Navy Department Building Without Regard to Labor Regulations1913President of the United States

Louise F. Bradley may be appointed charwoman in the State, War, and Navy Department Building without reference to her standing on the unskilled laborer register.

Wm. H. Taft.

The White House,

March 3, 1913.
