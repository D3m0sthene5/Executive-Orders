619301Executive Order 482 — Authorizing Appointment of Michael J. Holland as Clerk in Marine Corps Headquarters Without Regard to Civil Service Rules1906President of the United States

Michael J. Holland, an enlisted man in the Marine Corps, may be appointed clerk at its headquarters.

Theodore Roosevelt.

The White House,

July 20, 1906.
