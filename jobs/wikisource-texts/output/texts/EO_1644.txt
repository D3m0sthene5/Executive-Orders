1913 CSR 112

1329630Executive Order 1644 — Exception of Employees at the Neopit Lumber Mills1912President of the United States

Schedule A of classified positions excepted from examination under Rule II, clause 3, is hereby amended by adding to Subdivision VIII, Department of the Interior, the following paragraph:

All employees of the Neopit lumber mills on the Menominee Indian Reservation in Wisconsin.

Wm. H. Taft.

The White House,

November 15, 1912.
