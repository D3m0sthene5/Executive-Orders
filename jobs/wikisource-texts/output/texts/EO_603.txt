625604Executive Order 603 — Authorizing Appointment of Private Secretary to Commissioner of the General Land Office Without Regard to Civil Service Rules1907President of the United States

One clerk or examiner for duty as private secretary to the Commissioner of the General Land Office may be appointed without reference to civil-service rules.

Theodore Roosevelt.

The White House,

March 29, 1907.
