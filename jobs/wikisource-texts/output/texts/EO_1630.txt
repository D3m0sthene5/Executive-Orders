1329588Executive Order 1630 — Authorizing Appointment of Louis R. Krumm as Chief Inspector in Bureau of Navigation, Department of Commerce and Labor, Without Regard to Civil Service Rules1912President of the United States

Mr. Louis R. Krumm may be appointed as chief inspector, Radio Division, Bureau of Navigation, Department of Commerce and Labor, without reference to the requirements of the civil-service rules.

Wm. H. Taft.

The White House,

October 28, 1912.
