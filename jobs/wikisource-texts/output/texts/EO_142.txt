Signed by President Theodore Roosevelt Friday, November 1, 1901

611426Executive Order 142 — Authorizing Appointment of Steward in White House Without Examination1901President of the United States

Special Rule.

An appointment to the vacancy now existing in the position of steward of the White House may be made without examination under the civil-service rules.

Theodore Roosevelt.

White House,

November 1, 1901.
