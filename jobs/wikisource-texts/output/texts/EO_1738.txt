1330376Executive Order 1738 — Authorizing Appointment of Charles H. Shaw in New York Customs Service Without Regard to Civil Service Rules1913President of the United States

Mr. Charles H. Shaw, now employed in the Customs Service at the port of New York, may be appointed to a classified position in the New York customs service without reference to the civil-service rules.

Wm. H. Taft.

The White House,

March 3, 1913.
