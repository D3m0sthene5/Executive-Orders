827062Executive Order 1604 — Authorizing Promotion of George E. Long to Clerk in Bureau of Insular Affairs Without Examination1912President of the United States

George E. Long may be promoted from the position of messenger to a minor clerical position in the Bureau of Insular Affairs of the War Department without examination.

Wm. H. Taft.

The White House,

September 12, 1912.
