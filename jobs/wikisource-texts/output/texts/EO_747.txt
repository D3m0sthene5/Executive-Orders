626823Executive Order 747 — Authorizing Reinstatement of Isabel E. McKinley in War Department Without Regard to Civil Service Rules1908President of the United States

Mrs. Isabel E. McKinley may be reinstated in the War Department without regard to the length of her separation from that Department.

Theodore Roosevelt.

The White House,

February 4, 1908.
