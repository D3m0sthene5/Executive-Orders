Signed by President Woodrow Wilson Friday, November 23, 1917

U.S. Official Bulletin No. 180, p. 4, December 10, 1917

601548Executive Order 2760 — Returning William V. Hagar to Service in the United States Coast and Geodetic Survey1917President of the United States

Effective October 10, William V. Hagar who was transferred to the service and jurisdiction of the Navy Department by Executive order of September 24, 1917, is returned to his former status in the service and jurisdiction of the United States Coast and Geodetic Survey, Department of Commerce.

Woodrow Wilson 

The White House,

November 23, 1917.
