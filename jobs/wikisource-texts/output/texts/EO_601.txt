625590Executive Order 601 — Authorizing Appointment of Theresa S. Aubright as Clerk in Classified Service Without Examination1907President of the United States

Mrs. Theresa S. Aubright may be appointed to a clerical position in the classified civil service without examination.

Theodore Roosevelt.

The White House,

March 26, 1907.
