613496Executive Order 195 — Authorizing Appointment of F. L. Templeton as Clerk in Post Office Department Without Regard to Civil Service Rules1902President of the United States

White House, December 23, 1902.

Mr. F. L. Templeton, a clerk appointed on April 10, 1900, and paid from the appropriation for postal service in the territory held by military occupation, having failed to receive permanent appointment given to others paid from the same appropriation by reason of the fact that he was assigned to duty in Washington in connection with the furnishing of supplies, may be included within the classified service of the Post-Office Department.

Theodore Roosevelt.
