826982Executive Order 1530 — Authorizing Appointment of Louise Lester as Clerk in Classified Service Without Regard to Civil Service Rules1912President of the United States

Miss Louise Lester may be appointed a clerk in the classified executive civil service without compliance with the civil-service rules.

Wm. H. Taft.

The White House,

May 10, 1912.
