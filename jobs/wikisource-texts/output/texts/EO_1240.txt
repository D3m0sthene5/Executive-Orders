Amends Executive Order 209, March 20, 1903.

Signed by President William H. Taft Saturday, August 20, 1910

1910 CSR 108

648555Executive Order 1240 — Amending Civil Service Rules Regarding Exceptions from Examination in Treasury Department1910President of the United States

Schedule A of positions excepted from examination under Rule II, clause 3, of the Civil Service Rules is hereby amended by striking out paragraph 1 of Subdivision III relating to the Treasury Department, which reads:

Not exceeding one solicitor to the collector at the port of New York, if authorized by the Secretary of the Treasury.

The remaining paragraphs of the subdivision will be renumbered accordingly.

Wm. H. Taft.

The White House,

August 20, 1910.
