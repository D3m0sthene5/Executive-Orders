625849Executive Order 676 — Authorizing Appointment of Frank Huntington in Department of Agriculture Without Regard to Civil Service Rules1907President of the United States

Mr. Frank Huntington may be appointed to a suitable position in the Department of Agriculture, in order that his long editorial experience in the handling of technical or scientific articles may be utilized.

Theodore Roosevelt.

The White House,

August 9, 1907.
