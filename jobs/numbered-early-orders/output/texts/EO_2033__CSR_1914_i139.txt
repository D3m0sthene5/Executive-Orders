132 THIRTY-FIRST REPORT U. S. CIVIL SERVICE COMMISSION.
September 2, 1914.
The year limit upon eligibility for reinstatement is extended to permit the rein-
statement of Maurice B. landers as assistant examiner in the Patent Office, Depart-
ment of the Interior.

through examination, and resigned February 18, 1913. Since his separation he has
been engaged in the practice of patent law. He has a good professional and technical
education and is reported by the Commissioner of Patents to be a man of ability and
