May 11, 1916.
Mrs. Enna Wilson Noel may be reinstated to a position in the classified service in
the War Department without regard to the requirements of the civil service rules.
Mrs. Noelis the widow of Edward McC. Noel, whose death on April 16, 1915, termi-
nated a service of nearly 33 years in the office of the Surgeon General, and leaves her
dependent upon employment for support. Prior to her marriage she served as clerk
in the office of the Surgeon General from December 18, 1893, to February 9, 1900, and
previously in other offices since November 7, 1889. Her reinstatement is recom-
mended by the Secretary of War and the Surgeon General of the Army.
The Civil Service Commission does not concur in the recommendation.
