LOS COYOTES.
It is hereby ordered that Executive proclamation dated February 14, 1907,
reserving certain lands in California for the San Jacinto National Forest, now the
Cleveland National Forest, be, and the same is hereby, amended so as to eliminate
from the forest reserve the sections of land hereinafter described, which are hereby
added to the Los Coyotes Mission Indian Reservation in the State of California:
Township 10 south of range 5 east, San Bernardino meridian, sections 6, 7, 18,
19, 30, and 31.
This order shall not affect any existing valid right of any person.
Wooprow WILSON.
Tue Wuite House, April 13, 1914.
