120 THIRTY-SECOND REPORT V. S. CIVIL SERVICE COMMISSION.
December 1, 1914.
Mrs. Onie Cummings maybe appointed as a clerk in the Department of Agriculture
without reference to the civil-service rules at a salary of $900 per annum.
This order is issued upon the recommendation of the Secretary of Agriculture, who
states that Mrs. Cummings 's training and experience are such as to enable her to
render good service. She is the widow of Mr. R. F. Cummings, formerly a clerk at a
salary of $1,400 per annum in the Bureau of Soils, Department of Agriculture. Mr.
