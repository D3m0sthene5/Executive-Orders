May 13, 1915.
Michael L. Eidsness, jr., of Iowa, a minor clerk of class D ($900) in the Post Office
Department, may be promoted to the position of clerk without further civil service
examination.
This order was issued upon the recommendation of the Postmaster General, who
stated that while Mr. Eidsness was not able to pass the first-grade examination he had
