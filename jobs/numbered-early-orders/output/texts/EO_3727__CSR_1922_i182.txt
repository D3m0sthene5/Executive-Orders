August 28, 1922.
Mr. William M. Josephi may be reinstated in the Postal Service at San
Francisco, Calif., without regard to the length of time he has been separated
from the service.
This order is Issued upon the recommendation of the Postmaster General.
The Civil Service Commission did not concur In the recommendation.
September 6, 1922.
