Two  Military  Reservations In  the  Philippine  Islands Restored  to  Public  Use-
EXECUTIVE  ORDER.
Pursuant  to  the  authority  conferred upon  me  by  section  9  of  the  act  of  Con- gress approved  August  29;  1910,  entitled "An  act  to  declare  the  purpose  of  the people  of  the  United  States  as  to the  future  political  status  of  the  peo- ple of  the  Philippine  Islands,  and  to provide  a  more  autonomous  government for  those  islands,"  the  following  military reservations  In  the  Philippine  Islands, being  no  longer  required  for  the  purpose for  which  reserved,  are  hereby  placed under  the  control  of  the  Government  of the  Philippine  Islands  to  be  administered for  the  benefit  of  the  Inhabitants  thereof, as  contemplated  by  the  authority  above mentioned :
1.  Siassi  Military  Reservation,  situated In  the  municipality  of  Siassi,  Province  of Moro,  Department  of  Mindanao  mid  Sulu, Island  of  Siassi.
