November 8, 1913.
The temporary employment of Mr. Neil Senter, as clerk in the Quartermaster
Corps of the Army at Fort Bayard, N. Mex., may be continued for a period of six
months from November 11, 1913, without reference to the provisions of paragraph 4,
civil-service Rule VIII.
This order is issued in view of the fact that Mr. Senter' s separation from the service
at this time would be prejudicial to his health.
