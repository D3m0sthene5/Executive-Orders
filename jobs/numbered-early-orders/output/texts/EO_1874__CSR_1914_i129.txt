December 22, 1913.
Mr. Albert K. Brown, now employed as a classified laborer in the Bureau of Fish
and Fisheries, may be promoted to the position of clerk at $1,200 per annum without
examination under the civil-service rules.
Mr. Brown has for years performed without promotion work of a character that would
justify much larger pay. This action is taken on account of Mr. Brown's experience
