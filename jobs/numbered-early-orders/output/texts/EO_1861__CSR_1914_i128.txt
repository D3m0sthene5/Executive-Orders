November 18, 1913.
Mr. George U. Harn, jr., of Ohio, may be reinstated in the classified service under
the Department of Agriculture, notwithstanding the length of his separation from the
service.
Mr. Harn was removed from the position of assistant observer at a salary at the rate
of $1,000 per annum in the Weather Bureau on September 25, 1909; the reason given
at the time being: "for spitefully attacking his superior officer through the instru-
mentality of a third person." The Secretary of Agriculture reports that from a care-
ful examination of the case it does not appear that Mr. Harn was guilty of the charge
