122 THIBTY-FIBST REPORT U. S. CIVIL SERVICE COMMISSION.
December 5, 1913.
Mr. Herbert L. Solyom may be reinstated as an assistant examiner in the Patent
Office, the year limit upon eligibility in his case being waived.
Mr. Solyom served four and one-half years as an assistant examiner in the Patent
Office, resigning on August 9, 1907, to accept the position of chief forecaster in the
meteorological office of the Argentine Republic. While employed as assistant exam-
iner in the Patent Office he displayed unusual ability and scientific attainments, his
