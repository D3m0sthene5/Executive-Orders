Mrs. Inez M. Clark and Miss Emily I. Kramer may be appointed clerks in the
Bureau of War Risk Insurance in the Treasury Department without reference to the
civil -service rules.
This order is issued upon the recommendation of the Secretary of the Treasury, who
states that on account of the technical character of the work required specially trained
clerks are needed, and that Mrs. Clark and Miss Kramer have demonstrated that they
possess the peculiar Qualifications necessary.
The Civil Service Commission does not concur in the recommendation.
