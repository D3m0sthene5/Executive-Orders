Mr. W. T. Bateman may be appointed a watchman in a navy yard without compli-
ance with the civil-service rules.
This order is issued on the recommendation of the Secretary of the Navy, who says
the case is a deserving one and that the general character of Mr. Bateman is such that
he feels justified in recommending an exemption from the civil-service rules which
will permit his employment in a navy yard so that he may be able to support his family.
