The  representatives  of  the  spinners  and weavers  shall  be  designated  by  their  re- spective organizations,  two  in  each  case from  Catalonia  and  one  from  elsewhere in  Spain.  The  votes  of  the  spinners  and weavers  in  the  selection  of  delegates  shall be  based  on  the  number  of  spindles,  looms, and  laborers  employed  by  each.
EXECUTIVE  ORDER.
In  accordance  with  the  authority  vested in  me  by  the  "  act  to  temporarily  increase the  commissioned  and  warrant  and  en- listed strength  of  the  Navy  ,  and  Marine Corps,  and  for  other  purposes,"  approved May  22,  1917,  I,  Woodrow  Wilson,  Presi- dent of  the  United  States  of  America,  do hereby  declare  that  a  national  emergency exists,  and  that  there  shall  be  transferred to  the  service  and  jurisdiction  of  the  Navy Department  the  following-named  person now  part  of  the  commissioned  personnel of  the  Coast  and  Geodetic  Survey :
Maurice  Eli  Levy.
Woodrow  Wilson. The  White  House,
April  12,  1918.
