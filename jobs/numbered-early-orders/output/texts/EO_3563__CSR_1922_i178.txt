October 22, 1021.
Mr. Georgie E. Taylor may be reinstated in the postal service at Richmond,
Va., without regard to the length of his separation from the service.
Mr. Taylor was removed from the position of clerk in the Richmond post
office on May 23, 1911, charged with embezzlement. The Postmaster General
states that the case has been carefully reinvestigated and he is convinced that
Mr. Taylor's removal was not justified and that his reinstatement would, in a
measure, correct the wrong done him.
