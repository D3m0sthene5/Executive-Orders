This order is issued upon the recommendation of the Secretary of War and the Chief
of Engineers, with the concurrence of the Civil Service Commission.
January 20, 1015.
Mr. Newton C. Llewellyn maybe reinstated as a carrier in the Knoxville, Tenn.,
post office without regard to the length of his separation from the service.
Mr. Llewellyn entered the Postal Service as a substitute carrier at Knoxville on
January 18, 1895, and remained in the service until February 28, 1907, when he
resigned.
The Postmaster General states that Mr. Llewellyn's record is good, that it is a
meritorious case, and that the department has no objection to the issuance of an order.
