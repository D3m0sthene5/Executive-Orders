Tue White House, 7 January, 1921.
AGUA CALIENTE.
It is hereby ordered, under authority contained in the act of March 2, 1917 (39
Stat. L., 969-976), that the period of trust on lands patented to the Agua Caliente
Band of Mission Indians in California, which trust expires during the calendar year
1921, be, and the same is hereby, extended for a period of ten years from the date
of expiration.
Tue Wire House, April 30, 1921.
WARREN G. HARDING.
TEMECULA.
It is hereby ordered, under authority of the act approved June 21, 1906 (34
Stat., 325-326), that the trust period on allotments made to Indians of the Temecula
Band in California, which trust period expires during the calendar year 1922, be, and
the same is hereby, extended for a period of ten years from date of expiration.
