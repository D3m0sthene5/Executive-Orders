Tue White House, 18 June, 1917.
It is hereby ordered that the following-described land in Nevada be, and it is
hereby, reserved from entry, sale, or other disposal, and set aside for the use of a
band of homeless Shoshone Indians now residing near the town of Winnemucca,
Nevada: The SE. 4 of section 32, township 36 north, range 38 east, M. D. M.:
Provided, That this withdrawal shall not affect any existing legal right of any person
to any of the land herein described.
Woobrow WiLsoN.
Tue Ware House, 8 February, 1918.
NEW MEXICO.
