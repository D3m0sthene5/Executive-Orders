September 2, 1015.
Miss Bertha Goldberg, of Washington, D. C, may be appointed to a clerkship in
the Bureau of Foreign and Domestic Commerce, Department of Commerce, without
reference to the civil-service rules.
Miss Goldberg has passed the stenographer and typewriter examination with an
average of 84.29, and has been serving temporarily in the bureau since February 25,
1915. The chief of the Bureau of Foreign and Domestic Commerce and the Acting
Secretary of Commerce recommend her permanent appointment on account of her
