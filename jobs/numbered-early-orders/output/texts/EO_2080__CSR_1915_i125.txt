Mr. Smith's previous efficient service in the Interstate Commerce Commission and the
exceptional qualifications he has evinced in the performance of hia duties as confiden-
tial clerk to the Postmaster General.
November 11, 1914.
Mrs. Ellen McCabe Bain may be reinstated as an operative in the Bureau of Engrav-
ing and Printing without regard to the length of time she has been separated from the
service.
This order is issued on the recommendation of the Secretary of the Treasury, who
states that Mrs. Bain served 11 years in the Bureau of Engraving and Printii^; that
she rendered efficient service there; and it is believed that her reinstatement will be
in the interests of the service.
