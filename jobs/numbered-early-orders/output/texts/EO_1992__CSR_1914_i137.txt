Mrs. Anna B. Frost may be appointed to the position of copyist, at $900 per annum,
in the Bureau of Construction and Repair, Navy Department, without reference to
the requirement of examination under the civil-service rules.
Mrs. Frost is the wife of Mr. Floyd T. Frost, who has been continuously employed
in the Navy Department since March 8, 1890, and who has rendered faithful and
efficient service. In 1910 Mr. Frost suffered a stroke of paralysis, from which he has
never fully recovered, and at the present time he is wholly incapacitated for work,
having been absent from the office since the latter part of March, 1914.
