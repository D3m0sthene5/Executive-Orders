November 10, 1914.
Mrs. Alice B. Smith and Mrs. Eunice B. Neff may be appointed to clerical positions
in the classified service of the Post Office Department without compliance with the
civil-service rules.
Mrs. Smith is the widow of John H. Smith, formerly a letter carrier at the Washing- '
ton post office, who served the Government continuously for 42 years and died as a
result oi three strokes of paralysis (possibly due to exposure while serving his route as
a carrier) and left a widow and two small children without means of support.
Mrs. Neff is the widow of W. E. Neff, a former employee of the Post Office Depart-
ment, who, after 10 years' faithful service, had a nervous breakdown, which resulted
in insanity, and he recently died leaving three small children. Mrs. Neff is also the
sole support of an aunt, who is an elderly woman.
The Civil Service Commission does not reconunend the issuance of this order.
November 10, 1914.
Mr. Edwin B. Smith, who formerly held the position of clerk in the Interstate Com-
merce Commission, may be reinstated to a classified position in the Post Office Depart-
ment without regard to the length of his separation or the provisions of the civil-service
rules requiring reinstatement in the same department.
