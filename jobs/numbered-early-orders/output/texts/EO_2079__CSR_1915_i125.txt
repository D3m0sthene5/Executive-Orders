November 10, 1914.
Mr. Edwin B. Smith, who formerly held the position of clerk in the Interstate Com-
merce Commission, may be reinstated to a classified position in the Post Office Depart-
ment without regard to the length of his separation or the provisions of the civil-service
rules requiring reinstatement in the same department.
This order is issued on the recommendation of the Postmaster General because of
Mr. Smith's previous efficient service in the Interstate Commerce Commission and the
exceptional qualifications he has evinced in the performance of hia duties as confiden-
tial clerk to the Postmaster General.
