Miss Johnson has served in this position since November 16, 1911, and because of
her fidelity and industry and certain unusual features of the case, the Civil Service
Commission joins with the Treasury Department in recommending this order.
January 24, 1914.
Mr. Francis H. Duehay may be appointed superintendent of prisons, Department
of Justice, without reference to the civil service rules. This order is issued upon the
recommendation of the Attorney General, who believes that Mr. Duehay is especially
Sualified for the position, which requires a peculiar order of talent. The Civil Service
ommission concurs in the recommendation.
