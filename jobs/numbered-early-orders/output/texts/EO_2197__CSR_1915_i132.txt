May 14, 1915.
Mr. James J. Harley may be reinstated to a position in the post office at Philadel-
phia, Pa., without regard to the length of time he has been separated from the service.
Mr. Harley was a clerk in the Philadelphia post office from June 1, 1893, to November
15^ 1900, when he resigned. His record during that time was entirely satisfactory. His
reinstatement is recommended by the postmaster at Philadelphia, who states that he
is personally acquainted with Mr. Harley and feels that he is mentally and physically
capable of performing with satisfaction and efficiency the duties of a postal clerk.
The Civil Service Commission does not concur in the recommendation.
