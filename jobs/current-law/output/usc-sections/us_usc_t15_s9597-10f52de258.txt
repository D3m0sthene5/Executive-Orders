§ 9597.
Executive Order 11625
The powers and duties of the Agency shall be determined—
(1)
in accordance with this chapter and the requirements of this chapter; and
(2)
without regard to Executive Order 11625 (36 Fed. Reg. 19967; relating to prescribing additional arrangements for developing and coordinating a national program for minority business enterprise).
(Pub. L. 117–58, div. K, title VII, § 100707, Nov. 15, 2021, 135 Stat. 1467.)
