§ 428.
Fort Donelson National Battlefield; establishment; appointment of commission
A commission is created, to be composed of the following members, who shall be appointed by the Secretary of the Interior:
(1)
A commissioned officer of the Corps of Engineers, United States Army;
(2)
A veteran of the Civil War who served honorably in the military forces of the United States; and
(3)
A veteran of the Civil War who served honorably in the military forces of the Confederate States of America.
(Mar. 26, 1928, ch. 248, § 1, 45 Stat. 367; Ex. Ord. No. 6166, § 2, June 10, 1933; Ex. Ord. No. 6228, § 1, July 28, 1933; Pub. L. 86–738, § 5, Sept. 8, 1960, 74 Stat. 876.)
