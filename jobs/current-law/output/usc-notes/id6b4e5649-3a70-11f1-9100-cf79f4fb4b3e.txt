Update on Invasive Species Policy Guidance
Pub. L. 116–260, div. AA, title V, § 501, Dec. 27, 2020, 134 Stat. 2744, as amended by Pub. L. 117–263, div. H, title LXXXI, § 8305(d), Dec. 23, 2022, 136 Stat. 3779, provided that:
“(a)
In General.—
The Secretary [of the Army] shall periodically update the Invasive Species Policy Guidance, developed under section 104 of the River and Harbor Act of 1958 (33 U.S.C. 610) and the Nonindigenous Aquatic Nuisance Prevention and Control Act of 1990 (16 U.S.C. 4701 et seq.), in accordance with the most recent National Invasive Species Council Management Plan developed pursuant to Executive Order 13112 [42 U.S.C. 4321 note].
“(b)
Inclusion.—
The Secretary may include in the updated guidance invasive species specific efforts at federally authorized water resources development projects located in—
“(1)
high-altitude lakes;
“(2)
the Tennessee and Cumberland River basins; and
“(3)
the Sacramento-San Joaquin Delta, California.”
