DEPARTMENT OF STATE

[Public Notice 3291]


Revocation of December 21, 1999 Determination under Section 
2(b)(1)(B) of the Export-Import Bank Act of 1945, as Amended

    Pursuant to section 2(b)(1)(B) of the Export-Import Bank Act of 
1945, as amended, and Executive Order 12166 of October 19, 1979, the 
determination dated December 21, 1999, with respect to Export-Import 
Bank financing in connection with cases APO70202XX and APO67280XX is 
hereby revoked.
    This determination shall be published in the Federal Register.

    Dated: March 31, 2000.
Madeleine K. Albright,
Secretary of State, U.S. Department of State.
