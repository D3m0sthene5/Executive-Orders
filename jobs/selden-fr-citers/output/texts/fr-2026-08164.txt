DEPARTMENT OF COMMERCE

National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration

[RTID 0648-XF705]


North Pacific Fishery Management Council; Notice of Public 
Meeting

AGENCY: National Marine Fisheries Service (NMFS), National Oceanic and 
Atmospheric Administration (NOAA), Commerce.

ACTION: Notice of a hybrid public meeting.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: North Pacific Fishery Management Council will host a meeting 
of the Council Coordination Committee (CCC), consisting of the Regional 
Fishery Management Council chairs, vice chairs, and executive directors 
on May 19 through May 21, 2026.

DATES: The CCC will begin at 1 p.m. on Tuesday May 19, 2026, recess at 
5 p.m.; and reconvene at 9 a.m. on Wednesday, May 20, 2026, recess at 5 
p.m.; and reconvene at 9 a.m. Thursday, May 21, 2026 and adjourn at 5 
p.m. or when business is complete.

ADDRESSES: Lands End Resort, 4786 Homer Spit Rd., Homer, AK. Join 
online through the link at <a href="https://www.fisherycouncils.org/ccc-meetings/may-2026">https://www.fisherycouncils.org/ccc-meetings/may-2026</a>.
    Council address: North Pacific Fishery Management Council, 1007 
West 3rd Ave., Suite 400, Anchorage, AK 99501-2252; telephone: (907) 
271-2809.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION:

Agenda

Tuesday, May 19, 2026, Through Thursday, May 21, 2026

    CCC Session: The agenda for the CCC's plenary session will include 
the following issues.

1. Approval of Agenda
2. Magnuson-Stevens Fishery Conservation and Management Act (MSA) 50th 
Anniversary
3. Council Updates, Priorities, and Needs
4. NOAA Fisheries Update and 2026/2027 Priorities
5. Budget and Council Funding Update
6. NOAA Fisheries Science Updates
7. Executive Order 14276 Restoring American Seafood Competitiveness 
(NOAA Fisheries)
8. Risk/Value Matrix (NOAA Fisheries)
9. Legislative Outlook
10. Marine Resource Education Program MREP National Program
11. Joint Presentation from Marine Fisheries Commissions
12. Internal Affairs Updates
13. National Environmental Policy Act (NEPA) Updates
14. Council Coordination Committee (CCC) and Work Group Reports
15. Future Meeting Planning
16. Other Business

    The agenda is subject to change, and the latest version will be 
posted at <a href="https://www.fisherycouncils.org/ccc-meetings/may-2026">https://www.fisherycouncils.org/ccc-meetings/may-2026</a> prior 
to the meeting, along with meeting materials.
