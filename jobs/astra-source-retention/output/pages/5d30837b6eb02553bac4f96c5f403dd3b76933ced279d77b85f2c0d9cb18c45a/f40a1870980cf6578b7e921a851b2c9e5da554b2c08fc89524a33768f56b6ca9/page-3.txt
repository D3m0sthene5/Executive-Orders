of the Internal Revenue Code (53 Stat., 
1, 29, 111; 54 Stat., 974, 989), it is hereby 
ordered that income, excess-profits, and 
declared value excess-profits tax returns 
made under the Internal Revenue Code 
for the year 1939 and subsequent years, 
shall be open to inspection by the War 
Contracts Price Adjustment Board, sub­
ject to the conditions stated in the Treas­
ury decision relating to the inspection of 
such returns by such Board approved by 
me this date.1
This Order shall be published in the
