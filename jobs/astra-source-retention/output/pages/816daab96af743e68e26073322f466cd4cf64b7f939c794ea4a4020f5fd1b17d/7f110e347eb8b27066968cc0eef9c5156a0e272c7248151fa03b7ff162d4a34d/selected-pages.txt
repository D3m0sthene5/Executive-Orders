the Project Engineer that the lessee has 
fully complied with the terms of the 
lease contract relative to the payment 
of the annual operation and mainte­
nance assessment.
Indian water users farming their land, 
planted in crops other than gotton, may 
receive delivery of water without pay­
ment of assessment provided the Super­
intendent of the reservation certifies to 
the Project Engineer that such Indian is 
financially unable to make the payment. 
Under such condition the assessment for 
the water delivered shall be entered on 
the accounts and shall stand as a lien 
against the land, without penalty.
Indian water users farming their land, 
planted in cotton, may receive delivery 
of water upon payment of 50 percent of 
the assessments herein designated pro­
vided the Superintendent of the reserva­
tion certifies to the Project Engineer 
that such Indian is not financially able 
to make the full payment. The unpaid 
50 percent of the assessment in all such 
cases shall be entered on the accounts 
and shall stand as a lien against the 
land, without penalty.
