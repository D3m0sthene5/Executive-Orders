Executive Order 12212 of May 2, 1980
The Department of Education
By the authority vested in me as President by the Constitution and the statutes 
of the United States of America, including the Department of Education 
Organization Act (P.L. 96-88; 93 Stat. 668), it is hereby ordered as follows:
1-101. Sunday, May 4, 1980, shall, for the purposes of Section 601 of the 
Department of Education Organization Act (20 U.S.C. 3401 note), be the date 
on which the provisions of that Act shall take effect.
1-102. The Director of the Office of Management and Budget shall take all 
actions necessary or appropriate to effectuate the transfers provided for in the 
Department of Education Organization Act, including the transfer of funds, 
records, property, personnel and positions.
1-103. As required by Section 601 of the Department of Education Organiza­
tion Act, this Order shall be published in the Federal Register.
