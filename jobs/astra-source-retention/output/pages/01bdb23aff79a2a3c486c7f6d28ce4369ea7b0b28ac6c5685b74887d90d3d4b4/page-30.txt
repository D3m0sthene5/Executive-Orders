provided such notice to continue such service to such point for an 
additional 30-day period, or until another air carrier has begun to

no other air carrier can be secured to provide essential air transporta­
tion to such eligible point on a continuing basis, either with or without 
compensation, then the Board shall extend such requirement for such 
additional 30-day periods (making the same determination at the end 
of each such period) as may be necessary to continue air transportation 
to such eligible point until an air carrier can be secured to provide 
essential air transportation to such eligible point on a continuing basis.
