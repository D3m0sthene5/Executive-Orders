§  233.23 Maturity and interest rate.  
Notes secured by rice stored on farms and
in public Warehouses in Arkansas, Loui­
siana, Texas, and Missouri shall mature 
on demand, or June 30, 1943, whichever 
is earlier. All loans shall bear interest 
at the rate of 3 percent per annum.
