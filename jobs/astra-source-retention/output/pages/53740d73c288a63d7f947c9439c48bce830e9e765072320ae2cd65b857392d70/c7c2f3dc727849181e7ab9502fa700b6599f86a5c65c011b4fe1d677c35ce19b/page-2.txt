FEDERALSREGISTER
Published by the Division of the Federal 
Register, The National Archives, pursuant to  
the authority contained in the Federal  
Register Act, approved July 26, 1935 (49  
Stat. L. 500), under regulations prescribed 
by the Administrative Committee, with the  
approval of the President.
The Administrative Committee consists of  
the Archivist or Acting Archivist, an officer  
of the Department of Justice designated by  
the Attorney General, and the Public Printer  
or Acting Public Printer.
The daily issue of the Federal Register 
will be furnished by mail to subscribers, free 
of postage, for $1  per month or $10  per year; 
single copies 10  cents each; payable in ad­
vance.' Remit by money order payable to  
Superintendent of Documents, Government  
Printing Office, Washington, D. C.
Correspondence concerning the publica­
tion of the Federal Register should be ad­
dressed to the Director, Division of the  
Federal Register, The National Archives,  
Washington, D, C,
