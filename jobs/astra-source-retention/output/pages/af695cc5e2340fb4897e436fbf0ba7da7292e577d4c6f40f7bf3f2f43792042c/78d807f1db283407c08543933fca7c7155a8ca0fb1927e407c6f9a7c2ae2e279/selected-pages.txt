Presidential Documents
Title 3— THE PRESIDENT
Executive Order 11485
SUPERVISION AND CONTROL OF THE NATIONAL GUARD OF THE  
DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA
Correction
In Executive Order 11485 (F.R. Doc. 69-11875), appearing at page  
15411 of the issue for Friday, October 3, 1969, the last sentence of  
Section 1 should read:
Subject to the direction of the President as Commander-in-Chief, the  
Secretary may order out the National Guard under title 39 of the  
District of Columbia Code to aid the civil authorities of the District  
of Columbia.
FEDERAL REGISTER, VOL. 34, NO. 191— SATURDAY, OCTOBER 4, 1969
