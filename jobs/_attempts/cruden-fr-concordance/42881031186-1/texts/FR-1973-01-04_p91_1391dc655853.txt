Schedule of Fees, 1973
Pursuant to the authority vested in the
Secretary of the Interior, notice is here­
by given of the schedule of fees for the
1973 fee year beginning March 1, 1973,
and ending February 28, 1974, for live­
stock grazing on the public lands.
For the purpose of establishing
charges, 1 animal unit month (AUM)
shall be considered equivalent to grazing
use by one cow, five sheep, or one horse
for 1 month. The charge for one horse
is at twice the rate for one cow.
Bills shall be issued in accordance with
the rates prescribed in this notice.
