a basic school or the Academy for pre­
liminary training at an age of not less
than seventeen years and after satis­
factory completion of such training may
be assigned to a merchant vessel or a
training ship for sea training. In event
a candidate fails to report to a basic
school or the Academy and execute oath
as cadet-midshipman within six months
of date of approval of application his
name shall be removed from the eligible
list.
(d) No waivers for under age or over
age shall be granted.
