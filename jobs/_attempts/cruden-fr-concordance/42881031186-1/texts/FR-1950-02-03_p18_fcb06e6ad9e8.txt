which may be summarized as follows:
Northamption proposes to issue and

proposes to acquire such shares and will
use available cash for such purpose.

non-interest bearing. Northampton also
presently has outstanding promissory
2%% short-term notes in the aggregate

1951. The notes carry the privilege of
prior payment in whole or in part.
Northampton proposes to use the pro­
ceeds from the sale of additional shares
of capital stock to retire its indebtedness
aggregating $480,000, as indicated in the
preceding paragraph.
The Massachusetts Department of
Public Utilities has approved the issue
and sale by Northampton of the addi­
tional shares of capital stock at the price
of $400 a share.
