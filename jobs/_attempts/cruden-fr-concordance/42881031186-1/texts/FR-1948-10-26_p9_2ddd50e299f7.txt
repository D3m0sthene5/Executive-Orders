NOTICE OF HEARING ON HANDLING OF M ILK

Proposed amendments to the tentative
marketing agreement, and to the order,
as amended, regulating the handling of
milk in the Cincinnati, Ohio, milk mar­
keting area.
Pursuant to the Agricultural Market­
ing Agreement Act of 1937, as amended,
(7 U. S. C. 601 et seq.), and in accord­
ance with the applicablè rulés of practice
and procedure, as amended, (7 CFR, 900.1
et seq.; 12 F. R. 1159, 4904), notice is
hereby given of a public hearing to be
held at Hotel Sinton, Crystal Room, 4th
and Vine Streets, Cincinnati, Ohio, be­
ginning at 10:00 a. m., e. s-. t., October
30,1948, for the purpose of receiving evi­
dence with respect to proposed amend­
ments to the tentative marketing agree­
ment heretofore approved by the Secre­
tary of Agriculture and to the order, as
amended, regulating the handling of milk
in the Cincinnati, Ohio, milk marketing
area (7 CFR, Supps. 965.0 et .seq.; 12
F. R. 4931). These proposed amend­
ments have not' received the approval of
the Secretary of Agriculture.
This public hearing is for the purpose
of receiving evidence with respect to the
economic and emergency conditions
which relate to the establishment of
Class I and Class II prices for a limited
period of time in 1948 and 1949.

The following amendments have been
proposed:
1. Amend § 965.6 (a) (1) and (a) (2)
to delete the provisos contained therein
and to provide that the prices for Class
I and Class II milk for a limited period
in 1948 and 1949, but not beyond March
1949, shall not be less than the prices in
effect for such classes of milk in Sep­
tember 1948.
Copies of this notice of hearing and of
the tentative marketing agreement, and
the order, as amended, now in effect, may
be procured from the Market Adminis­
trator, 152 East 4th Street, Cincinnati,
Ohio, or from the Hearing Clerk, United
States Department of Agriculture, Room
1844, South Building, Washington 25,
D. C., or may be there inspected.
Dated: October 22, 1948.
