§ 101.0

Scope.

This part sets forth general regula­
tions governing the authority of Customs
officers, and the location of Customs re­
gions, districts, and ports of entry, and
of Customs stations. It further sets forth
regulations concerning the entry and
clearance of vessels at Customs stations
and a listing of Customs preclearance
offices in foreign countries. In addition,
this part contains provisions concerning
the hours of business of Customs offices,
the Customs seal, and the identification
cards issued to Customs officers and em­
ployees.
