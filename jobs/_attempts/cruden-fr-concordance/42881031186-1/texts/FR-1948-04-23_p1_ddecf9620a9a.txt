SUBPART— 1 9 4 8 PRICE SUPPORT PROGRAM

The United States Department of
Agriculture will support the price of the
1948 crop of Irish Potatoes at 90 percent
of parity as of the beginning of the m ar­
keting seasons through December 31,
1948. Such price support will be accom­
plished by means of purchase and loan
operations, of diversion operations to
other than normal channels of trade in­
cluding livestock feed and industrial out­
lets, and of export operations. In most
States purchase operations will termi­
nate generally in the early fall of 1948,
the exact date possibly varying by States.
With termination of purchase opera­
tions, price support will be effected by
loans. Loans must be completed by De­
cember 31, 1948. Final dates for sub­
mission of applications for loans will be
established by State PMA Committees.
