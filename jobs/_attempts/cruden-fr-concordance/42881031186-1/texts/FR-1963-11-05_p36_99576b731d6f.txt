(3) Meaning of “interest”. The word
“interest” means an interest in real prop­

constitutes either (i) an interest in fee in
such property, (ii) a leasehold interest in
such property extending or renewable
automatically for a period of at least 10
years beyond the date specified for the
final payment on such loan, or (iii) a
leasehold interest in such property held
subject to a redeemable ground rent de­
fined in section 1055(c).
(4) Meaning of “qualifying real prop­
erty”. The term “qualifying real prop­

ceding sentence, the term “improved real
property” means—
