SECTION 1. AUTHORIZATION OF APPROPRIATIONS. 
Section 5 of the Act entitled "An Act to provide for the estabUsh-

The amendment made by section 1 shall take effect on October 1,

SEC. 4. COOPERATIVE LAW ENFORCEMENT AGREEMENTS. 
Subsection 4(f) of the National Wildlife Refuge System Adminis­
tration Act of 1966 (16 U.S.C. 668dd(f)), is amended by adding after 
the last sentence of that subsection the following: "The Director of 
the United States Fish and Wildlife Service is authorized to utilize 
by agreement, with or without reimbursement, the personnel and 
services of any other Federal or State agency for purposes of enhanc­
ing the enforcement of this Act.".
