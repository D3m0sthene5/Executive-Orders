AUTHORIZING THE APPOINTMENT OF Mrs. Matrie Lowe SARTOR IN THE
Post OrricE DEPARTMENT WITHOUT REGARD TO CIVIL-SERVICE RULES

By virtue of and pursuant to the authority vested in me by the provisions of
paragraph Eighth of subdivision SECOND of section 2 of the Civil Service Act of
January 16, 1883 (ch. 27, 22 Stat. 403, 404), it is hereby ordered that Mrs. Mattie
Lowe Sartor may be appointed a clerk in the Oklahoma City, Oklahoma, Post Office,
without compliance with the requirements of the civil-service rules.

Mrs. Sartor is the widow of Elmer Sartor, who lost his life while a member of a
motorcycle escort during a visit of the Postmaster General to Oklahoma.

This order is recommended by the Postmaster General.

FRANKLIN D ROOSEVELT
Tue Waite Hovssz,
April 6, 19368.
