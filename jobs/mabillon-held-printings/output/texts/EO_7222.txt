Enuaraine St. Marxs Migratory Birp REFUGE
FLORIDA

By virtue of and pursuant to the authority vested in me as President of the
United States, and in order to effectuate further the purposes of the Migratory
Bird Conservation Act (45 Stat. 1222), it is ordered that the following-described
lands, consisting of 10,108 acres, more or less, acquired or to be acquired by the
United States, in Wakulla County, Florida, be, and they are hereby, reserved and
set apart for the use of the Department of Agriculture, subject to valid existing
rights, as an addition to the St. Marks Migratory Bird Refuge established by Execu-
tive Order No. 5740, of October 31, 1931: Provided, That any private lands within
the areas described shall become a part of the refuge upon the acquisition of title or
lease thereto by the United States:
