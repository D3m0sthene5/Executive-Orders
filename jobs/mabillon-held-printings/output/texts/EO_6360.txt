AUTHORIZATION TO APPOINT Mr. WabDE CRAWFORD

By virtue of the authority vested in me by the provisions of the last sentence
of the eighth paragraph of subdivision second of section 2 of the Civil Service Act of
January 16, 1883 (ch. 27, 22 Stat. 403, 404), it is hereby ordered that Mr. Wade
Crawford may be appointed to the position of superintendent of the Klamath Indian
Agency, Klamath Agency, Oregon, without compliance with the requirements of the
Civil Service rules.

This order is recommended by the Secretary of the Interior.

FRANKLIN D. ROOSEVELT.
