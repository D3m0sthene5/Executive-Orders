AMENDMENT OF EXECUTIVE ORDER No. 4601 or Marcu 1, 1927, as
AMENDED, PRESCRIBING REGULATIONS PERTAINING TO THE AWARD
OF THE DISTINGUISHED FLYING Cross

By virtue of and pursuant to the authority vested in me by section 12 of the act
of July 2, 1926, 44 Stat. 789 (U.S. C., title 10, sec. 1429), as amended by section 4
of the act of July 30, 1937, 50 Stat. 547, 549, Executive Order No. 4601 of March 1,
1927, as amended by Executive Order No. 7786 of January 8, 1938, prescribing rules
and regulations pertaining to the award of the distinguished flying cross, is hereby
further amended to authorize the Secretary of the Treasury to award the distinguished
flying cross to Lieutenant Richard L. Burke, United States Coast Guard, notwith-
standing the time limitations specified in paragraph 8 of Executive Order No. 4601 of
March 1, 1927.

FRANKLIN D ROOSEVELT
Tue Waite House,

August 22, 1938.
