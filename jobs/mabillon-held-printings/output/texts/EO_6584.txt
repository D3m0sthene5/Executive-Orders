Upon cessation of use of the lands hereby transferred to the Treasury Depart-
ment and the Department of Commerce and formal notice thereof by said Depart-
ments to the War Department, such lands shall revert to the control and jurisdiction
of the War Department as part of the military reservation of Sand Island.

It is also ordered that such portions of the land contemplated to be removed
under the plan and project of widening and deepening the channel and the enlarge-
ment of the harbor of Honolulu, as approved, are hereby set aside for use of the War
Department in connection with the improvements to the said harbor.

This order shall continue in full force and effect unless and until revoked by the

President or by act of Congress.
