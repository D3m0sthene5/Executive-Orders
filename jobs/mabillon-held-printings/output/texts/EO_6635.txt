Wicuira, Kansas, ABOLISHED AS A Customs Port or ENTRY

By virtue of the authority vested in me by section 1 of the act of August 1, 1914
(ch. 223, 38 Stat. 609, 623; 19 U.S.C., sec. 2), Wichita, Kansas, is hereby abolished
as a customs port of entry in customs collection district no. 45 (St. Louis), with
headquarters at St. Louis, Missouri, effective as of 30 days from the date of this order.

FRANKLIN D. ROOSEVELT.
