Executive Order 5485. November 14, 1930
Executive Order
Revocation of Withdrawal of Public Lands for Resurvey
New Mexico
It is hereby ordered that Executive Order No. 4638, dated April 26, 1927,
withdrawing the public lands in T. 20 N., Rs. 6 and 7 E., New Mexico principal
meridian, New Mexico, pending a resurvey, be, and the same is hereby, revoked.
HERBERT HOOVER
The White House,
November U, 1930,
[No. 5485]
