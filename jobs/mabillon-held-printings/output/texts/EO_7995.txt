EXEMPTION OF WILLIAM S. GARLAND FROM CoMPULSORY RETIREMENT
FoR AGE

WHEREAS, in my judgment, the public interest requires that William S. Garland,
civil engineer, Bureau of Aeronautics, Navy Department, who, during the month of
January, 1939, will reach the retirement age prescribed for automatic separation
from the service, applicable to him, be exempted from compulsory retirement for
age for a period of one year:

NOW, THEREFORE, by virtue of and pursuant to the authority vested in
me by section 204 of the act of June 30, 1932, 47 Stat. 382, 404 (U.S. C., title 5,
sec. 715a), I hereby exempt the said William S. Garland from compulsory retirement
for age for a period of one year ending January 31, 1940.

FRANKLIN D ROOSEVELT
THe Waite Hovsz,

October 28, 1988.
