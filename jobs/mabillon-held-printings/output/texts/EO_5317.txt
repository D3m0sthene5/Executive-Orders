
Executive Order 5317. April 3, 1930
Executive Order
Suspension of Executive Order Abolishing New Bern and Manteo,
North Carolina, as Customs Ports of Entry
The Executive order dated March 5, 1930, abolishmg New Bern and Manteo,
North Carolina, as customs ports of entry in Customs Collection District No. 15
(North Carolina) with headquarters at Wilmington, North Carolina, is hereby
suspended indefinitely.
HERBERT HOOVER
The White House,
April 5, 1930.
[No. 5317]
