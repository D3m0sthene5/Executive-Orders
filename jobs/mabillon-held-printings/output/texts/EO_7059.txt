REvocaTIon oF EXECUTIVE ORDER OF FEBRUARY 1, 1906, RESERVING
CERTAIN LAND AND WATER AREAS AS A SITE FOR A SALMON HATCHERY

ALASKA

The Executive order of February 1, 1906, reserving and setting apart the
following-described land and water areas in Alaska as a site for a salmon hatchery,
is hereby revoked, and the said areas are hereby declared to be a part of the Tongass
National Forest as established by proclamation of February 16, 1909 (35 Stat. 2226):

Yes Lake (otherwise known as Lake McDonald) and its catchment basin, its
outlet, and a strip of land one-eighth of a mile wide along each shore thereof; Yes

Bay, Back Bay, and a strip of land one-eighth of a mile wide along the shores thereof
and a strip of land one-eighth of a mile wide on each side of the old Indian trail.

FRANKLIN D ROOSEVELT
