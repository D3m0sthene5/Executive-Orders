Executive Order

AMENDMENT OF SCHEDULE A OF THE CIvIL SERVICE RULEs.

Paragraph 6, Subdivision VI, of Schedule A of the Civil Service Rules is hereby
amended to read:

All positions in the Division of Investigation except fingerprint classifiers.

This Order will be effective on the effective date of Section 3 of Executive Order
No. 6166, dated June 10, 1933.

FRANKLIN D. ROOSEVELT.
Tue Waite Hovse,
July 26, 1933.
