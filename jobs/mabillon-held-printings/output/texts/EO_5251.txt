Executive Order 5251. December 31, 1929
Withdrawal for Target Range
Colorado
Under authority contained in the act of June 25, 1910 (36 Stat. 847), as amended
by the act of August 24, 1912 (37 Stat. 497), and subject to the conditions, provisions
and Umitations of said acts and also subject to valid existing rights in and to the
same and to prior existing withdrawals, it is hereby ordered that the SW. ){ sec. 1,
S. K sec. 2, all of sec. 11, W. K sec. 12, NW. )i sec. 13, and N. K sec. 14, T. 49 N.,
K. 9 W., New Mexico Principal Meridian, Colorado, be and the same are hereby
withdrawn from settlement, sale, location, entry, and all forms of appropriation, for
use as a target range by Company "D'*, 157th Infantry, Colorado National Guard.
This order shall continue in full force and effect unless and imtil revoked by the
President or by an act of Congress.
