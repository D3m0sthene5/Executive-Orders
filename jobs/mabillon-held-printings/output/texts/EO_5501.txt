Executive Order 5501. December 2, 1930
Executive Order
Reinstatement of Mrs. Eva MacFate
Mrs. Eva MacFate, who served in the Bureau of Engraving and Printing,
under the name of Eva C. Small, by appointment from the skilled-laborer register,
from September 16, 1896, to July 15, 1901, under the designations of printer's assist-

ant and operative, may be reinstated to an appropriate position in that bureau
without regard to the fact that she served slightly less than the five years necessary

Mrs. MacFate is the widow of Harry C. MacFate, who rendered valuable service
to the Government in the Bureau of Engraving and Printing for more than thirty
years.
HERBERT HOOVER
The White House,
December 2, 1930.
