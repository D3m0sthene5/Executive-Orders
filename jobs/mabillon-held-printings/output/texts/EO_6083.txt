Executive Order

AMENDMENT OF SCHEDULE A OF THE CIVIL SERVICE RULES

Schedule A of the Civil Service Rules is hereby amended by the addition of a
new subdivision and paragraph as follows:
XXIII. Feperat Rapio Commission.
1. One Secretary of the Federal Radio Commission.

FRANKLIN D ROOSEVELT
Tue Waite House,
March 26, 1988.
