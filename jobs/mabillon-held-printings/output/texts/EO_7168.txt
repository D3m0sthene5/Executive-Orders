Executive Order

EsTaBLISHING ARROW-woop Migratory WATERFOWL REFUGE

NORTH DAKOTA

By virtue of and pursuant to the authority vested in me as President of the
United States, and in order to effectuate further the purposes of the Migratory Bird
Conservation Act (45 Stat. 1222), it is ordered that the following-described lands,
acquired or to be acquired by the United States, in Foster and Stutsman Counties,
North Dakota, consisting of 16,876 acres, more or less, be, and they are hereby, reserved
and set apart for the use of the Department of Agriculture, subject to valid existing
rights, as a refuge and breeding ground for migratory birds and other wild life:
Provided, That any private lands within the areas described shall become a part of
the refuge hereby established upon the acquisition of title or lease thereto by the
United States:
