Cuartorre, Nortu Carotina, Lirrte Rock, ARKANSAS, AND DusuaQuE,
Iowa, ABOLISHED AS Customs Ports or ENTRY

By virtue of and pursuant to the authority vested in me by the act of August 1,
1914, 38 Stat. 609, 623 (19 U.S. C., sec. 2), the following customs ports of entry in

the customs collection districts named are hereby abolished effective 30 days from
the date of this order:

Charlotte, North Carolina, customs collection district no. 15 (North Carolina)
Little Rock, Arkansas, customs collection district no. 43 (Tennessee)
Dubuque, Iowa, customs collection district no. 44 (lowa)

FRANKLIN D ROOSEVELT
Tur Waitt House,
November 27, 1936.
