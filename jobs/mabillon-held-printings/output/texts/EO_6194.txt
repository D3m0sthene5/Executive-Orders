Executive Order

REVOCATION OF WITHDRAWAL OF PuBLIc LANDS PENDING RESURVEY

OREGON

It is hereby ordered that Executive Order No. 5667, dated July 6, 1931, which
withdrew the public lands in secs. 13 to 36, inclusive, T. 16 S., R. 9 W. of the Willam-
ette meridian, Oregon, pending a resurvey, be, and the same is hereby, revoked.

FRANKLIN D. ROOSEVELT.
THE Waite House,
July 3, 1933.
