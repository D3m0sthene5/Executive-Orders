Executibe Order

AUTHORIZING AND DrrEcTING JOHN DICKINSON, ASSISTANT SECRETARY
OF COMMERCE, TO PERFORM THE DUTIES OF THE DIRECTOR OF THE
Bureau or ForeIGN AND Domestic COMMERCE, IN CASE OF AB-
SENCE, RESIGNATION, ETC., OF THE DIRECTOR OF SAID BUREAU.

Under the authority vested in me by section 179 of the Revised Statutes (U.S.C.,
title 5, sec. 6), I hereby authorize and direct John Dickinson, Assistant Secretary of
Commerce, to perform the duties of the Director of the Bureau of Foreign and
Domestic Commerce of the Department of Commerce in case of the resignation,
absence, or sickness of the Director of said Bureau until the sickness or absence of the
incumbent shall cease or in the event of resignation until a successor shall have been

appointed.
FRANKLIN D. ROOSEVELT.

Tue Waite Hovss,
May 11, 1984.
