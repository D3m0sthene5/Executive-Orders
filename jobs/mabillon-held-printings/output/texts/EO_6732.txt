| 7 Executive Order

-AMENDMENT OF SCHEDULE A, SUBDIVISION XXII, oF THE CIVIL-SERVICE
RuLEs

By virtue of the authority vested in me by the provisions of the last sentence
of the eighth paragraph of subdivision second of section 2 of the Civil Service Act of
January 16, 1883 (ch. 27, 22 Stat. 403, 404), subdivision XXII of schedule A of the
civil-service rules is hereby amended by adding thereto the following paragraph:

‘2. One position of Assistant Secretary, Federal Power Commission.”

FRANKLIN D. ROOSEVELT.
Tae White Hovss,
June 7, 1934.
