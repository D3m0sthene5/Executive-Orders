Executive Order

DESIGNATING THE Deputy ADMINISTRATOR, FEDERAL EMERGENCY
ADMINISTRATION OF PuBLIc Works, AS A MEMBER OF THE ADVI-
SORY COMMITTEE ON ALLOTMENTS

Executive Order No. 7034, of May 6, 1935, insofar as it establishes the Advisory
Committee on Allotments, is hereby amended so as to include in the membership of
said Committee the Deputy Administrator of the Federal Emergency Administra-
tion of Public Works.

FRANKLIN D ROOSEVELT

Tae Waite House,

May 28, 19388.
