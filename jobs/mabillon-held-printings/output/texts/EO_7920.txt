Executibe Order

AUTHORIZING THE APPOINTMENT OF Mrs. IRENE M. Mori To a Pos!tTIon
IN THE CLASSIFIED SERVICE OF THE GOVERNMENT WITHOUT REGARD
TO THE CiviL SERVICE RULES

By virtue of and pursuant to the authority vested in me by the provisions of para-
graph Eighth, subdivision SECOND, section 2 of the Civil Service Act (22 Stat. 403,
404), it is hereby ordered that Mrs. Irene M. Mori may be appointed to an appropriate
position in the classified service of the Government without compliance with the
requirements of the civil service rules.

Mrs. Mori is the widow of Alfred H. Mori, who had long and meritorious service
in the War Department and who died on December 18, 1937, leaving his widow and
two children without adequate means of support.
