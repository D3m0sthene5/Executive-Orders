Tue HonoraBLE HENry T. RAINEY

As a mark of respect to the memory of the Honorable Henry T. Rainey, late
Speaker and Member of the House of Representatives of the United States, it is
hereby ordered that the flags on the White House and public buildings in the Dis-
trict of Columbia and the State of Illinois be placed at half mast on the day of the
funeral service at Carrollton, Illinois, Wednesday, August 22, 1934.

FRANKLIN D. ROOSEVELT.
THe Waite Houssz,
August 21, 1934.
