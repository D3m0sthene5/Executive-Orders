STURGEON Bay, W1s., ABOLISHED As A CustoMs Port oF ENTRY

By virtue of the authority vested in me by section 1 of the act of August 1, 1914
(ch. 223, 38 Stat. 609, 623; 19 U.S.C., sec. 2), Sturgeon Bay, Wis., is hereby abol-
ished as a customs port of entry in customs collection district no. 37 (Wisconsin),
with headquarters at Milwaukee, Wis., effective as of 30 days from the date of this
order.

FRANKLIN D. ROOSEVELT.
Tue Waite House,
September 12, 19383.
