Executive Order 5481. November 14, 1930
Executive Order
Withdrawal of Public Lands for Agricultural Experiment Purposes
Idaho
Under authority of the act of Congress approved June 25, 1910 (36 Stat. 847),
as amended by the act of August 24, 1912 (37 Stat. 497), and subject to the conditions
therein expressed and vaUd existing claims, it is hereby ordered that the following
described tract of pubHc land in the State of Idaho be, and it is hereby, temporarily
withdrawn for use by the Department of Agriculture of the United States for agri-
cultural experiment purposes:
T. 12 S., R. 15 E., Boise meridian, NE. H SW. % sec. 4.
This order shall continue in full force and effect imless and until revoked by
the President or by act of Congress.
HERBERT HOOVER
The White House,
November H, 1930.
