Executive Order

Executive orders issued by me November 6, 1933, and November 13, 1933,
terminate on November 20, 1933, the stay now in effect of the provisions limiting
machine hours in the Code of Fair Competition for the Cotton Textile Industry, in-
sofar as such provisions apply to the production of tire yarns or fabrics for rubber
tires. Upon application by parties affected further hearings have been set for
November 27, 1933, upon the issues raised with respect to such provisions. Pur-
suant to the authority vested in me by title I of the National Industrial Recovery
Act, approved June 16, 1933, and otherwise, the above-mentioned Executive orders
are hereby modified by extending the termination date of said stay until the com-
pletion of said hearings and determination by me of the issues raised with respect to
the above provisions.

FRANKLIN D. ROOSEVELT.

Approval recommended:

Huau S. Jounson,
. Administrator for Industrial Recovery.

THe Waite House,
November 27, 1988.
