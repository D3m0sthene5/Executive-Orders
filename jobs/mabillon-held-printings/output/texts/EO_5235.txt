
Executive Order 5235. December 6, 1929
Reinstatement of Mrs. Marguerite Barrett
Mrs. Marguerite Barrett may be reinstated in the Treasury Department without
regard to the length of time she had been separated from the service.
Mrs. Barrett had a classified status in the War Trade Board and the Public
Health Service of the Treasury Department but rendered less than five years' service
and has been separated for more than five years. She was left by her husband with
three children to support.
This order is recommended by the Secretary of the Treasury.
HERBERT HOOVER
The White House,
December 6, 1929.
