Executive Order

AMENDING Executive OrDER No. 7027, oF APRIL 30, 1935, ESTABLISHING
THE RESETTLEMENT ADMINISTRATION

Subparagraphs (a) and (b) of the second paragraph of Executive Order No. 7027,
of April 30, 1935, establishing the Resettlement Administration, are hereby amended
to read as follows:

“(a) To administer approved projects involving rural rehabilitation, relief in
stricken agricultural areas, and resettlement of destitute or low-income families from
rural and urban areas, including the establishment, maintenance, and operation, in
such connection, of communities in rural and suburban areas.

“(6) To initiate and administer a program of approved projects with respect to
soil erosion, stream pollution, seacoast erosion, reforestation, forestation, flood con-
trol, and other useful projects.”

FRANKLIN D ROOSEVELT

THe Wuite Hovssz,

September 26, 1936.
