3. Whenever a dispute shall arise between any agency of the United States and
any bidder, contractor, supplier, or other person as to compliance with any code of
fair competition or with-an agreement with the President as aforesaid in connection

the Administrator for Industrial Recovery, or such agency as he shall designate, shall
decide such dispute and, for the purposes of action under this Executive order, such
decision shall be final and conclusive; but the determination of such agency of the
