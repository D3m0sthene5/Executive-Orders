DEPOSITED 2Y THE
UNITED STAics OF AMERICA

JUN 24 1938

& /
AS

Executibe Order

AUTHORIZING THE APPOINTMENT OF Mrs. LucILE ENSMINGER TO A CLASSI-
FIED POSITION IN THE NAVY DEPARTMENT WITHOUT REGARD TO THE
Civit SERVICE RULES

By virtue of and pursuant to the authority vested in me by the provisions of
paragraph Eighth, subdivision SECOND, section 2 of the Civil Service Act (22 Stat.
403, 404), it is hereby ordered that Mrs. Lucile Ensminger may be appointed to a
classified position in the Navy Department without compliance with the requirements
of the Civil Service Rules.

Mrs. Ensminger is the widow of Charles Lee Ensminger, Storekeeper, 1c, U.S. N.,
who met his death in line of duty on December 13, 1937 in the bombing and sinking of
the U.S.S. PANAY.

This order is issued upon recommendation of the Secretary of the Navy.

FRANKLIN D ROOSEVELT
Tue White Hovssg,
May 31, 1938.
