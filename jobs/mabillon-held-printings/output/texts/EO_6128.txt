AMENDMENT OF RULES GOVERNING NAVIGATION OF THE PANAMA CANAL
AND ADJACENT WATERS

By virtue of the authority vested in me by the provisions of the act of Congress
approved August 24, 1912 (37 Stat. 560-569), entitled ““AN ACT To provide for
the opening, maintenance, protection, and operation of the Panama Canal, and the
