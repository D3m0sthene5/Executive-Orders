DEPOSITED BY THE
UNITED STATES OF AMERICA

pec 30 "38

Executive Order

EXEMPTION OF Percy A. BAKER FRoM CompuLsory RETIREMENT
FoR AGE

WHEREAS, in my judgment, the public interest requires that Percy A Baker,
superintendent of buildings and grounds, Ellis Island, New York, Immigration and
Naturalization Service, Department of Labor, who, during the current month, will
reach the retirement age prescribed for automatic separation from the service, appli-
cable to him, be exempted from compulsory retirement for age for a period of one year:

NOW, THEREFORE, by virtue of and pursuant to the authority vested in me
by section 204 of the act of June 30, 1932, 47 Stat. 382, 404 (U.S. C., title 5, sec.
715a), I hereby exempt the said Percy A. Baker from compulsory retirement for age
for a period of one year ending December 31, 1939.

FRANKLIN D ROOSEVELT
Tae Waite Hovssz,
December 9, 1938.
