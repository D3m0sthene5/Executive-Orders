Executive Order 5492. November 18, 1930
Executive Order
Public Water Restoration No. 66
Colorado
So much of the order of withdrawal dated April 8, 1919, creating Public Water
Heserve No. 62, as affects the land hereinafter Usted is hereby revoked, and all such
land not otherwise reserved or withdrawn is hereby restored to the public domain
and shall become subject to disposition imder the laws applicable thereto upon such
date and after such notice as may be hereafter determined and announced.
