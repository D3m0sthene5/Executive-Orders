EXTENDING THE NATIONAL LABOR RELATIONS BOARD

By virtue of and pursuant to the authority vested in me under Title I of the
National Industrial Recovery Act (48 Stat. 195), as amended and continued by Public
Resolution 26, 74th Congress, approved June 14, 1935, and in order to effectuate the
policy of the said Title, Executive Order No. 7074 of June 15, 1935, as amended by
Executive Order No. 7090 of June 29, 1935, extending the existence of the National
Labor Relations Board until August 1, 1935, is hereby further amended by substi-
tuting for the date “August 1, 1935” the date ‘“‘September 1, 1935.”
