Executive Order 5109. May 13, 1929
Executive ^tbct
COLORADO
Under authority contained in the act of June 25, 1910 (36 Stat. 847), as amended
by the act of August 24, 1912 (37 Stat. 497), and subject to the conditions, provi-
sions and limitations of said acts, it is hereby ordered that the public lands in T. 49
N., R. 5 E., N. M. P. M. and Ts. 10 and 11 N., R. 85 W., 6th P. M., Colorado, be
and the same are hereby withdrawn from settlement, location, sale, entry and all
forms of appropriation, pending a resurvey of said townships under the act of March
3, 1909 (35 Stat. 845).
