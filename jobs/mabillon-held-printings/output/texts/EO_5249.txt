Withdrawal of Public Land for Classification
Michigan
Under authority of the act of Congress approved June 25, 1910 (36 Stat. 847),
as amended by the act of August 24, 1912 (37 Stat. 497), it is hereby ordered that the
following described public land in the State of Michigan be, and it is hereby tempo-
rarily withdrawn from settlement, location, sale, or entry, subject to the conditions
and limitations of said acts, for classification and pending determination as to the
advisability of including the land in a national forest:
In T. 43 N., K. 18 W., Michigan Meridian, NW. ji SW. % and lot 4, sec. 14.
This order shall continue in full force and effect unless and until revoked by
the President or by act of Congress.
HERBERT HOOVER
The White House,
December 31, 1929.
