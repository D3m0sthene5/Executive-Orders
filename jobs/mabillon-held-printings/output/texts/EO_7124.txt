MopiFIcaTION OF EXEcUTIVE OrpER No. 7070, or JUNE 12, 1935,
PRESCRIBING REGULATIONS GOVERNING APPOINTMENTS OF EMPLOY-
EES PAID FROM EMERGENCY FUNDS

Executive Order No. 7070, of June 12, 1935, prescribing regulations governing
appointments of employees paid from emergency funds, is hereby modified to the
extent that transfers of employees of the Federal Emergency Relief Administration
performing duties in the States of Massachusetts, Ohio, Louisiana, Georgia, and
North Dakota shall not be subject to the provisions of paragraphs 3 and 4 of the

order.
FRANKLIN D ROOSEVELT

Tue Waite Hovss,
August 5, 1935.
