Executive Order 5078. March 12, 1929
Executive ©rber
By virtue of the authority vested in me by the Act of Congress approved
August 1, 1914, making appropriations for the sundry civil expenses of the
Government for the fiscal year ended June 30, 1915, Lincoln, Nebraska, is hereby
created a port of entry in Customs Collection District No. 46 (Omaha) with
headquarters at Omaha, Nebraska, eflFective thirty days from the date of this order.
HERBERT HOOVER
The White House,
March 12, 1929,
