Lincotn, NEBRASKA, ABOLISHED AS A Customs Port or ENTRY

By virtue of the authority vested in me by section 1 of the act of August 1,
1914 (ch. 223, 38 Stat. 609, 623; 19 U.S.C., sec. 2), Lincoln, Nebraska, is hereby
abolished as a customs port of entry in Customs Collection District No. 46 (Omaha),
with headquarters at Omaha, Nebraska, effective as of 30 days from the date of this

order.
FRANKLIN D. ROOSEVELT,

Tue Wuirte Hovsz,
September 13, 1933,

[No. 6282]
