By virtue of and pursuant to the authority vested in me by the act of June 25,
1910 (ch. 421, 36 Stat. 847), as amended by the act of August 24, 1912 (ch. 369,
37 Stat. 497), it is ordered that Executive Order No. 6910, of November 26, 1934, as

public land in certain States for classification and other purposes, be, and it is hereby,
modified to the extent of authorizing the Secretary of the Interior to withdraw the
following-described tracts of public land for use by the Department of Commerce
