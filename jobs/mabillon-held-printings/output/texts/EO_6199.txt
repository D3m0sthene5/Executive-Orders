Executive Order

PROHIBITION OF TRANSPORTATION IN INTERSTATE AND FOREIGN Com-
MERCE OF PETROLEUM AND THE Propucts THEREOF UNLAWFULLY
PRODUCED OR WITHDRAWN FROM STORAGE.

By virtue of the authority vested in me by the Act of Congress entitled “AN
ACT To encourage national industrial recovery, to foster fair competition, and to
provide for the construction of certain useful public works, and for other purposes,”
approved June 16, 1933, (Public No. 67, 73d Congress), the transportation in inter-
state and foreign commerce of petroleum and the products thereof produced or
withdrawn from storage in excess of the amount permitted to be produced or with-
drawn from storage by any State law or valid regulation or order prescribed there-
under, by any board, commission, officer, or other duly authorized agency of a State,
is hereby prohibited.

FRANKLIN D. ROOSEVELT.

Tue Waite House,

July 11, 1933.
