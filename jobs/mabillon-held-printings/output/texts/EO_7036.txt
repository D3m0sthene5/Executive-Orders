Executive Order

EXTENSION OF TRUST PERIODS ON ALLOTMENTS MADE TO INDIANS OF THE
YAKIMA RESERVATION

WASHINGTON

By virtue of and pursuant to the authority vested in me by section 5 of the act
of February 8, 1887 (ch. 119, 24 Stat. 388-389), it is ordered that the periods of trust
on allotments made to Indians of the Yakima Reservation, Washington, which,
unless extended, would expire during the calendar year 1935, be, and they are hereby,
extended for a further period of 10 years from the date on which any such trust would

otherwise expire.
FRANKLIN D ROOSEVELT

Tue Wuite Hovsz,
May 8, 1986.
