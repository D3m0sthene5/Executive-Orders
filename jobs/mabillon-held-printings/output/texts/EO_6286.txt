WITHDRAWAL OF PuBLic LANDS FOR RESURVEY
NEW MEXICO

By virtue of the authority vested in me by the act of June 25, 1910 (ch. 421, 36
Stat. 847), as amended by the act of August 24, 1912 (ch. 369, 37 Stat. 497), and
subject to the conditions, provisions, and limitations of said acts, it is hereby ordered
that the public lands in Tps. 9 and 10 S., R. 8 W. of the New Mexico principal merid-
ian, New Mexico, be, and the same are hereby, withdrawn from settlement, location,
sale, entry, and all forms of appropriation, pending a resurvey of said townships
under the act of March 3, 1909 (ch. 271, 35 Stat. 845).

This order shall continue in full force and effect unless and until revoked by the
President or by an act of Congress.

FRANKLIN D. ROOSEVELT.
