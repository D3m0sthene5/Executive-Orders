Executive Order 5294. March 5, 1930
Executive Order
Withdrawal of Public Lands for Resurvey Partially Revoked
Nevada
Executive Order No. 5208, dated October 12, 1929, withdrawing the public
lands in T. 19 S., R. 56 E., Mt. Diablo meridian, Nevada, pending a resurvey in the
township, is hereby revoked as to sections 25, 26, 35, and 36, said township.
All of said lands are within the Dixie National Forest, having been reserved for
forest purposes by departmental order of January 4, 1906, proclamation of Novem-
ber 5, 1906, and Executive orders of April 6, 1916, and May 10, 1916, which remain
in full force and effect as to said lands.
HERBEET HOOVER
The White House,
March 5, 1930.
[No. 5294]
