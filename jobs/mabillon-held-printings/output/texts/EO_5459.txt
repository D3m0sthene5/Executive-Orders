Executive Order 5459. October 4, 1930
Executive Order
Placing the Position of Shipping Commissioner in the
Classified Service
The position of Shipping Commissioner at the several ports of the United States
is hereby placed in the competitive classified service.
This order supersedes Executive Order No. 3807 of March 15, 1923.
HERBERT HOOVER
The White House,
October 4, 1930,
[No. 5459]
