Executive Order

AMENDMENT OF EXECUTIVE ORDER No. 6657-A, oF Marcu 27, 1934

Section 1 (6) of Executive Order No. 6657-A, of March 27, 1934, prescribing
regulations for payment of losses sustained by officers, enlisted men, and employees
of the United States in foreign countries due to appreciation of foreign currencies in
their relation to the American dollar, is hereby amended to read as follows:

‘*(6) while en route through a foreign country or to or from such post,”’.

This order shall be retroactive to April 1, 1934, the effective date of the order

which it amends.

FRANKLIN D ROOSEVELT

THe Wuite House,
August 4, 1934.
