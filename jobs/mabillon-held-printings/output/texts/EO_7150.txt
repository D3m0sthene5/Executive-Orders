Executive Order

AMENDMENT OF EXECUTIVE ORDER No. 7126 or Aucust 5, 1935

Executive Order No. 7126 of August 5, 1935, entitled ‘‘Submission to the Bureau
of the Budget of Estimates of Expenditures for Administrative Expenses in Certain
Cases”, is hereby amended so as to make its provisions applicable in all respects to
the following-named additional agencies and instrumentalities of the United States,
except that the provisions of the last sentence of Paragraph 1 of the said Order shall be
applicable to the additional agencies and instrumentalities named herein from and
after October 1, 1935, instead of September 15, 1935:

. Federal Deposit Insurance Corporation

. Federal Surplus Relief Administration

. Export-Import Bank of Washington

. Second Export-Import Bank of Washington, D. C.

. Reconstruction Finance Corporation
. Electric Home and Farm Authority
