Executive Order

AUTHORIZING THE APPOINTMENT OF JACOB OBLOCK TO THE POSITION OF
ELEVATOR OPERATOR IN THE CusToDIAL SERVICE, Post OFFICE
DEPARTMENT, WITHOUT REGARD TO THE CIVIL SERVICE RULES

By virtue of and pursuant to the authority vested in me by the provisions of para-
graph Eighth of subdivision SECOND of section 2 of the Civil Service Act (22 Stat.
403, 404), it is hereby ordered that Jacob Oblock may be appointed to a position as
elevator operator in the Custodial Service, Post Office Department, without compli-
ance with the requirements of the Civil Service Rules.

Mr. Oblock sustained serious injuries while engaged in the performance of his
duties as laborer in the Custodial Service of the Post Office Department and has been
unable to resume his former occupation.

FRANKLIN D ROOSEVELT

Tue Wuite Hovsz,

July 9, 1937.

[No. 7654]
