Executive Order 5334. April 22, 1930
Executive Order
Appointment of Mrs. Selma F. Dinger
Mrs. Selma F. Dinger may be appointed to a suitable position in the War De-
partment without regard to the civil service rules.
Mrs. Dinger is the widow of Capt. Harry A. Dinger, an outstanding pilot in
the Air Corps, who was killed in an airplane accident on December 20, 1929, at
Boiling Field, Washington, D. C.
Captain Dinger served continuously during the World War, and as a pilot was
selected for many important missions.
This order is issued upon the recommendation of the Secretary of War.
HERBERT HOOVER
The White House,
April 22y 1930,
[No. 5334]
