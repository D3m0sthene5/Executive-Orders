Ae Executive Order

AUTHORIZATION TO APPOINT FRED W. BELTz

By virtue of the authority vested in me by the provisions of the last sentence
of the eighth paragraph of subdivision second of section 2 of the Civil Service Act
of January 16, 1883 (ch. 27, 22 Stat. 403, 404), it is hereby ordered that Fred W. Beltz
may be appointed as Assistant Deputy Commissioner in the Alcohol Tax Unit of
the Bureau of Internal Revenue, Treasury Department, without compliance with
the requirements of the civil-service rules.

This order is recommended by the Secretary of the Treasury.

FRANKLIN D, ROOSEVELT.
Tue White Hovussr,
August 23, 1934,
