Executive Order 5519. December 23, 1930
Executive Order
Retention of Robert Peet Skinner in the Foreign Service
Under the authority provided in subsection (g?), section 18, of the act approved
May 24, 1924 (43 Stat. 144), the President of the United States, in the exercise of
his discretion, and acting in the interest of the United States, hereby orders the
retention on active duty for such time as may be deemed necessary not in excess of
five years from February 24, 1931, of Robert Peet Skinner, of Ohio, envoy extraor-
dinary and minister plenipotentiary to Greece.
HERBERT HOOVER
The White House,
December 23y 1930.
