Executive Order 5513. December 13, 1930
Executive Order
Amendment of Schedule B, Positions Which May Be Filled upon
Noncompetitive Examinations under the Civil Service Rules
Subdivision X (State Department) of Schedule B, positions which may be filled
upon noncompetitive examinations under the civil service rules, is hereby amended
by the addition of a new paragraph, as follows:
2. Persons formerly employed abroad as United States diplomatic or consular
officers of career or foreign service officers of career for the period of at least four
years, for service in the Department of State as administrative officers or executive
