Executive Order

AUTHORIZING THE APPOINTMENT OF Miss SaraH WILSON REED TO A
CLASSIFIED PosITION IN THE Navy DEPARTMENT WITHOUT REGARD
TO THE CIVIL SERVICE RULES

By virtue of and pursuant to the authority vested in me by the provisions of
paragraph Eighth, subdivision SECOND, section 2 of the Civil Service Act (22 Stat.
403, 404), it is hereby ordered that Miss Sarah Wilson Reed may be appointed to a
classified position in the Navy Department without compliance with the requirements
of the Civil Service Rules.

Miss Reed is the daughter of Clyde Reed, who has been retired from his position
in the Navy Department on account of permanent and total disability.

This order is issued upon recommendation of the Secretary of the Navy.

FRANKLIN D ROOSEVELT
THe Wuire Hovss,

November 26, 1938.

[No. 8012]
