Executive Order

AUTHORIZING APPOINTMENT OF Louis R. Guavis TO ANY POSITION IN
THE CLASSIFIED CIVIL SERVICE

Mr. Louis R. Glavis, now serving in the excepted position of Director of Investiga-
tions, Department of the Interior, is hereby reinstated in the Civil Service without
regard to any charges heretofore preferred against him, and may be appointed in any
department, establishment, or office in the classified civil service of the United States
in any position for which his qualifications fit him and where there is need of his
services, without reference to the requirements of the civil service rules. This order
is issued on the recommendation of the Secretary of the Interior.

FRANKLIN D. ROOSEVELT.
Tue Waite House,
July 26, 19338.
