Executive Order

AUTHORIZING CERTAIN Persons To BE ContTINUED IN THEIR PRESENT
PosiTIons IN THE NationaL Bituminous CoaL CoMMISsION

By virtue of and pursuant to the authority vested in me by the provisions of
paragraph Eighth of subdivision SECOND of section 2 of the Civil Service Act (22
Stat. 403, 404), it is hereby ordered that Edgar C. Faris, Jr., Assistant Secretary,
William B. Roberts III, Chief, Code Membership Section, and Leonard W. Mosby,
Director of Public Relations, may be continued in their respective positions in the
office of the Secretary of the National Bituminous Coal Commission without regard
to the requirements of the Civil Service Rules and without thereby acquiring a

classified status.
FRANKLIN D ROOSEVELT

Tue Waite Hovssg,
Aug 2 1938.
