Gastonia, NorTH CAROLINA, DESIGNATED AS A Customs Port or ENTRY

By virtue of the authority vested in me by section 1 of the act of August 1, 1914
(ch. 223, 38 Stat. 609, 623; 19 U.S.C., sec. 2), Gastonia, North Carolina, is hereby
designated as a customs port of entry in customs collection district no. 15 (North
Carolina), with headquarters at Wilmington, North Carolina, effective as of this date.

FRANKLIN D. ROOSEVELT.
Tue Waite Hovss,
June 28, 1984.
