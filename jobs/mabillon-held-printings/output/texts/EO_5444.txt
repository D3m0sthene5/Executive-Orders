Executive Order 5444. September 16, 1930
Executive Order
Lawrence, Massachusetts, Desl^ated a Customs Port of Entry
By virtue of the authority vested in me by the act of Congress approved August
1, 1914 (38 Stat. 623), making appropriations for the sundry civil expenses of the
Government for the fiscal* year ended Jime 30, 1915, Lawrence, Mass., is hereby
designated a customs port of entry in Customs Collection District No. 4 (Massa-
chusetts) with headquarters at Boston, Mass., and the townships of Lawrence,
Methuen, and Andover are included within the limits of said port, effective 30 days
from the date of this order,
HERBERT HOOVER
