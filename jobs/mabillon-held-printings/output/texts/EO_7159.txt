Executive Order

APPOINTING LAURENCE J. Martin as AcTING ADMINISTRATOR OF THE
NATIONAL RECOVERY ADMINISTRATION

By virtue of and pursuant to the authority vested in me by Title I of the National
Industrial Recovery Act (48 Stat. 195), as amended by Public Resolution No. 26,
74th Congress, approved June 14, 1935, Laurence J. Martin of Virginia is hereby
appointed as Acting Administrator of the National Recovery Administration, vice

James L. O'Neill, resigned.
FRANKLIN D ROOSEVELT

Tue Waits Hovssz,
August 24", 19385.
