Executive Order

In THE MATTER OF THE APPLICATION OF ASSOCIATED CLOAK AND SUIT
MANUFACTURERS OF PORTLAND, OREGON, FOR CERTAIN EXEMPTIONS
FROM THE CoAT AND Suit CopE

A Code of Fair Competition for the Coat and Suit Industry has been heretofore
approved by me on certain terms and conditions. After such approval, and in
accordance with the provisions of my further Executive order dated July 15, 1933,
hearings have been granted by the Administrator to the named applicants, allegedly
directly affected by said code, who have claimed that applications thereof have been
unjust to them and have applied for an exemption therefrom with reference to the
minimum wage provided in the said code.

It appearing to me on the basis of the showing made at the hearings granted
the applicants above mentioned as set forth in the report thereon, dated September
28, 1933, rendered to me by the Administrator, which is hereby adopted and approved,
that no case of injustice and extreme hardship requiring special treatment has been
made out by the above applicants:

Now, Therefore, I, Franklin D. Roosevelt, President of the United States,
pursuant to the authority and discretion vested in me under title I of the
National Industrial Recovery Act, approved June 16, 1933, and otherwise, and in
accordance with the provisions of my Executive order dated July 15, 1933, providing
for hearing on the application of Codes under certain circumstances, do order that
the application for exemption by the above-named applicants, be and it is hereby
denied.

FRANKLIN D. ROOSEVELT.

Recommended for Approval by

Hues S. Joxnson,
Administrator.

Tas Waite Hovssz,
October 11, 1988.
