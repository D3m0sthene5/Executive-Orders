AMENDMENT OF EXECUTIVE ORDER No. 6440, or NoveMBER 18, 1933

The provisions of Executive Order No. 6440, dated November 18, 1933, pre-
scribing rates of compensation of Government employees in emergency agencies
not subject to the Classification Act and acts amendatory thereof, are hereby extended
to include, in addition to the agencies named in that order, all other existing emergency
agencies and those hereafter created, the rates of compensation of the officers and
employees of which are fixed without regard to the Classification Act of 1923, as

amended.
FRANKLIN D. ROOSEVELT.
THe Wuite House,

January 10, 1934
