Executive Order

DESIGNATION OF JOHN MoNnrROE JOHNSON AS ACTING SECRETARY OF
COMMERCE UNDER CERTAIN CONDITIONS

By virtue of and pursuant to the authority vested in me by section 179 of the
Revised Statutes (U.S. C., title 5, sec. 6), I hereby authorize and direct John Monroe
Johnson, Assistant Secretary of Commerce appointed under section 8 of the act of
May 20, 1926 (44 Stat. 568, 573), to perform the duties of the Secretary during the
absence of the Secretary of Commerce and the Assistant Secretary of Commerce
appointed under the act of February 14, 1903 (32 Stat. 825), as amended by the act

of March 4, 1913 (37 Stat. 736).
FRANKLIN D ROOSEVELT

Tue Waite House,
September 26, 1936.
