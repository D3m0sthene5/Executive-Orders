Executive Order

AUTHORIZING THE APPOINTMENT OF J. V. TAYLOR TO THE POSITION OF
SENIOR Sor CONSERVATIONIST IN THE SOIL CONSERVATION SERVICE,
DEPARTMENT OF AGRICULTURE, WITHOUT REGARD TO THE CIVIL
SERVICE RULES

By virtue of and pursuant to the authority vested in me by the provisions of
paragraph Eighth of subdivision SECOND of section 2 of the Civil Service Act of
January 16, 1883 (22 Stat. 403, 404), it is hereby ordered that J. V. Taylor may be
appointed to a position as Senior Soil Conservationist in the Soil Conservation Service,
Department of Agriculture, without compliance with the requirements of the civil
service rules and without being accorded a classified status.

This order is recommended by the Secretary of Agriculture, who states that Mr.
Taylor’s appointment will be in the interest of the service.

FRANKLIN D ROOSEVELT
Tue Waite House,

May 20, 1987.
