Executibe Order

EXEMPTION OF GEORGE M. Lewis From CompuLsory RETIREMENT
FoR AGE

WHEREAS, in my judgment, the public interest requires that George M. Lewis,
clerk, Office of the Collector of Taxes, Government of the District of Columbia, who,
during the current month, will reach the retirement age prescribed for automatic
separation from the service, applicable to him, be exempted from compulsory retire-
ment for age for a period of one year:

NOW, THEREFORE, by virtue of and pursuant to the authority vested in me
by section 204 of the act of June 30, 1932, 47 Stat. 382, 404 (U.S. C., title 5, sec.
715a), I hereby exempt the said George M. Lewis from compulsory retirement for
age for a period of one year ending December 31, 1939.

FRANKLIN D ROOSEVELT
Tue Waite Hovss,
December 2, 1988.
