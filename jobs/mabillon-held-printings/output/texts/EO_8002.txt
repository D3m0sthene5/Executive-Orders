EXEMPTION OF Harry PEALE FRoM COMPULSORY RETIREMENT FOR AGE

WHEREAS, in my judgment, the public interest requires that Harry Peale,
architect, Office of the Quartermaster General, War Department, who, during the
month of January, 1939, will reach the retirement age prescribed for automatic
separation from the service, applicable to him, be exempted from compulsory retire-
ment for age for a period of six months:

NOW, THEREFORE, by virtue of and pursuant to the authority vested in
me by section 204 of the act of June 30, 1932, 47 Stat. 382, 404 (U.S. C., title 5, sec.
715a), I hereby exempt the said Harry Peale from compulsory retirement for age

for a period of six months ending July 31, 1939.

FRANKLIN D ROOSEVELT
Tue Waite Hovsz,
November 7, 1938.

[No. 8002]
