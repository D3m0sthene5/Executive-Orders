Executive Order

REVOCATION OF EXECUTIVE ORDER OF JUNE 19, 1923, ESTABLISHING
CERTAIN PROCEDURE RELATIVE TO CLASSIFICATION OF ARMY
OFFICERS

By virtue of the authority vested in me by section 24b of the act of June 3, 1916
(ch. 134, 39 Stat. 166, 182), as amended by the act of June 4, 1920 (ch. 227, 41 Stat.
759, 773), Executive Order No. 3870, of June 19, 1923, establishing certain procedure
relative to the classification of officers of the Army provided for in said section 24b
of the act of June 3, 1916, as amended, is hereby revoked.

FRANKLIN D. ROOSEVELT.
Tue Waits Howss,
February 22, 1984.
