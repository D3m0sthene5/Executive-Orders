A Code of Fair Competition for the Underwear and Allied Products Manufac-
turing Industry having been approved by me September 18, 1933, on condition that
all manufacturers included within the provisions of part I, section 1(c) of said code
be granted a stay of fourteen (14) days after the effective date thereof, during which
period they might show cause to the Administrator why they should not be included
under the provisions of such section, and public hearings having been held thereon
October 14, 1933,

NOW, THEREFORE, T, Franklin D. Roosevelt, President of the United States,
pursuant to the authority vested in me by title I of the National Industrial Recovery
Act, approved June 16, 1933, and otherwise, do hereby grant an additional stay of
said provisions pending the determination by the Administrator of the issues with
respect thereto.

FRANKLIN D. ROOSEVELT.

Approval recommended:
Huan S. Jounson,
Administrator.
