AG Executive Order

EXEMPTION OF PERSONS FROM COMPULSORY RETIREMENT FOR AGE

By virtue of the authority vested in me by section 204 of part II of the act
entitled ““AN ACT Making appropriations for the Legislative Branch of the Gov-
ernment for the fiscal year ending June 30, 1933, and for other purposes”, approved
June 30, 1932, the following-named persons who, during the current month, reach
the retirement age prescribed for automatic separation from the service are hereby
exempted from the provisions of that section until April 30, 1935:

Name Establishment
Hugh A. Morrison Library of Congress
David E. Roberts Library of Congress

FRANKLIN D ROOSEVELT
Tue Waite Hovse,
April 10, 1983.
