Executive Order

DEsIGNATION OF Coast GUARD VESSELS FOR SPECIAL WINTER CRUISING

By virtue of and pursuant to the authority vested in me by section 1536, Revised
Statutes (U.S. C., title 14, sec. 53), and as President of the United States, I hereby
order that the Secretary of the Treasury cause a suitable number of Coast Guard
vessels, when the necessities of the service permit it, to cruise upon the coast in the
season of severe weather and to afford such aid to distressed navigators as their cir-
cumstances may require; and such vessels shall go to sea fully prepared to render
such assistance. Such vessels in the number prescribed by the Secretary of the
Treasury shall be assigned from time to time for the foregoing purpose by the Com-
mandant of the Coast Guard.

FRANKLIN D ROOSEVELT
Tae Waite Hovssz,
January 6, 1936.
