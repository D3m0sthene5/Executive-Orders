Executive Order

EXTENSION OF PERIOD OF ELIGIBILITY FOR APPOINTMENT AS OFFICERS IN
THE FOREIGN SERVICE

Notwithstanding the provisions of article I, paragraph 3, section (h) of Consular
Regulations, 1896, as amended by Executive Order No. 5642, of June 8, 1931, the
period of eligibility of those candidates for appointment as Foreign Service officers
whose names were on the eligible list on March 20, 1934, is hereby extended one year
and a half from the date on which their eligibility for appointment would otherwise
expire.
