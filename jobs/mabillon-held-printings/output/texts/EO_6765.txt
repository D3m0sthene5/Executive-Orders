Executive Order

ALLOCATION OF FUNDS FOR AN ADDITION TO THE EXECUTIVE OFFICE
BUILDING

By virtue of and pursuant to the authority vested in me under title II of the
Emergency Appropriation Act, fiscal year 1935, approved June 19, 1934 (Public,
No. 412, 73d Cong.), appropriating, among other sums, the sum of $325,000 for an
addition to the Executive Office Building and for the furnishings and equipment
thereof, the said sum of $325,000 is hereby allocated to the Secretary of the Interior
for expenditure for such purpose by the National Park Service of the Department
of the Interior, during the fiscal year 1935.

FRANKLIN D. ROOSEVELT.
Tue Wuite House,
June 29, 1984.
