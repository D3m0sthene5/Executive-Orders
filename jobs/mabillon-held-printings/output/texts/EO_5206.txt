Executive Order 5206. October 11, 1929
iSrecutive ®rbec
By virtue of the authority vested in me by the Act of Congress approved Aug-
ust 1, 1914, making appropriations for the sundry civil expenses of the Government
for the fiscal year ended June 30, 1915, the Customs station of Oroville, Washing-
ton, is hereby created a port of entry in Customs Collection District No. 30
(Washington) with headquarters at Seattle, Washington, effective thirty days from
the date of this order.
HERBERT HOOVER
The White House,
October 11, 1929.
