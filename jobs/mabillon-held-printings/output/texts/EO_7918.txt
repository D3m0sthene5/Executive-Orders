WHEREAS the initial appointments to certain executive positions in the Bureau
of Motor Carriers, Interstate Commerce Commission, were excepted from competitive
civil service requirements by Executive Order No. 7181 of September 6, 1935; and

WHEREAS the majority of such initial appointments have been made, and the
need for excepting the remainder of such appointments from competitive civil service
requirements no longer exists:

NOW, THEREFORE, by virtue of and pursuant to the authority vested in me
by the Civil Service Act (22 Stat. 403), the said Executive Order No. 7181 of Septem-
ber 6, 1935,is hereby revoked as to all such initia] appointments not made prior to the
