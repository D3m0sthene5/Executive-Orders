Executive Order

AMENDMENT OF EXECUTIVE ORDER OF JANUARY 28, 1873, RELATING TO
THE HoLpING oF State, MUNICIPAL, OR OTHER LOCAL OFFICE BY
FEDERAL OFFICERS AND EMPLOYEES

The Executive order of January 28, 1873, is hereby amended by striking out the
last two sentences and substituting the following in lieu thereof:

Mechanics and laborers employed by the day in armories, arsenals, navy yards, etc.,
and master workmen and others who hold appointments from the Government or
from any department whether for a fixed time or at the pleasure of the appointing
power, are embraced within the operation of the order.

It is intended by this order to bring all mechanics and laborers in armories,
arsenals, navy yards, etc., within the operation of the Executive orders of January 17
and January 28, 1873.

FRANKLIN D. ROOSEVELT.
Tue Waite Hovss,
August 27, 1933.
