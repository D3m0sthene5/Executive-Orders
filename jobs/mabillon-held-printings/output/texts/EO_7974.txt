EXEMPTION OF ALFRED R. GouLp From Computsory RETIREMENT
FOR AGE

WHEREAS, in my judgment, the public interest requires that Alfred R. Gould,
artistic operator of geometric lathe, Bureau of Engraving and Printing, Treasury
Department, who during the month of October, 1938, will reach the retirement age
prescribed for automatic separation froin the service, applicable to him, be exempted
from compulsory retirement for age for a period of one year:

NOW, THEREFORE, by virtue of and pursuant to the authority vested in me
by section 204 of the act of June 30, 1932, 47 Stat. 382, 404 (U.S. C., title 5, sec. .
715a), I hereby exempt the said Alfred R. Gould from compulsory retirement for
age for a period of one year ending October 31, 1939.

FRANKLIN D ROOSEVELT
Toe Waite Hovsz,
