DEPOSITED BY THE
UNITED STATES OF AMERICA

JUL 15 1938


Executive Order

EXEMPTION OF Davip A. Bropir From ComMpuLsorRy RETIREMENT FOR
AGE

WHEREAS, in my judgment, the public interest requires that David A. Brodie,
farm superintendent, St. Elizabeths Hospital, Department of the Interior, who during
the month of July, 1938, will reach the retirement age prescribed for automatic separa-
tion from the service, applicable to him, be exempted from compulsory retirement for
age for a period of one year:

NOW, THEREFORE, by virtue of and pursuant to the authority vested in me
by section 204 of the act of June 30, 1932, 47 Stat. 382, 404 (U.S. C., title 5, sec. 715a),
LI hereby exempt the said David A. Brodie from compulsory retirement for age for a

period of one year ending July 31, 1939.

FRANKLIN D ROOSEVELT
Tae Waite Hovse,
June 16, 1988.
