By virtue of the authority vested in me by the provisions of the last sentence
of the eighth paragraph of subdivision second of section 2 of the Civil Service Act
of January 16, 1883 (ch. 27, 22 Stat. 403, 404), paragraph 11, subdivision VIII of
schedule A of the civil-service rules is hereby amended to read as follows:

“11. Six special agents of the Division of Investigations, Office of the Secretary,
to investigate fraudulent entries and other matters of a criminal nature.”’

FRANKLIN D. ROOSEVELT.
