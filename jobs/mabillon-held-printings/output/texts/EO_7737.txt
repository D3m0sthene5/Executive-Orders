Executive Order

AUTHORIZING THE APPOINTMENT OF Mrs. MaryG. Birp To a PositTION IN
THE 'aRM CREDIT ADMINISTRATION WiTHOUT REGARD TO THE CIVIL
SERVICE RULES

By virtue of and pursuant to the authority vested in me by the provisions of
paragraph Eighth of subdivision SECOND of section 2 of the Civil Service Act (22
Stat. 403), it is hereby ordered that Mrs. Mary G. Bird may be appointed to a classified
position in the Farm Credit Administration without compliance with the requirements
of the Civil Service Rules.

Mrs. Bird would have become eligible to acquire a civil service status under
the provisions of Executive Order No. 6758 of June 29, 1934, had she not been ap-
pointed to an excepted position in the Farm Credit Administration on June 1, 1934.

This order is recommended by the Governor of the Farm Credit Administration.

FRANKLIN D ROOSEVELT

Tue Wuite House,
November 6, 1987.
