
Executive Order

AMENDMENT OF SECTION 1 oF CIVIL-SERVICE Rue VII

By virtue of the authority vested in me by the provisions of the last sentence of
the eighth paragraph of subdivision second of section 2 of the Civil Service Act of
January 16, 1883 (ch. 27, 22 Stat. 403, 404), paragraph (a) of section 1 of civil-
service rule VII, governing certification, is amended by eliminating the final sentence,
“Certifications shall be made without regard to sex unless the nature of the duties of
the position to be filled are such as, in the opinion of the Commission, can be per-
formed only by men or women, as the case may be,” and substituting therefor the
sentence, ‘‘Certifications shall be made without regard to sex unless sex is specified
in the request”.

FRANKLIN D ROOSEVELT

Tue Waite Hovss,

October 5, 1984.
