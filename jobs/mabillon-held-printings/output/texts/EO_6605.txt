Executive Order

AMENDMENT OF EXECUTIVE ORDER No. 4728 RELATING TO COMMUTATION
OF RATIONS AND QUARTERS TO ENLISTED MEN

Executive Order No. 4728 of September 29, 1927, as amended by Executive
Order No. 5022 of January 5, 1929, and restored to full force and effect by Executive
Order No. 5288 of February 26, 1930, is hereby amended by adding to item B in
table I the words ‘“‘or to the American Embassy, Moscow, U.S.S.R.,” and by adding
the following item to table I:

“T).—Special—American Embassy, Moscow, U.S.S.R.,

(a) Subsistence_._.__..-_..-_-.-.---------- $3.00 : $3.00
(b) Quarters. .-...-..-----.----- woe eee nee 1.00 : 1.00”

FRANKLIN D. ROOSEVELT.
Tue Wurre Hovse,
February 17, 1934.
