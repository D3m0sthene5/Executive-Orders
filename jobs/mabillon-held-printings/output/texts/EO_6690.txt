AG Executive Ordet

Dererrine ErrectivE DaTE oF ExEcuTIvVE OrpDER No. 6614,
oF FEBRUARY 26, 1934
Pursuant to the last paragraph of Executive Order No. 6614, of February 26,
1934, transferring the functions of the War Department pertaining to national
cemeteries and memorials in Europe to the American Battle Monuments Commis-
sion, it is ordered that the effective date of the said Executive order be, and it is

hereby, deferred until May 2), 1934.
FRANKLIN D. ROOSEVELT.

Tae Waite Hovsr,
April 26, 1934.

(No. 6690]
