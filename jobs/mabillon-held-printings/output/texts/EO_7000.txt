Executive Order

AMENDING Copr oF FatrR COMPETITION FOR THE DISTILLED SPIRITS
INDUSTRY

WHEREAS the Federal Alcohol Control Administration has submitted for
my approval certain amendments to the Code of Fair Competition for the Distilled
Spirits Industry and has rendered its report and recommendations and findings
thereon; and

WHEREAS due notice of a public hearing on said proposed amendments has
been given and a public hearing thereon has been held pursuant to such notice;

NOW, THEREFORE, by virtue of and pursuant to the authority vested in
me by Title I of the National Industrial Recovery Act of June 16, 1933 (ch. 90,
48 Stat. 195), and in order to effectuate the purposes of said Title, I hereby approve
said amendments and modify my previous approval of the Code of Fair Competition
for the Distilled Spirits Industry to include an approval of said Code in its entirety
as hereby amended.
