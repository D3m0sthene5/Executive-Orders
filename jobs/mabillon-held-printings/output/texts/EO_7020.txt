Executive Order

AMENDING CopE OF FAIR COMPETITION FOR THE WINE INDUSTRY

WHEREAS the Federal Alcohol Control Administration has submitted for my
approval amendments to the Code of Fair Competition for the Wine Industry and
has rendered its report and recommendations and findings thereon; and

WHEREAS due notice of public hearing on said proposed amendments has been
given and a public hearing thereon has been held pursuant to such notice;

NOW, THEREFORE, by virtue of and pursuant to the authority vested in me
by Title I of the National Industrial Recovery Act of June 16, 1933 (ch. 90, 48 Stat.
195), and in order to effectuate the purposes of said Title, I hereby approve said
amendments and modify my previous approval of the Code of Fair Competition for
the Wine Industry to include an approval of said Code in its entirety as hereby

amended.
FRANKLIN D ROOSEVELT

THe Waite Hovss,
April 19, 1936.
