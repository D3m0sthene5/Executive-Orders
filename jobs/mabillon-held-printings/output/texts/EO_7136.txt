Executive Order

Coat Lanp Restoration, New Mexico No. 53

By virtue of and pursuant to the authority vested in me by the act of June 25,
1910 (ch. 421, 36 Stat. 847), as amended by the act of August 24, 1912 (ch. 369,
37 Stat. 497), the Executive order of July 9, 1910, creating Coal Land Withdrawal,
New Mexico No. 1, is hereby revoked insofar as, and to the extent that, it affects the
following-described lands:
