Executive Order

Maxina Certain Funos ImmMepIATELY AVAILABLE TO THE FEDERAL
EMERGENCY RELIEF ADMINISTRATION

By virtue of and pursuant to the authority vested in me by the act of February
15, 1934 (Public, No. 93, 78d Cong.), appropriating $950,000,000 to carry out the
purposes of the Federal Emergency Relief Act of 1933 (ch. 30, 48 Stat. 55), and to
continue the civil-works program, and for other purposes, there is hereby made
immediately available to the Federal Emergency Relief Administration (in addition
to the sum of $100,000,000 heretofore made available by Executive Order No. 6709,
of May 14, 1934) the sum of $50,000,000 from the sum of $350,000,000 allocated by
Executive Order No. 6603, of February 15, 1934, to the said Administration for
expenditure during the fiscal year ending June 30, 1935.
