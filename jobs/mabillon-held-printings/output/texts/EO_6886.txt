Executive Order

TRANSFER OF LANDS FROM THE NICOLET NATIONAL FOREST TO THE
CHEQUAMEGON NATIONAL FOREST

WISCONSIN

By virtue of and pursuant to the authority vested in me by the act of June 4,
1897 (30 Stat. 11, 36, U.S.C., title 16, sec. 473), and on the recommendation of the
Secretary of Agriculture, it is ordered that the Mondeaux unit of the Nicolet National
Forest, as defined by Proclamation No. 2060, of November 13, 1933, be, and it is
hereby, transferred to the Chequamegon National Forest, as defined by Proclamation

No. 2061, of November 13, 1933.
FRANKLIN D ROOSEVELT

Tae Wuirte Hovss,
October 27, 1984.
