EXEMPTION OF WILLIAM H. GrirFIN From ComPpuLSORY RETIREMENT
FoR AGE

WHEREAS, in my judgment, the public interest requires that William H.
Griffin, senior engineer, Geological Survey, Department of the Interior, who was
exempted from compulsory retirement for age for a period of one year ending July 31,
1938, by Executive Order No. 7667 of July 17, 1937, be further exempted from such
compulsory retirement for a period of one year:

NOW, THEREFORE, by virtue of and pursuant to the authority vested in me
by section 204 of the act of June 30, 1932, 47 Stat. 382, 404 (U.S. C., title 5, sec.
715a), I hereby further exempt the said William H. Griffin from compulsory retire-
ment for age for a period of one year ending July 31, 1939.

FRANKLIN D ROOSEVELT
Toe Waite Hovss,
July 14, 1938.
