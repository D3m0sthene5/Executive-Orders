Executive Order

Warver oF Maximum AcE Limit To Permit Wiuuiam H. KuunkK AND
Wiiuiam H. BIeERHALTER TO COMPETE IN CIVIL-SERVICE EXamI-
NATIONS

By virtue of and pursuant to the authority vested in me by the provisions of
paragraph Eighth of subdivision SECOND of section 2 of the Civil Service Act of
January 16, 1883 (22 Stat. 403, 404), it is hereby ordered that the maximum age
limit for entrance to examinations under civil-service rules be waived to permit
William H. Klunk and William H. Bierhalter to compete in the pending civil-service
examinations for the positions of chief engineman and watchman, respectively,
custodial service, Columbus, Ohio, in which positions they have been serving satis-
factorily under extended temporary appointments.

This order is issued on the recommendation of the Civil Service Commission
because these employees reached the maximum age limit before the announcement
of the examinations, which was delayed on account of pressure of work.

FRANKLIN D ROOSEVELT
Tue Waits Hovss,
July 19, 1985.
