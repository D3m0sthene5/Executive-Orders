Executive Order

EXEMPTION OF WILLIAM H. EcsBerts From CoMPpuLsORY
RETIREMENT FOR AGE

WHEREAS, in my judgment, the public interest requires that William H.
Egberts, chief preparator in anthropology in the United States National Museum,
Smithsonian Institution, who, during the month of September, 1938, will reach the
retirement age prescribed for automatic separation from the service, applicable to
him, be exempted from compulsory retirement for age for a period of one year:

NOW, THEREFORE, by virtue of and pursuant to the authority vested in
me by section 204 of the act of June 30, 1932, 47 Stat. 382, 404 (U.S. C., title 5,
sec. 715a), I hereby exempt the said William H. Egberts from compulsory retirement
for age for a period of one year ending September 30, 1939.

FRANKLIN D ROOSEVELT
Tue Waite House,
August 9, 1988.
