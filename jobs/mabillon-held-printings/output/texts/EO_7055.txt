Executive Order

WAIVER OF THE CIVIL-SERVICE RULES TO PERMIT THE APPOINTMENT
oF Frep S. GILBERT TO A POSITION IN THE CUSTODIAL SERVICE
OF THE Post OrFriceE DEPARTMENT

By virtue of and pursuant to the authority vested in me by paragraph Eighth
of subdivision SECOND of section 2 of the Civil Service Act of January 16, 1883
(ch. 27, 22 Stat. 403, 404), it is hereby ordered that Mr. Fred S. Gilbert may be
appointed to the position of elevator operator in the custodial service of the Post
Office Department, assigned to the United States Courthouse and Post Office Build-
ing, Jacksonville, Florida, without compliance with the requirements of the civil-

service rules.
FRANKLIN D ROOSEVELT

Tue Wuite House,
May 28, 1936.
