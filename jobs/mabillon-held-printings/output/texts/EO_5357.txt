Executive Order 5357. May 29, 1930
Executive Order
Extension of Trust Period on Allotments of the Uncompah^e,
Uintah, and White River Bands of Ute Indians of Utah
It is hereby ordered, under authority contained in the act of June 21, 1906 (34
Stat. 325-326), that the trust period on the allotments made to members of the
Uncompahgre, Uintah, and White River Bands of Ute Indians of Utah, which trust
period expires during the calendar year 1930, be, and is hereby, extended for a period
of ten years from the date of expiration.
HERBERT HOOVER
The White House,
May 29, 1930.
