By virtue of and pursuant to the authority vested in me by the act of June 25,
1910 (ch. 421, 36 Stat. 847), as amended by the act of August 24, 1912 (ch. 369,
37 Stat. 497), it is ordered that Executive Order No. 5109, dated May 13, 1929,
withdrawing, with other lands, public lands in T. 49 N., R. 5 E. of the New Mexico
principal meridian, Colorado, be, and it is hereby, revoked as to said township,
effective upon the date of the official filing of the plat of resurvey of said township.

FRANKLIN D. ROOSEVELT.
