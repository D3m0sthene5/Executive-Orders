AUTHORIZING THE BEET SUGAR COMMITTEE TO ACCEPT THE VOLUNTARY
SERVICES oF W. LEwis ABBOTT

By virtue of and pursuant to authority vested in me under title I of the National
Industrial Recovery Act of June 16, 1933 (ch. 90, 48 Stat. 195), I hereby authorize
the Beet Sugar Committee appointed by the Secretary of Labor pursuant to my
authorization of October 27, 1933, to accept and utilize the voluntary and uncom-
pensated services of Professor W. Lewis Abbott.

FRANKLIN D. ROOSEVELT.
