
Executive Order

WAIVER OF Civit-SERVICE Rute IX To PERMIT THE REINSTATEMENT OF
Miss IRENE C. CLAVELOUX IN THE GENERAL ACCOUNTING OFFICE

By virtue of the authority vested in me by the provisions of the last sentence of
the eighth paragraph of subdivision second of section 2 of the Civil Service Act of
January 16, 1883 (ch. 27, 22 Stat. 403, 404), it is hereby ordered that the time limit
prescribed by civil-service rule 1X be waived to permit the reinstatement of Miss Irene
C. Claveloux to a position in the General Accounting Office.

FRANKLIN D. ROOSEVELT.
Tue Wuitr Houses,
March 31, 1984.
