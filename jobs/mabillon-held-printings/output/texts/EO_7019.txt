Executive Order

AMENDING CoDE oF Fair COMPETITION FOR THE BrEwING INDUSTRY

WHEREAS the Federal Alcohol Control Administration has submitted for my
approval amendments to the Code of Fair Competition for the Brewing Industry
and has rendered its report and recommendations and findings thereon; and

WHEREAS due notice of public hearing on said proposed amendments has
been given and a public hearing thereon has been held pursuant to such notice;

NOW, THEREFORE, by virtue of and pursuant to the authority vested in
me by Title I of the National Industrial Recovery Act of June 16, 1933 (ch. 90,
48 Stat. 195), and in order to effectuate the purposes of said Title, I hereby approve
said amendments and modify my previous approval of the Code of Fair Competition
for the Brewing Industry to include an approval of said Code in its entirety as
hereby amended.

FRANKLIN D ROOSEVELT

Tue Wuarre Hovss,

April 19, 1986.
