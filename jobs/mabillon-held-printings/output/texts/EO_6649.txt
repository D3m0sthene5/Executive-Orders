Executive Order

WAIVER oF CiviL-SERVICE Rute II to Permit THE APPOINTMENT OF
Mrs. EvizABETH Bass IN THE TREASURY DEPARTMENT

By virtue of and pursuant to the authority vested in me by the provisions of
the last sentence of the eighth paragraph of subdivision second of section 2 of the
Civil Service Act of January 16, 1883 (ch. 27, 22 Stat. 403, 404), it is hereby ordered
that Mrs. Elizabeth Bass may be appointed as district supervisor, Bureau of Nar-
cotics, Treasury Department, without compliance with the requirements of civil-
service rule IT.

This order is recommended by the Secretary of the Treasury, who states that
Mrs. Bass has been serving temporarily as district supervisor, with the approval of
the Civil Service Commission, since July 31, 1933, and that her permanent appoint-
ment will be in the interest of the service.

FRANKLIN D. ROOSEVELT.
Tue Wuitre House,
March 23, 1984.
