Executive Order 5192. September 14, 1929
Bxecutive ®rbet
By virtue of the authority vested in me by the Act of Congress approved
August 1, 1914, Lewiston, New York, is hereby abolished as a port of entry in
Customs Collection District No. 9 (Buffalo) with headquarters at Buffalo, New
York, effective thirty days from the date of this order.
HERBERT HOOVER
The White House,
September U, 1929,
