Executive Order

TRANSFER OF FUNDS TO THE FEDERAL EMERGENCY RELIEF ADMINIS-
TRATION FROM FuNps ALLOCATED TO THE FEDERAL CiIviL WorKS
ADMINISTRATION

By virtue of and pursuant to the authority vested in me by the act of
February 15, 1934 (Public, No. 93, 73d Cong.), appropriating $950,000,000 to
carry out the purposes of the Federal Emergency Relief Act of 1933 (ch. 30, 48
Stat. 55) and to continue the civil-works program, and for other purposes, there
is hereby transferred to the Federal Emergency Relief Administration, to be
available for expenditure during the fiscal year ending June 30, 1934, the sum of
$75,000,000 from the sum of $450,000,000 heretofore allocated to the Federal Civil
Works Administration from the said appropriation of $950,000,000.

FRANKLIN D. ROOSEVELT.
Tue White Houses,
April 24, 1934.
