Executive Order

WITHDRAWAL OF PusBiic Lanps ror USE IN CONNECTION WITH WALKER
River Inpran IRRIGATION PROJECT

NEVADA

By virtue of the authority vested in me by the act of June 25, 1910 (ch. 421, 36
Stat. 847), as amended by the act of August 24, 1912 (ch. 369, 37 Stat. 497), and
subject to the conditions therein expressed and to all valid existing rights, it is ordered
that the following-described public lands in Nevada be, and they are hereby, with-
drawn from settlement, location, sale, entry, and all forms of appropriation, for use
in connection with the Walker River Indian irrigation project, Nevada:

Mount Diasto Base MeERIDIAN
T. 13 N., R. 28 E., sec. 7, lots 1 and 2, and EXNW.

This order shall continue in full force and effect unless and until revoked by the
President or by act of Congress,
FRANKLIN D. ROOSEVELT.
THe Waite Hovsz,
February 26, 1934.
