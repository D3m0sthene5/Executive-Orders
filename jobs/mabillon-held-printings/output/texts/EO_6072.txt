TRANSFER OF CONTROL AND JURISDICTION OF CERTAIN BUILDINGS AT
CRISTOBAL TO THE Navy DEPARTMENT

CANAL ZONE

By virtue of the authority vested in me by law, it is hereby ordered that Buildings
Nos. 1113, 1409, and 1411, located at Cristobal, Canal Zone, that were transferred,
exclusive of the sites thereof but including free use of such sites, by Executive Order
No. 3917, dated October 16, 1923, from the Panama Canal to the War Department, be,
and the same are hereby, transferred under like conditions to the Navy Department.

FRANKLIN D ROOSEVELT
Tue Wuite House,
March 8, 19383.
