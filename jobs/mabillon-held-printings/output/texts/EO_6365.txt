GREENWICH, MIDDLETOWN, NORWALK, SOUTH MANCHESTER, AND STAM-
FORD, CONNECTICUT, ABOLISHED aS Customs Ports OF ENTRY

By virtue of the authority vested in me by section 1 of the act of August 1, 1914
(ch. 223, 38 Stat. 609, 623; 19 U.S.C., sec. 2), Greenwich, Middletown, Norwalk,
South Manchester, and Stamford, Connecticut, are hereby abolished as customs
ports of entry in customs collection district no. 6 (Connecticut), with headquarters
at Bridgeport, Connecticut, effective as of 30 days from the date of this order.

FRANKLIN D. ROOSEVELT.
