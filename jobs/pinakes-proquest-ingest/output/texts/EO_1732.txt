Executive etber
Miss Alice E. Gibbs may be appointed to a classified position in the
government service without compliance with the Civil Service rules.
Miss Gibbs has had extensive experience as a stenographer and type-
writer in private employ and at the Capitol, where she has been employed
for the past seven years in a secretarial capacity, and has proven herself to
be exceptionally competent and trustworthy.
The Civil Service Commission does not recommend the issuance of
this order.
WM H TAFT
THE WHITE HousE,
March 3, 1913.
[No. 1732.]
