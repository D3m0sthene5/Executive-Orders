Executive Order.
Mrs. Amelia V. Briscoe may be pointed to a clerical position in the
classified service without examination.
This order is issued on the recommendation of the Secretary of War who
states that Mrs. Briscoe is the widow of Captain William J. Briscoe, Philippine
Scouts, who lost his life last year in the Philippines from burns caused while
fighting fire in an attempt to save the lives of soldiers.
Mrs. Briscoe was temporarily appointed without examination in the Bureau
of Pensions under an Act of Congress which permitted temporary appointments
to be made without regard to the Civil Service Rules. It is reported that her
work is above the average and that she has faithfully and conscientiously dis-
charged all the duties of the position.
The Civil Service Commission did not concur in the issuance of this order.
WARREN G HARDING
THE WHITE HOUSE,
June 18, 1Q23.
