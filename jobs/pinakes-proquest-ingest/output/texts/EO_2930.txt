Under authority of the Act of Congress approved June 25, 1910 (36 Stat.,
847), as amended by the Act of August 24, 1912 (J Stat., 49) and subject
to the conditions therein expressed and to prior valid rights, it is hereby ordered
that the hereinafter described lands in the State of Oregon be, and the same are
hereby, temporarily withdrawn from settlement, location, sale or entry until
March 5, 1919, i11 aid of legislation proposed in pending S. 3864.
If said legislation be not enacted before the adjournment of the last session
of the Sixty-fifth Congress, and no other direction is given regarding the dis-
posal of such lands, they will, on March 5, 1919, become subject to disposal
under any law then applicable thereto without further order.
Such lands are described as follows:
In T. 7 S., R. 43 E., W. M., Secs. 34, 3S and N I Z of Sec. 36;
In T. 8 S., R. 43 1., Secs. 2 and 3.
