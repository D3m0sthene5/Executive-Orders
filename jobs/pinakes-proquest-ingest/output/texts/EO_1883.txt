Executive rber
ALASKA
Under authority of the Act of Congress entitled "An Act To Author-
ize the President of the United States to make withdrawals of public lands
in certain cases," approved June 25, 1910 (36 Stat., 847), as amended by
the Act of August 24, 1912 (37 Stat., 497), it is hereby ordered that the
Executive Order dated March 28, 1911, withdrawing "all tracts of public
lands in the District of Alaska upon which hot springs, or other springs,
the waters of which possess curative medicinal properties, are located, to
the extent of 16o acres surrounding each spring, in rectangular form with
side and end lines equidistant, as near as may be, from such spring or
group of springs," be revoked so far as it applies to lands within National
Forests.
WOODROW WILSON
THE WHITE HOUSE,
