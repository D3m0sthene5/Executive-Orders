Mr. Claude T. Worford may be appointed to the position of draftsman at
$1200 per annum in the Office of Naval Intelligence, Navy Department, without
regard to the civil service rules.
Efforts of the Navy Department to fill this vacancy by certification and by
transfer have failed. The work is represented as confidential and secret to the
highest degree, and the Secretary of the Navy states that it is imperative that
the Office should know in advance the qualifications, antecedents, affiliations,
and sympathies of the appointee. He states that Mr. Wforford possesses the
necessary qualifications, and has to his credit four years of service in the Navy
with an honorable discharge and a good conduct medal.
The Civil Service Commission concurs with the Secretary of the Navy in
recommending this order.
WOODROW WILSON
THE~ WHIT HOUSE~,
29 March, .1917.
