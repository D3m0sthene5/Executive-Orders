GUNNISON NATIONAL FOREST
COLORADO
It is hereby ordered that on and after July x, 1 90, the land within the
boundary shown on the attached diagram, heretofore set apart, reserved,
and proclaimed as parts of thec(Gunnison, Leadville, and Cochetopah
National Forests, shall be known as the Gunnison National Forest. The
remaining portions of the Gunnison National Forest shall continue reserved
and constitute parts of the Holy Cross and Leadville National Forests.
It is not intended by this order to release any land from reservation or to
reserve any land not heretofore embraced in a National Forest.
