Executive Order.
Mr. Be njamin Grady may be appointed as expert (with inventive ability)
in the Bureau of the Census, Department of Commerce, without regard to the
requirements of the civil service rules.
This order is issued upon recommendation of the Secretary of Commerce
and the Director of the Census, who state that there is immediate need in the
mechanical laboratory of the Bureau for the services of a person with inventive
ability to assist in the devising of machinery, the application of mechanlical
principles, and in experimentation; that it is not deemed practicable to obtain a
competent person through the usual civil service chaniiels; that Mr. Grady is a
person of known and tried mechanical ability with inventive talent; and that
the Bureau will regard itself as fortunate in securing, under present conditions,
the services of a person with Mr. Grady's qualifications. For these reasons it
is believed that an exception to the rules in this instance is fully justified in the
interests of the service.
THE WHITE HOUSE,
30 July, 1917.
WOODROW WILSON
