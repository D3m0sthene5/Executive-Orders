]Executive Orber.
In accordance with the provisions of Section 2253 of the Revised
Statutes of the United States, and by virtue of the authority therein given,
it is hereby ordered that such parts of townships twenty-three, twenty-four,
twenty-five, twenty-six, and twenty-seven north as lie south and east of the
Missouri River in the State of Montana be detached from the Yellowstone
land district and attached to and made a part of the Glasgow land district,
and the business and archives pertaining to the lands within said townships
as lie south and east of the Missouri River be transferred from the land
office at Mliles City to the land office at Glasgow.
THEODORE ROOSEVELT
THE XWHIT EHOUSE,
February 1-5, 1908.
