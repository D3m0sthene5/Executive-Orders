SIL ETZ INDIAN RESERVATION.
Under the provisions of the Act of Congress approved June 25, 1910,
Public 303, an act authorizing the President of the United States to make
withdrawals of the public lands in certain cases, it is hereby ordered that
the public lands within the boundaries of the Siletz Indian Reservation,
located in the State of Oregon, which were conveyed to the United States
by treaty agreement entered into October -I, 1892, which agreement was
ratified by act of Congress dated August 15, 1894, 28 Stat. 326, more par-
ticularly shown on the attached diagram, are hereby temporarily withdrawn
for the purpose of classifying said lands, and pending the enactment of
legislation under which same can be properly disposed of; Provided, that
all said lands shall at all times be open to exploration, discovery, occupa-
tion and purchase under the mining laws of the United States, so far as
the same applies to minerals other than coal, oil, gas, and phosphate; and
the rights of any person who at the date hereof is a bona fide occupant or
claimant of oil or gas bearing lands and who at such date is in diligent
prosecution of work leading to discovery of oil or gas, shall not be affected
or impaired by this order, so long as such occupant or claimant shall con-
tinue in diligent prosecution of such work; and Provided, further, that
there shall be excepted from the force and effect of this order of with-
drawal all lands which are on this date embraced in any lawful homestead
entry heretofore made or upon which any valid settlement has been made,
and is at such date being maintained and perfected pursuant to lawv; but
the terms of this proviso shall not continue to apply to any particular tract
of land unless the entryman or settler shall continue to comply with the
law under which the entry or settlement wvas made.
WM H TAFT
THE WHITE HOUSE,
July 13, 1910.
