Executive Order
By virtue of the authority vested in me by Section 4075 of the Revised
Statutes of the United States, as amended by the Act of Congress approved June
14, 1902, which provides that "the Secretary of State may grant and issue pass-
ports, and cause passports to be granted, issued, and verified in foreign countries

other executive officer of the insular possessions of the United States, and under
such rules as the President shall designate and prescribe for and on behalf of the
