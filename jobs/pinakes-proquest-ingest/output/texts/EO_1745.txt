)Executive Orber,
Pursuant Ato the authority contained in section 179 of the Revised
Statutes, I hereby authorize and direct Charles Earl, Solicitor of the
Department of Commerce, to perform the duties of Secretary of Labor,
during the absence of the Secretary of Labor and the Assistant Secretary
of Labor.
WOODROW WILSON
THE WHITE HOUSE,
March 11, 1913.
[No. 1745"]
