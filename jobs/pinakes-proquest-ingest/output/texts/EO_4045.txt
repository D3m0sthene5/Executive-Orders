contained, I hereby, in the public interest, make the following determination,

My reasons for the foregoing determination, order, rule and regulation are:
(a) An advantageous offer for the purchase of the said real estate has been
received by the Alien Property Custodian;
(b) An attempted sale at public auction would be a useless formality and
would involve unnecessary expenses, delay and inconvenience, which should be
avoided.
This Executive Order is an amendment to Executive Order No. 4001, dated
April 30, 1924, authorizing the Alien Property Custodian to sell certain property at
private sale.
