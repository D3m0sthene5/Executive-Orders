Pursuant to the provisions of the Act of Congress, approved August
1, 1914, making appropriations for the sundry civil expenses of the Gov-
ernment for the fiscal year ended June 30, 1915, the port of entry at
Somers Point, New Jersey, located in customs collection district No. 11,
of which Pniladelphia is the headquarters port, is hereby abolished.
This or or will become effective on January 1, 1916.
WOODROW WILSON
THE WHITE HOUSE,
30 November, 1915.
