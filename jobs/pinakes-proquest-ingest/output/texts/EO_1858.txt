Under authority of the act of Congress approved June 25, 1910 (36
Stat., 847), as amended by act of August 24, 1912 (3 Stat., 497), and
subject to the conditions therein expressed, it is hereby ordered that the
lands hereinafter described be, and the same are, hereby temporarily with-
drawn from settlement, location, sale, entry and other forms of disposal
until March 5, 1915, in aid of the legislation proposed in pending H. R.
Bill No. 5993.
If this bill is not enacted before the adjournment of the last session of
the Sixty-Third Congress, and no other direction is given as to the dispo-
sition of the lands, they will, on March 5, 1915, become subject to disposal
under any law then applicable thereto, without further orders.
