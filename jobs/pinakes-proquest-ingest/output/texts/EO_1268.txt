Schedule A of classified positions excepted from examination under
Rule II, clause 3, is hereby amended by the insertion of a new subdivision
to b~e numbered V, immediately after Subdivision IV, War Department,
all subdivisions following to be renumbered accordingly:
V. NAVY DEPARTMENT.
i. Paymaster's clerks acting as principal clerks to general store-
keepers at navy yards and naval stations.
2.Civilian professors, instructors and teachers in the U. S. Naval
Academy at Annapolis.
Paragraph i i of Subdivision ITT of Schedule A is amended by striking
out the following words
"and in the U. S. Naval Academy at Annapolis"
The exception of the paymaster's clerks acting as principal clerks to
general storekeepers at navy yards and naval stations is recommended by
the Navy Department, in that their status is an anomalous one and of
doubtful legality, as they occupy positions in 1)th the civil and naval serv-
ices, deriving benefit from both and thus have special privileges above
their fellows in the Navy. This present status is believed to be detri-
mental to the best interest of the Government and of the Naval Service,
in that it prevents the Decpartment from having these paymaster's cerks
perform the sea service which is necessary for their efficiency, and deprives
the general storekeeper of a yard or station of the privilege of nominating
his clerks, which privilege is enjoyed by all other paymasters in the Navy
wvho are entitled by lawv to the services of a payTmasters clerk. The Com-
mission concurs in the recommendation of the Department.
The exception of civilian professors, instructors, and teachers in the
U. S. Naval Academy at Annapolis is merely stricken out of that part of
the Schedule pertaining to the War Department and inserted under the
new heading " Navy Department" where it more properly belongs.
