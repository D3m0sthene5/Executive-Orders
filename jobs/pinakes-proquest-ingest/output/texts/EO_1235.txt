Executive cOrber.
Mr. Robert T. Frazier may be reinstated in the examining corps of
the Patent Office without regard to the length of time he has been sepa-
rated from the service. The Civil Service Commission and the Interior
Department concur in recommending this action in view of the excellent
record made by Mr. Frazier in the Patent Office from 1887 to 1893; his
experience in preparing applications for patent and in conducting extensive
patent litigation; the large number of employees recently appointed to the
examining corps; and the demand of the Patent Office for all persons w~ho
are available and qualified.
WM H TAFT
THE WIUTE HOUSE,
August 8/hi, 1910.
