Mr. Louis H. Eberk:in may be reinstated to an appropriate classified
position in the Customrs Service at Newv York without regard to the length
of time he has beet separated from the service. Mr. Eberlein was sepa-
rated from the service on May 26, 1910, on a charge of accepting money
from importers and underweighing dutiable merchandise. Upon a rehear-
ing of the case by the Surveyor of Customs at New York the opinion wvas
reached that neither the charges as a whole nor any specification had been
sustained, and in view of Mr. Eberlein's previous good record recommenda-
tion w~as made that the charges be dismissed.
The Attorney General, after review of the case, stated that he deemed
it a case where an honest moan suffered from the general atmosphere of his
surroundings, and that the recommendation of the Surveyor of Customs
should be acted upon and Mr. Eberlein reinstated.
The Department reached the conclusion that Mr. Eberlein's dismissal
from the service wvas not justified and that his reinstatement would be in
the interests of good administration.
The Civil Service Commission joins in recommending this order.
