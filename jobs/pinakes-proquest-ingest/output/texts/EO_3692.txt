AUTHORIZING THE ALIEN PROPERTY CUSTODIAN TO SLL CERTAIN PROPERTY
AT PRIVATE SALE
By virtue of the authority vested in me by an Act of Congress entitled
"An act to define, regulate, and punish trading with the enemy and for other
Purposes", approved October 6th, 191, known as the "Trading with the Enemy
Act" and the Amendment to such Act embodied in "An act making appropria-
tions to supply urgent deficiencies in the appropriations for the fiscal year
ending June 30, 191i8, and prior fiscal years on account of war expenses and for
other Purposes," approved March 28, 1918, and the Amendment to such Act
embodied in "An act making appropriations to supply deficiencies in appro-
priations for the fiscal year ending June 30, 1919, and prior fiscal years on
account of war expenses, and for other Purposes", approved November 4, 1918,
to make certain orders such as that herein contained, I hereby, in the public
interest, make the following determination, order, rule and regulation:
The Ale ryu odian is hereby authorized to sell at private sale,
at such time and place as to him or to his authorized agent may seem advisable,
without public or other advertisement, certain real estate now being held and
administered by him as such Alien Property Custodian, and heretofore, upon
requirement, conveyed, transferred, assigned and delivered to him as the
property of "the widow and heirs-at-law and next of kin of Edward Schmitt,
deceased, names unknown, whose address is Germany", which said persons he
did after investigation determine to be enemies within the purview of said Act
as amended, which property is described as follows, to-wit:
Lot eleven (i i), in Block thirty-four' (34), in Ogden's Addition to Chiag
being a part of the NE 14 of Section eight (8), Township Thirty-nie 39)
north, Range fourteen (14), East of the 3rd P. M, as recorded in Book 2722,
Page 189, Recorder's Office, Cook County, Illinois, with the improvements
thereon.
My reasons for the foregoing determination, order, rule and regulation are:
(a) That property such as that herein described can not usually be sold to
advantage at public sale after public or other advertisement.
(b) That an advantageous offer for the purchase of said property has been
received by the Alien Property Custodian.
(c) That an attempted sale at public auction would be a useless formality
and would involve unnecessary expenses, delay and inconvenience which should
be avoided.
(d) That the said enemies, whose names have been ascertained to be Clara
M. Schmitt, Martha S. Selzam, Lulu von Scanzoni, Elizabeth Schultheiss, and
Dr. William Schmitt-Marcel, have expressed their approval of the private sale
of said property at the price offered.
WARREN G HARDING
THE WHITE HOUSE,
June z, 1922.
