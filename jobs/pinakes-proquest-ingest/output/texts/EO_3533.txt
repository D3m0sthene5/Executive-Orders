Temporry
Executive Order
Mrs; Nellie J. Latta (formerly Nellie W. Jenkins) may be reinstated to
the position of clerk in the classified service without regard to the civil service
rules. &.f,
Mrs, Latta, who is now serving as temporary clerk in the Post Office
Department, is the widow of Albert E, Latta, a former employee of that
Department, who after twenty four years of faithful service was dropped from
the rolls September 30, 1918, on account of insanity, and died in March, 1919,
leaving Mrs. Latta in destitute circumstances.
Mrs, Latta before her marriage served in the Post Office Department from
March t, 1899, to 'November 24, 1903, as Miss Nellie W. Jenkins, and was again
appointed in a temporary position. October 1, 19x9, in which position she is
now serving.
