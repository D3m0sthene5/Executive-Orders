Executive Orber.
John H. Dickinson, formerly employed as an inspector in the Quarter-
master's Department of the Army, who was dismissed more than one year
ago on account of alleged irregularities which were afterwards found to be
groundless, may be reinstated as an inspector without regard to the pro-
vision of the civil service rules limiting reinstatement to one year after
separation.
This order was recommended by the Secretary of War and the Civil
Service Commission.
WM H TAFT
TFHE WURIE HousE,
