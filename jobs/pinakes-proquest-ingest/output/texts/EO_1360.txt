Mrs. Mary E. Gunion, a former employee of the Government Printing
Office, may be appointed to any position in the clerical grade in any
department.
Mrs. Gunion is a daughter of Edward McQuade, late driver for the
Secretary of War, who while driving the Secretary's horses was killed in a
successful effort to save the lives of the occupants of the carriage, and by
his death Mrs. Gunion became the sole support of her widowed mother,
her brother, and her own child.
The order of April 15~, 191 I, relating to Mrs. Gunion, is hereby revoked.
