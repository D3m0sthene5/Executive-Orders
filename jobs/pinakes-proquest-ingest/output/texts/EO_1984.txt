Executive Orber.
In accordance with the provisions of Section 2253 of the Revised
Statutes of the United States, and by virtue of the authority therein
given, it is hereby ordered that:
The townships lying North and East of the following described lines
of survey be detached from the Missoula land district, and attached to
and made a part of the Kalispell land district in the State of Montana',
and that the archives and business pertaining to the public lands affected
thereby be transferred from the land office at Missoula to the land office
at Kalispell:
Beginning at the Southeast corner of Township twenty-six North,
Range twenty-seven West; thence West along the south boundaries of
Townships twenty-six North, Ranges twenty-seven, twenty-eight, twenty-
nine, thirty and thirty-one West, to the Southwest corner of Township
twenty-six North, Range thirty-one W~est; thence North on the range
line between Ranges thirty-one and thirty-two West to its intersection
with the Southwest corner of Township twenty-eight North, Range
thirty-one WVest; thence West along the south boundary of Township
twenty-eight North, Ranges thirty-two, thirty-three, thirty-four and thirty-
five West to its intersection with the West Boundary of the State.
WOODROW WILSON
THE WHITE HOUSE,
July 7, 1914.
