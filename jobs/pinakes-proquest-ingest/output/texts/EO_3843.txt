An Executive Order concerning certain sales to be conducted by the Alien
Property Custodian pursuant to the "Trading with the Enemy Act"
and amendments thereof.
By virtue of the authority vested in mne by "An Act to define, regulate and
punish trading with the enemy, and for other purposes", as amended March 4 th,
1923, known as the "Trading with the Enemy Act", I hereby, in the public
interest, make the following determination, order, rule and regulation :
The Alien Property Custodian may sell at private sale, without public or
other advertisement, property of any nature whatsoever, held by him as Alien
Property Custodian, in parcels, lots or quantities for market value at the time
of sale not exceeding Fifty Thousand Dollars ($5o,ooo.) for each parcel, lot or
quantity sold. Such sales may be made by thme Alien Property Custodian
whenever hie determines that the same are necessary for the adjustment of claims
or for the benefit of the particular trust concerned, or for the best administration
of time property, or for the interests of tihe United States in liquidating such
property. Any such sale may be conducted at such place and upon such deter
iniiiation and conditions as the Alien Property Custodian or his authorized agent
may see proper.
