Executive Other.
Alaskan Townsite.
Under and pursuant to the provisions of the Act of Congress, approved
March 1 2, 1914 (38 Stat., 305), entitled "An Act to authorize the President of
the United States to locate, con-, uct and operate railroads in the Territory
of Alaska, and for other purpose,", it is hereby ordered that the following
described land, withdrawn by Executive Order of June 23, 1914, and reserved
for townsite purposes under said act, be, and the same is hereby eliminated
from said order and said order revoked so far as it relates to said described
land, and the land restored to entry, subject to any valid existing rights of the
Chena Private Townsite Survey No. 436, and of the Tanana Valley Railroad
Company's right of way and station grounds, to wit :
All of Sec. 28, T. i S., R. 2W.
Fairbanks Meridian, Alaska.
WOODROW WILSON
THE WHITE HOUSE~,
4 April, 1917.
(No. 2577.]
