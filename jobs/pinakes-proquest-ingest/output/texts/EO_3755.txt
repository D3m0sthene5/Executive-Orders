Utah--Colorado-
Under authority of the act of Congress approved June 25, 1910 (36 Stat.,
847), as amended by the act of August 24, 1912 (37 Stat., 497), the following
described lands in Utah and Colorado are hereby temporarily withdrawn,
subject to the conditions, provisions and limitations of said acts:
Utah, Salt Lake Base and Meridian
In township forty south, range twenty-five east, northwest quarter northwest
quarter of section twenty-one ; in township thirty-nine south, range twenty-
six east, northeast quarter of section twenty and west half northwest quarter of
section twenty-one.
Colorado, New Mexico Principal Meridian
In township thirty-six north, range twenty west, lot six and southwest
quarter southeast quarter of section three and lot one and northwest quarter
northeast quarter of section ten.
The purpose of this withdrawal is to permit such examinations of the lands
as may be necessary to determine their availability for a permanent reservation
under the provisions of the National Monument Act approved June 8, i 906
(3 Stat., 225) and to protect in the public interest the prehistoric ruins found
thereon.
