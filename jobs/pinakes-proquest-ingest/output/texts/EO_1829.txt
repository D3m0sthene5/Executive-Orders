)Executive order
Paragraph 3, Subdivision IX, Schedule A, is hereby amended by sub-
stituting therefor the following :
3. Guards and field assistants for reconnaissance parties employed
temporarily during the season of danger from fires or when special
work requires additions to the regular forest force. They shall serve
only so long as absolutely required and in no case beyond tbe usual
field season. So far as the Commission may deem practicable such
appointments shall be made from the registers of eligibles.
This action is taken upon the joint recommendation of the Secretary
of Agriculture and the Civil Service Commission.
WOODROW WILSON
THE WHITE HOUSE,
22 Septtember, 1913.
[No. 1829.]
