Executive Order Number 1566, of July 19, 1912, is hereby amended
to read as follows:
Mr. Dorsey E. Phillips of Missouri may be appointed to a clerical
position or as Immigrant Inspector in the competitive classified service
without reference to the Civil Service Rules. Mr. Phillips served for five
years in the regular army prior to May 30, 1898. He passed the clerk
examination held at St. Joseph, Missouri, on March 22, 1909, with a rating
of 78.35 and was temporarily employed as a result of this examination
from October 25, 1909, to February r8, 1910. He also passed the census
clerk examination with a rating of 8o.2c as a result of which he received
appointment in the Bureau of the Census and served from September 6,
1910, to February 29, 1912, when he was separated on account of a neces-
sary rdcino force. The Civil Service Commission does not recom-
mend the approval of this order.
