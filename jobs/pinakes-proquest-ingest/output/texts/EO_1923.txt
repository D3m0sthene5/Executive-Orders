Executive Orber.
Mrs. Virginia Winslow may he appointed as charwoman under the
War Department without regard to the requirement of the labor regula-
tions governing the employment of laborers and charwvomen in the depart-
ments in Washington, except that she be subject to examination to test
her physical fitness for the work.
Mrs. Winslow is the widow of Lindsey Winslow, who wvas a faithful
and deserving employee in the War Department in various capacities from
July , 1872, until the time of his death, March 13, 1914.
The Acting s cretary of War recommends that an order be issued
authorizing the appointment of Mrs. Winslow. The Civil Service Com-
mission does not concur in the recommendation.
WOODROW WILSON
TIE WHITE HOUSE,
25 April, 1914.
