Executive 'Orber
Mr. Frederick J. Crocker may be reinstated in his former position of Chief
of the Stamp Gumming Division of the Bureau of Engraving and Printing, from
which he was dismissed on March 31, 1922. Mr. Crocker has been thus far pre-
cluded from reinstatement by the fact that he was granted an annuity as a mechanic
retirable at sixty-five which action, it appears from a review of the case, was based
on misleading information, his proper retirement age being seventy. The Treasury
Department, the Department of the Interior, and the Civil Service Commission
are in agreement upon this action.
CALVIN COOLIDGE
THE WHITE HOUSE,
July 17, 1924.
