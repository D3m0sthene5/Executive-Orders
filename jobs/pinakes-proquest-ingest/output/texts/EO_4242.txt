Executive Orber
At the request of Honorable Emilio del Toro y Cuebas, Chief Justice of
the Supreme Court of Porto Rico, recently designated as Acting Judge of the
District Court of the United States for Porto Rico, and under the authority
delegated to me in Section 41 of the Act of March 2, 1917, I hereby designate
Honorable Adolf G. Wolf as Acting Judge of the District Court of the United
States for Porto Rico, during any interval that may occur between Judge Odlin's
leaving the Islands and the arrival of Judge Wells.
During the incumbency of Judge Adolf G. Wolf, and in accordance with said
Section 41 of the Apt of March 2, 1917, he is fully authorized and empowered to
perform the duties of said office, and to sign all necessary papers and records as
the Acting Judge of said Court, without extra compensation
CALVIN COOL IDGE
THE WHYFE HOUSE,
June 4, 1925.
