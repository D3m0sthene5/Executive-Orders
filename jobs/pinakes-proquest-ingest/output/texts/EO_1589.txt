RAINY CREEK ADMINISTRATIVE SITE
(Near Palisade National Forest)
IDAHO
Under authority of the Act of Congress approved June 25, 1910 (36
Stat., 847), and on the recommendation of the Secretary of Agriculture,
it is hereby ordered that a tract of land containing 8o acres which by pro-
traction of the public survey in Township 2 North, Range 44 East, Boise
Meridian, Idaho, would be the SE/4 SE'4 , Section 33, and SW'4 SW' 4 ,
Section 34, of said Township, be temporarily withdrawn from settlement,
location, sale or entry, except as provided in said Act, and be reserved for
use by the Forest Service as a Ranger Station in the administration of the
Palisade National Forest.
WM H TAFT
