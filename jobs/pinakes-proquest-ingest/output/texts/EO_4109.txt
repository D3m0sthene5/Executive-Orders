FLORIDA.
Under authority contained in the act of Congress approved June 25, 1910
(36 Stat., 847), as amended by the act of August 24, 1912 (37 Stat., 497), and
subject to the conditions, provisions and limitations of said acts, it is hereby
ordered that all islands belonging to the United States in Florida situated in
the waters off the coast or in the coastal waters of the State, be and the same
are hereby withdrawn from settlement, location, sale, entry and all forms of
appropriation, subject to any valid existing rights in and to the. same, for classi-
fication and in aid of legislation.
CALVIN COOLIDGE
THE WHITE Housu,
December 8, 1924.
