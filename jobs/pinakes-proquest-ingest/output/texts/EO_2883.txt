In order to effece the more expeditious performance of contracts for the
construction, repair and alteration of vessels, structures, and aids to navigation,
and for the obtaining of equipment for the Bureau of Lighthouses, Department

Emergency the President is authorized to suspend provisions of law prohibiting

rate of eight hours work, with overtime rates to be paid for at not less than
time and one-half for all hours work in excess of eight hours," I do hereby
suspend during the present National Emergency the provisions of law pro-
