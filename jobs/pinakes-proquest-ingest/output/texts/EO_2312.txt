Executive orber
The temporary appointment of Miss Helen Wrenn as clerk in the
Bureau of Foreign and Domestic Commerce, Department of Commerce,
may he made permanent without regard to the civil service rules.
This order is issued upon recommendation of the Secretary of Comn-
merce who states that she wvas certified for temporary appointment after
passing the civil service examination in stenography and typewriting, and
that with the exception of a brief period she has been serving in the
Bureau named continuously since April I, 1915; that she is unusually
efficient and has shown special aptitude in the performance of the various
duties assigned to her, and that it is therefore believed that her permanent
appointment will prove of very great value to the Bureau which is especi-
ally in urgent need of competent and trained assistants at this time owing
to the extraordinary amount and character of work devolving upon it.
WOODROW WILSON
TILE WHITE HOUSE,
ii February, 1916.
[No. 2312.]
