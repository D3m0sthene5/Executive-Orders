Under authority contained in the act of Congress approved June 25, 1910
(36 Stat., 847), as amended by the act of August 24, 1912 (37 Stat., 497), and subject
to the conditions, provisions and limitations of said acts, it is hereby ordered that
the public lands in Ts. 6 S., Rs. 4 and 5 W., T. 7 S., R. 4 W., and Sec. 1, T. 8 S.,
R. 5 W., N. M. P. M., New Mexico, be, and the same are hereby withdrawn from
settlement, location, sale, entry and all forms of appropriation pending a resurvey
thereof under authority contained in the act of March 3, 1909 (35 Stab,., 845) ; this
withdrawal to remain in full force and effect from the date hereof until the resurvey
is duly approved and accepted and the approved plats thereof are officially filed
in the proper United States local land office, unless another date be fixed by the
Commissioner of the General Land Office, whereupon this withdrawal will become
of no effect.
