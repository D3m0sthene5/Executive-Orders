Mrs. Martha A. Davis may be appointed to an appropriate position in the
Government service without regard to the civil service rules.
Mrs. Davis is the widow of Orin Davis who at the time of his death, Septem-
ber 24, 1928, had faithfully served the Government as a railway postal clerk and as
tariff clerk for the Interstate Commerce Commission for more than thirty years.
His death left her without income or adequate means of support.
The Chairman of the Interstate Commerce Commission states that it is not
the policy of that Commission to ask for individual Executive orders, but he inter-
poses no objection in the case of Mrs. Davis. The Civil Service Commission can
not recommend this order, its recommendations for waivers of the rules being limited
to those cases where the needs of the service can not be met under the procedure
under the rules.
For charity.
CALVIN COOL DGE
