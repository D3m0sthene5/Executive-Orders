]Executive Orber
AGRICULTURAL EXPERIMENT STATION
(Matanuska Valley)
ALStIt is hereby ordered that the lands hereinafter described be, and the same
are, eliminated from the operation of Executive Order No. 1919 12, dated April
21, 1914, withdrawing and reserving the same for townsite purposes under the
Act of Congress, approved March 12, 1914 (38 Stat., 305) ; and, under the author-
ity of the Act of Congress approved June 25, 1910 (36 Stat., 847), as amended by
the Act of August 24, 1912 (.37 Stat., 497), said lands are hereby withdrawn
from settlement, location, sale, entry, or other disposition, and reserved for the
use of the Department of Agriculture for use by the States Relations Service
in the conduct of investigations and agricultural experiments authorized by
Congress.
DESCRIPTION.
Entire Sec. 14, T. 17 N., R. ' E., Seward Meridian, Alaska.
This order shall not affect the withdrawal of any other lands by said Execu-
tive Order of April 21, 1914.
WOODROW WILSON
THE WHITE House,
10 July, 1917.
