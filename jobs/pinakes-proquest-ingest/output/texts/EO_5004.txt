Under authority of the act of Congress approved June 25, 1910 (36 Stat. 847),
as amended by the act of August 24, 1912 (37 Stat. 497), and subject to the con-
ditions expressed therein, it is hereby ordered that the land of the United States in
the State of Montahia in T. 4 N., Rs. 50, and 51 E., Ts. 5 and 6 N., Rs. 49, 50 and
51 E., P. M., as lie betweer Mizpah and Pumpkin Creeks, are hereby temporarily
withdrawn from settlement, location, sale or entry and reserved for classification
and in aid of legislation.
This order shall continue in full force and effect unless and until revoked'by
the President or by act of Congress.
