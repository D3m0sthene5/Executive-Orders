Executive Order
Mr. Edwin B. Smith, who formerly held the position of clerk in the
Interstate Commerce Commission, may be reinstated to a classified position
in the Post Office Department without regard to the length of his separation
or the provisions of the civil service rules requiring reinstatement in the
same Department.
This order is issued on the recommendation of the Postmaster General
because of Mr. Smith's previous efficient service in the Interstate Commerce
Commission and the exceptional qualifications he has evinced in the per-
formance of his duties as confidential clerk to the Postmaster General.
WOODROW WILSON
THE WHITE HOUSE,
November 10, 1914.
[No. 2076. ]
