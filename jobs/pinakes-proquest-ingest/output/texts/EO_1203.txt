Miss Helen M. Bullis may be appointed special inspectress in the
Immigration Service, Department of Commerce and Labor, without
examination under the civil-service rules.
This action is authorized on the recommendation of the Secretary of
Commerce and Labor because of Miss Bullis' ascertained fitness for con-
ducting investigations necessary in connection with the new statute con-
cerning white slavery (amendment of Immigration Act, approved March
26, 1910o) and with the concurrence of the Civil Service Commission.
Miss Bullis has been employed since September i, 1907, either temporarily
or in a position not subject to examination upon similar work.
