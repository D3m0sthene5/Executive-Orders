Executive Order.
In consideration of the fact that the Executive order of June 13, 1924, (No.
4026), fixing the number of hours to constitute a day's work on Saturdays from
the second Saturday of June to the second Saturday of September, both inclusive,
was not received in certain branches of the Federal Government in sufficient time
to allow employees to enjoy the benefits thereof, it is hereby ordered that four
hours, exclusive of time for luncheon, shall constitute a day's work on Saturday,
September 20, 1924, for such employees as were entitled to the benefit of the afore-
mentioned order on Saturday, June 14, 1924, but who were deprived thereof by
reason of the fact that the order was not received in sufficient time to comply with
the provisions thereof.
CALVIN COOL IDGE
THE WHITE HOUSE,
September 17, 1924.
