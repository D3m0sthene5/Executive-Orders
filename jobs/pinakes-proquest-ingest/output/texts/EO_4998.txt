Executive orber
WASHINGTON
The Executive order of September 13, 1859, which reserved certain land in Ts.
40 and 41 N., RI. 3 W., W. M., Washington, for public purposes is hereby revoked
and set aside in so far as it applies to Lot 2 Sec. 9, T. 40 N., R. 3 W., W. M., and
the Secretary of the Interior is hereby authorized and directed to take such action,
as may be necessary to restore said lot to settlement and entry under the general
provisions of the public land laws.
CALVIN COOLIDGE
THE WHITE HousE,
November 21, 1928.
