Executive 0rber.
In accordance with the provisions of Section 2253 of the Revised
Statutes of the United States, and by virtue of the authority therein given,
it is hereby ordered that:
Such lands as lie cast and south of the following described boundaries
be detached from the Seattle land district and attached to, and made a part
of the North Yakima land district in the State of Washington, and that
the business and archives pertaining to said lands be transferred from the
land office at Seattle to the land office at North Yakima.
Beginning on the Fifth Standard Parallel north at the southeast corner
of township twventy-one north, range ten east, thence north on the range
line between ranges ten and eleven east to the Sixth Standard Parallel
north to the northeast corner of township twenty-four north, range ten
east; thence east along the Sixth Standard Parallel north to its intersec-
tion with the summit of the Cascade range of mountains.
WOODROW WILSON
THE WHITE HOUSE,
March 28, 1914.
[No. 1907.]
