Executive orer,
Schedule A, . subdivision I, paragraph i of the civil service rules
excepting positions named from the requirement of examination is hereby
amended by the addition of the following words:
* * * and one to the Public Printer.
As amended the exception contained in paragraph i will read as
follows:
i. Two private secretaries or confidential clerks to the head of
each of the executive departments and one to each assistant head and
one to the Public Printer.
It was found necessary to permit the appointment of a private secre-
tary to the Public Printer without examination by the Executive order of
January 30, 1903, and again by the Executive order of June 9, 1908.
The present amendment will permit the appointment of a new private
secretary or confidential clerk as necessity arises without resort to a special
Executive order and will also give the occupant of the position the status
of an excepted rather than a competitive employee.
WOODROW WILSON
THIE WHITE HOUSE,
23 July, 1913.
