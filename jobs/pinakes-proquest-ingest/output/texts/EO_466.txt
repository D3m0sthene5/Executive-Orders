Executive Orber.
It is hereby ordered that on Saturdays during July, August and Sep-
tember, until further notice, four hours, exclusive of time for luncheon,
shall constitute a day's work for skilled mechanics, laborers, and employees
in the Classified Civil Service at the Government Printing Office, Wash-
ington. Provided, that if any skilled mechanic, laborer or employee in
the Classified Civil Service shall work more than four hours on any Sat-
urday during the months aforesaid, he shall be paid for such extra work at
the rate of fifty per centum more than his regular and normal compensation
and no more.
THEODORE ROOSEVELT
THE WRITE HOUSE,
June 23. 1906.
