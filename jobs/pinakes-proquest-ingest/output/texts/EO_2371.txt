Miss Ella Lee, of Mississippi, may be appointed to a clerical position
in the Postal Service, with a view to her appointment in the Post Office
at Jackson, Mississippi, without reference to the requirements of the civil
service rules.
Miss Lee is the daughter of the late Robert C. Lee, who for many
years was United States District Attorney for the Southern District of
Mississippi, and whose death about a year ago, together with the recent
death of her brother, renders it necessary for Miss Lee to secure employment
in order to provide a livelihood for herself and mother. It is stated that
Miss Lee is a graduate of the High School of Jackson, and well qualified
to fill a clerical position in the postal service; and that the postmaster will
appoint her to such a position if authorized to do so.
WOODROW WILSON
THE WHITE HOUSE,
28 April, 1916.
[No. 2371.]
