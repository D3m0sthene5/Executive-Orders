Executive 'rber.
Mr. Charles H. Shaw, now employed in the customs service at the
port of New York, may be appointed to a classified position in the New
York Customs Service without reference to the Civil Service Rules. This
appointment is recommended by the Collector of the port of New York
and the Secretary of the Treasury, but is not concurred in by the Civil
Service Commission.
WTM H TAFT
THE WHITE HOUSE,
Marchk3, 1913.
