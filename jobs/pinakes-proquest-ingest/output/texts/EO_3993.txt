Included with the land hereby transferred are all buildings and other
improvements, property of the United States, located thereon. The Navy
Department is permitted to occupy as living quarters the dwelling houses until
such time as the United States Veterans' Bureau desires to occupy or remove
them, and the furniture and furnishings now in such dwellings, property of
the United States, are excluded from this transfer and reserved to the control
of the Navy Department.
Any further improvements that may be placed on the land hereby trans-
ferred, and all repairs to and extensions of existing improvements thereon, shall
be at the cost of other than naval appropriations.
The United States Veterans' Bureau shall bear an equitable part of the
cost of repairs to and improvements of any structures under the control of the
Navy Department and located within the Naval Training Station, Great Lakes,
Illinois, or the Naval Hospital, Great Lakes, Illinois, that are or may become
necessary to the use by the United States Veterans' Bureau of the land hereby
transferred, including the construction, operation, or maintenance of any im-
provements placed there by the United States Veterans' Bureau.
