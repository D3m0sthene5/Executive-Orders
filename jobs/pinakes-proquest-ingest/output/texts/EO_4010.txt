AUTHORIZING THE ALIEN PROPERTY CUSTODIAN
TO SELL CERTAIN PROPERTY AT PRIVATE SALE
By virtue of the authority vested in me by an Act of Congress entitled
"An act to define, regulate, and punish trading with the enemy and for other
purposes", approved October 6, 1917, known as the " Trading with the Enemy
Act", and the amendment to such act embodied in " An act making appropria-
tions to supply urgent deficiencies in the appropriations for the fiscal year
ending June 30, 19x 8, and prior fiscal years on account of war expenses and for
other purposes", approved March 28, 1918, and the amendment to such act
embodied in " An act making appropriations to supply deficiencies in appro-
priations for the fiscal year ending June 30, 1919, and prior fiscal years on
account of war expenses, and for other purposes", approved November 4,
1918, to make certain orders such as that herein contained, I hereby, in the
public interest, make the following determination, order, rule and regulation :
The Alien Property Custodian is hereby authorized to sell at private sale,
at such tn an place as to him or to his authorized agent may seem advis-
able, without public or other advertisement, for and in consideration of the
sum of One hundred sixty thousand ($x6o,ooo.oo) Dollars, certain real estate
now being held and administered ay him as such Alien Property Custodian,
and heretofore, upon requirement, conveyed, transferred, assigned and de-
livered to him as the property of Kiara Kombst, Gertrude Luckhardt and Ilka
Wichmann, residing in Germany, whom after investigation, he did determine
to be enemies within the purview of said Act as amended, which said real estate
is situate in the County of Los Angeles, State of California, and known and
described as follows, to-wit:
Lot Thirty-six (36), Oak Knoll, in the City of Pasadena, County of LosAngeles, State of California, as per map re corded in Book 10, Pages
114, 115-A, i 18, andi i 19-B, of Maps in the office of the County Recorderof said County, together with the improvements thereon.
My reasons for the foregoing determination, order, rule and regulation are,.
(a) An advantageous offer for the purchase of the said real estate has been
received by the Alien Property Custodian ;
(b) An attempted sale at public auction would be a useless formality
and would involve unnecessary expenses, delay and inconvenience, which
should be avoided.
CALVIN COOL IDGE
