Executive Ode
It is hereby ordered that those employees of the several Executive
LDepartments and independent government establishments in the District
of Columbia who are members of the organizations which are to participate
in the parade in connection with the unveiling of the statue of Baron
Frederick William von Steuben on Wednesday, December 7, 1910, may
be excused from duty for that purpose.
WM H TAFT
THE WHIE HOUSE,
December 6, 1910.
