LIMESTONE ADMINISTRATIVE SITE
(Near Beartooth National Forest)
M ONTANA
Under authority of the Act of Congress approved June 25, 1910 (36
Stat., 847), as amended by the Act of August 24, 1912 (37 Stat., 497),
and on the recommendation of the Secretary of Agriculture, it is hereby
ordered that the NE. /4 of SW. /4 Section 27, T. 4 5., R. 15 E., M. P. M.,
Montana, area 40 acres, be temporarily withdrawn from settlement, loca-
tion, sale or entry, except as provided in said Acts, and be reserved for use
by the Forest Service as a corral in connection with the counting of sheep
that graze under permit on the Beartooth National Forest.
WOODROW WILSON
THE WHITE HOUSE,
14 July, 1913.
