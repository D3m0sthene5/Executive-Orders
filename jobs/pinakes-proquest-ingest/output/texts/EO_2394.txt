]Executive Orber
Upon the recommendations of the Secretary of the In:_ nor and of
the Alaskan Engineering Commission, and under authority given in Act
of Congress approved March 12, 1914 (38 Stat., 305), it is hereby ordered
that free transportation shall be furnished employees of the United States
Government when their official duties require them to travel over the lines
of the Government railway in Alaska, and free transportation shall he pro-
vided for equipment, material and supplies necessary in the conduct of
Government investigations and work by officers and employees of the
United States when it is expedient to forward such equipment, material
and supplies over the lines of said railway.
WOODROW WILSON
THE WHITE HOUSE,
5 June, 1916.
