AGRICULTURAL RANGE EXPERIMENT STATION ENLARGED
ARIZONA
Under authority of the act of Congress approved June 25, 1910 (36 Stat., 847),
as amended by the act of August 24, 1912 (37 Stat., 497), and on the recommenda-
tion of the Secretary of Agriculture, it is hereby ordered that the public lands
within the following described areas in Arizona be, and the same are hereby, with-
drawn, subject to existing valid claims, as an addition to an Agricultural Range
Experiment Station:
Gila and Salt River Meridian,
In T. 18 S., R. 13 E., fractional Secs. 24, 25 and 36;
In T. 18 S., R. 14 E., Secs. 29, 30, 31 and 32;
In T. 19 5., R.. 14 E., Sec. 6.
