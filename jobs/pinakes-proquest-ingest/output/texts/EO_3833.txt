Mr. Henry T. Ellington may be reinstated to the position of clerk in the
Railway Mail Service without regard to the length of his separation from the
service.
Mr. Ellington was removed from the position of clerk in the Railway Mail
Service on May 17, 1916, charged with embezzlement. The Postmaster General
states that he was tried and acquitted of the charge, that a most careful investi-
gation has convinced him that Mr. Ellington's removal was an injustice and
that he was entirely innocent of the charges made against him, and that his
reinstatement by executive order would serve the public interest better than
the appointment of a new clerk.
The Civil Service Commission concurs in recommending this order.
WARREN G HARDING
THE WHITE HOUSE,
May ,5, 1923.
