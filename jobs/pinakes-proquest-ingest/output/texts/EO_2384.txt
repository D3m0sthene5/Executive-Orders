]Executive Orber
It is hereby ordered that Steven D. Cline, who was appointed post-
master at Enterprise, Kentucky, on June 13, 1914; Mrs. Alma Phillips,
who was appointed postmaster at Martha, Kentucky, on June 23, 1914;
Thomas F. Henry, who was appointed postmaster at Repton, Kentucky,
on June 19, 1914; Harry A. Dronenburg, who was appointed postmaster
at Tuscarora, Maryland, on July 23, 1914, and Frank P. Harris, who wvas
appointed postmaster at Walnut, Pennsylvania, on October 10, 1914, all of
whom have complied with the civil service requirements prescribed by
Executive Order No. 1776, of May 7, 1913, be placed in the classified
service.
This order is issued upon the recommendation of the Postmaster
General.
WOODROW WILSON
THE WHITE HOUSE,
22 May', 1916.
