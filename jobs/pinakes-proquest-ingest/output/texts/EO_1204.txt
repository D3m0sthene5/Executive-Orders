Executive Order.
Henry Suberkrup may be appointed to the position of guard to act as
musical instructor to the prisoners at the United States Penitentiary,
Leavenworth, Kans., without examination under the civil service rules, in
view of the Attorney General's report of the desirability of undertaking
the musical instruction of prisoners at that institution and of Mr.
Suberkrup's availability and fitness for the position, the Civil Service
Commission concurring.
WM H TAFT
THlE WHITE HOUSE,
May 12, 1910.
