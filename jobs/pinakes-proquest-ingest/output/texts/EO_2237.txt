Erecutive Othrer.
It is hereby ordered that all veterans of the Civil War in the service
of the Government of the United States who desire to attend the Grand
Army Encampment to be held in Washington, D. C., September 27th to
October 2nd, 1915, shall be granted leave of absence with pay, in addition
to the annual leave provided for by statute, from September 26th to Octo-
ber 3rd, inclusive, that they may have the opportunity to attend the En-
campment, and that they be granted as many more days additional leave
with pay in each case as are necessary for the journey to Washington and
return to their posts of duty.
WOODROW WILSON
THE WHITE HOUSE,
17 Aug., 1915.
[No. 2237.]
