Under authority contained" in the act of Congress approved June 25, 1910
(36 Stat., 847),, as amended by the act of August 24, 1912 (37 Stat., 497), and
subject to the conditions, provisions and limitations of said acts, it is hereby
ordered that the public lands in T. 7 N., R. 30 E., G. and S. IR. M., Arizona, be
and the same are hereby withdrawn from settlement, location, sale, entry and all
forms of appropriation, pending a resurvey of said township; this withdrawal to
remain in full force and effect from the date hereof until the resurvey is duly ap-
proved and accepted and the approved plat thereof is officially filed in the proper
U. S. local-land office, unless another date be fixed by the Commissioner of the
General Land Office, whereupon this withdrawal will become of no effect.
