James W. Grady and Andrew E. Carey may be reinstated as Night Inspec-
tors in the Customs Service at New Orleans, Louisiana, without regard to the
length of time they have been separated from the service. Messrs. Grady and
Carey were removed from the service on July 31, 1904, the reason being given
that a reduction of force was necessary and that they were the least efficient
officers in their grade. The matter of their removal was investigated by repre-
sentatives of the Treasury Department and the Civil Service Commission, who
reported it to be their belief that these officers had been unfairly dealt with in
removing them from the service. This order is issued on the recommendation
of the Secretary of the Treasury.
