Section 3 of the Regulations Governing Appointments to the Navy
Yard Service is hereby waived so as to permit of the appointment of Pedro
Abramn to the position of general helper at $2.32 per diem at the Navy
Yard, Mare Island, Cal.
This order is issued upon the recommendation of the Secretary of the
Navy and is based upon the fact that Abram was injured in the line of duty
as a fireman on board the U. S. S. PROMETHEUS and is unable to per-
form the duties of a fireman.
WOODROW WILSON
THE WHITE HousE,
