Executive Orber.
Any officer who is separated from the diplomatic or consular service
for the purpose of accepting an appointment as Commercial Attache under
the Department of 'Commerce, shall upon his retirement from the position
of Commercial Attache, with a satisfactory record for conduct and effi-
ciency, be reinstated without examination in the branch of the service
from which he was separated.
