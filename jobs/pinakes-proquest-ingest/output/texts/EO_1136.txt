Executive Orbet.
It is hereby ordered that sections 19, 30 and 3[, township 14 south,
range 15 east, Gila and Salt River Meridian, Arizona, be, and the same are
hereby, reserved for military purposes, as a Government Rifle Range, for
use of the Organized Militia of the Territory of Arizona.
WM H TAFT
THE WHITE HOUSE,
November 13, 190.
