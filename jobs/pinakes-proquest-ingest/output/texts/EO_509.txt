Executive Orber.
Prizes for excellence in gunnery exercise and target practice, both
afloat and ashore, in all competitions occurring subsequently to June 30,
1906, shall be awarded and paid to enlisted men of the Marine Corps in
like manner, in the same amounts, and under the same conditions as to
enlisted men of the Navy.
THEODORE ROOSEVELT
THE WHITE HOUSE,
October r, 1906.
