Executive Order.
The following-named persons may be permanently appointed in the com-
petitive classified service of the Treasury Department without reference to the
civil service rules :
Miss Margaret R. Blackford,
Miss Edyth M. Branson,
Miss Tillie Burchell,
Miss Catherine Easton,
Mrs. Mary E. Richey,
Miss Elva A. Leonard,
Miss Irma E. Dudley,
Miss S. Katherine Rudasill,
Miss Jessie H. Libhart,
Miss Jennie B. Clark,
This order is issued upon the recommendation of the Secretary of the
Treasury, who states that the persons named have been temporarily employed
in the Treasury Department in the absence of civil service registers as expert
calculating machine operators, and that they have rendered very efficient
and faithful service, and that their permanent appointment will be greatly in
the interests of the service.
WOODROW WILSON
T HE WHITE HOUSE,
.7 April, 1917.
