Executive Order
The present vacancy in the position of Editor in the Bureau of Edu-
cation may be filled by appointment without regard to the requirement of
examination.
This action is taken on the recommendation of the Secretary of the
Interior and the Civil Service Commission in view of the failure of two
Civil Service examinations to secure eligibles with the required qualifica-
tions of a broad general education, familiarity with the educational literature
of the United States and foreign countries, acquaintance with educational
leaders, ability to do good report writing and to address educational gath-
erings, and good judgment in the examination of manuscripts submitted
with a viewv to publication.
WM H TAFT
THE WHITE HOUSE,
July is/, 1910.
