Executive Orer
The Executive Order of October 9, 1903, transferring to the 'War
Department for use as a military reservation the tracts of public land in
the State of Wyoming known as the "Crow Creek. Forest Reserve",
originally declared as a forest reserve by Proclamation dated October to,
1900o, with the understanding that the use'of the lands for the purposes of
a military reservation shall not interfere with the object for which the
forest reserve was established, is hereby amended so as to exclude from
reservation for military purposes the southwest quarter of section six,
township fourteen north, range seventy-one west, and the south half of
the northeast quarter and the north half of the southeast quarter of section
seventeen, township fifteen north, range seventy-one west ; sixth principal
meridian.
WM H TAFT
THE WHITE HOUSE,
1ahy 28, 1909.
