1, Woodrow Wilson, President of the United States, under the authority
conferred by an Act authorizing the President to coordinate or consolidate
executive bureaus, agencies, and offices; and for other purposes in the interest
of economy and the more efficient concentration of the Government, approved
May loth, 1918, do hereby order and direct, that whenever, in his judgment,
it becomes expedient, the Chairman or the Vice-Chairman of the War Trade
Board at his discretion, shall relinquish and transfer to the Department ox State
degrees, all or any integral part of the personnel, duties, powers, and func-
tions of the War Trade Board and its records and public property in the District
of Columbia and elsewhere in the United States.
WOODROW WILSON
