Executive Orbet
In accordance with the provisions of Section 2252 of the Revised Statutes of
the United States, and by virtue of the authority therein given, it is hereby
ordered that the Vernal land district in the State of Utah, with office at Vernal,
be discontinued and the lands, business and archives of said land district and office
transferred to and consolidated with the Salt Lake City land district, with office
at Salt Lake City, Utah, at the close of business February 28, 1925.
CALVIN COOLIDGE
THE WHITE HOUSE,
February 6, 1995.
