Under authority delegated to me under Section 41. of the Act of March 2, 1917,
I hereby designate Honorable Adolf G. Wolf as Acting Judge of the District Court
,of the United States for Porto Rico, from July 19, 1924, until such time as nthe
Honorable Arthur F. Odlin, Judge of the District Court of the United States for
Porto Rico, returns to duty.
During the incumbency of Judge Wolf, and in accordance with said Section 41,
of the Act of 1917, he is fully authorized and empowered to perform the duties of
said office during such absence of Judge Odlin, and to sign all necessary papers and
records as the Acting Judge of said Court without extra compensation.
CALVIN COOL IDGE
THE WHITE HOUSE,
July 8, 1921j.
