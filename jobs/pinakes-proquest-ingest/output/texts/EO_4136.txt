Executive (Order
AUTHORIZING THE ALIEN PROPERTY CUSTODIAN
TO SELL CERTAIN PROPERTY AT PRIVATE SALE
By virtue of the authority vested in me by an Act of Congress entitled "An
act to define, regulate, and punish trading with the enemy and for other purposes ",
approved October 6, 1917, known as the "Trading with the Enemy Act" and the
amendment to such act embodied in "An act making appropriations to supply
urgent deficiencies in the appropriations for the fiscal year ending June 30, 1918,
and prior fiscal years on account of war expenses and for other purposes", approved
March 28, 1918, and the amendment to such act embodied in "An act making
appropriations to supply deficiencies in appropriations for the fiscal year ending
June 30, 1919, and prior fiscal years -n account of war expenses, and for other
purposes ", approved November 4, 1918, to make certain orders such as that herein
contained, I hereby, in the public interest, make the following determination,
order, rule and regulation:
The Alien Property Custodian is hereby authorized to sell at private sale, at
such time and place as to him or to his authorized agent may seem advisable, with-
out public or other advertisement, for and in consideration of the sum of SEVENTY-
FIVE HUNDRED ($7,500) DOLLARS, certain real estate now being held and adminis-
tered by him as such Alien Property Custodian, and heretofore, upon requirement,
conveyed, transferred, assigned and delivered to him as the property of BARTEL
BUCK, CATHERINE BUCK, SOPHIE BUCK; META BUCK, HERMAN BUCK, HENRY
PRINGLE, HERMAN PRINGLE, LENA PlUNOLE, FRANZ BUCK, JOHAN BUCK and
GEORGE BUCK, residing in Germany, whom after investigation, he did determine to
be enemies within the purview of said Act as amended, which said real estate is
situate in the Borough of Brooklyn, County of Kings, City and State of New York,
bounded and described as follows:
Beginning at a point on the southerly side of 56th Street distant 260 feet
easterly from the corner formed by the intersection of the southerly side
of 56th Street, with the easterly side of Third Avenue; running thence
southerly, parallel with Third Avenue and part of the distance through
a party wall, one hundred feet two inches, thence easterly, parallel with
56th Street, twenty feet; thence northerly parallel with Third Avenue
and part of the distance through a party wall, one hundred feet two
inches to the southerly side of 56th Street, and thence westerly along the
southerly side of 56th Street, twenty feet to the point of beginning. And
being known on the tax maps as Section 3, Block 838, Lot 19, Borough
of Brooklyn, City and State of New York.
My reasons for the foregoing determination, order, rule and regulation are:
(a) An advantageous offer for the purchase of the said real estate has been
received by the Alien Property Custodian;
(b) An attempted sale at public auction would be a useless formality and would
involve unnecessary expenses, delay and inconvenience, which should be avoided.
CALVIN COOLIDGE
THE WIT~E HousE,
January 27, 1925.
