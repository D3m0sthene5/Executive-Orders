Executive Orber
Exception of Persons Designated as "Special Employees" In Treasury Department,
Internal Revenue Service.
Schedule A, Subdivision III, paragraph 14, of the civil service rules,
making exceptions from the requirement of examination in the Treasury
Department, is amended to read as follows:
14. Informers and posse men, and special employees employed tempo-
rarily for detective work in the Internal Revenue Service, under the appro-
priation for detecting and bringing to trial and punishment persons
violating the Internal Revenue laws.
This amendment is made for the reason that the appropriation from
which special employees are paid, " Punishment for violation of internal
revenue laws", contemplates the employment of special employees for
purely detective work of a temporary character ; that the personal services
to he rendered are of a temporary, confidential and secret character, and
the classification of "special employees" by Executive order of August
24, 1912, has had the effect of defeating the object of the appropriation in
that another class of permanent officers, generally known to the public,
has been created wvho are performing duties similar to those of deputy col-
lectors of internal revenue, but paid from the aforesaid appropriation, with
the result that the payment of salaries of these permanent officers exhausts
said appropriation to such an extent that the Commissioner of Internal
Revenue is unable to employ the necessary detective force to prevent
frauds on the revenue. All such employments shall be reported to the
Civil Service Commission.
This amendment is in accordance with the views of the Secretary of
the Treasury, the Commissioner of Internal Revenue and the Civil Service
Commission.
WOODROW WILSON
