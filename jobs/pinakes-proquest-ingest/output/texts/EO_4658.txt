John T. Marchand, senior examiner, Interstate Commerce Commission, may
be reinstated to his former competitive clsifie status without regard to the time
limit on reinstatement under thCi sevc rules. &
Mr. Marchand resigned from the classified position of clerk Dui the Interstate
Commerce Commission on March 1, 1907, after more than 17 years' service. He
was reappointed November 1, 1910, to theposition of attorney without civil service
status, the rules at that time not permitting reinstatement for a longer period than
one year from date of separation. His services since 1910 have been continuous.
Examiners in the Interstate Commerce Commission have been appointed in accord-
ance with civil service rules since 1916. Mr. Marchand has passed the examination
for that position but has not been reached for certification for appointment.
This order is issued in recognition of Mr. Marchand's good record of more than
thirty three years, and is recommended by the Civil Service Commission.
