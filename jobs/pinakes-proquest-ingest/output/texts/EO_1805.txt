Pursuant to the authority contained in Section 179 of the Revised
Statutes, I hereby authorize and direct William J. Harris, Director of
the Census, to perform the duties of the Secretary of Commerce during
the absence of the Secretary, the Assistant Secretary, and the Solicitor
of the Department of Commerce.
WOODROW WILSON
THE WHITE HOUSE,
July 26, 1913.
