AUTHORIZING THE ALIEN PROPERTY CUSTODIAN
TO SELL CERTAIN PROPERTY AT PRIVATE SALE
By virtue of the authority vested in me by an \Lc of Congress entitled "An
act to define, regulate, and punish trading with the enemy and for other purposes",
approved October 6, :917, known as the " Trading with the Enemy Act", and the
amendment to such act embodied in "An act making appropriations to supply
urgent deficiencies in the appropriations for the fiscal year ending June 30, 1918,
and prior fiscal years on account of war expenses and for other purposes", approved
March 28, 1918, and the amendment to such act embodied in "An act making
appropriations to supply deficiencies in appropriations for the fiscal year ending
June 30, 1919, and prior fiscal years on account of war expenses, and for other
purposes", approved November 4, 1918, to make certain orders such as that herein
contained, I hereby, in the public interest, make the following determination,
order, rule and regulation:
The Alie n Property ustodian is hereby authorized to sell at private sale, at
such time and place as to him or to his authorized agent may seem advisable,
without public or other advertisement, for and in consideratin of the sum of
SEVENTY-SEVEN HUNDRED FIFTrY ($7,750.00) DOLLARS, certain real estate now
being held and administered by him as such Alien Property Custodan, and here-
tofore, upon requirement, conveyed, transferred, assigned and delivered to him as
the property of MAX L. HIRSCHHORN and BLANKA LEHMAN, reading in Germany,
whom, after investigation, he did determine to be enemies within the purview of
said Act as amended, which said real estate is situate in the Borough of Manhattan,
City of New York and State of New York, and described as follows, to-wit:
ALL that certain lot, piece or parcel of land, with building
thereon erected, beginning at a point on the southerly side of
Last One hundred and tenth Street, distant one hundred (100)
feet westerly along thi' same from the corner formed by the
intersection of the soy ,I erly side of One hundred and tenth

erly, parallel with said westerly side of First Avenue, and part

My reasons for the foregoing determination, order, -die and regulation are:
(a) An advantageous offer for the sale of said real :state has been received
by the Alien Property Custodian, as shown by the appraisals of the property sold;
(b) An attempted sale at public auction would be a useless formality and
would involve unnecessary expenses, delay and inconvenience;
(c) It appears that it is to the interest of the trust held, in that its value will
be conser'ed by this sale by the substitution of the proceeds in lieu of the property
sold, because of the difficulty of the administration of such real estate and its
consequent deterioration;
(d) The character of this property is such that there are greater possibilities
of a decrease in its value than an increase, and that such sale made at this time
would, therefore, be to the best advantage of the trust;
(e) The fact that Municipal violations of approximately Three Thousand
($3,000.00) Dollars have accrued against this property makes it advisable to sell
at the present time.
