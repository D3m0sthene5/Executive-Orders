1AYETTF NATIONAL FOREST
IDAHO
It is hereby ordered that on and after July r, 1908, the land within the
boundary shown on the attached diagram, heretofore set apart, reserved,
and proclaimed as parts of the Payette, Weiser, and Sawtooth National
Forests, shall be known as the Payette National Forest. The remaining
portion of the present Payette National Forest shall continue reserved and
constitute the Idaho National Forest in Idaho. It is not intended by this
order to release any land from reservation or to reserve any land not here-
tofore embraced in a National Forest.
THEODORE ROOSEVELT
THE \WHITHOUSE.
