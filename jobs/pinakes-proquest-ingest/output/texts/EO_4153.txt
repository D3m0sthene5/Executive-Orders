AUTHORIZING THE ALIEN PROPERTY CUSTODIAN
TO SELL CERTAIN PROPERTY AT PRIVATE SALE
By virtue of the authority vested in me by an Act of Congress entitled "An
act to define, regulate, and punish trading with the enemy and for other purposes"
approved October 6, 1917, known as the "Trading with the Enemy Act", and the
amendment to such act embodied in "An act making appropriations to supply
urgent deficiencies in the appropriations for the fiscal year ending June 30, 1918,
and prior fiscal -years on account of war expenses and for other purposes", approved
March 28, 1918, and the amendment to such act embodied in "An act making
appropriations to supply deficiencies in appropriations for the fiscal year ending
June 30, 1919, and prior fiscal years on account of war expenses, and for other
purposes", approved November 4, 1918, to make certain orders such as that herein
contained, I hereby, in the public interest, make the following determination,
order, rule and regulation:
The Alien Property Custodian is hereby authorized to sell at private sale, at
such time and place as to him or to his authorized agent may seem advisable, with-
out public or other advertisement, for and in consideration of the sum of TWENTY-
SIX HUNDRED ($2600) DOLLARS, certain real estate now being held and admin-
istered by him as such Alien Property Custodian, and heretofore, upon requirement,
conveyed, transferred, assigned and delivered to him as the property of MAX L.
HIaSCHHORN and BLANKA LEHMAN, residing in Germany, whom, after investigation,
he did determine to be enemies within the purview of said Act as amended, whih
said real estate is situate in the County of Queens, State of New York, and
described as follows:
ALL those two certain lots or parcels of land, situate, lying and being in
the County of Queens, which are known and distinguished as Lots numbered
one hundred twenty-five (125) and one hundred twenty-seven (127) on Map
of the property of the Jamaica Homestead Association, in the Town of
Jamaica, Long Island, filed the 13th day of February, 1872, in the Queens
County Clerk's office, as ?v No. 423.
My reasons for the forego determination, order, rule and regulation are:
(a) An advantageous offer for the sale of said real estate has been received by
the Alien Property Custodian, as shown by the appraisals of the property sold;
(b) An attempted sale at public auction would be a useless formality and
would involve unnecessary expenses, delay and inconvenience as the sum received
by this sale in excess of all appraisals is in excess of the value as indicated by
appraisals;
(c) It appears that it is to the interest of the trust held, in that its value will
be conserved by this sale by the substitution of the proceeds in lieu of the property
sold, because of the difficulty of the administration of such real estate and its con-
sequent deterioration;
(d) The character of this property is such that there are greater possibilities
of a decrease in its value than an increase, and that such sale made at this time
would, therefore, be to the best advantage of the trust.
CALVIN COOLIDGE
THE WHITE HOUSE,
February 7, 1925.
