ORDER OF WITHDRAWAL.
Military Reservation, Coos Bay, Oregon.
It is hereby ordered that the following tract of land upon the south
side of the entrance to Coos Bay, Oregon, described as Lot 4, Section 3,
Township 26 South, Range 14 West, Willamette Meridian, Oregon, con-
taining 32.06 acres, he, and the same is hereby, withdrawn from settle-
ment, location, sale, entry, or other disposition, and set apart for military
purposes.
