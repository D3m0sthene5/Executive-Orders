Charles J. Brown may be reinstated as a clerk in the classified service
of the Treasury Department without observance of the rules regarding
reinstatement after the expiration of one year from date of separation
from the service.
This order is recommended by the Secretary of the Treasury in view
of Mr. Brown's former efficient service in that Department.
WOODROW WILSON
THE WHITE HOUSE,
30 October, 1913.
[No. 1853.]
