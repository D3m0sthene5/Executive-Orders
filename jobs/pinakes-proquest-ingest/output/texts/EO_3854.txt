Executive Order
Mr. Daniel R. Edwards may be appointed a contact representative in the
United States Veterans Bureau without examination under the civil service
rules. Mr. Edwards is a permanently disabled beneficiary of the Veterans
Bureau through the loss of an arm and a leg. He has been decorated with
the Distinguished Service Cross and twice with the Congressional Medal of
Honor.
The Director of the Veterans Bureau recommended Mr. Edwards' appoint-
ment, as he has been on trial and has given evidence that he can render good
service. The Director regards the case as exceptional, in that the appointment
would have a favorable psychological effect upon disabled ex-service men
because of Mr. Edwards' own disability and his clear conception of vocational
work, gained through his own training; and because of the association he has
had with welfare ex-service men's organizations.
The Civil Service Commission concurred in this recommendation.
WARREN G HARDING
THE WHITE HOUSE,
MUaY 22, 1923.
