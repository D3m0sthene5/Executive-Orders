Mr. Samuel E. Fouts may be reinstated as an assistant examiner in
the Patent Office, Department of the Interior, without reference to the
period of his separation from the service.
Mr, Fouts wvas appointed an assistant examiner through examination
on April 6, 1894, and resigned on September I i, 1899. Since that date
he has been actively engaged in the practice of patent law. He attained
a high rank as an examiner of ability and efficiency in the Patent Office,
ania his experience since his separation has given him unusual qualifica-
tions for resuming his Patent Office work. The Commissioner of Patents
earnestly desires the reinstatement of Mr. Fouts, and the Secretary of the
Interior believes his reinstatement would be of distinct benefit to the
service. The supply of eligibles for the position of patent examiner is
barely sufficient to meet the needs of the service from year to year.
The Civil Service Commission joins in recommending the order.
