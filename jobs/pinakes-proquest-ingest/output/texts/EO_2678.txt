Executive Orber,
ALASKA RAILROAD NO. 9.
Upon the reconinidation of the Secretary of the Interior, and by virtue of
authority contained in section 2 of "An Act Limiting the hours of daily service
of laborers aid mechaniics employed upon work done for the United States, or
arty Territory, or for the District of Columbia, and for other purposes " (37 Stat.,
13 7), approved June 19, 1912, and of the act of Congress approved March 4,
1917 (39 Stat., I192), I hereby direct that subject to the provisions of the act
of Congress last named, relative to computation of wages and of overtime rates
to be paid for at not less than time and one-half for all hours of work in excess
of eight hours, that the provisions and stipulations of the act of June 19, 1912,
supra, may be waived so far as they relate to purchase orders placed with the
Seattle Car and Foundry Company, by the Alaskan Engineering Commission,
as follows : Purchase order No. D-60 37, dated March 26th, 1917, calling for six,
34-foot, steel underframe refrigerator cars, in accordance with specifications and
descriptions contained under Circular No. 249; Purchase order No. 61 40, dated
April 3rd, 1917, for four eight wheel caboose cars, Class No. 26, in accordance
with specifications and descriptions contained under Circular No. 244; and
Purchase order No. 6139, dated April 3rd, 1917, for one eight wheel caboose
car, Class No. 26, in accordance with specifications and descriptions contained
under Circular No. 244; all as bid upon by the contractor.
WOODROW WILSON
THE WHITE HOUSE,
3 August, 1917.
[No. 2678.]
