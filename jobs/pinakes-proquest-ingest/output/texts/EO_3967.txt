WYOMING
Under authority contained in the act of Congress approved June 25, 1910
(36 Stat., 847), as amended by the act of August 24, 1912 (37 Stat., 497), and
subject to the conditions, provisions and limitations of said acts, it is hereby
ordered that the public lands in T. 51 N., R. 8o XW., 6th P. M., Wyoming, be,
and the same are hereby withdrawn from settlement, location, sale, entry and
all forms of appropriation, pending a resurvey of said township under the
provisions of the act of Congress approved March 3, 1909 (3 Stat., 845) ; this
withdrawal to remain in full force and effect from the date hereof until the
resurvey is duly approved and accepted and the approved plat thereof is officially
filed in the proper U. S. local land office, unless another date be fixed by the
Commissioner of the General Land Office, whereupon this order will become of
no effect.
CALVIN COOLIDGE
THE WHITE HOUSE,
March 5, 1924.
