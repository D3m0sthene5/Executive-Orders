It is hereby ordered that the following reservoir sites, namely: Salt q
River, Arizona; East Park, California; Deer Flat and Minidoka, Idaho; 4
Willow Creek, Montana; Carlsbad and Rio Grande, New Mexico; Cold ;
Springs, Oregon; Belle Fourche, South Dakota; Strawberry Valley, Utah; i)
Keechelus Lake, Kachess Lake, Clealum Lake, Bumping Lake and Con- f
conully, Washington, and Shoshone and Pathfinder, Wyoming, together 4
with the smallest legal subdivisions of land adjoining the flow lines thereof a
- “as segregated by the broken lines upon the diagrams hereto attached and |
made a part of this order, are hereby reserved, subject to Reclamation Serv- |
ice uses under the provisions of the act approved June 17, 1902 (32 Stat., |
388), and to any other valid existing rights, and are set apart for the use of q
the Department of Agriculture as preserves and breeding grounds for native 4
birds. It is unlawful for any person to hunt, trap, capture, wilfully dis- i
turb or kill any bird of any kind whatever or take the eggs of such birds q
within the limits of these reservations, except under such rules and regula- |
tions as may be prescribed by the Secretary of Agriculture. Warning is 4
expressly given to all persons not to commit any of the acts herein enumer- q
ated and which are prohibited by law. |
These reservations to be known by the names designating the reserved q
tracts upon the diagrams attached. q
