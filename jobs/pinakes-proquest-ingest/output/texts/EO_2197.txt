Mr. Bernard R. Jett may be transferred in the Office of the Secre-
tary of the Interior from wireman on the per diem roll to electrician at
$1000 per annum.
Mr. Jett is employed on the "Repair of Buildings" roll in a position
for many years not regarded by the Department as classified. He was
one of a small number whose retention and reemployment without a com-
petitive status were authorized by an executive order of June 17, 1909.
He has been emnnloyed intermittently since September 9, 192 has passed
the examination for electrician, and by reason of extensive and varied
electrical work has become familiar with the complicated systems of light-
ing, being one of the fewv persons wvho wvill be able to assist materially in
the preparation of long deferred electrical plans for the several buildings.
The action proposed will also save several hundred dollars annually by
permitting the continuous employment of skilled mechanics who must
otherwise be laid off when their services cannot wvell be spared.
In view of these facts the Commission concurs with the Department
in recommending the transfer of Mr. Jett by executive order.
WOODROW WILSON
THE WHITE HOUSE,
14 May, 1915.
