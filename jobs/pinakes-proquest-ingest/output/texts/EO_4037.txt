In consideration of the faithful, meritorious, and valuable services of Boat-
swain Arthur Dietrich, who was killed in line of duty at the U. S. Naval Air
Station, Anacostia, D. C., on March 3, 1924, after serving in the Navy continuously
since October 6, 1909, his widow, Mrs. Bess Vivian Dietrich, may be employed
as messenger under the Navy Department without regard to the Civil Service
Act or rules. This action is recommended by the Secretary of the Navy.
The Civil Service Commission does not concur in this recommendation.
Issued for charity.
