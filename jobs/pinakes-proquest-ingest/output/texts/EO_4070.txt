Executive Other.
ORDER OF TEMPORARY WITHDRAWAL
Under and pursuant to the provisions of the act of Congress approved June
25, 1910 (36 Stat. 847), as amended by the act of August 24, 1912 (37 Stat. 497),
and subject to the conditions therein expressed, it is ordered that the land here-
inafter described be, and the same is hereby, temporarily withdrawn from settle-
ment, location, sale, entry and other forms of disposal preliminary to proposed
legislation to protect the domestic water supply of the towns of Bountiful and
Centerville, Utah.
