Miss Anne M. Roden may he permanently appointed in the classified
service of the Treasury Department without compliance with the civil
service requirements.
This order is issued upon the recommendation of the Secretary of
the Treasury, who states that Miss Roden is temporarily employed, with
the concurrence of the Civil Service Commission, in the office of the
Auditor for the War Department, where her services have proven most
valuable as a calculating machine operator, and it is believed that her
permanent appointment will be greatly in the interests of the service.
WOODROW WILSON
THE WHITE HOUSE,
28 Dec., 1916.
