It is hereby ordered, under authority contained in the Act of March 2, 1917
(39 Stat. L., 969-976), that the period of trust on lands held for the use and
benefit of the Cabazon and Twenty-nine Palms Bands of Mission Indians in
California, which trust expires during the calendar year 1920, be, and is hereby,
extended for a period of five years from the date of expiration thereof.
WOODROW WILSON
THE WHITE HOUSE,
7 July, 1920,
[No. 3302.]
