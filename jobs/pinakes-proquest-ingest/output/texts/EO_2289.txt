Mrs. Virginia Campbell may be appointed to a clerical position in
the competitive classified service without reference to the civil service
rules.
Mrs. Campbell, who has served under temporary appointments in the
State Department and the Census Bureau of the Department of Com-
merce, has shown her qualifications for a clerkship in the classified service.
WOODROW WILSON
THE WHITE HOUSE,
18 December, 1915.
