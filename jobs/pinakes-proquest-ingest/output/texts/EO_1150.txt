Executive Orber
It is hereby ordered that those persons now on the rolls of the Immi-
gration Commission, who are named in the list submitted by the Director
Gf the Census, may be temporarily employed in the Bureau of the Census,
Department of Commerce and Labor, in positions authorized by the Act
of March 6, 1902, upon passing such examination as may be prescribed by
the Director of the Census. These employments shall in no instance
extend beyond June 30, 1 9 12, and the persons so employed shahl thereby
acquire no status for transfer or appointment in the classified civil service.
The entrance salary in each case shall be at the rate of fifty dollars a month.
This order is based upon the recommendation of the Director of the
Census, approved by the Secretary of Commerce and Labor, and is made
in order to give the Census Bureau the benefit of the experience in gov-
ernment work which the employees concerned have acquired during their
service with the Immigration Commission.
WM H TAFT
THE WHITE HOUSE,
December 29, 1909.
[No. 1150.]
