Executive Order
By virtue of the authority vested in me by the act of Congress approved
August 1, 1914, making appropriations for the sundry civil expenses of the Govern-
ment for the fiscal year ended June 30. 1915, Longview, Washington, is hereby created
a Lof entry in Customs Collection District #29 (Oregon), with headquarters at
Portland, Oregon, effective thirty days from the date of this Order.
CALVIN COOLIDGE
THE WHITE HOUSE.
August 31, 1928.
