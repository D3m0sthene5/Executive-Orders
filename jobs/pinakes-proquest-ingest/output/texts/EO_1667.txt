)Executive order.
Mr. Thomas E. Price may be promoted from the position of mes-
senger to that of clerk in the Post Office Department without examination.
The Postmaster General states that for more than ten years Mr. Price
has been engaged in opening letters in the Division of Dead Letters and
the incessant use of his right hand has resulted in partial paralysis. He
recommends this order, believing that a change in duties will prevent
permanent injury. The Civil Service Commission does not concur in the
recommendation.
WM H TAFT
THE WHITE HOUSE,
December 19, 1912.
