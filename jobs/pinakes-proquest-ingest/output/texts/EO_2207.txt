Executive orter.
It is hereby ordered, under the provisions of Section 3 of the Act of
Congress approved January 28, 1915, "To create the Coast guard by
combining therein the existing Life-Saving Service and Revenue-Cutter
Service," that when a commissioned officer, warrant officer or enlisted man
of the Coast Guard who has served thirty years makes suitable application
for retirement, the Secretary of the Treasury may, "by direction of the
President," retire such person from active service.
WOODROW WILSON
THE WHITE HOUSE,
8 June, 1915.
