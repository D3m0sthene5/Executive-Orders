Executive Order
In accordance with the provisions of Section 2253 of the Revised
Statutes of the United States, and by virtue of the authority therein given,
it is hereby ordered that
All of the Standing Rock and Cheyenne River Indian Reservations
in the State of South Dakota, with the exception of townships nineteen to
twventy-three north, inclusive, ranges seventeen to twenty-three east, inclu-
sive, of the Black Hills Base Line and Meridian, be attached to and made
a part of the Aberdeen land district, and that all business pertaining to and
connected with the entry and disposal of the public lands within said reser-
vations, excepting the public lands within the townships above indicated,
be transacted b~y the Register and Receiver of the land office at Aberdeen,
South Dakota.
