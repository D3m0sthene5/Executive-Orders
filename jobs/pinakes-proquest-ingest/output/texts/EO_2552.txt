Executive Other.
It is hereby ordered that Spike Island, Orca Inlet, situate near Cordova,
Alaska, in latitude 600 32' 53" N., longitude 105° 43' 7" W., and included
in United States nonmineral survey 830, containing 8.49 acres, together with
the tide lands in front of and abutting thereon, be, and the same is hereby
reserved for lighthouse purposes.
WOODROW WILSON
THE WHITE HOUSE,
2 1 March, 1Q 17.
[No. 2552.]
