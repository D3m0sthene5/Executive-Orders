No officer or employee of the Government shall, directly or indi-
rectly, instruct or he concerned in any manner in the instruction of any
person or classes of persons, with a view to their special preparation for
the examinations of the Boards of Examiners for the diplomatic and
consular services.
The fact that any officer or employee is found so engaged shall be
considered sufficient cause for his removal from the service.
