Mr. Ralph Izard, who wvas dismissed from the position of Chinese
Inspector in the Immigration Service February 3, 1903, may be rein-
stated to the position of immigrant inspector in the Immigration Service,
Department of Labor, without regard to the length of his separation from
the service.
This order is issued up)on the recommendation of the Secretary of
Labor, who states that he made a thorough investigation of the case and
arrived at the conclusion that Mr. Izard was improperly dismissed from
the service.
The Civil Service Commission does not concur in recommending the
issuance of this order.
WOODROW WILSON
THE WHITE HOUSE,
4e February, 1915.
