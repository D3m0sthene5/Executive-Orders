John G. Curran, long employed in the Public Building at Cleveland,
Ohio, as Assistant Custodian-Janitor, may he reappointed in the Assistant
Custodian and Janitor Service under the designation of Assistant Cus-
todian, United States Post-Office, Custom-House and Court-House build-
ing at Cleveland, Ohio, notwithstanding he has been more than one year
separated from the service because of the abandonment of the old building
This order is issued because of Mr. Curran's long and satisfactory
service in a similar position, and because in a short time the new public
building at Cleveland, Ohio, will be ready for occupancy, and the Depart-
ment desires to avail itself of Mr. Curran's experience and known fitness
as Assistant Custodian of that building.
