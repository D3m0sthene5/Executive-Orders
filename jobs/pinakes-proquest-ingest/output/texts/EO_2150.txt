]Executive Orber.
In accordance with the provisions of Section 2253 of the Revised
Statutes of the United States, and by virtue of the authority therein given,
it is hereby ordered that:
Townships nineteen, twenty and twventy-one North, of Range twventy-
three East of the Black Hills Base Line and Meridian in the State of
South Dakota, be transferred from the Lemmon land district to, and be
made a part of the Timber Lake land district.
WOODROW WILSON
THE WHITE HOUSE,
r8 March, 1915.
[No. 2150.]
