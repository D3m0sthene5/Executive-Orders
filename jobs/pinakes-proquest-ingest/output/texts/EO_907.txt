CALIFORNIA NATIONAL FOREST
CALIFORNIA
It is hereby ordered that on and after July i, i908, the land within the
boundary shown on the attached diagram, heretofore set apart, reserved,
and proclaimed as parts of the Stony Creeks and Trinity National Forests,
shall be known as the California National Forest. The remaining portion
of the Stony Creek National Forest shall continue reserved and constitute
a part of the Trinity National Forest. It is not intended by this order to)
release any land from reservation or to reserve any land1 not heretofore
embraced in a National Forest.
