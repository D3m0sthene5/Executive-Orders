Mr. Norris R. Welsh, a skilled laborer in the Government Printing
Office, at $780 per annum, may be transferred to a position in the Division
of Publications, Department of Agriculture, at a salary of $720 per annum-
a reduction of $6o.
This transfer has been requested by the Secretary of Agriculture for
the reason that Mr-. Welsh has a thorough knowledge of the publications
of the Agricultural Department, and occupies a position in the Government
Printing Office which peculiarly fits him for the position to which it is
proposed to assign him, and that the transfer is in the interest of good
administration; and for the further reason that the position cannot be
adequately filled by transfer or promotion within the Department.
The Civil Service Commission does not approve of the transfer of
Mr. Welsh, on the ground that the transfer is an appointment within the
meaning of the Civil Service Act, and that a transfer from a nonappor-
tioned to an apportioned service involves a charge to a district which has
already received an excessive share of apportioned appointments; and that
the evidence submitted does not establish the fact that a man selected from
the eligible list would not be just as satisfactory.
