Mr. Arthur S. Kellom, formerly a letter carrier at the Chico, California,
post office, may be reinstated in the postal service without regard to the
length of time he has been separated from the service.
Thc reinstatement of Mr. Kellonm is recommended by the Postmaster
General, who states that a careful review of the papers in this case discloses
that the charges on which the carrier wvas removed were not wvholly true
anu that the action taken by the Department was based upon statements
which for the most part are not borne out by facts recently developed and
were the result of prejudice on the part of the former postmaster and,
possibly, the post office inspector who conducted the case, and it is believed
that an injustice wvas done and that the offenses charged against Carrier
Kellom, even if true, were not such as to justify so severe a punishment as
that inflicted.
WOODROW WILSON
THE WHITE HOUSE,
21 April, 1915.
