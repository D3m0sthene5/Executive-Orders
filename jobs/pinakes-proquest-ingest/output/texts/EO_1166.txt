In accordance with the provisions of Section 22513 of the Revised
Statutes of the United States, and by virtue of the authority therein given,
it is hereby ordered that:
All that part of the Standing Rock - Indian Reservation west of the
range line between ranges eighty-four and eighty-five west of fifth principal
meridian, now included in the Bismarck land district in the State of North
Dakota, be detached from said land district and attached to and made a
part of the Lemmon land district, and the business and archives pertaining
to the lands above described be transferred from the land office at Bis-
marck. North Dakota, to the land office at Lemmon, South Dakota.
WM H TAFT
THE WHITE HOUSE,
February 8 1910.
