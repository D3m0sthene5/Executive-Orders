Mrs. Emma S. Rea may be appointed to a clerkship in the Treasury Depart-
ment without compliance with the requirements of the civil service rules.
This order is recommended by the Secretary of the Treasury, who states that
Mrs. Rea is the widow of John L. Rea, a Spanish War veteran and World War
veteran, who died November 2, 1927, after a service in the Interior and Treasury
Departments of more than thirty years. This order is issued in recognition of Mr.
Rea's record in the military and civil service of the United States. The Civil
Service Commission is unable to concur.
