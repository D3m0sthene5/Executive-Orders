]Executive order.
Twenty-five positions of clerk at $900 in the Office of the Auditor for
the Post Office Department may, as vacancies occur therein, be filled and
refilled by promotion on noncompetitive clerk examination from among
the employees heretofore transferred from the Bureau of Engraving and
Printing who have had seven years of service, at least two years of which
shall have been in the Office of the Auditor for the Post Office Department.
(The order, which is designed to offer to this class of employees some
opportunity for advancement as a reward and incentive to faithful work,
was recommended by the Secretary of the Treasury and has received the
approval of the Civil Serv~ice Commission.)
WM H T AFT
THE WHITE HOUSE,
April 28, 1910.
