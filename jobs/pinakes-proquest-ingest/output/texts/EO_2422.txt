Executive Other
1. The following described tract of public land at Richardson, in the
Territory of Alaska, is hereby reserved from sale or other disposition, sub-
ject to private rights, if any there be, and set apart as a military reserva-
tion for the use of the Signal Corps, United States Army, in the operation
of the military telegraph lines in Alaska, viz:
That tract of land included within metes and hounds as follows:
Beginning at Stake No.i on the north side of the Valdez-Fairbanks
trail where the east line of the Hart James survey crosses this trail; thence
North 22 East, iooo feet to Stake No. 2: thence South -o East, 1235 feet
to Stake No. 3; thence South 22 West, iooo feet to Stake No. 4 on the
north side of the Valdez-Fairbanks trail; thence following the meander-
ings of the said Valdez-Fairbanks trail to Stake No. i, the point of begin-
ning. Area, 28 acres, more or less. The bearings are true. Lat., 64° 20'
North; Long., 1460 28' West.
11. I n pursuance of the provisions of section of the Act of Congress
approved July 5, 1884 (23 Stat. 103). entitled "An Act to provide for the
disposal of abandoned and useless military reservations", the parcel of land
reserved at Richardson, Alaska, as a military reservation for the use of the
Signal Corps, United States Army, in connection with the operation of
the military telegraph lines in Alaska, by Executive Order Number 792,
dated May 4, 1908 (G. 0. No. 90, W. D., May 23, 1908), comprising
0.327 acre, more or less, is placed under the control of the Secretary of
the Interior for disposition as provided in said act, or as may be otherwise
provided lby law, the same in the opinion of the President having become
useless for military purposes.
WOODROW WILSON
THE WHITE HOUSE,
14 July, 1916.
