Pursuant to the Act of Congress approved August 1, 1914, making appro-
priations for the Sundry Civil expenses of the Government for the fiscal year
ended June 30, 1915, Empire, Oregon, is hereby abolished as a port of entry in
customs collection district o. 29 (Oregon), effective November 15, 1924.
By virtue of the same authority Marchfield, Oregon, is hereby created a port
of entry in customs collection district No. 29 (Oregon) with headquarters at Port-
land, Oregon, effective November 15, 1924.
