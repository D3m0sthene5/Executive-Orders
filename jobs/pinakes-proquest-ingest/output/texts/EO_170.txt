Executive Orber.
Paragraph 451 of the Consular Regulations of 1896 is hereby amended bythe addition of the following:
No consular officer shall accept an appointment to office from any foreignState as administrator, guardian, or in any other fiduciary capacity for thesettlement or conservation of the estates of deceased persons or of their heirsor of other persons under legal disabilities without hating been previouslyauthorized by the Secretary of State so to do.
THEODORE ROOSEVELT
Wnit Hos,
March 24, 1902.
