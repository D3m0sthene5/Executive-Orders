Executive Orber.
Mrs. Eliza Ryon may be reinstated in the classified nonapportioned
force of the Bureau of Engraving and Printing without reference to
the year limitation.
This order is recommended by the Secretary of the Treasury in
view of the fact that Mrs. Ryon. was before marriage an efficient
employee. She married an employee of the Bureau of Engraving and
Printing, who is now afflicted with tuberculosis and will not be able to
resume duty. The Civil Service Commission concurs in the recom-
mendation.
WOODROW WILSON
THE WHITE HOUSE,
24 January, 1914.
