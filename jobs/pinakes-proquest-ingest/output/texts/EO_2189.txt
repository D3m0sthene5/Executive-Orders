Executive Orbe*,.
Schedule A, Subdivision IV, paragraph 6 of the Civil Service Rules
excepting certain positions from the requirement of examination is hereby
amended to read as follows:
6. All positions on torpedo and mine planters and cable boats,
both navigating and operative, of the Quartermaster Corps of the
Army.
This amendment is recommended by the War Department and ap-
proved by the Civil Service Commission, on representations of the
Department that the same reasons exist for excepting positions on cable
boats as on torpedo and mine planters. The qualifications for employ-
ment on these vessels differ from those required in corresponding posi-
tions on other vessels, and the frequent sailing and the fact that the
vessels do not sail between fixed points render it difficult to make appoint-
ments under the civil service rules.
WOOD ROW WILSON
THE WHITE HOUSE,
4t May, 1915"
