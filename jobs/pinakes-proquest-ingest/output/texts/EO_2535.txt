Erecutive Orber.
Mr. John E. Anis may be appointed to a clerical position in any executive
Department or independent office, without reference to the civil service rules.
Mr. Anis has been employed since September 3, 1915, as office deputy
marshal in the Office of the United States Marshal at Nashville, Tennessee,
having been appointed without examination as the position is excepted from
examination by authority of lawv.
The Civil Service Commission does not recommend this order.
WOODROW WILSON
THE WHITE HOUSE,
21 February, 1917.
[No. 2535.]
