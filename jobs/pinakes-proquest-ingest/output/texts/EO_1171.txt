Executive be
In accordance with the provisions of Section 2253 of the Revised
Statutes of the United States, and by virtue of the authority therein given
it is hereby ordered that townships twenty-two, twenty-three and twenty-
four north, ranges twenty-four and twenty-five west, in the State of Mon-
tana, be detached from the Missoula land district and attached to and made
a part of the Kalispell land district, and the business and archives pertaining
to the lands within said townships be transferred from the land office at
Missoula to the land office at Kalispell. HT F
THE WHITE HOUSE
February 25, 1910.
[No. 1171.]
