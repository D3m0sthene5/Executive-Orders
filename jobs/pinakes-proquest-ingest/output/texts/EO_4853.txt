]Executive Order
By virtue of his appointment as War Claims Arbiter the salary payable to
Judge Edwin B. Parker in such capacity is hereby fixed at $7,500 per annum, under
the provisions of Section 3 (a) of the "Settlement of War Claims Act of 1928 ".
This amount added to his present salary of $7,500 per annum as Umpire of the
Mixed Claims Commission and Commissioner of the Tripartite Claims Commission
will make his total salary received from the United States within the limit fixed in
Section 3 (a) of said Act.
CALVIN COOLIDGE
THE WHITE HOUSE,
April 6, 1928.
