Executive Orber
Under the authority and provisions of the act of Congress approved
June 25, 1910 (36 Stat., 847), as amended by the act of August 24, 1912
(37 Stat., 497), it is hereby ordered that all unappropriated land in Secs. 25
and 36, T. 3 S., R. 18 XW., and Secs. 30, 3 1, and 32, T. 2 S., R. 17 W., Tall.
M., in the State of Florida, be and the same is hereby temporarily withdrawn
from all forms of settlem nt, location, sale or entry, in view of the legislation
proposed in pending H. 8 . bill 8522, subject to any valid existing rights in
and to the same initiated under the public land laws.
