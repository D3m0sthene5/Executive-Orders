Schedule A, Subdivision XI of the Civil Service Rules is hereby
amended by the addition of the following paragraph to be known as
paragraph 3 :
;. Commercial agents to investigate trade conditions abroad
and in the United States, including the insular possessions, with the
object of promoting the foreign commerce of the United States.
Prior to July 1, 19! I, commercial agents were employed abroad
exclusively and owing to the character of their duties were appointed
without examination under the exemption of any person employed in a
foreign country under the State Department or temporarily employed in
a confidential capacity in a foreign country under any Department or
office. Under authority of law the scope of the investigations of corn.
mercial agents wvas extended to take in the United States, including the
insular possessions, effective July I, 1911, s0 that the exemption referred
to does not apply to such agents as may be appointed or employed in the
United States. The duties of such agents are more or less confidential in
character and men are needed who are particularly qualified along some
technical lines relating to manufacture in some definite industry and must
necessarily have the personal qualifications of good appearance, address,
social training and knowledge of the world.
Most of the se: vice of the commercial agents will be rendered abroad
but in order to make the best use of these agents it is desirable that the
Department be free as to assignment to duty in the United States to
secure more definite information with respect to the wishes of the manu-
facturing interests in this country in regard to the work abroad. The
Civil Service Commission therefore joins with the Secretary of Commerce
and Labor in recommending the issuance of the above order.
WM H TAFT
THE WHITE HOUSE,
September 4/12, 1911.
