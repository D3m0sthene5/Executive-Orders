Executive Order
Pursuant to the provisions of Section 6 of the Act entitled "An Act To
provide a temporary government for the West Indian Islands acquired by the
United States from Denmark by the convention entered into between said
countries on the fourth day of August, nineteen hundred and sixteen, and
ratified by the Senate of the United States on the seventh day of September,
nineteen hundred and sixteen, and for other purposes", approved March 3,
1917, it is directed that on April first, 1917, the sum of $ioo,ooo appropriated
in said Section 6 be placed to the credit of Rear Admiral James H. Oliver,
U. S. Navy, Governor of the Virgin Islands of the United States, to be expended
in his discretion for all necessary expenses in connection with the taking over
and occupying said islands and of carrying the above mentioned Act into effect
and to meet any deficit in the revenues of the said Islands resulting from the
provisions of said Act; and this money shall be available for meeting the expenses
of fitting up and maintaining the residences and establishment of the Governor
in said Islands under such limitations as may be prescribed by the Secretary
of the Navy upon the recommendation of the Governor.
WOODROW WILSON
THlE WHITE HOUSE,
29 March, 1917.
[No. 2568.]
