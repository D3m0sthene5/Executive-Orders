Executive order~
Alaska
Fort Davis Cemetery
Pursuant to authority conferred upon me by the act of Congress approved
June 25, }1910 (36 Stat., 847) as amended by the act of August 24, 1912 (37 Stat.,
497)-it is hereby ordered that the following described land be and the same is hereby
withdrawn from settlement, location, sale or entry and reserved for cemetery
purposes to be known as Fort Davis Cemetery, Alaska:
"Beginning at a point 115 feet from high water mark, and 3420 feet south of
east from stake from point of spit at mouth of Nome River; running thence north
42 feet to a point; thence east at right angles 30 feet to a point; thence south at right
angles 42 feet to a point 115 feet from high'water mark; thence west at right angles
30 feet to place of beginning."
CALVIN COOLIDGE
