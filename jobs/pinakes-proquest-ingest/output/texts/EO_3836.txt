Executive Orber
The Executive Order of May 10, 1921, requiring candidates to take civil
service examinations for Presidential postmaster hips, is hereby waived so as
to permit the appointment of Henry M. Stowe to the position of postmaster
at Bedford, Virginia, without examination.
WARREN G HARD ING
THES WHITE HOUSE~,
May 7, 1923.
[No. 3836.]
