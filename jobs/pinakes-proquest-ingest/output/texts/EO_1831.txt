.Executive Orber
I. Under authority of the Act of Congress approved July 5, 1884,
entitled "An Act to provide for the disposal of abandoned and useless
military reservations" (23 Stat. 103), that portion of the right of way for
military telegraph lines in Alaska reserved by Executive Order No. L1746,
dated March 14, 1913, which is located between Fort Gibbon and Hot
Springs, Alaska, being useless for military purposes, is hereby placed under
the control of the Secretary of the Interior for disposition under said act
of Congress or as may be otherwise provided for by law.
II. In lieu of the right of way placed under the control of the Secre-
tary of the Interior for disposition, under Paragraph I of this Order, there
is hereby reserved, subject to private rights it any there be, a strip of land
100 feet wide (5o feet on each side of the center of the telegraph line)
along the United States military telegraph line between Fort Gibbon and
Hot Springs as follows, viz.:
Beginning on the south bank of the Yukon River opposite Mission
Hill and extending in a southeasterly direction about two miles to where
it joins the mail trail ; thence along the general course of this trail to
Long Lake; thence, continuing in a southeasterly direction, to American
Greek, where it crosses the winter mail trail and joins the summer trail;
thence continuing in the same direction for about one and one-half miles;
thence in a northeasterly direction to the junction of the two trails, from
which it extends along the trail to within one-half mile of Tofty; thence
in a southeastern direction, for a distance of six miles, to the saddle on.
the divide between Hot Springs and Tofty; thence in an easterly direction
four miles into the town of Hot Springs ; thence along the main street of
said town, 5o feet from the bank of Hot Springs Slough, for a distance
of approximately 6oo feet; and thence across the slough above 'the bridge
to the United States telegraph office on the other side.
WOODROW WILSON
THE WHITE HOUSE,
25 September, 1r913.
