By virtue of the authority vested in me by an Act of Congress entitled
"An Act to define, regulate, and punish trading with the enemy and for other
purposes ", approved October 6, 19 17, known as the " Trading with the Enemy
Act", and the amendment to such Act embodied in "An Act making appro-
priations to supply urgent deficiencies in the appropriations for the fiscal year
ending June 30, 1 918, and prior fiscal years on account of war expenses and for
other purposes", approved March 28, 1918, and the amendment to such .Act
embodied in "An Act making appropriations to supply deficiencies in appro-
priations for thc fiscal year ending June 30, 1919, and prior fiscal years on account
of war expenses, and for other purposes", approved November 4, 1918, to make
certain orders such as that herein contained, I hereby, in the public interest,
m ake the following determination and order:
The Alien PropryCusodjin is hereby authorized to sell at private sale,
at such time and place as to him or to his authorized agent may seem advisable,
without public or other advertisement, Nine Hundred Eighty-Nine (989) shares
of the common capital stock of the Busch-Sulzer Brothes-pDiesl nave Comf-
pany, a corporation organized under the laws of the State of Missouri, out of a
total issue of Twelve Thousand Five Hundred (1 2,500) shares of the par value
of One Hundred Dollars ($xoo.oo) each, which said Nine Hundred Eighty-Nine
(989) shares were heretofore seized by the Alien Property Custodian under the
provisions of the said Act, as the property of Rudolph Diesel, Jr., of Munich,
Germany.
My reasons for the foregoing determination and order are:
(a) That property such as that herein described cannot usually be sold to
advantage at public sale after public or other advertisement.
(b) That advantageous offers, all of Forty Dollars ($40.00) a share, have
been received by the Alien Property Custodian for the purchase of the said
Nine Hundred Eighty-Nine (989) shares.
(c) That the said Rudolph Diesel, Jr., has expressed his assent to the sale
of the said Nine Hundred Eighty-Nine (989) shares at the price offered.
(d) That an attempted sale at auction would be a useless formality and
would involve unnecessary expense, delay and inconvenience, which should be
avoided.
