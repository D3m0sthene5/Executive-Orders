Mr. E. C. Yeats, who was removed from the Railway Mail Service on
December 23, 1916 for improper use of his travel commission, may be reinstated
as a postal clerk in the postal service, without reference to the civil service rules.
This order is issued in view of the statement of the Postmaster General
that the case of Mr. Yeats had received his personal attention and that he had
concluded that the charges were not sufficiently sustained to warrant his
removal.
WOODROW WILSON
THE WHITE HOUSE,
17 July, 1917.
