WYOMING
Under authority contained in the act of Congress approved June 25, 1910
(36 Stat., 847), as amended by the act of August 24, 1912 (37 Stat., 497), and
subject to the conditions, provisions and limitations of said acts, it is hereby
ordered that the public lands in Ts. 45 and 46 N., Rs. 88, 89, 90 and 91 W., and
T. 47 N., Rs. 90 and 91 W., 6th P. M., Wyoming, be, and the same are hereby
withdrawn from settlement, location, sale, entry, and all other forms of appropria-
tions, pending a resurvey of said townships under the act of Congress approved
May 29, 1908 (35 Stat., 465). This order shall continue in full force and effect
unless and until revoked by the President or by act of Congress.
