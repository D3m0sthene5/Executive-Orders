Under authority of the act of Congress approved June 25, 1910 (36 Stat.,
847), as amended by the act of August 24, 1912 (37 Stat., 497), and on the
recommendation of the Secretary of Agriculture, it is hereby ordered that the
NE/4 SE/ Section 1, T. 5 S., R. 82 W., 6th P. M., containing 40 acres, be
temporarily withdrawn from settlement, location, sale or entry, except as
provided in said acts, and be reserved for use by the Forest Service as an
addition to a ranger station in connection with the administration of the Holy
Cross National Forest, Colorado.
WOODROW WILSON
THE WHITE HOUSE,
11 May, 1917.
