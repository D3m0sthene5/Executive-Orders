Schedule A of positions excepted from examination under the civil
service rules is hereby amended by the addition of a new subdivision, to
be numbered XIII, and a new paragraph therein, to be numbered I, in
language as follows:
XII1. Department of Labor.
i. Commissioners of conciliation in labor disputes to be ap-
pointed by the Secretary of Labor whenever, in his judgment, the
interests of industrial peace may require it.
The act of March 4, 191;, creating the Department of Labor, pro-
vides that the Secretary of Labor shall have power to act as mediator and
to appoint commissioners of conciliation in labor disputes. The services
of these commissioners are temporary, the demand for their services arises
suddenly, and the requisite qualifications are of unusual character. In
many cases it has been necessary for the Department to make the appoint-
ments before securing the consent of the Civil Service Commission.
WOODROW WILSON
