Erecutive Othrer
Pursuant to the powers vested in me I hereby revoke and rescind the Executive
Order issued July i 5, 1918, which authorized the Secretary of Labor, in the
construction of buildings in the District of Columbia, to make use of certain
premises hereinafter described; and
I hereby authorize the War Industries Board, for the purpose of erecting a
temporary office building and maintaining and using the same during the
present war emergency, to take possession of Squares 229 and 230 of the
District of Columbia, such premises being owned by the United States and
having not heretofore been dedicated by Act of Congress for specific buildings.
WOODROW WILSON
THE WHITE HOUSE,
September 6, 1918.
[No. 2952.]
