Executive Order No. 1041, dated February 27, 1909,' reserving and setting
aside for the use of the Department of Agriculture as a preserve and breeding
ground for native birds the treeless tundra of the delta of the Yukon River,
Alask, situated west of longitude one hundred and sixty-two degrees and
twenty minutes west from Greenwich, and south of the Yukon, and designating
said reservation as the Yukon Delta Reservation, is hereby revoked.
WARREN G HARD ING
TEE WHITE HOUSE,
February 27, 1922.
