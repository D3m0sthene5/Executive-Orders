Executive ether.
Mr. Carroll W. Dunning may be reinstated in the Office of Markets
and Rural Organization in the Department of Agriculture, where his
services are needed to cooperate with the Forest Service in investigations
in the marketing of wvoodlot products. He served as statistician in forest
products from November i, 1911, to March 16, 1914, and the Department
states that he has had unusually valuable training and experience at home
and abroad. Since leaving the service he has been engaged in a marketing
proposition, representing on the Pacific Coast a number of eastern manu-
facturers, this employment being in line with his qualifications and ex-
perience.
This order is issued upon the recommendation of the Secretary of
Agriculture with the concurrence of the Civil Service Commission.
WOODROW WILSON
THE WHITE HOUSE,
14 May, 1915.
[No. 2196.]
