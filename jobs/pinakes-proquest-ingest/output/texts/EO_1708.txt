Executive cOrber
The Secretary of the Treasury is hereby authorized to promote,
transfer and appoint Mr. Martin J. Murphy, now a Sugar Sampler in the
Office of the Appraiser of Merchandise at New York, to the position of
Clerk in that office, without compliance with the Civil Service Rules.
This Order is issued because Mr. Murphy has a serious throat trouble
which is aggravated by exposure on the docks in sampling sugar, but is
relieved by inside work, and because during a period of detail to clerical
work he has demonstrated clerical ability of a high order, and the Appraiser
certifies that it will be in the interest of the service to make the promo-
tion and transfer proposed. The Civil Service Commission disapproves
this order.
WM H TAFT
THE WHITE HOUSE,
Feby. 20, 1913.
