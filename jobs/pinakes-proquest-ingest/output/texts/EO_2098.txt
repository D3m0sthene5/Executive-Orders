A
Executive Orber
Miss Laura E. Scott may be appointed to a clerical position in the
classified service of the Department of State without examination under
the civil service rules.
This action is based upon the recommendation of the Acting Secre-
tary of State because of Miss Scott's efficient service with several interna-
tional arbitration commissions, the exceptional qualifications she has
evinced in the performance of her duties as confidential clerk to the
Acting Secretary of State, and his representation that the loss of the
services of Miss Scott, who has been with him in the capacity of confiden-
tial clerk for several years, would be a great inconvenience.
The Civil Service Commission concurs in the recommendation of this
order.
WOODROW WILSON
THE WHITE HOUSE,
November 28, 1914.
[No. 2098.]
