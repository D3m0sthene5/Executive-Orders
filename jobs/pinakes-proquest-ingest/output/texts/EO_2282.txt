Executive rber.
Mrs. Margaretta G. H. Neale may be transferred from the position
of special agent in the Bureau of the Census to a clerical position in the
Department of Commerce without reference to the civil-service rules.
This order is issued upon recommendation of the Secretary of Com-
merce who states that there has existed in the Division of Publications
since September 7, 1915, a vacancy which the Division has been trying to
fill by selection from the editorial clerk register of the Civil Service Com-
mission, and that of the twelve eligibles certified by the Commission,
eleven have declined to accept appointment at the stipulated salary and
that the papers of the other eligible wvho made a very lowv rating did not
indicate that he has had the necessary experience or possesses qualifications
which in the opinion of the Division would make him a useful employee.
The Secretary further states that Mrs. Neale wvas temporarily employed in
a clerical capacity in the Bureau of the Census during the Twelfth and
Thirteenth Decennial Census periods; that since February 12, 1915, she
has been serving as a special agent in connection with the census of manu-
factures; and that in view of the efficiency and aptitude displayed by her
in the performance of the various duties assigned her, he believes that she
is exceptionally capable and is better qualified than would be an untrained
eligible who might eventually be obtained through civil service channels,
for filling the vacancy in question.
WOODROW WILSON.
THE WHITE HOUSE,
1, December, 1915.
