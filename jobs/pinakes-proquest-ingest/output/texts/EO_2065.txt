Executive Otber.
Mrs. Margaret A. Hampton may be transferred from the position of
clerk at $1000 per annum in the Census Bureau, Department of Com-
merce, to the position of clerk at $ 1200 per annum in the Children's
Bureau, Department of Labor,, without regard to the requirements of the
Civil Service Rules.
Mrs. Hampton was appointed temporary clerk in the Thirteenth
Census on April 1, 1810, and served until July 1, 1912, when she received
a permanent appointment as clerk at $iooo per annum through Executive
Order. She has rendered excellent service with the Census Bureau, and
her transfer to the Children's Bureau is recommended by the Secretary of
Labor and the Chief of the Children's Bureau.
The Civil Service Commission does not concur in the recommendation.
WOODROW WILSON
THE WHITE HOUSE,
October 27, 1914.
[No. 2065.]
