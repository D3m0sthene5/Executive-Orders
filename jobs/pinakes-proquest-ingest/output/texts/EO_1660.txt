ALTA ADMINISTRATIVE SITE
(Near Bitterroot National Forest)
MONTANA
Under authority of the Act of Congress approved June 25, 1910 (36
Stat., 847), as amended by the Act of August 24, 1912 (37 Stat., 497), the
order of the Secretary of the Interior dated December 16, 1908, withdraw-
ing from appropriation under the public land laws, subject to prior valid
adverse claims, and reserving for use by the Forest Service as Alta ranger
station in the administration of the National Forest, a certain tract of land
near the Bitterroot National Forest, Montana, described by metes and
bounds as containing 26 acres in approximately the SE. Ig4 Sec. 34, T. 2 S.,
R. 22 WV., then unsurveyed, and shown by recent survey to be in Lot 4 of
said section, is hereby amended to include all of Lot 4, Sec. 34, T. 2 S.,
R. 22 WV., M. M., 27.42 acres.
WM H TAFT
THE WHITE HOUSE,
Dec. Toth, 1912.
[No. 166o.]
