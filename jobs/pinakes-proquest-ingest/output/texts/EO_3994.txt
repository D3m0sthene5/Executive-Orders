It is hereby ordered, under authority contained in Section 5 of the Act
of February 8, 1887 (24 Stat. L., 388) and the Act of June 21, 1906 (34, Stat.
L., 325-326), that the trust period on all, allotments of the Sisseton and
Wahpeton Band of Sioux Indians of the Lake Traverse Reservation, North
and South Dakota, the title to which has not passed from the United States,
be, and the same is hereby, extended for a further period of fifteen years.
CALVIN COOL IDGE
Tuxi WHITE HousE,
April 19, 1924.
