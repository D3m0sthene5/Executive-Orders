Executive Other
Item I Subdivision II of Schedule A, classified positions excepted from
examination under Rule II, clause 3, of the Civil Service Rules, which reads
" Eight officers to aid in important drafting work", is hereby amended by the
elimnation of the word "Eight", so as to read "Officers to aid in important
drafting work."
WOODROW WILSON
THE WHITE HOUSE,
22 March, .1917.
[No. 2.555.]
