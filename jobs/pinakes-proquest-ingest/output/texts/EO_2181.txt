Mrs. Lucy Jones Harris, of Kentucky, may be appointed to a position
in the Immigration Service, Department of Labor, without reference to
the civil service rules.
This order is issued in view of the statement of the Secretary of
Labor that the Department can use to advantage the services of Mrs.
Harris in the Immigration Service, in which the Commissioner-General of
Immigration informs him there is urgent need of a woman of Mrs. Harris'
peculiar qualifications in the labor distribution branch of the service at
New York City, where it is deemed advisable, in the development of this
new service, to have a woman of her high character and sympathetic initia-
tive, who is able at once to take care of the requirements of the woman
labor problem and greatly assist in carrying out the plan of distribution
inaugurated by the Department.
The Civil Service Commission does not recommend the issuance of
an order in this case.
WOODROW WILSON
THE WHITE HOUSE,
27 April, 1915.
[No. 2181.]
