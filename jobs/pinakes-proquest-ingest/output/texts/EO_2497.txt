By virtue of the power and authority vested in and conferred upon
me by the laws of the United States in that behalf made and provided, it
is hereby ordered that the lands hereinafter more particularly described
shall be, and the same are hereby withdrawn from settlement, entry, sale
and any and every other manner and form of appropriation or disposition

of them are and will be reserved to the United States so long as the con-
tinuance of such reservation shall be necessary to provide for the water
supply of Fort D. A. Russell and the City of Cheyenne, in the County of
Laramie, and State of Wyoming, and until such time as Congress or the
President of the United States shall otherwise direct, and the withdrawal
hereby made shall be unqualified and absolute, except to the extent that
any of the lands herein described may be covered byvli xitn rights
heretofore initiated pursuant to any of the public land laws of the United
