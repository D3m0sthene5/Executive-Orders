It is hereby ordered, under authority contained in the Act of March 2, 1917
(Public 369), that the period of trust on lands held in trust for the use and ben-
efit of the Porr (La Piche or La Jolla) and Rincon bands or villages of Indians
in California, which trust expires during the calendar year 1917, be and is hereby
extended for a period of ten years from the date of expiration thereof.
