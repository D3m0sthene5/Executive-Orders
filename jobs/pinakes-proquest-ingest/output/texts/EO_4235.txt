The term of Honorable Arthur F. Odlin, Judge of the District Court of the
United States for Porto Rico, having terminated, and Honorable Ira K. Wills hav-
ing been appointed Judge of the District Court of the United States for Porte Rico
to succed him, under the authority delegated to me in Section 41 of the Act of
March 2, 1917, I hereby desgnte Honorable Emilio del Toro y Cuebas, Chief
Justice of the Supreme Court of Porto Rico, as Acting Judge of the District Court
of the United States for Porto Rico during any interval that may occur between
Judge Odlin's leaving the Islands and the arrival of Judge Wells.
During the incumbency of Judge Emilio del Toro y Cuebas, and in accordance
with said Section 41 of the Act of March 2, 1917, he is fully authorized and em-
powered to perform the duties of said office, and to sign all necssry papers and
records as the Acting Judge of said Court, without extra compensation.
CALVIN COOLIDGE
