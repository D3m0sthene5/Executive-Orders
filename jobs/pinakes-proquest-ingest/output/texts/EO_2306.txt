Executive ether.
Miss Margaret S. Neate may be appointed a printer's assistant in the
Bureau of Engraving and Printing without certification by the Civil
Service Commission.
Miss Neate passed the examination for printer's assistant in January,
1915, but has not been reached for certification. She is the daughter of
Dr. John S. Neate, who served for twenty-two years in the United States
Army, and rendered most valuable service in fighting yellow fever, sub-
mitting himself for experiment, and died in 1912 from a disease believed
to have been brought on by this service.
This order is issued upon the recommendation of the Secretary of the
Treasury. The Civil Service Commission does not concur in the recoin-
mend at ion.
WOODROW WILSON
THE WHITE HousE,
7 February, 1916.
