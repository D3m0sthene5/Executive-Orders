It is hereby ordered, under authority contained in Section S, of the Act of
February 8, 1887, (24 Stat. 388), that the trust period on allotments made to
the Indians on the Winnebago Reservation in Nebraska, which trust expires
during the calendar year 1918, be and is hereby extended for a period of ten
years.
WOODROW WILSON
THE~ WHITE HOUSE,
20 September, 1918.
[No. 2965.]
