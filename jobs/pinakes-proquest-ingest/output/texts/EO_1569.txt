Mr. Isaac D. Laferty may he reinstated in the Bureau of Pensions,
Department of the Interior without reference to the time limit of eligi-
bility for reinstatement under the civil service rules.
Mr. Laferty was appointed to a clerical position in the Pension Office
on August 11, 1884, and resigned on February 28, [901. The Cornmis-
sioner of Pensions recommends his reinstatement on account of his excel-
lent record and the need of the Bureau of Pensions for experienced men.
The Civil Service Commission did not join in recommending the
order.
WM H TAFT
THE WHITE HOUSE,
July 23, 1912.
