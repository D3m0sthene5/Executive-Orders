ordered that the following described lands at Waimanalo, District of Koolau-

II. The net area of the above described Waimanalo Military Reservation con-
tains 1510 acres, more or less.
III . All azimuths are true geodetic and except where otherwise specified the
boundary extends in straight lines between monuments.
IXV. The standard concrete monument consists of concrete in the shape of a
truncated pyramid having an upper base 8 inches square and a lower base
12 inches square. In the center of the upper base is set a brass plate 3 inches
square.
V. Exceptions and leases.
(a) All tracts of land, described by Land Commission Awards and Royal
Patent Grants, Grants and Patents that have been awarded by the Authorities
of Hawaii and are contained within the limits of the above described Waimanalo
Military Reservation, are excepted from said reservation.
(b) The above described Waimanalo Military Reservation is subject to
General Lease No. 165 to john Cummins, which said lease expires November
22, 1920.
