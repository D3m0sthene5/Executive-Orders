Executive Orber.
ALASKAN TOWNSITE WITHDRAWAL NO. 8.
Under and pursuant to the provisions of the Act of Congress,
approved March 12, 1914 (38 Stat., 305), entitled "An Act to authorize
the President of the United States to locate, construct, and operate rail-
roads in the Territory of Alaska and for other purposes", it is hereby
ordered that the following lands be and the same are hereby withdrawn
from settlement, location, sale, entry, or other disposition, and reserved for
towvnsite purposes and for other purposes in connection with the construc-
tion and operation of railroad lines under said Act:
A tract at Resurrection Bay, Territory of Alaska,
Beginning at a point identical with Corner No. 2 of rejected
United States Survey No. 607, and running northwesterly along the
shore of Resurrection Bay to a point identical with Corner No. 3 of
said rejected survey; thence due west to Corner No. 3 of United
States Survey No. 243, thirty chains, more or less; thence due
south five chains, more or less, to the point of intersection with the
north end line (line 3-4) of United States Survey No. 703; thence
due east five chains, more or less, to Corner No. 4 of said United
States Survey No. 703; thence south fifteen chains, more or less, to
Corner No. 4 of United States Survey No. 726 North; thence east to
place of beginning, thirty chains, more or less;
Also a tract of land at Resurrection Bay, Alaska, beginning at
Corner No. 5 of United States Survey No.2 4 3 ; thence in a northerlydirection along the shore of Resurrection Bay to Corner No. 2 of
United States Survey No. 241, twenty chains, more or less; thence
due west fifteen chains, more or less; thence in a southerly direction
to Corner No. 4 of United States Survey No. 243, twenty chains,
more or less; thence due east to point of beginning, eleven and
seventy nine one-hundredths chains, more or less. Said tracks will
be, when surveyed, in T. I S., R. I W., Seward Principal Meridian.
WOODROW WILSON
THE WHITE HOUSE,
19 July, 1915.
