]Executive Order
Mr. Gilbert J. Murray may be appointed Assistant to the Director of the~
Bureau of War Risk Insurance in the Treasury Department without compliance
with civil service rules.
This order is issued on the recommendation of the Secretary of the Treasury,
who states that the position of Assistant to the Director of the Bureau of War
Risk Insurance is a technical one; that there is no person in the Treasury Depart-
ment qualified to fill the position, and that Mr. Murray possesses the necessary
qualifications, and that his appointment will be in the interest of the service.
WOODROW WILSON
THE WHITE HOUSE,
3 April, 1917.
[No. 2573.]
