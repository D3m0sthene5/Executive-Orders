The temporary employment of Mr. Neil Senter, as clerk in the
Quartermaster Corps of the Army at Fort B~ayard, New Mexico, may be
continued for a period of six months from November I I, 19 13, without
reference to the provisions of paragraph 4, Civil Service Rule VIII.
This order is issued in view of the fact that Mr. Senter's separation
from the service at this time would be prejudicial to his health.
WOODROW WILSON
THE WHITE HOUSE,
8 November, 1913.
