Censorship of Submarine Cables, Telegraph and Telephone Lines.
Trhe Executive Order of September 26, 191$, relating to the censorship of
submarine cale, telegraph and telephone lines, is hereby amended to read as
follows:;
Whereas the armistice between the United States and the Imperial
German Government has removed the occasion for a strict military censorship
along the Mexican border.
TnCHEKEORE, by virtue of the power vested in me under the Constitution
and by the joint resolution passed by Congress on Apirl 6, 1917, declaring the
existence of a state of war, the specific restrictions imposed upon all companies
or other persons owning, controlling, or operating telegraph and telephone
lines in the Executive Order of September 26, 1918, are hereby revoked.
The War Department is relieved of the duty of enforcing the rules and
regulations necessary to the carrying out of the telegraph and telephone
censorship, and the personnel engaged shall be transferred to other duties or
discharged.
This order shall take effect from date.
