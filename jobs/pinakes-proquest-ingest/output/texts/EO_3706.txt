]Executive Othrer
Under authority of the Act of June 30, 1922, the offices of Register and
Receiver of the United States land offices at Del Norte and Sterlin Colorado,
are hereby consolidated and the offices of Receiver are abolished. All the
peers, duties, obligations and penalties imposed by law upon both the
Amended Res and Receiver of said offices shall be exercised by and imposed upon
~ Registers to be appointed, whose compensation shall be a salary of $5oo per
annum, together with the fees and commissions otherwise allowable to both
Register and Receiver, but the salary, fees and commissions of such officers
shall not exceed $3,ooo per annum.
The consolidation of said offices shall become effective upon the appoint-
ment and qualification of such Registers.
WARREN G HARDING
THE WHITE HOUSE,
July 10, 1922.
