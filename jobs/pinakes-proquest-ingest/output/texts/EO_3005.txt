Executive Orber.
Whereas, by an Executive Order under (late of August 8, 1915S, prescribing
rules and regulations governing departure from and entry into the United
States, it was provided b~y Sect ion 12 thereof as follows:
"No person registered or enrolled or subject to registry or enrollment for
military service in the United States shall depart from the United States with-
out the previous consent of the Secretary of War or such person or persons as
lie may appoint to give such consent. The Secretary of State shall issue no
passport or permit entitling such person to depart without securing satisfac-
tory evidence of such consent. Reference should be had to section 156, Selec-
tive Service Regulations, and amendments thereto"; and
Whereas it is no longer necessary that the above quoted regulations with
regard to the departure from the United States of persons registered or enrolled
or subject to registry or enrollment for military service in the United States
should continue in force,
Now, therefore, it is ordered that Section 12 of the said Executive Order
ofAgus 8, 1918, is hereby revoked.
This order shall take effect from and after the date thereof.
