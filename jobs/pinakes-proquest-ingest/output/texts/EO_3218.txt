Executive Orber.
Mr James Hagerman, Jr., may be appointed as Deputy Commissioner of
Internal Revenue at five thousand dollars ($5000) per annum without examnui-
tion under the civil service rules, He is well qualified. The Civil Service
Commission, not having authority to approve the appointment without com-
petition as requested by the Department, suggested executive action,
WOODROW WILSON
THE WHITE HOUSE,
29 January, 1920.
