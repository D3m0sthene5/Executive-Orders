For the period of the war, positions of elevator conductor may be filled
by the appointment of women and when so filled need not be apportioned
among the States and Territories and the District of Columbia on the basis
of population, as is provided in the case of certain minor positions mentioned
in Rule VII, Section 2, of the Civil Service Rules. Persons so appointed shall
not be eligible for transfer or promotion to apportioned positions nor for assign-
ment to other work.
WOODROW WILSON
THE W~HTE HOUSE,
31 May, 1918.
