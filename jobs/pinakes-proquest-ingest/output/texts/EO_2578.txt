ALASKA RAILROAD WITHDRAWAL
Under and pursuant to the provisions of the act of Congress approved
March z 2, 1914 (38 Stat., 305) , entitled "An Act to authorize the President of
the United States to locate, construct, and operate railroads in the Territory
of Alaska, and for other purposes," it is hereby ordered that the following lands,
be, and the same are hereby, withdrawn from settlement, location, sale, entry,
or other disposition and reserved for purposes in connection with the construction
and operation of railroad lines under said act under such regulations as have been
or may hereafter be prescribed: Provided, that nothing herein contained shall
be held to preclude the use or disposition of any of the timber on said lands, not
embraced within a national forest, in accordance with the provisions of Section i z
of the Act of Congress approved May 14, 1898 (30 Stat., 414), when it shall
appear to the Secretary of the Interior or to the Alaskan Engineering Commis-
sion, that the granting of such privileges will not interfere with the purposes
for which the withdrawal is made :
Commencing at a point on the east bank of the Susitna River opposite
Engineers station, 11125 of the Government railroad, as said station is marked
and delineated on the ground, thence following along the shore of said river in
a northerly direction a distance of one-half a mile, thence east one-half a mile,
thence south one mile, thence west to bank of said river, thence northerly along
the bank of said river to point of beginning.
WOODROW WILSON
THE WHITE HOUSE,
4 April, 1917.
[No. 2578.]
