Executive ether.
Miss Gertrude Printz, now an employee of the sub-clerical grade in
the Treasury Department, may be promoted to the grade of clerk in that
Department without compliance with Civil Service rules.
This order is issued on the recommendation of the Secretary of the
Treasury for the reason that because of the excellent record of Miss Printz
in the performance of the special class of work on which she is engaged,
and because of her faithful service in the Treasury Department, she is
entitled to advancement. WM H TAFT
THE WHITE HOUSE,
January 24, 1913.
[No. 1691.]
