Executive Orber.
AMENDMENT TO THE CIVIL SERVICE RULES.
Rule X is hereby amended by the addition of a paragraph, to be
numbered ii, to read as follows:
i i. Any person who has served for at least two years in the office of
the President of the United States may be transferred to a competitive
classified position upon such tests of fitness as the Commission may deem
proper.
THEODORE ROOSEVELT
THE WHITE HOUSE,
January 9, 1909.
[No. 1004.]
