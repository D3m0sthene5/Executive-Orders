Pursuant to the provisions of the Act of Congress approved August 1,
1914, making appropriations for the Sundry Civil Expenses of the Govern-
ment for the fiscal year ended June 30, 1915, the port of entry at Ludington,
Michigan, located in customs collection district No. 38, of which Detroit is
the headquarters port, is hereby abolished.
WOODROW WILSON
THE WHITE HOUSE,
3 January, 1917.
[No. 2504.]
