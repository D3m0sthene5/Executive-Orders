MOKELUMNE (E) ADMINISTRATIVE SITE
(Near Stanislaus National Forest)
CALIFORNIA
On the recommendation of the Secretary of Agriculture it is hereby
ordered that the withdrawal of a tract of land containing 8o acres, described
as the W r SWV5 Sec. 25, T. 6 N., R. 14. E., M.D.M., reserved as a ranger
station for use of the Forest Service in connection with the administration
of the Stanislaus National Forest, California, made by Executive Order
(No. 1336) of April 19, 1911, under authority of the Act of Congress
approved June 25, 1910 (36 Stat., 847), be revoked.
WOODROW WILSON
THE WHITE HOUSE,
10 March, 1915.
