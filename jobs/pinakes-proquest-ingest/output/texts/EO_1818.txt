Executive Order
RIO GRANDE NATIONAL FOREST
COLORADO
Under authority of the Act of Congress of June 4, 1897 (30 Stat., i i
at 34 and 36), and on the recommendation of the Secretary of Agiulue
it is hereby ordered that the boundaries of the Rio Grande National Forest,
as modified by the proclamation of August 15, 1910, 1be further modified
by eliminating therefrom the following described lands, in the State of
Colorado:
IN TOWNSHIP 36 NORTH. RANGE 4 EAST,
NEWV MEXICO PRINCIPAL MERIDIAN;
Of section 22, the south half of the northeast quarter and the north half
of the southeast quarter.
The lands herein eliminated from the Rio Grande National Forest are
hereby withdrawn for classification under the Act of June 25, 1910 (36
Stat., 847), as amended by the Act of August 24, 1912 (7 Stat., 497),
and will, when compatible with the public interests, be restored to settle-
ment and entry under the laws applicable thereto on such dates as shall be
fixed b~y the Secretary of the Interior and after such notice as he may deem
advisable.
WOODROW WILSON
