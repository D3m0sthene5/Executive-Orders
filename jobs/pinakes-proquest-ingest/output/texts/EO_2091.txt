Under authority of the act of Congress, approved June 25, 1910 (36 Stat.,
847), as amended by act of August 24, 1912 (37 Stat., 497), and subject to the
conditions therein expressed, it is hereby ordered that the land hereafter
described be, and the same is, hereby temporarily withdrawn from settle-
ment, location, sale, entry, and other forms of disposal, until March 5, 1915,

If said bill is not enacted before the adjournment of the last session of
the 63rd Congress, and no other direction is given as to the disposition of
the land, it will, on March 5, 1915, become subject to disposal under any law
" then applicable thereto without further orders.
