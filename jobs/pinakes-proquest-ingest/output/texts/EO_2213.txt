Executive Orber.
Mrs. Mabel P. Walker may be appointed a Clerk in the Classified
Service of the Treasury Department without compliance with Civil Service
Rules.
This order is issued on the recommendation of the Secretary of the
Treasury in view of the expert services rendered by Mrs. Walker under
temporary appointment in that Department in operating the comptometer
machine whereby much time, labor, and expense has been saved, and in
viewv of the fact that there is no eligible register of the Civil Service Com-
mission for employees of this character.
WOODROW WILSON
THE WHITE HOUSE,
I8 June, 1915.
(No. 2213.]
