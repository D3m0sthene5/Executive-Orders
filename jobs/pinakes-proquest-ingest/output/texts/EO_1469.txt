Mr. John La Fon, Jr., may be reinstated as lumberman in the Forest
Service of the Department of Agriculture, the year limitation on eligibility
for reinstatement being waived to permit his reemployment. Mr. La Fon
ceased to render service on June 30, 1909. The Civil Service Commission
joins with the Secretary of Agriculture in recommending this Executive
(rder on account of Mr. La Fon's long experience in forestry work, his
initimate acquaintance with forests in the region in which his services are
to he utilized, and because he is regarded as one of the most capable men
in the United States in the work of examining, valuing, arnd reporting upon
the timber resources of the section of the country in which lands are to be
acquired under the Weeks Law for National Forest purposes.
THE WHITE HOUSE,
February 7, 1912.
