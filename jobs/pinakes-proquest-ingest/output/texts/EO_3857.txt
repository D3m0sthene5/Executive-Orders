Executive Orber
Wyoming
Under authority contained in the Act of Congress approved June 25, 1910
(36 Stat., 847), as amended by the Act of August 24, 1912 (37 Stat., 49),and
subject to the conditions, provisions and limitations of said acts, it is hereby
ordered that the public lands in Ts. 26, 27 and 28 N., R. 114. W., anid Ts. 26
and 27 N., R. 115 W., Sixth Principal Meridian, State of Wyoming, be, and
the same are hereby withdrawn from settlement, location, sale, entry and all
forms of appropriation, pending the resurvey of said townships under authority
contained in the Act of Congress approved March 3, 1909 (35 Stat., 845) ; this
withdrawal to remain in full force and effect from the date hereof until the
resurvey is duly approved and accepted and the plats thereof officially filed in
the proper U. S. local land office, unless another date be fixed by the Commis-
sioner of the General Land Office, whereupon this withdrawal will become of
no effect.
WARREN G HARDING
THE WHrrE House,
May 31, 1923.
