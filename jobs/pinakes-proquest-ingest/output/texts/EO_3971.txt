Under authority contained in the act of Congress approved June 25, 1910
(36 Stat. 847), as amended by the act of August 24, 1912 (37 Stat. 497), and
subject to the conditions, provisions, and limitations of said acts, it is hereby
ordered that the public lands in Secs. 3, 4, 5 and 6, T. 42 S., R. 7 W., S. L. M.,
Utah, be and the' same are hereby withdrawn from settlement, location, sale,
entry and all forms of appropriation, pending an examination and conditional
resurvey of said sections under authority contained in the act of Congress
approved March 3, 1909 (35 Stat., 845) ; this withdrawal to remain in full force
and effect from the date hereof until the resurvey, if made, is duly approved
and accepted and the approved plat thereof is officially filed in the proper
United States local land office, unless another date be fixed by the Commissioner
of the General Land Office, whereupon this withdrawal will become of no effect.
