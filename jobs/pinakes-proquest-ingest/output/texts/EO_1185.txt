Executive Orber.
AMENDMENT TO THE CIVIL SERVICE RULES.
Paragraph 6 of subdivision I II of Schedule A of classified positions
excepted from examination under the Treasury Department, which reads;
"One private secretary or confidential clerk to the superintendent, and
one cashier in each mint or assay office." is hereby amended to read as
follows: "One private secretary 'or confidential clerk to the superintend-
ent and one cashier in each mint, and one cashier in the Assay Office
at New York."
This amendment is approved on the recommendation of the Secretary
of the Treasury, the Director of the Mint, and the Civil' Service Commis-
sion for the reason that the duties required of cashiers in the minor assay
offices are not dissimilar from the duties of chief clerks, most of the pay-
ments being made by check and but little money handled.
WM H TAFT
THE WHITE HousE,
April 4 1910.
