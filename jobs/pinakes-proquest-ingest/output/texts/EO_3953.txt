Executive O'ber.
Edward K. Kraemer may be reinstated to his former position of rural carrier
at Elmer, New Jersey, without regard to the time limitation prescribed by the
Civil Service Rules.
He was appointed on September 1, 1904, and was discontinued with his
route in connection with the revision of service in Salem County on April 30,
1916. A vacancy has existed since May 1, 1922, and Mr. Kraemer has been
continuously employed as temporary carrier since February 6, 1922, during the
absence of the regular carrier on account of illness and subsequent to the dis-
continuance of the carrier. His services have been exceedingly satisfactory and
his reinstatement is desired in the interest of good service and because his restora-
tion to duty is an act of common justice, lie having been arbitrarily separated
from the service.
The Civil Service Commission concurred in the view of the Postmaster
General that Mr. Kraemer should be reinstated.
CALVIN COOL IDGE
THE WHITE HOUSE,
February 5, 1924.
