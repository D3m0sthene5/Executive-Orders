Executive 'Drber.
Mr. Edwvard K. Ketchum may be reinstated as a letter carrier in the
Philadelphia, Pennsylvania, Post Office, without reference to the year
limitation of the Civil Service Rules. This order is issued in view of
injuries received by Mr. Ketchum while serving as a letter carrier and
engaged in making collections of mail which resulted in serious disability,
compelling him to be absent from duty frequently to such an extent that
on January 14, 1904, he was compelled to resign. For this reason and in
viewv of Mr. Ketchum's good character, his record for faithfulness and
efficiency, and the fact that he is now physically able to perform the duties
of a letter carrier as testified to by the Postmaster General, and as he
is not entitled to compensation under the Act of May 30, 1908, grant-
ing to certain employees of the United States the right to receive from
the government compensation for injuries sustained in the course of their
employment, the Civil Service Commission joined with the Postmaster
General in recommending waiver of the one year limitation of the rein-
statement rule in the interests of the service.
WM H TAFT
THE WHITE HOUSE,
December 16, 1910o.
