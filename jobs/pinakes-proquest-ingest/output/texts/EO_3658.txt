MICHIGAN,-
Under authority of the Act of Congress approved June 25, 191o (36 Stat.,
847), as amended by the Act of August 24, 1912 (3 Stat., 497), the following
described islands in the Isle Royale Group in the State of Michigan are hereby
temporarily withdrawn, subject to the conditions of said acts, until March 5,
1923, in aid of pending legislation embodied in H. R. Bill No. 8625, 67th Con-
gress:
Islands No. 2 in Sec. 2, and Nos. i and 2 in Sec. 4, in T. 66 N., R. 34 W.;
Green Isle in Secs. 25 and 36, Island No. 1 in Sec. 36, T. 67 N., R. 34 W.,
and Dean. Island in Secs. 31 and 36, Ts. 67 N., Rs. 33 arid 34 W.;
Islands Nos. I and 2 in Sec. 32, and No. 3 in Secs. 29 and 32, T. 67 N.,
R. 33 W.
If said legislation be not enacted before the adjournment of the last session
of the Sixty-seventh Congress, and no other direction is given regarding the
disposition of such lands, they will, on March 5, 1923, become subject to disposal
under the provisions of Public Resolution No. 36, approved January 21, 1922,
and the regulations issued thereunder without further order.
WARREN G HARDING
THE WHITE HOUSE,
Ajril 4, 1922.-
