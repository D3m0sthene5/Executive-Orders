The following special. rule is hereby promulgated:
SPECIAL RULE.
Enlisted yeomen now on duty in the Bureau of Navigation
and the bureau of Medicine and Surgery, Navy Departunent, may
be transferred and appointed, without examination, as civilian
employes in said Bureaus in grades no higher than that of copyist,
according to the character of work which they are now performin,.
