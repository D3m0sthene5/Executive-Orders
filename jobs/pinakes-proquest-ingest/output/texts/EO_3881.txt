TONGASS NATIONAL FOREST
ALASKA.
Under authority of the Act of Congress approved June 4, 1897 (30 Stat.,
34-36), and on the recommendation of the Secretary of Agriculture, it is hereby
ordered that the tract of land lying within the hereinafter described boundaries,
heretofore occupied for fish cannery purposes, be, and the same is hereby,
excluded from the Tongass National Forest, in Alaska, and restored to entry
under the applicable public land laws :
Beginning at corner No. 1 on east shore of Kasaan Bay, a granite
boulder set in a mound of stones at line of average high tide and marked
F.S.M.-X1, approximately in latitude 550 30' N., longitude 1320 20' W.,
thence with meanders of said bay, south 23 ° 30' east 2.06 chains and thence
south 50 45' east 2.04 chains to corner No. 2, a granite ledge at average
high tide line marked X-2 on north face; thence north 31 0 30' east 13
chains to corner No. 3, which is identical with corner No. 4 of U. S. Survey
No. 280; thence south 470 30' west io.6o chains to the place of beginning,
containing 1.83 acres.
