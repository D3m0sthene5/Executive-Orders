Mr. Collinson Lewis Warwick may be appointed nautical expert in
the Coast and Geodetic Survey without examination under the civil. service
rules, it appearing that his services are essential in the prosecution of the
work of the Coast Pilot Division.
This order is made upon the concurrent recommendation of the Sec-
retary of Commerce and Labor and the Civil Service Commission, based
upon Mr. Warwick's long experience at sea, his large acquaintance with
the masters, mates and local pilots of the country upon whose good will
and confidence the obtaining of much valuable information depends, and
his former satisfactory service as a mate in the Coast and Geodetic Survey,
all of which tend to show his qualifications for the position.
WM H TAFT
THE WHITE HOUSE,
April 18, 1970.
[No. 1191.]
