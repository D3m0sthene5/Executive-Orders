EXECUTIVE ORDER.

The heads of the executive departments and independent
offices are directed to furnish to the Secretary of the Interior,
for the preparation of the Official Register, under the act
approved January 12, 1893, the date of the appointment of each
officer and employee and his age and length of service under
the United States, and a statement whether the position occupied
by him is subject to appointment by the President, or is ex-
cepted, competitive, unclassified, temporary, or subject to
contract.

Theodore Roosevelt
WHITE HOUSE.
Approved March 31, 1903.
