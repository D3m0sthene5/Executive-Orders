Executive Orber
CORONADO NATIONAL FOREST
ARIZONA
Under authority of the Act of Congress approved June 4, 1897 (30
Stat., i 1, 34-36), and on the recommendation of the Secretary of Agricul-
ture, it is hereby ordered that the boundaries of the Coronado National
Forest as modified by Proclamation of June 19, 1912, be further modified
b) excluding therefrom an unsurveyed tract of land, in the State of Ari-
zona, which by protraction of the lines of the public survey in the town-
ship would probably be described as follows:
In T. 13 S., R. 15 E., N,4 Sec. 5 and NE/4 Sec. 6;
Gila and Salt River Meridian.
WOODROW WILSON
THE WHITE HOUSE,
7 December, 1916.
