Executive Other.
WHEREAS, The Commissioners of the District of Columbia have requested as
an emergency measure relating to the conduct of the War that the Snare and
Triest Company of New York City, New York, who have contracted to construct
a viaduct to carry the Benning Road over the several railroad tracks, west of
and near Benning, D. C., be excepted from the provisions of the Eight Hour
Law governing the hours of laborers and mechanics employed by them under
their contract;
THEREFORE, Be it ordered, that I, Woodrow WVilson, President of the
United States, do hereby declare that the prosecution of te activity herein-
before mentioned is an emergency measure relating to the conduct of the present
War, and this activity is, for the period of the W'ar, excepted from the provisions
of the Eight Hour Law governing the hours of laborers and mechanics employed
in connection therewith :
PROVIDED, however, that pay be allowed to those entitled thereto in the
construction of the said viaduct with compensation of time and a-half for over
time work spent upon said construction.
WOODROW WILSON
THE WHITE HOUSE,
7 October, 1918.
[No. 2969.]
