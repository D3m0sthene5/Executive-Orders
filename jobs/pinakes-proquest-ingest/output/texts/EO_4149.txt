Executive OIrder~
Pursuant to the authority conferred upon me by the act of June 25, 1910
(36 Statt., 847), entitled "An Act to authorize the President of the United States to
make withdrawals of public lands in certain cases," as amended by the act of
August 24, 1912 (37 Stat., 497), it is hereby ordered that the East Half of Section
29, Township 11 South, Range 17 East, B. M., Idaho, be temporarily withdrawn
from settlement, location, sale or entry, for use as a rifle range for Company C,
Two hundredth Infantry, Idaho National Guard.
'The foregoing order shall remain in full force and effect unless and until
revoked by Congress or order of the President.
CALVIN COOLIDGE
THE WHITE HOUSE,
February 6, 1925.
