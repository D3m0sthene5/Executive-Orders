It is herebyr ordered that on and after July i, 1908, the land within

and proclaimed as the Heppner National Forest and a part of the Blue
Mountains National Forest, shall be known as the Umatilla National
Forest. It is not intended by this order to release any land from reserva-
tion or to reserve any land not heretofore embraced in a National Forest.
