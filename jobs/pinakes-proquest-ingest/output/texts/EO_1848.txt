Executive ether.
William A. Ryan may be appointed to the position of Comptroller
(in charge of all finances and accounting) in the Reclamation Service.
This order is issued upon the recommendation of the Secretary of the
Interior with the concurrence of the Civil Service Commission, and is
based upon Mr. Ryan's unusual qualifications for the position and the close
personal relationship which he will bear to the Secretary of the Interior.
Mr. Ryan has been in the employ of the Interstate Commerce Commission
during the past six years assigned to the duty of examining the books and
accounts of transportation companies, and conducted a notable investi-
gation which covered a period of three years. He is thoroughly familiar
with large financial affairs, it having been his business for several years to
draft the plans for the financing of great industrial corporations, and more-
over he has an intimate personal knowledge of irrigation experiments and
projects. He has been known to the Secretary of the Interior for twventy-
five years, and knows his attitude so perfectly that he will be able to state
his policies and deal with the more than twenty thousand water users with-
out embarrassment to the Secretary.
WOODROW WILSON
THE WHITE HOUSE,
29 October, 1913.
[No. 1848.]
