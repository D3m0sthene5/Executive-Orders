Executive Other.
Executive order of February 26, 1913, detaching the lands north of
the center of White River and east of the center of the Missouri River,
from the Chamberlain, South Dakota, land district, and attaching them to
and making them a part of the Pierre, South Dakota, land district, is
hereby so amended as to eliminate from said order the lands east of the
center of the Missouri River.
WOODROW WILSON
THE WHITE HOUSE,
March 24, 1913.
[No. 1756.]
