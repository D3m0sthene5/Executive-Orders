Executive Orber
Miss Agnes 0. Tansill and Miss Marjorie A. Weaver may be transferred from
the non-apportioned to the apportioned service of the Treasury Department
and promoted to the grade of clerk without compliance with civil service rules.
This order is issued upon the recommendation of the Secretary of the Treasury,
who states that Miss Tansil and Miss Weaver are efficient calculating machine
operators, and that it is believed that their transfer to apportioned positions
will be in the interests of the service.
WOODROW WILSON
THES WHITE HOUSE,
[No. 2582.)
