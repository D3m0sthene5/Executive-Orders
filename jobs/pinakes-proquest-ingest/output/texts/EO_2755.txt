Executive Ober.
It is hereby ordered that Blaley Rock, unsurveyed, represented upon
U. S. Coast and Geodetic Survey Chart No. 6445 as being in Puget Sound
opposite Port Blakely in approximate Latitude 470 35' 45" N., Longitude
1220 28' 45" W., Washington, be, and the same is hereby reserved for light-
house purposes.
WOODROW WILSON
THE WHITE HousE,
14 November, 1917.
