Erecutive ether:
The military reservation of Camp Jossman near the town of Buena
Vista, Island of Guimaris, Philippine Islands, as reserved by Executive
Order dated September 1, 1903 (G. 0. No. 34, W. D., October 13, 1903),
and modified by Executive Order dated November 22, 1909 (G. 0. No.
240, W. D., December 2, 1 909), and the military reservation of Camp
Wilhelm, situated at Lucena, Province of Tayabas, Island of Luzon,
Philippine Islands, as reserved by Executive Order dated September i,
1903 (G. 0. No. 34, W. D., October 13, 190-), having become useless for
military purposes and having been abandoned as military stations, they are
hereby restored to the control of the Government of the Philippine
Islands, under the provisions of Section 12 of the Act of Congress
approved July 1, 1902, entitled "An Act temporarily to provide for the
administration of the affairs of civil government in the Philippine Islands,
and for other purposes," (32 Stat. L. 695), and will no longer be held in
reservation for military purposes.
WM H TAFT
THE WHITE HOUSE,.
September 30, 1912.
[No. 1617j.]
