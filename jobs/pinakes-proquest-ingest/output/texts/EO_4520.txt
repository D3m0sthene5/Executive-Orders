Mrs. T. R. Egbert may be employed as clerk or typewriter at the United
States Naval Academy, Annapolis, Maryland, without regard to the examination
requirements of the civil service act and. rules.
This Order is issued upon recommendation of the Secretary of the Navy.
Mrs. Egbert's husband was Ensign Edmund W. Egbert who met his death in the
line of duty, leaving Mrs. Egbert one minor child 4o support, when the submarine
S-51 sank on September 25, 1925.
The Civil Service Commission is unable to concur in this recommendation.
Done for charity.
CALVIN COOLiDGE
THE WHITE HOUSE,
October 5, 1926.
