Executive Order
Mr. Jacob B. DeHart may be reinstated to a clerical position in the Treasury
Department without regard to the provisions of the civil service rules requiring
examination for advancement to clerical positions from subclerical positions. This
order is issued on the recommendation of the Secretary of the Treasury, who states
that Mr. DeHart was employed with a subclerical civil service status in several
branches of the Treasury Department from 1905 to 1924, with the exception of
about one month, and that he is suffering from an injury incurred in the Spanish-
American War which makes it impossible to fill a position where manual labor is
required and it is desired to assign him to a position in the United States Coast
Guard.
The Civil Service Commission submits the order without prejudice but does
not concur in recommending its issuance.
Done for charity.
