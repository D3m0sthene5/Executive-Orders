Executive rer
Mr. Lawvrence A. Baker may be appointed a clerk in the competitive
class of the Post Office Department without examination. The Civil
Service Comm~ission concurs in the recommendation of the Postmaster
General for the appointment.
The Postmaster General states that for the past three years the De-
partment has been engaged in a campaign of reorganizing post offices to
systematize and standardize methods, which has saved in the aggregate the
services of hundreds of employees and effected many other important
economies; that Mr. Baker, who was appointed to the excepted position
,)f confidential clerk to the First Assistant Postmaster General July i,
1913, has in the discharge of his duties become an expert on reorganization
of this character; and that in order to conserve the special and valuable
knowledge he has gained on this subject and utilize it to the fullest extent
in the extension of reorganization to all post offices his appointment to a
classified position is requested.
WOODROW WILSON
THE WHITE HOUSE,
I December, 1915.
