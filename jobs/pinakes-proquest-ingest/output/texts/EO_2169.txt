Executive Orber
SEQUOIA-KERN NATIONAL FORESTS
CAT 4 IY'OH\NIA
Under authority of the Act of Congress of June 4, 1897 (30 Stat.,
11), and upon recommendation of the Secretary of Agriculture, it is hereby
ordered that on and after July 1, 1915, all the lands included within the
boundaries of the Kern National Forest, California, as fixed and defined
by proclamation of November 23, 1914 (No. 1288), be transferred to and
administered as a part of the Sequoia National Forest.
It is intended by this Executive Order for economy of administration
to merge into one National Forest, thereafter to be known as the Sequoia
National Forest, all the lands on July 1, 1915, in the Kern and Sequoia
National Forests, and the boundaries of the Sequoia National Forest as
they exist at that date are hereby modified accordingly.
This Executive Order is not intended to add to the Sequoia National
Forest any lands which on July 1, 1915, are not embraced in the Kern
National Forest or to release from reservation for National Forest purposes
any lands at that date within the Kern National Forest.
WOODROW WILSON
THE WHITE HOUSE,
13 April, 1916.
[No. 2169.]
