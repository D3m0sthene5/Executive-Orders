Executive Orbet
Mrs. Penelope L. Matthews may be appointed to a clerical position in the
classified service without regard to the civil service rules.
Mrs. Matthews is the widow of William Clarence Matthews, formerly Assist-
ant Attorney General, who at death left her without adequate resources. She is a
graduate of Tuskegee Institute with several years' experience in educational work.
This order is issued upon the statement of the Postmaster General that he would
interpose no objection to her appointment in the Post Office Department.
The Civil Service Commission does not concur.
For charity.
CALVIN COOLIDGE
THE WHITE HOUSE,
June 283, 19.28
