Executive Orbet
The executive order of January 17~, 1873, prohibiting the holding of federal
and state offices at the same time is hereby amended so as to permit the appoint-
ment as special agents of persons holding state territorial, or municipal positions
when such action is deemed necessary by the Secretary of Labor to secure a
more efficient administration of any law coming within the purview of the
Department of Labor. - ,
WARREN G HARDING
THE WHITE HousE,
January 2, 1923.
