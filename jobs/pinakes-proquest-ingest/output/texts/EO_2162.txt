TOIYABE-MOAPA NATIONAL FORESTS
NE8VADA
Under authority of the Act of Congress of June 4, 1897 (30 Stat., rI)
and upon recommendation of the Secretary of Agriculture, it is hereby
ordered that on and after July i, 1915, all the lands inclu& d within the
boundaries of the Moapa National Forest, Nevada, as fixed and defined
by proclamation of December 8, 1911 (No. 1174), be transferred to and
administered as a part of the Toiyabe National Forest, Nevada.
It is intended by this Executive Order, for economy of administra-
tion, to merge into one National Forest, thereafter to be known as the
Toiyabe National Forest, all the lands which on July j, 1915, are within
the Moapa and the Toiyabe National Forests, and the boundaries of the
Toiyabe National Forest, as they exist at that date, are hereby modified
accordingly.
This Executive Order is not intended to add to the Toiyabe National
Forest any lands which on July 1, 1915, are not embraced within the
Moapa National Forest, nor to release from resrvation for National
Forest purposes any lands at that date within the Moapa National Forest.
WOODROW WILSON
THE WHITE HOUSE,
6 April, 1915.
[No. 2162.]
