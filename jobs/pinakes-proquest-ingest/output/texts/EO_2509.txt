Executive rer
Mr. Frank Kearney may be promoted from the position of Messenger
to the one thousand dollar clerical grade in the classified service of the
Treasury Department. This order is issued on the recommendation of the
Secretary of the Treasury, who states that Mr. Kearney is a hard working,
faithful and efficient employee, who has given more than thirty-five years
service to the Government, and that on July I9, 191 1, while in the per-
formance of his official duties and without negligence on his part, he
received injuries from which he will never recover; yet he performs all his
duties most capably; that there is a vacancy in the one thousand dollar
clerical grade in the office of the Comptroller of the Treasury for which
Mr. Kearney is in every way qualified because of his long experience in
that office, and it is believed that his promotion to this grade will be in the
interests of the service.
WOODROW WILSON
THE WHITE HOUSE,
5 January, 1917.
