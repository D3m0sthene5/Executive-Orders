Execcutive ether.
Mr. John J. Donahue, who has occupied an excepted position of
special examiner in the Bureau of Pensions since January 19, 1907, may
be transferred to an appropriate competitive position in the same bureau.
The Secretary of the Interior recommends this action because Mr. Donahue
has performed the duties required of him in a very efficient manner and
is considered one of the best men in the field service in the handling of
intricate and difficult cases.
The Civil Service Commission does not concur in recommending the
order.
THE WHITE HOUSE,
January 9/k, 1913.
WM H TAFT
[No. 1679.]
