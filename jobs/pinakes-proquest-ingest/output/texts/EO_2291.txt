By virtue of the power and authority vested in and conferred upon
me by the laws of the United States in that behalf made and provided, it
is hereby ordered that the lands hereinafter more particularly described
shall be, and the same are hereby withdrawn from settlement, entry, sale
and any and every other manner and form of appropriation or disposition
under the public land laws of the United States, and said lands and all of
them are and will be reserved to the United States so long as the continu-
ance of such reservation shall be necessary to provide for the water supply
of Fort 1). A. Russell and the City of Cheyenne, in the County of Laramie,
and State of Wyoming, and until such time as Congress or the President
of the United States shall otherwise direct, and the withdrawal hereby
made shall be unqualified and absolute, except to the extent that any of the
lands herein described may be covered by valid existing rights heretofore
initiated pursuant to any of the public land laws of the United States,
and, further, that this withdrawals is in addition to the withdrawal made by
proclamation~ No. 1259 on December 20, 1913.
