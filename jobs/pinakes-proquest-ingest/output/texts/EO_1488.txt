Executive Orber,
Mr. Edward \V. Cady may be reinstated as ani examiner in the
Patent Office, the year limit upon eligibility for reinstatement being
waived in his case.
Mr. Cady had more than ten years' experience as a patent examiner
prior to his separation in i886, and from that time until within a fewv
months ago he has been engaged in the practice of his profession of
patent law. He recently served for nearly three months as a patent
examiner under temporary appointment and his work, both as to quantity
and quality, proved satisfactory. The Commissioner of Patents states
that Mr. Cady is in full mental and physical vigor and his extended
experience would make him a very valuable assistant examiner.
In view of the technical character of the position and the evident
qualifications of Mr. Cady for the position, the Commission joins with the
Department in recommending the issuance of this order.
