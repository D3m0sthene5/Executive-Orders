Executive Ocer
AUTHORIZING THE ALIEN PROPERTY CUSTODIAN
TO SELL CERTAIN PROPERTY AT PRIVATE SALE
By virtue of the authority vested in me by an Act of Congress entitled
"An act to define, regulate, and punish trading with the enemy and for other
purposes", approved October 6, 1917, known as the " Trading with the Enemy
Act", and the amendment to such act embodied in "An act making appropria-
tions to supply urgent deficiencies in the appropriations for the fiscal year
ending June 30, 1918, and prior fiscal years on account of war expenses and for
other purposes", approved March 28, 1918, and the amendment to such act
embodied in " An act making appropriations to supply deficiencies in appro-
priations for the fiscal year ending June 30, 1919, and prior fiscal years on
account of war expenses, and for other purposes", approved November 4' 1918,
to make certain orders such as that herein contained, I hereby, in the publicinterest, make the following determination, order, rule and regulation:
The Alien Property Custodian is hereby authorized to sell at private
sale, at such time and place as to himt or to his authorized agent may seem
advisable, without public or other advertisement, for and in consideration ofthe sum of Three hundred twelve thousand ($3 12,ooo) Dollars, certain' real
estate now being held and administered by him as such Alien Property
Custodian, and heretofore, upon requirement, conveyed, transferred; assignedand delivered to him as the property of HELMUTH H1EYL and RICINHARD HEY,,
residing in Germany, whom, after investigation, he did determine to be enemies
within the purview of said Act as amended, which said real estate is situate inthe City of Miwukee, County of Milwaukee, State of Wisconsin, and described
as follows, to-wit:--
Lot 1 2 and the north 40 feet of Lot 11 in Block 9, 7th Ward, im-proved by premises known as 430-436 East Water. Street and 8 1-85Mason Street, " Kirby House"
My reasons for the foregoing determination, order, rule and regulation are :
(a) An advantageous offer for the purchase of the said real estate has
been received by the Alien Property Custodian;
(b) An attempted sale at public auction would be a useless formality and
would involve unnecessary expenses, delay and inconvenience, which should be
avoided.
CALVIN COOL IDGE
THE~ WHITE HOUSE,
May 15, 1924.
