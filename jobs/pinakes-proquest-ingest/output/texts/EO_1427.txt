Mr. Edwin H. Peery may be reinstated to the position of clerk,
class 4, in the Office of the Judge Advocate General without regard to the
length of his separation from the service. Mr. Peery was separated on
January 28, [909, from the position of law assistant at $4000 per annum
under the Provisional Government of Cuba by the termination of that
Provisional Government after many years of experience in legal work, in
the classified service, and in view of his qualifications and of the needs of
the Department for a person of his training and experience, the Commis-
sion concurs in the recommendation for the issuance of an Executive order
permitting his reinstatement.
WM H TAFT
THE WHITE HOUSE,
Octobe.' 28, 1911.
