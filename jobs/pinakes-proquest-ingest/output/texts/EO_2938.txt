Under authority of the Act of Congress approved June 25, 1910 (36 Stat.,
847), as amended by the Act of August 24, 1912 37 Stat., 497), it is hereby
ordered that, with a view of enlarging the withdrawal made by executive order
of May 2, 1918 (2854), the tract of land in Alasa lying south of the parallel of
58 degrees 5o minutes north latitude and west of the meridian of 137 degrees 40
minutes west longitude, and bounded on the west by the waters of the Gulf of
Alaska, be, and the same is hereby, temporarily withdrawn from settlement,
location, sale or entry, except as provided by said act, for the purpose of supply-
ing timber for use in the construction of aeroplanes for the United S ..ates.
WOODROW WILSON
THE WHITE HOUSE,
16 August, 1918.
