Mrs. Rose Tyler, who has passed the required tests for appointment
under the labor regulations and is well qualified, may be appointed a char-
woman without reference to her relative standing among the competitors.
She has been deserted by her husband, who is a Spanish War veteran,
and is nowv required to support herself and four little children, one of
whom is blind.
The Civil Service Commission does not recommend this order.
