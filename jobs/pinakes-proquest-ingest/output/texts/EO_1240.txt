Executive Orber.
Schedule A of positions excepted from examination under Rule II,
clause 3, of the Civil Service Rules is hereby amended by striking out
paragraph i of Sub-division III relating to the Treasury Department,
which reads:
L. Not exceeding one solicitor to the Collector at the port of
New York, if authorized by the Secretary of the Treasury.
The remaining paragraphs of the sub-division will he renumbered
accordingly.
This order is made on the recommendation of the Secretary of the
Treasury and the Collector of Customs at New York, with the concurrence
of the Civil Service Commission, for the reason that it is believed that the
position should, in accordance with the announced policy of the Depart-
ment, be made competitive and a part of the permanent staff of the Cus-
toms Service.
