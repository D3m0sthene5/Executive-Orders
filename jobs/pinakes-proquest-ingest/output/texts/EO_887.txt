RIO GRANDE NATIONAL FOREST
COLORADO
It is hereby ordered that on and after July 1, 1908, the land within the
boundary shown on the attached diagram, heretofore set apart, reserved,
and proclaimed as parts of the San Juan and Cochetopah National Forests,
shall be known as the Rio (Grande National Forest. It is not intended
by this order to release any land from reservation or to reserve any land
not heretofore embraced in a National Forest.
THEODORE ROOSEVELT
THE WHITE HOUSE.
