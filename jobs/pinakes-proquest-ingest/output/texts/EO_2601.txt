Executive Ober.
Mr. D. Brockman Winfrey may be appointed a clerk in the competitive
classified service of the Treasury Department without compliance with civil
service rules.
This order is issued on the recommendation of the Secretary of the Treasury,
who states that Mr. Winfrey has been serving in the excepted position of private
secretary to the Director of the Mint since November i , 1915, and that on
account of his thorough knowledge of the general work of the Bureau of the
Mint and the efficient manner in which he has performed his duties, it is believed
that his permanent appointment will be in the interests of the service.
