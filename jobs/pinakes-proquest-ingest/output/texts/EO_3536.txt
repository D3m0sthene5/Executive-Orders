Mr. Mason Bragg may be reinstated in the postal service at Beckley, West
Virginia; without regard to the length of his separation from the service.
Mr. Bragg was removed from the position of clerk in the Beckley post
office on August 7, 1917, charged with disloyalty. The case has since been re-
investigated and it has been ascertained that an injustice was done Mr. Bragg
in authorizing his removal. His reinstatement as a clerk in the post office is
recommended by the Postmaster General.
The Civil Service Commission does not concur in the recommendation.
WARREN G HARDING
THE WHITE HOUSE,
August 17, 1921.
