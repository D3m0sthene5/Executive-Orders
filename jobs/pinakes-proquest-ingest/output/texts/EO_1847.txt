Mr. John H. Jenkins may be reinstated to the position of Immigrant
Inspector in the Immigration Service without regard to the length of his
separation from the service.
This order is issued upon the recommendation of the Secretary of
Labor who, after a thorough investigation of the matter, is satisfied that
Mr. Jenkins' dismissal from the service was not warranted by the facts.
The Civil Service Commission does not concur in recommending the
issuance of an Executive Order in this case.
WOODROW WILSON
THE WHITE HOUSE,
29 October, 1913.
[No. 1847.]
