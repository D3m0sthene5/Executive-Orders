Executive Order
Mr. William Leroy Fairbanks may be transferred from chief clerk of
the American Embassy at London to a clerkship in the Department of
Commerce. He has served since 1908, at first in the State Department,
then in the American Legation at Peking, and since iqio in his present
position.
The Secretary of Commerce, with the concurrence of the Civil Serv-
ice Commission, recommends the issuance of this order in view of the
special qualifications of Mr. Fairbanks for the work of the Bur-eau of
Foreign and Domestic Commerce.
WOODROW WILSON
THE WHITE HOUSE,
4t May, 1915.
