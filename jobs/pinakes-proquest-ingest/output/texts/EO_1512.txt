Executive ether
Animal Quarantine Station
It is hereby ordered that all the public lands on Near Island, being a
small island lying off the northeastern shore of Kodiak Island, Alaska, and
near the village of Kodiak, be and the same are hereby. withdrawn from
settlement, location, sale, or entry, and reserved for the use of the Depart-
ment of Agriculture as a temporary pasture and animal quarantine station,
subject to all the provisions, limitations, exceptions and conditions con-
tained in the Act of Congress of June 25, 1910, entitled "An Act to
authorize the President of the United States to make withdrawals of public
lands in certain cases," (36 Stat., 847).
WM H TAFT
THE WHITE HOUSE,
