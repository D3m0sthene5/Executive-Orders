Under authority contained in the act of Congress approved' June 25, 1910
(36 Stat., 847), as amended by the act of August 24; 1912 (37 Stat., 497), and
subject to the conditions, provisions and limitations of said acts, it is hereby
ordered that the public lands in T. 18 N., R. 12 E., N. M. P. M., New Mexico,
be and the same are hereby withdrawn from settlement, location, sale, entry and
all forms of appropriation, pending a resurvey of said. township under the act'
of Congress approved March 3, 1909 (35 Stat., 845); this withdrawal to remain
in full force and effect from the date hereof until the resurvey is duly approved
and accepted and the approved. plat thereof is officially filed in the proper U. S.
local land office, unless another date be finced by the Commissioner of the
General Land Office, whereupon this withdrawal will become of no effect.
