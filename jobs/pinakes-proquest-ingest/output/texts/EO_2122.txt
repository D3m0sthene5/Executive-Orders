Executive Order
Mr. Newton C. Llewellyn may be reinstated as a carrier in the
Knoxville, Tennessee, post office without regard to the length of his
separation from the service.
Mr. Liewellyn entered the postal service as a substitute carrier at
Knoxville, on January 18, 1895, and remained in the service until February
28, 1907, when he resigned.
The Postmaster General states that Mr. Llewellyn's record is good,
that it is a meritorious case, and that the Department has no objection to
the issuance of an order.
The Civil Service Commission does not recommend this order.
WOODROW WILSON
THE WHITE HOUSE,
20 Jaanuary, 1915j.
