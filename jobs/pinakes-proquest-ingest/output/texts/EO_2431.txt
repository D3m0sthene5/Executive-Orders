Executive Orber.
Albert W. Smith may be promoted to a sub-clerical position in the
Treasury Department without reference to the civil service rules.
This order is issued upon the recommendation of the Secretary of the
Treasury, who states that Mr. Smith has served very satisfactorily as an
unclassified laborer in the Treasury Department since April 25, 1906, and
has passed the sub-clerical examination, but, being from the District of
Columbia, is not eligible under the civil service rules for promotion to an
apportioned position.
The Civil Service Commission does not concur in the recommendation.
WOODROW WILSON
THE WHITE HOUSE,
r August, 1916.
