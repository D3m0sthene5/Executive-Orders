Executive Order
MADISON NATIONAL FOREST
MONTANA
It is hereby ordered that on and after July I, 19oS, the land within the
boundaries shown on the attached diagram, heretofore set apart, reserved,
and proclaim~ed as parts of the Madison and Gallatin National Forests, shall
be known as the 'Madison National Forest. The remaining portions of the
Madison National Forest shall continue reservedl and constitute a part of
the Gallatin National Forest. It is not intended by this order to release
any land from reservation or to reserve any land not heretofore embraced
in a National Forest.
