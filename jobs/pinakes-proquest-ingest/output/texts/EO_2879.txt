]Executive Orber
Under the authority and provisions of the Act of Congress approved June
25, 1910 (36 Stat., 847), as amended by the Act of August 24, 1912 (3 Stat.,
497), it is hereby ordered that all public lands in T. 20 S., R. 9 W.; Ts. 21 S.,
,Rs. 8,~9 and xo W., and Ts. 22 S., Rs. 8 and io W., New Mexico be and the
same are hereby temporarily withdrawn from settlement, entry, sale or other
forms of disposal, and reserved for military purposes for occupation and use
for artillery practice, subject to any existing vested rights in and to the same.
WOODROW WILSON
THE WHITE HOUSE,
6 June, 1918.
