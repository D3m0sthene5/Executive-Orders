Executive Order.
In accordance with the provisions of Section 2253 of the Revised
Statutes of the United States, and by virtue of the authority therein given,
it is hereby ordered that townships eighteen, nineteen and twenty north of
the Base line, in ranges forty-six, forty-seven, forty-eight, forty-nine, fifty,
fifty-one, fifty-twvo, fifty-three, fifty-four, fifty-five, flfty.six, fifty-seven, and
fifty-eight west of the Sixth Principal Meridian in the State of Nebraska
be detached from the North Platte land district and attached to and made
a part of the Alliance land district in said state, and the business and
archives pertaining to the lands embraced within said townships be trans-
ferred from the land office at North Platte, Nebraska, to the land office at
Alliance, Nebraska.
