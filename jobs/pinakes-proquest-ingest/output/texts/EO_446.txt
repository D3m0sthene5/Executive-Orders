Executive Order.
Executive order of November 18, 1905, directing, under authority of
Sections 2250 and 2252 of the Revised Statutes of the United States, the
discontinuance of the Bent land district in the State of Colorado, with
office at Lamar, Colorado, and the transferrance of the lands, business and
archives of said land district to, and their consolidation with the Arkansas
Valley land district, with office at Pueblo, Colorado, is hereby revoked.
THEODORE ROOSEVELT
TtIE WHITE HOUSE,
111ay' 6, 1906.
