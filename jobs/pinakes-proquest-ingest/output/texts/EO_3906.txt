Executive Order
AUTHORIZING THE ALIEN PROPERTY CUSTODIAN TO SELL CERTAIN
PROPERTY AT PRIVATE SALE.
By virtue of the authority vested in me by an Act of Congress entitled
"An act to define, regulate, and punish trading with the enemy and for other
purposes", approved October 6, 1917, known as the "Trading with the Enemy
Act", and the amendment to such act embodied in "An act making appropria-
tions to supply urgent deficiencies in the appropriations for the fiscal year
ending June 30, 1918, and prior fiscal years on account of war expenses and for
other purposes", approved March 28, 1918, and the amendment to such act
embodied in " An act making appropriations to supply deficiencies in appropria-
tions for the fiscal year ending June 30, 1 919 , and prior fiscal years on account
of war expenses, and for other purposes ", approved November 4, 1918, to make
certain orders such as that herein contained, I hereby, in the public interest,
make the following determination, order, rule and regulation:
The Alien Property Cusodian is hereby authorized to sell at private sale,
at such time and place as to him or to his authorized agent may seem advisable,
without public or other advertisement, certain real estate now being held and
administered by him as such Alien Property Custodian, and heretofore, upon
requirement, conveyed, transferred, assigned and delivered to himd as the prop-
erty of E RNST HAERTEL, of Leipzig, Germany, whom, after investigation,
he did determine to be an enenmy within the purview of said Act as amended,
which said real estate is described as follows, to-wit.
Lot Fifteen (15) in Block Eighteen hundred seventy-nine (1879),
Section Seven (7), Borough of Manhattan, City, County and State of
New York adknown as premises 233-235 W'est 10 7 th Street.
My reasons for the foregoing determination, order, rule and regulation are:
(a) An advantageous offer for the purchase of the said real estate has been
received by the Alien Property Custodian;
(b) Investigation has shown it to be impossible to sell the property at a
better price;
(c) An attempted sale at public auction would be a useless formality and
would involve unnecessary expenses, delay and inconvenience, which should be
avoided.
