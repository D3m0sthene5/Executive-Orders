Executive Order.
It is ordered that Colonel 0. H. Ernst, Corps of Engineers, U. S.
Army, now a member of the International Waterways Commission created
by section four of the Act approved June 13, 1902, shall continue on duty
as a member of said Commission after his retirement with the rank of
Brigadier General on the 27th instant.
The compensation to be allowed to General Ernst for this service
from and after the date of his retirement from active service shall be the
same as that allowed to the other American members of the Commission.
To cover the expenses of travel on duty for the Commission, he shall be
allowed mileage at the rate of 7 per mile.
THEODORE ROOSEVELT
THE WHITE HousE,
June 23, 1906.
