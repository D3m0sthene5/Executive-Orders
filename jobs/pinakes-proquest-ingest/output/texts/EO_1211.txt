)Executive order.
In accordance with the provisions of Section 2253 of the Revised
Statutes of the U nited States, and by virtue of the authority therein given,
it is hereby ordered that:
All that part of the Yakima Indian Reservation situated within the
Vancouver and Walla Walla land districts in the State of Washington,
be detached from said land districts and attached to and made a part of
the North XYakima land district, and the business and archives pertaining
thereto transferred from the land offices at Vancouver and Walla Walla to
the land office at North Yakima.
WM H TAFT
THE WHITE HOUSE,
June 13 1910.
[No. 1211.]
