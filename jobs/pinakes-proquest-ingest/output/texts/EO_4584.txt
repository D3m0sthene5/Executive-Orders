By virtue of the authority vested in me as President of the United States of
America, and pursuant to the provisions of Section 6 of the Act of Congress
approved March 12, 1926, entitled "An Act authorizing, the -use for permanent
construction at military posts of the proceeds from the sale of surplus War Depart-
ment real property, and authorizing the sale ~f ;certain military reservations, and
for other purposes," the Secretary of Commerce having made application to the
Secretary of War for the transfer thereof and given specific reasons therefor, within
ninety days after the approval of the above mentioned Act of Congress, and such
action having received my approval, it is hereby ordered that the portion of the
Ship Island Military Reservation, in the State of Mississippi, hereinafter described,
be and the same is hereby transferred to the Department of Commerce for use as
a lighthouse reservation:
All of that portion of the Ship Island Military Reservation located
off the coast of Mississippi, bounded on the North by the low water
shore line of Ship Island; bounded on the East by a true North and
South line approximately 400 feet East of the center of the present
lighthouse tower and indicated by two stone monuments on said
boundary marked "USLHE", which said tower is located in latitude
300 12' 48" North and longitude 880 57' 56" West; bounded on the
SSouth by the low water shore line of Ship Island, and bounded on the
West by a true North and South line 1400 feet West of and parallel
with the East line hereinbefore described.
