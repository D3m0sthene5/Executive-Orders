Executive order.
John M. Masten may be appointed to a position as Investigator in the
General Accounting Office without compliance with Civil Service rules. Mr.
Masten has had wide and varied experience in investigating work in the Post
Office Department and it is believed that in view of his qualifications and
experience in this line of work it would be in the interest of the service to make
this appointment.
WARREN G HARDING
THES WHrIE HousE,
April 22, 1922.
