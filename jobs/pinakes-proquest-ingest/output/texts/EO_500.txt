Executive Order.
In pursuance of the Act of Congress approved June 28, 1894, (28
Stat. 96), making a legal public holiday the first Monday of September in
each year, being celebrated and known as Labor's Holiday, emlloyees
of the Washington Navy Yard wvill be excused from work on Monday,
September 3, 1906.
THEODORE ROOSEVELT
AUGUST 29, 1906.
