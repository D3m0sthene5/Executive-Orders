Employees appointed without examination under exceptions contained in
War emergency Executive orders, to positions which were then and are now in the
classified service, and who are serving in classified positions on the date of this
order, are hereby included in the classified service ; and unskilled laborers still in
service who were appointed under such orders are hereby included in the service
with the same status as though they had been employed under the labor regula-
tions; PROVIDED, That this order shall not apply to employees on the Isthmus
of Panama or to employees on a consulting basis.
CALVIN COOL IDGE
THE WHITE HOUSE,
August 22, 1925.
