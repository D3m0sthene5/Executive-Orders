It is hereby ordered, under authority contained in the Act of February 8,
1887 (24 Stat. L., 388-389), that the trust period on allotents made to the
Indians. the Winnibigoshish Reservation in Minnesota, which trust period
expires during the calendar year 1922, be, and the same is hereby extended for
a period of ten years from the date of expiration.
WARREN G HARDING
THE WHITm HOUSE~,
January 12, 1922.
