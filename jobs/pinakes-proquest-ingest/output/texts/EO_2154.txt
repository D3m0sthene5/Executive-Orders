Executive Orber.
The provisions of the Executive Order of January 17, 1873, prohibiting
federal employes from holding office under any state, territorial or municipal
government, are hereby waived to permit Mark Eisner, who has been
appointed Collector of Internal Revenue for the Third District of the State
of New York, to act in the capacity of Delegate to the Constitutional Con-
vention of the Statc of New York, he having been duly elected thereto
before his appointment as Collector. Mr. Eisp:;r is a man who would be of
great value in the Constitutional Convention, and his services in the Con-
vention will not interfere with his work as Collector of Internal Revenue.
This order is recommended by the Acting Secretary of the Treasury.
WOODROW WILSON
THE WHITE HousE,
25 March, 1915.
