Executive oer.
Mr. Francis J. Reinhard, of Indiana, may be appointed to the position
of Law Clerk in the office of the Solicitor of the Treasury, without refer-
ence to the Civil Service Rules.
This order is issued upon the recommendation of the Attorney Gen-
\eral, who states that the relationship existing between the Solicitor and
the man holding this position is quite confidential; that the duties of the
office require a man of considerable experience as a lawyer; that Solicitor
Becker of the Treasury, who personally knows Mr. Reinhard, is familiar
with his qualifications, has confidence in him, and has recommended that
he be appointed, and that an effort has been made to find some one within
the civil service possessing the special qualifications needed, but without
success. The Attorney General further states that he believes the interests
of the public service would be subserved by exempting the appointment.
WOODROW WILSON'
THE WHITE HOUSE,
6 August, 19,15.
