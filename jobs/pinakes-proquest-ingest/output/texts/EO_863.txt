CASCADE NATIONAL FOREST
OREGON

It is hereby ordered that on and after July 1, 1908, the land within

the boundary shown on the attached diagram, heretofore set apart, reserved,

and proclaimed as part of the Cascade National Forest, shall be known as

the Cascade National Forest. The remaining portions of the Cascade

National Forest shall continue reserved and constitute parts of the Oregon,

Umpqua, and Crater National Forests. It is not intended by this order

to release any land from reservation or to reserve any land not heretofore

embraced in a National Forest.
