Dr. David C. Hanawalt, who served as a veterinary inspector in the
Bureau of Animal Industry from March I, 1900, to October 15, 1900,
when he resigned voluntarily, may be reinstated, the year limit fixed in the
rules being waived, a comparatively short period having elapsed since his
resignation.
In view of Dr. Hanawalt's good record and demonstrated efficiency
while in the service of the Government, and of the comparatively short
period which has elapsed since his resignation, the Commission joins the
Department in recommending that the year limit fixed in the rules be
waived.
WM H TAFT
THE WHITE HOUSE,
February 20th, 1913.
