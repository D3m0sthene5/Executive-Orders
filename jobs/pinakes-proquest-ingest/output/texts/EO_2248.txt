Mr. Shelly Inch may be appointed a clerk in the post office at.Placer-
yille, California, without reference to civil service rules. This order is
issued on the recommendation of the Postmaster General, who states that
Mr. Inch was appointed postmaster at Placerville in 1898 and after serving
sixteen years in that capacity he was recommended by his successor for
appointment as a temporary clerk,.in which capacity he has been employed
since the new postmaster took charge of the office. In the opinion of the
Postmaster General Mr. Inch is. thoroughly qualified for the position. The
Civil Service Commission does not concur in the recommendation for the
issuance of this order.
WOODROW WILSON
THE WHITE HOUSE,
23 .September, 1915.
