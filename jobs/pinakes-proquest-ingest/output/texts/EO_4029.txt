Executive Orbet
It is hereby ordered under authority contained in Section 5 of the Act of
February 8, 1887 (24 Stat. L., 388), that the trust period on allotments made
to the Mexican-Kickapoo Indians in Oklahoma, which trust expires during the
calendar year one thousand nine hundred and twenty-four, be and is hereby
extended for a period of ten years.
CALVIN COOL IDGE
THE WHITE HOUSE,
June 19, 1924.
[No. 4029.]
