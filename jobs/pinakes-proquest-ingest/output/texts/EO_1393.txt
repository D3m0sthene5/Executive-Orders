Executive Order.
oe
It is hereby ordered that an unsurveyed island, represented upon
U. S. Coast and Geodetic Survey Chart No. 8160 as being in Clarence
Strait, about 1600 feet in an easterly direction from “Lincoln Rock,”
locally known as “Lincoln Island” situate approximately in Latitude
M4 36° 03’ 26” North, Longitude 132° 41' 08” West from Greenwich, be, and
the same is hereby reserved for lighthouse purposes, subject to any exist-
ing legal rights thereto.

THe Wuire House
Aug. 4, 1911.
