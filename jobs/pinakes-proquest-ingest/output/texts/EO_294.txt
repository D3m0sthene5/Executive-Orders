EXECUTIVE ORDER.

The requirements as to United States citizenship may be
waived for applicants for examination for the positions of offi-
cers of ships of the Coast and Geodetic Survey on duty in the
Philippine Islands: under such regulations as may be provided by
the United States Civil Service Commission.
