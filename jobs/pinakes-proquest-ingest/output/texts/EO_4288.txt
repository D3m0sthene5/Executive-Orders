Mrs. Berthe T. R. Gilchrist may be reinstated in the classified civil service
without regard to the length of her separation from the service.
This order is issued upon the recommendation of the Attorney General and
the United States Attorney for the District of Columbia, who state that Mrs.
Gilchrist is the widow of William Gilchrist, deceased, late Assistant United States
Attorney for the District of Columbia, and that she formerly had a classified status
in the civil service.
The Civil Service Commission submits the order without prejudice but is
without authority to recommend its issuance.
Done for charity.
CALVIN COOL IDGE
THE WHITE Housz,
August 22, 1925.
