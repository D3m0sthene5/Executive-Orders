Mrs. Letitia E. Stewart may be reinstated to a clerical position in the classified
service without regard to the ime limit on reinstatement under the civil service
rules.
Mrs. Stewart is the widow of James M. Stewart who at the time of his death,
February 13, 1928, had a record of more than twenty-eight years' Government
service. She served in the War Department for approximately three years and
ten months and lacks less than two months' service of being eligible for reinstate-
ment under the rules.
This order is issued upon the recommendation of the Secretary of War who
states that Mrs. Stewart rendered extremely efficient and conscientious service.
The Civil Service Commission submits the case without prejudice, being without
authority to concur.
For charity.
CALVIN COOLIDGE
THE WHITE HousE,
July 3, 1928.
