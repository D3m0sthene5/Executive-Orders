Executive Orber
TAHOE NATIONAL FOREST
CALIFORNIA AND) NEVADA
It is hereby ordered that on and after July ,190o8, the land within the
boundaries shown on the attached diagram, heretofore set apart, reserved,
and proclaimed as parts of the Tahoe, Stanislaus, and Plurnas National
Forests, shall be known as the Tahoe National Forest. The remaining
portions of the Tahoe National Forest shall continue reserved and con-
stitute parts of the Mono and Plumas National Forests. It is not intended
by this order to release any land from reservation or to reserve any land
not heretofore embraced in, a National Forest.
THEODORE ROOSEVELT
THE WHITE HOUSE.
July 2, 1908.
