By virtue of the authority vested in me by the Act of Congress approved
August 1, 1914, making appropriations for the sundry civil expenses of the
Government. for the fiscal year ended June 30, 1915, the ports of Monticello and
Houlton, in customs collection district No. 1 (Maine and New Hampshire), are
hereby abolished, effective March 1, 1925.
By virtue of the same authority a new port ofenty is hereby established
which shall be known as the port of Houlton, in customs collection district
No. 1 (Maine and New Hampshire), and which shall comprise the townships of
Joulton, Monticello, Littleton, Hodgdon, Cary, Amity, Orient, Weston, Danforth
and Forest City, effective March 1, 1925.
CALVIN COOL IDGEJ
THE WRITE HOUSE,
February 14, 1926.
