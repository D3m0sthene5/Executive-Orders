Executive Orber.
Lot 6, Sec. 30, and Lots 2 and 3 Sec. 31, all in T. 138 north, Range
29 wvest, 5 th Principal Meridian, in the State of Minnesota, which lands
were, by proclamation dated August 27, 1892 (No. 957) withheld from
sale or disposal under the various acts for the sale and disposal of the public
lands, in pursuance of the acts of March 3, 189 (20 Stat., 370), June 14,
1880 (21 Stat., i 9 and 197), and March 3, 1881 (21 Stat., 481), providing
for the improvement of navigation of the headwaters of the Mississippi
River, are hereby restored to the public domain, it appearing that the lands
are not needed for flowage purposes.
WM H TAFT
THE WHITE HOUSE,
March 30 1909.
