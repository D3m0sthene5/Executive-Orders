Mr. Marion T. Edwards may be appointed to the position of assistant
messenger, qualified in the operation and repair of automobiles, in the
Bureau of Standards at $-20 per annum, without reference to the civil
service rules.
This order is issued upon the recommendation of the Secretary of
Commerce and the Director of the Bureau of Standards, who state that
the position to which it is proposed to appoint Mr. Edwards is a confi-
dential post, that by experience and training Mr. Edwards is especially
qualified for the position, and that his appointment would be in the inter-
ests of good administration.
The Civil Service Commission does not concur in the recommenda-
tion.
WOODROW WILSON
THE WHITE HOUSE,
