Under authority contained in the act of Congress approved June 25, 1910
(36 Stat., 847), as amended by the act of August 24, 1912 (37 Stat., 497), and sub-
ject to the conditions, provisions and limitations of said acts, it is hereby ordered
that the public lands in T. 27 N., R. 113 W., 6th P. M., Wyoming, be and the same
are hereby withdrawn from settlement, location, sale, entry and all forms of appro-
priation, pending a resurvey of said township under the act of Congress approved
March 3, 1909 (35 Stat., 845), as amended by the Joint Resolution of June 25, 1910
(36 Stat., 884); this withdrawal to remain in full force and effect from the date
hereof until the resurvey is duly, approved and accepted and the approved plat
thereof is officially filed in the proper district land office unless another date be
fixed by the Commissioner of the General Land. Office, whereupon this withdrawal
will become of no effect.
CALVIN COOLIDGE
THE WHITE HOUSE
August 1 2,1926.
