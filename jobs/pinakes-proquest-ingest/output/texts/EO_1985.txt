Executive other.
Mrs. Mary L. Brown may be appointed without examination as a
clerk in the War Department in place of her mother, Mrs. Elizabeth F.
Long.
Mrs. Brown has had several years of experience in the Census Office
and with the Immigration Commission where she wvas unusually compe-
tent as statistical clerk and supervising clerk. Mrs. Brown and her
mother belong to a family greatly distinguished for services rendered to
the government in its military and naval branches. In view of these facts
the Secretary of War recommends the appointment of Mrs. Brown.
The Civil Service Commission does not concur in the recommenda-
tion of this order.
WOODROW WILSON
[HE WHITE HOUSE,
8 July, 191#.
[No. 1985.]
