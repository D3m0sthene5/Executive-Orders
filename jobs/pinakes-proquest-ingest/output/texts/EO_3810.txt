Mrs. Maude Morrison may be appointed to a clerical position in the Treasury
Department without regard to the requirements of the civil service rules. This
order is issued upon recommendation of the Secretary of the Treasury, who
states that Mrs. Morrison is the wife of George R. Morrison, who served for
seventeen years in the Treasury Department and four years in the Marine Corps
of the United States, and whose failing health caused his retirement from the
service upon a small annuity.
The Civil Service Commission did not concur in recommending this order.
WARREN G HARDING
THE WHITE HOUSE,
April 9, 1923.
