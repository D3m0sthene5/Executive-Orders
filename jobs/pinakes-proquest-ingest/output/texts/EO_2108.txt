Mrs. John L. Evans may be appointed to the position of charwoman
under the Superintendent of the State, War and Navy Departments
Building without regard to the labor regulations governing appointments
of laborers in Washington.
Mrs. Evans is the widow of john L. Evans who was discharged a
corporal from Company H, 22d United States Infantry, July 12, 1902,
character excellent. From July, 1902, until January 24, 1914, he was
employed in the navy yard in this city. On said date, while working as a
machinist's helper at the navy yard, a hammer flew off the handle and
struck him on the head, from the effects of which he died June 18, 1914,
after a long illness and much suffering, leaving a wife and two small
children.
The Acting Superintendent of the State,War and Navy Departments
Building recommends that an Executive Order be issued authorizing the
appointment of Mrs. Evans.
The Civil Service Commission does not concur in the recommendation.
WOODROW WILSON
THE WHI'rEHOUSE,
19 December, 1914.
