Mr. Charles J. O'Neill, a messenger in the Pension Office at $840
per annum, may be promoted to a clerkship in the Pension Office without
reference to the civil service rules.
Mr. O'Neill has been employed in the Pension Office since June 15,
1893, and has rendered faithful and efficient service during that time; he
is performing with entire satisfaction duties ordinarily performed by clerks
at $900 to $i6oo per annum.
The Secretary of the Interior states that if Mr. O'Neill is rendered
eligible for promotion, lie will take pleasure in approving any recommen-
dation looking to his advancement.
The Civil Service Commission does not recommend this order.
WOODROW WILSON
THE WHITE HOUSE,
27 February, 1915.
[No. 2140.]
