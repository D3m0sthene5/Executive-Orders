WYOMING.
By virtue of the power and authority vested in and conferred upon
me by the laws of the United States in that behalf made and provided, it
is hereby ordered that the lands hereinafter more particularly described
shall be, and the same are hereby withdrawvn from settlement, entry, sale
and from other manner and form of appropriation or disposition under
the public land laws of the United States, and said lands and all of them
are and will be reserved to the United States so long as the continuance
of such reservation shall be necessary to provide for the water supply of
Fort D. A. Russell and the City of Cheyenne, in the County of Lararnie,
and State of Wyoming, and until such time as Congress or the President
of the United States shall otherwise direct, and the withdrawal hereby
made shall be unqualified and absolute, except to the extent that any of
the lands herein described may have been heretofore lawfully entered or
acquired, pursuant to any of the public land laws of the United States,
and, further, that this withdrawal is in addition to the withdrawal made by
proclamation No. 1259 on December 20, 1913.
