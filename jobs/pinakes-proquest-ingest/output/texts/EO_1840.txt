Mr. William J. Levy may be reinstated as a clerk in the Treasury
Department without compliance with Civil Service rules.
This order is issued on the recommendation of the Secretary of the
Treasury in view of Mr. Levy's former efficient service in the Treasury
Department, where he proved to be an exceptionally able and expert
technical bookkeeper. His services in that line are especially desired
now because of the new accounting system inaugurated in that Depart-
ment. The needs of the office of the Treasurer of the United States at
the present time are unusual and extraordinary, and Mr. Levy possesses
special and peculiar qualifications as a technical bookkeeper.
WOODROW WILSON
TILE WHITE HOUSE,
17 Oct., 1913.
