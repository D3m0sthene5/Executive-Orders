Mrs. Olivia Blanchard may be appointed to a position in the classified
service under the Department of Labor without reference to the Civil
Service rules.
This order is issued upon the recommendation of the Secretary of
Labor, wvho states that the Department contemplates the establishment of
a branch of the United States Employment Service, in the city of Newv
Orleans, Louisiana, and that it is necessary that a woman employee be
assigned thereto to attend to applications of women for employment or
help, and it is believed that a woman as favorably known in New Orleans
as Mrs. Blanchard is would possess advantages calculated to promote the
interests of the new employment service, and that her acquaintance would
make her services desirable in the labors of Woman's and Girl's Division
of said service involving vocational direction as to industrial training of
girls.
The Civil Service Commission does not concur in the recommenda-
tion.
WOODROW WILSON
THE WHITE HOUSE,
14 July, 1916.
