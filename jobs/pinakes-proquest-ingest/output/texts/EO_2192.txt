Miss Agnes Decry may be appointed as a nurse in the Indian Service
without reference to the requirement of examination under the civil service
rules. This action, based upon the recommendation of the Secretary of
the Interior, is in fulfillment of an agreement between representatives of
the Departments of the Interior and War in October, 1912, when it wvas
understood that Miss Deery wvas eligible for transfer.
Miss Decry served from June 12, 1902, to May 7, 1914, at the Apache
Indian Hospital at Fort Sill, Oklahoma, with distinguished faithfulness
and success in the positions of matron and nurse. The Apache prisoners
having been removed to the Mescalero Agency, New Mexico, it is desired
to appoint Miss Decry as nurse in the Indian Service.
The Civil Service Commission concurs in the recommendation for this
order.
WOODROW WILSON
THE WHITE HOUSE,
11 May, 1915.
