Executive Orber.
ARKANSAS.
Under authority of the Act of Congress, approved June 25, 1910 (36
Stat., 847), as amended by the Act of August 24, 1912 (37' Stat., 497),
and subject to the conditions therein expressed, it is hereby ordered that
the land hereinafter described be, and the same is, hereby temporarily with-
drawvn from settlement, location, sale, entry, and other forms of disposal,
until March 5, 1917, in aid of legislation, proposed to be enacted, perma-
nently withdrawing said land for an approach to Sugar Loaf Mountain,
Arkansas.
If said legislation is not enacted before the adjournment of the last
session of the Sixty-fourth Congress, and no other direction is given as to
the disposition of the land, it will, on March 5, i1917, become subject to
disposal under any law then applicable thereto, without further orders.
Said land is described as follows:
Township io N., R. 9 W., SE/4 of SW Y, Sec. 17, Fifth Principal
Meridian.
