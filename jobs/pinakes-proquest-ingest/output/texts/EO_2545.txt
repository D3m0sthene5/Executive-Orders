Erecutive Orer.
Twenty-eight deputy collectors of internal revenue named in letter of the
Secretary of the Treasury dated March 8, 1917, appointed without reference to
the civil service rules under authority of the act of October 22, 1913, may be
transferred to competitive classified positions of field deputy collector, income
tax roll, to serve until not later than August 31, 1917. Owing to the demand
for war munitions, there has been a heavy increase in the production of tax-
free alcohol. This has made necessary the employment of an unusual number
of storekeepers and gaugers, with the result that the appropriation from which
storekeeper-gaugers and agents are paid has been depleted to a point where a
deficiency of considerable magnitude is threatened, notwithstanding the utmost
reduction of the force of storekeeper-gaugers. This deficiency can be averted
only by the transfer of the force known as " special gaugers " (storekeeper-
gaugers assigned to special duty under division revenue agents) to the deputy
collector roll. By transferring them to positions as general deputy collectors on
the regular roil, the storekeeper-gaugers-agents appropriation can be relieved.
It is desired to transfer deputy collectors appointed without examination but
assigned to income tax work to the income tax roll.
This will enable the Treasury Department to utilize the several separate
appropriations from which the field force of the Internal Revenue Service is
paid, and to thus continue the service of competitive classified special gaugers
whose services would otherwise have to be dispensed with though needed.
Employees so transferred do not thus acquire any rights of competitive
employees under the civil service rules, but will be retransferred to excepted
positions unless separated from the service.
The Civil Service Commission concurs in this order.
WOODROW WILSON
THE WHITE HousE,
17 March, 1917.
