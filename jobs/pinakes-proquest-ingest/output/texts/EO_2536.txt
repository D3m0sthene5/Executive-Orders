Executive Orber
Schedule A, excepting from examination certain positions under the Civil
Service Rules, is hereby amended by striking out paragraph 4 of Subdivision
III, Treasury Department, which reads as follows:
4. Storekeepers and gaugers whose compensation does not exceed
$3 per diem when actually employed and whose aggregate compensation
shall not exceed $500 per annum.
This exception from the requirement of examination shall not apply
to the fifth internal-revenue district of North Carolina.
WOODROW WILSON
THE WHITE HousE,
21 February, 1917.
[No. 2536.]
