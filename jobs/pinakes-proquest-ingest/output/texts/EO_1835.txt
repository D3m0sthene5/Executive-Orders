]Exrecutive Orber.
Mr. Leroy E. Reed may be appointed to a competitive classified
position in the Department of Justice.
Mr. Reed has twice passed the stenographer and typewriter examina-
ticon and held a competitive classified position in the Department of
Justice from July 9, 1910, to February 1, 1912, when he accepted a
position in the State Department regarded by that Department as not
subject to the civil service rules. He was subsequently given a com-
petitive classified status under an Executive order and on April I, 1913,
wvas appointed confidential clerk to the Attorney General. This order is
therefore issued to restore Mr. Reed to a competitive classified status in
the Department of Justice.
WOODROW WILSON
THE WHITE HOUSE,
October I, 1913.
[No. 1835.]
