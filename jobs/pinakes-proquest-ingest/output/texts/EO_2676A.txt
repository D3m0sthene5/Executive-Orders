Pursuant to the authority vested in me by Act of Congress approved July 27,
19 17, (Public No. 31, 65th Congress), it is hereby ordered that possession be taken
forthwith, on behalf of United States, for use for national defense and in con-
nection therewith as sites for permanent aviation stations for the Army and
