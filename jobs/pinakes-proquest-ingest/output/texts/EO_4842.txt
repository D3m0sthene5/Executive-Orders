)Executive Order
TONGASS NATIONAL FOREST
ALASKA
Under authority of the act of Congress approved June 4, 1897 (30 Stat.. 34-36),
and on the recommendation of the Secretary of Agriculture, it is hereby ordered
that the tracts of land on Baranof Island, in Alaska, lying within the hereinafter
described boundaries, be and the same are hereby excluded from the Tongass
National Forest:
Beginning at corner No. 1 M. Con east shore of Port Alexander,
approximately in latitude 56° 15' N., longitude 1340 39' W., from which
point F. S. M. bears south 11 3°' W. 28.70 chains distant; thence east
2.41 chains to corner No. 2, not set; thence south 13.18 chains to corner
No. 3, not set; thence by meanders along the line of mean high tide of
Chatham Straits and Port Alexander to corner No. 1 M. 0., the place
of beginning, containing approximately 23.13 acres;
Beginning at corner No. 1, identical with corner No. 2, of special
use survey and marked S. U. M. 0. 2, approximately in latitude 560
15' N., longitude 134° 39' W.; thence by meanders in a general north-
easterly direction, along the line of mean high tide of Port Alexander
to corner No. 2 M. C. which is identical with corner No. 2 M. C. of
listing survey 8-73; thence west 22.68 chains to corner No. 3, not set;
thence south 45.70 chains to corner No. 4, not set; thence east 9.50
chains to corner No. 1, the place of beginning, containing approximately
63.47 acres.
Subject to valid existing rights, the lands excluded from the national forest by
this order are hereby reserved to be disposed of for .townsite purposes as provided
by section 11 of the act of March 3, 1891 (26 Stat. 1095), and the act of May 25,
1926 (44 Stat. 629).
CALVIN COOLIDGE
THE WHITE HOUSE,
March 22, 1928.
