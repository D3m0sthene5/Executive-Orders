Mr. William R. Carpenter of Illinois may be appointed to a
clerical position in the competitive classified service of the Post Office
Department,
Since November 29, 1910, Mr. Carpenter has served in the excepted
position of confidential clerk in the Post Office Department, and on
account of his excellent service the Postmaster General recommends his
appointment to the competitive classified service. He has passed a
number of civil service examinations, and served for five years in the
competitive classified service prior to his service in the Post Office
Department. The Civil Service Commission joins with the Postmaster
General in recommending this order, in view of Mr. Carpenter's long
service and proved capacity.
WM H TAFT
THE WHITE HOUSE,
November 27, 1912.
[No. 1650.]
