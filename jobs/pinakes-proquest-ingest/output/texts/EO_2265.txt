Executive Orber.
Mrs. Frederick Gillman may be appointed as seamstress or flaguwaker
at the Navy Yard, Mare Island, California, without reference to the civil
service rules or navy yard regulations.
Mrs. Gillman's husband was a gunner on the submarine F-4 , which
was lost in Honolulu harbor, and she has a child twelve years of age to
support.
This order is recommended by the Secretary of the Navy with the
concurrence of the Civil Service Commission.
WOODROW WILSON
THE WITE HOUSE,
28 October, 1915.
