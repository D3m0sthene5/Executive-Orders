Otto Lewis Evans, of Amherst, Virginia, a law graduate of the University
of Virginia, and a practicing lawyer from 1885 to 1915, and who has served in
the Department of State since August 1916, may be appointed a Law Clerk in
the Department of State without reference to the requirements of the Civil
Service Rules.
This action is based upon the recommendation of the Secretary of State,
because of the efficient service of Mr. Evans, and his special qualifications and
experience as a lawyer.
WOODROW WILSON
THE WVHIT HOUSE,
22 March, 1917.
[No. 2556.]
