re-establishing, by virtue of the authority therein given, the boundary lines
of the Bismarck and Fargo land districts in the State of North Dakota, is

Beginning at a point where the line between ranges seventy'-one and
seventy-two west of the fifth Principal MeIcridian intersects the twelfth
standard parallel north, thence south along said range line to its intersec-
tion with the ninth standard parallel north ; thence east along said standard
parallel to its intersection with the ninth Guide Meridian, between ranges
sixty-six and sixty-sevecn west of the fifth Principal Meridian; thence north
along the guide meridian to its intersection with the twelfth standard paral-
lel north; thence west along said standard parallel to the point of beginning.
And it is hereby ordered that the lands included in the foregoing
(escribed boundaries be (detached from the Bismarck land district and
attached to, and made a part of, the Fargo land district, and that the
archives, records and business pertaining to said lands be transferred from
the land office at Bismarck, North Dakota, to the land office at Fargo,
North Dakota.
