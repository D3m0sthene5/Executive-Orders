Executive Order of February 21I, 1910, detaching townships one hun-
dred and thirty-seven to one hundred and forty-eight North, inclusive,
ranges seventy-two, seventy-three and seventy-four West be detached from
the Bismnarck land district and attached to and made a part of the Fargo
land district in the State of North Dakota, and the business and archives
pertaining to the public lands within said townships transferred from the
land office at Bismarck to the land office at Fargo, is hereby rescinded.
WM H TAFT
[HE WHITE HOUSE,
Marcia 25, 1910.
