American Section of the International Joint Commission for the adjust-
ment of questions regarding use of boundary waters between the United
States and Canada and other purposes, under Article 8 of the treaty signed
at Washington, January I I, 1909, is fixed at the rate of $7,500 per annum,
and that there be paid to each of them $ ro per day as additional com-
pensation in lieu of all travel expenses, except transportation charges,
while engaged in travel on official business away from their respective
places of residence, and that each of them is to be considered in a travel
status while engaged in official duties at Washington, D. C., or in go ing to
and returning from their respective places of residence to Washington,
D. C., on the business of the Commission. WM H TAFT
