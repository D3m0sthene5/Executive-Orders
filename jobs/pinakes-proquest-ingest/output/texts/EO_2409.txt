Executive cOrber
Mrs. Fannie Taylor Colmesnil may be appointed to a position in the
classified service of the Government without regard to Civil Service rules.
This order is issued on the recommendation of the Secretary of the
Treasury, who states that Mrs. Colmesnil rendered very efficient and satis-
factory service while employed by the Reserve Bank Organization Com-
mittee and the Federal Reserve Board, and that, in view of her experience
and ability, it is believed her appointment to a classified position will be in
the interest of the service.
The Civil Service Commission does not concur in the recommendation.
WOODROW WILSON
THE WHITE HOUSE,
29 June, 1916.
[No. 2409.]
