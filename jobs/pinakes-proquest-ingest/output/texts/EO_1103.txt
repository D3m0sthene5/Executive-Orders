Executive Orber.
The unclassified laborers employed in the Customs Service at the port
of New York whose names appear upon a list of persons recommended for
classification as watchmen by the Treasury Department with the approval
of the Civil Service Commission may be classified as watchmen at $960 per
annum but shall not be eligible for transfer or promotion beyond the watch-
man force without examination. This order is made upon the recommen-
dation of the Civil Service Commission.
WM H TAFT
THE WHITE HousE,
June 29, 1909.
[No. 1103.]
