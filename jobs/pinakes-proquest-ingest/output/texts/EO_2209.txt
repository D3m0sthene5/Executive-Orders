Executive Orber.
Mrs. Emma L. Guckert may be reinstated as an operative in the
Bureau of Engraving and Printing, without regard to the length of time
she has been separated from the service.
This order is issued on the recommendation of the Secretary of the
Treasury on account of Mrs. Guckert's former efficient service in the
Bureau, and for the further reason that her husband, who has been
employed in the Bureau since July, 1901, is sick and will probably never
be able to resume his duties, and the burden of supporting a family con-
sisting of the husband, wife and two children has fallen on Mrs. Guckert.
WOODROW WILSON
THIE WHI'I'E Housi;
to June, 1915.
