TONGASS NATIONAL FOREST
ALASKA
Under authority of the act of Congress approved June 4, 1897 (30 Stat., 34-36),
and on the recommendation of the Secretary of Agriculture, it is hereby ordered
that the tract of land at Union Bay on Cleveland Peninsula in Alaska, lying within
the hereinafter described boundaries, heretofore occupied for fish cannery purposes,
be and the same is~ hereby excluded from the Tongass National Forest and restored
to entry under the applicable public land laws :
Beginning at corner No. 1, on east shore of Union Bay, approximately in lati-
tude 550 45' N., longitude 1320 12' W., which is identical with a Forest Service
monument; a post 1x4 ins, firmly set in the ground and nailed to a cedar log and
appropriately marked from which a hemlock 8 inches in diameter bears north 850
E. 30 feet blazed and appropriately scribed, and the NE corner of a dock bears S.
520 W; thence in a southerly direction following the mean high water of Union
Bay to corner No. 2 (not marked), which is 3.03 chains east and 19.39 chains south
of corner No 1; thence north 19.39 chains to corner No. 3 (not marked); thence
west 3.03 chains to corner No. 1, place of beginning, containing approximately
16.90 acres.
