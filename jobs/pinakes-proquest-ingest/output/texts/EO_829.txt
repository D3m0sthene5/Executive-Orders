Executive Orber.
In order that the Embassy at Tokyo and the Legation at Peking may
keep in closer touch With the corps of interpreters and student interpreters
under their respective supervision, and in order also that each of these-offi-
cers who is properly qualified may have the benefit of service at the em-
bassy or legation in the country where he is stationed, hereafter the offices
of Assistant Japanese Secretary and, Assistant Chinese Secretary will be
filled in rotation by different qualified interpreters or student interpreters.
The Secretary of State therefore will from time to time. ascertain the
suggestions of the chiefs of mission and will advise the President of his
recommendations for appointment.
THEODORE ROOSEVELT
THE WHITE HOUSE,
Junel 20, 1908.
[No. 829.]
