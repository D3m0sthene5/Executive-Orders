Mr. Charles W. Fowle of Massachusetts, may be appointed without
examination as a clerk in the Department of State, as recommended by*
the Secretary of State with the concurrence of the Civil Service Commis-
sion, because of his services for the past four years as clerk in the American
Embassy at Constantinople, during which he acquired such special knowvl-
edge of the affairs in the Near East, that his transfer to the Department of
State will be of distinct advantage to the service.
