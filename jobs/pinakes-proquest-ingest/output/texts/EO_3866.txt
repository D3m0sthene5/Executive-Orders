]Executive Orber
COLORADO
Under authority contained in the act of Congress approved June 25, 1910
(36 Stat., 847), as amended by the act of August 24, 1912 (3? Stat., 497), and
subject to the conditions, provisions and limitations of said acts, it is hereby
ordered that the public lands in Ts. 38 N., Rs. 4 and 5 E., and Ts. 39 N., Rs. 3
and 4 E., N. M. P. M., Colorado, be, and the same are hereby withdrawn from
settlement, location, sale, entry and all forms of appropriation, pending the
resurvey of said townships under authority contained in the act of Congress
approved March 3, 1909 (3 Stat., 845) ; this withdrawal to remain in full
force and effect from the date hereof until the resurvey is duly approved and
accepted and the approved plats thereof are officially filed in the proper U. S.
local land office, unless another date be fixed by the Commissioner of the General
Land Office, whereupon this withdrawal will become of no effect.
WARREN G HARDING
THE WITS HOUSE~,
June 14, 1923.
