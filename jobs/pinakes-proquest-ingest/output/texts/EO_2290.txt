Executive Orber.
Miss Harriet M. Sweet may be appointed to a clerical position on
the permanent roll of the Treasury Department without compliance %xt
the requirements of the civil service rules.
This order is issued on the recommendation of the Secretary of the
Treasury, who states that Miss Sweet, during the Pan American Financial
Conference. rendered exceptional service to the office of the Secretary
General of that conference; that her familiarity with the Latin-American
correspondence and the details of the office have made her services of
great value; that the place could not be adequately filled by selection
from the register of eligibles; and that without her services the work of
that office would be severely handicapped.
In view of the representation of the Secretary of the Treasury, the
Civil Service Commission concurs in his recommendation for an executive
order.
WOODROW WILSON
TH-E WITuE HOUSE,
18 December, 1915.
