Mr. James D. Scott may be reinstated in the Railway Mail Service,
without regard to the length of time he has been out of the service.
The Postmaster General reports that he was appointed a railway mail
clerk on October 17, 1900, at $800 per annum, and promoted through the
successive grades until he received a salary of $i ioo per annum; that he
resigned voluntarily, April 17, 1911x; that his record as a clerk is good, and
his reinstatement would be to the benefit of the service.
The Civil Service Commission does not concur in recommending that
an Executive Order be issued in this case.
