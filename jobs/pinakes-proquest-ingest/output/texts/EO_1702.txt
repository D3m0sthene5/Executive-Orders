)Executive Orber.
It is hereby ordered that members of the Grand Army of the Re-
public, the United Spanish war veterans, the Army and Navy' Union, and
the Woman's Relief Corps who are in the employ of the Government and
who desire to attend the Battleship Maine services, to be held at Arling-
ton National Cemetery, Saturday, February 1 5, 1913, beginning at 2:oo0
o'clock p. in., be excused from their respective duties at such time as will
enable them to attend said services.
WM H TAFT
THE WHITE HOUSE,
February 12, 1913.
[No. 1 702.]
