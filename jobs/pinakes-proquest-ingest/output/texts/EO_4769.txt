Mrs. Lillian Capron may be appointed as welfare worker in the War Depart-
ment without regard to the examination requirements of the civil service rules
Mrs. Capron is the widow of Captain Allyn A. Capron, killed in action in Cuba
during the Spanish War. The outstanding service of Mrs. Capron with the Red
Cross and as a welfare worker at home and abroad, both in peace and in war, from
the relief work in Cuba in 1899 thru the Word War, seems to constitute a basis
for her appointment purely on the ground of merit aitho the Civil Service Com-
mission cannot certify that the needs of the service cannot be met in the manner
provided by the civil service rules.
The Secretary of War recommends the order.
