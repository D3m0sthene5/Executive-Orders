Executive Orer
Mr. Michael Flaherty may be promoted to a sub-clerical position or
to the position of wireman in the Treasury Department without compli-
ance with the requirements of the civil service rules.
This order is issued upon the recommendation of the Secretary of
the Treasury, who states that Mr. Flaherty has been employed as an un-
classified laborer in the electrician's office for the last four years and has
made himself particularly valuable in that office, and that it is believed his
promotion to a classified position will be in the interests of the service.
The Civil Service Commission does not concur in the recommendation.
WOODROW WILSON
