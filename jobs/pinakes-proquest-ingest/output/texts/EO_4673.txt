CALIFORNIA
It is hereby ordered that Executive Order No. 4326, dated October 20, 1925,
withdrawing the public lands in Ts. 42,_ 43, 44 and 45 N., R. 10 W., M. D. M.,
California, pending a. resurvey of said townships be, and the same is hereby revoked.
And it is hereby ordered, pursuant to Public Resolution No. 29, of February
14, 1920 (41 Stat., 434), as amended January 21, and December 28, 1922 (42 Stat.,
358, 1067), that the above described area, if otherwise subject to disposition, sub-
ject to valid rights, shall be opened only to entry under the homestead or desert land
laws, by qualified ex-service men of the war with Germany, under the terms and
conditions of such resolutions and the regulations issued thereunder for a period; of 91
days, beginning with the official filing of the lat of resurvey and thereafter to
appropriation under any public land law applicable thereto by the general public.
Subsequent to the date hereof and prior to the date of restoration to general
disposition as herein provided, no right may, be acquired to the released land by
settlement in advance of entry, or othewise except strictly in accordance herewith.
