Executive ether,
COLORADO.
Under authority of the act of Congress approved June 25, 1910 (3
Stat., S47), as amended by the act of August 24, 1912 (37 Stat., 497), and
subject to the conditions therein expressed, it is hereby ordered that the
lands hereinafter described be, and the same are, hereby temporarily with-
drawn from settlement, location, sale, entry, and other forms of disposal
until March 5, 19 17, in aid of legislation proposed to be enacted granting
to the City of Black Hawk, Colorado, a right to purchase said lands for
the protection of its water supply.
If said legislation is not enacted before the adjournment of the last
session of the sixty-fourth Congress, and no other direction is given as to
the disposition of the lands, they will on March 5, 1917, become subject
to disposal under any lawv then applicable thereto, without further orders.
