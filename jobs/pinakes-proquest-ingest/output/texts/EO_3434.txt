RELATING to Licensing of Vehicles, Road Rules, use of Lights, Tags and
Signals, and Speed Regulations in the Canal Zone.
By virtue of the Act of Congress entitled "An Act Extending certain privi-
leges of Canal employees to other officials on the Canal zone and authorizing
the President to make rules and regulations affecting health, sanitation,
quarantine, taxation, public roads, self-propelled vehicles, and police powers on

promulgate rules and -reulations from tm to time to carry out this Order.
Section 2. The term "motor vehicle~", as used herein, shall apply to and
include every vehicle which may be drawn or propelled by means of steam,

when a bicycle is equipped with a contrivance commonly known as a motor
wheel, such bicycle shall be deemed a motor-cycle and included in the term
motorr vehicle".

merchandise or other property, or employed by any merchant in the trauns-

tion to any penalty which may be prescribed for larceny of property.
Section 25. The operator of any vehicle concerned in any accident on any
street, road or other place in the Canal Zone, whether involving injury to per-
son or to property, shall remain at the scene of the accident until the arrival
of the police authorities, or shall communicate without delay to the nearest
police authorities a full report of the accident in which he was involved. Any
concealment of identity of person or property in an accident in which involved
shall he a violation of this Order.
Section 26. The operation, management, driving or riding of any vehicle
on any of the roads or streets of the Canal Zone in a reckless or negligent
manner shall be a violation of this Order.
