Executive either
Not exceeding one hundred fifteen persons appointed as experts and
agents without examination under subdivision IX, section I of Schedule
A, of the civil-service rules, and who now are engaged upon work similar
to that of competitive employees, and who are especially recommended by
the Secretary of Agriculture because of their proved capacity for efficient
service may be transferred to appropriate competitive positions in the
DepartmAent of Agriculture.
By Executive order of this date the provision of the civil-service rules
permitting the appointment of agents and experts of the Department of
Agriculture without examination is amended to fix more definitely the
conditions under which appointments can be made. This order will thus
permit the classification of efficient employees who can not be properly
regarded as agents or experts under the amended procedure. Many of
these persons are college graduates and, having been appointed at a time
when agricultural colleges had not developed courses of study along certain
technical lines pursued by the Department, have become technologists and
experts while engaged in the work of the Department.
The Civil Service Commission recommends this order.
WM H TAFT
THE WHITE HOUSE,
Aug'ust 26, 1912.
