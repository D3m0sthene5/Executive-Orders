OCT 16 193 W
Executive rber.
The officer in charge of Public Buildings and Grounds is hereby
authorized to employ a copyist in connection with the preparation of the
inventory and return of property belonging to the White House required
by law, at a compensation not to exceed $6o. per month to be paid from
the appropriation for "Care, repair and refurnishing of the Executive
Mansion", the employment to continue for such length of time as may
be necessary, but not to exceed three months.
THEODORE ROOSEVELT
THE WHIIT-ousE,
June 13, 1906.
