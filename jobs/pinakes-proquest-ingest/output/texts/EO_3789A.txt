)Executive Orber
WHERE AS by Section 2 of the Act of Congress entitled "An Act Authorizing
the President to scrap certain vessels in conformity with the provisions of the
treaty limiting naval armament, and for other purposes", approved July i,
1922, it is provided :
"That the President of the United States is hereby authorized to discontinuethe construction of and to cancel or modify any contract entered into forand on behalf of the United States for the construction of seven first-classbattleships and six battle cruisers authorized in the Act entitled "An Actmaking appropriations for the naval service for the fiscal year endingJune 30, 1917, and for other purposes," approved August 29, 1916, andfor the machinery, materials, equipment, armor, armament, and ordnanceequipment therefor: PROVIDED, That whenever any such contract is can-celed or modified settlement of claims arising therefrom shall be made bythe President upon a fair and equitable basis, as he may determine, outof any funds hereafter to be appropriated for that purpose: PROVIDED,'t'hat if the terms of such settlement are unsatisfactory to any claimant suchclaimant shall be lpaidl 75 per centum of the amount awarded by the Presi-dent and shall be entitled to sue the United States to recover such furthersums as added to the said 75 per centum shall make up such amount aswill be just compensation for such claims, in the manner provided bysection 24, paragraph 20, and section 145 of the judicial Code"; and
WHEREAS, the work on certain vessels under construction for the Navy
by contract has been suspended since the signing of said treaty and certain
obligations incurred by the United States on account thereof ;
Now, THiEREFORE, f hereby direct that the Secretary of the Navy shall
have and exercise all power and authority vested in me by said section of said
Act of Congress, and whatever action may have been taken by the Secretary
of the Navy heretofore preliminarily to the limitation of the naval armament
of the United States in conformity with the provisions of said treaty or any
act of Congress relating thereto is hereby approved.
The powers herein delegated to the Secretary of the Nav may, in his
discretion, be exercised directly by him or through such agency or agencies as
he may authorize.
WARREN G HARDING
THE WHITE House,
February r6, 1923.
