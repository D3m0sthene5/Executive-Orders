Mr. Calvin Satterfield, of Pennsylvania, may be appointed to the
position of Chief of the Division of Accounts, Department of justice,
without reference to the Civil Service Rules.
This order is issued upon the recommendation of the Attorney Gen-
eral, wvho states that the position of chief of the division of accounts is
highly confidential, and requires a peculiar order of talent; that a diligent
effort has been made within the classified service to find some one possessing
the required qualifications, but without success. The Attorney General
believes that Mr. Satterfield, wvho is known to him personally, is especially
qualified for the position, and that the interests of the public would be
subserved by exempting the appointment.
The Civil Service Commission does not concur in the recommendation.
WOODROW WILSON
THE WHITE HOUSE,
December 30, 19'14
[No. 2113.]
