Executive ether
.--
Mrs. Mary E. Netherland may be transferred from clerk to sign tribal
deeds, $1200o per annum, in the Office of the Secretary, Department of the
Interior, to a regular clerkship.
She has served efficiently three years in her present position and has
demonstrated her ability as a clerk.
The Commission concurs with the Secretary of the Interior in recom-
mending her appointment by Executive order.
WOODROW WILSON
THLE WHITE HOUSE,
May I, 1913.
