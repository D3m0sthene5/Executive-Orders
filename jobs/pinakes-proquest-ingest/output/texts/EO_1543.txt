. Executive Order

. | —

, : Mrs. Margaret A. Hampton, a clerk at $1000 per annum on the
temporary force of the Chirteenth Census, may be appointed as a clerk at
$1000 per annum in the classified service.

Mrs. Hampton was appointed through appropriate examination with
a rating of 77.45, as a clerk at $600 per annum on the temporary force of
; the Thirteenth Decennial Census on April 1, 1910, and by successive pro-
motions reached her present rate of $1000 per annum. She has twice
passed the departmental clerk examination. On June 24, 1910, while
leaving the Census Building, she lost her balance and pitched head first
down the full flight of stairs, her skirt having caught on one of the iron
. plates of the stairs, sustaining injuries which necessitated her absence from :
; the office until July 13 and again from September 30 until December 4.
There is grave doubt whether she will ever recover her health, according to
statements of several physicians who have attended her. The Bureau has
endeavored to make all possible restitution but, under existing law, she
cannot be retained beyond June 30, 1912. Notwithstanding the accident
her value as a clerk is above the average.
In view of the facts the Secretary of Commerce and Labor recom-
mends the appointment of Mrs. Hampton.
The Civil Service Commission does not concur in the recommenda-
tion for an executive order.
