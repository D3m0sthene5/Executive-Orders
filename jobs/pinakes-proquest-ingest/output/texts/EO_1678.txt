It is hereby ordered that Gravel Island and Spider Island, in Lake
Michigan, unsurveyed, but located approximately in latitude 45° 15' 15"
North, longitude 860 57' 45", and latitude 45° 12' 30" North, longitude 860
58' 30", respectively, West from Greenwich, as shown upon the diagram
hereto attached and made a part of this Order, be and the same are hereby
reserved and set apart for the use of the Department of Agriculture as a
preserve and breeding ground for native birds. It is unlawful for any per-
son to hunt, trap, capture, wilfully disturb, or kill any bird of any kind
whatever, or take the eggs of any such bird, within the limits of this reser-
vation, except under such rules and regulations as may be prescribed by
the Secretary of Agriculture. Warning is expressly given to all persons
not to commit any of the acts herein enumerated and which are prohibited
by law.
This reservation to be known as Gravel Island Reservation.
WM H TAFT
THE WHITE HOUSE,
