Under authority of the acts of Congress, approved March 3, 1891 (26' Stat.
1095), and June 4, 1897 (30 Stat. 11 at 34 and 36), and on the 'recommendation of
the Secretary of Agriculture, it is hereby ordered that Lots 3, 4, 5 and 6, Sec. 1, T.
16 S., R. 4 E., S. L. M., Utah, be and the same are hereby included in and made
a part of the Manti National Forest.
The withdrawal made by this order shall, as to any lands which are at this date
legally appropriated under the public land laws, be subject to, and shall not inter-
fere with or defeat legal rights thereunder, so long as such appropriation is legally
maintained.
