Under authority of the Act of October 28, 192, (Public-No. 84), the offices
of Register and Receiver of the United States land office at Williston, North
Da xa, are hereby consolidated and the office of Receiver is abolished. All
the powers,,duties, obligations and penalties imposed by law upon both the
Re ter and Receiver of said office shall be exercised by and imposed upon
the Register so appointed, whose compensation shall be a salary of $5oo per
annum, together with the fees and commissions otherwise allowable to both
Register and Receiver, but the salary, fees and commissions of such officer shall
not exceed $3000 per annum.
WARREN G HARD ING
THE WHITE HOUSE,
January 12, 1922.
