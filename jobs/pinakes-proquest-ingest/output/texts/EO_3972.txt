Kaibab National Forest
ARIZONA
Under authority of the Act of Congress of June 4, 1897 (30 Stat., 11), and
upon recommendation of the Secretary of Agriculture, it is hereby ordered that
the Mt. Trumbull Division of the Dixie Na Tonanl Forest, situated within
Townships 34 and 35 North of Ranges 8 and 9 West, G. and S. R. Mer., as,
fixed and defined by proclamation dated May 1o, 1916 (No. 1334), be and the'
same is hereby transferred and merged into the Kaibab National Forest,
Arizona, established by proclamation May 19, 1913 (No. 1238), and modified
by the Act of February 26, 1919 (40 Stat., 1175) , and all of said lands shall
hereafter be designated and administered as the Kaibab National Forest.
