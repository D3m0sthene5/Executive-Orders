)Executive O er
Mr. Francis H. Duehay may be appointed Superintendent of Prisons,
Department of justice, without reference to the civil service rules. This
order is issued upon the recommendation of the Attorney General, who
believes that Mr. Duehay is especially qualified for the position, which
requires a peculiar order of talent. The Civil Service Commission con-
curs in the recommendation.
WOODROW WILSON
THE WHITE HOUSE,
21 January, 191¢.
