Executive c'rber
By virtue of the authority vested in me, the Chairman and Chief
Engineer of the Isthmian Canal Commission is hereby authorized to
execute revocable licenses for lots in townsites in the Canal Zone, either
directly or through such agency as he may direct, such licenses to be
revocable at the pleasure of the Chairman and Chief Engineer or such
other executive officer as may be in charge in the Canal Zone, licensees to
vacate and remove improvements at once without indemnity.
WM H TAFT
THE WHITE HOUSE,
June 28, 1911.
