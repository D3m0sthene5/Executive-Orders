Owing to the increased permanent clerical force provided for the
Department of State in the legislative, executive and judicial appropriation
bill, approved May io, 1916, the Secretary of State, without reference to
the civil service law, may make appointments to the non-clerical grades
and to the clerical grades up to and including class one, therein provided,
from such employes who, upon July i, 1916, shall have served upon the
temporary roll of the Department of State for a continuous period of at
least six months immediately prior thereto, or wvho may have passed an
appropriate examination by the Civil Service Commission; provided, that
such temporary employes have satisfactorily performed their respective
duties and shall have attained a competent rating by the Department
Board of Efficiency.
This order is issued upon the recommendation of the Secretary of
State because of the experience acquired by the temporary employes
through faithful and efficient service in the actual work of the Department
of State and to obviate the serious interruption to the Department's
pressing and important duties which would b)e occasioned by the loss of a
large specially trained and efficient force.
