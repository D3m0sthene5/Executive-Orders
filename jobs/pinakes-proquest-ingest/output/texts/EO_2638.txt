]Erecutive Omer.
It is hereby ordered that an unsurveyed island shown upon the official plat
on file in the General Land Office, approved January 31, 1846, as being in the
Straits of St. Mary south of Lime Island in Sec. 6, T. 42 N., R. 4 E., Michigan,
known as "Little Lime Island," be, and the same is hereby reserved for light-
houses purposes, subject to any existing valid rights in and to the same.
WOODROW WILSON
THES WHITEHousiE,
r2 June, 1917.
[No. 2638.]
