Executive Curb r
Mr. George H. Hamilton may be appointed to the vacancy existing
in the position of assistant postmaster at Newvark, Ohio, without reference
to the civil service rules. This appointment is authorized in view of the
unavoidable absence of the postmaster from his office on account of
illness.
WM H TAFT
THE WHITE HOUSE,
November 29, 1912.
