Mrs. Olive Crook Henderson, widow of Mr. John A. Henderson, who died
suddenly at his desk in the office of the War Labor Board as the result of
heart failure, believed to have been accelerated by the nervous strain result-
ing from the character of his work, may be appointed to an appropriate
clerical position in the casifed civil service of the United States.
Having sole regard to the requirements of the service, the Civil Service
Commission finds itself unable to recommend a waiver of the rules in this
case. The order is granted as a matter of executive benevolence.
