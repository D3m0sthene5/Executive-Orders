Executive Orber.
I n accordance with the provisions of Section 2253 of the Revised
Statutes of the United States, and by virtue of the authority therein given,
it is hereby ordered that the boundaries of the Lincoln land district in the
Territory of New Mexico be reestablished as follows:;
Beginning on the boundary line between the State of Texas and the
Territory of New Mexico where the first standard parallel south intersects
the same ; thence west along said parallel to the range line between ranges
sixteen and seventeen east ; thence north along said range line, allowing
for proper offset on the base line, to the township line between townships
two and three north; thence west along said township line to the range
line between ranges eight and nine east ; thence south along said range line,
allowing for proper offsets on the base line and the first standard parallel
south, to the second standard parallel south; thence east along said stand-
ard parallel to the range line between ranges ten and eleven east ; thence
south along said range line to the township line between townships twelve
and thirteen south; thence east along said township line to the range line
between ranges thirteen and fourteen east; thence south along said range
line, allowing for proper offsets on the third, fourth and fifth standard par-
allels south, to the boundary line between the State of Texas and the
Territory of New Mexico; thence east along said boundary line to the
southeast corner of the Territory of New Mexico; thence north along
the boundary line between the State of Texas and the Territory of Newv
Mexico to the place of beginning.
WM H TAFT
THE WHITE HOUSE,
August i4, 1910.
