Executive Other.
Miss Myra Magee may be appointed a clerk in the classified service
of the Government without reference to the civil service rules.
This order is issued on the recommendation of the Secretary of the
Treasury, who states that Miss Magee has rendered very satisfactory serv-
ice under temporary appointments, and that it is believed that her perma-
nent appointment will be in the interest of the service.
WOODROW WILSON
THE WHITE HOUSE,
i December, 1915.
