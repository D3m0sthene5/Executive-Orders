Mr. Charles W. Carson may be given a classified status for the pur-
pose of effecting his transfer to the United States.
Mr. Carson was temporarily employed as a clerk in the province of
Ambos Camarines from March 16, 1903 to July 26, 1903, and from Sep-
tember 24, 1905, to the present time, he has servTed as postmaster at Mer-
cedes in that province. He qualified in the first grade examination. In
view of the long service of Mr. Carson and the statement of the Executive
Secretary of the government of the Philippines that he has been an active
factor in the material progress and good government of the province, the
Commission joins with the Department in recommending his classification
for tihe purpose of transfer to the United States.
THE WHITE HOUSE,
December S, 1911.
