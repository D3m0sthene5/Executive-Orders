By virtue of the authority vested in we by the Act of Congress, approved
August 1, 1914, making appropriations for the sundry civil expenses of the
Government for the fiscal year ending June 3o, 1915, the port of entry at Ier
Alaska, in Customs Collection District No, 3z, of which Juneau, Alaska, is the
headquarters JM.t, is hereby abolished. By virtue of the same authority a port
of entry is hereby created at Crag, Alasca, in Customs Collection District No. 3t,
of which Juneau, Alaska, is the headquarters port.
This order will become effective November x, 1920.
