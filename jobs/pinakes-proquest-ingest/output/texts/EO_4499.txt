Mrs. Elizabeth Sheehan may be appointed to the position of operative in the
Bureau of Engraving and Printing without regard to the civil service rules.
This Order is issued upon the recommendation of the Acting Secretary of the
Treasury who states that Mrs. Sheehan is the widow of a veteran of the Spanish
American War and is forced to support herself and five children. She was em-
ployed in the Bureau of Engraving and Printing from May 1, 1920, to April 22,
1924, without civil service status.
The Civil Service Commission submits the case without prejudice.
Done for charity.
CALVIN COOLIDGE
THE WHITE HOUSE,
Ault~t, 14, 1926.
