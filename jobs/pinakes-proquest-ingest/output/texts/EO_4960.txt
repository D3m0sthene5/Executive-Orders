Under authority contained in the act of June 25, 1910 (36 Stat. 847), as
amended by the act of August 24, 1912 (37 Stat. 497), and subi~ct to the conditions,

amended by the Joint Resolution of June 25, 1910 (36 Stat. 884).
This order shall continue in full force and effect unless and' until revoked by
the President or by an act of Congress.
