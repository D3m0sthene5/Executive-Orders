Executive cOrder
It is hereby ordered that all veteraiis of the Civil War in the service of the
Government of the United States who desire to attend the Grand Army Encamp-
ment to be held in Boston, Massachusetts, August 20th to August 27th, 1917,
shall be granted leave of absence with pay, in addition to annual leave provided
for by statute, from August 20th to August 2 7th, inclusive, that they may
have the opportunity to attend the Encampment, and that they be granted as
many more days additional leave with pay in each case as are necessary for the
journey ~o Boston and return to their posts. of duty.
WOODROW WILSON
THE WHITE HousE,
