Mr. Horace M. Albright may be appointed as Assistant Director of the
National Park Service.
This order is issued on the recommendation of the Secretary of the Interior
wvho states that Mr. Aibright, as Assistant Attorney of the Department, has
been assisting in the management of the national park system for several years;
that lie is very familiar with the administration of the parks and with their
problems; that lie is personally acquainted with the field force and with the
park concessioners who are under contract with the LDepartment to provide
hotel accommodations and transportation service for the comfort and con-
venience of the traveling public; that he knows the conditions under which
these concessioners operate, and has drafted the contracts under which several
of the larger concessions are conducted; that lie understands thoroughly the
policies that have been adopted for the development and promotion of the
national park system ; that lie is concerned with the preparation of legislation
relating to the national parks, reports to Congress on national park legislation,
and is charged with the duty of dlefending departmental estimates for appro-
priations for the park system.
The Secretary also states that it is necessary that the Assistant Director
of the National Park Service be acquainted with the parks and their problems
at the time he assumes his office; that he be a lawyer in order that he may
give consideration to all legal matters relating to the National Park Service,
and that Mr. Albright possesses these qualifications.
The Civil Service Commission concurs in the recommendation for the
issuance of this order.
