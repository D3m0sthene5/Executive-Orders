Mr. William S. Phelps may be reinstated to the position of Examiner
of Merchandise in the Customs Service at the port of New York, without
reference to the length of his separation from the service.
Mr. Phelps was formerly an Examiner of Merchandise in the Customs
service at the port of New York, from which position he was removed in
March. 1911, for carelessness, inattention and unsatisfactory conduct of
official business.
This order is issued upon the recommendation of the Secretary of the
Treasury, wvho states that the matter of Mr. Phelps' removal wvas investi-
gated by it committee on personnel in the Appraiser's Office at New York,
and that the committee in its report, dated March 25, 1915, found that his
removal was not warranted by the facts and that his inefficiency and inat-
tention were not established.
The Civil Service Commission does not concur in the recommendation.
