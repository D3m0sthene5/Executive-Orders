Executive Orber
By virtue of the authority vested in the President by the Act approved
August 29, 1916, entitled " An Act Making appropriations for the Naval Service
for the fiscal year ending June thirtieth, nineteen hundred and seventeen, and
for other purposes," it is hereby directed that the authorized enlisted strength
of the Marine Corps be increased to seventeen thousand four hundred men.
WOODROW WILSON
THE WHITE HousE,
,March 26, 1917.
FNo. 2561.]
