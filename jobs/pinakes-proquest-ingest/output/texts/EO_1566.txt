Mr. Dorsey E. Phillips of Missouri may he appointed to a clerical
position in the competitive classified service without reference to the
Civil Service Rules. Mr. Phillips served for five years in the regular
army prior to May 30, 1898. He passed the clerk examination held at
St. Joseph, Missouri, on March 22, 1909, with a rating of 78.35 and wvas
temporarily employed as a result of this examination from October 25,
1909g, to February 18, 1910. He also passed the census clerk examination
with a rating of 80.25 as a result of which he received appointment in the
Bureau of the Census and served from Septenmber 6, 1910, to February 29,
1912, when he was separated on account of a necessary reduction of force.
The Civil Service Commission does not recommend the approval of this
order.
WM H TAFT
THE WHITE HOUSE,
July 19, 1912.
[No. 1566.]
