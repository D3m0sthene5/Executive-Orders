Executive Other
Designating Honorable Carlos Franco Soto to temporarily
perform the duties of the Judge of the Disric
Court of the_Unite L~tos for Porto Rico
Whereas, it appearing that the Honorable Ira K. Wells, Judge of the District
Court of the United States for Porto Rico, will be absent from his District, begin-
ning June 1, 1927:
Now, Therefore, in pursuance of the authority vested in me by Section 41 of
the Act to provide a civil government for Porto Rico, and for other purposes (3
Stat. L. 966) it is hereby ordered that the Honorable Carlos Franco Soto, Associ-
ate Justice of the Porto Rico Supreme Court, be and he is hereby designated,
authorized and empowered to perform the duties of the office of Judge of the Dis-
trict Court of the United States for Porto Rico, and to sign all necessary papers
and records as the Acting judge of said Court, during the absence of the Ju!dge
thereof, and until such absence is removed.
CALVIN COOLIDGE
THE WHITE HousE,
April 21, 1927.
