)Executive Orber
The time limit on reinstatement under the civil service rules is hereby waived
to prolong the eligibility for reinstatement of John Charles Steinberg to an appro-
priate position in the Government service.
Mr. Steinberg resigned from the position of assistant engineer, Freedmen's
Hospital, October 14, 1925. He lacks only twenty-two days' service of being
eligible for reinstatement under the civil service rules. He served with the United
States Marines from 1906 to 1908.
The Civil Service Commission submits the case without prejudice.
CALVIN COOLIDGE
THE WHITE HOUSE,
June 8, 1928.
