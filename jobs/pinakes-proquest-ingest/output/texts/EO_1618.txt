TIHE WHITE HOUSE,
September 30, 1912.
I, WILL.I H. TAFT, President of the United States, by virtue of the
authority vested in me by Sections 3141 and 3142, Revised Statutes of the
United States, hereby order that the Twvelfth Internal Revenue Collection
District of Pennsylvania, embracing the Counties of Bradford, Carbon,
Center, Clinton, Columbia, Lackawvana, Luerne, Lycoming, Monroe,
Montour, Northampton, Northumberland, Pike, Potter, Sullivan, Susque-
hanna, Tioga, Union, Wayne, Wyoming, is hereby consolidated with the
Ninth District of Pennsylvania, and Henry L. Hershey is hereby desig-
nated as collector of the consolidated district.
This order is to take effect October i, 1912.
WM H TAFT
