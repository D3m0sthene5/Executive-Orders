Executive Orber.
All employees of plants commandeered by the Ordnano Jeputnient and
all employees under agency contracts, either for the construction or for the
construction and operation of Ordnance plants, are hereby excepted from the
provisions of the civil service act and rules.
WOODROW WILSON
THE~ WHITE ROUSE,
19 March., 1918.
