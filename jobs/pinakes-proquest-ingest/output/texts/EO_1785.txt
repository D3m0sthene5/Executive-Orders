Dr. Park Howvell may be appointed as acting assistant surgeon in the
Public Health Service, for a period not to exceed one year, without com-
pliance with the civil service rules and regulations.
This order is recommended by the Secretary of the Treasury because
the work to which Dr. Howell is to be assigned demands the services of an
additional officer and there is no commissioned officer available for assign-
ment to the duty. The Civil Service Commission does not concur in the
recommendation for the issuance of the order.
WOODROW WILSON
THE WHITE HOUSE,
June 9, 1913.
