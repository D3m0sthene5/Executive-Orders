The Executive Order (No. 1237) of August 15, 1910, reserving for naval pur-
poses the following described area of land on Cordova Point, Prince William Sound,
Alaska:
Beginning at a point on. the east bank of Orca Inlet represented upon
the United States Coast and Geodetic Survey Chart No. 8520 in red ink
lines, N. 690 30' W. true, 2180 feet from a Bench Mark of said survey and
shown on said chart in approximate longitude 1450 57' W., and latitude
590 27' N., thence in a southerly and easterly direction following the line
of Orca Inlet to the Bench Mark,- thence easterly continuing along said
shore line to a point on the north shore of a creek N. 160 30' E., true, 2600
feet from said bench mark, thence westerly in a straight line to the place'
of beginning, containing about eighty-two (82) acres.
which area was previously reserved and set aside as a part of the Chiugach National
Forest, is hereby rescinded and the said area reserved thereby for naval purposes
is hereby restored to its former National Forest status.
