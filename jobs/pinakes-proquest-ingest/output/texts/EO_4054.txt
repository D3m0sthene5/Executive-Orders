Executive Ober
WASHINGTON -T
It is hereby ordered, under authority of the act of Congress approved June 25,
1910 (36 Stat., 847), as amended by the act of August 24, 1912 (37 Stat., 497),
and subject to the conditions therein expressed, that the part of the NW % of NW 4
of Sec. 2, T. 16 N., R. 2 W., W. M., lying north and west of Deep Lake, in the
State of Washington, be and the same is hereby temporarily withdrawn in aid of
legislation to authorize the use of such land for park purposes.
CALVIN COOLIDGE
THE WHITE HOUSE,
July 24, 1924.
