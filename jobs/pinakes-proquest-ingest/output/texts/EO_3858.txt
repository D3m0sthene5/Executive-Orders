Under authority contained in the Act of Congress approved June 25, 1910
(36 Stat., 847), as amended by the Act of August 24, 1912 (37 Stat., 497), and sub-
ject to the conditions, provisions and limitations of said Acts, it is hereby ordered
that the public lands in TV. 23 S., R. 31 E., and Sees. 10, 11, 12, 13, 14 and 24,
TV. 23 S., R. 30 E., M. D. M., California, be, and the same are hereby withdrawn
from settlement, location, sale, entry and all forms of appropriation, pending a
resurvey of the lands under authority of the Act of Congress approved March 3,
1909 (3 Stat., 845) this withdrawal to remain in full force and effect from the
date hereof until the resurvey is duly approved and accepted and the plats
thereof are officially tied in the proper U. S. local land office, unless another
(late be fixed by the Commissioner of the General Laud Office, whcretipoxt this
withdrawal will become of no effect.
