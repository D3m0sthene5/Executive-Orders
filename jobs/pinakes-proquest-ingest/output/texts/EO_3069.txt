Inasmuch as Edward John Norton entered the Consular Sevie, on March
30, 1907, after attaining the prescribed rating upon examination, under existing
rules, served efficiently for more than six years, during which period he was
twice promoted for meritorious service; voluntarily resigned in 1913; and again
entered the Service in 1916 in an unclassified position of Vice Consul in which
lie has served efficiently for more than two years, he may he reinstated, without
re-examination, in the grade of the Service from which lie resigned, when a
vacancy shall occur.
