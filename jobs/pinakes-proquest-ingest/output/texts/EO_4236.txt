Executive Order
By virtue of the authority vested in me by the act of Congress approved August
1, 1914, making appropriations for the sundry and civil expenses of the Government
for the fiscal year ended June 30, 1915, the customs station at Westhope, North
Dakota, is hereby created a customspot of entiy in customs collection district
No. 34 (Dakota) with headquarters at Pembina, North Dakota, effective June 1,
1925.
