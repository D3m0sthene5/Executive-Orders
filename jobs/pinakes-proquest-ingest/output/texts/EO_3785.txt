In accordance with authority granted by the Act of September 7, 1916,
U. S. Statutes at Large, Volume 39, page 730, it is heicby ordered that the
following-named vessels be transferred from the Navy Department to the U. S.
Shipping Board:
U. S. S. PEACC
U. S. S. W4BI4,R
U. S. S. WILLET
It is further ordered that the U. S. Shipping Board, or its successors, return
the above mentioned vessels to the Navy Department on 30 days' notice from
the Secretary of the Navy; that any and all arrangements the U. S. Shipping
Board may make with any other party or parties concerning these vessels be
made subject to this condition, and the U. S. Shipping Board's ownership of
said vessels is hereby restricted accordingly.
Should these vessels cease at any time to be needed by the U. S. Shipping
Board they are to be returned to the Navy Department. Return of said vessels
for any reason shall be made without exchange of funds.
WARREN G HARDING
THE WHITE HOUSE,
February 13, 1923.
