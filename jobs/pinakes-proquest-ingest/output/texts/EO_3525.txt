Schedule " A," Subdivision VIII, of persons excepted from examination
under the civil service rules, is hereby amended by the addition of a new para-
graph to be numbered 27, and to read as follows:
27. Clerk to sign under the direction of the Secretary, in his name
and for him, his approval of all tribal deeds.
The Secretary of the Interior believes that the position should be within
the excepted class because of the individual and confidential character of its
duties. The Civil Service Commission concurs in recommending this order.
