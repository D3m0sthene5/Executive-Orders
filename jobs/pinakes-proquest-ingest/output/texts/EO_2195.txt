Mr. James J. Harley may be reinstated to a position in the Post Office
at Philadelphia, Pennsylvania, without regard to the length of time he has
been separated from the service.
Mr. Harley was a clerk in the Philadelphia Post Office from June t,
1893, to November 15, 1900o, when he resigned. His record during that
time was entirely satisfactory. His reinstatement is recommended by the
postmaster at Philadelphia, who states that he is personally acquainted with
Mr. Harley and feels that he is mentally and physically capable of per-
forming with satisfaction and efficiency the duties of a postal clerk.
The Civil Service Commission does not concur in the recommendation.
WOODROW WILSON
THE WHITE HOUSE,
14t May, 1915.
