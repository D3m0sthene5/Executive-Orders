ALASKA LIGHTHOUSE RESERVES
Pursuant to authority conferred upon me by the act of Congress approved
June 25, 1910 (36 Stat., 847), as amended by the act of Congress of August 24,
1912 (37 Stat., 497),. entitled "An Act to authorize the President of the United
States to make withdrawals of public lands in certain cases", it is hereby ordered
that the following described lands in Alaska be and the same are hereby withdrawn
from settlement, location, sale or entry, and reserved for the use of the Department
of Commerce for lighthouse purposes:
That part of the Point Spencer Abandoned Military Reservation on the penin-sula embracing Point Spencer, Alaska, that lies north of a true east and west linedrawn across the peninsula at a distance of 3,040 feet, south true from the mean
high water line at the northern most part of the point;
Rugged Island located in approximately latitude 59° 52' 00" N., longitude
1490 22' 00" W., from Greenwich, in Resurrection Bay south coast of Alaska.
CALVIN COOLIDGE
THE WHITE HOUSE,
May 11, 1925.
