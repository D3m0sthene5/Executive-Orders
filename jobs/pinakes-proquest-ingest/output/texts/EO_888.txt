Executive Orber
PIKE NATIONAL FOREST
COLORADO
It is hereby ordered that on and after July i, 1908, the land within the
boundary shown on the attached diagram, heretofore set apart, reserved,
and proclaimed as parts of the Pikes Peak and Medicine Bow National
Forests, shall be known as the Pike National Forest. The remaining por-
tions of the Pikes Peak National Forest shall continue reserved and con-
stitute parts of the Arapaho and Leadville National Forests. It is not
intended by this order to release any land from reservation or to reserve
any land not heretofore embraced in a National Forest.
THEODORE ROOSEVELT
Ti- V HITS; HOUSE,
July I, 1908.
