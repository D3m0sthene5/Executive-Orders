AMENDMENT TO THE CIVIL SERVICE RULES.
Schedule A of classified positions excepted from examination under
Rule II, clause 3, is hereby amended by adding to Subdivision VIII,
Department of the Interior, the following paragraph:
26. All employees of the Neopit Lumber Mills on the
Menominee Indian reservation in Wisconsin.
The Neopit Lumbering Project is a commercial enterprise conducted
for the profit and sole benefit of the Menominee tribe of Indians, and in
its operation is in competition with private enterprises of the same kind.
It thus differs from any other enterprise conducted for the benefit of the
Indians, and as it was believed by the Secretary of the Interior that the
work could be carried on more economically and therefore with more
benefit to the Indians with freedom of employment he recommended that
the employees be excepted from examination. After full and careful
consideration, the Civil Service Commission reached the conclusion that
the conditions surrounding the operation of the Neopit Lumber Mills
warrant it in concurring in the recommendation of the Secretary of the
Interior.
WM H TAFT
THE WHITE HOUSE,
November 19, 1912.
