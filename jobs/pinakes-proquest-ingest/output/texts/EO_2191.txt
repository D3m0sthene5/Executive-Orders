Executive Order
Mrs. Enna Wilson Noel may be reinstated to a position in the classi-
fied service in the War Department without regard to the requirements
of the civil service rules.
Mrs. Noel is the widow of Edward McC. Noel, whose death on April
16, 1915, terminated a service of nearly thirty-three years in the office of
the Surgeon General, and leaves her dependent upon employment for
support. Prior to her marriage she served as clerk in the office of the
Surgeon General from December 18, 1893, to February 9, i 9oo, and pre-
viously in other offices since November 7, 1 889. Her reinstatement is
recommended by the Secretary of War and the Surgeon General of the
Army.
The Civil Service Commission does not concur in the recommendation.
WOODROW WILSON
THE WHITE HOUSE,
i i May, 1915.
