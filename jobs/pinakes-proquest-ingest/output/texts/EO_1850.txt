T, Woodrow Wilson, President of the United States, by virtue of the
authority vested in me by Section 3141 of the Revised Statutes of the
United States, hereby order that the counties of Guilford, Randolph and
Rockingham, now a part of the Fourth Internal Revenue Collection Dis-
trict of North Carolina, be transferred to and made a part of the Fifth
Internal Revenue Collection District of North Carolina.
This order to take effect on the First day of November, 1913.
WOODROW WILSON
THE WHITE HOUSE,
29 October, 1913.
