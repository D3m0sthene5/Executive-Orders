ORDER
Of restoration of certain lands included in Alaska Townsite and Rail-
road Withdrawal No. 2.
Whereas, by Executive order of June 23, 1914, certain lands were
withdrawn, pursuant to the provisions of the act of March 12, 1914 (38
Stat., 305), and reserved for townsites and railroad purposes, or other
construction work contemplated by said act; and,
Whereas, it is now made to appear that some of the tracts so with-
drawn, as described below, were settled upon prior to withdrawal, and
valuable improvements have been placed thereon; and,
Whereas, the Alaska Engineering Commission has reported that the
said tracts so-settled upon may be patented without interference with the
construction of railroads in Alaska; now-
Therefore, It is ordered that the aforesaid withdrawal be, and the
same is, hereby revoked as to the following tracts, viz.:
