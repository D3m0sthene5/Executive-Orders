Executive Orer
It is hereby ordered that an unsurveyed island or rock represented
upon U. S. Coast and Geodetic Survey Chart of Los Angeles Harbor and
vicinity No. 5143, as being in the Pacific Ocean opposite Point Vincente,
the same also appearing from the official plat on file in the General Land
Office of the survey of the Rancho Los Palos Verdes, although not shown
thereon, as being opposite Lot 37 and what is designated as El Codo in
approximate Sec. 14, T. 5 S., R. 15 WV., S. B. M., California, be, and the same
is hereby reserved for lighthouse purposes, subject to any existing valid
rights in and to the same.
WOODROW WILSON
THE WHITE HOUSE,
14 February, 1917.
[No. 2528.]
