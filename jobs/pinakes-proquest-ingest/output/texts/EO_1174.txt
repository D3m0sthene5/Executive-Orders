Executive Orber,
Henry Bartalot, formerly employed as a marine engineer in the Quar-
termaster's Department at Large, may be reinstated as a marine engineer
without regard to the length of separation from the service.
This action is taken on recommendation of the Secretary of War and
the Civil Service Commission, based on a report of the Quartermaster
General, indicating that Mr. Bartalot's discharge from the position of ma-
rine engineer on March 17, 19o6, was not justified and as the Commission,
if the case had been presented within one year from Bartalot's discharge,
would have permitted his reinstatement under the rules.
WM H TAFT
THE WHITE HOUSE,
Marcha 7, 1910.
