Executive Orber
Regulating Operation of Street Railway Cars at Crossings.
By virtue of authority vested in me I hereby establish the following
Exective Order for the Canal Zone:
Section i. It shall be unlawful for a motorman or any person in con-
trol of a street railway car to run same over or upon any street crossing, road
crossing, or street railwaycrsig at a speed of more than fv ie e
hour, and without commencing to sound gong, horn or whistle when at least
one hundred feet from said crossing, and continuing to sound same until
the crossing has been passed.
Section 2. It shall be unlawvful for a motorman or any person in con-
trol of a street railway carnto run same over or upon any railroad crossing,
without bringing the car to a full stop at least ten feet from the nearest rail,
and without ascertaining from a viewv of the railroad track made either by
himself or by the conductor that the crossing may be safely passed.
Section 3. A violation of any of the provisions of this order shall be
punished by a fine of not more than one hundred dollars, or imprisonment
for not more than thirty days, or by both fine and imprisonment in the
discretion of the Court.
Section 4.. This order shall take effect thirty days from and after this
date.

CHALLIS, LEMHI, SALMON, AND SAWTOOTH NATIONAL FORESTS,
IDAHO.
The several Proclamations dated May 19, 1913 , withdrawing for clas-
sification under the provisions of the Act of June 25, 1910 (36 Stat., 847),
certain areas thereby excluded from the Challis, Lemhi, Salmon, and Saw-
tooth National Forests, in the State of Idaho, and providing for their sub-
sequent restoration, arc hereby amended to revoke such withdrawals and
to admit of the restoration of the public lands therein in a mnanner- author-
ized by the Act of September 30, 19 13 (38 Stat., r''3).
It appearing proper and necessary, in the interest of equal opportunity
and good administration, that all of the lands excluded by said Proclama-
tions subject to disposition should be restored to settlement in advance of
entry, it is hereby ordered, pursuant to the authority reposed in me by the
aforesaid Act of September 30, 1913, that such lands, subject to valid
rights and the provisions of existing withdrawals, shall be open to actual
settlers only under the provisions of the homestead laws for a period of
twventy-eight days from and including nine o'clock a. ini., standard time, on
the fifty-sixth day from and after the date of this order, and thereafter to
entry and disposition under any and all of the public land laws applicable
thereto: Provided, that the rights of the State under the provisions of the
Act of March 3, 1893 (27 Stat., 592), shall not be abridged in so far as
any of such lands are affected thereby. Unsurveyed lands are not subject
to the provisions of said Act, but in the absence of a prior valid adverse
right, the preference accorded the State thereby, where the township has
leen surveyed and the plat thereof filed while the lands were reserved for
forestry purposes or pending classification, will attach immediately upon
the restoration of such lands to selection and entry under the general land
laws of the United States as herein provided and continue for sixty days.
Persons who go upon any of the lands to be restored hereunder and
perform any act of settlement thereon from and including the date :,f this
order until nine o'clock a. in., standard time, on the fifty-sixth day from
and after the date hereof, or who arc on or are occupying any part of such
lands at said hour, except those having valid subsisting settlement rights
initiated prior to reservation and since maintained, will be considered and
dealt with as trespassers and preference will he given the prior legal appli-
cant, notwithstanding such unlawful settlement or occupancy; Provided,
however, that nothing herein shall prevent persons from going upon and
over the lands to examine them with a viewv to thereafter going upon and
making settlement thereon when the lands shall become subject thereto in
accordance with this order. Pcirsons having prior settlement rights, as
above defined, will be allowed to make entry in conformity with existing
law and regulations.
