Executive 'rber.
Officers and employees of the Federal Government may serve as
election officers in Porto Rico, when appointed for that purpose by the
Governor of Porto Rico.
The effect of this order is to waive so much of section i of Civil
Service Rule I, prohibiting political activity, and of the Executive order
of January 17, 1873, prohibiting the holding of local office, as would pre-
vent federal officers and employees in Porto Rico from serving in the
capacity of election officer w11en their service is desired by the Governor
of Porto Rico.
The Civil Service Commission, which recommends the issuance of
this order, may revoke the authority granted if it finds that the privilege
is abused.
WOODROW WILSON
THE WHITE HOUSE,
October 31, 1914.
[No. 207a.]
