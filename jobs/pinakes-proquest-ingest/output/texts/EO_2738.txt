Under authority of the act of Congress approved June 25, 1910 (36 Stat.,
847) as amended by the act of August 24, 1912 (3 Stat., 497), it is hereby
ordered that two unsurveyed islands in the Straits of Juan de Fuca, San Juan
Channel, one known as Reef Island situated approximately in Sec. 13 and the
other known as Yellow Island situated approximately in Sec. 26, T. 36 N.,
R. 3 WV., XV. M., Washingt~on, represented upon U. S. C. and G. Survey Chart
No. 6380, be, and the same are hereby temporarily withdrawn from all forms
of disposal pending a survey of the same, if found to be public lands, under the
direction and supervision of the Commissioner of the General Land Office, in
order to determine they exact parts on each island desired to be reserved for
lighthouse purposes.
K WOODROW WILSON
THE XVHITE HOUSE,
24 October, 1917.
