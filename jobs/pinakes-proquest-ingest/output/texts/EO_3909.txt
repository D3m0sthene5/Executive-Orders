]Executive Order
Under authority contained in the act of Congress approved June 25, 1910
(36 Slat., 847), as amended by the act of August 24, 1912 (37 Stat., 497), and
subject to the conditions, provisions, and limitations of said acts, it is hereby
ordered that the public lands in T. 52 N., R. So W., 6th P. M., Wyoming, be and
the same are hereby withdrawn from settlement, location, sale, entry and all
forms of appropriation, pending a resurvey of said township under authority
contained in the act of Congress approved March 3, 1909 (3 Stat., 845) ; this
withdrawal to remain in full force and effect from the date hereof until the
resurvey is duly approved and accepted and the approved plat thereof is officially
filed in the proper U. S. local land office, unless another date be fixed by the
Commissioner of the General Land Office, whereupon this withdrawal will
become of no effect.
CALVIN COOLIDGE
THE WHITE HousE,
September 29, 1923.
