Executive Omer
Mrs. Edna Rosier may be appointed to the position of telephone operator in
the post office of Dayton, Ohio, without regard to the requirements of the civil
service rules.
This Order is issued upon the recommendation of the Postmaster General who
states that Mrs. Rosier, who is exceptionally skilled as a telephone operator, has
been employed as operator since July 1, 1924, under an allowance for telephone
service, as it was thought that such an appointment was permissible without regard
to the civil service rules. She is dependent upon her own labor for the support of
herself and two small children, her husband being an inmate of the Dayton State
Hospital.
The Civil Service Commission does not concur.
Done for charity.
CALVIN COOLIDGE
THE WHITE HOUSE,
May 18, 1927.
