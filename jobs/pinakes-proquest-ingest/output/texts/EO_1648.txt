In accordance with the provisions of Section 2252 of the Revised
Statutes of the United States, and by virtue of the authority therein
given, it is hereby ordered that:
The United States Land Office located at Vernal, Utah, be discon-
tinued, and the business and archives of said office transferred to, and
made a part of the United States Land Office at Salt Lake City, Utah.
WM H TAFT
THE WHITE HOUSE,
Nov. 23, 1912.
