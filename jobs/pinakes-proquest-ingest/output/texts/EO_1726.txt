AARON BURNETT, now a laborer in the Treasury Department, may be
transferred to any sub-clerical classified grade in that Department without
compliance with Civil Service rules.
This order is recommended by the Secretary of the Treasury in view
of the long and faithful service rendered by Mr. Burnett in his present
position. The Civil Service Commission does not concur in this order.
