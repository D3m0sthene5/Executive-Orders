Paragraph 2 of Subdivision I of Schedule B of the Civil Service
Rules, of classified positions which may be filled upon non-competitive
examination, is hereby amended by the insertion before the word "mes-
senger" of the words "junior clerk". As amended the paragraph will
read as follows:
2. Junior clerk, messenger, assistant messenger, and messenger
boy in the Office of Indian Affairs when filled by Indians.
Twvo positions of junior clerk at $720 per annum were created by
Congress in the Indian Office in lieu of the two positions of assistant
clerk formerly in the Office of the Supervisor of Indian Employment and
paid from the field appropriations. The positions are designed entirely
for the benefit of graduates of the various Indian Schools and are filled
only by Indians, who thus acquire necessary clerical experience.
WOODROW WILSON
THE WHITE HOUSE,
ite October, 1913.
