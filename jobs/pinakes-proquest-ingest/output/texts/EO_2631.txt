HUMBOLDT- RUBY-SANTA ROSA NATIONAL FORESTS
NEVADA
Under authority of the Act of Congress of June 4, 1897 (30 Stat., 11),
and upon recommendation of the Secretary of Agriculture, it is hereby ordered
that on and after July I, 1917, all the lands included within the boundaries
of the Rb, and the Santa Rosa National Forests. Nevada, as fixed and defined !r i -71 7
_. f by proclamations of June 19, 1912 (No. 1202), and April x, 1911 (No. 1120),
and modified by Executive Order No. 1429, of November 3, 1911, be trans-
ferred to and administered as a part of the Humboldt National Forest Nevada. .' / 7' 6~
It is intended by this Executive Order, for economy of administration,
to merge into one National Forest, thereafter to be known as the Humboldt
National Forest, all the lands which on July 1, 1917, are within the Ruby,
the Santa Rosa, and the Humboldt National Forests, and the boundaries of
the Humboldt National Forest, as they exist at that date, are hereby modified
accordingly.
This Executive Order is not intended to add to the Humboldt National
Forest, any lands which on July 1, 1917, are not embraced within the Ruby
or the Santa Rosa National Forests, or to release from reservation for National
Forest purposes any lands at that date within the Ruby or Santa Rosa National
Forests.
WOODROW WILSON
THE WHITE HOUSE,
6 June, 1917.
