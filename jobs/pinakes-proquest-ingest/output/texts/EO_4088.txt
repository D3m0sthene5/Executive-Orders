TETON NATIONAL FOREST
Under authority of the act of Congress approved June 4, 1897 (30 Stat, 11,
34-36), and on the recommendation of the Secretary of Agriculture, it is herby
ordered that the boundaries of the Teton National Forest in Wyoming be modified
by excluding therefrom Lot 6 of Sec. 27, T. 45 N., R. 113 W., 6th P. M, containting
1.96 acres.
It is further ordered, pursuant to Public Resolution No. 29 of February 14,
1920 (41 Stat., 434), as amended by Public Resolutions Nos. 36 and 79, approved
January 21 and December 28, 1922, respectively, (42 Stat., 358, 1087), that the
above described tract, which is included in an existing withdrawal for coal clul
fication, shall, subject to valid rights, be opened only to surface entry under the
homestead laws by a qualified ex-service man of the War with Germany, under
the terms and conditions of said resolutions and the regulations issued thereunder,
for a period of 91 days, beginning with the 63rd day from and after the date hareo,
and thereafter if unentered to appropriation under any public land law applicable
thereto by the general public.
Subsequent to the date hereof and prior to the date of restoration to general
disposition as herein provided, no right may be acquired to the excluded land by
settlement in advance of entry, or otherwise except strictly in accordance hereith.
