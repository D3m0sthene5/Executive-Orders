IT Is HEREBY ORDERED, That the following-described tracts of land
in the townsite of Port Angeles, Washington, be, and the same are hereby,
reserved for Government use in accordance with the Act of Congress,
approved March 16, 1912 (37 Stat., 74), namely : Lots six to fifteen,
inclusive, in block thirty-two, for a site for a Federal building, to be under
the supervision and control of the Treasury Department; and lot one for
a storm-warning tower and lots seventeen and eighteen, in said block thirty-
twvo, for the use of the Weather Bureau, and Forest Service of the Agri-
cultural Department.
WM H TAFT
THE WHITE HOUSE,
December 12, 1912.
