Executive Orber
Mrs. Ida Diamond may be appointed to the classified position of guide in the
Bureau of Engraving and Printing without compliance with the requirements of
the civil service rules.
This order is issued on the recomimendlation of the Secretary of the Treasury,
who states that Mrs. Diamond is the widow of Constantine Diamond, a veteran of
the Civil War, and that she has been employed with the exception of a little more
than two years in the Bureau of Engraving and Printing in the unclassifiedl positions
of charwoman and forewoman of charwomen from May 7, 1593, to the present time.
The Civil Service Commission reports that it is without authority to make an
affirmative recommendation, but states that in view of her long and faithful service
her appointment by Executive Order would not (10 violence to the spirit of the
civil service rules.
Done for charity.
CALVIN COOLIDGE
THE WHITE HOUSE,
September 18, 19N.5.
