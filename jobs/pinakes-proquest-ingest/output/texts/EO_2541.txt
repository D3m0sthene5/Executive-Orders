Executive Orber.
Schedule A, Subdivision V, paragraph 2, of the civil service rules, which
at present excepts from examination civilian professors, instructors, and
teachers in the Naval Academy, is hereby amended to read as follows:
2. In the United States Naval Academy:
(a) Professors, instructors, and teachers;
(b) Baker helpers, coffeemen, firemen, linenman, pantrymen,
dish pantrymen, scullions, utility mein waiters, and, when
promoted from the position of wvaiter, head and assistant
head waiter.
WOODROW WILSON
THE WHITE HOUSE,
27 February, 1917.
[No. 2541.]
