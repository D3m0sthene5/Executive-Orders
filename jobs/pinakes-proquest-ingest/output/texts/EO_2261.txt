Mr. Alexander C. Dowvney may be appointed a clerk in the competi-
tive classified service of the Treasury Department without compliance with
the civil service rules.
Mr. Downey has served since September 2, 1913, in the excepted
position of private secretary to the Comptroller of the Treasury. In addi-
tion to the duties of private secretary it appears that Mr. Downey has
been engaged on the work of passing upon the contracts of Army officers,
which is considered very complicated work. The Auditor for the War
Department states that he is in great need of a clerk capable of auditing
the contracts of Army officers and believes that Mr. Downey's experience
ini the Comptroller's office in similar duties eminently qualifies him to per-
form this work.
This order is issued on the recommendation of the Secretary of the
Treasury, the Civil Service Commission concurring because of testimony
as to Mr. Downey's special training.
WOODROW WILSON
THE WHITE HOUSE,
21 October, 1915.
