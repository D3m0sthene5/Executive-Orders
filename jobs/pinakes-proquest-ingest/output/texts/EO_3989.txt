Executive Othber
Major John D. Cutter may be appointed to a classified position in the
United States Veterans Bureau without regard to the provisions of the Civil
Service Act and Rules.
Major Cutter is serving under temporary appointment in an executive
and administrative capacity as personal aid to the Director of the Veterans'
Bureau, and his services are considered necessary for the proper administration
of the office.
This Order is issued on the recommendation of the United States Civil
Service Commission.
CALVIN COOLIDGE
THE WHITE HC: sE,
April '4, 1924.
