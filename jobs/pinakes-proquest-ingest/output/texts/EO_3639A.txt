the laws of Ohio.
My reasons for the foregoing determination, order, rule, and regulation, are :
(a) An offer to make such a lease has been made and is now pending, and
if accepted, must be accepted at once;
(b) 'rhe said real estate above described adjoins a similar tract of thirty-
seven and one-half (37,k') feet to the north thereof, which two tracts constitute

years been improved with a substantial building which constitutes one structure
on said entire seventy-five (75) feet, and said The Swan Company is the owner
of the said similar tract to the north and in position to lease said premises in
their present condition, whereas lease to another party would require remodeling
of buildings.
(c) The said real estate has always been profitably disposed of by long ti'e
leases, the present lease expiring March 31, 1925, and it being necessary in ors
to arrange for a new lease upon said property that the same be made in ample

States, England and lF rance.
