Executive Orbet.
WHEREAS, on the 3rd day of November, 1922, an executive order was
promulgated creating the Southwestern Alaska Fisheries Reservation to in-
sure the protection of the fisheries in the waters therefore; and
WHEREAS, the Act of Congress of June 6, 1924, entitled "An Act for the
Protection of the Fisheries of Alaska and for other Purposes" provides for
the protection and administration of the fisheries in all Alaskan waters, and
the executive reservation aforesaid has therefore become unnecessary;
THEREFORE, the said executive order of November 3, 1922, creating the
Southwestern Alaska Fisheries Reservation, is hereby revoked.

creating the Aleutian Islands Reservation, which shall remain in full force
and effect.
