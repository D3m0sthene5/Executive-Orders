In consideration of the long, faithful and meritorious service of Lawrence
Fortune, employed for more than twenty years in the Bureau of Supplies and
Accounts, Navy Department, who died January 25, 1923, his widow, Mrs.
Nellye C. Fortune, may be appointed as clerk at $iooo per annum, in the Bureau
of Supplies and Accounts, Navy Department, without regard to the civil service
rules or regulations. The Civil Service Commission does not concur in the
recommendation of the Acting Secretary of the Navy for this order.
WARREN G HARDING
THE WHITE HOUSE,
June 20, 1923.
