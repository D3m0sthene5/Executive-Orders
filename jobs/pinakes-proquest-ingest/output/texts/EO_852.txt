SANTA BARBARA NATIONAL FOREST
CALIFORNIA
It is hereby. ordered that on and after July i, 1908, the land within the
boundary shown on the attached diagram, heretofore set apart, reserved,
and proclaimed as parts of the Santa Barbara, San Gabriel, and San Luis
O)bispo National Forests, shall be known as the Santa Barbara National
Forest. The remaining portion of the Santa Barbara National Forest
shall continue reserved and constitute part of the Angeles National Forest.
It is not intended by this order to release any land from reservation or to
reserve any land not heretofore embraced in a Nyational Forest.
