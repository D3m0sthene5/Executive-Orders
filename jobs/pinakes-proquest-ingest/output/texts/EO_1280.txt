In accordance with the provisions of Section 2251 of the Revised
Statutes of the United States, and by virtue of the authority therein given,
it is hereby ordered that:
The location of the United States Land Office for the New Orleans
Lind district in the State of Louisiana be changed from Newv Orleans to
Baton Rouge.
WM H TAFT
THE WHITE HOUSE,
Jan. 6, 1911.
