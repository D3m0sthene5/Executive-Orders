By virtue of the authority vested in me by the Act of Congress approved
August 1, 1914, making appropriations for the sundry civil expenses of the
Government for the fiscal year ended June 30, 1915, K oloa, on the Island of
Kauai, is hereby abolished as a port of entry in customs collection district No. 32
(Hawaii) effective thirty days from this date.
By virtue of the same authority the customs station of Port Allen in collection
district No. 32 (Hawaii) is hereby created a customs port of entry with head-
quarters at Honolulu, effective thirty days from this date.
CALVIN COOL IDGE
THE WHITE HousE,
