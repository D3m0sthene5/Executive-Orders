Executive Qrder.
In accordance with the provisions of Section 2253 of the Revised
Statutes of the United States, and by virtue of the authority therein given,
it is hereby ordered that:
All those parts of townships one hundred and twenty-nine and one
hundred and thirty north, ranges eighty-seven to ninety west, inclusive, in
the State of North Dakota as are not embraced within the Standing Rock
Indian Reservation, and all of townships one hundred and thirty-one and
one hundred and thirty-two north, ranges eighty-seven to ninety west,
inclusive, in North Dakota, be detached from the Lemmon land district in
the State of South Dakota, and attached to and made a part of the
Bismarck land district in North Dakota, and the business ahd archives
pertaining to the lands embraced within said townships and parts of
*townships transferred from the land office at Lemmon, South Dakota, to
the land office at Bismarck, North Dakota.
WM H TAFT
THE WHITE HousE,
October 4 1910.
