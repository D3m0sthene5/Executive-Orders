Stats., 847), and on the recommendation of the Secretary of Agriculture,

of Section 7, Township [3 North, Range 12 West, 5th Principal Meridian,
Arkansas be temporarily withdrawn from settlement, location, sale or entry,
except as provided in said Act, and be reserved for use by the Forest
Service in the administration of the Nation~al Forest.
