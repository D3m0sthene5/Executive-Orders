OREGON NATIONAL FOREST
OREGON
Executive Order No. 1622, dated October I I, 19 12, excluding under
authority of the Act of June 4, 1897 (30 Stat., 11-34), a tract of 200 acres
described as SE. 3 NW. 3, SW. 3/ NE. /, W. I2 SE. 3, and SE.
SE. 14 of Sec. 28, T. 2 N., R. 7 E., W. M., then unsurveyed, from the
Oregon National Forest, Oregon, is hereby modified by striking therefrom
the following provisions:
"The lands hereby eliminated from the Oregon National Forest are
hereby withdrawn for classification under the Act of June 25, 1910 (36 Stat.,
847), and will, when compatible with public interests, be restored to settle-
ment and entry under the laws applicable thereto on such dates as shall be
fixed by the Secretary of the Interior and after such notice as he may deem
advisable."
And it appearing proper and necessary, in the interest of equal oppor-
tunity and good administration, that the tract of land so excluded and
withdrawn should be restored to entry in advance of settlement, it is hereby
ordered, pursuant to the authority reposed in me by the Act of September
30, I913 (38 Stat., 1 i3), that such land, subject to valid rights, shall be
open to entry and selection only for a period of twenty days from and
including 9 o'clock a. in., standard time, May 8, 1914, and thereafter to
disposition under any and all of the public land laws applicable thereto.
WOODROW WILSON
THE WHITE HousE,
