Mr. John J. Farrell may be appointed a letter carrier in the Brooklyn,
New York, post office without reference to the civil service rules.
Mr. Farrell was appointed to the position of special delivery messenger,
which is excepted from examination, on September io, 1906. On August
31, 1907, while in the performance of his duty, his bicycle collided with a
street car, resulting in an injury which required the amputation of his right
arm above the elbow. The postmaster at Brooklyn is of the opinion that
Mr. Farrell is fully competent to discharge the duties of a letter carrier,
and in view of the circumstances under which he was injured favors his
appointment. The Civil Service Commission does not concur in recoin-
mending this order on the ground that the injury unfits him to discharge
the duties of letter carrier.
