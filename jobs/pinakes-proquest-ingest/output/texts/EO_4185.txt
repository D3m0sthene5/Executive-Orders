Executive 'Order
Documents Required of Aliens Entering the Philippine Islands, Guam
and American Samoa
By virtue of the authority vested in me by the Act of Congress approved
May 22, 1918, entitled, "An Act to Prevent in Time of War Departure from and
t w Entry into the United States Contrary to the Public Safety" , as extended by the
Act of Congress of March 2, 1921, entitled, "An Act making appropriations for
Sq1 the Diplomatic and Consular Service for the fiscal year ending June 30, 1922 ",
I hereby prescribe the following regulations governing the entry of aliens into the
Philippine Islands, G and American Samoa.
Aliens entering the Philipine Island. must present passports or documents
in the nature of passports, duly visaed by consular officers of the United States.
Exception:
Seamen on vessels of all nationalities touching at a port of the Philippine
Islands are not required to be documented. Masters of such vessels are, therefore,
not required to present visaed crew lists.
II
Aliens entering Guam or American Samoa do not require documents of any
kind.
This order shall take effect May 1, 1926i.

TILE WHITE HOUSE
