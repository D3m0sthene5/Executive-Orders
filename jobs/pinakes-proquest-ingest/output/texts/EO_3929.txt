AUTHORIZING THE ALIEN PROPERTY CUSTODIAN
TO SELL CERTAIN PROPERTY AT PRIVATE SALE
By virtue of the authority vested in me by an Act of Congress entitled
"An Act to define, regulate, and punish trading with the enemy and for other
purposes", approved October 6, 1917, known as the "Trading with the Enemy
Act", and the Amendment to such Act embodied in "An Act making appro-
priations to supply urgent deficiencies in the appropriations for the fiscal year
ending June 30, 1918, and prior fiscal years on account of war expenses and for
other purposes", approved March 28, 1918, and the Amendment to such Act
embodied in "An Act making appropriations to supply deficiencies in appro-
priations for the fiscal year ending June 30, 1919, and prior fiscal years on
account of war expenses, and for other purposes", approved November 4, 1918,
and all other amendments thereto, to make certain orders such as that herein
contained, I hereby, in the public interest, make the following determination
and order :
The Alien Propet utodin is hereby authorized to sell at private sale,
at such time and place as to him or to his authorized agent may seem advisable,
without public or other advertisement, Five Hundred and Ten (510o) shares of
the common stock of the National Liberty Insurance Company, a corporation
of the State of New York, of the par value of One Hundred ($ioo.oo) Dollars
each, at a price of One Hundred and Eighty-Five ($i85.oo) Dollars per share for
each share of said National Liberty Insurance Company stock, which said shares
were heretofore seized by the Alien Property Custodian under the provisions
of said Act.
My reasons for the foregoing determination and order are:
(a) That property such as that herein described cannot usually be sold to
advantage at public sale after public or other advertisement.
(b) That an advantageous offer for the purchase of said shares of stock
has been received by the Alien Property Custodian.
CALVIN COOL IDGE
