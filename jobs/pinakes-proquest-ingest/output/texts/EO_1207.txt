Executive Order
It is hereby ordered that two unsurveyed islands situated in the
Columbia River in section 5, Township 21 North, Range 22 East, W. 1V.,
Washington, and four unsurveyed islands situated in the river in sections
77, 28, 33, 34, and 35, Towvnship 14 North, Range 23 East, W. M., Wash-
ington, which islands are colored red upon the photolithographic copies of
the official plats approved May 25, 1882, and January 28, 1882, on file- in
the General Land Office, herewith, be, and the same are hereby, reserved
for use of the War Department in connection with the improvement of
the river.
Wm H TAFT
THE WHITE HOUSE,
May 27, 1910.
[No. 1207._
