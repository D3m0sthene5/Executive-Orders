ALASKA ROAD COMMISSION RESERVE
Pursuant to authority conferred upon me by the act of Congress approved
June 25, 1910 (36 Stat., 847), entitled "An Act to authorize the President of the
United States to make withdrawals of public lands in certain cases," as amended
by the act of Congress approved August 24, 1912 (37 Stat., 497), it is hereby
ordered that the following described lands and buildings thereon, forming part of
the old military reservation of Fort Davis, Alaska, transferred to the Department
of the Interior by Executive Order No. 3574, approved November 4, 1921, be, and
the same are hereby, withdrawn from settlement, location, sale or entry, and reserved
for the use of the Alaska. Road Commission in the construction and maintenance of
military and post roads, bridges and trails in Alaska:
Beginning at a point on the southern boundary of the Military
Reservation of Fort Davis, Alaska, which bears south 72 deg. 35' east,
1720 feet from 4" x 4" Post marked U. S.. N. R. at the southwest corner
of *said reservation, and extending thence north 17 deg. 25' east 300 feet;
thence south 72 deg. 35' east 200 feet; thence south 17 deg. 25' west
300 feet; thence north 72 deg. 35' west 200 feet to the place of begin-ning. Area 1.378 acres, more or less.
It is further ordered that building No. 29, a small frame building on the extreme
southeast limit of said reservation, be reserved for the use of the Alaska Road.
Commission, to be demolished or transferred to the tract herein reserved.
