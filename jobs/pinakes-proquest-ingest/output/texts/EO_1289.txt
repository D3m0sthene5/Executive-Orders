Schedule A of classified positions excepted from examination under
Civil Service Rule Ii, clause 3, is hereby amended by the addition of a newv
paragraph to Sub-division V, Navy Department, to be numbered 3, and to
read as follows
3. All positions in the Island of Guam.
The Civil Service Commission joins the Navy Department in recom-
mending the exception of these employees from the requirements of
examination, in view of the isolation of the service, the small number
involved, low salaries paid which make it difficult to fill the positions from
general registers and limited competition, because of the small number of
available applicants, should examinations be held in the Island of Guam.
