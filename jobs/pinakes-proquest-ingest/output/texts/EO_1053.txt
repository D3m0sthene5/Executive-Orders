Executive Orber.
Mr. F. B. Simons may be reinstated as a clerk in the Bureau of
Internal Revenue without reference to the length of time since his separa-
tion from the service. This order is made to enable the Commissioner
of Internal Revenue to secure a civil servant with the needed technical
knowledge for cases involving denatured alcohol. It is made upon the
recommendation of the Civil Service Commission the Secretary of the
Treasury and the Commissioner of Internal Revenue-
WM H TAFT
THE WHITE HOUSE,
March 2¢, 1909.
