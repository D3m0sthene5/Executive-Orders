Executive Orbet.
It is hereby ordered that on Saturdays during July, Augrust and Sep-
tember, until further notice, four hours, exclusive of time for luncheon,
shall constitute a day's work for skilled mechanics and laborers, and em-
ployees in the Classified Civil Service at the Navy Yard, Washington:
Provided, that if any skilled mechanic, laborer or employee in the Classified
Civil Service shall work more than four hours on any Saturday during the
months aforesaid, he shall be paid for such extra work at the rate of fifty
Per ceczuu more than his regular and normal compensation and no more.
THEODORE ROOSEVELT
THE WHITE House,
June r8, 1906.
