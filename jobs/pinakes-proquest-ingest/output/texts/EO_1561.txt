M r. Leroy A. McGee may be reinstated to the position of post
office inspector without reference to the time limit upon eligibility for
reinstatement.
Mr. McGee served for four years as a teaches in the Indian Service,
for three years as a clerk in the Office of the Auditor for the Post Office
Department, and three years as a post office inspector, resigning from the
latter position on October 7, 1910. He has passed the teacher examina-
tion, the post office inspector examination, and the immigrant in spector
examination with good ratings. The Chief Post Office Inspector reports
that he wvas a good inspector, that his work was satisfactory, that there is
nothing in the files which would be considered against his reinstatement,
and that since his resignation Mr. McGee has been practicing law. In
view of his good record and the fact that his experience since leaving the
service has been such as to increase his efficiency, the Civil Service
Commission joins with the Postmaster General in recommending the
reinstatement.
