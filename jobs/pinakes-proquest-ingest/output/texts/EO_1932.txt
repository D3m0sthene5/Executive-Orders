Mr. Raymond Augusterfer may be promoted from the position of
mechanician (electrician) on the statutory roll of the Office of the Secretary
of Agriculture, at a salary of $1200 per annum, to the position of chief
engineer on the same roll, at a salary of $2000 per annum, without refer-
ence to Civil Service Rule XI, Clause (b) of Section 2.
The Secretary of Agriculture recommends the promotion of Mrs
Augusterfer, who has been in the Department since April 1904, for the
reason that he has assisted in installing a large portion of the present equip-
ment, and possesses an intimate knowledge of the power plant and of the
intricate system of connections between the various buildings served by it.
He regards the thorough practical qualifications of Mr. Augusterfer to be
of much value to the Department and believes it to be in the interest of
efficient and economical service to promote Mr. Augusterfer to the position
in question.
The Civil Service Commission does not concur in the recommendation.
