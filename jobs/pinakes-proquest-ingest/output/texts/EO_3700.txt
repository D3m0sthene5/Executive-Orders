I, WARREN G. HARDING, President of the United States of America, by
virtue of the authority vested in me as Chief Executive and by virtue of the
power vested in me by the Congress of the United -States of America, hereby
accept the resignation of Edwin P. Shattuck, as a Director and as President of
the United States Grain Corporation designated as such by executive Order,
dated August 21, 19g20, and I hereby designate Edward M. Flesh, the Vice-
President and Treasurer of said United States Grain Corporation, to act here-
after and during the pleasure of the President as the Director and as President
and Treasurer of said United States Grain Corporation, and I direct that the
other directors and executive officers of said Corporation shall be selected by
said Edward M. Flesh, with the approval of the President, and that the capital
stock of said Corporation shall be held and voted or caused to be voted by said
Edward M. Flesh, as President thereof, in the name and for the use and benefit
of the United States as heretofore directed by Executive Order, or as may here-
after from time to time be directed by such order.
WARREN G HARDING
THE WHITE House,
