Schedule A, Subdivision VIII, of the Civil Service Rules is hereby,
amended by the addition of the following paragraph to be numbered 25:
"a25. The Assistant to the Secretary in the Office of the
Secretary of the Interior."
In view of the statement of the Secretary of the Interior that the
position is one which involves duties which are peculiarly personal and
confidential, the Civil Service Commission concurs in the recommendation
that the position be excepted from the requirement of examination.

THE WHITE HOUSE,
