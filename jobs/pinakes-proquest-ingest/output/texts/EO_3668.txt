The Executive order of February 14, 1912, as subsequently amended, per-
mitting employees in the executive civil service permanently residing in certain
incorporated municipalities adjacent to the District of Columbia to become
candidates for or -hold murngipal office therein, is hereby further amended so
as to include the incorporated municipality of Bladensburg, Maryland, among
those named in the order. The first paragraph of, the order of February 14,
1912, as amended, will read as follows.
Employees of the executive civil service permanently residing in the
following incorporated municipalities adjacent to the District of Columbia
will not be prohibited from becoming candidates for or holding municipal
office in such corporations:
In Maryland-Takoma Park, Kensington, Garrett Park, Chevy Chase,
Glen Echo, Hyattsville, Mount Rainier, Somerset,
North Beach, Capitol Heights, Laurel, Riverdale,
Bladensburg.
In Virginia-Falls Church, Vienna, Herndon, Potomac.
This order is recommended by the Civil Service Commission because it
seems necessary to the domestic interests of the government employees, and
for the reason that similar conditions exist in Bladensburg, Maryland,. as in the
other municipalities named in the original order and its amendments.
WARREN G HARDING
THEs WHITE HousE,
April 29, 1922.
