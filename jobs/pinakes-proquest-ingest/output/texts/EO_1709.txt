Executive 'thet,
Paragraph 5, Subdivision 111 of Schedule A of the civil service rules
relating to the Treasury Department is hereby amended to read as follows :
5. One private secretary or confidential clerk to the superin-
tendent in each mint and in the assay office at New York.
This paragraph heretofore read :
5. One private secretary or confidential clerk to the superin-
tendent, one cashier in each mint, and one cashier in the assay
office at Newv York.
The effect of this order is to restore to the competitive classified
service the position of cashier in the Mit Service and to give to the occu-
pants of the position a competitive classified status. Cashiers in the Mint
Service were in the competitive classified service from May 6, 1896, to May
29, 1899. The Director of the Mint on July 31, 1907, recommended that
cashiers in the Mint Service be included in the competitive classified
service. The Secretary of the Treasury on January 23, 1908, requested
the Commission to concur in recommending an Executive order. The
Commission did not believe it advisable at that time to include cashiers in
the competitive classified service on account of the confidential relation-
ship, more or less personal in character, which existed between the super-
intendent of the mint and his cashier. The Commission subsequently
concurred in recommending that cashiers at the smaller assay offices be
made competitive as their duties did not differ essentially from those of
chief clerks as all payments in such offices were made by check as they are
now. At present no cashiers except those in the Mint Service are
excepted from examination.
Of the four cashiers in the Mint Service one wvas promoted from the
competitive classified service and the others have served for periods varying
from two and one-half years to ten years. The Director of the Mint cer-
tified that all these men were selected on account of their known qualifica-
tions, that their records are unblemished and their abilities have been fully
demonstrated. They have been commended by the superintendents as
capable and trustworthy.
The Commission concurs in recommending this order.
WM H TAFT
THE WHITE HOUSE,
