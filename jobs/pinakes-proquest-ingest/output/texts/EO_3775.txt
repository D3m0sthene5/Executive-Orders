]Executive Orber
Under authority contained in the act of Congress approved June 25, 1910
(36 Stat., 847), as amended by the act of August 24, 1912 (37 Stat., 49 7 ), and
subject to the conditions, provisions and limitations of said acts, it is hereby
ordered that the unsurveyed islands belonging to the Government, situated in
the bed of Cross Lake in Tr. 17 N., R,. 14 WV., and Ts. 17 and x 8 N., R. 15 XV.,
Louisiana Meridian, Louisiana, be, and the same are hereby temporarily with-
drawn from settlement, entry and all forms of disposal, for classification and
in aid of contemplated legislation by Congress looking to the disposal of the
same and such withdrawal shall remain in force until revoked by the President
or by act of Congress.
WARREN G HARDING
THEmXWHITE House,
,January 12, 1923.
