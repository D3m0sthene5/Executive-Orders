AUTHORIZING THE AIEN PROPERTY CUSTODIAN TO SEL, CERTAIN PROPERTY
AT PRIVATE SALE.
By virtue of the authority vested in me by an Act of Congress entitled "An
Act to define, regulate, and punish trading with the enemy and for other pur-
poses ", approved October 6, 1917, known as the "Trading with the Enemy
Act ", and the amendment to such Act embodied in "An Act making appropri-
ations to supply urgent deficiencies in the appropriations for the fiscal year
ending June 30, 1918, and prior fiscal years on account of war expenses and for
other purposes", approved March 28, 1918, and the Amendment to such Act
embodied in "An Act making appropriations to supply deficiencies in appropria-
tions for the fiscal year ending June 30, 1919, and prior fiscal years on account
of war expenses, and for other purposes", approved November 4, 1918, and all
other amendments thereto, to make certain orders such as that herein contained,
I hereby, in the public interest, make the following determination and order :
The Ale Propert Custodian is hereby authorized to sell at private sale,
at such time and place as to him or to his authorized agent may seem advisable,
without public or other advertisement One Thousand One Hundred Thirty-two
shares of the 8% preferred stock of Stern Brothers, a corporation of the State
of New York, formerly belonging to A. Schaaffhausenscher Bankverein A. G.,
Cologne, Germany,' at a price of One Hundred Fifteen ($1 15.) Dollars per share
plus interest at the rate of 8% per annum from the last dividend payment to the
date of purchase, which said shares were heretofore seized by the Alien Property
Custodian under the provisions of said Act.
My reasons for the foregoing determination and order are :
(a) That property such as that herein described cannot usually be sold to
advantage at public sale after public or other advertisement.
(b) That an advantageous offer for the purchase of said shares of stock has
been received by the Alien Property Custodian.
(c) That this sale is very advantageous to the original enemy owners, as
the Alien Property Custodian will be getting the highest price at which the stock
can ever sell, as its call price of $115. per share substantially limits the price of
the stock to that figure-as the company has the right to call the stock at any
time on thirty days' notice, either in whole or in part.
CALVIN COOLIDGE
THE WHITE HOUSE,
February 19, 1924.
