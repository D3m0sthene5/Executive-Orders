Executive Order of August 16, 1919, No. 3149, reserving a tract of land
at Seward, Alaska, for use by the Navy Department for naval purposes, is
hereby revoked in so far as the same affects the portion of such tract within
the following described boundaries:
Beginning at the point of intersection of a line drawn parallel to, and
2oo feet easterly from the center line of the Government railroad, with the
south line of Washington Street extended, thence northeasterly, 500 feet
parallel to and 200 feet easterly from the center line of the Government
railroad ; thence south 710 east, 215 feet ; thence south 290 37' west, 422
feet to an intersection with the south line of Washington Street extended,
thence west along the south line of Washington Street extended, 230
feet to the place of beginning, all lying in front of the Town of Seward,
Alaska, and containing approximately 2.15 acres.
And it is hereby ordered, under and pursuant to the provisions of the
Act of Congress approved March 12, 1914 (38 Stat., 305), that the above
described area be, and the same is hereby, reserved for use by the Alaskan
Engineering Commission in connection with the operation of railroad lines
under said act.
WARREN G HARD ING
THE W HITZ HOUSE,
May 3, 1923.
