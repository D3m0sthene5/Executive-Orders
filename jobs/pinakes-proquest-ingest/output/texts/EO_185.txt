AMENDMENT TO THE CIVIL SERVICE RULES.

| In the exercise of the power vested in the President by the
| constitution, and by the virtue of the seventeen hundred and fifty-
third section of the Revised Statutes and of the civil service
| act approved January 16, 1883, the following special service

| rule is hereby promulgated:

j SPECIAL CIVIL SERVICE RULE.

An appointment to the vacancy now existing in the position
of clerk at $1000 per annum in the War Department, the person
thus appointed to be detailed for duty at the White House, may
be made without examination under the civil service rules.
| WHITE HOUSE.
' Approved July 30th 1902.

; Theodore Roosevelt
