Mr. Edwvard H. Dyer, of Washington, D. C., may be appointed to a
position in the Division of Efficiency, United States Civil Service Com-
mission, without reference to the civil service rules.
Mr. Dyer wvas employed as an accountant by the Division of Efficiency
in the work of (developing and installing a simplified system of accounting
in the Treasurer's Office, Treasury Department, and had charge of the
general books until they were turned over to the regular employees. He
rendered valuable service in connection with said work, and his appoint-
ment to a permanent position ini the Division of Efficiency is recommended
by the Chief of the Division, wvho states that almost every piece of work
which the Division undertakes involves some phase of accounting for which
Mr. Dyer is especially qualified.
