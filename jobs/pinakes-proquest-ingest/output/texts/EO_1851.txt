Executive ether.
Amendment of the Civil Service Rules.
Schedule A, Subdivision XII, relating to the Interstate Commerce
Commission, is hereby amended as follows:
i. The word "three" in the first paragraph is stricken out and the
word "five" substituted therefor.
2. New paragraphs are added as follows:
3. One director under said act.
4. One supervisor of land appraisals under said act.
5. One chief accountant under said act.
As amended the Subdivision will read as follows:
X II. Interstate Commerce Commission.
i. Five experts to be members of an advisory board under the act
of March i, 1913, providing for the valuation of the prop-
erty of carriers.
2Five members of a board of engineers under said act.
3. One director under said act.
4. One supervisor of land appraisals under said act.
5. One chief accountant under said act.
The Interstate Commerce Commission, with the concurrence of the
Civil Service Commission, recommends the exception of these additional
supervisory and advisory positions because of the peculiar and unusual
qualifications required.
WOODROW WILSON
THE WHITE HOUSE,
29 October, 1913.
