Monico Lopez may be appointed a messenger in the office of the
Chief of the Quartermaster Corps, U. S. A., without reference to the
civil service rules.
Mr. Lopez has been employed at the White House continuously
since the beginning of the present administration, and the Chief of the
Quartermaster Corps has signified his willingness to appoint him as mes-
senger. Although employed at the White House he has not been on the
Government pay roll, and for that reason is ineligible for transfer under
the civil service rules.
The Civil Service Commission concurs in recommending this order.
THE WHITE HOUSE,
January 21, 1913.
Wm H TAFT
[No. 1685.]
