ORDER OF WITHDRAWAL
Pursuant to Section 9 of the Act of Congress approved August 29, 1916,
entitled "An Act to declare the purpose of the people of the United States as
to the future political status of the people of the Philippine Islands, and to
provide a more autonomous government for those Islands" (3 Stat. 547) the
following property of the United States situated in the Province of Cavite,
Island of Luzon, Ph ili inc Is , is hereby reserved and set apart for military
purposes, subject to private rigs if any there be:
All of the Island of Litubones. Said island lies off the coast of the Island
of Luzon in latitude 14'-14' 1311 North and longitude 1 20°-35'-26"I East and
is on the south sidle of the entrance to Manila Hay.
The Executive Order of June 26, 1920, on this subject is hereby revoked. ~3 -
