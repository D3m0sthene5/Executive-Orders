Executive Orber
Mrs. Catherine Griffin may be reinstated a an operative in the Bureau of
Engraving and Printing without regard to the length of time she has been separated
from the service.
This order is issued on the reconimnendatjon of the Secretary of the Treasury,
who states that Mrs. Griffin is the wife of Peter J. Griffin, who incurred disability
in the line of duty while fighting in the War with Germany and who is unable to
earn enough to support himself, his wife, and a four-year old child. The Civil
Service Commission concurs in recommending this order.
CALVIN COOLIE
