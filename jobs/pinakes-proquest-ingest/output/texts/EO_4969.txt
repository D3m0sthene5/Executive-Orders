Mrs. Lillian Cecile Keys may be employed as a clerk under the Navy Depart-
ment, without regard to the Civil Service Act or Rules.
Mrs. Keys is the widow of Reuben B. Keys, Chief Aviation Pilot, U. S. Navy,
who met his death in line of duty as a result of injuries sustained in an airplane
crash on August 22, 1928, at Corrie Field, Pensacola, Fla.
This Order is issued upon recommendation of the Secretary of the Navy.
The Civil Service Commission is unable to concur in this recommendation.
For patriotism and charity.
CALVIN COOL IDG E
THE WHITE HOUSE,
September 28, 1928.
