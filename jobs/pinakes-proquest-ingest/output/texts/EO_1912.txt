Dr. W. R. P. Thompson, of Houston, Texas, may be appointed to a
position in the Public Health Service, to be stationed at some point within
the United States, without regard to the civil service rules and regulations.
Dr. Thompson is a physician and surgeon of character, skill, learning
and experience. He was Acting Assistant Surgeon in the Public Health
Service, stationed at foreign ports in Mexico from 1907 to November i,
1913.
WOODROW WILSON
THE WHITE HOUSE,
