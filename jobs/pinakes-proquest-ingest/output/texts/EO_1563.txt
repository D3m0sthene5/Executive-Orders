Executive ether.
In accordance with the provisions of Section 2252 of the Revised
Statutes of the United States, and by virtue of the authority therein give,
it is hereby ordered that:
The United States Land Office located at Redding, California, be
discontinued, and the business and archives of said office transferred to
and made a part of the United States Land Office at Sacramento,
California.
WM H TAFT
THE WHITE HOUSE,
Juy 18, 1912.
