Executive. Order
Richard Green may be promoted from messenger to clerk in the classi-
fled executive civil service of the Treasury Department without compliance
with the Civil Service rules.
This Order is issued on the recommendation of the Secretary of the
Treasury, because of the long and faithful service of Richard Green as
personal messenger to all Secretaries of the Treasury since 1877, and as a
reward for especially meritorious service.
The Civil Service Commission does not concur in this order.
WOODROW WILSON
THE WHITE HOUSE,
3 August, 1914.
