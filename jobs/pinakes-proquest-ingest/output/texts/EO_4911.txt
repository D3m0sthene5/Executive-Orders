The following named Filipinos may be given a classified status in the positions
which they now hold with the Quartermaster Service, War Department, Fort Mills,
Philippine Islands:
Esteban Castillo
Jose Paguio
Eduardo Bautista
Paulino Jover
Eugenio Salinas
Estanislac Waret
Jose Landas
Saturnino Fajardo
Jacinto Marquez
Paulino Dancel
This order is issued in recognition of the demonstrated loyalty of these ten
employees, who in 1924, when an effort was made by a secret organization in the
Philippines to shut down the Power Plant at Fort Mills, remained on duty day and
night, keeping the plant in operation until a crew could be organized to properly man
the plant.
This order is recommended by the Secretary of War. The Civil Service Com-
mission concurs.
CALVIN COOLIDGE
THE WHITE HOUSE,
June 18, 1928.
