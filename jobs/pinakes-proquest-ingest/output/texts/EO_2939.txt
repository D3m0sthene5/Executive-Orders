Under authority of the Act of Congress approved June 25, 1910 (36 Stat.,
847), as amended by the Act of August 24, 1912 (37 Stat., 497), and subject to
the conditions therein expressed, it is hereby ordered that the hereinafter
describe l lands in the State of Oregon be, and the same are hereby, temporarily
withdrawn from settlement, location, sale or entry until March 5, 1919, in aid of
legislation proposed in pending S. 4698.
If said legislation be not enacted before the adjournment of the last session
of the Sixty-fifth Congress, and no other direction is given regarding the disposal
of such lands, they will, on March 5, 1919, become subject to disposal under
any law then applicable thereto without further order.
