Mr. James Hands, an unclassified laborer at $660 per annum in the
office of the Superintendent of the State, War and Navy Department
Building, may be promoted to the position of classified laborer at $840
per annum in said office, without reference to civil service rules.
This order is issued upon the recommendation of the Secretary of the
Navy, who states that lie is informed by the Superintendent that on
account of the special experience in electrical work gained by Mr. Hands
since his employment in August, 1Q06 , he is better qualified to perform
the duties of the position of skilled laborer than an eligible wvho would be
secured from the skilled laborer register of the Civil Service Commission;
that he has made a study of electrical work, arid in addition is familiar
with the building and operation of the electrical force. The Superin-
tendent speaks in the highest terms of Mr. Hands' record as a workman,
and considers him well qualified to perform the duties of a skilled laborer
assigned with the electrician as helper.
The Civil Service Commission does not concur in recommending the
issuance of this order.
