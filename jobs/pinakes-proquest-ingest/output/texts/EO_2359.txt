Mrs. Mary E. Cline may be reinstated as an operative in the Bureaui.
of Engraving and Printing without regard to the length of time she has
been separated from the service.
This order is issued on the recommendation of the Acting Secretary

The Civil Service Commission does not concur in the recommendation.
