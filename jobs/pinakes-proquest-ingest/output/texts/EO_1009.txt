In accordance with the provisions of sections 2251 and 2253 of the
Revised Statutes of the United States, and by virtue of the authority
therein given it is hereby ordered that:
The land office at Mitchell, South Dakota, be removed, with its records,
archives and business, to Gregory, South Dakota, and that all of the lands
lying west of the Missouri River, south of White River and east of range
twventy-five west of the Sixth Principal Meridian, now included within the
Chamberlain land district in South Dakota lbe detached from said Chain
berlain land district and attached to and made a part of the present Mitchell
land district, and the business and archives pertaining to the public lands
above described transferred from the land office at Chamberlain, South
Dakota, to the land office at Gregory, South Dakota.
