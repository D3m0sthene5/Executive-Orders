Executive ether
Order of Withdrawal
Lighthouse Reservation.
It is hereby ordered that the following lands in the territory of
Alaska be, and the same are hereby, withdrawn from settlement, location,
sale, entry, or other disposition, and set apart for lighthouse purposes
in connection with the construction of a light and fog signal station
at, or near, Cape Saint Elias, Alaska: All of those unsurveyed lands
of the United States in the territory of Alaska, described as follows:
Small islet off Cape Saint Elias named Pinnacle Rock on the
charts, the reefs lying to the southeastward of it in the ocean and
those connecting it with the Kayak Island, and all the southerly end
of Kayak Island included within and bounded by the shores of said
island and an east and west line (magnetic) drawn across the island
from shore to shore at a distance of a mile and a half from the
southermost (magnetic) point of the island.
WOODROW WILSON
THE WHITE HOUSE,
5 September, 1914j.
