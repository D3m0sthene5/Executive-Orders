Eliminating certain lands from Executive Order No. 2538, Alaska Townsite Withdrawal
No. 13.
Whereas, by Executive Order No. 2538, dated February 26, 1917, certain
lands were withdrawn, pursuant to the provisions of the act of March 12, 1914
(38 Stat., 305), and reserved for townsite and railroad purposes, contemplated
by said act; and
Whereas, it is now made to appear that a portion of the lands so withdrawn,
as described below were settled upon prior to withdrawal and valuable improve-
inents have been placed thereon; and
Whereas, the Alaskan Engineering Commission has reported that the said
tract so settled upon may be patented without interference with the construction
of railroads or required for townsite purposes;
Therefore, it is ordered that the aforesaid Executive Order and withdrawal
be, and the same is, hereby revoked as to the following tract of land, viz:
SE/~ Sec. 26, T. 20 N., R. 5 E., S. M.
This order shall not affect any other lands withdrawn by Executive Order
No. 2538, or by any other order.
WARREN G HARDING
THE WHITE HOUSE,
