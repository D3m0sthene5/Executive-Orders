)Executive Orber
AUTHORIZING THE ALIEN PROPERTY CUSTODIAN TO SELL~, CERTAIN PROPERTY
AT PRIVATE SALE.
By virtue of the authority vested in nue by an Act of Congress entitled "An
Act to define, regulate, and punish trading with the enemy and for other pur-
poses", approved October 6, 1917, known as the "Trading with the Enemy
Act '", and~ the amendment to such Act embodied in "'Ant Act making appro-
priations to supplly urgent deficiencies in the appropriations for the fiscal year
ending Junie 30, 1918, anl prior fiscal years on account of war expenses and for
other purposes", approved March 28, 1918, and tihe amendment to such Act
embodied in "An Act making appropriations to supply deficiencies in appro-
priations for the fiscal year ending June 30, 1919, and prior fiscal years on
account of war expenses, and1 for other purposes", approved November 4' 1918,
to make certain orders such as that herein contained, I hereby, in the public
interest, make the following detenrmination and order:
The Alien Property Custodiani is hereby authorized to sell at private sale,
at such time and place as to him or to his authorized agent may seem advisable,
without public or other advertisement, Ninety-three (93) shares of common
stock of theAcme Cmn Plaster Company, a corporation organized under the
laws of the State of Missouri, of the par value of One Hundred Dollars ($Ioo.oo)
each, which said Ninety-three (93) shares were heretofore seized by the
Alien Property Custodian under the provisions of the said Act, as the property
of Charles N. Regnier, of Heidelberg, Germany.
My reasons for the foregoing determination and order are:
(a) That property such as that herein described cannot usually be sold to
advantage at public sale after public or other advertisement.
(b) That an advantageous offer, namely, One H-undred and Fifty Dollars
($15o.oo) per share has been received by the Alien Property Custodian for the
purchase of the said Ninety-three (93) shares, which, as the Alien Property
Custodian is informed, represents an increase of Fifty per centum (50%) over
the price which the said Charles N. Regnier paid for the said shares.
(c) That, as the Alien Property Custodian is informed, the holders of
Nineteen Thousand, Seven Hundred and Thirty-six (19,736) out of Twenty
Thousand (20,000) shares of the said stock have already agreed to sell their
respective holdings at the above named price of One Hundred and Fifty Dollars
($15o.oo) per share.
(d) That an attempted sale at auction would be a useless formality and
would involve unnecessary expense, delay and inconvenience, which should
be avoided.
WARREN G HARDING
THE WHITE HOUSE,
May 9, 1923.
