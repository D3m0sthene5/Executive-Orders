Executive Orber.
- The Executive Order of May 9, 1898, which reserved the lands lying
in Townships forty and forty-one North, Range three West, Willamette
Meridian, in the State of Washington, which form the peninsula or jutland
on the east side of the Gulf of Georgia, is hereby revoked and set aside,
and the Secretary of the Interior is hereby authorized and directed to take
such action as may be necessary to restore all of the lands affected by said
order to settlement and entry under the general provisions of the public
land lawvs, except the Northwest quarter of Lot two, of Section thirty-
three, in Township forty-one North, of Range three West, Willamette
Meridian.
THEODORE ROOSEVELT
WHITE HOUSE,
April r, 1908.
