In accordance with the provisions of Section 225; of the Revised
Statutes of the United States, and by virtue of the authority therein given
it is hereby ordered that the boundary lines of the Billings land district in
the State of Montana, established by the Act of Congress, approved Feb-
ruary 5, 1 906, be changed and re-established as follows :
Beginning at a point where the line between ranges twenty and twenty-
one east of the Montana Principal Meridian intersects the south boundary
of the State of Montana; thence north along said range line to its inter-
section with the First Standcard Parallel North; thence east along said
parallel to its intersection with the line between ranges twenty-six and
twenty-seven east ; thence north along said range line to its intersection
with the Second Standard Parallel North; thence east along said parallel
to its intersection with the line between ranges thirty-three and thirty-four
east; thence south along said range line to the south-west corner of town-
ship seven north, range thirty-four east; thence east along the line between
townships six and seven north, to its intersection with the mid channel of
the Yellowstone River; thence down the mid channel of said river to its
intersection with the line between townships six and seven north; thence
east along said township line to the north-east corner of township six north,
range thirty-eight cast ; thence south along the line between ranges thirty-
eight and thirty-nine east to its intersection with the south boundary of
the State of Montana ; thence west along said boundary to the place of
beginning.
THEODORE ROOSEVELT
THLE WHITE HOUSE,
March 28/1h 1906.
