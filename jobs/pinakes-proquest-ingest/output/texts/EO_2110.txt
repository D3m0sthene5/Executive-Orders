]Executive 'Drber.
Under authority of the act of Congress approved June 25, 1910 (36
Stat., 847) as amended by the act of August 24, 1912 (37 Stat., 497) it is
hereby ordered that the unsurveyed island situated in Padilla Bay near the
southeast part of Guemes Island, approximately in Sections 8 or 9, or both
of said sections, T. 35 N., R. 2 E., W. M., Washington, as represented
upon U. S. Coast and Geodetic Survey Chart No. 6300, be temporarily
withdrawn for lighthouse purposes subject to the conditions, provisions
and limitations of said acts.
WOODROW WILSON
THE WHITE HOUSE,
December 28, 1914 .
[No. 2110.]
