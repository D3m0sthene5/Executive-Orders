ALASKA ROAD COMMISSION' RESERVE
Pursuant to authority conferred upon me by the act of Congress approved
June 25, 1910 (36 Stat. 847), entitled "An Act to authorize the President of the
United States to make withdrawals of public 'lands in certain cases ", as amended
by act of Congress approved August 24, 1912 (37 Stiat. 497), it is hereby ordered
that the following described lands and buildings thereon, forming part of thatportion of the old military reservation of Fort St. Michael, Alaska, which wastransferred to the Department of the Interior by Executive Order No. 4131,approved January 22, 1925, be, and the same are hereby, withdrawn from settle-
ment, location, sale or entry, and reserved for the use of the Alaska Road
Commission in the construction and maintenance of roads, bridges and trails in.
Alaska.
Beginning at the intersection of the East side of Fort St. Michaelwharf with mean high water line of St. Michael Bay; thence N. 23040' W. 150 feet; thence S 66° 20' W 200 feet; thence S 230 40' E 150feet more or less, to the mean high water line; thence northeasterlyalong the high water line to the point of beginning; with the buildingsthereon located, namely, Storehouse No. 2, and the wharf extending
therefrom into St. Michael Bay.
This order shall continue in full force and effect unless and until revoked by
the President or by act of Congress.
CALVIN COOLIDGE
THE WHITE HOUSE,
November 19, 1927.
