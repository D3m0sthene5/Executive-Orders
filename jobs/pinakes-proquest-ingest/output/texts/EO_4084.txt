Executive Orber
Under authority contained in the act of Congress approved June 25, 1910 (36
Stat., 847) as amended by the act of August 24, 1912 (37 Stat., 497) and subject to
the conditions, provisions and limitations of said acts, it is hereby ordered that
Lot 1, Sec. 32, containing 79.40 acres and Lot 1, Sec. 33, containing 4.96 acres in
T. 16 S., R. 34 E., Florida, according to the official plat approved January 6, 1915,
on file in the General Land Office, which lots are now reserved for lighthouse pur-
poses by Executive order dated July 25, 1842, be and the same are hereby with-
drawn from settlement, location, sale, entry and all forms of appropriation and
reserved for Coast Guard 'urposes subject to any prior valid existing reservation
of the lands.
CALVIN COOLIDGE
THE WHITE HOUSE,
October 10, 192J4.
