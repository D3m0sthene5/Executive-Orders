The Executive Order of February 14., 1912, as subsequently amended, per-
mitting employees in the executive civil service permanently residing in certain
incorporated municipalities adjacent to the District of Columbia to become
candidates for or hold municipal office therein, is hereby further amended so as
to include the incorporated municipality of Brentwood1, Maryland, among those
named in the order. The first paragraph of the order of l'ebruary 14, 1912, as
amended will read as follows:
Employees of the executive civil service permanently residing in the
following incorporated municipalities adjacent to the D istrict of Columbia
will not be prohibited from becoming candidates for or holding municipal
office in such corporations :
In Maryland-Takoma Park, Kensington, Garrett Park, Chevy Chase,
Glen Echo, Hyattsville, Mount Rainier, Somerset, North
Beach, Capitol Heights, Laurel, Riverdale, Bladensburg,
Brentwood.
& In Virginia-Fails Church, Vienna, Herndon, Potomac.
This order is recommended by the Civil Service Commission because it seems
necessary to the domestic interests of the Government employees, and for the
reason that similar conditions exist in Brentwood, Maryland, as in the other
municipalities named in the original order and its amendments. Brentwood is
almost surrounded by incorporated municipalities in which Government
employees have permission to participate in local political affairs.
WARREN G HARD ING
THE WHITE HOUSE,
June 7', 1923.
