Miss Louisa W. Happersett may be appointed to a position in the
classified civil service without 'reference to the civil service rules.
This order is issued upon the recommendation of the Secretary of
War, who states that the Quartermaster General, the Surgeon General,
and the Judge Advocate General of the Army unite in requesting the
issuance of an executive order. Miss Happersett is the daughter of the
late John C. G. Happersett, Major and Surgeon, United States Army,
who rendered highly efficient military service during a period of thirty-
two years, including the four years of the Civil War. The Surgeon Gen-
eral and the Judge Advocate General call special attention to the services
rendered by Major Happersett and his family during the yellow fever
epidemic at Fort Brown, Texas, in 1882.
The Civil Service Commission does not concur in recornmevw,_ng the
order.
