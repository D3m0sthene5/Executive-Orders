Mr. H. H. Chapman may be reinstated as assistant district forester underthe Department of Agriculture without regard to the year limitation upon eligi-bility for reinstatement.
Mr. Chapman worked as a student assistant in the Forest Service as earlyas 1899. In July, 1904, he was appointed forest assistant through appropriateexamination, and continued in this position until 1906, when he was appointedinstructor in the Yale Forest School, and the following year became an assistantprofessor. At various intervals or during summer vacations between 1906and 1910 he was employed by the Department as a collaborator. His resigna-tion from the Forest Service was effected in February, 1914. Since his separa-tion from the service, his work has been along lines tending to enhance his valueto the Government. He is considered one of the best foresters in the country,and in the opinion of the Department the best interests of the service will bemet by his reemployment.
The Commission concurs in recommending the order.
