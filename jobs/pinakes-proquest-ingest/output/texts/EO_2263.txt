The following-named persons who were formerly employes of the
United States Commission on Industrial Relations may be appointed to
positions in the competitive classified service without reference to the
civil service rules.
William L. Soleau Aloysius A. Bauer
Mary R. Burr Charles J. Stowell
Adelaide L. Patrick Florence A. Cooke
Maymne M. Davidson Minetta Hargraves
Elizabeth A. Hyde Effie M. Nelson
Florence Rimsnider Inez L. Bowser
Elizabeth G. Simmons Estelle Johnson
Thomas J. Eagan Margie N. Conner
Josephine M. Jones Catherine M. Etter
Nelle B. Curry Rosamond G. Krueger
This order is issued upon the recommendation of the Commission on
Industrial Relations. In a resolution unanimously adopted by the Com-
mission on August 1 1, 1915, it is stated that the employes named have
been exceptionally faithful, that they discharged 'their duties with marked
ability, and have thus shown their fitness in a practical way for employ-
ment in the classified civil service of the United States.
The Civil Service Commission does not concur in the recommendation.
WOODROW WILSON
THE WHITE HOUSE,
26 October, 1915.
[No. 2263.]
