First Sergeant William Feldman, U. S. A., Retired, may be admitted
to examination for the classified service. Paragraph I, Civil Service
Rule V, which provides that no person shall be admitted to examination
unless he be a citizen of or owe allegiance to the United States, is hereby
waived in his favor.
Mr. Feldman is technically not a citizen of the United States, not-
withstanding his service in the Army. In view of his honorable Army
service of over twenty-five years, he being retired as first sergeant of
Troop F, Sixth Cavalry, the Commission concurs with the Secretary of
War in recommending that Mr. Feldman be admitted to examination
under waiver of the rule and with the understanding that he will without
delay take the necessary steps to acquire citizenship.
WOODROW WILSON
THE WHITE HousE,
19 August, 1915.
