Executive Orber.
The Civil Service Commission is hereby authorized to except from
competitive examination, as contemplated by the civil-service act, Section
2, Clause Second, subject to the limitations prescribed by the Statute and
those now set forth in Rule II, certain positions arising, of unusual char-
acter, as to duties, and for which qualified persons are so rare, that, in the
judgment of the Commission, they cannot, in the interests of good civil
service administration, be filled through open competitive examination.
In every instance of the exercise of this power the same procedure shall
he followed as required in the Executive order of November 22, 1907.
WM H TAFT
THE WHITE HOUSE,
December I, 1.r.
