The official title of the United States Board on Geographic Names is
changed to UNITED STATES GEOGRAPHIC BOARD.
In addition to its present duties, advisory powers are hereby granted
to this Board concerning the preparation of maps compiled, or to be com-
piled, in the various bureaus and offices of the Government, with a special
viewv to the avoidance of unnecessary duplications of work; and for the
unification and improvement of the scales of maps, of the symbols and
conventions used upon them, and of the methods of representing relief.
Hereafter all such projects as are of importance shall be submitted to this
Board for advice before being undertaken.
