Mrs. Burr J. Ramage may be appointed a clerk in the classified
-service of the Treasury Department without compliance with Civil Service
Rules.
This order is issued on the recommendation of the Secretary of the
Treasury, in view of the faithful and meritorious services of her deceased

Ramage is without means of support.
