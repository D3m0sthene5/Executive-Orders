Executive tuber.
William P. Fix may be appointed a Skilled Helper in the Bureau of
Engraving and Printing without compliance with the Civil Service rules
pertaining to that position.
Mr. Fix was formerly a Plate Printer in the Bureau of Engraving
and Printing, but on account of a physical disability for the proper per-
formance of the work of a Printer was compelled to resign. A physical
examination given by the Civil Service Commission develops the fact that
he is able to perform duty as a Skilled Helper.
This order is issued on the recommendation of the Secretary of the
Treasury and is concurred in by the Civil Service Commission.
WOODROW WILSON
THE WHITE HOUSE,
z6 Nov'r., 1914.
