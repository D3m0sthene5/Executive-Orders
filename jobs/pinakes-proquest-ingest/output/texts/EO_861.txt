CHEYENNE NATIONAL FOREST
WYOMING
It is hereby ordered that on and after July 1, 19oS, the land within the
boundaries shown on the attached diagram, heretofore set apart, reserved,
and proclaimed as the Crow Creek National Forest and a part of the Medi-
cine Bow National Forest, shall be known as the Cheyenne National
Forest. The remaining portions of the Medicine Bow National Forest
shall continue reserved and constitute the Medicine Bow National Forest
in Colorado, and parts of the Arapaho and Pike National Forests in Colo-
rado. It is not intended by this order to interfere with, or disturb the
rights which the W~ar Department has under existing executive orders in
the Crowy Creek National Forest, which will form part of the Cheyenne
National Forest; nor is it intended to release any land from reservation
or to reserve any land not heretofore embraced in a National Forest.
