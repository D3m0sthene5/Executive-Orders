Mrs. Edith L. F~. Richter may b appointed to a classfied position at $720
per annum in the Treasury Department with the restriction that she shall not
be promoted beyond $900 per annum without the prior approval of the Com-
mission.
The Secretary of the Treasury recommends the appointment and states:
"Mrs, Richter has made an excellent record for industry and efficiency in a
temporary position which she has been holding in the Treasury Department;
and she is the widow of Charles W. Richter who served in a sub-clerical capacity
in the Treasury Department for almost twenty-two years and died leaving his
widow with five minor children to support."
The Civil Service Commission submits the case without prejudice stating that
it is without jurisdiction.
