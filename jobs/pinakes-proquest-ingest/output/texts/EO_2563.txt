Executive Other.
The Executive Order dated January 4, 1911, placing the military reserva -
tion of Fort Ringgold, Texas, under the control of the Secretary of the Interior,
for disposition as provided for by the act of July 5, 1884 (23 Stat., 103) or as
may be otherwise provided by law, is hereby vacated ; and the lands included
in said military reservation are hereby placed under the control of the War
Department for military purposes.
WOODROW WILSON
THE WHITE HOUSE,
27 March, 1917.
[No. 2,563.]
