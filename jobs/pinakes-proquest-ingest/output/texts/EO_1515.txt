Executive Orber.
Miss Margaret C. Leonard may be appointed as a clerk in the War
Department without reference to the civil service rules involved.
Miss Leonard passed the clerk examination and is on the eligible
register from the District of Columbia but in view of her standing on that
register and of the fact that the District has received a number of appoint-
ments greatly in excess of its share under the apportionment, the prob-
ability that her name would b)e reached for appointment is very remote.
The Secretary of War recommends her appointment, stating that she is
the daughter of Mrs. Margaret Leonard, a Union soldier's widowv, who has
been employed for over twventy years in the Adjutant General's Office but
who, by reason of illness, w~ill probably never be able to resume her work,
and that the salary of the mother is the sole support of the two women.
