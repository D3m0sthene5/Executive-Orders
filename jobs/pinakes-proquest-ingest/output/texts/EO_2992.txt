Executive Order.
Under and by virtue of an Act of Congress, entitled "An Act to Provide
Further for the National Security and Defense by Encouraging the Production,
Conserving the Supply, and Controlling the Distribution of Food Products and
Fuel," approved August 1O, 1917, I, WoonRow WILSON, President of the
United States, hereby authorize and direct Albert S. Burleson, Postmaster-
General, to requisition the premises at 1407 G Street, N.W., Washington, D. C.,
owned by Lieutenant Arthur Hellen, U. S. N., and now occupied by C. G.
Sloan & Company, for storage facilities for supplies necessary to public use
connected with the common defense, the occupancy of such premises being
deemed necessary for such use, and to ascertain and pay a just compensation
therefor.
DONE in the District of Columbia, this 13th day of November, in
the year of our Lord one thousand nine hundred and eighteen,
and of the independence of the United States the one hundred
and forty third.
WOODROW WILSON
[No. 2992.]
