)Executive order.
It is hereby ordered that the unsurveyed island situate in the
Columbia River in the SW Y4 of Sec. 15, T. 2 N., R. 15 E., W. M.,
Oregon, containing about 6 acres, and represented upon a blue print
diagram submitted by the Secretary of War, be, and the same is hereby,
reserved for use of the War Department in connection with the improve-
ment of Upper Columbia River and its tributaries, as a site for marine
ways, on which the floating plant employed in the work may be repaired.
WM H TAFT
THE WHITE HOUSE,
June 22, 1910.
[No. 1212.]
