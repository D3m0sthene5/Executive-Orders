Executive Order
ANGELES NATIONAL FOREST
CALIFORNIA
Under authority of the Act of Congress approved June 4, 1897 (30 Stat.
I I, 3 4-36) and on the recommendation of the Secretary of Agriculture, it is
hereby ordered that the boundaries of the Angeles National Forest be modified
by excluding therefrom the following described lands in the State of California:
San Bernardino Meridian
In T. i N., R. 4 XW., fractional Sec. 6;
In T. i N., R. 5 WV., fractional Sec. i.
WARREN G HARDING
THE WHITE HOUSE,
M'Cay 3, 1923.
