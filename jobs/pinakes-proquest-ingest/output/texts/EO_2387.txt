Executive Orber
Mr. Neil McGee, of Carson City, Nevada, may be appointed to a
position in the classified service without reference to the civil service rules.
This order is issued upon the recommendation of the Secretary of
Labor, who states that it is contemplated at an early date to initiate em-
ployment work of the Department in the State of Nevada and that the
services of a capable man who has a general acquaintance in that State
would materially assist the United States Employment Service in promot-
ing the work of the Department of Labor in Nevada. It is stated that
Mr. McGee, who has for more than a year been connected with the Com-
mission of Labor and Industry in the State of Nevada, is peculiarly quali-
fled for this work.
The Civil Service Commission does not concur in the recommenda-
ti on.
