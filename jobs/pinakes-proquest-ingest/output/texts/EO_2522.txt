DREW ADMINISTRATIVE SITE
(Near Harney National Forest)
SOUTH DAKOTA
Under authority of the Act of Congress approved June 25, 1910 (36 Stat.,
847), as amended by the act of August 24, 1912 (37 Stat., 497), and on the
recommendation of the Secretary of Agriculture it is hereby ordered that a
tract of unsurveyed land which by protraction of the lines of the public
survey in T. 5 S., R. 1 E., B. H. M., would probably be described as the NE4
SE4 Sec. 2, said township, containing 40 acres, be temporarily withdrawn
from settlement, location, sale or entry, except as provided in said acts, and
be reserved for use by the Forest Service as an addition to a ranger station
in connection with the administration of the Harney National Forest.
WOODROW WILSON
THE WHITE HOUSE,
30 January, 1917.
[No. 2522.]
