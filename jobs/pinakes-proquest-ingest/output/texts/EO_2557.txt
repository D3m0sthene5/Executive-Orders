]executive Order
Charles P. Geyer and Frank B. Gehring may be appointed as lithographic
draftsmen in the Coast and Geodetic Survey, Department of Commerce, with-
out regard to the civil service rules.
This order is issued upon the recommendation of the Secretary of Commerce
and the Superintendent of the Coast and Geodetic Survey, who state that, as
there are no eligibles on the registers of the Civil Service Commission qualified
for this work, and as it is impracticable, in view of the urgent need of the Navy
for coast survey charts, to fill the positions through open competitive exami-
nation, the issuance of this order is believed to be fully justified in the interests
of the service.
WOODROW WILSON
THE WHITE HOUSE,
23 March, 1917.
[No. 2557.]
