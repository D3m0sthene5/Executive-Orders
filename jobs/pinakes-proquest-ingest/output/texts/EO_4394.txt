ALASKA TOWNSITE
Under and pursuant to the provisions of the act of Congress approved March
12, 1914 (38 'Stat., 305), it is hereby ordered that the following described land,
withdrawn by Executive Order No. 3264 of April 29, 1920, and reserved for
townsite and other purposes under said act, be, and the same is hereby eliminated
under said order and reserved for cemetery purposes for entry under the act of
September 30, 1890 (26 Stat., 502), said land being a portion of survey No. 242,
described as follows:
Beginning at a point on the south line of Survey No. 242, 8.14 chains west of
Cor. No. 1 of Survey No. 242 and 200 feet from the center line of the right of way
of The Alaska Railroad, thence west 10 chains along the south line of said Survey
No. 242, thence north, 40 chains to the north line of Survey No. 242, thence east
10 chains along the north line of survey No. 242, thence south 40 chains to place
of beginning, containing 40 acres, more or less, within Sec. 34, T. 1 N., R. 1 W.,
S. M., Alaska.
