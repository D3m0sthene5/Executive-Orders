It is hereby ordered that all veterans of the Civil War in the service
of the Government of the United States who desire to attend the Grand
Army Encampment to be held in Kansas City, Missouri, August 26th to
September 3rd, [916, shall be granted leave of absence with pay, in addi-
tion to the annual leave provided for by statute, from August 26th to Sep-
tembler 3rd, inclusive, that they may have the opportunity to attend the
Encampment, and that they be granted as many more days additional leave
with pay in each case as are necessary for the journey to Kansas City and
return to their posts of duty.
