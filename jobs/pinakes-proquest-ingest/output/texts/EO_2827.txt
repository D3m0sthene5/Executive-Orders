Mrs. Margaret Frazier, widow of Joseph WV. Frazier, formerly employed as
a general helper at the Naval Torpedo Station, Newport, Rhode Island, who
was killed in the explosion at that Station on January 26, 1918, may be employed
as a primer worker at that Station without regard to the provisions of the civil
service rules.
The compensation allowed Mrs. Frazier on account of the death of her
husband for such period as she remains a widow is said by the Secretary of the
Navy to be inadequate for her support, and he believes, in simple justice, that
she should be given further aid in the way of employment.
