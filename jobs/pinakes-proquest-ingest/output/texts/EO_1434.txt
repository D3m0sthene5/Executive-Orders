Executive Oater.
Mr. Richard W. Barkley may be reinstated as an examiner in the
Patent Office, the year limit upon eligibility for reinstatement being waived
in his case.
Mr. Barkley had more than four years of successful experience as a
patent examiner prior to his separation in November, 1890, and since that
time has been engaged on work of the sort which he wvill resume in the
Patent Office. Hie is a graduate of the United Statesr..Naval Academy.
In viewv of the technical character of the position and the evident qualifi-
cations of Mr. Barkley for the position the Commission joins with
the Department in recommending the Executive order to permit his
rein statement.
WAM H TAFT
THE WHITE HOUSE,
November 17, 1911.
