Executive Order.
Mr. Theodore T. Kirk may be appointed to the position of assistant
mechanical draftsman in the Navy Department.
Mr. Kirk was appointed a teacher in the Philippine service through
examination on August 27, 1907, and served toMa2, 1910. If he had
served to August 27, 1910, his reinstatement and transfer would be per-
missible under existing rules. Since his separation from the Philippine
service he has served one year and seven months as junior engineer temnpo-
rarily in the Bureau of Mines. H~e passed an examination for surveyor
for the Philippine service, and an examination for mechanical draftsman
held on February 3, 1913. The last examination was held for the field
service and does not render him eligible under the terms of the aninounce-
ment of the examination for certification for service in Washington.
In viewv of Mr. Kirk's excellent record and demonstrated qualifiations,
the Civil Service Commission recommends his appointment.
WOODROW WILSON
THE WHITE HOUSE,
April21, 1913.
