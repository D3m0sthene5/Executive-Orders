It is hereby ordered that the tract of land in Alaska, on the northeast
bank of Chilkat River, six and three-quarters miles from the village of
Haines, described as follows, be, and the same is hereby reserved, subject
to any existing vested rights, for the use of the United States Bureau of
Education and of the natives of indigenous Alaskan race:
Chilkat Fisheries Reserve.
Description.
Beginning at a stone marked for meander corner No. i, on the
northeast bank of Chilkat River, beside the U. S. road to Klukwvan and
63~ miles from Haines, Alaska; thence running North 7.40 chains to a
stone, corner No. 2; thence west 22.10 chains to a stone, corner No. 3;
thence south 5.60 chains to a stone, corner No. 4, meander corner on
shore; thence down stream with bank of said river, 2 3.93 chains to the
place of beginning, containing 17.21 acres.
