MISSOULA NATIONAL FOREST
MONTANA r
Under authority of the Act of Congress approved June 4, 1897 (30 Stat.,
11I, 3 4-36), and on the recommendation of the Secretary of Agriculture, it is
hereby ordered that the boundaries of the Missoula National Forest, as modified
by Proclamation of September 24, 1912, be further modified by excluding there-
from the following de'cribed lands in the State of Montana:
Montana Principal Meridian
In T. 5 N., R. 14. W., E Y., Sec. 4.
It is hereby ordered pursuant to Public Resolution No. 29, of February 14,
1920, that the public lands in the excluded area, subject to valid rights and the
provisions of existing withdrawals or reservations, shall be opened only to
homestead and desert land entry by qualified ex-service men of the War with
Germany, under the terms and conditions of said resolution and the regulations
issued thereunder, for a period of sixty-three days beginning with the sixty-
third day from and after the date hereof, and thereafter any lands which remain
unentered within such area will become subject to settlement and to entry,
selection and location under any public land law applicable thereto, by the
general public, in the manner prescribed by existing regulations
