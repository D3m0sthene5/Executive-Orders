Executive Orber.
TFhe Executive Order, dated October 14, 1911 (No. 1422), placing
the military reservation of Fort Brown, Texas, under the control of the
Secretary of the Interior for disposition as provided for by the act of July
5, 1884 (23 Stat., 103), or as may be otherwise provided by law, is hereby
vacated; and the lands included in said military reservation are hereby
placed under the control of the War Department for military purposes :
Provided, That a tract of land containing about twenty acres, lying to the
east and southeast of a line drawn from a point on the Rio Grande River,
shown on the official plat of survey, approved December 16, 1913, as angle
point No. 6, to the nearest point on the northeast boundary of the reserva-
tion, is hereby set apart for the use of the Department of Agriculture, said
20-acre tract being n ow occupied by the Weather Bureau and the Bureau
of Entomology, for the work of said Bureaus.
WOODROW WILSON
THE WHI'T'E HOUSE,
2 May, 1916.
