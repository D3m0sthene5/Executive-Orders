Mrs. Mary G. Overn may be appointed as clerk in the Boston, Massachusetts,
post office without regard to the requirements of the civil service rules.
Mrs. Overn is the widow of Edward F. Overn who died April 28, 1928, after
more than twenty-three years' employment in the postal service. She has two
young children dependent upon her and is without adequate means of support.
This order is recommended by the Postmaster General. The Civil Service
Commission does not concur, as it can recommend waivers only when the needs of
the service can not be met under the procedure under the civil service rules.
For charity.
CALVIN COOLIDGE
THE WHITE HOUSE,
November 7, 19f28.
