Mrs. Bessie L. Dudley may be reinstated as a junior clerk in the classified
service without regard to time limit on reinstatement under civil service rules.
Mrs. Dudley is the daughter of a former officer of the Coast Guard Service
and is a widow with a family of five children. She was employed from May 16,
1925, to March 8, 1926, in the Treasury Department, Coast Guard Service. The
Department of Agriculture proposes to reinstate Mrs. Dudley to a temporary
vacancy in that Department and afford her opportunity to seek permanent
employment elsewhere.
The Civil Service Commission does not concur as it can recommend waiver of
the rules only when demanded in the interest of the service.
For charity.
