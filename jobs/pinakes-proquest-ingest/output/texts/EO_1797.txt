]Executive order
In accordance with the provisions of section 40 of the act entitled
"An act to increase the efficiency of the permanent military establishment
of the United States", approved February 2, 1901, which authorizes the
President to "prescribe the kinds and quantities of the component articles
of the Army ration, and to direct the issue of substitutive equivalent articles
in place of any such components whenever, in his opinion, economy and
a due regard to the health and comfort of the troops may so require," the
Executive Order of January 21, 1913, is amended as follows:
The authority for the addition of lard compound as a substitutive
article for lard in the garrison ration is revoked, and the following
authorized instead:
"Garrison Ration.
Lard substitute is added as a substitutive article for lard, at the
rate of .64 oz. to the ration."
WOODROW WILSON
THE WHITE HOUSE,
July '5, 1913.
