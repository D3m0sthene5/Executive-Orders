Executive Orber.
WHEREAS, by Act of Congress approved March 2, 1917, (39 Stat. 951)
entitled "An Act to provide a civil government for Porto Rico and for other
purposes'", it is provided, "That the President may from time to time, in his
discretion, convey to the people of Porto Rico such lands, buildings, or interests
in lands or other property now owned by the United States and within the ter-
ritorial limits of Porto Rico, as in his opinion are no longer needed for purposes
of the United States":
AND WHEREAS, a tract of land at Cayey, Porto Rico, formerly a portion
of the military reservation of Henry Barracks known as b'aaz _ ilwas
transferred from the War Department and placed under the control and juris-
diction of the Navy Department for use for naval purposes under the provisions
of the Naval Appropriation Act approved August 29, 1916, (39 Stat. L. 568)
is no longer needed for the purposes of the United States but is required for
public use by the people of Porto Rico and application has been made by the
municipality of Porto Rico for its transfer thereto.
Now, THEREFORE, in pursuance of the authority vested in me by the Act
of March 2, 1917, aforesaid, the following described tract of land is hereby
transferred to the people of Porto Rico, viz :
That part of the military reservation of Henry Barracks known as Magazine
Hill, comprising that portion of said reservation lying westerly of Las Lleras
(quebrada) and south and east of the Caguas and Guayama Roads, excepting
a rectangular area in the northern extremity of said tract marked " Board
Public Work" on the map of Henry Barracks prepared under the direction of
Major H. C. Price, Porto Rico Regiment of Infantry, by Lieutenant Carlos
M. Lopez, Porto Rico Regiment of Infantry, April twenty-seventh, nineteen
hundred and fifteen,
the land transferred to the people of Porto Rico being shown in red on blue
print of said map bearing Navy Department Bureau of Yards & Docks Serial
No. 75070 on file in the Navy Department.
As a condition of this transfer the people of Porto Rico will without expense
to the United States relocate the road shown by a line of X's in black on said
blue print in the position shown by dotted line in black and the relocated road
will be the western boundary line between the Naval Reservation and
Magazine Hill.
WARREN G HARDING
THE WVHT. HOUSE,
March 5, 1923.
