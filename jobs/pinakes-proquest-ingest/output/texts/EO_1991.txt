Executive Orber.
The Executive order of January 17, 1873, prohibiting certain civil
officers from accepting offices under the authority of States or Territories,
is hereby amended so as to permit the acceptance, with the approval of the
Secretary of the Interior, by employees of the Reclamation Service and of
the National Park service, of appointments as deputy State fish or game
wardens under the laws of the States or Territories wherein such employees
may be on duty, provided that the service as deputy warden shall be with-
out compensation and shall in no manner interfere or be inconsistent with
the duties of such employees in the Government service wherein they are
employed.
WOODROW WILSON
THE WHITE HOUSE,
9 July, 19T4
