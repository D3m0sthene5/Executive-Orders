Mr. Arthur J. Hannan may be appointed as Inspector of Safety Appliances
in the Interstate Commerce Commission without Civil Service Examination.
Mr. Hannan has had long and intimate experience in the operation of railroad
trains, including eighteen years’ service as locomotive fireman and engineer, and
has also held responsible positions requiring administrative ability.

WARREN G HARDING
Tae Wurre House,
September 8, 1922.
