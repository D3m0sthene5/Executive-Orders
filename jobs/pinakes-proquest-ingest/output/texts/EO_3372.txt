The Executive Order of December 0, I920, which provides that, subject
to certain limitations, four hours, exclusive of time for luncheon, shall consti
tute a day's work on December 24th and December 31st, 1920, for all clerks
and other employees, including per diem employees, of the Federal Govern-
ment and the Government of the District of Columbia in said District of Colum-
bia, is hereby so amended as to exclude therefrom the employees of the follow-
ing named bureaus and offices of the Treasury Department:
The Bureau of Engraving and Printing;
The National Bank Issue Division, Office of the Comptroller of the Currency;
The Federal Reserve Issue Division, Office of the Comptroller of the Cur-
rency;
The Division of Issue, Office of the Treasurer of the United States;
The Office of the Custodian of Paper, Division of Loans and Currency; and,
The Coupon Bond Section, Division of Loans and Currency.
All employees in the offices thus excluded whose services can be spared
will be excused for the entire day on Friday, December 24 th, 1920, and will
perform a full day's work on Friday, December 3 1st, 1920.
This order is recommended by the Secretary of the Treasury who states
that it will be in the interest of the Government service,
WOODROW WILSON
Tun Wm'tE House,
2I DVecember, 1920.
