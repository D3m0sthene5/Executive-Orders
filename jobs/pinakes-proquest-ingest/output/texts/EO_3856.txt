Mr. George L. Williams may be appointed to a position of laborer in the
Post Office Service without examination under the Civil Service Rules.
This order is issued on the recommendation of the Postmaster General
who states that while Mr. Williams was temporarily employed as mail mes-
senger at Jefferson City, Missouri, he was held up by bandits on March 1, 192 1,
who carried him into the country and bound him to a tree and escaped with
the registered pouches. He helped to identify the thieves when they were
arrested, and was many times threatened by their friends. He has been em-
ployed as a temporary laborer since June 1, 192 r.
Examinations have been held by the Civil Service Commission but no eli-
gibles have been obtained. Mr. Williams is 58 years of age and did not take
the examination as persons more than 45 years of age are not appointed in the
Postal Service.
The Civil Service Commission concurred in the recommendation.
WARREN G HARDING
THE WHITE HOUSE,
May 28, 1923.
