Mr. John W. Addison, now serving in the excepted position of attorney andexaminer in the Federal Trade Commission, may be reinstated to his former competi-tive classified status without regard to time limit on reinstatement under the civil
service rules.
Mr. Addison has had approximately seventeen years of Government service butat the time of his appointment to his present excepted position he was not eligible forreinstatement as a classified employee. When the rules were amended, June 25,1921, extending eligibility for reinstatement, in meritorious cases, one year for eachfull year of service not exceeding five, he had been separated from his former
classified position for approximately five years and three months.
This order is recommended by the Federal Trade Commission in view of his longand faithful service, which it is stated has been eminently satisfactory.
The Civil Service Commission concurs.
CALVIN COOLIDGETHIE WHITE HOUSE,
December 15, 1928.
