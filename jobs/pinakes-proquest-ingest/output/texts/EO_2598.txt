Mr. Henry Fox may be reinstated as assistant engineer at $2400 per annum
in the Engineer Department at Large, Little Rock, Arkansas, without regard
to the length of time hie has been separated from the service.
Mr. Fox served about fourteen years under the Engineer Department at
Large in various responsible positions, and was furloughed on August 31, 1909.
He has since been engaged in important engineer work in connection with rivers,
harbors, and canals. In addition to his large experience in river and harbor
work, he has passed several high grade examinations for responsible positions
in the classified service. His services are urgently required in connection with
the completion of the survey of the Arkansas River, a difficult work and one
which cannot be satisfactorily performed except by a person having broad
experience.
The Civil Service Commission joins with the Secretary of War in recom-
mending this order.
WOODROW WILSON
THE WHITE HOUSE,
i6 April, 1917.
