Signal Corps, United States Army, in the operation of the military telegraph

become useless for military purposes, the same is hereby placed under the con-

law.
This reservation contains an area of 1.3 acres, more or less, and is situated
in approximate Latitude 65° North and Longitude 150 20' West.
