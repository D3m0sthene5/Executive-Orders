(Executive Orber)
SEPTEMBER 1, 1914.
The President:
By Executive Order of November 13, 1889, certain public lands in Coos
County, in the State of Oregon, were withdrawn from entry and reserved
for the use of the War Department in the improvement of Coos Bay and
Harbor. Embraced in this Order is the W I of the NW of Section 27,
Township 24 South, Range 13 West, Willamette Meridian.
It appearing that the foregoing tract is no longer needed for the purpose
aforesaid, I have the honor to recommend that it be restored to the public
domain for disposal under existing land laws.
Very respectfully,
LINDLEY M. GARRISON
Secretary of War.
1st Indorsement.
WHITE HousE,
September 3, 1914.
rrhe foregoing recommendation of the Secretary of War is approved.
The Secretary of the Interior will cause this action to be noted on the records
of the General Land Office.
