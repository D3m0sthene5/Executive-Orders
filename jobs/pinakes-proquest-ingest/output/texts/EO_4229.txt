Under authority contained in the act of Congress approved June 25, 1910
(36 Stat., 847), as amended by the act of August 24, 1912 (37 Stat., 497), and subject
to the conditions, provisions and limitations of said acts, it is hereby ordered that
the public lands in Ts. 21 N., Rs. 4 and 5 W., N. M. P. M., New Mexico, be and
the same are hereby withdrawn from settlement, location, sale, entry and all forms
of appropriation, pending a resurvey of said townships under the act of Congress
approved March 3, 1909 (35 Stat., 845) ; this withdrawal to remain in full force
and effect from the date hereof until the resurvey is duly approved and accepted
and the approved plats thereof are officially fied in the proper U. S. 'local land
office, unless another date be fixed by the Commissioner of the General Land
Office, whereupon this withdrawal will become of no effect.
