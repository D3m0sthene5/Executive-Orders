Executive Crber
Mrs. Blanche N. Bailey may be appointed to a clerical position in the
Department of Agriculture without examination under the civil service rules.
Mrs. Bailey is the widow of Mr. Robert Victor Bailey who entered the
Department through the stenography and typewriting examination on January
4, 1902, and by many promotions, due to faithful and efficient service, reached
the position of assistant to chief of bureau. He died on October 30, 1923,
leaving Mrs. Bailey and two children destitute. Granted as a matter of charity.
CALVIN COOL IDGE
THE WHITE HOUSE,
November 17, 192.
