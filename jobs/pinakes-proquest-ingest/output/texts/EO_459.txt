The Executive Order of April 27, 1906, discontinuing and abolishing
the office of United States Surveyor-General for the State of Florida is
hereby changed and modified to read as follows:
In compliance with the provisions of section 2118 of the Revised.
Statutes of the United States it is hereby ordered that the office of the
United States Surveyor-General for the State of Florida, now located at
Tallahassee, Florida, be and the same is hereby discontinued and abolished
from and after June 30, 1907.
THEODORE ROOSEVELT
THE WHITE HOUSE,
June 14 1/1, 1906.
