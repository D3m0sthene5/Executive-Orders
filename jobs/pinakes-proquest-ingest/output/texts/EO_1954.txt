Executive Orber,
It is hereby ordered, in addition to the public lands reserved by
Executive Order No. 1927, dated May 2, 1914, that all of the public lands
belonging to the United States, situated in the NE 4 of the NE 4 of
Sec. 12, T. 18SS., R.13 W., N. M. P. M., in the State of New Mexico, be
and the same are hereby temporarily withdrawn from settlement and all
forms of disposal for use of the War Department in connection with the
construction through said lands of a military road from the railroad station
at Bayard to Central, en route to Fort Bayard.
WOODROW WILSON
THE WHITE HOUSE,
6/1une, 1914.
[No. 1954."
