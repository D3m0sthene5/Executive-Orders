Executive Order
AUTHORIZING THE ALIEN PROPERTY CUSTODIAN
TO SELL CERTAIN PROPERTY AT PRIVATE SALE
By virtue of the authority vested in me by an Act of Congress entitled "An
Act to define, regulate, and punish trading with the Enemy and for other purposes",
approved October 6, 1917, known as the " Trading with the Enemy Act", and the
amendment to such Act embodied in "An Act making appropriations to supply
urgent deficiencies in the appropriations for the fiscal year ending June 30, 1918,
and prior fiscal years on account of war expenses and for other purposes", approved
March 28, 1918, and the Amendment to such Act embodied in "An Act making
appropriations to supply deficiencies in appropriations for the fiscal year ending
June 30, 1919, and prior fiscal years on account of war expenses, and for other
purposes", approved November 4, 1918, and all other amendments thereto, to make
certain orders such as that herein contained, I hereby, in the public interest, make
the following determination and order :
The Alien Property Custodian is hereby authorized to sell at private sale, at
such time and place as to himo to his authorized agent may seem advisable, with-
out public or other advertisement, 4050 shares of stock of Banco Mexic no de
Comercio e Industria of the City of Mexico, which said bank is now in a state of
liudto n uo which liquidation dividend payments were made during the
year 1924, at a price of 25 pesos per share for each and every share of said stock,
and which said sharer were heretofore seized by the Alien Property Custodian as
the property of the Deutsche Bank of Berlin, Germany.
My reasons for the foregoing determination and order are:
(a) That property such as that herein described cannot usually be sold to
advantage at public sale after public or other advertisement.
(b) That an advantageous offer for the purchase of said shares of stock has
been received by the Alien Property Custodian.
(c) That the owner or owners of said property at the time of the seizure thereof
by the Alien Property Custodian, have consented to the sale of said property by
the Alien Property Custodian.
(d) That the stock is of an issue of a Mexican Bank which is in process of
liquidation. The delays which must ensue in connection with the further liquida-
tion of the assets make action now desirable. The amount which might eventually
be realized, owing to the speculative character of many of the obligations, and the
further expense of liquidation, is uncertain. It appears from the above reasons to
be for the best interests of all concerned that said stock be now sold in accordance
with the terms of this order.
CALVIN COOL IDGE
THE WHITE HousE,
April 23, 1925.
