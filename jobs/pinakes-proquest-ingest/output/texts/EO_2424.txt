Executive Orber,
Mr. Jacob P. Smith may be appointed a storekeeper-gauger in the
Internal Revenue Service without compliance with civil service rules.
The Collector of Internal Revenue for the Seventh District of
Indiana states that Mr. Smith served temporarily as a storekeeper-gauger
in that District from September, 1915, until June 26, 1916; that he is
qualified to competently fill any position in the storekeeper-gauger service,
and that his permanent appointment will be in the interest of efficient
administration. The Collector further states that the stress of work at
this time in his District necessitates the employment of an experienced
man.
This order is issued on the recommendation of the Secretary of the
Treasury.
Mr. Smith is on the Civil Service Register.
WOODROW WILSON
THE WHITE HOUSE,
18 July, 1916.
[No. 2424.]
