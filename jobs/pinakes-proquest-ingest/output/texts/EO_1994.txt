Erecutive Order.
WHEREAS, it has come to my notice that the motor boat Capo d'Ano
has been forfeited and condemned to the United States for violation of
the Immigration Laws under the provisions of Section io of the Act of
July 5, 1884, ch. 220 (23 Stat., 115);
AND WHEREAS, the Secretary of Labor desires to have this boat turned
over to the Immigration officials at Los Angeles for the official use of his
Department;
Now, THEREFORE, I, WOODROW WILSON, President of the United
States, do hereby authorize and direct the United States Marshal for the
Southern District of California, or any other persons whom it may concern,
to turn over this boat to such officer in the Immigration Service at Los
Angeles as may be regularly designated by the Secretary of Labor, for the
official use of said Immigration Service, upon the payment of the proper
charges and expenses.
WOODROW WILSON
THE WHITE HousE,
July 22, 19141.
