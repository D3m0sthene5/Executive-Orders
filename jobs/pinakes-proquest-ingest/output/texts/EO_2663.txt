]Executive Order
ALASKA RAILROAD NO. 8.
Upon the recommendation of the Secretary of the Interior, and by virtue
of authority contained in section two of "An Act Limiting the hours of daily
service of laborers and mechanics employed upon work done for the United
States, or for any Territory, or for the District of Columbia, and for other pur-
poses," (37 Stat., 137) approved June 19, 1912, I hereby direct that the provi-
sions and stipulations of the foregoing Act may be waived so far as they may
relate to Contract No. 43, executed on February 1 9, 1917, by the Alaskan
Engineering Commission with the Seattle Car and Foundry Company, whereby
the latter company agrees to furnish 30 Hart Convertible Cars, Class C. S.
4i'-6", 50-ton steel underframe cars, in accordance with specifications and de-
scriptions contained under Circular No. 238 as bid upon by the contractor.
WOODROW WILSON
THE WHITE HOUSE,
11 July, 1917.
