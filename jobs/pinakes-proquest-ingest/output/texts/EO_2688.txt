Executive orber,
The following classified positions under the Navy Departme[j may be
filled upon non-competitive examination :
Aeronautical Engineers, Aeronautical Mechanical Engineers, Inspectors of
Aeroplanes and Aeroplane Motors; and Superintendents and Foremen in
Government owned aircraft factories.
This order is issued upon the recommendation of the Secretary of the
Navy and the Chief of the Bureau of Construction and Repair, who states
that on account of the scarcity of men already experienced in these callings,
and the demands of the present emergency, it is deemed impracticable now to
fill these positions by open competitive examination.
WOODROW WILSON
THE WHITE HOUSE,
23 August, 1917.
