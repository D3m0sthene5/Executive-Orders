SMAR 10 1925
]Executive OIrder
It is hereby ordered, under authority contained in the Act of March 2, 1917
(39 Stat. L., 969-976) that the period of trust on lands held for the use and benefit
of the Cabazon and Twenty-nine Palms Bands of Mission Indians in California,
which trust expires during the calendar year 1925, be, and is hereby, extended for
a period of ten years from the date of expiration
CALVIN COOL IDG E
THE WHITE HOUSE,
February 19, 1925.
