Executive Orber.
Wyoming.
By virtue of the power and authority vested in and conferred upon me
by the laws of the United States in that behalf made and provided, it is
hereby ordered that the lands hereinafter more particularly described shall
be, and the same are hereby withdrawvn from settlement, entry, sale and
any and every other manner and form of appropriation or disposition under
the public land laws of the United States, and said lands and all of them
are and will be reserved to the United States so long as the continuance of
such reservation shall be necessary to provide for the water supply of Fort
D. A. Russell and the City of Cheyenne, in the County of Laramie, and State
of Wyoming, and until such time as Congress or the President of the
United States shall otherwise direct, and the withdrawal h reby made shall
be unqualified and absolute, except to the extent that any of the lands herein
described may be covered by valid existing rights heretofore initiated pur-
suant to any of the public land laws of the United States, and, further, that
this withdrawal is in addition to the withdrawal made by proclamation No.
1259 on December 20, 1913.
Description.
The NJ of the Ni of Sec. 32, T. 15 N., R. 70 W., Sixth Principal Meridian,
Wyoming.
WOODROW WILSON
THE WHITE HOUSE,
30 January, 1917.
