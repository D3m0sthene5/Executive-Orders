Erecutive Orber
Mr. George H. Woodson may be appointed to a clerical position in the
Treasury Department without examination under the civil service rules.
The Civil Service Commission does not concur in recommending this order.
WARREN G HARDING
THE WHITE HOUSE,
February 26, 1923.
