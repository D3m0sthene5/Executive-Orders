)Executive Orber.
OREGOIN.
Under the provisions of the act of June 25, 1910, entitled "An Act
to authorize the President of the United States to make withdrawvals of
public lands in certain cases," it is hereby ordered that the lot numbered
four, of section twenty-five, in township one south of range ten west, of
the Willamette meridian, in the State of Oregon, he withdrawn from settle-
ment, location, sale, or entry pending action by Congress on H. R. 23235,
Sixty-first Congress, Second Session, entitled "A bill granting a tract of
land to the City of Tillamrook, Oregon, for the purpose of widening the
waterway of that-city."
WM H TAFT
THE WHITE HOUSE,
July 28, 1910.
[No. I232.]
