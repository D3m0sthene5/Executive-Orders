Executive Orber.
In accordance with the provisions of section 1 79 of the Revised
Statutes, as amended by an Act making appropriations for the legisla-
tive, executive, and judicial expenses of the Government, approved
August 5, 1882 (22 Stats., 238) Major General W. W. Wotberspoon,
United States Army, Chief of Staff, is authorized and directed to per-
form the duties of Secretary of War during the illness or temporary
absence from the seat of Government of the Secretary of War, when-
ever during such illness or absence the Assistant Secretary of War is
also absent.
WOODROW WILSON
THE WHITE HOUSE,
7 A ugaust, 1914.
