Mr. Henry J. Cokayne may be reinstated as a mail carrier in the
postal service without regard to the length of his separation from the
service.
This order is issued for the reason that Mr. Cokayne's dismissal does
not appear to have been warranted by the facts. His reinstatement is
recommended by the Post Office Inspector who made an investigation of
the case, and by the First Assistant Postmaster General. The Civil
Service Commission does not concur in recommending that an Executive
Order be issued authorizing the reinstatement of Mr. Cokayne.
WOODROW WILSON
THE WHITE HOUSE,
22 October, 1913.
[No. 1843.]
