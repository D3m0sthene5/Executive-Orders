Executive ether
Mr. John D. Campbell may be reinstated to a position in the Railwvay
Mail Service without regard to the length of time he has been separated
from the service.
Mr. Campbell was appointed a clerk in the Railway Mail Service on
October 29, 1887, classified with that service on May i, 1889, as a clerk at
$ iooo per annum, and resigned November 3, 1902, on account of his
impaired physical condition. It appears that he has now fully recovered
his health. The Postmaster General states that his services were satis-
factory to the Department during his fifteen years' service and that the
Department will interpose no objection to his reinstatement through the
issuance of an Executive Order.
The Civil Service Commission does not recommend this order.
WOODROW WILSON
THE WHITE HOUSE,
6 April, 1915.
