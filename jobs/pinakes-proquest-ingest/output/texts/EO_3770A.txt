Executive 'Oter
AUTHORIZING THE ALIEN PROPERTY CUSTODIAN TO SELL CURTAIN PROPERTY
AT PRIVATE SALE
By virtue of the authority vested in me by an Act of Congress entitled
"An act to define, regulate, and punish trading with the enemy and for other
Purposes", approved October 6th, 1917, known as the "Trading with the
Enemy Act", and the amendment to such ;act embodied in "An act making
appropriations to supply urgent deficiencies in the appropriations for the fiscal
year ending June 30, 1918, and prior fiscal years on account of war expenses
and for other purposes", approved March 28, 1918, and the amendment to
such Act embodied in "An act making appropriations to supply deficiencies in
appropriations for the fiscal year ending June 30, 1919, and prior fiscal years
on account of war expenses, and for other purposes", approved November 4,
1918, to make certain orders such as that herein contained, I hereby, in the
public interest, make the following determination, order, rule and regulation:
The Alien Property Custodian~ is hereby authorized to sell at private sale,
at such time and place as to him or to his authorized agent may seem advisable,
without public or other advertisement, certain real estate now being held and
administered by him as such Alien Property Custodian, and heretofore, upon
requirement conveyed, transferred, assigned, and delivered to him as the prop-
erty of MARIA HAU, formerly Maria Browning, of Sievern, Kreis Lehe, Ger-
many, whom he dlid after investigation determine to be an enemy, within the
purview of said Act as amended, which said real estate is described as follows,
to-wit:
That certain lot situated at the Northwest corner of Nineteenth and
Mission Streets, in the City and County of San Francisco, State of California,
being a lot of the dimensions of thirty-fie 35 Tetby eighty (8o) feet,
with a frontage of thirty-five (35) feet on Mission Street and a uniform
depth of eighty (8o) feet on Nineteenth Street.
Also that certain lot situated at the Notheast corner of Nineteenth
and Mission Streets, in the City and County of San Francisco, State of
California, being a lot of the dimensions of thirty (30) feet by eighty (8)
feet, with a frontage of thirty (30) feet on Mission Street and a uniform
depth of eighty (8o) feet on Nineteenth Street.
My reasons for the foregoing determination, order, rule and regulation
are:
(a) The property is bringing a very small income and there is only a small
amount of cash to the credit of the trust under which it is held.
(b) An advantageous offer for the purchase of said property has been
received by the Alien Property Custodian.
(c) An attempted sale at public auction would b~ a useless formality and
would involve unnecessary expenses, delay and inconvenience which should be
avoided.
WARREN G HARDING
