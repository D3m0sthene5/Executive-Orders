Mr. Horace P. DeHart may be transferred from the excepted position
of confidential clerk to the Assistant Secretary of the Interior to an appro-
priate competitive position in that Department.
Mr. DeHart has served continuously as confidential clerk for more
than nine years to four assistant secretaries. During that period he has
acquired a comprehensive knowledge of the varied businessassigned to the
assistant secretary, which includes numerous Indian matters, including con-
tracts and bonds, leases, heir-ship cases, petitions for the approval of sale,
etc., appeals in land cases, pension appeals, business relating to eleemosy-
nary institutions, expense and transportation accounts, and many miscella-
neous items. His practical experience has made him a useful, efficient
and well-trained clerk, and according to the Secretary of the Interior his
retention in the service will contribute effectively to the despatch of impor-
tant business.
In view of the proved capacity and exceptional qualifications of Mr.
DeHart, the Civil Service Commission joins in the recommendation for
his transfer to a competitive position by Executive order.
WM H TAFT
THE WHITE HOUSE,
December 19, 1912.
[No. 1668.]
