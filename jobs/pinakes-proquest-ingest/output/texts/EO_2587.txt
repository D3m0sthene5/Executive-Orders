Executive Orber.
Pursuant to the power vested in me by the Act of Congress approved Jan-
uary 28, 1915, it is hereby directed that the Coast Guard shall from this date,
until further orders, operate as a part of the Navy, subject to the orders of the
Secretary of the Navy.
WOODROW WILSON
THE~ WarriEHousiE,
7 April, 1917.
[No. 2587.]
