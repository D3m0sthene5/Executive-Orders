Executive Orber.
In accordance with the provisions of Sections 2250 and 2252 of the
Revised Statutes of the United States, and by virtue of the authority
therein given, it is hereby ordered that:
The Woodward land district, with office at Woodwvard be discontinued
and the lands, business and archives of said land district and office trans-
ferred to, and consolidated with the Guthrie land district, with land office
at Guthrie, in the State of Oklahoma.
WOODROW WILSON
THE WHITE HOUSE,
41 Sept., 1915.
[No. 224 5-A.]
