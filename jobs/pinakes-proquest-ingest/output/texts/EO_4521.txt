Mrs. Barbara Glascock may be employed as stenographer and typewriter in
the Eleventh Naval District, San Diego, California, without regard to the exami-
nation requirements of the Civil Service Act and rules.
This order is issued upon recommendation of the Secretary of the Navy.
Mrs. Glascock's husband, Lieutenant Turner A. Glascock, met his death in the
line of duty, leaving Mrs. Glascock without means of support when the submarine
S-51 sank on September 25, 1925.
The Civil Service Commission is unable to concur in this recommendation.
Done for charity.
CALVIN COOLIDGE
THE WHITE HOUSE,
October 11, 192~6.
