Erecutive Order
It is hereby ordered that Executive Proclamation dated February 14,
1907, reserving certain lands in California for the San Jacinto National
Forest, nowv the Cleveland National Forest, be, and the same is hereby,
amended so as to eliminate from the forest reserve the sections of land
hereinafter described, which are hereby added to the Los Coyotes Mission
Indian Reservation in the State of California:
Township i o South of Range 5 East, San Bernardino Meridian.
Sections 6, 7, 18, 19, 30 and 31.
This order shall not affect any existing valid right of any person.
THE WHITE HOUSE,
April 13, 1914.
WOODROW WILSON
