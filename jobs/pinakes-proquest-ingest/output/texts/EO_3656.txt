By virtue of the authority vested in me by "An act to define, regulate and
punish trading with the enemy, and for other purposes" approved October 6,
1917, known as the "Trading with the Enemy Act", and the amendments
thereto, I hereby, in the public interest, make the following determination,
order, rule and regulation, to wit:
The Alien Property Custodian is hereby authorized to sell at private sale,
without public or other advertisement, all the real and personal property,
heretofore constituting the assets of the firm or copartnership ofMuhn&
Kropff_ of New York, N. Y., including chores in action, the business and good
will of said firm, and all patents, trademarks and copyrights belonging or
appertaining thereto and now held by him or as to which title is vested in him
by virtue of the requirement or requirements heretofore made by him, or his
predecessors in office, whereby the same was conveyed, transferred, assigned or
delivered to him as the property of Ferdinand Mulhens of Cologne, Germany
whom he, or his predecessors in office, did after investigation determine to be
an enemy, within the purview of said act.
My reasons for the foregoing determination, order, rule and regulation are
as follows:
(i) To more effectually secure to and vest in William Kropff, of New York,
N. Y., and his assignees or successors in business, title to all of the aforesaid
real and personal property, it appearing that the said William Kropff has com-
pletely liquidated the interest or share in said firm of his enemy partner, the
said Ferdinand Mulhens, by the payment to the Alien Property Custodian of
a sum of money which was determined to be and accepted by the Alien Prop-
erty Custodian as the cash value of the entire right, title and interest of the
said Ferdinand Mulhens in and to the aforesaid property.
(2) The proposed sale to William Kropff of New York, N. Y., is in the
public interest.
