Executive tOrber.
In accordance with the provisions of Sections 2248 and 2252 of the
Revised Statutes of the United States, and by virtue of the authority
therein given, it is hereby ordered that:
The Lawton land district and land office at Lawvton, in the State of
Oklahoma, be discontinued, and the lands within said district transferred
to, and consolidated with the Guthrie land district, and the business and
archives of said land office transferred to and made a part of the land
office at Guthrie, Oklahoma.-
WM H TAFT
THE WHITE HOUSE,
Aug-. 17, 1911.
