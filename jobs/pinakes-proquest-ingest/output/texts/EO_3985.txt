Executive 0rbe.
It having been brought to my attention that Hon. John D. Wallingford,
United States District Judge for the Canal Zone, will be temporarily absent
from his post of duty during the present calendar year, I hereby designate
Major Lucius Meriwether Smith to be Special Judge for the Canal Zone during
the necessary absence of judge Wallingford.
During the incumbency of judge Smith he shall be paid at the same rate of
compensation, and the same mileage and per diem as that paid to the District
Judge of the Canal Zone, as provided in Section 2 of the Act of September 21,
1922.
CALVIN COOLIDGE
ThxE WHITE Hovei,
April 3, 1924.
