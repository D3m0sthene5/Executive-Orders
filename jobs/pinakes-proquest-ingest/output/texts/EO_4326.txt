Under authority contained in the act of Congress approved June 25, 1910 (36.
Stat., 847), as amended by the act of August 24, 1912, (37 Stat., 497).,'and subject to
the conditions, provisions, and limitations of said acts, it is hereby ordered that
the public lands: in Ts. 42, 43, 44 and 45 N., R. 10 W., M. DW M., California., be and
the, same are hereby withdrawn from settlement, location, sale, entry, and. all forms
of appropriation, pending the resurvey of said townships; this withdrawal to remain
in full force and effect from the date hereof until the resurvey is duly approved and
accepted and the approved plats thereof are officially filed in the proper U. S.
local land office, unless another; date be fixed by the Commissioner of the General
Land Office, whereupon this withdrawal will become of no effect.
