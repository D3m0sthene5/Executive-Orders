No information of any kind whatever shall be furnished by the Sec-
retary of State or any officer or employee of the Department of State, to
any one other than an officer of that Department entitled thereto for
official use, in regard to moneys expended and accounted for by certificate
in pursuance of Section 291 of the Revised Statutes of the United States,
except upon due direction in writing from the President; nor shall any
vouchers or other papers in relation to such expenditures be produced in
pursuance of subpoena or otherwise except upon direction in writing from
the President in each case.
WM H TAFT
THE WHITE HOUSE,
July 7, 1911.
[No. 1382.]
