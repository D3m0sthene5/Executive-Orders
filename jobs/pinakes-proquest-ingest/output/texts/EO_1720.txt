JExecutive Other.
Mr. Ralph Hoagland may be reinstated to the position of laboratory
inspector in the Bureau of Animal Industry, Department of Agriculture,
without regard to the length of time he has be'pn separated from the
service.
Mr. Hoagland was appointed on August i, 1907, as scientific assistant
in chemistry through appropriate examination under the civil service rules,
and resigned November 15,, 1909, to accept the position of professor of
agricultural chemistry in the University of Minnesota. The Secretary of
Agriculture states that he is eminently qualified, both by education and
experience, to perform the work to which it is proposed to assign him, which
is of a special and important nature, and that he is the only competent and
reliable man known to the Department who will accept the position at the
compensation available, for research work on the chemistry of meats.
In view of the facts the Commission joins with the Secretary of Agri-
culture in recommending an order permitting the reinstatement of Mr.
Hoagland.
WM H TAFT
THE WHITE HOUSE,
February 26, 1913.
[No. 1720.]
