Executive 'rber
AUTHORIZING THE ALIEN PROPERTY CUSTODIAN TO SELL CERTAIN
PROPERTY UPON THE NEW YORK STOCK EXCHANGE.
By virtue of the -authority vested in me by an Act of Congress entitled "An
Act to define, regulate and punish trading with the enemy and for other purposes ",
approved October 6, 1917, known as the " Trading with the Enemy Act ", and the
amendments to such Act embodied in "An Act making appropriations to supply
urgent deficiencies in the appropriations for the fiscal year ending June 30, 1918,
and prior fiscal years on account of war expenses and for other purposes ",
approved March 28, 1918, and the amendment to such act embodied in "An act
making appropriations to supply deficiencies in appropriations for the fiscal year
ending June 30, 1919 and prior fiscal years on account of war expenses, and for
other purposes ", approved November 4, 1918, and all other amendments thereto, to
make certain orders such as that herein contained, I hereby, in the public interest,
make the following determination, order, rule and regulation :
The Alien Propert C ustodia is authorized to sell at $113.50 per share (with-
out the dividend payable to stockholders March 1, 1925, of record February 19,
1925) to W. A. Harriman & Company Inc. and Blair and Company. Inc. through
the Irving Bank-Columbia Trust Company, New York City, without public or
other advertisement, one thousand, six hundred fifty-five shares of the common
stock of the New Orleans Texas and Mexico Railway Company, a corporation
organizedune the laws of the State of Louisiana, of the par value of one hun-
dred dollars ($100.00) per share, which said shares were heretofore required to be
conveyed, transferred, assigned and delivered to the Alien Property Custodian as
the property of Lazard Speyer-Ellissen under the provisions of the said " Trading
with the Enemy Act ".
My reasons for the foregoing determination and order are :
First : That more than fifty-one percent (51%c) of the common stock of the
New Orleans, Texas and Mexico Railway Company has been acquired by the
Missouri Pacific Railroad Company, and that the acquisition by the Missouri
Pacific Railroad Company of control of the New Orleans, Texas and Mexico Rail-
way Company by the said purchase of stock makes it desirable to dispose of stock
of the latter Company.
Second : That the offer extended by the Missouri Pacific Railroad Company
to stockholders of the New Orleans, Texas and Mexico Railway Company for the
remaining portion of the outstanding stock, if accepted, would result in the realiza-
tion of a lesser amount of cash than would be realized by the outright sale of the
stock at the present offer.
Third : That the sale of this stock on the New York Stock Exchange at the
present quotation, or a sale at auction, would involve unnecessary expense, and
would result in the realization of a sum less than that realized by sale at the price
authorized in this Order.
CALVIN COOLIDGE
