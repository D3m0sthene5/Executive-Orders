Executive Order.
By virtue of the authority vested in mue by Section 3141, Revised
Statutes of the United States, it is hereby ordered that the counties of San
Luis Obispo, Kern, San Bernardino, Santa Barbara, Ventura, Los An-
gele s, Orange, Riverside, Imperial and San Diego, now part of the First
Internal Revenue District of California, be detached from said First Dis-
trict, and constitute one district to be known as the Sixth District of
California.
This order to become effective upon the appointment and qualifica-
tion of a Collector.
THEODORE ROOSEVELT
THE WHITE HOUSE
February r6, 1909.
