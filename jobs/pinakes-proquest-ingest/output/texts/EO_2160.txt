SANTE FEJEMEZ-PECOS NATIONAL FORESTS
NX1W MEXICO
Under authority of the Act of Congress of June 4, 1897 (30 Stat.,
II), and upon recommendation of the Secretary of Agriculture, it is
hereby ordered that on and after July 1, 1915, all the lands included within
the boundaries of the Jemez National Forest, New Mexico, as fixed and
defined by proclamation of September 17, 1913 (No. 1250), and as modi-
fied by Executive Order of October 28, 1914 (No. 2069), and all the lands
included within the boundaries of the Pecos National Forest, New Mexico,
as fixed and defined by Proclamation of April 20, 1910 (No. 1022), be
mrurged into one National Forest, to be thereafter known and administered
as the Santa Fe National Forest.
It is intended by this Executive Order, for economy of administration,
to merge into one National Forest, thereafter to be known as the Santa
Fe National Forest, all the lands which on July 1, 1915, are within the
J emez and the Pecos National Forests, and the respective names of the
J emez National Forest and the Pecos National Forest as they exist at that
date are hereby changed accordingly.
This Executive Order is not intended to add to the Santa Fe National
Forest any lands which on July 1, 1915, are not embraced within the Jemez
and the Pecos National Forests, nor to release from reservation for
National Forest purposes any lands at that date within either National
Forest.
