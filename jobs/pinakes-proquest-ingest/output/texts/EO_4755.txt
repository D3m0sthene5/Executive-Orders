Under authority of Section 1 of the Act of July 5, 1884 (23 Stat. 103), it is
hereby ordered that the lands comprised within the Montague Island Military Reser-
vation, Territory of Alaska, which were reserved for military purposes by Executive
Order No. 960, dated October 26, 1908, the same having become useless-for military
purposes, be, and are hereby placed under the control of the Secretary of the
Interior for disposition as provided in the above Act, or as may be otherwise pro-
vided by law.
Montague .Island Military Reservation is more particularly described in said
Executive Order No. 960 as follows:
The northern part of. Montague Point, Montague Island, Prince William Sound,
Alaska, embracing all public lands included within metes and bounds as follows,
subject to private rights, if any there be, viz:
Beginning, for the northwest corner, at a small stone monument
at high-water mark (latitude 600 22' 25" north; longitude 1470 08' 001"
west), the position of which is determined by left tangent of Seal
Island bearing S. 810 W., and right tangent of Knowles Head bearing
N. 120 E.; thence S. 280 E. 880 yards, to the southwest corner (latitude
600 22' 00" north; longitude 1470 .08' 00" west); thence N. 620 E. 2640
yards, to the southeast corner (latitude 600 22' 00" north; longitude
1470 05' 30" west), the position of which is determined by right
tangent of Knowles Head bearing N. 90 E.; thence following the
coast line, which bounds the reservation to the northward and east-
ward, to the point of beginning. All bearings are magnetic.
