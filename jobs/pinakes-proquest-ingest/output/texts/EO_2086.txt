Executive Order
PLUMAS NATIONAL FOREST
CALIFORNIA
Under authority of the Act of Congress approved June 4, 1897 (30 Stat.,
11, 34-36), and on the reconmmendation of the Secretary of Agriculture, it
is hereby ordered that the boundaries of the Pluinas National Forest as
modified by the Proclamation of July 28, 1910, be further modified by elimi-
nating therefrom the following described land, in the State of California :
In T. 25 N., R. 9 E., Sec. 22: SEA NW4;
Mount Diablo Meridian.
And it appearing proper and necessary, in the interest of equal oppor-
tunity and good administration, that all of such excluded land subject to
disposition should be restored to entry in advance of settlement, it is hereby
ordered, pursuant to the authority reposed in me by the Act of September
30, 1913 (38 Stat., 113), that such land, subject to valid rights, shall be open to
enitry only for a period of twenty-eight days from and including 9 o'clock
a. In., standard time, on the fifty-sixth day from and after the date of this
order, and thereafter to disposition under any and all of the public land laws
applicable thereto.
WOODROW WILSON
THE WHITE HOUSE,
17 November, 1914t.
