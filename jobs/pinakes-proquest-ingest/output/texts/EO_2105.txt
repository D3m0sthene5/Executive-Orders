Mrs. Sallie G. Jones, who has been employed in the Indian Bureau
of the Interior Department for a period of four years, may be promoted
without examination under the civil service rules.
Mrs. Jones has been employed in the Interior Department in various
capacities since May, 1900. Her record for efficient and faithful service
is good. She was placed in the classified service under an Executive
Order issued October 9, 1908, and appointed copyist at $720 per annum.
In October, 1910, she was promoted to $900, and in April, 1914, in view
of the meritorious service which she was rendering, the Commissioner of
Indian Affairs promoted her to a clerical position at $iooo per annum.
However, owing to a ruling of the Civil Service Commission that under
the provisions of the Executive Order placing her in the classified service,
she wvas not eligible for promotion to said grade without examination, she
wvas, on August 16, 1914, reduced to $900 per annum.
Mrs. Jones is the widow of Robert Tyler Jones, a grandson of Presi-
dent Tyler, who was born in the White House during the administration
of President Tyler, and wvho, it is stated, wvas the only male child ever born
in the White House.
WOODROW WILSON
THE WHITE HOUSE,
11 December, 1914.
