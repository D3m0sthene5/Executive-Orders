Under authority contained in the act of Congress approved June 25, 1910
(36 Stat., 847), as amended by the act of August 24, 1912 (37 Stat., 497), and
subject to the conditions, provisions and limitations of said acts, it is .hereby
ordered that the public lands in T. 8 S., R. 87 W., 6th P. M., Colorado, be, and
the same are hereby withdrawn from settlement, location, sale, entry and all
forms of appropriation pending a resurvey thereof under authority contained in
the act of Congress approved March 3, 1909 (35 Stat., 845) ; this withdrawal to
remain in full force and effect from the date hereof until the resurvey is duly
approved and accepted and the approved plat thereof is officially filed in the
proper U. S. local land office, unless another date be fixed by the Commissioner
of the General Land Office, whereupon this withdrawal will become of no effect.
