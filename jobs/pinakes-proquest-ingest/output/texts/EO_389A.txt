EXECUTIVE ORDER
In accordance with the provisions of Section 2252
of the Revised Statutes of the United States, and by virtue
of the authority therein given, it is hereby ordered that:
The Stockton land district in the State of Cali-
fornia, with office at Stockton, California, be discontinued,
1 and the lands, business and archives of said district and
office transferred to and consolidated with the Sacramento
land district, with office at Sacramento, California.
THE WHITE HOUSE, Theodore Roosevelt
January 3rd, 1906.
