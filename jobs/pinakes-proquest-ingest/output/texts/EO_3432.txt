The year limit upon eligibility for reinstatement is waived to permit the
reinstatement of Hugh C. Mitchell as computer in the Coast and Geodetic Survey
of the Department df Commerce. Mr. Mitchell was regularly employed in the
Coast and Geodetic Survey from August 22, 1898, to December 7, 19 1 , and has
since been engaged upon work in the line of his profession. This Order is issued
upon the recommendation of the Secretary of Commerce in view of Mr, Mitchell's
long, faithful, and efficient service and the present need of his services. The
Civil Service Commission concurs in the recommendation in view of Mr.
Mitchell's having originally entered the service through competitive examina-
tion, and in view of his having continued to the present time in work in the
line of his profession, thereby tending to maintain his efficiency.
