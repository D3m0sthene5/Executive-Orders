Executive Orber.
In accordance with the provisions of Section 2253 of the Revised
Statutes of the United States, and by virtue of the authority therein given,
it is hereby ordered that the boundaries of the Lincoln land district in the
Territory of New Mexico be reestablished as follows:
Beginning on the boundary line between the State of Texas
and the Territory of New Mexico, where the first standard parallel
south intersects the same ; thence west along said parallel to the
range line between ranges fifteen and sixteen east; thence north
along said range line, allowing for proper offsets on the base line,
to the township line between townships twvo and three north; thence
west along said township line to the range line between ranges eight
and nine east; thence south along said range line, allowing for
proper offsets on the base line and the first standard parallel south,
to the second standard parallel south; thence east along said stand-
ard parallel to the northwest corner of township eleven south, range
ten east; thence south to the southwest corner of said township;
thence east to the boundary line of the Mescalero Apache Indian
Reservation ; thence south, east and south, following the boundary
line of said Indian Reservation, to the southwest corner thereof;
thence east, along the south boundary of said Indian Reservation,
to the range line between ranges eleven and twelve east; thence
south along said range line to the third standard parallel south;
thence east along said standard parallel to the range line between
ranges thirteen and fourteen east; thence south along the range
line between ranges thirteen and fourteen east, allowing for proper
offsets on the fourth and fifth standard parallels south, to the
boundary line between the State of Texas and the Territory of Newv
Mexico; thence east along said boundary line to the southeast
corner of the Territory of New. Mexico; thence north along the
boundary line between the State of Texas and the Territory of Newv
Mexico to the place of beginning.
Executive order of August 4, 1910, reestablishing the boundary lines
of said Lincoln land district is hereby rescinded.
WM H TAFT
THlE WHITE HOUSE,
Sy5/. 2, 1910.
