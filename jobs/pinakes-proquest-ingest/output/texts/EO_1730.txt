Colonel Heber M. Creel of North Dakota may be appointed a
Supervisor of Indian Schools without regard to the civil service rules.
His education at the United States Military Academy, his military experi-
ence during many years in the West, and present physical condition,
qualify him for the duties of such Supervisor.
The Secretary of the Interior recommends the issuance of this order.
The Civil Service, however, disapproves it.
WM H TAFT
THE WHITE HousE,
March 3rd, 1913.
