JEFFERSON NATIONAL FOREST
MONTANA ' :
Under authority of the Act of Congress approved June 4, 1897 (30 Stat.,
i11, 34--3 6), and on the recommendation of the Secretary of Agriculture, it is
hereby ordered that the boundaries of the Jefferson National Forest in Montana
be modified by excluding therefrom Lot 9 Sec. 19, Lots 7, 8 and 9 Sec. 20 and
Lot 5 Sec. 29, in T. io N., R. 13 E., M. P. M., containing 160.84 acres.
It is hereby ordered, pursuant to Public Resolution No. 29, approved
February 14, 1920, as amended by Public Resolution No. 36, approved January
21, 1922, that the public lands in the excluded area, subject to valid rights,
shall be opened only to entry under the homestead and desert land laws by
qualified ex-service men of the war with Germany, under the terms and con-
ditioiis of said resolutions and the regulations issued thereunder, for a period of
ninety-one days beginning with the sixty-third day from and after the date of
this order, and thereafter to appropriation under any public land law applicable
thereto by the general public: Provided that, in the absence of a prior adverse
right, the preference accorded the State of Montana under the provisions of the
Act of Congress approved March 3, 1893 (27 Stat., 592), will attach immediately
upon restoration of said lands to selection and entry under the general land laws,
as herein provided, and continue for sixty days.
Subsequent to the date hereof and prior to the date of restoration to general
disposition as provided herein, no rights may be acquired to the excluded land
by settlement in advance of entry, or otherwise, except strictly in accordance
herewith.
