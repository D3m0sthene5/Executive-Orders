WYOMING
Under authority contained in the act of Congress approved June' 25, 1 910
(36 Stat., 847), as amended by the act of August 24, 1912 (37 Stat., 497), and
subject to the conditions, provisions and, limitations of said acts, lit is hereby
ordered that the public lands in Secs. 1 to 19, inclusive, T. 22 N., R. 105 W.; Sees.
1 to 24, inclusive, 27 to 30, inclusive, and Sec. 33, T. 22 N., R. 106 W.; Secs. 1 to
17, inclusive, 22 to 25, inclusive, T. 22 N., R. 107 W.; Sees. 1 to 6, inclusive, and
Secs. 10 to 12, inclusive, T. 22 N., R. 108 W., 6th P. M., Wyoming, be and the same
are hereby withdrawn from settlement, location, sale, entry and all forms of appro-
priation pending a resurvey of said sections under the act of Congress approved
March 3, 1905 (33 Stat., 992) ; this withdrawal to remain in full force and effect
from the date hereof until the resurvey is duly approved and accepted and the
approved plats thereof are officially filed in the proper U. S. local land office, unless.
another date be fixed by the Commissioner of the General Land Office, whereupon
this withdrawal will become of no effect.
CALVIN COOLIDGE
THE WHITE HOUSE,
May 11, 1925.
