Schedule A, Subdivision V of the Civil Service Rules is amended by
adding the following paragraph, to. be numbered 5:
5. Artisan and supervisory artisan positions at the Naval
Stations at Cavite, Olongapo, and Guantanamo.
The Commission joins the Navy Department in recommending this
amendment, which has the effect of excepting artisan and supervisory
artisan positions at the Naval Stations mentioned from the operation of
the Navy Yard Regulations, upon representation of the Department that
more than half of the laborers at Guantanamo are unskilled; that the force
will be somewhat reduced in the future ; that it is largely composed of native
labor:; that the conditions of employment are not such as to warrant the
inclusion of these positions in that Station in the competitive classified
service ; and that because of the various nationalities and races employed
at Cavite and Olongapo, it is deemed impracticable to enforce the regula-
tions at those Stations. WM H TAFT
THE WHITE HOUSE,
February 4, 1913.
