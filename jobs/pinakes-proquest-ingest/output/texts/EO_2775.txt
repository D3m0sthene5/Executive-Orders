Executive Order
Executive Order No. 2707 of September 24, 1917, is modified as follows
The name of John T. Watkins is to be stricken from the list of Coast and
Geodetic Survey officers therein named to serve with the War Department and
added to the list of those named to serve with the Navy Department.
WOODROW WILSON
THE WHrITE HousE,
A9 December, 1917.
