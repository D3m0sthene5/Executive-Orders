Executive 'rber,
In accordance with the provisions of section 179 of the Revised
Statutes, as amended by an Act making appropriations for the legislative,
executive, and judicial expenses of the Government, approved August 5,
1882 (22 Stats., 238) Major General Hugh L. Scott, United States Army,
Chief of Staff, is authorized and directed to perform the duties of Secre-
tary of War during the illness or temporary absence from the seat of Gov-
ernment of the Secretary of War, whenever during such illness or absence
the Assistant Secretary of War is also absent.
WOODROW WILSON
THE WHITE HOUSE,
10 November, 1915.
[NO. 2270.]
