Executive Orber
FLORIDA
Under authority of the act of Congress approved June 25, 1910 (36 Stat., 847),
as amended by the act of August 24, 1912 (37 Stat., 497), it is hereby ordered that
Executive order of December 8, 1924, No. 4109, withdrawing certain islands in
Florida, belonging to the United States situated in the waters off the coast or in
the coastal waters of the State, be revoked in so far as it applies to the lands
included in the abandoned military reservation on Batton Islands in T. 1 S., Rs. 28
and 29 E., Tal. M., Florida, and those included in the abandoned" military reserva-
tion at Key Biscayne, in T. 54 S., R. 42 E., Tal. M., Florida, the disposal of which
is authorized by the act of July 5, 1884 (23 Stat., 103).
