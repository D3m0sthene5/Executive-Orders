Mr. Andrew K. Armstrong may be reinstated as Chief, Section of Box
Tests, at $3240 per annum, Forest Products Laboratory, of the Forest Service,
for duty at Madison, Wisconsin, without regard to the length of his separation
from the service.
Mr. Armstrong served for more than seven years under the Department of
Agriculture as an engineer of timber tests and assistant engineer in forest
products and his experience since his resignation on December 31T, 1917 has been
along lines increasing his capacity for the successful performance of the duties
of the position to which he will be reinstated. The Secretary of Agriculture
regards him as unquestionably the best qualified man obtainable and represents
the need of his services as urgent. In view of these facts the Commission
recommends the order.
