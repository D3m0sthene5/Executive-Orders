CALIFORNIA
Under authority contained in the act of Congress approved Juno 25, 1910 (36
Stat. 847), as amended by the act of.August 24, 1912 (37 Stat. 497), and subject
to the conditions, provisions and limitations of said acts, it is hereby ordered that
an unsurveyed island; known as Whaler Island, situated in the Pacific Ocean,
approximately in Sec. 33, T. 16 N., R. 1 W., H.M., California, be and the same is
hereby withdrawn from settlement, location, sale, entry, and all forms of appro-
priation, subject to any valid existing rights in and to the same, pending classifica-
tion and legislation.
CALVIN COOLIDGE
THE WHITE HOUSE,
January 28, 1927.
