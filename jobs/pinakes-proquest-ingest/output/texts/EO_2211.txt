Section i of Regulation VII of the regulations governing the ap-
pointment of unclassified laborers to Federal offices outside of Washington
is hereby amended by inserting the words "and branches of the service"
after the word "cities." As amended section i of Regulation VII will
read as follows:
Sec. i. If a position of laborer requires, in connection with the
usual duties of mere laborer, the performance of work of the grade
done by classified employees, it should be-.filled from a register for the
classified service and not under these regulations. No person who is
to perform manual labor merely,; in cities and branches of the service
where the labor regulations are in force, shall be appointed otherwise
than in accordance with these regulations.
Regulation VIII of the regulations governing the appointment of
unclassified laborers to Federal offices outside of Washington is hereby
amended by inserting the words "any or all" before the word "Federal."
As amended the Regulation will read as follows:
The commission is authorized to extend the application of these
regulations to any or all Federal offices in other cities than those in
which they are nowv in force.-
These amendments are proposed by the Commission on the repre-
sentation of the Treasury Department that it has experienced considerable
embarrassment owing to the manner of selecting unclassified laborers and
charwomen in the custodian service, and that it wishes to bring the service
to a higher degree of efficiency. It is not deemed practicable, however, at
this time to extend the labor regulations to all branches of the service in
the smaller cities and towns of the United States.
