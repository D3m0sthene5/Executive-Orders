]Executive Orber
By virtue of the power and authority vested in and conferred upon me
by the laws of the United States made and provided, it is hereby ordered
that the NEj Sec. 21, T. 28 S., R. 8 W., N. M. P. M., New Mexico, containing
160 acres according to the plat on file in the General Land Office, be, and
the same is hereby reserved for military purposes.
WOODROW WILSON
THE WHITE HOUSE,
21 November, 1916.
[No. '2489.]
