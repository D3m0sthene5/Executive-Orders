Executive Order.
Mrs. Laura E. Langley and Miss Elizabeth M. Bowling may be appointed
to the positions of telephone operator in the Department of State without
reference to civil service rules and requirements.
Mrs. Langley and Miss Bowling have been employed temporarily upon the
State Department switchboard for the periods of nine andl five months respec-
tively.
They have thus acquired technical proficiency and necessary knowledge
of the Departw ent and its personnel and to replace theni at this time with
inexperienced operators would seriously effect the efficiency of the Department
telephone service.
