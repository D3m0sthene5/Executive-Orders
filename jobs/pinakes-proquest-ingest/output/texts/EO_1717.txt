In accordance with the provisions of Sections 2252 and 2253 of the
Revised Statutes of the United States, and by virtue of the authority
therein given, it is hereby ordered that:
The Chamberlain land district, in the State of South Dakota, with
land office at Chamberlain, be discontinued, and the lands, business and
archives of said land district and office transferred to and consolidated
with the Gregory land district, with land office at Gregory, South Dakota.
