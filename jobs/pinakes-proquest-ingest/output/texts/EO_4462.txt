The time limit on reinstatement under the civil service rules is hereby waived
to permit the reinstatement of Mrs. Ursula B. Lees to a clerical position in the
Department of Justice.
Mrs. Lees had been a classified employee for approximately thirteen years at
the time of her resignation from the Treasury Department on May 19, 1911. Since
January 16, 1920, she has been employed in a position excepted from examination
in the Department of Justice. She is a graduate of Cornell University with post
graduate library work at the Iowa State University. Her record with the Depart-
ment of Justice is satisfactory and the character of her work is excellent.
The Civil Service Commission does not recommend this order.
Done for charity.
CALVIN COOLIDGE
THtE WHITE HousE,
June 25, 1926.
