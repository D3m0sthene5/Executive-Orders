Executive Orbcr.
Mr. Seward Charles may be appointed a Structural Engineer
Draughtsman in the Office of the Supervising Architect in the Treasury
Department without regard to his standing on the eligible register of the
Civil Service Commission for said position.
Mr. Charles has passed the required examination for this position
with a high average. Because of his services in the Architect's Office
under temporary appointment he has demonstrated ability of a high order,
and the issuance of this order is recommended by the Secretary of the
Treasury. This order is not approved by the Civil Service Commission.
WM H TAFT
THE WHITE HOUSE,
Feby. 27, 1913.
[No. 1724.]
