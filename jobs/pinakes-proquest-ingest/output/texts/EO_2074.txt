Mr. William W. Lemmnond may be transferred after reinstatement in
the Department of justice to the position of law clerk at $2400 per an-
num in the Office of the Judge Advocate General, War Department, with-
out reference to the requirement of six months' service immediately pre-
ceding transfer.
Mr. Lemmond was a clerk in the classified service of the War De-
partment from December 1 2, 1903, to February 25, 1907, when he was
transferred to the Attorney General's Office. May i6, 19 11, he was pro-
moted to the position of assistant attorney, which he held until August 31,
1914, when he resigned. This order is issued upon the recommendation
of the Secretary of War with the concurrence of the Civil Service Com-
mission in view of the statement of the Judge Advocate General that he
believes Mr. Lemmond to be especially qualified for the position of law
clerk by reason of his education, training, and experience in legal work in
the Department of Justice.
