]Executive Order
Mrs. Maud Fickling Hollis may b)e reinstated as a clerk in the Treasury
Department without regard to the length of time she has been separated
from the service.
This order is issued upon the recommendation of the Secretary of theTreasury, who states that Mrs. Hollis served in the Treasury Department
from October 1, [907, to September 16, 1912, when she resigned onaccount of marriage, and that as she has been out of the service only alittle more than three years, it is believed that her services can be used to
good advantage in the Department.
The Civil Service Commission does not concur in the recommendation.
WOODROW WILSON
TIHE WHITE HOUSE,
S Marc/i, 1916.
