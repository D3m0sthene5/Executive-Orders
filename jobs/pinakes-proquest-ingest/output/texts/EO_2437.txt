ALASKA RAILROAD AND TOWNSITE WITHDRAWAL.
It is hereby ordered, under authority of the Act of Congress approved
June 4, 1897 (30 Stat., 11, 34-36),.that the tract of land in Alaska as here-
inafter described be excluded from the Chugach National Forest, and that
said tract, pursuant to the provisions of the Act of Congress approved
March 12, 1914 (38 Stat., 305), be, and it is hereby withdrawn from set-
tlement, location, sale, entry, or other disposition, and reserved for town-
site purposes and for other purposes in connection with the construction
and operation of railroad lines under said act, under such regulations as
have been or may hereafter be prescribed, as an addition to the "Alaska
Railroad Terminal Reserve", withdrawn by Executive Order of August 31,
1915, No. 2242, namely; the SE/4 of Sec. 8, T. 13 N., R. 3 W., Seward
Meridian, Alaska.
WOODROW WILSON
THE WHITE HOUSE,
