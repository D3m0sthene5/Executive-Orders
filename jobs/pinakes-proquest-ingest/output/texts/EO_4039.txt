Executive Orber
Jerry Sullivan may be appointed guard in the United States Penitentiary,
Leavenworth, without reference to the requirements of the Civil Service "Rules.
Mr. Sullivan was appointed to this position by the Attorney General on July
14, 1897, prior to the date on which the first examination for the position of guard
was held, and he has served continuously in that position ever since.
This action is based upon the recommendation of the Attorney General, who
states that there is an abundance of testimony to the effect that Mr. Sullivan is
a very efficient, capable guard, and there is unanimity of opinion among those who
are familiar with the case, that he should enjoy a Civil Service status.
The Civil Service Commission does not concur in the recommendation.
For the good of the service and justice to a veteran employee.
CALVIN COOL IDGE
THE WHITE HOUSE,
June 27, 1924.
