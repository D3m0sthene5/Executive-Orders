Mr. James H. Shirley may be reinstated to an appropriate position in
the service under the Post Office Department without regard to the length
of his separation from the service.
Mr. Shirley entered the Railway Mail Service as a result of an open
competitive examination and served from June 21, 1899, until August 31I,
1906, in that service and in the Columbia, South Carolina, post office,
resigning on the latter date. On August 1, 1907, he was appointed postal
clerk on the Isthmus of Panama without examination and without a competi-
tive status for transfer although at the time he could have been reinstated
to a competitive position in the post office service. His preparations to
acquire a competitive status by examination on the Isthmus were inter-
rupted by malaria followed by a paralysis of the lower limbs which necessi-
tated his resignation and return to the States for medical treatment. He
has since entirely recovered his health. The Civil Service Commission
joins with the Postmaster General in recommending an Executive order
to permit the reinstatement of Mr. Shirley.
WOODROW WILSON
THE WHITE HOUSE,
June 17, 1913.
