WHEREAS, by an Executive Order of January 17, 1873, it was de-
clared that "persons holding any federal civil office by appointment under
the Constitution and the laws of the United States will be expected, while
holding such office, not to accept or hold any office under any State or
Territorial Government, or under the charter or ordinances of any munici-
pal corporation," I deem it proper to give public notice that hereafter, in
order to secure a more efficient administration of the work of the Bureau
of the Census, certain State and county officials, such as sheriffs, deputy
sheriffs, tax collectors, assessors, deputy assessors, school commissioners,
superintendents, etc., may accept appointments as special agents for the
collection of statistics of cotton.
WM H TAFT
