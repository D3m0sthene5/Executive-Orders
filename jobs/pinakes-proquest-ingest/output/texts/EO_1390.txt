The provisions of the Executive order of January 17, 1873, prohibit-
ing Federal employees from holding office under any state, territorial or
municipal government, are hereby waived to permit Sylvester P. Bartlett,
a held superintendent in the Bureau of Fisheries, to hold the position of
Secretary to the Illinois State Fish Commission, in view of the mutual
advantages thereby secured to the State Commission and the Bureau of
Fisheries. This order is recommended by the Civil Service Commission.
