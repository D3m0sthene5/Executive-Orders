Executive Order
California
Under authority contained in the act of Congress approved June 25, 1910
(36 Stat., 847), as amended by the act of August 24, 1912 (37 Stat., 497), and
subject to the conditions, provisions and limitations of said acts, it is hereby
ordered that the public lands in Secs. 5, 6, 7, 8, 17, 18, 19, 20, 29,30,31 and 32,
T. 32 N., R. 12HE., M. D. M., California, be and the same are hereby withdrawn
from settlement, location, sale, entry and all forms of appropriation, pending
a resurvey of said sections under authority contained in the act of Congress
approved March 3, 1909 (3 Stat., 845) ; this withdrawal to remain in full force
and effect from the date hereof until the resurvey is duly approved and accepted
and the approved plat thereof is officially filed in the proper U. S. local land
office, unless another date be fixed by the Commissioner of the General Land
Office, whereupon this withdrawal will become of no effect.
CALVIN COOLIDGE
ThE WWTS Housz,
January 21, 1924.
