Section i of Regulation II of the regulations governing the employ-
ment of unskilled laborers in Federal offices outside of Washington ,D. C.,
promulgated December 12, 1904, and amended July 12, 1905, and Novem-
ber 22, 1907, is amended so as to reduce from 6o to 50 years the maximum
age limit for admission to the examination. As amended the section
referred to is as follows
Sec. I. No person shall he admitted to examination who is not
a citizen of the United States or wvho is less than 20 or more than
5o years of age, except that veterans of the Civil War who have
been honorably discharged may be examined without regard to
their age.
This amendment wvas recommended by the Treasury Department as
being for the good of the service, and wvas indorsed by all but two of the
other departments interested. The Civil Service Commission joins in
recommending the amendment.
WOODROW WILSON
THE WHITE HOUSE,
June 1.5, 1914.
[No. 1963.]
