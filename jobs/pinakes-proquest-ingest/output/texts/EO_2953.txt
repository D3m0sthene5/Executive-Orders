My reasons for the foregoing determination, order, rule, and regulation, are:
(a) The said Lilly Busch has filed with said Alien Property Custodian her
petition pursuant to the provisions of section 9 of said "Trading with the
enemy Act", asking that said property be returned to her, and because of the
pendency of said petition said property can not be sold at public sale;
(b) An offer for the purchase of said real estate has been made hereto-
fore, and is now pending, but said offer, if accepted, must be accepted at once;
(c) Said proposed sale is an advantageous one, and said Lilly Busch has
consented in writing that said sale be made, and it is for the best interest of
the United States, and of all persons who are or may be interested in said
property, that the same be sold at once.
