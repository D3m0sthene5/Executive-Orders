9 0The yea limit upon eligibility for reinstatement is hereby waived to
permit the reinstatement of Mr. Albert J. Phillips as associate chemist at
$2000 per annum in the service of the Bureau of Standards, Department
of Commerce, at Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania.
Mr. Phillips was in the service from August 13, 1906b, to June 15,
1913, when he resigned. He is especially recommended by the Director
of the Bureau of Standards on account of his satisfactory and efficient
service. Difficulty has been experienced in obtaining a suitably qualified
person for filling the position.
The Civil Service Commission concurs in recommending this Execu-
tive order because of Mr. Phillips long and efficient service, the compara-
tively short period of his separation from the service, and the need of an
employee with his training, which it has been difficult to meet through the
usual procedure.
WOODROW WILSON
THE WHITE HOUSE,
25 Maiy, 1915.
