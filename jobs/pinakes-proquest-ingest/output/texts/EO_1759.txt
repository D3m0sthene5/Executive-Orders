In accordance with the provisions of Section 2253 of the Revised
Statutes of the United States, and by virtue of the authority therein given,
it is hereby ordered that:
Townships one hundred and forty-five, one hundred and forty-six, one
hundred and forty-seven and one hundred and forty-eight North, ranges
forty-nine to sixty-seven West, inclusive, and townships one hundred anid
forty-four North, ranges forty-nine to sixty-one W~est, inclusive, of the
Fifth Principal Meridian, and of the Red River of the North, be detached
from the Fargo land district in the State of North Dakota, and attached
to and made a part of the Devils Lake land district, and the business and
archives pertaining to the lands within said townships transferred from the
land office at Fargo to the land office at Devils Lake.
WOODROW WILSON
THE WHI-ITE HOUSE,
15 April, 1913.
[No. 1759.]
