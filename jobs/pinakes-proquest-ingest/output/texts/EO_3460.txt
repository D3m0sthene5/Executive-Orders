The year limit up)on eligibility for reinstatement is hereby waived to permit
the reinstatement of Mr. Alonzo P. Owen as Post Office Inspector in the Post
Office Department.
Mr, Owen was appointed a letter carrier on May 12z, 1890; was promoted
to Post Office Inspector and served continuously until June 11, 1917, when he
resigned. The Postmaster General recommends this order in view of Mr.
Owen's previous service in classified positions and the excceptional qualifications
he evinced in the performance of his duties through his entire service.
The Civil Service Commission reports that upon the facts as they appear
by the entire record in this case, the Postal Service will probably be benefited
by the waiver of the year limitation.
