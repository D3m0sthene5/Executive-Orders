Executive Orbe?
It is hereby ordered that on Saturdays, until further notice, during the months
and at the jas tations hereinbelow named, four hours, exclusive of time for
luncheon, shall constitute a day's work for all clerks and other employees at said
naval stations; viz.:
Naval Stations:
Cavite, P. I.:April, May, June, and July.
Olongapo, P. I.: April, May, June, and July.
Guam: February, March, April, and May.
Tutuila, Samoa: December, January, February, and March.
Pearl Harbor, Honolulu, T. H.: July, Augut, September, and October.
Provided, however, That any clerk or other employee who may for special public
reasons be excepted therefrom by the Commandant or Secretary of the Navy shall
not be paid any extra compensation.
