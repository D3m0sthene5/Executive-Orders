Schedule A, Subdivision III, Paragraph 13, of positions excepted from the
requirement of examinations under Schedule A of the Civil Service Rules is
hereby amended to read as follows:
13. All persons actually employed in leprosy investigation stations.
This amendment, which is requested by the Treasury Department and
concurred in by the Civil Service Commission makes general the exception from
examination' which formerly applied only to the leprosy investigation station
at Ntolokai, Hawaii. It is based upon the fact that it is impracticable to fill the
positions in the usual manner through competitive examination because of the
class of patients treated.
