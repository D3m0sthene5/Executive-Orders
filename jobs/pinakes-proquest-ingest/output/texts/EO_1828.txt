Executive ether
Mr. William B. Green may be reinstated as a compositor in the
Weather Bureau without reference to the time limit imposed by the civil
service rules.
Mr. Green's services as a compositor were terminated on December
31, 1910, after more than twenty years' service. The reason given at the
time was that a reduction of the force was necessary because of the trans-
fer of certain work from the Weather Bureau to the Government Printing
Office. The Secretary of Agriculture with the concurrence of the Civil
Service Commission recommends the reinstatement because there is reason
to believe that Mr. Green was unfairly dealt with because of certain testi-
mony he had given in a case which resulted in the reprimand of the Chief
of the Weather Bureau.
WOODROW WILSON
THlE WHITE HOUSE,
18 September, 1913.
[No. 1828.]
