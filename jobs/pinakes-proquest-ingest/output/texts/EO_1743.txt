NEBO NATIONAL FOREST
UTAH
Under authority of the Act of Congress of June 4, 1897 (30 Stat.,
1 1-34), and on the recommendation of the Secretary of Agriculture, it is
hereby ordered that the boundaries of the Nebo National Forest, as modi-
fied by the Proclamation of July z, 1910, be further modified by eliminating
the following described lands, in the State of Utah :
IN TOWNSHIP 15 SOUTH, RANGE 3 WEST,
SALT LAKE MERIDIAN:
Of section io the southeast quarter and the east half of the southwest
quarter,
Of section I i the southwest quarter,
Section 15,
Of section 22 the northeast quarter and the east half of the southeast
quarter, and
Unsurveyed land which by protraction of the public survey in the west
half of said township would be section i6.
The lands herein eliminated from the Nebo National Forest are
hereby withdrawn for classification under the Act of June 25, 1910 (36
Stat., 847), as amended by the Act of August 24, 1912 (3 Stat., 497), and
will, when compatible with the public interests; be restored to settlement
and entry under the laws applicable thereto on such dates as shall be fixed
by the Secretary of the Interior and after such notice as he may deem.
advisable.
WM H TAFT
THE WHITE HOUSE,
March 3rd, 1913. W
