Executive Orber.
It is hereby ordered that Rugged Island, located approximately in
latitude 590 52' o0" North, longitude 149° 22' 00" 'Nest from Greenwich,
in Resurrection Bay, South coast of Alaska, be and the same is hereby
withdrawn from settlement, location, sale, or other disposition, and set
apart as a military reservation.
WOODROW WILSON
TILE WHITE HOUSE,
15 Scp/embcr, 1916.
