SALMON NATIONAL FOREST
ID AHO
It is hereby ordered that on and after July i, 1908, the land within the
boundaries shown on the attached diagram, heretofore set apart, reserved,
and proclaimed as parts of the Salmon River, Bitter Root, and Lemhi
National Forests, shall be known as the Salmon National Forest. The
remaining portion of the Salmon River National Forest shall continue
reserved and constitute a part of the Challis National Forest. It is not
intended by this order to release any land from reservation or to reserve
any land not heretofore embraced in a National Forest.
THEODORE ROOSEVELT
THE WHITE HousE.
