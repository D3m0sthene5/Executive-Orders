Mrs. Laura V. Rooney may be reinstated as an operative in the Bureau of
Engraving and Printing without regard to the length of time she has been separated
from the service.
Mrs. Rooney, as Laura V.' Walker, served in the Bureau of Engraving and
Printing as a classified employee from November 9, 1901, to January 18, 1908.
Her husband, Theodore A. Rooney, served in the Bureau almost continuously from
April 25, 1890, to the date of his death, February 7, 1928.
"This Order is recommended by the Secretary of the Treasury who states that in
view of her former service and the long and faithful service of her husband, a
waiver of the rules to permit the reinstatement of Mrs. Rooney would be justified.
The Commission is unable to concur in this recommendation.
