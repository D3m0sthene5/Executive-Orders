Executive Orbet
Under authority of law (Act March 3, 1919, 40 Stat. p. 1302), it is hereby
ordered that the use of land comprising regimental units Nos. 1 9 and 20 of
Camp Lawrence, United States Naval Training Station, Great Lakes, Illinois,
as defined by the Navy Department, together with buildings, fixtures and ap-
pliances thereon situated, not required for the purposes of the Navy Depart-
ment, and suitable for the uses of the United States Public Health Service, be
transferred by the Secretary of the Navy to the Treasury Department, for the
use of the United States Public Health Service. ~
The Treasury Department (United States Public Health Service) shall
defray from its own funds all costs incident to upkeep, repair and operation,
including pro rata share of the cost of fire protection, supply of electricity and
water, and sewage disposal, as may be agreed upon from time to time by the
Secretary of the Treasury and the Secretary of the Navy.
There is reserved to the Navy Department the uninterrupted use and
control of the water mains and sewage system, and of such streets and railway
tracks as may be necessary.
WARREN G HARDING
THE WHITE House,
February 27, 1922.
