Executive order.
Section ; of the Regulations Governing Appointments to the Navy
Yard Service is hereby waived so as to permit of the appointment of
Edmond Pawellik to the position of general helper at $1.76 per diemi at
the Navy Yard, Norfolk, Va.
This order is issued upon the recommendation of the Secretary of the
Navy and is based upon the fact that Pawellik was discharged from the
Navy owing to injuries received in the line of duty and is unable to obtain
civil employment. W O R W WLO
THE WHITE HOUSE,
25 August, 1913.
[No. 1815.1
