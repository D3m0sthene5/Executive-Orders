Mrs. Marian K. Ferguson may be appointed to a clerkship in the
Navy Department without reference to the civil service rules.
Mrs. Ferguson is the wife of William J. Ferguson, who enlisted in
the Navy at the outbreak of the Spanish American War, and who ad-
vanced rapidly and was about to achieve the rating of chief machinist when
he was stricken with paralysis, and upon the recommendation of a Board
of Medical Survey was discharged because of his unfitness for further
service.
This order is issued upon the recommendation of the Secretary of the
Navy, who states that during the organization of the- Federal Reserve
Board, Mrs. Ferguson was employed temporarily by the Organ ir?-tion
Committee; that the quality of her work has been heartily commelied by
the Comptroller of the Currency and the Counsel of the Federal Reserve
Board; and that he is of the opinion that she is fully qualified for a clerical
position.
The Civil Service Commission does not concur in the recommendation.
WOODROW WILSON
THE WHITE HOUSE,
29 December, 1915.
[No. 2292.]
