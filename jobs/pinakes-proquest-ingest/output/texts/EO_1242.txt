The following-described tract of public land, embraced within what is
known as the " Kalena Tract", and situated on the Island of Oahiu, Terri-
tory of Hawvaii, is hereby reserved from sale or other form of disposition
and set apart for military purposes, as an addition to the United States
Military Reservation of Schofield Barracks (Waianae-Uka), viz:
Beginning at a point on the summit of Kalena peak, which
point is Station 7 of the boundaries of the Waianac-Uka Military
Reservation; thence by true bearings: N 710 401 E, 3850 feet,
more or less, to a stake; thence N 5° 07' E, 1 299.5 feet, to a stake ;
thence S 83' 52' W, 6747.2 feet, to a point on the ridge of the
Waianae Mountains; and thence along said ridge, and binding
thereon, to the place of beginning; the bearing of which point is
S 580 54 E, 3431.5 feet : containing 214.02 acres, more or less.
