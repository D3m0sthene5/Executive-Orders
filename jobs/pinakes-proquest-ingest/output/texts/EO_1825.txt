Executive Order
Employees of the Department of State in the United State:s Despatch
Agencies at Newv York City, San Francisco and New Orleans, who have
been or may be authorized by that Department to take applications for
passports, and who have obtained or may obtain appointments as notaries
public, are hereby granted authority to accept fees for executing passport
applications and administering oaths in connection therewvith, not to exceed
twventy-five cents in each case; such fees may be retained not to exceed
twenty-five dollars in any one month in each office, any receipts in excess
thereof to be turned into the Treasury. The Executive Order of De-
cember 31, 1904, as amended by the Executive Order of March 31, 1905,
is hereby further amended.
WOODROW WILSON
THE WIrE HOUSE,
September 17, 1913.
[No. 1825.]
