Executive Ode
STANISLAUS NATIONAL FOREST
CALIFORNIA
It is hereby ordered that on and after July , 1908, the land within the
boundary shown on the attached diagram, heretofore set apart, reserved,
and proclaimed as parts of the Stanislaus and Sierra National Forests, shall
be known as the Stanislaus National Forest. The remaining portions of
the Stanislaus National Forest shall continue reserved and constitute parts
of the Tahoe and Mono National Forests. It is not intended by this order
to release any land from reservation or to reserve any land not heretofore
embraced in a National Forest.
THEODORE ROOSEVELT
THE WHITE HousE.
J/udy 2, 1908.
