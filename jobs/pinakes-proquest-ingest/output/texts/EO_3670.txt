Executive Order.
By virtue of the powers conferred upon me by Section 8 of the Act of
Congress approved September 24, 1914, entitled-' An Act to create a' Federal
Trade Commission, to define its powers, and for other purposes" I hereby
order and direct:
That the Secretary of Commerce is authorized and directed to detail to
the Federal Trade Commission employees for the Department of Commerce, ~<
not to exceed ten persons in number, to assist the Federal Trade Commission
in the tabulation of records in the files of the Federal Trade Commission bearing
upon the cost of production of coal in relation to tonnage produced, and such
other aspects of the mining and distribution of coal affecting intermittent
operation of coal mines and irregular employment of coal miners as may be
mutually agreed upon by the Secretary of Commerce and the Federal Trade
Commission.
WARREN G HARD ING
THE WHITE HOUSE,
May 6, 1922.
