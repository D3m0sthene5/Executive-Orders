Executive order.
On the 28th day of October, 1912, I issued an Executive Order
authorizing the appointment of Mr. Guy Emerson as a special agent of
the Treasury Department without compliance with the Civil Service Rules,
for the reasons therein stated. Since that time conditions have arisen
which make it advisable for the Secretary of the Treasury to have a broader
discretion in the matter of Mir. Emerson's appointment in the interest of a
better accomplishment of the purposes had in view in that Order than the
Order gives him. The Order is therefore hereby amended so as to
authorize the Secretary of the Treasury to appoint Mr. Guy Emerson,
without compliance with the Civil Service Rules, to any competitive classi-
fied position in the Treasury service which may be available for the pur-
pose, and to transfer him as the interest of the service may require.
WM H TAFT
THE WHITE HOUSE,
December 16, 1912.
[No. 1663.]
