Executive Orber,
Alaskan Timber Reserve No. 2.
Pursuant to authority conferred upon me by the act of Congress
approved Jun- 25, 1910 (36 Stat., 847), as amended by the act of Con-
gress of August 24, 1912 (37' Stat., 497), entitled "An Act To authorize
the President of the United States to make withdrawals of public lands in
certain cases," it is hereby ordered that the following described lands be,
and the same are hereby, withdrawn from settlement, location, sale, or
entry and reserved for the use of the Alaskan Road Commission in the
construction, operation, and maintenance of the Valdez-Fairbanks Military
Road :
A tract of land one mile wide on each side of the Valdez-Fair-
banks Military Road, and extending from mile post No. 3, from
Valdez, to mile post No. 1 2, from Valdez.
WOODROW WILSON
THE WHITE HOUSE,
14 October, 1915.
[No. 2259.]
