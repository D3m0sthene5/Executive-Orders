)Executive order.
It is hereby ordered that a strip of land one and one-half rods wide,
extending along the western side of Section 26, Township 28 North,
Range 25 East, 4 th Principal Meridian, a part of the Government Reser-
vation of Sturgeon Bay, in Door County, Wisconsin, being no longer
required for public purposes, be and the same is hereby transferred to the
Secretary of the Interior for disposition as authorized by law; the purpose
of this order being to permit of the use of said strip of land, under Sec-
tion 2477, Revised Statutes of the United States, by the Town of Nase..
waupee, Door County, Wisconsin, in the extension of an existing public
road along the west side of said section.
WOODROW WILSON
THE WHITE HOUSE,
28 January, 1916.
[No. 2304.]
