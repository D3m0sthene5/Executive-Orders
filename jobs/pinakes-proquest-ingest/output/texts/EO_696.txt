Executive Orber.
Charles E. Knight, at present a Bank Examiner, may be transferred
from his position and appointed Shipping Commissioner at the Port of
San Francisco, California, another position under the government service,
without examination and certification under the civil service rules. There
is now no register of eligibles for this position.
THEODORE ROOSEVELT
TinE WI-IniEHOUSE,
Sep/emiber 28, 1907.
