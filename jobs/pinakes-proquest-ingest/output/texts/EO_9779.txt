By virtue of the authority vested in me by section 2
of the Civil Service Act (22 Stat. 403, 404), and in the
interest of the internal management of the Government of
the District of Columbia, I hereby authorize the appoint-
ment of Rbert J. Barrett to a position as Captain in the
Metropolitan Police Force of the District of Columbia
without compliance with the competitive provisions of the
Civil Service Act and Rules.
Mr. Barrett was assigned on September 15, 1941, to
the Detective Bureau with the rank of Captain, and on
September 1, 1943, was given the rank of Inspector and
assigned to his prc ent duties as Chief of Detectives
with the rank: of Assi'tant Superintendent.
