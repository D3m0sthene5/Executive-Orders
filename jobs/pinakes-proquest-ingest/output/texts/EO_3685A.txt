Executive 'Order.
Any services which American. consular officers shall be called upon to perform
under items 31, 33, 38, 39, 40, 41, 42, and 43 of the Tariff of United States
Consular Fees for the use of any person in the collection of claims from the
United States or from any State for compensation, pensions, back nay, bounty,
bonus, or for property lost in the military service shall be rendered free of
charge.
WARREN G HARDING
TUE WHITS HOUSE,
May 27, 1922.
