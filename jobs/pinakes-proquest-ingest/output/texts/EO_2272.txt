Mr. Charles Hardin may be reinstated as a clerk at not to exceed
$1200 per annum in the Post Office Department without regard to the
length of his separation from the service.
Mr. Hardin was appointed August 15, 1887, through appropriate
examination as a clerk at $900 in the Post Office Department at Washing-
ton, and was removed from the position of clerk class 3 ($i6oo) on January
26, I901, for irregularities in connection with the dead letter sale. The
records of the Department show that Mr. Hardin's removal was based on
the report and recommendation of a post office inspector who investigated
the charges. The Postmaster General reports that this report, which it is
understood was favorable to Mr. Hardin, was apparently extracted from
the files by the previous administration. He further states that as it is
believed that the removal of Mr. Hardin, and the manner by which he
was removed, was unjust to him, the Department feels that it would be in
the interest of justice to reinstate him.
The Civil Service Commission does not recommend the issuance of
an order in this case.
