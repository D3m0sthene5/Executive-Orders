Mrs. Maud N. Jackson may be reinstated to a position in the classified
service of the Treasury Department without regard to the length of her
separation from the service.
Mrs. Jackson was originally appointed in the Bureau of Engraving
and Printing as a Printer's Assistant on September 6, 1890, under her
maiden name of Maud Nichols. She served in various bureaus of the De-
partment until May 7, 1900o, when she was transferred to the office of the
Supervising Architect and promoted to $i1,ooo. She was promoted to
$1200 January 3, igo1, and was receiving that salary when she resigned
on June 3oth of that year.
This order is issued upon the recommendation of the Secretary of the
Treasury, who states that Mrs. Jackson wvas an especially satisfactory em-
ployee under her former appointment, and that he believes her reinstate-
ment will be in the interest of the service.
The Civil Service Commission does not concur in the recommendation.
