The Executive Orders, dated October 3, 1924 (No. 4081), April 10, 1925 (No.
4197), April 10,. 1925 (No. 4200), and April 10,' 1925 (No. 4193),'establishing as
national forests, pursuant to the provisions of Section 9 of the Act of Congress
approved June 7, 1924 (43 Stat. 655), certain described areas within the respective
military, reservations of Fort Benning, Georgia, Fort Eustis, Virginia, Camp Meade,
Maryland, and Pine Plains, New York, are hereby rescinded and the said national
forests are restored to their former status as parts of the said military reservations
and are no longer subject to use and administration for national forest purposes.
CALVIN COOLIDGE
THE WHITE HOUSE,
December 2, 1927.
