)Executive Orber.
Mr. Charles M. Irelan may be reinstated as a clerk at $900 per annum
in the Office of the Chief of Engineers, War Department, notwithstanding
the fact that somewhat more than~a year elapsed between the date of his
separation and the date of request for a certificate for his reinstatement.
The Commission joins with the Department in this recommendation,
in view of the unusual service and exceptional efficiency of Mr. Irelan for
more than thirty years in. the Patent Office, and in view of the fact that
he would have been reinstated in the Office of the Chief of Engineers
within the year of his eligibility if a suitable clerical vacancy had occurred.
WM H TAFT
THE WHITE HOUSE,
December I2, 1910.
[No. 1270.]
