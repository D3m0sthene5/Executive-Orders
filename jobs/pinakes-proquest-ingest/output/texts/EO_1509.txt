Executive Orber,
Mr. Andrew M. Latham may be appointed to the position of clerk in
the Department of Agriculture without reference to the provisions of the
Civil Service Rules.
Mr. Latham passed the civil service examination and received certifi-
cation and appointment to a temporary clerkship in the Department of
Agriculture. Circumstances are such that lie would probably not be
reached for certification for permanent appointment.
The Secretary of Agriculture states that Mr. Latham is a young man
of twenty-seven years of age, of good habits, industrious, and thoroughly
efficient; that he is crippled, having been deprived of the use of his limbs,
but in all other respects is of good physique and of exceptional good
health.
The order is based on the ground that the Secretary of Agriculture
recommends such action in the interest of the public service, It is not
intended to form a p~receden~t that because one is a cripple he is entitled to
a waiver of the regulations of the Civil Service Law. The Civil Service
Commission is un~ab~le to concur in the recommendation.
