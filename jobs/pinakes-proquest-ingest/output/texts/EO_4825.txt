Executive Other
By virtue of the authority vested in me by the act of Congress approved
August 1, 1914, making appropriations for the sundry civil expenses of the Govern-
ment for the fiscal year ended June 30, 1915, Plattsburg, N. Y., is hereby abolished
as a port of entry in Customs Collection District No. 7 (St. Lawrence), with head-
quarters at Ogdensburg, N. Y., effective this date.
CALVIN COOLIDGE
THE WHITE HOUSE,
March 10, 1928.
