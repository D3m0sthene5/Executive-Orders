Executive 'Orbe,
The Executive Order No. 3985, dated April 3, 1924, designating Major
Lucius Meriwether Smith to be Special Judge for the Canal zone during the
necessary absence of judge Wallingford is hereby rescinded and I hereby desig-
nate judge James W. Blackburn to be Special Judge of the Canal zone during
the necessary absence of Hon. John D. Wallingford, District Judge of the Canal
zone. -'...rv
During the incumbency of judge Blackburn, he shall be paid at the same
rate of compensation, and the same mileage and per diem as that paid to the
District Judge of the Canal Zone, as provided in Section 2 of the Act of Sep-
tember 21, 1922.
CALVIN COOLIDGE
THE WHITE HousiE,
May 7, 1924.
