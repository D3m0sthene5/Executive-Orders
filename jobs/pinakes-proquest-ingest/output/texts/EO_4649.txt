Miss Florence J. Howell may be appointed to a clerkship in the United States
Customs Court without compliance with the requirements of the Civil Service Act
and Rules.
This order is recommended by the Secretary of the Treasury in recognition of
the long, faithful, and distinguished service of Miss Howell's brother, Judge William
B. Howell, whose death, April 4, 1927, as Chief Justice of the United States Customs
Court, terminated a continucu. service for the Treasury Department which began
June 6, 1882. Judge Howell supported his sister and his mother, who is now
eighty-eight years of age, and their support and that of his widow and her mother
now devolves upon Miss Howell.
For charity.
CALVIN COOLIDGE
THE WnnE HOUSE,
may 18, 1927.
