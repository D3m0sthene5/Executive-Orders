Executive Orber.
Miss Eulalie A. Chase may be appointed a clerk in the competitive class in
the postal service without regard to the Civil Service Rules.
The Postmaster General states that Miss Chase has been in charge of a
contract postal station in San Diego, California, for about five years and that
it has been decided to change this station from a contract to a classified branch
of that office, thus necessitating the employment of clerks who are under the
civil service.
With respect to the qualifications of Miss Chase, the Postmaster of San
Diego says that she is one of the very best postal clerks in the city, efficient
and exceptionally bright in handling mail and the public; that the tremendous
volume of business at Station i was built up mainly by the amazing popularity
andl capability of Miss Chase; that it would be an actual calamity to lose her
services; and that it would be almost impossible to put a new clerk in her place
because she knows how to take care of every branch of the work.
WOODROW WILSON
THE WHITE HOUSE,
6 September, 197
