Miss Bertha Goldberg, of Washington, D. C., may be appointed to a
clerkship in the Bureau of Foreign and Domestic Commerce, Department
of Commerce, without reference to the civil service rules.
Miss Goldberg has passed the stenographer and typewriter examina-
tion with an average of 84.29, and has been serving temporarily in the
Bureau since February 25, 1915. The Chief of the Bureau of Foreign
and Domestic Commerce and the Acting Secretary of Commerce recom-
mend her permanent appointment on account of her unusual ability and
efficiency as a stenographer and typewriter, and the exceptional aptitude
she has shown in the particular work of the Bureau, which she has been
called upon to perform.
The Civil Service Commission does not concur in the recommendation.
WOODROW WILSON
THE WHITE HOUSE,
2 September, 1915.
