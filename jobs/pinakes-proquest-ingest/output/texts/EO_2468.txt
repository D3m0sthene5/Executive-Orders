Schedule A of classified positions excepted from competitive exami-nation under Rule II, clause 3 is hereby amended by adding to Subdivi-
sion IX, Department of Agriculture, a paragraph to be numbered 4, as
follows:
4. Special meteorological observers at stations in Alaska, inthe West Indies, and in portions of foreign territory bordering
on the Gulf of Mexico and the Caribbean Sea.
The foregoing amendment is recommended by the Secretary of Agri-
culture and is concurred in by the Civil Service Commission. The pay of
the observers will be small, rarely exceeding $50 a month, and the duties
will require only a portion of their time. In view of the small pay and
the lack of facilities for competitive examination in these places, the posi-
tions can not be appropriately filled through competitive examination.
