Executive Orber.
Mr. Charles R. Pickard may be appointed to an appropriate position
in the competitive classified service without compliance with the provisions
of the civil service rules.
This action is taken because of the long and varied experience of Mr.
Pickard in the judicial service and in the executive service in positions not
subject to the civil service rules, in all of which positions he has served
efficiently and well. The Secretary of Commerce and Labor, under whom
M~r. Pickard is temporarily employed as a special agent in the Bureau of
the Census, also expresses the opinion that his appointment to a competi-
tive position will be in the interests of the Government. The Civil
Service Commission is unable to recommend the issuance of this order.
WM H TAFT
THE WHITE HOUSE,
December 17, 1912.
[No. 1665.]
