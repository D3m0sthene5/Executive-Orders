Pursuant to Section 9 of the Act of Congress approved 'August 29, 1916 (39
Stat. 545, 547), entitled "An Act To declare the purpose of the people of the United
States as to the future political status of the people of the Philippine Islands, and
to provide a more autonomous government for those islands", the following tracts
of public land, property of the United States, in the Philippine Islands, are hereby
reserved and set apart for military purposes, subject to private rights if any there
be:
I. All that certain tract of land situated in the Province of Albay,
Island of Luzon, included within metes and bounds described as follows:
Beginning at a point south 840 19' east, 2911.26 meters, from Daraga
church tower; thence north 41 ° 23' west, 594.00 meters; thence north 40
16' west, 520.22 meters; thence due east 335.40 meters; thence south 40 23'
east, 969.81 meters, to point of beginning, containing an area of 264,365.67
square meters, more or less.
* The same being a portion of the former military reservation of
Regan Barracks, reserved by Excutive Orders of November 10, 1904, and
November 23, 1911, and placed under the control of the government of the
Philippine Islands to be administered for the benefit of the inhabitants
thereof by Executive Order of May 11, 1919.
II. All that certain tract of land in the City of Manila, comprising
Blocks Nos. 9 and 10, including the portions of the streets and ways sep-
arating the same, as indicated on plan of reclamation, No. 1, Manila Har-
bor, Philippine Islands, surveyed during January, 1908, by Lewis P. Bailey,
under the direction of the Director of Lands, the said tract being bounded
on the north by the Pasig River, on the East by Tacoma Street, on the
South by 2nd Street, and on the West by Canal, and being more particu-
larly described by metes and bounds as follows:
Beginning at a point north 760 39' west, 939.62 meters from
B.L.L.M. No. 42, the said point being also the southeast corner of Block
No. 10; thence north 50 00' east, 122.60 meters; thence south 870 42' west,
33.30 meters; thence south 87° 35' west, 153.98 meters; thence south 880 10'
west, 15.05 meters; thence south 490 36' west, 21.84 meters; thence south
30 13' west, 12.10 meters; thence south 40 35' west, 97.35 meters; thence
north 870 30' east, 216.76 meters to point of beginning, containing an area
of 26,352 square meters, more or less.
Bearings true. Declination Variable.
