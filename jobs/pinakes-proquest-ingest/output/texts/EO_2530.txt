WYOMING
Under authority of the Act of Congress approved June 25, 1910 (36 Stat.,
847), as amended by the Act of August 24, 1912 (37 Stat., 497), it is hereby
ordered that the public lands in the following described areas in the State
of Wyoming be temporarily withdrawn in aid of pending legislation
embodied in Bill H. R. 19413, subject to all valid adverse claims initiated
prior to the date hereof and maintained pursuant to law, and to the pro-
visions of existing withdrawals:
In T. 34.N., R. 113 W., NE} Sec. 6, Secs. 17 and 18;
In T. 35 N., R. 113 W., Secs. 6, 7, 18, 19, 30 and E Sec. 31; .
In T. 28 N., R. 114 W., Secs. 5, 6, 7, 8, 17, 18, 19, 20, 29, 30, 31 and 32;
W3 of T. 29 N., R. 114 W.;
W3 of T. 30 N., R. 114 W.; :
W3 of T. 31.N., R. 114 W.;
W3 of T. 32.N., R. 114 W.;
In T. 33 N., R. 114 W., Sees. 2, 10, 11, 15, E4 Sec. 20, Secs. 21, 22, 27, 28,
Ed of Secs. 29 and 32, all Secs. 33 and 34;
In T. 34N., R. 114 W., Secs. 26 and 35;
, In T. 27 N., R. 115 W., Secs. 1 to 12, inclusive, 18, 19 and 30;
Sixth Principal Meridian.
