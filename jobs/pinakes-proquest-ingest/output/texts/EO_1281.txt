In accordance with the provisions of Section 2252 of the Revised
Statutes of the United States, and by virtue of the authority therein given,
it is hereby ordered that:
The Natchitoches land district be discontinued and attached to and
made a part of the Baton Rouge land district in the State of Louisiana,
and that the archives arid business of the United States land office at
Natchitoches be transferred to the land office at Baton Rouge.
WM H TAFT
THE WIIV"E HOUSE,
Jan. 6, 1911.
