Executive orber
Mr. Solon J. Boughton may be reinstated as assistant examiner in
the Patent Office, Department of the Interior, without reference to the
period of his separation from the service.
Mr. Boughton served as an assistant patent examiner from December
1, 1907, to August 9, 1911. He resigned for the purpose of taking up his
residence in Colorado when it was found that he had contracted tuberculosis.
A cure wvas effected within a short time and during his period of eligibility
he communicated with the Patent Office relative to reinstatement but wvas
advised to remain in Colorado and was promised that a recommendation
wvould be made for his reinstatement when he desired to resume his duties.
The Secretary of the Interior recommends the reinstatement on account
of Mr. Boughton's experience and the fact that he served with great
ability. The Civil Service Commission concurs in recommending this
executive order.
WOODROW WILSON
THE WHITE HOUSE,
24 October, 1913.
[No. 1846.11
