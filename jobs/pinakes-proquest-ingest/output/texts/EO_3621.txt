Under authority of the Act of October 28, 1921, (Public No. 84), the offices
of Register and Receiver of the United States land office at Xalispell. Montana,
are hereby consolidated and the office of Receiver is abolished. All the powers,
duties, obligations and penalties imposed by law upon both the Re ster and
Receiver of said office shall be exercised by and imposed upon the Register so
appointed, whose compensation shall be a salary of $5oo per annum, together
with the fees and commissions otherwise allowable to both Register and Receiver,
but the salary, fees and commissions of such officer shall not exceed $3,ooo per
annum.
WARREN G HARDING
THE WHITE HOUSE,
January 23, 1922.
