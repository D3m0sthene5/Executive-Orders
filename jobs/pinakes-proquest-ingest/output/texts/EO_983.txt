Executive Orber.
Fourth-class postmasters having been classified in certain States and
time being necessarily required before any positions in such offices can be
filled by examination and certification from the Civil Service Commission,
any vacancy in such post offices occurring prior to the time at which the
Civil Service Commission shall certify to the Post Office Department that
it is able to hold examinations and make certifications to such positions,
may he filled by the Post Office Department without examination.
THEODORE ROOSEVELT
THE WHITE HOUSE,
November jo/h 1908.
