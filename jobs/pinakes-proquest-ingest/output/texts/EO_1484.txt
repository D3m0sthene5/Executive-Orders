Mr. Embree E. Hoss, Jr., may be appointed without examination
under the Civil Service rules as a post office inspector.
This action is based upon the statement of a representative of the
Department of Justice that his services and qualifications are needed in
connection with certain important matters, and the recommendation of
the Post Office Department.
In viewv of the fact that selections for the position may be made from
the entire post office service, including a large force of trained and experi-
enced inspectors, the Civil Service Commission is unable to concur in the
recommendation for the appointment of Mr. Hoss by Executive order.
THE WHITE HOUSE,
February 19, 1912.
WM H TAFT
