It is hereby ordered that the following described tract of land in
Alaska bordering upon the right bark of the Yukon River, in and
surrounding the native village at Fort Yukon, situated approximately
in latitude 660 32' north, longitude 1450 17' west of Greenwvich, be and
the same is reserved, subject to any vested rights existing, for the use
of the U. S. Bureau of Education and the natives of Alaska of indige-
nous race, residing n.uw or hereafter at said village or within the limits
of said reservation, viz:
Beginning on the right or north bank of the Yukon River, at corner
No. I of the accepted survey of the Protestant Episcopal Mission site, sur-veyed by C. E. Davidson in 1906, and represented on official plat approved
April 2, 1908 ; or if said corner does not now exist, beginning at the witness
corner thereof, or at a point where the northwest boundary of said survey
reaches the bank of said river;
Running thence with that boundary on the same course through corner
No. 4 20 chains to a point; thence northwvesterly 40 chains to a point 20
chains distant from the river bank ; thence southwesterly on a course parallel
to the first line, 20 chains to the bank of the Yukon River; thence with said
bank to the place of beginning; but excluding and excepting a tract along
the river front claimed and occupied by T. H. Beaumont, and marked for
him by survey in 1913, with an estimated area of 75 acres, as represented upon
a diagram accompanying this order and made a part thereof.
WOODROW WILSON
THE WHITE HOUSE,
February 24, 1914.
