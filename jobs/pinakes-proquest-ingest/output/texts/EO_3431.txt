the Consular Regulations are hereby amended as follows:
Paragraph 541 is amended to read as follows:
541. To be stated in currency of the United States. All accounts of consularofficers must be stated in the currency of the United States; and all draftson the Secretary of State must be drawn, not in foreign money of account orcurrency, but in the money of the United States. In all cases when the dis-bursements are actually made in foreign currency the vouchers themselvesshould be taken and rendered to the Treasury in the same currency in whichthe disbursements are made; and when the value quoted in the Secretary of theTreasury's quarterly proclamation of value of foreign coins is not used inthe reduction of the payments to the currency of the United States, satisfactoryevidence should accompany the accounts as to the correct valuation of suchforeign currency in the coinage of the United States, or the standard moneyof the country in which the transaction occurred, either in the form of a cer-tificate of a responsible banker or of the consular officer himself based on au-thentic quotations. Where a currency foreign to the standard currency of thecountry where the consular officer is located, or a depreciated currency of saidcountry is involved, a certificate must be furnished showing the relative valueof the foreign or depreciated currency to the standard currency of the foreigncountry where the consular officer is located; or, where the facts are such andthey are stated in the account, the account may be rendered to show the valueof such foreign or depreciated currency in the standard currency of the UnitedStates, and the evidence required to show such value shall be a proper banker'scertificate of commercial exchange rates unless otherwise required by theaccounting officers of the Treasury.
