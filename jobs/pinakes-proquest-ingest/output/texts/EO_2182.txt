Executive orber.
Mr. Charles W. G. Brett, may be transferred from the position of
mechanician at $1200 per annum to a clerkship at the same salary, in the
Bureau of the Census, Department of Commerce, without reference to the
civil service rules.
Mr. Brett was appointed as mechanician on September 16, 1908. On
March 19, 1912, he met with an accident, in the line of duty, which re-
sulted in the loss of his left eye. His transfer to the clerical roll is recom-
mended by the Director of the Census, who states that Mr. Brett's
experience in connection with the various machines used in the Bureau,
his familiarity with the matter to be tabulated, and his general education
are believed to he such as to fit him for clerical duties, and that his services
have always been satisfactory.
The Civil Service Commission does not concur in the recommendation.
WOODROW WILSON
THE WHITE HOUSE,
27 April, 1915.
