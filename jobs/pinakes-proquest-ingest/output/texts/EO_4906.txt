Executive Orber
Mrs. Kate McLaughlin may be reinstated to an appropriate position in the
Government Printing Office without regard to time limit on reinstrt went under
the civil service rules.
This order is recommended by the Public Printer, who states that
Mrs. McLaughlin is the widow of a former employee of the Government Printing
Office who had a record of more than thirty-one years of competent and faithful
service. His death left her without means of support. Mrs. McLaughlin was
employed in the Government Printing office from January 11, 1892, to May 19, 1894.
The Civil Service Commission does not concur.
For charity.
CALVIN COOL IDGE
THE WHITE HOUSE,
June 6, 1928.
