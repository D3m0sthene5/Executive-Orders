Executive Oter
By virture of the authority vested in me, the Executive Order of December 3""-~
1921, prescribing the basis for making charges against employees of The Panama
Canal and Panama Railroad on the Isthmus of Panama for rent, fuel, electric
current, water, and services in connection with the use of quarters, on and after
January 1, 1922, is hereby amended effective July 1, 1922, so as to authorize the
Governor of The Panama Canal, with the approval of the Secretary of War, to
prescribe a lower rate of charges for official and other quarters which officers
and employees of The Panama Canal and Panama Railroad Company are re-
quired to occupy by reason of the office held or the duties performed by them,
this to apply only in cases in which the rate of pay to the employee is substan-
tially what it was in 1914.
