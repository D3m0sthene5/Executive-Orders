Executive order.
Schedule B of classified positions which may be filled upon non-
competitive examination is hereby amended by adding a newv subdivision
and paragraph as followvs:
IV. WAR DEPARTMENT.
i. Aeronautical engineers, aeronautical mechanical engineers,
instructors in flying, and aviation mechanicians in the Aviation
Section of the Signal Corps.
WOODROW WILSON
THE WHITE HOUSE,
28 December, 1914.
[No. 2112.]
