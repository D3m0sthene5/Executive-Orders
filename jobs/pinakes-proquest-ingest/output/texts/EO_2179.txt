Executive OQder
CACHE - POCATELLO NATIONAL FORESTS
IDA hO ANI) UTAII
Under authority of the Act of Congress ot June 4, 1897 (30 Stat., 11), and
upon recommendation of the Secretary of Agriculture, it is hereby ordered
that on and after July 1, 1915, all the lands included within the boundaries
of the Pocatello National Forest, Idaho and Utah, as fixed and defined by
Proclamation of May 16, 1910 (No. 1031), and as modified by the Act of
Congress of February 18, 1911 (36 Stat., 919), be transferred to and adminis-
tered as a part of the Cache National Forest, Idaho and Utah.
It is intended by this Executive Order, for economy of administration,
to merge into one National Forest, thereafter to be known as the Cache
National Forest, all the lands which on July 1, 1915, are within the Pocatello
and Cache National Forests, and the boundaries of the Cache National
Forest, as they exist at that date, are hereby modified accordingly.
This Executive Order is not intended to add to the Cache National
Forest any lands which on July 1, 1915, are not embraced within the Pocatello
National Forest, or to release from reservation for National Forest purposes
any lands at that date within the Pocatello National Forest.
WOODROW WILSON
THE WHITE HOUSE,
21 April, 1915.
