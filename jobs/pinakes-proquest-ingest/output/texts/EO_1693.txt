ESxecutive Order.
Miss Laura A. Thompson may be appointed as clerk (librarian-
translator) at $ 1800 per annum in the Children's Bureau without reference
to the civil service rules.
This order is issued upon recommendation of the Secretary of Com-
merce and Labor and the Chief of the Children's Bureau. Miss Thornson
was recommended by the Librarian of Congress as especially fitted for the
position, and has served temporarily in the position to the entire satisfac-
tion of the Chief of the Bureau. In an open competitive examination
for the position Miss Thompson stood second in point of rating but is
ineligible for certification under the law and rule requiring the apportion-
ment of appointments among the States, Territories and the District of
Columbia. The Civil Service Commission does not join in recommending
this order. WM H TAFT
THE WHITE HOUSE,
January 27, 1913.
