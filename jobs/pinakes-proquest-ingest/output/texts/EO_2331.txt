Executive Qrber.
Mrs. Annie K. Campbell, a special agent in the Bureau of the Census,
may he appointed without examination under the civil service rules to act
as private secretary to the Director of the Census.
The Secretary of Commerce states that Mrs. Campbell has had twenty
years of practical business experience, is an accomplished stenographer and
typewriter and a trained correspondent, and that her experience has cov-
ered both commercial work and the technical side of manufacturing in
both domestic and foreign trade. She has been serving since April 5,
1915, a- a special agent in connection with the census of manufactures,
and has had experience in that work both in the field and at the central
office. In securing her permanent services the Secretary of Commerce
states that he is sure the Department wvill benefit by her very unusual
experience and by her exceptional ability.
In view of these facts and the exception of private secretaries allowed
by the ci', it service rules to certain other heads of bureaus, the Commission
concurs with the Secretary of Commerce in recommending the issuance of
this order.
