Under authority of the Act of June 30, 1922, the offices of Register and
Receiver of the United States land office at Dickinson, North Dakota, are
hereby consolidated and the office of Receiver abolished. All the powers,
duties, obligations and penalties imposed by law upon both the Re __ se. nd
Receiver of said office shall be exercised by and imposed upon a Register to
be appointed, whose compensation shall be a salary of $5oo per annum, together
with the fees and commissions otherwise allowable to both Register and Re-
ceiver, but the salary, fees and commissions of such officer shall not exceed
$3,000 per annum.
The consolidation of said offices shall become effective upon the appoint-
ment and qualification of such Register.
WARREN G HARDING
THE WHITE HOUSE,
July 19, 1922.
