Executive O~rder~
Michael B. Ellis may be appointed to the position of clerk or carrier in the
Postal Service at St. Louis, Missouri, without regard to Civil Service requirements.
This order is issued because of the exceptionally meritorious service rendered
by Sergeant Ellis in France during the World War and upon the recommendation
of the Postmaster General.
CALVIN COOLIDGE
THE WHITE HOUSE,
September 29, 1924.
[No. 4079.]
