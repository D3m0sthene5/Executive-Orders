Erecutive Orber.
Alexander J. Stormont, Clerk to the Secretary of War, may be
transferred to a clerkship in the competitive classified service, without
examination.
The Secretary of War advises that Mr. Stormont has rendered valu-
able service during his occupancy of his present position and that it will
be in the interest of the service to retain him in the regular classified
service. The Civil Service Commission does not concur in this action.
WM H TAFT
THE WHITE HOUSE,
February 28, 1r913.
[No. 1725.]
