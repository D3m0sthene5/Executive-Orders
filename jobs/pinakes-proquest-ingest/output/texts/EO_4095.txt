Executive 'Order
Schedule A of classified positions excepted from examination under Rule II,
section 3, is hereby amended by the addition of the following:
XVIII. U. S. VETERANS' BUREAU.
1. Six assistant directors
2.One medical director
3. One general counsel
4. One assistant to the director
5. Chairman, central board of appeals
6. Fourteen area control officers
7. Any local physician employed on a fee basis or a part-time basis, where,
in the opinion of the Commission, the establishment of registers
is impracticable.
8. Professional or technical specialists when employed temporarily for
consultation purposes.
This order is issued upon the joint recommendation of the U. S. Veterans'
Bureau and the Civil Service Commission.
CALVIN COOL IDGE
Tim, WHITE HOUSE,
October 29, 1924.
