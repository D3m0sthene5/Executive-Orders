ordered that a tract of approximately 240 acres of unsurveyed land in
Montana, which by protraction of the public survey in T. i N., R. 5 W.
would probably be the SW /4 and S 5 2 N W 4 , Sec. 9 , T. I N., R. 6 WI.,
M. M., be temporarily withdrawn from settlement, location, sale or entry,

Deerlodge National Forest.
