Executive Orber.
It is hereby ordered that all per diem employees and other day labor-
ers in the Federal public service, wherever employed, whose employment
extends thru and beyond the first Monday in September, commonly known
as Labor Day and set apart as a legal public holiday for certain branches
of the public service by the act of June 28, A. D., 1894, be excused from
work on said day, and the said day is declared to be a holiday for all pur-
poses for said per diem employees and laborers.
THEODORE ROOSEVELT
THE WHITE HOUSE,
August 10, 1908.
[No. 926.]
