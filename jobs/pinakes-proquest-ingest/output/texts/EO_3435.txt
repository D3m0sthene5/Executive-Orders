after the date of this order, and thereafter to appropriation under any public
land law applicable thereto by the general public. Subsequent to the date
hereof and prior to the date of restoration to general disposition as provided
herein, no rights may be acquired to the excluded lands by settlement in ad,
vance of entry, or otherwise, except strictly in accordance herewith.
