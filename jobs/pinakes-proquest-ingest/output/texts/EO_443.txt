Executive order.
Owing to the destruction of the Government building in San Fran-
cisco, California, in which the United States land office was situated, it is
hereby ordered, in accordance with the provisions of Section twenty-two
hundred and fifty-one of the Revised Statutes, that the United States land
office at San Francisco, California, be removed to, and temporarily located
at, the city of Oakland, California.
THEODORE ROOSEVELT
THlE WHITE HOUSE,
April 28 1906.
