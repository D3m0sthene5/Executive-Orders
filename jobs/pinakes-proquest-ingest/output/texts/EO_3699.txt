Executive Order.
It is hereby ordered under authority of the Act approved June 21, 1906
(34 Stat. 325-26), that the trust period on allotments made to Indians of the
Temecula Band in California which trust period expires during the calendar
year 1922, be, and the same is hereby extended for a period of ten years from
date of expiration.
WARREN G HARDING
THE WHITE House,
June 27, 1922.
