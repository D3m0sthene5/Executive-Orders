Executive 'Orber
It is hereby ordered, under authority contained in the Act of March 2,
1917 (3 Stat. L. 969-976), that the period of trust on lands held in trust for the
use and benefit of the following named bands or villages of Mission Indians in
California, which trust expires during the calendar year 1918, be, and is hereby,
extended for a period of ten years from the date of expiration thereof:
Campo
Augustine
Cuyapipe
Inaj a
Laguna
La Posta
Manzanita
Mesa Grande
Pala
Ramona
Santa Ysabel
Sycuan
Temecula
San Manuel
WOODROW WILSON
THE WHITE HOUSE,
26 January, 1918.
