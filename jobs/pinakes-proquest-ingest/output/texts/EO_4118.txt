Dr. Edward J. Drake may be reinstated in the grade of junior veterinarian,
Department of Agriculture, without regard to the length of his separation.
" Dr. Drake resigned with the classified status of assistant inspector on December
28, 1906, and is now beyond the age limit of forty-five for admission to examination.
While engaged recently under temporary appointment in suppressing foot and mouth
disease in California he originated an original and efficient method of burying the
carcasses of diseased animals by the use of dynamite, and he is said to be exception-
ally well-fitted for this class of work. The Commission concurs with the Depart-
ment in recommending this order.

THE WHITE HOUSE,
