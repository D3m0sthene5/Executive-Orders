Executive Orber
The Executive Order of December 29, 1915, authorizing the appoint-
ment of Mrs. Marian K. Ferguson to a clerkship in the Navy Department
is hereby amended to permit of her appointment to a clerkship in any
Executive Department.
Prior to her appointment in the Navy Department, Mrs. Ferguson
was employed temporarily as a clerk in the office of the Comptroller of
the Currency, and it is on account of her experience and familiarity with
general clerical work and in order that she may find employment in the
line to which she is best adapted that this order is issued.
WOODROW WILSON
THE WAITE HOUSE,
29 May, 1916.
[No. 2389.]
