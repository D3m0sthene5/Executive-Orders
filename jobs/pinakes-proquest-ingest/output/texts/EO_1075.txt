Executive Orber.
It is hereby ordered that the following tracts of public land near
Sparta, Wisconsin, be reserved from sale and set apart for military pur-
poses in connection with a target range, as authorized by an Act of Con-
gress approved March 4, 1909 (Public-- No. 328, p. 65), viz:
The northeast quarter of the northwest quarter, section four; the
southwest quarter of the northwest quarter, section six; the northeast
quarter of the northeast quarter, section 8; all in township seventeen
north, range two west, fourth principal meridian. Also the northwest
quarter of the northeast quarter, the southeast quarter, and the south
half of the southwest quarter, section two; the northeast quarter of
the northeast quarter, section ten ; and the southeast quarter of the
northwest quarter, section twelve; all in township seventeen north,
range three west, fourth principal meridian.
WM H TAFT
THE WWVITE HOUSE,
May 21, 1909.
