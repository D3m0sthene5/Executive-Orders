Schedule A of classified positions excepted from examination under
Civil Service Rule II, clause 3, is hereby amended by the addition to
Subdivision II, relating to the Treasury Department, of a paragraph to
read as follows:
4. All persons actually employed in the Public Health and
Marine Hospital Service at the Leprosy Investigation Station, Molo-
kai, T. H.
This action is based on a recommendation of the Civil Service Com-
mission, concurred in by the Secretary of the Treasury, in viewv of the
conditions attending employment at that establishment, Its proximity to
the Territorial Leper Settlement introduces an element of hazard requiring
the services of volunteers which can not be adequately secured through
competitive examination.
