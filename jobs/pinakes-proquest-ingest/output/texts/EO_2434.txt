Executive ether.
The year limit upon eligibility for reinstatement is hereby waived to
permit the reinstatement of Ernest Szemelenyi as a clerk at $1200 per
annum in the Patent Office.
Mr. Szemelenyi served from 1881 to 190o9 as a translator in the
Patent Office. The Commissioner of Patents, who has been personally
cognizant of the character of his work for twenty years, states that his
acquaintance with foreign languages and the practical arts and science
combined gives him a range of ability for handling certain business of the
Patent Office which is unique; that no one has ever been able to dis-
tribute foreign patents among the different divisions as acceptably as Mr.
Szemnelenyi did while he was so employed; and that he desires to have the
appointment authorized by Executive order because of the needs of the
office and because it is impossible to get any one to do the work which
Mr. Szemeienyi can do within the salary which the office can pay.
The Civil Service Commission concurs with the Secretary of the In-
terior in recommending this Executive order.
WOODROW WILSON
THE WHITE HOUSE,
2 August, 1916.
