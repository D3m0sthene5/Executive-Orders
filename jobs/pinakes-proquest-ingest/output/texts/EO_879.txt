GALLATIN NATIONAL FOREST
MONTANA
It is hereby ordered that on and after July t, 1908, the land within the
boundaries shown on the attached. diagram, heretofore set apart, reserved,
and proclaimed as parts of the Gallatin, Big Belt, and Madison National
Forests, shall be known as the Gallatin National Forest. The remaining
portion of the Gallatin National Forest shall continue reserved and con-
stitute a part of he Madison National Forest. It is not intended by this
order to release any land from reservation or to reserve any land not,
heretofore' embraced in a National Forest.
THEODORE ROOSEVELT
