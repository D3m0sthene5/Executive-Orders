Zrecutive Order.
Paragraph i, Subdivision IV, Schedule B of classified positions which may
be filled upon noncompetitive examination under the civil service rules, is
hereby amended by inserting after the phrase " instructors in flying " the words
"student instructors in flying." As amended the paragraph will read as follows:
i. Aeronautical engineers, aeronautical mechanical engineers, instructors
in flying, student instructors in flying, aviation mechanicians, and inspectors of
airplanes and airplane engines in the Aviation Section of the Signal Corps.
This amendment also includes a change of designation previously reported
by the War Department from inspectors of aeroplanes and aeroplane motors to
inspectors of airplanes and airplane engines.
WOODROW WILSON
THE WHITE HOUSE,
8 June, 1917.
[No. 2634.]
