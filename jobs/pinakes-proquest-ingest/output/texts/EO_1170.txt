Executive Orber
In accordance with the provisions of Section 2253 of the Revised
Statutes of the United States, and by virtue of the authority therein given
it is hereby ordered that townships one hundred and thirty-seven to one
hundred and forty-eight north, inclusive, ranges seventy-two, seventy-three
and seventy-four west, be detached from the Bismarck land district and
attached to and made a part of the Fargo land district in the State of North
Dakota, and the business and archives pertaining to the public lands within
said townships be transferred from the land office at Bismarck to the land
office at Fargo.
WM H TAFT
THE WHITE HOUSE
February 2!, 1910.
[No. 1170.]
