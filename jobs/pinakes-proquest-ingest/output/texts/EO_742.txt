Executive Orber.
On1 and after March 1st 1908, the classification and pay of Mess
Attendants in the Navy who are citizens of the United States, shall be
as follows:
Mess Attendants, 1st Class, $30.00, per month.
Mess Attendants, 2nd Class, 25.00, per month.
Mess Attendants, 3rd Class, 20.00, per month.
On and after March 1st 1908, all Stewards and Cooks in the messman
branch who are citizens of the United States and who hold, or may receive,
certificates of qualification as Stewards or Cooks, shall receive $5.0o per
month additional to the pay of their rating w hile holding such certificate,
such additional pay to be of a permanent character as regular pay.
THEODORE ROOSEVELT
TnlE WHITE HOUSE,
January 28, 1908.
