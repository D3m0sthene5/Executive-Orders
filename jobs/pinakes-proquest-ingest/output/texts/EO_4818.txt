Executive, Orber
ALASKA ISL.ANDS
Pursuant to the authority vested in me by the act of February 10, 1927
(44 Stat. 1068), 10 islands along the southern coast of Alaska, known as Aghiyuk,
Chirikof, Chowiet, Elizabeth, Little Koniuji, Long, Marmot, Middleton, Pearl and
Simeonof, jurisdiction of which was transferred to the Department of Agriculture
by the act of May 31, 1920 (41 Stat. 716), are hereby turned over to the control
and jurisdiction of the Department of the Interior.
This order shall continue in full force and effect unless and until revoked by
the President or by act of Congress.
CALVIN COOL IDGE
THE WHITE House,
February 29, 1928.
