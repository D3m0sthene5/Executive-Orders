AUTHORIZING THE ALIEN PROPERTY CUSTODIAN
TO SELL CERTAIN PROPERTY AT PRIVATE SALE.
By virtue of the authority vested in me by an Act of Congress entitled"An Act to define, regulate, and punish trading with the enemy and for otherpurposes ", approved October 6, 1917, known as the "Trading with the EnemyAct", and the Amendment to such Act embodied in "An Act making appro-priations to supply urgent deficiencies in the appropriations for the fiscal yearending June 30, 1918, and prior fiscal years on account of war expenses and forother purposes", approved March 28, 1918, and the Amendment to such Actembodied in "An Act making appropriations to supply deficiencies in appro-priations for the fiscal year ending June 30, 1919, and prior fiscal years onaccount of war expenses, and for other purposes ", approved November 4, 1918,to make certain orders such as that herein contained, I hereby, in the publicinterest, make the following determination and order:The Alien Property Custodian is hereby authorized to sell at private sale,at such time andjuace~ as to him or to his authorized agent may seem advisable,without public or other advertisement, Fourteen (14) shares of capital stockof the American Vanadium Company, a corporation organized under the lawsof the State of New Jersey, of the par value of One Hundred Dollars ($o.o)each, which said (14) shares were heretofore seized by the Alien Property Custo-dian under the provisions of said Act as the property of Mrs. Adelayda Heintschel,of Vienna, Austria.
My reasons for the foregoing determination and order are:(a) That property such as that herein described cannot usually be sold toadvantage at public sale after public or other advertisement.(b) That the American Vanadium Company is in liquidation and that, asthe Alien Property Custodian is informed, if the said Company proceeds with theliquidation each stockholder will receive One Thousand Two Hundred andSixty Dollars ($1,260.00) in cash and Seven () shares of stock of the CollierLand Company for each share of stock of the American Vanadium Company.(c) That, however, the Estate of James J. Flannery, deceased, has offeredto pay One Thousand Seven Hundred Dollars (1,700.o) in cash per share forall the outstanding stock of the American Vanadium Company, which, as theAlien Property Custodian is informed, represents Ninety-four and Thirty-nineOne Hundredths per centum (9.3% of the present value of all the holdingsof the American Vanadium Company.
(d) That, as the Alien Property Custodian is informed, stockholders own-ing Ninety-eight per centum (98%0 ) of stock of the American Vanadium Company(exclusive of the above named Fourteen (14) shares) have already accepted thesaid offer made by the Estate of James J. Flannery, deceased, and the accept-ance thereof by the Alien Property Custodian is deemed to be advantageousto the trust involved.
(e) That an attempted sale at auction would be a useless formality andwould involve unnecessary expense, delay and inconvenience, which should beavoided.
WARREN G HARDING
THE WHITE HOUSE,
May r6, 1923.
