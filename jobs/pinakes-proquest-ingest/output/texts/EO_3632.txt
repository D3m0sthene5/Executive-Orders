Executive Order
Under the authority of the Act of October 28, 1921, (Public No. 84), the
offices of Register and Receiver of the United States land office at Bismarck,
North Dakota, are hereby consolidated and the office of Receiver is abolished.
All the powers, duties, obligations and penalties imposed by law upon both
the Regiter and Receiver of said office shall be exercised by and imposed upon
the Register so appointed, whose compensation shall be a salary of $500 per
annum, together with fees and commissions otherwise allowable to both Register
and Receiver, but the salary, fees and commissions (.2 such officer shall not
exceed $3,ooo per annum.
WARREN G HARDING
THE~ WHITE HOUSE,
February 6, 1922.
[No. 3632.]
