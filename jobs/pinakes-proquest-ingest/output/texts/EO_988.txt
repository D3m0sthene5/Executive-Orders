In accordance with the provisions of Sections 2 248 and 2252 of the
Revised Statutes of the United States, and by virtue of the authority
therein given, it is hereby ordered that:
The Colby land district in the State of Kansas, with office at Colby,
be discontinued and the lands, business and archives of said land district
and office transferred to and consolidated with the Topeka land district,
with office at Topeka, Kansas.
THEODORE ROOSEVELT
THE WHITE HOUSE,
December 7, 1908.
