Executive Orber.
It is hereby ordered that lots 6, ; and 8, fractional Sec. 29, 'F. 9 S., R. I tXW.,
florid, be and the same are hereby reserved for lighthouse purposes. Said

of the section were reserved for lighthouse purposes.
WOODROW WILSON
