)Executive Orber.
Mr. William J. Meyers may be permanently appointed as a statis-
tician, at $5,ooo per annum, in the Interstate Commerce Commission,
without examination under the civil service rules.
It appears that the position demands not only a man who is capable
of directing the assembling of statistical data contained in the reports of
carriers subject to the law, in such a manner as to make it most useful to
the public and to the Commission in the administration of the law, but
who can' also aid in directing special inquiries from time to time and is per-
sonally qualified in temperament and manner to deal with his official subor-
dinates as wvell as with the officers and employees of carriers and of tele-
graph and telephone companies.
It is regarded as impracticable to fill the position through examina-
tion, and transfer to it is precluded by the absence of persons now in the
service who have the required experience.
The order is issued upon the joint request of the Civil Service Coin-
mission and the Interstate Commerce Commission.
