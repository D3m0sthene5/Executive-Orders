Schedule B of the Civil Service Rules is hereby amended by the
addition of a new subdivision, to be numbered V, as follows:
V. DEPARTMENT OF JUSTICE.
i. Any competitive position at a United States penitentiary
when filled by a paroled prisoner wvho is recommended for such em-
ployment by the officers of the penitentiary in which the employment
is proposed, by the board of parole, and by the Department of Justice.
This amendment is recommended by the Department and concurred
in by the Commission in the belief that it is in line with the movement
toward a more rational and humane treatment of prisoners with a view to
their reformation and resumption of free life.
