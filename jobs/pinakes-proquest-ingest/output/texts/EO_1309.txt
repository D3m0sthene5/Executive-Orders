Mr. William E. Lewvis may be reinstated in the Government Printing
Office without regard to the length of his separation from that Office.
He resigned on January 4, 1908, because of ill health and failed to
recover sufficiently within the year of his eligibility. The order is issued
in view of the above fact and by reason of his service in Cuba and his
special qualifications as stated by the Public Printer. This action was
recommended by the Civil Service Commission.
WM H TAFTI
THE WHITE HOUSE,
February 25, 19!!.
