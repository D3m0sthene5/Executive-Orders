It is hereby ordered that the SE/4 SE/4 Sec. 8, T. 11 N., R. 3 W., N. M. P. M.
New Mexico, be, and it is hereby, temporarily withdrawn from settlement, entry,
sale, or other disposition until March 5, 1927, in aid of proposed legislation.

69th Congress, and no other direction is given regarding the disposition of such
land, it will, on March 5, 1927, become subject to disposal under any law then
applicable thereto without further order.
CALVIN COOLIDGE
