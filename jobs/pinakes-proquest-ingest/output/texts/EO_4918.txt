WHEREAS, by certain leases executed under dates of June 15 and July 5, 1918,
the cities of Oakland and Alameda, municipal corporations of the State of California,
leased to the United States Shipping Board Emergency Fleet Corporation, now
known as the United States Emergency Fleet Corporation, for a term of 25 years,
certain premises known as Government Island, comprising approximately 110 acres,
situated in the estuary between said cities; and
WHEREAS, by the terms of each of said leases the United States Emergency
Fleet Corporation is given the right to assign its interests therein to the United
States; and
WHEREAS, under date of November 1, 1922, the United States Emergency
Fleet Corporation did assign its interests in said leases to the United States; and
WHEREAS, since such assignment and until August 10, 1927, in accordance with
Executive Order No. 4703, of that date, control of the uses of the Island by various
Federal activities has been under the Department of Agriculture; and
WHEREAS, on the latter date and in accordance with said Executive Order of
August 10, 1927, such control was vested in the War Department; and
WHEREAS, the War Department has requested relief from the burdens inci-
dent to such assignment and control; and
WHEREAS, the Department of Agriculture desires administrative control ' e
vested i~n it;
NOW, THEREFORE, by virtue of the authority vested in me, as President of
the United States of America, it is hereby ordered that Executive Order No. 4703,
of August 10, 1927, be and hereby is revoked, and that the duties, rights, powers
and responsibilities of the United States under such leases be, and the same are,
hereby retransferred to the jurisdiction and control of the Department of Agriculture;
provided that the Federal agencies now occupying Government Island shall not be
disturbed except by direction of the President, exercised through the Chief Coordi-
nator under the provisions of Executive Order No. 3578, of November 8, 1921.
CALVIN COOLIDGE
THE WHITE HOUSE,
June 23, 1928.
