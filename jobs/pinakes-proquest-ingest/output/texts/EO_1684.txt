Executive ether.
Mrs. Ollie M. Croghan may be appointed to a position in the clerical
grade in the Department of Justice.
Mrs. Croghan is the widow of John S. Croghan, who served for more
than 20 years in the United States Navy and wvho, while in command of
the Tug Nina in 1910, wvas lost with the entire crewv and the vessel. By
his death Mrs. Croghan became the sole support of her young son and an
aunt. Mrs. Croghan had three years' experience as a stenographer and
typewriter previous to her marriage and has since had a varied experience
as a stenographer and typewriter.
This order is issued on the recommendation of the Attorney General,
the Civil Service Commission concurring, in view of her husband's long
service and the circumstances under which he lost his life, and Mrs.
Croghan's qualifications for the position she seeks.
WM H TAFT
THE WHITE HOUSE,
January 17, 1913.
[No. 1684.]
