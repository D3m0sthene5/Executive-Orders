ARIZONA
Under authority contained in the act of Congress approved June 25, 1910
(36 Stat., 847), as amended by the act of August 24, 1912 (37 Stat., 497), and sub-
ject to the conditions, provisions and limitations of said acts, it is hereby ordered
that the public lands in T. 13 N., R. 1 E., G. & S. R. M., Arizona, be and the
same are hereby withdrawn from settlement, location, sale, entry and all forms of
appropriation, pending a resurvey of said township under the act of Congress.
approved March 3, 1909 (35 Stat., 845); this withdrawal. to remain in full force
and effect from the date hereof until 'the resurvey is duly approved and accepted
and the approved plat thereof is officially filed in the proper United States local
land office, unless another date be fixed by the Commissioner of the General Land
Office, whereupon this withdrawal will become of no effect.
CALVIN COOLIDGE
THE WHITE HousE,
April 25,' 1925.
