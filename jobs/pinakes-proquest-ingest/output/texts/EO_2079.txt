Mrs. Alice B. Smith and Mrs. Eunice B. Neff may be appointed to clerical
positions in the classified service of the Post Office Department without
compliance with the Civil Service Rules.
Mrs. Smith is the widow of John H. Smith, formerly a letter carrier at
the Washington post office, who served the Government continuously for
42 years and died as a result of three strokes of paralysis (possibly due to
exposure while serving his route as a carrier) and left a widow and two
small children without means of support.
Mrs. Neff is the widow of W. E. Neff, a former employee of the Post
Office Department, who after ten years' faithful service had a nervous
breakdown which resulted in insanity, and he recently died leaving three
small children. Mrs. Neff is also the sole support of an aunt who is an
elderly woman.
The Civil Service Commission does not recommend the issuance of this
order.
WOODROW WILSON
THE WHITE HOUSE,
10 November, 1914.
