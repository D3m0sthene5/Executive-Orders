Executive Orber.
Whereas, one acre of land in square form, known as the "Govcrnment
Acre", in the southeast corner of the southeast quarter of the northwest
quarter of section fifteen, in township sixteen north of range seven west of
the Indian Meridian, in the Territory of Oklahoma, as designated on the
plat of " Fossett's Addition to Kingfisher" Towvnsite, covering said southeast
quarter of northwest quarter, approved by the Secretary of the Interior on
April 8, 1892, was reserved for land office purposes, and said acre being
no longer required for said purposes, but being in part used for postoffice
purposes:
N ow, therefore, it is hereby ordered that said acre of land be and the
same is hereby transferred to the Treasury Department for postoffice and
such other government purposes as may be required.
THEODORE ROOSEVELT
TILE WhITi1E HOUSE,
June 6, 1906.
