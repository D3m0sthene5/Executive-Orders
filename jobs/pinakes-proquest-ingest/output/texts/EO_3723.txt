Under authority of the Act of Congress approved June 30, 1882 (22 Stat. L.,
12 n), which prescribes that the Army and Navy General Hospital at Hot Sprins
7Arkansas, "shall be subject to such rules, regulations, and restrictions as shall
4 '~ be provided by the President of the United States," the following amendments,
effective July i, 1922, are hereby made to the rules and regulations prescribed
for the government of the said hospital by Executive Order of August 25, 1892,
as amended by later orders, viz:
i. This hospital shall be devoted to the treatment of the personnel indicated
below:
(a) Beneficiaries of the United States Veterans' Bureau entitled to hospitali-
zation, except women, upon the requests of the proper representatives thereof.
(b) Officers, commissioned warrant officers, warrant officers, ad enlisted
men of the Armmy, the Navy, and the Marine Corps, on the active lists, Army
Field Clerks (male), Field Clerks Quartermaster Corps, (male), and cadets at the
Military Academy and midshipmen at the Naval Academy, subject to such
rules as may be prescribed by the Wa and Navy Departments respectively.
(c) Officers and enlisted men of the Coast Guard, and officers of the Public
Health Service, on the active lists, and cadets and cadet engineers of the Coast
Guard, under such rules as may be prescribed by the Treasury Department; and
officers and enlisted mnen of the Coast and Geodetic Survey, on the active lists,
tinder such rules as may be prescribed by the Department of Commerce.
(d) Officers, commissioned warrant officers, warrant officers, pay clerks, and
enlisted men of the Army, the Navy, the Marine Corps, the Coast Guard, and
the Coast and Geodetic Survey, and officers of the Public Health Service, on the
retired lists or the equivalent thereof, under such rules as may be prescribed by
the Secretary of War
(e) Honorably discharged officers, commissioned warrant officers, warrant
officers, Army Field Clerks (male), Field Clerks Quartermaster Corps (male),
cadets, cadet engineers and enlisted men of the Army, the Navy, the Marine
Corps (including National Guard Forces, Naval Militia, volunteers and drafted
or selected men in the service of the United States), tie Coast Guard and the
Coast and Geodetic Survey, and honorably discharged officers of the Public
Health Service, under such conditions and regulations as may be prescribed by

() When practicable, and for a reasonable time, under such conditions as

United States in H-ot Springs and its vicinity who sustain personal injuries
while in the performance of duty.
2. The rations of enlisted men of the Army on the active list while under
treatment in the hospital will be commuted at the rate of 6o cents a ration to be
paid to the commanding officer of the hospital by the post finance officer or such
other officer as may be designated, conformably to the regulations governing
the subsistence of enlisted patients at other Army hospitals.
3. Since there is no issuing quartermaster at this post, and rations in kind
can not in any~ case be economically issued, the rations of the enlisted men of the
Army on duty at this hospital will be commuted at the rate of 6o cents a ration,
to be paid to the men by the finance officer upon their individual receipts, con-
formable to tile regulations governing such payments in other Army cases.
4. The subsistence of enlisted men of the Navy, Marine Corps, Coast Guard,
and Coast and Geodetic Survey, on the active lists, under treatment in this
hospital in pursuance of orders, will be paid for to the commanding officer of the
hospital 
at the rate of 6o cents a day by the proper officers of the Navy Depart-Flo

ment, Coast Guard, and Coast and Geodetic Survey, upon monthly statements
of amounts due certified by the commanding officer of the hospital direct to the
Surgeon General of the Navy, the Captain Commandant of the Coast Guard,
and the Director of the Coast and Geodetic Survey, respectively.
S. The subsistence of members of the Nurse Corps on duty in this hospital
will be paid for by them monthly to the commanding officer of the hospital at
the rate of 6o cents a day.
6. Subsistence charges will be collected from other pay patients as follows:
Enlisted men of the Army, Navy, Marine Corps, Coast Guard, and Coast and
Geodetic Survey, on the retired lists, and honorably discharged officers, commis-
sioned warrant officers, warrant officers, Army Field Clerks, Field Clerks Quar-
termaster Corps, cadets, midshipmen, cadet engineers, and enlisted men of the
Army, the Navy, the Marine Corps (including National Guard forces, Naval
Militia, volunteers, and drafted or selected men, in the service of the United
States), the Coast Guard, and the Coast and Geodetic Survey, and honorably
discharged officers of the Public Health Service, subsisted on the status of en-
listed men, at 8,9 cents a day.
Military and naval cadets, midshipmen, and cadets and cadet engineers of
the Coast Guard, at 8o cents a day.
Officers, commissioned warrant officers, warrant officers, Army Field
Clerks, Field Clerks Quartermaster Corps, and Pay Clerks, of the Army, Navy,
Marine Corps, Coast Guard, Coast and Geodetic Survey, and Public Health
Service, and honorably discharged officers, commissioned warrant officers, war-
rant officers, Army Field Clerks, Field Clerks Quartermaster Corps, cadets, mid-
shipmen, cadet engineers, and enlisted men, of the Army, Navy, Marine Corps,
Coast Guard, Coast and Geodetic Survey and Public Health Service, subsisted
on the status of officers, at not exceeding $1.5o a day.
7. Medicine charges will be collected at 25 cents a day from pay patients as
follows :
Honorably discharged officers, commissioned warrant officers, warrant offi-
cers, Army Field Clerks, Field Clerks Quartermaster Corps, cadets, midshipmen,
cadet engineers and enlisted men of the Army, Navy, Marine Corps, Coast
Guard, Coast and Geodetic Survey, and Public Health Service.
8. In the cases of patients admitted upon account of the United States
Veterans' Bureau, subsistence charges at 85 cents a day will be billed and
submitted with W. D. Form No. 351 properly accomplished, by the commanding
officer of the hospital direct to the local finance officer for settlement by check.
Other expenses of Veterans' Bureau patients will be adjusted between the
appropriations concerned in conformity with orders issued by the Secretary of
War from time to time.
9. In the cases of patients admitted upon account of the United States
Employees' Compensation Commission, subsistence charges at 8,9 cents a day
for those on the status of enlisted men, and at not exceeding $ .50o a day for
those on the status of officers, and medicine charges at 25 cents a day for those
in either status, will be billed by the commanding officer direct to the Com-
mission. No other charges will be billed or collected in these cases.
io. Collections arising from subsistence charges will be taken up and
accounted for as hospital funds. Collections arising from medicine charges
will be turned over, on War Department Form No. 322a, to the nearest dis-
bursing or agent officer for deposit with the Treasurer of the United States to
the credit of the proper appropriations, which will be designated on W. D.
Form No. 322a, and accounted for in due form accordingly.
