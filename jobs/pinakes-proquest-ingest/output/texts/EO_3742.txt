Executive Orer
By virtue of the authority vested in me by the act of Congress approved
August x, 1914, making appropriations for the sundry civil expenses of the
Government for the fiscal year ended June 30, 1915, Carrabelle is abolished as
a customs port of entry in the Eighteenth, Florida, Customs Collection District,
effective at the close of business September 30, 1922.
