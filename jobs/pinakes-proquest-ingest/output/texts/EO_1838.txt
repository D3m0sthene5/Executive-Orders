Executive 'Order.
Mr. Sam W. Smniley may be reinstated as a clerk in the Railway
Mail Service without reference to the period which has elapsed since his
separation.
Mr. Smiley wvas severely injured in a wreck in May, 1895, in the line
of duty, which necessitated his leaving the service. He applied for rein-
statement as soon as he recovered from his injuries but was not reinstated.
It appears that his present physical condition is good.
The Civil Service Commission concurs with the Postmaster General
in recommending this order.
WOODROW WILSON
THE WHITE HOUSE,
October 16, r1913.
[No. 1 838.]
