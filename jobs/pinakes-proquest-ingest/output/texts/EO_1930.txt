The Executive order of February 14, 1912, permitting employees in
the executive civil service permanently residing in certain incorporated
municipalities adjacent to the District of Columbia to become candidates
for or hold municipal office therein, is hereby amended so as to include the
incorporated municipality of Somerset, Maryland, among those named in
the order. The first paragraph of the order of February 14, 1912, as
amended, will read as follows:
Employees of the executive civil service permanently residing in the
following incorporated municipalities adjacent to the District of Columbia
will not be prohibited from becoming candidates for or holding municipal
office in such corporations:
In Maryland-Takoma Park, Kensington, Garrett Park, Chevy Chase,
Glen Echo, Hyattsville, Mount Rainier, Somerset.
In Virginia-Falls Church, Vienna, Herndon.
This order is recommended by the Civil Service Commission, for the
reason that she same conditions exist in Somerset, Maryland, as in the
other municipalities named in the original order.
WOODROW WILSON
THE WHITE HOUSE,
May 5, 1914.
[No. 1930.]
