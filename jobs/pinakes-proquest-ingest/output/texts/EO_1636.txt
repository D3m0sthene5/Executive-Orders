Mr. E. 0. Merchant may be appointed a special agent in the Bureau
of Corporations without regard to the civil service rules.
This Order is issued upon recommendation of the Secretary of
Commerce and Labor, and for the purpose of enabling the Bureau of
Corporations to obtain the services of Mr. Merchant w~ho, by exceptional
training and experience, is especially adapted for the work of that Bureau.
The Civil Service Commission advises against this order.
Wm H TAFT
THE WHITE HOUSE,
