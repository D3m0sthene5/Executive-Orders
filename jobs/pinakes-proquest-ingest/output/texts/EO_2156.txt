Executive Orber.
It is hereby ordered that the provisions of an Executive Order dated
March 5, 1907, are hereby waived, for the purpose of permitting the
republication of the opinions of the Solicitor for the former Department
of Commerce and Labor, and the publication of subsequent opinions of
the Solicitor for the Department of Labor in reference to compensation
for certain employees of the Government who are injured in the course of
employment as provided for by the Act of May 30, 1908, and the amend-
ments thereto.
WOODROW WILSON
THE "WHITE HOUSE,
30 Marck, 1915.
[No. 2156.]
