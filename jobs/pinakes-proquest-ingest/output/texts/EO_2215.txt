Mrs. Lillian Peyton King may be appointed as Clerk in the classified
service of the Treasury Department without compliance with Civil Service
rules.
Mrs. King is the widow of the late General John Floyd King, who
was for many years a prominent member of Congress, and recently
Assistant Register of the Treasury. The issuance of this order is recom-
mended by the Secretary of the Treasury in recognition of General King's
distinguished services in the National House of Representatives and other
public positions, and in view of the fact that his widow is entirely compe-
tent to perform the duties of a Clerk, and is left without means of
support.
