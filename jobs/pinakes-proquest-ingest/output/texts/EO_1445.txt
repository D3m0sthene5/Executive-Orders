Executive Orber,
Mr. Thomas Dawson may be appointed as a watchman in the Light-
House Service.
Mr. Dawson is a veteran who was injured in the military service in
the line of duty by a stroke of lightning, which incapacitated him for the
performance of clerical, laboratory or pharmaceutical work which he was
performing as Hospital Steward of the H-ospital Corps of the United
States Army. A medical examination by a representative of the Com-
mission indicated that he was physically fitted to perform the duties of a
watchman, and he has passed the watchman examination with a high
average. In view of these facts the Commission joins the Secretary of
Commerce and Labor in recommending the appointment of Mr. Dawson.
