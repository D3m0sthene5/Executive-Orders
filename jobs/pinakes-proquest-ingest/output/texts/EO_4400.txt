Executive Order~
By virtue of the authority vested in me by the Act of Congress, approved
August 1, 1914, making appropriations for the sundry civil expenses of the Govern-
ment for the fiscal year ended June 30, 1915, Qafien is hereby abolished as a, port
of entry in customs collection district No. 17 (Georgia), with headquarters at
Savaninah, GEL., effective thirty days from the date of this order.
CALVIN COOL IDGE
THE WHIE HOUSE,
March 28, 1926.
[No. 4400]
