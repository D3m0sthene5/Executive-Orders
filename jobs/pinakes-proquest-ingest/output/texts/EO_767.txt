&. Lmergency Ration.
An emergency ration, prepared under direction of the War Department, will be issued, in
addition to the regular ration, as required for troops on active campaign or in the field for pur- 4
poses of instruction, and will not be opened except by order of an officer or in extremity. Com- “4
| pany and detachment commanders are responsible for the proper care and use of emergency 4
: rations carried on the person of the soldier. :
: Savings on the garrison and travel rations will be paid for at the current prices of the q
component articles, and not in any instance at prices of substitutive articles, the use of the ]
latter being limited to issue in kind where economy and a due regard to the health and comfort
of the troops may so require.
q After troops have, through the exigencies of the service, subsisted on the field or haver- 4
sack ration, the commanding general may, in written orders, direct the issue in kind of |
specified articles of subsistence stores of money value equal to the difference in price |
: between. the field or haversack rations issued and used by such troops and the price of the
same number of garrison rations. Such issue to be made only when necessary for the health
| and comfort of the troops and to he independent of and in addition to the ration issues being
made, but such issues will not be ordered after sixty days from the last date on which the
| troops were subsisted on the field or haversack ration.
