]Executive Orber
I, Woodrow Wilson, President of the United States, by virtue of
the authority vested in me by Section 3141, Revised Statutes of the
United States, hereby order that the State of South Carolina, now a part
of the Fourth Internal Revenue Collection District of North Carolina,
be detached from said district and constitute one collection district, to be
known as the District of South Carolina.
This order to become effective upon the appointment and qualifica-
tion of a collector.
WOODROW WILSON
THE WHITE HOUSE,
July 21, 1913.
[No. 8o2.]
