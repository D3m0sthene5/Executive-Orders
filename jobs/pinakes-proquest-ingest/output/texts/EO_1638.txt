Executive Irber.
Mrs. Robert L. Bowman may be appointed to the position of matron
in the Indian Service without examination under the Civil Service Rules.
Mrs. Bowman is the widow of Robert L. Bowmran, Deputy Special
Officer for the Suppression of the Liquor Traffic among Indians, who was
murdered on September 19g, 1912, in the performance of his official duties.
Mr. Bowman left three dependent children. Mrs. Bowman is a woman
of excellent character and the Secretary of the Interior believes that she
will render efficient service in the capacity of matron in the Indian School
Service.
In view of the facts, the Civil Service Commission joins with the
Interior Department in recommending the issuance of an Executive Order.
WM H TAFT
TILE WHIT:E HOUSE,
November 1, 1912.
