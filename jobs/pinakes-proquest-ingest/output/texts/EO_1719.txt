In accordance with the provisions of Section 2253 of the Revised
Statutes of the United States, and by virtue of the authority therein given,
it is hereby ordered that:
The lands north of the center of White River and east of the center
of the Missouri River, now within the Chamberlain, South Dakota, land
district, be detached from said district and attached to and made a part of
the Pierre, South Dakota, land district.
WM H: TAFT
THE WHITE HOUSE,
Febjy. 26th, 1913.
