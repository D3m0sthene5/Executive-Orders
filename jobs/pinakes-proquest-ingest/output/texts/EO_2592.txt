It is hereby ordered that Henry J. Cantey, a laborer in the unclassified
service, at a salary of $66o per annum, in the Bureau of Labor Statistics, Depart-
ment of Labor, may be promoted to the position of assistant messenger in the
classified service, in the same Bureau, at a salary of $720 per annum, without
reference to Civil Service rules.
It is stated by administrative officials of the Department of Labor that
Mr. Cantey possesses qualifications of unusual value in the work of the Bureau
of Labor Statistics. He performs labor which demands a degree of skill and
judgment beyond that usually demanded from those in the grade he occupies.
H~e has been with the Bureau of Labor Statistics nearly ten years, during all
of which time his record has been excellent.
WOODROW WILSON
