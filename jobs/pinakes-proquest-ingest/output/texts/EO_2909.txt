Executive Orber.
ALASKA
Signal Corps Radio Station.
By virtue of the power and authority vested in and conferred upon me by
the laws of the United States in that behalf made and provided, it is hereby
ordered that the tract of land hereinafter described be and the same is hereby
withdrawnl from settlement, location, sale, entry or other disposition, and re-
served and set aside for the use of the War Department as a Signal Corps Radio
Station :
Lots numbered 6i, 65, and 78 to 93 inclusive, and the land in the inter-
vening streets, in the Town of Cri ,Aaska, as per survey made by the Forest
Service, Department of Agricu r, approved March 31, 1914, said town being
situated on Prince of Wales Island, and approximately in Latitude 550 25' N.
and Longitude 133° W.
WOODROW WILSON
THES WHITE HOUSE,
12 July, 1918.
