In accordance with the provisions of Section 2253 of the Revised
Statutes of the United States, and by virtue of the authority therein
given, it is hereby ordered that:
All that part of the Waterville land district in the State of Wash-
ington embraced in townships twenty-three and twenty-four north, ranges
eleven, twelve, thirteen, fourteen and fifteen east, lying east of the Cascade
range of mountains, is hereby attached to and made a part of the North
XVakima land district, and the business and archives pertaining to the lands
within the above described townships be transferred from the land office at
Waterville to the land office at North Yakima.
WM H TAFT
THE WHITE HOUSE,
February 20, 19!!.
