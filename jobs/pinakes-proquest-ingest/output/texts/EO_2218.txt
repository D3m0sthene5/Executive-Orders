Executive Irber.
Paragraph 16 of Sub-division VIII, Department of the Interior, of
Schedule A of the Civil Service Rules, is hereby amended by the addition.
of the words-%
"and among the natives of Alaska."
As amended the paragraph will read :
16. Special officers to assist in the supresssion of the liquor traffic
in the Indian Service and among the natives of Alaska."
This order is recommended by the Secretary of the Interior with the
concurrence of the Civil Service Commission.
WOODROW WILSON
THE WHITE HOUSE,
22 June, 1915.
[No. 2218.]
