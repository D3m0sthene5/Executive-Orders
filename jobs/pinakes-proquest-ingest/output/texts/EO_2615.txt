]Executive Orber.
Henry S. Grogan, of Minnesota, may be reinstated as a postoffice inspector
without regard to the year limitation prescribed by Civil Service Rule IX.
This order is issued on the recommendation of the Postmaster General
because of Mr. Grogan's long and efficient service in various branches of the
postal service, including eleven years as a letter carrier and subsequent service
from 19o6 to 1912 as postoffice inspector, superintendent of station, and assistant
superintendent of mails.
The Civil Service Commission concurs in the recommendation of the
Postmaster General for an executive order.
~ ~ WOODROW WILSON
THE WHIT HOUSE,
9 May, 1917.
