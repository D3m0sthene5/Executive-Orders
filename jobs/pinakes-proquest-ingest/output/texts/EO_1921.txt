Executive rber
Mr. James W. Cole may be reinstated in the Railway Mail Service
without regard to the length of his separation from that service.
Mr. Cole was appointed on March 6, 19o1, a regular clerk, and served
continuously until July 3,,[910, upon one of the heavy lines of the Rail-
wvay Mail Service. His record was good. On the latter date, while on
duty, he was seriously injured in a wreck. and although he has now
entirely recovered, he did not recover sufficiently during the period of
eligibility fixed by the civil service rules to permit his reinstatement. He
wvas thus forced out of the service by an injury incurred in the line of duty.
In viewv of these facts and of the statement of the Department that
his reinstatement will be in the interests of the service, the Commission
concurs with the Department in recommending an executive order.
WOODROW WILSON
THE WHhITE HOUSE,
22 April, 1914/.
