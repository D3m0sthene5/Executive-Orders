Mr. Ulysses S. Price, who failed in the deputy shipping commissioner
examination on June 12, 1912, may be appointed to the position of deputy
shipping commissioner without compliance with the Civil Service Rules.
This order is made on the recommendation of the Acting Secretary of
Commerce and Labor and the Commissioner of Navigation, which is not
concurred in by the Civil Service Commission.
WM H TAFT
THE WHITE HOUSE.
October 18th, 1912.
