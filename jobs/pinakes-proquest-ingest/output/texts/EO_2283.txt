Executive 'Otber
Miss Emma I. Windridge, who has passed the civil service examina-
tion in stenography and typewriting, may be appointed to a clerkship in
the War Department without reference to the civil service rules.
Miss Windridge is the daughter of Charles E. Windridge, formerly a
clerk in the War Department. Her father was appointed to a position in
the Post Office Department in November, 1902, where he served until
April, 1905, when he was appointed to a position in the Navy Depart-
ment. He resigned in November, 1905, to accept a clerkship in the War
Department, which he held until the time of his death, September 14.,
1915. Mr. Windridge had an excellent record while employed in the
War Department.
The Secretary of War recommends the issuance of an order in this
case. The Civil Service Commission does not concur in the recom-
mendation.
WOODROW WILSON
THE WHITE HOUSE,
14t December, 1915.
