Executive rber.
Mr. James B. Brunemer may be appointed a watchman in the Gov-
ernment Printing Office. He is a veteran of the civil war and served as a
watchman in the Post Office Department and later as a clerk in the
Treasury and Interior Departments. He is eligible for reinstatement
under the rules in any of these Departments because of his former service
therein; but not in the Government Printing Office. This order is issued
upon the recommendation of the Public Printer, with the concurrence of
the Civil Service Commission, as there are no eligible applicants among
the veterans employed in the Government Printing Office. This action
merely permits reinstatement in an office other than one for which the
applicant is already eligible.
WM H TAFT
THE WHI-ITE HOUSE,
