Executive ether.
Under authority contained in the Act of Congress approved June 25, 1910
(36 Stat., 847) as amended by the Act of August 24, 1912 (37 Stat., 497) and sub-
ject to the conditions, provisions and limitations of said acts, it is hereby ordered
that the public lands erroneously omitted from survey within the areas of what
are shown upon the official plat on file in the General Land Office as two mean-
dered lakes, one each in Secs. 28 and 33, T. 149 N., R. 40 W., 5 th P. M., Minne-
sota, be, and the same are hereby temporarily withdrawn from settlement,
entry and all forms of disposal for the purposes of classification and in aid of
legislation proposed in H. R. 10007, Sixty-seventh Congress, Second Session,
and such withdrawal shall remain in effect until revoked by the President or
Act of Congress.
WARREN G HARDING
THE~ WHITE HousrB,
February 3, 1922.
