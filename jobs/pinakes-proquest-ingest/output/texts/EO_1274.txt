)Executive Orbet.
It is hereby ordered that on and after January I, 1911, all official
communcations or reports from and to executive officers of the following
territorial possessions of the United States, to wit:
The Island of Guam in the Ladrones and the Island of Tutuila of the
Samoan Group and all other islands of the group east of longitude one
hundred seventy-one degrees west of Greenwich, and all official communi-
cations or reports relating to territorial matters from and to all executive
officers of the United States stationed in such territorial possessions, shall
be transmitted through the Secretary of the Navy, in such manner, and
under such regulations, as he may prescribe. The Executive Orders of May
ii, 1907 and May 25, 1909, respectively, are hereby amended accordingly.
WM H TAFT
THE WHITE HOUSE,
December 20, 1910.
