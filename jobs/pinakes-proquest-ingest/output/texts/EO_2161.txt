]Executive Order
BLACK HILLS-SUNDANCE NATIONAL FORESTS
SOUTH DAKOTA-WYOMING
Under authority of the Act of Congress of June 4, 1897 (30 Stat., I I),
and upon recommendation of the Secretary of Agriculture, it is hereby
ordered that on and after July I, 1915, all the. lands included within the
boundaries of the Sundance National Forest, Wyoming, as fixed and
defined by proclamation of December 16, 1910 (No. 11 io), be trans-
ferred to and administered as a part of the Black Hills National Forest,
South Dakota.
It is intended by this Executive Order, for economy of administra-
tion, to merge into one National Forest, thereafter to be known as the
Black Hills National Forest, all the lands which on July 1, 1915, are
within the Sundance and the Black Hills National Forests, and the boun-
daries of the Black Hills National Forest as they exist at that date are
hereby modified accordingly.
This Executive Order is not intended to add to the Black Hills
National Forest any lands which on July I, 1915, are not embraced within
the Sundance National Forest, nor to release from reservation for
National Forest purposes any lands at that date within the Sundance
National Forest.
WOODROW WILSON
THE WHITE HOUSE,
6 April, 1915.
[No. 2161.]
