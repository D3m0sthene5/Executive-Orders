Mrs. Mary A. Wells may be reinstated as a clerk in the Bureau of the
Census without reference to the year limitation of the reinstatement rule.
Mrs. Wells was separated from the Bureau of the Census by resignation
on June i6, 1909g, and in view of the short time which has elapsed since
her eligibility for reinstatement expired and because of her good record of
efficiency while in the service, the Civil Service Commission believes that
her reinstatement is in the interests of good administration and joins the
Department in recommending the approval of an Executive order permit-
ting her reinstatement.
WM H TAFT
THE WHITE HOUSE,
November 7th 1910.
