Executive Order.
It is hereby ordered that the following land in Alaska described as
follows, viz:
Beginning at a point at the head of Sheep Bay, in approximate lati-
tude 600 43' N., longitude 145° 54' WV., from Greenwich ; thence due east
to the line of longitude 1456 40' W.; thence due south on the last named
line to the north shore of Orca Bay ; thence following the lines of Orca,
Simpson and Sheep bays, to the place of beginning; also the three islands
in Orca Bay, situate east and wvest of Hawkins Island, all shown upon a
diagram submitted by the War Department and represented upon U. S.
Coast and Geodetic Survey chart No. 8520, which islands are designated
on said chart as "Observation Island ", "North Island" and '"Channel
Island", be, and the same are hereby reserved for use of the War Depart-
ment for defensive purposes. HT F
THE WHITE HOUSE,
April 8, 1910.
[No. 1 186.]
