Executive ether,
Inter-Departmental Board on International Service of Ice Observation, Ice
Patrol, and Ocean Derelict Destruction.
The Secretary of the Treasury having, in cooperation with the Secre-
taries of other Departments, organized an informal committee for the
purpose of arranging for scienltific observation during the cruises of Coast
Guard cutters detailed for international ice patrol in the North Atlantic,
the members of this committee are hereby designated as members of an
inter-departmental board. This board consists of the Captain Commandant
of the Coast Guard (Treasury Department), the Hydrographer (Navy
Department), the Chief of the Weather Bureau (Department of Agriculture),
the Director of the Bureau of Standards, and the Deputy Commissioner of
the Bureau of Fisheries, (Department of Commerce).
It shall be the duty of this board to-
(a) Prepare a systematic program for the work in connection with
the international service of ice observation, ice patrol and
ocean derelictt destruction, undertaken by the United States,
especially that part which pertains to observations and related
scientific work,
(b) Direct the scientific observers, receive their reports and prepare
the same for publication,
(c) Make recommendation for such special appropriations or legis-
lation as may be necessary.
A complete record and minutes of the proceedings of this board shall be
kept, and such recommendations of the board which involve special appro-
priations or additional legislation will be submitted to the President for
appropriate executive action.
WOODROW WILSON
THE WHITE HOUSE,
20 Sept., 1916.
[No. 2458.]
