]Executive Orber.
Mr. Guy Emerson may be appointed a Special Agent of the Treasury
Department without compliance with Civil Service Rules.
This Order is issued for the reason that Mr. Emerson, who is Private
Secretary to Assistant Secretary Curtis, has been brought into close touch
with the practical administration of customs affairs through his service as
Chairman of the Customs Committee, and as a member of the Commission
on the Reorganizati on of Appraising Methods in the Customs Service.
He has also been assigned to numerous special missions for the Secretary
and Assistant Secretary, and his work in these special directions has reached
such a magnitude that it has exceeded in importance his duties as Private
Secretary, and has made it expedient to have him so situated that he can
devote all of his time to administrative questions that fall within the juris-
diction of the Special Agents' force.
Mr. Emerson is a graduate of law and is unusually well equipped men-
tally for the discharge of the duties which will fall to him as a Special
Agent.
It is the desire of the Secretary of the Treasury that, as a Special
Agent, Mr. Emerson shall be able to continue uninterruptedly the various
investigations relating to remedial and constructive measures for the benefit
of customs administration. For the reasons stated the Civil Service Coin-
mission approves this order.
WM H TAFT
THE WVHITrE HOUSE,
Oct Ober 28, 1912.
