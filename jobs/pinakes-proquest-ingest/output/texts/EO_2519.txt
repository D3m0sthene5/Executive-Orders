Executive Crber.
Pursuant to the provisions of an act of Congress approved August 1,
1914, making appropriations for the sundry civil expenses of the Govern-
menit for the fiscal year ended June 30, 1915, the port of entry at Corry,
Pennsylvania, located in Customs Collection District No. 41, of which
Cleveland, Ohio is the headquarters port, is hereby abolished.
WOODROW WILSON
THE WITE HOUSE,
23 January, 1917.
