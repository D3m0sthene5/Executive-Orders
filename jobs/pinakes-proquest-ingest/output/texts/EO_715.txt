Executive ether,
When transfers or promotions are properly applied for, and are other-
wise permissible, the Civil Service Commission is hereby authorized, in its
discretion, looking to the good of the public service only, to waive require-
ments for examination and to substitute for such examination so waived
such other tests of fitness and capacity as the Commission may decide.
I n each case where such waiver is made the reasons therefor shall be
stated at length by the Commnission and made matter of record in its
minutes.
THEODORE ROOSEVELT
THE WHIITE HOUSE,
November 22, 1907.
