Executive irber.
Mr. Edward W. Cronin may be reinstated in the Railway Mail Serv-
ice without regard to the length of his separation from the service.
Mr. Cronin was appointed to the Railway Mail Service on August 14,
1901, and retained in the service until March 29, 1902, when he was
dropped from the service. H-is reinstatement is recommended by the
Postmaster General, wvho states that his separation from the service does
not appear to have been warranted by the facts. The Civil Service Com-
mission does not concur in recommending that an Executive Order be
issued in this case.
WOODROW WILSON
THE WHITE HOUSE,
22 October, 1913.
