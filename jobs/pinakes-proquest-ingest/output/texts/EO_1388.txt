Mr. Thomas Richardson, who has been serving as postmaster at Port
Gibson, Mississippi, since July 12, 1898, may be appointed to an appro-
priate position in the competitive classified service at Washington where
his postal experience will be available, without reference to the require-
ment of examination under the civil service rules.
Investigation and report made to the satisfaction of the President
shows the fitness of Mr. Richardson, after fifteen years' service in the Post
Office Department, for the appointment at %Vashington in the competitive
classified service.
The Commission recommends the issuance of the order.
WM H TAFT
THE WHITE HOUSE,
July 31, 1911.
