Pursuant to the Act of Congress approved January 31, 1922 (42 Stat. 360),
entitled " An Act to provide for exchange of Government land for private owned
lands in the Territory of Hawaii " as extended by an Act of Congress of March 3,
1925 (43 Stat. 1115), and as further extended by Act of Congress of April 24, 1926
(Pub. No. 146, 69th Congress), the following described parcel of land is hereby set
aside for military purposes; atnd pursuant to Section 91 of the Act of Congress
approved April 30, 1900 (31 Scat. 141), entitled " An Act to provide a government
for the Territory of Hawaii ", as amended by Section 7 of the Act of Congress of
May 27, 1910 (36 Stat. 443), the said parcel is hereby, restored to its previous
status under the control of the Government of the Territory of Hawaii, the said
parcel being described as follows:
The parcel of land situate in the City and County of Honolulu
consisting of portions of Esplanade Lots Nos. 33 and 42 and described
as follows:
BEGINNING at the North corner of said parcel, and the West
corner of the present Fumigating House Lot, and on the Southeast
side of Kekuanaoa Street said point of beginning being by true azi-
muth and distance 590 35' 119.03 feet from the South corner of Keku-
anaoa and Halekauwila Streets and the coordinates of said point of
beginning being 115.56 feet North and 217.46 West of a Government
Survey Monument near the West corner of Kilauea and Halekauwila
Streets, and on an offset of 10.0 feet from the Southwest side of Halek-
auwila Street, and on offset of 9.75 feet from the Northwest side of
Kilauea Street, as shown on Territorial Governnment Survey Registered
Map No 1867, and running thence by true Azimuths:
1. 3290 35' 116.22 feet along the present Fumigating House Lot;
2. 520 50' 31.19 feet along the Northwest side of Bishop Street
Extension;
3. 149° 35' 119.89 feet along Lots 41 and 34, along remaining por-
tion of U. S. Military Reservation;
4. 2390 35' 30.97 feet along Southeast side of Kekuanaoa Street to
the point of beginning, and containing an area
of 3,656 square feet.
CALVIN COOLIDGE
THE WHITE HOUSE,
April 30, 1927.
