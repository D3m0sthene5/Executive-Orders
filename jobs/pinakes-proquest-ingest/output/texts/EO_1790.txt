Executive Orber.
Section 6 of Civil Service Rule XI, prohibiting the transfer or pro-
motion of messenger boys to positions in the apportioned service, is
hereby waived so as to permit of the promotion of Thomas R. Stewart to
the apportioned position of assistant messenger in the Office of the Secre-
tary of Commerce.
This Order is issued upon recommendation of the Secretary of Com-
merce and is based upon M'r. Stewart's very high record for efficiency
during his service as messenger boy in the Department of Commerce
since his appointment on January 24, 1910.
WOODROW WILSON
THE WHITE HOUSE,
June 27, .1913.
