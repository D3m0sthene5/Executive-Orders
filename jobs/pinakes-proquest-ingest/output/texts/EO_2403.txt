Section 7, Civil Service Rule X, is hereby amended by striking there-
from the following words:
"money-order assorter and skilled laborer acting as
money-order assorter in the Office of the Auditor
for the Post Office Department ".
Section 7 of Rule X permits the transfer of operatives and other em-
ployees in the Bureau of Engraving and Printing to certain positions in
other parts of the Department, among them those mentioned above, with-
out examination and without charge to the apportionment, for the pet-
formance of certain work. As the audit of money-orders is now done by
labor-saving devices, the part of the rule quoted is no longer in effect.
