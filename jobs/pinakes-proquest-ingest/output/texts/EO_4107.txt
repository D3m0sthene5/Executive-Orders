Executive Order
ALASKAN TIMBER RESERVE NO. 1
REDUCED
It is hereby ordered that Executive Orders No. 2217, dated June 22, 1915, No.
2226, dated July 31, 1915, and No. 2589, dated April 11, 1917, reserving the timber
on certain lands in Alaska for use of the United States in connection with the
location, construction and operation of Government railroad or railroads in Alaska,
be, and they are hereby, revoked, in so far as they affect the timber on the following
described areas:
Mat anuska Valley. A tract of land five miles wide on each side of the right of
way of the Government railroad, lbeginning at the point of intersection with the
Knik River, thence along the line of said railroad right of way to the end of the
branch line of railroad leading into the Matanuska coal field;
Susitna Valley. A tract of land five miles wide on each side of the riff(t of way
of the Government railroad, beginning at the junction of the main ling, with the
Matanuska branch line, and extending along and through the Susitna Valley to
Broad Pass;
Nenana River and Goldstream Cek. A tract of land five miles wide on each
side of the right of way of the Government railroad, beginning at Broad Pass and
adjoining and being at continuation of the Susitna Valley portion of the reserve
heretofore established, thence along the line of said railroad right of way down the
.Nenana River to its mouth; thence across the divide between the mouth of Nenana
River and Goldstream Creek, thence up Goldstream Creek and along the line of
said railroad right of way to Fairbanks;
Part Seward to Kinile River Area. Beginning at Mile Post 89 on The Alaska
Railroad and extending along the right of way of said railroad to the left limit of
Knik River.
