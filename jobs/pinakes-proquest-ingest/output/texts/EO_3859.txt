Navy Department Buildings, may, (luring the remainder of the Fiscal Year
1923 and during the Fiscal Year 1924, be filled by the Superintendent without

retired enlisted meni from the Army, Navy, or Marine Corps, or retired firemen
or policemen of the District of Columbia Government who have been retired
therefrom under honorable conditions.
The excessive turnover in the guard force, due to inadequate salaries under
existing conditions, and the desirability of assigning to the temporary frame
buildings as guards men who have had training and experience in the Fire
Department indicate the desirability of the exception proposed. It is antici-
