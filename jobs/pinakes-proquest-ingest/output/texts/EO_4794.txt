Mrs. Anna Polk Boush may be reinstated as a clerk in the Treasury Department
without regard to the length of time she has been separated from the service. This
order is recommended by the Secretary of the Treasury who states that in view of
her satisfactory record under a previous appointment, her service extending from
1891 to 1905, and the need at this time of experienced persons in the Office of the
Supervising Architect, where she was formerly employed, her appointment will be
in the interest of the service. The Civil Service Commission is unable to concur.
CALVIN COOLIDGE
THE WHITE HousE,
January 13, 1928.
