X09
Executive Other.
In accordance with the provisions of Sections 2249, 2250 and 2252 of
the Revised Statutes of the United States, and by virtue of the authority
therein given, it is hereby ordered that:
The United States land office now located at Olympia be discon-
tinued, and the business and archives of said office transferred to, and
made part of, the United States land office at Seattle, in the State of
Washington. WOODROW WILSON
THlE WHITE HOUSE,
28 November, 1914.
