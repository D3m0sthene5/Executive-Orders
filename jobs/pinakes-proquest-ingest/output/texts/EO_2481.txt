Schedule B of the civil service rules, enumerating classified positions
which may be filled upon noncompetitive examination, is hereby amended
by the addition of the following paragraph to Subdivision 1V:
2. Positions of military storekeeper in the Signal Service at
Large when filled by retired noncommissioned offic'ra of the Signal
Corps.
Military storekeepers at Aviation Stations and Depots handle delicate
technical apparatus and carry a large property accountability, and are
therefore required to have a thorough practical knowledge of the equip-
ment used by the Signal Corps and of the methods and regulations used
in the accounting for the same. The War Department states that it
knows of no persons possessing the qualifications necessary for this work
except retired noncommissioned officers of the Signal Corps, who, through
long service in the Corps, have become fully acquainted with its technical
apparatus.
As an open competitive examination which would limit competition
to noncommissioned officers of the Signal Corps coula not properly be
held under the civil service act and rules, the Civil Service Comsioi
view of the statement as to the necessary qualifications, joins the War
Department in recommending the issuance of this order.
WOODROW WILSON
THIE WHITE HOUSE,
