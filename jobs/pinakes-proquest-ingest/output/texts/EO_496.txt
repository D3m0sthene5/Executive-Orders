Executive Order.
By the Act of April 5, 1906, all fees and charges at Consulates of the
United States have been made official. The appended tariff of fees to be
charged by Consular officers of the United States is accordingly prescribed.
All charges must be in strict accordance with this tariff, and must be col-
lected in United States gold or its equivalent. The tariff shall become
effective on November x, 19o6.
THEODORE ROOSEVELT

WASHINGTON, D. C.

TARIFF OF AMERICAN CONSULAR FEES.
Tariff of fees prescribed by order of the President to be charged by consular
officers of the United States. All consular charges must be in strict accordance
with this tariff, and be collected in gold or its equivalent. No fee or compensation
will be collected for any service not covered by this tariff.
(The fees in this Tariff are not prescribed for American vessels and seamen,
because they are exempted by law from the payment of consular fees. Consular
agents will make the fees in this tariff the basis of collection from the Treasury for
services to such vessels, Foreign-built vessels, unregistered, owned by American
citizens, are not exempt from the payment of the fees prescribed herein.)
