Mrs. Cornelia H. Campbell may be appointed to a position as skilled laborer
in the classified service without reference to the civil service rules.
Mrs. Campbell is the widow of Edward B. Campbell, formerly an employee
in the Government Printing Office, who died November 3, 1917, as the result
of an accident which occurred in the discharge of his duties in that office on
February 19, 1917.
This order is issued upon the recommendation of the Public Printer, who
states that he is of the opinion that her entry into the classified service would be
in the interest of the service.
WOODROW WILSON
