Executive Order.
Under authority of the act of Congress approved June 25, 1910 (36 Stat.,
847), as amended by the act of August 24, 1912 (37 Stat., :{9) the following
described lands in the State of Florida are hereby temnporarily withdrawn, sub-
ject to conditions of said acts, in aid of pending legislation (S. 3641), and such
withdrawal shall remain in effect until revoked by the President or act of
Congress:
All of the unsurveyed sections 1 6 within the exterior limits of the area
patented to the State of Florida April 23, 1903, under the provisions of the act
of September 28, 185o (9 St t., 519), embracing the so-called Everglades.
