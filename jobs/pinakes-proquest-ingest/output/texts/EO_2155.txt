Executive ether,
Pursuant to the authority contained in Section 179 of the Revised
Statutes, I hereby authorize and direct Edward E. Pratt, Chief of the
Bureau of Foreign and Domestic Commerce, to perform the duties of the
Secretary of Commerce during the absence of the Secretary, the Assist-
ant Secretary, and the Solicitor of the Department of Commerce.
WOODROW WILSON
THE WHITE HOUSE,
30 March, 1915.
[No. 2155.]
