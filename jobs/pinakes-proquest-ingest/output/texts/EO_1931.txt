Executive Orber.
It is hereby ordered that those employees of the several Executive
Departments and Independent Government Establishments in the District
of Columbia who are members of the organizations which are to partici-
pate in the parade in connection with the ceremony of unveiling the statue
to the memory of Commodore John Barry on Saturday, May 1,6, 1914,
may be excused from their official duties at I2 o'clock noon, on said date,
for that purpose.
WTOODROW WILSON
THE WHITE HOUSE,
May 5, 1914.
