Mrs. Georgianna Webb may be appointed to a clerical position in the Customs
Field Service, Treasury Department, without regard to the civil service rules.
This Order is recommended by the Secretary of the Treasury who states that
Mrs. Webb is the widow of Edward B. Webb, customs agent, who was shot and
killed in the performance of his duty by a smuggler on October 7, 1926, leaving
Mrs. Webb with very small means and with three children to support, the oldest
ten years of age.
The Civil Service Commission is unable to concur.
For charity.
CALVIN COOLIDGE
THE WHITE HOUSE,
November 13, 1926.
