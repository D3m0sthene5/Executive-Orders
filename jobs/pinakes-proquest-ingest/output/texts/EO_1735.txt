]Executive Orber
Thomas Butler of the District of Columbia may be appointed without
examination to the position of Laborer in the classified civil service.
Mr. Butler was appointed four years ago as a Messenger in the
immediate office of the Postmaster General to one of the several positions
in each Cabinet officer's department that are excepted from the usual civil
service requirements. The Postmaster General states that he has proved
to be efficient ; that he quickly learned the duties of his position and the
run of the Department, and that he is conscientious and reliable. The
Postmaster General further states that on practical grounds it would be
desirable for the Department to take advantage of Mr. Butler's four years'
training and continue him in the service. The Civil Service Commission
does not concur in recommending this Order.
WM H TAFT
THE WHITE HousE,
March 3, 1913.
