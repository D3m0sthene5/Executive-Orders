Executive Drber
It is hereby ordered that the boundaries of the reservation for the natives
of Alaska residing at the village of Klukwan, as created by Executive Order
No. 1764, dated April 21i, 1913, and described therein by metes and bounds,
be conformed to the legal subdivisions of the public land surveys when
extended from the Copper River Base and Meridian so as to cover the area
involved, as follows:
All those portions of the public lands on the left bank of Chilkat River
included in Sec. 32, S'2 Sec. 33, SXXT 4 Sec. 34 T. 28 S., R. 56 E; NW 14 Sec. 5,
NE'14 Sec. 6, T. 29 S., R. 57 E., Copper River Base and Meridian, Alaska, and
the same is hereby reserved, subject to any vested rights and the right of way
of a U. S. wagon road, for the use of the natives of Alaska residing now or
hereafter at said village of Klukwan.
WARREN G HARDING
THE WHITE HOUSE,
May 15, 1922.
[No. 36730.]
