Executive Order.

Indian Meridian; and all of Sections i, 11, 12, 13, and the east half
of Section 2, in Township 3 North, Range 15 West, Indian Meridian.
Area about 16,420 acres.
IT IS FURTHER ORDERED that as to so much of the lands as is comn-
prised within the said Game Preserve, the reservation for a Gamne Preserve
shall be the dominant one, and the use of the same for military purposes

reservation.
