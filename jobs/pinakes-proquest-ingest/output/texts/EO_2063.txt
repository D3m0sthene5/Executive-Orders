Executive ther
Mrs. Adele R. Redick may be appointed to a competitive classified
position in the Bureau of the Census without reference to the civil service
rules.
This order is issued upon the recommendation of the Director of the
Census on account of the excellent record of Mrs. Redick while employed
as an unclassified special agent from November xo, 1913, to June 30, 1914.
The Civil Service Commission does not concur in recommending the
order.
WOODROW WILSON
THE WHITE HOUSE,
October 19, 191i4.
[No. 2063.]
