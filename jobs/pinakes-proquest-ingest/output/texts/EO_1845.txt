Executive order.
Mrs. Mary R. Geddings may be appointed to a position in the classi-
fied service without reference to the requirement of examination under
the Civil Service Rules.
Mrs. Geddings is the widowv of Surgeon Henry D. Geddings, of the
Public Health Service, who was a commissioned medical officer in that
service for about twenty-five years, and who rendered distinguished service
in matters relating tor sanitation and public health. He also rendered
valuable service in several epidemics of yellow fever at a time when few
medical men were immune to that disease and its cause but little known.
The Secretary of the Treasury states that it would be a fitting testi-
monial to the services of Surgeon Geddings to waive the Civil Service
Rules to permit the appointment of Mrs. Geddings in the classified service.
The Civil Service Commission does not recommend that an Executive
Order be issued in this case.
WOODROW WILSON
THE WHITE HOUSE,
22 October, r913.
