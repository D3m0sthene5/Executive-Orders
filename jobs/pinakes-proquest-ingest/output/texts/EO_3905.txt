Executive Orber.
I,: is HEREBY ORDERED that the records, files, and property of the agency
of the United States known as Federal Fuel Distributor, and created by Act of
Congress (public No. 348, 67th Congress) approved September 22, 1922, be
transferred to the Department of Commerce, and the Secretary of Commerce
is hereby designated as custodian to receive said records, files, and property.
IT is FURTHER ORDERED that the Secretary of Commerce, the Acting
Secretary of Commerce, or the Assistant Secretary of Commerce, each or either
of them, shall approve for settlement from the appropriation" Federal Fuel
Distribution" any outstanding accounts that may be found to be due and
payable from said appropriation after the expiration of existence of the Federal
Fuel Distributor's organization at the close of September 2 n, n1923.
CALVIN COOLIDGE
THE WHITE HousE,
