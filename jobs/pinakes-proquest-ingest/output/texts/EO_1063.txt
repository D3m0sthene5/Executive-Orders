Executive Order.
Mr. Archibald S. Pinkett, of Pennsylvania, may be appointed to a
clerkship in the Department of State without examination under the Civil
Service Acts and rules. This order is made at the request and upon the
recommendation of the Secretary of State who has knowledge of Mr.
Pinkett's fitness from his clerical service under the Secretary while he wvasN
a Senator in Congress.
WM H TAFT
