Mr. Fred Roege may be reinstated as assistant postmaster in the post.
office at Pensacola, Florida, without regard to the length of time that he
has been separated from the service.
Mr. Roege was appointed to the classified service on August 28, 1900o,
and served continuously till July 13, 19[11, when he resigned. He held
the position of clerk in the Treasury Department, money order clerk in
the Washington post office, a'nd post office inspector. He made applica-
tioni fur reiustatemenl within the year of his eligibility but was misinformed
to the effect that his military service and honorable discharge therefrom
rendered him eligible for reinstatement at any time under the rules. In
view of the fact that he wvas misinformed as to his rights of reinstatement
the Commission concurs in the recommendation of the Postmaster General,
the First Assistant Postmaster General and the postmaster of Pensacola
that he be reinstated by Executive order.
