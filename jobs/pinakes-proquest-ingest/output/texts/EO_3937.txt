Executive Orber.
Mr. Frank E. McMillin, who served as railway mail clerk, post-office
inspector, assistant postmaster at Boston, Massachusetts, and Chief Post-office
Inspector from September 5, 1890, to July 31, 1 909, when he resigned from the
service, may be reinstated to the position of post-office inspector.
This order was issued upon the recommendation of the Postmaster General
and was concurred in by the Civil Service Commission.
CALVIN COOLIDGE
THE WHITE HOUSE,
December 19, 1923.
