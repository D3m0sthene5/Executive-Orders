Miss Sadie A. Killeen may be appointed a clerk in the classified serv-
ice of the Treasury Department without compliance with the civil service
rules.
Miss Killeen has served under temporary employment for more than
nine months, having been appointed without examination because of her
expert knowledge of the operation of a machine which was to be tested to
determine its applicability to the posting of disbursing officers' accounts in
the Office of the Treasurer of the United States. The plan having proved
satisfactory, Miss Killeen was retained to demonstrate and assist in intro-
ducing it. Her services have been of great value, and the installation of
the machines has resulted in a large saving in expenses. The Civil Service
Commission therefore concurs with the Secretary of the Treasury in rec-
ommending this order.
WOODROW WILSON
THE WHITE HousE,
19 February, 1915.
