Executive order
Beatty Townsite, Nevada
It is hereby ordered that Executive order of March 27, 1926, withdrawing
from disposition all undisposed of lots in the townsite of Beatty, Nevada, pending
classification and legislation, be and the same is hereby revoked, the purpose of
the withdrawal having been accomplished. The lots may now be sold under Sees.
2382 to 2386, U. S. Revised Statutes.
