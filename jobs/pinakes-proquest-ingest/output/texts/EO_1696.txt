Executive Orber.
Mr. Will S. Cox, who was removed on March i 1, 1907, from the
position of railway mail clerk, may be reinstated without regard to the fact
that he has been separated more than one year.
Mr. Cox was removed following an investigation of certain charges
preferred by him against his chief clerk; a subsequent investigation of the
chief clerk resulted in his arrest and trial for misconduct. The Depart-
ment is of the opinion that in view of the facts, Mr. Cox should be
reinstated, and the Civil Service Commission joins with the Department
in the recommendation for his reinstatement by Executive order.
