Executive Order.
Mr. Harry C. Daniels, now employed as a clerk in the Solicitor's Office, Depart-
meat of Agriculture, may be transferred and appointed to a clerkship in the
Navy Department at $1,500 per annum, without reference to the civil service
rules.
This order is issued upon the recommendation of the Secretary of the Navy
and the Chief of Naval Operations, who state that it is proposed to assign
Mr. Daniels to the position of Secretary to the Chief of Naval Operations, the
duties of which are of a confidential character and should be performed by a
trained man of unquestioned character and reputation, and that Mr. Daniels
has been selected for the place after careful consideration, on account of his
ability and the fact that he has had active service in the Navy as an enlisted man,
assigrr I to clerical work, which training particularly fits him for the duties
he vl be called upon to perform.
WOODROW WILSON
THE WHITE HOUSE,
31 July, 1917.
