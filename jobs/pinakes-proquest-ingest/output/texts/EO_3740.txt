M N A A -,Executive Order of March 1, 1915 (No. 2148), temporarily withdrawing
from settlement, location, sale, or entry under the Act of June 25, 1910 (36 Stat.,
847), as amended, on the recommendation of the Secretary of Agriculture,
Lots 1, 2, S aNEK and SE I Sec. 6, T. 9 N., R. 1i XV., M. P. M., containing 320

with the administration of the Deerlodge National Forest, Montana, is hereby
revoked.
It is hereby ordered pursuant to Public Resolution No. 29, approved
February 14, 1920, as amended by Public Resolution No. 36, approved January
21, 1922, tiU.L the public lands in the excluded area, subject to valid rights,
shall be opened only to entry under the homestead and desert land laws by
qualified ex-service men of the war with Germany, under the terms and conditions
of said resolutions and the regulations issued thereunder, for a period of ninety-
one days beginning with the sixty-third day from and after the date of this
order, and thereafter to appropriation under any public land law applicable
thereto by the general public.
Subsequent to the date hereof and prior to the date of restoration to general
disposition, as provided herein, no rights may be acquired to the released land
by settlement in advance of entry or otherwise except strictly in accordance
herewith.
