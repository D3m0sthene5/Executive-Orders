Mrs. Mary IE. Donnelly may be appointed to a clerkship in the classified civil
service without regard to the requirements of the civil service rules.
Mrs. Donnelly is the widow of Major Walter M. Donnelly who rendered
conspicuous service during the World War, which service so impaired his health
that he survived but a few years.
The Secretary of War recommends this order. The Civil Service Commission
is unable to concur.
For charity.
CALVIN COOL IDGE
THE WHITE HOUSE,
March 2, 1928.
