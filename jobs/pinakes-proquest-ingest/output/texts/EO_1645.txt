By virtue of the authority vested in me by Section 3 141 of the
Revised Statutes of the United States, it is hereby ordered that the
County of Miami, now a part of the Tenth Internal Revenue Collection
District of Ohio, be transferred to and made a part of the First Internal
Revenue Collection District of Ohio.
This order to take effect on the 1 st day of Januiary, 1913.
WVM H TAFT
THE: WHITE HOUSE,
November 22, 1912.
[No. 1645.]
