In pursuance of the authority conferred upon the President of the
United States by Section 2 of the Act of August i8, 191 +, entitled "An
Act to provide for the admission of foreign-built ships to American registry
for the foreign trade, and for other purposes, it is hereby ordered:
That the provisions of section 4488 of the Revised Statutes, as amended
by section 14 of the Act of March 4, 1915, entitled "An Act to promote
the welfare of American seamen in the merchant marine of the United
States; to abolish arrest and imprisonment as a penalty for desertion and
to secure the abrogation of treaty provisions in relation thereto ; and to
promote safety at sea," are hereby suspended for a period ending Septem-
ber 3d, 1916.
WOODROW WILSON
THE WHITE HOUSE,
2 November, 1915.
[-No. 2269.]
