[EXECUTIVE ORDER.]
WASHINGTON, D. C.
In order that there shall be uniformity in the manner of designating
naval vessels, it is hereby directed that the official designation of vessels of
war and other vessels of the Navy of the United States, shall be the name
of such vessel preceded by the words, United States Ship, or the letters,
U. S. S., and by no other words or letters.
THEODORE ROOSEVELT
THE WHITE HOUSE, January. 8, 1907.
