The year limit upon eligibility for reinstatement is hereby waived to per*
mit the reinstatement of Mr. Bartlett S. Taylor in the Postal Service at Sharon,
Pennsylvania.
Mr. Taylor entered the service as a substitute carrier July i, 1906, at
Sharon, Pennsylvania, and was advanced to a regular position March x, 19o7;
he was promoted through the various grades until he was receiving the maxi-
muns salary for carriers of $i~oo when he resigned October 25, 1919, from the
service in the post office at Sharon, giving as his reason his inability to support,
his family, which consists of a wife and eight children, on the salary he was
receiving, dume to the increased cost of living, and illness in his family.
The Postmaster General states that from the facts presented, it would
seem that an Executive order reinstating Mr. Taylor would be warranted,
and so recommends. The Civil Service Commission believing that the service
would be benefited by the reinstatement of Mr. Taylor joins the Postmaster
General in recommending the order.
