SAN JUAN NATIONAL FOREST
COLORADO
It is hereby ordered that on and after July , 1908, the land within the
boundary shown on the attached diagram, heretofore set apart, reserved,
and proclaimed as parts of the San Juan and Montezuma National Forests,
shall be known as the San Juan National Forest. The remaining portion
of the San Juan National Forest shall continue reserved and constitute a
part of the Rio Grande National Forest. It is not intended by this order
to release any land from reservation or to reserve any land not heretofore
embraced in a National Forest.
THEODORE ROOSEVELT
THE WVHITE HOUSE.
July r , 1908.
