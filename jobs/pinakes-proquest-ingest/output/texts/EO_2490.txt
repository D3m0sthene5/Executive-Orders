Executive Orber
WHITETAIL ADMINISTRATIVE SITE
(Near Deerlodge National Forest)
MONTANA
On the recommendation of the Secretary of Agriculture, it is hereby
ordered that the withdrawal of the tract of hand containing 320 acres,
described as the W! of Sec. 17, T. 4 N., R. 4 W., M. P. M., reserved as a
ranger station for use by the Forest Service in connection with the admin-
istration of the Deerlodge National Forest, Montana, made by Executive
Order (No. 1552) of June 19, 1912, under the authority of the Act of Congress
approved June 25, 1910 (36 Stat., 847), be revoked.
WOODROW WILSON
THE WHITE HousE,
21 November, 1916.
