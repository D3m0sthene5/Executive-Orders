The Acting Secretary of War states that the waiving of the civil
service rules to authorize the appointment of Mr. Brockensh ire is requested
by the Acting Quartermaster General of theme Army in viewv of his long
service in the Army as chief musician and his varied experience with army
bands, which especially qualify him for the position. The Acting Quar-
termaster General reports that he is a thorough musician, able to playalmost any instrument in army bands, and is just the type of inspector
needed by the Depot Quartermaster at Philadelphia, under whom he hasbeen employed at different times during the past two years rendering
entirely satisfactory service.
Mr. Brockenshire wvas in the military service as musician for nearly
thirty years, voluntarily retiring on June 1, 1914, with a "most excellent
character." During his service, with the exception of about one year, he
was with the 7th Cavalry. The Acting Adjutant of the 7th Cavalry states
that he had been held in the highest esteem by every officer and enlisted
man with whom he had come in contact officially and personally and wvasuniversally admired and respected both as a soldier and as a man becauseof his constant devotion to duty and courteous treatment of his associates.
Major General J. F. Bell, wvho knew him about thirty years, states that heis one of the best men he had ever known, being entirely sober, reliable,
upright, and honorable, always courteous and most obliging because he is
a gentleman at heart ; and further that he is a disciplinarian with a special
faculty for getting along with and managing bands. Almost if not equally
strong testimonials were given by Inspector General Garlington, Brigadier
General Charles Morton, Brigadier General T. N. Baldwin, Retired, and
numerous officers of the 7th Cavalry.
Because of Mr. Brockenshire's unusual service, splendid character, and
musical ability, so strongly stated b~y army officers who have known him
intimately,.and his demonstrated qualifications for the position of inspector
of musical instruments, the Civil Service Commission recommends this
order.
