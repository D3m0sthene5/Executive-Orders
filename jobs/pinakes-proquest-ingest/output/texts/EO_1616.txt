George E. Long may be promoted from the position of messenger to
a clerkship of the lowest class in the Bureau of Insular Affairs of the War
Department, without examination.
This order, which supersedes the Executive order of September 12,
19 12, is made upon the recommendation of the Chief of the Bureau of
Insular Affairs, on account of Mr. Long's long service and unusual record,
and is approved by the War Department and the Civil Service Commission.
THE WRITE HOUSE,
September 30, 1912.
[No. 1616.]
Wm H TAFT
