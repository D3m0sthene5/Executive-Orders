Schedule A, Subdivision V, paragraph ;, of the Civil Service Rules
is hereby amended by the addition at the end thereof of the following
words:
"and the Island of Samoa
As amended the paragraph will read as follows and will except from
the requirement of examination
"All positions in the Island of Guam and in the Island of
Samoa
This action is recommended by the Civil Service Commission in view
of the remoteness of the location, of the fact that there are understood to
be only three classified position; under the Navy Department in Samoa,
and that efforts initiated on October 31, 1910, have failed to fill a vacancy
in a position requiring the qualifications of stenographer and typewriter
with a knowledge of law.
WM H TAFT
THE WHITE HOUSE,
February 21, 1911.
