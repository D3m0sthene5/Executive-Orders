Executive ether.
Mrs. Emily C. P. Shepperd may be appointed a Clerk in the Treasury
Department without compliance with the Civil Service rules.
This order is issued in viewv of the long and efficient services in the
Treasury Department of Mrs. Shepperd's husband who is now confined in
St. Elizabeth's Asylum for the Insane.
WOODROW WILSON
THE WHITE HOUSE,
2-1 October, 1913.
