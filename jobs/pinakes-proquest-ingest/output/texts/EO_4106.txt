Executive Order
ALASKA ROAD COMMISSION RESERVE
Pursuant to authority conferred upon me by the act of Congress approved
June 25, 1910 (36 Stat., 847), as amended by the act of Congress of August 24,
1912 (37 Stat., 497), entitled "An Act to authorize the President of the United
States to make withdrawals of public lands in certain cases," it is hereby ordered
that the following described lands be, and the same are hereby, withdrawn from
settlement, location, sale or entry and reserved for the use of the Alaska Road
Commission in the construction and maintenance of military and post roads, bridges
and trails in the vicinity of Kanatak, Portage Bay, Alaska:
(1) A tract of land five hundred feet wide extending from tidewater on the
south to the contour line at an elevation of fifty feet above sea level on the
north and lying east of and contiguous to the eastern boundary line of U. S.
Survey No. 1419, Kanatak, Portage Bay, Alaska;
(2) A tract of land three hundred feet square the southern boundary of which
begins at Cor. No. 2 of U. S. Survey No. 1449 Kanatak, Portage Bay, Alaska,
and extends eastward a distance of three hundred feet along the northern
boundary line of said survey.
CALVIN COOL IDGE
THE WHITE HOUSE,
November 24, 1924.
