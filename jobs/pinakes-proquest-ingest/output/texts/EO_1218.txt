Schedule A, Section VII, Civil Service Rules, relating to the Depart-
ment of the Interior, is hereby amended by the addition of a paragraph,
as follows:
24. One specialist in higher education in the Bureau of Education.
The exception is recommended by the Secretary of the Interior on
the ground that it will be difficult, if not impossible, to secure a satisfac-
tory person through a competitive examination, and the recommendation
is concurred in by the Civil Service Commission:
WM H TAFT
THE WHITE HOUSE,
July ist, 1910.
