The United States Civil Service Commission is directed to render
such assistance as may be practicable to the Porto Rican Civil Service
Commission, created under the insular legislative act of March 14, 1907,
and officers and employees in the Federal service in Porto Rico shall facili-
tate the holding of examinations and other work of the Porto Rican Civil
Service Commission and render such other assistance as may be practicable.
The United States Civil Service Commission, may, in its discretion, author-
ize the transfer of employees from the civil service of Porto Rico to that
of the United States, subject to the limitations respecting transfer within
the Federal civil service.
THEODORE ROOSEVELT
TL WHITE HOUSE,
