AAK.It is hereby ordered that the following described lands on the north shore of
S Prince William Sound in Alaska withdrawn from disposal and reserved by Executive
Order No. 1987, dated July 9, 1914, for use of the Bureau of Education and the
natives of Alaska of indigenous race under the care of said bureau, be, and the same
are hereby restored to the public domain, viz :
Gravrina Reserve :
Beginning at a point near the SW. corner of Parsha's house on the north
shore of Port Gravina, thence running north five miles to a point; thence east
five miles to a point; thence south five miles to a point; thence west five miles
0.91 chs. to point of beginning, estimated to contain approximately 11,000
acres of Land, in Lat. 600 46' N., Long. 1460 04' W.
Fish Bay Reserve :
Beginning at the most southern point of the outermost island at the south-
west entrance of ish Bay, thence running one mile westerly to a point on the
main shore; thence N. 450 E. to a point due north of the most northern point
of Fish Bay; thence east two miles to a point; thence south to a point on the
shore of Port Fidalgo; thence westerly along the shore to a point due east of
the point of beginning; thence to the point of beginning; estimated to contain
5500 acres of land, in Lat. 60° 50' N., Long. 1460 24' W.
Long Bay Reserve :
Beginning at the southeast point of the largest island in Long Bay; thence
running southwesterly to the nearest point on the main shore; thence west 11
miles to a point; thence north three miles to a point; thence east to a point on
the west shore of Long Bay; thence in a straight line to the point of beginning;
estimated to contain 3,000 acres, in Lat. 61° 00' N., Long. 1470 18' W.
