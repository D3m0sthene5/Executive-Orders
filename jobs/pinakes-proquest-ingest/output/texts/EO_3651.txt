Under authority of the Act of Congress approved June 25, 1910 (36 Stat.,
847), as amended by the Act of August 24, 1912 (37 Stat., 497), the following
described lands in the State of California are hereby temporarily' withdrawn,
subject to the conditions of said Acts, until March 5, 1923, in aid of pending
legislation embodied in the Bill H. R. 10554, 67th Congress:
In T. 37 N., R. ii E., M. D. M., NE34 Sli4 Sec. 4.
If said legislation be not enacted before the adjournment of the last session
of the Sixty-seventh Congress, and no other direction is given regarding the
disposition of such lands,' they will, on March 5, 1923, become subject to disposal
under any law then applicable thereto, without further order.
WARREN G HARDING
THE WHITE HOUSE,
March 22, 1922.
