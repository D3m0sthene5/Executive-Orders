Executive 'Orber.
It is hereby ordered that employees of the executive departments and
independent government establishments in the District of Columbia who
desire to attend the memorial services in honor of the sailors who lost
their lives in the destruction of the battleship MAINE in Havana harbor
on February 15, 1898, may be excused from duty in their respective offices
at two o'clock on the afternoon of March 23, 1912.
THE WHITE HOUSE,
March 15, 1912.
Wm H TAFT
