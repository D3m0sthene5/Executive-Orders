Executive Orber.
Miss Hattie M. Miller, a copyist at $ 720 per annum in the Patent
Office, may be promoted to $900 per annum, without examination.
This order is issued upon the recommendation of the Secretary of the
Interior and the Commissioner of Patents, w~ho state that Miss Miller has
been employed in the Patent Office at various times since 1889, her last
service beginning July i, 1909.
The Civil Service Commission does not concur in the recornmenda-
tion.WOODROW WILSON
