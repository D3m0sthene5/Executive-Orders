Executive Orber
IDAHO NATIONAL FOREST
IDAHO
It is hereby ordered that on and after July I, 1908, the land within the
boundary shown on the attached diagram, heretofore set apart, reserved,
and proclaimed as a part of the Payette National Forest, shall be known
as the Idaho National Forest. The remaining portion of the Payette
National Forest shall continue reserved and constitute a part of the
Payette National Forest. It is not intended by this order to release any
land from reservation or to reserve any land not heretofore embraced in
a National Forest.
THEODORE ROOSEVELT
