Mr. J. D. Moakley may be reinstated to a position in the classified
service without reference to the length of his separation from the service.
Mr. Yoakley was appointed to a position in the Post Office service in
1894 through the classified civil service, having passed the prescribed ex-
amination. He remained in this position for a period of about five years,
during which time it is stated he rendered faithful, efficient, and loyal
service. His reinstatement is requested by the Postmaster General in view
of the method by which Mr. Moakley was separated from the service.
The Postmaster General states that he is convinced that Mr. XMoakley is
well equipped for a position in the Post Office Department.
The Civil Service Commission does not concur in the request of the
Postmaster General.
WOODROW WILSON
THE WHITE HOUSE,
28 February, 1916.
