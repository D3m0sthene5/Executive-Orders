Executive Orbcr.
Mr. John Garcin may be transferred from the position of clerk at
$2000 per annum, Customs Service, New Orleans, Louisiana, to the
position of clerk at $1200o per annum, Forest Service, Flagstaff, Arizona,
without reference to the requirement of six months' actual service next
preceding transfer.
Mr. Garcin was appointed through examination in 1899 and was
promoted several times, and wvas a highly efficient employee. On Septem-
ber 24, 1911, he was granted leave of absence without pay and went to
Santa Fe, Newv Mexico, for treatment of a throat affection. His physician
reported that his condition was improving under treatment and that he
should remain in the same climate for at least six months more. The
Secretary of Agriculture requested approval of the transfer, believing that it
*would be in the interest of the service, and the Civil Service Commission
concurs in recommending the order.
