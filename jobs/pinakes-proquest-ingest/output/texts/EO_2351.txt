Mr. Herbert D. Dean may be reinstated as a fish culturist in theBureau of Fisheries, Department of Commerce, without regard to thelength of time he has been separated from the service.
Mr. Dean entered the service of the Bureau of Fisheries in a sub-ordinate capacity in 1884 and in 1891, after four promotions, became asuperintendent of station, which position, with the exception of fivemonths in 1895, he held until March i i, 191 2, when at his own requesthis services were discontinued without prejudice. Mr. Dean's record wassatisfactory and he was generally recognized as an efficient fish culturist
and superintendent.
This order is issued upon recommendation~ of the Secretary of Com-merce, who is of the opinion that Mr. Dean's reentrance into the Fisheriesservice will be beneficial thereto, and who therefore believes that an ex-ceptioni to the one year limitation in cases of separation from the serviceimposed by civil service Rule IX, is justified and advisable.
The Civil Service Commission does not concur in the recommendation.
