Executive Ober.
Hon. Charles B. Elliott, Secretary of Commerce and Police of the
Philippine Islands, Lieut.-Colonel George P. S criven, U. S. Army, and
Commander Chester M. Knepper, U. S. Navy, are hereby appointed a
board to study and make recommendations relating to the construction,
operation, maintenance, and management of a system of wireless telegraphy
for the Philippine Islands, and as to the relations of the Army, the Navy,
the government of the Philippines, and the commercial community to it.
The report of the board will be forwarded to the Secretary of War for
the information of a hoard which will be appointed at Washington to
determine finally the questions involved. WM H TAFT
THE WHITE HousE,
Apftril 30, 1910.
