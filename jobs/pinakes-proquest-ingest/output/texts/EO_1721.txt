Mr. Matthew A. H-enson may be appointed to any suitable position
in the classified service without compliance with the Civil Service Rules.
Mr. Henson was with Admiral Peary for twenty-one years, on one
trip remaining four years in the arctic regions after the other members of
the expedition had returned home on a relief ship. He was the only man
who accompanied Admiral Peary at the Pole. Speaking of Henson and
another companion, Admiral Peary says :
No man could have been more fortunate than I in having
two. such brave, loyal comrades * * * * * . And no
man could have been more unfortunate than I that I was unable
to reward their courage and loyalty.
The Commission concurs with the Secretary of the Treasury in
recommending this order.
WM H TAFT
THE WHITE HOUSE,
February 26, 191r3.
