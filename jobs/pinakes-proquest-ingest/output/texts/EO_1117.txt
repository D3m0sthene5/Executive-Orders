Executive Order
It is hereby ordered that the employees of the Executive Depart-
meats, the Government Printing Office and the Navy Yard and Station
who are members of the Grand Army of the Republic, be granted such
portions of their annual leave as they may desire, not exceeding fourteen
days, to enable them to attend the National Encampment of the Grand
Army- of the Republic at Salt Lake City, beginning on the 9th and ending
on the 14 th of August.
WM H TAFT
THE WHITE HOUSE,
