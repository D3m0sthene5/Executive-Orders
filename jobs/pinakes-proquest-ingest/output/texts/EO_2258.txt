Executive 'rber.
Mr. William H. H. Bowen may be reinstated in the field service of
the Internal Revenue Service without regard to the year limit required by
the civil service rules.
This order is issued on the recommendation of the Secretary of the
Treasury with the concurrence of the Civil Service Commission. The
Secretary of the Treasury states that Mr. Bowen served very efficiently in
the Internal Revenue Service for nineteen years and resigned from the
position of internal revenue agent to accept the appointment of postmaster
at Covington, Kentucky, retiring from that position on September 1, 1914;
that the records of the Department show that the work performed by
Mr. Bowen as internal revenue agent wvas of an unusually efficient char-
acter; and the Internal Revenue Service at this time is in need of such
men as Mr. Bowen, men who are able to take up successfully any and
every phase of the internal revenue work. Mr. Bowen, acting on infor-
mation inadvertently given by a Commissioner, failed to make application
for reinstatement in the Internal Revenue Service before the expiration of
the year limit, required by the rules.
