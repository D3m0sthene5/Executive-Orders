Executive Orber
Wyoming.
Under authority contained in the Act of Congress approved June 25, 1910
(36 Stat., 847), as amended by the Act of August 24, 1912 (37' Stat., 497), andsubject to the conditions, provisions and limitations of said acts, it is hereby
ordered that the public lands in T. 30 N., R. 55 XW., 6th P. M., Wyoming, be,and the same are hereby withdrawn from settlement, location, sale, entry andall forms of appropriation, pending a resurvey of said township under authority
contained in the Act of Congress approved March 3, 1909 (35 Stat., 845) ; thiswithdrawal to remain in full force and effect from the date hereof until theresurvey is duly approved and accepted and the approved plat thereof is officially
hiled in the proper U. S. local land office, unless another date be fixed by the
Commissioner of the General Land Office, whereupon this withdrawal will
become of no effect.
THE WHITE HOUSE,
August 21, 192 3.
