In pursuance of the provisions of Section I of the Act of Congress
approved July 5, 1884 (23 Stat. 103), entitled "An Act to provide for the
disposal of abandoned and useless Military Reservations", the military
reservations on Virginia Key and Key Biscayne, Florida, set apart for
military purposes by Executive Order of February I I, 1897, are hereby
placed under the control of the Secretary of the Interior for disposition as
provided in said act, or as may be otherwise provided by law, the same, in
the opinion of' the President, having become useless for military purposes.
WOODROW WILSON
THE WHITE HOUSE,
28 April, 1916.
