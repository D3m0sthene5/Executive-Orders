Executive Orber.
James A. Compton, William E. Dulin, and George P. Meyer may be
reinstated in the Pension Office without regard to the fact that they have
been separated from the service for more than one year. This action is
based upon the statement of the Secretary of the Interior that under similar
circumstances separations from the service would not in general be imposed
for a similar offense and that the men suffered an injustice in their removal.
Their reinstatement is favored by the Civil Service Commission.
WM H TAFT
THE WHITE HOUSE,
March 15, 1910.
