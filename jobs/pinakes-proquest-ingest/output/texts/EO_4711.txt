By virtue of authority vested in me by the Panama Canal Act, approved
August 24, 1912, as amended by the Act approved December 29, 1926, the follow-
ing regulations are hereby established to govern the granting of leaves of absence to
the Judge of the District Court of the Canal Zone, the District Attorney of the
Canal Zone, and the Marshal of the Canal Zone:
1. Leave of absence allowed the above officials by the said Act shall
be reckoned by calendar years.
2. Should any one of such offices be held by more than one person in
any one calendar year, the leave authorized shall be prcr ated to such per-
sons in proportion to the length of time they have held the office in that
calendar year.,
3. Unused leave of absence in any one calendar year may be accumu-
lated and allowed to such officer in the next calendar year, provided no
leave in excess of 120 days shall be taken in any one calendar year.
4. The above officials will submit applications for leave to the Secre-
tary of War through the Governor of the Panama Canal, and shall report
to the Governor of the Panama Canal their time of departure on such leave
and of return to duty therefrom.
