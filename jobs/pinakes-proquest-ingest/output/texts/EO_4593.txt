It is hereby ordered that the following described areas in Alaska be, and they
are hereby set apart and designated as preserves and breeding grounds for muskrat
and beaver:
The area lying to the eastward and within one-hal mile of the center line of
The Alaska Railroad between mile posts Nos. 40.5 and 41.5;
The area lying to the westward and within one-half mile of the center line of
said railroad between mile posts Nos. 176 and 177;
The area lying to the westward and within one-halt mile of the center line of
said railroad between mile posts Nos. 181.5 and 182.5;
The area lying to the westward and within one-half mile of the center line of
said railroad between mile posts Nos. 190 and 191;
The area lying to the westward and within one-half mile of the center line of
said railroad between mile posts Nos. 195.5 and 196.5;
The area lying on each side of and within one-half mile of the center line of
said railroad between mile posts Nos. 234.5 and 236.5;
The area lying on each side of and within one-half mile of the center line of
said railroad between mile poets Nos. 242 and 243;
The area lying to the eastward and within one-half mile of the center line of
said railroad between mile posts Nos. 250 and 252.
Within the limits of the above described areas it is unlawful for any person at
any time to hunt, trap, capture or kill any muskrat or beaver, except under such
rules and regulations as may hereafter be prescribed by the Secretary of Agriculture
in accordance with the Alaska game laws. Nothing herein contained shall, how-
ever, interfere with the hunting, trapping, capturing or killing of birds or wild
animals other than muskrat and beaver or the taking of fish within the above
described areas under applicable laws or regulations.
.and it is hereby ordered that the area within the hereinafter described bound-
aries be and the same is hereby designated and set apart as a bird, fish and game
refuge for the protection of wild birds and game and fur-bearing animals other than
browi and grizzly bears, wolves and wolverines:
Beginning at a point six miles due north of the Government hotel at Curry,
Alaska, thence east one mile, thence south seven miles, thence west two miles,
thence north seven miles, thence east one mile to the place 'f beginning.
Within the limits of the reservation last above described it is unlawful to take
fish, except under such regulations as may hereafter be prescribed by the Secretary
of Commerce, or to hunt, trap, capture or kill any wild bird or game or fur-bearing
animal other than brown or grizzly bears, wolves and wolverines, except under such
regulations as may hereafter be prescribed by the Secretary of Agriculture. The
open and close season for the animals named within said area shall be governed by
such regulations relating to said animals as have been or may be issued by the
Secretary of Agriculture in accordance with the Alaska game laws.
Warning is hereby expressly given to all persons rat to commit any of the acts
herein enumerated under the penalties prescribed by section 84 of the U. S. Penal
Code approved March 4, 1909 (35 Stat., 1088), as amended April 15, 1924 (43
Stat., 98).
CALVIN COOLIDGE
