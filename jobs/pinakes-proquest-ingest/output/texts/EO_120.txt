AMENDMENT TO THE CIVIL SERVICE RULES.
RULE VI.
POST OFFICE DEPARTMENT.
Rule VI is hereby amended by inserting after clause No. 33, the
following as clause No. 34, and renumbering the succeeding clauses to
the -end:
34. One assistant postmaster or the chief assistant to the
postmaster of whatever designation, at each post office.
Approved:
William McKinley
Executive Mansion,
June 6, 1899.
NOTE: This clause was in the draft of Amendments to the Civil Service
Rules of May 29, 1899, and was intended to be approved by me; but
was omitted inadvertently in the copy sent to the printer. This
exception had existed; prior to that date.
