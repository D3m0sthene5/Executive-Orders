Mr. Theodore A. Bradford may be appointed to an appropriate clerical posi-
tion in the General Accounting Office without reference to the examination require-
ments of the civil service rules.
The father of Mr. Bradford was in the Government service from 1904 until
February 27, 1928, when he was killed by a truck ;n the performance of his duties
as a clerk in the General Accounting Office.
The issuance of this order is recommended by the Comptroller General. The
Civil Service Commission is unable to concur.
