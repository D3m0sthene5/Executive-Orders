In accordance with the provisions of Sections 2248, 2252 and 2253 of
the Revised Statutes of the United States, and by virtue of the authority
therein given, it is hereby ordered that
The Devils Lake land district, in the State of North Dakota, with
land office at Devils Lake, be discontinued, and the lands, business and
archives of said land district and office be transferred to and consolidated
with the Minot land district, with land office at Minot, North Dakota.
