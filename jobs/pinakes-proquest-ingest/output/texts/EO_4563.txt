Executive 'Order
Mrs. Bessie G. Thatcher may be reinstated, without regard to the length of
her separation, in an appropriate position in the Post Office Service. Mrs. Thatcher,
nee Greene, was a classified employee in the post offices at Savannah and Denver
from August 26, 1902, to January 20, 1914.
The Civil Service Commission recommends this Order on account of the excel-
lent service rendered by Mrs. Thatcher, several years of which was as a member of
the Local Civil Service Board at the Savannah office.
Done for charity.
CALVIN COOL IDGE
THE WHITE HOUSE,
January 12, 1927.
