16. Whenever the work to be done is in the nature of job
employment, temporary in character, and after its completion the
services of the employee will not be further needed, and a cer-
tificate to that effect is made by the nominating or appointing
officer, a temporary appointment may be made, if approved by
the Commission, without regard to the condition of any register,
for a period of three months, which appointment may, upon re-
quest, stating the fact that the work is not yet completed, be
extended for a further period of three months, with the ante-
cedent approval of the commission, but shall cease at the end
of such second period.

Section 14 will be stricken out.
Sections 15 and 16 will be renumbered sections 17 and 18,
respectively.
