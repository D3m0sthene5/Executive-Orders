Executive Orber
Horatio Whitted may be appointed to an appropriate position in the
classified service without regard to the civil service rules. Mr. Whitted
was appointed as a laborer at .$660 per annum on May 9, 1901, and assigned
to the General Land Office, where he still is employed. It appears that,
although his principal duties were of the grade performed by classified
employees, his name was erroneously omitted from the list of such
employees who were classified by the Executive Orders of January 12, 19015,
and March 30, 1905, and this order is issued on the recommendation of the
Acting Secretary of the Interior to correct the error. The Civil Service
Commission does not concur in this recommendation.
WOODROW WILSON
THE WHITE HOUSE,
11 November, 1914.
[ No. 2083.]
