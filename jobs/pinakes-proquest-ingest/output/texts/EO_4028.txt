It is hereby ordered under authority of the act of Congress approved June
25, 1910 (36 Stat., 847), as amended by the act of August 24, 1912 (37 Stat., 497),
that the following described lands in the State of Wyoming be, and they are, hereby
temporarily withdrawn subject to the conditions of said acts, until March 5, 1925,
in aid of the legislation proposed in pending S. 3452:
Sixth Principal Meridian
In T. 53 N., R. 104 W., those portions of Sees. 1, 12, 13, 24, 25 and 36 not
embraced in the Shoshonie National Forest;
In T. 54 N., R. 104 W., those portions of Sees. 1, 25 and 36 not embraced in
said national forest;
In T. 55 N., R. 104 W., those portions of Sees. 1, 12, 13, 24, 25 and 36 not
embraced in said national forest;
In T. 56 N., R. 104 W., those portions of Sees. 1, 12, 13, 24, 25 and 36 not
embraced in said national forest.
If such legislation be not enacted before the adjournment of the last session
of the 68th Congress and no other direction is given as to the disposition of these
lands, they will, on March 5, 1925, become subject to disposal under any law
then applicable thereto without further order.
CALVIN COOL IDGE
THE WHITE HOUSE,
June 17, 1924.
