AMENDING THE CIVIL SERVICE RULES.
Schedule B of classified positions which may be filled upon noncom-
p)etitive examination is hereby amended by adding to sub-division I,
Interior Department, the following paragraph:
6. One superintendent of irrigation in the Indian Service, who shall
be competent to pass upon water rights.
The duties of this employee will be wvholly legal in cases which here-
tofore have been usually handled by attorneys in the Department of Justice.
These duties relate to the legal rights of the Indians to the use of water
and the employee must have had wide experience in prosecuting cases of
water rights before the courts and be able to protect the rights of the
Indians.
This amendment is recommended by the Secretary of the Interior
with the concurrence of the Civil Service Commission.
WOODROW WILSON
THE WHITE HOUSE,
r8 December, 1914.
