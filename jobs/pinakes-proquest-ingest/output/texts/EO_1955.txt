CHALLIS NATIONAL FOREST
IDAHO
Executive Order No. 194.6, dated May 21, 1914, is hereby so amended
to describe the tract in Sec. 6, T. io N., R. 13 E., B. M., excluded from
the Challis National Forest, and to be restored under the provisions of
said order, as follows: That part of Sec. 6 lying north and east of Valley
Creek, in lieu of "those parts of Lots i and 2 of Sec. 6 lying north of
Valley Creek". the description given in said order.
WOODROW WILSON
THE WHITE HOUSE,
6 June, 1914.
