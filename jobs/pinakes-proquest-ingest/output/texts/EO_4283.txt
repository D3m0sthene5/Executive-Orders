Executive Orber
At the request of the Attorney General, and with the consent of the Director
of the United States Veterans' Bureau and the Secretary of War, there is hereby
transferred back -to the War Department all that portion of the United States
Military Reservation known as Camp Sherman, together with all improvements
thereon, in the State of Ohio, assigned to the United States Veterans' Bureau by
Executive Order ##3558, dated October 11, 1921, for use in carrying out the provi-
sions of the Act of Congress approved August 9, 1921, (42 Stat. 147-150), excepting:
(1) The area transferred back to the War Department by Executive' Order
$#3633, dated February 7, 1922;
(2) The so-called "Mound City Group", returned to the custody and control
of the War Department by Executive Proclamation ##1653, dated
March 2, 1923, declaring said group to be a national monument site;
and
(3) That part of Camp Sherman north of a line beginning at a point on East
Liverpool Street at the Scioto River and running west on East Liverpool
Street to Columbus Avenue; thence south on Columbus Avenue to
Moundsville Street; then west on Moundsville Street to the junction
point of Frankfort and Egypt Pikes; then following along Egypt Pike
to the junction of Egypt Pike and Sandusky Boulevard; then due west
to the boundary line of the Government Reservation, as will appear on
a map of Camp Sherman, United States Army Cantonment, Chilli-
cothe, Ohio, known as the Building Location Plan, dated January, 1919.
This order is issued to enable the War Department to grant a revocable license
to the Department of Justice covering all that part of Camp Sherman, together
with all improvements thereon, lying south of the above line and including approxi-
mately six hundred and twenty acres east of the Scioto River known as the Rifle
Range, for use. for jail or penitentiary purposes as in the opinion of the Attorney
General may be needed.
CALVIN COOLIDGE
THE WHITE HOUSE,
August 14, 1925.
