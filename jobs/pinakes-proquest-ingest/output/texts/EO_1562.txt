Executive Orber.
The following-named employees of the Tariff Board who have been
especially recommended for retention in the public service may be trans-
fer-red or appointed to appropriate positions in the competitive classified
service with the approval of the Civil Service Commission; but in the
case of any such person who has not a competitive status or who has not
passed an appropriate examination for the position to which appointment
is proposed the Civil Service Commission may apply such tests of fitness
as it deems necessary before approval.
Mr. Leo Rulimann Miss Mary E. Bishop
Mrs. B. C. Woodford Mr. A. L. Colton
Mr. Herve XV. Kitzmiller Mr. J. L. F. Ingold
Mrs. Rose R. Aydelotte Mr. James Otey
Miss Anna E. Hutchinson Mr. Wilson Evans
Mrs. Max WNest Mr. Travers Pinn
Mr. N. I. Stone Mr. J. Garfield Harris.
Eligibility under this order shall cease at the expiration of one year
from the date of the person's separation from the Tariff Board.
This order is issued upon the recommendation of the Chairman of
the Tariff Board, with the approval of the Civil Service Commission, on
account of the faithful and meritorious service of the employees, most of
whom have had considerable experience in the Government service,
although not in all cases in the classified service, but in no case less than
eighteen months.
WM H TAFT
