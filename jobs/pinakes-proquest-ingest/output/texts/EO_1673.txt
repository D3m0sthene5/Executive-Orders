COMANCHE ADMINISTRATIVE SITE
(Near Dixie National Forest)
UTAH
Under authority of the Act of Congress approved June 25, 1910 (36
Stat., 847), as amended by the Act of August 24, 1912 (37 Stat., 497),
and on the recommendation of the Secretary of Agriculture, it is hereby
ordered that the SE. 14 N W. 54 of Section 21, Township 38 South, Range
13 West, Salt Lake Meridian, Utah, containing 40 acres, be temporarily
withdrawn from settlement, location, sale or entry, except as provided in
said acts, and be reserved for use by the Forest Service as a Ranger Station
in the administration of the Dixie National Forest.
Wm H TfAFT
THE WHITE HOUSE,
Dec. 31st, 1912.
