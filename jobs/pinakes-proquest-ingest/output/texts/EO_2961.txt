\VHIEREAS, The District of Columbia Appropriation Act approved August
3!, 1918, provides the sum of 585,0oo for grading, paving, improvement and
repair of streets, avenues and roads constituting necessary and adlequlate means
of approach and access to existing buildings or to temporary buildings which
may be erected in the District of Columbia for the use of the United States; and
WHEREAS, Street paving in the area between 17th, 23rd, B and Virginia
Avenue N .W., is necessary for proper ingress and egress to office buildings erected
and in course of erection by the United States Government in connection with
the prosecution of the present war; and
WHEREAS, The Commissioners of the District of Columbia have requested
that these paving operations be declared an emergency matter and have asked
that the eight hour law relating to the employment of laborers and mechanics
in the Iistrict of Columbia be suspended in so far as it relates to the operations
referred to :
THEREFORE, Be it ordered, that I, Woodrow Wilson, President ofH the
United States, do hereby declare that the street paving operations in the area
referred to is emergency work relating to the conduct of the present wvar, and are
hereby excepted from the provisions of the eight hour law governing the hours
of laborers and mechanics employed in that connection.
PROVIDED, however, that pay be allowed to those entitled thereto in con-
nection with the prosecution of said operations with compensation of time and
half for over time work spent upon said construction.
WOODROW WILSON
THE WHITE HOUSE,
16 September, 1918.
