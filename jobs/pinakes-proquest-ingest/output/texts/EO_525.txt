It is hereby ordered that the hereinafter described tract of land in the
District of Alaska, be, and it is hereby reserved and set apart for lighthouse
purposes, to-wit:
All that portion of Cape Hinchinbrook, Hinchinbrook Island, Alaska,
lying south of the parallel 600°i15' north latitude, also that further portion
of said island lying west of the meridian drawn of a statute mile east of
Signal Mountain (about 146° 38' 14" west longitude,) as shown on a blue
print from a part of Coast and Geodetic Survey Chart No. 8520, published,
April, 1904, accompanying the order.
T. ROOSEVELT
THE WHITE HOUSE,
November 27, 1906.
