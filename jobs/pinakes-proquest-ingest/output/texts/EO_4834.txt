The time limit on reinstatement under the civil service rules is hereby waived
to permit the reinstatement of Mrs. Alice C. Murphy to a position in the Treasury
Department for which she has the requisite civil service examination status.
Mrs. Murphy served in the Bureau of Engraving and Printing from May 28,
191?, to February 28, 1921, as a printer's assistant. Her husband served in that
Bureau for approximately 28 years. His death on February 8, 1928, left her with
four young children to support.
This order is recommended by the Secretary of the Treasury. The Civil
Service Commission can not certify that the interests of the service demand a waiver
of the rules to permit her reinstatement, but submits the case without prejudice.
For charity.
CALVIN COOLIDGE
THE WHITE HousE,
March 19, 1928.
