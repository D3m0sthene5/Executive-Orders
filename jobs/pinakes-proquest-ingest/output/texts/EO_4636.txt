Executive Orber
By virtue of the authority vested in me by law, that part of Section 5 of the
Executive Order of April 14, 1921, relating to the issuance of vehicle licenses to
bona fide tourists in the CanalZfie, and establishing the fee therefor, is amended
to read as follows:
"A ninety-day motor vehicle license will be issued without charge to a bona
fide tourist or non-resident who has brought his private vehicle to the Canal
Zone, providing the vehicle carries a current license plate issued therefor by
some State of the United States. In such cases the United States tag will
suffice, and no Canal Zone tag need be issued."
This order shall take effect from and after this date.
CALVIN COOLIDGE
THE WHITE HOUSE,
April 2$, 1927.
