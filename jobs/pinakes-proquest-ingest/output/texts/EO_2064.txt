Executive order.
Mrs. Mary H. McTwiggan, a charwoman in the Bureau of Engraving
and Printing at $25 per month, may be appointed to a position in the
classified service of said Bureau without reference to the Civil Service
Rules.
Mrs. McTwiggan is the widowv of a Civil War veteran and has three
sons now serving in the United States Navy.
The Director of the Bureau of Engraving and Printing states that
Mrs. McTwviggan is capable of performing the duties of an operative and
that he will be very glad to recommend her to the Secretary of the
Treasury for appointment to such a position.
The Civil Service Commission does not recommend that an order be
issued in this case.
WOODROW WILSON
THE WHITE HOUSE,
October 23, 1914.
