RELATING TO LICENSING BICYCLES FOR OPERATION IN THE CANAL ZONE
By virtue of the Act of Congress entitled "An Act Extending certain privileges
of Canal employees to other officials on the Canal Zone and authorizing the President
to make rules and regulations affecting health, sanitation, quarantine, "Ixation,
public roads, self-propelled vehicles, and police powers on the Canal Zone, and for
other purposes, including provision as ~o certain fees, money orders and interest
deposits," approved August 21, 1916, I hereby establish the following Executive
Order for the Canal Zone'.3
Those parts only of Executive Orders of April 14, 1921, and December
23, 1924, prescribing that privately-owned bicycles operated in the Canal
Z~u shall be licensed and carry license tags, and prescribing fees therefor,
are hereby revoked, effective on and after July 1, 1928. All other regula-
tions at present in force respecting the operation of bicycles shall continue
in force as heretofore.
CALVIN COOLIDGE
THE WHITE HOUSE,
February 2, 1928.
