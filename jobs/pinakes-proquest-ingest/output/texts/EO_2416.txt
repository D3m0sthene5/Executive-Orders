Schedule B, Subdivision IN', paragraph i, of classified positions under
the War Department which may he filled upon non-competitive examina-
tion is hereby amended by the addition of the positions of inspector of
aeroplanes and aeroplane motors in the Signal Service at Large. As
amended the paragraph will read as follows
i. Aeronautical engineers, aeronautical mechanical engineers,
instructors in flying, aviation mechanicians, and inspectors of aero-
planes and areoplane motors in the Aviation Section of the Signal
Corps.
The positions of inspector of aeroplanes and aeroplane motors belong
to the same general class as the other positions enumerated in the
amended clause, and it is deemed impracticable in the present stage of
development of aviation by the Government to subject them to open
competitive examination.
WOODROW WILSON
THE WHITE HOUSE,
8 July, 1916.
