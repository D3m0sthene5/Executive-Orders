Executive Orber.
COLORADO.
Under the provisions of the act of June 25, 1910 (Public No. 303),
entitled "An Act To authorize the President of the United States to
make withdrawals of public lands in certain cases", it is hereby ordered
that the wvest half of section twenty-four, in township five south of range
sixty-two west of sixth Principal Meridian, in the State of Colorado, he
withdrawn from settlement, location, sale, or entry pending action by Con-
gress on H. R. Bill 25J960, Sixty-first Congress, Second Session, entitled
"A Bill To authorize the Secretary of the Interior to issue patent in fee
simple to the National Lincoln Douglas Sanatorium and Consumptives'
Hospital Association (a corporation), of Denver, Colorado, for a certain
described tract of land and for other purposes".
WM H TAFT
THE WHITE HOUSE,
August 23, 1910.
