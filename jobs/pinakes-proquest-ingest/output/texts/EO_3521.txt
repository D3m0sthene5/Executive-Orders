The Executive Order of May z10, 1921, prescribing examinations for the
appointment of presidential postmasters, is hereby amended by striking out in
the second paragraph the words "such vacancy" and substituting therefor the
words "the date of examination". As amended, the paragraph will read:
No person who has passed. his sixty-fifth birthday, or who has not
actually resided within the delivery of such office for two years next pre-
ceding the date of examination, shall be given the examination herein
provided for.
WARREN G HARDING
THE WHITE House,
July 27, 1921.
