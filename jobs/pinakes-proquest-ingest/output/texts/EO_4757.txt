CHUGACH NATIONAL FOREST
ALASKA'
Under authority of the acts of Congress approved March 3, 1891 (26 Stat..1095) ,
and June 4, 1897 (30 Stat. 11 at 34 and 36), and on the recommendation of the
Secretary of Agriculture, it is hereby ordered that the tract of land in Alaska, lying
within the following described boundaries, be and the same is hereby included in
and made a part of the Chugach National Forest :
Beginning at Point Doran on the boundary of the Chugach National Forest,
thence west to a point due north of Mile Post 90 on the Alaska Railroad, thence
due south to said Mile Post 90, and continuing to the line of mean high tide of
Turnagain Arm, thence along the north shore of Turnagain Arm to the former
boundary of the Forest on Turnagain Arm.
