Executive Orber4
Schedule A, Subdivision VII, of the civil service rules, relating to
the Department of the Interior, is hereby amended by the addition of the
following paragraph:
23. One histopathologist temporarily engaged in research work
at the Government Hospital for the Insane.
This order is approved on the concurrent recommendation of the
Superintendent of the Government Hospital for the Insane and the Civil
Service Commission, in view of the fact that it would be difficult or impos-
sible to obtain persons with the required qualifications through competitive
examination and to enable the Government Hospital for the Insane to
avail itself of the services of advanced students of foreign laboratories for
nominal pay.
WM H TAFT
THE WHITE HOUSE,
Afarcit 23, 1910.
