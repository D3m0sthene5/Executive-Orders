Charles B. Bateman may be transferred from the navy yard service to
any classified mechanical position in the Departments at Washington for
which his experience of fourteen years as a machinist qualifies him.
This order, which is concurred in by the Civil Service Commission,
is issued in view of an unfortunate situation in which Mr. Bateman is
shown to have been placed, and the fact being recognized that the good
of the service is advanced by increasing the comfort and peacefulness, and
hence the efficiency, of its working force. This matter is fully set forth
in the Commission's letter of August I, 1911.
WM H TAFT
THE WHI'E HOUSE,
Aug ust 2, 1911.
