Executive Order.
The presentation of a Medal of Honor to any enlisted man in the
Naval Service, as awarded by Section 1407 of the Revised Statutes and
the Act of March 3, i1901i, and also to officers whenever the same may be
authorized by law, will always be made with formal and impressive cere-
monial.
The recipient will, when practicable, be ordered to Washington, D. C.,
and the presentation will be made tby the President as Commander-in-Chief,
or b~y such representative as the President may designate.
When not practicable to have the presentation at WVashington, the
details of time, place and ceremony will be prescribed by the Secretary of
the Navy for each case.
In time of war the presentation will be made by the Comnmander-in-
Chief of the Fleet, or the squadron or division commander.
