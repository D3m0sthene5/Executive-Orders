Executive Orber
Mrs. Effie A. Deane may be appointed to a position in the Government service
without compliance with the civil service rules.
Mrs. Deane is the widow of William C. Deane, who died in September, 1924,
after a service of twenty-eight years in the Bureau of Engraving and Printing.
This order is issued on the recommendation of the Secretary of the Treasury
as a matter of executive benevolence.
The Civil Service Commission submits the case without prejudice, stating that
it is without jurisdiction.
For charity.
CALVIN COOL IDGE
THE WHITE HOUSE,
December 24, 1924.
