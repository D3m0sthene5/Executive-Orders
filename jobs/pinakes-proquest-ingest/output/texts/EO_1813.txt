Executive Oraer.
Mr. Charles M. McCabe may be reinstated as a clerk in the Postal
Service without reference to the year limitation of the reinstatement rule.
Mr. McCabe was removed from the Nashville, Tennessee, post-office
on September Ia, [898, notice of his removal alleging "inefficiency". No
charge of inefficiency had been preferred against him and he wvas allowed
no opportunity to reply to the charge as required by the rules then in
effect. Mr. McCabe as a member of the Board of Civil Service Exam-
iners for the Nashville post-office rendered assistance to the Commission
in connection with investigations involving the Collector of Internal
Revenue, who was removed by the President for violation of the Civil
Service Rules. The Postmaster General states that it appears that Mr.
McCabe was removed on political grounds, and the issuance of an Execu-
tive order for his reinstatement seems to be justified by the facts in the case.
In view of the facts stated above the Civil Service Commission con-
curs in recommending this order.
WOODROW WILSON
THE WHITE HOUSE,
20 August, 1913.
[No. 181;.]
