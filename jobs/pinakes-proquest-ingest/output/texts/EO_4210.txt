Diplomatic Visas for Reentry into the United States
By virtue of the authority vested in me by the Act of Congress approved
May 22, 1918, entitled, "An Act to Prevent in Time of War Departure from and
Entry into the United States Contrary to the Public Safety", as extended by the
Act of Congress of March 2, 1921, entitled, "An Act making appropriations for the
Diplomatic and Consular Service for the Fiscal year ending June 30, 1922",
I hereby authorize the Secretary of State to grant diplomatic visas to diplomatic
and consular officers of foreign governikL. in the United States, to enable them
to reenter this country. Such visas shall be valid during the validity of the pass-
ports upon which they are placed. The Secretary of State may grant such visas
through such officials in the Department of State as he may designate, and under
such rules as he may prescribe.
Diplomatic and consular officers bearing passports visaed1 as provided above
shall not be required, in order to reenter the United States. to have their passports
visaed by diplomatic or consular officers of the United States abroad.
This order shall take effect immediately.
CALVIN COOLIDGE
THE WHITE HousE,
April 23, 1925.
