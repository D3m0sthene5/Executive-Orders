Executive Order
PINE MOUNTAIN ADMINISTRATIVE SITE
(Near Deschutes National Forest)
OREGON
Under authority of the Act of Congress of June 25, 1910 (36 Stat.,
847), as amended by the Act of August 24, 1912 (37 Stat., 497), and on
the recommendation of the Secretary of Agriculture, it is hereby ordered
that the following lands be temporarily withdrawn from settlement, loca-
tion, sale, or entry, except as provided in said Acts, and be reserved for
use by the Forest Service as a Ranger Station in the administration of the
Deschutes National Forest:
Township 21 south, Range 15 east, W. Meridian, Sec. io
The E 54 W 54 and the E 54 W 54 NW /
WOODROW WILSON
THE WHITE HOUSE,
