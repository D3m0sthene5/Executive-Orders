The Executive Order of March 30, 1915, authorizing and directing
Edward E. Pratt, Chief of the Bureau of Foreign and Domestic Com-
merce, to perform the duties of the Secretary of Commerce during the
absence of the Secretary and certain other officials, is hereby amended to
read as follows:
Pursuant to the authority contained in Section 179 of the Revised
Statutes, I hereby authorize and direct that during the absence of
the Secretary of Commerce, the Assistant Secretary, and the Solicitor
for the Department of Commerce, Samuel W. Stratton, Director of
the Bureau of Standards, or Edward E. Pratt, Chief of the Bureau of
Foreign and Domnestic Commerce, will perform the duties of the Sec-
retary. Their relative standing will be coordinate, and either official
may be called upon to perform the duties of the Secretary as the latter
may elect.
WOODROW WILSON
THE WHITE HOUSE,
30 April, 1915.
