Captain J. WV. Powell may be reinstated to a clerical position in the
Government Printing Office, without regard to the length or cause of his
separation therefrom. This action is ordered on the concurrent recom-
mendation of the Public Printer and the Civil Service Commission, on
account of Captain Powell's military record and long and satisfactory
service in the Government Printing Office, and in the belief that he has
suffered sufficient penalty for any misconduct of which he may have been
guilty.
WM H TAFT
THE WHITE HOUSE,
July' 2, 1910.
