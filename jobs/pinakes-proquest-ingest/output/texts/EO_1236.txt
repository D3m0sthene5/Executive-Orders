John J. Scully, a Laborer in the customs service at New York, N. Y.,
may be placed on the list of Classified LaborC~rs at that port.
This Order is issued on the request of the Secretary of the Treasury
in view of the fact that Mr. Scully was performing classified duties on
August 5, 1909, when the laborers at that port were classified, and because
he has continued to perform said duties until this time, and also because his
name was not included in the list under the said Executive Order as it
wvas expected he would be taken off the work he was then performing.
The order is approved by the Civil Service Commission.
WM H TAFT
THE WHITE HOUSE,
Aug. 8, 1910.
