COCHETOPA NATIONAL FOREST
COLORADO
It is hereby ordered that on and after July i, i908, the land within the
boundary shown on the attached diagram, heretofore set apart, reserved,
and proclaimed as parts of the Cochetopah and Leadville National Forests,
shall be known as the Cochetopa National Forest. The remaining portions
of the Cochetopah National Forest shall continue reserved and constitute
parts of the Rio Grande, Gunnison, and Leadville National Forests. It
is not intended by this order to release any land from reservation or to
reserve any land not heretofore embraced in a National Forest.
THEODORE ROOSEVELT
THE WHITE HousE.
July i, 1908.
