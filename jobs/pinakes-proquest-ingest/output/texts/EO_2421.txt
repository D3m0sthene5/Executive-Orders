Esrecutivea Other.
Miss Ruth L. O'Donnell may be appointed to a position in the
Bureau of Efficiency, without reference to the civil service rules.
This order is issued upon the recommendation of the Chief of the
Bureau of Efficiency, who states that Miss O'Donnell has been employed
in his office at various times during the past two years, that her services
have been most satisfactory, and that her permanent appointment to the
force of the Bureau of Efficiency would be a distinct advantage to the
Bureau.
The Civil Service Commission does not concur in the recommendation.
WOODROW WILSON
THE WHITE HOUSE,
,q July, 1916.
[No. 2421.]
