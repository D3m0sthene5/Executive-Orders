By virtue of the authority vested in me by the act of Congress approved
August 1, 1914, making appropriations for the sundry civil expenses of the
Government for the fiscal year ended June 30, 1915, the state of Arkansaa_
which is at present in the forty-fifth (St. Louis) customs collection district, is
hereby transferred to and made a part of the forty-third (Tennessee) customs
collection district, headquarters Memphis, Tennessee, effective October 1, 1923.
WARREN G HARDING
Thz WHITE HOUSE,
July 27, 1923.
