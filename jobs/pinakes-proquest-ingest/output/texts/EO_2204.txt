Executive Ortder.
By virtue of the authority vested in me, it is hereby ordered that the
ExecuLive Order of January 15, 1915, relative to charges for rent, fuel
and electric current furnished employees of The Panama Canal and the
Panama Railroad Company on the Isthmus of Panama, is modified by
suspending from the operation thereof so much as relates to rent, fuel and
lights during the period of actual construction of The Panama Canal but
not later than June 30, 1916.
WOODROW WILSON
THE WHITE HOUSE,
25 May, 1915.
