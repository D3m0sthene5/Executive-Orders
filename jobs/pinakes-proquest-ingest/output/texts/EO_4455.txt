)Executive Order
Pursuant to Section 91 of the Act of Congress approved April 30, 1900, entitled
"An Act to provide a government for the Territory of Hawaii" (31 Stat. 141), as
amended by Section 7 of 'the Act of Congress approved May 27, 1910 (36k Stat. 443)
the property hereinafter described, being a part of the Hawaii Arsenal Military
Reservation, formerly the Hawaiian Ordnance Depot, said Arsenal Reservation being
included in the Kahauiki (Fort Shafter) Military Reservation as described by Exec-
utive Order No. 2521, January 26, 1917, and as modified by Executive Order No.
3080, April 30, 1919, and by Territorial Executive Order No., 56, December 18, 1918,
is hereby restored to its previous status for use by the Territory of Hawaii for. the
purpose of widening 'the public street in the City of Honolulu, known as Middle
Street.
Beginning at a United States Military Reservation monument "B"
at the West corner of this piece of land, being also the present North
corner of King and Middle Streets in the City of Honolulu, and run-
ning by true azimuths as follows: (1) 1390 16' 02", twenty (20) feet
along the present Northeast side of King Street; thence, (2) 228° 03'
21 ", one thousand four hundred ninety-seven and eighty-one hundredths
(1497.81) feet along the remainder of the Hawaii Arsenal Reservation;
thence, (3) 313° 42' 31 ", twenty and six one hundredths (20.06) feet
along the boundary of the Fort Shafter Reservation between monu-
ments 26 and 27 to monument 27 of the above cited Executive Order
No. 2521; thence, (4) 48° 03' 21", one thousand four hundred ninety-
nine and eighty one hundredths (1499.80) feet along the Southeast side
of the Fort Shafter Reservation to the point of beginning and contain-
ing twenty-nine thousand nine hundred seventy-six (29,976) square
feet, more or less,
Provided, that if the land transferred by this instrument is not used in widen-
ing Middle Street as above indicated, or if at any time thereafter it shall cease to be
used for such purpose, then it shall revert to its former status as a part of the
Hawaii Arsenal Military Reservation.
CALVIN COOLIDGE
THE WHITE HOUSE,'
* June 5, 1926.
