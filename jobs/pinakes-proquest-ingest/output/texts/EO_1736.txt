Executive ttrber
J oab P. Foster may be reinstated as a Veterinary Inspector in the
Department of Agriculture.
Mr. Foster was appointed on May i6, 1908, through the Veterinary
Inspector examination, as an Inspector at $1400 per annum, and served in
that capacity until October 14, 1910 when he was granted leave of absence
without pay on account of ill health. He continued on leave of absence with-
out pay until December 31, 1910 when his appointment wvas terminated.
It appears that he has fully regained his health and is in proper condition
to resume his duties. Because of his past experience in Federal and state
livestock work, the Secretary of Agriculture states that as the services of
Mr. Foster would be of especial value to the Department, his reinstate-
ment would be distinctly in the interest of good administration. The
Civil Service does not recommend the issuance of this order.
WM H TAFT
THE WHITE HOUSE,
March 3, 1913.
[No. 1736.]
