Schedule A, Section III, of the Civil Service Rules relating to the
Treasury Department is hereby amended by adding the following para-
graph:
6. Laborers at $480 per annum in the Customs Service, Dis-
trict of Hawaii, who are to perform the duties of opener and packer.
This exception is recommended by the Civil Service Commission
because it is deemed appropriate to permit these low-salaried positions to
be filled by native Hawvaiians who would be unable to pass an examina-
tion by reason of their insufficient knowledge of English.
WM H TAFT
THE WHITE HOUSE,
July 12, 1910.
