Mrs. Ellen C. Watkins may be appointed as a clerk in the Govern-
ment Printing Office without reference to the civil service rules.
This order is issued upon the recommendation of the Public Printer,
wvho states that Mrs. Watkins is the widow of the late Harry A. Watkins,

twventy years, during which he rendered faithful service. Mrs. Watkins
has the qualifications of a clerk and bookkeeper.
The Civil Service Commission does not concur in the recommendation.
