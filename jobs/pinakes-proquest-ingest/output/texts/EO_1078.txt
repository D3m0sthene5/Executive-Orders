Mr. F. 13. Simons may be reinstated in the service of the Treasury
Department as an Analytical Chemist in the office of the Appraiser of
Merchandise at Baltimore, Md., without regard to the length of time since
his separation from the service. This order is made to enable the Secre-
tary of the Treasury to secure a civil servant for a vacant place in the office
above named, who is peculiarly wvell fitted for the performance of its
duties, by reason of his ten years service as a Chemist in the Division of
Chemistry in the Bureau of Internal Revenue.
The Executive Order of March 24, 1909, (No. 103) authorizing the
reinstatement of Mr. Simons in the Bureau of Internal Revenue, Treasury
Department, is hereby revoked.
