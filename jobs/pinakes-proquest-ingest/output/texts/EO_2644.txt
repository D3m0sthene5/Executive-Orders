Mr. John J. Crowley may be appointed Assistant to the Director of the
Bureau of War Risk Insurance in the Treasury Department, without regard to
civil service requirements.
-- This order is issued on the recommendation of the Secretary of the Treas-
ury, who states that Mr. Crowley is a life and accident insurance expert and
that his services are necessary in connection with the new branch of the Bureau
of War Risk Insurance which has to do with the insurance of masters, officers,
and crews of American merchant vessels against loss of life or personal injury
by the risks of war.
WOODROW WILSON
THE WHITE HousE,
20 June, 1917.
