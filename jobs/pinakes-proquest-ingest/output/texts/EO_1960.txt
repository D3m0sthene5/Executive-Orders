Executive Orber
As matters which relate to the clearance of vessels fall within the
jurisdiction of the Department of Commerce, it is hereby ordered that
instructions to customs officers concerning the exportation of arms and
munitions of war to Mexico by sea shall he given by that Department.
Similar instructions concerning such e xportations by land shall be issued
by the Treasury Department.
WOODROW WILSON
THE WHITE HOUSE,
9 Junfe, 1914.
[No. 1960.]
