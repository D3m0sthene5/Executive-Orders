]Executive Urger
Mrs. Mary D. Hanna may be appointed to a clerkship in the
classified service subject to such tests of fitness as the Civil Service
Commission may require.
Mrs. Hanna is the widow of Captain Guy B. G. Hanna, Coast
Artillery, who miet his death while in the line of duty, by the premature
explosion of a service charge during night target practice. In view
of the tragic circumstances of the case and the fact that it is now
necessary for Mrs. Hanna to earn her own livelihood the Secretary
of War recommended the issuance of an Executive order waiving the
requirement of examination under the civil service rules to permit her
appointment. The Civil Service Commission recommended that the
appointment be permitted, subject to appropriate tests of fitness.
WOODROW WILSON
THE WHITE HOUSE,
Oc/ober I, 1913.
