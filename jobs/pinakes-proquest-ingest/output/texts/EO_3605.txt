Executive Obe
White River-Holy Cross National Forest
Colorado
Under authority of the Act of Congress of June 4, 1897 (30 Stat., ii),
and upon the recommendation of the Secretary of Agriculture, it is hereby
ordered that on and after July i, 1922, all the lands included within the bounda-
ries of the Holy Cross National Forest which are located in Township 5 South,
Ranges 87, 88, and 89 West, and in Township 6 South, Range 88 West, as
fixed and defined by Proclamation of December x6, 1910 (36 Stat., 2693), be,
and the same are hereby transferred to the White River National Forest, Colo-
rado, and shall thereafter be administered as a part of said White River National
Forest.
It is intended by this Executive Order, for economy of administration, to
merge into one National Forest, thereafter to be known a.s the White River
National Forest, all lands heretofore designated by legal subdivisions and the
lands now within the White River National F~orest; and the boundaries of the
White River National Forest and the Holy Cross National Forest as they now
exist are hereby modified accordingly.
