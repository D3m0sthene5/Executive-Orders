Mr. WV D. Lundy may be appointed to a clerical position without
regard to the length of his separation from the service.
Mr. Lundy was appointed a clerk in the War Department through
the stenographer and typewriter examination on October 1, 1902. He
was subsequently transferred to the Treasury Department and resigned on
May 6, 1910, as a clerk at $1400 to become assistant clerk of the Finance
Committee of the Senate. He remained in that position until May 1,
1911, when he became personal clerk to Senator Aldrich. On December
12, 1911, he was appointed to clerical position on the National Monetary
Commission, where he remained until the termination of that Commission
on March ;1, 19 12 ; he then resumed his position with Senator Aldrich,
which he held until August 18, 1912. Senator Aldrich states that Mr.
Lundy performed important services for the Government during all the
period referred to, although part of the time being paid by the Senator
upon whose personal affairs he was also engaged.
If Mr. Lundy has been continuously in the government employ since
his resignation from the Treasury Department he would be eligible for
retransfer to the classified service.
This order is made without the approval of the Secretary of the
Treasury or of the Civil Service Commission.
