graphic draftsman in the Coast and Geodetic Survey, Department of
Commerce, without regard to the requirements of the civil-service rules.
This order is issued up)on recommendation of the Secretary of Com-
merce and the Superintendent oft he Survey who state that Mrs. Moncure
served satisfactorily and very efficiently in a statutory position of topo-
graphic draftsman in the Geological Survey during a period of seven
years; that upon applying for a short leave of absence she was transferred
to a position on a per diem, when actually employed, basis with the posi-
tive assurance of her superior officers that such trar-er would not deprive
her of her status in the classified civil ser-vice; that she did apply for
restoration to duty prior to the expiration of the time during which she
was advised that she would he eligible for such return to duty and then
learned that she had been misinformed and as a result of the action of her
superiors she lost her civil-service status; that she later obtained a position,
of draftsman in the Bui-eau of Lands in the Philippines and while serving
in that capacity was detailed for work under the Director of Coast Surveys
at Manila who has certified to her faithfulness, efficiency and attention to.
duty; and that in view of these circumstances it is believed that her rein-
statement in the Coast and Geodetic Survey is fully) justified and desirable
in the interests of good administration and wvill remedy a manifest injustice.
which has been done in her case.
