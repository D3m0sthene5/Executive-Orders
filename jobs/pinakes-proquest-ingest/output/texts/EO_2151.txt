2/6/
Executive Orber
Edward D. White may be reinstated as a letter carrier in the Oakland,
California, post office, without regard to the length of time he has been
separated from the service. He wvas appointed substitute carrier in the
Oakland office on September i, 1902; was made a regular carrier on
October i, 1903, and served until January 31, 1913. The Postmaster
General states that as Mr. White is intelligent and wvas a very competent
and faithful employe while in the service, the Department will not inter-
pose any objection to his reinstatement in the service. The Civil Service
Commission does not recommend the issuance of this order.
WOODROW WILSON
THE WHITE HOUSE,
23 March, 1915.
[Nc). 2151.]
