In the matter of the compensation for the members of the commission
to investigate questions pertaining to the issuance of stocks and bonds by
railroad corporations, appointed by the President, under and by virtue of
Section 16 of the Act of June 18, 1910, (36 Stat. 556) entitled, "An act
to create a Commerce Court and to amend the act entitled 'An Act to
regulate commerce', approved February 4, 1887, as heretofore amended
and for other purposes", I hereby order and direct that there shall be
awarded to Arthur T. Hadley, Frederick N. Judson and Frederick Strauss
as compensation for their services upon the above named commission,
$2,000 each, and that there shall be awarded to W~alter L. Fisher and
Baithasar H. Meyer the suim of $1,5o0 each for their services as members
of such commission. The difference in the amounts thus awarded is due
to the fact that during a part of the time when these services were
rendered by Mr. Fisher and Mr. Meyer, they wcre serving as regular
officers of the Federal Government and receiving an annual salary theref or.
