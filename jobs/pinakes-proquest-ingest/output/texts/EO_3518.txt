Milton S. Wolfenden may be appointed skilled laborer in the Mail Equip-
ment Shops, Post Office Department, without examination, under the Civil
Service Rules.
The Postmaster General recommends the appointment and states: "Mr.
Wolfeinden is almost totally blind, and on account of his great affliction the
Department desires to offer him permanent employment as a stringer of mail
bags, for which work he is well fitted. He has been ei 'ployed on this work in a
temporary capacity since January 3, 1921, and is rendering good service."
The Civil Service Commission submits the case without prejudice, stating
that it is without jurisdiction.
WARREN G HARDING
THES WHITE HOUSE,
