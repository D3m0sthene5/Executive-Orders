Mrs. IKatherine F. R'ogrers may lbe reinstated in the classified service
of the Treasury Department without regard to the length of time she has
been separated therefrom.
This order is issued on the recommendation of the Secretary of the
Treasury, who states that Mrs. Rogers served very efficiently in the
Treasury Department for ten years, resigning her position in 1910, and
that he believes on account of her experience and her high efficiency, her
services wvill he of real value to the Department. Mrs. Rogers is the
daughter of the late Mr. James N. Fitzpatrick, for many years executive
clerk in the Division of Appointments of the Department and later
Assistant Chief of that Division, and one of the most efficient and valu-
able employees the Department ever had. Mr. Fitzpatrick has recently
died, and the burden of the support of his widow and herself falls mainly
upon Mrs. Rogers.
In view of the facts the Civil Service Commission concurs in the
recommendation for an executive order.
