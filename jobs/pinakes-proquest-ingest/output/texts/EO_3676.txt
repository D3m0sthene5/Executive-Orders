Executive Orber.
Employees of the Federal Government and of the District of Columbia,
who, under the terms of Executive Order of November 4, 1921, were granted a
holiday on November i 1, 1921, incident to the burial of the unknown and
unidentified American Soldier in the Arlington National Cemetery, shall not
lose their pay for that day.
WARREN G HARDING
THE WHITE HOUSE,
May 19, 1922.
