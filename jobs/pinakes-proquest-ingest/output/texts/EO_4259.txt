Executive Order~
The height requirement for the Post Office Service and for the position
of guard in the Immigration Service is waived in favor of Mr. Angelo L. Manghise
who on account of his height was refused enlistment in the Army, Navy and
Marine Corps but who served eighteen months as an ambulance driver for the
American Red Cross in France during the World War.
The Civil Service Commission was unable to recommend the issuance of this
order.
CALVIN COOLIDGE
THE WHITE HouSE,
July 1, 1925.
