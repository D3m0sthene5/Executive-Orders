ALASKA
It is hereby ordered that the following described land, reserved by Executive
order of June 21, 1890, known as U. S. Reserve for Public Common, as shown on
U. S. Survey No. 1473 of Sitka, Alaska, be, and the same is hereby eliminated from
said reservation for a public common and reserved for U. S. school purposes under
the jurisdiction of the U. S. Bureau of Education, to wit:
Beginning at a point common to Corners No. 1 U. S. Reserve for Public Com-
mon and No. 5 of U. S. Reserve No. 2 U. S. School, thence S. 410 08' E. 146.30 feet
along line 1-2 of the U. S. Reserve for Public Common; thence S. 580 19' W. 121 feet
along the line of a picket fence to the corner of the fence; thence N. 420 30' W. 108
feet along the line of a picket fence and parallel to a cement sidewalk which leads
from the corner of the picket fence to the Indian Village, to a point on line 6-7
U. S. Reserve for Public Common, from which Meander Corner No. 14 U. S. Survey
No. 1473 bears S. 350 00' W. 57.28 feet; thence N. 350 E. 91.40 feet to Corner No.
7 U. S. Reserve for Public Common; thence N. 790 16' E. 17.54 feet, to Corner No. 8
U. S. Reserve for Public Common; thence N. 350 00' E. 17.50 feet to the point of
beginning, containing approximately 15,717 square feet.
