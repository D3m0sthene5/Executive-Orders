Executive Order
+
CALIFORNIA,
It is hereby ordered that all the public lands of the United States, in
the State of California, situated within the limits as below described, be
and the same are hereby withdrawn from settlement, location, entry, or .
sale until otherwise directed, and reserved for the purposes contemplated
by the act of December 19, 1913 (Public No. 41):
T.1S., R.14 E, M.D. M., Secs. 31 to 36, inclusive; :
T.2S, R14 E,M. D.M.,, Secs. 1, 2, 3, 4, 7, 8 and 9;
T.1S., R. 15 E, M. D. M., Secs. 23 to 36, inclusive;
T.2S.,R.15 E, M. D. M., Secs. 4, 5 and 6;
T.1S., R. 16 E., M. D. M., Secs. 19 to 34, inclusive.
WOODROW WILSON
. Tue Wuirte House,
or January, 1914.
