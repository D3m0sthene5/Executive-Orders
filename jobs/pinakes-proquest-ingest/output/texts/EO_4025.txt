Executive O~rder
ALASKA
SITKA NATIONAL CEMTERY
WHEREAS, by Executive Order dated June 21, 1890, a certain tract of land
containing ten acres situated at Sitka, Alaska, was withdrawn from the public
domain for use as a Military and Naval Cemetery; and
WHE~REAS, it has now been determined that a smaller tract of land is
sufficient for said purpose;
Now, THE~REFORE~, by virtue of the power and authority vested in and
conferred upon me by the laws of the United States in that behalf made and
provided, the said Executive Order of June 21, 1890, insofar as it withdrew the
ten acre tract at Sitka, Alaska, is hereby revoked and superseded; and it is
ordered that the following tract of land at Sitka, Alaska, be and the same is
hereby withdrawn from settlement, location, sale, entry or other disposition,
and reserved and set aside for cemetery purposes, to be known as the Sitka
National Cemetery, Alaska :
Beginning at Cor. #i, which is a point in common with Cor. ##2 of
U. S. Sur. #14.73, and also in common with Cor. #9 of U. S. Sur. #407;
thence running N. 15°45 W., 9.08 chs., to Cor. #2; thence. N. 630 i8'
E., 4.46 chs. to Cor. #3, which latter is a point in common with Cor.
#7 of U. S. Survey #407; thence along the boundary of U. S. Survey
#407, S. 15° 45' E., 9.08 chs. to Cor. #4, which is a point in common
with Cor. #8 of U. S. Survey #407; thence continuing along the
boundary of U. S. Survey #407, S. 630° i8l W., 4.46 chs., to the place
of beginning, containing 3.98 acres, more or less.
