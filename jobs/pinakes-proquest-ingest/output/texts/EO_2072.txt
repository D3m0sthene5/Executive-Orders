Mr. David A. Gates may be reinstated to a position of internal
revenue agent in the Internal Revenue Service without reference to the
period of his separation from the service.
Mr. Gates was appointed an internal revenue agent on February 1i,
1895, was made chief of agents on June 20, 1906, agent in charge of
division on June r, 1908, and resigned July io, 1909, to accept a position
with the Arkansas Tax Commission, of which he has been chairman.
He served satisfactorily in every capacity in which he wvas employed.
This order is issued on the recommendation of the Secretary of the
Treasury and the Commissioner of Internal Revenue with the concurrence
of the Civil Service Commission because of Mr. Gates' long and valuable
experience, his efficient service, and technical knowledge of th. internal
revenue laws.
WOODROW WILSON
THE WHITE HOUSE,
31 October, 1914.
