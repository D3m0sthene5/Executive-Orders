Erecutive Order
DISMISSING THE ChARGES PREFERRED BY W. S. FLEMING AGAINST THE
JUDGE OF THE UNITED STATES COURT FOR CHINA.
A full investigation of the. charges preferred by W. S. Fleming against the
judge of the United States Court for China having been made are determined
to be unfounded and are, therefore, dismissed.
The judge of the court is hereby directed to return to his post of official
duty at the earliest practicable moment.
WARREN G HARD ING
THES WHnTE HOUSE~,
June 23, 1922.
