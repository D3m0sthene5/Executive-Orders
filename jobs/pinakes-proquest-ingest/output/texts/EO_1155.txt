Executive order.
Mr. Henry S. Graves may be reinstated in the classified service in the
Department of Agriculture, without regard to the fact that he has been
separated from the service more than one year, in order that he may be
appointed Forester and Chief of the Forest Service of the Department of
Agriculture. He was formerly in the classified service as Assistant Chief
of the Division of Forestry, having been appointed as such through comn-
petitive examination.
WM H TAFT
THE WHITE HOUSE.
Jan uary 13, 1910.
