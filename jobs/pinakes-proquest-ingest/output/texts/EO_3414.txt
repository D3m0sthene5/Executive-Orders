Executive Order.
There are hereby excluded from the operation of the Retirement Act of
May 22, 1920) noneducational employees in the Reclamation Service, inter-
mittently and irregularly employed, and educational employees on daily rates,
similarly employed, and the continuance of the present practice of making no
deductions from their pay is hereby approved. This action is deemed necessary
because of the intermittent character of the employment and the exceedingly
frequent changes in personnel.
WOODROW WILSON
THTE WHITEi HOUSE,
