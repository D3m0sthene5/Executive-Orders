Exrecutive Order.
Mr. John Moynihan may be reemployed as an unclassified laborer in
the post office at Kansas City, Missouri, notwithstanding the fact that he
has been separated from the service for more than one year.
Mr. Moynihan was appointed an unclassified laborer on January 23,
1902, and resigned on September 30, 1907, as a result of an accidental
injury received while in the performance of his duty. He wvas incapacitated
for several years but has recovered sufficiently to assume his duties. His
reemployment is recommended by the postmaster and the First Assistant
Postmaster General on account of his efficient services, and the Civil
Service Commission concurs in recommending this order.
WM H TAFT
THE WHITE HOUSE,
January 24, 1913.
