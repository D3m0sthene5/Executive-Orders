By virtue of the authority vested in me by law, the Executive Order of
October 22, 1916, providing for the payment of interest on deposit money
orders issued in the Canal zone, is hereby amended to read as follows:
i. Deposit money orders issued by the Canal Zone Postal Service shall
bear interest at the rate of three-fourths of one percentum for each period of
three full calendar months, from January i, 1923, or subsequent date of issue.
Interest shall be payable when the order is paid but shall not accrue on any
order for more than three years.
2. On deposit money orders issued prior to January 1, 1923, interest will
be paid as specified in Executive Order of October 22, 19 16, except that interest
for the period or periods of three full calendar months, ending subsequent to
December 31, 1922, shah be paid at the rate of three fourths of one percentumi
for each of such periods.
3. The Governor of The Panama Canal is authorized to prescribe such
detailed rules and regulations as may be necessary to carry out this order.
WARREN G HARD ING
TanII WHITE HoUSE,
Dec. 6, 1922.
