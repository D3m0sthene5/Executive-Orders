Executive ether.
In accordance with the provisions of section 2253 of the Revised
Statutes of the United States, and by virtue of the authority therein
given, it is hereby ordered:
That townships fifty-six, fifty-seven and nifty-eight North, of Range
ninety-six West, in the State of Wyoming be detached from the Buffalo
land district and attached to and made a part of the Lander land district,
and the business and archives pertaining to the lands within said towvn-
ships be transferred from the land office at Buffalo to the land office at
Lander.
WOODROW WILSON
THE WHITE HOUSE,
19 July, 1916.
