Miss Maude H. Bundy may be appointed to a position in the Gov-
ernment Printing Office, without reference to the civil service rules.
Miss Bundy is the daughter of the late Alvin A. Bunidy, a Civil War
veteran, and a former employe of the Government Printing Office. He
was appointed to a position in that office on May 4, 1881, and continued
in such employment until the time of his death, August 29, 1910. The
reports show that his record was good.
In consideration of the long and faithful service of Mr. Bundy in the
military and civil service of the Federal Government, the Public Printer
recommends that an Executive Order be issued authorizing her appoint-
ment.
The Civil Service Commission does not concur in the recommendation.
WOODROW WILSON
THE WHITE HOUSE,
14 October, 1915.
[No. 2256.]
