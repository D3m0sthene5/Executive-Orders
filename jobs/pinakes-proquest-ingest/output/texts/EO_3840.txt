Executive Order.
That part of the Executive order of May io, 1921r, which pertains to the
age limit of candidates for the Presidential postmastership examinations is
hereby waived so as to permit the examination papers of Isaac I. Bargen, candi-
date for the position of postmaster at Mountain Lake, Minnesota, to be rated
by the Civil Service Commission. He has served as postmaster since 1902.
The Civil Service Commission concurs in recommending this order.
WARREN G HARD ING
THE X WHITE H SE,
May ii, 1923.
