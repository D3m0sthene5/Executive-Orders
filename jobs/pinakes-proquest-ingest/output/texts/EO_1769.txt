Executive Orber.
Schedule A of the Civil Service Rules specifying classified positions
which may be filled without examination or upon noncompetitive exami-
nation is hereby amended by adding at the end thereof a new subdivision
to be numbered XII, as follows:
XII. INTERSTATE COMMERCE COMMISSION.
i. Three experts to be members of an advisory board under the act
of March 1, 1913, providing for the valuation of the property
of carriers.
2. Five members of a board of engineers under said act.
WOODROW WILSON
THE WHITE HOUSE,
April 23, 1913.
