EIxecutive Orber.
Mr. Nathan C. Grover may be reinstated as chief engineer of the
Land Classification Board of the United States Geological Survey without
regard to the length of time that he has been separated from the service.
The Secretary of the Interior states that the position is of a confiden-
tial nature requiring matured judgment and ripened engineering experi-
ence ; that it is practically that of final advisor to the Director of the
Survey on certain engineering matters ; that the highest qualifications
obtainable are required, and that it is especially essential that the position
he filled by an engineer of known conservatism that he may have at the
outset the confidence of the Director and the Secretary. Mr. Grover
was eligible for reinstatement under the rules until June 4, 1910. This
recommendation is concurred in by the United States Civil Service
Commission.
WM H TAFT
THE WHITE HOUSE,
February 27, 1911.
[No. 13 10.]
