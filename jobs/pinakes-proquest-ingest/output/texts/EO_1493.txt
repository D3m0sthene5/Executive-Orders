In the matter of compensation for the members of the Commission
to investigate the matter of employers' liability and workmen's compensa-
tion, appointed by the President under and by virtue of the provisions of
the Joint Resolution of June 25, 1910 (36 Stats., 884), 1 hereby order and
direct that there shall be awarded to Daniel L. Cease as compensation for
his services upon the above named Commission the sum of $3000.
William C. Brown, the other member of the Commission appointed
by the President, has declined to accept any compensation for his services
on the Commission.
WM H TAFT
THE WHITE HOUSE,
