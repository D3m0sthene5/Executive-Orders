Executive Otet.
Mr. Ralph R. Mulcare may be reinstated as letter carrier in the post
office at Brooklyn, N. Y., without regard to the year limit prescribed by
the civil service rules. He resigned as carrier, after having served satis-
factorily for thirteen years, by reason of ill health on March i6, 1909, with
the assurance that he would be reinstated if he recovered his health within
a year, which wvas in accord with the policy of the Post Office Department
and in the interests of efficient service. His application for reinstatement
wvas made within a year but owing to a change of postmasters and pressure
of business, wvas not forwarded in season to the Department as the post-
master had intended. The Civil Service Commission joins the Department
in recommending this order.
W M H TAFT
THE WHITE HOUSE,
April1 24, 1911.
