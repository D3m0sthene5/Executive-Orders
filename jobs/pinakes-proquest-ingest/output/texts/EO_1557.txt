Executive Orcer,
It is hereby ordered that a tract of land containing about three acres,
on the left bank of Tanana River, just above its confluence with Delta
River, Alaska, in approximate latitude 640 09' N. and longitude 145° 5' W.,
and hereinafter described, be withdrawn from settlement, location or entry,
and that the same be and is hereby reserved for military purposes, subject
to any existing rights thereto.
Description :
Beginning at a point where a stake has marked the northwest corner
of the homestead claim o~f Alonzo L. Maxey, thence running south 350
feet to a point, thence east 400 feet to a point, thence north 290 feet to a
point at the bank of Tanana River, thence westerly with the shore of the
river to the point of beginning, to include the buildings of the McCarty
U. S. Telegraph Station.
WM H TAFT
TIlL, WHITE HOUSE,
July' 3, 1912.
