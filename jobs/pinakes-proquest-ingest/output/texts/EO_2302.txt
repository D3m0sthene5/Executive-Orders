It appearing proper and necessary, in the interest of equal oppor-
tunity and good administration, that all of such excluded lands subject to
disposition should be restored to settlement in advance of entry, it is hereby
ordered, pursuant to the authority reposed in me by the Act of September
30, 1913 (38 Stat., 113), that such lands, subject to valid rights and the
provisions of existing withdrawals, shall be opened and become subject to
settlement only, under the general provisions of the homestead laws, at
and after, but not before, 9 o'clock a. in., standard time, on the sixty-third
day after the date of this order; and to entry and other disposition, under
anyr public land law applicable thereto, at and after, but not before, 9 o'clock
a. in., standard time, on the ninety-first day after said date : Provided, that
the rights of the State of Idaho under the provisions of the Act of March
3, 1893 (2; Stat., 592), shall not be abridged insofar as any of such lands
are affected thereby. Unsurveyed lands are not subject to the provisions
of said Act of March 3, 1893, but in the absence of a prior valid adverse
right, the preference accorded the State of Idaho thereby, where the town-
ship has been surveyed and the plat thereof filed while the lands were
reserved for forestry purposes, will attach immediately upon the restoration
of such lands to selection and entry under the general land laws of the
United States as herein provided and continue for sixty days.
