Mr. John K. Robinson may be reinstated as a printer in the Weather
Bureau, without reference to the period of his separation from the service.
Mr. Robinson was an enlisted man and noncommisioned officer of the
Signal Corps of the Army for a number of years, and when the meteoro-
logical division of the Signal Corps became a part of the Weather Bureau
he was transferred to the Weather Bureau and remained for several years,
when he was discharged on false charges without proper opportunity to
defend himself.
The Acting Secretary of Agriculture and the Civil Service Commis-
sion recommend the reinstatement.
