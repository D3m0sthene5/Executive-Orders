MICHIGAN-MARQUETrE NATIONAL FORESTS
MICHIGAN
Under authority of the Act of Congress of June 4, 1897 (30 Stat.,
I I) and upon recommendation of the Secretary of Agriculture, it is hereby
ordered that on and after July 1, 1915, all the lands included within the
boundaries of the Marquette National Forest, Michigan, as fixed and
defined by proclamation of February io, 1909 (No. 838), be transferred to
and administered as a part of the Michigan National Forest.
It is intended by this Executive Order, for economy of administra-
tion, to merge into one National Forest, thereafter to be known as the
Michigan National Forest, all the lands which on July 1, 1915, are within
the Marquette and the Michigan National Forests, and the boundaries
of the Michigan National Forest, as they exist at that date, are hereby
modified accordingly.
This Executive Order is not intended to add to the Michigan National
Forest any lands which on July 1, 1915, are not embraced within the Mar-
quette National Forest, nor to release from reservation for National Forest
purposes any lands at that date within the Marquette National Forest.
WOODROW WILSON
THE WHITE HOUSE,
6 April, 1915.
