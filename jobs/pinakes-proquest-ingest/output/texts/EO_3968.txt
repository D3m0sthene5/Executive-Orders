Relating to Pardons, Paroles, the Remission of Fines and
Forfeitures, and Kindred Subjects.
By virtue of the authority vested in me by Section 7 of the Act of Congress
approved August 24, 1912, known as " The Panama Canal Act," as amended by
the Act of Congress of September 21, 1922, I hereby establish the following
Order for the Canal Zone :'
Section i. The Goer of The Panama Canal is hereby empowered to
grant pardons, paroles, and reprieves for offenses against the laws, orders and ~
regulations of the Canal Zone, and to commute sentences and remit fines and' '
forfeitures. He may estab "sh by order a system for the pardon, parole, reprieve,
disposition, or treatment o prisoners, and alter or amend the same from time
to time.
Section 2. The Governor byore may establish regulations governing
the labor and employment of prisoners and provide awards for their good be-
havior, and may make regulations upon other matters affecting the welfare of
the prisoners and their management in the penal institutions of the Canal zone,
and may alter and amend such regulations from time to time.
Section 3. Where the Governor issues an order for parole of a person against
whom judgment of conviction has been given he shall specify therein the terms
and conditions upon which the parole is granted; and if at any time the Governor
is satisfied that such person has violated his parole the Governor shall have
power to revoke the order of parole and to order that such person shall be com-
mitted to serve the full term or unexpired term of the sentence imposed upon
him, even though the period of time covered by such sentence may have elapsed
before such revocation.
Section 4. The existing rules and regulations upon the subjects mentioned
in this Order shall continue in force until altered or amended by the Governor.
Section 5. All Executive Orders heretofore promulgated in conflict with
this Order are hereby repealed.
Section 6. This Order shall take effect thirty days after the date hereof.
