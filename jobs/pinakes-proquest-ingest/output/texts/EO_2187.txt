Schedule A, Subdivision IV, of the civil service rules is hereby
amended by the addition of a paragraph as follows:
14. Watchmen on fortifications under construction when the
positions are filled by men wvho have served at least two enlistments
in the Army, Navy, or Marine Corps.
This amendment, which has the effect of excepting from the require-
ment of examination the appointment of watchmen under the conditions
mentioned, is recommended by the War Department and the Civil Service
Commission in recognition of the fact that watchmen on fortifications
under construction should be men who are alert, vigorous, courageous,
patriotic, and of unquestioned loyalty, their service being regarded by the
Department as almost military in character.
WOODROW WILSON
THE WHITEr HOUSE,
