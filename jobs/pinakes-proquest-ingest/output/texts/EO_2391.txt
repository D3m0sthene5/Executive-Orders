Mrs. Ada E. B. Snyder may be appointed an Operative in the Bureau
of Engraving and Printing without complying with the requirements of
the Civil Service Commission.
This order is issued on the recommendation of the Secretary of the
Treasury, who states that Mrs. Snyder's husband, William H. M. Snyder,
wvas employed in the Bureau for a number of years and has recently be-
come insane. His wife and twvo children are without means of support,
and it is believed that Mr. Snyder's record in the Bureau justifies the
waiving of the rules in order that his wife may have employment to sup-
port herself and two small children.
The Civil Service Commission does not concur in the recommendation.
WOODROW WILSON
THE WHITE HOUSE,
31 May, 1916.
