The year limit upon eligibility for reinstatement is waived to permit the
reinstatement of Dr, William S. Newman as veterinary inspector, B~ureau of
Animal Industry, Department of Agriculture.
Dr, Newman was regularly employed in the position of veterinary inspector
from February 1 5, 1908, to April 10, 1919, when he resigned. The Secretary
of Agriculture bases his recommendation for reinstatement upon the fact that
there is urgent need for the services of a veterinary inspector for assignment
to some class of work upon which Dr. Newman was previously engaged. lHe was
one of the bewst field inspectors in the service, and since his separattion from the
Government service has continued in work in the line of his profession thereby
maintaining his efficiency. The Civil Service concurs in the recommendation.
