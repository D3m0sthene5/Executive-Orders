Executive order
ALASKA ROAD COMMISSION RESERVE
WHEREAS, By Executive Order dated May 29, i926, a certain tract of land
containing 1.378 acres, more or less, situated at Fort Davis, Alaska, was withdrawn
from the public domain for the use of the Alaska Road Commission; and
WHEREAS, it has now been discovered that a modification of Executive Order
No. 3574 is desirable:
Now, THEREFORE, pursuant to authority conferred upon me by the act
of Congress approved June 25, 1910 (36 Stat., 847), entitled "An Act to authorize
the President of the United States to make withdrawals of public lands in certain
cases," as amended by the act of Congress approved August 24, 1912 (37 Stat., 497),
the said Executive Order of May 29, 1926 in so far as it withdrew the 1.378 acre
tract at Fort Davis, Alaska; is hereby modified and .superseded; and it is hereby
ordered that the following described lands and buildings thereon, forming part of
the old military reservation of Fort Davis, Alaska, transferred to the Department
of the Interior by.Executive Order No. "3574, approved November 4, 1921, be' and
the same are hereby, withdrawn from settlement, location, sale or entry, and reserved
for the use of the Alaska Road Commission in the construction and maintenance
of military and post roads, bridges and trails in Alaska:
Beginning at a point on the southern boundary of the Military
Reservation of Fort Davis, Alaska, which bears south 72 deg. 35' east,
1720 feet from 4" x 4" Post marked U.S.M.R. at the southwest
corner of said reservation, and extending thence north 17 deg. 25'
east 300 feet; thence south 72 deg. 35' east 400 feet; thence south 17
deg. 25' west 300 feet; thence north 72 deg. 35' west 400 feet to the
place of beginning. Area 2.755 acres, more or less.
