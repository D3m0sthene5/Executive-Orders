I hereby create under the direction of the Committee on Public Information,
created by executive order of April 14th, 1917, a Service Bureau, for the purpose
of establishing a central office in the city of Washington, where complete infor-
mation records mnay be available as to the function, location and personnel of
all government agencies,
I hereby ask the several departments of Government, when so requested
lby the Chairman of the Committee on Public Information, to detail such person
or persons as may be necessary in gathering the information needed and carrying
on the work of the Bureau so far as it relates to such departments; to give
opportunity to the director of the Bureau, or such person as lie may designate,
to secure information from time to time for the purpose of keeping the records
tip to date; to supply the director of the Bureau on form cards, furnished by
him, with information as to personnel, function and location.
WOODROW WILSON
THE WHITE HOUSE,
March'ch'9, 1918.
