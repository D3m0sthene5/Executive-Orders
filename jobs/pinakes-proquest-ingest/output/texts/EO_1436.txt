In pursuance of the provisions of Section 1 of the Act of Congress
approved July 5, 1884, entitled "An Act to provide for the disposal of
abandoned and useless military reservations," the Fort Assinniboine Mili-
tary Reservation, and the whole thereof, situated in the County of Choteau,
State of Montana, which reservation is fully described in General Orders
No. 59 of the War Department, dated April 18, 1908, is hereby placed
under the control of the Secretary of the Interior for disposition as pro-
vided in said act, or as may be otherwise provided by law, the same, in the
opinion of the President, having become useless for military purposes.
WM H TAFT
THE WHITE HOUSE,
November 20, 1911.
