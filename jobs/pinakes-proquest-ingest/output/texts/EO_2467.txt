WHEREAS, it has come to my notice that the gasoline boat " BLANCHE
W" has been condemned and forfeited to the United States for violation
of the Immigration Lawvs under the provisions of Section 10 of the Act
of July 5, 1884, ch. 2420 (23 Stat. 15 );
AND WHIEREAS, the Secretary of Labor desires to have this boat
turned over to the Immigration officials at Seattle, Washington, for the
official use of his Department;
Now, THEREFOR~E, I, WO)oDow WILSON, President of the United
States, do hereby authorize and direct the United States Marshal for the
Western District of Washington, or any other persons whom it may con-
cern, to turn over this boat to such officer in the Immigration Service at
Seattle, Washington, as may he regularly designated by the Secretary of
Labor, for the official use of such Immigration Service, upon the payment
of the proper charges and expenses.
WOODROW WILSON
THE WHITE HOUSE,
9 October, 1916.
[No. 2467.]
