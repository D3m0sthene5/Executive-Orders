Mrs. Dorothy C. Bennett, who resigned February 2, 1920, from the classified
service of the War Department as a stenographer and typist, may be reappointed
to the classified position of private secretary in the United States Customs Court,
New York, without regard to the length of her separation from the service.
This action is desired by Justice Cline of the Court. The Civil Service Com-
mission does not concur.
For good of the service.
CALVIN COOLIDGE
THE WHITE HOUSE,
October 12, 1.928.
