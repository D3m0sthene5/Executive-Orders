AL~ASKAN TIMBER RESERVE No. 1.
Executive Orders No. 2217, approved June 22, 1915 and No. 2226, approved
July 31, 19j,5, creating and amending Alaskan Timber Reserve No. 1, respec-
tively, are hereby further amended to include the timber upon the following
described areas, additional to the areas described in said orders, subject to the
conditions, limitations and provisions set forth therein :
SEWARD TO KNIT{ RIVER
A tract of land five miles wide on each side of the right of way of the Gov-
ernment railroad, commencing at Seward, on Resurrection Bay, and extending
along the right of way of said railroad to the left limit of Knik River; including
within this reservation all tracts or parcels of land which have heretofore or
which may hereafter be excluded from the Chugach National Forest, Alaska,
under the Act of June 11, 1906 (34. Stsat., 233); provided, however, that said
timber reservation shall not disturb or interfere with any vested rights which
shall have accrued at or prior to the creation thereof.
WOODROW WILSON
THE WHITE HOUSE,
