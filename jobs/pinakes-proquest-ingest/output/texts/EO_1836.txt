Executive ether,
In accordance with the provisions of Section 2253 of the Revised
Statutes of the United States and by virtue of the authority therein
given, it is hereby ordered that the boundaries of the Hailey land district
in the State of Idaho be reestablished as follows:
Beginning at the southern boundary of Idaho where the range line
between ranges nine (g) and ten (ro) east of the Boise Meridian intersects
the same ; thence north along said range line, allowing for the proper
offsets, to the left bank of the Salmon river; thence up the left bank of
the Salmon river to a point opposite where the divide between Horse and
Squaw creeks intersects the same; thence northeasterly along said divide
and the divide between the drainage of the Salmon and Clearwvater rivers
to the intersection of same with the boundary line between Idaho and
Montana; thence southeasterly along said State boundary line to its inter-
section with the range line between ranges twenty-nine (29) and thirty
(30) east; thence south along said range line, allowing for the proper
offsets, to the southern boundary of Idaho; thence west along said
boundary to its intersection with the range line between ranges nine (g)
and ten (io) east,-the place of beginning.
WOODROW WILSON
THE WHITE HOUSE,
October r, 1913.
[No. 1836.]
