Under authority of the Act of Congress of June 4, x897, (30 Stat. Ix), and
upon recommendation of the Secretary of Agriculture, it is hereby ordered that
on and after July i, 1922, all the lands included within the western division of
the Sevier National Forest, as fixed and defined by Proclamation of December 23,
1910, (No.zi ri) , and Executive Order of November 2 7, 1918, (No. 3009), be,

Forest and the lands now within the Dixie National Forest; and the boundaries
of the Sevier National Forest and the D ie ~inaal Forest as they now exist
