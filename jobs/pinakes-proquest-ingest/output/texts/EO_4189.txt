Mrs. Anna T. Gibson may be appointed to a clerkship in the post office at
New York City without regard to the requirements of the civil service rules. Mrs.
Gibson is the widow of William A. Gibson, for thirty-seven years a faithful em-
ployee of the New York City post office who, at his death, left her with one depend-
ent child. This order is issued upon the recommendation of the Postmaster
General. The Civil Service Commission is unable to concur.
Done for charity.
