AUTHORIZING THE ALIEN PROPERTY CUSTODIAN TO SELL CERTAIN
PROPERTY AT PRIVATE SALE
By virtue of the authority vested in me by an Act of Congress entitled " An
act to define, regulate, and punish trading with the enemy and for other Pur-
poses", approved October 6th, 1917, known as the "Trading with the Enemy
Act", and the amendment to such act embodied in "An Act making appropria-
tions to supply urgent deficiencies in the appropriations for the fiscal year
ending June 30, 1918, and prior fiscal years on account of war expenses and for
other purposes", approved March 28, 1918, and the amendment to such Act
embodied in "An Act making appropriations to supply deficiencies in appropria-
tions for the fiscal year ending June 30, 191, and prior fiscal years on account
of war expenses, and for other purposes", approved November , 1918, to make
certain orders such as that herein contained, I hereby, in the public interest,
make the following determination, order, rule and regulation:
The Alien Propert Custodian is hereby authorized to sell at private sale,
at such time and place as to him or to his authorized agent may seem advisable,
without public or other advertisement, certain real estate now being held and
administered by himt as such Alien Property Custodian, and heretofore, upon
requirement conveyed, transferred, assigned and delivered to him as the property
of F. C. M. KUNZ and JEANETTE KUNZ, residing in Austria, whom he did
after investigation determine to be enemies, within the purview of said Act as
amended, which said real estate is described as follows, to-wit:
All that certain lot, tract or parcel of land and premises, situate, lying
and being in Ventnor City, in the County of Atlantic and State of New
Jersey, bounded and described as follows :
Beginning in the southerly line of Ventnor Avenue (said Ventnor
Avenue being eighty feet wide) where the same is intersected by the easterly
line of Newport Avenue and extending: thence () southwardly in the
easterly line of Newport Avenue one hundred and sixteen feet; thence (2)
eastwardly and parallel with Ventnor Avenue fifty feet; thence (3) north-
wardly and parallel with Newport Avenue one hundred and sixteen feet to
the southerly line of Ventnor Avenue (eighty feet wide) ; thence () west-
wardly along the southerly line of Ventnor Avenue fifty feet to the place of
beginning.
My reasons for the foregoing determination, order, rule and regulation are:
(a) The property is unimproved and produces no income;
(b) An advantageous offer for the purchase of said property has been
received by the Alien Property Custodian;
(c) An attempted sale at public auction would be a useless formality and
would involve unnecessary expenses, delay and inconvenience which should be
avoided.
WARREN G HARDING
THE WHITE HOUSE,
April 9, 1923.
