range eleven east; thence east on the township line between town-

ship seventeen north, range eleven east; thence north on the range
line between ranges eleven and twelve east, to the northwest corner
of township twenty north, range twelve east, on the fifth Standard
Parallel north; thence west on the fifth Standard Parallel north be-
tween townships twenty and twenty-one north, to the southwest
corner of township twenty-one north, range eleven east,
and that the business and archives pertaining to the lands affected by the
change in ho undary be transferred to the respective land offices at North
