Eecutive Orbet,
Mr. W. B. Johnson may be reinstated to an appropriate position in
the service of the General Land Office without reference to the fact that
he has been separated for more than a year.
This order is made on the recommendation of the Secretary of the
Interior and the Commissioner of the General Land Office, but without
the concurrence of the Civil Service Commission.
WM H TAFT
THE WHITE HousE,
December 3, 1912.
[No. 1653j.]
