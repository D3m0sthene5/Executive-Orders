Siletz Indian Reservation.
Executive order No. 1228 of July 13, 1910, by which the public lands
within the boundaries of the former Siletz Indian Reservation, located in
the State of Oregon, were temporarily withdrawn for the purpose of classi-
fying said lands and pending the enactment of legislation, is hereby modi-
fied to exclude from the operation thereof lands within the Siuslaw
National Forest created by the proclamation of March 2, 1907, 34 Statutes
at Large, page 3310, and Executive order No. 86o, of June 30, 1908:
Provided, That nothing herein contained is intended in any way to alter,
amend or revoke said proclamation of March 2, 1907, and Executive order
No. 86o of June 30, 1908.
WOODROW WILSON
THE WHITE HOUSE,
19 July, r915.
