Executive Tbe
Mrs. Susan T. Waite may be reinstated to a permanent clerkship in the Bureau
of Labor Statistics, Department of Labor, without regard to the time limit on rein-
statement under the civil service rules.
Mrs. Waite is at the head of the register for statistical clerk but may not be
certified for permanent appointment because of the law of the apportionment. In
addition to some two years of temporary employment she served as a permanent
clerk in the Pension Bureau from December 16, 1901, to June 1, 1907. Her hus-
band, who was a Government employee for more than twenty-four years, is now in
a hospital a hopeless invalid and she is the sole support of two minor children.
This Order is recommended by th~e Secretary of Labor. The Civil Service
Comnmissioni interposes no objection.
Done for charity.
