Executive ether.
Samuel Z. C. Westerfield of Nebraska, now a messenger with the
British Arbitration Claims Commission may be appointed without exami-
nation to an assistant messengership in the Department of State. The
position now filled by Mr. Westerfield is abolished by legislation effective
July 1 st, and because of his efficiency it is deemed in the interest of the
public service to continue him in the employ of the Department of State.
WOODROW WILSON
THE WHITE HOUSE,
17 April, 1915.
