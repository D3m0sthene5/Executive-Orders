Under authority contained in the act of Congress approved June 25, 1910
(36 Stat., 847), as amended by the act of August 24, 1912 (37 Stat., 497), and
subject to the conditions, provisions, and limitations of said acts,' it is hereby
ordered that the public lands in T. 15 5., R. 4 E., S. B. M., ,' California, be and
the same are hereby withdrawn from settlement, location, sale, 'entry, and all
forms of appropriation, pending a resurvey of said township under authority
contained in the act _of Congress approved March 3, 1909 (35 Stat., 8415); this
withdrawal to remain in full force and effect from the date hereof until the resurvey
is duly approved and accepted and the approved' plat thereof is officially filed in
the proper United States 'local land office, unless another date be fixed by the
Commissioner of the General. Land Office, whereupon this withdrawal will become
of no effect.
