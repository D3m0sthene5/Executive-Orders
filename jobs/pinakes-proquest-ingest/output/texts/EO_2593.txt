Withdrawing certain public lands within the St. Francis River Basin, in the State of Arkansas.
Pending preliminary examination and survey, with a view to ascertaining
the practicability of improving the St. Francis River in the States of Arkansas and
Missouri, and of incidentally reclaiming by drainage the contiguous lands, pur-
suant to an Act of Congress making an appropriation for that purpose, approved
July 27, 1916 (3 Stat., 409), and in contemplation of any further legislation
that may be enacted in connection therewith, it is hereby ordered that all
public lands in the following enumerated townships situate in the State of
Arkansas be and the same are hereby withdrawn from settlement, location,
sale or entry, pursuant to the authority conferred by and subject to all the
provisions, limitations, exceptions and conditions of the Act of Congress ap-
proved June 25, 1910 (36 Stat., 847) entitled " An Act to authorize the President
of the United States to make withdrawals of public lands in certain cases", as
amended by the Act of Congress approved August 24, 1912 (37 Stat., 497):
