Executive Orber.
In accordance with the provisions of Section 2218 of the Revised
Statutes of the United States, it is hereby ordered that the office of
United States Surveyor General of the State of Florida, now located at
Tallahassee, Florida, be, and the same is, hereby discontinued and abolished
from and after June 3othi, 1906.
THEODORE ROOSEVELT
THE WHLITE HOUSE,
April 27/1, 1906.
