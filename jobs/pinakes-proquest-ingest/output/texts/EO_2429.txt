PAYETTE NATIONAL FOREST
IDAHO
Under authority of the Act of Congress approved June 4, 1897 (30
Stat., 1 1, at 34 and 36), and on the recommendation of the Secretary of
Agriculture, it is hereby ordered that the boundaries of the Payette
National Forest, Idaho, be modified by eliminating therefrom the following
described land, to wit:
In T. 9 N., R. 3 E., Lot 2, Sec. 29
and Lot 1, Sec. 32;
Boise Meridian.
The tract herein eliminated from the Payette National Forest is
hereby reserved for townsite purposes under Section Twenty-three Hun-
dred Eighty, to be hereafter disposed of under Section Twenty-three
Hundred Eighty-one, U. S. Revised Statutes, subject to the right of wvay
of the Idaho Northern Railway Company, Limited, acquired under the
Act of March 3, 1875 (18 Stat., 482).
WOODROW WILSON
THE WHITE HOUSE,
