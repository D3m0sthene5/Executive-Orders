under the appropriation of Three Hundred Thousand Dollars carried in
the Sundry Civil Act approved August 24, 1912, the thirty persons here-
tofore employed in the Office of the Auditor for the Post Office Depart-
ment whose names are on the list furnished to the President in a letter of
the Secretary of the Treasury dated August 22, 1912, said appropriation
act authorizing the Commissioner of Pensions to employ clerks hereto-
fore employed in other Departments of the Government service. All of
these persons except one have a competitive ciassified status and will be
eligible for reinstatement in the Treasury Department under the Civil
Service Rules.
