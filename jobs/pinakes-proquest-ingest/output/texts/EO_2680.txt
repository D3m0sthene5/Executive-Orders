Robert Gaines, of Raleigh, North Carolina, may be appointed to the
positions of messenger in the Navy Department at $840 per annum, without
reference to the civil service rules.
This order is issued uponi the recommendation of the Secretary of the
Navy, who states that he has recently lost his personal messenger on account
of his having received a clerical appointment is the War Department, and that
it is necessary, especially at this time, that the position be filled by a person
of known integrity, and that lie possess somewhat higher qualifications than
those of a mere messenger. He states that Robert Gaines is personally known
to him as a man who can fill the requisites of the position.
WOODROW WILSON
THE WHITES HOUSE,
13 August, '917.
