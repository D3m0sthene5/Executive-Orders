Executive Orber.
Mrs. Pauline Munford may be appointed to a position in the classified
service of the Departnient of Labor, without reference to the civil service rules.
This order is issued upon the recommendation of the Secretary of Labor,
who states that in view of the fact that Mrs. Munford has already passed a
civil-service examination and the further fact that she has had extensive
experience in the Government Service the Department of Labor could utilize
to advantage the services of Mrs. Munford.
WOODROW WILSON
THE WVHTE HOUSE,
16 August, 1917.
[No. 2683.]
