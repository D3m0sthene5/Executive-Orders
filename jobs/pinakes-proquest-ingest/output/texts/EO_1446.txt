Executive rber.
In accordance with the provisions of Section 2253 of the Revised
Statutes of the United States, and by virtue of the authority therein given,
it is hereby ordered that townships number eight, nine, ten and eleven
south, range seventeen east, and one, two and three north, range seventeen
east, all of the Salt Lake Base and Meridian, be detached from the Salt
Lake City land district and attached to and made a Ipart of the Vernal land
district in the State of Utah, and the business and archives pertaining to
the lands within said townships transferred from the land office at Salt
Lake City to the land office at Vernal.
WM H T AFT
TIHE WITE HOUSE,
December z6, 1911.
