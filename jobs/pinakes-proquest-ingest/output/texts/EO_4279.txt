It is hereby ordered that Executive Order No. 2593, dated April 13, 1917, as
modified by Executive Orders Nos. 3189, 3248 and 3453, dated December 2, 1919,
March 20, 1920, and May 3, 1921, respectively, issued pursuant to an' act of Con-
gress, approved June 25, 1910 (36, Stat.., 847), as amended by an act of Congress,
) approved August 24, 1912 (37 Stat., 497), withdrawing all of the public land within
the following enumerated townships within the St. Francis River Basin in the
State of Arkansas, pending an examination and survey authorized by an act of
Congress approved July 27, 1916 (39 Stat., 409), be, and the same is hereby revoked
and all of the public lands situated therein are hereby restored to the public domain
and shall become subject to disposition under the laws applicable thereto upon such
date and after such notice as hereinafter provided:

Pursuant' to the provisions of Public Resolution No. 29 approved February
14, 1920(41 Stat., 434), as amended by Public Resolutions Nos. 36 and 79 approved
January 21, and December 28, 1922, respectively, the surveyed lands above
described will be subject to homestead entry by qualified ex-service men of the
War with Germany under the terms and conditions of said resolutions and the
regulations issued thereunder for a 'period of 91 days beginning with such date
and after such notice as may be hereafter determined upon and announced. Other
persons having valid existing rights or equitable claims subject to allowance or
confirmation- will be allowed to make their entries within the 91 day period allowed
ex-service men of the War with Germany. Any lands remaining vacant at the end
of said preference right period will become subject' to entry under any applicable
public land law by the general public.
Subsequent to this order and prior to the date of restoration to general dis
position as herein provided, no right may be acquired to' said lands by settlement
in advance of entry, or otherwise, except strictly in accordance herewith.
