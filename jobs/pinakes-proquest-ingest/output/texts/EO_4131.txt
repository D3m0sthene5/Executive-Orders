Under authority of Section 1 of the Act of July 5, 1884 (23 Stat. 103), it is
hereby ordered that the lands comprised within the following military reservations
in the territory of Alaska, including Signal Corps Stations and rights-of-way, which
were reserved for military purposes by the Executive Orders hereinafter designated,
the same having become useless for military purposes, be and are hereby placed
under the control of the Secretary of the Interior for disposition as provided in
the above Act or as may be otherwise provided by law:
