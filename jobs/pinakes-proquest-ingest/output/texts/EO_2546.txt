Executive Other.
Mr. William J. Martin may be appointed to a competitive position in the
Treasury Department.
This order is issued on the recommendation of the Secretary of the Treasury,
who states that Mr. Martin has rendered very efficient service for several years
in an excepted position as executive clerk, has made an excellent record, has
a grasp of the many details and difficult problems connected with the work of
the Office, has become peculiarly familiar with the personnel and appointments
under the internal revenue laws, and that the action proposed is desired in order
to permit the extension of Mr. Martin's activities.
For the reasons stated by the Secretary the Civil Service Commission
concurs in the recommendation.
WOODROW WILSON
THE WHITE HOUSE,
2r March, 1917.
[No. 2546.]
