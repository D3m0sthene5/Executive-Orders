Mrs. Agnes E. Mahoney may be reinstated in a suitable vacancy in the
Department of Agriculture without regard to the length of her separation from
the service.
Mrs. Mahoney entered the Department of Agriculture on November i,
1918, through open competitive examination, and resigned on April 12, 1919,
having married. The Secretary of Agriculture states that her services were
satisfactory and that he would be glad to reinstate her in the first suitable
vacancy. The Civil Service Commission does not recommend the reinstate-
ment of Mrs. Mahoney.
Done as charity.
CALVIN COOLIDGE
THE WHITE HOUSE,
April 30, 1924.
