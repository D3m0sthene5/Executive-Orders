In accordance with the provisions of Section 2252 of the Revised Statutes of
the United States and by virtue of the authority therein given, it is hereby ordered
that the Montrose .land district and land office in the State of Colorado be dis-
continued and the lands, business and archives of said land district, and land office
transferred to and consolidated with. the Glenwood Springs land district and land
office, at the close of business November 30, 1925.
CALVIN COOL IDGE
THE WHITz HOUSE,
October 19, 1925.
