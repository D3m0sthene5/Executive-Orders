Executive Orber.
Under authority contained in the act of Congress approved June 25, 1910
(36 Stat., 847), as amended by the act of August 24, 1912 (37 Stat., 497), it ishereby ordered that an unsurveyed island locally known as ro a Rock,
situated in the Pacific Ocean in Sees. 26 and 35, T. 5 S., R. 11 W., W. M.,
Oregon, be and the same is hereby temporarily withdrawn from all forms of
settlement, location or disposal, for classification and in aid of legislation.
CALVIN COOL IDGE
THE WHITE ROUSE,
.October 6, 1924.
