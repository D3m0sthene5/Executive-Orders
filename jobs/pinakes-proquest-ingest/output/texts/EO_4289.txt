ARIZONA
Under authority of the act of Congress approved June 25, 19'10 (36 Stat.,
847), as amended by the act of August 24,1912 (37 Stat., 497), and subject to the
conditions therein expressed, it is hereby ordered that the tract of land described
as the SW 4 SE% Sec. 9, T. 9 S., R. 23 W., G. and S. R. M., Arizona, contain-
ing 40 acres, be, and the same is hereby, temporarily withdrawn from settlement,
location, sale or entry, and reserved for use by the United States War Depart-
ment as an emergency landing field for airplanes in connection with the operation
anal maintenance of the Army Air Service, subject to the provisions of an existing
withdrawal for reclamation purposes.
CALVIN COOLIDGE
THE WHITE HOUSE,
August 22, 1925.
