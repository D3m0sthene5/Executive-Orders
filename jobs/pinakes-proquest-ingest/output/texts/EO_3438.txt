342. Drafts and accounts to be in the currency of the Un~ited States. -All
drafts drawn by diplomatic officers for salaries and for expenses in connection
with their offices and all accounts rendered by such officers must be stated in
the currency of the United States, If foreign currency is received for a draft,
or for fees collected, it must be reduced in the accounts to its equivalent in the
currency of the United States, In making such reductions a diplomatic offi-
cer will take as the value of the foreign currency that estimated by the Direc-
tor of the Mint in the values of foreign coits proclaimed quarterly by the Secre"
tary of the Treasury, unless such currency be foreign to the standard currency
of the country involved, or a depreciated currency. Where a currency foreign
to the standard currency of the country or a depreciated currency is received,
the reduction to its equivalent in United States currency must be made at the
relative value of such foreign or depreciated currency to the standard currency
of the country, provided that evidence of such value or depreciation is fur-
nished in the form of a banker's certificate, In case it is not possible to secure
such a certificate, the commercial rate of exchange between the foreign or
depreciated currency and United States currency at the time of cashing a draft
or settling an account, which must be shown by a banker's certificate, shall be
used in reducing such currency to its equivalent in United States currency
unless otherwise required by the accounting officers of the Treasury.
