Schedule A, Subdivision I, paragraph 2 of the Civil Service Rules is
hereby amended by striking out the words :
"and confirmed by the Senate".
As amended clause 2 will read as follows:
"2. One private secretary or confidential clerk to each of the heads
of bureaus appointed by the President in the executive depart-
ments, if authorized by law."
This amendment is issued upon the recommendation of the Secretary
of Commerce to permit appointment without examination of a "clerk to
commissioner", Bureau of Corporations, a position authorized by law, and
is concurred in by the Civil Service Commission.
The Commissioner of Corporations is appointed by the President
without confirmation, and he wvas not therefore entitled to the exception
from examination of a private secretary, a privilege limited to heads of
bureaus appointed by the President and confirmed by the Senate. The
amendment will obviate further recourse to the President in future similar
cases.
WOODROW WILSON
THE WHITE HOUSE,
June eb, 1913.
