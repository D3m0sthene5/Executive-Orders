Pursuant to the authority contained in Section 1 79 of the Revised
Statutes, I hereby authorize and direct S. W. Stratton, Director of the
Bureau of Standards, Department of Commerce, to perform the duties of
the Secretary of Commerce during the absence of the Secretary, the
Assistant Secretary, the Solicitor, and the Director of the Census.
THE WHITE HOUSE,
2 June, 1914.
WOODROW WILSON
[No. 1952.]
