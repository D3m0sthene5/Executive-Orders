,Erecutive order
By virtue of the authority vested in me as President of the United States of
America, and pursuant to the provisions of Section 9 of the Act of Congress approved
on June 7, 1924, entitled, "An Act To provide for the protection of forest lands,
for the reforestation of denuded areas, for the extension of national forests, and for
other purposes, in order to promote the continuous production of timber on lands
chiefly suitable therefor," the following described lands which comprise a part of
the Fort Humphreys Military Reservation in the State of Virginia, and which, in
the opinion of the Secretary of War and the Secretary of Agriculture, are suitable
for the production of timber, hereby are established as a National Forest, to wit:
All of that part of the Fort Humphreys Military Reservation as showir upon
the map hereto attached and made a part hereof, excluding the Belvoir Road from
its junction with the Washington-Richmond Highway southward to the North
Boundary of that portion of the Fort Humphreys Military Reservation retained
under the exclusive jurisdiction of the War Department.
The National Forest hereby created shall be known as the Humphreys National
Forest.
The said Humphreys National Forest shall be administered by the Secretary
of Agriculture under such rules and regulations and in accordance with such general
plans as may be jointly approved- by the Secretary of Agriculture and the Secre-
tar ofWar fr te ueoccupation and protection of such lands and for the sale
of products therefrom.
The lands comprising' the said Humphreys National Forest shall remain sub-
ject t h unhampered use of the War Department for purposes of national de-
fense and nothing in this order shall be construed to affect or resrc heatoiy
over such land for purposes of national defense now vested in the Secretary of War.
The articles of war and all other laws, rules and regulations applicable to the
military establishment continue in full force and -effect at all times and under all
conditions upon all lands included in the Humphreys National Forest hereby created
except insofar as they are inconsistent with Section 9 of said Act of June 7, 1024.
