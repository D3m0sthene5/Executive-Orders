Executive Drber
It is hereby ordered under authority contained in the Act of June twenty-first,
nineteen hundred and six (Thirty-four Statutes at Large, pages 325-326), that the
trust period on the allotment of Sarah Jones on the Santee Reservation, Nebraska,
which trust expires September twentieth, nineteen hundred and twenty-four, be
and is hereby extended for a period of ten years.
CALVIN COOLIDGE
Tu-E WHITEHOUSE,
