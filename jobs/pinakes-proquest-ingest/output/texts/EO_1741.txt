)Executive Orber.
Mr. Edward Hawvkins may be appointed a Laborer in the Department
of Agriculture without reference to the civil service rules.
Mr. Hawvkins is now on temporary duty in the Department of Agri-
culture, performing emergency work. He was previously employed for
four years by the Commissioners of the District of Columbia and bears an
excellent testimonial. The Department ot Agriculture reports that the
Department "would be ga to place him in the Department, as he is a
clean, reliable sort of man."
-The Civil Service Commission does not recommend the issuance of
this order.
WM H TAFT
THE WHITE HOUSE,
March 3, 1913.
[No. 1741.]
