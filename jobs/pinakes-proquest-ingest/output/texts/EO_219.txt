In the matter of the reservations of certain lands in Porto Rico made
hy me by proclamation dated at Washington on the 26th day of June,
A. D. 1903, reserving and setting apart certain lands for naval uses, it is hereby
* directed that on or before the first day of October, 1903, such memoranda may
be submitted to me with respect to any modifications of such proclamation
as may be agreed upon concerning lands reserved for naval uses between the
Secretary of the Navy and the Governor of Porto Rico; and the right to
amend or modify the order heretofore made concerning lands so reserved on
June 26, 1903, is hereby expressly reserved.
THEODORE ROOSEVELT
WHITE HOUSE,
June 30, 1903.
[No. 219.]
