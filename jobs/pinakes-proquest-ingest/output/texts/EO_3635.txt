Executive Orber
Powell-Sevier National Forest
Utah
Under authority of the Act of Congress of June 4, 1897, (30 Stat. 1I), and
upon recommendation of the Secretary of Agriculture, it is hereby ordered that
on and after July 1, 1922, all the lands included within the eastern division of
the Sevier National Forest, as fixed and defined by Proclamation of December 23,'7/
1910, (No. IIII) and Executive Order of November 27, 1918, (No. 3009), be,
and the same are hereby transferred to the Powell National Forest, Utah, and
all said lands and those now included within the Powell National Forest, as fixed
and defined by Proclamation of September 26, 19 10, (No. io86) , as shown upon ''
the attached diagram, shall thereafter be administered as the Powell National
Forest.
It is intended by this Executive Order, for economy of administration, to
merge into one National Forest, thereafte: to be known as the Powell National
Forest, all lands heretofore within the eastern division of the Sevier National
Forest and the lands now within the Powell National Forest; and the boundaries
of the Sevier National Forest and the Powell National Forest as they now exist
are hereby modified accordingly.
WARREN G HARDING
THE WHITE HOUSE,
February 14, 1922.
