Under and pursuant to the provisions of the act of Congress of March 12,1914
(38 Stat. 305), entitled "An act to authorize the President of the United States to
locate, construct and operate railroads in the Territory of Alaska, and for other
purposes," it is hereby ordered that the following described lands, withdrawn by
Executive Order No. 1967-A, approved June 23, 1914, and reserved for townsite
purposes and in connection with railroad or other construction work contemplated
by said act, be eliminated from such withdrawal:
T. 1 S., R. 1 W., F. M., Alaska.
Sec. 3 lots 6, 10, 16, 17, 18,. 19, 20.
Sec. 4 lots 11, 13, 14, 15, 18, 20, and that portion of lot 9 not eliminated
from said withdrawal of June 23, 1914, by Executive Order No. 2236
of August 17, 1915.
