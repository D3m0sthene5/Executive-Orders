WYOMING NATIONAL FOREST
WYOMING
It is hereby ordered that on and after July ,1908, the land within the
boundary shown on the attached diagram, heretofore set apart, reserved,
and proclaimed as a part of the Yellowstone National Forest, shall be known
as the Wyoming National Forest. The remaining portions of the Yellow-
stone National Forest shall continue reserved and constitute the Shoshone,
Teton, and Bonneville National Forests in Wyoming, parts of the Absa-
rota and Beartooth National Forests in Montana, and a part of the Tar-
ghee National Forest in Idaho and Wyoming. It is not intended by this
order to release any land from reservation or to reserve any land not here-
tofore emhbrac'1d in a National Forest.
