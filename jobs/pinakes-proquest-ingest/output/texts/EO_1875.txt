Executive Orber.
Miss Anita H. Stephens, formerly an employee in the Philippine
Civil Service, may be appointed to the position of clerk (first grade) in
the Department of Commrerce, without further examination under the
Civil Service Rules.
Miss Stephens wvas formerly employed in the Library of Congress
and wvas transferred to the position of assistant librarian in the Bureau of
Science at Manila, at an annual compensation of $1400, the Civil Service
Commission having certified that she had submitted evidence of her train-
ing and experience from which it appeared that she had the requisite quali-
fications to render her eligible for appointment.
In view of the excellent service of Miss Stephens in the Philippines
and the fact that the condition of her health made it necessary for her to
leave the Islands two months before the expiration of the three year period
of service wvhich would have rendered her eligible for transfer this order is
issued. The transfer and appointment of Miss Stephens is recommended
by the Assistant S& ;retary of War and the Chief of the Bureau of Insular
Affairs, and is concurred in by the Secretary of Commerce. The Civil
Service Commission does not concur in recommending that an order be
issued in this case.
WOODROW WILSON
TILE WITuE HOUSE,
22 December, 1913.
