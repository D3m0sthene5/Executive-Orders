N13 9 52 AM 1905 EXECUTIVE ORDER
Laborers in the Executive Departments and Offices in
Washington, who were reported in the lists furnished by the
4 Departments to the Civil Service Commission in 1902, in com-
| pliance with section 3, Civil-Service Rule XII, as amended
January 23, 1902, whose principal duties were at that time
and are now of the grade performed by classified employes, i
will be regarded as classified laborers in the positions occu-
pied by them on the date of this order and may be promoted
| to clerical grade, or be transferred, in the manner provided
by the Civil-Service Rules and Regulations.
| Theodore Roosevelt
The White House
