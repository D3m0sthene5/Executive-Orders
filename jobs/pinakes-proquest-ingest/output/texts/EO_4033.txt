~Eecutive Otber.
It having been brought to my attention that the Honorable Arthur F. Odlin,
Judge of the District Court of the United States for Porto Rico, will be absent
from his post of duty from June 24th to July 19, 1924, under the authority dele-
gated to me in Section 41, of the Act of March 2, 1917, I hereby designate
Honorable Carlos Franco Soto, of the Porto Rico Supreme Court, as Acting Judge
of the District Court of the United States for Porto Rico during the absence of
Judge Odiin.
During the incumbency of Judge Soto, and in accordance with said Section
41, of the Act of 1917, he is fully authorized and empowered to perform the duties
of said office during such absence of Judge Odlin, and to sign all necessary papers
and records as the Acting Judge of said Court without extra compensation.
CALVIN COOLIDGE
THE WHITE HOUSE,
Jun~e 23, 1924.
