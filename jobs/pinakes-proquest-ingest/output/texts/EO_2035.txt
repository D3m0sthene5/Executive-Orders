The period of eligibility of Mrs. ;Nora C. Gleason is hereby extended to
permit her reinstatement in the Bureau of Engraving and Printing.
Mrs. Gleason rendered many years of exceptionally efficient service in
the Bureau of Engraving and Printing. It is necessary for her to have em-
ployment in order that she may be able to support herself and that her hus-
band, who is suffering from tuberculosis, may have medical attention.
The Director of the Bureau of Engraving and Printing states that he be-
lieves that the reappointment of Mrs. Gleason would be to the best interests
of the service.
In viewv of the facts the Commission concurs with the Treasury Depart-
mient in recommending this order.
WOODROW WILSON
THE WHITE HOUSE,
September 2, 1914.
