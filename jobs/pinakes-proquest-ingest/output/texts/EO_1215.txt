Executive Orber.
It is hereby ordered that the tract or parcel of land situated in the
City of Cebu, Philippine Islands, heretofore, by Executive Order of June
19, 1903, set apart and reserved for naval purposes, be and the same is
hereby transferred to and placed under the control of the Secretary of War
to be used for military purposes; excepting that a site therein, to be deter-
mined upon by mutual agreement between the Navy Department and
the War Department, suitable for the storage of five hundred tons of coal
shall remain under the control of the Secretary of the Navy to be used for
naval purposes, and that the Navy Department shall have the unrestricted
use of the wharf on this reservation or of any wharf that may be built
thereon.
It is further ordered that the naval reservations at Isabela de Basilan
and Polloc, Mindanao, heretofore by the Executive Order of June 19,
1903, set apart and reserved for naval purposes, be and the same are hereby
transferred to and placed under the control of the Secretary of War to be
used for military purposes.
WM H TAFT
THE XVHITE HOUSE,
June 17 1910
