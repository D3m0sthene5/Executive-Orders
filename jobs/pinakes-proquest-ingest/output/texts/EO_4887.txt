Mrs. Rita C. Regan may be appointed to a clerkship in the Boston, Massa-
chusetts, post office without regard to the requirements of the civil service rules.
Mrs. Regan is the widow of Dennis L. Regan, for twenty-one years an
employee of the Boston post office, who died suddenly on December 18, 1927,
leaving her without funds and with an infant child five years old depending upon
her for support.
This Order is issued upon the recommendation of the Postmaster General.
The Civil Service Commission is unable to concur.
For charity.
CALVIN COOLIDGE
THE WHITE HOUSE,
May 23, 1928.
