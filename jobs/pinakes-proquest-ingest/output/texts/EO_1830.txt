Executive order 1
LAKE BRYANT ADMINISTRATIVE SITE
(Near Florida National Forest)
Under authority of the Act of Congress approved June 25, 1910 (36
Stat., 847), as amended by the Act of August 24, 1912 (37 Stat., 497),
and on the recommendation of the Secretary of Agriculture, it is hereby
ordered that the SW. / NW. / Sec. 24, T. 15 S., R. 24 E., T. M., out-
side but near the boundaries of the Florida National Forest, Florida, area
40 acres, be temporarily withdrawn from settlement, location, sale or entry,
except as provided in said Acts, and be reserved for use by the Forest
Service as a ranger station in the administration of the Florida National
Forest.
WOODROW WILSON
THE WHITE HOUSE,
24 Sept., 1913.
[No. 1830.]
