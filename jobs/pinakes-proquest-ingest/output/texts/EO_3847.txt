Executive Orbet
By virtue of the authority vested in me by the Act of Congress approved
August r, 1914, making appropriations for the sundry civil expenses of the
Government for the fiscal year ended June 30, 1915, the port of Boston, the
headquarters port of customs collection district No. t~ (Massachusetts), as at
present constituted, is hereby abolished.
By virtue of the same authority a new or of entry is hereby established
which shall be known as the port of Boston, the headquarters port of customs
collection district No. 4 (Massachusetts), and which shall cover all of the area
necessary in order to include the territory embraced in the cities of Boston,
Chelsea, Everett, Medford, Somerville, Cambridge, and Quincy, and the adjacent
waters.
