AUTHORIZING THE ALIEN PROPERTY CUSTODIAN
TO SELL CERTAIN PROPERTY AT PRIVATE SALE
By virtue of the authority vested in me by an Act of Congress entitled "An
act to define, regulate, and punish trading with the enemy and for other purposes ",
approved October 6, 1917, known as the " ITrading with the Enemy Act", and the
amendment to such at embodied in "An act making appropriations to supply
urgent deficiencies in the appropriations for the fiscal year ending June 30, 1918,
and prior fiscal years on account of war expenses and for other purposes",
approved March 28, 1918, and the amendment to such act embodied in "An act
making appropriations to supply deficiencies in appropriations for the fiscal year
ending June 30, 1919, and prior fiscal years on account of war expenses, and for
other purposes", approved November 4, 1918, to make certain orders such as that
herein contained, I hereby, in the public interest, make the following determination,
order, rule and regulation:
The Aien Propet ustodian is hereby authorized to sell at private sale, at
such time and place as to him or to his authorized agent may seem advisable,
without public or other advertisement, for and in consideration of the sum of
TWELVE THOUSAND FIVE HUNDRED (312,500) DOLIARs, certain real estate now
being held and administered by him as such Alien Property Custodian, and hereto-
fore, upon requirement, conveyed, transferred, assigned and delivered to him as
the property of M.x L. HmESOEHORN and BLANKA LEHMIAN, residing in Germany,
whom, after investigation, he did determine to be enemies within the purview of
said Act as amended, which said real estate is situate in the Borough of Bronx,
City of New York. State of New York, bounded and described as follows:
Beginning at the corner formed by the intersection of the northerly
side of 138th Street with the westerly side of Rider Avenue, running
thence westerly along the northerly side of 138th Street, twenty-five
(25) feet; thence northerly parallel with Rider Avenue one hundred (100)
feet; thence easterly and parallel with 138th Street, twenty-five (25) feet
to the westerly line of Rider Ave., and thence southerly along the west-
erly line of Rider Avenue one hundred (100) feet to the point of begin-
ning; being the same premises conveyed by James A. Allen, Referee,
to Leopold Gusthal, by deed dated November 13, 1907, and recorded
November 13, 1907, in the office of the Register of the County of New
York, in Liber 74, of Section 9 of Conveyances, page 7.
My reasons for the foregoing determination, order, rule and regulation are:
(a) An advantageous offer for the purchase of the said real estate has been received
by the Alien Property Custodian;
(b) An attempted sale at public auction would be a useless formality and would
involve unnecessary expenses, delay and inconvenience, which should be avoided.
CALVIN COOLIDGE
THE WHITE HoUSE,
January 27, 1925.
