CORONADO-CHIRICAHUA NATIONAL FORESTS
ARIZONA AND NEW MEXICO
Under authority of the Act of Congress of June 4, 1897 (30 Stat., i i), and
upon recommendation of the Secretary of Agriculture, it is hereby ordered that
on and after July 1, 1917, all the lands included within the boundaries of the
Chiricahua National Forest Arizona and New Mexico, as fixed and defined by
proclamation of July I, 1910, (No. i o66), be transferred to and administered as a ,2S'
part of the Coronado National Forest, Arizona and New Mexico.
It is intended by this Executive Order, for economy of administration to
merge into one National Forest, thereafter to be known as the Coronado National
Forest, all the lands which on July 1, 1917, are within the Chiricahua and the
Coronado National Forests, and the boundaries of the Coronado National
Forest, as they exist at that date, are hereby modified accordingly.
This Executive Order is not intended to add to the Coronado National
Forest any lands which on July 1, 1917, are not embraced within the Chiricahua
National Forest, or to release irom reservation for National Forest purposes
any lands at that date within the Chiricahua National Forest.
