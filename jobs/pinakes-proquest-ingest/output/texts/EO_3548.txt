Executive Orber
Mr. Artenmus A. Rowe, formerly a post office inspector who entered the
Postal Service in May, 1890, and who served continuously until July 31, 1914,
when he was separated, may be reinstated to the position of post office inspector
without regard to the time limitation.
This action is taken on the recommendation of the Postmaster General
who states that on account of Mr. Rowe's twenty-four years' experience and
interest in tL Postal Service it is his opinion that he would be a valuable
acquisition to the inspection force.
WARREN G HARDING
THE WHITE HousE,
September 19, 1921.
