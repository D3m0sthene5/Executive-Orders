Executive Order.
IT Is HEREBY ORDERED AND DIRECTED that HENRY C. EMERY,
JAMEs B. REYNOLDS and AIXvIN H. SANDERS, members of the Tariff
Board, and each of them be and are hereby appointed special disbursing
agents to disburse any funds appropriated by Congress for the use of said
Board, to act as such disbursing agents when either of the mne pers of
said Board is traveling awvay from his official station, and the bond of each
is hereby fixed at the sum of $5,000.00. When the Chairman of said
Board is acting as said disbursing agent under this appointment the
voucher to be submitted by him shall be approved by any other member
of said Board.
WM H TAFT
THE WHITE HOUSE
May 5 1kz
1910
