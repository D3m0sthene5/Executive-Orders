Executive order.
In accordance with the provisions of Section 2253 of the Revised
Statutes of the United States, and hy virtue of the authority therein given,
it is hereby ordered that:
All those parts of the Gregory and Timberlake land districts as lie
East of the Missouri River, in the State of South Dakota, be detached
from said land districts and attached to and made a part of the Pierre land
district, and that the archives and business pertaining to the public lands
affected thereby be transferred from the land offices at Gregory and
Timberlake to the land office at Pierre.
WOODROW WILSON
THE WHITE HOUSE,
June 30, 1914.
[No. 1969.]
