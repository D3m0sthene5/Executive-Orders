The Secretary of the Treasury is hereby authorized to promote and
appoint Mr. John Fitzgerald, now an Opener and Packer in the Office
of the Appraiser of Merchandise at New York, to the position of Mer-
chandise Sampler in the same office, without compliance with the Civil
Service Rules.
This Order is issued because of most valuable and distinguished service
rendered by Mr. Fitzgerald in the Spanish-American War, for which he
has been awarded a gold medal of honor, and for the further reason that
he is unable to pass the Civil Service examination required for such pro-
motion and transfer owing to limited early education, although his native
intelligence and experience satisfy those who are familiar with the condi-
tions that he is entirely qualified to perform the duties of the office to
which he is to be appointed, and, therefore, the appointment will be in the
interest of the service. The Civil Service Commission disapprove of
)-his order.
WM H TAFT
THE WHITE HoUSE,
Feb'. 20, 1913.
