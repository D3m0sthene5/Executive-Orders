Executive Order
TARGHEE NATIONAL FOREST
IDAHO ANI) WYOMING
It is hereby ordered that on and after July I, 1908, the land within the
boundaries shown on the attached diagram, heretofore set apart, reserved,
and proclaimed as the Henrys Lake National Forest and a part of the
Yellowstone National Forest, shall be known as the Targhee National
Forest. The remaining portions of the Yellowstone National Forest shall
continue reserved and constitute the Shoshone, Teton, Bonneville, and
Wyoming National Forests in Wyoming, and parts of tile Absaroka and
Beartooth National Forests in Montana. It is not intended by this order
to release any land from reservation or to reserve any land not hereto-
fore embraced in a National Forest.
THEODORE ROOSEVELT
THE WHITE HOUSE.
July z, 1908.
[No. 871.]
