Executive Orger.
Mrs. Louise M. Foster may be reinstated as an unclassified laborer
in the Department of Agriculture, the year limit of the labor regulations
being waived.
Mrs. Foster is the widow of Henry Foster, who served more than
two years during the civil war, two full enlistments in the Regular Army
and again in the Spanish-American war, from which he was discharged on
account of disability incurred in line of duty. On account of the services
of Mr. Foster to his country, which were of such a character as to place
this case in a class apart, the Civil Service Commission recommends this
order.
WM H TAFT
THE WHITE HOUSE,
June 22 191G.
[No. I214.]
