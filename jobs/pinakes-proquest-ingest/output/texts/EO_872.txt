TETON NATIONAL FOREST
WYOMING
It is hereby ordered that on and after July I, 1908, the land within the
boundaries shown on the attached diagram, heretofore set apart, reserved,
and proclaimed as a part of the Yellowrstone National Forest, shall be
known as the Tcton National Forest. The remaining portions of the
Yellowstone National Forest shall continue reserved and constitute the
Shoshone, Bonneville, and Wyoming National Forests in Wyoming, parts
of Absaroka and Beartooth National Forests in Montana, and a part of
the Targhee National Forest in Idaho and Wyoming. It is not intended
by this order to release any land from reservation or to reserve any land
not heretofore embraced in a National Forest.
THEODORE ROOSEVELT
THE WHITE HOUSE.
July I, 1908.
