Walter W. Grose may be reinstated as Clerk in the Quartermaster Corps
of the Army, War Department, without regard to the year limitation upon
eligibility for reinstatement.
Mr. Grose entered the service of the Quartermaster Corps June 1, i1898,
and was continuously employed until May 27, 1912, when he resigned. His
services are now urgently needed in the Quartermaster Corps of the Army at
Jeffersonville, Indiana. In the opinion of the Department, this case is one in
which the paramount consideration is the benefit of the service.
WOODROW WILSON
THE WHITE HOUSE,"
