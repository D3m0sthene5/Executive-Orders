Executive Order 9094-Abolishing the Board of Surveys and Maps and
authorizing the Director of the Office of Management and Budget to
perform its functions
SOURCE : The provisions of Executive Order 9094 of Mar. 10, 1942, appear at 7 FR
1972, 3 CFR , 1938-1943 Comp ., p . 1120, unless otherwise noted .
By virtue of the authority vested in me as President of the United
States, and in order to further coordinate and promote the improve-
ment of the surveying and mapping activities of the Government , it is
hereby ordered as follows :
1. The Federal Board of Surveys and Maps , established by Executive
Order No. 3206 of December 30 , 1919 , is hereby abolished .
2. The Director of the Office of Management and Budget is author-
ized and directed to perform the functions heretofore performed by the
said Board , and such other functions as may be necessary to carry out
the purposes of this order .
[Title and para . 2 amended by EO 12608 of Sept. 9, 1987, 52 FR 34617 , 3 CFR, 1987
Comp ., p . 245 ]
3. The Director of the Office of Management and Budget is author-
ized and directed to call upon the heads of the several departments and
independent establishments to furnish , through such representatives as
they may designate for the purpose , such information as may be re-
quired by the Director in carrying out the provisions of this order .
[Para. 3 amended by EO 12608 of Sept. 9, 1987, 52 FR 34617, 3 CFR , 1987 Comp ., p .
