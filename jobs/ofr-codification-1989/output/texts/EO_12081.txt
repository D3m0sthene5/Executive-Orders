Executive Order 12081 -Termination of expeditious naturalization
based on military service
SOURCE : The provisions of Executive Order 12081 of Sept. 18, 1978, appear at 43 FR
42237 , 3 CFR , 1978 Comp ., p . 225 , unless otherwise noted .
By the authority vested in me as President of the United States of
America by Section 329 of the Immigration and Nationality Act , as
amended by the Sections 1 and 2 of the Act of October 24, 1968 ( 82
Stat . 1343 ; 8 U.S.C. 1440) , and by the authority of Section 3 of that Act
of October 24 , 1968 ( 82 Stat . 1344 ; 8 U.S.C. 1440e ) , it is hereby ordered
that the statutory period of Vietnam hostilities which began on Febru-
ary 28 , 1961 , shall be deemed to have terminated on October 15 , 1978 ,
for the purpose of ending the period in which active -duty service in the
Armed Forces qualifies for certain exceptions from the usual require-
ments for naturalizations , including length of residence and fees .
