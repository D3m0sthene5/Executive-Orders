Executive Order 9704- Functions of the American Battle Monuments
Commission
SOURCE : The provisions of Executive Order 9704 of Mar. 14, 1946, appear at 11 FR
2675 , 3 CFR , 1943-1948 Comp ., p . 519, unless otherwise noted .
By virtue of the authority vested in me by the Constitution and Stat-
utes , including Title I of the First War Powers Act, 1941 , and as Presi-
dent of the United States and Commander in Chief of the Army and
the Navy , it is hereby ordered that the American Battle Monuments
Commission shall control as to materials and design , and provide regu-
lations for and supervise the erection of, all memorial monuments and
buildings in American cemeteries located outside of the United States
and its territories and possessions, and shall also, when authorized by
any foreign country or political subdivision thereof in which the Amer-
ican armed forces have served , control as to materials and design , and
provide regulations for the erection of, all memorial monuments and
buildings located in , and commemorating the services of the American
armed forces in, any such foreign country or political subdivision .
