Executive Order 12101 -Privileges , immunities and liability insurance
for diplomatic missions and personnel
SOURCE : The provisions of Executive Order 12101 of Nov. 17, 1978, appear at 43 FR
54195 , 3 CFR, 1978 Comp ., p. 258 , unless otherwise noted .
By the authority vested in me as President of the United States of
America by the Diplomatic Relations Act (Public Law 95-393 , 92 Stat .
808 ; 22 U.S.C. 254a et seq.) and Section 301 of Title 3 of the United
States Code , in order to implement the liability insurance and other re-
quirements relating to diplomatic personnel , I hereby designate and em-
power the Secretary of State to perform , without the approval , ratifica-
tion , or other action of the President , the functions vested or to be
vested in the President by Section 4 of Diplomatic Relations Act (92
Stat . 809 ; 22 U.S.C. 254c) .
