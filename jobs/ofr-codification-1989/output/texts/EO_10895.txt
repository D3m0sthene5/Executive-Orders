Executive Order 10895- Extension of the provisions for rotation of ci-
vilian employees of the Defense Establishment assigned to duty out-
side the United States
SOURCE : The provisions of Executive Order 10895 of Nov. 25, 1960, appear at 25 FR
10913, 3 CFR, 1959-1963 Comp ., p . 425 , unless otherwise noted .
By virtue of the authority vested in me by section 1586 (f) of title 10
of the United States Code , and as President of the United States , and
having determined that such action is necessary in the national interest ,
it is ordered as follows :
SECTION 1. Assignment of an employee to duty in the State of Alaska
or Hawaii under regulations prescribed pursuant to section 1586 of title
10 of the United States Code shall be held and considered , for the pur-
poses of that section , to be an assignment to duty outside the United
States .
SEC . 2. The Secretary of Defense shall from time to time , and at least
annually , consider the need for continuing this order in effect , and he
shall recommend the revocation thereof at such time as he may deem
such action advisable .
