Executive Order 12642 - Designation of the Secretary of Defense as
the Presidential designee under title I of the Uniformed and Over-
seas Citizens Absentee Voting Act
SOURCE : The provisions of Executive Order 12642 of June 8, 1988, appear at 53 FR
21975 , 3 CFR , 1988 Comp ., p . 575, unless otherwise noted .
By virtue of the authority vested in me as President by the Constitu-
tion and laws of the United States of America, including section 101 (a)
of the Uniformed and Overseas Citizens Absentee Voting Act (Public
Law 99-410 ) ("the Act") , it is hereby ordered as follows :
SECTION 1. The Secretary of Defense is hereby designated as the
"Presidential designee " under Title I of the Act .
SEC . 2. In order to effectuate the purposes of the Act, the Secretary
of Defense is hereby authorized to delegate any or all of the functions ,
responsibilities , powers , authority , or discretion devolving upon him in
consequence of this Order to any person or persons within the Depart-
ment of Defense .
