Executive Order 11190 - Providing for the screening of the Ready
Reserve of the Armed Forces
SOURCE : The provisions of Executive Order 11190 of Dec. 29 , 1964, appear at 29 FR
19183, 3 CFR , 1964-1965 Comp ., p . 272 , unless otherwise noted .
By virtue of the authority vested in me by section 301 of title 3 of
the United States Code , and as President of the United States and Com-
mander in Chief of the Armed Forces of the United States , it is ordered
as follows :
SECTION 1. There is delegated to the Secretary of Defense (and to
the Secretary of Transportation with regard to the United States Coast
Guard ) the authority vested in the President by section 271 of title 10
of the United States Code to prescribe regulations for the screening of
units and members of the Ready Reserve of the Armed Forces.
[Sec . 1 amended by EO 11382 of Nov. 28 , 1967, 32 FR 16247, 3 CFR , 1966-1970 Comp .,
p. 691]
SEC . 2. Executive Order No. 10651 of January 6, 1956 , is revoked .
