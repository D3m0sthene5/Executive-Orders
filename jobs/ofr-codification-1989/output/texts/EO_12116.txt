Executive Order 12116 —Issuance of food stamps by the Postal Service
SOURCE : The provisions of Executive Order 12116 of Jan. 19, 1979, appear at 44 FR
4647 , 3 CFR , 1979 Comp ., p . 362, unless otherwise noted .
By the authority vested in me as President of the United States of
America by Section 11 (k) of the Food Stamp Act of 1977 (91 Stat. 974;
7 U.S.C. 2020 (k)) , the United States Postal Service is hereby granted
approval for post offices in all or part of any State to issue food stamps
to eligible households , upon request by the appropriate State agency , as
defined in Section 3(n) of the Food Stamp Act of 1977 (91 Stat . 960 ; 7
U.S.C. 2012 (n)) .
