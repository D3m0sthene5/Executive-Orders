Executive Order 11298 - Prescribing the interest rate for deposit of
savings of members of the uniformed services
SOURCE : The provisions of Executive Order 11298 of Aug. 14, 1966, appear at 31 FR
10915, 3 CFR , 1966-1970 Comp ., p . 575, unless otherwise noted .
1 EDITORIAL NOTE : On Jan. 26 , 1973, a terminal date of Mar. 28 , 1973, was prescribed
(DOD Instruction 1348.15) .
By virtue of the authority vested in me by Section 1035 of Title 10 of
the United States Code , as amended by the Act of August 14 , 1966 , I
hereby prescribe that amounts deposited by members of the uniformed
services under that Section shall accrue interest at the rate of ten per-
cent per annum , compounded quarterly .
This order shall be effective September 1, 1966 .
