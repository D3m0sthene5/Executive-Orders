Executive Order 11830 - Enlarging the membership of the Interagency
Committee on Handicapped Employees
SOURCE : The provisions of Executive Order 11830 of Jan. 9, 1975, appear at 40 FR
2411 , 3 CFR , 1971-1975 Comp ., p . 936 , unless otherwise noted .
By virtue of the authority vested in me by section 501 ( a ) of the Re-
habilitation Act of 1973 (Public Law 93-112 ; 87 Stat . 390 ) , it is hereby
ordered as follows :
SECTION 1. In accord with Section 501 of the Rehabilitation Act of
1973 (29 U.S.C. 791 ) and Section 4 of Reorganization Plan No. 1 of
1978 (43 FR 19808 ) , the Interagency Committee on Handicapped Em-
ployees is enlarged and composed of the following , or their designees
whose positions are executive level IV or higher :
(1) Secretary of Defense .
(2) Secretary of Labor .
(3) Secretary of Education , Co -Chairman .
(4) Director of the Office of Personnel Management .
(5) Administrator of Veterans Affairs .
(6) Administrator of General Services .
(7) Chairman of the Federal Communications Commission .
(8) Chairman of the Equal Employment Opportunity Commission ,
Co -Chairman .
(9) Secretary of Health and Human Services .
( 10 ) Such other members as the President may designate .
