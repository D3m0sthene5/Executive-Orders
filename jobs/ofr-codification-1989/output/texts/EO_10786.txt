Executive Order 10786 -Transferring functions of the Airways Modern-
ization Board to the Administrator of the Federal Aviation Adminis-
tration
SOURCE : The provisions of Executive Order 10786 of Nov. 1, 1958, appear at 23 FR
8573, 3 CFR , 1954–1958 Comp ., p . 424 , unless otherwise noted .
By virtue of the authority vested in me by section 304 of the Federal
Aviation Act of 1958 (Public Law 85-726 , 72 Stat . 749 ) and by section
202 (b) of the Budget and Accounting Procedures Act of 1950 (64 Stat .
838 ; 31 U.S.C. 581c (b ) ) , and as President of the United States , it is or-
dered as follows :
SECTION 1. All functions (including powers , duties , activities , and
parts of functions ) of the Airways Modernization Board , including
those of the Chairman thereof , are hereby transferred to the Adminis-
trator of the Federal Aviation Administration ; and all records , proper-
ty , facilities , employees , and unexpended balances of appropriations , al-
locations , and other funds of the Airways Modernization Board , are
hereby transferred to the Federal Aviation Administration .
[Title and sec . 1 amended by EO 12608 of Sept. 9, 1987, 52 FR 34617, 3 CFR , 1987
Comp ., p . 245]
SEC . 2. Such further measures and dispositions , if any , as the Director
of the Office of Management and Budget shall determine to be neces-
sary in connection with the transfers provided for hereinabove in re-
spect of records , property , facilities , employees , and balances shall be
carried out in such manner as he shall direct and by such agencies as he
shall designate .
[Sec . 2 amended by EO 12608 of Sept. 9, 1987, 52 FR 34617 , 3 CFR , 1987 Comp ., p. 245]
SEC . 3. The provisions of this order shall become effective concur-
rently with the entering upon office as Administrator of the Federal
Aviation Administration of the first person appointed as Administrator .
The functions transferred by section 1 hereof may be performed by the
Administrator until the effective date of the repeal of the Airways
Modernization Act of 1957 effected by section 1401 (d ) of the Federal
Aviation Act of 1958.
[Sec . 3 amended by EO 12608 of Sept. 9, 1987, 52 FR 34617, 3 CFR , 1987 Comp ., p . 245 ]
