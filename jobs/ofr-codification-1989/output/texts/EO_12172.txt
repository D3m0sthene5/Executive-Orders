Executive Order 12172 - Delegation of authority with respect to entry
of certain aliens into the United States
SOURCE : The provisions of Executive Order 12172 of Nov. 26 , 1979, appear at 44 FR
67947 , 3 CFR , 1979 Comp ., p . 461 , unless otherwise noted .
By virtue of the authority vested in me as President by the Constitu-
tion and laws of the United States , including the Immigration and Na-
tionality Act, as amended , 8 USC 1185 and 3 USC 301 , it is hereby or-
dered as follows :
SECTION 1-101 . Delegation of Authority . The Secretary of State and
the Attorney General are hereby designated and empowered to exercise
in respect of Iranians the authority conferred upon the President by
section 215 (a)( 1) of the Act of June 27 , 1952 ( 8 USC 1185 ) , to prescribe
limitations and exceptions on the rules and regulations governing the
entry of aliens into the United States .
[Sec . 1-101 amended by EO 12206 of Apr. 7, 1980, 45 FR 24101 , 3 CFR , 1980 Comp ., p .
249]
SEC . 1-102 . Effective Date . This order is effective immediately .
