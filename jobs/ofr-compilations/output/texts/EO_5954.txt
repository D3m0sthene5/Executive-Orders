Executive Order 5954. November 26, 1932
Executive Order
EXEMPTION OF PERSONS FROM COMPULSORY RETIREMENT FOR AGE
By virtue of the authority vested in me by section 204 of Part II of the act
entitled " AN ACT Making appropriations for the Legislative Branch of the Govern-
ment for the fiscal year ending June 30 , 1933 , and for other purposes ," approved June
30 , 1932 , the following -named persons who , during the current month , reach the retire-
ment age prescribed for automatic separation from the service are hereby exempted
from the provisions of that section .
Name
John L. Bateman
Mrs. Otilla A. Reagan
Department or Establishment
Office of the Architect of the Capitol
Department of the Interior
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
November 26 , 1932 .
