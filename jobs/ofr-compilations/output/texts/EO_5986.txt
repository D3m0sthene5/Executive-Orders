Executive Order 5986. January 3, 1933
Executive Order
APPOINTMENT OF JAMES W. HIGGINS , JR .
Mr. James W. Higgins , jr., may be appointed as Special Agent in the Internal
Revenue Bureau of the Treasury Department without regard to the requirements
of the civil -service rules .
Mr. Higgins has served in the excepted position of Deputy Collector of Internal
Revenue since April 10, 1922 , and in that capacity has gained knowledge and
experience in regard to the investigation of which it is desired to use him as a
Special Agent .
This order is recommended by the Secretary of the Treasury and is in the
interest of the service .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
January 3, 1933 .
