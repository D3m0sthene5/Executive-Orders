Executive Order 6038. February 20, 1933
Executive Order
ΑΡΡΟΙΝΤΜΕNT OF LAURENCE GOUVERNEUR HOES
Mr. Laurence Gouverneur Hoes may be appointed to a position in the Veterans '
Administration without regard to the requirements of the civil -service rules .
This order is issued upon the recommendation of the Administrator of Veterans '
Affairs .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
February 20, 1933 .
HERBERT HOOVER
[No. 6038]
