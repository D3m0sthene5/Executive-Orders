Executive Order 5859. June 21, 1932
Executive Order
REVOCATION OF SCHEDULE A, SUBDIVISION III , PARAGRAPH 10 , OF THE
CIVIL-SERVICE RULES
Schedule A, Subdivision III, paragraph 10 , of the civil -service rules , which
permits the appointment , without competitive examination , of mounted inspectors
in the customs service on the Mexican border , is hereby revoked . The present incum-
bents of these positions in the four customs collection districts bordering on Mexico ,
namely , San Antonio , El Paso , Arizona , and San Diego, will be included in the
classified service with their positions upon recommendation by the Treasury Depart-
ment , subject to such tests of fitness as the Civil Service Commission shall prescribe .
The Treasury Department concurred with the Civil Service Commission in the
recommendation .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
June 21 , 1932 .
HERBERT HOOVER
[No. 5859]
