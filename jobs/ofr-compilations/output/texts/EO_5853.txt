Executive Order 5853. June 3, 1932
Executive Order
WAIVER OF MAXIMUM AGE LIMIT , GEORGE H. ROLFE
The maximum age limit is hereby waived to permit George H. Rolfe to enter the
open competitive examination for the position of postmaster at Concord, N. H.
HERBERT HOOVER
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
Junc 3, 1932 .
