Executive Order 5750. November 23 , 1931
Executive Order
REVOCATION OF WITHDRAWAL OF LANDS FOR LIGHTHOUSE PURPOSES
CALIFORNIA
It is hereby ordered that so much of Executive order of January 26 , 1867 , as
withdrew a tract of land at Point Buchon, Calif., for lighthouse purposes , and
Executive order of November 27, 1905 , which withdrew lots 8 and 9, fractional
sec . 36 , township 29 south , range 10 east of the Mount Diablo meridian , California ,
for lighthouse purposes be , and are hereby , revoked .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
November 23 , 1931 .
[No. 5750]
