Executive Order 5655. June 22 , 1931
Executive Order
Public Water Restoration No. 69
Arizona
So much of Executive order of May 25, 1920, creating Public Water Reserve
No. 72 , Arizona , as affects the land hereinafter listed is hereby revoked , and all such
land not otherwise reserved or withdrawn is hereby restored to the public domain
and shall become subject to disposition under the laws applicable thereto upon such
date and after such notice as may be hereafter determined and announced .
