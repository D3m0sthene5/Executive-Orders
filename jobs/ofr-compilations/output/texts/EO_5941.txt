Executive Order 5941. October 26, 1932
Executive Order
PUBLIC WATER RESTORATION No. 74
NEW MEXICO
So much of Executive order of July 10, 1919, creating Public Water Reserve
No. 65 , as affects the land hereinafter listed is hereby revoked , and all such land
not otherwise reserved or withdrawn is hereby restored to the public domain and
shall become subject to disposition under the laws applicable thereto upon such date
and after such notice as may be hereafter determined and announced .
NEW MEXICO MERIDIAN
T. 3 N. , R. 18 E. , sec. 6, lot 3 .
T. 4 N. , R. 18 E. , sec. 31 , SE . ¼ of SW . 4.
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
October 26 , 1932 .
