Executive Order 5768. December 30, 1931
Executive Order
EXTENSION OF TRUST PERIOD ON INDIAN RESERVATION ALLOTMENTS
It is hereby ordered , under authority contained in section 5 of the act of February
8, 1887 (24 Stat . 388 , 389 ) , and the act of June 21 , 1906 (34 Stat . 325 , 326) , that the
period of trust covering allotments made in favor of Indians on the following-named
reservations , which trust will expire during the calendar year 1932 , be , and the same
is hereby , extended for a period of 10 years from the respective dates of expiration
thereof:
Temecula or Pechanga , California ;
Sac and Fox , Kansas and Nebraska ;
Grand Portage, White Earth , and Winnibigoshish , Minnesota ;
Crow , Montana ; Santee , Nebraska;
Devils Lake and Standing Rock , North Dakota ;
Eastern Shawnee , and Otoe and Missouria , Oklahoma;
Cheyenne River, Crow Creek, Pine Ridge , and Rosebud, South Dakota ;
Quinaielt , Washington; Shoshone or Wind River, Wyoming .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
December 30 , 1931 .
