Executive Order 5557. February 13, 1931
Executive Order
Extension of Trust Period on Allotments of Indians on the Pine
Ridge Reservation , South Dakota
It is hereby ordered , under authority contained in the act of June 21 , 1906
(34 Stat . 325-326 ), that the trust period on allotments made to Indians on the Pine
Ridge Reservation , S. Dak., which trust period expires during the calendar year 1931,
be, and the same is hereby , extended for a period of 10 years .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
February 13 , 1931 .
