Executive Order 6020. February 9, 1933
Executive Order
REINSTATEMENT OF RICHARD H. KNIGHT
Mr. Richard H. Knight may be reinstated as a substitute clerk , foot of the roll,
in the New York, N. Y. , Post Office , without regard to the requirements of the civil-
service rules .
This order is recommended by the Postmaster General .
HERBERT HOOVER
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
February 9, 1933 .
