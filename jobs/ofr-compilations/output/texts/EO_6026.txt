Executive Order 6026. February 14, 1933
Executive Order
APPOINTMENT OF MR. STERLING L. MORELOCK
Mr. Sterling L. Morelock may be given a probational appointment as guard
in the Department of Agriculture without reference to his relative standing on
the civil -service register . He is a disabled veteran of the World War, having been
awarded the Congressional Medal of Honor as well as the Order of the Purple Heart .
This order is recommended by the Secretary of Agriculture .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
February 14 , 1933 .
[No. 6026]
