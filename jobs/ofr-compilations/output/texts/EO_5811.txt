Executive Order 5811. February 27, 1932
Executive Order
APPOINTMENT OF MRS . WANDA COOLIDGE CARVER
Mrs. Wanda Coolidge Carver may be appointed as under clerk, grade CAF - 1,
$1,260 per annum , navy yard , Washington , D. C., in lieu of an existing vacancy in
the rating of typewriter , grade CAF -1, without regard to the civil -service act or rules .
Mrs. Carver is the widow of William E. Carver, employed as leadingman ord-
nanceman at the navy yard , Washington , D. C., who was killed in an explosion of
fulminate of mercury , December 30 , 1931 , at the naval magazine , Bellevue , D. C.
This order is issued upon recommendation of the Secretary of the Navy .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
February 27 , 1932 .
[No. 5811]
