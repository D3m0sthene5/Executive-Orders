Executive Order 6067. March 3, 1933
Executive Order
APPOINTMENT OF THOMAS H. ALLEN
Thomas H. Allen may be appointed special investigator in the Immigration
Service of the Department of Labor without regard to the requirements of the civil-
service rules . This employee has rendered highly meritorious service under most
trying and difficult circumstances , and I feel the issuance of this order is warranted .
HERBERT HOOVER
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
March 3, 1933 .
