Executive Order 5804. February 23, 1932
Executive Order
APPOINTMENT OF MISS ANN HALLORAN
Miss Ann Halloran may be appointed to the position of stenographer in the
Veterans ' Administration without compliance with the requirements of the civil-
service rules .
24-5190-74 vol. II- 21
This order is issued upon the recommendation of the Administrator of Veterans '
Affairs .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
HERBERT HOOVER
February 23, 1932.
