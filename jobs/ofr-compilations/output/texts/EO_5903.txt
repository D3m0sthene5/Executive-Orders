WITHDRAWAL OF PUBLIC LANDS FOR CLASSIFICATION
NEVADA

1910 (36 Stat . 847-848 ; U. S. Code, title 43 , sec . 141), as amended by the act of

conditions therein expressed , and to valid existing rights and an existing stock -drive-

described area in Nevada be, and the same are hereby , temporarily withdrawn for
