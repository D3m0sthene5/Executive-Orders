Executive Order 5781. January 28 , 1932
Executive Order
APPOINTMENT OF MRS . FLORENCE C. BROCK
Mrs. Florence C. Brock , widow of Thomas S. Brock who served for over 30
years in and under the Department of the Interior and the Department of Agri-
culture, may be appointed to the position of junior clerk, Bureau of Public Roads ,
Department of Agriculture , without reference to the requirements of the civil -service
rules.
THE WHITE HOUSE,
January 28, 1932 .
[No. 5781 ]
