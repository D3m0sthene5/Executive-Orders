Executive Order 5839. April 19 , 1932
Executive Order
APPOINTMENT OF MR . RALPH A. VESTAL
Mr. Ralph A. Vestal may be appointed to the position of junior clerk in the
Department of State without compliance with the requirements of the civil-service
rules.
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
HERBERT HOOVER
April 19, 1932.
