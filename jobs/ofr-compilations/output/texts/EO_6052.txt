Executive Order 6052. February 27 , 1933
Executive Order
APPOINTMENT OF MRS . MARIE K. KALEY
Mrs. Marie K. Kaley may be appointed an inspectress of customs in the Office
of the Collector of Customs at New York , N. Y. , without compliance with the
requirements of the civil -service rules .
This order is recommended by the Secretary of the Treasury , who states that
Mrs. Kaley is the widow of Deputy Collector of Customs Francis Kaley , who gave
44 years of exceptionally faithful and intelligent service to the Government and died
on November 30 , 1932, leaving his widow without adequate means of support .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
February 27, 1933 .
[No. 6052 ]
