Executive Order 6056. February 28 , 1933
Executive Order
CLASSIFICATION OF MR. G. HAROLD KEATLEY
G. Harold Keatley , of Pennsylvania, who , since April 4, 1929 , has been serving
in a position in the Department of State excepted from civil -service requirements ,
may be appointed in the classified service without compliance with civil -service
rules .
This order is issued upon the recommendation of the Secretary of State .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
February 28 , 1933 .
[No. 6056]
