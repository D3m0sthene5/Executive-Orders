Executive Order 5879. July 7, 1932
Executive Order
APPOINTMENT OF MRS . A. BLONDELL HOWE
Mrs. A. Blondell Howe may be appointed to a clerical position in the Internal
Revenue Bureau of the Treasury Department without compliance with the rules
of the Civil Service Commission .
Mrs. Howe is the widow of Wyman R. Howe who served in the Treasury as
telegrapher and assistant supervisor of the Internal Revenue Bureau Telegraph
Office from January 10, 1918, to the date of his death, November 4, 1930. Mr.
Howe's death left his widow with four young children without adequate means of
support.
This order is recommended by the Secretary of the Treasury .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
July 7, 1932 .
