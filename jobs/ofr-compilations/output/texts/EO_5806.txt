Executive Order 5806. February 25, 1932
Executive Order
PARTIAL RESTORATION OF PUBLIC LAND FOR AIRPORT
ALASKA
Executive Order No. 3672 , dated May 8 , 1922 , reserving certain therein -described
land inAlaska for town -site purposes , is hereby revoked , in so far as it applies to
tract 32 , containing 140.24 acres , shown on a supplemental plat of survey, Fourth
Addition , Anchorage Town Site, township 13 north , range 3 west , Seward meridian ,
Alaska , accepted May 29 , 1931 .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
February 25, 1932 .
HERBERT HOOVER
