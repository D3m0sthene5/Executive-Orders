Executive Order 6059. March 2, 1933
Executive Order
ΑΡΡΟΙΝΤΜΕNT OF IRVIN S. GOLDBARTH
Irvin S. Goldbarth may be appointed as Clerk in the Custodian Service at the
Federal Building in Richmond , Virginia , without compliance with the requirements
of the Civil Service rules .
This order is recommended by the Secretary of the Treasury.
HERBERT HOOVER
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
March 2, 1933 .
[No. 6059 ]
