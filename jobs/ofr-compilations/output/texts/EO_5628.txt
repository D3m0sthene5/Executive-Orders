Executive Order 5628. May 20 , 1931
Executive Order
Appointment of Mrs. Amy Cowing
Mrs. Amy Cowing may be appointed as a clerk-typist in the Department of
Agriculture without reference to the provisions of the civil -service rules .
This order is issued upon the recommendation of the Secretary ofAgriculture .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
May 20, 1931 .
