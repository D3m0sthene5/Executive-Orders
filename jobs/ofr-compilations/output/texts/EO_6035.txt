Executive Order 6035. February 18 , 1933
Executive Order
APPOINTMENT OF MR. KENNETH C. MACPHERSON
Kenneth C. Macpherson , who , since July 11 , 1922, has been employed in the
Department of Commerce and the Post Office Department in positions excepted from
civil -service requirements , may be appointed in the classified service without com-
pliance with civil -service rules .
This order is issued upon the recommendation of the Postmaster General .
HERBERT HOOVER
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
February 18, 1933 .
