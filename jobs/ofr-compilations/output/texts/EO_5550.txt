Executive Order 5550. February 6, 1931
Executive Order
Withdrawal of Public Lands for Classification
California
Under authority of the act of Congress approved June 25, 1910 (36 Stat . 847) , as
amended by act of August 24, 1912 (37 Stat . 497) , it is hereby ordered that the public
lands in the areas hereinafter described be , and the same are hereby , withdrawn
from settlement , location, sale , entry, or other disposition , and reserved for classifi-
cation and in aid of the administration and control of the Government -owned oil
and gas deposits in the Kettleman Hills field :
