Executive Order 5733. October 16, 1931
Executive Order
Order of Modification
Coal Withdrawal Wyoming No. 1
Executive order of July 13 , 1910, effecting Coal Withdrawal Wyoming No. 1 ,
under the provisions of the act approved June 25 , 1910 (36 Stat . 847) , is hereby modi-
fied to permit the withdrawal and use of the following-described land as an air navi-
gation site under the provisions of the act approved May 24, 1928 (45 Stat . 728) :
SIXTH PRINCIPAL MERIDIAN
T. 20 N. , R. 91 W. , sec. 4 , SW . ¼ SW . 4.
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
October 16 , 1931 .
