Executive Order 5556. February 11 , 1931
Executive Order
Extension of Trust Period on Allotments of Potawatomi
Indians of Kansas
It is hereby ordered , under authority contained in the act of June 21, 1906 (34
Stat . 325-326 ), that the trust period on allotments made to members of the Prairie
Band of Potawatomi Indians of Kansas,which trust period expires during the calendar
year 1931 , be, and the same is hereby , extended for a period of 10 years .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
February 11 , 1931 .
[No. 5556]
