Executive Order 5666. July 3 , 1931
Executive Order
Permitting Persons Holding Certain Positions to Receive Appoint-
ments under the Department of Commerce
Executive Order No. 9 of January 17, 1873 , prohibiting the holding of Federal
and State offices at the same time, is hereby amended so as to permit persons holding
State , Territorial , or municipal positions to receive , unless prohibited by law,
appointments under the Department of Commerce when the Secretary of that

tration of the duties of his department .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
