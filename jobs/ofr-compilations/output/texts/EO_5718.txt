Executive Order 5718. September 17 , 1931
Executive Order
Revocation of Withdrawals of Public Lands for Classification
California
The withdrawal made by Executive Order No. 4331 of October 28 , 1925, of lot 6
sec. 6, lots 1 and 2 sec . 7, T. 7 N. , R. 1 E. , Humboldt meridian, California , is hereby
revoked , and the withdrawal made by Executive Order No. 5237 of December 10,
1929, is also hereby revoked in so far as it affects the above -described lands .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
September 17, 1931 .
