Executive Order 6057. March 1, 1933
Executive Order
APPOINTMENT OF CLARA L. HESS
Clara L. Hess , who , since March 16 , 1927, has been serving in a position in the
Post Office Department excepted from civil -service requirements , may be appointed
in the classified civil service without compliance with civil -service rules .
This order is issued upon the recommendation of the Postmaster General .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
March 1 , 1933 .
[No. 6057]
