Executive Order 5669. July 14, 1931
Executive Order
Public Water Restoration No. 70
Montana and Utah
So much of Executive orders of April 28, 1917, creating Public Water Reserve
No. 49, Montana No. 4, and March 29, 1912 , creating Public Water Reserve No. 1 ,
Utah No. 1, as affects the land hereinafter listed is hereby revoked , and all such land
not otherwise reserved or withdrawn is hereby restored to the public domain and shall
become subject to disposition under the laws applicable thereto upon such date and
after such notice as may be hereafter determined and announced .

T. 20 S. , R. 19 W. , sec. 10, SW . ¼ NE . ¼, NW . ¼ SE . 4.
