Executive Order 5720. September 18, 1931
Executive Order
Appointment of Mrs. Gladys W. Eldridge
Mrs. Gladys W. Eldridge may be appointed to a clerical position in the Depart-
ment of Agriculture without regard to the civil -service requirements .
This order is issued upon the recommendation of the Secretary of Agriculture .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
HERBERT HOOVER
September 18 , 1931 .
