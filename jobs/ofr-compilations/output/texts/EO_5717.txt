Executive Order 5717. September 17 , 1931
Executive Order
Modification of Public Water Reserve No. 116
California
The Executive order of May 16, 1928, creating Public Water Reserve No. 116 ,
is hereby modified to the extent of authorizing the Federal Power Commission to
issue a license for Project No. 1123 on the following-described lands :
SAN BERNARDINO MERIDIAN
T. 1 N. , R. 1 W. , sec . 14, SW . ¼ SW . 4.
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
September 17 , 1931 .
