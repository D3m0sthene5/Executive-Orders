Executive Order 6042. February 21 , 1933
Executive Order
ΑΡΡΟΙΝΤΜΕNT OF MRS . GRACE F. SMYTHE
Mrs. Grace F. Smythe , who , since February 8, 1924 , has been serving in a posi-
tion in the Post Office Department excepted from civil -service requirements , may be
appointed in the classified service without compliance with civil -service rules .
This order is issued upon the recommendation of the Postmaster General .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
February 21 , 1933 .
[No. 6042]
