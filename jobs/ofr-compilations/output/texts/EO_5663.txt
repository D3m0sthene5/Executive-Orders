Executive Order 5663. July 1, 1931
Executive Order
Designation of Horace Paul Bestor as Farm Loan Commissioner
Under the provisions of section 3 of the Federal Farm Loan Act, approved July
17, 1916 (39 Stat . 360) , Horace Paul Bestor, who has been duly appointed a member
of the Federal Farm Loan Board, is hereby designated Farm Loan Commissioner of
said board until otherwise directed .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
HERBERT HOOVER
July 1, 1931 .
