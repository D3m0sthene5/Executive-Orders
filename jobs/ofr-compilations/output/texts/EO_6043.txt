Executive Order 6043. February 23, 1933
Executive Order
AMENDMENT OF CIVIL SERVICE RULE IX
Civil Service Rule IX, section 1 (6), is hereby amended to read as follows :
(b) A former classified employee entitled to military preference in appointment
may be reinstated without time limit .
The object and result of this amendment is to make the military -preference
benefits uniform .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
February 23, 1933 .
[No. 6043]
