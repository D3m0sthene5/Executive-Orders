Executive Order 5977. December 19, 1932
Executive Order
EXCUSING EMPLOYEES OF THE GOVERNMENT DECEMBER 24 AND 31, 1932
It is hereby ordered that the several executive departments and independent
Government establishments in the District of Columbia , including the Government
Printing Office and the Navy Yard and stations , be closed on Saturday , December
24, 1932 , and Saturday, December 31 , 1932, and all cierks and other employees in
the Federal service in the District of Columbia, except those who may for special
public reasons be excepted from the provisions of this order , or those whose absence
from duty would be inconsistent with the provisions of existing law, are hereby
excused from duty on those days.
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
December 19, 1932.
HERBERT HOOVER
[No. 5977 ]
