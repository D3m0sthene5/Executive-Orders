WITHDRAWAL OF PUBLIC LANDS IN AID OF LEGISLATION
AND
REVOCATION OF EXECUTIVE ORDER No. 1529 OF MAY 9 , 1912
CALIFORNIA

amended by the act of August 24 , 1912 (37 Stat . 497) , and subject to the conditions ,
provisions , and limitations of said acts , it is hereby ordered that the unappropriated
public lands in the townships hereinafter described be, and the same are hereby ,
withdrawn from settlement , location , sale , entry, and all forms of appropriation ,
subject to all valid existing rights, for classification and in aid of legislation :
