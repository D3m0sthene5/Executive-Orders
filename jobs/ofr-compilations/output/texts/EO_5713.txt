Executive Order 5713. September 14 , 1931
Executive Order
Order of Modification
Coal Withdrawal Wyoming No. 1
Executive order of July 13 , 1910, effecting Coal Withdrawal Wyoming No. 1 ,
under the provisions of the act approved June 25, 1910 (36 Stat . 847), is hereby
modified to permit the withdrawal and use of the following-described land in the
maintenance of air-navigation facilities under the provisions of the act approved
May 24, 1928 (45 Stat . 728 ).
SIXTH PRINCIPAL MERIDIAN
T. 20 N. , R. 91 W. , sec. 4 , SW . ¼ SE . .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
September 14, 1931 .
