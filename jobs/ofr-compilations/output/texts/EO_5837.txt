Executive Order 5837. April 13 , 1932
Executive Order
AMENDMENT TO TARIFF OF UNITED STATES CONSULAR FEES
The following is established as item 47 of the Tariff of United States Consular
Fees:
47. For certifying to the correctness of a copy of any certificate of American
origin, manufacture , or inspection issued by any department or office of the Govern-
ment of the United States , where such certified copy is required for the purpose of
advancing the export trade of the United States No fee .
HERBERT HOOVER
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
April 13, 1932 .
