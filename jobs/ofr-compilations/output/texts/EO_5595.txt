Executive Order 5595. April 9 , 1931
Executive Order
The Honorable Nicholas Longworth
As a mark of respect to the memory of the Honorable Nicholas Longworth , late
Speaker and Member of the House of Representatives of the United States , it is
hereby ordered that the flags on the White House and public buildings in the District
of Columbia and the State of Ohio be placed at half mast to-day and on the day of the
funeral service at Cincinnati , Ohio, Saturday, April 11 , 1931 .
HERBERT HOOVER
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
April 9, 1931 .
