Executive Order 5890. July 16, 1932
Executive Order
PARTIAL REVOCATION OF WITHDRAWAL OF PUBLIC LANDS
CALIFORNIA
It is hereby ordered that Executive Order No. 4652 , dated May 18 , 1927 , with-
drawing certain lands in California and Nevada for use by the Department of Com-
merce as air-mail beacon sites and landing fields , is hereby revoked in so far as it
affects the NW . 4 sec . 20, T. 9 N. , R. 2 W. , San Bernardino meridian , California .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
July 16, 1932.
[No. 5890]
