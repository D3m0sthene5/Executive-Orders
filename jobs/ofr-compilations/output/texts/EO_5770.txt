Executive Order 5770. December 30, 1931
Executive Order
PORT EVERGLADES , FLA. , DESIGNATED A CUSTOMS PORT OF ENTRY
By virtue of the authority vested in me by the act of Congress approved
August 1, 1914 (U. S. Code, title 19, sec . 2), Port Everglades, Fla ., is hereby desig-
nated a customs port of entry in Customs Collection District No. 18 (Florida ) with
headquarters at Tampa , Fla ., effective January 1, 1932 .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
December 30 , 1931 .
