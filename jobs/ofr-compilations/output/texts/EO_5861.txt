Executive Order 5861. June 23, 1932
Executive Order
APPOINTMENT OF EUGENE R. CAPOZIO
Eugene R. Capozio may be appointed to the position of clerk at $1,260 per
annum in the Veterans ' Administration without reference to the requirements of the
civil-service rules .
This order is issued upon the recommendation of the Administrator of Veterans '
Affairs .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
June 23, 1932 .
HERBERT HOOVER
[No. 5861 ]
