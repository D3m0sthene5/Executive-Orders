Executive Order 6007. January 26, 1933
Executive Order
ΑΡΡΟΙΝΤΜΕNT OF CARL R. SHEPARD
Carl R. Shepard , of Washington, D. C. , may be appointed a junior construction
engineer under the Office of the Supervising Architect of the Treasury Department
without compliance with the requirements of the civil -service rules .
This order is recommended by the Secretary of the Treasury, who states that
Mr. Shepard's services can be used to advantage in connection with the large building
program in which the Office of the Supervising Architect is now engaged .
THE WHITE HOUSE ,
January 26, 1933 .
[No. 6007 ]
