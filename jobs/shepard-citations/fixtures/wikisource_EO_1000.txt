Executive Order 1000 (1908)
by President of the United States
Executive Order 1001

It is hereby ordered that the lands withdrawn from settlement and set apart for the use of the Indians as an addition to the Navajo Reservation by Executive orders dated November 9, 1907, and January 28, 1908, be, and the same are hereby, restored to the public domain.

Theodore Roosevelt.

The White House

December 30, 1908.
