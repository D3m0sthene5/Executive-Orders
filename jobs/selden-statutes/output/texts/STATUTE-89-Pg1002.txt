Public Law 94-.166 
94th Congress 
An Act 
To provide for allotment or assignment of payments from civil service annuities, 
and for other purjwses. 
Be it enacted hy the Senate and House of Representatives of the 
United States of America in Congress assemhled, That section 8345 
of title 5, United States Code, is amended by adding at the end thereof 
the following new subsection: 
"(g) An individual entitled to an annuity from the Fund may make 
allotments or assignments of amounts from his annuity for such 
purposes as the Civil Service Commission in its sole discretion con­
siders appropriate.". 
SEC. 2. Section 8346(a) of title 5, United States Code, is amended 
to read as follows: 
"(a) The money mentioned by this subchapter is not assignable, 
either in law or equity, except under the provisions of section 8345(g) 
of this title, or subject to execution, levy, attachment, garnishment, or 
other legal process, except as otherwise may be provided by Federal 
laws.". 
Approved December 23, 1975.
