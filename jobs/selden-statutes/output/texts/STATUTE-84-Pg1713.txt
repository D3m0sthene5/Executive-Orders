submitted to the Secretary of Health, Education, and Welfare, or to

of this Act may be approved under section 110 of the Clean Air Act

"§ 215. Territorial highway program 
" (a) Recognizing the mutual benefits that will accrue to the Virgin

lishment of advance acquisition of right-of-way and relocation assist­

necessary interisland connectors, built in accordance with standards 
approved by the Secretary; (2) will not impose any toll, or permit any
