FORTY-NINTH CONGRESS. Sess . II. Chs . 55-59. 1887.
CHAP. 55.—An act granting an increase of pension to Colonel Charles E. Capehart.
Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United 
States of America in Congress assembled, That the Secretary of the In-
terior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the 
pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension 
laws, the name of Charles E. Capehart, late lieutenant-colonel of the 
First Regiment of West Virginia Cavalry, at the rate of forty-five dol-
lars per month, in lieu of the pension he now receives.
Approved, January 27, 1887.

CHAP. 58.—An act granting a pension to Mrs. Abbie B. Heath.
Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United 
States of America in Congress assembled, That the Secretary of the In-
terior be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the 
pension-roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension

CHAP. 59.—An act granting an increase of pension to Elvira Bliss Sheldon.
Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the United 
States of Américain Congress assembled, That the Secretary of the Interior 
be, and he is hereby, authorized and directed to place on the pension-
roll, subject to the provisions and limitations of the pension laws, the 
name of Elvira Bliss Sheldon, an ex-Army nurse and widow of Joel S. 
Sheldon, late a commissary and lieutenant of volunteers, and pay her 
a pension at the rate of thirty dollars per month, in lieu of the pension 
now received by her.
Approved, January 27, 1887.
