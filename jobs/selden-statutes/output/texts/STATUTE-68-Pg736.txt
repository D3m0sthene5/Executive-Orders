SEC. 2. (a) Except as provided in (b) of this section, each appoin­

shall be issued only after consultation with the head of the depart­
ment, establishment, agency, or other employing authority concerned: 
Provided^ That no employee or group of employees shall be excluded 
solely on the basis of the hazardous nature of employment.

SEC. 12. (a) There is hereby established an Advisory Council on

without compensation, to advise the Commission regarding matters of 
concern to employees under this Act.
