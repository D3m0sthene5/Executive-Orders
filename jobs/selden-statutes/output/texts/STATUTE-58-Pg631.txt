CHAPTER 325] AN ACT
To amend the Emergency Price Control Act of 1942, as amended, and the Stabili-
zation Act of October 2, 1942, as amended, and for other purposes.
Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the 
United States of America in Congress assembled, That this Act may-
be cited as the “Stabilization Extension Act of 1944”.
