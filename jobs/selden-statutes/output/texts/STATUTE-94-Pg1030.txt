An Act 
To amend title 38, United States Code, to promote the recruitment and retention of 
physicians, dentists, nurses, and other health-care personnel in the Department of 
Medicine and Surgery of the Veterans' Administration, and for other purposes. 
Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the 
United States of America in Congress assembled. That (a) this Act 
may be cited as the "Veterans' Administration Health-Care Amend­
ments of 1980". 
(b) Except as otherwise expressly provided, whenever in this Act an 
amendment or repeal is expressed in terms of an amendment to, or 
repeal of, a section or other provision, the reference shall be consid­
ered to be made to a section or other provision of title 38, United 
States Code.
