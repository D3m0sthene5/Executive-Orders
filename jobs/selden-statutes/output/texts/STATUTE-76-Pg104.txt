To authorize the Bureau of the Census to make appropriate reimbursements

Be it en/icted hy the Senate and House of Representatwes of the

I of chapter I of title 13, United States Code, is amended by adding 
at the end of such subchapter the following new section: 
**§ 14. Reimbursement between appropriations 
"Subject to limitations applicable with respect to each appropriation 
concerned, each appropriation available to the Bureau may be charged, 
.at any time during a fiscal year, for the benefit of any other appropria­
tion available to the Bureau, for the purpose of financing the procure­
ment of materials and services, or financing activities or other costs, 
for which funds are available both in the financing appropriation so 
charged and in the appropriation so benefited; except that such ex­
penses so financed shall be charged on a final basis, as of a date not 
later than the close of such fiscal year, to the appropriation so bene­
fited, with appropriate credit to the financing appropriation.". 
(b) The table of contents of such subchapter I is amended by
