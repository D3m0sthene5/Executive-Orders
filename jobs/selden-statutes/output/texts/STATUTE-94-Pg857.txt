expended for personal services, or for purposes involving personal

hereby increased to the extent necessary to meet increased pay costs 
authorized by or pursuant to law.

are made under this Act shall take immediate action (1) to improve 
the collection of overdue debts owed to the United States within the 
jurisdiction of that department or agency; (2) to bill interest on 
delinquent debts as required by the Federal Claims Collection 
Standards; and (3) to reduce amounts of such debts written off as 
uncollectible. 
SEC. 307. (a) Effective October 1,1981, for application in fiscal year 
1982, a department, agencjr, or establishment, as defined by section 2,
