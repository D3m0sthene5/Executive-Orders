TWO ISLANDS NEAR ST. AUGUSTINE.
These are small Islands in the main channel of the Mantanzas River
near St. Augustine and contain an area aggregating about 2 acres.
The title is as follows: As part of the public domain they were re-
served for military purposes by Executive Order dated May 31, 1892.
For jurisdiction see General Act of Cession.
