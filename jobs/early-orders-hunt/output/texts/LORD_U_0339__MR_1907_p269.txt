FORT RENO.
This reservation contains an area of 9,493 acres, and is situated on
the North Fork of the Canadian River, in Canadian County. The
title is as follows :
Set apart from the public domain by being included within the
limits of the Cheyenne and Arapahoe Indian Reservation, by Execu-
tive Order dated August 10, 1809. It was proclaimed a military
reservation by Executive Order dated July 17, 1883.
Revocable Licenses: License, January 9, 1891, to the Choctaw,
Oklahoma and Gulf Railroad Company "to enter upon, construct and
maintain a railroad upon the reservation.
License, October 11, 1894, to William Sigismond Jr. to erect at his
own expense a small frame building for use as post office.
License, March 9, 1896, to Chas. H. Todd to keep small livery
stable, and to Maggie Hawkins, laundress, to reside upon the reser-
vation.
License, March 18, 1897, to George Washington Lodge No. 13*,
Knights of Pythias to erect a lodge building on the reservation.
License, May 28, 1897, to the Choctaw, Oklahoma and Gulf Rail-
way Company to build a spur track upon the reservation.
License, September 23, 1897, to the Missouri, Kansas and Texas
Telephone Company to operate a telephone line upon the reservation.
License, January 10, 1898, to the Choctaw, Oklahoma and Gulf
Railway Company to extend its spur track on the reservation.
License, March 8, 1898, to the Choctaw, Oklahoma and Gulf Rail-
road Company to extend and operate its line of railroad through the
reservation.
