OKLAHOMA.
Oklahoma Station , situated in Oklahoma County. Established by Executive order
of April 9, 1889. Relinquished September 28, 1892, for disposal under the act of July
5, 1884, or as may be otherwise provided by law. Surveyed. Area 160 acres, being
the SW. J sec. 34, T. 12 N., R. 3 W. The act of August 8, 1894, grants this reser¬
vation to the city of Oklahoma lor school purposes.
