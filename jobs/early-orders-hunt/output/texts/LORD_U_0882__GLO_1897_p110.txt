ARKANSAS.
Little Rock Barracks, or Arsenal, situated in the city of Little Rock. Estab¬
lished April 11, 1839. Relinquished October 3, 1890, under act of July 5, 1884, with
32 buildings, fences, etc., valued at $60,081. Surveyed. Area, 36.01 acres.
Granted to the city of Little Rock, Ark., by the act of April 23, 1892 (29 Stat. L.,
20), under certain conditions, which h,ave been complied with.
