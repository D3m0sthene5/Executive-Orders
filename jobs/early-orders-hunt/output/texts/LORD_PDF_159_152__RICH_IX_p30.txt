AMENDMENT OF CIVIL- SERVICE RULES .
JULY 26 , 1889 .
Clause (h ) of section 2 of General Rule III is hereby amended by
adding to that clause , at the end thereof , the following : " or for tempo-
rary appointment for not exceeding thirty days in any part of the classi-
fied service ."
Approved :
BENJ . HARRISON .
