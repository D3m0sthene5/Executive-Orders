July 18, 1898
In nearly all these cases the findings and recommendations of this
office have received the approval of the Department, which in return-
ing them gave instructions as above stated relative to the recovery of
title to erroneously certified or patented lands or of the value thereof.
Readjustments of the grants to aid in the construction of Northern
Pacific Railroad in Minnesota and Washington, the St. Louis, Iron
Mountain and Southern Railroad in Arkansas, the Brainerd branch of
the St. Paul, Minneapolis and Manitoba Railroad, and St. Paul and
Northern Pacific Railroad in Minnesota, the Oregon and California
Railroad in Oregon are in process, and it is expected will be completed
in a short time.
The adjustment of the Cedar Rapids and Missouri River Railroad
grant was returned to this office, but is again before the Department
on motion for re-review.
