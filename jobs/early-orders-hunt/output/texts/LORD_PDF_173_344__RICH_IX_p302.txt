AMENDMENT OF CIVIL - SERVICE RULES .
JANUARY 20 , 1892 .
Special Departmental Rule No. 1 is hereby amended by adding at the
end of paragraph 10 the following : "and elevator conductors ;" so that
as amended the paragraph will read :
In all the Departments : Bookbinders and elevator conductors .
BENJ . HARRISON .
