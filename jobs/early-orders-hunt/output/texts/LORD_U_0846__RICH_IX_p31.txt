AMENDMENT OF CIVIL - SERVICE RULES .
OCTOBER 29 , 1889 .
Section 2 of Special Departmental Rule No. 1 is hereby amended by
adding to the places excepted from examination in the Bureau of
Engraving and Printing the following : " plate cleaners , transferrers ,
hardeners , provers , pressmen , machinists , plumbers , carpenters , and black-
smiths ."
Approved :
BENJ . HARRISON .
