SAFETY HARBOR.
By Executive Order of September 21, 1905 (G. O. 161, W. D.,
September 30, 1905), a tract of about 14.776 acres at this place was
reserved, subject to private rights, for the use of the Signal Corps,
U. S. Army, in the matter of the operation of military cable and
telegraph Imes in Alaska.
