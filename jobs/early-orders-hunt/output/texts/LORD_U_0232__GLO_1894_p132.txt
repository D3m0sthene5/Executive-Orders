MISSOURI.
Island in Missouri River, situated in Jackson County, in secs. 28 and 33, T. 50 N.,
R. 33 W. Established by Executive order of March 10, 1865. Relinquished July 22,
1884. Area, 54.70 acres. No improvements. Surveyed. Ready for appraisal.
