dated August 19, 1893, has, pursuant to law, directed the establishment of the fol¬
lowing-named land districts in the lands referred to, and now forming a part of the
Territory of Oklahoma, and the location of the offices for said land districts at the
points named :
1. The Perry land district, comprising counties K, P, and Q and a part of Payne
County, and including the lands within the following-described boundaries: Begin¬
ning at the middle of the main channel of the Arkansas River, where the same is
intersected by the northern boundary of the Oklahoma Territory ; thence west to the
northwest corner of T. 29 N., E. 2 ff.; thence south on the range line between Rs. 2
and 3 W. to the southwest corner of lot 3, sec. 31, T. 20 N., R. 2 W. ; thence east to
the southeast corner of lot 4, sec. 36, T. 20 N., R. 4 E. ; thence south on the range
line between Rs. 4 and 5 E. to the middle of the main channel of the Cimarron River;
thence down said river in the middle of the main channel thereof to the western
boundary of the Creek country; thence north to the northwest corner of the Creek
country; thence east on the northboundary of said Creek country to the middle of
the main channel of the Arkansas River; thence up said river in the middle of the
main channel thereof to the place of beginning. The same to constitute the Perry
land district, and the local land office for said district to be located at the town of
Perry, in county P.
2. The Enid land district, comprising counties L and O and including the lands
within the following-described boundaries: Beginning at the northeast corner of I.
29 N., R. 3 W. of the Indian meridian; thence west to the northwest corner of T. 29 N.,
R. 8 W. ; thence south on range line between Rs. 8 and 9 W. to the southwest corner of
lot 3, sec. 31, T. 20 N., R. 8 W. ; thence east to the southeast corner of lot 4, sec. 36, T.
20 N., R. 3 W. ; thence north on the range line between Rs. 2 and 3 W . to the place of
beginning. The same to constitute the Enid land district, and the local land office
for said district to be located at the town of Enid, in county O.
3. The Alva land district, comprising county M and including the lands within
the following described boundaries: Beginning at the northeast corner of f. 29 N.,
R. 9 W. of the Indian meridian ; thence west to the northwest corner of T. 29 N., R. 16
W. ; thence south on the range line between Rs. 16 and 17 W. to the southwest corner
of lot 3, sec. 31, T. 20 N., R. 16 W. ; thence east to the southeast corner of lot 4, sec.
36, T. 20 N., R. 9 W. ; thence north on range line between Rs. 8 and 9 W. to the place
of beginning. The same to constitute the Alva land district, and the local land
office for said district to be located at the town of Alva, in county M.
