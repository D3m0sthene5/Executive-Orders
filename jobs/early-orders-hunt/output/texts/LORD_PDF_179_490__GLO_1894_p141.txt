No. 966. — Notice of the discontinuance of the land office at Grand Island , Nehr., and its
consolidation with the land office at Lincoln, Nebr.
Notice is hereby given that the President of the United States, by Executive order
dated September 11, 1893, has, pursuant to law, directed that the U. S. local land office
now located at Grand Island, in the State of Nebraska, be discontinued, and its
business and archives transferred to and consolidated with the Lincoln land office
in said State.
Further notice of the precise time when the land officers at Grand Island will cease
to receive application for and transact business relating to the lands in their district,
preparatory to removal, and when the land officers at Lincoln will be in readiness
to receive application for the lands transferred, will be given by the register and
receiver at Lincoln by publication.
Given under my hand, at the city of Washington, this 25th day of October, A. D.
1893.
By the President : S. W. Lamoreux,
Commissioner of the General Land Office.
No. 967. — Notice of the discontinuance of the land office at Kirwin, Kans., and its consol¬
idation ivith the land office at Oberlin, Kans.
Notice is hereby given that the President of the United States, by Executive order
dated September 11, 1893, has, pursuant to law, directed that the U. S. local land
office now located at Kirwin, in the State of Kansas, be discontinued, and its busi¬
ness and archives transferred to and consolidated with the Oberlin land office in said
State.
And by said Executive order the President has further directed that the office for
the consolidated land district shall be located at Colby, Kans.
Further notice of the precise time when the land officers at Kirwin will cease to
receive application for and transact business relating to the lands in their district,
preparatory to removal, and when the land officers at Colby will be in readiness to
receive application for the lands transferred, will be given by the register and
receiver at Colby by publication.
Given under my hand, at the city of Washington, this 20th day of December, A. D.
1893.
By the President: S. W. Lamoreux,
Commissioner of the General Land Office .
