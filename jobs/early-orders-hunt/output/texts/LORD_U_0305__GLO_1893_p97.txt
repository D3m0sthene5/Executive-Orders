KANSAS.
Fort Dodge (remainder), situated in Ford County. Established by Executive order
of June 22, 1868. Relinquished January 12, 1885, with 41 structures, valued at
$20,000 if sold with the ground. Surveyed. Area, 14,661 acres. All of this tract
except 1,882.89 acres is within the limits of the Osage Indian trust lands, and under
date of July 9, 1886, the district officers at Garden City, Kans., were directed to
allow entries of said Osage Indian trust lands as provided by act of May 28, 1880
(21 Stats., 143), with the exception of tracts upon which buildings erected by the
Government for military purposes are located, which latter tracts were found to be
lots 3, 5, 6, and 7, sec. 3, T. 27 S., R. 24 W. By act of March 2, 1889 (25 Stats., 1012),
authority was given to sell and convey to the State of Kansas the said lots, and on
June 13, 1889, the same were purchased by the State.
