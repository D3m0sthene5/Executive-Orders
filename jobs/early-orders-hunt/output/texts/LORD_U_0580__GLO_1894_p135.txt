UTAH.
Fort Cameron, formerly Beaver Canon, in T. 29 S., R. 7 W. Established May 12,
1873. Enlarged by President’s orders of April 13, 1877, and November 10, 1879.
Relinquished July 2, 1885, under act of July 5, 1884. No improvements. Surveyed,
but plats not yet examined.
