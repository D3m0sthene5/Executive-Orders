Washington , May 27 , 1878.
SIR :* I am directed by the President to say that the several Depart-
ments of the Government will be closed on Thursday , the 30th instant , in
respect to the memory of those who fell in defense of the Union , and to
enable the employees to participate in the commemorative ceremonies
of the day.
Very respectfully , your obedient servant ,
W. K. ROGERS , Private Secretary .
* Addressed to the heads of the Executive Departments , etc.
