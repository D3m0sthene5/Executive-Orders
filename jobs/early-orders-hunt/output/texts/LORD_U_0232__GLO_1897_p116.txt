MISSOURI.
/
Island in Missouri River, situated in Jackson County, in secs. 28 and 33, T. 50 N.,
R. 33 W. Established by Executive order of March 10, 1865. Relinquished July
22, 1884. Area, 130.15 acres. No improvements. Surveyed. Appraised and
advertised to be sold August 11, 1896, but not sold for want of bidders.
