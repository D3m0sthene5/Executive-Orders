EXECUTIVE MANSION , March 6, 1894 .
Exceptions from examination are hereby made as follows : One super-
intendent and the necessary teachers , not exceeding four in number , for
the organization and equipment of a normal school to be established at
Albuquerque , N. Mex . , this rule to expire by limitation six months after
the date of its approval .
Approved : GROVER CLEVELAND .
