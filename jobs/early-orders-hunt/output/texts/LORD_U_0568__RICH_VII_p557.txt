Washington , D. C. , May 31 , 1879 .
To Medical Officers of the Marine -Hospital Service and others whom it may concern:
Official information having been received to the effect that the " plague " which
existed in southern Russia is now almost extinct , the regulations issued March 3,
1879,* imposing certain restrictions upon the importation of rags , etc. , into the United
States , are hereby revoked .
By order of the Secretary of the Treasury :
Approved :
J. B. HAMILTON ,
Surgeon - General United States Marine - Hospital Service .
R. B. HAYES .
