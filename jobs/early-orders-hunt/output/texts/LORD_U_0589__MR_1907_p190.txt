FORT ASSINNIBOINE.
The present area of this reservation is about 220,000 acres. The
Post was established May 19, 1879, under authority of an Act of Con-
gress approved June 18, 1878, and lies within the limits of Choteau
County. As a part of the public domain the reservation was declared
by Executive Order dated March 4, 1880, and re-declared June 16,
1881. A new reservation, with the addition of Hay and Coal Field
reserves, was declared May 2, 1888, making a total of about 704,000
acres reserved. This area was reduced October 9, 1891, by trans-
ferring the Hay and Coal Field reserves to the Interior Department,
