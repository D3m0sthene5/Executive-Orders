DIAMOND HEAD.
This reservation contains an area of about 720 acres, and is situated
at or near Diamond Head and Kupikipikio Point, near Waialae Bay,
Island of Oahu. Part of the public domain, it was reserved for mili-
tary piirposes by Executive Order of January 18, 1906 (G. O. No. 21,
War Department, January 27, 1906). See Kapahuli, post.
Honolulu.
(Lots in.)
These reservations, three in number, and known as the Emmes
Wharf site, The Esplanade Lots and the Barracks Lot, contain respec-
tively 42,050, 55,830 and 98,260 square feet. They were reserved sub-
ject to private rights by Executive Order, dated December 19, 1899.
Lease: Lease for 5 years, August 17, 1903, of the Emmes Wharf
site to the Department of Public AVorks, Honolulu, Lease granted
in accordance with Act of Congress, approved July 28, 1892.
Revocable License^ January 12, 1907, to Territory of Hawaii for
storage for militia property on " Barracks Lot."
Kahauiki.
This reservation contains 1,344 acres, and is situated at Kahauiki.
Kona District, about three miles northwest of Honolulu, on the Island
of Oahu. A part of the Government domain, it was reserved for
military purposes, subject to unexpired leases, by Executive Order,
dated July 20, 1899 (G. O. No. 147, A. G. O., August 10, 1899).
