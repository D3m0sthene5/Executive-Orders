ASHLAND CEMETERY.
(Soldiers' lot)
This reservation contains 7,740 square feet of gi-ound, and is situ-
ated in "Ashland Cemetery," in the Borough of Carlisle and Coimty
of Cumberland. The title is as follows :
Deed from Valeria M. Penrose, Trustee, etc., dated September 19,
1878, conveying Lots 212 to 265, inclusive, in Section D of D. S.
Ettinger's plan No. 3 of Ashland Cemetery, containing 540 by 16
feet.
Deed given as a substitute for a deed dated in March, 1866, which
was lost.
For jurisdiction see Gleneral Act of Cession.
