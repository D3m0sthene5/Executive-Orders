TEXAS.
Block 108, located in the city of Houston, Tex. Area, 1.35 acres. Relinquished
January 16, 1891, for disposal under act of July 5, 1884. Act of March 1, 1889 (25
Stat. L., 781), provides, however, that said lot shall be disposed of by the Secretary
of the Treasury.
Fort Elliott, situated in Wheeler County. Date of establishment does not appear.
Relinquished October 2, 1890, with 38 buildings, valued at $32,320. Surveyed by
the State of Texas. Area, 2,560 acres.
Fort Hancock, situated in El Paso County, on the Rio Grande, 54 miles southwest
of El Paso. War Department on October 8, 1895, reported that the property was
acquired by purchases made April 14 and August 31, 1883. Relinquished October
25, 1895, with 38 buildings. Area, 469.2 acres. Not surveyed by the Land
Department.
