WASHINGTON , October 19 , 1869 .
All communications in writing intended for the executive department
of this Government and relating to public business of whatever kind ,
including suggestions for legislation , claims , contracts , employment , ap-
pointments , and removals from office , and pardons , must be transmitted
through the Department to which the care of the subject -matter of the
communication properly belongs . Communications otherwise transmit-
ted will not receive attention .
By order of the President :
