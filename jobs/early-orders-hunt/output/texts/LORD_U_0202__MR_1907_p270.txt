SAND ISLAND.
This reservation contains an area of 192.07 acres, according to a sur-
vey made prior to 1877. It is a shifting island, comprising parts of
Sections 14, 23 and 24, in Township 9 North, Range 11 West, of
Willamette Meridian, lying in or near the entrance to the Columbia
River. Taken from the public domain and declared a military reser-
vation by Executive Order dated August 29, 1863.
Jurisdiction was ceded to the United States (as also title) by an
act of the State Legislature approved October 21, 1864, which pro-
