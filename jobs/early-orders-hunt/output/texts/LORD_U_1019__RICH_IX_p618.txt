BY NONCOMPETITIVE EXAMINATION .
So much of Executive orders heretofore issued under General Rule III ,
section 2 , clause (c ) , as provides for the appointment of engineers and
assistant engineers by noncompetitive examination is hereby revoked , and
hereafter engineers and assistant engineers will be appointed by competi-
tive examination .
Approved , June 25 , 1895 .
GROVER CLEVELAND .
