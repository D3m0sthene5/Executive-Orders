EXECUTIVE MANSION , May 26, 1894 .
It is hereby ordered , That the several Executive Departments and the
Government Printing Office be closed on Wednesday , the 30th instant , to
enable the employees to participate in the decoration of the graves of the
soldiers and sailors who fell in defense of the Union during the War of
the Rebellion .
GROVER CLEVELAND .
