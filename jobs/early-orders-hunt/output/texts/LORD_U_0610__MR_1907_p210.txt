PORT WINGATE.
This reservation contains an area of 130 square miles and is situ-
ated in Bernalillo County on the line of the Atlantic and Pacific Rail-
road, in Townships 13, 14 and 16 North, Ranges 16, 16 and 17
West of the New Mexico Principal Meridian. Taken from the pub-
lic domain by Executive Order, reserving 100 square miles for mili-
tary purposes, dated February 18, 1870. Reservation enlarged by
Executive order dated March 26, 1881, by adding 30 square miles for
the purpose of supplying Post with timber, etc.

pany, successor to the Atlantic and Pacific Railroad Company, has
