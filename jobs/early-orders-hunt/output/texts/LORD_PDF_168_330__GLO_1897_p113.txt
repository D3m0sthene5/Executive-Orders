INDIAN TERRITORY.
Fort Gibson , situated in the Cherokee Nation. It was established by Executive
order of January 25, 1870. Relinquished by Executive order December 22, 1890,
to the Department for disposal under the act of July 5, 1884, “or as may be other¬
wise provided bylaw.” Executive order of February 9, 1891, excludes national
cemetery at this post from the transfer. By article 3 of the treaty of December 29,
1835 (7 Stat. L., 480), the lands in this reservation revert to the Indians.
