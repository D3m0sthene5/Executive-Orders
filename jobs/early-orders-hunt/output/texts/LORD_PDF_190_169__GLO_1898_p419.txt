ARREARS IN OFFICE WORK.
There are no arrears in office work requiring an additional appropriation for
clerk hire, as there only remain unworked up at this time returns of survey under
five contracts of special instructions. Much of the work on them has been per-
formed, leaving an amount of work sufficient only to keep the office force employed
until the returns of survey under contracts let this year are filed.
Very respectfully,
E. W. Beattie,
United States Surveyor-General for Montana.
The Commissioner of the General Land Office,
Washington, D. C.
