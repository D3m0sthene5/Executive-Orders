PUBLIC LANDS DIVISION. 157
Fort Laramie (wood and timber reserve), situated in Albany County, in Ts. 24
and 25 N., Rs. 70 and 71 W. Established by Executive order of February 9, 1881.
Placed under control of this Department by Executive order October *29, 1897.
Not surveyed. Estimated area, 89,680 acres.
Fort Sanders, situated in Albany County, in Ts. 14 and 15 N., Rs. 73 and 74 W.
Established January 7, 1867. Relinquished September 6, 1884, without improve-
ments. Act of May 28, 1888 (25 Stat. L., 158), grants 640 acres to the State for
the establishment of a fish hatchery. Surveyed. Area, 19,428.03 acres Disposed
of by the act of July 10, 1890 (26 Stat. L., 227).
