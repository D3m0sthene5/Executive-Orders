IDAHO.
Camp Three Forks Owyhee, situated in Owyhee County, probably in T. 8 S.,
R. 6 W. Established by Executive order April 6, 1860. Relinquished July 22,
1884, without improvements. Not surveyed.
Fort Cceur dAlene (winter pasturage), situated in Kootenai County, probably
in Ts. 50 and 51 N., R. 4 W. Established by Executive order of August 25, 1879.
Relinquished April 27, 1886, without improvements. Surveyed. Area, 640 acres.
Disposed of in accordance with law.
