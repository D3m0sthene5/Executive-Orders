BENICIA.
(Reservation of Post and Arsenal.)
That portion set apart for a Post or Barracks contains 98.78 acres,
and the Arsenal portion 238 acres. Total, 336.78 acres. This reserva-
tion is situated on Suisun Bay and the Straits of Carquinez, in Solano
County.
Title is as follows: The reservation for military purposes was de-
clared by Executive Orders dated October 7 and 10, 1862. Title to
certain tide-water lands, etc., was ceded by the State March 9, 1897.
