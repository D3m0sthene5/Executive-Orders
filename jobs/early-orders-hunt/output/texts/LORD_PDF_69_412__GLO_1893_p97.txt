IDAHO.
Camp Three Forks Owyhee, situated in Owyhee County, probably in T. 8 S., R. 6
W. Established by Executive order April 6, 1869. Relinquished J uly 22, 1884, with¬
out improvements. Not surveyed.
Fort Coeur D’Alene {winter pasturage ), situated in Kootenai County, probably in
T. 50 and 51 N., R. 4 W. Established by Executive order of August 25, 1879. Relin¬
quished April 27, 1886, without improvements. Contract was given for the survey
and definite location of this reservation, and subsequently canceled. The reserva¬
tion, therefore, has not been definitely located.
