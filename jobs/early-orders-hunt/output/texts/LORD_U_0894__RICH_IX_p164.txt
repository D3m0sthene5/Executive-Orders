AMENDMENT OF CIVIL -SERVICE RULES .
JANUARY 26 , 1891 .
Special Departmental Rule No. 1 is hereby amended by adding to the
exceptions from examination therein declared the following :
In the Department of Agriculture , in the office of the Secretary , division of illus-
tration and engraving : One artist .
BENJ . HARRISON .
