The War Department, on April 9, 1892, reported that there were no improve-
ments on the hay and coal reserve. Partially surveyed. Estimated total area,

By the act of April 18, 1896, the relinquished lands, together with lands which
might be thereafter excluded from the reservation, were excepted from the opera-
tion of laws relating to abandoned military reservations and made subject to dis-
posal under the homestead (except 2301, R.S.), town-site, coal, desert, andnrhieral
land laws, with a certain exception. (See instructions to register and receiver,
Helena, Mont., May 18, 1896.)
