PUBLIC LANDS.
129
Fort Yuma , situated in San Diego County, in T. 16 S., Rs. 22 and 23 E., S. B. M.
Portion in Yuma County, Ariz. Established by Executive order of January 22, 1867.
Transferred by Executive order of January 9, 1884, to the Interior Department for
Indian uses. This reservation was placed under the control of the Interior Depart¬
ment, tor disposal under the act of July 5, 1884, but under date of March 5, 1892, the
Interior Department held that under the order of January 9, 1884, the lands in this
reservation became a part of the Yuma Indian Reservation.
