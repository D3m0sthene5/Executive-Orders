INDIAN TERRITORY.
Fort Gibson, situated in the Cherokee Nation. It was established by Executive
order of January 25, 1870. Relinquished by Executive order December 22, 1890, to
this Department for disposal under the act of July 5, 1884, “ or as may be otherwise
provided by law.” Executive order of February 9, 1891, excludes national cemetery
at this post from the transfer.
The lands, being in the Indian Territory, will probably revert to the Indians.
