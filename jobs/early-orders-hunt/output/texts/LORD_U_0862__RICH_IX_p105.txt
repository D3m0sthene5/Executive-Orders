Washington , D. C. , May 31 , 1890 .
SIR : This Commission has the honor to recommend that Special Departmental
Rule No. I be amended by adding to the exceptions from examination therein
declared the following :
"In the Department of the Treasury , in the life -saving service : Topographer and
hydrographer ."
We have the honor to be , your obedient servants ,
Approved , June 3 , 1890 .
CHAS . LYMAN ,
THEODORE ROOSEVELT ,
HUGH S. THOMPSON ,
United States Civil Service Commissioners .
BENJ . HARRISON .
