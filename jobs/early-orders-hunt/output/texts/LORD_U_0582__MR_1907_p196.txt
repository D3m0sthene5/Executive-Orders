FORT NIOBRARA.
This reservation contains 94 square miles and 133 acres. It is sit-
uated on the Niobrara River about 4^ miles from Valentine, in Cherry
County. The Post was established under authority of an Act of Con-
gress approved Ju^ie 23, 1879, and lands, being a part of the public
domain, reserved for military purposes by Executive order dated
December 10, 1879 ; enlarged by Executive order dated June 6, 1881,
and modified by Executive order dated April 29, 1884. By Executive
order, dated May 7, 1896, a tract of 720 acres was transferred to the
Interior Department for disposition.
An addition of about 26,000 acres was made to this reservation by
Executive order of June 22, 1904, which, with the lands theretofore
reserved, aggregate about 60,000 acres.
Revocable Licenses: License, May 25, 1905, to J. O. Vincent, Post-
master at Fort Niobrara, for dwellmg and plot of ground to be occu-
pied in connection therewith.
