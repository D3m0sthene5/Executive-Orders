November 27, 1877, so as to contain 36 square miles, or 23,010 acres.
Old Camp Grant, situated at the junction of Arivapa Creek and Rio
San Pedro, contains 2,031.70 acres. The Secretary of War reports the
post as abandoned, and recommends to Congress that the reserve be
sold.
Camp Grant (new), at the foot of Graham Peak, in townships 8, 9,
and 10 south, ranges 23 and 21 east, reserved by President’s order dated
April 17, 1876, contains 12,311 acres.
