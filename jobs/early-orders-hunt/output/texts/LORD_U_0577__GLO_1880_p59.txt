dated November 4, 1879, sections 20 and 30, township 15 north, range
71 west, were reserved for wood and timber for Forts Sanders and D.
A. Russell and for Cheyenne Depot. By Executive order dated Feb¬
ruary 25, 1880, said wood and timber reservation was enlarged by add¬
ing to it sections 28 and 32 of same township. Area of wood and tim¬
ber reservation, 2,540.64 acres.
Fort Fred Steele, declared by Executive order dated June 28, 1869.
It is situated in townships 20 and 21 north, ranges 84 and 85 west, and
contains an area of 23,040 acres.
Sulphur Creek (coal lands for military purposes), declared by the
President April 6, 1859, situated in section 35, township 14 north, range
119 west. Area, 100 acres.
Fort McKinney, on Clear Fork of Powder River, originally declared
by Executive order dated July 2, 1879, containing 16 square miles,
enlarged by Executive order dated February 2, 1880, by the addition
on the west of the post reserve of a tract 6 by 4 miles for wood and
timber. Area of Fort McKinney, 25,600 acres.
Depot McKinney, declared by the President July 2, 1879; situated on
the Powder River. Area, 640 acres.
