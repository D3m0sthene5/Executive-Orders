FORT ROSECRANS. (POINT LOMA AND BALLAST POINT.
This reservation, containing 940.3 acres, consists of that portion of
the peninsula tying on the west side of the entrance to San Diego
Harbor, included between the southernmost point of the peninsula
(Point Loma) and a line drawn across said peninsula from the har-
bor to the ocean at the distance of 1J miles above Punta de Guiranos.
Title is as follows : Passed under the treaty of Guadalupe Hidalgo,
concluded February 2, 1848, to the United States. Reserved for mili-
tary purposes by Executive Order dated February 26, 1852.- See also
act of the State Legislature approved March 9, 1897, as to tide- water
lands, etc.
For jurisdiction, see General Act of Cession.
Revocable License. — License, May 14, 1889, to the Treasury De-
partment to occupy a portion of the reservation for light-house pur-
poses.
