EXECUTIVE MANSION , May 27, 1889 .
Departmental Rule VIII is hereby amended as follows :
At the end of section 1 insert an additional clause , as follows :I
( d) From the office of the President of the United States , after two years ' continu-
ous service therein immediately preceding the transfer , to any place in the classified
service without examination , upon the requisition of the head of the Department to
which the transfer is to be made and the certification of the Commission .
In section 2 , line 1 , after the word " authorized , " insert the following :
" except as provided in section 1 , clause ( d) . ”
BENJ . HARRISON .
