under the act of J uly 12, 1886 (24 Stats., 144), except a certain part, which may

Transfer made July 31, 1886.

Louisiana, appear to have been established by Executive order of March 5, 1844, as
follows:
Reservation near the eastern mouth of Bayou La Fourche ; area, 720 acres. It was
relinquished September 23, 1886, without improvements.

relinquished September 23, 1886, without improvements.

out improvements.

relinquished September 23, 1886, without improvements.
