Under authority vested in me by law it is ordered :
Effective August 12, 1907, the salaries of Lieut.-Col. W. C. Gorgas
and Jackson Smith, Isthmian Canal Commissioners, are fixed at
$14,000 per annum.
Theodore Roosevelt,
The White House, August 12, 1907.
