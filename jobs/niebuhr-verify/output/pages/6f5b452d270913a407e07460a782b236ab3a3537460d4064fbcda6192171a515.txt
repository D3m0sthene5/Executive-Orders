Roosevelt 32nd President of the United States: 1933 ‐ 1945 Executive Order 6405—Power-Site Restoration No. 478, Utah November 04, 1933 By virtue of the authority vested in me by the act of June 25, 1910 (ch. 421, 36 Stat. 847), as amended by the act of August 24, 1912 (ch. 369, 37 Stat. 497), so much of the Executive order of July 26, 1911, creating Power-Site Reserve No. 191, as affects the lands hereinafter described is hereby revoked: Salt Lake Meridian UTAH T. 29 S., R. 5 E., sec. 19, N½NE¼, NE¼NW¼; sec. 20, S½NE¼, S½NW¼. T. 29 S., R. 6 E., sec. 13, NW¼; sec. 14, N½NE¼, NW¼; sec. 21, SW¼NW¼; sec. 22, SE¼SW¼, S½SE¼; sec. 23, NE¼SW¼; sec. 24, S½N½, S½; sec. 26, NW¼NW¼; sec. 27, lots 1 and 2, W½NE¼. sec. 14, N½N½; sec. 15, N½NE¼, NE¼NW¼; sec. 19, N½S½; sec. 20, N½S½, SE¼SW¼, S½SE¼; sec. 21, all; sec. 22, all; sec. 23, E½NE¼, NE¼SE¼; sec. 24, N½S½.
