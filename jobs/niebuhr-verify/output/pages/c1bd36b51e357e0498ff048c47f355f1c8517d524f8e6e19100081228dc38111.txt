Roosevelt 32nd President of the United States: 1933 ‐ 1945 Executive Order 7969—Partial Revocation of Executive Order of February 19, 1920, Creating Power Site Reserve No. 730 September 10, 1938 [Power Site Restoration No. 490] OREGON By virtue of and pursuant to the authority vested in me by the act of June 25, 1910, ch. 421, 36 Stat. 847, as amended by the act of August 24, 1912, ch. 369, 37 Stat. 497, the Executive order of February 19, 1920, creating Power Site Reserve No. 730, is hereby revoked as to the following-described lands: WILLAMETTE MERIDIAN T. 4 S., R. 5 E., sec. 19, lot 1. FRANKLIN D ROOSEVELT The White House, September 10, 1938. Franklin D.
