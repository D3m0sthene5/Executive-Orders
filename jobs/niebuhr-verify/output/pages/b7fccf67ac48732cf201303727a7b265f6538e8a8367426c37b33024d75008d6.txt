The Secretary of State is hereby authorized to conclude an agreement, by exchange of notes, with any foreign country whereby citizens or subjects of such country who are not immigrants as defined in the Immigration Act of 1924, may receive passport visas for visiting the United States without payment of the application and visa fees prescribed in the Act of June 4, 1920, or upon payment of a reduced fee. in consideration of a waiver of visas or visa fees or reduction of fees being made for citizens of the United States of a similar class visiting such other country. Upon the conclusion of any such agreement the fee charges specified therein shall become effective, when and as provided therein, as if specifically stated in this order. CALVIN COOLIDGE THE WHITE HOUSE, May 15, 1925 . Calvin Coolidge, Executive Order 4224 1/2—Waiver or Reduction of Application and Visa Fees for Non-Immigrants Online by Gerhard Peters and John T.
