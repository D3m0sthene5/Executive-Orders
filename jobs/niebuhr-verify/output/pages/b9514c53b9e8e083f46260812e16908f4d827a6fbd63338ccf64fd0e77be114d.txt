Roosevelt 32nd President of the United States: 1933 ‐ 1945 Executive Order 8175—Partial Revocation of Executive Order of March 21, 1914 June 21, 1939 [Public Water Restoration No. 63] WYOMING By virtue of and pursuant to the authority vested in me by section 1 of the act of June 25, 1910, c. 421, 36 Stat. 847, the Executive Order of March 21, 1914, creating Public Water Reserve No. 18, is hereby revoked insofar as It pertains to or affects the following-described land in Wyoming: WYOMING SIXTH PRINCIPAL MERIDIAN T. 45 N., R. 64 W., sec. 28, S½SE¼ .
