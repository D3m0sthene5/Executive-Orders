Roosevelt 32nd President of the United States: 1933 ‐ 1945 Executive Order 8239—Power Site Restoration No. 492, Partial Revocation of Executive Orders of December 19, 1910, Creating Power Site Reserve No. 165 and of January 23, 1912, Creating Power Site Reserve No. 241 September 06, 1939 IDAHO By virtue of the authority vested in me by the act of June 25, 1910, ch. 421, 36 Stat. 847, it is hereby ordered as follows: 1.
