# Mabillon scan inventory: bound and digitized sets of numbered executive orders

Lists, from two keyless public catalogues, the digitized volumes of numbered executive orders and the number range
each title gives: the Wikimedia Commons category of executive-order scans (category HTML pages, recursive, at most
`JOB_MAX_PAGES` = 60 pages, one request a second, robots.txt obeyed) and the Internet Archive's advanced search
(texts titled "executive orders", 1862-1945). Downloads no scans. `SCANS.csv` has one row per volume and range,
with the count of Mabillon frame orders inside the range (a ceiling, not a verified printing); `SUMMARY.json` has
the totals and any refusal. No status, identity, census, codebook or count changes.
