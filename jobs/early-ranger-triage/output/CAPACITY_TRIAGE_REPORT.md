## Checks that cannot be skipped

Compare item images/transcripts and provenance to identify a presidential instruction as opposed to a presidential paper, secretary's independent direction, nomination or later editorial copy. Match duplicate representations before admitting an identity. Keep statutory authority, publication and operative first cessation separate.
