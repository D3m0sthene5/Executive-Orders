Corridor, north of the Yukon River.  The opening order for PLO 7966 took effect on March 27, 
2026. Accordingly, the lands subject to PLOs 5150 and 5180 are open to appropriation under the 
public land laws, including selection by the State of Alaska. As a result, the State’s top filings made 
pursuant to Sec. 906(e) of ANILCA are now effective selections.
