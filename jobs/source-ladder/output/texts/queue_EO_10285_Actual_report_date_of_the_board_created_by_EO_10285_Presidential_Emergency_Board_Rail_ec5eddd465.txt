1. Mileage limitations.-Mileage limitations, the primary subject 
of dispute, have been a much vexed question in the industry for several 
decades not only between the carriers and their employees but more 
so between the engineer and fireman crafts.

The object of mileage limitations is to regulate the working list; 
that is, the number of men to be employed as engineers at a given

carrier. 
Also, when an individual engineer has reached the maximum limit 
he will be required to lay off the balance of the month. When the 
average mileage of the engineers has dropped below the minimum

the carrier. When the volume of business may increase it becomes

demoted ones are recalled when the average mileage of those
