is $1.064 per hour (Tr. 467, 569G). Their request is for an increase 
of 20 cents per hour. (The employees before this Board will here- 
inafter be referred to as North Shore employees.)

with those of others as a justification of their demands. Such com-

same kinds of work. For some 50 years in this industry both manage- 
ment and employees for convenience in handling wage comparisons 
generally have accepted the rate of the top senior operator, bus driver, 
or motorman (Tr. 45). The rate of pay for that type of service on 
the North Shore Line is $1.125 per hour (Tr. 50).
