In view of our conclusions hereinafter

stead, we have applied to the proposed

out in §  1.365 as follows: “Requests to 
amend an application after it has been 
designated for hearing *  *  *  will
be granted only for good cause shown.” 
Consequently, the petitions are exam­

of requirements stated by that rule 
provision.
