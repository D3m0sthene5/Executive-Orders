particular class of transactions), the credit which may be

will reach the consumer in proper condition; and to pre­
scribe conditions under which a vendor of bulk wine may 
furnish proprietary and private brand labels at cost to re­
tailers bottling such wine. ,
2. To amend Section 3 (e) (3) of the regulations in such

such products, and to further provide, in the case of all 
distilled spirits, wines and malt beverages furnished to re­
tailers as samples, that a distinctive form of labeling be 
adopted and that detailed records of distribution be kept.
