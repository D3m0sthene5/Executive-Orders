R ule AN23. Temporary exemption from Sections 12 (a) and

for a period of one week from the date of consolidation, to the 
extent necessary to render lawful the effecting of transactions 
therein on the exchange on which such listed security of the con­

the word “consolidation” means a consolidation, by agreement, of

the execution or upon the filing of such agreement, a new cor­

transactions of succession in which the issuer of the new securi­

consolidation” means the date on which the new corporation 
resulting from the consolidation is created. The word “listed” 
means either (1) listed on a national securities exchange as a 
security registered pursuant to Sections 12 (b), (c) and (d), or 
(2) listed on a national securities exchange as a security exempted 
from the operation of Section 12 (a), or (3) admitted to unlisted 
trading privileges on a national securities exchange pursuant to 
Section 12 (f).
