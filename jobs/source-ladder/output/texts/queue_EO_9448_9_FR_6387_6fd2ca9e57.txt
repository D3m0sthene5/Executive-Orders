FLAG SM UT QUARANTINE
Because of a temporary national short­
age of feedstuffs and rail transportation,  
increased importations of feedstuffs haVe  
become necessary. Australia being one  
of the immediate sources of supply for  
which water transportation is available  
it is proposed as an emergency measure  
to modify the existing prohibition against  
the importation of wheat from Australia  
contained in 7 CPR 319.59 (Flag Smut
