NATIONAL MEDIATION BOARD 
WASHINGTON, D.C. 20572 
EMERGENCY BOARD NO. 165 
The dispute to be presented to Emergency Board No. 165 created 
by Executive Order 11243 was settled in conference between repre- 
sentatives of the Atchison, Topeka and Santa Fe Railway Company - 
Lines East and West and the Brotherhood of Railroad Trainmen. 
On September Z7, 1965, the Chairman of the National Mediation 
Board reported as follows to the President: 
The President 
The White House 
Washington, D. C. 2(.5C0 
Dear Mr. President: 
On September 13, 1965, you signed Executive Order 
11Z43 which created Emergency Board No. 165 to 
investigate a dispute between The Atchison, Topeka 
and Santa Fe Railway Company - Lines East and West, 
and certain of their employees represented by the 
Brotherhood of Railroad Trainmen, pursuant to Section 
1O of the Railway Labor Act. 
I am pleased to be able to advise you that on Saturday, 
September Z5th, the carrier and the labor organization 
involved in this dispute, following mediation conferences 
in Washington, D. C. with the Assistant Secretary of 
Labor, James J. Reynolds, and the undersigned, executed 
an agreement in complete disposition of the issues which 
created the emergency and required the establishment of 
Emergency Board No. 165. 
On the basis of this settlement, it will not be necessary 
to appoint the members of Emergency Board No. 165 
which you created by Executive Order 11243. 
Respectfully yours, 
Leverett Edwards 
Chairman 
National Mediation Board
