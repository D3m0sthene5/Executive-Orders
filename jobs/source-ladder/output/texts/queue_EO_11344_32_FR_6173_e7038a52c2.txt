Title 3— THE PRESIDENT
Executive Order 11344
CREATING A BOARD OF INQUIRY TO REPORT ON A LABOR DISPUTE
AFFECTING THE MILITARY AIRCRAFT INDUSTRY AND THE MILITARY
AIRCRAFT ENGINE INDUSTRY OF THE UNITED STATES
WHEREAS, there exists a labor dispute between Avco Corporation, 
a Delaware corporation, and certain of its employees at the Lycoming 
Division Plant, Stratford, Connecticut, represented by International 
Union, United Automobile, Aerospace and Agricultural Implement 
Workers of America, AFL—CIO, and Locals 376 and 1010 thereof; and
WHEREAS, such dispute threatens to result in a strike affecting a 
substantial part of an industry or industries of the United States 
engaged in trade, commerce, transportation, transmission, or communi­
cation among the several states or with foreign nations, or engaged in 
the production of goods for commerce, which strike, if permitted to 
occur or continue, would, in my opinion, imperil the national safety;
NOW, THEREFORE, by virtue of the authority vested in me by 
Section.206 of the Labor Management Relations Act of 1947 (61 Stat. 
155 ; 29 U.S.C. 176), I  hereby create a board of inquiry, consisting of 
Mr. Leo C. Brown, Chairman, Mr. James C. Hill and Mr. Clyde W. 
Summers, whom I  appoint to inquire into the issues involved in this dispute.
The board shall have powers and duties as set forth in Title I I  of 
such act. The board shall report to the President in accordance with 
the provisions of Section 206 of such act on or before April 17,1967.
Upon the submission of its report^ the board shall continue in 
existence to perform such other functions as may be required under such act.
